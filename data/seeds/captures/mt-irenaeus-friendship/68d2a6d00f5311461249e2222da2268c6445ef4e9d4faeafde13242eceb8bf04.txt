Mass at the Mountain – Every Sunday at 11 a.m.
November 22, 2026 @ 11:00 am - September 22, 2032 @ 12:00 pm
Mt. Irenaeus,
3621 Roberts Rd
Friendship ,
14739
United States
We welcome everyone to join us for Mass each Sunday at 11 a.m. held in Holy Peace Chapel. A few things to know: If you are not feeling well, please visit us at a different time. Those who might find walking to be a challenge may be dropped off at the Chapel door, and then the vehicle should be moved to the parking lot. Following Mass on Sundays, we invite you to join us for brunch. Not sure what to bring? …
