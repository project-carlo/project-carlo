Campus Ministry
The Office of Campus Ministry at Canisius University strives to help students find, share, and reflect on where they see God in their lives. We walk with students as they explore the connections between faith and justice. We are committed building community and welcome students of all faith traditions.
Some ways to get involved in Campus Ministry include:
Go on a retreat: Take a weekend away to discover more about yourself and others.
Encounter others and explore the root cause of injustice on a service-learning experience.
Serve in the city of Buffalo with one of our community partners.
Come to Mass: Sunday Mass on campus is at 9:30am and 7:00pm. We also have Weekday Mass on M-F at 12:05pm in Christ the King Chapel. All are welcome.
Join us for community nights and faith sharing groups.
Become a lector, eucharistic minister, or sing in our music group, Exalt.
Discern Your Future: Use the tools of Ignatian Discernment to guide you to a career or help make a weighty decision.
The richness of a spiritual adventure awaits you!
