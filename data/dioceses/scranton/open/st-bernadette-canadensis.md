## What is known

St. Bernadette, Canadensis (GCatholic: 1365 Route 390) was one of the three parishes
joined into Most Holy Trinity (parish site; consolidated 11/2011 per the diocese's list).
Most Holy Trinity's 18 October 2026 bulletin and its Mass-schedule page hold every Mass at
212 Route 390, Cresco, and the diocese finder lists only that site, so there is no current
Mass at St. Bernadette. Unlike St. Mary of the Mount and St. Ann (called "former ...
church buildings" by the diocese's paper), no source read says St. Bernadette's building
closed or was sold.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to closed, but nothing states it, so it is not excluded. A Catholic
Light article, a sale notice or the parish office would settle it (then `exclude
--reason closed`).
