## What is known

GCatholic lists St. Lawrence O'Toole, 620 South Main Street, Old Forge, as a church of Prince
of Peace Parish (the Old Forge parish formed in 2009). The diocesan finder lists only Saint Mary
Assumption Church for the parish, and its old ParishesOnline organization publishes nothing.
That suggests no public Mass now.

## Why it is not decided

No source read says it closed, was sold or still holds Mass, and Prince of Peace has no
bulletin to check. An exclusion needs a source that states the fact; see `revisit`.
