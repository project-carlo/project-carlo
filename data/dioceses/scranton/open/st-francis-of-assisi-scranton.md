## What is known

GCatholic lists St. Francis of Assisi Church, 630 Genet Street, as a church of St. Paul of
the Cross Parish, which merged with St. John Neumann into Saint Teresa of Calcutta Parish on
26 November 2023. The Catholic Light's article on that merger names the new parish's worship
sites as Nativity of Our Lord and Sacred Hearts only, St. Teresa of Calcutta's bulletins from
October 2024 to October 2026 never mention St. Francis or Genet Street, and the diocesan finder
lists only Sacred Hearts for the parish. So it has no public Mass now, and it was probably
closed well before 2023 (GCatholic keeps churches closed in the 2008-2010 consolidation).

## Why it is not decided

Every source read is silent about it; none says it closed, was sold or holds Mass. An
exclusion needs a source that states the fact. The `revisit` lists what would settle it.
