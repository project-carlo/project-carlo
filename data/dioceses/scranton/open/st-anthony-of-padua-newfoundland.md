## What is known

GCatholic lists "St. Anthony of Padua", 889 Main St., Route 191, Newfoundland 18445
(gcatholic:23966, with a Plus Code), and per a web search of its page calls it a mission of
St. Rita, Gouldsboro. Nothing current mentions it: the diocese's finder gives St. Rita Parish
one worship site (St. Rita); the diocese's Find a Mass page does not contain
"Newfoundland"; St. Rita's Mass Schedule page and its 4 October 2026 bulletin list only the
Gouldsboro Masses; and a web search (2026-10-16) found no schedule, closure notice or
relegation decree for it.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to a mission no longer used for Mass, but no source says it closed, so it
cannot be excluded as `closed`; and no source shows a Mass there, so it cannot be a church.
See `revisit`: a parish answer or a Catholic Light notice settles it.
