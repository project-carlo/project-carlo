## What is known

The 18 October 2026 bulletin of the Parishes of Saint Ann & Saint Boniface, Williamsport lists,
in its Regular Schedule of Masses, "Wednesday 7:00 AM at Divine Providence Hospital Chapel": the
parish's only Wednesday Mass. Divine Providence Hospital is a Williamsport hospital campus. No
roster (diocesan finder, GCatholic) lists the chapel. No street address was found: a web search
on 2026-10-16 turned up nothing for the chapel.

## Why it is not decided

A hospital chapel is usually excluded as `no-public-mass`, but this Mass is printed in the
parish's public weekly schedule alongside its churches, which suggests anyone may attend. That
is an inference, not something a source says, and without the chapel's address there is no row
to put on a map. See `revisit`.
