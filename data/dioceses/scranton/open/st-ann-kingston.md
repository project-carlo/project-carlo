## What is known

GCatholic lists "Chapel of St. Ann", Hoyt St. & Loveland Ave., Kingston, as a mission of St.
Ignatius Loyola, with a Plus Code (87H67455+XR) about 800 m from St. Ignatius. The St. Ignatius and
Holy Family bulletin (11 and 18 October 2026) schedules no Mass there; the 11 October issue says the
high-school youth ministry enjoyed "pizza after working at St. Ann's Chapel last Sunday", so the
chapel exists and is used by the parish. The diocese's finder does not list it, and a web search
found nothing else about it.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing shows a public Mass there, so it is not a church row; it is clearly not closed, so it is
not an exclusion. It may be a cemetery chapel or a chapel used only occasionally (if it has no
public Mass it becomes `no-public-mass`).
