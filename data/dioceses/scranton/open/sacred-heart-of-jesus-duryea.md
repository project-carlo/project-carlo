## What is known

GCatholic lists Sacred Heart of Jesus, 529 Stephenson St., Duryea, as a church of Nativity of Our
Lord Parish. The diocese's consolidated-parishes list (updated 1 July 2024) gives "Sacred Heart of
Jesus Church (11/2010)" as a building of that parish, the same form it uses for Holy Rosary, while
it marks St. Joseph Church, Duryea "closed 3/21/2010". The diocese's finder lists only Holy Rosary
Church for the parish, and the parish's 11 October 2026 bulletin has one schedule at 127 Stephenson
Street and never names Sacred Heart as a place (checked the 27 September and 11 October issues). A
web search found no news of a closure or sale.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing shows Mass here now, so it is not a church row; and no source says it closed (the
diocesan list, which marks closures, does not), so it is not an exclusion. It may be kept for
funerals or occasional Masses, or closed without a note on that list.
