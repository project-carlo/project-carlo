## What is known

GCatholic (23987) lists a "Church of the Queen of Angels" at 605 Church Street, Jessup, with
St. Michael's (322 First Avenue) as its mission. A web-search snippet of an old genealogy list (rootsweb,
which refused a direct read with 403) gives 605 Church Street as St. James's church, one of the four Jessup parishes merged into Queen of Angels (parish
site, About). Today the address is Queen of Angels' mailing address; the 18 October 2026
bulletin gives all weekend Masses at Saint Michael's Church and weekday Mass in "the parish
rectory" (perhaps also at 605 Church Street). The diocesan finder lists only St. Michael as
a worship site. No source I found says the 605 Church Street church was closed, relegated or
sold.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to no regular Mass in the church at 605 Church Street, but nothing says it
closed, so it is not excluded. It also cannot be a church row without evidence of Mass. Ask
the parish office (570-489-2252), or find the diocesan decree or Catholic Light report on
the St. James church; also check whether the "rectory" where weekday Mass is said is at 605
Church Street (that would be a chapel in the rectory, not the church).
