## Why it is excluded

Closed, last Mass 2 August 2026. The clergy's June 2026 letter says the bishop approved
"that Our Lady of Grace Church will close effective August 2, 2026", and the Our Lady of
Peace bulletin of 2 August 2026 (page 5) announces "The final Mass at Our Lady of Grace
church ... Sunday, August 2, 2026, beginning at 1:30 pm. At the conclusion of the Mass, the
doors will be locked to the church for the last time", the church being "relegated to
profane but not sordid use". The 26 July issue lists "Closing Mass at Our Lady of Grace is
at 1:30 P.M." The 4 October 2026 masthead names Most Precious Blood alone, and the city-
wide schedule effective 3 August 2026 has no Mass here. GCatholic still lists it (group
"Queen of Heaven Parish", the pre-merger parish); the diocese finder no longer does.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the church was relegated to profane use.
