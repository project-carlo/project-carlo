## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists this church (id 17226) among the churches in the Diocese of Scranton's territory but marks it with the Ruthenian rite: it belongs to the Byzantine Catholic Eparchy of Passaic, an Eastern Catholic jurisdiction, not to the Roman Catholic Diocese of Scranton. This is the scope rule settled in `diocese.md` (Eastern Catholic churches are `other-jurisdiction`), not a judgement about the church: it was not checked for current services. The diocesan finder does not list it. The rite field on GCatholic is the evidence.

## What would change this

A project decision to cover Eastern Catholic eparchies (here the Byzantine Catholic Eparchy of Passaic).
