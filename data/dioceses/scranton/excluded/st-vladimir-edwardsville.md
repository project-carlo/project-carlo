## Why it is excluded

St. Vladimir, 87 Zerby Ave., Edwardsville, is an Eastern Catholic church: GCatholic (17506) lists it among the churches in
the Diocese of Scranton's territory but marks it with the Ukrainian rite, so it belongs to the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia,
not to the Roman Catholic diocese. The diocese's finder does not list it. This is a scope decision
(diocese.md: Eastern Catholic churches are `other-jurisdiction`), not a judgement about whether it
holds services.
