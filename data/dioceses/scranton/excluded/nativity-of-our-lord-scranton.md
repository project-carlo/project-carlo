## Why it is excluded

Closed. Nativity of Our Lord, 633 Orchard Street, was one of Saint Teresa of Calcutta Parish's
two churches (the 6 October 2024 bulletin cover lists it with Saturday 4:30 and Sunday 9:30 and
11 am Masses). The Catholic Light reports that it "closed its doors for the final time on
Sunday, June 8, 2025", and the parish's own history page says the same. The parish's 18
October 2026 bulletin lists only Sacred Hearts and reports that the sale of "the Nativity
Church and Rectory" is close to closing. GCatholic still lists it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
