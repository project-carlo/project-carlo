## Why it is excluded

Closed. The diocese's list of church buildings of consolidated parishes (updated 2 July
2025, page 4, Hazleton) reads "St. Stanislaus (closed 10/20/2013)", with its records at
Ss. Cyril and Methodius Parish. That list writes "(closed ...)" for buildings that shut
and a bare date for buildings still in use (it gives "Saint Gabriel Church (7/1/2025)",
which holds Mass), so this entry states a closure. The current SSCM bulletin (18 October
2026) has Mass only at St. Joseph Church, the diocese finder lists no worship site at 652
Carson Street, and the Hazleton clergy's city-wide Mass schedule (effective 3 August 2026)
does not include it. `last_mass` is the list's closing date, not a dated last Mass. The
building's later use (sold or not) was not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
