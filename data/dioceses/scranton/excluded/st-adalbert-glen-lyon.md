## Why it is excluded

Closed, last Mass 11 January 2026. The Diocese of Scranton's report of the Closing Mass says
the church in Glen Lyon was "filled one final time on Sunday, Jan. 11, 2026" and that "the
decision was made to close Saint Adalbert Church and suppress Holy Spirit Parish". Holy
Spirit's other two churches moved: St. Mary, Mocanaqua to St. Faustina Kowalska Parish
(`st-mary-our-lady-of-perpetual-help-mocanaqua`) and St. Martha, Stillwater to Our Lady of
Mount Carmel (`st-martha-stillwater`). The diocese's finder no longer lists Holy Spirit
Parish; GCatholic still lists St. Adalbert under it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
