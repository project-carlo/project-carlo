## Why it is excluded

Closed; last Mass 7 June 2026. Sacred Heart of Mary, 624 Madison Avenue, Jermyn, was a
worship site of Christ the King Parish (Archbald). Christ the King's notice to parishioners
of 28 February / 1 March 2026 reports that Bishop Bambera signed a relegation decree on 18
February 2026: the church "will be permanently closed as of June 7, 2026", with a Closing
Mass that day. The diocese's news report confirms the Closing Mass was held on 7 June 2026
("the final celebration of the Eucharist in the church before its permanent closure"). The
7 June 2026 bulletin cover still named the church; the 18 October 2026 issue names only St.
Thomas Aquinas and St. Mary of Czestochowa, and the diocesan finder no longer lists it.

GCatholic (23984) still lists it, under the former "Jermyn Sacred Hearts of Jesus and Mary
Parish".

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the property's future is to be decided by the parish, but the building is
relegated to profane use.
