## Why it is excluded

Holy Ghost, 313 First Avenue, Jessup, is a Byzantine Catholic (Ruthenian) church: GCatholic
(17217) marks it Ruthenian rite, and the Queen of Angels bulletin of 18 October 2026 calls it
"HOLY GHOST BYZANTINE CHURCH" and says its priest celebrates in the Roman rite only as a
help. It belongs to the Byzantine Catholic Eparchy of Passaic, not the Diocese of Scranton
(diocese.md: Eastern Catholic churches are `other-jurisdiction`).

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the dataset may later cover Eastern Catholic eparchies separately.
