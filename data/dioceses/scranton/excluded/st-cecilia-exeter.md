## Why it is excluded

Closed on 24 April 2022. The diocese's list of church buildings of consolidated parishes (updated
1 July 2024) gives "St. Cecilia Church (closed 4/24/2022)" in Exeter, under Corpus Christi Parish,
which absorbed St. Barbara Parish (St. Anthony and St. Cecilia, Exeter) on 1 July 2024. The parish's
11 October 2026 bulletin schedules no Mass there, and the finder does not list it. GCatholic still
lists it under St. Barbara Parish. The `last_mass` date is the list's closing date, not a dated
last-Mass announcement.
