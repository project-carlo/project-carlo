## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists "St. Thomas More" as a parish at St. Joseph Church, 1625 North Main Avenue,
Scranton, among the churches in the diocese's territory, with stmscranton.org as its site. It
is a parish of the Personal Ordinariate of the Chair of Saint Peter, not of the Diocese of
Scranton: its own announcements (11 October 2026) speak of the pastor, Father Bergman,
attending "the Ordinariate Clergy Convocation", its liturgy uses the Ordinariate's "Said",
"Sung" and "Solemn" Masses, and the diocesan finder does not list it. Following Rochester's
settled rule ("Eastern Catholic churches and the Ordinariate are `other-jurisdiction`"), it
is `other-jurisdiction`. This is a scope decision: it holds public Mass and publishes weekly
PDFs on eCatholic (site id 20309), so it could become a row if the project covers the
Ordinariate.

Not checked: that "Personal Ordinariate of the Chair of Saint Peter" is named on the parish
site itself (its text mentions the Ordinariate only in the announcements read).

## What would change this

A project decision to cover Personal Ordinariate parishes (then: a parish row on
ecatholic:20309 with St. Joseph Church, 1625 N. Main Ave., as its church).
