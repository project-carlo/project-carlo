## Why it is excluded

Closed. Holy Name of Jesus Church, 1414 East Elm Street on East Mountain, was a secondary
worship site of St. John Neumann Parish (now part of Saint Teresa of Calcutta). The Catholic
Light reports its closing Mass on 17 July 2022, after which parishioners "locked the doors of
the church". The 2023 consolidation article names only Nativity and Sacred Hearts as the new
parish's worship sites, and no St. Teresa of Calcutta bulletin read (October 2024, June 2025,
January and October 2026) mentions Holy Name. GCatholic still lists it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
