## Why it is excluded

St. Clare Church is St. Paul Parish's second building and holds no public Mass. The diocesan
finder lists it as "Available for Funerals, Baptisms & Weddings" with no Mass times; St.
Paul's 18 October 2026 bulletin cover and its homepage give St. Clare a weekday Holy Hour
(3 to 4 pm, Adoration, Rosary and the Divine Mercy Chaplet) and the food pantry in its
basement, and put every Mass at St. Paul Church. Under the rule in `diocese.md` it is
`no-public-mass`. The building is open and in use, so not `closed`.

GCatholic gives its address as "N Washington Ave. & Park St."; the parish and the finder give
2235 North Washington Avenue ("Corner of Park St. & N. Washington Ave."), which is used here.

## What would change this

A Mass at St. Clare in the St. Paul bulletin's schedule.
