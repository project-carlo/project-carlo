## Why it is excluded

St. Mary, 321 Chestnut Ave., Kingston, is an Eastern Catholic church: GCatholic (17218) lists it among the churches in
the Diocese of Scranton's territory but marks it with the Ruthenian rite, so it belongs to the Byzantine Catholic Eparchy of Passaic,
not to the Roman Catholic diocese. The diocese's finder does not list it. This is a scope decision
(diocese.md: Eastern Catholic churches are `other-jurisdiction`), not a judgement about whether it
holds services.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the dataset may later cover Eastern Catholic eparchies separately.
