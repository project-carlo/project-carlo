## Why it is excluded

St. Rocco Church is one of St. John Paul II Parish's three churches, but it has no scheduled
Mass. The parish's 11 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists it with its address after a Mass
schedule that names only St. Anthony's and St. Mary's, and the diocesan finder lists it as a
worship site with no Mass times at all (St. Anthony's and St. Mary's have full schedules on the
same page). The only Mass found there in 2026 is the outdoor "Festival Mass (St. Rocco’s
Grounds)" of the annual Italian festival (2 August 2026 issue, page 4); in the issues read
(2 and 16 August, 11 October) the building otherwise appears only as a meeting and breakfast
hall. It is still parish property and not closed, so this is `no-public-mass`, not `closed`.

Address: the finder and GCatholic give 122 Kurtz Street (GCatholic: 18510), the bulletin 118
Kurtz Street, 18510.

## What would change this

A regular Mass at St. Rocco's in the parish's schedule. If the parish one day announces a
sale or closing, this becomes `closed`.
