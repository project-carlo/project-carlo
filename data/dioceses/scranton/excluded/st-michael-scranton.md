## Why it is excluded

Closed, last Mass 28 September 2025. St. Michael Church, 1703 Jackson Street, was the home of
St. Michael the Archangel Parish (the FSSP's Latin Mass parish) until it moved to St. Lucy's
Church. The Catholic Light reports that on Sunday 28 September 2025 the day began "with a final
8:00 a.m. Mass at Saint Michael Church. Following the celebration, the church officially
closed its doors", and the parish processed to Saint Lucy Church. The parish's Mass page now
prays for "the successful sale of the St. Michael’s campus", its 11 October 2026 bulletin is
headed "At St. Lucy’s Church" with no Mass at St. Michael's, and the diocesan finder lists only
St. Lucy. GCatholic still lists the Jackson Street church.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
