## Why it is excluded

St. Michael Church, 1703 Jackson Street, was the home of St. Michael the Archangel Parish (the
FSSP's Latin Mass parish) until the parish moved to St. Lucy's Church. The parish's Mass page
speaks of "the move from St. Michael’s Church (on Jackson St.) to St. Lucy’s Church" and prays
for "the successful sale of the St. Michael’s campus"; a blog post of October 2025 quotes the
diocese announcing "the closing Mass of Saint Michael Church and the first official Mass at
their new home at Saint Lucy’s Church". The parish's 11 October 2026 bulletin is headed "At
St. Lucy’s Church" and has no Mass at St. Michael's, and the diocesan finder lists only St.
Lucy as the parish's worship site. GCatholic still lists the Jackson Street church.

The closing Mass is dated only to "recently" before the October 2025 post; `last_mass` is
left empty.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. The diocese's Facebook post or a Catholic Light article would date the
closing Mass.
