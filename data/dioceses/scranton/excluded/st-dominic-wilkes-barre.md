## Why it is excluded

Closed, last Mass 11 June 2022. The diocese reported the final Mass at St. Dominic, 155
Austin Avenue (Parsons, Wilkes-Barre), then a secondary worship site of Saints Peter & Paul
Parish, Plains (`saints-peter-paul-plains`). The parish kept the building as its food pantry
and clothing closet, so it still stands, but holds no Mass: the Plains bulletin lists only
its own church. GCatholic still groups it under "St. Benedict Parish".

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
