## Why it is excluded

Closed, last Mass 12 January 2025. The diocese reported the final Mass at St. Mary's Church
of the Immaculate Conception, 134 S. Washington Street, celebrated by Fr. Joseph Verespy,
pastor of St. Nicholas-St. Mary Parish (`st-nicholas-st-mary-wilkes-barre`), after which the
church "closed its doors for the final time". The parish's current bulletin and the diocese's
finder list St. Nicholas as its only church. GCatholic still lists St. Mary's under "Our Lady
of Fatima Parish".
