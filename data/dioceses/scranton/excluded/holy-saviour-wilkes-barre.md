## Why it is excluded

Closed. GCatholic lists Holy Saviour, 54 Hillard St., Wilkes-Barre under St. Andre Bessette
Parish. The diocese's 22 May 2022 notice on suppressing that parish describes it as having
"as its sole church, Saint Stanislaus Kostka", so Holy Saviour had already stopped being a
worship site by then, and St. Andre Bessette itself was suppressed into Saints Peter & Paul,
Plains in July 2022, whose bulletin lists only its Plains church. A WNEP story titled "Final
Mass at Holy Saviour" (not readable here) reportedly gives a 29 June final Mass; its year
was not confirmed, so `last_mass` is left empty.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. The WNEP article, read in a browser that it admits, would date the last
Mass.
