## Why it is excluded

Closed. The Immaculate Heart of Mary Chapel, 1605 Oram Street, West Scranton, was a secondary
worship site of Saint Patrick Parish. The Catholic Light reports its Closing Mass on Saturday
27 June 2026 at 5:30 pm, celebrated by the pastor, Father Richard Fox. The diocesan finder now
lists only Saint Patrick Church for the parish. GCatholic still lists the chapel.
