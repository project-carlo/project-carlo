## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists it among the churches in the Diocese of Scranton's territory but marks it
with the Ukrainian rite: it belongs to the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not to the Latin diocese. Under the rule
settled in `diocese.md`, Eastern Catholic churches are excluded as `other-jurisdiction`.
This is a scope decision, not a judgement about whether the church is open. The diocese's
Find a Parish finder does not list it.

## What would change this

A project decision to cover Eastern Catholic eparchies.
