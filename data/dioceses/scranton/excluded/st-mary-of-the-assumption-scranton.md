## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists a "Shrine of St. Mary of the Assumption" at 422 South River Street with the
history line "Fraternité Notre-Dame 2020": the former St. Mary of the Assumption church has
been held since 2020 by the Fraternité Notre-Dame, an order "not in union with the Pope". It
is not a church of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Scranton: the diocesan finder lists nothing
at this address and no diocesan parish names it. `not-catholic` is used in the sense of
not in communion with the Catholic Church; this is a scope decision, as for other
non-Catholic churches in a roster.

Not checked: whether the Fraternité still holds services there (it has no site or bulletin
that turned up). That does not affect the exclusion.

## What would change this

A reconciliation of the Fraternité with Rome, or the building returning to the diocese.
