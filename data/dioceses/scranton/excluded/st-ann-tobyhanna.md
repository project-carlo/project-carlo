## Why it is excluded

Closed. The diocese's paper, describing the new Most Holy Trinity church in 2022, says its
pews "all come from the former Saint Mary and Saint Ann church buildings", i.e. the
buildings no longer serve as churches. Most Holy Trinity Parish (formed from St. Mary of
the Mount, St. Ann and St. Bernadette; consolidated 11/2011 per the diocese's list) now
holds every Mass at 212 Route 390, Cresco: the 18 October 2026 bulletin and the parish's
Mass-schedule page list no other place, and the diocese finder lists only that site.
GCatholic still lists St. Ann, Tobyhanna at Main St. No last-Mass date was found.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
