## Why it is excluded

Closed; last Mass 2 June 2024. The diocese's own news report says a closing liturgy was held
at Saint Michael's Church, Simpson, on 2 June 2024, "one final time", ending its use after
121 years. Its successor parish, Our Lady of the Rosary (Carbondale), lists Masses only at
St. Rose of Lima and Our Lady of Mt. Carmel in its 11 October 2026 bulletin, and the
diocesan finder lists only those two worship sites. GCatholic (23932) still lists St.
Michael's at 46 Midland Street as a mission of St. Rose of Lima.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
