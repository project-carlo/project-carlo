## Why it is excluded

Closed, last Mass 19 September 2021. The diocese's report: a closing Mass at 1:00 p.m. on
Sunday 19 September 2021 celebrated by Fr. Vincent H. Dang, after which the doors "of Holy
Family Church" were ceremonially locked; parishioners now belong to Saint Leo the Great
Parish, Ashley (`st-leo-ashley-st-andrew-wilkes-barre`), whose bulletin masthead still
names Holy Family among the parishes it was formed from but lists no Mass there.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
