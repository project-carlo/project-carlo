## Why it is excluded

Closed, last Mass 19 June 2022. The diocese's 22 May 2022 notice says St. Stanislaus Kostka
Church, the sole church of St. Andre Bessette Parish, "will be closed (... relegated to
profane but not sordid use) on Sunday, June 19, 2022", and that the parish would be
suppressed into Saints Peter & Paul Parish, Plains (`saints-peter-paul-plains`). The
diocese reported the closing Mass on that date. GCatholic gives 666 N Main St.; the decree
gives 668.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
