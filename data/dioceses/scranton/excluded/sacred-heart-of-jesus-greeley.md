## Why it is excluded

Closed: relegated to profane use by decree. St. Ann Parish's own site has a page for
"Sacred Heart of Jesus Greeley, Pennsylvania" that lists the steps (decree issued and
published, recourse period 8-19 May) and says "Relegation went into effect June 16th with
a closing Mass at 6:00pm." The decree, linked from that page and dated 11 April 2023,
reduces "Sacred Heart of Jesus Church, located at 228 Route 590, Greeley" to profane use
"effective with the closing Mass on June 16, 2023". GCatholic still lists it; the
diocese's finder does not, and the joint St. Ann / St. John Neumann / St. Vincent de Paul
bulletin of 18 October 2026 does not mention it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: a relegated church does not return to worship.
