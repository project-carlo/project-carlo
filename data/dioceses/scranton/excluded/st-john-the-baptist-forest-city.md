## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists it among the churches in the Diocese of Scranton's territory but marks it
with the Ruthenian rite (read in `rosters/gcatholic.json`, 2026-10-16): it belongs to the
Byzantine Catholic Eparchy of Passaic, not to the Roman Catholic diocese. Under this
diocese's settled rule (`diocese.md`, as in Syracuse) Eastern Catholic churches are
excluded as `other-jurisdiction`. This is a scope decision, not a judgement about whether
the church is open. The diocese's Find a Parish lists no such site; the Latin parish in
Forest City is Ascension (St. Joseph, 741 Delaware St.), whose bulletin does not mention it.

## What would change this

A project decision to cover the Eastern Catholic eparchies (here the Ruthenian Eparchy of
Passaic).
