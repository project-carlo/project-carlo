## Why it is excluded

Ascension of Our Lord, 110 N Higgins Ave., Sayre is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church. Its
GCatholic page (read 2026-10-16) gives "Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of
Philadelphia", "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Scranton", "Rite: Ukrainian
(Byzantine)". It is in the Scranton roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by
territory. The diocese's own finder does not list it, and Epiphany Parish, Sayre's bulletin
does not mention it. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`
for Eastern Catholic churches; whether it is open was not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it would belong to an Archeparchy of Philadelphia dataset, not Scranton's.
