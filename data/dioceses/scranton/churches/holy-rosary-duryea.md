## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 11 October 2026 Nativity of Our Lord bulletin gives the parish's schedule
(weekdays 7 am, Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 8 am, 11 am and 7 pm) under the masthead address 127
Stephenson Street, without naming a church. That the Masses are here comes from the diocese's
finder, which lists Holy Rosary Church at 127 Stephenson Street as the parish's only worship site
with the same times.

## Name and address

The finder ("Holy Rosary Church", 127 Stephenson Street, Duryea 18642) and GCatholic ("Holy Rosary",
127 Stephenson St., 18642) agree, and it is the bulletin's masthead address; both flags are true.
