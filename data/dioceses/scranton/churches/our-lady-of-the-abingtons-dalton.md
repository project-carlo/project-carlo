## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 bulletin of the three NEPA Catholic parishes (page 2) lists the
Saturday 4:00 pm Mass and the Tuesday 8:00 am Mass at OLA; the diocesan finder gives the same
(Tue 8:00 am, Sat 4:00 pm).

## Name and address

The name agrees everywhere (finder, GCatholic, bulletin), so name_verified is true. The
address does not:

- The diocesan finder's worship-site entry gives **207 Seminary Road**, Dalton 18414.
- The finder's parish header and GCatholic (23939) give **700 West Main Street**, Dalton.

I kept GCatholic's 700 West Main Street and its Plus Code (41.5429, -75.7636), because two
sources give it, but address_verified stays false. OpenStreetMap places Seminary Road about
400 m east of the Plus Code point, so 207 Seminary Road may be the rectory or office, or the
finder may be right about a corner building. The bulletin gives no street address. A look
at the building (Street View or OSM) would settle it.
