## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin (Discover Mass, page 2) lists Masses on Tuesday
and Thursday at 8 am, Saturday 4 pm and Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 am, the same schedule as the
parish homepage and the diocesan finder.

## Name and address

The parish site and the diocesan finder give 214 Blue Shutters Road, Roaring Brook Township
(the finder abbreviates "Roaring Brook Twp."), 18444; GCatholic gives "St. Eulalia", 214 Blue
Shutters Rd., Roaring Brook, 18444. The Discover Mass slug and the mailing town are Elmhurst,
the post office for 18444. Name and address agree across the parish and two independent
sources, so both flags are true; the town follows the parish and the finder. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code (87H69F73+QP).
