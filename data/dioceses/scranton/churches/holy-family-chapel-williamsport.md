## Holds Mass

Yes, every weekday. The 18 October 2026 St. Joseph the Worker bulletin: "Daily Masses: Monday thru
Friday 12:10pm in the Holy Family Chapel, 720 W. Fourth St.", and the week's intentions mark them
"(HF)". The chapel is open for prayer during office hours, so these are public Masses.

church at 702); the diocesan finder names "Holy Family Chapel" in Annunciation's Mass times but
no address, and GCatholic does not list it. One source, so both flags are false. It is in no
roster, which `coverage` will report under churches in no roster. No coordinates; `pnpm geocode`
