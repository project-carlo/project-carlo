## Holds Mass

Yes. The 17-18 October 2026 Communion of Saints bulletin schedules Sunday 8 am and Tuesday and
Thursday 8 am here, and the parish's St. Jude Novena (19-28 October) is held here. The finder lists
only the Tuesday and Thursday Masses; the bulletin, which is current, adds the Sunday 8 am.

## Name and address

The finder ("Saint Mary, Help of Christians Church", 535 North Main Street, Pittston 18640),
GCatholic ("St. Mary, Help of Christians", 535 N Main St., 18640-2244) and the bulletin (535 North
Main Street) agree; both flags are true. GCatholic groups it under the former Our Lady of the
Eucharist Parish. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H686P7+XC).
