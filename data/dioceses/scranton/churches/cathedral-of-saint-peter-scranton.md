## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 11 October 2026 bulletin (page 2) gives Monday to Friday 6:30 am and
12:10 pm (livestreamed and on CTV), Saturday confessions 10:30 to 11:30 am and vigil 5:30 pm,
and Sunday 6:30, 10, 12:15 and 5. The diocesan finder lists the same times.

## Name and address

The Cathedral of Saint Peter, 315 Wyoming Avenue, Scranton 18503, in the bulletin masthead,
the cathedral site, the diocesan finder and the ParishesOnline organization. GCatholic's
roster for the diocese does not list the cathedral, so the only source independent of the
parish is the diocese's finder. That is one independent source, not two, so the flags stay
false until OSM or GCatholic is checked. Coordinates are the ParishesOnline organization's
point (parishes-online-org), not a building-precise source.
