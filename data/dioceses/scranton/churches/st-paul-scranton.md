## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 18 October 2026 bulletin cover (an image) and the parish homepage give Mass
Monday to Saturday 8 am, Saturday 4:30 pm vigil and Sunday 8 and 10:30 am at St. Paul Church.

## Name and address

St. Paul Church, 1510 Penn Avenue, Scranton 18509: in the bulletin, the parish site, the
diocesan finder, the ParishesOnline organization (18509-2388) and GCatholic ("St. Paul",
1510 Penn Ave., 18509-2352). They agree, and GCatholic and the finder are independent of the
parish, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H6C9G2+7F); the
ParishesOnline point (41.426017, -75.64885) is within about 60 m.
