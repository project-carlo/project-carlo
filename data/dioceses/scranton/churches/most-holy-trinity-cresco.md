## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 8 am, 10:30 am and noon
(Spanish), weekdays 8 am and Wednesday 7 pm (Spanish). The weekday Masses are in the day
chapel of the same building (the parish site; the diocese finder calls it the "Msgr.
McHugh Center"), so they are not a separate worship site.

## Name and address

212 Route 390, Cresco 18326 in the bulletin, the parish site and the diocese finder (whose
worship site is named "Most Holy Trinity Parish"); both flags true. The church, dedicated
15 May 2022, is in the former Msgr. McHugh School gymnasium (diocese article). GCatholic
has no entry for this building: it lists the three pre-merger churches instead (see the
parish row), so only the directory entry links here. Coordinates are the ParishesOnline
organization's point (41.132048, -75.29855), not building-checked.
