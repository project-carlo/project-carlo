## Holds Mass

Yes, every weekend. The Mass Schedule on page 1 of the 11 October 2026 bulletin of
Ascension and St. Katharine Drexel parishes (an image; read rendered) has Saturday Vigil
4:00 PM, Sunday 9:30 AM and Monday and Wednesday 8:30 AM at St. Joseph, with a Friday 8:30
communion service. The parish homepage, read the same day, gives the same weekend times.
It is the church of the canonical Ascension Parish.

## Name and address

The diocese's finder ("Saint Joseph Church", 741 Delaware Street, Forest City 18421) and
GCatholic ("St. Joseph", 741 Delaware St., Forest City, group Ascension Parish) agree, and
the bulletin and homepage call it St. Joseph, Forest City, so both flags are true.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H6MG3J+3X). Discover Mass's slug for it
(`st-joseph-slovenian-forest-city-pa`) suggests it was the Slovenian national church before
the 2008-2010 merger.
