## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 11 October 2026 St. Monica bulletin gives Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 8:30 and 11 am and
Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 8 am under the parish address, 363 West 8th St. It names the
parish, not the building; the finder places the parish's one worship site, Our Lady of Sorrows,
there. The finder's times differ on Friday (12:10 pm, with Adoration 12:30-4:30 pm); the bulletin,
which is current, says Friday 8 am followed by Adoration.

## Name and address

The finder ("Our Lady of Sorrows Church", 363 West Eighth Street, West Wyoming 18644), GCatholic
("Our Lady of Sorrows", 363 W 8th St., 18644, under St. Monica Parish) and the bulletin masthead
(363 West 8th St.) agree; both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H68594+2M).
