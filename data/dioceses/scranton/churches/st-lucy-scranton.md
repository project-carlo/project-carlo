## Holds Mass

Yes, daily (Traditional Latin Mass). The 11 October 2026 St. Michael the Archangel bulletin
(pages 1-2) and the parish's Mass page give Sunday 8 am (Low) and 10:30 am (High),
Monday-Thursday 7:30 am, Friday 6 pm and Saturday 9 am at St. Lucy's; the diocesan finder
lists the same.
