## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 11 October 2026 St. John Paul II Parish bulletin (page 1) schedules Monday to
Friday 8 am, Saturday 5:15 pm and Sunday 8:30 and 11:15 am at St. Anthony's, plus Monday
confessions 5:30 to 6:30 pm. The parish homepage and the diocesan finder give the same times.

## Name and address

The bulletin and the diocesan finder call it St. Anthony of Padua Church, 208 Smith Street,
Dunmore 18512; GCatholic has "St. Anthony", 208 Smith St., 18512-2919. Name and address agree
across the parish and two independent sources, so both flags are true. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code (87H6C98G+WX) for the building.
