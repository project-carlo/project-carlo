## Holds Mass

Yes, daily and at weekends. The 11 October 2026 bulletin (page 2) has Saturday 4:00pm
(bilingual), Sunday 12:30pm and Monday and Thursday noon; from 31 October (page 7) the
weekend becomes Saturday 6:30pm, Sunday 8:00am (English) and Sunday 1:00pm (Spanish).
