## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 bulletin of Holy Name of Mary, Montrose (same pastor, which now
carries St. Brigid Parish's schedule) has Saturday 6:30pm, Sunday 7:30am and Friday noon
here; from 31 October the Saturday Mass moves to 4:00pm (page 7). The diocese's finder
gives the same Saturday, Sunday and Friday times. St. Francis Xavier is the main church of
the canonical St. Brigid Parish.

## Name and address

The name agrees everywhere (finder "Saint Francis Xavier Church", GCatholic "St. Francis
Xavier", bulletin "St. Francis Xavier Friendsville"), so `name_verified` is true. The
street does not:

- the finder: 17 Cottage Street, Friendsville 18818, which is also the parish office's
  address on the St. Brigid site ("PO Box 75, 17 Cottage St.");
- GCatholic: Main St., Friendsville; St. Brigid's "Our Churches" page also puts the church
  "on Rt. 4014 (Main Street)".

I kept the finder's address (the roster's) but left `address_verified` false: the church
may stand on Main Street with the rectory at 17 Cottage Street. Coordinates are GCatholic's
Plus Code (87H5WX92+2X), on the building by its own account; a map check would settle the
street.
