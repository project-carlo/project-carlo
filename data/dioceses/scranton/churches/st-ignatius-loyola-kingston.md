## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 18 October 2026 bulletin's cover schedules St. Ignatius Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 7 and
10:30 am, and Monday-Friday 7:30 am. The finder's schedule is the same.

## Name and address

The bulletin cover gives 339 North Maple Avenue, Kingston 18704, and GCatholic ("St. Ignatius
Loyola", 339 N Maple Ave.) and the finder's parish address ("339 North Maple Avenue") agree. The
finder's worship-site entry says "339 North Maple Street"; there is no North Maple Street in
Kingston that the other sources know of, so Avenue is followed. Name: the finder's "Saint Ignatius
Church" and GCatholic's "St. Ignatius Loyola" are the same dedication. Both flags are true.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H67484+G3).
