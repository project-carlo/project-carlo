Yes. The 11 October 2026 St. John Vianney bulletin lists Sunday 9:30 am and Wednesday 6:00
pm at S.P. (St. Pius X), and page 2 moves the Wednesday Mass to noon from November. The

gives 3615 State Route 106, Royal, PA 18413; GCatholic (24036) gives "Route 106, Royal",
Clifford, PA 18470, with no number. Same road and hamlet; the ZIP codes differ (18413 is
Clifford, 18470 Union Dale). I kept the finder's numbered address and left
address_verified false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code for 24036.
