## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 Queen of Angels bulletin gives the parish's weekend Masses
(Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 am) at Saint Michael's Church; the parish homepage
gives the same weekend times. The diocesan finder also lists weekday 8:00 am Masses here,
but the bulletin currently has weekday Mass at 5:30 pm in the rectory instead.

## Name and address

The diocesan finder and GCatholic (23988) give 322 First Avenue; the bulletin masthead
gives 320 First Avenue. Probably one lot with two numbers; I kept 322 (two sources) and left
address_verified false. Name agrees (St. Michael); name_verified true. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code.
