## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 St. Leo / St. Andrew bulletin lists Saturday 5:30 p.m. and Sunday
11:00 a.m. at "St. Andrew" (and holy days 5:15 p.m.); it says St. Andrew "only has Mass on
weekends and holydays".

## Name and address

The building is St. Patrick Church, the only worship site of St. Andrew Parish: the
diocese's finder lists "Saint Patrick Church" under Saint Andrew Parish and GCatholic lists
"St. Patrick" with group "St. Andrew Parish", both at 316 Parrish Street, Wilkes-Barre
18702, the address the bulletin gives for St. Andrew Parish. The bulletin itself says "St.
Andrew", so the name records both. Both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
