## Holds Mass

Yes, three times a year: the St. John Neumann section of the joint bulletin's standing
schedule (page 11, 11 October 2026) says Good Shepherd has a Saturday 5:30pm Mass on
Memorial Day, July Fourth and Labor Day weekends, and the 30 August 2026 issue's
intentions list a 5:30pm Mass at "(GS)" for Labor Day weekend. A few Masses a year still
makes it a worship site. The diocese's finder has an older pattern ("Sun: 7:00am (Memorial
Day – Columbus Day)"); the bulletin wins.

## Name and address

The finder ("Good Shepherd Church", Blooming Grove 18428, no street) and GCatholic ("Good
Shepherd", Route 402, Blooming Grove) agree on name and town, so `name_verified` is true;
only GCatholic gives a street (Route 402), so `address_verified` stays false. Coordinates
are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H69VCF+G4).
