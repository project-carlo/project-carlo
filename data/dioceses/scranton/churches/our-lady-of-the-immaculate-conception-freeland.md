## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (page 4) lists Masses here on Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 11
am, Monday 7 pm and Tuesday to Friday 7 am; the parish site's Mass schedule gives the same
weekend times.

## Name and address

Address: 898 Centre Street, Freeland, in the parish site, the diocese finder and GCatholic
(address flag true). Name: the sources disagree. The parish site and bulletin call the
church "Immaculate Conception" ("Immaculate Conception Church", "Mass of Remembrance will
be held at Immaculate Conception"); GCatholic calls it "Our Lady of the Immaculate
Conception"; the diocese finder lists the worship site as "Saint Ann Church". The diocese's
consolidated-parishes list gives "Freeland St. Ann (closed 2008)", so the finder's name is
probably the building's pre-2008 name, kept after the merger into Our Lady of the
Immaculate Conception (an inference; no source says the building was St. Ann's). The
parish's own usage is followed; `name_verified` stays false. Coordinates are GCatholic's
Plus Code (87H62492+XW).
