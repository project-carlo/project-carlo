## Holds Mass

Yes, three times a year. The 18 October 2026 Immaculate Heart of Mary bulletin: "Sts. Philip &
James / St. John Neumann Shrine holds three masses annually. Masses of the 2027 season begin at
2:30 PM and are as follows: Sunday, July 11; Sunday, August 1; Sunday, September 5 (Healing
Mass)". A place with a few public Masses a year counts as a church row; the site will show its
Masses only in season.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it "Sts. Philip and James Church / The Bishop Neumann Shrine, 1082 Burke Road,
New Albany, Pa 18833" and, in the masthead, "Sts. Philip and James, St. John Neumann Shrine,
Sugar Ridge" (Sugar Ridge is the locality; New Albany the post office). The diocese's Find a Mass
page lists "Saint John Neumann Shrine, Sugar Ridge, PA" with no address. Neither GCatholic nor the
diocesan finder's worship-site list has it, so it has no roster reference. Only the parish gives
the full name and the street address, so both flags are false. No coordinates: Nominatim found
nothing for the address or the shrine name on 2026-10-16; `pnpm geocode` will try after
promotion.
