## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 18 October 2026 Immaculate Heart of Mary bulletin says daily Mass "is held at St.
Basil the Great, Dushore Monday—Friday at 9:00 AM", the Saturday vigil at 4:00 pm, and Sunday
9:30 am there.

## Name and address

- The bulletin's Worship Sites list and GCatholic: 101 Churchill Street, Dushore 18614 (the
  ParishesOnline organization also gives 101 Churchill Street).
- The diocesan finder: 123 Churchill Street.

Followed the bulletin and GCatholic; `address_verified` stays false because the diocese
disagrees (123 may be the old rectory; the bulletin gives the parish office as 1 St. Francis
Drive, Mildred, and its mail as PO Box 307, Dushore). The name agrees everywhere. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code (87H5GHGX+62); the ParishesOnline point (41.5271, -76.4014) is within
about 200 m.
