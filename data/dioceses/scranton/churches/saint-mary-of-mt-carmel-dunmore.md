## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 11 October 2026 St. John Paul II Parish bulletin (page 1) schedules Monday to
Friday noon, Saturday 4 pm and Sunday 10 am at St. Mary's. The parish homepage and the
diocesan finder give the same times.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it St. Mary of Mount Carmel Church and the finder "Saint Mary of Mt.
Carmel Church", both at 322 Chestnut Street, Dunmore 18512; GCatholic lists it as "Our Lady of
Mount Carmel" (its name before the parish merger), 322 Chestnut St., 18512-2902. The address
agrees in all three. The name differs only in GCatholic's older dedication title, and the
bulletin and finder agree, so both flags are true; the name follows the bulletin.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H6C98C+Q7).
