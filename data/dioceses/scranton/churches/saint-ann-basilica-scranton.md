## Holds Mass

Yes, several times daily. The 11 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, an image) and the parish's
bulletin page give Monday novena Masses at 8 am, noon, 3:30 and 6 pm; Tuesday to Friday
8:30 am and 7 pm; Saturday 8:30 am and 4 pm; Sunday 8:30 am, 11 am and 7 pm.

## Name and address

The bulletin gives the rectory as 1250 Saint Ann Street, Scranton 18504, and the diocesan
finder lists "Saint Ann Basilica" at 1250 St. Ann Street, 18504. GCatholic's roster for the
diocese does not include the basilica (GCatholic pages name it "Basilica of the National Shrine
of St. Ann", but it is not in the fetched list), so there is no second independent source
yet and both flags stay false. No coordinates: `geocode` fills them after promotion.
