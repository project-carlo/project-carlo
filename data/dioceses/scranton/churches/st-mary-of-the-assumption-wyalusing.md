## Holds Mass

Yes. Its own parish's bulletin could not be read (olophparish.org is behind Cloudflare), but the
18 October 2026 bulletin of Saints Peter and Paul, Towanda, whose pastor (Rev. Jose Joseph
Kuriappilly) also has Wyalusing, prints "Schedule at Our Lady of Perpetual Help, Wyalusing":
Saturday 6:00 pm, Sunday 11:00 am, Wednesday and Friday 12:00 pm. The Towanda homepage gives the
same times. The diocesan finder's older times (Saturday 5:00 pm; Monday, Tuesday, Thursday noon)
differ; the Towanda bulletin is newer and wins.

## Name and address

The diocesan finder ("Saint Mary of the Assumption Church, 245 State Street, Wyalusing 18853") and
GCatholic ("St. Mary of the Assumption", 245 State St., grouped under Our Lady of Perpetual Help
Parish) agree, as does Discover Mass's listing for the parish (245 State St.). The parish is
"Our Lady of Perpetual Help"; the church building is St. Mary of the Assumption. Both flags true.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H5MP8M+XG).
