## Holds Mass

Yes, according to the diocesan finder: Sunday 8:30 and 11 am, Monday to Friday 8 am in the
lower church, Saturday 4 pm. No bulletin was found to confirm it (the parish row is
`no_bulletin`); the Catholic Light's account of the IHM Chapel's closing (June 2026) shows
the parish active under its pastor and moving the chapel's community to Saint Patrick Church.
