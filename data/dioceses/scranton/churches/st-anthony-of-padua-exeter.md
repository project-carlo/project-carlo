## Holds Mass

Yes, weekends. The 11 October 2026 Corpus Christi bulletin schedules Saturday 4 pm and Sunday
7:30 am at St. Anthony, with Saturday confessions there. The finder gives the same times.

## Name and address

The name agrees everywhere (St. Anthony of Padua). **The street number does not:** the parish's
own homepage says 224 Memorial Street, the finder 212 Memorial Avenue, and GCatholic 28 Memorial
Ave. (all Exeter 18643). `line1` follows the parish site, the source closest to the building, but
`address_verified` stays false until a second source agrees (OpenStreetMap or a bulletin masthead
with the street). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H685FR+F7), which places the building
regardless of the number. GCatholic still groups it under St. Barbara Parish, merged into Corpus
Christi on 1 July 2024.
