## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 Christ the King bulletin (page 1, an image) lists Saturday 4:00 pm
and Sunday 9:00 and 10:30 am here, and says all daily Masses and Adoration are at St. Thomas
Aquinas Church (Monday, Wednesday, Friday 7:30 am; Tuesday, Thursday 12:00 pm). The diocesan
finder gives the same times.

## Name and address

429 Church Street, Archbald: the diocesan finder, GCatholic (23916) and the bulletin footer
agree, so both flags are true. The parish office (church-bulletin.org listing) is at 411
Church Street next door. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
