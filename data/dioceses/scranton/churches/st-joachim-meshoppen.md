## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 Nativity BVM bulletin (pages 1-2) gives Sunday 9:00 am and Thursday
9:00 am at St. Joachim. The finder's times differ slightly (Sunday 9:00 am, First Friday
12:15 and 6:30 pm); the bulletin was followed.

(24004) and the bulletin agree; both flags true. GCatholic groups it under "Our Lady of
Perpetual Help Parish", which the finder and bulletin contradict (it is Nativity BVM's).
