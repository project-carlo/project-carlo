## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 Abington Catholic bulletin (page 5) lists the Saturday 4:00 pm
vigil Mass at St. Benedict's; abingtoncatholic.org and the diocesan finder give the same
(Saturday 4:00 pm only).

## Name and address

The diocesan finder and the parish site give 1849 Newton-Ransom Boulevard, Clarks Summit;
GCatholic (23936) gives "Newton-Ransom Blvd." with no number. Same road, so both flags true,
using the numbered address. Clarks Summit is the postal town; GCatholic's Plus Code (used
for the coordinates) puts the building in Newton Township, west of Clarks Summit.
