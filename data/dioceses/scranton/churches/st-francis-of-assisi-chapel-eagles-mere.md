## Holds Mass

Yes, in summer only. The 18 October 2026 Immaculate Heart of Mary bulletin's "Seasonal
Offerings": "St. Francis of Assisi Chapel in Eagles Mere is open July 4, 2027 through September
5, 2027. Mass is held at 11:30 AM." The diocesan finder says "Sun: 11:00am (July 4th – Labor
Day)"; the bulletin's 11:30 is the newer statement. A summer chapel is a place with public Mass,
so it is a church row; the schedule will show nothing from September to July.

## Name and address

The bulletin and the diocesan finder both give St. Francis of Assisi Chapel, 226 Eagles Mere
Avenue, Eagles Mere 17731 (parish and diocese, two sources), so both flags are true. GCatholic
does not list the chapel. No coordinates: `pnpm geocode` will fill them after promotion.
