## Holds Mass

Yes, Sunday 8:00 am only. The 18 October 2026 bulletin of St. Michael Parish, Canton (page 2)
lists "8:00 am, Ralston" on Sundays 18 and 25 October, and the masthead says the bulletin covers
"worship sites in Ralston, Canton & Troy". The parish Mass-times page names it "Saint Aloysius
Church, Ralston".

## Name and address

Sources disagree on the address, so `address_verified` is false:

- GCatholic: "Green St. & Division Alley, Ralston, Pennsylvania 17771".
- The parish's About page: "Saint Aloysius Church at the corner of Green Street and Division
  Alley, Ralston".
- The diocesan finder: "10969 Route 14 (Ralston Fire Department), Ralston, PA 17763".

Followed the parish and GCatholic (Green Street and Division Alley) for the street: the finder's
parenthesis suggests either that Mass moved to the fire hall or that the finder gives a
mailing/landmark address; the bulletin only says "Ralston". The ZIP is 17763 (Ralston's own,
as the finder has it); GCatholic's 17771 is Trout Run's. What would settle it: a bulletin or
parish notice saying where the Ralston Mass is held, or a look at the church on a map/street
view. Name agrees everywhere (St. Aloysius). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code
(87H5G23W+P6).
