## Holds Mass

Yes, Saturday 7:00 pm. The 18 October 2026 bulletin of the Parishes of St. Peter and St. Thomas
the Apostle lists "Saturday: 7:00pm (St. Catherine)" in St. Thomas's weekend schedule and names
St. Catherine of Siena as St. Thomas's mission church. The finder also has Saturday 7:00 pm.

## Name and address

The bulletin and GCatholic (a "Mission") give 146 Lincoln Street, Westfield 16950; the finder
calls it "Saint Catherine Church" at the same address. The bulletin's full name "St. Catherine
of Siena" is used. Two independent sources agree, so both flags are true. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code (87H4WFC4+4G).
