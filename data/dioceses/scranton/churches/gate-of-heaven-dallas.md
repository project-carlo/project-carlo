## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 18 October 2026 bulletin of Gate of Heaven and Our Lady of Victory (container
04/0475, page 1) schedules Saturday 5:30 pm and Sunday 9 am at GOH, plus Monday 6 pm and Wednesday
9 am. The same page says renovations at Gate of Heaven have moved Saturday confessions to Our Lady of
Victory for the rest of 2026, but no Mass is moved. The finder's schedule (Sun 9:00am, Mon 6:00pm,
Wed 9:00am, Sat 5:30pm) agrees.

## Name and address

The diocese's finder ("Gate of Heaven Church", 40 Machell Avenue, Dallas 18612) and GCatholic
("Gate of Heaven", 40 Machell Ave., 18612-1611) agree, and the bulletin's page 4 gives 40 Machell
Avenue too, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H682MM+XQ), on the
building; the ParishesOnline organization's point (41.33465, -75.96826) is about 230 m west.
