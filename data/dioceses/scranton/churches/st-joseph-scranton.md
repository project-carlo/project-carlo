## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 18 October 2026 Divine Mercy bulletin cover gives Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 8 and
10 am and weekdays noon; the diocesan finder lists the same times for Saint Joseph Church.

Parish) both give 312 Davis Street, Scranton 18505, the address on the bulletin cover. Two
independent sources agree with the parish, so both flags are true. The bulletin itself names
the parish rather than the church; the church name follows the finder. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code (87H698H3+JH).
