## Holds Mass

Yes, seasonally. Every weekly issue of the joint bulletin from 2 August to 4 October 2026
has the Saturday 5:30pm vigil at St. Mary's, Lackawaxen ("(SM)"); from Saturday 17 October
the vigil moves to St. Ann's, and page 11 of the 11 and 18 October issues says "St.
Mary's Church - Closed for Season". The finder agrees: "Sat: 5:30pm (Vigil – Summer/Fall)".
So a weekly Mass for about half the year; it stays a church row.

## Name and address

The finder ("Saint Mary of the Assumption Church", 720 Route 590, Lackawaxen) and
GCatholic ("St. Mary of the Assumption", 720 Route 590, Lackawaxen) agree, so both flags
are true. The ZIP differs (finder 18435, GCatholic 18428); the record has GCatholic's.
Neither gives coordinates (GCatholic has no Plus Code for it), so they are left for geocoding.
