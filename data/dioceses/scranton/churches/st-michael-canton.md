## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (page 2, "LITURGY SCHEDULE") has the Saturday 4:00 pm
vigil and Sunday 9:30 am Mass at Canton, and a Wednesday 5:30 pm Mass followed by a holy hour;
the Monday and Friday 7 am Masses are in the rectory chapel next door. The parish's Mass-times
page gives the same Sunday time.

## Name and address

GCatholic has "St. Michael", 107 N Washington St., Canton 17724, and the parish's About page
"Saint Michael the Archangel Church, 107 North Washington Street, Canton": two independent
sources agree, so both flags are true. The diocesan finder gives 106 North Washington Street,
which is the parish office and rectory address printed in the bulletin masthead, not the
church. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H5M542+JF), on the building.
