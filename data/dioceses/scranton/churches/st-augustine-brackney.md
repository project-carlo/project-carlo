## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 bulletin of Holy Name of Mary, Montrose (same pastor; it carries
St. Brigid Parish's schedule) has Sunday 10:00am and Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday 8:30am
at St. Augustine, Silver Lake, and the 10:00am Sunday Mass continues under the schedule
from 31 October (page 7). The diocese's finder still says Sunday 10:30am and the St.
Brigid homepage 10:00am; the current bulletin wins.

## Name and address

The finder ("Saint Augustine Church", 295 Church Road, Brackney 18812) and GCatholic ("St.
Augustine", Church Rd., Brackney 18812) agree on Church Road in Brackney; St. Brigid's own
page puts it on Church Road in Silver Lake Township (Brackney is the post office for that
township). The bulletin calls it "St. Augustine Silver Lake". Both flags are true; the town
is Brackney as the two rosters give it. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code
(87H6X28W+2F).
