Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (image pages, read rendered) has Sunday 8:30 AM at St.
John's and Tuesday and Thursday 8 AM in the IHM Chapel at St. John's; page 2's intentions
list the 8:30 AM Sunday Masses on 18 and 25 October. The parish office is at the same
address.
