## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 St. Faustina bulletin: Saturday 4:00 pm and 5:30 pm (Spanish),
Sunday 8:00 and 11:00 am, Wednesday 6:00 pm, Thursday and Friday 8:00 am. The bulletin
names no Nanticoke church because the parish has one: it sends only the Monday 8 am and
Saturday 5:30 pm English Masses to Mocanaqua, and its site's About Us page has a single
"Our Worship Site".

Kowalska Parish), the finder ("Holy Trinity Church, Nanticoke") and the bulletin masthead.
