## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 Corpus Christi bulletin schedules Sunday 9 am and Tuesday and Thursday
8:30 am at Holy Redeemer (that week's Tuesday Mass was cancelled, a one-off). The finder's schedule
is the same. **Sources disagree:** the diocese's consolidated-parishes list says "Harding Holy
Redeemer (closed 9/2010)". The bulletin, the finder and the parish site all show it in use now, and
the current bulletin wins; the 2010 date is probably when Holy Redeemer parish was merged into
Corpus Christi, not a closing of the building.

## Name and address

The finder ("Church of the Holy Redeemer", 2435 State Route 92, Falls 18615), GCatholic ("Holy
Redeemer", 2435 State Route 92, 18615-9416) and the parish homepage agree; both flags are true.
The diocese's list calls the place Harding, a village of Exeter Township next to Falls. Coordinates
are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H6C557+7H).
