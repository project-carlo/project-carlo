## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (container 04/0475, page 1) schedules Saturday 4 pm,
Sunday 7:30 am and 10:30 am, Tuesday 9 am and Friday noon at OLV, and Saturday confessions are held
here while Gate of Heaven is renovated. The finder's schedule is the same.

## Name and address

The finder ("Our Lady of Victory Church", 16 Second Street, Harveys Lake 18618), GCatholic ("Our
Lady of Victory", 16 2nd St., 18618) and the bulletin's page 3 ("16 Second Street") agree; the
diocese and GCatholic are independent, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code
(87H59X68+GM).
