## Holds Mass

Yes, on Sundays. The 11 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, an image; read rendered) and the
homepage both have one Mass here, Sunday 8:00 AM. One of the two churches of the
canonical Saint Katharine Drexel Parish; the parish site also has a page and a 150th
anniversary book for St. Juliana, Rock Lake.

## Name and address

The name agrees everywhere (finder "Saint Juliana Church", GCatholic "St. Juliana", the
bulletin "St. Juliana"), so `name_verified` is true. The address does not:

- the diocese's finder: 2048 Creamton Drive (Route 247), Pleasant Mount 18453;
- GCatholic: 612 Hudson St., Rock Lake 18453, which is the parish office's street address
  in Forest City (the bulletin masthead's "612 Hudson Street, Forest City"), so a
  mailing address, not the building.

I followed the finder's street address and left `address_verified` false. The coordinates
are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H6QMJ4+RH, 41.782, -75.344), which lies in the Rock Lake area
north of Forest City, not at 612 Hudson Street, so it looks like a point on the building;
an OSM or map check of 2048 Creamton Drive would settle both.
