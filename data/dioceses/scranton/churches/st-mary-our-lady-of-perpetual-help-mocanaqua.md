## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 St. Faustina bulletin: Monday 8:00 am and Saturday 5:30 pm
(English) at St. Mary Mocanaqua. It became a secondary worship site of St. Faustina
Kowalska Parish in January 2026, when Holy Spirit Parish was suppressed.

## Name and address

The sources disagree on the street. GCatholic gives 150 Main St., Mocanaqua 18655, with a
Plus Code; the diocese's finder gives 130 Rectory Road, which the St. Faustina bulletin
uses as its Mocanaqua office address. The church may be on Main Street with the rectory on
Rectory Road, but nothing read says so. GCatholic's address and Plus Code are kept;
`address_verified` stays false until a map or the parish settles it. The name agrees in
GCatholic and the finder.
