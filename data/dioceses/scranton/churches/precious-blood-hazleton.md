## Holds Mass

Yes. The 4 October 2026 Our Lady of Peace bulletin (page 1, an image) gives Saturday
5:30 pm, Sunday 8:30 and 11 am, and Thursday and Friday 8 am at Most Precious Blood, the
parish's only church since Our Lady of Grace closed on 2 August 2026. The Hazleton
clergy's schedule effective 3 August 2026 agrees.
