## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 SSCM bulletin's masthead (page 1, an image) gives Saturday 4 pm,
Sunday 7:30 am, 10:30 am and noon (Spanish), English weekday Masses Monday to Wednesday at
8 am and Spanish ones Tuesday to Friday, all at the parish's one church. The Hazleton
clergy's schedule effective 3 August 2026 agrees.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead ("the Church of St. Joseph, 604 N. Laurel Street"), the diocese
finder ("Saint Joseph Church", 604 North Laurel Street, 18201) and GCatholic ("St.
Joseph", 604 N Laurel St.) agree, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus
Code (87G6X27G+8G), on the building; the ParishesOnline organization's point (40.963428,
-75.973669) is within a few metres.
