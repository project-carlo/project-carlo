## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 Christ the King bulletin (page 1, an image) lists Sunday 7:30 am
here; weekday Masses are all at St. Thomas Aquinas. The diocesan finder gives the same
Sunday 7:30 am. The February 2026 relegation notice calls this one of the parish's two
secondary worship sites and reports needed repairs, but announces no change to it.

## Name and address

417 Main Street, Eynon (Archbald borough, ZIP 18403): the diocesan finder, GCatholic (23917)
and the bulletin footer agree, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus
Code.
