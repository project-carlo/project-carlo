## Holds Mass

Yes, daily except Wednesday. The 18 October 2026 Seton Sentinel gives the parish schedule under
116 Hughes Street (Saturday 4 pm; Sunday 8:30 am, 11 am, 5:30 pm; Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday
8 am). The bulletin names the parish, not the building; the finder places the parish's one worship
site, "Holy Trinity Church, Swoyersville", at the same address with the same times.
