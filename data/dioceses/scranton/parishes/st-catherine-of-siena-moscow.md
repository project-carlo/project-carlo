## Publisher

Saint Catherine of Siena Parish, Moscow, publishes its own weekly bulletin through
ParishesOnline (container 03/1138). I read the 11 October 2026 issue: the masthead is
"SAINT CATHERINE OF SIENA PARISH", 220 Church Street, and its Mass schedule has weekend
Masses (Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 and 11:30 am) and Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8:00
am at St. Catherine of Siena, plus Tuesday and Thursday 8:00 am "at St. Eulalia Parish".

St. Eulalia (Elmhurst) is a separate canonical parish, outside this region's assignment;
the Tuesday/Thursday line suggests the two share clergy ("North Pocono"). This masthead
names St. Catherine alone, so St. Eulalia is not a church of this row unless it turns out to
publish no bulletin of its own (left to the region that has St. Eulalia).
