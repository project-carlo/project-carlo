## Publisher

Corpus Christi Parish, West Pittston publishes its own weekly bulletin on its website. The 11
October 2026 issue's masthead (page 1) names its three churches, Immaculate Conception, Holy
Redeemer and St. Anthony, and the pastor, Msgr. John J. Sempa, with one schedule: Saturday 4 pm St.
Anthony and 5:30 pm Immaculate Conception; Sunday 7:30 am St. Anthony, 9 am Holy Redeemer, 10:30 am
Immaculate Conception; weekdays 7:30 am Immaculate Conception, and Tuesday and Thursday 8:30 am Holy
Redeemer.

## Worship sites

Three, all in the schedule: `immaculate-conception-west-pittston`, `holy-redeemer-falls` and
`st-anthony-of-padua-exeter`. The finder lists the same three. The diocese's consolidated-parishes
list (updated 1 July 2024) puts four Exeter churches under Corpus Christi: St. Anthony of Padua
(6/2011), St. Cecilia "closed 4/24/2022" (`excluded/st-cecilia-exeter.md`), St. John the Baptist
"closed 5/29/2011" and St. Barbara (7/1/2024; St. Barbara was the parish of St. Anthony and St.
Cecilia, merged into Corpus Christi that day). The same list says "Harding Holy Redeemer (closed
9/2010)", which the current bulletin contradicts: Holy Redeemer has two Masses a week (see its
record).
