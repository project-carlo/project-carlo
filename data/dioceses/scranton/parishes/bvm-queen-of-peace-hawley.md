## Publisher

The Parish of Blessed Virgin Mary, Queen of Peace, Hawley publishes one weekly bulletin for
its two churches through eCatholic (site 24839). The 11 October 2026 issue's front page is
headed "The Parish of Blessed Virgin Mary, Queen of Peace & St. Veronica", Rev. Bryan
Wright, pastor, with both churches' addresses and a "New Mass & Confession Times" box coding
each Mass QP (Queen of Peace) or SV (St. Veronica). The diocese groups it with no other
parish.
