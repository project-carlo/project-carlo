## Publisher

All Saints Parish (Plymouth) and St. John the Baptist Parish (Larksville) share a pastor,
Rev. Augustine Thomas, a website (larksvilleplymouthcatholiccommunity.org) and one weekly
bulletin. The 11 October 2026 issue is headed "All Saints and St. John the Baptist Parish
Families", gives both rectory offices, and its Mass list codes every Mass (AS) or (SJB).
One bulletin, one row; the finder's two canonical parishes are in `group`.

## Worship sites

Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary Church, 66 Willow Street, Plymouth
(`nativity-of-the-blessed-virgin-mary-plymouth`), the church of All Saints Parish (the
bulletin's "AS"), and St. John the Baptist Church, 126 Nesbitt Street, Larksville
(`st-john-the-baptist-larksville`). These are the only worship sites the diocese's finder
lists for the two parishes.
