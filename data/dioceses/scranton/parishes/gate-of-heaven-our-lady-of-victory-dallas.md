## Publisher

Gate of Heaven Parish (Dallas) and Our Lady of Victory Parish (Harveys Lake) are two canonical
parishes in the diocese's finder with one pastor, Rev. Andrew S. Hvozdovic, and they publish one
weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container 04/0475). The 18 October 2026 issue's masthead
(page 1) reads "GATE OF HEAVEN & OUR LADY OF VICTORY / Two Parishes, One Faith Community", with one
staff list and one Mass schedule coded GOH and OLV: Saturday 4 pm OLV and 5:30 pm GOH; Sunday 7:30 am
OLV, 9 am GOH, 10:30 am OLV; weekdays Monday 6 pm GOH, Tuesday 9 am OLV, Wednesday 9 am GOH, Friday
noon OLV. Page 3 gives OLV's address (16 Second Street) and page 4 GOH's (40 Machell Avenue).

## Worship sites

Two: `gate-of-heaven-dallas` and `our-lady-of-victory-harveys-lake`, the two buildings the
schedule codes. The finder lists exactly these two worship sites for the two parishes, and GCatholic
has the same two. The same page notes renovations at Gate of Heaven, with Saturday confessions moved
to Our Lady of Victory "for the rest of 2026"; the Masses at Gate of Heaven are still scheduled.

## Website and bulletin provider

goholv.org is the joint site of the two parishes (named in the bulletin's masthead, and the
finder's website for both). Its BULLETIN menu item links the ParishesOnline organization
`gate-of-heaven-and-our-lady-of-victory`; `probe po` gave the LPi id and container, with weekly
issues through 18 October 2026. Fetching goes by `provider_id`, not the site (which answers 403 to
plain requests).

## Not yet confirmed

Whether the Gate of Heaven renovation will suspend Masses there; the 18 October schedule still
lists them.
