## Publisher

Most Holy Trinity, Cresco and St. Maximilian Kolbe, Pocono Pines are two canonical
parishes with one pastor (Rev. Paschal Mbagwu) and one weekly LPi bulletin, ParishesOnline
organization "Most Holy Trinity and St. Maximilian Parishes" (container 03/1122). The 18
October 2026 masthead reads "MOST HOLY TRINITY PARISH ST. MAXIMILIAN KOLBE PARISH", with
each parish's office and Mass schedule side by side, and the inner pages have a section
for each (page 2 Most Holy Trinity, page 3 St. Maximilian Kolbe). St. Maximilian Kolbe's
own eCatholic site posts the same joint issue (eCatholic 22041, 18 October), so both
channels carry one bulletin: one row.

## Worship sites

Two, one per parish:
- Most Holy Trinity Church, 212 Route 390, Cresco (`most-holy-trinity-cresco`): weekend and
  weekday Masses (weekdays in the day chapel of the same building, the former Msgr. McHugh
  School).
- Our Lady of the Lake Church, 5112 Pocono Crest Road, Pocono Pines
  (`our-lady-of-the-lake-pocono-pines`), St. Maximilian Kolbe's church.

Most Holy Trinity was formed from St. Mary of the Mount (Mount Pocono), St. Ann (Tobyhanna)
and St. Bernadette (Canadensis); its church in Cresco was dedicated on 15 May 2022.
GCatholic still lists the three old churches under Most Holy Trinity: St. Mary of the Mount
and St. Ann are `excluded/` (closed; the diocese's paper calls them "the former Saint Mary
and Saint Ann church buildings"), St. Bernadette is in `open/` (no source states its
closure). The diocese's consolidated-parishes list also gives "Blakeslee Christ the King
Church (closed 9/6/2009)" with records at St. Maximilian Kolbe; it is in neither roster and
has no record.
