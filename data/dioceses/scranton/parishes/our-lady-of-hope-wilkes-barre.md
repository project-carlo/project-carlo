Our Lady of Hope Parish, Wilkes-Barre publishes its own weekly bulletin. The 11 October 2026
issue gives the parish alone at 40 Park Avenue, with Mass Saturday 4:00 p.m., Sunday 9:00
a.m. and First Friday 8:00 a.m. in the "Main Church". The parish is led by a Parish Life
Coordinator (Deacon Steve Frye) with a sacramental minister.
