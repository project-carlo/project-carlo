## Publisher

Saints Peter and Paul, Towanda publishes its own weekly bulletin. The 18 October 2026 issue's
masthead is "Saints Peter and Paul Church, Pastor: Rev. Jose Joseph Kuriappilly, 106 Third
Street, Towanda", with its own Mass schedule (Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am, weekdays
Tuesday to Friday).

The same page carries a second box, "Schedule at Our Lady of Perpetual Help, Wyalusing", and the
parish homepage lists Wyalusing's Masses too: the two canonical parishes appear to share a
pastor. But the homepage sends readers to Wyalusing's own bulletin for its times ("or as posted
in their bulletin", linking olophparish.org/bulletins/), and this masthead names Towanda alone,
so Wyalusing is a separate publisher and is not folded in here (see
`our-lady-of-perpetual-help-wyalusing`).

## Worship sites

One: `sts-peter-and-paul-towanda`. The finder lists only this site for the parish, and
GCatholic has no other Towanda church.

## Website and bulletin provider

The bulletin page (a mychurchwebsite.net site) lists one PDF per Sunday at
`files.mychurchwebsite.net/c2794/<liturgical-sunday>_2026.pdf`; names carry the Sunday, not
a date, and some have suffixes (`fourth_sunday_of_easter_20266.pdf`). No ParishesOnline,
eCatholic or Discover Mass id found. `provider_id` is left empty until a `fetch:check` run
after promotion suggests a recipe.
