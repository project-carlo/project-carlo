## Publisher

Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish, Hunlock Creek publishes its own weekly bulletin. The 11
October 2026 issue's masthead names its two churches, Saint Martha's (Stillwater) and Our
Lady of Mount Carmel (Hunlock Creek), and its Mass list codes each Mass OLMC or SM.

## Worship sites

Our Lady of Mount Carmel, 2011 State Route 29, Hunlock Creek
(`our-lady-of-mount-carmel-hunlock-creek`), and St. Martha, 260 Bonnieville Road, Stillwater
(Fairmount Township) (`st-martha-stillwater`). St. Martha came to this parish in January 2026
when Holy Spirit Parish (Glen Lyon) was suppressed: the diocese's report of St. Adalbert's
closing Mass (11 January 2026) says St. Martha "has become a secondary worship site of Our
Lady of Mount Carmel Parish". Holy Spirit's other churches went to St. Faustina Kowalska
(St. Mary, Mocanaqua) or closed (St. Adalbert, Glen Lyon).
