## Publisher

Epiphany Parish, Sayre (Rev. Daniel A. Toomey, pastor; Rev. Michael Amo Gyau, assistant)
publishes one weekly bulletin through Discover Mass. The 18 October 2026 issue's masthead lists
"Epiphany Church, Office, and Rectory: 304 S. Elmer Avenue, Sayre" and "Our Lady of Perpetual
Help (OLPH): 3418 Chapel Road, Milan", and its Mass Schedule has Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday
9:00 and 11:00 am (Epiphany) and Saturday 6:00 pm at OLPH, Ridgebury. No neighbouring parish
shares it.

## Worship sites

- `epiphany-sayre` (Epiphany, Sayre; church and parish row share the slug)
- `our-lady-of-perpetual-help-milan` (OLPH, Chapel Road, Milan / Ridgebury Township)

These are the two sites the diocesan finder lists for Epiphany Parish; GCatholic's
"Our Lady of Perpetual Help, Ridgebury" is the same OLPH chapel. GCatholic also lists the
Ukrainian Catholic Ascension of Our Lord, 110 N Higgins Ave., Sayre, which is not part of this
parish (`excluded/ascension-of-our-lord-sayre`, other-jurisdiction).

## Website and bulletin provider

thechurchoftheepiphany.com is JavaScript-rendered; in a browser its "Bulletins & Inserts" page
embeds the Discover Mass church `church-of-the-epiphany-sayre-pa`, whose page lists weekly
issues through 18 October 2026. Provider `discover_mass`, read by that slug.
