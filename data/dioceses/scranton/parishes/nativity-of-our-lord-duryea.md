## Publisher

Nativity of Our Lord Parish, Duryea publishes its own weekly bulletin on its website. The 11
October 2026 issue's masthead (page 1) names "NATIVITY OF OUR LORD PARISH, DURYEA", 127 Stephenson
Street, Rev. Michael M. Bryant, pastor, with one schedule: weekdays Monday-Friday 7 am, Saturday vigil
4 pm, Sunday 8 am, 11 am and 7 pm. No shared pastor or bulletin with a neighbouring parish appears in
the issue.

## Worship sites

One: `holy-rosary-duryea`, the church at the parish address, which the finder lists as the parish's
only worship site. The bulletin names no building, so the Mass schedule is placed there by the
finder (Holy Rosary Church, 127 Stephenson Street, with the same Mass times) and by the masthead's
address. GCatholic also lists Sacred Heart of Jesus, 529 Stephenson Street, under this parish; the
diocese's consolidated-parishes list gives it as a building of the parish since 11/2010 without
calling it closed, but neither the bulletin nor the finder schedules anything there. It is
`open/sacred-heart-of-jesus-duryea.md`. St. Joseph Church, Duryea, is "closed 3/21/2010" on the same
list and is in no roster here.
