## Publisher

St. Robert Bellarmine Parish (Wilkes-Barre) and Exaltation of the Holy Cross Parish (Hanover
Township) share a pastor, Rev. Richard J. Cirba, V.F., and one weekly bulletin. The 11
October 2026 issue's page-1 masthead names both parishes, gives each parish's Mass schedule
and office, and welcomes readers "to St. Robert Bellarmine and Exaltation of the Holy Cross
Parishes". Both parishes' websites link the same Bonventure page (3087) as their bulletin,
so this is one bulletin and one row.

## Worship sites

St. Aloysius Church, 143 West Division Street, Wilkes-Barre (`st-aloysius-wilkes-barre`), where
St. Robert Bellarmine Parish "celebrat[es] liturgy" (Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 7:00 and 11:00
am, weekday Masses at 8:00 am as listed), and Exaltation of the Holy Cross Church, 420 Main
Road, Hanover Township (`exaltation-of-the-holy-cross-hanover-township`; Saturday 5:30 pm,
Sunday 9:30 am). These are the only worship sites the diocese's finder lists for the two
parishes.
