## Publisher

Queen of Angels Parish, Jessup, publishes its own weekly bulletin through Discover Mass. I
read the 18 October 2026 issue: page 1 is headed "QUEEN OF ANGELS PARISH at SAINT MICHAEL'S
CHURCH, 320 FIRST AVENUE, JESSUP" (mailing address 605 Church St.), with Masses Saturday
4:00 pm and Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 am; weekday Masses are for now said at 5:30 pm in the
parish rectory because the pastor, Fr. Gerard McGlone, is preparing for surgery. The parish
was formed in the 2008-2010 consolidation from all of Jessup's Latin-rite parishes: St.
James, St. Michael the Archangel, St. Mary of the Assumption and St. Stanislaus (parish
site, About).

The same issue announces a "transition team" for a "linkage to consolidation" with "Fr. Andy
and his team", including "bulletin creation and publication"; Fr. Andrew Kurovsky is pastor
of Sacred Heart of Jesus, Peckville (`sacred-heart-of-jesus-peckville`), the next parish
south. That is a plan; for now each publishes its own bulletin, so separate rows, with a
`revisit`.
