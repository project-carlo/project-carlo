## Publisher

St. Joseph the Worker Parish, Williamsport (Rev. David W. Bechtel, pastor) publishes its own
weekly bulletin through Discover Mass. The 18 October 2026 masthead gives the parish, "702 West
Fourth Street", the church (702 W. 4th St.) and the office (711 W. Edwin St., the address the
diocesan finder gives for the parish), with weekend Masses "all in the Church" and weekday Masses
in the Holy Family Chapel. No neighbouring parish shares it; St. Ann & St. Boniface, a mile away,
have their own bulletin (`st-ann-st-boniface-williamsport`).

## Worship sites

- `annunciation-williamsport`: the parish church, Annunciation, 702 West Fourth Street (weekend
  Masses, Saturday 9:00 am).
- `holy-family-chapel-williamsport`: Holy Family Chapel, 720 West Fourth Street (weekday Masses
  Monday-Friday 12:10 pm). No roster lists the chapel, but the diocesan finder's Annunciation
  entry gives the weekday Masses as "(Holy Family Chapel)".
