## Publisher

St. Elizabeth Parish publishes one weekly bulletin for its two churches. The 11 October 2026
issue's masthead reads "St. Elizabeth of Hungary/St. Mark", and its page-2 worship schedule
codes every Mass (E) for St. Elizabeth Church, Bear Creek or (M) for St. Mark Church,
Thornhurst: Saturday 4:00 PM (M) and 6:00 PM (E), Sunday 8:30 AM (M) and 10:30 AM (E),
weekdays 9:00 AM (E). Administrator Rev. Rawel Toppo.

## Worship sites

Both churches the diocese's finder lists for the parish: St. Elizabeth, 5700 Bear Creek
Boulevard (`st-elizabeth-bear-creek`), and St. Mark, 1052 Bear Lake Road, Thornhurst
(`st-mark-thornhurst`). The finder says St. Mark's Sunday 8:30 am Mass runs Memorial Day to
Labor Day; the 11 October bulletin still lists it.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`: the parish's own site churchofstelizabethstmark.org posts each issue as a PDF on
its /bulletin page (and older ones on /bulletin-archive). Recipe `page-pdfs
https://www.churchofstelizabethstmark.org/bulletin` pinned from `fetch:check` on 2026-10-17, after promotion, which picked
the 11 October issue (`current`).
