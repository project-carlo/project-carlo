## Publisher

Communion of Saints Parish, Pittston publishes one printed weekly bulletin, "The Weekly Word",
through ParishesOnline (container 03/1275). The 17-18 October 2026 issue's masthead (page 1) names
the parish and its three churches, Our Lady of Mt. Carmel, St. John the Evangelist and St. Mary, Help
of Christians, with one Mass schedule for all three; the pastor is Rev. Joseph P. Elston. The
parish site says "two weekly bulletins for our three Parishes": the other, "Monday Messenger", is an
email newsletter, not a bulletin the pipeline reads. ParishesOnline's org slug
(`st-john-evangelist-church-st-joseph-marello-church`) carries older parish names; GCatholic still
places Mt. Carmel under St. Joseph Marello Parish and St. Mary, Help of Christians under Our Lady of
the Eucharist Parish, the canonical parishes the diocese's finder now lists as one.

## Worship sites

Three, all in the masthead and the schedule: `our-lady-of-mount-carmel-pittston`,
`st-john-the-evangelist-pittston` and `st-mary-help-of-christians-pittston`. The finder lists the
same three. The diocese's consolidated-parishes list gives several closed Pittston-area churches of
the predecessor parishes (St. Casimir, St. John the Baptist, St. Mary's Assumption, St. Rocco
closed 7/1/2021; St. Joseph, Port Griffith; Blessed Sacrament, Hughestown); none is in a roster here.

## Website and bulletin provider

stjohnspittston.com is the finder's website for the parish and its own site ("The Roman Catholic
Parishes of Pittston"); its Weekly Word page embeds the ParishesOnline widget, from which `probe po`
gives the LPi id and container. Fetching goes by `provider_id`.

## Not yet confirmed

The 69th St. Jude Novena (19-28 October 2026) says "All Masses and services will be celebrated in
St. Mary, Help of Christians Church"; that reads as the novena's own services, not a move of the
parish schedule, but the 25 October issue would confirm it.
