## Publisher

Three canonical parishes in Pike County share a pastor (Rev. Edward J. Casey, with Rev.
Binesh Joseph Kanjirakattu as senior priest) and one weekly bulletin printed by J.S.
Paluch and posted on Discover Mass: "the Catholic Communities of St. Ann, St. John Neumann
& St. Vincent de Paul". The 18 October 2026 issue (12 pages) opens with that title, then
gives each parish a page of its own Mass intentions (page 2 St. John Neumann, Lords Valley;
page 3 St. Ann, Shohola; page 4 St. Vincent de Paul, Milford), and page 11 lists each
parish's standing schedule. The same issue appears under three Discover Mass church pages
(St. Ann, St. John Neumann, St. Vincent de Paul), and St. John Neumann's own site sends its
"BULLETIN" link to the St. Ann page. One bulletin, so one row for the three parishes.

## Worship sites

Five, all named in the bulletin:

- St. Ann, Shohola (`st-ann-shohola`): daily and Sunday Masses; Saturday vigil in the
  winter and spring.
- St. Mary of the Assumption, Lackawaxen (`st-mary-of-the-assumption-lackawaxen`):
  Saturday 5:30pm vigil in summer and autumn ("(SM)" in the intentions through Saturday 10
  October 2026; "St. Mary's Church - Closed for Season" on page 11 since).
- St. John Neumann, Lords Valley (`st-john-neumann-lords-valley`).
- Good Shepherd, Blooming Grove (`good-shepherd-blooming-grove`): a Saturday 5:30pm Mass
  on Memorial Day, July Fourth and Labor Day weekends only.
- St. Vincent de Paul, Milford (`st-vincent-de-paul-milford`), and its weekday chapel
  ("(C)-Chapel"), which is in the same building and not a separate row.

Ruled out: Sacred Heart of Jesus, Greeley (GCatholic only), a St. Ann's church relegated to
profane use in 2023 (`excluded/sacred-heart-of-jesus-greeley.md`). St. Ann's "St. Eligius
Room" (SE) is a meeting room, not a worship site.
