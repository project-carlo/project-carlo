## Publisher

Saint Eulalia Parish (Roaring Brook Township, Elmhurst post office) publishes its own weekly
bulletin through Discover Mass (church slug `st-eulalia-elmhurst-pa`). The 18 October 2026
issue, the newest on 16 October, is Saint Eulalia's alone: page 2 opens "Welcome to Saint
Eulalia Parish" and gives the Mass intentions for 19 to 25 October (Tuesday and Thursday
8 am, Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 am), page 3 the attendance at those three weekend
Masses. The diocesan finder lists it as a single-site parish, and no neighbouring parish shares
it.

St. Eulalia shares its pastor, Father Thomas M. Muldowney, with St. Catherine of Siena,
Moscow (region B's row; ParishesOnline container 03/1138), whose bulletin names Father
Muldowney as pastor and sends its parishioners to "St. Eulalia Parish" for Tuesday and Thursday
8 am Mass. The two still publish separate bulletins, checked on 16 October 2026: St.
Eulalia's issues on Discover Mass carry only St. Eulalia's own Masses, intentions and
collections (the 18 October issue does not mention St. Catherine at all; earlier ones mention
it only for joint events such as a women's gathering "of St. Catherine's and St. Eulalia's"),
and St. Catherine's ParishesOnline masthead names St. Catherine alone. Two live channels with
two mastheads, so two rows.

## Worship sites

One: St. Eulalia Church, 214 Blue Shutters Road (`st-eulalia-roaring-brook`). The finder and
GCatholic list no other building for this parish.

## Website and bulletin provider

sainteulalias.com is the parish's site (in the finder and GCatholic). `probe url` found the
Discover Mass id embedded in it, and its "Weekly Bulletin" block also links each issue as a
Wix PDF (bulletin-2026-10-11.pdf on 16 October, a week behind Discover Mass, which already had
18 October). Discover Mass is the fuller and earlier channel, so the row reads it by
`provider_id`. Page 1 of each issue is an image (the masthead); the rest is text.
