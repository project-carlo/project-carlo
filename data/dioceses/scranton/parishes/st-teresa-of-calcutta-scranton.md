## Publisher

Saint Teresa of Calcutta Parish (South Scranton), formed on 26 November 2023 from St. John
Neumann and St. Paul of the Cross, publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline
(container 04/0680). The 18 October 2026 issue's cover names one church, Sacred Hearts of
Jesus & Mary, with Mass Tuesday-Friday 8 am, Thursday 7 pm (Spanish), Saturday 4 pm and Sunday
8, 9:30, 11 (Spanish) and 6 pm (Spanish). The 6 October 2024 cover still listed Nativity of
Our Lord Church too; it closed on 8 June 2025. No other parish shares the bulletin.

## Worship sites

One: Sacred Hearts of Jesus & Mary Church, 1217 Prospect Avenue
(`st-teresa-of-calcutta-scranton`; GCatholic files it under the parish's name).

Ruled out, with records:
- Nativity of Our Lord, 633 Orchard St.: closed, last Mass 8 June 2025; the church and rectory
  are being sold (`excluded/nativity-of-our-lord-scranton.md`).
- Holy Name of Jesus, 1414 E. Elm St. (St. John Neumann's second site): closed, last Mass
  17 July 2022 (`excluded/holy-name-of-jesus-scranton.md`).
- St. Francis of Assisi, 630 Genet St. (GCatholic: St. Paul of the Cross Parish): in no
  bulletin or diocesan list read, but no closing source found yet
  (`open/st-francis-of-assisi-scranton.md`).
- GCatholic's "St. Mary of the Assumption" shrine, 422 S. River St., belongs to the
  Fraternité Notre-Dame, which is not in communion with Rome
  (`excluded/st-mary-of-the-assumption-scranton.md`).
