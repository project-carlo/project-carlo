## Publisher

St. Peter, Wellsboro and St. Thomas the Apostle, Elkland are two canonical parishes in the
diocesan finder with one pastor, Rev. Jacek Bialkowski, and **one bulletin**. The 18 October
2026 issue (posted on St. Peter's site) is headed "The Parishes of St. Peter and St. Thomas the
Apostle"; page 1 has one column per parish with its own office, staff and weekend Masses:
St. Peter's Saturday 5:00 pm (livestreamed) and Sunday 8:30 am; St. Thomas's Saturday 7:00 pm
at St. Catherine (its mission in Westfield) and Sunday 11:30 am at St. Thomas. Later pages carry
St. Peter's intentions and St. Thomas's / St. Catherine's lector and collection lists.

St. Thomas's own website posts the same combined bulletin, as JPG page images, and stopped at
the 21 June 2026 issue; its masthead is identical. So there is one publisher, current on
St. Peter's site, and no live separate channel for Elkland: one row.

## Worship sites

Three, all with weekend Mass in the 18 October 2026 issue:

- `st-peter-wellsboro` (St. Peter, Wellsboro)
- `st-thomas-the-apostle-elkland` (St. Thomas the Apostle, Elkland)
- `st-catherine-westfield` (St. Catherine of Siena mission, Westfield)

These are the three sites the finder lists for the two parishes, and the three GCatholic has
in Wellsboro, Elkland and Westfield.

## Website and bulletin provider

`website` is St. Peter's "Weekly Bulletin" page, which links one PDF per Sunday as
`/storage/<Month> <d> <yyyy>.pdf` (dated file names; the 18 October issue was up on 16
October). It is self-hosted (`other`); no ParishesOnline, eCatholic or Discover Mass id was
found. St. Thomas's page (stthomaselklandpa.com/bulletins.html) is recorded as a second
`publication` because it posts the same bulletin, as images. Recipe `page-pdfs` (the
website is the bulletin page) pinned from `fetch:check` on 2026-10-17, after promotion, which picked `October 18 2026.pdf`
(`current`).
