## Publisher

St. Paul Parish (Green Ridge, Scranton) publishes its own weekly bulletin through
ParishesOnline (container 03/1135). The 18 October 2026 issue's cover (page 1, an image) is
St. Paul's alone: "St. Paul Parish, St. Paul Church & Rectory, 1510 Penn Ave.", with Mass
Monday to Saturday 8 am, Saturday 4:30 pm vigil and Sunday 8 and 10:30 am, all at St. Paul
Church. The same cover lists St. Clare Church (2235 N. Washington Ave.) for a weekday Holy Hour
and the food pantry only. No other canonical parish shares the bulletin.

## Worship sites

One: St. Paul Church, 1510 Penn Avenue (`st-paul-scranton`). St. Clare Church, the parish's
second building, holds no Mass: the bulletin cover and the homepage give it Adoration, the
Rosary and the food pantry, and the diocesan finder says "Available for Funerals, Baptisms &
Weddings". It is `excluded/st-clare-scranton.md` (`no-public-mass`, per the rule in
`diocese.md`).
