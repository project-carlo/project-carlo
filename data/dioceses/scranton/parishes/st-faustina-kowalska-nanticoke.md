## Publisher

St. Faustina Kowalska Parish, Nanticoke publishes its own weekly bulletin through
ParishesOnline (container 03/1404). The 11 October 2026 masthead gives two addresses, 520
South Hanover St., Nanticoke and 130 Rectory Rd., Mocanaqua, and page 2 says the Monday 8am
and Saturday 5:30pm English Masses are at St. Mary Mocanaqua; the rest are in Nanticoke.

## Worship sites

Holy Trinity Church, 520 South Hanover Street, Nanticoke (`holy-trinity-nanticoke`), the
parish church, and St. Mary, Our Lady of Perpetual Help, Mocanaqua
(`st-mary-our-lady-of-perpetual-help-mocanaqua`), which joined in January 2026 when Holy
Spirit Parish was suppressed (diocesan report quoted in evidence). The parish was formed in
July 2010 from Nanticoke's six churches (its site's About Us page); GCatholic still lists
one of the others, St. Mary of Czestochowa, 1030 S Hanover St., which is closed and for sale
(`excluded/st-mary-of-czestochowa-nanticoke.md`). Holy Spirit's St. Adalbert, Glen Lyon
closed on 11 January 2026 (`excluded/st-adalbert-glen-lyon.md`).

## Website and bulletin provider

`parishes_online`, org `st-faustina-kowalska-parish`. The website is nanticokecatholic.com,
which the ParishesOnline org gives as its website and whose BULLETINS page

HTTP 503). The bulletin itself now gives "www.sfkparish.com", which reset every connection
from this sandbox on 2026-10-16.
