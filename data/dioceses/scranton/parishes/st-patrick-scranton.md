website in the diocesan finder ("Website:" is empty), its ParishesOnline organization
(st-patrick-church-18504) has no publications, Discover Mass lists no bulletins for it, it is

Saint Lucy, West Scranton, became official on Dec. 3"). St. Lucy's Church has since become
the home of St. Michael the Archangel Parish (FSSP), which publishes its own bulletin
(`st-michael-the-archangel-scranton`).
