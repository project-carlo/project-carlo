## Publisher

Saints Cyril & Methodius Parish publishes its own weekly LPi bulletin (ParishesOnline
container 04/0750). The 18 October 2026 issue's masthead (page 1, an image) reads "SS Cyril
& Methodius Parish at the Church of St. Joseph, 604 N. Laurel Street, Hazleton", with
Saturday 4 pm and Sunday 7:30 am, 10:30 am and noon (Spanish) Masses, and weekday Masses
in English (Mon-Wed 8 am) and Spanish (Tue, Thu, Fri 7 pm; Wed 5:30 pm). It names no
other church. The parish is not grouped with another: the Hazleton clergy's June 2026
letter on the new city-wide Mass schedule lists four separate parishes (Saint Pius of
Pietrelcina, SSCM, Holy Rosary, Our Lady of Peace), each with its own bulletin (see their
rows).

## Worship sites

One: St. Joseph Church, 604 N. Laurel Street (`st-joseph-hazleton`). GCatholic also lists
St. Stanislaus, 652 Carson St., which the diocese's consolidated-parishes list gives as
"St. Stanislaus (closed 10/20/2013)", records with Ss. Cyril and Methodius Parish:
`excluded/st-stanislaus-hazleton.md`. The same list assigns several other closed Hazleton
churches (Holy Trinity German and Slovak, Our Lady Mt. Carmel, Ss. Peter and Paul, St.
Ladislaus) to Saint Pius or SSCM; none of them is in either roster, so none has a record.

## Website and bulletin provider

www.sscmparish.com is the parish's site (named in the masthead and in the diocese finder).
Its /bulletins page re-posts each LPi issue as a PDF on its own CDN, but the canonical copy
is the ParishesOnline organization `ss-cyril-methodius-parish` (found with `probe po`, its
container matching the "04-0750" footer), so provider `parishes_online`. **Pages 1-3 of
the issue are images with no text layer**; only the ad page has text, so the Mass schedule
must be read as an image.
