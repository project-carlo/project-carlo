## Publisher

The Cathedral of Saint Peter and Immaculate Conception, two canonical parishes with one pastor
(Father Jeffrey Tudgay), publish one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container
04/1034). In the 11 October 2026 issue, the newest on 16 October, page 1 is the cathedral's
masthead, page 2 gives one "Mass and Confession Schedule" for "Cathedral of Saint Peter" and
"Immaculate Conception", the inside pages run under "The Parishes of the Cathedral of Saint
Peter and Immaculate Conception", the Mass intentions mark each Mass CSP or ICC, and the
registration form offers either parish. The ParishesOnline organization is named
"st-peter-cathedral-and-immaculate-conception-church", and the LPi footer reads "St. Peter
Cathedral and Immaculate Conception Church". So the two finder parishes are one row here.

Immaculate Conception has no channel of its own: its site, iccscranton.org, posts the Mass
times and a 2025 financial report but no bulletin. That is why the row is not split.

## Worship sites

- `cathedral-of-saint-peter-scranton`: daily 6:30 am and 12:10 pm, Saturday 5:30 pm, Sunday
  6:30, 10, 12:15 and 5.
- `immaculate-conception-scranton` (800 Taylor Avenue, Hill Section): Monday, Wednesday and
  Thursday 8 am, Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 8:30 am.

The finder lists one worship site for each parish, and GCatholic has only Immaculate
Conception (it leaves out the cathedral). No other building is named in the bulletin.

## Website and bulletin provider

stpeterscathedral.org is the cathedral's site (finder, masthead). Its "Weekly Bulletin" page
(/bullitens) sends readers to the ParishesOnline organization, and `probe po` gives its LPi id
and container. The bulletin is a text PDF. `group` is the cathedral parish's finder name; the
row also covers the finder's "Immaculate Conception Parish".
