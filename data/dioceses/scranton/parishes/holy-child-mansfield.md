## Publisher

Holy Child Parish, Mansfield (Rev. Philip Rayappan) publishes one weekly bulletin for its two
churches. The 11 October 2026 issue's masthead names "Holy Child Catholic Church, 242 S Main St,
Mansfield" and "Saint Mary of Częstochowa Catholic Church, 144 St Marys St, Blossburg", and its
"Regular Schedule of Masses" has Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 11:15 am at Holy Child, Sunday
9:00 am at Saint Mary's, and weekday Masses at both. No neighbouring parish shares the bulletin
or the pastor.

## Worship sites

- `holy-child-mansfield` (Holy Child, Mansfield; church and parish row share the slug)
- `st-mary-of-czestochowa-blossburg` (St. Mary of Częstochowa, Blossburg)

These are the two worship sites the diocesan finder lists for Holy Child Parish and the two
GCatholic has in Mansfield and Blossburg.

## Website and bulletin provider

holychildparish.com/about-us/bulletins/ (a Diocesan/Crossroads WordPress site, like Canton's)
links one PDF per Sunday under `cdn.diocesanweb.org/crossroads/holychildparish/wp-content/uploads/<yyyy>/<mm>/<yyyy>-<Nth>-Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time-year-a.pdf`.
File names carry the liturgical Sunday, not a date. No ParishesOnline, eCatholic or Discover
Mass id found. Recipe `page-pdfs` (the website is the bulletin page) pinned from `fetch:check` on 2026-10-17, after promotion, which
picked the first-listed `2026-29th-Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time-year-a_high-quality.pdf`: the 29th
Sunday is 18 October 2026, this week's issue. The pick is `first-listed` because the names
carry no date, so it relies on the page listing the newest issue first.
