## Publisher

St. Jude Parish, Mountain Top publishes a weekly bulletin through Bonventure (sponsor page
1647). Its pastor, Rev. Joseph J. Evanko, is also pastor of Our Lady Help of Christians
Parish (St. Mary's), Dorrance, and the bulletin carries Dorrance's Mass schedule: page 2 of
the 11 October 2026 issue has a "St. Mary's, Dorrance Liturgy Schedule" column with every
Mass there (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday 8:00 AM; Saturday 5:30 PM; Sunday 9:00 AM) next to St.
Jude's. Earlier issues (7 June, 5 July and 16 August 2026) have the same column.

Dorrance also printed a bulletin of its own (four scanned pages; the 16 August 2026 issue
names Fr. Evanko as pastor), but its site has posted nothing since 16 August, two months
before this was written. With Dorrance's own channel silent and St. Jude's current issue
carrying its full schedule, the two are one row here. See `revisit`.

## Worship sites

St. Jude Church, 420 South Mountain Boulevard (`st-jude-mountain-top`), and Our Lady Help of
Christians (St. Mary's) Church, 3529 St. Mary's Road, Wapwallopen (Dorrance Township)
(`our-lady-help-of-christians-wapwallopen`). Each is the only worship site the diocese's
finder lists for its parish.
