## Publisher

The canonical Saint John the Evangelist Parish, Honesdale has four churches and publishes
one weekly bulletin, posted on Discover Mass under St. John the Evangelist. The 18 October
2026 issue's masthead is "The Catholic Community of St. John the Evangelist, St. Mary
Magdalen, Honesdale, St. Joseph, Rileyville, St. Bernard, Beach Lake", parish office 414
Church Street, and its page 4 schedule ("The Lord's Day") covers all four. The diocese does
not group it with any other parish.

## Worship sites

Four, all in the 18 October 2026 schedule:

- St. John the Evangelist, Honesdale (`st-john-the-evangelist-honesdale`): Sunday 10:30AM.
- St. Mary Magdalen, Honesdale (`st-mary-magdalen-honesdale`): Saturday 5:30PM, Sunday
  7:30AM, daily Masses.
- St. Joseph, Rileyville (`st-joseph-rileyville`): Sunday 9:00AM.
- St. Bernard, Beach Lake (`st-bernard-beach-lake`): Saturday 4:00PM.

The finder and GCatholic list the same four. Perpetual adoration is in the Parish Center
next to St. Mary Magdalen, not a separate worship site.

## Website and bulletin provider

honesdalecatholic.com/weekly-bulletins/ is the parish site's bulletin page, which links the
Discover Mass listing; `discover_mass` reads by `provider_id`
`st-john-the-evangelist-honesdale-pa`. The issue has a text layer. The other three churches'
Discover Mass pages are empty.
