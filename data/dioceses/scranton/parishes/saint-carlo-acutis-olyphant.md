## Publisher

Saint Carlo Acutis Parish publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container
03/0879). I read the 18 October 2026 issue: the page-1 masthead names "Saint Carlo Acutis
Parish" with the Church of St. Patrick (Olyphant) and the Church of St. Anthony of Padua
(Throop), and one Mass schedule for both (SP and SA). The parish was formed on 1 July 2025
by consolidating Holy Cross Parish (St. Patrick, Olyphant) and Blessed Sacrament Parish
(St. Anthony of Padua, Throop), which already shared a pastor (diocesan announcement).
Blessed Sacrament's old ParishesOnline org has no publications.
