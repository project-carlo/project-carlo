## Publisher

Two canonical parishes, Our Lady of the Snows (Clarks Summit) and Saint Gregory (Clarks
Green), publish one weekly bulletin, "Thresholds", as "Abington Catholic", through
ParishesOnline (container 03/1016). I read the 18 October 2026 issue: page 1 is "the
Abington Catholic Parishes of Our Lady of the Snows and the Church of Saint Gregory", and
the page-5 Mass list covers three churches: St. Gregory's, Our Lady of the Snows and St.
Benedict's. Both parishes' finder websites (olsparish.net and thechurchofstgregory.com)
redirect to abingtoncatholic.org, which presents them as one community with "3 Worship
Sites"; St. Gregory's own ParishesOnline organization has no publications. So one row.
