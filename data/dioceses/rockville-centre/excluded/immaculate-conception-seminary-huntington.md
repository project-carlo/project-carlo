## Why it is excluded

GCatholic (40401) lists the chapel of Immaculate Conception Seminary, 440 West Neck Road, Huntington. The seminary closed in 2012: its site icseminary.edu now redirects to retreatsic.org, the Retreat & Conference Center of the Immaculate Conception, whose About page (read 2026-11-13) says it "served as a seminary for priestly and diaconate formation" until 2012, "until it began its transition to a fully-functioning retreat and conference center".

The center publishes no Mass schedule. Its home page offers "Weekly Exposition & Adoration of the Blessed Sacrament / Tuesdays 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. All are Welcome" (adoration, not Mass) and tells visitors to "check in at the Main Office for a pass"; groups staying there "plan your own liturgies". Mass appears only inside its events, which are "open to the public with advance registration" and charge fees (events page). The 2026 calendar lists one such Mass for the rest of the year ("Celebration of Mary ... Mass & Lunch", Sunday, December 6, 9:30 am to 1 pm). Masses for retreat groups and ticketed events are not a public Mass schedule, so this is `no-public-mass`. That is my reading of these pages; the center was not asked.

## What would change this

A regular Mass open to anyone who walks in (a weekly, Sunday or daily Mass on retreatsic.org or in a nearby parish bulletin) would make the chapel a church row. No parish bulletin read in this batch lists Mass there.
