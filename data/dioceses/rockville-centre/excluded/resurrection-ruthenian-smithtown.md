## Why it is excluded

GCatholic (church 17199, read 2026-11-13) lists the Resurrection Byzantine Catholic Church at Edgewater & Mayflower, Smithtown, New York 11787 with "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic", "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Rockville Centre", rite Ruthenian (Byzantine), Type: Church, website resurrectionsmithtown.org; GCatholic gives only the cross streets. It belongs to an Eastern Catholic eparchy, not to the Diocese of Rockville Centre, so its services and bulletin are another jurisdiction's and it gets no parish row here. Neither the diocesan parish locator nor the drvc.org Mass-times page lists it (their rosters were searched for the name and town on 2026-11-13); GCatholic is the only roster that does.

Whether it still holds services was not checked: the reason applies either way.

## What would change this

Nothing in this diocese's data: only a transfer of the church to the Latin Diocese of Rockville Centre, or a Latin parish starting public Mass in the building, would give it a church row here.
