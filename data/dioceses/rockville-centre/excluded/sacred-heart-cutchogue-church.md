## Why it is excluded

The Sacred Heart church building on Main Road, Cutchogue, no longer holds Mass. The Suffolk Times (6 December 2012) reported "This will be the final Christmas for Sacred Heart Church in Cutchogue, which will be closed at the end of the month", after an engineering inspection put repairs at $2 million, and that "In the new year, all Masses will be held in Our Lady of Good Counsel church, on Main Road in Mattituck". Newsday's report published 23 December 2012 describes "The final Mass at Sacred Heart Church in Cutchogue on Saturday", i.e. 22 December 2012. The parish site says today (2026-11-13) "** all Masses are at Our Lady of Good Counsel, Mattituck", and its 15 November 2026 bulletin's schedule names only Our Lady of Good Counsel.

The diocesan locator (directory:104), GCatholic (16830) and the Mass-times page (church-of-the-sacred-heart-cutchogue) still list the building; the Mass-times entry's times are marked "(Our Lady of Good Counsel Mission)", so they are the parish's Masses at Mattituck. The address follows the locator (27905 Main Rd); GCatholic gives 27700 Main Rd., the same block. Whether the building still stands was not checked.

## What would change this

A Sacred Heart bulletin or site schedule listing a Mass in the Cutchogue church again.
