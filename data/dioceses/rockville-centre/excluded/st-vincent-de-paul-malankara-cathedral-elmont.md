## Why it is excluded

The diocesan locator's entry 68 is "St. Vincent De Paul Syro-Malankara Catholic Cathedral", 1500 Depaul St, Elmont, linking the cathedral's own site. GCatholic (church 5047, read 2026-11-13) lists the St. Vincent de Paul Malankara Cathedral under the "Syro-Malankara Diocese of Saint Mary, Queen of Peace, of the United States of America and Canada", "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Rockville Centre": built as a Roman Catholic church in 1953, pro-cathedral 2010, cathedral 2013. It is an Eastern Catholic eparchy's church, so its services are another jurisdiction's. This record is the eparchy's entry, as the diocese's rule for shared buildings says.

The building is shared: GCatholic says "Sharing this church building: Church of St. Vincent de Paul, Elmont", the Latin parish (gcatholic:16839). Whether that Latin parish still has public Mass here is not settled (see `open/st-vincent-de-paul-elmont`). If it has, the building gets a church row under the Latin parish and this record stays for the eparchy's entry; if it hasn't, this record covers the building.

## What would change this

Nothing about the eparchy's services. A current Latin (Roman Rite) Mass schedule or bulletin for St. Vincent de Paul would add a church row for the building under the Latin parish.
