## What is known

Only the diocese's Mass-times page (read 2026-11-12) lists a "Mission Church (St. Joseph parish mission church)" under Oak Beach, linked to St. Joseph, Babylon, with no address. The schedule printed under it (two weekday Masses, eight Sunday Masses including "Chapel", "Spanish-Chapel" and "Youth Mass") is a large parish's, not a barrier-beach chapel's, and matches nothing St. Joseph publishes; it appears copied from another entry. The diocesan locator (drvc.org/maps) and GCatholic list no Oak Beach church. St. Joseph's own bulletins of 28 June, 2 August and 8 November 2026 never mention Oak Beach, and a site search for "oak beach" returns 0 results (all read 2026-11-13).

## Why it is not decided

Nothing shows Mass there now, and nothing says the mission closed. A beach chapel would hold summer Mass only, and the summer 2026 bulletins read show none, which is absence, not a closure notice. The June-August 2027 bulletins, or the parish office, would settle it.
