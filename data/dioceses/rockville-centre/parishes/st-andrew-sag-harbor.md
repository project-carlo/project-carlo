## Publisher

St. Andrew, Sag Harbor publishes its own weekly bulletin, made by the parish (no printer footer) and posted on its WordPress site. The November 15, 2026 issue (`https://www.standrewchurchsagharborny.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/November-15-2026.pdf`, read 2026-11-13) opens "St. Andrew Church, 122 Division Street, Sag Harbor, New York 11963" with "ST. ANDREW MASS SCHEDULE: SATURDAY: 5:15pm SUNDAY: 8:00am, 10:45am (Family Mass) MONDAY THROUGH FRIDAY: 9:00 am" and "Rev. Peter Devaraj, SAC, Pastor".

Fr. Devaraj is also pastor of Queen of the Most Holy Rosary, Bridgehampton, and St. Andrew's issue prints that church's Mass schedule too ("QUEEN OF THE MOST HOLY ROSARY MASS SCHEDULE: SATURDAY: 4:00pm SUNDAY: 9:30am", page 1; again on page 4). Queen of the Most Holy Rosary nonetheless posts its own bulletin with its own masthead ("Queen of the Most Holy Rosary Church ... Bridgehampton", the 1 November 2026 issue on qmhr.org), so the two are separate rows and the Bridgehampton church belongs to its own parish's row (see `publication_reviews`).

## Worship sites
