## Publisher

St. Joseph (Hewlett), Our Lady of Good Counsel (Inwood) and St. Joachim (Cedarhurst) publish one weekly bulletin as "The Catholic Community of the Five Towns" on their shared site, 5townsparishesny.com. Page 1 of the 8 November 2026 issue (`https://www.5townsparishesny.com/uploads/1/5/4/3/154337448/bulletin_nov_8_2026.pdf`, 10 pages, read 2026-11-12) reads "The Catholic Community of the FIVE TOWNS ... St. Joseph | Our Lady of Good Counsel | St. Joachim". Page 3, "Mass Times & Parish Directory", lists the three churches with their addresses (1346 Broadway, Hewlett; 68 Wanser Ave, Inwood; 614 Central Ave, Cedarhurst), one pastor (Rev. Henry W. Reid) and a Sunday schedule for each: St. Joseph Saturday 5PM and Sunday 10:30AM; OLGC 9am (Español) and 11am (English); St. Joachim 9:45am. It adds "FOR ALL ST. JOACHIM PARISH & RECTORY BUSINESS: PLEASE CONTACT ST. JOSEPH".

The pastor's booklet on the Five Towns parishes says they "are still three separate Canonical and Corporate entities" (read 2026-11-12). That is the canonical structure; the bulletin is one, so this is one row (README, *The model*).

## Worship sites

Three churches, each with Sunday Mass in the 8 November 2026 schedule: `st-joseph-hewlett-church`, `our-lady-of-good-counsel-inwood-church` and `st-joachim-cedarhurst-church`. The diocesan locator, GCatholic and the drvc.org Mass-times page list these three buildings and no others for Hewlett, Inwood and Cedarhurst. A grep of the issue for "Chapel" found no other place of Mass.
