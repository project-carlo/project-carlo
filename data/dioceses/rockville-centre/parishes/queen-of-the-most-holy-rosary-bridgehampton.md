## Publisher

Queen of the Most Holy Rosary, Bridgehampton posts its own bulletin, made by the parish (no printer footer), on its Weebly site. The issue embedded there on 2026-11-13 (`https://www.qmhr.org/uploads/4/9/1/3/49137859/012821.102526`, a PDF without a .pdf extension) is the 1 November 2026 issue ("This Sunday, November 1st"), with the masthead "Queen of the Most Holy Rosary Church, P.O. Box 3035, 2350 Montauk Highway, Bridgehampton, New York 11932" and "Rev. Peter Devaraj SAC, Pastor". It was twelve days old when read: the parish may post every other week or late.

Fr. Devaraj is also pastor of St. Andrew, Sag Harbor, whose bulletin prints this church's Saturday 4:00pm and Sunday 9:30am Masses beside its own. The two parishes' issues have different mastheads, so they are separate rows (see `publication_reviews`).

## Worship sites

One: the church at 2350 Montauk Highway (`queen-of-the-most-holy-rosary-bridgehampton-church`). The issue names no other place; the diocesan locator, GCatholic and the Mass-times page list only this church in Bridgehampton.

qmhr.org is a Weebly site whose Bulletin page embeds one document, replaced in place each time, at an upload path with no .pdf extension (`/uploads/4/9/1/3/49137859/012821.102526`, which answers as a PDF). fetch:check on 2026-11-13, unpinned and with `page-pdfs https://www.qmhr.org/bulletin.html`, both failed with "NoBulletin: No PDF links found on bulletin page". The row is `unsupported` with fetch gap `extensionless-download-link` (the same as Trenton's Eatontown `opendoc.asp` links); the pinned recipe stays in `provider_id` for when a collector follows such links.

St. Andrew, Sag Harbor's bulletin (collected) prints this church's weekend Mass times, but it is another parish's publication; the church stays on this row.
