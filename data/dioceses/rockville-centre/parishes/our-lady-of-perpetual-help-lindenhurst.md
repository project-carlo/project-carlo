## Publisher

The November 8, 2026 issue (`https://www.olphlindenhurst.com/uploads/2/5/2/5/25250560/november_8_2026.pdf`, read 2026-11-13) has the masthead "Our Lady of Perpetual Help Church, 210 South Wellwood Avenue, Lindenhurst" and one Mass schedule: Monday-Friday 6:30 a.m. and 12:15 p.m.; Saturday 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m.; Sunday 8:00 a.m., 10:00 a.m. and 12:00 p.m.; a Special Needs Mass on the first Saturday at 4:00 p.m. "in the auditorium".
