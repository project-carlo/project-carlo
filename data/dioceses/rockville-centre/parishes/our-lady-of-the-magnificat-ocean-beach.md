## Publisher

Our Lady of the Magnificat, Ocean Beach (Fire Island) is a summer parish of its own: its "2026 Seasonal Bulletin" (`https://www.oceanbeachcatholic.org/uploads/1/4/5/0/145015448/bulletin_2026_final.pdf`, 28 pages, created 18 May 2026, read 2026-11-13) calls it "the summer parish of Our Lady of the Magnificat in Ocean Beach", names "Father Edward Sheridan, Administrator" with three other priests, and its history page says it was "Founded first as a mission of Saint Patrick's Church in Bay Shore" and "Eventually, OLM became an independent parish". Mass intentions go to the diocese's Office of the Vicar General, not to a mainland parish. It publishes one booklet a season, not a weekly bulletin. The 2026 booklet's Mass schedule: Saturdays 5pm from May 23, and Saturdays 5pm and Sundays 9am from June 28 through September 6, plus a 9-11 Mass on Friday, September 11.

## Worship sites

One: the church on Midway between Bungalow Lane and Ocean Road (`our-lady-of-the-magnificat-ocean-beach-church`). The booklet names neither Our Lady Star of the Sea, Saltaire (a mission of St. Patrick, Bay Shore, which runs its own page for it) nor Most Precious Blood, Davis Park, though the diocesan locator links both to pages on this site (`saltaire-parish.html`, `davis-park-parish.html`, which answer 404; the Davis Park page was last archived in 2018).
