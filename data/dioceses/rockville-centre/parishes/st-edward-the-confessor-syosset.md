## Publisher

St. Edward the Confessor, Syosset has its own weekly bulletin, printed by The Church Bulletin Inc., West Babylon (pages 11-12: "#155 FOR ADVERTISING: THE CHURCH BULLETIN INC."), which posts no copies online; the parish posts each issue on its site. The newest issue on 2026-11-13, `https://img1.wsimg.com/blobby/go/17d7e365-3902-4196-80f4-d29d22b9288f/downloads/bce45fde-7cd0-4ff8-9595-149c3f08dd6f/Edward11-8.pdf?ver=1793910152095` (12 pages), opens with the masthead "St. Edward the Confessor / 205 Jackson Avenue, Syosset, New York 11791" and "Thirty-second Sunday in Ordinary Time / November 8th, 2026". Its text layer is subset-font symbol soup, so pages 1-3 were rendered and read as images. Page 3's presider schedule lists Saturday 5:00 PM and Sunday 7:30, 9:30, 11:30 AM and 6:00 PM.

Rev. Michael T. Maffeo is also pastor of Holy Name of Jesus, Woodbury (its November 8, 2026 masthead), which publishes its own bulletin; recorded as a `separate` review with `holy-name-of-jesus-woodbury`.

## Worship sites

One: the church at 205 Jackson Avenue (`st-edward-the-confessor-syosset-church`). ## Website and bulletin provider

The GoDaddy site's "Our Bulletin" page links each issue as a PDF on img1.wsimg.com, oldest first, named `Edward<m>-<d>.pdf` (once `Edwards-0920.pdf`). The provider is `other` with `page-pdfs` on that page. The pipeline will see a garbled text layer and needs to read pages as images.

`fetch:check` (2026-11-13) with this recipe picked `Edward11-8.pdf` (url_date 2026-11-08), the newest issue, although the page lists it last. A filename off the `Edward<m>-<d>` pattern (such as `Edwards-0920.pdf`) would need its url_date checked.
