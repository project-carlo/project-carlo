## Publisher

St. Joseph, 45 Church Street, Ronkonkoma publishes its own weekly bulletin, laid out in Canva and printed by The Church Bulletin Inc., West Babylon (back page "#180 FOR ADVERTISING: THE CHURCH BULLETIN INC."), which posts no listing online. The 8 November 2026 issue (`https://www.stjosephronkonkoma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/St.-Joseph-Bulletin-11-8-26.pdf`, 12 pages, read 2026-11-13 as an image of page 1, because the text layer is a shifted-font cipher) has the masthead "St. Joseph Roman Catholic Church, Ronkonkoma, NY" and names Rev. Michael J Rieder, Pastor, and Rev. Anthony Osuagwu, Associate Pastor. Masses: Saturday 5:00 PM; Sunday 7:00, 10:00 and Noon; Misa en Español 6:30 PM; Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday 8:00 AM; Tuesday and Thursday 7:00 and 9:00 AM.

## Worship sites

One: the church at 45 Church Street (`st-joseph-ronkonkoma-church`). The schedule names no other building; the bulletin's other address, 2855 Pond Rd, is the Parish Outreach office. The diocesan locator (directory:116), GCatholic (16906) and the Mass-times page list only this church.
