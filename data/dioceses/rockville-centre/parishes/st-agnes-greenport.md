## Publisher

St. Agnes, 523 Front Street, Greenport publishes its own weekly bulletin, laid out by the parish and printed by LPi (footer "St. Agnes Church | 03-4114"). The November 15, 2026 issue in container 03/4114 (`https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/03/4114/20261115B.pdf`, read 2026-11-13) has the page 1 masthead "Church of Saint Agnes, 523 Front Street, Greenport, NY 11944" and page 3 names "Fr. Michael Bartholomew – Pastor". The parish's own copy of the 8 November issue gives the schedule: Monday-Thursday 9:00 AM; Saturday 4:00 PM and 7:30 PM (Spanish); Sunday 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM from the first Sunday of November to the last of March, 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM from April to October; and "Fr. Jose Kuruvila – Associate".

Fr. Bartholomew is also pastor of Sacred Heart (Mattituck/Cutchogue), Our Lady of Ostrabrama (Cutchogue) and St. Patrick (Southold); each of those prints its own bulletin with its own masthead and printer job (LPi 03-4112, John Patrick 4037, LPi 03-4110): see `publication_reviews`.

## Worship sites

One: the church at 523 Front Street (`st-agnes-greenport-church`). The bulletin says the parish serves Greenport, East Marion and Orient, but its schedule names no other building. The diocesan locator, GCatholic and the Mass-times page list only this church.

The same bulletin is on ParishesOnline as `st-agnes-church-11944` (8 pages with the LPi footer), so the provider is `parishes_online` with that slug.
