## Publisher

Most Holy Trinity Parish, East Hampton publishes its own weekly bulletin, made by the parish (no printer footer) and posted on its Wix site. The November 15, 2026 issue (`https://www.mostholytrinityeh.com/_files/ugd/ad8d48_7fd14bad4bc64e068a35e01bad3e1bfe.pdf`, read 2026-11-13) has the masthead "Most Holy Trinity Parish / East Hampton with St. Peter, Amagansett / November 15, 2026" and "Rev. Ryan Creamer, Pastor".

Fr. Creamer is also pastor of St. Therese of Lisieux, Montauk from 2026-09-23 (the June 2026 appointments, per the batch brief). Montauk did not join this bulletin: the 15 November issue never names Montauk or St. Therese, and St. Therese still prints its own LPi bulletin (03-4096), whose 15 November page 2 lists "Father Ryan Creamer, Pastor" (see `publication_reviews`).

## Worship sites

Two, from the 15 November schedule: the church at 79 Buell Lane (`most-holy-trinity-east-hampton-church`; Monday-Thursday 8am, Sunday 9:30 and 11:30am "in MHT Church", Spanish Mass Wednesday 7:30pm and Sunday 7pm "en el Parish Hall", the hall on the same campus) and the mission St. Peter, Amagansett (`st-peter-amagansett-church`; "4pm Confessions, 5pm Mass in St. Peter's" every Saturday). The DRVC Mass-times page's "Sunday Mass: 2:00 PM (2nd & 5th Sundays)" for St. Peter is out of date: the bulletin puts the Saturday vigil there. The diocesan locator, GCatholic and the Mass-times page list no other place.
