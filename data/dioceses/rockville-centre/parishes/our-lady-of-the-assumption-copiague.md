## Publisher

Our Lady of the Assumption, 1 Molloy Street, Copiague publishes its own weekly bulletin (QuarkXPress layout, printed by The Church Bulletin Inc. of West Babylon, "#165"; that printer posts no bulletins online). The November 8, 2026 issue (`https://olacopiague.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/81/2026/11/nov8_2026_olacopia_compressed.pdf`, read 2026-11-13) has the masthead "Church of Our Lady of the Assumption" and one Mass Schedule across two buildings: Saturday 4:00 PM (New Church, English) and 7:00 PM (New Church, Spanish); Sunday 7:45 AM (Church, English), 9:00 AM (New Church, Polish), 10:30 AM (Church), 11:45 AM (New Church, Spanish), 1:30 PM (Church) and 5:00 PM (Church, Polish); weekdays 7:30 and 9:00 AM and Saturday 8:00 AM in the Church. The parish's Mass-schedule page (read 2026-11-13) gives the same.

The diocese's Mass-times page (2026-11-12) is older: a 5:00 PM vigil, Sunday 1:15 PM Spanish and 2:30 PM Polish, and "Shrine" where the bulletin now says "New Church".

## Worship sites

One row, `our-lady-of-the-assumption-copiague-church`, at 1 Molloy Street. The bulletin's schedule uses two buildings there, the "Church" and the "New Church". The parish site's photo page records the "Groundbreaking for the new Church, September 3, 2021" and "Construction updates for the new Church, October 25, 2022", and the site and bulletin give no address but 1 Molloy Street, so the New Church is taken to be on the same parish grounds and is not a separate row. The diocesan locator (directory:86), GCatholic (16827) and the Mass-times page each list one entry.
