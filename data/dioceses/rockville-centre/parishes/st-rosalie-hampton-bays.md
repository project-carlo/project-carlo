## Worship sites

Two, from the 15 November masthead: St. Rosalie's in Hampton Bays (`st-rosalie-hampton-bays-church`; weekdays 8 a.m. and noon, Saturday 8 a.m. and 5 p.m., Sunday 8, 9:30 and 11 a.m., Spanish Sunday 4 p.m.) and Saint Rosalie's in East Quogue (`st-rosalie-east-quogue-church`; "Sunday Mass 9 a.m.", which the parish site says runs "from Palm Sunday to End of Year"). The diocesan locator and GCatholic list only the Hampton Bays church; the DRVC Mass-times entry mentions "Sunday morning in East Quogue: 9:00 AM" under it. The parish's summer beach Masses (Tiana Beach, per a listing site's summary) are outdoor Masses, not a church.
