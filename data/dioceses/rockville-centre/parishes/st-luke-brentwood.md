## Publisher

St. Luke, 266 Wicks Road, Brentwood, administered by the Idente Missionaries, publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed with advertising by The Church Bulletin Inc. of West Babylon ("Publisher: The Church Bulletin Inc." on the last page), which posts nothing online. The November 15, 2026 issue (`https://sanlukes.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/20261115__-web-The-Roman-Catholic-Church-of-Saint-Luke.pdf`, read 2026-11-13) has the page 1 masthead "The Roman Catholic Church of Saint Luke" and one schedule: Saturday Vigil 5:00pm (English) and 7:00pm (Spanish); Sunday 7:30am and 11:00am (English), 9:15am, 12:30pm and 6:30pm (Spanish); Monday-Friday 8:30am (English) and 6:45pm (Spanish); Saturday 8:30am. The diocese's Mass-times page has Sunday 8:00 AM rather than 7:30; the bulletin and the parish home page (7:30 AM) are followed.

## Worship sites

One: the church at 266 Wicks Road (`st-luke-brentwood-church`); the Chapel the bulletin names for youth meetings and adoration is part of the parish plant. The diocesan locator (directory:121), GCatholic (16817) and the Mass-times page list only this building.
