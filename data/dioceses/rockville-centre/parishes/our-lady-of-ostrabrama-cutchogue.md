## Publisher

Our Lady of Ostrabrama, 3000 Depot Lane, Cutchogue publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (job 4037). The November 8, 2026 issue (`http://www.olochurch.org/images/BULLETIN/2026/703400-11-08-2026.pdf`, read 2026-11-13 by rendering: its pages are images) has the page 1 masthead "Our Lady of Ostrabrama Roman Catholic Church ... 3000 Depot Lane, Cutchogue, NY 11935". Page 2 lists "Rev. Mike Bartholomew, Pastor" and "Rev. Dante Sabban, Associate" and one schedule: daily 8:30am Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday; Saturday Vigil 5:30 pm; Sundays 8:00 am, 9:30 am (Polish), 11:00 am. The page 4 footer reads "4037 Our Lady of Ostrabrama, Cutchogue, NY (B) John Patrick Publishing Co."

Fr. Bartholomew is also pastor of Sacred Heart (Mattituck/Cutchogue), St. Agnes (Greenport) and St. Patrick (Southold), but each prints its own bulletin: see `publication_reviews` (Sacred Heart and St. Patrick are LPi-printed, St. Agnes's is parish-made, and each masthead names one parish). The group records the shared pastor.
