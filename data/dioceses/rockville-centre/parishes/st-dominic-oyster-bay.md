## Worship sites

One: the church at 93 Anstice Street (`st-dominic-oyster-bay-church`). The bulletin divides Masses between "Church" and "Chapel" and says "the altars in both the chapel and the church" have new candlesticks, but gives no separate address for the chapel; the site's map (`/apps/maps/`) shows one location, 93 Anstice St., and the diocesan locator (directory:40), GCatholic (16895) and the Mass-times page list one church. The chapel is taken as part of the parish's site at 93 Anstice Street (see *Not yet confirmed*). St. Dominic High School (110 Anstice St.) appears in the bulletin as the parish school, with no public Mass listed.
