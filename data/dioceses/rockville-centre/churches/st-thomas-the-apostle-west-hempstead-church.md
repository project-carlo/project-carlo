## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the parish bulletin for 15 November 2026 (`https://www.stawh.church/bulletin/231-11-15-2026/file`, read 2026-11-13) gives Masses at the church on Saturday at 5:00pm, Sunday at 9:00am, 10:30am, 12:00 noon and 6:00pm, and weekdays at 9:00am in the Lower Church (the parish site says Monday-Saturday). The drvc.org Mass-times page agrees. The Sunday 7:30, 10:00 and 11:30 Masses are at the Chapel on Hempstead Avenue, a separate building (`st-thomas-the-apostle-chapel-west-hempstead-church`).

GCatholic also places the Syro-Malabar St. Mary community in this building; that community's roster entry belongs to the eastern-catholic batch, and this row stays with the Latin parish under the diocese's rule because the Latin parish still has public Mass here.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead (24 Westminster Road, West Hempstead 11552), the diocesan locator ("St. Thomas the Apostle Church", 24 Westminster Road) and GCatholic (24 Westminster Rd.) agree; name and address are verified. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8P944+JM, about 30 m from the locator pin.
