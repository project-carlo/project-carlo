## Holds Mass

Yes, publicly, on the parish's weekend schedule. Page 1 of Infant Jesus's 8 November 2026 bulletin (`https://www.infantjesus.org/bulletin/236-11-08-2026-bulletin/file`, read 2026-11-13) lists "Chapel" Masses weekdays 12:00 Noon, Saturday Vigil 4:30 PM and Sunday 9:00 AM and 12:00 Noon, and page 8 places the chapel: "in the Chapel at St. Charles Hospital". The diocese's Mass-times page lists the same Chapel weekend Masses under Infant Jesus. These are parish Masses advertised to all, not a private hospital chapel.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it "Infant Jesus Chapel" and "the Chapel at St. Charles Hospital"; the record joins the two. The address is the hospital's, 200 Belle Terre Road (Catholic Health's St. Charles Campus page, read 2026-11-13); no source gives the chapel its own street address, and no roster (GCatholic, the diocesan locator) lists it, so name and address are unverified and coordinates are left for the geocode stage.
