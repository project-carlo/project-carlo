## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 3 of the 8 November 2026 Catholic Community of the Five Towns bulletin (`https://www.5townsparishesny.com/uploads/1/5/4/3/154337448/bulletin_nov_8_2026.pdf`) gives OLGC Sunday Masses at 9am (Español) and 11am (English), with Spanish adoration and confession on Wednesday at 7pm. The drvc.org Mass-times page (read 2026-11-12) also lists a Saturday 4:00 PM Mass, which the bulletin does not; the bulletin is followed.
