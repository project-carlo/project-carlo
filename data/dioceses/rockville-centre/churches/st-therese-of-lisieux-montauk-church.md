## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, an image; read 2026-11-13) lists Saturday Vigil 5pm and 7pm (Spanish), Sunday 8:30am and 10:30am, and daily Mass Tuesday-Friday 8:30am. The parish site gives the same, with the Spanish vigil at 8pm in summer.

GCatholic gives 55 S Etna Ave., probably the other street of a corner lot; I followed the parish and the diocese.
