## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 2 of the parish bulletin for November 8, 2026 (`https://www.stcatherineofsienna.org/whats-happening/bulletin/48-november-8-2026/file`, read 2026-11-13, rendered) gives Masses in the Church Monday through Friday at 8:00 A.M. and 12:10 P.M., Saturday 8:00 A.M., and Sunday 7:30, 8:45 and 11:30 A.M. The vigil and two Sunday Masses are in the SCS Chapel (its own record). The parish home page and the drvc.org Mass-times page give the same schedule.

## Name and address

The diocesan locator ("St. Catherine of Sienna Church", 33 New Hyde Park Road, 11010), GCatholic ("St. Catherine of Siena", 33 New Hyde Park Rd.) and the bulletin masthead agree on the address, so it is verified; the parish and the locator spell the name "Sienna", which is used here. The parish office is at 990 Holzheimer Street. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8P85F+8M, beside the locator pin.
