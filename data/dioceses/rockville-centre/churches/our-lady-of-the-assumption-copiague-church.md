## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 8 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives eight weekend Masses in English, Spanish and Polish, split between the "Church" (Sunday 7:45, 10:30 AM, 1:30 and 5:00 PM; all weekday Masses) and the "New Church" (Saturday 4:00 and 7:00 PM; Sunday 9:00 and 11:45 AM). Both are counted in this row; see the parish's *Worship sites*.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("Church of Our Lady of the Assumption", 1 Molloy Street, Copiague, New York 11726), the diocesan locator ("Our Lady of the Assumption Church", 1 Molloy Street) and GCatholic ("Our Lady of the Assumption", 1 Molloy St.) agree, so name and address are verified. The parish's Map & Directions page says it "is located at 1 Molloy Street". Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8MHMW+6P, about 25 m from the locator pin; which of the two buildings it marks was not checked.
