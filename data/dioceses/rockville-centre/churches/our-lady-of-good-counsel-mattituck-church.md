## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's November 15, 2026 bulletin (page 2, a scan) gives Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 am and 10:00 am, and weekday Mass at 9:00am Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday, and the parish home page (2026-11-13) says all Masses are here. Its livestream list shows Masses here on 8-11 November 2026.

## Name and address

The parish calls the building Our Lady of Good Counsel Church (bulletin, site); the diocese lists it as "Our Lady Of Good Counsel Mission" (locator) and "Our Lady of Good Counsel Mission Church" (Mass-times page), the same name. The bulletin and site give 14300 Main Road, Mattituck 11952, as does the diocesan locator, so name and address are verified. GCatholic does not list it. Coordinates are the locator's pin (40.99269, -72.52902), on Main Road in Mattituck; it was not checked against a building footprint.
