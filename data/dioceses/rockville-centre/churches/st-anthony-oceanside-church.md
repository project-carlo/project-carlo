## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the parish bulletin for Sunday, November 8, 2026 (`https://files.ecatholic.com/21516/bulletins/20261108.pdf`, read 2026-11-13) gives Masses on Saturday (Anticipated) at 5:00 PM; Sunday at 8:00, 9:30 and 11:00 AM, 12:30 PM (Chapel-in Spanish) and 5:00 PM; and weekdays at 9:00 AM and 12:00 NOON. The parish's Sacred Heart Chapel, where the Spanish and First Saturday Masses are said, is taken to be on these grounds (no source gives it an address).

## Name and address

The bulletin ("Saint Anthony's Oceanside, 110 Anchor Avenue, Oceanside, NY 11572"), the diocesan locator ("St. Anthony Church", 110 Anchor Avenue, 11572) and GCatholic (110 Anchor Ave., 11572) agree; name and address are verified. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8J9P5+P8, about 40 m from the locator pin.
