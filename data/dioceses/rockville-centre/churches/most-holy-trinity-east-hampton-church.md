## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 bulletin (read 2026-11-13) lists Monday-Thursday 8am and Sunday "9:30am Mass in MHT Church" and "11:30am Mass in MHT Church", with Spanish Masses on Wednesday 7:30pm and Sunday 7pm in the parish hall on the same campus. The Saturday vigil is now at St. Peter, Amagansett.

## Name and address

The parish calls it Most Holy Trinity (bulletin masthead); the diocesan locator says "Most Holy Trinity Church", GCatholic "Holy Trinity". Addresses differ: the locator gives 79 Buell Ln, GCatholic 57 Buell Ln. The bulletin gives 79 Buell Lane for the parish office, "to the right of the Church", so 79 may be the office's number rather than the church's. I recorded 79 Buell Lane (the diocese's address, and the parish's own for the campus) and left `address_verified` false; 57 Buell Lane may be the church building's own number. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G9XR53+9F, which sits on the building; the locator pin is about 115 m west.
