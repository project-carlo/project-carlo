## Holds Mass

Yes. The bulletin for November 15, 2026 (`https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/03/4100/20261115B.pdf`, read 2026-11-13) gives "Masses: SATURDAY 4:30pm • SUNDAY 8am, 10am, 12pm • MON - FRI 8:30am", a Haitian Mass every third Saturday at 6:30pm, and intentions for each Mass of the week.
