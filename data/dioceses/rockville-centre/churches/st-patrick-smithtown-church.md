## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 St. Patrick bulletin (page 3, "Masses for the Week", read from rendered pages 2026-11-13) lists Saturday 5:00 PM, Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 10:30 AM, 12:00 and 5:00 PM, and weekday Masses at 6:45 AM, 9:00 AM and 12:10 PM. The drvc.org Mass-times page also lists a Saturday 8:00 PM vigil, which the bulletin's list for 14 and 21 November does not show.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead gives "St. Patrick R.C. Church, 280 East Main Street, Smithtown, New York 11787"; the diocesan locator gives 280 E Main St, Smithtown 11787 and GCatholic 280 E Main St., so name and address agree across independent sources. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code, which sits within about 100 m of the locator's pin.
