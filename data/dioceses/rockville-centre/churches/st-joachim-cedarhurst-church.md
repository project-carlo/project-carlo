## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 3 of the 8 November 2026 Catholic Community of the Five Towns bulletin (`https://www.5townsparishesny.com/uploads/1/5/4/3/154337448/bulletin_nov_8_2026.pdf`) gives a Sunday 9:45am Mass at St. Joachim. Its rectory business now goes through St. Joseph ("FOR ALL ST. JOACHIM PARISH & RECTORY BUSINESS: PLEASE CONTACT ST. JOSEPH"), but the church keeps its Sunday Mass. The drvc.org Mass-times page (read 2026-11-12) gives an older schedule (Saturday 4:30 PM; Sunday 8:30 and 11:00 AM); the bulletin is followed.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("St. Joachim, 614 Central Ave, Cedarhurst, NY 11516"), the diocesan locator ("St. Joachim Church", 614 Central Avenue, 11516) and GCatholic ("St. Joachim", 614 Central Ave.) agree; name and address are verified. Coordinates are GCatholic Plus Code 87G8J7FH+JJ, about 20 m from the locator pin.
