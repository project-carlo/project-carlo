## Holds Mass

Yes. The Mass Times table on page 1 of the 8 November 2026 Glen Cove Catholic bulletin (`https://irp.cdn-website.com/b5c3ff4d/files/uploaded/Patrick11-8.pdf`, rendered to read the columns) gives St Hyacinth Saturday 9:00 AM, Saturday 5:00 PM and Sunday 9:00 AM and 10:15 AM (Polish); no weekday Mass in its column.

## Name and address

The bulletin names "St Hyacinth Church" but gives no street address. The diocesan locator ("St. Hyacinth Church", 319 Cedar Swamp Road, Glen Head 11545) and GCatholic ("St. Hyacinth", 319 Cedar Swamp Rd.) agree, so name and address are verified.

The two pins disagree by about 4 km: GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8R9XM+2P (40.8476, -73.6157) sits on Cedar Swamp Road at the north end of Glen Head, near the Glen Cove line, where a low street number like 319 belongs; the locator pin (40.8212, -73.5815) lies to the south-east, towards Brookville (2534 Cedar Swamp Road, St. Paul the Apostle, is at 40.7996, -73.5479). GCatholic's Plus Code is used. The parish's old site churchsthyacinth.com, which the Mass-times page links, is now an unrelated blog (2026-11-13).
