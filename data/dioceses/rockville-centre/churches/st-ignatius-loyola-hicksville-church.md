## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 15 November 2026 bulletin (`https://www.stignatiushicksville.org/uploads/9/7/9/7/97974144/st._ign._11-15-26.pdf`, page 1) lists Saturday 5:00 PM and Sunday 7:30 AM, 9:30 AM, 11 AM, 12:30 PM, 5:00 PM (Spanish) and 8:00 PM (Vietnamese) Masses, and weekday Masses at 7:00 and 9:00 AM.

## Name and address

The bulletin gives "129 Broadway (Rt. 107) Hicksville, New York 11801" and GCatholic "129 Broadway"; the diocesan locator gives "129 South Broadway". Route 107 in central Hicksville is signed both as Broadway and South Broadway, and all three point at the same building (locator pin and GCatholic Plus Code 87G8QF8H+F7 are about 50 m apart). The parish's own form, 129 Broadway, is recorded; the bulletin and GCatholic agree on it, so the address is verified.
