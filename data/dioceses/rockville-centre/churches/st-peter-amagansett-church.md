## Holds Mass

Yes, every Saturday. Most Holy Trinity's 15 November 2026 bulletin (masthead "East Hampton with St. Peter, Amagansett") gives the Saturday Mass schedule as "4pm Confessions, 5pm Mass in St. Peter's", and the parish's St. Peter page says the same (read 2026-11-13). The DRVC Mass-times page still gives "Sunday Mass: 2:00 PM (2nd & 5th Sundays)", an older schedule.

## Name and address

The parish's page calls it "St. Peter the Apostle (Mission Church)", 286 Montauk Highway at Miankoma Lane, Amagansett, NY 11930; the diocesan locator agrees ("St. Peter the Apostle Church", 286 Montauk Hwy, 11930). GCatholic ("St. Peter") has no street and gives ZIP 11937. The Mass-times page spells the town "Amangansett". Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G9XVG6+C9, which matches the locator pin within a few metres.
