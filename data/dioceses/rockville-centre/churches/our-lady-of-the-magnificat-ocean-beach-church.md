## Holds Mass

Yes, in summer. The parish's 2026 Seasonal Bulletin (read 2026-11-13) lists Saturday 5pm Masses from May 23 and Saturday 5pm and Sunday 9am Masses from June 28 through September 6, with a 9-11 Mass on Friday, September 11; it lists no Mass outside that season. Following the Camden/Trenton precedent for seasonal shore churches, the church keeps its row with `revisit_after` 2027-06-01, just after the 2027 season should open (2026's opened on the Saturday of Memorial Day weekend).

## Name and address

The booklet places the church "On Midway, between Bungalow and Ocean" (its history page: "located on Midway, between Bungalow Lane and Ocean Road"); GCatholic gives "Bungalow & Midway" and the diocesan locator 36 Bungalow Lane. These agree on the corner; the locator's street number is recorded as the only one any source gives, and `address_verified` stays false because no second source gives the number. The name is the same in the booklet, the locator and GCatholic. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8JRWR+HR; the locator pin is about 5 m from it.
