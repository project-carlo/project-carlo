## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 8 November 2026 bulletin (`https://www.smtbethpage.org/news/bulletin/756-november-8-2026/file`, page 1) gives Monday-Friday 9 a.m., Saturday 9 a.m. and 5 p.m., and Sunday 7:30 a.m., 10 a.m., 12 Noon and 5:30 p.m. Masses; page 2 lists intentions for 9-15 November.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead and the diocesan locator ("St. Martin of Tours Church") give 40 Seaman Avenue, Bethpage 11714; GCatholic ("St. Martin of Tours") gives only "Central Ave.", the cross street the church stands near. The parish's and the diocese's street address is recorded, and the two agree, so the address is verified. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8PGQC+6P (on the building), about 45 m from the locator pin.
