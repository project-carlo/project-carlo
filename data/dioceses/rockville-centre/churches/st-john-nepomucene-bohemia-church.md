## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the parish's November 8, 2026 bulletin lists Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 am, 10:00 am and 12 noon, Monday through Saturday 9:00 am and a Spanish Mass Saturday at 7:00 pm.
