## Holds Mass

Yes, seasonally. St. Rosalie's 15 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, read 2026-11-13) lists "SAINT ROSALIE'S IN EAST QUOGUE Sunday Mass 9 a.m.", and page 4 announces Mass there on 15 November at 9 a.m. The parish site says the East Quogue Mass runs "from Palm Sunday to End of Year", and the DRVC Mass-times page "only from Palm Sunday through Solemnity of Mary Mother of God, Jan. 1". A church with Mass for nine months a year keeps its row; the revisit checks the 2027 season.

## Name and address

The parish calls it "St. Rosalie's in East Quogue" (masthead, site) and gives only "Montauk Highway, East Quogue, NY 11942 (Does not receive mail)".
