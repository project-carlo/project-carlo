## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish bulletin for November 8, 2026 (flipbook.so viewer `https://flipbook.so/flip/ztexS2pcMP1b5jODqlXk`, read 2026-11-13) gives Saturday 5:00 p.m. vigil; Sunday 7:30 a.m., 9:30 a.m. and 11:30 a.m.; Spanish Mass 9:30 a.m. (Lower Church); weekdays Monday-Saturday 8:30 a.m. The drvc.org Mass-times page gives an older Sunday schedule (7:30, 9:00, 10:30, Noon) and calls the Spanish Mass's room the "School Church"; the parish's bulletin and home page say "Lower Church", taken here as the lower level of this building.
