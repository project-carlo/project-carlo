## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the St. Thomas the Apostle bulletin for 15 November 2026 (`https://www.stawh.church/bulletin/231-11-15-2026/file`, read 2026-11-13) lists "St. Thomas the Apostle Chapel, Hempstead Ave. & Nassau Blvd., Celebration of the Eucharist Sunday: 7:30am, 10:00am, 11:30am", and page 3 gives named intentions for the Chapel's 7:30, 10:00 and 11:30 AM Masses on 15 and 22 November. The parish home page and the drvc.org Mass-times page give the same three Sunday Masses at the chapel. The bulletin adds that the Chapel "is not in use on weekdays from mid-December through mid-March" and that weddings and funerals are generally in the main church.

## Name and address

The diocesan locator calls it "Lakeview Mission" at 875 Hempstead Ave (directory:11); the parish calls it "St. Thomas the Apostle Chapel" at 876 Hempstead Avenue, "Intersection of Hempstead Ave & Nassau Blvd" (its Directions page). GCatholic does not list it. The name and address follow the parish, its own building's owner and the more current source; the locator's 875 is one number off and would find the same corner. Neither is verified, since only one source gives each. Coordinates are the locator's pin, which sits on Hempstead Avenue near Nassau Boulevard; `pnpm geocode` or an OSM building would refine them.
