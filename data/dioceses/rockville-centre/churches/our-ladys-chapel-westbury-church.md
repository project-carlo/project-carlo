## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Brigid's bulletin for 15 November 2026 (`https://saintbrigid.net/bulletin/864-november-15-2026/file`, read 2026-11-13), page 22, lists "Sunday (Our Lady's Chapel in the School - 101 Maple Avenue) 7:30 am (Spanish) - 9:15 am (English) - 11:00 am (Spanish) 12:45 pm (Spanish)", and the page 21 Mass intentions for the weekend name each of these as "(Chapel/ ...)". The drvc.org Mass-times page lists the chapel under St. Brigid with an older set of times (8:00, 9:30, 10:45 AM, 12:15 and 6:00 PM). Four public Sunday Masses make it a worship site of its own.

## Name and address

Only the parish's bulletin gives the name and address: page 21 "Our Lady's Chapel is located in our school building at 101 Maple Avenue" and page 22 "Our Lady's Chapel in the School - 101 Maple Avenue". The diocesan Mass-times page calls it "Our Lady Chapel" with no address; the diocesan locator and GCatholic do not list it, so it is linked to no roster entry, and name and address are unverified. Coordinates are left null for the geocode stage (Nominatim answered 429 on 2026-11-13).
