## Holds Mass

Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 11:15 am, Wednesday and Friday 9:00 am, per page 2 of the 25 October 2026 bulletin and the parish home page. The summer guide's Sunday 10:15 am applied from 31 May to 6 September; the October bulletin is back to 11:15. One bulletin line reads 'NO MORNING MASS/CONFESSIONS' for Wednesday 28 October at St. Elizabeth, a one-off.

The directory's number (106) is used.
