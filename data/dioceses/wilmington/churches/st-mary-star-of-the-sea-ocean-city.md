## Holds Mass

Year-round. The 1 November 2026 bulletin lists Sunday 7:00 a.m. and Monday to Thursday 8:30 a.m. Mass at this church, and its intentions table assigns the 1, 2-5 and 8 November Masses to St. Mary. **The summer guide's '(Memorial Day – Labor Day)' on the Sunday 7 AM Mass is wrong or out of date:** the parish's Mass-times page prints the same Sunday 7:00 a.m. under both its summer and its 'Fall to Spring' schedule, and 109 people attended it on 17-18 October. So this is not a seasonal site and needs no revisit. **Daily Mass location:** the Mass-times page says weekday Mass is at Holy Savior 'until October' in the winter schedule, but the November bulletin and the directory put the 8:30 a.m. weekday Mass at St. Mary; follow the bulletin.

## Name and address

200 S. Baltimore Ave. (bulletin, directory, summer guide, parish site). GCatholic has 208 S Baltimore Ave., and the parish site's own map pin sits about 350 m north-east of the directory/GCatholic pins; I did not settle which, so `address_verified` stays false. ZIP 21842. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (matches the directory pin to about 30 m). Shares the St. Mary & Holy Savior bulletin.
