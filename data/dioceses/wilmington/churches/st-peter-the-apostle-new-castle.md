## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin (container 03/1089, page 1) gives St. Peter the Apostle Church Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8:00AM, Saturday vigil 5:30PM and Sunday 8:00AM and 11:00AM. The diocese map lists the same times.

## Name and address

The bulletin and the diocese map give 521 Harmony Street, New Castle 19720; GCatholic gives the corner 'E 5th St. & Harmony St.', which is the same block (the school is at 515 Harmony Street). The ParishesOnline org also lists 521 Harmony St. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within about 30 m of the diocese pin. The street address uses the bulletin's number rather than GCatholic's cross-street form.
