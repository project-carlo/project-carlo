## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin cover (image page) and the diocese map agree on Sunday 8:00 and 10:30, weekday 9:00 and a Saturday 7 pm Spanish Mass; the bulletin adds Tuesday 7 pm in Spanish. It is the parish's only church.

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic and the diocese map agree on 2503 Centerville Road, Wilmington 19808. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within 40 m of the diocese pin. (The ParishesOnline org's own pin, 39.7350, -75.6598, is off by about 3 km and not used.)
