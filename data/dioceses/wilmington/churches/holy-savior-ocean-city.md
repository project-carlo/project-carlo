## Holds Mass

Year-round: Saturday vigil 5:15 p.m. and Sunday 10:00 a.m. in the 1 November 2026 bulletin and the parish's 'Fall to Spring' schedule (the bulletin marks the parish 'Off Season'). From Memorial Day weekend a Sunday 8:30 a.m. Mass is added (parish page; summer guide). The summer guide's heading 'beginning Memorial Day weekend' would read as the whole church being summer-only, but the November bulletin and attendance counts (172 and 301 on 17-18 October) show it is open now, so it is not a seasonal site and needs no revisit. The bulletin calls it 'Holy Savior Mission Church'; it is a worship site of the St. Mary Star of the Sea & Holy Savior parish.

## Name and address

1705 Philadelphia Avenue, Ocean City, ZIP 21842 (bulletin, parish site, directory, summer guide; GCatholic '17th St. & N Philadelphia Ave.'). ParishesOnline's org record gives ZIP 21843 and a pin 20 km off, not used. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within 5 m of the directory pin. Shares the St. Mary & Holy Savior bulletin.
