## Holds Mass

The shared bulletin lists Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 a.m. and Tuesday-Thursday 9:00 a.m. Mass here. Its page 6 says the Saturday 5:00 p.m. Mass is discontinued effective 1 November 2026; the Saturday vigil is still listed on page 1 and by the diocese map (Sat 17:00). The row stays open: Sunday and weekday Masses remain, and the schedule change should be read in the next issues (revisit).
