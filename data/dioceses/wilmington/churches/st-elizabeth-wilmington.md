## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists a Saturday 4:00 PM vigil, Sunday 7:30 AM and 10:00 AM, and weekday Masses (Monday and Friday 12:15 PM, Tuesday and Wednesday 7:15 AM, Saturday 8:15 AM). The diocese map gives the same times.

## Name and address

The bulletin (parish office), the diocese map and OpenStreetMap say 1500 Cedar Street, Wilmington 19805; GCatholic gives the cross-streets Cedar and Clayton. Coordinates: the diocese pin is about 110 m east of GCatholic's Plus Code and of OSM's building outline, which agree within about 30 m, so GCatholic's is used. The 1500 Cedar Street address is shared with the parish school in OSM.
