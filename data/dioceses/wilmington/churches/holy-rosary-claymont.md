## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists the Saturday 4:30 pm vigil and Sunday 8:00, 10:00 and 12:30 pm (Spanish) Masses. The diocese map shows the same times. The Spanish Mass is held in this church, so there is no second site. Weekdays are 8:00 am with no Monday Mass (the bulletin's 'Weekdays' block reads '8:00 am (No Monday Mass)'); the diocese map lists Tuesday to Saturday 8:00, and the IHM bulletin's deanery box says 'Tuesday - Saturday: 8:00 a.m.'.

## Name and address

GCatholic, the diocese map and the bulletin cover agree on 3200 Philadelphia Pike, Claymont 19703. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, agreeing with the diocese pin to about 30 m.
