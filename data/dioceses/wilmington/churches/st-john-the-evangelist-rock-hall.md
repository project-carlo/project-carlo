## Holds Mass

Sunday 8:00 am and Thursday 8:30 am, per the 27 September 2026 Sacred Heart bulletin cover; the diocese map has the same times and the summer guide Sunday 8 AM. The summer guide calls it 'mission of Sacred Heart, Chestertown'; the parish home page says 'One Parish - Two Churches'. It shares Sacred Heart's bulletin (see `parishes/sacred-heart-chestertown.md`).
