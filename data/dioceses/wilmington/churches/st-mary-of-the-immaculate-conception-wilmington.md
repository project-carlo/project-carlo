## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 Downtown Catholic bulletin (page 1) lists Saint Mary: Sunday 8 am and First Saturdays 11 am. The diocese map lists Sun 8:00 and Sat 11:00 (it omits that the Saturday Mass is First Saturdays only). So Mass is celebrated here weekly on Sunday.

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic, the diocese map and OpenStreetMap agree on the church and its corner, 6th and Pine Streets. The street number 600 E 6th St. comes from the diocese map and OpenStreetMap (GCatholic gives only the cross streets). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within about 25 m of the diocese pin and OpenStreetMap's centre. The diocese's deanery page still lists it as a parish; see the parish record for the realignment question.
