## Holds Mass

The 11 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday vigil 5:00 pm, Sunday 10:00 am and Tuesday-Thursday 12:10 pm. The diocese map's times are the same. The Mass intentions on page 2 name Masses on 10, 11, 13, 14, 15, 17 and 18 October at those hours.

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic, the diocese map and OpenStreetMap agree on St. Joseph at 1012 (N.) French Street, Wilmington 19801. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within about 10 m of the diocese pin and OpenStreetMap's centre. This is the downtown St. Joseph, not St. Joseph on the Brandywine (Greenville) or St. Joseph, Middletown.
