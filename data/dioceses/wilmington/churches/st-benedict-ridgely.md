## Holds Mass

Sunday 8:00 am, Tuesday and Thursday 9:00 am, Wednesday 6:00 pm, and first Saturdays 9:00 am, per page 2 of the 25 October 2026 bulletin and the parish home page; no Monday Mass. The diocese map's list also shows Friday and Saturday-afternoon times that the bulletin assigns to St. Elizabeth, Denton, so those are not St. Benedict's. The summer guide's Sunday 7:15 am (to 31 May) changed to 8:00 am.

## Name and address

Name and address (408 Central Avenue, Ridgely 21660) agree in the bulletin, site, directory and GCatholic. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 10 m from the directory pin (38.943897, -75.881383).
