## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday vigil 4 pm, Sunday 10 am and Wednesday 9 am at St. Bernadette. The parish site, the diocese map and the summer guide agree. It is a church of the St. John the Apostle/St. Bernadette parish and prints its own address, phone and office hours in the masthead.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, GCatholic and the diocese map agree on 109 Dixon St, Harrington, DE 19952. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 45 m from the diocese pin (the ParishesOnline account's own pin is also near: 38.926701, -75.571073, about 125 m off, not used). The diocese map has no deanery for this entry; the parish site places both churches in the Central Deanery.
