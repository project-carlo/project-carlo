## Holds Mass

The 18 October 2026 bulletin (page 7) lists the vigil Saturday 4:00 p.m., Sunday 8:30 and 11:00 a.m., and weekday Mass Monday to Thursday 9:00 a.m. The diocese map's Mass times are the same. This is the parish's only church.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead names it 'Church of the Holy Child'; GCatholic and the diocese map call it 'Holy Child'. Address 2500 Naamans Road, Wilmington 19810 agrees in GCatholic, the diocese map, ParishesOnline. Coordinates are the diocese map pin (39.825419, -75.515745), which sits about 40 m from ParishesOnline's. GCatholic's Plus Code is on Holy Family, Newark, about 20 km away, so the roster's coordinates are ignored.
