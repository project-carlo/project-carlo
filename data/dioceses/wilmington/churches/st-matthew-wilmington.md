## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin's box 'STM MASS TIMES' gives Tuesday, Thursday and first-Saturday 8:30am, Saturday 5:15pm and Sunday 7:00am (no music) and 9:00am; page 2 lists intentions at those times. The diocese map also lists a Friday 18:00 Mass that the bulletin box doesn't show (the bulletin has a First Friday 8:30am Sacred Heart devotion at Corpus Christi); the church holds Mass regardless.

## Name and address

GCatholic, the diocese map and the bulletin's page-1 address agree on 901 E. Newport Pike, Wilmington 19804. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, which agrees with the diocese pin to about 30 m. The bulletin names the church 'St. Matthew R. C. Church'; the parish office for both churches is here.
