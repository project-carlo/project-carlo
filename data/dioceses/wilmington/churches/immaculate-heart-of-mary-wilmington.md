## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin gives Mass Monday to Friday 6:30 am, Saturday 9:00 am and 5:00 pm, Sunday 7:00, 9:00 and 11:00 am. The diocese map lists the same times. It is the parish's only church.

The bulletin, ParishesOnline and the diocese map give 4701 Weldin Road, Wilmington 19803; GCatholic gives the intersection 'Shipley Rd. & Weldin Rd.', the same corner. The three coordinates (GCatholic Plus Code, diocese pin, ParishesOnline) are within about 130 m of each other, and the Plus Code is kept as building-precise.
