## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday vigil 5 pm, Sunday 7:30, 9:30 and 11:30 am (Spanish) and weekday Mass at 8 am (Wednesday 8:15). The parish's own site and the diocese map give the same times. The map also lists Saturday 8:00 (the bulletin's Saturday 8 am).

## Name and address

The parish site, GCatholic and the diocese map agree on 631 S State St, Dover 19901. The parish's own name is 'Church of the Holy Cross'; the diocese and GCatholic say 'Holy Cross'. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 35 m from the diocese pin.

The directory's website for this church (sub.ireland724.info) is a spam page and GCatholic's (holycrossnet.org) has no DNS; the working site is holycrossdover.org.
