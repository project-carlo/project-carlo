## Holds Mass

Year-round. The 1 November 2026 bulletin: Saturday vigil 4:00 p.m., Sunday 9:00 and 11:00 a.m., Monday, Tuesday and Friday 9:00 a.m. The parish page, the directory and the summer guide agree (the summer guide has only the weekend). The parish is in Ocean Pines, which the diocese's city alias maps to Berlin.

## Name and address

11211 Beauchamp Road, Berlin MD 21811 (bulletin, parish site, directory, summer guide); GCatholic has 'Beauchamp Ave.', a typo for the same place. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 25 m from the directory pin.
