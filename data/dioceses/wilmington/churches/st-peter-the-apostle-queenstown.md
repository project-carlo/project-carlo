## Holds Mass

Saturday 5:00 pm vigil, with confession 3:30-4:45 pm, per the 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, an image) and the parish home page. The summer guide of May 2026 announced it as 'Reopening July 4, 2026 following renovation'; the October bulletin shows the vigil running, so it has reopened.
