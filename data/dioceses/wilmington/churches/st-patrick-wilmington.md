## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 Downtown Catholic bulletin (page 1) lists Saint Patrick's Masses: Sunday 8 am (Latin), 9:30 am, 11 am (Latin) and 5 pm, Monday and Wednesday 5:30 pm (Latin), Tuesday and Thursday 5:30 pm, First Friday 6 pm (Latin), First Saturday 9 am (Latin). The diocese map lists the same Sunday and weekday times. It is the Downtown Catholic church with the most Masses.

## Name and address

The bulletin cover, GCatholic and the diocese map agree on 'Saint Patrick' at the corner of 15th and King Streets, Wilmington 19801. None gives a street number for the church (the Downtown Catholic office address, 1414 N. King Street, is the parish office and may or may not be the church), so address_verified stays false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 12 m from the diocese pin. A Nominatim search found no OpenStreetMap entry to compare. What would settle the number: a source that gives the church's own street address.
