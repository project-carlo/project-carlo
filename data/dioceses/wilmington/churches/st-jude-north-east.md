## Holds Mass

Sundays 9:00 am, in the 25 October 2026 bulletin, the summer guide and the diocese map. It is a mission of Immaculate Conception and appears on that parish's masthead.

## Name and address

The bulletin and summer guide give 928 Turkey Point Road; the diocese map has the same with ZIP 21901, GCatholic only 'Turkey Point Rd., Rt. 272' with ZIP 21921 (the bulletin gives no ZIP). 21901 is North East's ZIP and is used; the discrepancy is only the ZIP. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 20 m from the diocese pin.
