## Holds Mass

Year-round. The 25 October 2026 bulletin: daily 8:30 a.m., Saturday vigil 4:00, Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 10:30 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. In summer the Saturday vigil adds 5:30 p.m. (May 23-September 5; 19 July 2026 bulletin and summer guide). The summer guide's Saturday '4 PM, 5:30 PM' and the directory's Saturday 4:00 and daily 8:30 agree with the bulletin.

## Name and address

401 King Charles Ave. is the church's address on the parish contact page and in the directory. The summer guide's 409 King Charles Ave. and the ParishesOnline org's 409 are the parish office next door. GCatholic gives the corner King Charles Ave. & Laurel St. (the bulletin's summer masthead says the same). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, which falls on the church.
