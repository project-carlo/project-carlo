## Holds Mass

The Newman Center's home page lists daily and Sunday Masses (Sunday 11:30 AM and 8:15 PM; Monday-Thursday 4:30 PM; Friday 12:45 PM), restricted by its own wording to UD students and actively employed UD faculty and staff. The diocese map shows no Mass times. See the parish record for the open question of whether this counts as public Mass.

## Name and address

The site, the diocese map and GCatholic agree on 45 Lovett Ave., Newark 19711. The name differs: the site says 'The Newman Center', the diocese 'The Newman Center @ St. Thomas More Oratory' (deaneries page: 'Saint Thomas More Oratory, Newark'), GCatholic 'Oratory of St. Thomas More'. They are the same building.
