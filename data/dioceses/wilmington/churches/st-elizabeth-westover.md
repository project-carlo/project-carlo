## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Wednesday 8:30 a.m., Saturday 5:30 p.m., Sunday 8:00 a.m. and a Spanish Mass Sunday 5:00 p.m., from the parish's Mass page and the diocese's directory, which agree. It is the second church of the Holy Name of Jesus parish row; it is on neither the deaneries page nor the summer guide.

## Name and address

The name is on the parish site and in both rosters. The address differs: the directory gives 8734 Old Westover Marion Road, Westover 21871, GCatholic only 'Old Westover Marion Rd.' with ZIP 21851 (Pocomoke City's). The street number rests on the directory alone and the parish site gives no address, so `address_verified` is false; 21871 is Westover's ZIP and is used.
