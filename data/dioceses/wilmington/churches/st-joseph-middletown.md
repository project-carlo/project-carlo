## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, an image) lists Saturday 5:00 PM, Sunday 8:30 AM, 10:30 AM and 1:00 PM (Spanish), and weekdays Monday to Friday 8:30 AM. The parish website and the diocese map give the same times.

## Name and address

The name agrees in the bulletin, website, directory and GCatholic. The street number does not: the bulletin cover, the website's mailing address and GCatholic say 371 East Main Street; the directory and the website's 'St. Joseph' contact box say 319 E. Main Street (the John Patrick listing also prints 319). One campus, the two numbers about 60 m apart on the same street (directory pin 39.451136, -75.704801; GCatholic 39.4510625, -75.7055625). 371 is followed because the bulletin and GCatholic agree; the address flag stays false until the 319 / 371 split is settled (319 may be the parish center or office).
