## Holds Mass

Daily and weekend Masses per the issue dated 1 November 2026 (page 2) and the parish Mass Times page, read 27 October 2026: Monday-Friday 8:30am, Saturday 5:30pm and a Spanish Mass Saturday 7:30pm, Sunday 8:30am and 10:30am. The summer guide lists Saturday 5:30pm and Sunday 8:30am and 10:30am (without the weekday and Spanish Masses), and the directory agrees with the bulletin. The parish office is next door at 1214 S. Washington Street, the address ParishesOnline holds.

## Name and address

The bulletin and site say 'Saints Peter and Paul' / 'Ss. Peter and Paul'; the directory and GCatholic say 'Sts. Peter and Paul'. The street address agrees in the bulletin, directory, GCatholic and summer guide. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 70 m from the directory pin. ParishesOnline's own org pin (38.7677, -76.0604) is about 2.6 km north and is not used.
