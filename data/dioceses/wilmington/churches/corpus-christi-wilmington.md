## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin's 'CC MASS TIMES' box and page-2 intentions show weekday Mass Monday and Wednesday (and first Friday) 8:30am, Saturday vigil 4:00pm and Sunday 8:00am and 10:30am. It shares a bulletin, pastor and office (at St. Matthew's) with St. Matthew.

## Name and address

The bulletin and GCatholic say 903 New Road; the diocese map says 905 New Road, so the street number is left unverified. The town is Wilmington (directory, bulletin) or Elsmere (GCatholic, deaneries page): the same place, a Wilmington mailing address (19805). Coordinates are the diocese pin, because GCatholic's Plus Code is on Holy Child, Naamans Rd (a known rotated code in this diocese). The pin is not building-checked.
