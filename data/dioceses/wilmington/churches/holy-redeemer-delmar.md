## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Saturday 4:00 p.m. and Sunday 9:00 a.m., plus a Korean Mass on the 2nd and 4th Saturdays, in the 25 October 2026 bulletin, on the parish site and in the summer guide. It is the parish's second church, which the summer guide calls a 'mission of St. Francis de Sales' and the parish site 'one parish with two churches'; both appear on one masthead and Mass schedule.

## Name and address

The bulletin, parish site, directory and summer guide agree on 501 East Chestnut Street, Delmar, 21875. GCatholic gives ZIP 21801 (Salisbury's) with the same street; 21875 is Delmar's and is used. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 20 m from the directory pin.

## Disagreement

The Korean Mass time: 6:00 p.m. in the 25 October bulletin, 7:00 p.m. on the parish site, the summer guide (as of 12 May 2026) and the directory. The church is not in doubt; record the time from the newer bulletin when a schedule is built, and check it against a later issue.
