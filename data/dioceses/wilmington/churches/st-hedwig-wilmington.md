## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Saturday 4:00 p.m., Sunday 9:00 a.m., 11:30 a.m. (Polish) and 4:00 p.m., and weekday Masses Monday-Friday 6:30 p.m. and Saturday 8:00 a.m. in the Pope Saint John Paul II Chapel. The diocese map agrees. The Polish Mass is Sunday 11:30.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the diocese map, GCatholic and OpenStreetMap agree on St. Hedwig, 408 S. Harrison Street, Wilmington 19805. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within about 15 m of the other pins. The chapel is taken to be inside the same property: the bulletin gives it no separate address.
