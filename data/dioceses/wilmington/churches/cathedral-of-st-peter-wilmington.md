## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 Downtown Catholic bulletin (page 1) lists the cathedral's Masses: Sunday 9:30 am, Monday, Wednesday and Friday 12:10 pm, Saturday 4 pm. The diocese map agrees exactly. The cathedral is the seat of the Diocese of Wilmington and is not on GCatholic.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the diocese map and OpenStreetMap give 'Cathedral of Saint Peter' (the map: 'Cathedral of St. Peter'). The bulletin listing and OpenStreetMap give 500 North West Street, Wilmington 19801, which is the corner of 6th and West Streets named by the bulletin cover and the diocese. Coordinates are OpenStreetMap's way centre (the 'osm-node' source value); the diocese pin is about 30 m off. No GCatholic entry exists to compare.
