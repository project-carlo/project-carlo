## Holds Mass

A summer-only Sunday 9:00 a.m. Mass in the parish hall next to the church, 24 May to 13 September in 2026. Three sources agree on the dates: the 19 July 2026 bulletin box, the summer guide and the parish's schedule page. It is a worship site of St. Edmond's own bulletin, listed in the summer guide as a 'Seasonal Mass site', so under the seasonal-site rule it keeps a church row with a revisit for next season. The Hall is not the church: the contact page names '407 King Charles Ave. (Education & Social Center/Hall)' separately from the church at 401.

## Name and address

407 King Charles Ave. comes from the parish contact page; OSM has a building at that number, which gives the coordinates. The bulletin and summer guide give no address for the Hall, so the address is inferred from the 'Hall' label and the contact page: the flags stay false. Nothing says the Hall Mass is in any other building.
