## Holds Mass

Year-round, weekly. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday vigil 5:00 pm (English) and 7:00 pm (Spanish); Sunday 7:30 am and 9:00 am (English) and 12:00 pm (Spanish); Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 9:00 am; Thursday 7:00 pm (Spanish); plus a 9:00 am Saturday Mass on the first Saturday only. The directory's `mass_times` agrees except for a Saturday 9:00 am Mass listed as weekly: the bulletin says 1st Saturday only, so don't read the directory's Saturday 9:00 as weekly (see the settled rule on directory Mass times). Page 2 shows Monday 'No Mass'.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the Bon Venture listing and GCatholic say 532 E. Stein Hwy; the parish's own home page and the directory say 528 E. Stein Highway. Both are ZIP 19973 and the pins are about 10 m apart, so it is one building with two numbers in use. I kept the bulletin's 532 and left `address_verified` false; a county parcel record or the church's signage would settle which number is current. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
