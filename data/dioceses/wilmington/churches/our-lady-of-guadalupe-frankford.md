## Holds Mass

Year-round: Saturday vigil 5:30 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM in Spanish (25 October 2026 bulletin; its Friday 6:30 PM Spanish prayer service and rosary is a devotion, not Mass). The summer guide adds a Sunday 11 AM from 28 June to 6 September. It is a mission church of the Parish of St. Ann and prints no bulletin of its own.

## Name and address

35318 Church Rd., Frankford 19945 in the bulletin, parish footer, directory, GCatholic, summer guide and OSM. **The pins disagree**: the directory's (38.5007, -75.3965) is about 21 km west of OSM's church building and GCatholic's Plus Code (38.5116, -75.1177) about 3 km east of it, all for one address. I used OSM's way, the one that names the building 'Our Lady Of Guadalupe Mission Church' at 35318 Church Road, so the directory pin is the outlier. Not checked on the ground; `geocode` or a map check can settle the ~3 km gap with GCatholic.
