## Holds Mass

Sunday 9:00 am and 11:00 am; Monday and Tuesday 8:30 am; Wednesday to Friday 8:30 am is a prayer service, not Mass (25 October 2026 bulletin, page 1, read as an image; the parish home page agrees). Reconciliation and adoration Mondays 4:45-6:00 pm. The directory's Saturday 17:00 belongs to St. Peter, Queenstown.

## Name and address

Name agrees in the bulletin, site, directory, GCatholic and summer guide. The street number disagrees: the bulletin, the parish site and the summer guide give 303 Chesterfield Ave; the diocese map and GCatholic give 301 Chesterfield Ave (the parish office is 301 Homewood Ave). The pins coincide (GCatholic's Plus Code is about 10 m from the directory's 39.04755, -76.068691), so it is one building; `address_verified` stays false because of the number. The bulletin's number is used. ParishesOnline's own pin (about 250 m away) is not used.
