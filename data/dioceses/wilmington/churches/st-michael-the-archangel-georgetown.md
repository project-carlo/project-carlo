## Holds Mass

Year-round, weekly. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (ParishesOnline 04/0868, page 3, rendered) lists Saturday 4:30 PM (Spanish) and 7:00 PM; Sunday 7:00 AM (Spanish), 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM (Spanish); Monday and Tuesday 9:00 AM; Tuesday and Thursday 7:00 PM (Spanish). The parish site's Mass Schedule page, the directory's `mass_times` and the summer guide give the same times. The bulletin says 'we offer ... Hispanic Masses at St. Michael's' (page 5), so all Spanish Masses are here, none at Mary Mother of Peace.

## Name and address

202 Edward St., Georgetown 19947 in the bulletin (the parish office), the parish site, the directory, GCatholic and the summer guide. The diocese's deaneries page names the parish 'Saint Michael the Archangel/Mary Mother of Peace'. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; the directory pin is 55 m away. The parish office and the church are on the same street address; the religious education building is at 200 Edward St. (bulletin page 3).
