## Holds Mass

Year-round. The 1 November 2026 bulletin: Saturday vigil 4:00 and 5:30 p.m.; Sunday 8:30, 10:00, 11:30 a.m. and 5:00 p.m.; Monday to Saturday 9:00 a.m. (All Saints' Day, Immaculate Conception also listed). The directory's times and the summer guide's weekend times agree with it, so there is no summer difference at St. Luke.

## Name and address

9903 Coastal Highway (bulletin, summer guide, parish office page) is the same place as '100th & Coastal Hwy.' (directory, ParishesOnline) and '100 St. & Coastal Hwy.' (GCatholic); ZIP 21842 in all. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 50 m from the directory pin. The parish office is at St. Andrew's (14401 Sinepuxent Ave.), not here.
