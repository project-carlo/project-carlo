## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin cover lists Holy Angels Church at 82 Possum Park Road with Masses on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday (mostly Spanish, with an English Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 9:00 am; see the evidence). It also has a 24-hour Eucharistic Adoration chapel at the far end of the parish centre.

## Name and address

The directory (directory:120) and GCatholic (gcatholic:19338) list this address as 'St. John the Baptist' and 'St. John the Baptist-Holy Angels'. The bulletin shows that 82 Possum Park Road is Holy Angels Church and that St. John Church is a separate building on East Main Street (see st-john-the-baptist-newark). So the rosters' single entry is matched to Holy Angels, and the name here follows the bulletin. `name_verified` is false: no source other than the bulletin names the church 'Holy Angels' alone (GCatholic's 'Holy Angels' appears only in the hyphenated name). Address: bulletin, directory and GCatholic agree. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6M7XG+HW), within 30 m of the directory pin.
