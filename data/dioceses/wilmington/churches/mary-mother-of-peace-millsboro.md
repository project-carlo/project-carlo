## Holds Mass

Year-round, weekly. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (ParishesOnline 04/0868, page 3, rendered) lists Saturday 4:00 PM; Sunday 7:30 AM and 11:00 AM; Wednesday and Friday 9:00 AM, all in English. The parish site's Mass Schedule page, the directory and the summer guide give the same times. The church shares its bulletin with St. Michael the Archangel, Georgetown (parish record).

## Name and address

The directory and the summer guide give 30839 Mount Joy Road, Millsboro 19966; GCatholic gives 'Route 24 & Route 5, Oak Orchard', also 19966. The two describe one spot (pins 35 m apart; Mount Joy Road is the street the bulletin names for Mary's Cupboard at 30807), but no source read here prints the church's own street number: the bulletin and parish site don't, so `address_verified` stays false until the number is read from the church's or the parish's own page (the site's Contact Us page was not read). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code. The diocese's city alias 'Oak Orchard' maps to Millsboro.
