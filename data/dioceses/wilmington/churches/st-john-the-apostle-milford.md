## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday vigil 5 pm, Sunday 9 am, 11 am and 1 pm (Spanish), a 9 am Mass on the first Saturday only, and weekday Mass Monday 12 noon and Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 9 am. The parish site, the diocese map and the summer guide give the same times (the map and guide omit the first-Saturday Mass).

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, GCatholic and the diocese map agree on 506 Seabury Ave, Milford, DE 19963. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 25 m from the diocese pin. GCatholic's website (stjohn-stbernadette.org) has no DNS; the working site is stjohnsmilford.com.
