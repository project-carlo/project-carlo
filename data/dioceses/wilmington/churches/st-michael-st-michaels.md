## Holds Mass

Saturday 5:30pm, Sunday 10:30am and Wednesday 12:00 noon per the issue dated 1 November 2026 (page 2), the parish's Mass Times page and the directory (all read 27 October 2026). The summer guide (12 May 2026) lists Saturday 5:30pm and Sunday 10:30am only. It is a mission of Sts. Peter and Paul and shares that parish's bulletin.

## Name and address

Street agrees in every source: 109 Lincoln Avenue. **ZIP disagrees:** the bulletin and the parish site say 21663 (St. Michaels' own code); the directory and GCatholic say 21601 (Easton's, copied from the parish). I followed the bulletin and site, and left `address_verified` false because both sources that give 21601 appear to derive it from the parish's Easton address. The directory and GCatholic spell the town 'St Michaels' / 'St. Michaels'. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 20 m from the directory pin. Name: 'St. Michael Mission Church' in bulletin and site, 'St. Michael' elsewhere.
