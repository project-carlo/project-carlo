## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Masses Sunday 9:00 and 11:00 am and 5:00 pm, Saturday 5:00 pm and Monday-Friday 6:45 am, and intentions for 26 October-1 November. The diocese map gives the same times.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the diocese map, GCatholic and OpenStreetMap all say St. Anthony of Padua, Wilmington 19805. 901 N. Dupont St. is the bulletin's rectory address and the diocese's and OSM's church address; GCatholic puts the church at the corner of W. 9th and Dupont. That is the same corner, an inference not stated by any one source. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 30 m from the diocese pin and OSM.
