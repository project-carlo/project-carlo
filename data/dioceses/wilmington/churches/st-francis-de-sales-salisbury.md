## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Saturday 5:30 p.m. and four Sunday Masses (7:30, 10:30, noon in Spanish, 4:30 p.m. in Creole) and weekday Masses, in the 25 October 2026 bulletin and on the parish site. This is the parish's main church.

## One church, not two

The directory lists it twice. `directory:175` (535 Riverside Dr., with all the Mass times) is the church. `directory:184` ('Saint Francis de Sales', 413 Wicomico St., no Masses) is the same church under another address: the bulletin and the parish site give only 535 Riverside Drive for the church and 514 Camden Ave for the office and 500 Camden Ave for the school; OSM maps 413 Wicomico St. as a private house in Riverside Homes, about 90 m from the church; GCatholic's single entry reads 'Riverside Dr. & Wicomico St.' (the church stands at that corner). No source puts a second Mass site at 413 Wicomico. Both entries are linked to this church.

## Name and address

Name: bulletin masthead, the parish site, GCatholic and the directory agree. Address: the bulletin and the parish site give 535 Riverside Drive; the directory entry 175 and the summer guide agree. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 20 m from the OSM church building and about 25 m from the directory-175 pin; the directory-184 pin is a house.
