## Holds Mass

Sunday 9:00am in season. Three sources give the season: the 1 November 2026 bulletin 'Memorial Day Weekend though Labor Day Weekend', the parish's mass-schedule page 'From Memorial Day to the last Sunday in September', and the summer guide 'Memorial Day weekend through late September'. **They disagree on the end** (Labor Day weekend or the last Sunday of September); the bulletin has Mass ending earlier, and I have not chosen. At 27 October 2026 it is off-season, so no Mass was seen being held. It keeps its church row as a seasonal site (settled for this diocese) with a revisit for late May 2027. The summer guide calls it 'the Tubman Chapel' and gives the town as Church Creek (Golden Hill); the diocese's alias maps Golden Hill to Church Creek.

## Name and address

917 Hoopers Island Road (Route 335), Church Creek, ZIP 21622 in the bulletin, the parish site, the directory and the summer guide; GCatholic has 21613 (Cambridge's), which I did not follow. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 20 m from the directory pin. Mission of St. Mary Refuge of Sinners; shares that parish's bulletin.
