## Holds Mass

Seasonal, and **closed at 27 October 2026**: the 1 November 2026 bulletin and the parish home page both say 'Closed for the season. All Masses at St. Luke'. In season the summer guide gives Saturday 5 PM and Sunday 7:30 AM (the directory's times are the same). **Sources disagree on the season:** the summer guide says 'open May 23 – Sept. 6' (2026), the parish's 'Our Parish' page says the parish center 'is only open for masses during July and August'. I have not chosen; no in-season bulletin was read. It keeps its church row as a seasonal site (settled for this diocese) with a revisit for late May 2027. The St. Andrew Catholic Center also hosts the parish office, parish hall events and a 'Holy Apostles’ Chapel' open during office hours; no Mass is listed for the chapel.
