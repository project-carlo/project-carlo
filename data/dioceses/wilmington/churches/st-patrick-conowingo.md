## Holds Mass

Yes, but occasionally, not weekly. The parish's Church Addresses page (2026-10-27) lists St. Patrick Chapel with 'Scheduled Masses throughout the year', and the 24-25 October 2026 bulletin cover gives its Mass as 'As Announced'. The bulletins of 13, 19 and 27 August and 3 September 2026 announce the chapel's annual Mass for Saturday 12 September 2026 at 10 AM, with refreshments by the Ladies AOH Cecil County Division 1 afterwards; the 3 September calendar lists it ('St. Patrick’s Annual Mass'). The diocesan directory's 'Sat 10:00' is probably that annual Mass presented as a weekly time; no weekly Saturday Mass is in any bulletin read. The chapel is the sort of place that counts under the model: a public Mass a few times a year. The 24-25 October calendar shows no Mass there that week. The chapel was built in 1819 (bulletin ad) and its old site stpatschapel.org does not load in the browser or curl (probe url: HTTP 409).

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish site and GCatholic give 237 Pleasant Grove Road, Conowingo, MD 21918; the directory alone says 287 Pleasant Grove Rd. Its pin matches GCatholic's, so 287 is probably a typo, but with a source disagreeing the address is left unverified and 237 followed (three sources, one of them the bulletin). Name: the parish site and GCatholic's type say 'St. Patrick Chapel'/'Chapel'; the directory and GCatholic's title say 'St. Patrick'. The bulletin cover places it in 'Pilottown, Maryland' (a hamlet; the mailing town is Conowingo). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87F5PQ8X+C6).
