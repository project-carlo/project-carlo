## Why it is excluded

St. Nicholas, Wilmington is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic parish, not a parish of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Wilmington. Its own site says 'The parish belongs to the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia' (stnicholaschurchde.org, read 2026-10-27; the directory's `st-nicholas-church.org` redirects there). The Archeparchy's parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, the former ukrarcheparchy.us) carries it as 'St. Nicholas Ukrainian Catholic Church, Wilmington', and GCatholic gives 'Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia', rite Ukrainian (Byzantine), 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Wilmington'. The cdow.org parish map lists it as 'Saint Nicholas [Ukrainian]' (directory:130), but the deaneries page (56 parishes) does not name it.

The church is open and holds Divine Liturgy (directory: Sat 9:00, Sun 10:00), but its bulletins belong to another jurisdiction, so it is left out of this diocese's parish and church rows, as the diocese's settled rule for Eastern Catholic churches says. The address differs a little between sources (directory and the church: 800 Lea Blvd; GCatholic: 801 Lea Blvd; the Archeparchy: 800-801).

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it is an eparchial parish. If the project later covers Eastern Catholic churches, the Archeparchy of Philadelphia's own page is the place to start.
