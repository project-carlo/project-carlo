## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Perpetual Help is the Catholic chapel community of the 436th Airlift Wing at Dover Air Force Base. It is served by the Air Force chaplaincy, whose priests are endorsed by the Archdiocese for the Military Services, USA (AMS), not by the Diocese of Wilmington. GCatholic lists it with 'Jurisdiction: Military Ordinariate of Archdiocese for the Military Services of the United States', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Wilmington', as a Community. AMS's own page on chaplains says it 'is the sole endorsing agent recognized under U.S. law and the Department of Defense to endorse Catholic priests to minister in the U.S. Armed Forces'. The base's chaplain page (dover.af.mil, read in the browser 2026-10-27; plain fetches get 403) gives a Catholic Mass Sunday 0830 and 11:30 a.m. and daily Mass Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and the 1st Friday at 11:30 a.m. in Chapel 1 (Bldg 417), 'Until Further Notice due to HVAC difficulties in Chapel 2', and describes the chaplaincy as serving 'Airmen, their families, and other authorized personnel'. That page never names AMS or 'Our Lady of Perpetual Help'; the AMS link rests on GCatholic and on AMS's role for all military chaplains.

The cdow.org parish map lists it as directory:138 (436 AW/HC, 419 12th Street, phone 302-677-3932, Sun 09:00 and 11:30 and a weekday 11:30), but its deaneries page does not. Mass happens, but the chapel is on a military installation reached only with base access, and no bulletin of the kind the pipeline reads was found (only a Facebook page, facebook.com/dover436chapel, unread). So `other-jurisdiction` is the primary reason; `no-public-mass` would also fit if the owner counts base access as non-public.

## What would change this

An AMS or base source that names the chapel's patron and shows it open to the general public with a posted schedule and bulletin would make it a church row (probably under its own parish). The base page notes Mass is in Chapel 1 only "until further notice", so reread it if the chapel is reconsidered.
