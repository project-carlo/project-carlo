## Why it is excluded

St. Basil the Great, Chesapeake City (Maryland) is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church of the Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Wilmington. The Archeparchy's parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-27) carries it as 'St. Basil Ukrainian Catholic Church, Chesapeake City', 231 Basil Avenue, with the same phone number as St. Nicholas, Wilmington (13027625511, chesapeake@ukrcatholic.org). GCatholic gives 'Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia', rite Ukrainian (Byzantine). The cdow.org parish map lists it as 'Saint Basil [Ukrainian]' (directory:155, with Sat 16:00 and Sat 17:00 as Mass times), but the deaneries page does not name it.

The directory's website (ccea4u.com/churches/St-Basil.htm) is dead: the old https address fails certificate verification (expired) and http answers 404 (2026-10-27). The shared phone and Archeparchy listing suggest St. Basil is served from St. Nicholas, Wilmington; that is an inference from the number, not read in a bulletin. No Ukrainian bulletin was needed to settle the jurisdiction.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. A Mass-times source for the Ukrainian parish would only matter if the project covered Eastern Catholic churches.
