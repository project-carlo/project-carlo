## Why it is excluded

Christ Our King (28th and Madison Streets, Wilmington) closed. The Dialog (2020-07-30) says 'Christ Our King closed in November 2016 after serving Wilmington's Ninth Ward for 90 years', and that the former church is now a funeral home (Congo Funeral Homes). A 2018-07-02 Dialog article gives the final Mass as October 2016, which is the `last_mass` here (the closure decree took effect the next month). The diocese map and cdow.org/deaneries do not list it; GCatholic still does, without marking the closure. The John Patrick listing for it (no. 313) has no bulletin files. Not in the City Deanery list or the Downtown Catholic bulletin's Masses either.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building is a funeral home.
