## Why it is excluded

GCatholic's 'Shrine of Our Lady of the Highways' (Oblates Retreat Center, 1120 Blue Ball Rd., Childs) is an outdoor statue, not a place where Mass is said. The Oblates of St. Francis de Sales' own page (read 2026-11-07) says that after a fatal 1968 crash on I-95 beside their property 'the Oblates erected a statue of Our Lady ... as a reminder to motorists to drive safely'; in 1986 'it was replaced with a 12-foot marble statue carved from Carrara stone', and the 2022 restoration cleared greenery 'to better highlight the statue from the highway'. Its 2022 rededication with Bishop Koenig was 'a simple ceremony held in Annecy Hall chapel', indoors. No source gives a Mass at the shrine; the Mary's Travelers programme offers Masses said by the Oblates for enrolled members, with no public schedule. Reason `other`: a roadside devotional monument, not a worship site. It was an `open/` record from map 012 until this reconcile (hand refresh, 2026-11-07).

No diocesan list has it: not the Find A Parish map, the deaneries page or the Summer Vacation Mass Guide, and Immaculate Conception, Elkton's bulletin (the nearest parish) never mentions it.

## What would change this

A source giving public Mass at Childs. That would be in the Oblates' Annecy Hall chapel, a building of its own: give it a church row under the bulletin that lists the Mass. Until then the community's chapel is a private religious house's chapel.
