## Why it is excluded

The 1883-84 church on W Cochran Street was sold in November 2006 when the parish began its new church (parish history page), and GCatholic types it 'Former Church ... now Protestant church'. The new St. Joseph Church, 371 East Main Street, was dedicated on 25 November 2007; the 25 October 2026 bulletin lists no Cochran Street church. `last_mass` is left empty: no source gives the old building's last Mass (it was sold in November 2006, a year before the new church was dedicated, so Masses may have been elsewhere in between). The building is a different place from the Main Street church, GCatholic's 1.1 km away.

## What would change this

A bulletin or the diocese listing public Catholic Mass at 15 W Cochran St.
