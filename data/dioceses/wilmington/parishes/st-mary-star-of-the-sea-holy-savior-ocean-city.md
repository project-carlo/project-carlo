## Publisher

St. Mary Star of the Sea and Holy Savior are one parish with one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline, container 04/0790 (org `st-mary-star-of-the-sea-holy-savior-parish`, lpi `0018000000Qx8zFAAR`). The 1 November 2026 issue has one Mass-schedule box for both churches (St. Mary Star of the Sea Church, 200 S. Baltimore Avenue; Holy Savior Mission Church, 1705 Philadelphia Avenue), one office, one pastor (Rev. Lance Martin), and a Mass-intentions table that assigns each Mass to one church. The directory lists both churches as 'St. Mary & Holy Savior Parish', and the two roster websites are the same site.

## Worship sites

Two churches, both open year-round: St. Mary Star of the Sea (Sunday 7:00 a.m. and weekday Masses) and Holy Savior (Saturday 5:15 p.m. and Sunday 10:00 a.m.; Sunday 8:30 a.m. is added from Memorial Day weekend). The summer guide's '(Memorial Day – Labor Day)' on St. Mary's Sunday 7 AM is contradicted by the bulletin (Sunday 7:00 a.m. Mass in the 1 November issue, 109 attending on 17-18 October) and by the parish's own Mass-times page, which lists St. Mary's Sunday 7:00 a.m. under both 'Spring to Fall' and 'Fall to Spring'. So St. Mary needs no seasonal revisit. See the two church records.

stmaryholysavior.com (WordPress); the directory's website stmarystaroftheseaocmd.com redirects to it, and GCatholic's stmarys-holysavior.com did not answer. The /bulletins/ page embeds the ParishesOnline widget, so the bulletin is read through `parishes_online` with the org slug as `provider_id`.
