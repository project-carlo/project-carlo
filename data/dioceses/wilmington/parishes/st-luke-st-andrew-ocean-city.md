## Publisher

St. Luke with St. Andrew publishes one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline, container 04/0763 (org `st-luke-and-st-andrew-church`, lpi `0018000000Qc275AAB`). The 1 November 2026 issue is headed 'St. Luke Church, 9903 Coastal Highway' with Rev. Paul F. Jennings, Jr. as pastor and Rev. Ravi Kumar Voliganti as associate, and its Mass-schedule box carries a line for St. Andrew, so St. Andrew is the same bulletin's seasonal mission, not a publisher of its own. The deanery box on the cover lists 'St. Mary Star of the Sea (Ocean City)' as a separate parish; St. Mary & Holy Savior publishes its own container (04/0790; see that row).

## Worship sites

Two churches: St. Luke (year-round) and St. Andrew (seasonal mission, 14401 Sinepuxent Ave.). The summer guide names St. Andrew 'seasonal mission of St. Luke – open May 23 – Sept. 6'; the directory gives both the website stlukeoc.com. The St. Andrew Catholic Center also holds the parish office and a 'Holy Apostles’ Chapel Open during office hours' (cover, 1 November 2026); no Mass is listed there, so it is not a church row (see Not yet confirmed).

## Website and bulletin provider

stlukeoc.com is a Duda/WeConnect site. The directory's website is right (the same site is given for both St. Luke and St. Andrew). `/mass-times` is a 404; the Mass times are on the home page. The bulletins page embeds the ParishesOnline widget, so the bulletin is read through `parishes_online` with the org slug as `provider_id`.
