## Publisher

Saints Peter and Paul Parish, Easton publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (organization `ss-peter-paul-catholic-parish`, container 04/0450). The issue dated 1 November 2026 (page 1 'Saints Peter and Paul Parish', page 2 the Mass schedule) lists the parish church and two mission churches, St. Michael (St. Michaels) and St. Joseph (Cordova), each with its own address and Mass times, under one pastor, Rev. James Nash, and one parish office. The diocese's summer guide calls both 'mission of Saints Peter & Paul'. So the nine roster entries for the three buildings are one parish row with three churches.

## Worship sites
