## Publisher

St. Benedict, Ridgely and St. Elizabeth of Hungary, Denton publish one weekly bulletin on the eCatholic site beparish.com (id 16009). The 24-25 October 2026 issue is headed by both church names and lists both churches' Masses in a single schedule, under one pastor (Vy. Rev. Christopher Markellos, V.F.) and one parish office. The diocese's directory gives both churches the same website and phone. So one parish row, two churches.

## Worship sites

St. Benedict, 408 Central Avenue, Ridgely (`churches/st-benedict-ridgely.md`) and St. Elizabeth of Hungary, 106/108 South First Street, Denton (`churches/st-elizabeth-of-hungary-denton.md`). The bulletin's own Mass lines name both as 'Church' with Mass held there.

## Website and bulletin provider

`ecatholic`, read from the website, which hosts its own bulletins. Not shared with St. Christopher, Chester, although St. Benedict's pastor administered it from November 2025 to April 2026: St. Christopher now prints its own (`st-christopher-chester`, eCatholic 17976).
