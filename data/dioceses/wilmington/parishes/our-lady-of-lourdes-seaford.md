## Publisher

Our Lady of Lourdes, Seaford publishes its own weekly bulletin through Bon Venture (listing page 2167); the parish's GoDaddy site only links it. The 25 October 2026 issue (`pdfs/2167/2026-10-25.pdf`, read 2026-10-27) is headed 'Our Lady of Lourdes Roman Catholic Church, 532 E. Stein Highway, Seaford, DE 19973' and lists this church's Mass times alone; page 2 is its week of Mass intentions (Saturday 3:00, 5:00, 7:00 pm; Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 12:00; Tuesday and Wednesday 9:00 am; Thursday 7:00 pm; Friday 9:00 am; Monday no Mass). It is a separate bulletin from St. Michael/Mary Mother of Peace (Georgetown/Millsboro, ParishesOnline 04/0868): a different publisher and masthead, and no cross-reference in the issue read.

## Worship sites

One church, Our Lady of Lourdes (directory 142, GCatholic 19357). The bulletin's page 2 calendar also mentions a 'Cry/Overflow Mass' at noon in the Parish Hall on Sunday (an overflow of the 12:00 pm Spanish Mass in the church, not a separate site) and, once, a wedding Mass; neither is a separate worship site.

## Website and bulletin provider

ollseaford.org is a GoDaddy site; its 'weekly-bulletin-1' page is a stub with a button to Bon Venture, whose https host fails TLS from Python, so the recipe uses the http form (page-pdfs on the listing; each issue is `/pdfs/2167/<YYYY-MM-DD>.pdf`, newest first, no filter needed).
