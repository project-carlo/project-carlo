## Publisher

St. Edmond, Rehoboth Beach publishes its own weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline, container 04/0887 (org `saint-edmond-roman-catholic-church`, lpi `0018000000Qx8z1AAB`). The 25 October 2026 issue is headed 'St. Edmond Roman Catholic Church' and carries the footer 'St. Edmond, Rehoboth Beach, DE 04-0887'. Pages 1-6 are images, so the masthead was read from a rendered page 1. No other parish is named on the cover. St. Jude, Lewes, the nearest parish, publishes separately (container 03/1013).

## Worship sites

The church (401 King Charles Ave.) and two summer-only Mass sites, which the diocese's summer guide lists as entries of their own: the Parish Hall and Rehoboth Elementary School. The 19 July 2026 bulletin lists both in its Mass-schedule box, so they are St. Edmond's own Masses and need no bulletin of their own; both keep church rows under the seasonal-site rule, with a revisit for next May. The Parish Hall is the 'Education & Social Center/Hall' at 407 King Charles Ave., next to the church (contact-us page). The directory pin (401 King Charles), GCatholic (King Charles Ave. & Laurel St.) and the summer guide (409, the parish office) all describe the same campus.
