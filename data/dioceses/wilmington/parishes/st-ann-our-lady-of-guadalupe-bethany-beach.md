## Publisher

The Parish of St. Ann, Bethany Beach publishes one weekly bulletin for St. Ann and its Our Lady of Guadalupe Mission Church, Frankford, distributed through Discover Mass (J.S. Paluch) and posted on stannbb.org. The 25 October 2026 issue's cover gives both churches with their addresses and Mass times; one staff list (Rev. John P. Klevence, pastor) and one footer name both. Our Lady of Guadalupe has no bulletin or site of its own; the directory and GCatholic give it St. Ann's websites.

## Worship sites

St. Ann, 691 Garfield Parkway, Bethany Beach; Our Lady of Guadalupe Mission Church, 35318 Church Rd., Frankford. Summer adds a Sunday 9 AM Mass at St. Ann (from 28 June) and an 11 AM Mass at Guadalupe (to 6 September), per the diocese's summer guide; both are in the guide's year-round parish entries, not separate sites.
