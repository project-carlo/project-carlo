## Publisher

Our Lady of Good Counsel, Secretary self-hosts a one-sheet weekly bulletin: the /bulletin page of `ourladyofgoodcounselchurch.com` links dated PDFs `/images/Bulletin_<M.D.YY>.pdf`. The 1 November 2026 file (2 pages) lists this church's own Masses (Saturday 5:00pm vigil, Sunday 11:00am, Friday 8:00am morning Mass) and its own news. It is not the Cambridge bulletin: that is ParishesOnline container 04/0661, headed 'Saint Mary Refuge of Sinners and Star of the Sea', and carries no Secretary Mass schedule. The two parishes share a pastor (see the Cambridge record); that does not make one bulletin.

## Worship sites

One church: Our Lady of Good Counsel, Secretary (`churches/our-lady-of-good-counsel-secretary.md`). The directory, GCatholic and the summer guide each list one entry.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`, with a `page-pdfs` recipe on the bulletin page filtered to `Bulletin_*.pdf`. The directory and GCatholic give the home page, which links the same page. The bulletin page lists files oldest first (the newest is last); `fetch:check` decides whether the resolver picks the latest.

## Not yet confirmed

The PDFs have no printed masthead, so parish identity rests on the hosting site and the text.
