## Publisher

St. Michael the Archangel (Georgetown) and Mary Mother of Peace (Millsboro, Oak Orchard) publish **one** weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline, container 04/0868 (org `st-michael-mary-mother-of-peace-parish`, lpi `0018000000Qx8yzAAB`). The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-27) carries the footer 'St. Michael/Mary Mother of Peace Parish | 04-0868'. Its cover is an image; rendered, it is headed 'Saint Michael the Archangel & Mary Mother of Peace Parish'. The diocese's deaneries page names the two as one parish ('Saint Michael the Archangel/Mary Mother of Peace, Georgetown, DE'), and both directory entries and both GCatholic entries give smammop.com and the same phone, 302-856-6451. This is the shared-bulletin case the batch plan expected; no other parish prints on this container, and Our Lady of Lourdes, Seaford publishes separately (its own parish record).

## Worship sites

Two churches, both in the 'Mass Intentions of the Week' box on page 3 of the 25 October 2026 issue and on the parish site's Mass Schedule page (https://www.smammop.com/mass-schedulef0423bfb, read in the browser 2026-10-27): St. Michael the Archangel, Georgetown (Saturday 4:30 PM Spanish and 7:00 PM; Sunday 7:00 AM Spanish, 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM Spanish; Monday and Tuesday 9:00 AM; Tuesday and Thursday 7:00 PM Spanish) and Mary Mother of Peace, Millsboro (Saturday 4:00 PM; Sunday 7:30 AM and 11:00 AM; Wednesday and Friday 9:00 AM). The bulletin's community-assistance page also lists 'MH: Mary's House' and 'MMOPH: MMOP Hall/Activity Room' as rooms at Mary Mother of Peace and 'Mary's Cupboard' (food distribution, 30807 Mt. Joy Road, Millsboro): these are parish facilities, not Mass sites. The summer guide lists both churches with the same weekly times, so there are no seasonal Mass sites. Roster entries: directory 147 and 141, GCatholic 19362 and 19356, summer-guide entries for each; each maps to one of the two church records.
