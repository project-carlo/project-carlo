## Publisher

Holy Spirit and St. Peter the Apostle, New Castle, publish one weekly ParishesOnline bulletin (container 03/1089, org `holy-spirit-st-peter-the-apostle`). The 25 October 2026 issue is a joint bulletin: page 1 gives each church its own Mass schedule, both sit under one parish office (521 Harmony Street, 302-328-2335) and page 2 names one pastor, Rev. Timothy M. Nolan, for both. The issues of 18 and 11 October are in the same container, so it is current and weekly.

## Worship sites

Two churches: St. Peter the Apostle (521 Harmony Street, in the historic town) and Holy Spirit (12 Winder Road). The cover also names a 'HS Church/Our Lady Queen of Peace statue, 1 Church Drive, Garfield Park'; that is a shrine statue near Holy Spirit, not a Mass site, and no Mass is listed there. The diocese map and GCatholic list the same two buildings. The bulletin's food-pantry page footer reads 'St.Peter † HolySpirit † OurLadyofFatima † St. Paul', a shared-ministry strip across four New Castle-area parishes; it is not a masthead, and Our Lady of Fatima and St. Paul print their own bulletins (see their rows).

## Website and bulletin provider

sphsnc.org is the parish site, the diocese gives it to both directory entries, and it embeds the ParishesOnline org, so `parishes_online` with the org slug is the provider. GCatholic still lists holyspiritchurchde.org and stpetertheapostlede.org; the first is a third-party archive page ('Holy Spirit Catholic Church — Wilmington, Delaware (Archive) ... This domain is maintained as an archive by its current owner, who is not connected to Holy Spirit Catholic Church', seen 2026-10-27) and the second has no DNS. Neither is a bulletin source.

## Not yet confirmed

The cover mentions Spanish Mass at noon on 24-25 October (flea market weekend) and 15 November; where that Mass is held (Holy Spirit, or Our Lady of Fatima, which has a regular noon Sunday Mass) is unclear, so it is not counted as a regular Mass here.
