## Publisher

St. Christopher, Chester (Kent Island) publishes its own weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site (id 17976). The 25 October 2026 issue's masthead names St. Christopher alone, with its own pastor (Rev. Clement Vadakkedath, C.Ss.R.) and associate pastor, and its own Mass schedule. It is not shared with St. Benedict/St. Elizabeth: St. Benedict's pastor was Administrator of St. Christopher from 12 November 2025 until Fr. Vadakkedath was appointed its pastor (The Dialog, 27 April 2026), and the St. Benedict, Ridgely bulletin (eCatholic 16009) is a separate publication (see `st-benedict-st-elizabeth-ridgely`).
