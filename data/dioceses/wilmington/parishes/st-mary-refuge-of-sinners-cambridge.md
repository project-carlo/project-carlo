## Publisher

St. Mary Refuge of Sinners, Cambridge and its mission St. Mary Star of the Sea, Church Creek publish one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (organization `st-mary-church-refuge-of-sinners`, container 04/0661). The issue dated 1 November 2026 is headed 'Saint Mary Refuge of Sinners and Star of the Sea' and gives both churches' addresses and Mass times on page 1, and its mass-intention and lay-minister lists use the codes ROS and SOS for the two churches. The summer guide calls Star of the Sea 'mission of St. Mary Refuge of Sinners, Cambridge'. The six roster entries for the two buildings are one parish row with two churches.

The pastor, Rev. Stephen C. Lonek, is also the pastor of Our Lady of Good Counsel, Secretary: page 1 prints 'Phone: 410-943-4300' and 'Olgc25@yahoo.com' for him, the rectory phone on Good Counsel's site. That is a shared pastor, not a shared bulletin: Good Counsel prints its own two-page bulletin (see `parishes/our-lady-of-good-counsel-secretary.md`), and this container's issue has no Secretary schedule.

## Worship sites

Two churches: St. Mary Refuge of Sinners, Cambridge (`churches/st-mary-refuge-of-sinners-cambridge.md`) and St. Mary Star of the Sea, Church Creek (`churches/st-mary-star-of-the-sea-church-creek.md`, seasonal). The directory's `catholicmasses.org` link for Cambridge is a listing, not the parish; GCatholic's `stmaryscambridge.org` has no DNS. The parish's own site is `stmarycambridgemd.org`, which the directory gives only for Church Creek.
