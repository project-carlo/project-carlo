## Publisher

Our Mother of Sorrows, Centreville and its mission St. Peter the Apostle, Queenstown publish one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container 04/0487, organization `our-mother-of-sorrows-st-peter-the-apostle`). The 25 October 2026 issue's page 1 (an image, read by rendering it) is headed by both names and carries a single Mass schedule for both churches, one mailing address and one office. The diocese's summer guide calls St. Peter 'mission of Our Mother of Sorrows, Centreville'. So the three roster pairs for the two churches are one parish row with two churches.

## Worship sites

Two churches: Our Mother of Sorrows, Centreville (`churches/our-mother-of-sorrows-centreville.md`) and St. Peter the Apostle, Queenstown (`churches/st-peter-the-apostle-queenstown.md`). St. Peter had been closed for renovation: the summer guide (as of 12 May 2026) says 'Reopening July 4, 2026 following renovation' and the diocese map lists no Mass for it; the October bulletin and the parish home page both show a Saturday 5:00 pm vigil there, so it holds Mass now.
