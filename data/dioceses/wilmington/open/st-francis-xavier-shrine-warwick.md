## What is known

St. Francis Xavier Shrine, Old Bohemia, is one of the three churches of St. Joseph Parish, Middletown (the oldest Catholic site in the diocese, 1704; rebuilt after a 1912 fire). It holds no public Mass now. The 25 October 2026 bulletin cover lists it with 'The Church is currently closed for repairs' and no times; The Dialog (2 September 2026) says public events stopped in late 2023 for safety reasons and the shrine reopens on 6 December 2026, when Bishop Koenig celebrates Mass, with Mass afterwards 'once a month between April and December on the first Sunday at 2 p.m.' The diocese map and GCatholic list it without times.

## Why it is not decided

It is not `closed` (no source says it has shut for good; the renovation is finished and a reopening is dated) and it has no Mass to schedule today, so a church row would be wrong for now. The 6 December Mass and the monthly Sundays from April 2027 will make it a seasonal worship site under St. Joseph's single bulletin, like the other seasonal sites in this diocese (diocese.md, Rules settled). Whether the monthly Mass appears in the bulletin's schedule is not known; the cover is an image and the schedule on later pages was not read for the shrine. Check after 6 December 2026.
