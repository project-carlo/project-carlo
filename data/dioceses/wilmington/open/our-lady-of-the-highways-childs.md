## What is known

GCatholic lists a Shrine of Our Lady of the Highways at the Oblates Retreat Center, 1120 Blue Ball Rd., Childs, Maryland, in the Diocese of Wilmington. It is on no diocesan list: not the Find A Parish map, not the deaneries page, not the Summer Vacation Mass Guide, and no parish bulletin read in this batch mentions it. The Oblates of St. Francis de Sales' Wilmington-Philadelphia Province has its address at Childs.

## What would settle it

A Mass schedule or a statement from the Oblates or the diocese. If Mass is public, the shrine belongs to whichever bulletin lists it (none found; Immaculate Conception's, the nearest, does not); if only the community and retreatants attend, it is `no-public-mass`. Nothing found says either, so it stays open rather than excluded.
