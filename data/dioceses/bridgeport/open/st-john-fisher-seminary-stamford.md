## What is known

GCatholic (40356) lists St. John Fisher Seminary, the diocese's college seminary, at 894 Newfield Avenue, Stamford, as type "Seminary", with a website (`http://www.bridgeportvocations.org/fisher-seminary/`) that answered 404 on 2026-11-12. The diocese's vocations office site (`bridgeportpriest.org`, read in the browser) has no page for the seminary and no Mass schedule. The diocese's directory and its Mass-times roster don't list it. The neighbouring St. Cecilia - St. Gabriel bulletin (15 November 2026) lists no Mass there. The Haitian quasi-parish's 2022 flyer used 894 Newfield Ave. as its office address.

## Why it is not decided

A seminary chapel usually has the community's daily Mass, which may or may not be open to the public, and nothing I read says either way: the evidence is absence from the lists above, not a statement. Nothing says the seminary closed either. It stays open until a source states whether its Mass is public (a church on the host parish's row if so, or excluded `no-public-mass`).
