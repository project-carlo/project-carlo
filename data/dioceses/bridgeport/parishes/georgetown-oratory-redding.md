## Publisher

The Georgetown Oratory of the Most Sacred Heart of Jesus publishes its own bulletin, separate from Sacred Heart & St. Patrick Parish's LPi bulletin. It posts the issues as PDFs on its Duda site's Bulletin page (`https://www.georgetownoratory.org/bulletin`). The newest, 8 November 2026 (`https://irp.cdn-website.com/69586475/files/uploaded/Bulletin+November+8-+2026.pdf`, read 2026-11-12 with probe pdf), is two pages: page 1 "THE RECTOR'S NOTE" and Mass intentions (one "for Deceased Members of Former Sacred Heart Parish"), page 2 "ORATORY NEWS" and the masthead "GEORGETOWN ORATORY, 30 Church Street, Redding, CT 06896", "Rev. Michael P. Novajosky, Rector", with a MASS SCHEDULE: Sunday 7:30 AM (1962), 9 AM, 10:30 AM, 12 Noon (1962); Tuesday and Thursday 8:30 AM; Wednesday 6 PM (1962); Friday 8:30 AM (1962); Saturday 8:30 AM (1962) and 4:00 PM (Vigil).

The parish's 15 November 2026 LPi bulletin (container 04/0116) has a different masthead (Sacred Heart~Saint Patrick Parish, Rev. Terrence P. Walsh, Pastor) and lists only its own Masses at Sacred Heart (Saturday 4:00pm, Sunday 9:00am and 10:30am, Tuesday and Thursday 8:30am), which the oratory's schedule also carries. Two bulletins, so two rows (survey-review's settled rule for the oratory).

## Worship sites

One church, `sacred-heart-georgetown` (Sacred Heart Church, 30 Church Street). The oratory's homepage says the community "is responsible for sacred heart church", and its bulletin gives the church's full schedule, so the building is on this row rather than the parish's. That choice is flagged (advisory) for the owner; the parish's own Masses there are the same times as the oratory's 4 PM, 9 AM, 10:30 AM and weekday 8:30 AM.
