## Publisher

Bon Venture prints the weekly bulletin of The Parish of St. Catherine of Siena and St. Agnes (ad-page footer "050A (CK) - St. Catherine of Siena, Riverside"); the parish posts the issues on its own Bulletins page (`https://www.stc-sta.org/about-us/bulletins`) as `/images/050A_<YYYY>_<MM>_<DD>.pdf`. The 15 November 2026 issue (`https://www.stc-sta.org/images/050A_2026_11_15.pdf`, newest listed on 2026-11-12) is image-only apart from the ad pages; page 1, rendered, reads "THIRTY THIRD SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME / NOVEMBER 15, 2026", "The Parish of ST. CATHERINE OF SIENA AND ST. AGNES", "4 RIVERSIDE AVE | RIVERSIDE, CT 06878".

## Worship sites

The parish was formed when "On July 1, 2019, St. Catherine of Siena church merged with St. Agnes parish" (parish History page, read 2026-11-12). One bulletin covers both churches (settled rule: a merged parish's second church still listed with Mass is a church of the one row):

- `st-catherine-of-siena-riverside-ct` (STC, "Main Church"): Saturday 5:00pm vigil; Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 10:30am, 5:00pm and 6:30pm (Spanish); Monday-Friday 7:00am.
- `st-agnes-greenwich-ct` (STA, 247 Stanwich Road): the bulletin's "11:00am Italian Mass STA- Every 3rd Sunday of the month" and its "5:15pm Daily Mass - CHAPEL", which the parish's Mass Times page places "in St. Agnes Chapel". The diocese's schedule (`/wp-json/custom/v1/mass-times?parish_id=1423`) likewise has the 11:00 Italian Mass "Chapel, 3rd Sunday only" and the weekday 17:15 "In chapel". That "St. Agnes Chapel" is the Stanwich Road building rather than a chapel inside St. Catherine's is inferred: the bulletin uses "STA" for the Italian Mass, and the History page calls the converted Carriage House at 247 Stanwich Road a chapel that "is currently St. Agnes Church"; no source gives the chapel a different address.

GCatholic lists only St. Agnes (21950) for this parish and has no entry for St. Catherine of Siena; the diocese's directory (directory:1423) lists the parish at St. Catherine's address, and its mass-times roster has "St. Catherine's Church" (mass-times:1423-st-catherine-s-church).
