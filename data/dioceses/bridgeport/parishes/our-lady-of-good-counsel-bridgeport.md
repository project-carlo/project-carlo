## Publisher

No bulletin found. Our Lady of Good Counsel, 163 Ortega Avenue, Bridgeport, is a parish run since about 2022 by the Koinonia John the Baptist community, with Fr. Adriano Biccheri as Parish Administrator (diocesan news of 8 June, 22 July and 18 August 2026, read 2026-11-12). The diocese's directory (77 entries, 2026-11-12) has no entry for it; GCatholic (21993) lists it as a chapel, a mission of St. Andrew. The diocesan articles call it both "Our Lady of Good Counsel Parish" and "Our Lady of Good Counsel Chapel" and name its own pastor/administrator, so it is a row of its own, not a church of `st-andrew-bridgeport`, whose bulletins and Mass Times page don't name it.

Its ParishesOnline org (`our-lady-of-good-counsel-church-06606`, LPi id 0018000000QxAzUAAV) exists but has no issues, Discover Mass says it has no bulletins, and its own site has no bulletin page (see research). So `bulletin_provider: no_bulletin`, kept off the site until a bulletin turns up (Settled policy 4).

## Worship sites

One church, `our-lady-of-good-counsel-bridgeport` (the chapel at 163 Ortega Avenue). The 8 June 2026 article quotes Fr. Biccheri: "the Spanish has more than 300 and the English is more than 80" at its Masses; the chapel's site and Discover Mass give Sunday 9:30 AM English and 11:30 AM Spanish. Until 2026-11-12 this place was an `open/` record (absence from St. Andrew's bulletins); the reconcile job found the diocesan news and made it a parish row.
