## Holds Mass

Yes. The diocese's news of 8 June 2026 quotes the administrator, Fr. Adriano Biccheri, on attendance at the parish's Masses now ("the Spanish has more than 300 and the English is more than 80"), and its 18 August 2026 article reports a priestly ordination in the chapel on 15 August 2026 and quotes a "parishioner of Our Lady of Good Counsel Chapel".
