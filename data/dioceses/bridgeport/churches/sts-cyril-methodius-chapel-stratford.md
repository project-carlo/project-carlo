## Holds Mass

The 14-15 November 2026 Holy Name of Jesus bulletin (page 1, an image, read 2026-11-12) gives Mass on Tuesday and Thursday at 7:30am "in the parish Chapel of Saints Cyril & Methodius, 2 Mary Ave", and private prayer there Tuesday-Friday 9:00am-2:00pm. The parish home page (read 2026-11-12) also lists a "7:30 am Mass in the Sts. Cyril & Methodius Chapel". Weekday-only, but public and in a building of its own, so it is its own church row of the Holy Name of Jesus parish.

## Name and address

The name and the address "2 Mary Ave" come from the parish alone: its bulletin ("Chapel of Saints Cyril & Methodius") and its home page ("Sts. Cyril & Methodius Chapel", no address), which count as one source, so neither is marked verified. Neither the diocese's directory nor GCatholic lists the chapel; the directory's mass note for Holy Name, "In chapel", is the only diocesan hint. Coordinates are OSM's address node for 2 Mary Avenue (nominatim), about 90 m from the church; the node does not name the chapel, so the building itself is not confirmed. This is not the Sts. Cyril & Methodius church at 79 Church Street, Bridgeport (directory:1329, gcatholic:21998), a separate parish.
