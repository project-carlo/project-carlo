## Holds Mass

The 15 November 2026 bulletin of the Parish of St. Cecilia – St. Gabriel (read 2026-11-12) lists Saturday 8:00 a.m. and Vigil 4:00 p.m. at St. Gabriel Church, and under "Notre Dame du Perpetuel Secours / St. Gabriel Church" the Haitian community's French Creole Masses Saturday 6:00 p.m. and Sunday 8:00 a.m., an English Mass Sunday 11:00 a.m., and Thursday Mass with Exposition 6:30-8:30 p.m.

The Haitian quasi-parish Notre Dame du Perpétuel Secours (directory:1435) is linked here: its Masses are at this church and are printed in the host parish's bulletin, and it has no bulletin of its own (see the parish row). The directory gives the quasi-parish St. Cecilia's address, 1184 Newfield Avenue, but the bulletin and the quasi-parish's 2022 flyer both place it at St. Gabriel.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead and the Haitian community's 2022 flyer give 948 Newfield Ave; GCatholic (21964) gives 914 Newfield Ave. I followed the parish's own 948 (the flyer is hosted by the diocese but is the community's own sheet, so not counted as independent; address not verified). GCatholic's Plus Code 87H83FR8+J8 sits about 80 m north-east of its St. John Fisher Seminary point (894 Newfield Ave), consistent with a church on the same stretch, so I used it for the coordinates. Name: the bulletin's "St. Gabriel Church", matching the diocese's mass-times roster and GCatholic.
