## Holds Mass

St. Anthony of Padua's 15 November 2026 bulletin (read 2026-11-12) lists a daily (Monday-Friday) 12:30 pm Mass at "Egan Chapel, Fairfield University". Fairfield University's Campus Ministry page (read 2026-11-12) also speaks of weekday and Sunday Masses at Egan Chapel, with a Sunday 7:30 p.m. Mass led by the student choir.

## Name and address

The university calls it "Egan Chapel of St. Ignatius Loyola" and OSM names the building the same (name verified). No source gives the chapel a street address of its own: OSM puts the building on Bellarmine Road, inside the campus; the university's address is 1073 North Benson Road, Fairfield 06824, which is recorded here as the postal address (address not verified). Coordinates from the OSM building (way 743533572).
