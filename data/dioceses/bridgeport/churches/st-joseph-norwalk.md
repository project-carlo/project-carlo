## Holds Mass

The 15 November 2026 bulletin's offertory table (page 2, image, rendered 2026-11-12) lists Masses at SJ on Saturday 7:00 PM and Sunday 9:30 AM, 12:30 PM and 5:15 PM. The parish site's 2026 schedule (read 2026-11-12) gives the same weekend Masses plus Saturday 8:00 AM, weekday 7:30 AM and Wednesday 7:00 PM (French Creole) in the "SJ Chapel", which the diocese's mass-times list calls the San Joselito Chapel of St. Joseph Church; it is recorded here as part of this church (see the parish's *Worship sites*).

The diocese's list gives the English Sunday Mass at 10:00; the bulletin and parish site both give 9:30 AM.
