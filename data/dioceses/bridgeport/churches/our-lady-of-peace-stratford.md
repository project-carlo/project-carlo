## Holds Mass

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead and the diocese's directory (directory:1459) give 651 Stratford Road; GCatholic (22010) gives 230 Park Blvd.; the ParishesOnline org and the bulletin's office line give 10 Ivy Street, which the bulletin labels the parish office. OSM places 'Our Lady of Peace Church' (way 929346349) on Maple Street, about 30 m from GCatholic's Plus Code, so all these appear to be frontages or neighbouring buildings of one Lordship site; I did not check the street layout on a map. I followed the parish and the diocese, which agree (address verified). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H85V3J+P2, near the OSM building.
