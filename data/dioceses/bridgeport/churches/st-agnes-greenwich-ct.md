## Holds Mass

The 15 November 2026 bulletin of The Parish of St. Catherine of Siena and St. Agnes (page 1, read 2026-11-12) lists "11:00am Italian Mass STA- Every 3rd Sunday of the month" and "5:15pm Daily Mass - CHAPEL" Monday-Friday; the parish's Mass Times page gives "5:15 pm in St. Agnes Chapel", and the diocese's schedule for parish 1423 has the 11:00 Italian Mass "Chapel, 3rd Sunday only" and weekday 17:15 "In chapel". That this chapel is the Stanwich Road building is inferred from the parish's History page (the converted Carriage House at 247 Stanwich Road "is currently St. Agnes Church") and the bulletin's "STA"; no source gives a different place for it. Even without the weekday Mass, the monthly Italian Mass at STA makes it a worship site.
