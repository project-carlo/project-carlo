## Holds Mass

The 15 November 2026 Cathedral Parish bulletin (pages 1-2, read 2026-11-12) names "St. Patrick Church" in its masthead and schedules Saturday 4:00 pm Vigil, Sunday 8:30 am, 4:00 pm every 4th Sunday (Igbo community), weekdays 7:00 am and Tuesday 7:00 pm (Spanish) there. The diocese's mass-times API lists the same Sunday and weekday times under 'Saint Patrick Church'.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it "St. Patrick Church"; the diocese's Mass-time notes agree (name verified). Only GCatholic (21996) gives an address, 851 North Ave., Bridgeport 06606; neither the bulletin, the parish site's home page nor the directory gives one, so the address is unverified. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H85RV3+2H.
