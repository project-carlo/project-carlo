## Holds Mass

The 15 November 2026 Cathedral Parish bulletin (page 2, read 2026-11-12) schedules Sunday 7:00, 10:00, 11:30 am (Spanish) and 2:00 pm (Vietnamese), weekdays 12:10 pm and Friday 7:00 pm (Vietnamese) at St. Augustine; the diocese's mass-times API agrees.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead calls it "St. Augustine Cathedral"; the directory (directory:1158) and GCatholic (2046) agree on the name. The street number differs: the directory and Wikipedia give 399 Washington Avenue, GCatholic 359 Washington Ave. I followed the diocese's 399, which Wikipedia independently supports. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H85RJ3+3R, which sits on the building about 20 m from Wikipedia's point. The parish office is at 170 Thompson Street (bulletin, site), not the church.
