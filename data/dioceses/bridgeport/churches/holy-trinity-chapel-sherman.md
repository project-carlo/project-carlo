## Holds Mass

The 15 November 2026 St. Edward the Confessor bulletin (read 2026-11-12) lists "Holy Trinity Chapel | 10 AM" among the Sunday Masses (page 2) and the intention for "10:00 am Holy Trinity Chapel" on Sunday 22 November (page 3). The parish site's Holy Trinity page gives the same Sunday 10:00 am Mass and a 5:30 pm Mass there on 8 December.

Holy Trinity was a parish of its own until 1 July 2022, when it "was merged into St. Edward the Confessor" (Fr. Wolfe's letter of 10 September 2022). His letter of 2 July 2022 says the church building on the Sherman property was in too poor a condition to repair and a new chapel would be built there; his update of 14 December 2025 set the new chapel's dedication Mass for 21 December 2025. That is why the diocese's directory has no entry for it while GCatholic (22031) still lists "Church of the Holy Trinity" with its old phone and a now spam-squatted website. This record is the chapel in use now; what became of the old building was not checked and needs no record unless a source shows it still holds Mass.

## Name and address

The parish calls it "Holy Trinity Chapel" (bulletin, site), and OSM names the building "Holy Trinity Chapel": name verified. No parish or diocesan source gives a street address. GCatholic gives "Rts. 37 & 39, Sherman, Connecticut 06784"; OSM places the chapel on "Route 37 Center", Sherman 06784. I recorded GCatholic's road junction, which OSM's road agrees with but doesn't confirm, so the address is not verified. Coordinates are OSM way 1284506752 (41.5752363, -73.4983811), about 60 m from GCatholic's Plus Code 87H8HGG2+5W; the OSM feature is tagged as the chapel and was added recently, so it is more likely the new building.
