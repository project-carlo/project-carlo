## Why it is excluded

GCatholic's page for Protection of the Blessed Virgin Mary, 255 Barnum Ave., Bridgeport (read 2026-11-12 with `probe get`) gives "Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Diocese of Stamford" and "Location: within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Bridgeport". So it belongs to the Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Stamford, not to the Diocese of Bridgeport, and this dataset's Bridgeport records don't carry it. The survey-review rule in `diocese.md` (*Rules settled*, 003-survey-review, 2026-11-12) excludes Fairfield County's Eastern Catholic churches as other-jurisdiction.

Checked as well (2026-11-12): neither the diocese's directory roster (`rosters/directory.json`) nor its Mass-times roster (`rosters/mass-times.json`) lists this church or its address. Whether it holds public Divine Liturgy now was not checked, and neither was whether it publishes a bulletin; the exclusion rests on jurisdiction alone.

## What would change this

A decision to map Eastern Catholic eparchies in their own right. That would be done under the eparchy, not under Bridgeport.
