## Why it is excluded

The diocese's release of 31 October 2025 (posted 3 November 2025) merged the Parish of Holy Family–St. Emery into Assumption (Fairfield), St. Ann and St. Peter (Bridgeport) from 1 November 2025, and says: "regular weekday and Sunday worship at Holy Family will continue until the Solemnity of Epiphany, Sunday, January 4, 2026. After this date, the church will remain available for funerals and special occasions, with families arranging for a celebrant and musicians." So `last_mass` is the release's end date for regular Mass, not a date a bulletin reported.

What I checked that agrees, read 2026-11-12: the three receiving parishes' current bulletins list no Mass at Holy Family. Assumption's 15 November 2026 issue mentions the building only for a Thursday 6:00 PM rosary "in the front vestibule of Holy Family Church" (page 6); St. Ann's 15 November 2026 issue and St. Peter's 8 November 2026 issue don't name Holy Family at all. Assumption's issues of December 2025 and January 2026 carry no Holy Family Mass times either. The parish domain `holyfamilyfairfield.com` (GCatholic's website for it) no longer resolves (probe url DEAD-DNS). The diocese's directory and Mass-times page don't list it. Funerals and occasional Masses arranged by families are not public scheduled Mass.

## What would change this

A receiving parish's bulletin or the diocese's Mass times listing a scheduled public Mass at Holy Family, 700 Old Stratfield Road.
