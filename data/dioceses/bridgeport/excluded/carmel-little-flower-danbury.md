## Why it is excluded

The Discalced Carmelite Nuns of the Monastery of the Little Flower of Jesus moved to Danbury in September 2025 (Fairfield County Catholic, 1 July 2026). Their site (read 2026-11-12) gives 71 Southern Blvd, Danbury and says: "Please note that in our current temporary location we do not have a public chapel or gift shop for visitors." Their daily Mass is the cloistered community's, so this is not a public worship site. No roster lists it; reconcile recorded it (2026-11-12) so the next researcher does not have to rediscover it.

## What would change this

A move to a permanent monastery with a public chapel, or a notice of a Mass open to visitors.
