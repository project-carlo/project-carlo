## Why it is excluded

GCatholic's page for Mother of God of Tenderness, 79 Golden Hill Rd., Danbury (read 2026-11-12 with `probe get`) gives "Jurisdiction: Greek-Melkite Diocese of Newton" and "Location: within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Bridgeport". So it belongs to the Melkite Greek Catholic Eparchy of Newton (Our Lady of the Annunciation in Boston); GCatholic calls it a community (chapel), not to the Diocese of Bridgeport, and this dataset's Bridgeport records don't carry it. The survey-review rule in `diocese.md` (*Rules settled*, 003-survey-review, 2026-11-12) excludes Fairfield County's Eastern Catholic churches as other-jurisdiction.

Checked as well (2026-11-12): neither the diocese's directory roster (`rosters/directory.json`) nor its Mass-times roster (`rosters/mass-times.json`) lists this church or its address. Whether it holds public Divine Liturgy now was not checked, and neither was whether it publishes a bulletin; the exclusion rests on jurisdiction alone.

## What would change this

A decision to map Eastern Catholic eparchies in their own right. That would be done under the eparchy, not under Bridgeport.
