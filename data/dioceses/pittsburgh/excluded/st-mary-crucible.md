## Why it is excluded

St. Mary, Carmichaels Rd., Crucible PA 15325 (GCatholic 24223, a church of the old Our Lady of Consolation parish), closed on 7 July 2019 per the St. Matthias history page (https://www.stmatthiasgreene.org/st-matthias-parish, read 2026-10-23): "07/07/2019 ~ Saint Mary Church, Crucible". The building is still in use: the bulletins of September and October 2026 announce a Community Reach Out Ministry (Catholic Charities) there, with volunteer orientation at 'St. Mary Church in Crucible'. That is a charity ministry, not a Mass: none of the 60 issues I grepped schedules a Mass there, and the Mass schedule of 25 Oct 2026 does not name it. I judged it `no public Mass` in practice, but the reason stays `closed` because the parish's own page says the church closed.

## What would change this

A St. Matthias bulletin or other source showing Mass there (even occasional) would make it a church row of st-matthias-parish-greene-county. I grepped all 60 weekly issues on the parish's /bulletins page (2025-09-07 to 2026-10-25) and none lists a Mass there.
