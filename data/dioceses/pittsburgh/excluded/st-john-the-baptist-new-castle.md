GCatholic lists this place, within the territory of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Pittsburgh, under the Maronite Diocese of Saint Maron of Brooklyn (Maronite (Antiochian) rite), at 2 W Reynolds St., New Castle, Pennsylvania 16101.
