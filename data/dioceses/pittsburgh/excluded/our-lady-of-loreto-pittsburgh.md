## Why it is excluded

Closed, last public Mass 30 August 2020. The Brookline Connection history page for the parish says: "On Sunday, August 30, 2020, a 'Mass of Farewell' was held at Our Lady of Loreto church. It was the final Mass celebrated at the church." GCatholic still lists the church (1905 Pioneer Ave., Pittsburgh 15226) as an ordinary entry, with no closure noted. The current Saint Teresa of Kolkata bulletin (25 October 2026, eCatholic 21158) lists Masses at St. Catherine of Siena, St. Pius X and Resurrection only, and the parish site and the diocese directory name those three.

**Disagreement recorded:** diopitt.org's Our Lady of Loreto page says that at the 1 July 2019 merger the church "remained open to serve the new parish"; that is the 2019 situation, and the building was closed 13 months later. The demolition in November 2025 comes only from a search snippet of a Facebook group, so treat it as unconfirmed; it does not affect the exclusion.

## What would change this

A Mass schedule, bulletin or intention list showing a public Mass at 1905 Pioneer Avenue after August 2020.
