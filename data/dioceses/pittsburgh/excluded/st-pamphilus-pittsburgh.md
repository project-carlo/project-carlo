## Why it is excluded

The building (GCatholic "St. Pamphilus", 948 Tropical Ave.; the Maronite parish and the diocese history page give 1000 Tropical Avenue, Pittsburgh 15216) no longer has a Latin-rite Saint Teresa of Kolkata Mass: the parish's About page and the 25 October 2026 bulletin name three churches and not this one, and a "Mass of Farewell" was held on 14 June 2020. Since about 2021 it has housed Our Lady of Victory Maronite Catholic Parish, under the Eparchy of St. Maron (its bulletin of 20 February 2022 is headed "At Saint Pamphilus Church"; olovpgh.org on 2026-10-23 lists Liturgies Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday 6:00PM, Saturday 4:00PM and Sunday 8:30AM and 11:00AM at 1000 Tropical Ave). So public Mass does continue at the address, in another jurisdiction: `other-jurisdiction` (Rules settled 6).

**Disagreement recorded:** diopitt.org's Saint Pamphilus page says the church "remained open to serve the new parish" at the July 2019 merger; it was in use for the farewell Mass 11 months later. GCatholic still lists St. Pamphilus as a Latin church and its separate Maronite entry (gcatholic:17045, Our Lady of Victory, 1000 Lindsay Rd., Carnegie) appears to carry the Maronite parish's earlier address, now superseded by 1000 Tropical Ave (see the manifest lead).

## What would change this

A Saint Teresa of Kolkata bulletin listing Mass at St. Pamphilus, or the Maronite parish leaving the building. The Maronite parish itself belongs to the Eastern Catholic exclusion record for gcatholic:17045, not to this diocese's parish rows.
