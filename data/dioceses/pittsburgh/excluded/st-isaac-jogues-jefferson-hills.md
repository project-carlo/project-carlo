## Why it is excluded

**St. Isaac Jogues, 1216 Collins Avenue, Jefferson Hills (Elrama) - closed, last Mass 2026-06-03.** The 18 October 2026 issue of the St. John XXIII bulletin (container 14/1156), page 3, says: "Fr. Bob celebrated the final Mass at St. Isaac on June 3 of this year, and the church building was officially relegated on July 1." The 7 June 2026 issue (pages 6-7) shows "Final Mass at St. Isaac Jogues, June 3, 2026". Its masthead on 7 June still names the church; the 25 October masthead and Mass schedule list only St. Benedict the Abbot and St. Francis of Assisi. The diocese directory and Mass Finder (read 2026-10-23) list no St. Isaac Jogues church for the parish, and GCatholic still lists it (history shows only the 2020 merger), so GCatholic lags.

The year of the 3 June / 1 July dates is inferred from the issue date, since the 18 October 2026 text says 'this year'; it agrees with the 7 June 2026 issue's photographs.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building is relegated. A later bulletin announcing a Mass there would reopen the question.
