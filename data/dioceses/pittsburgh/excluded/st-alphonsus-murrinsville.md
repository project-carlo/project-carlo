## Why it is excluded

St. Alphonsus, 270 W Sunbury Rd, Murrinsville, PA 16020, is closed; last Mass 2019 (year only: no source gives the day). The diocese's history page for the former St. Alphonsus parish says "two of the three churches, St. Alphonsus and Epiphany closed in 2019" (read 2026-10-23). The Butler Eagle (22 Feb 2025) calls it "the now-closed St. Alphonsus Catholic Church", being converted by Murrin descendants to a community center. The current St. Faustina bulletin (18 Oct 2026, page 3) and the parish's Worship Locations page list five places for Mass and not this church. GCatholic still lists it ("parishes merged 2021.01.04", website stalspar.com) and the predecessor list maps St. Alphonsus to St. Faustina; neither says anything about whether the building is open.

## What would change this

A St. Faustina bulletin or a diocesan page listing Mass at Murrinsville (the community-center group could hold an occasional Mass, which would need a schedule to count).
