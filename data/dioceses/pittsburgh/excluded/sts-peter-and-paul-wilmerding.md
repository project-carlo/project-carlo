## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists the Ukrainian (Byzantine) Church of Sts. Peter and Paul, 163 State St., Wilmerding, Pennsylvania 15148, within the territory of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Pittsburgh, under the Ukrainian Diocese of Saint Josaphat in Parma, but as a 'Former Church' ('now not in use', History 'closed 2013'). Two grounds point away from the Latin diocese's map: it is Eastern Catholic, so `other-jurisdiction` under the diocese's Rule 6 (kept as the reason for consistency with the rest of the batch), and GCatholic says it closed in 2013, so it has no public Mass now. `last_mass` 2013 is the closure year GCatholic gives, not a Mass date. GCatholic gives the same telephone number as St. John the Baptist, McKeesport (excluded/st-john-the-baptist-mckeesport.md), which suggests the parish moved or merged there; that is an inference, not a statement on the page.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; a closed Eastern Catholic church.
