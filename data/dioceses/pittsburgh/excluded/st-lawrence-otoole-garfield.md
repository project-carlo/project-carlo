## Why it is excluded

St. Lawrence O'Toole Church (Garfield, 5323 Penn Ave.) is the third building of the former St. Maria Goretti Parish; the diocese's history page says it closed in 2020, before Immaculate Conception (2022) and St. Joseph (2023), and all three were closed before the parish merged into St. Pio of Pietrelcina on 2024-01-01. last_mass 2020 is that year (no day given). Disagreement: a parishioner quoted by WTAE (May 2023) puts it a year before Immaculate Conception's 2022 closing, i.e. 2021; I followed the diocese's page and the year is not critical. The current St. Pio bulletin lists one church, Saint Augustine, Lawrenceville. The mapping of this GCatholic entry to St. Lawrence O'Toole rests on the Penn Ave address (Mapquest and Mass Times USA listings), not on a source on gcatholic itself.

## What would change this

A bulletin or the parish announcing Mass here again.
