## Why it is excluded

**Holy Name, 32 S 1st St, Duquesne: closed 2023-08-15, last (closing) Mass 2023-07-31.** The diocese's history page for St. Thomas the Apostle Parish (read 2026-10-23) says 'On August 15, 2023 both Holy Name Church, Duquesne and Resurrection Church, West Mifflin closed.' The 6 August 2023 bulletin (container 14/0675, page 7) prints a flyer 'Closing Mass, Holy Name Church, July 31, 2023'. `last_mass` is that closing-Mass date (from the flyer; a later issue confirming it was held was not read); the diocese's 15 August date is the formal closure. Holy Name was one of the two churches of the predecessor Christ the Light of the World Parish (GCatholic 24186, 'merged as Christ the Light of the World Parish 1994'). The ParishesOnline orgs for this parish carry the address '32 S 1st St' (Christ the Light of the World's office), which is this building, not St. Joseph's (817 W Grant Ave). The current bulletin (25 October 2026) lists six worship sites and not Holy Name.
