## Why it is excluded

**Our Lady, Queen of Peace, 907 Middle St (North Side), Pittsburgh: closed; relegated to profane but not sordid use on 2022-03-28.** The diocese's Christ Our Savior history page (read 2026-10-23) says: "On March 28, 2022, St. Ambrose Church and Our Lady, Queen of Peace Church were relegated to profane but not sordid use. This left the new parish with three church buildings, St. Boniface, St. Peter and St. Cyril of Alexandria." The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin schedules Mass at those three only. The date of the last Mass is not given, so `last_mass` is left blank.

**Sources disagree on the old page.** The diocese's page for the former Our Lady Queen of Peace parish still says the church "remains open and continues to serve the new parish" (a 1993 statement; it was open through the 2019 merger, which the Christ Our Savior page confirms: four churches remained open then). GCatholic lists it as a church of 'St. Peter Parish' with no history text. I followed the later, more specific 2022 statement. It is not in the directory or Mass Finder.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule naming a Mass at 907 Middle St. A closing-Mass date from the press would let `last_mass` be filled.
