## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists the Mission of St. Teresa of Calcutta as Maronite, within the territory of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Pittsburgh, under the Maronite Diocese of Saint Maron of Brooklyn, at 3357 Constitution Blvd., Darlington, Pennsylvania 16115, 'Using the building: Church of St. Monica, Darlington'.
