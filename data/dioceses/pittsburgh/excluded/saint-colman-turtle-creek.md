## Why it is excluded

**St. Colman, 100 Tri-Boro Ave.: closed, last Mass Wednesday 11 March 2026 (announced), decree effective 12:01 AM 12 March 2026.** Bishop Eckman's decree of 7 February 2026 closed the building for worship and relegated it to profane but not sordid use (read from the scan linked in the evidence). The 15 February 2026 bulletin announces the closing Mass: 'Wednesday, March 11 at 7:00 p.m. — St. Colman Church'. The 25 October 2026 bulletin prints no Mass here and a single Forest Hills schedule. The directory and Mass Finder (read 2026-10-23) already list only St. Joseph the Worker Church (Forest Hills); GCatholic (24281) still lists this building. Bulletin address 100 Tri-Boro Ave., Turtle Creek 15145 (8 February 2026 masthead) used; GCatholic's address differs in form.

## What would change this

A later bulletin or the parish announcing Mass here again (a reuse of the building by another Catholic body would be a new record). The last-Mass date is the announced closing Mass; the March 2026 issues are images and were not read.
