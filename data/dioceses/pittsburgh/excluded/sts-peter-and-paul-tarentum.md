## Why it is excluded

GCatholic's Latin entry 24201 'Sts. Peter and Paul', 339 E 10th Ave., Tarentum has the same address and Plus Code as the Ruthenian (Byzantine) Church of Sts. Peter and Paul (GCatholic 17321), and each entry says it shares the building with the other. A Byzantine Catholic parish site gives that address, so the building is, or recently was, used by a Ruthenian parish, not by Guardian Angels (its bulletin lists no Tarentum Mass; the 2022 relegation list names Holy Martyrs, at 344 W 9th Ave). I treat the entry as the Ruthenian church: `other-jurisdiction` under the diocese's rule 6. This is inference from GCatholic's cross-reference; I did not read a Latin closure source for this address, and I could not confirm that the Ruthenian parish still holds Mass (its site did not load).

## What would change this

A Latin-rite source (Guardian Angels bulletin, diocese page) naming Mass at 339 E 10th Ave. Whether the Ruthenian parish is open is for the Ruthenian row; it is not a Latin-diocese bulletin publisher here.
