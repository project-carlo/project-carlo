## Why it is excluded

**Saint Michael the Archangel, 20 Bank St., Fredericktown, PA: closed (decree effective March 2019), sold in 2022.** The 23 August 2026 bulletin (page 4) says Pastor Shawn Davis "purchased the former Saint Michael the Archangel church in Fredericktown, PA in the year 2022. The Fredericktown complex is now home to Pillar of Faith Church." The 2019 press (Observer-Reporter, WTAE, KDKA) reports the decree closing it in March 2019. The diocese history page for it (https://diopitt.org/saint-michael-the-archangel-fredericktown) says 'remains open and serves the new parish' about the 1994 merger and the diocese page for Saint Oliver Plunkett says both churches 'remain open for occasional worship' (2017); both predate the closing.

## What would change this

A bulletin showing Mass here again.
