## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Consolation, 837 Wood St., Nemacolin PA 15351 (GCatholic 24224, 'history 1923'), was one of the five parishes merged into St. Matthias on 1 July 2019 and its church closed on 21 July 2019 per the St. Matthias history page (https://www.stmatthiasgreene.org/st-matthias-parish, read 2026-10-23): "07/21/2019 ~ Our Lady of Consolation Church, Nemacolin". No issue in the current bulletin schedules a Mass there. GCatholic still lists it with a phone number (724-966-7270, St. Hugh's).

## What would change this

A St. Matthias bulletin or other source showing Mass there (even occasional) would make it a church row of st-matthias-parish-greene-county. I grepped all 60 weekly issues on the parish's /bulletins page (2025-09-07 to 2026-10-25) and none lists a Mass there.
