## Why it is excluded

**Saint Thomas Aquinas, 409 Union St., California, PA: closed March 2019, sold 14 August 2026.** The pastor's letter in the 23 August 2026 issue of the Catholic Partner Parishes bulletin (page 4, read 2026-10-23) says the "former Saint Thomas Aquinas church complex in California, PA has been sold" and that it "formally closed in March 2019". The Observer-Reporter of 3 February 2019 reported the decree: scheduled for closure March 4, 2019. The diocese history page (https://diopitt.org/saint-thomas-aquinas) still says 'St. Thomas Church remains open, but will only be used for occasional worship' (a 2017 merger-era statement, now superseded). A churchfinder.com listing (213 4th St, old Sunday times) is a stale third-party page, not used.

## What would change this

A bulletin listing Mass here again (the new owner is a non-denominational congregation, Pillar of Faith Church). The California University student Mass is a separate record (open/).
