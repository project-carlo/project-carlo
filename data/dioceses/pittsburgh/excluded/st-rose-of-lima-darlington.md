## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists a Roman-rite 'Church of St. Monica' at 3357 Constitution Blvd., Darlington (24354). That address is the former St. Rose of Lima church (web directories name it St. Rose at the same address; the identification is inferred from the address, not stated by the diocese). The diocese's St. Monica Parish history page (read 2026-10-23) says: "In 2020, St. Rose of Lima Church closed.  The closing Mass for the church was celebrated on March 28, 2020." Since 2015 it had been a church for devotion only (weddings, funerals and limited prayer hours), with all Sunday Masses at Christ the Divine Teacher, Chippewa, which is now Saint Monica in the St. Augustine Parish bulletin. The current bulletin lists no Mass at Darlington.

## What would change this

A St. Augustine bulletin or parish page that lists a Mass at the Darlington building, or evidence that GCatholic's address is a different building from St. Rose of Lima.
