## Why it is excluded

St. John the Baptist, 377 Linmore Ave, Baden, closed when its parish merged into Saint Luke the Evangelist on 2022-07-01. The diocese's history page says 'As part of the merger St. John the Baptist Church was closed at the time of the merger and the parish planned to sell the building to the Maronites'. The exact last Mass is not given, so `last_mass` is the year only. The current St. Luke Mass schedule (parish home page) and the 25 October 2026 bulletin name two churches, Good Samaritan and Saints John & Paul, and none in Baden. GCatholic (24314) lists the building as a Latin Roman Catholic church with no closure note; the diocese's own history page is the closure source here.

A Maronite mission (St. Teresa of Calcutta, Eparchy of Saint Maron of Brooklyn) is listed at 375 Linmore Ave, Baden with a Sunday 11:00 am liturgy. That is an Eastern Catholic place, other-jurisdiction, and is recorded separately as `st-teresa-of-calcutta-maronite-mission-baden`.

## What would change this

A Latin-rite Mass schedule for this address in a St. Luke bulletin, or a sale that did not happen and Masses resumed.
