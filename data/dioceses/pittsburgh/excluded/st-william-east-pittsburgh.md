## Why it is excluded

**St. William, 808 Main St. East Pittsburgh: closed 2014-04-28 (last Mass not given; `last_mass` is the month).** The diocese's history page for Holy Cross, East Pittsburgh (the parish formed in 1994 from St. Helen and St. William) says the parish merged into Good Shepherd, Braddock on 28 April 2014 and that 'both of the Holy Cross churches, St. Helen and St. William closed on the merger date'. The successor Saint Joseph the Worker Parish's bulletins (8 February and 25 October 2026) list no East Pittsburgh church, and the directory and Mass Finder do not either. GCatholic (24195) still lists it (group Holy Cross Parish); the batch plan paired it with the Forest Hills row by distance only.
