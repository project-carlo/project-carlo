## Why it is excluded

**St. Stanislaus Kostka, 301 Penn Ave, Lyndora (parish: All Saints, Butler) - closed 2020-07-01.** The diocese's history page for St. Fidelis of Sigmaringen Parish (https://diopitt.org/saint-fidelis-of-sigmaringen, read 2026-10-23) says: "On July 1, 2020 St. Stanislaus Kostka Church, Lyndora closed, leaving only St. Conrad Church open." GCatholic agrees (type Former Church, "closed 2020.07.01"). The building had belonged to St. Fidelis of Sigmaringen Parish since the 1995 merger of St. John and St. Stanislaus Kostka (Lyndora) with St. Conrad (Meridian); that parish merged into All Saints on 2021-07-01. The current All Saints bulletin (25 Oct 2026, container 14/1322, page 1) schedules five other churches and none in Lyndora, and the diocese directory lists the same five. `last_mass` is the month of closure: no source gives the final Mass date.

The batch's roster entry was a planning guess placing this GCatholic entry with All Saints, which is right: it was part of St. Fidelis, All Saints' predecessor.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. A Mass listed at Lyndora in a later All Saints bulletin would reopen it.
