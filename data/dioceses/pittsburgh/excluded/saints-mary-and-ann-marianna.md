## Why it is excluded

**Saints Mary and Ann, 117 First St., Marianna, PA: closed by decree, scheduled for March 4, 2019; in other use by 2021.** The Observer-Reporter article of 3 Feb 2019 lists it among the four buildings 'scheduled for closure March 4' and the 16 Sept 2022 Observer-Reporter says the Marianna Christian Outreach (an extension of Bethlehem Lutheran Church) 'moved to a new location in 2021 -- the old Sts. Mary and Ann Catholic Church at 117 First St.'. The diocese history page (https://diopitt.org/saints-mary-and-ann-marianna) still says it 'remains open and serves the new parish' (about the 1994 merger; stale).

The last Mass date was not found; the 4 March 2019 closure date is the decree's, so 2019-03 is a month, not a day.

## What would change this

A bulletin listing Mass here (the building belongs to a Lutheran-linked charity).
