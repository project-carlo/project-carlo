## Why it is excluded

St. Martin Church, 1030 Logue St., West End, Pittsburgh (GCatholic 24192) was the last open church of Guardian Angels Parish, which was absorbed by Saint Philip on 1 August 2016. The diocese's Guardian Angels page says 'As part of the merger, Guardian Angels Church closed' (read 2026-10-23). That sentence does not name the building; I read it as the St. Martin building because the other two churches of the 1994 grouping are accounted for by the same page (Guardian Angels church closed and sold after 1994; St. James closed 2004) and the parish's address and phone stayed 1030 Logue St. / 412-921-4077 (GCatholic). That identification is inference. Saint Philip's current bulletin lists no Mass there, and the parish site says it keeps 'two worship centers' (saintphilipparishpgh.com home page, Saint Philip and Ascension). Last Mass recorded as 2016.

## What would change this

A Saint Philip bulletin listing a Mass at 1030 Logue St., or a diocesan or press statement that the building was kept open as a chapel.
