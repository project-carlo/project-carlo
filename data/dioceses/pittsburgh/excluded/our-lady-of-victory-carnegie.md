## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists Our Lady of Victory as a Maronite church, within the territory of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Pittsburgh, under the Maronite Diocese of Saint Maron of Brooklyn (Maronite (Antiochian) rite), at 1000 Lindsay Rd., Carnegie, Pennsylvania 15106 (the older address). The Maronite eparchy's directory and the parish's own site give 1000 Tropical Ave., Pittsburgh, PA 15216, so this record uses that address and the GCatholic address is a stale one: the sources disagree and the roster entry keeps the Carnegie address. It is an Eastern Catholic parish under another jurisdiction, so `other-jurisdiction` under the diocese's Rule 6.

## Relation to st-pamphilus-pittsburgh

The Maronite parish now uses the former St. Pamphilus Church building (Latin, GCatholic 24360, 948 Tropical Ave.), which is recorded separately in excluded/st-pamphilus-pittsburgh.md; that record already cites the parish's 20 February 2022 bulletin for the move. The two records stay separate: St. Pamphilus is the Latin church that stopped, this is the Maronite parish that now occupies the plant. The address difference (948 versus 1000 Tropical Ave.) is the sources' (GCatholic for St. Pamphilus; the parish and eparchy for the Maronite parish); I did not resolve it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the place stays with its own eparchy.
