## Why it is excluded

**St. Januarius Church, 1450 Renton Rd., Plum (GCatholic 24306): closed, September 2024.** TribLive reported on 25 August 2024 that "St. Januarius Catholic Church in Plum is closing next month", the final day "set for Sept. 23, according to a decree from Bishop David Zubik", because of the cost of a roof, boiler and gas line (estimated $236,000). The Holy Family bulletin of 25 October 2026 lists Masses at four churches and none at St. Januarius (grep of all 20 pages for 'Januarius': no hits), and the parish's Mass list in the diocese directory has four churches. The diocese's older history page still says the church 'remained open', which predates the decree. The 23 September date is the decree's announced closing date, so `last_mass` is given as 2024-09 only.

## What would change this

A Holy Family bulletin or the parish site listing Mass at Renton Road again, or an article saying the closing slipped past September 2024.
