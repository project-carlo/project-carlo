## Why it is excluded

**St. Wendelin, 2728 Custer Avenue, Carrick (Pittsburgh 15227) - closed and demolished, last public Mass 2023-11-26 (disputed).** WPXI reported on 27 November 2023 that "Sunday's masses were the last the public would attend" at St. Wendelin, after the diocese said in October that the church had to close because of its poor condition and repair costs; WTAE reported the 128-year-old building being demolished on 7 June 2024. One source disagrees on the last use: WPXI's 6 June 2024 piece says the church "was last used in 2020". The diocese's Holy Apostles history page (read 2026-10-23) says St. Wendelin "remained open as part of the new parish" at the 2020-07-01 merger, which is consistent with Mass until 2023. The current Blessed Trinity bulletin (18 October 2026) lists four other churches and does not mention St. Wendelin; the diocese directory and Mass Finder do not list it. GCatholic still lists "St. Wendelin" (Church, 1873, dedicated 1897) with no closure note.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building is gone. The last-Mass date could be refined from the November 2023 Blessed Trinity bulletin if a reader wants to settle the 2020 versus 2023 conflict.
