## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Perpetual Help (10th Ave. and Carolina St., Natrona Heights; GCatholic 24233) is one of the seven churches of Guardian Angels Parish and, per the TribLive article of 18 June 2025, one of the five the diocese closed in 2022; the parish history page dates the relegation to profane but not sordid use to 1 October 2022. The article's photo caption reads 'Our Lady of Perpetual Help church in Harrison, closed by the Catholic Diocese of Pittsburgh in 2022, remains for sale.' The current bulletin lists no Mass here. Last Mass recorded as 2022; no source gives the day.

## What would change this

A Guardian Angels bulletin listing a Mass here, or a sale to a congregation that holds public Catholic Mass.
