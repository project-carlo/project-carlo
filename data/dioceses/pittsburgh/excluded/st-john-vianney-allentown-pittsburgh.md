## Why it is excluded

**St. John Vianney (former St. George), 225 Allen Street, Allentown (Pittsburgh 15210) - closed, last Mass 2016-04-03.** The diocese's history page for the parish says: "The final Mass of the parish was celebrated on April 3, 2016 and the church and parish were closed on that day." It predates the 2020 Blessed Trinity merger and belongs to no current parish, so the batch's "nearest" planning guess (1.1 km from a Blessed Trinity church) was wrong. It is in neither the diocese directory, the Mass Finder nor the predecessor list (it closed before the 2016 plan's mergers); GCatholic still lists it as a church. Not in the current bulletins of Blessed Trinity or Mary, Queen of Peace.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the Allentown community has tried to preserve the building (St. George Church Preservation Society), which is not a Catholic worship use.
