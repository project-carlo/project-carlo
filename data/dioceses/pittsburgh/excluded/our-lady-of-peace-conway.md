## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Peace, 1000 3rd Ave, Conway, closed when its parish merged into Saint Luke the Evangelist on 2022-07-01. The diocese's history page says 'As part of the merger Our Lady of Peace Church was closed at the time of the merger'. The exact last Mass is not given, so `last_mass` is the year only. The current St. Luke Mass schedule (parish home page) and the 25 October 2026 bulletin name two churches, Good Samaritan and Saints John & Paul, and none in Conway. GCatholic (24230) lists it as a Latin church (1941; ded. 1946) with no closure note.

## What would change this

A Mass schedule for this address in a St. Luke bulletin.
