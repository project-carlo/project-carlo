## Why it is excluded

Holy Rosary Church (7114 Kelly Street, Homewood; GCatholic 24278, listed under the former St. Charles Lwanga Parish) is no longer a worship site. Saint Mary Magdalene's history page says the parish 'owns only Holy Rosary Church ... and Mother of Good Counsel' and 'the only church still open for worship is Mother of Good Counsel'; Father Pitt (12 June 2022) says it 'is no longer a worship site'; and none of the twelve bulletins on Discover Mass (9 August - 25 October 2026) names it in a masthead, Mass Schedule or intention (the staff box lists the 'Holy Rosary Food Pantry', which runs from the campus). Not in the diocese directory or Mass Finder either. The diocese's own 1992-era history page still says it 'remains open'; I treated that as stale. No closing-Mass date was found, so `last_mass` is blank. Reason `closed` rests on the parish's statement plus the 2022 report, not on a dated closure notice.

## What would change this

A Mass at Holy Rosary named in a Saint Mary Magdalene bulletin or on the parish site (for example an annual or event Mass) would make it a church row under saint-mary-magdalene-pittsburgh. A dated closure/relegation decree would replace the evidence here.
