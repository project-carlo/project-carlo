## Why it is excluded

**St. Gerard Majella Church, 121 Dawn Dr., Penn Hills (GCatholic 24295, listed as 'Verona' 15147): closed by decree effective 2021-08-06.** Bishop Zubik's letter of 10 July 2021 says the petitions asked that the church be "closed permanently for worship" and decrees it "closed for worship effective August 6, 2021" (evidence, first item). The diocese's Christ the Divine Shepherd history page repeats it. The parish's 25 October 2026 bulletin schedules Mass only at St. Bernadette and St. Susanna. Nothing found shows a later Mass; I did not search a year of bulletins for occasional Masses here because the decree closes the building for worship.

## What would change this

A Christ the Divine Shepherd bulletin or parish page scheduling Mass at this address again. `last_mass` is the month of the decree's effective date, not a confirmed final Mass.
