## Why it is excluded

**St. Robert Bellarmine, 1313 Fifth Avenue, East McKeesport: closed 2024-12-02, final Sunday Mass 2024-11-24.** The 24 November 2024 bulletin of Mary, Mother of God Parish says 'This Sunday, November 24 at 1:00 p.m. is the final Sunday Mass at St. Robert Bellarmine Church. The church will officially close on Monday December 2 at 12:01 a.m.'; the 6 April 2025 issue says the church 'was closed this past November' and describes cataloguing its sacred items. Regular Mass had already ended in January 2023 (WTAE, Mon Valley Independent 2022-12-22), with two canonically required Masses a year after that (the 25 August 2024 issue prints the 17 September 2024 feast-day Mass). The current bulletins (23 August and 25 October 2026) list St. Angela Merici, Corpus Christi and St. Patrick only; the directory and Mass Finder do not list St. Robert. GCatholic (24380) still lists it (history '1950; ded. 1972.08.19'). `last_mass` is the final Sunday Mass as announced on the day; a later issue confirming it was held was not read.

## What would change this

The diocese's own page for the church (diopitt.org/saint-robert-bellarmine) still says it 'remained open' at the 2020 merger: that is the 2020 statement, superseded. A source showing Mass in the building again would reopen it.
