## Why it is excluded

Epiphany, 141 Middendorf St, Boyers, PA 16020, closed in 2019 (year only). The diocese's history page for the former St. Alphonsus parish says "two of the three churches, St. Alphonsus and Epiphany closed in 2019" (https://www.diopitt.org/saint-alphonsus-boyers-murrinsville-west-sunbury, read 2026-10-23). Sources disagree: the diocese's own Epiphany page (https://diopitt.org/epiphany-boyers) ends "Epiphany Church remains open and serves the new parish", which is 1993-era text, never updated; I followed the St. Alphonsus history page because it dates the closure and was written after it. Corroboration is the absence of Epiphany from the current St. Faustina bulletin (18 Oct 2026 Mass intentions; no mention of Boyers or Epiphany in ten 2026 issues grepped) and from the parish's Worship Locations page. Only the one diocesan page states the closure; I found no news article (one search, nothing found).

## What would change this

A St. Faustina bulletin or other source showing Mass at Boyers (even occasional) would reopen it as a church row.
