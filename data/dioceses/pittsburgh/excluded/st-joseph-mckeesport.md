## Why it is excluded

**St. Joseph, 1125 Romine Ave, McKeesport: closed by decree 2020-03-23.** The parish site's St. Joseph page (read 2026-10-23) says 'The Saint Joseph Church was closed on March 23, 2020 according to the Bishop's Decree.' GCatholic (24340) says 'merged as St. Mark parish 1993; parishes merged 2021.01.04': St. Joseph was one of the two churches of St. Mark, whose other church St. Eugene is the surviving site. The 25 October 2026 Saints Joachim and Anne bulletin (container 14/0524) lists Mass at St. Michael, Queen of the Rosary and St. Eugene only, and the directory and Mass Finder list the same three. Discover Mass still has a church listing under the address 1125 Romine Ave. ('Sts. Joachim and Anne', discovermass.com/church/saint-joseph-st-mark-port-vue-pa/; search snippet), a stale listing that this check did not open. GCatholic's own website field points at saint-michael-elizabeth, a naming artefact.
