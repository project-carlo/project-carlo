## Why it is excluded

Saint John Cantius, Sharpsburg (the Korean Catholic Community's church since 2008, per the parish page) holds public Masses in Korean: Sunday 11:00 am and Thursday 11:30 am, listed by the parish website, the diocese parish page and the Mass Finder, all read 2026-10-23. Christ the King's weekly bulletin, the only bulletin found for it, never prints them: the 18 October 2026 Sunday and daily Mass lists (page 10) and the intentions (page 6) leave it out, and the earlier seven issues grepped (30 Aug to 11 Oct) name neither the church nor Korean except the chaplain line. Under this diocese's settled rule (diocese.md, Rules settled 1) a place with public Mass that no bulletin prints is excluded as `no-bulletin`, with the source that shows the Mass. It is open, not closed.

The address differs by source: the parish page gives 623 High St, the diocese and GCatholic the corner 6th and High Streets; coordinates (Mass Finder 40.4968, -79.9359; GCatholic Plus Code 87G2F3W7+MP) agree within 20 m.

## What would change this

A bulletin issue, a Korean Catholic Community bulletin or a Discover Mass listing that prints these Masses would make it a church row of Christ the King (or a new row if the community publishes its own bulletin). No separate Korean-community bulletin was searched beyond one web search (2026-10-23) that found only the parish page, the diocese history page and third-party Mass-time listings. Retry when a bulletin changes or the parish site adds a bulletin link for the community.
