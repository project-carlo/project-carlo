## Why it is excluded

**St. Ambrose, 1015 Haslage Ave (Spring Hill), Pittsburgh: closed, last Mass 2018 (year only).** The diocese's Christ Our Savior history page (read 2026-10-23) says: "St. Ambrose from the former Holy Wisdom Parish closed for worship in 2018 but remained a parish church building", and that on 28 March 2022 it "was relegated to profane but not sordid use". The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin (container 14/1701, rendered pages 1-2) schedules Mass at St. Boniface, St. Cyril and St. Peter only. GCatholic still lists it as a church of the former Holy Wisdom Parish and the diocese directory and Mass Finder do not list it, so no source contradicts the closure.

## What would change this

A bulletin or parish schedule naming a Mass at 1015 Haslage Ave (the history page says the building was kept, then relegated to profane use, so it is unlikely). The exact last-Mass date is not given; 2018 is the page's own year.
