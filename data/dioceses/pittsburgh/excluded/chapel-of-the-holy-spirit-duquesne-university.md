## Why it is excluded

The Chapel of the Holy Spirit in Duquesne University's Administration Building holds Mass every day in term (Monday to Friday 7:45 a.m. and noon, Saturday 5:30 p.m., Sunday 11:00 a.m., 6:00 p.m., 9:00 p.m.; Spiritan Campus Ministry page read 2026-10-23, quoted in the evidence). Rules settled 1 counts in-term campus Masses. The page addresses anyone ('Are you Catholic and looking for a place to worship?') and restricts no one, so I read the Mass as public; that is an inference from the invitation, not a statement that outsiders may attend. No bulletin prints it: the campus ministry pages link no PDF or image bulletin, and no Pittsburgh parish bulletin read so far, nor the diocese's Mass Finder or directory, lists it. So it is `no-bulletin` (open Mass, nothing the pipeline can read). The schedule is only on a web page that varies week by week ('reference our events calendar').

The chapel is not on any roster (GCatholic, directory, Mass Finder). It is unrelated to the borough of Duquesne's Saint Joseph Church.

## What would change this

A bulletin (campus ministry PDF or a parish bulletin) that prints the chapel's Masses would make it a church row. Confirmation from campus ministry that the Mass is for the university community only would make it `no-public-mass`.
