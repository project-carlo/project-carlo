## Why it is excluded

**Resurrection, 1 Majka Dr, West Mifflin: closed 2023-08-15, closing Mass 2023-08-07.** The 6 August 2023 bulletin of St. Thomas the Apostle (container 14/0675, page 4) announces 'Closing Mass- The closing Mass at Resurrection will take place on Monday, August 7 at 6:00 PM'; the diocese's history page for the parish (read 2026-10-23) says 'On August 15, 2023 both Holy Name Church, Duquesne and Resurrection Church, West Mifflin closed.' `last_mass` is the announced closing Mass (not confirmed from a later issue). Resurrection was one of the eight predecessor parishes merged on 2020-07-01. The current bulletin (25 October 2026) lists six worship sites and not Resurrection. GCatholic (24241) still lists it with no closure note. It is not the same place as Resurrection, Brookline (a separate predecessor of another parish).
