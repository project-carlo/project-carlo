## Why it is excluded

**Saint Joseph, 105 Good St., Roscoe, PA: closed by decree, scheduled for March 4, 2019.** The Observer-Reporter article of 3 Feb 2019 (also WTAE, WPXI, KDKA) lists it first among the four buildings of Saint Katharine Drexel Parish to close. The parish's own current bulletin lists only Bentleyville and Richeyville as churches and no issue since June 2025 mentions Roscoe. The diocese history page (https://diopitt.org/saint-joseph-roscoe) says the building 'was retained and will be used for occasional worship', a 2017 merger-era statement that predates the decree.

What I did not find: a dated closing-Mass notice, or the building's present owner. The closure rests on the decree reports in 2019 plus six years of silence in the parish bulletin. Last Mass is recorded as 2019-03.

## What would change this

A bulletin or parish page listing Mass at Saint Joseph, Roscoe, or news that the decree was not carried out.
