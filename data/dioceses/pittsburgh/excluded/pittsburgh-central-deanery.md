## Why it is excluded

The Pittsburgh Central Deanery is an administrative grouping of seven parishes (Divine Mercy, Saint Benedict the Moor, Saint Jude, Saint Mary Magdalene, Saint Paul Cathedral, Saint Pio of Pietrelcina, Shrines of Pittsburgh), as its home page says: 'The Deanery brings together parishes to foster cooperation and shared pastoral activities.' It has no church, no address of its own (its footer is the Diocesan Pastoral Center) and no Mass schedule; the Mass Finder pin (id 39162, no address, 0,0) exists to link the deanery's website. Reason is `no-public-mass`: an administrative body, like an office, with no worship site.

The seven member parishes are covered in other batches (this check did not read their bulletins; the deanery pages read link no combined bulletin). Nothing here belongs to any of them.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. If the deanery began to hold a regular public Mass in a named building, that building would be the place.
