## Why it is excluded

The address 344 W 9th Ave., Tarentum, which GCatholic 24200 labels 'St. Clement', is the address of Holy Martyrs Church (the TribLive piece on the sale gives '344 W. Ninth Ave.' for Holy Martyrs; directory listings and GCatholic's own website field for the entry point to Holy Martyrs). Holy Martyrs was one of the five churches closed in 2022 (TribLive 18 June 2025) and has been sold to a fitness center. Disagreement: the diocese's Holy Martyrs page says the original St. Clement church was sold in 2006 and 'Sacred Heart-St. Peter church was renamed Holy Martyrs Church', so GCatholic's name for this entry is probably the old parish's name, not the building's. Either way no church at this address holds Mass: it is closed in 2022 as Holy Martyrs, and a St. Clement church elsewhere in Tarentum (location not found) would have closed in 2006. The current bulletin lists no Tarentum Mass. Last Mass recorded as 2022.

## What would change this

A bulletin listing a Mass in Tarentum. Identifying the 2006 St. Clement building would only add an `excluded/` record.
