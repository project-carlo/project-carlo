## Why it is excluded

**St. Hedwig, 105 S 5th St, Duquesne: closed 2022-01-31.** The diocese's history page for St. Thomas the Apostle Parish (read 2026-10-23) says 'On January 31, 2022 St. Hedwig Church, Duquesne closed.' It was one of the two churches of the predecessor Christ the Light of the World Parish (with Holy Name), merged into St. Thomas the Apostle on 2020-07-01 (GCatholic 24185 history: 'merged as Christ the Light of the World Parish 1994; parishes merged 2020.07.01'). `last_mass` is '2022-01' because the page gives the closing date, not the day of the last Mass. The current bulletin (25 October 2026) lists six worship sites and not St. Hedwig. The 2022 bulletins of container 14/0675 are not served (403), so no bulletin of the time was read. The directory and Mass Finder do not list it; GCatholic still does.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. A 2022 bulletin announcing the closing Mass would give the exact last-Mass date.
