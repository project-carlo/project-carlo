## Why it is excluded

Immaculate Conception Church (Bloomfield, dedicated 1960) closed in May 2022 (CBS Pittsburgh; the diocese's history page agrees on 2022) and was demolished in 2025 (WPXI, 25 July 2025). last_mass 2022-05 is the closing month from CBS, not a found last Mass. The GCatholic entry is labelled 'St. Maria Goretti' because that was the merged parish's name; I identified it as Immaculate Conception by its 300 Edmond St. address (parish listings and a funeral notice give that address for the Immaculate Conception church; the other two GCatholic entries are St. Joseph, 4712 Liberty Ave., and St. Lawrence O'Toole, 5323 Penn Ave.).

## What would change this

Nothing: the building is gone.
