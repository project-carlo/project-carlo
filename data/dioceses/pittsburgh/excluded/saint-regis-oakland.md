## Why it is excluded

**St. Regis Church, 3256 Parkview Avenue (GCatholic 3250 Parkview Ave.), Oakland: closed for worship, decree effective 2025-09-29.** Bishop Eckman's decree of 30 August 2025 (read 2026-10-23, image PDF; quotes in the evidence) 'close[s] for worship Saint Regis church building' and relegates it to profane but not sordid use, effective 12:01AM on 29 September 2025. The parish's June 2025 letter had already said regular worship moved away in June 2021, leaving only annual Masses, funerals and occasional weddings and baptisms. The current parish bulletin (25 October 2026) lists three worship sites and not St. Regis. The diocese's older history pages still say 'St. Regis Church remained open as part of the new parish' (diopitt.org/saint-regis, 2020 merger text): that is the plan of 2020 and is superseded. A commercial listing calls it 'Former St Regis Church'.

last_mass 2025-09 is the month of the decree's effective date, not a found last Mass; the closing Mass, if any, was not located.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; a decree of relegation is reversed only by appeal. Appeals were possible under the decree, none was found.
