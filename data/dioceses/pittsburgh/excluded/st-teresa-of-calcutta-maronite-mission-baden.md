## Why it is excluded

The Eparchy of Saint Maron of Brooklyn's parish list (https://www.stmaron.org/parishes-p, read 2026-10-23) lists a Maronite mission, 'St. Teresa of Calcutta Maronite Mission, 375 Linmore Ave, Baden, PA 15005', with a Sunday 11:00 am liturgy. Maronite Catholics are an Eastern Catholic church under their own eparchy, so the place is `other-jurisdiction`. It sits beside the former Latin church St. John the Baptist, 377 Linmore Ave (see `st-john-the-baptist-baden`), which the diocese said it planned to sell to the Maronites; whether the mission uses that building or the next lot was not checked. GCatholic does not list it among Pittsburgh's Maronite churches under this name (the Pittsburgh roster has none in Baden); it was found by search, not from a roster.

## What would change this

Nothing about the jurisdiction. If the mission turns out to use the former St. John the Baptist building, that closed-church record stays correct for Latin Mass.
