## Why it is excluded

**St. Barnabas, 2540 Woodstock Ave., Swissvale: closed, last worship 2011, formally closed 2014-06-30.** The diocese's history page for St. Barnabas parish says the Word of God parish 'discontinued using St.Barnabas Church' in 2011 and 'on June 30, 2014, formally closed the church'; TribLive (2014) says none of the three Word of God buildings now closing 'has been used for worship since 2011'. `last_mass` is the year only (2011). The successor, Saint Joseph the Worker Parish (Word of God merged into it on 2020-07-01), lists eight churches in its 8 February 2026 masthead and not St. Barnabas; the directory and Mass Finder do not list it. GCatholic (24407, 'merged as Word of God Parish 1994') still lists the building.

## What would change this

A later source putting Mass in the building again (current occupant not checked).
