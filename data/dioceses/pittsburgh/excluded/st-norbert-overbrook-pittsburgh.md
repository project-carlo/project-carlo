## Why it is excluded

**St. Norbert, 2413 Saint Norbert Street, Overbrook (Pittsburgh 15234) - closed, last Mass on or before 2020-07-01.** The diocese's history page for Holy Apostles Parish says: "The fourth church, St. Norbert closed permanently on July 1, 2020, the date of the merger." The current Blessed Trinity bulletin (18 October 2026, eCatholic 26316, read 2026-10-23) lists Masses at four churches only (St. Albert, St. Basil, Holy Angels, St. Sylvester), and grep for "Norbert" in the full 16 pages finds nothing, so no occasional Mass is announced. The diocese directory and Mass Finder do not list it. The date is the day of the merger; no source read gives the final Mass itself, so last_mass is the closure date, not a Mass date. GCatholic still lists the church under "Parish of the Blessed Trinity" without saying it closed; masstimesusa.com titles a listing "St. Norbert (Closed) - Blessed Trinity Parish" (search-result snippet only, not opened).

## What would change this

A Blessed Trinity bulletin announcing Mass at St. Norbert, or a reopening. Nothing expected.
