## Why it is excluded

**Holy Spirit, 2603 Old Elizabeth Rd, West Mifflin: closed, relegated 2023-08-01, sold 2026-03-19.** The diocese's parish history page for Triumph of the Holy Cross (read 2026-10-23) says 'Holy Spirit Church was relegated for profane but not sordid use on August 1, 2023. The other three churches remained open.' The parish bulletin of 29 March 2026 (container 14/1225, page 3) says the sale of the property to the Society of St. Vincent de Paul 'has officially been finalized as of March 19th'. The current bulletin (25 October 2026) prints no Mass here and its masthead lists three churches only. The 16 July 2023 issue of the same container announces a closing Mass of Thanksgiving at Holy Spirit Church on 23 July 2023 at 11:00 a.m.; `last_mass` uses that date (announced; not confirmed from a later issue). GCatholic (24206, 'Holy Spirit', parish of the Triumph of the Holy Cross) still lists the building; the Mass Finder and the diocese directory do not.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building is sold and reused as a thrift store and charity headquarters.
