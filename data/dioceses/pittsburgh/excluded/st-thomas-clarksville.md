## Why it is excluded

St. Thomas, 30 Main St., Clarksville PA 15322 (GCatholic 24389, a predecessor parish of St. Matthias), closed on 14 July 2019. The parish's own history page (https://www.stmatthiasgreene.org/st-matthias-parish, read 2026-10-23) lists "07/14/2019 ~ Saint Thomas Church, Clarksville" among four closures at the 2019 merger and says only St. Ann, St. Hugh, St. Ignatius and St. Marcellus remain open. The diocese's predecessor list calls the town 'Clasksville' (a typo). Corroboration: no issue of the current bulletin (60 grepped) schedules a Mass at Clarksville. The GCatholic record still lists the church with phone and website, which is the stale part.

## What would change this

A St. Matthias bulletin or other source showing Mass there (even occasional) would make it a church row of st-matthias-parish-greene-county. I grepped all 60 weekly issues on the parish's /bulletins page (2025-09-07 to 2026-10-25) and none lists a Mass there.
