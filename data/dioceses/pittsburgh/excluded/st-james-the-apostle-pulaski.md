## Why it is excluded

**St. James the Apostle, 4019 US 422, Pulaski - closed, formally closed by the parish 2023-10-02.** The diocese's Holy Spirit history page says: "On October 2, 2023, the parish formally closed ... Saint James the Apostle Church, Pulaski". Date of the last Mass not given.

Bulletin check (read 2026-10-23): the 25 Oct 2026 issue of the Holy Spirit Parish bulletin lists Masses at St. Camillus, St. Vitus and St. Mary only; grepping that issue and those of 27 Sep, 30 Aug and 5 Jul 2026 for this church's name, town and street found no Mass or event there. The diocese directory and Mass Finder list only the same three churches for the parish. GCatholic still lists the church (as an ordinary church, no 'former' tag): it lags on closures.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule listing a Mass there (including an occasional one in the intentions or calendar), which would make it a church row under Holy Spirit Parish.
