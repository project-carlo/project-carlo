## Why it is excluded

St. Joseph, 48 Spruce St., Natrona Heights (GCatholic 24322, originally St. Ladislaus) is the building the press calls St. Ladislaus in Natrona. I equated them from GCatholic's 'originally St. Ladislaus' history note and the diocese's 'St. Joseph, Natrona' parish in the merger list: that identification is inference from two sources, the press never gives the street. It was among the five churches closed in 2022 (history page: relegated 1 October 2022) and TribLive says St. Ladislaus 'was demolished early last year' (2024). Last Mass recorded as 2022.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building is reported demolished.
