## Why it is excluded

St. Joseph Church, 4712 Liberty Ave., Bloomfield (1886), was the last of the three buildings of the former St. Maria Goretti Parish to close. Closed: the diocese's history page says 'St. Joseph closed in 2023' and that all three buildings were closed before the 2024-01-01 merger into St. Pio of Pietrelcina Parish; CBS reported on 12 January 2023 that it 'hasn't celebrated mass in five months' (so the last Mass was about August 2022, which I used for last_mass 2022, inferred), WTAE on 3 May 2023 that the pastor was asking the bishop to close it permanently, and the diocese and parish sold the building in July 2025. The current St. Pio bulletin (container 14/0730, 25 October 2026) prints a single schedule for one church, Saint Augustine in Lawrenceville.

The disagreement is only the year: the diocese says 2023 (the formal closing), the news says Masses stopped in 2022.

## What would change this

A bulletin or the parish announcing Mass in the building again; it was sold in 2025, so this is unlikely.
