## Why it is excluded

**St. Peter, Sarah Street at South 28th Street, South Side (Pittsburgh 15203; the diocese lists the property as 2801 Sarah Street) - closed for worship by decree of 2019-12-06.** The diocese's news item of 11 December 2019 says Bishop Zubik's decree of 6 December 2019 closed the St. Peter church building of Prince of Peace Parish for worship, and the parish later offered the building for sale. The last Mass date is not given in anything read, so last_mass is the year. The Mary, Queen of Peace bulletin of 25 October 2026 (container 14/0020) names Masses only at St. Adalbert and St. Mary of the Mount, and a grep for "St. Peter" finds nothing; the diocese directory and Mass Finder list those two only. GCatholic's entry (24238) is typed "Former Church" (merged to Prince of Peace 1992.11.01) and is the South Side building.

**Roster note for the orchestrator:** `gcatholic:24238` is listed in the roster of churches/saint-peter-pittsburgh.md (St. Peter, North Side, Christ Our Savior, 720 Arch Street), which belongs to another batch. GCatholic 24238 is the South Side building at Sarah Street (merged to Prince of Peace), not the North Side church, so that link looks wrong; it is reported as a lead and not edited here.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the building was relegated to profane use and offered for sale.
