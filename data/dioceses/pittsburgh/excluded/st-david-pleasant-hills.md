## Why it is excluded

**St. David (Springdale Dr, Willock / Pleasant Hills): closed, last Mass 2018-04-01.** The diocese's history page for St. Elizabeth of Hungary, Pleasant Hills (read 2026-10-23) says 'The final Mass in St. David Church was celebrated on Easter Sunday April 1, 2018.' (The 1 April 2018 date is Easter Sunday in that year.) GCatholic still lists the church as a mission of St. Elizabeth. The 25 October 2026 Triumph of the Holy Cross bulletin (container 14/1225) lists Mass at three churches and names no St. David; the directory and Mass Finder list none.
