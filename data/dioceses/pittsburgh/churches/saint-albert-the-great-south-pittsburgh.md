## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 18 October 2026 Blessed Trinity bulletin (eCatholic 26316, read 2026-10-23) lists Saturday Vigil 4:00 pm and Sunday 8:00am and 11:00am at St. Albert, and its page-2 notice moves St. Sylvester's daily Mass intentions to "ST. ALBERT" on Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 26 October to 8 November. The diocese directory shows "Saturday Vigil 4:00 p.m. / Sunday 8:00 a.m. | 11:00 a.m.", the same. It is the parish office church.
