## Holds Mass

Occasional Mass only, no weekly or Sunday Mass (Rule 2). The directory says 'No Masses Scheduled' and the 25 October 2026 bulletin prints no Mass here. But the church is an active worship site: the 25 October 2026 masthead lists it with its address, the 25 January 2026 FAQ says St. Joseph and St. Rita 'remain worship sites' and that each church's feast day and its predecessor parish's founding anniversary will still be celebrated, the 17 May 2026 intentions page lists an 8:00 AM Mass at St. Rita on Friday 22 May 2026 (the feast of St. Rita of Cascia; image page, so it is quoted in a note rather than as a recheckable quote), and a snow make-up Mass was added at St. Rita on Sunday 15 February 2026 at 10 AM. Until 31 January 2026 it had a Sunday 10:00 AM Mass. Next expected Mass: the feast of St. Rita, 22 May 2027, unconfirmed.

## Name and address

**Street number disagrees**: the diocese directory, the 2026 bulletin mastheads (10 May and 25 October 2026) and the ParishesOnline org (st-rita-church-15120) say 219 West Schwab Avenue, Munhall 15120, while GCatholic (24379) says 229 W Schwab Ave. I used 219 (three sources, one of them the parish's own bulletin) and kept `address_verified` false because the bulletin and the directory are the parish's own channels and nothing independent confirms 219. Coordinates: GCatholic's Plus Code (87G2C424+2P, 40.4000625,-79.8931875), 60 m from the ParishesOnline org pin (40.400127,-79.892625) for 219 W Schwab; the directory gives no pin for this church. Name: 'St. Rita' (bulletin, GCatholic), 'Saint Rita Church (Munhall)' (directory); the predecessor parish was in 'Munhall Gardens/Whitaker' (predecessor list).
