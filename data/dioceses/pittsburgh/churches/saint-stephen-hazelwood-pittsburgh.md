## Holds Mass

Occasional public Mass only (Rules settled 2). The directory lists 'No Masses Scheduled' and the 25 October 2026 bulletin prints no Mass here, but the 6 September 2026 issue (container 14/1180, page 7) announces the annual dedication-anniversary Mass at Saint Stephen Church, Hazelwood, on Tuesday 15 September 2026 at 6:00 pm with 'All are welcome', and the 30 August 2025 decree closing St. Regis says parishioners worship 'at the nearby Saint Paul Cathedral, Saint Rosalia, and Saint Stephen church buildings' (read from the image, see excluded/saint-regis-oakland). Monthly samples from 2025-10 to 2026-10 found no other Mass here; the church is still on the bulletin's worship-site list. Whether the dedication Mass on 15 September actually happened was not checked (the bulletin of 20 September could say); the announcement is the evidence.
