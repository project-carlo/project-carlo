## Holds Mass

Yes, current and daily. The 18 October 2026 parish bulletin (read 2026-10-23, rendered) lists Sunday 8am and 11am and a weekday Mass every day of the week, the Institute's home page lists the same, and the diocese directory gives 'Sunday 8:00 a.m. (Low Mass) | 11:00 a.m. (High Mass)'.

## Name and address

The church is the former Risen Lord Church (originally St. Francis Xavier; GCatholic 24242 'Precious Blood of Jesus', 'originally St. Francis Xavier; merged 1993'), transferred in 2019 to this personal parish per the diocese's Risen Lord history page. Directory and Mass Finder: 'Most Precious Blood of Jesus Church (Brighton Heights)', 3250 California Avenue 15212; bulletin and Institute site: same address; GCatholic: 3250 California Ave. All agree. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; the directory pin is about 30 m away. The diocese's headings call the neighbourhood Brighton Heights, the history page 'North Side', and the predecessor list 'Marshall/Shadeland'; same building.
