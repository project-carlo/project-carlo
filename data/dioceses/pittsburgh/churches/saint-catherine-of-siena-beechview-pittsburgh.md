## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Saint Teresa of Kolkata bulletin (eCatholic 21158, read 2026-10-23) lists, with code SCS, Saturday vigil 4:00pm, Sunday 9:30am, 1:00pm (Spanish) and 6:00pm Portuguese Mass (the Portuguese Mass is on the 2nd and 4th Sundays per page 2 and the parish site), and weekday Mass at 8:45am Tuesday to Friday (Wednesday 28 Oct is also 8:45am). The parish site's Mass times agree. A published note moves the 8:45am Friday 13 November Mass to 7pm for the feast of St. Frances Xavier Cabrini.

## Name and address

Name: Saint Catherine of Siena in the bulletin, the parish site, the directory ("Saint Catherine of Siena Church (Beechview)") and GCatholic ("St. Catherine of Siena"). **Address disagreement:** the diocese directory, the Mass Finder and GCatholic give 1810 Belasco Avenue, Pittsburgh 15216; the bulletin's page-1 header and the parish site's address block give the church as 1909 Broadway Avenue, and the same site gives 1810 Belasco Avenue as the "MAIN OFFICE" (the bulletin also prints it as 'Parish Office'). I followed the parish, which labels each address, and wrote 1909 Broadway Avenue; the two are on the same block (Nominatim puts 1909 Broadway about 30 m from the diocese pin, GCatholic's Plus Code 86GXCX4F+P3 about 25 m). Coordinates are the diocese directory pin (40.40659, -80.02730), which falls on the site either way. address_verified stays false because the sources split on the street number; a county parcel record or the USPS address would settle it.
