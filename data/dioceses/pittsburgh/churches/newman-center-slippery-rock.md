## Holds Mass

Yes, current, but term-time only for the Sunday Mass. The 18 October 2026 issue of the St. Faustina Parish bulletin (container 14/1535, page 3, read 2026-10-23) lists Mass at the Newman Center on Sunday 7:00 PM (18 and 25 Oct) and Wednesday 7:00 PM (21 Oct), and page 2 lists Wednesday 5:30 PM confession there. The diocese directory gives "Sunday 7:00 p.m.* / *during the school year". Bulletin and parish page do not mark the Mass as closed to the public: it is in the parish's own public schedule, so under Rules settled 1 it is a church row of St. Faustina. The weekly issues are re-read, and the Sunday Mass lapses outside the Slippery Rock University school year.

## Name and address

Named "Newman Center" in the bulletin, on the parish Worship Locations page and in the diocese directory and Mass Finder ("Newman Center (Slippery Rock)"). Address 342 Normal Avenue, Slippery Rock, PA 16057 from the diocese directory/Mass Finder; the bulletin masthead gives the same address for the parish office, and OpenStreetMap has a church building at 342 Normal Avenue named "Rock Catholic Center", so the building has a second name locally (OSM). Coordinates are the diocese pin (41.0622825, -80.051615); OSM is 41.0623065, -80.0516550, about 5 m away. GCatholic has no entry for it.
