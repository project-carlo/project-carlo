## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-23), page 10, lists Saturday vigil 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am and 11:00 am, and weekday Masses Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8:00 am; page 6 intentions agree. The parish site and the diocese page give the same weekend times. Saint Joseph is the parish's administrative center.

The bulletin, parish site, diocese directory, Mass Finder and GCatholic all give 342 Dorseyville Road, ZIP 15215; the mailing city is Pittsburgh although the church is in O'Hara Township (the diocese's former parish list says 'St. Joseph, O'Hara Twp.'). Coordinates are the GCatholic Plus Code, agreeing with the Mass Finder pin to about 10 m.
