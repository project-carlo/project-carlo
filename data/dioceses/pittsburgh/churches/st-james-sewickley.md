## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 18 October 2026 Divine Redeemer bulletin (files.ecatholic.com/24435/bulletins/20261018.pdf, read 2026-10-23; cover page is an image, read from a render) lists Saturday vigil 4:30 pm and Sunday 8:00 am and 11:00 am at Saint James, plus weekday Masses (M/W/F 6:45 am, T/Th 9:00 am, Saturday 9:00 am), adoration and confessions.
