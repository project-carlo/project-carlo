## Name and address

The directory, Mass Finder, parish site and bulletin masthead give 310 32nd Street, McKeesport 15132; GCatholic (24362, listed as 'St. Perpetua', 'MeKeesport' typo) gives 312 32nd St., two door numbers off. The diocese history page says St. Perpetua was one of the three parishes merged into St. Patrick in 1993 and the 2023 press calls this church 'the former St. Perpetua', so GCatholic's entry is read as the same building; no source states the street number of the church itself (OSM returns a house at 310).
