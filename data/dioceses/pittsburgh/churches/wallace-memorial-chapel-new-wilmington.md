## Holds Mass

Yes, in term only. The 25 October 2026 Holy Spirit Parish bulletin (page 3) and hsplc.org/mass (both read 2026-10-23) list a Sunday 7:00pm Mass at Westminster College 'when classes are in session'. The parish's Instagram post of 30 August 2026 (seen only as a search snippet) names the Wallace Memorial Chapel, says 'All are welcome' and that the Mass starts that day, and the college's intranet page (also a snippet) says the Mass is in Wallace Memorial Chapel on Sundays at 7:00 PM with Fr. John Ferguson of Holy Spirit Parish. The college's public Campus Worship page has not been updated (it still says the day and time will be posted). Under the diocese rule 1 a campus Mass whose schedule a parish bulletin prints is a church row under that parish; 'All are welcome' supports public access. The bulletin's schedule is the only source that makes it a worship site of this parish, so the chapel name rests on the two snippets.

## Name and address

Name: the bulletin says only 'Westminster College'; the chapel name is from the Instagram and college snippets and OSM. Address: the college's mailing address is 319 South Market Street, New Wilmington, PA 16172 (college site); OSM puts Wallace Memorial Chapel on Maple Street with ZIP 16142. The row keeps the college address, with the OSM building's coordinates (about 350 m from the college's OSM centre point).
