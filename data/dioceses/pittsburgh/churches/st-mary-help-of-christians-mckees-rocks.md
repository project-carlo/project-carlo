## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (container 14/0723, read 2026-10-23) lists the church in the masthead and weekend Mass box, and page 2 announces the 11:00 AM Mass there for 1 November. The diocese directory gives 'Saturday Vigil 6:00 p.m. / Sunday 11:00 a.m.'. It is the surviving church of the former St. John of God parish in McKees Rocks (formed 1993).
