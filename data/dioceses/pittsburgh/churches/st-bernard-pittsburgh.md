## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Saint Michael the Archangel bulletin (ParishesOnline 14/1161, read 2026-10-23, page 1 rendered from an image) lists Masses at Saint Bernard on Sunday (7:00am, 9:00am, 11:00am and a 7:30pm Candlelight Mass), every weekday at 7:00am, Saturday 8:00am, and a First Friday 7:00pm Mass. This is the parish's main church and office.
