## Holds Mass

St. Anthony Chapel (a shrine, GCatholic type Shrine) holds a public Sunday 11:30 AM Mass and weekday 8:30 AM Masses Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday, printed on the cover and in the intentions table of the Shrines of Pittsburgh bulletin of 25 October 2026 (read 2026-10-23). The directory ('Sunday 11:30 a.m.') agrees on the Sunday Mass; the weekday Masses are in the bulletin only. The chapel is run by its own chairperson (Carole Brueckner) but is not a separate bulletin publisher.
