## Holds Mass

Yes, in term. The Sunday 5:00 pm Mass moved in August 2026 from the Newman Center on South College Street to the media room on the lower floor of the Rossin Campus Center, 60 South Lincoln Street (23 August 2026 issue, page 5, item "Masses at W & J College Resume!", read 2026-10-23). It is in the parish's own public Mass schedule (parish Masses page) and in the bulletin's Mass Intentions (25 October and 1 November 2026, page 7), so under Rules settled 1 it is a church row of this parish. The diocese directory (still under its old name) says "only when classes are in session, excluding summer sessions"; the 2025 issues printed it all summer (June to August), so the bulletin and diocese differ on the summer break and the weekly bulletins decide. The weekend schedule box on page 2 of the 25 October 2026 issue omits it but page 2 prints the 4:00 pm Penance and Adoration there. The chaplain is Rev. Alec P. Scheuer (diocese clergy list).

## Name and address

The diocese still calls it the Washington & Jefferson College Newman Center (no address); the bulletin since August 2026 names the venue the Rossin Campus Center (W&J), 60 South Lincoln Street, Washington, PA 15301 (page 7 legend), which matches OSM's Rossin Campus Center. `name_verified` stays false: the diocese name refers to the old Newman Center building (a ParishesOnline org 'newman-center-15301', 1 S College St, exists but publishes nothing), which the bulletin says no longer hosts Mass. The Mass Finder pin (40.165681, -80.2423998) is about 550 m from the OSM building and was not used.
