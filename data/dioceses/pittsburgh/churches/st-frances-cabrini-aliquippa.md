## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Mary, Queen of Saints bulletin (container 14/1396, page 1) lists St. Frances Cabrini, 115 Trinity Drive, Aliquippa, with the Saturday vigil at 6 PM and the Sunday Mass at 11 AM, and weekday Masses and confessions in the same box. The diocese directory gives the same weekend times (Saturday Vigil 6:00 p.m. / Sunday 11:00 a.m.). The parish's administrative office is at this address.

## Name and address

The bulletin (page 1 and the page 2 mailing address '115 TRINITY DRIVE, ALIQUIPPA, PA, 15001'), the diocese directory and Mass Finder, and GCatholic ('115 Trinity Dr.', 1961, dedicated 1964-07-19) agree on name and address. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code pin (40.6466875, -80.2944375); the Mass Finder pin (40.646746, -80.294279) is about 15 m away.
