## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Saint Matthew Parish bulletin (container 14/1151, read 2026-10-23), page 3: Saturday vigil 4:00 pm, Sunday 10:00 am, and weekday Mass 8:00 am Monday and Wednesday. The diocese directory gives 'Saturday Vigil 4:00 p.m. / Sunday 10:00 a.m.'. The weekly calendar's 'SB Chapel' (Eucharistic adoration, novenas, confessions) is the adoration chapel on this campus.

Coordinates are the GCatholic Plus Code position; the Mass Finder pin is 40.5330406, -79.9690159 (about 20 m away). This is a different building from St. Mary of the Assumption (2510 Middle Road), the other Glenshaw church, which belongs to Our Lady of Perpetual Help.
