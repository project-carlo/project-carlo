## Holds Mass

Yes, current. This is the lead from the survey (the Mass Finder lists Holy Redeemer and the batch note said the parish pages did not; in fact the diocese directory lists it under Divine Grace too, https://diopitt.org/divine-grace-parish, "Saturday Vigil 5:00 p.m. / Sunday 9:00 a.m. | 11:00 a.m."). The 25 October 2026 Divine Grace Parish bulletin (container 14/1741) names Holy Redeemer Church on its masthead and prints Masses here in its page 2 intentions: Sat 24 Oct 5:00 pm; Sun 25 Oct 9:00 am and 11:00 am; Mon-Sat 9:00 am; Wed 28 Oct 7:00 pm; Sat 31 Oct 9:00 am and 5:00 pm; Sun 1 Nov 9:00 and 11:00 am. The parish Mass Times page (read 2026-10-23) agrees. It still holds public Mass.

## Name and address

The name is the same in the bulletin, parish site, diocese directory and Mass Finder. Address disagreement: the bulletin masthead, parish site, diocese directory ("415 Fourth Street") and Mass Finder ("415 4th Street") say 415 4th Street, Ellwood City 16117; GCatholic says 300 Crescent Ave., Ellwood City 16117 (history "merged 2000"). Web directories list 300 Crescent Ave #1 as the Holy Redeemer parish office, so GCatholic probably recorded the rectory/office. Followed the bulletin and diocese (415 4th Street); address_verified is left false because GCatholic differs and the office address has not been confirmed on the parish site. Coordinates are the Mass Finder pin (diocese-directory), about 20 m from OSM's Holy Redeemer Church node on Cherry Way and about 250 m from GCatholic's Crescent Ave pin.
