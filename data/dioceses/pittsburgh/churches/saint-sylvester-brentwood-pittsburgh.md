## Holds Mass

Yes, current, with a dated temporary pause. The 18 October 2026 Blessed Trinity bulletin (eCatholic 26316, read 2026-10-23) lists Saturday Vigil 4:00pm and Sunday 10:00am at St. Sylvester and weekday Masses there (Monday 19 October 9:00 AM). Its page 2 announces "No Masses at St. Sylvester's Church Building DUE TO PARKING LOT RECONSTRUCTION" from Monday 26 October through Sunday 8 November 2026 and moves those Masses to St. Albert, St. Basil and Holy Angels. Because the notice names an end date and a cause (a parking lot, not the building) this is a temporary suspension, not a closure; the church stays a worship site. The diocese directory ("Saturday Vigil 4:00 p.m. / Sunday 10:00 a.m") predates it. Check the 8 November bulletin that Masses resume.
