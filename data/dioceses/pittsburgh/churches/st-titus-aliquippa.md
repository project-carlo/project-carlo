## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Mary, Queen of Saints bulletin (container 14/1396, page 1) lists St. Titus, 952 Franklin Avenue, Aliquippa, with one Sunday Mass at 10 AM (no Saturday vigil) and weekday Mass and confession times. The diocese directory gives the same (Sunday 10:00 a.m.).

## Name and address

The bulletin (page 1), the diocese directory and Mass Finder, and GCatholic ('952 Franklin Ave.', merged with St. Joseph 1994) agree on name and address. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code pin (40.6128125, -80.2558125); the Mass Finder pin (40.611879, -80.253586) is about 214 m away.

The Mass Finder pin is the outlier. Nominatim (read 2026-10-23, query '952 Franklin Avenue Aliquippa PA') returns an OSM address node for 952 Franklin Avenue, Woodlawn, Aliquippa at 40.612778, -80.255813, which is within about 1 m of GCatholic's pin, so GCatholic's pin was kept.
