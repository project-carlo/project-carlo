## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Saint Mark the Evangelist Parish bulletin (container 14/1775, page 1) prints the Mass times for St. Kilian: Sat 4:00pm and 5:30pm; Sun 8:00am, 10:00am, 12:00pm and 5:00pm; Mon-Fri 7:00am. Page 2 reports attendance for the 17-18 October weekend at all six weekend Masses (468, 423, 351, 487, 322, 518). The parish Mass Times page and the diocese directory agree. This is the larger of the parish's two churches and holds its parish office.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("7076 Franklin Rd., Cranberry Township"), the diocese directory and Mass Finder, the bulletin and the parish site all give 7076 Franklin Road, Cranberry Township 16066. Coordinates: GCatholic's Plus Code pin (40.68419,-80.07444, gcatholic-plus-code) was kept; the Mass Finder pin is 40.68403,-80.07062 (about 330 m east), ParishesOnline's org pin 40.68335,-80.07573 (about 130 m west) and Nominatim's address point 40.68151,-80.07408 (about 300 m south), so the pin is within a few hundred metres but not confirmed on the building; geocode/verify may refine it.
