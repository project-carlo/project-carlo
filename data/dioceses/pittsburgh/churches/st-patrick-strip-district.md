## Holds Mass

St. Patrick (Strip District) holds a weekly Thursday 12:10 PM Mass. The diocese directory lists it 'Sunday Masses at Saint Stanislaus Church' (no weekend Mass here), and the weekend Masses are indeed all at St. Stanislaus; but the Shrines of Pittsburgh bulletin prints 'Daily Mass: Thursday 12:10 pm' on the cover and a Thursday 12:10 PM intention in every issue checked (23 Aug-25 Oct 2026, read 2026-10-23). Per Rules settled 2 the bulletin decides: church row, not an exclusion. The Mass Finder lists no pin for St. Patrick (the survey found none), so the directory entry is its only roster link.

The bulletin cover column writes '1711 Penn Ave', which disagrees on the street; Liberty Avenue is followed because page 7 and GCatholic say it and the directory's cross street fits; address_verified stays false while the cover disagrees (README).
