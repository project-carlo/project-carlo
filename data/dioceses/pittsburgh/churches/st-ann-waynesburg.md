## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 St. Matthias bulletin (read 2026-10-23) prints the weekend Masses Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 8:30 am at St. Ann in its page-3 Mass schedule (an image) and a Wednesday 6:30 pm Mass, and the parish site's Mass Times page agrees. A 2025 restoration of the church is mentioned (the university Mass moved to a campus chapel meanwhile) but weekend parish Masses are still printed here.

## Name and address

The name (St. Ann, Saint Ann Church (Waynesburg)) and the address 232 E. High Street, Waynesburg agree across the bulletin, the parish site, the diocese directory, the Mass Finder and GCatholic 24259 (232 E High St., 15370). The bulletin's 'Wayneburg PA 15320' is a typo; 15370 is Waynesburg's ZIP in four other sources. Coordinates are the Mass Finder/directory pin (39.8961224, -80.1836269), about 20 m from GCatholic's Plus Code (39.89631, -80.18369).
