## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 18 October 2026 Saint Paul of the Cross bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/1799, read 2026-10-23, page 1) gives Saint Winifred a Saturday vigil at 5:30 PM and Sunday Mass at 11:30 AM, with confession Saturday 4:45-5:15 PM; the Mass-intentions list marks them "(St. W)". The diocese directory gives the same times, with the Sunday 11:30 Mass annotated "(Religious Ed Mass, all are welcome)", so it is public. Weekend Masses only; no weekday Mass at this church appears in the bulletin.

## Name and address

Name: the diocese directory calls it "Saint Winifred Chapel (Mount Lebanon)"; the bulletin ("Saint Winifred Church"), the Mass Finder ("St. Winifred Church (Mount Lebanon)") and GCatholic ("St. Winifred") do not say chapel. I followed the bulletin and wrote Church; the directory's "Chapel" is the only dissent. Address: 550 Sleepy Hollow Road, Pittsburgh 15228 in the bulletin, directory, Mass Finder and GCatholic (550 Sleepy Hollow Rd.), all agree. Coordinates are the diocese directory pin 40.37546, -80.02335 (Mass Finder 40.37546, -80.02335); GCatholic's Plus Code 86GX9XGG+58 is about 70 m west. Same building.
