## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Divine Grace Parish bulletin (container 14/1741, page 2 Mass Intentions) lists Masses here: Sat 5:00 pm; Sun 8:00 am, 10:00 am and 6:00 pm; weekdays 8:00 am (Mon-Thu per the Mass Times page). The parish Mass Times page (read 2026-10-23) and the diocese directory give the same weekend times. Published weekly, so re-read each issue.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("2 W Beaver St., Zelienople"), the diocese directory and Mass Finder ("2 West Beaver Street") and the bulletin masthead and parish site agree. OpenStreetMap places the church on Pine Street, same block. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code pin (gcatholic-plus-code), about 60 m from the OSM node. Predecessor note: the diocese says the Evans City mission church St. Matthias was absorbed by this parish in 2016; see open/st-matthias-evans-city.
