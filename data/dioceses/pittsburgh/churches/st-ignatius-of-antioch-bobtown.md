## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 St. Matthias bulletin (read 2026-10-23) prints Sunday 11:30 am and Thursday 9:00 am Mass at St. Ignatius, Bobtown; the parish site's Mass Times page agrees.

## Name and address

The name agrees everywhere. The street address does not, in four ways: the diocese directory and Mass Finder say 305 Grant Street, Bobtown 15315 (pin 39.7596067, -79.9841195); the 25 October 2026 bulletin says 129 Franklin Ave. Bobtown 15315; the parish site and history page say 'Bobtown Hill Road, Bobtown'; GCatholic 24300 gives only 'Bobtown, 15315' with a Plus Code pin at 39.76094, -79.98094, about 330 m north-east of the directory pin. I kept the directory address and pin as the Mass Finder is the diocese's own map, but I could not tell which is the building (street number or road name may be an old or alternate address; that is a guess). address_verified stays false. What would settle it: a street-level look at the pin, or the parish office confirming the church address.
