## Holds Mass

Yes, current. Page 2 of the 18 October 2026 Guardian Angels bulletin (container 14/0710, read 2026-10-23) lists Saturday 4:00pm, Sunday 8:00am and 11:00am and a weekday morning Mass at MBS, with intentions for 17-25 October. The diocese directory gives 'Saturday Vigil 4:00 p.m. / Sunday 8:00 a.m. | 11:00 a.m.'. ## Name and address

The bulletin ('Most Blessed Sacrament, 800 Montana Ave, Natrona Heights PA 15065'; the parish office is Suite 2 at the same address), the diocese directory and Mass Finder ('Most Blessed Sacrament Church', 800 Montana Avenue, 15065) and GCatholic 24234 (800 Montana Ave., named 'Blessed Sacrament') agree on the address; GCatholic shortens the name. The directory's 'Natrona Heights' is the Harrison Township postal town used by every source. Coordinates are the GCatholic Plus Code (87G2J79F+X7); the Mass Finder pin is about 40 m away.
