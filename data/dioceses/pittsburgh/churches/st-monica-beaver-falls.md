## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 St. Augustine bulletin (container 14/1198, page 1 and the page 10 intentions) and the parish Mass Times page list Sat vigil 4:00 p.m., Sun 7:30, 9:00 and 11:00 a.m. (the 9:00 a.m. is broadcast on 99.3FM and 1230AM) and weekdays Monday to Friday 9:00 a.m. The parish's main office is here.

## Name and address

Bulletin masthead, parish site, diocese directory/Mass Finder and GCatholic agree on 116 Thorndale Drive, Beaver Falls 15010 (Chippewa Township). The directory calls it 'Saint Monica Church (Chippewa)'. It is the former Christ the Divine Teacher church (diocese history page, quoted in the evidence). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code pin; the Mass Finder pin is within about 25 m. The diocese's church list links this church to a dead page; /saintaugustineparish is live.
