## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Divine Mercy Parish bulletin (container 14/1839, read 2026-10-23 from the rendered image pages) lists Epiphany weekday Mass Monday to Friday 12PM, Saturday 7:30AM (first-Saturday Marian Mass) and Sunday 10AM (ad orientem), and its intentions table has an EP Mass at each of those times from 24 October to 1 November. The diocese directory gives 'Sunday 10:00 a.m.'. The bulletin's weekday and Saturday Masses are not in the directory.

## Name and address

Name 'Epiphany' in the bulletin and GCatholic; 'Epiphany Church (Uptown)' in the directory and Mass Finder. Address disagreement: the bulletin (cover and page 2 letterhead) and GCatholic say **164** Washington Pl.; the diocese directory, the Mass Finder and the ParishesOnline org record say **184** Washington Place. All agree on the street, ZIP 15219 and, by coordinates, on the building (diocese pin, GCatholic Plus Code 87G2C2Q5+XJ and OSM all within about 25 m). I kept 164, the parish's own letterhead, and left address_verified false because the sources split two against two. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code. A look at the building's number or the postal record would settle it.
