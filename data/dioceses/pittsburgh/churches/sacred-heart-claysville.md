## Holds Mass

Yes, occasionally (Rules settled 2: the directory's 'No Masses Scheduled' is not a closure). The Saint James parish's bulletin calendar prints Mass here on Friday 12 June 2026 at 6:30 pm (Feast of the Sacred Heart; 7 June 2026 issue, page 8) and on Friday 25 September 2026 at 6:30 pm (anniversary of the dedication in 1875; 20 September 2026 issue, page 8), both read 2026-10-23. The church is also still in the bulletin's 'Our Churches' list (25 Oct 2026) and the 2026 financial pages report a Sacred Heart campus. The weekly Sunday 9:00 am Mass ran through the 28 December 2025 issue and the weekend schedule of 4 January 2026 onward no longer lists it, so what is left is a few annual Masses (feast day, dedication anniversary, probably more: the Mass Intentions pages are images and were not all read). The diocese directory's 'No Masses Scheduled' is consistent with that.

## Name and address

Sacred Heart, Claysville. Bulletin (page 2, 'Our Churches'): 1280 US 40, Claysville, PA 15323. Diocese directory: 'Main Street', Claysville 15323 with no coordinates. OpenStreetMap has Sacred Heart Church on Bell Avenue and puts '1280 National Road' (the same road as US 40) within 30 m of it, so these are one building under two street names; I used the bulletin's number and the OSM building's coordinates (40.1159337, -80.4155793). GCatholic has no entry for Claysville (the roster has none).
