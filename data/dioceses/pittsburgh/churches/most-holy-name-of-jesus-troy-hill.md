## Holds Mass

Most Holy Name of Jesus (Troy Hill) holds a Sunday 9:00 AM Mass, printed on the cover and in the intentions table of the Shrines of Pittsburgh bulletin of 25 October 2026 (read 2026-10-23). The diocese directory lists 'Sunday 9:00 a.m.' and no vigil. Neither a vigil nor a weekday Mass at this church appears in the intentions of that issue.
