## Holds Mass

Daily public Mass. The Oratory's own weekly bulletin for 25 October 2026 (page 2, read 2026-10-23) lists Sunday 9:00am, 11:00am, 4:00pm and 9:00pm; Monday to Thursday 7:00am and 5:15pm (and 9:00pm in the academic year); Friday 7:00am and 5:15pm; Saturday 10:00am and 4:00pm. The same times are on https://pittsburghoratory.org/liturgy ('All are welcome at any Mass'). Only the Sunday 11:00am Mass is held elsewhere (Heinz Chapel, see churches/heinz-memorial-chapel-pittsburgh); the rest are in the Oratory Chapel. Rules settled 1 applies: the Oratory's bulletin is its own publisher row (parishes/pittsburgh-oratory).

## Name and address

GCatholic names it 'St. Philip Neri' (kind Pittsburgh Oratory); the bulletin cover and site say 'The Pittsburgh Oratory of Saint Philip Neri, Catholic Newman Center'; ParishesOnline says 'Catholic Newman Center'. The row uses the bulletin's name with GCatholic's saint, so the name flag stays false (three names, no single agreed one). The address, 4450 Bayard Street, Pittsburgh 15213, is the same in GCatholic, the LPi org and the bulletin footer, hence verified. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (40.44869, -79.95206); the LPi pin (40.448522, -79.952135) is about 20 m away, so they agree.
