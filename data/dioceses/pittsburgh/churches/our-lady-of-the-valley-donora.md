## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (container 14/1146, read 2026-10-23) lists Saturday 4:00pm and Sunday 7:30am Mass at Donora (code DON on page 2), weekday Masses Tuesday and Thursday 8:30am, and Baptism preparation Saturdays 3:00pm. The directory gives the same weekend times.

## Name and address

The name agrees across the directory, Mass Finder and GCatholic. The address does not quite: the parish site and bulletin give the Donora site as 1 Park Manor Road (the bulletin prints '1 Park Manor'), GCatholic '1 Park Road', and the directory and Mass Finder 'Second Street Extension & Park Manor Rd' (no number). I used '1 Park Manor Road' from the parish, which reads as the same place; because GCatholic's number differs and OSM puts a church of the same name on Thompson Avenue 1.4 km east, `address_verified` stays false. Coordinates are the Mass Finder pin; the GCatholic Plus Code is 350 m from it. Settle by looking at the building on a map or the parish's Mass-times page.
