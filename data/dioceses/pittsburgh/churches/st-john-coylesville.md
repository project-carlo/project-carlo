## Name and address

GCatholic, the diocese's parish page and Mass Finder, and the parish's own site and bulletin agree on the name and street address. GCatholic lists the same building twice, as 24308 'St. John' (668 Clearfield Rd., Fenelton) and 83946 'St. John the Evangelist' (Coyleville, no street, same coordinates to 0.0 km; both carry the website of the former St. John the Evangelist parish): treated as one church, both roster ids linked. 'Coylesville' is the hamlet name the diocese uses; the postal city is Fenelton. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code pin (coordinates_from gcatholic-plus-code), within about 150 m of the Mass Finder pin.
