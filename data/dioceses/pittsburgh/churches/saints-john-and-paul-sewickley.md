## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The parish home page (saintluke.net, read 2026-10-23) lists Saturday 5:00 PM, Sunday 7:30 AM, 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM at Saints John & Paul, plus weekday Masses in the church and in its Grotto Chapel. The Grotto Chapel is on this campus ('Livestream from the Grotto Chapel at Saints John & Paul Church', a member of Saint Luke the Evangelist Parish, per the parish's YouTube listing in a DuckDuckGo result), so its Masses are this church row, not a separate place. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (container 14/1208, image pages) announces the All Souls Memorial Mass 'Monday, November 2, 2026, at 7:00 PM at Saints John & Paul Church' and a Confirmation at '2586 Wexford Bayne Road Sewickley, PA 15143' on 1 November. The diocese directory gives the same weekend times.
