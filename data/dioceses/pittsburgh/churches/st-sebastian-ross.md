## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish bulletin (https://files.ecatholic.com/21944/bulletins/20261025.pdf, page 4 (Mass Schedule), read 2026-10-23) schedules Mass here: Sunday 8:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m., Monday-Friday 12 noon. The diocese parish page gives the same weekend times ('Sunday 8:00 a.m. | 10:00 a.m.') and the Mass Finder lists the church.

## Name and address

GCatholic, the diocese parish page, the Mass Finder and the bulletin agree on the name and street address; page 5 lists 'Saint Sebastian Church, 311 Siebert Road, Pittsburgh, PA 15237' (Holy Cross Academy is next door at 307 Siebert Road). Its Saint Sebastian Chapel (weekday noon Mass) is on the same campus and is not a separate row. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code pin (40.5301875, -80.0114375); the Mass Finder pin (40.529402, -80.011221) is about 89 m away. All sources give postal city Pittsburgh; the directory adds the municipality in the name.
