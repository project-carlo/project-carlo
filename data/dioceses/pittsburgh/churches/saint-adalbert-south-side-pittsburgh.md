## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Mary, Queen of Peace bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/0020, read 2026-10-23) lists confession "Every Saturday @ St. Adalbert" 3:00-3:45 p.m. and, in its Mass-intentions list, Masses coded (A) at 4:00pm on Saturday (livestreamed) and 9:00am on Sunday, plus 10:00am entries on weekday dates (the text layer detaches the date column from the entries, so which weekday goes with which church is not recoverable). The diocese directory and Mass Finder give the same weekend times, "Saturday Vigil 4:00 p.m. / Sunday 9:00 a.m.". The (A)/(M) code key is inferred (A = St. Adalbert, M = St. Mary of the Mount) from the confession lines that name the two churches; the bulletin text layer does not spell it out.

## Name and address

Name: the bulletin prints "St. Adalbert", the diocese directory "Saint Adalbert Church (South Side)", GCatholic "St. Adalbert". Address 160 South 15th Street, Pittsburgh 15203: the directory and Mass Finder give "160 South 15th Street", GCatholic "160 S 15th St."; they agree. The parish office is a separate address (81 S. 13th Street, per the ParishesOnline org).
