## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Mary, Queen of Saints bulletin (container 14/1396, page 1) lists St. John the Baptist, 1409 Pennsylvania Avenue, Monaca, with one Sunday Mass at 8 AM (no Saturday vigil) and a weekday Mass at 8 AM. The diocese directory gives the same (Sunday 8:00 a.m.).

## Name and address

The bulletin (page 1), the diocese directory and Mass Finder, GCatholic ('1409 Pennsylvania Ave.', ded. 1913 and 1932) and the ParishesOnline org address (1409 Pennsylvania Ave, Monaca, PA, 15061) agree on name and address. GCatholic's separate 'St. John the Baptist, Baden' (24314) is a different, closed church. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code pin (40.6888125, -80.2739375); the Mass Finder pin (40.689142, -80.273884) is about 37 m away.
