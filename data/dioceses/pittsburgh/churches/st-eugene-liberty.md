## Holds Mass

Yes, current. Saturday vigil 6:00 pm (StE) in the Mass Schedule on page 1 of the 25 October 2026 bulletin (container 14/0524); the intentions list shows 'StE 6:00 pm' on both Saturdays. Page 1 of the announcements adds Midnight Mass at St. Eugene at Christmas. The Church Hall is under construction (announcements), not the church. The diocese directory gives 'Saturday Vigil 6:00 p.m.'. Liberty is the borough; the postal city is McKeesport in every source.

## Name and address

Four sources agree on the street address and the name: the 25 October 2026 parish bulletin (page 1 masthead, read 2026-10-23), the diocese directory ('Saint Eugene Church (Liberty)'), the Mass Finder and GCatholic. GCatholic's Plus Code position is within 100 m of the diocese directory's pin (34, 90, 30, 2, 57 and 29 m for the six churches of this batch), and that Plus Code is used for the coordinates. Postal city and 'Church' suffix differ only as noted above. The parish bulletin and the parish site are one source; GCatholic and the diocese are two others.
