## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Mary, Queen of Saints bulletin (container 14/1396, page 1) lists Our Lady of Fatima, 2270 Brodhead Road, Aliquippa, with the Saturday vigil at 4 PM and the Sunday Mass at 9 AM, plus weekday Masses and confessions. The diocese directory gives the same (Saturday Vigil 4:00 p.m. / Sunday 9:00 a.m.).

## Name and address

The bulletin (page 1), the diocese directory and Mass Finder, and GCatholic ('2270 Brodhead Rd.', 1954) agree on name and address. The directory calls it 'Our Lady of Fatima Church (Hopewell)', the Mass Finder 'Hopewell Twp.'; the postal town is Aliquippa. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code pin (40.5849375, -80.2578125); the Mass Finder pin (40.584872, -80.258529) is about 61 m away.
