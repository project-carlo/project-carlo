## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 issue of the Saints Francis & Clare Parish bulletin (John Patrick Publishing, page 4 'Weekly Mass Intentions', an image page read rendered 2026-10-23) lists Masses at this church: Saturday vigil 4:00 PM; Sunday 7:00 AM and 10:30 AM; weekday Tue 8:00 AM, Thu 8:00 AM. The 9 August 2026 issue (text layer) lists it in the same intentions page. The diocese directory (https://diopitt.org/saints-francis-and-clare-parish, read 2026-10-23) gives the same weekend times. Published weekly, so the schedule is re-read each issue.

## Name and address

GCatholic, the diocese's parish page and Mass Finder, and the parish's own site and bulletin agree on the name and street address. Postal city: the parish site, bulletin and diocese say Butler, PA 16002; GCatholic says Carbon Center (the hamlet). The 9 August bulletin mentions a parish picnic here and the parish site a Man of the Shroud exhibit in St. Wendelin Hall. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code pin (coordinates_from gcatholic-plus-code), within about 150 m of the Mass Finder pin.
