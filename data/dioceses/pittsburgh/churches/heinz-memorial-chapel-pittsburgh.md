## Holds Mass

A university chapel, not a Catholic church, where the Oratory (the Catholic Newman Center for Pitt) celebrates its Sunday 11:00 am Mass during the academic year. The Oratory's bulletin for 25 October 2026 (page 2) says 'During the academic year, the 11:00am Mass on Sunday is celebrated at Heinz Chapel on the University of Pittsburgh campus, not in the Oratory Chapel' and lists '11:00am (Heinz Chapel) Newman Center Students'; the Oratory site repeats it. The schedule says 'All are welcome at any Mass', so it counts as public under Rules settled 1 (in-term Masses count). It is a separate place from the Oratory Chapel (about 0.4 km away), so it has its own row under the Oratory's publisher row; it has no Mass outside the academic year. Whether the Mass is wanted as its own map point is the orchestrator's call; the alternative is to fold it into the Oratory church row.

## Name and address

Only one Catholic source (the Oratory) names it, and it says 'Heinz Chapel'; OSM and Wikipedia call it Heinz Memorial Chapel. No street number was found on the university's page (heinzchapel.pitt.edu answered 200 but the address was not in the text read), so line1 gives the intersection from Wikipedia; the postcode 15213 and coordinates come from Nominatim/OSM.
