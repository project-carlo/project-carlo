## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 18 October 2026 Most Sacred Heart of Jesus bulletin (container 14/1171, read 2026-10-23), page 3, lists Mass at St. Margaret Mary on Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 9:30 AM and weekday mornings (8:30 AM Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri); the diocese directory gives Sat 4:00 p.m., Sun 9:30 a.m. This is also the parish's administrative office (One Parish Place). The page 3 heading is jumbled in the text layer (it runs 'Eucharistic Adoration Schedule' over the Mass list); the times match the directory's weekend Masses.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the diocese directory ('One Parish Place') and GCatholic 24335 ('1 Parish Pl.') agree on the address, Moon Township, PA 15108 (the Mass Finder files the city as Coraopolis). Coordinates disagree: GCatholic's Plus Code (40.51531, -80.21731) is about 4.6 km west of the other sources, so it was not used. Followed the Mass Finder pin (40.51840, -80.16672), which sits within 350 m of the ParishesOnline org's coordinates (40.52167, -80.16446).
