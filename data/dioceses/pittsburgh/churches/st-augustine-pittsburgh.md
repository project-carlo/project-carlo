## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 bulletin of Saint Pio of Pietrelcina Parish (container 14/0730, read 2026-10-23 from rendered images) prints the parish's only Mass schedule on page 3: Saturday 4:00 pm; Sunday 8:00, 9:30 and 11:30 am; weekday Masses at 6:30 am and 11:30 am (Monday to Friday; Saturday 11:30 am) with 6:30 pm Masses on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. The cover names Saint Augustine Church as the parish's church. The diocese's page gives the same weekend times (Saturday 4:00 p.m.; Sunday 8:00, 9:30, 11:30 a.m.). The bulletin prints no church name beside the times, so that these are all Saint Augustine's rests on the cover (one church named) and the directory (one church listed).

## Name and address

Name: bulletin cover ('Saint Augustine Church'), the diocese directory and Mass Finder ('Saint Augustine Church (Lawrenceville)') and GCatholic ('St. Augustine') agree. Address: the bulletin cover, directory and Mass Finder say 234 37th Street; GCatholic and the ParishesOnline org say 225 37th Street, which the bulletin cover gives as the Parish Office. The two numbers are the same block (GCatholic's pin 40.46531,-79.96381; the directory pin 40.46522,-79.96393; ParishesOnline 40.46517,-79.96401: within about 25 m). I followed the bulletin's 234 for the church and kept the GCatholic Plus Code coordinates; address_verified stays false because one source set says 234 and another 225.
