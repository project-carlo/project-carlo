## Holds Mass

Occasional Mass only, no weekly or Sunday Mass (Rule 2: an occasional Mass found in the bulletin makes it a church row). The diocese directory says 'No Masses Scheduled' and the 25 October 2026 bulletin prints no Mass here. But the church is an active worship site: the 25 October 2026 masthead lists it, the 25 January 2026 FAQ says it 'remain[s] [a] worship site' with the feast-day and predecessor-anniversary Masses continuing, the 15 March 2026 intentions list the 8:00 AM Feast of St. Joseph Mass on 19 March 2026 (quoted above), and the 1 February 2026 issue added a one-weekend Mass here on Sunday 8 February 2026 8:30 AM. Until 31 January 2026 it had a Sunday 8:30 AM Mass (4 January 2026 issue: '8:30 AM St. Joseph (AN) Anna Basser'). Next expected Mass: the feast of St. Joseph, 19 March 2027, unconfirmed.

## Name and address

Directory and GCatholic (24321, '817 W Grant Ave.') agree on 817 West Grant Avenue, Duquesne 15110; the bulletin prints '817 Grant Avenue, Duquesne'. The directory pin is empty (not in the Mass Finder, which omits no-Mass churches), so coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G294CW+JM). Two other Duquesne ParishesOnline orgs (christ-the-light-of-the-world..., st-joseph-church-15110) give '32 S 1st Street', which is the Holy Name church and rectory (closed 2023), not this building.
