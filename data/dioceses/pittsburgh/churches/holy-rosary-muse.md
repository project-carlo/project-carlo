## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Saint Oscar Romero bulletin (container 14/1837, read 2026-10-23) lists Sunday 8:30am and Tuesday and Thursday 8:00am Mass at Holy Rosary (HR on page 2). The parish site's Mass Times page gives the same.

## Name and address

The name agrees across the bulletin, the parish site, the directory and GCatholic. The street address does not: the bulletin and the parish site say 246 Muse Bishop Road, and OpenStreetMap's Holy Rosary Catholic Church carries the same number; the diocese directory, the Mass Finder and GCatholic say 1 Orchard Street (possibly the building's older mailing address on the street beside it; that is my guess, not read anywhere). I followed the parish and OSM because the Mass Finder pin (40.29507,-80.20106) falls on the OSM building. `address_verified` stays false because of the disagreement. Coordinates are the Mass Finder pin; GCatholic's Plus Code is about 100 m away.
