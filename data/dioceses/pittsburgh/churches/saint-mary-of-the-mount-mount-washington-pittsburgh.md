## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Mary, Queen of Peace bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/0020, read 2026-10-23) lists confession "Every Thursday @ St. Mary" 6:00-6:45 p.m., adoration at St. Mary on the third Thursday, and Mass-intentions entries coded (M) at 5:30pm on Saturday and 11:00am on Sunday, plus 10:00am entries on weekday dates (the text layer detaches the date column, so which weekday goes with which church is not recoverable). Page 10 says the 24 Hour Chapel at St. Mary of the Mount is locked outside of Mass times. The diocese directory gives "Saturday Vigil 5:30 p.m. / Sunday 11:00 a.m. (English and American Sign Language)", the same times. The (A)/(M) key is inferred from the confession lines that name the two churches.
