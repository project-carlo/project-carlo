## Holds Mass

Yes, current: the Saint Katharine Drexel parish church ('Bentleyville Main church'). Saturday Vigil 4:00 pm and weekday Mass Mon/Fri 9:00 am in the Drexel Hall chapel next door (25 October 2026 bulletin page 2 and page 7 intentions, read 2026-10-23). The Drexel Hall chapel is a chapel on the same 126 Church Street campus (the bulletin's Bentleyville Campus: Church/Admin. Center/Drexel Hall); it is not a separate address, so it has no row of its own. First-Friday adoration 9:30 am to noon is in the Main church.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it 'Bentleyville Main church'; the diocese directory, Mass Finder and GCatholic call it Ave Maria (the pre-merger parish), 126 Church Street, Bentleyville, PA 15314. Three sources agree on the address. Coordinates are the Mass Finder pin (40.1123869, -80.0143001); GCatholic's Plus Code is about 30 m away.
