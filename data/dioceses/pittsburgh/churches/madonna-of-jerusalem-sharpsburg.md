## Holds Mass

Yes, current, weekdays only. The directory's 'No Weekend Masses Scheduled' is true for weekends, but the 18 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-23) schedules Mass here Tuesday and Thursday 9:00 am (page 10), with intentions for 20 and 22 October on page 6, and the parish site lists the same. Settled under the rule that the bulletin decides a 'No Masses Scheduled' church; it is a church row, not an exclusion.
