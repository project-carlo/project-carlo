## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin (eCatholic file 20261018.pdf, read 2026-10-23) prints 'Sunday 11 am' as its Mass Schedule and lists the 11 am Masses of 18 and 25 October in the intentions; the diocese directory also gives 'Sunday: 11:00 a.m.'. The 25 October issue (Discover Mass mirror) has the same box. No weekday or Saturday Mass is printed.

## Name and address

Name: bulletin and site 'St. Benedict the Moor Parish' (the church is the parish's only building), directory 'Saint Benedict the Moor Church (Hill District)', Mass Finder 'St. Benedict the Moor Church (Hill District)', GCatholic 'St. Benedict the Moor' (1977). Address disagreement: the bulletin, the parish site footer, the LPi account and GCatholic give 91 Crawford Street, Pittsburgh 15219; the diocese directory and Mass Finder give 1500 Centre Avenue, 15219. Both pins sit within about 20 m of each other (directory 40.441343, -79.985906; GCatholic Plus Code 87G2C2R7+FM = 40.44119, -79.98581), so they describe one building at the Crawford/Centre corner. I followed 91 Crawford (the parish's own mailing and bulletin address, matching GCatholic) and left `address_verified` false because the diocese's own list differs; a county parcel or OSM check would settle it. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
