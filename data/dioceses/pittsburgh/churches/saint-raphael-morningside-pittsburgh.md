## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 25 October 2026 Saint Jude Parish bulletin (container 14/1148, read 2026-10-23; text layer) prints one Mass & Confession Schedule on page 1: Saturday vigil 4:00pm St. Raphael; Sunday 9:00am St. Raphael, 9:00am Sacred Heart, 11:00am St. Raphael, 11:30am Sacred Heart; weekdays Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 9:00am St. Raphael and 12:00pm Sacred Heart; Wednesday 12:00pm Sacred Heart and 7:00pm St. Raphael. Saint Raphael has the Saturday vigil, two Sunday Masses, weekday morning Masses and a Wednesday evening Mass followed by confession and adoration.
