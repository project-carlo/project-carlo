## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (Discover Mass, read 2026-10-23) lists the Saturday vigil at 4:00 pm at St. James; the diocese directory ('Saturday Vigil 4:00 p.m.') and the parish home page agree. It is the parish's only Saturday-evening Mass. The 'St. James Chapel' named in the events column is part of this campus (the Dec. 5 Fair Trade Fair is 'in the St. James Chapel' after the 4 pm Mass) and has no separate Mass.

## Name and address

Name: bulletin 'St. James Church', directory 'Saint James Church (Wilkinsburg)', Mass Finder 'St. James Church (Wilkinsburg)', GCatholic 'St. James' (founded 1869). Street and ZIP agree everywhere: 718 Franklin Avenue, 15221. The municipality differs: the bulletin masthead prints 'Wilkinsburg PA 15221', while the directory, Mass Finder and GCatholic say 'Pittsburgh'; Wilkinsburg is a separate borough that shares Pittsburgh's postal area, so I used the bulletin's 'Wilkinsburg' and left `address_verified` false until a source such as the county or USPS settles which city name to publish. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G2C4R8+82 (the directory pin is 40.44083, -79.88527, about 30 m away).
