## Publisher

Triumph of the Holy Cross Parish (formed 1 July 2020 from Holy Spirit, West Mifflin; St. Clare of Assisi, Clairton; St. Elizabeth of Hungary, Pleasant Hills; St. Thomas a Becket, Jefferson Hills, per https://diopitt.org/triumph-of-the-holy-cross-jefferson-hills, read 2026-10-23) publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 14/1225. The 25 October 2026 issue is headed with the parish name and the three open churches' addresses on page 1, and gives one parish office (139 Gill Hall Road, Jefferson Hills). Administrator Rev. Michael R. Peck (diocese page). Its pastor is not shared with any other parish row: the one shared-pastor pair in the diocese is St. James / St. Katharine Drexel.

## Worship sites

Three churches have public Mass in the 25 October issue: St. Clare of Assisi (Sun 9:00 AM), St. Elizabeth of Hungary (Sat 5:00 PM vigil; Sun 8:00 and 11:00 AM) and St. Thomas a Becket (Sat 4:00 PM vigil; Sun 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM; weekday 8:00 AM Saturday). The diocese directory lists the same three with the same weekend times. Ruled out: Holy Spirit, West Mifflin (relegated 1 August 2023, sold 19 March 2026; excluded record `holy-spirit-west-mifflin`) and St. David, Pleasant Hills (St. Elizabeth's chapel, final Mass 1 April 2018; excluded record `st-david-pleasant-hills`).
