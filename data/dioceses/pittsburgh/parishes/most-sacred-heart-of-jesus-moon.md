## Publisher

Most Sacred Heart of Jesus Parish (Coraopolis / Crescent / Moon Township; formed 2021-01-04 from St. Joseph, St. Margaret Mary and St. Catherine of Siena per GCatholic history) publishes one weekly bulletin for its three churches. The 18 October 2026 ParishesOnline issue (container 14/1171, read 2026-10-23) is headed "MOST SACRED HEART OF JESUS PARISH" with all three churches and the administrative office, and its page 3 Mass list covers St. Catherine of Siena (Sat 4:00 PM; Sun 10:30 AM), St. Margaret Mary (Sat 4:00 PM; Sun 9:30 AM) and St. Joseph (Sat 5:30 PM; Sun 10:00 AM). Administrator Rev. Mark L. Thomas (bulletin pages 1 and 13, diocese page).

## Worship sites

Three churches: St. Catherine of Siena (199 McGovern Blvd, Crescent), St. Joseph (1304 Fourth Ave, Coraopolis) and St. Margaret Mary (One Parish Place, Moon Township). All three are in the diocese directory with the same weekend times and in the bulletin Mass lists. The administrator is also part-time chaplain to Robert Morris University (Moon Township); the bulletin and the parish home page print no campus Mass (see research), so no RMU record was made. No other predecessor church is known.

## Website and bulletin provider

www.mshj.org is the parish's diocese-provided eCatholic site (id 23074) behind Cloudflare; its /bulletin page links to the ParishesOnline org st-margaret-mary-st-joseph-st-catherine-of-siena-parishes (lpi 0018000000Qx7peAAB, container 14/1171), which is the row's channel.
