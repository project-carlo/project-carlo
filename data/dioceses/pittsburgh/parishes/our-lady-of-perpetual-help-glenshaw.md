## Publisher

Our Lady of Perpetual Help Parish (Rev. Timothy F. Whalen, pastor per the diocese page and the bulletin) publishes one weekly bulletin for its two churches, posted on its own eCatholic site. The 25 October 2026 issue (https://files.ecatholic.com/21151/bulletins/20261025.pdf, read 2026-10-23) names the parish in the header and prints one WEEKEND LITURGY SCHEDULE and WEEKDAY LITURGY SCHEDULE for St. Mary of the Assumption and St. Ursula.

## Worship sites

Two churches, both with weekend and weekday Mass in the bulletin box and in the diocese directory: St. Mary of the Assumption (2510 Middle Road, Glenshaw) and St. Ursula (Kirk Avenue, Allison Park). No other predecessor church is on the diocese page or the predecessor list. The bulletin's Saturday 4:00pm St. Mary Mass is marked '(Livestream)' but is a public Mass in the schedule.

## Website and bulletin provider

www.olphpgh.org is the parish's diocese-provided eCatholic site (id 21151). The bulletin is read from the site's own file store: files.ecatholic.com/21151/bulletins/<yyyymmdd>.pdf (Sundays). No ParishesOnline org for St. Mary of the Assumption (Glenshaw) or St. Ursula was found in the geo-search list (scratch raw lpi-catholic.tsv, 140 Catholic orgs, 2026-10-23), so the eCatholic site is the only channel found.
