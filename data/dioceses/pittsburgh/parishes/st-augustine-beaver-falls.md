## Publisher

St. Augustine Parish (Beaver Falls and Midland; pastor Rev. Kim J. Schreck) publishes one weekly bulletin for its two churches through ParishesOnline, container 14/1198. The 25 October 2026 issue, read 2026-10-23, is headed "SAINT AUGUSTINE PARISH", gives Saint Monica and Saint Blaise with their Mass times on page 1 and prints both churches' Mass intentions on page 10. The ad-page footer reads "St. Augustine Parish, Beaver Falls, PA 14-1198".

## Worship sites

Two churches: Saint Monica (Chippewa Township, 116 Thorndale Drive) and Saint Blaise (Midland, 772 Ohio Avenue), both with Masses in the bulletin and on the parish Mass Times page. The diocese directory and Mass Finder list the same two. GCatholic also lists a 'St. Monica, Darlington' (24354, 3357 Constitution Blvd); that is the former St. Rose of Lima church, closed in 2020 (see excluded/st-rose-of-lima-darlington). The St. Joseph Adoration Chapel in the bulletin is perpetual adoration at the Saint Monica campus, with no Mass listed.
