## Publisher

Christ the King Parish (merged 2021-07-01 from St. Joseph, O'Hara; St. Pio of Pietrelcina, Blawnox; St. Scholastica, Aspinwall; and the Sharpsburg parishes) publishes one weekly bulletin on its own eCatholic site, 22570, as `files.ecatholic.com/22570/bulletins/<yyyymmdd>.pdf`. The 18 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-23) lists Masses for Saint Joseph, Saint Scholastica, Saint Edward, Madonna of Jerusalem and Saint Mary in one schedule and names no other parish. The first-page masthead is a graphic with a letter-split text layer, so it is not quotable; the identity rests on the page 6 intentions line 'The people of Christ the King Parish', the page 13 Parish Information block and the site that posts it. Seven earlier issues (30 Aug to 11 Oct 2026) were grepped for St. John Cantius and Korean: neither appears except the chaplain line.

## Worship sites

Five churches have Mass in the bulletin: Saint Joseph (O'Hara, administrative center), Saint Scholastica (Aspinwall), Saint Edward (Blawnox), Saint Mary (Sharpsburg) and Madonna of Jerusalem (Sharpsburg, weekday Masses Tuesday and Thursday only; the diocese page says 'No Weekend Masses Scheduled'). Saint John Cantius (Sharpsburg) holds a public Korean-language Mass (Sunday 11:00 am, Thursday 11:30 am per the parish site and the diocese page) that the bulletin never prints; it is `excluded/saint-john-cantius-sharpsburg` as `no-bulletin` under the settled campus/no-bulletin rule. GCatholic 24374 'St. Pio of Pietrelcina' (450 Walnut St.) is Saint Edward under its former parish name (same address and phone 412-828-4066 as the parish site's Saint Edward), linked to that church, not a separate place. The predecessor list also names St. Juan Diego, Sharpsburg: a former parish name with no GCatholic or directory building; not separately researched.
