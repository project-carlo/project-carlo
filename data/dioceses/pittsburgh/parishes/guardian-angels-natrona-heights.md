Guardian Angels Parish (formed 2020-07-01 from Holy Family, Creighton; Holy Martyrs, Tarentum; Our Lady of the Most Blessed Sacrament, Natrona Heights; Our Lady of Victory, Harwick; St. Alphonsus, Springdale and St. Joseph, Natrona, per https://diopitt.org/guardian-angels-natrona-heights read 2026-10-23) publishes one weekly ParishesOnline bulletin, container 14/0710. The 18 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-23) gives the parish office address on page 1 and names two campuses on page 2.

## Worship sites

Two churches with Mass now: Most Blessed Sacrament (800 Montana Avenue, Natrona Heights; the bulletin abbreviates it MBS) and Our Lady of Victory (1319 Low Grade Road, Harwick; OLOV). The parish history page says that on 1 October 2022 five of the seven church buildings were relegated to profane but not sordid use and only these two remained open; the bulletin agrees. The five are Our Lady of Perpetual Help, St. Joseph (St. Ladislaus), Holy Family, St. Alphonsus and the Tarentum church; each has its own excluded record. A grep of eleven 2026 issues (Jan-Oct, ParishesOnline container 14/0710) for those names found only unrelated ads and St. Joseph High School notices. The Wednesday 21 October 10:00am 'SJHS (MBS) Mass with Bishop Waltersheid' is at Most Blessed Sacrament, and the high-school Masses 'church door access' note refers to the same church.
