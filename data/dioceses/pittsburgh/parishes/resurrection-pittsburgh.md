## Publisher

Resurrection Parish (the 2021 merger of St. John Capistran, Upper St. Clair, and St. Thomas More, Bethel Park) publishes one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline container 14/1223 for its two churches. The 25 October 2026 issue's cover (page 1 is an image; rendered 2026-10-23) reads "Resurrection Parish, St. John Capistran & St. Thomas More Churches", with a Mass box for each church and resurrectionpgh.org. The text-layer footer of the ad pages (15-16) reads "Resurrection Parish, Pittsburgh, PA 14-1223". Pastor Rev. Michael R. Ackerman (diocese parish page, 2026-10-23); office 412-833-0031, 126 Fort Couch Road (bulletin page 2).

## Worship sites

Two churches, both named on the page-1 cover with their own Mass times: St. John Capistran (1610 McMillan Road) and St. Thomas More (126 Fort Couch Road). The diocese directory and Mass Finder list the same two. No other Resurrection predecessor church appears in the roster for this batch.

## Website and bulletin provider

www.resurrectionpgh.org is the diocese-provided eCatholic site (21555) behind Cloudflare; its Bulletin page embeds the ParishesOnline listing for org saint-thomas-more, so the bulletin is read from the container. Two ParishesOnline orgs (saint-thomas-more, st-john-capistran-church) resolve to container 14/1223 and are recorded as one bulletin. The LPi org church-of-the-resurrection-15226 belongs to St. Teresa of Kolkata (1100 Creedmoor Ave) and is not this parish. The bulletin pages are mostly images, so the pipeline needs to read them as images.
