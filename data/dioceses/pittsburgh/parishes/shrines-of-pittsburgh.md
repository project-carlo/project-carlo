## Publisher

The Shrines of Pittsburgh publish one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline container 14/1226 for four canonical parishes (Immaculate Heart of Mary, Most Holy Name of Jesus, St. Nicholas, St. Patrick-St. Stanislaus Kostka) under one team ministry (Rev. Thomas G. Kadlick, Rev. Nicholas Vaskov, Deacon Gregory Jelinek). Read 2026-10-23: the 25 October 2026 issue's page 1 is headed "SHRINES OF PITTSBURGH" with one cover column per place and its Mass times; page 2 is one Mass Intentions table naming every place; page 14 is a single directory. The issues of 23 August, 6 September, 20 September, 4 October and 18 October (grepped for St. Patrick and the footer) carry the same structure and the ad-page footer "Shrines of Pittsburgh, Pittsburgh, PA 14-1226". The diocese's own page puts the same six places under one entry (see evidence). So this is one row (Rules settled 3), not four.

Three LPi orgs point at the container: shrines-of-pittsburgh, st-nicholas-church-15209 and st-patrick-st-stanislaus-kostka-parish; they list identical issues and are one bulletin, so all their ids are recorded here. The parish-level Mass Finder pin 23091 is the eCatholic site id of pghshrines.org, not a publication; it stays unlinked (batch instruction).

## Worship sites

Six places print Mass in the 25 October issue and have church records: St. Anthony Chapel (Troy Hill; Sunday 11:30 and weekday 8:30 Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri), Most Holy Name of Jesus (Troy Hill; Sunday 9:00), Immaculate Heart of Mary (Polish Hill; Sat 4:30, Sun 10:00), St. Nicholas (Millvale; Sat 5:00), St. Stanislaus Kostka (Strip District; Sat 4:00, Sun 9:00 and 11:00) and St. Patrick (Strip District; Thursday 12:10 pm, in the weekly intentions of the issues of 23 Aug, 6 Sep, 20 Sep, 4 Oct, 18 Oct and 25 Oct). The directory's line for St. Patrick ('Sunday Masses at Saint Stanislaus Church', no weekend Mass) is true of the weekend, but the bulletin prints a weekly weekday Mass there, so it is a church row, not an exclusion (Rules settled 2). Not recorded as a church: the St. Anthony Lyceum (1626 Hatteras St), a Catholic social club the bulletin lists for fellowship hours, with no Mass.

pghshrines.org is a diocese-provided eCatholic site (id 23091) behind Cloudflare; it embeds the ParishesOnline org shrines-of-pittsburgh, so the provider is parishes_online with provider_id shrines-of-pittsburgh.
