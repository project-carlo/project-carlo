## Publisher

The Parish of St. Raphael the Archangel (Carnegie, Green Tree and Scott; 'established in 2021' per its own bulletin; GCatholic dates the St. Elizabeth Ann Seton merger 2021-01-04) publishes one weekly bulletin on its own eCatholic site, id 22104 (files.ecatholic.com/22104/bulletins/<yyyymmdd>.pdf). The 18 October 2026 issue read on 2026-10-23 carries one Mass schedule and one list of Mass intentions for the three churches under the parish name.

## Worship sites

Three churches, all with weekly Mass in the 18 October issue: Saint Elizabeth Ann Seton (Carnegie; also the Parish Administration Center), Saint Margaret of Scotland (Green Tree) and Saints Simon and Jude (Scott). The diocese parish page lists the same three and the predecessor list names exactly these three as merged into the parish. No closed predecessor building turned up in GCatholic or the predecessor list.

A Mass Finder pin for the parish itself (massfinder:22104, 'The Parish of St. Raphael the Archangel', no address) is the parish-level pin, not a church; it is left unlinked. The ParishesOnline org `st-raphael-church-15206` is St. Raphael Church, Morningside, in Saint Jude Parish (container 14/1148, already a record under saint-jude-pittsburgh) and has nothing to do with this parish.

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site https://www.straphaelcgs.org is eCatholic 22104 and hosts the bulletin PDFs. Grecco is pastor of this parish alone; Rev. Michael L. Yaksick is parish chaplain here and at Saint Michael the Archangel, which keeps its own ParishesOnline bulletin (different masthead and schedule), so the two are separate rows.
