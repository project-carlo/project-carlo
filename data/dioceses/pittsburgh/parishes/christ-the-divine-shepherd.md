## Publisher

Christ the Divine Shepherd Parish (Monroeville and Penn Hills), formed 2020-01-06 from St. Bernadette, North American Martyrs (Monroeville and St. Michael, Pitcairn), St. Bartholomew, St. Gerard Majella and St. Susanna, publishes one weekly bulletin on its own eCatholic site 21574, as `files.ecatholic.com/21574/bulletins/<yyyymmdd>.pdf`. The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-23, two days ahead of its date) has a cover that names the parish and prints one MASS SCHEDULE box for two churches (see the evidence). The three ParishesOnline orgs bearing the parish's name publish nothing, so there is no second channel.

## Worship sites

Two churches with Mass: St. Bernadette (Monroeville) and St. Susanna (Penn Hills). Four predecessor buildings were closed by decree effective 2021-08-06 (diocese history page, quoted in the evidence): North American Martyrs (Monroeville), St. Michael (Pitcairn), St. Bartholomew and St. Gerard Majella (Penn Hills). The bulletin schedule lists none of them. See excluded/north-american-martyrs-monroeville.md, st-bartholomew-penn-hills.md, st-gerard-majella-penn-hills.md and st-michael-pitcairn.md. North American Martyrs, St. Bartholomew and St. Gerard Majella are GCatholic entries (24221, 24263, 24295); St. Michael is in no roster (the GCatholic roster has no Pitcairn entry).
