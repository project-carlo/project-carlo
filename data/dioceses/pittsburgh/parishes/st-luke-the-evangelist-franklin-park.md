## Publisher

Saint Luke the Evangelist Parish (pastor Rev. Joseph A. Carr) publishes one weekly bulletin for its two churches through ParishesOnline, container 14/1208 (org saint-luke-the-evangelist-parish-15143). The 25 October 2026 issue, read 2026-10-23, is an image-only PDF; its rendered cover is headed 'SAINT LUKE the EVANGELIST' with both churches' phone numbers (Good Samaritan and Saints John & Paul), and its ad pages carry the footer 'Saint Luke the Evangelist Parish, Sewickley, PA 14-1208'. No sibling parish prints in this container.

## Worship sites

Two churches with weekend Masses on the parish site, the diocese page and the Mass Finder: Good Samaritan (Ambridge) and Saints John and Paul (Franklin Park, postal city Sewickley). The parish was formed 2022-07-01 from four parishes; the diocese history pages for Saint John the Baptist, Baden and Our Lady of Peace, Conway say those two churches were closed at the merger (see `st-john-the-baptist-baden` and `our-lady-of-peace-conway`; a Maronite mission at 375 Linmore Ave, Baden is excluded as `st-teresa-of-calcutta-maronite-mission-baden`). Saints John & Paul also has a Grotto Chapel with weekday Masses and perpetual adoration; the parish's YouTube listing calls it 'the Grotto Chapel at Saints John & Paul Church', so it is on that campus and is covered by the Saints John and Paul church record, not a separate row.
