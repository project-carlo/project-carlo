## Publisher

Saints Francis and Clare Parish (Butler County) publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing at church-bulletin.org/saints-francis-clare-parish-chicora-pa-16025. The 9 August 2026 issue (read 2026-10-23, text layer) has the masthead "Saints Francis & Clare Parish, August 9, 2026" and a single "Weekly Mass Intentions" page covering six churches; the newest issue, 25 October 2026 (an image PDF, rendered), has the same cover and the same six-church intentions page. The parish was established 2026-07-01 by decree of Bishop Eckman from the former parishes of Saint Francis of Assisi (Cabot, Coylesville, Herman) and Saint Clare of Assisi (Carbon Center, Chicora, North Oakland), quoted from the parish site. The diocese directory gives one pastor, Rev. Matthew R. McClain, and one phone, 724-352-2149, for all six.

## Worship sites

Six churches, each with Masses in the 24 Oct - 1 Nov intentions page: St. John (Coylesville/Fenelton), St. Joseph (Cabot), St. Joseph (North Oakland, Chicora), St. Mary of the Assumption (Herman, Butler), Mater Dolorosa (Chicora), St. Wendelin (Carbon Center, Butler). The Mass Finder still names the old parishes (St. Clare of Assisi: Mater Dolorosa, St. Joseph N.O., St. Wendelin; St. Francis of Assisi: St. John, St. Joseph, St. Mary); GCatholic and the diocese predecessor list agree on that split. Not part of this parish: GCatholic 38861 St. Patrick, Cowansville ("Former Church", Armstrong County), which was assigned here by distance only; see excluded/st-patrick-cowansville.md.
