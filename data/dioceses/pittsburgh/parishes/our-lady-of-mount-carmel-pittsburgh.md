## Publisher

Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish (West View, Ross and Perrysville, merged 2020-07-01 per GCatholic's history field) publishes one weekly bulletin for all its churches. The 25 October 2026 issue (https://files.ecatholic.com/21944/bulletins/20261025.pdf, read 2026-10-23) is headed 'Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish', carries one Mass Schedule box for St. Teresa of Avila, St. Athanasius and St. Sebastian (page 4) and gives the Parish Center address on page 5. Administrator: Rev. Paul Zywan (bulletin page 2 'A Message from Father Zywan'; diocese page). The bulletin is on the parish's own diocese-provided eCatholic site (id 21944) and nowhere else I found: the only ParishesOnline org for the parish, `incarnation-of-the-lord-church` (lpi 0018000000Qx7sHAAR, titled 'Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish - Incarnation of the Lord Church'), lists no issues (`probe po`, 2026-10-23: 'this organization does not publish a bulletin here now'), so it is not recorded as a publication id. The PDF footer reads 'View this bulletin online at www.DiscoverMass.com', so the parish may also post on Discover Mass; not searched, since the eCatholic archive is complete.

## Worship sites

Three churches have public Mass now (bulletin Mass Schedule, 25 Oct 2026): St. Athanasius (West View), St. Sebastian (Ross) and St. Teresa of Avila (Perrysville). St. Sebastian's weekday Masses and a First Wednesday service are at 'Saint Sebastian Chapel', which the bulletin's Rosaries and Devotions page lists beside Saint Sebastian Church (page 5) and the 17 May 2026 issue (page 2, flooring project) calls 'the church and the chapel'; treated as part of the St. Sebastian campus, so not a separate row. The fourth building, Incarnation of the Lord (Perry North), is in `open/incarnation-of-the-lord-perry-north`: its last regular Sunday Mass was 12 April 2026 and the bulletin no longer schedules any Mass there, but nothing says it is closed. Predecessor list: Incarnation, St. Athanasius, St. Sebastian and St. Teresa of Avila all map to this parish (diopitt.org predecessor list, 2026-10-23); no other former church is attached to it.
