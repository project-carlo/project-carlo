## Publisher

Saint Matthew Parish (Rev. James R. Gretz, pastor per the diocese page) publishes one weekly bulletin for its four churches through ParishesOnline, container 14/1151. The 25 October 2026 issue (https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/14/1151/20261025B.pdf, read 2026-10-23) has a cover reading 'Saint Matthew Parish', page 3 carries one 'St. Matthew Parish Mass Schedule effective 1 January 2026' covering All Saints, Holy Spirit, St. Aloysius and St. Bonaventure, and page 4 one list of Mass intentions with campus codes.

## Worship sites

Four churches, each with public Mass in the schedule: All Saints (19 Wilson Street, Etna, mailing city Pittsburgh 15223), Holy Spirit (608 Farragut Street, Millvale 15209), St. Aloysius (3616 Mount Troy Road, Reserve Township, Pittsburgh 15212) and St. Bonaventure (2001 Mount Royal Boulevard, Glenshaw 15116). The diocese directory and Mass Finder list the same four; the predecessor list shows no fifth church. The weekly calendar's 'SB Chapel' (adoration, novenas, confessions) is the adoration chapel on the St. Bonaventure campus, not a separate Mass site; the bulletin gives no Mass there outside the St. Bonaventure Masses.

## Website and bulletin provider

saintmatthewpgh.org is the parish's diocese-provided eCatholic site (id 21951), behind Cloudflare (headed Chrome reads it). It reposts the container PDF a week later (byte-identical 18 October file), so the bulletin of record is ParishesOnline container 14/1151, the newer channel. Three ParishesOnline orgs (saint-matthew-parish, holy-spirit-15209, st-aloysius-parish-15212) all serve that one container; `st-matthew-church-15201` (5322 Carnegie St, Pittsburgh 15201) is an unrelated same-named org with no issues and is not recorded.
