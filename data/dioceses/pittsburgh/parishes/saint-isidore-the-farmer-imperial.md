## Publisher

Saint Isidore the Farmer Parish (formed 2020-07-01 from Our Lady of Lourdes, St. Alphonsus, St. Ann, St. Columbkille, St. Michael and St. Patrick, per the diocese predecessor list and GCatholic's 'parishes merged 2020.07.01') publishes one weekly bulletin for all six churches. The 18 October 2026 issue (files.ecatholic.com/21470/bulletins/20261018.pdf, 12 pages) has a cover listing the six churches with addresses, the parish office at 103 Church Road, Imperial, and a page 2 'Liturgy Schedule & Mass Intentions' headed 'Saint Isidore the Farmer Parish' with one intentions list across the six churches. Earlier issues (20260920, 20260816, 20260712) carry the same page 2 header and the same six-church intentions list.

## Worship sites

Six churches, all with Mass in the 18 October 2026 intentions: St. Alphonsus (McDonald), St. Ann (Bulger), St. Columbkille (Imperial), Our Lady of Lourdes (Burgettstown), St. Patrick (Oakdale) and St. Michael (Avella). The diocese directory lists St. Ann, Bulger as 'No weekend Masses scheduled' but the bulletin gives it weekday Masses (8:00am Monday 19 Oct and Thursday 22 Oct; also 20260920 and 20260712), so it is a church row (Rules settled 2). The bulletin also announces a Mass of Remembrance at St. Columbkille (Mon 2 Nov, 7:00pm) and an Anointing Mass at Our Lady of Lourdes (Sun 8 Nov, 1:00pm), both churches already counted. No other site appears in the issue. GCatholic and the predecessor list show no other church for this parish.

## Website and bulletin provider

www.stisidorethefarmer.org is the diocese-provided eCatholic site 21470 behind Cloudflare (probe url: 403; headed Chrome reads it). The bulletin page links `files.ecatholic.com/21470/bulletins/<yyyymmdd>.pdf`, which downloads without a browser. The 25 October file returned 403 on 2026-10-23 (not yet posted), as usual for eCatholic before the Sunday. No ParishesOnline org exists for this parish in the LPi geo-search list. The page lists old pre-merger channels (/bulletins-2 St. Columbkille, /bulletins-4 St. Patrick, /common-bulletin) whose newest files date from 2020: not channels.
