## Publisher

Mary, Mother of God Parish (formed 2020-01-06 from St. Angela Merici, White Oak; Corpus Christi and St. Patrick, McKeesport; and St. Robert Bellarmine, East McKeesport, per the diocese's Corpus Christi and St. Patrick history pages and its news item 'Eight new, merged parishes to be created Jan. 6') publishes one weekly ParishesOnline bulletin, container 14/0560. The 25 October 2026 issue's page 1 is headed 'MARY, MOTHER OF GOD PARISH' and prints one Mass schedule for St. Angela Merici and St. Patrick. Pastor Rev. Terrence P. O'Connor (diocese page; bulletin page 2 clergy list).

## Worship sites

Two churches hold weekly Mass in the 25 October 2026 bulletin: St. Angela Merici (White Oak) and St. Patrick (McKeesport, the former St. Perpetua). Corpus Christi (McKeesport; the former St. Mary of Czestochowa, GCatholic 24187) is on the masthead but has no weekly Mass since January 2023: see open/corpus-christi-mckeesport. St. Robert Bellarmine (East McKeesport, GCatholic 24380) closed 2024-12-02: see excluded/st-robert-bellarmine-east-mckeesport.
