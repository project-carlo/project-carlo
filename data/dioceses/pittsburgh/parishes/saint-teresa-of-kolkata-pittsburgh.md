## Publisher

Saint Teresa of Kolkata Parish (Beechview and Brookline; formed 1 July 2019 from Saint Catherine of Siena, Church of the Resurrection, Saint Pius X, Our Lady of Loreto and Saint Pamphilus, per the parish's own About page and the diocese's Saint Pamphilus history page) publishes one weekly bulletin on its own eCatholic site, 21158 (files.ecatholic.com/21158/bulletins/<yyyymmdd>.pdf). The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-23) names the parish and its three churches on pages 1, 2 and 6 and has one Mass schedule and one list of Mass intentions for all three.

## Worship sites

Three churches, all with Mass in the 25 October issue: Saint Catherine of Siena (Beechview), Saint Pius X (Brookline) and Resurrection (Brookline). The parish site says Mass is celebrated "at three churches". Two former churches of the merged parish are not in the bulletin or on the parish site: Our Lady of Loreto (closed, final Mass 30 August 2020, demolished November 2025) and Saint Pamphilus (Latin Mass of farewell 14 June 2020; the building now houses the Maronite parish Our Lady of Victory). Each has its own excluded record.

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site https://www.stteresakolkatapgh.org is eCatholic 21158; the bulletin is at the same id. ParishesOnline has an org `saint-teresa-of-kolkata-parish` that is empty (it appears in this diocese's list of empty orgs: those parishes publish elsewhere). Same pastor as no other parish: the directory lists Fr. Fernando Torres for this parish alone.
