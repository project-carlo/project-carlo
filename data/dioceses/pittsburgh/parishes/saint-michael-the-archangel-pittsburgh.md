## Publisher

Saint Michael the Archangel Parish (Mount Lebanon and Scott; formed by the merger dated 2020-07-01 in GCatholic's history of both churches, 'parishes merged 2020.07.01') publishes one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline, container 14/1161 (org `st-michael-the-archangel1-parish`). The 25 October 2026 issue read on 2026-10-23 carries one Mass schedule for both churches under the parish name. Nothing in it names another parish.

## Worship sites

Two churches, both with Mass in the 25 October issue: Saint Bernard (Mount Lebanon; also the parish office) and Our Lady of Grace (Scott). The diocese parish page lists the same two, and the predecessor list names exactly these two as merged into St. Michael the Archangel. The bulletin also mentions the St. Bernard Fontenay Chapel as the place for overnight adoration (Tuesday 4:00pm to Wednesday 6:40am, second Tuesday of the month); it is a chapel within the St. Bernard complex with adoration only in the schedule box, so it is not a separate church row. Nothing else turned up for this parish; no closed predecessor building is listed by GCatholic.

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site https://www.smapgh.org is eCatholic 22700 behind Cloudflare, like every parish site in this diocese. Its Bulletin page sends readers to the ParishesOnline org, so the bulletin of record is the container, not an eCatholic file. Pastor Rev. Brian J. Welding is pastor of this parish alone (diocese page, 2026-10-23); the parish chaplain Rev. Michael L. Yaksick is also listed on St. Raphael the Archangel's clergy team, but as chaplain, not pastor, and the two parishes keep separate bulletins (St. Raphael's is eCatholic 22104, see saint-raphael-the-archangel-carnegie).
