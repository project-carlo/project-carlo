## Publisher

St. Catherine Labouré Parish (the 2021-01-04 merger of St. Joan of Arc, Library/South Park, and St. Louise de Marillac, Upper St. Clair) publishes one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline container 14/1380. The 25 October 2026 issue's cover names the parish and both church addresses; ad-page footers read "St. Catherine Laboure Parish, Pittsburgh, PA 14-1380". Pastor Rev. Jeffrey M. Craig (cover; diocese parish page 2026-10-23). Scout 1's lead (ParishesOnline org st-joan-of-arc-church-15129, titled "St. Catherine Laboure - St. Joan of Arc Church") was checked: its latest issues (11 and 18 October 2026) are in container 14/1380, so it is the same bulletin as the org for St. Louise de Marillac.

## Worship sites

Only St. Louise de Marillac (320 McMurray Road) has Mass in the bulletin, the parish site and the diocese directory. St. Joan of Arc (South Park) is named on the cover as a parish location but has no Mass anywhere read; see open/saint-joan-of-arc-south-park.

## Website and bulletin provider

www.sclpgh.org is the diocese-provided eCatholic site (22954) behind Cloudflare. Its bulletins page lists files.ecatholic.com/22954 PDFs only to 1 June 2025, so the bulletin is read from the ParishesOnline container. Two orgs (st-louise-de-marillac-church-106947, st-joan-of-arc-church-15129) resolve to 14/1380 and are one bulletin.
