## Publisher

Mary, Queen of Saints Parish (pastor Rev. John B. Gizler, III) publishes one weekly bulletin for its four churches through ParishesOnline, container 14/1396. The 25 October 2026 issue, read 2026-10-23, gives one Weekend Mass Times box for Our Lady of Fatima, St. Frances Cabrini, St. John the Baptist and St. Titus, and one clergy and staff directory with the administrative office at 115 Trinity Drive, Aliquippa. No sibling parish prints in this container.

## Worship sites

Four churches, each with a weekend Mass in the 25 October bulletin: Our Lady of Fatima (Hopewell Twp.), St. Frances Cabrini (Center Twp.), St. John the Baptist (Monaca) and St. Titus (Aliquippa). The diocese directory, the Mass Finder and GCatholic list the same four. The 25 October bulletin names no other Mass site (grep for Baden, Conway and Grotto found nothing).

## Website and bulletin provider

maryqueenofsaints.org is the diocese-provided eCatholic site 22365 behind Cloudflare. Its parish-bulletin page embeds the ParishesOnline org and container, so the bulletin is read from ParishesOnline by org slug. Two orgs (the parish-titled one and the St. John the Baptist Church one) resolve to the same container and issue list and are recorded together.
