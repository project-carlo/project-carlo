## Publisher

Mary, Queen of Peace Parish (South Side and Mount Washington) publishes one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline container 14/0020. Anthony Sciarappa, Administrator; office 412-481-8380). Two ParishesOnline orgs (prince-of-peace-and-saint-mary-of-the-mount-parishes, the parish's current org with website mqpeace.org, and prince-of-peace-saint-mary-of-the-mount-parishes, a predecessor-named account) both serve this one container, so both are recorded on this row.

## Worship sites

Two churches: St. Adalbert (South Side) and St. Mary of the Mount (Mount Washington). Both appear in the bulletin's Mass-intentions list (codes A and M), its confession schedule and its accessibility notes. The diocese directory and Mass Finder list the same two. Ruled out: the former St. Peter church on Sarah Street (South Side), closed for worship by decree of 2019-12-06 (see excluded/st-peter-south-side.md). The parish was formed 2021-01-04 from Prince of Peace and St. Mary of the Mount (GCatholic history for both churches); Prince of Peace itself was created in 1992 from several South Side parishes.
