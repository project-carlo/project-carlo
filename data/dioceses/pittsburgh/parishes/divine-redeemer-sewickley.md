## Publisher

Divine Redeemer Parish (Sewickley) publishes one weekly bulletin for both its churches. The 18 October 2026 issue (https://files.ecatholic.com/24435/bulletins/20261018.pdf, read 2026-10-23) has an image cover whose masthead reads "DIVINE REDEEMER PARISH", with both church addresses, Pastor Rev. Brian W. Noel, and one Mass schedule box covering St. James and St. Mary. The cover is an image with no text layer; I read it from a render (parish-site-access render.py), so the quoted evidence line is the page 2 text that names St. James Church. The PDF footer reads "View this bulletin online at www.DiscoverMass.com": the parish also appears to post on Discover Mass, but the eCatholic file is the one the website's /bulletins page links, and no Discover Mass listing was searched for.

## Worship sites

Two churches, both in the cover Mass box and in the diocese directory: St. James (200 Walnut St, Sewickley) and St. Mary (444 Glenfield Rd, Aleppo, mailing city Sewickley). No other predecessor church is on the page. Divine Redeemer is the only parish in this batch's Sewickley area with this bulletin; the other Sewickley-area parish, Saints John and Paul (existing record saints-john-and-paul-sewickley), belongs to another batch.
