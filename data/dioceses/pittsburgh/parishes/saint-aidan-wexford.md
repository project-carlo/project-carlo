## Publisher

Saint Aidan Parish, Wexford (formed 2020-07-01 from St. Alexis and St. Alphonsus per the diocese's predecessor list and GCatholic history; pastor Rev. David J. Egan, Jr. per the diocese page and bulletin page 16) publishes one weekly bulletin for its two churches. The 25 October 2026 ParishesOnline issue (container 14/1439) names the parish on page 1 over the two church addresses and gives one combined Mass schedule on page 16.

## Worship sites

Two churches, both with weekend and weekday Mass in the bulletin and on the parish Mass Times page: Saint Alexis (10090 Old Perry Highway) and Saint Alphonsus (221 Church Road), both Wexford. The diocese directory and Mass Finder list the same two. The predecessor list shows no other predecessor church. Not here: nothing else named in the bulletin's Mass schedule. The parish also lists a Nursing Home Ministry priest (Rev. Daniel T. Straughn) but no nursing-home Mass place is named in the schedule.
