## Publisher

Regina Coeli Parish publishes one weekly bulletin for its three churches. The 25 October 2026 issue (https://files.ecatholic.com/31293/bulletins/20261025.pdf, read 2026-10-23) has a masthead naming Assumption, Sacred Heart and St John Neumann, and one Mass Times box per church on page 1; page 2 gives the parish office numbers for all three. Administrator: Rev. David G. Poecking, STL (diocese parish page, 2026-10-23); the bulletin text I read does not print the pastor. The bulletin sits on the parish's own diocese-provided eCatholic site (id 31293). No ParishesOnline org for the parish appears in the LPi Catholic list (`scratch/map-pittsburgh/raw/lpi-catholic.tsv`, no Regina Coeli, Bellevue, Emsworth or Franklin Park entry). The PDF footer reads 'View this bulletin online at www.DiscoverMass.com', so a Discover Mass listing may exist; not searched.

## Worship sites

Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary (Bellevue), Sacred Heart (Emsworth) and St. John Neumann (Franklin Park) all have Mass in the bulletin. They are the diocese page's three churches and the three predecessor parishes the diopitt.org predecessor list maps to Regina Coeli (Assumption, Bellevue; Sacred Heart, Emsworth; St. John Neumann, Franklin Park). No other former church is attached to the parish.
