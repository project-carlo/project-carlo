## Publisher

St. Faustina Parish (Slippery Rock, Prospect, West Sunbury and Forestville) publishes one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline container 14/1535 (org www-stfaustinaparish-org, lpi 0018000000Qx7rcAAB). The 18 October 2026 issue masthead (page 1) reads 'St. Faustina Parish', 342 Normal Avenue, Slippery Rock; the 27 September and 25 October 2026 issues carry the same masthead and the 30 August issue the same address. Pastor Rev. Adam M. Verona (page 2 directory; diocese page). The parish was formed 2021-01-04 by merging St. Alphonsus (Boyers/Murrinsville/West Sunbury), St. Christopher (Prospect) and St. Peter (Slippery Rock), per the diocese's history page for St. Alphonsus.

## Worship sites

Five places hold public Mass: St. Peter (Slippery Rock), St. Christopher (Prospect), St. Louis (West Sunbury), St. Anthony (Forestville) and the SRU Newman Center (Slippery Rock; Sunday and Wednesday 7:00 PM in term). All five are named in the 18 Oct bulletin's Mass intentions and on the parish's Worship Locations page. The diocese directory lists the same five. Two further buildings of the former St. Alphonsus parish, St. Alphonsus (Murrinsville) and Epiphany (Boyers), closed in 2019: see their excluded records.

## Website and bulletin provider

www.stfaustinaparish.org is diocese-provided eCatholic (site 21902) behind Cloudflare (probe url 403; read in headed Chrome). It embeds the ParishesOnline widget; the bulletin is read from the container. The eCatholic site posts only DMI survey PDFs, not bulletins. The ParishesOnline org lists its address as 670 S Main St, Slippery Rock (an older address); the bulletin masthead gives 342 Normal Avenue, the same address the diocese directory gives for the Newman Center.
