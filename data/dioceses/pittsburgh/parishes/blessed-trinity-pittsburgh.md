## Worship sites

Four churches, each in the 18 October Mass times box and in its intentions list: St. Albert the Great (3198 Schieck Street, Baldwin), St. Basil (1735 Brownsville Road, Carrick), Holy Angels (408 Baldwin Road, Hays) and St. Sylvester (3754 Brownsville Road, Brentwood). The diocese directory and Mass Finder list the same four. St. Sylvester has no Masses 26 October to 8 November 2026 because of a parking-lot reconstruction; that is a stated, dated, temporary suspension, so the church stays a worship site (its Masses resume 9 November per the notice's end date; check the 8 November bulletin). Ruled out, all closed predecessor-parish buildings that GCatholic still lists: St. Norbert, Overbrook (closed 2020-07-01), St. Wendelin, Carrick (last public Mass 2023-11-26, demolished June 2024). Ruled out for another reason: St. John Vianney, Allentown, a different parish's church closed in 2016 (see excluded/ records). The predecessor parishes were Holy Apostles (St. Albert the Great, St. Basil, St. Wendelin, St. Norbert; 2016-09-01), St. Sylvester and Holy Angels, merged 2020-07-01.
