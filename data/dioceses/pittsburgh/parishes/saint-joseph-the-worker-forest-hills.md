## Publisher

Saint Joseph the Worker Parish (formed 2020-07-01 from Saint Colman, Good Shepherd, Saint John Fisher, Saint Jude the Apostle, Madonna del Castello, Saint Maurice and Word of God, per the closing decrees' recital) publishes one weekly ParishesOnline bulletin, container 14/1182. Before the closures its masthead listed eight churches; since 14 March 2026 only St. Maurice (the 'Forest Hills Campus') holds Mass. Pastor Rev. Michael J. Stumpf (diocese page).

## Worship sites

One church holds Mass now: the Forest Hills church at 2001 Ardmore Blvd (the St. Maurice building; the diocese directory and Mass Finder call it 'Saint Joseph the Worker Church (Forest Hills)'). Seven others closed by decrees effective 12:01 AM on 12 March 2026: Good Shepherd, Madonna del Castello, Sacred Heart, St. Anselm, St. Colman, St. John Fisher and St. Jude the Apostle. Each has an excluded/ record. St. Barnabas, Swissvale (GCatholic 24407) closed in 2014 and St. Helen and St. William, East Pittsburgh (24196, 24195) closed in 2014 with Holy Cross parish's merger into Good Shepherd: also excluded/ records.
