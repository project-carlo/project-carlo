## Publisher

The 25 October 2026 issue names the parish and the three churches on page 1 and prints one Mass schedule for all three. Pastor Rev. Thomas J. Burke (bulletin page 2 and diocese page); no other parish row shares him.

Three churches have public Mass in the 25 October issue: St. Michael, Elizabeth (Sat 4:00 pm; Sun 9:30 am; weekday Mass 9:00 am Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat), Queen of the Rosary, Glassport (Sun 11:30 am) and St. Eugene, Liberty / McKeesport (Sat 6:00 pm).
