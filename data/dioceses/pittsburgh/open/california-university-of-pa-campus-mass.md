## What is known

The Diocese of Pittsburgh's parish page and Mass Finder list a Sunday 10:00 a.m. Mass for California University of PA, 250 University Avenue, California, PA 15419 (in term only), under Saint Katharine Drexel Parish. The diocese's own Cal U Catholic page (https://diopitt.org/calucatholic, read 2026-10-23) instead has Sunday 7:00 p.m. at the Vulcan Theater, 805 3rd Street, for the fall 2024 semester. The parish's bulletin (74 issues, June 2025 to 25 Oct 2026) and Masses page print no Cal U Mass, though the 19 April 2026 second collection supports 'Campus Ministry for both W & J College ... and Penn West California University' (Cal Rocks Newman Club).

## Why it is not decided

Rules settled 1: a campus Mass that is public but in no bulletin is `excluded/` as `no-bulletin`; absence of a schedule is not evidence either way. Here the diocese's two pages disagree on time and place, one of them is two years old, and the parish does not print a Cal U Mass at all, so neither a current public Mass nor its absence is shown. The Mass Finder pin is on the campus (250 University Avenue). If a bulletin prints the Mass it becomes a church row of the Catholic Partner Parishes row; if the parish confirms there is no public campus Mass it goes to `excluded/` (`no-public-mass`) or, if public but unprinted, `no-bulletin`.
