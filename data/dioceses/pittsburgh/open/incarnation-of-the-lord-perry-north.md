## What is known

Incarnation of the Lord (4071 Franklin Road, Perry North; originally Nativity of Our Lord, built 1925) is one of Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish's four churches. The 4 January 2026 bulletin (page 2) said regular usage of the building 'would need to be reduced' and that 'The final 8:00 am Sunday Mass at Incarnation of the Lord will take place on April 12'; the new schedule began with the weekend of 18 and 19 April, after a Centennial Mass there on Saturday 18 April at 10:00 am (3 May bulletin, page 2). The 19 April bulletin's Mass box still listed 'Sunday, 8:00 a.m., Incarnation of the Lord', which looks like a stale schedule box: the 17 May bulletin speaks of 'the removal of the Sunday Mass at Incarnation'. From 26 April to 25 October 2026 (26 issues) every grep of 'Incarnation' returns only the address line or the Centennial report; no Mass, weekday or occasional, is scheduled there. The diocese directory says 'No Masses Scheduled'. The 25 October bulletin still counts it as one of four churches. Sources: parish evidence above, dated.

## Why it is not decided

Rule 2 of this diocese's settled rules: 'No Masses Scheduled' does not close a church, and a closure needs a source that says the building closed, was sold or no longer holds Mass. None says that: the parish describes reduced use. No occasional Mass (a funeral, wedding or devotion) was found either, so it is not a church row. Funerals and weddings were moved elsewhere for the Saint Sebastian floor work (26 April issue), which says nothing about Incarnation. A later bulletin or the parish office could settle it; I did not call the office.
