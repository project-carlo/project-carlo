## What is known

Waynesburg University (Waynesburg, Greene County) has a Catholic Campus Ministry run by St. Matthias Parish (Director of Youth Evangelization Mary Sampey, 6 Sep 2026 bulletin). In the 2025-26 academic year the St. Matthias bulletin printed a student Mass: at Roberts Chapel on campus from September 2025 while St. Ann was being restored, then a Sunday 1:00 pm Mass (and some Monday 1:00 pm Masses) at St. Ann in spring 2026 labelled 'Waynesburg Univ. Mass'. The 6 September 2026 issue (read 2026-10-23) says those Sunday afternoon and evening Masses are discontinued and a monthly University Student Mass is planned, with no date or place yet. The 25 October 2026 schedule prints none.

This is not a church row now: the Mass at St. Ann was held in an existing church (a St. Matthias row), and no campus Mass is currently scheduled. Rule 1 (diocese.md) applies: a Mass printed in a parish bulletin is a church row under that parish.

## Why it is not decided

A monthly Mass is announced but not yet printed with a date or place; whether it will be public and on campus is unknown. The roster lists no Waynesburg University entry (directory, Mass Finder, GCatholic).
