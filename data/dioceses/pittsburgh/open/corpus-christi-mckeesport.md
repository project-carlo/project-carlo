## What is known

Corpus Christi Church, 2515 Versailles Avenue, McKeesport (the former St. Mary of Czestochowa, GCatholic 24187; the only church left of the 2010 Corpus Christi parish) belongs to Mary, Mother of God Parish. Weekly Mass there ended on 7 January 2023 (Tube City Online 2023-01-11; Mon Valley Independent 2022-12-22). The building was not closed: the parish's 25 August 2024 and 24 August 2025 bulletins say that a church without regular Sunday worship 'must still' have two Masses a year, on the dedication anniversary (2 May) and the feast day (Our Lady of Czestochowa, 26 August), and print those Masses (26 Aug 2024, 23 Oct 2024 make-up, 26 Aug 2025 at 6:30 p.m.). Baptisms, weddings and funerals are also held there. The parish masthead (23 August 2026) and site footer still list the church.

The 2026 bulletins read do not show the annual Masses: the intentions for 2 May 2026 (26 April issue), the week of 26 August 2026 (23 August issue), and the weeks of 7 June, 18 October and 25 October carry no Corpus Christi Mass. Bulletins from October 2025 are images and I did not read every issue.

## Why it is not decided

Rule 2 of this diocese makes occasional Masses printed in the bulletin a church row, but the last one printed was announced for 26 August 2025, and the 2026 dates show none, so a row now would give the map a church with no schedule. Nothing says the building was closed or sold, so `excluded/` with `closed` is not supported either. It was left here rather than modelled. Next step: read the bulletin for 2 May and 26 August 2026 around the grids (box on an inner page: the parish prints the notice a week or two before) and for 2027; if a Corpus Christi Mass reappears, add a church row under mary-mother-of-god-white-oak; if a decree or sale is found, exclude it as closed.
