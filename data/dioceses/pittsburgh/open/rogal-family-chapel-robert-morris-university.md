## What is known

Robert Morris University (6001 University Blvd., Moon Township) has a Catholic Campus Ministry/Newman Club. Its page (read 2026-10-23) lists a Sunday 'Evening Mass 8:00 p.m. in Rogal Chapel' preceded by confession at 7:30, and mentions 'weekly mass on campus'. The Ann and Alvin Rogal Family Chapel is described as non-denominational, open to all, and used 'for structured worship' by faiths on campus. Scout 9 found no RMU Mass in Most Sacred Heart's bulletin (that parish covers Moon Township churches), so no parish bulletin prints it. The Mass Finder and diocese directory have no RMU entry.

## Why it is not decided

Rule 1 needs the Mass to be public before it becomes a `no-bulletin` exclusion. The RMU pages show a campus-ministry Mass for students and say the chapel building is open to all, but none says the Mass itself is open to non-students, and the Mass is run by a student club. Unlike Duquesne, whose page invites any Catholic looking for a place to worship, nothing here invites the public. Next step: ask campus ministry, or find a bulletin that prints it. If it is public: excluded as `no-bulletin`; if it is for the community only: `no-public-mass`.
