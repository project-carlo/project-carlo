## What is known

Mother of Good Counsel Church, 7705 Bennett Street (Brushton/Homewood), founded 1907 as an Italian parish, merged in 1992 into St. Charles Lwanga and in 2020 into Saint Mary Magdalene. The diocese directory lists it with 'No Masses Scheduled' (2026-10-23). The parish bulletin's masthead still names it as one of its three churches, and the parish site keeps a page for it, a nav entry and a video of its windows, but in the twelve bulletins on Discover Mass (9 August to 25 October 2026) no Mass, intention, confession or adoration is placed there; its Sunday choir rehearses at St. Bede. The parish home page lists Mass only at St. Bede and St. James. No source I found says it has closed, been sold or been relegated.
