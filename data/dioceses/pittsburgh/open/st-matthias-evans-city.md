## What is known

St. Matthias, 417 E Main St., Evans City, is a church built in 1939 as a mission of St. Gregory, Zelienople; it became a parish in 1946 and on 1 July 2016 was absorbed by St. Gregory, now part of Divine Grace Parish (diocese history page, quoted above). The diocese says it "remained open after the merger" but does not say that Mass continues. GCatholic still lists the building. The diocese directory and Mass Finder do not list it. The 25 October 2026 Divine Grace bulletin and the parish Mass Times page list Masses only at Saint Ferdinand, Saint Gregory and Holy Redeemer. A stale Discover Mass listing says no Masses or services as of October 2018.

## Why it is not decided

No source I found says the building is closed, sold or no longer used (the diocese says it stayed open), so an exclusion with reason `closed` is not supported. Equally, no bulletin or schedule I read (the 25 Oct 2026 issue plus the May issues of 2024-2026 listed in the research item) shows a Mass there, so it is not a church row. A search-engine snippet suggested an annual Mass around the 14 May feast and the 30 May dedication anniversary since 2019; the May 2024-2026 bulletins show no such Mass, but the 30 May and following-week issues were not all checked for 2024 and 2025. The next step is the May 2027 bulletins and a call to the parish office. If Divine Grace confirms the building holds no public Mass, move this to excluded/ with reason `no-public-mass` or `closed` per what the office says.
