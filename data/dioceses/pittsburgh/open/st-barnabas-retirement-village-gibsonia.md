## What is known

The Saints Martha and Mary parish Mass Times page (read 2026-10-23) lists a 9:30 AM Mass at 'St. Barnabas Retirement Village' on the first Friday of each month. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Mass schedule (page 2, image) does not list it. The community is a retirement and care campus in Richland Township area (Gibsonia), address 5850 Meridian Road per web-search snippets.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing found says whether the Mass is public or for residents only; a care-community chapel Mass is usually residents-only, but that is an inference, not a source. Under the diocese's rule 1, a place whose public Mass is printed in a parish bulletin is a church row; the bulletin does not print it, and the parish site's mention does not say 'public'. Not a church row and not excluded until a source settles it.
