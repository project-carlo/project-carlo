## Holds Mass

Yes, daily except Monday. The parish bulletin of 18 October 2026 (page 1, an image) gives
Saturday 8:00 am and 4:00 pm (vigil), Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 am, and Tuesday–Friday 8:00 am.

## Name and address

The bulletin's masthead says "Divine Redeemer Catholic Church"; GCatholic, the diocese and
OSM agree on Divine Redeemer. Name flag true.

The address is not settled. The bulletin, GCatholic and the diocese's directory heading all
give 438 West Avenue, but the directory page also says "Church Address: 300 West Avenue
Mount Carmel, PA 17851". OSM has two place-of-worship buildings named Divine Redeemer Church:
one tagged 438 West Avenue (where the diocese's map pin sits) and one by West Cherry Street,
about 150 m east, where GCatholic's Plus Code (87G5QHXJ+7M) sits. The parish's Halloween
bingo is advertised at "Divine Redeemer Parish 400 Block W. Cherry St." (Our Lady of Hope
bulletin, 18 October 2026), so the Cherry Street building may be a hall. Kept 438 West Ave.
(bulletin and GCatholic) and GCatholic's coordinates; address flag false until a source says
which building is the church.
