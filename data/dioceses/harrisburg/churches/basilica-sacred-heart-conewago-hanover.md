## Holds Mass

The Basilica's own 18 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives "Saturday: 5:30pm, Sunday:
7:30am & 10:00am, First Friday of the Month: 7:00pm"; page 2 lists the weekend's intentions
and a Saturday 11:00 am Healing Mass. (Weekday Masses that week were cancelled for the
priests' retreat, as everywhere in the diocese.)

## Name and address

The bulletin and the diocesan directory both give "Basilica of the Sacred Heart of Jesus",
30 Basilica Drive, Hanover. Those two do agree, but **GCatholic has no entry for the
Basilica** (Conewago Chapel) at all, and OpenStreetMap was not checked, so the flags are
left false until a non-Church source (OSM) is compared. The church is in Conewago Township, Adams County, with a Hanover postal
address. Coordinates are the diocesan directory's map pin (39.818847, -77.03789), which
sits on the Basilica grounds; OpenStreetMap was not checked (Nominatim was rate-limiting).
