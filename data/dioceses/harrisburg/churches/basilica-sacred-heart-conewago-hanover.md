## Holds Mass

The Basilica's own 18 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives "Saturday: 5:30pm, Sunday:
7:30am & 10:00am, First Friday of the Month: 7:00pm"; page 2 lists the weekend's intentions
and a Saturday 11:00 am Healing Mass. (Weekday Masses that week were cancelled for the
priests' retreat, as across the diocese.)

## Name and address

The bulletin and the diocesan directory both give "Basilica of the Sacred Heart of Jesus",
30 Basilica Drive, Hanover; OpenStreetMap has the building (way 1251791839) on Basilica
Drive in Conewago Township, Adams County, which has a Hanover postal address. **GCatholic
has no entry for the Basilica** (Conewago Chapel): its two "St. Joseph, Hanover" entries
are St. Joseph's on Grandview Road and St. Joseph's former Baltimore Street site, neither
near Basilica Drive (6.9 km and 5.5 km away by GCatholic's own "nearby" list).
Coordinates from the OSM way, which agrees with the directory pin (39.818847, -77.03789).
