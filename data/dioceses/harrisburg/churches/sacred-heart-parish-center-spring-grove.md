## Holds Mass

The parish's 18 October 2026 Discover Mass bulletin gives Saturday 4:30 pm, Sunday 9:30 am
and Tuesday and Friday 12 noon, with that weekend's intentions, and its Mass-times page
says "All Masses and Services are now held at Parish Center, 1031 Sprenkle Rd". The diocesan
directory marks every Mass "(Parish Center)". Its chapel is the "Parish Center Chapel" the
bulletin mentions for the Tuesday Rosary.
