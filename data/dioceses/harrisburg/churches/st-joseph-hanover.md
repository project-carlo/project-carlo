## Holds Mass

The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday Vigil 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:00
and 10:00 am and 12 noon in Spanish, and Monday-Saturday 9:00 am; page 2 lists the
intentions for each. Neighbouring parishes' bulletins that week sent their people to
"Saint Joseph's Church in Hanover, Monday through Friday at 9:00am".

## Name and address

The bulletin gives the church (with the school) as **5125** Grandview Road and the parish
administrative center as **5055** Grandview Road; GCatholic 23759 and the diocesan
directory give 5055, the office address. I followed the bulletin for `line1` and left
`address_verified` false because the two other sources give the office number. The name
agrees everywhere. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87F5Q2C6+5X (39.7704375,
-76.9875625), on the Grandview Road campus, which agrees with the directory pin (39.771273,
-76.987933) to about 100 m. GCatholic's second "St. Joseph, Hanover" (23765, 236 Baltimore
St.) is the parish's former town-centre site: see `excluded/st-joseph-baltimore-street-hanover`.
