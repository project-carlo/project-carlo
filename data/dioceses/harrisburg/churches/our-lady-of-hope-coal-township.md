## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 11:00 am, and weekday
Masses here on Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 8:00 am; the deanery retreat on 24 October is
at "Our Lady of Hope Church".

## Name and address

Name: "Our Lady of Hope Church" in the bulletin; GCatholic, the diocese and OSM agree. Name
flag true.

The street number disagrees four ways: the bulletin's masthead gives 801 W. Chestnut St. for
the church and 863 for the "Office and Chapel"; GCatholic gives 832 W Chestnut St.; the
diocese's directory and ParishesOnline give 863 (the office); OSM's node is tagged 863. I
followed the bulletin's church address (801). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code
(87G5QCQP+22); OSM's node and the diocese's pin are 130–350 m west, likely on the office.
Address flag false. The diocese and GCatholic file it under Coal Township (17866); OSM puts it
in Shamokin 17872.
