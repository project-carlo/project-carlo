## Holds Mass

Yes, in summer. The diocese's page for St. Patrick, Carlisle lists "Saint Eleanor Regina
Chapel, Located in Pine Grove Furnace State Park" with Sunday 11:30AM Mass from Memorial
Day through Labor Day, and the parish's own bulletins carry "11:30 am ERC" in the Sunday
schedule from 24 May to 6 September 2026 and not in October. Reading "ERC" as Eleanor
Regina Chapel is an inference from that match (the bulletin doesn't spell it out). Kept as a
seasonal church, as Allentown kept St. Casimir, Shenandoah; `revisit` checks that the 2027
season returns.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Eleanor Regina", Chapel, gcatholic:23802, a mission of St. Patrick) gives
2140 Centerville Rd., Gardners, PA 17324, with no Plus Code. OpenStreetMap has the chapel on
Centerville Road at Pine Grove Furnace, Cooke Township (Cumberland County); its node gives
the coordinates. The diocese gives only "Pine Grove Furnace State Park". Name flag false
(GCatholic and the diocese agree on the name, but the bulletin abbreviates it); address flag
false (only GCatholic gives a street number). Gardners is the postal town (Adams County
ZIP) though the park is in Cumberland County. This GCatholic entry was not on the region's
assigned list but belongs to St. Patrick.
