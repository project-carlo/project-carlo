## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin lists Saturday 5:00 p.m., Sunday 9:00 a.m. (English) and
10:30 a.m. (Vietnamese), and weekday Masses Wednesday to Friday at noon.

## Name and address

GCatholic (23730) and the diocesan directory give 2121 N 3rd St., Harrisburg 17110, as does the
bulletin. GCatholic names the church "Our Lady of the Blessed Sacrament"; the directory and
bulletin use the parish's full name, adding "and the Vietnamese Martyrs". Both flags true.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G574J4+6R).
