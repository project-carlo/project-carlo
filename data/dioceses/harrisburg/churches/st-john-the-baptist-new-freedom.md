## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 7:30, 9:00 and 11:00 am
and weekday 9:00 am Masses "all in the Main Church except where noted", and a First
Friday 6:00 am Mass in the Historic Church; page 2 lists the intentions for 17–25 October.

This row is the parish campus at 315 N. Constitution Avenue: the Main Church, and the
Historic Church on the same campus (First Friday Mass only). The two buildings share the
address and nothing gives the Historic Church one of its own, so it has no separate row; a
reader of the schedule should know that the First Friday Mass is in the older building.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. John the Baptist", 315 N Constitution Ave.), the diocesan directory ("Saint
John the Baptist", 315 N Constitution Ave) and the bulletin agree. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code (87F5P8R5+P6), about 60 m from the directory's pin (both on the
campus).
