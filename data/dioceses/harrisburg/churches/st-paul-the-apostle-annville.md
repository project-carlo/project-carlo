## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin (Discover Mass) lists Saturday 5:00 PM and Sunday
8:00 and 10:30 AM Masses on both weekends it covers; the weekdays of 19-23 October have "NO
MASS" (the priests' continuing-education week). The diocese's page gives weekday Masses
Tuesday to Friday.

## Name and address

The bulletin and GCatholic give 125 S. Spruce Street, Annville; the diocese's directory
gives "125 Spruce St". Both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G58FJX+36),
within a few metres of the diocese's pin.
