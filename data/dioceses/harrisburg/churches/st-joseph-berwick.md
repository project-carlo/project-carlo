## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin: Thursday and Friday 8:30 am, Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:30
am. St. Mary's bulletin of the same date lists the same Masses "(St. Joseph's)".

## Name and address

The bulletin and GCatholic give the church at 721 Monroe Street, Berwick 18603;
ParishesOnline gives the same. The diocese's directory gives 730 Washington Street, which the
bulletin calls the "Rosello Parish Office". Followed 721 Monroe St. (bulletin and GCatholic
agree). Both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87H53Q42+98); OSM has the
building on the Fair Street side of the block.
