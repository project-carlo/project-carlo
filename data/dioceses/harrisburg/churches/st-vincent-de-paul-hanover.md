## Holds Mass

The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday 4:30 pm, Sunday 7:30 and
10:30 am, and Monday-Friday 8:00 am. Other parishes' bulletins that week (Basilica,
Abbottstown, Bonneauville) named St. Vincent's 8:00 am weekday Mass as the one still held
during the priests' workshop.
