## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 joint bulletin of St. Mary's, Fairfield and St. Rita's prints St.
Rita's schedule under its Blue Ridge Summit address (Saturday 4:30 pm, Sunday 8:30 and
11:00 am, weekdays 9:00 am) and, on page 2, "St. Rita Mass Intentions" for every day from
17 to 25 October. The parish site's footer (stritacc.org, 2026-10-19) and the diocesan
directory give the same weekend times.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Rita", 13219 Monterey Ln., "Blue Ridge Smt"), the diocesan directory
("Saint Rita", 13219 Monterey Ln, Blue Ridge Summit) and the bulletin (13219 Monterey Lane,
PO Box 365) agree; GCatholic's "Blue Ridge Smt" is the USPS abbreviation, written out here.
