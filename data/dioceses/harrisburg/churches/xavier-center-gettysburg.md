## Holds Mass

The 18 October 2026 Discover Mass bulletin (page 1) holds two Sunday Masses here: 9:00 am
bilingual and 12:00 pm Spanish, listed in the intentions on page 2 as "CJ/XC". The parish
school, on the same campus, also has a Friday 8:30 am school Mass ("8:30 am (School)" on
page 2; cancelled that week for the priests' workshop).

## Name and address

The bulletin ("Xavier Center/Centro Javier, 465 Table Rock Road Gettysburg, PA") and the
diocesan directory ("Saint Francis Xavier School & Xavier Center, 465 Table Rock Rd.",
with the same two Sunday Masses) agree. The parish office is next door at 455 Table Rock
Road. Neither GCatholic nor the directory has an entry of its own for this worship site.
No coordinates yet: OpenStreetMap's Nominatim found nothing for the address (and was
rate-limiting), so `pnpm geocode` should fill them; the directory's parish pin
(39.858678, -77.228068) is on this campus and can be used if geocoding fails.
