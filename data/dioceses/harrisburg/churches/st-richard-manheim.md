## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 am, and weekday
Mass Tuesday-Friday 9:00 am, with intentions for each. The directory agrees.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead gives "201 Adele Avenue", which the directory calls the "Rectory and
Mailing Address"; the directory, GCatholic and OSM all put the church at 110 N Oak St., which
is used. Both flags true. Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code 87G55J94+9C, on the OSM building.
