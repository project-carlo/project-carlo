## Holds Mass

Yes, once a month. The 18 October 2026 St. Peter, Columbia bulletin lists Mass on the first
Wednesday of each month at 6:00 pm, with adoration and confessions before it; the diocese's St.
Peter entry and Holy Trinity's Mass page give the same.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it "Mother of Holy Purity Chapel (St. Mary), Wrightsville" and "St. Mary's
Chapel"; the directory and Holy Trinity's site say "St. Mary Chapel"; OSM "Saint Marys Chapel".
The bulletin's full name is used; name_verified stays false because only the bulletin uses it.
Address 217 Maple St., Wrightsville from Holy Trinity's Mass page and OSM (address_verified
true). Coordinates from OSM way 1081900442. Wrightsville is in York County, across the
Susquehanna from Columbia; no roster lists the chapel.
