## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 5:00pm, Sunday 8:00am and 11:00am, and daily
Mass Monday-Saturday 9:00am and Thursday 6:00pm (none during the 19-23 October priests'
workshop).

Lancaster) agree, as does Discover Mass's listing; both flags true. The postal city is
Lancaster; the church is in Lancaster Township near Millersville, hence the slug.
