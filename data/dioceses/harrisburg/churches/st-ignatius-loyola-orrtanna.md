## Holds Mass

The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday Vigil 5:00 pm and Sunday 8:00 and
10:30 am, with weekday Masses Monday, Tuesday and Friday 10:00 am and Thursday 5:00 pm;
page 2 lists that weekend's Masses with intentions.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("St. Ignatius of Loyola Catholic Church", "1095 Church Road, Orrtanna"),
GCatholic 23753 ("St. Ignatius Loyola", 1095 Church Rd.) and the diocesan directory
("Saint Ignatius Loyola", 1095 Church Road) agree; the parish itself writes "of Loyola".
The church is in Hamiltonban/Franklin Township, Adams County, with an Orrtanna postal
address. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87F4WJ84+X3, a few metres from the
directory pin (39.917417, -77.394755). GCatholic's website for it,
saintignatiusmission.org, no longer resolves (probe url: DEAD-DNS).
