## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 4:30pm, Sunday 8:30am and 11:00am, and daily
Mass 8:30am Monday to Saturday.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead and the diocese's directory give 625 Union School Road, Mount Joy;
GCatholic gives 530 St Marys Dr., and OSM names the church on Saint Marys Drive (node
2043904735). Saint Marys Drive is probably the parish campus's internal road off Union School
Road, so both may be right; the parish's own address is followed and address_verified is
left false. The name agrees everywhere. Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code 87G54F2H+8P, which
sits on the OSM node and within 60 m of the directory's pin.
