## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 St. Peter bulletin lists the Sunday 1:00PM "College Mass HC" (Historic
Church); the parish's Mass page puts the Monday-Wednesday and Friday 8:00am Masses and the
7:00 am Holy Day Mass here, and the Elizabethtown College Mass on Sundays at 1:00 pm while
the college is in session. During the 19-23 October priests' workshop there is no daily Mass.

## Name and address

Only the parish names it: "Historic Church ~ One St. Peter Place, Elizabethtown" and "Historic
St. Peter Church". Neither the directory nor GCatholic lists it separately (the directory's
weekday line says "(Historic Church)"). OSM has a node "St. Peter's Catholic Church" on Aspen
Alley downtown, which is taken to be this building (the old parish church); its coordinates
are used. Flags false: one source.
