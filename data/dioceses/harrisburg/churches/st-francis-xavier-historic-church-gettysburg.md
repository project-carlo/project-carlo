## Holds Mass

The 18 October 2026 Discover Mass bulletin (page 1) holds most of the parish's Masses
here: Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 7:30 and 10:30 am (English), Monday-Thursday 7:00 am,
Wednesday 6:30 pm (Spanish) and Friday 12:00 pm, with Friday Adoration. Page 2 lists the
intentions, marked "(HC)".

## Name and address

The bulletin and the diocesan directory give the Historic Church as **25** W. High
Street; GCatholic 23752 gives **22** W High St. I followed the bulletin and the directory
(two sources against one), and left `address_verified` false because GCatholic disagrees on
the number; the street and the building agree. Its Plus Code 87F4RQH9+C6 (39.8285625,
-77.2319375) is used for the coordinates; it falls on the south side of West High Street,
where the church stands. The directory's own address for the parish, 455 Table Rock Road,
is the parish office, and its pin (39.858678, -77.228068) is on the Table Rock Road campus,
not this church.
