## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 St. Patrick bulletin lists Saturday 8:30 a.m., Sunday 8 am and
Monday and Tuesday 8:30 am Masses at the "Shrine". This is GCatholic's "St. Patrick" typed
Shrine (gcatholic:23801), the historic downtown church; the diocese's page lists it as
"Shrine Church" with Sunday 8:00AM and Saturday and Monday 8:30AM. It is public Mass, so it
is a church row.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("Shrine & Parish Office 152 E. Pomfret Street, Carlisle, PA 17013"), the
parish site ("Shrine Church, 152 East Pomfret Street"), the diocese's page and GCatholic
agree. Both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G45RX7+QP). The Saint
Katharine Drexel Perpetual Adoration Chapel next door (140 E. Pomfret Street) has no Mass.
