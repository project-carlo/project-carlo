## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin: Saturday 5:30 pm, Sunday 10:00 am, and weekday Masses
Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday at 9:00 am, "All Masses in Main Church" (the weekday
Masses of 19–23 October are cancelled for the priests' workshop).

## Name and address

The bulletin heads itself "Saint Monica Church, 109 Market Street, Sunbury" (the office is
"Rear 109 Market Street"); the diocese also gives 109 Market St. GCatholic and ParishesOnline
give 20 N Front St. The church stands at the corner of Market and Front Streets, so these are
probably two frontages of one property (inferred, not read). Kept GCatholic's 20 N Front St.
with its Plus Code (87G5V673+FH), about 160 m from the diocese's pin for 109 Market St.;
address flag false until a source says which street the church door is on.
