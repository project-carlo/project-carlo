## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin (John Patrick Publishing; page 1 is an image)
gives Saturday Vigil 4pm, Sunday 8:30am and 11am, and daily Masses Monday (in the chapel),
Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. The diocese's page marks the Sunday 8:30AM and Friday 7:00AM
Masses "(Outdoor Mass)", held on the church grounds in season.

directory (300 W Pine St); GCatholic gives 245 W Pine St. Followed the bulletin and the
diocese (two sources). GCatholic's name is "Holy Spirit"; the bulletin's "Holy Spirit
Catholic Church". Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G58C23+5C), about 50 m from the
diocese's map pin; both are on the church property.
