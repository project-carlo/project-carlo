## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 joint bulletin (self-hosted on stmarysfairfieldpa.org and on
Discover Mass) prints St. Mary's schedule under its Fairfield address (Saturday 5:00 pm,
Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 am, weekdays and Saturday 8:00 am) and, on page 2, "St. Mary Mass
Intentions" for each day from 17 to 25 October. The diocesan directory gives the same
weekend times.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("Immaculate Conception", 256 Tract Rd.), the diocesan directory ("Immaculate
Conception of the Blessed Virgin Mary", 256 Tract Road) and the bulletin ("Immaculate
Conception of the BVM “St. Mary’s”", 256 Tract Road) agree. Coordinates are GCatholic's
Plus Code. This church is in Adams County, outside the York/Franklin region; it is here
because it shares St. Rita's bulletin (coordinator's decision, 2026-10-19).
