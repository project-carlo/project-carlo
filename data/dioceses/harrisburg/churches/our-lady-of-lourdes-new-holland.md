## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 5:00 p.m., Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 a.m., and
weekdays Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 9:00 a.m. (no weekday Mass 19-23 October during
the priests' workshop). The directory agrees.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish site and the directory give 150 Water Street, New Holland; OSM has the
church building there (way 542288683). GCatholic gives no street and its Plus Code
(87G54W34+JJ) falls about 2.2 km east, on the First Baptist Church of New Holland (149 W.
Conestoga St., per OSM), so it is not used. Coordinates are OSM's (osm-node), within 30 m of the
directory's pin. Flags true: bulletin, directory and OSM agree.
