## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 bulletin lists Saturday Vigil 4:00 p.m. and Sunday 8:00 a.m. here,
weekday Mass Monday, Tuesday and Friday 8:30 a.m., and the holy-day Mass at 11 a.m.

## Name and address

GCatholic (23725) gives "732 Main St., Lykens"; the diocesan directory and the bulletin give
732 East Main Street. Same building (the borough's Main Street is numbered East/West); I used
"East Main" as the bulletin does. Both flags true.

Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G5H883+QC). The directory's map pin is wrong: it
repeats New Oxford's coordinates (39.864567, -77.056997), about 80 km south.
