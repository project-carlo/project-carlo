## Holds Mass

The St. Francis Xavier bulletin of 18 October 2026 lists a Sunday "5:00 pm (College)" Mass
with its intention, and Gettysburg College's Catholic Campus Ministry page says Mass is
"typically celebrated in Christ Chapel on Sundays at 5:00 p.m. when school is in session".
The parish's parochial vicar, Fr. Michael Pray, is named on both. This is a campus Mass in
term time only; it is public (no registration is asked). Christ Chapel is the college's
(Lutheran-founded) chapel, not a Catholic building; the place is in the dataset because
the Catholic Mass is celebrated there weekly.

## Name and address

Christ Chapel stands at the corner of North Washington and Stevens Streets on the
Gettysburg College campus (the campus ministry page) and OpenStreetMap has it as way
777729854 on North Washington Street. No street number was found in either source, so
`line1` is the street and `address_verified` stays false. Neither the diocesan directory
nor GCatholic lists it. Coordinates from the OSM way's centre (recorded as `osm-node`).

*Not yet confirmed:* that the 5:00 pm Mass continues outside term (the campus page says
"when school is in session"); check a December or summer bulletin.
