## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 St. Patrick bulletin (ParishesOnline 04/0437) holds the parish's
main weekend Masses here, Saturday 5:15 pm and Sunday 9:30 and 11:30 am in the "PAC Gym" of
the Parish Activity Center, plus Wednesday to Friday weekday Masses. The diocese's page
calls this the "Marsh Drive Church" (weekend Masses 5:15PM, 9:30 and 11:30AM).
