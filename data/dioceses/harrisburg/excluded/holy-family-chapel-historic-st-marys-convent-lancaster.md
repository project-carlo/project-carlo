## Why it is excluded

Open, with a weekly public Mass, but nothing the pipeline can read publishes it. The
Lancaster Latin Mass Community (of St. Joseph's parish) says on its schedule page, read 20
October 2026, that "Daily Mass is typically offered every Tuesday at 7:30 a.m." in Holy Family
Chapel, "on the 2nd floor of the old convent at Historic St. Mary's Church, 119 South Prince
St." Its other Mass, Sundays at 1:00 p.m., is in St. Joseph Church, which is already a church
row (`st-joseph-lancaster`).

Neither bulletin prints the chapel's Mass: the 18 October 2026 issue of St. Mary's
(`assumption-bvm-lancaster`) gives one schedule for the church itself, and St. Joseph's 18
October issue does not list the Latin Masses (see those parish records). The community's own
newsletter archive stops in 2019, and cancellations go out by email, so there is no current
issue to read. The chapel has no row of its own and is not in either roster.

## What would change this

The community posting a current newsletter or schedule PDF, or St. Mary's or St. Joseph's
bulletin printing the Tuesday Mass: then this becomes a church row under that publisher.
