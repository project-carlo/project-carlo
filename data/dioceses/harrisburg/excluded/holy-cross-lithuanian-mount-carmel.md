## Why it is excluded

Holy Cross, Mount Carmel was the town's Lithuanian parish (its school was staffed by the
Sisters of St. Casimir). The sisters' archive catalogue (read 2026-10-20) holds
"Correspondence regarding closing of Church (1995)", "Closing of Holy Cross Parish Remarks
by Sr. Joanella, and program (1995)" and correspondence on "selling of church building". So
the parish closed in 1995 and the church was to be sold. `last_mass` is the year only; the
catalogue gives no day. No current bulletin in Mount Carmel (Divine Redeemer, 18 October
2026) names it. Neither roster lists it; it is recorded because the brief asked for the
coal-region consolidations. Street address not found; which parish took its records was
not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. A 1995 news item (Shamokin News-Item) would give the day of the last Mass.
