## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Refuge Mission, 21169 Cross Road, Doylesburg (Path Valley, northern Franklin
County) appears to be open: the diocesan directory, read 2026-10-19, lists "Weekend Mass:
Sunday 8:30 AM" with confession before Mass and gives Very Rev. Allan F. Wolfe, V.F. (pastor
of Corpus Christi, Chambersburg) as its pastor. But nothing the pipeline could read
publishes its Mass: the mission has no website (the directory links none; GCatholic links
only the diocesan page), its Discover Mass page lists no bulletins, and the Corpus Christi
bulletin does not mention it in any of the 12 issues on Discover Mass (2 August to 18
October 2026, every page read for "Refuge" and "Doylesburg"). The Shippensburg bulletin
(the nearest other parish, read 11 and 18 October) does not mention it either.

So it is excluded as `no-bulletin`, not `closed`: nothing says it closed, and the
diocese's page still gives a Sunday Mass. Whether that Mass is still celebrated is only as
current as the directory page; I found no dated source for it.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule that lists its Mass (for example if Corpus Christi starts printing
the mission's Sunday 8:30 Mass), or a dated notice that the mission has closed. Ask the
Corpus Christi office (717-264-6317) or the mission's number (717-349-7953).
