## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists two "St. Joseph" churches in Hanover with the same phone number: 23759 at
5055 Grandview Rd. (the parish's administrative center, next to the church at 5125, now
`churches/st-joseph-hanover`) and this one, 23765, at 236 Baltimore St., in the town
centre about 3 km north. The parish's own history on the diocesan directory says its
Baltimore Street church (a former Methodist church bought in 1864, rebuilt and dedicated
in 1880) "was declared unsafe for public assembly in 1963" and razed, that Mass was then
said in the school auditorium until "a new church was completed in 1975", and that the
school left Baltimore Street for the Grandview Road campus in 2017, after which "the
former Baltimore Street school building ... is for sale". The current bulletin (18 October
2026) lists only the Grandview Road church and office. So no Mass is held at the Baltimore
Street site.

The exact date of the last Mass there is not stated: the history does not say where the
1975 church was built, only that the school auditorium was used until then (the school
was the Baltimore Street building of 1949-50; that it held the 1963-75 Masses is my
inference), so `last_mass` is left empty. It is not known which building GCatholic's Plus Code
87F5Q2XC+34 marks (the razed church's lot or the former school).

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the parish's worship site is on Grandview Road.
