## Why it is excluded

GCatholic (read 19 October 2026) lists an unnamed Ukrainian Catholic "(Mission)" in Lancaster:
"Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia ... Rite: Ukrainian
(Byzantine) ... Using the building: Church of St. John Neumann, Lancaster", 601 E Delp Rd. It is
an Eastern Catholic community of another jurisdiction, in the Harrisburg roster only because
GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the diocese's directory does not list it.
Excluded as `other-jurisdiction`, as for the other Eastern Catholic churches in this diocese.
The building itself is the church row `st-john-neumann-lancaster`. Whether the mission still
holds Divine Liturgy there (ParishesOnline has an empty "St Andrew Ukrainian Catholic Church"
at 1834 Lititz Pike) was not checked; the St. John Neumann bulletin of 18 October 2026 does
not mention it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs to the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia.
