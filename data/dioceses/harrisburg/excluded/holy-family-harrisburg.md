## Why it is excluded

Holy Family, 555 S. 25th St., Harrisburg (Allison Hill) is closed. The diocese's "Closed
Parishes and Records Locations" page (as of 21 November 2024, read 19 October 2026) lists "Holy
Family, Harrisburg" as a closed/merged parish under Saint Francis of Assisi, Harrisburg, and the
parish is gone from the diocese's directory. A drone-video page about the building ("Closed in
Summer 2023") says "The church had its last Mass on June 25th, 2023", which is the `last_mass`.
The 18 October 2026 bulletin of St. Francis, the parish it went into, lists Masses only at St.
Francis. GCatholic (23713) still lists Holy Family as a church.

The date rests on a secondary source (the video page); no diocesan announcement or news article
was found to confirm it.

## What would change this

A current St. Francis bulletin or diocesan source listing Mass at 555 S. 25th St. The
cathedral's directory page says Harrisburg Catholic Elementary School has a "Holy Family Campus"
(grades K-4), presumably the former Holy Family School; a school is not a public Mass site.
