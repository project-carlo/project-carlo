## Why it is excluded

GCatholic 23737 lists Sacred Heart, Spring Grove at 146 N Main St. The parish's own site
says, on its Mass-times page and homepage (read 19 October 2026), "All Masses and Services
are now held at Parish Center, 1031 Sprenkle Rd", and its general-information page "All
Masses at now at the Parish Center". The diocesan directory gives every Mass as "(Parish
Center)", and the 18 October 2026 bulletin names only the Parish Center. So there is no
Mass at the Main Street church; the worship site is `churches/sacred-heart-parish-center-spring-grove`.

When Mass moved, and whether the Main Street building was sold, is not stated anywhere I
found (web search for news turned up nothing), so `last_mass` is empty. Listings on
devout.org still show both addresses.

## What would change this

A bulletin announcing Mass at the Main Street church again.
