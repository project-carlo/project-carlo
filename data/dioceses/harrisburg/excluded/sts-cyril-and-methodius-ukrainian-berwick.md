## Why it is excluded

Sts. Cyril and Methodius, 706 N. Warren St., Berwick is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church. Its GCatholic page (read 2026-10-20) gives
"Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia", "within the territory of
the Roman Diocese of Harrisburg", "Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)". It is in the Harrisburg
roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the diocese's parish
directory does not list it. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction`, as for Eastern Catholic
churches in Allentown, Scranton and Syracuse. St. Mary's and St. Joseph's bulletins (18 October 2026) invite parishioners to its first-Saturday rosary ("SS. Cyril and Methodius Ukrainian Catholic Church prays the Rosary on the 1st Saturday of each month"), which confirms it is a Ukrainian Catholic church; whether it is open otherwise was not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it would belong to an Archeparchy of Philadelphia dataset, not Harrisburg's.
