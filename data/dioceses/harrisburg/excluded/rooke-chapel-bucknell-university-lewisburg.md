## Why it is excluded

Rooke Chapel on Bucknell University's campus in Lewisburg holds public Catholic Masses
("All are welcome to join the Catholic Campus Ministry for Mass: Sunday, 4 p.m. and 8 p.m.
at Rooke Chapel", bucknell.edu/ccm, read 2026-10-19), with a Thursday 5:30 pm Mass in the
Newman House chapel, 610 St. George St. But the campus ministry's weekly bulletin goes out
only by email ("please email ccm@bucknell.edu"), and the Sacred Heart of Jesus bulletin,
which names the chaplain, does not list these Masses. So it is open with nothing the
pipeline can read: `no-bulletin`. No roster lists it. The chapel is Bucknell's
interdenominational chapel, on Dent Drive per OpenStreetMap (way 38457589); no second source
for the address.

## What would change this

A campus-ministry bulletin or Mass schedule posted on the web (the blog
catholic.blogs.bucknell.edu, or a Discover Mass page), or the Sacred Heart bulletin starting
to list the campus Masses. Academic-year Masses only; the times above are from an undated
page.
