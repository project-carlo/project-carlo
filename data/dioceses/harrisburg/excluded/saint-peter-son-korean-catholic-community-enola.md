## Why it is excluded

Open, with public Mass, but no bulletin. The diocese lists St. Peter Son Korean Catholic
Community as a parish at 571 Valley Road, Enola, with Korean Mass Sunday and Wednesday at
10:00AM, pastor Rev. Sanghun Lee, and its website spskcc.weebly.com. That site (read
2026-10-19) gives the same address and Masses but publishes no bulletin, and says its
Facebook page is under construction; ParishesOnline's organization for it has no
publications, and Discover Mass's search finds no page. Under the brief, the community needs
its own parish row only if it publishes its own bulletin, so the place is excluded
`no-bulletin` rather than made a church of another parish: no other parish's bulletin lists
its Masses, and it worships in its own building at 571 Valley Road (not in Our Lady of
Lourdes, Enola, 225 Salt Road).

## What would change this

A weekly bulletin (주보) posted on the Weebly site, a Naver Band/Facebook page that posts
one, or a ParishesOnline/Discover Mass issue: then it becomes its own parish row with this
building as its church.
