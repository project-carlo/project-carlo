## Why it is excluded

St. Ann, 5408 Locust Lane, Harrisburg is a Byzantine (Ruthenian) Catholic parish, under the
Ruthenian Byzantine Catholic Metropolia of Pittsburgh, not the Diocese of Harrisburg. GCatholic (17214) lists
it in the diocese's territory with rite "Ruthenian"; its own site (read 19 October 2026) says
"Saint Ann's is a Byzantine Catholic parish in Harrisburg" that "belongs to the Ruthenian Church"
and celebrates the Divine Liturgy. Eastern Catholic churches are excluded as `other-jurisdiction`,
as in Allentown and Scranton.

## What would change this

A decision to include Eastern Catholic parishes in the dataset.
