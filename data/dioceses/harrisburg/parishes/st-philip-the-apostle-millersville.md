## Publisher

St. Philip the Apostle Parish, 2111 Millersville Pike (Lancaster postal address, Millersville
area), publishes its own weekly bulletin through J.S. Paluch. The 18 October 2026 issue (read
19 October on Discover Mass) gives on page 1 one "Liturgical Schedule": weekend Masses Saturday
5:00pm, Sunday 8:00am & 11:00am; daily Mass Monday-Saturday 9:00am, Thursday 6:00pm. The pastor
is also "Millersville University Campus Minister". The same PDFs are posted on the parish's own
bulletins page.
