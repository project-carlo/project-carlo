## Publisher

Iglesia Católica San Juan Bautista, 425 South Duke Street, Lancaster (the city's Hispanic
parish), publishes its own bilingual weekly bulletin on its Squarespace site. The 18 October
2026 issue (read 19 October) heads page 2 "Iglesia Católica San Juan Bautista ... 18 de Octubre
del 2026"; page 3 gives the address, "Párroco: Padre Luis R. Rodríguez", "Vicario parroquial:
Padre Kevin Key" and the weekday Masses. The weekend schedule is on the homepage and on the
linked May 2026 schedule: Saturday 5:00 PM (Spanish), Sunday 9:00 AM (English) and 12:30 PM
(Spanish) at San Juan Bautista.

Since 20-21 June 2026 San Juan Bautista and St. Anthony of Padua share a pastor and vicar
(Bishop's letter of 23 May 2026, on this site), and the 11:00 AM Spanish Mass at St. Anthony
is advertised on San Juan's homepage. The parishes stay canonically separate, and this bulletin
(all text pages grepped, image pages viewed) never mentions St. Anthony. St. Anthony's own
site could not be opened from the sandbox (`open/st-anthony-of-padua-lancaster`), so whether
it still prints a bulletin of its own is not known; the two are not merged.

## Worship sites

One church, `san-juan-bautista-lancaster`. The parish's Plaza San Juan Bautista / youth and
cultural centre at 441 S Lime St. hosts events (a retreat on 31 October), not Mass.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`, website the homepage, which links the two newest issues as
/s/Bulletin-MM-DD-YYYY.pdf. Half of each issue's pages are images (cover, flyers); the text
pages carry the masthead and Mass times. The LPi organization
st-john-the-baptist-san-juan-bautista has no publications; the Discover Mass page
san-juan-bautista-lancaster-pa lists none.
