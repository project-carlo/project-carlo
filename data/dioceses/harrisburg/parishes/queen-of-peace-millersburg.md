## Publisher

Queen of Peace, 202 Zimmerman Road, Millersburg publishes its own two-page weekly bulletin on its
eCatholic site (29106). I read the 18 October 2026 issue: its masthead is "QUEEN OF PEACE CATHOLIC
CHURCH, 202 Zimmerman Road, Millersburg", with Rev. Fr. Michael Opoki, A.J. as pastor, Sunday Mass
10:30 a.m., and Wednesday noon Mass in the week's intentions.

Fr. Opoki is also pastor of Our Lady Help of Christians, Lykens, which prints a separate bulletin
on its own eCatholic site (29061). Queen of Peace's masthead gives the Lykens office phone, and the
Lykens bulletin lists Queen of Peace's Masses, but each parish has its own issue every week, so
they are two rows.
