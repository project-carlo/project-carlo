## Publisher

St. John the Baptist, 315 North Constitution Avenue, New Freedom publishes its own weekly
bulletin and posts it through FATA Online (fataonline.com/bulletins/StJohnNF), linked as
"BULLETIN" from its site. I read the 18 October 2026 issue (8 pages): page 1 is headed
"Saint John the Baptist Catholic Church, October 18, 2026", names Rev. Philip G. Burger,
pastor, and gives one schedule "(all in the Main Church except where noted)": Saturday
5:00 pm; Sunday 7:30, 9:00 and 11:00 am; weekdays 9:00 am; First Friday 6:00 am (Historic
Church). Page 2 lists the week's Mass intentions.

## Worship sites

- St. John the Baptist, 315 N. Constitution Avenue, New Freedom (`st-john-the-baptist-new-freedom`).

The parish campus has a Main Church and a Historic Church ("the beautiful campus which
holds our Main Church, Historic Church, Parish Education building, and Parish outreach
building", sjbnf.org/welcome); the Historic Church has a First Friday 6:00 am Mass. It is
on the same campus and address, so it is carried in this church row rather than as a
separate one (see the church file). The Corpus Christi Perpetual Adoration Chapel is
attached to the Main Church.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`. The diocesan directory's "Parish Website" link is sjbnf.org (GCatholic's
stjncath.org does not resolve: `probe url` DEAD-DNS). The bulletin is on FATA Online, whose
page has a fixed "View Current Bulletin" link and a dated archive list of per-issue PDFs
(`/bulletin/bulletin-<n>.pdf`); `url:` publication is that page. The 18 October PDF is 48 MB,
large enough that `probe pdf` truncates it. Page 3 of the issue warns of a change: "as we
transition to a new publishing program", so the channel may move.
