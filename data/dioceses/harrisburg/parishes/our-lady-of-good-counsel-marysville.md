## Publisher

County, one pastor, Fr. Walter Guzmán) publish one weekly bulletin through J.S. Paluch / Discover
Mass. I read the 18 October 2026 issue: the page-1 masthead has a column for each church, Our Lady
of Good Counsel (121 William Street, Marysville: Saturday 4:00 p.m., Sunday 9:00 a.m., weekdays)
and Saint Bernadette (901 North High Street, Duncannon: Saturday 6:00 p.m., Sunday 11:00 a.m.),
and page 2's intentions list Masses at both ("OLGC" and "SB").
