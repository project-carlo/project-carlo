## Publisher

The Cathedral Parish of Saint Patrick, 212 State Street, Harrisburg publishes its own weekly
bulletin through J.S. Paluch / Discover Mass. I read the 18 October 2026 issue: page 1 is the
cathedral's bicentennial devotions, and page 2's "The Week Ahead in Our Parish" lists Saturday
5:30 p.m., Sunday 7:30 and 9:30 a.m. and 12:15 p.m., and weekday Masses at noon (Very Rev. Joshua
R. Brommer is rector and pastor per the directory).

The diocese's closed-parishes list puts St. Patrick, Sacred Heart of Jesus and St. Lawrence
(Harrisburg) under the Cathedral Parish. St. Lawrence's church is now the FSSP quasi-parish
(`st-lawrence-harrisburg`), which prints its own bulletin. Sacred Heart of Jesus, Harrisburg is in
neither roster and this bulletin lists no Mass there.

## Worship sites

- Cathedral of St. Patrick, 212 State St., Harrisburg (`st-patrick-cathedral-harrisburg`).

Bicentennial-week Masses at Harrisburg Catholic Elementary School (a school Mass on 30 October)
are not a public worship site.

## Website and bulletin provider

The cathedral's site embeds the Discover Mass id; the church page lists issues to 18 October
2026. `discover_mass`. GCatholic omits the cathedral, so its church row comes from the directory
alone.
