## Publisher

Christ the King Mission, Benton publishes its own weekly bulletin, posted both on its
Squarespace site and on Discover Mass. The 18 October 2026 issue (5 pages) has the mission's
contact block (300 Mendenhall Lane, P.O. Box 297, Benton; Very Rev. Richard J. Mowery,
pastor; Rev. Aaron Lynch, parochial vicar; its own secretary), its own Mass intentions
(Saturday and Sunday each weekend; a Monday communion service this week) and its own
collection report.

It is a mission of St. Columba, Bloomsburg (same pastor), which prints its contact details,
but St. Columba's bulletin gives no Mass here and the mission's own channels are live, so it
is a row of its own.
