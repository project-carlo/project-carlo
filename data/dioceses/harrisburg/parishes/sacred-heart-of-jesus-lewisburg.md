## Publisher

Sacred Heart of Jesus Parish, Lewisburg publishes one weekly bulletin for its two churches,
through Discover Mass. The 18 October 2026 issue (8 pages) describes "Sacred Heart Parish,
Sacred Heart & Saint George Churches" and gives one schedule: Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:00
am and 12:00 noon at Sacred Heart Church (SHC), Sunday 10:00 am at Saint George Church (SGC),
Mifflinburg; weekday Masses Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 9:00 am at SHC.

The diocese lists St. George Mission, Mifflinburg as its own entry, with Sacred Heart's
website, phone and pastor; the bulletin shows it is part of this one.
