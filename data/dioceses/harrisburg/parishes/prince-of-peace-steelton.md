## Publisher

Prince of Peace, Steelton publishes its own weekly bulletin through J.S. Paluch / Discover Mass.
I read the 18 October 2026 issue: page 1's masthead is "Prince of Peace Roman Catholic Parish,
Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary Church", 815 South 2nd Street, Steelton, with Rev. Matthew
R. Larlick as pastor and weekend Masses Saturday 4:00 p.m. and Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 a.m.

Prince of Peace is the parish formed from Steelton's ethnic parishes; the diocese's
closed-parishes page lists Assumption BVM, St. Ann, St. James, St. Peter the Apostle and St. John
the Evangelist (Enhaut) under it. Its worship site is the former Assumption BVM church.

## Worship sites
