through Discover Mass (J.S. Paluch). I read the 18 October 2026 issue: the page-1 masthead
names "Annunciation of the Blessed Virgin Mary Church", 26 North Third Street, with Rev.
Richard Lyons as pastor, and gives the schedule Saturday Vigil 4:00 pm; Sunday 8:00, 10:00
