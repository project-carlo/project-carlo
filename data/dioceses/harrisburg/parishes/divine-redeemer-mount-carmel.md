## Publisher

Divine Redeemer Parish, Mount Carmel publishes its own weekly bulletin through LPi /
ParishesOnline (container 03/1006). I read the 18 October 2026 issue: page 1 (an image) has
the masthead "DIVINE REDEEMER CATHOLIC CHURCH", Rev. Bernard Wamayose, pastor, and one Mass
schedule (Saturday 8:00 am and 4:00 pm vigil; Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 am; Tuesday–Friday
8:00 am; no Mass Monday), with the parish address 438 West Avenue. It names no other worship
site. Page 8's LPi footer reads "Divine Redeemer Church, Mount Carmel, PA 03-1006".

The diocese gives Divine Redeemer no grouping. Our Lady of Mount Carmel, two blocks away, has a
different pastor (Msgr. Karwacki) and is not in this bulletin.

## Worship sites

- `divine-redeemer-mount-carmel`: Divine Redeemer Church, the only site the bulletin names.

The parish was formed from Mount Carmel's older ethnic parishes; their buildings are not in
either roster for this region and no current source read here names them, so none is
recorded (see *Not yet confirmed*).
