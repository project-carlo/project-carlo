## Publisher

St. Andrew the Apostle, 12 North Broad Street, Waynesboro publishes its own weekly
bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing and listed at church-bulletin.org (id 253).
The 18 October 2026 issue is image-only; rendered, page 1 reads "12 North Broad Street in
Waynesboro, PA 17268 / Sunday, October 18, 2026 / A parish staffed by the Missionaries of
the Sacred Hearts of Jesus and Mary", names Rev. Christopher Onyeneke, MSSCC, pastor, and
page 3's "Mass Schedule" lists the Saturday 5:00 pm anticipated Mass, Sunday 8:00 and
11:00 am, and weekday Masses with intentions. One church.
