## Publisher

St. Columba, Bloomsburg publishes its own weekly bulletin through Discover Mass (J.S.
Paluch). The 25 October 2026 issue (already posted on 19 October) is headed "Saint Columba
Catholic Church, 342 Iron Street, Bloomsburg" with one schedule: Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday
8:00 and 10:30 am, Tuesday–Friday 7:30 am, Saturday 8:00 am.

Its pastor, Very Rev. Richard Mowery, also has Christ the King Mission, Benton, and the
bulletin prints the mission's contact block ("Christ the King Mission, Benton – See next page
for contact information"). But it gives no Mass at the mission, and Christ the King
publishes **its own** bulletin (its own Discover Mass page and its own PDFs on ctkbenton.org,
live the same week), so the mission is its own row.
