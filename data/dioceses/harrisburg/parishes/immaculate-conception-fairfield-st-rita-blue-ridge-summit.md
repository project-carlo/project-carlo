## Publisher

Immaculate Conception of the BVM ("St. Mary's"), Fairfield (Adams County) and St. Rita,
Blue Ridge Summit (Franklin County) share a pastor (Rev. Peter DiTomasso, MSSCC, with
Rev. Marsianus Leu, MSSCC) and one office at 256 Tract Road, Fairfield, and publish **one**
weekly bulletin. I read the 18 October 2026 issue (4 pages) in three copies: on Discover
Mass under St. Rita's slug, on the Fairfield site (`irp.cdn-website.com/.../2026-10-18.pdf`)
and on St. Rita's site. Page 1 is headed "The Catholic Churches of Immaculate Conception of
the BVM “St. Mary’s” Saint Rita’s Parish" and prints both addresses, each with its own Mass
schedule; page 2 lists "St. Mary Mass Intentions" (Sat 5:00 pm, Sun 8:00 and 10:30 am,
weekdays 8:00 am) and "St. Rita Mass Intentions" (Sat 4:30 pm, Sun 8:30 and 11:00 am,
weekdays 9:00 am), and the collections for each church.

The two are canonically separate parishes in different counties (and the diocese lists
them separately), but one bulletin means one row. Fairfield sits in the Adams County
region; the coordinator agreed (2026-10-19) that this row carries both churches.

## Worship sites

- St. Rita, 13219 Monterey Lane, Blue Ridge Summit (`st-rita-blue-ridge-summit`).
- Immaculate Conception of the BVM, 256 Tract Road, Fairfield (`immaculate-conception-fairfield`).

No other building appears in the bulletin's schedules, and the diocese and GCatholic list
none for either parish.

## Website and bulletin provider

`discover_mass`, slug `st-rita-blue-ridge-summit-pa`: the Discover Mass page listed the 4,
11 and 18 October issues on 2026-10-19 and is the one channel that needs no site-specific
recipe. The same PDFs are self-hosted on both parish sites, recorded as `url:`
publications: the Fairfield site's `/bulletin` page (Duda, dated file names such as
`2026-10-18.pdf`) and St. Rita's `/bulletins/` page, a WordPress "WP File Download"
widget with one folder per month (October 2026 is category 169; the files are listed by
`admin-ajax.php?action=wpfd&task=files.display&view=files&id=169`). `website` is St. Rita's
bulletin page. The PDFs have a text layer.
