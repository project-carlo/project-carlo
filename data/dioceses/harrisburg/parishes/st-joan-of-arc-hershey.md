## Publisher

Saint Joan of Arc, Hershey publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by J.S. Paluch: the same
issue is on Discover Mass and on the parish's own (Weebly) site. I read the 18 October 2026 issue
in both places; page 1 is identical, naming this parish alone (Very Rev. Alfred P. Sceski, V.F.,
pastor) with the schedule Saturday Vigil 5:00 p.m.; Sunday 7:30, 9:30, 11:30 a.m. and 5:00 p.m.;
weekdays 6:45 and 9:00 a.m.

## Worship sites

- Saint Joan of Arc, 359 W. Areba Ave., Hershey (`st-joan-of-arc-hershey`).

The directory lists the Dominican chaplains at Penn State Health Milton S. Hershey Medical Center
among the parish clergy; the hospital chapel is not in this bulletin's Mass schedule and is not a
public parish Mass site, so it has no row.
