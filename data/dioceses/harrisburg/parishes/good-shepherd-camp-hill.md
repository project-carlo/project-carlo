## Publisher

Good Shepherd, 3435 Trindle Road, Camp Hill, publishes its own weekly bulletin through
Diocesan (Discover Mass). The 18 October 2026 issue (12 pages) names Good Shepherd Catholic
Church alone, Rev. Neil S. Sullivan, pastor, with Saturday 5:00 p.m. and Sunday 7:30, 9:30
and 11:30 a.m. Masses in the church and weekday Masses Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and
Friday at 8:30 a.m.

## Worship sites

- Good Shepherd, Camp Hill (`good-shepherd-camp-hill`). The church's weekday chapel is in
  the same building (confessions, rosary, adoration "Chapel • Entrance A"). The diocese's
  page lists an "Associated Location", the Good Shepherd Parish Education Center at 3400
  Market Street, which is a school/education building, not a worship site.
