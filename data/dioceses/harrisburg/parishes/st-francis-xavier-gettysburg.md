## Publisher

Saint Francis Xavier Catholic Parish, Gettysburg publishes one bilingual weekly bulletin
through Discover Mass. I read the 18 October 2026 issue: its page-1 masthead lists three
addresses (Parish Office, 455 Table Rock Road; Historic Church, 25 W. High Street; Xavier
Center/Centro Javier, 465 Table Rock Road), Fr. Timothy D. Marcoe as pastor and Fr. Michael
Pray as parochial vicar, and a Mass schedule split between the Historic Church and the
Xavier Center. Page 2's intentions add a Sunday "5:00 pm (College)" Mass and a Friday
"8:30 am (School)" Mass. No partner parish.

## Worship sites

- St. Francis Xavier Historic Church, 25 W High St (`st-francis-xavier-historic-church-gettysburg`):
  Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 7:30 and 10:30 am, weekdays.
- Xavier Center / Centro Javier, 465 Table Rock Rd (`xavier-center-gettysburg`): Sunday
  9:00 am bilingual and 12:00 pm Spanish; the school Mass is on the same campus.
- Christ Chapel, Gettysburg College (`christ-chapel-gettysburg-college`): the Catholic
  Campus Ministry's Sunday 5:00 pm Mass in term, served by Fr. Pray. Listed in no roster.

The diocesan directory's single entry (address 455 Table Rock Road, the office) covers the
first two; GCatholic's single entry (23752, "22 W High St.") is the Historic Church. The
parish cemetery (386 West High Street) has no Mass.
