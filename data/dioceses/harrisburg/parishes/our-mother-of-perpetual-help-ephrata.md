## Publisher

The Church of Our Mother of Perpetual Help, 320 Church Avenue, Ephrata, publishes its own weekly
bulletin on its eCatholic site. The 18 October 2026 issue (read 20 October) has the page 1
masthead "Church of Our Mother of Perpetual Help, Diocese of Harrisburg / Staffed by the
Redemptorists, 320 Church Avenue, Ephrata, PA 17522", Fr. Paul Borowski, C.Ss.R. as pastor, and
one Mass schedule: Saturday 5:00 p.m.; Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 11:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. (Life
Teen/Edge); weekdays 9:00 a.m. (Thursday 7:00 a.m.). No other public worship site.

## Worship sites

One church, `our-mother-of-perpetual-help-ephrata`. St. Clement's Mission House (the
Redemptorists' residence and assisted-living house) has Mass "in private", so it is not a
public worship site and gets no record. Our Mother of Perpetual Help School (330 Church Ave.)
is a school.
