## Publisher

Corpus Christi, Chambersburg publishes its own weekly bilingual (English/Spanish) bulletin
through J.S. Paluch's Discover Mass. I read the 18 October 2026 issue (8 pages): page 1 is
headed "October 18, 2026 / 320 Philadelphia Avenue, Chambersburg, PA 17201 / Corpus Christi
Catholic Church / Iglesia Católica Corpus Christi", names Very Rev. Allan F. Wolfe, V.F.,
pastor, and gives one Mass schedule (Saturday 5:00 pm; Sunday 8:00, 10:00 am, 12:30 pm
Spanish, 5:00 pm, 6:30 pm Spanish; weekday Masses), all at the parish church; page 3, "This
Week at Corpus Christi", lists the week's Masses with intentions.

## Worship sites

- Corpus Christi, 320 Philadelphia Avenue, Chambersburg (`corpus-christi-chambersburg`).

The diocesan directory gives Fr. Wolfe as pastor of Our Lady of Refuge Mission, Doylesburg
too, but the 12 Corpus Christi issues on Discover Mass (2 August to 18 October 2026, all 8
pages read) never mention Doylesburg or "Refuge"; that mission is excluded as
`no-bulletin` (`our-lady-of-refuge-doylesburg`). The directory's "Corpus Christi Adoration
Chapel" (perpetual adoration) is at the church's own address and has no Mass schedule of
its own; Corpus Christi School (305 N Second St.) is a school.
