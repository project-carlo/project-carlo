share a pastor (Fr. Augusty Valomchalil), an office at St. Mark, a website
(stmarkstlukeparish.org) and **one** weekly bulletin on Discover Mass. I read the 18 October
2026 issue: the cover is titled "St. Mark the Evangelist / St. Luke the Evangelist

Sunday 9:00 am, Monday–Saturday 9:00 am; St. Luke, Overhill Drive & Black Road,
Mercersburg: Sunday 11:00 am) and lists the week's intentions, with "St. Luke Mission 11:00
AM" on each Sunday.
