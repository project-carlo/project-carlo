## Publisher

Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary ("St. Mary's"), 2 North 8th Street, Lebanon, and its
mission, Our Lady of Fatima, Jonestown, publish one weekly bulletin, printed by Diocesan
(Discover Mass). I read the 18 October 2026 issue: the page-1 masthead names both churches
with their addresses and gives each its own Mass schedule, and the page-2 footer reads
"Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary Church Lebanon ● Our Lady of Fatima Mission
Jonestown". The intentions mark each Mass "BVM" or "FM".

## Worship sites

- Assumption of the BVM, Lebanon (`assumption-bvm-lebanon`): Saturday 5:30pm, Sunday 7:30 and
  10:30am, weekdays 8:00am.
- Our Lady of Fatima Mission, Jonestown (`our-lady-of-fatima-jonestown`): Sunday 9:00am, Holy
  Day vigils 7:00pm. The diocese lists the mission as its own entry
  (`directory:our-lady-of-fatima-mission-jonestown`), with the parish's website and the note
  that weekday Masses are "To Be Announced on a weekly basis on Assumption BVM" pages.

St. Mary's.
