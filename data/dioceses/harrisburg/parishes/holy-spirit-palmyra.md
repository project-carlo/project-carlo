## Publisher

Holy Spirit, 300 West Pine Street, Palmyra, publishes its own weekly bulletin through John
Patrick Publishing (church-bulletin.org). I read the 18 October 2026 issue: the page-1
masthead names Holy Spirit Catholic Church alone, Fr. Michael Metzgar, pastor, with
Saturday 4pm and Sunday 8:30am and 11am Masses and daily Masses Monday to Friday.

## Worship sites

- Holy Spirit, Palmyra (`holy-spirit-palmyra`). The diocese's page lists the Sunday 8:30am
