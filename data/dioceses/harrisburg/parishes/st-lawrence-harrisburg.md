## Publisher

The quasi-parish of Saint Lawrence of Rome, Deacon and Martyr (the Mater Dei community, staffed by
the Priestly Fraternity of St. Peter, Traditional Latin Mass) publishes its own weekly bulletin on
its own site. I read the 18 October 2026 issue: the page-1 masthead is "St. Lawrence Catholic
Church, Home of the Mater Dei Community, Staffed by the Priestly Fraternity of St. Peter, 110 State
Street, Harrisburg", with Sunday Masses at 7:30, 9:00 and 10:30 a.m. (High) and 6:00 p.m., and
daily Masses.

St. Lawrence was once a parish of its own; the diocese's closed-parishes list puts "Saint
Lawrence, Harrisburg" under the Cathedral Parish of St. Patrick, which keeps its records. The
building is now this quasi-parish's church.
