## Publisher

Our Lady of Hope, Coal Township publishes its own weekly bulletin through LPi /
ParishesOnline (container 03/0080). I read the 18 October 2026 issue (4 pages, text). Its
masthead is "OUR LADY OF HOPE CHURCH … 801 W. CHESTNUT ST", with "Our Lady of Hope Office and
Chapel, 863 W. Chestnut St." and its own staff. Weekend Masses are Saturday 4:00 pm and
Sunday 11:00 am. The weekday schedule is shared with the other two parishes of the friars
(Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8:00 am here; Tuesday and Thursday at St. Patrick's; Tuesday,
Thursday and Saturday at Mother Cabrini).

Fr. Nicholas Spano is pastor of Our Lady of Hope, St. Patrick and Mother Cabrini, but each
parish prints its own bulletin (see `mother-cabrini-shamokin`), so this is its own row.

## Worship sites

- `our-lady-of-hope-coal-township`: Our Lady of Hope Church, W. Chestnut St.

The bulletin also names an "Office and Chapel" at 863 W. Chestnut St. ("12:00 PM - Holy Hour
at OLOH Chapel"); it lists no Mass in that chapel apart from the church, so it is not a
separate row.
