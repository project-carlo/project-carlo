## Publisher

Immaculate Conception BVM ("Saint Mary's Parish"), Berwick publishes its own weekly bulletin
on its eCatholic site (id 29170). The 18 October 2026 issue's masthead names St. Mary's
Parish, 1730 Fowler Avenue, with Fr. William Barbee as pastor and its own office and staff;
its Mass schedule is Tuesday and Wednesday 8:30 am, Saturday 5:30 pm, Sunday 10:00 am, and a
Spanish Mass at 12:30 pm on the third Sunday.

Fr. Barbee is also pastor of St. Joseph, Berwick, and page 2 of this bulletin lists the week's
Masses at both churches. But St. Joseph prints **its own** bulletin (ParishesOnline 04/0818,
"Saint Joseph Parish" masthead, its own office at 730 Washington Street and staff), live the
same week, so the two are separate rows grouped by the shared pastor.

## Worship sites

- `immaculate-conception-berwick`: St. Mary's church, 1730 Fowler Ave.
