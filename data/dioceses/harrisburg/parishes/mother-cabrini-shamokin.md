## Publisher

Mother Cabrini, Shamokin publishes its own weekly bulletin and posts it on the shared
revivingtogether.com site. I read the 18 October 2026 issue (5 pages): its masthead is
"Mother Cabrini Church, 214 North Shamokin Street", with "Cabrini Parish Staff" and a
"Cabrini Mass Schedule" (Saturday 4 pm vigil, Sunday 8:30 am; weekday Masses Tuesday,
Thursday and Saturday 8 am at Mother Cabrini and Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8 am at Our
Lady of Hope).

The friars (Conventual Franciscans) serve three parishes: Fr. Nicholas Spano is "Pastor of
Our Lady of Hope, Mother Cabrini and St. Patrick Churches". They share the site and the
weekday Mass rota, but **each prints its own bulletin**: the 18 October issues of Our Lady of
Hope (ParishesOnline 03/0080, "Our Lady of Hope Church" masthead and staff) and of St.
Patrick, Trevorton (a 2-page sheet) are different documents with their own staff,
intentions and collections. So three rows, grouped by the shared pastor.

## Worship sites

- `mother-cabrini-shamokin`: Mother Cabrini Church, 214 N. Shamokin St.

The weekday Masses this bulletin lists at Our Lady of Hope belong to that church's own row.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`: the bulletin is a PDF in a Wix pdf-flipbook widget on
https://www.revivingtogether.com/shamokin (the diocese's website for the parish). The archive
page holds older, hash-named PDFs with no dates. The ParishesOnline organization and the
Discover Mass page for Mother Cabrini publish nothing. See the fetch check in *Not yet
confirmed*.
