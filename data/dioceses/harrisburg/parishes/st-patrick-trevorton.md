## Publisher

St. Patrick, Trevorton puts out its own short weekly sheet and posts it on the shared
revivingtogether.com site. The 18 October 2026 issue (2 pages) has no masthead, but it is
the PDF in the flipbook on the St. Patrick page, its Masses are this church's (Sunday 9:00
am; Tuesday and Thursday 8:00 am, as the Our Lady of Hope bulletin also gives "Tuesday and
Thursday, 8:00 AM @ St. Patrick's"), and it reports a burial in Saint Patrick Cemetery and the
Trevorton American Legion dinner.

Fr. Nicholas Spano, OFM Conv. is pastor of St. Patrick, Our Lady of Hope and Mother Cabrini;
each parish prints its own bulletin (see `mother-cabrini-shamokin`).

## Worship sites

- `st-patrick-trevorton`: St. Patrick Church, 331 W. Shamokin St.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`: a Wix pdf-flipbook on https://www.revivingtogether.com/trevorton, file names like
"101826.pdf". The ParishesOnline organization and the Discover Mass page publish nothing.
