## What is known

Sacred Heart of Jesus, 140 E. Market St., Williamstown (Dauphin County), a former mission of
Lykens and later a parish, is listed by GCatholic (23794, website the defunct
catholiclykenswilliamstown.org, which no longer resolves) but not by the diocesan directory. The
diocese's closed-parishes page (as of 21 November 2024) lists "Sacred Heart of Jesus,
Williamstown" under Our Lady Help of Christians, Lykens, meaning the parish was closed or merged
into Lykens. The current Lykens bulletin (11 October 2026) and the Lykens website list Mass only
at Lykens (and at Queen of Peace, Millersburg); neither mentions Williamstown.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to the church no longer having Mass, but nothing I found says the building
closed or when: the closed-parishes page is about the parish (corporation), not the building, and
a web search found no closure notice or news story. A dated source (a Lykens bulletin
announcement, a diocesan decree, local news) would let this be excluded as `closed`; a Lykens
bulletin listing Mass there would make it a church of `our-lady-help-of-christians-lykens`.
