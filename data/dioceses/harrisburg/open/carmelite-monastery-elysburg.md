## What is known

The Carmelite Monastery of Jesus, Mary and Joseph, 430 Monastery Rd., Elysburg, is an
enclosed community of Discalced Carmelite nuns (traditional Latin rite), founded in 2009 from
the Carmel of Valparaiso, Nebraska, with an FSSP chaplain who "will provide daily Mass for
the convent" (FSSP, 2009). The 2009 enclosure Mass was public ("many faithful present"), and
monastery chapels of this kind usually admit the public to the daily Mass behind the grille.
But I found no current Mass schedule: not on GCatholic, not in the Elysburg parish bulletin
(18 October 2026), and no monastery website. The diocese's directory does not list it.

## Why it is not decided

Whether the chapel holds a public Mass now, and at what time, is unknown, and there is no
bulletin. If a current source shows a public Mass it becomes `excluded/` with `no-bulletin`
(as the Padre Pio Centre in Allentown); if it says the chapel is private, `no-public-mass`.
