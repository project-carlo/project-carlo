## What is known

Ss. Peter and Paul Church, 130 Indiana St., Grassflat, is a church of the Catholic Parishes of Eastern Clearfield County (canonically a secondary church of St. Agnes, Morrisdale; the directory mails c/o Christ the King, Houtzdale). The bulletin of 25 October 2026 (page 4) says 'No regular Masses'. The 2023 Clearfield Deanery plan removed its weekend Mass and the directory lists no Mass times. The bulletin's own Church Name Key defines a code for it (as for every church) but the 13 issues from 2 August to 25 October 2026 never use it on a Mass-intentions line. GCatholic files it under Frenchville 16836 on Grassflat Ave. (the erie city alias Grassflat to Frenchville covers the naming); the bulletin and the directory detail page give 130 Indiana Street, Grassflat 16839, and the printable directory 'Grassflat Ave., Grassflat'. The directory pin (40.995751, -78.122912) is about 1 km from GCatholic's Plus Code.

Other Roman Catholic church of the same dedication in the cluster: the sister Ss. Peter and Paul church (Hawk Run / Grassflat) has the same status; the two share the Church Name Key. An Eastern Catholic church (St. John the Baptist Byzantine, Hawk Run) is a separate building and belongs to another batch.

## Why it is not decided

No recurring Mass is shown, so it is not a church row. No current source says that a patronal-feast Mass is the only Mass (the bulletin says only 'No regular Masses'), so it cannot yet be excluded as no-public-mass under the Erie secondary-church rule. The issues on the parish's page start on 2 August 2026, so the patronal feast (29 June) is outside what could be read. The June 2027 bulletins (or the parish office) would settle it.
