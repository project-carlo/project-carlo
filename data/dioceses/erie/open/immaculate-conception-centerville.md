## What is known

Immaculate Conception Church, 17932 Wright Road, Centerville (the directory also files it under the hamlet of Mageetown), is a church of St. Titus Parish, Titusville. The diocese's 6 October 2026 printable directory calls it 'Secondary Church' (the 2023-24 plans' term for a church with no weekend Mass and, at most, Mass on the patronal feast) and lists no Mass times; the web detail page shows 'Saturday: None | Sunday: None'. The 2016 decree merging St. Walburga into St. Titus says Immaculate Conception mission church in Mageetown 'was still in use at the beginning of 2016'. No decree relegating it appears on the diocese's decrees page (74 PDFs read 2026-10-23), so the building is not known to be closed.

St. Titus's bulletin (53 weekly issues, 26 October 2025 to 25 October 2026, all read) names no Mass at the church. Discover Mass has a page for it (`immaculate-conception-centerville-pa`, 'no bulletins available') with no Mass times.

## Why it is not decided

The church is neither shown holding a recurring Mass (so it is not a church row) nor shown to have only an annual feast Mass (so it is not yet `no-public-mass`): no current source describes its Masses at all. Its patronal feast is 8 December (Immaculate Conception), which falls in the next St. Titus bulletins; a Mass there on that day, or a dedication-anniversary Mass, would settle it as an occasional-Mass secondary church. Likewise the church is not excluded as closed, because nothing says it closed.
