## What is known

St. Venantius Church, 403 Main St., Rouseville, is a secondary church of St. Joseph Parish, Oil City (the 6 October 2026 printable directory: 'Secondary Church', no Mass times; web detail page 'Saturday: None | Sunday: None'). It came to St. Joseph through two mergers: St. Venantius Parish into St. Stephen Parish (decree effective 13 February 2017; the church became a secondary church without mission status), then St. Stephen into St. Joseph (effective 1 January 2020). The 2016 decree forbids Sunday, holy-day and vigil Masses there and requires an annual Mass on the church's dedication anniversary (dedicated 24 June 1923) and its title solemnity (5 August). No decree relegating it appears on the diocese's decrees page (74 PDFs read 2026-10-23); the 'Oil City Parishes' letter of 10 July 2023 (image-only, rendered and read) concerns the Stephen-Joseph recourse, not Venantius.

St. Joseph's bulletin (12 issues, 2 August to 18 October 2026) lists no Mass at Rouseville. The one trace is stale hidden text in the 2 August issue: 'Thursday, April 30th 6:00pm @ St. Venantius', a week-of-26-April Mass intention left under the layout. It suggests some Mass (not a weekly one) at the church on 30 April 2026, but no current issue confirms it.

## Why it is not decided

No current source states which Masses the church has. The annual decree Masses (24 June, 5 August) fall outside the issues read (the 2 August issue's Mass-intention panel is stale April text, so it does not show an 5 August Mass either way). Until a bulletin shows a recurring Mass (church row) or confirms only annual Masses (`no-public-mass`), it is not a church row. Not excluded as closed: nothing says it closed.
