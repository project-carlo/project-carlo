## What is known

The 25 October 2026 All Saints (Waterford) bulletin lists, under its Mass times, a "Thursday 6:00 pm Healing Mass Maria House". It gives no town or address. Maria House Project is a men's recovery ministry based in Erie (130 W. 19th St.) with a lodge in Spartansburg; search results do not say where or whether a Thursday Mass is held or whether the public may attend.

Checked again 2026-10-27: Maria House Project's home page lists "Thursdays 5:30 PM – 7:30 PM
Weekly Bible Study & Fellowship Meal" without a place or any Mass, and its contact page gives the
Lodge at 630 Hyde Rd, Spartansburg. All Saints' own Mass Times say "No Daily Mass". Still
unsettled.

## Why it is not decided

The line appears in one issue only, names no place, and nothing read says the Mass is public or in Waterford. It is not added as a church of All Saints. Next step: check the following All Saints issues and the Maria House Project contacts for a public Thursday Mass and its address.
