## What is known

Our Lady of Lourdes Church, 251 S. Franklin St., Cochranton, is a secondary church of St. Hippolyte Parish without mission status. The decree effective 2023-08-07 ended its Sunday and holy-day Masses and requires one Mass a year on the solemnity of its title (Our Lady of Lourdes, 11 February), plus possible regularly scheduled weekday celebrations. The directory (read 2026-10-23) and the parish's own 4 October 2026 bulletin show only 'Friday 8:30am - Adoration and Rosary' there. The 8 February 2026 bulletins of St. Hippolyte and of the Epiphany show no Mass at Cochranton on 11 February; the Epiphany's only Mass that day is at Seton School at 9:30 am.

## Why it is not decided

There is no current source that states a recurring public Mass here, so it is not a church row; the decree alone states a yearly Mass, but no bulletin read shows it happened, so it is not settled as `no-public-mass` either (policy: one whose only Mass is the patronal feast, stated by a current source, is `no-public-mass`). The next step is the St. Hippolyte and Epiphany bulletins around 11 February 2027: a titular-feast Mass printed there settles it as `no-public-mass`; weekday Masses printed at 'OLL' would make it a church row under St. Hippolyte. Any `OLL` Mass line in a St. Hippolyte issue before then would also settle it.
