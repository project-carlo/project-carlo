## What is known

St. Patrick Parish's bulletin (read 2026-10-23, issues 4, 11, 18 and 25 October 2026) lists its weekday Mass intentions every week at '7:45am Cathedral PREP Chapel', and says the pastor 'celebrates three Masses total each week' at the parish itself; the parish has no weekday Mass in its own church. The intentions are for parishioners, the diocese and deceased family members of the parish, so the Masses are the parish's. The chapel is Cathedral Preparatory School's, 250 W. 10th St. (the bulletin's pastor e-mail address is at cathedralprep.com, which suggests he also works at the school; an inference).

## Why it is not decided

The Erie rule says a campus Mass a parish bulletin lists every week is a church row under that parish; the batch lead said a school Mass is only that unless a source calls it public. No source I found says whether parishioners or the public may attend at the school (the bulletin lists intentions, not an invitation; the school's site has no text on it; no Discover Mass page for it). The call is therefore left open, not made. If the orchestrator reads the weekly bulletin listing as enough, this becomes a church row under `st-patrick-erie`; the evidence above is in the form needed for that.
