## What is known

A weekly Sunday 7 p.m. Mass is held in Larry and Kathryn Smith Chapel on the Behrend campus (Catholic Campus Ministry's own site, read 2026-10-23), and the diocesan clergy list names a Mass celebrant assigned to Penn State Behrend. The chapel's street address was not found on the pages read (Behrend's Smith Chapel page and contact page gave none), so the record has city and state only.

## Why it is not decided

No source says the public may attend (both pages address students and staff), no bulletin lists the Mass, and Discover Mass has no Behrend page in the Pennsylvania sitemap list of 2026-10-23. Under the Erie rule, a campus Mass no source calls public goes to `open/`.
