## Holds Mass

This is the parish church of Holy Spirit Parish and the only DuBois building with a regular Mass schedule. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-23; page 1 'Please note all Weekday Masses are held at St. Catherine, DuBois, unless otherwise noted') tags every weekday Mass of 19-23 October, the Saturday 4:00 p.m. vigil and the Sunday 7:30, 9:00 and 11:00 a.m. Masses 'at St. Catherine' on page 2. The parish home page (weareholyspirit.com, read the same day) gives the same times. The 2023 merger decree (August-September 2023) names it the parish church of the new parish.

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic 'St. Catherine of Siena', the directory 'St. Catherine of Siena Church' and the bulletin 'St. Catherine' agree; OpenStreetMap has 'St. Catherine of Siena' on South State Street (way 642330110, 41.12015, -78.76957), about 30 m from the GCatholic pin, so name_verified is true.

Address: the directory, the parish site footer and the eCatholic map embed (41.1203, -78.7700) give 123 S State St.; GCatholic gives 116 S State St.; the parish office is 118 S State St. (bulletin masthead and mailing address). All sit on the same block and the coordinates (GCatholic Plus Code, the site's map, OSM) agree to within ~30 m, so this is one building. I kept GCatholic's 116 (it came with the building-precise Plus Code) and flag the disagreement: address_verified stays false until a source gives the church's own street number. Coordinates: gcatholic-plus-code.

Roster: directory record 20824 'Holy Spirit Parish' (123 S. State St., the parish record, whose Mass times are St. Catherine's) is the same parish church and is linked here as well as directory 20805, the record for the St. Catherine church itself.
