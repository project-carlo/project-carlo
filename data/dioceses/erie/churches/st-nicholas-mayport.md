## Holds Mass

Yes, seasonally, from 31 October 2026. The 25 October bulletin announces a new schedule: Saturday vigil Mass at 5:30 PM at St. Nicholas from May to October (and at St. Richard from November to April), starting with Saturday 31 October at St. Nicholas. Before this change the directory and the parish site list no Mass here at all ('Sunday Mass: None'); the pastor's letter says the new Saturday Mass went to 'the churches without a Sunday Mass'. This is one bulletin, one week before the schedule starts, so the first Mass is announced rather than seen; check a November or later issue to confirm the pattern held. Mass is not announced for November to April.
