## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (container 14/1073) lists Sunday 8:30 a.m. at St. John's, Wednesday and Thursday 8:00 a.m., and the Saturday 4:30 pm vigil here from June 1 to November 30 (at Holy Cross December-May).

## Name and address

The bulletin, ParishesOnline, Nominatim and the directory's address field agree on 1001 Main Street E (a former parish center that serves as the parish church since the 2022 decree). Both flags true. Coordinates are the ParishesOnline organization pin, corroborated by Nominatim.

**Source disagreement:** GCatholic 24515 gives 101 Olin Ave. with Plus Code 86JX2M2M+W9 (42.0023, -80.3166): that is the old church relegated in 2022 (Nominatim places 101 Olin Avenue at 42.00251, -80.31686), so that roster entry is attached to excluded/st-john-the-evangelist-olin-ave-girard and not to this church. The diocese directory's locator pin for record 10308 sits at the Olin Ave location too.
