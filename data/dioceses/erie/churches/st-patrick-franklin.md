## Holds Mass

Yes, now. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-23) gives Saturday vigil 4:30pm (plus a 9:00am Saturday Mass), Sunday 8:00am and 10:30am, and weekday Masses Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday 9:00am and Wednesday 5:00pm; the directory lists the same times.

## Name and address

The church is at 949 Liberty St. (bulletin masthead and GCatholic). The diocese directory gives 952 Buffalo St., which the bulletin names as the **parish office** ('Parish Office, 952 Buffalo Street'), so the directory's street address is the office, not the church. I used 949 Liberty St.; coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, which agrees with the directory's locator pin to about 60 m.
