## Holds Mass

Yes, now. The 25 October 2026 St. Luke / OLMC bulletin (read 2026-10-23) lists OLMC Masses Saturday 4:00 (vigil), Sunday 8:00 and Tuesday and Thursday 7:30, with intentions for 24 October through 1 November. OLMC is canonically a parish partnered with St. Luke (clergy list: Rev. Lawrence R. Richards is pastor of both) and shares the St. Luke bulletin, so its church sits under the st-luke-erie row.

## Name and address

The name agrees across the bulletin ('Our Lady of Mt. Carmel'), the directory, GCatholic and OSM. The street number does not: the bulletin masthead, both directory forms and the ParishesOnline organization say 1531 E. Grandview Blvd.; GCatholic and OpenStreetMap (church node and parish school) say 1553. The coordinates of the church agree to within about 40 m across GCatholic's Plus Code (42.1071875, -80.0279375), the OSM church node and the diocese pin (42.107475, -80.027714), and Nominatim places 1531 on a house 150 m away, so the building is the one at those coordinates; I did not settle which number the building carries. I followed the parish's own 1531 and left `address_verified` false. A site photo or the county parcel would settle it.
