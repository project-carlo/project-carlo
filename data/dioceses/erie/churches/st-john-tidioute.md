## Holds Mass

Probably yes, but on the directory's word only. The diocese's printable directory (2026-10-06) and web record 10404 give Saturday 4:30 p.m., Sunday 11 a.m. and Monday and Thursday 9:30 a.m. at St. John, Tidioute; the aggregator sites catholicchurchtimes.com and masstime.us repeat similar times in search snippets and are not independent. No bulletin was found (see the parish record), and no 2026 funeral notice naming a Mass at St. John was found: searches turned up St. Anthony, Tionesta funerals with burial at 'St. John Catholic Cemetery in Tidioute', and an obituary that says the deceased married in St. John's Catholic Church in Tidioute in 1959. No source says the church closed and no decree relegates it; the diocese's news release of 2023-10-17 for McKean, Potter and Warren counties (https://www.eriercd.org/images/sections/news/pdf/NR-McKean-Potter-Warren.pdf, read 2026-10-23) does not mention Tidioute, Tionesta, Sheffield, Youngsville or Warren, so it changes nothing here.
