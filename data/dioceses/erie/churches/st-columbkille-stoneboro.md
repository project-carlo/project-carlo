## Holds Mass

Yes, now: one weekend Mass, **Saturday 6 PM**. The 25 October 2026 bulletin of Immaculate Heart of Mary and St. Columbkille (read 2026-10-23) gives 'Saturday Vigil Mass: ... 6 PM - St. Columbkille' and dated Saturday 6pm intentions for 24 and 31 October, marked in red as at St. Columbkille. The diocese directory (record 31112) lists the same single Mass, Saturday 6 p.m. Stoneboro's own Discover Mass page lists Saturday 4:30pm, Sunday 9:00am and weekday 9:00am Masses, which neither the bulletin nor the directory supports (it also has no bulletin); I followed the bulletin.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead ('St. Columbkille Parish ... 70 Franklin St | Stoneboro, PA | 16153'), the directory (31112), GCatholic (24497) and Discover Mass agree on name and address; flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (41.3363125, -80.1049375); the directory pin (41.336477, -80.104691) is about 20 m away.
