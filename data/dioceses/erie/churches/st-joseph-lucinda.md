## Holds Mass

Yes. The 4 October 2026 bulletin of the St. Joseph/St. Mary/St. Michael partnership (page 1, text layer) lists Mass at St. Joseph Saturday 4:00pm (Oct 10), Sunday 9:30am (Oct 11) and Friday 8:30am (Oct 9); the 18 October issue (image-only, rendered) lists Friday Oct 23 8:30am, Saturday Oct 24 4:00pm and Sunday Oct 25 9:30am. The directory says the Saturday 4 p.m. Mass is held in the even months only (St. Michael has it in the odd months); October is an even month, consistent with the bulletin. Parish church of the partnership and seat of its office.

## Name and address

112 Rectory Lane, Lucinda, PA 16235 is given identically by the diocese directory (record 30906), the parish site's Church Addresses page and GCatholic (as 'Rectory Ln.'). The parish site and its bulletin are one source, so the second independent source is the diocese plus GCatholic. Coordinates come from GCatholic's Plus Code (41.3114, -79.3656), 0.3 km from the directory's locator pin (41.31094, -79.367). `name_verified` stays false: 'St. Joseph' with no town suffix is how GCatholic and the bulletin name it, and the directory calls it 'St. Joseph Parish'.
