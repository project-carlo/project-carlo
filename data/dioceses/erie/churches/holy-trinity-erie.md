## Holds Mass

Yes, now. The 25 October 2026 St. John/Holy Rosary/Holy Trinity bulletin (read 2026-10-23) lists the Saturday 4:00 PM vigil at Holy Trinity, a Sunday 10:30 AM Mass 'based on the availability of Msgr. Daniel Magraw', and weekday 8:00 AM Masses; the week's intentions show the Saturday 31 October 4:00 pm and Sunday 1 November 10:30 am Masses at HT, and mark the weekday HT Masses of that week 'No Mass'.

## Name and address

Bulletin, diocese directory (web and printable), GCatholic and OSM agree on Holy Trinity, 2220 Reed St., Erie 16503. Holy Trinity is canonically a parish partnered with St. John the Baptist (clergy list: Piszker is pastor of both) but prints no bulletin of its own, so its church sits under the St. John row. Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code, about 40 m from the OSM church.
