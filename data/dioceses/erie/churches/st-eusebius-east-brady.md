## Holds Mass

Yes, every Sunday. The 25 October 2026 bulletin of St. Charles lists Sunday Mass at St. Eusebius at 8:00 AM from 1 November; the pastor's letter says the church has had an 11:15 AM Mass for the last few months (the directory's time), now moving to 8 AM. East Brady has no bulletin of its own that I could find (see the parish record's `research`); this church sits under the St. Charles row because that bulletin lists its Masses and its pastor writes about it.
