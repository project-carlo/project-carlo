## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly on Tuesday at 12:05 pm. The Epiphany's 11, 18 and 25 October 2026 bulletins (page 2 rendered as an image, read 2026-10-23) each list a '12:05 pm St M' Mass for the Tuesday (27 October is marked 'No Mass', a one-off), and the diocesan directory gives 'Tuesday at 12:05 p.m.' with no weekend Mass. The text-layer issue of 8 February 2026 has the same line. It is a secondary church with a recurring public weekday Mass, so a church row under the Epiphany's bulletin.

## Name and address

The bulletin (page 1), GCatholic and the directory agree on St. Mary of Grace, 1085 Water St., Meadville, PA 16335; both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code. The old parish site catholic-meadville.com in the directory is dead (Epiphany's pre-merger name for itself); GCatholic's stmaryofgrace.net is the pre-merger site and was not used.
