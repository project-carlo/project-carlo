## Holds Mass

Yes, now. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-23) lists the Sunday 10:45 am Mass and every weekday Mass (Mon and Tue 7 am, Wed 6 pm, Thu and Fri 7 am) at St. Joseph Bread of Life, and the Monday-to-Friday Mass intentions are all 'at St. Joseph'. It also houses the parish office and the Perpetual Adoration chapel.

## Name and address

The name varies by source: the bulletin says 'St. Joseph Church' / 'St. Joseph BOL' / 'Saint Joseph Bread of Life Community'; the directory 'St. Joseph/Bread of Life Community Parish'; GCatholic 'St. Joseph' (group Bread of Life Community); ParishesOnline 'St. Joseph Church Bread of Life Community'. These are one church; the name used here is the directory's parish name without 'Parish'. The address 147 W. 24th St., Erie 16502 is the same in the bulletin, directory, GCatholic and ParishesOnline, so address_verified is true; name_verified is true on the same four sources. Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code 86JX4W6C+RP (42.1120625, -80.0781875); the locator pin is about 40 m away.
