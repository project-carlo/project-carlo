Yes, seasonally. The 25 October 2026 bulletin sets the Saturday vigil Mass at 5:30 PM at St. Richard from November to April (St. Nicholas holds it May to October), starting after 31 October; the Sunday 12:30 PM Mass the directory gives is not in the new schedule (Sunday: 8:00 AM St. Eusebius, 10:30 AM St. Charles).
