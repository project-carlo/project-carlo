## Holds Mass

Yes, now. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-23) lists Sunday 11:00 AM Mass at St. Casimir and gives intentions for it on 25 October and 1 November. The diocese directory lists no other Mass here. It is the parish church of Our Mother of Sorrows Parish (designation decree), a partner of St. Stanislaus.

## Name and address

Street number disagreement: the diocese (web record 10216 and the printable directory), the parish's own address block and OpenStreetMap give 630 Hess Ave., Erie 16503; GCatholic (24494) gives 629 Hess Ave. (the neighbouring building in OSM). I used 630, so the verified flag stays false until a source settles the number. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (86JX4WQV+M2: 42.1391875, -80.0574375), about 20 m from the OSM church node; the directory locator pin for this church (42.139408, -80.057069) agrees. The directory's separate Our Mother of Sorrows parish record (10221) has the same street address but a locator pin about 450 m south (42.135634, -80.05764); I treat that pin as the parish record's, not a second building, since the printable directory and the decree place the parish church here.
