## Holds Mass

Yes, per the diocese. The printable directory of 22 October 2026 (the only current schedule found) lists Saturday 4 p.m. at St. Timothy with confession Saturday 3:15-3:50 p.m.; the web directory record says the same and puts Sunday 11 a.m. at St. Bonaventure. A funeral Mass was celebrated here on 9 January 2026 with Very Rev. John J. Detisch, V.F. (Progress obituary of Mary Ann Brock, legacy.com). No bulletin has been found, so the times are not confirmed by the parish (see the parish record). The Discover Mass page lists older times (Sat 4:00pm, Sun 8:30am, Mon-Thu 9:00am); I did not follow them.
