## Holds Mass

Yes, now. The Holy Redeemer bulletin of 25 October 2026 (container 14/0961, read 2026-10-23) names St. Anthony of Padua, Sheffield in its masthead and lists 'Sunday: ... 11:00am (St. Anthony)' in its Mass Schedule; the 1 November 11:00 am intention is for 'Holy Redeemer and St. Anthony'. The printable directory (2026-10-06) calls it a secondary mission of Holy Redeemer with Sunday 11 a.m. and a 5 p.m. Mass the vigil of a holy day. There is no Saturday or weekday Mass here.

## Name and address

Name: 'St. Anthony of Padua' in the bulletin, the directory and GCatholic (24480). Address: the bulletin and the web directory record 10402 say 7226 Route 6, Sheffield 16347; GCatholic and the printable directory's town index say 7222 Route 6; OpenStreetMap's church is at 7222 US-6. The two numbers are taken to be the same building on Route 6 (the OSM node and the directory pin are 150 m apart); the bulletin's 7226 is kept as the address and 7222 is recorded here. Coordinates are the OSM node, which agrees with the diocese's pin; GCatholic's Plus Code point is about 2.3 km south-east and looks wrong. A Ruthenian church (St. Michael, Sheffield; GCatholic's roster, DM `st-michael-byzantine-sheffield-pa`) is a different place and not this one.
