## Holds Mass

Yes, now. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-23) lists Saturday 4:00 PM and Sunday 9:00 AM Mass at St. Stanislaus, and weekday Mass (Monday-Friday 8 AM per the diocese's directory) 'Call Parish Office the Day ahead'; page 2 gives intentions for the Saturday 31 October vigil and Sunday 1 November Mass. It is the parish church of St. Stanislaus Parish, which absorbed St. Hedwig (2016 decree) and St. Mary of the Immaculate Conception (2015).
