## Holds Mass

Yes, per the diocese and Discover Mass. The 2023 merger decree made St. Bonaventure a secondary mission church of St. Timothy (effective 2 December 2023) and closed nothing. The diocese's web record and printable directory of 22 October 2026 list Sunday 11 a.m. here, and Discover Mass's page lists 'Sunday 11:00am'; these are listings, not a bulletin (none found), so the Mass is unconfirmed by the parish. A funeral notice for Mary Ann Brock (Jan 2026) names interment in St. Bonaventure Cemetery, not a Mass in the church. No relegation decree for Grampian is in the diocese's decree list (read 2026-10-23).

## Name and address

GCatholic, the diocese directory and Discover Mass agree on 461 Main St., Grampian 16838 (ParishesOnline lists 461 Main St, 16838-0127), so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G3X98M+MG, within about 10 m of the diocese pin.
