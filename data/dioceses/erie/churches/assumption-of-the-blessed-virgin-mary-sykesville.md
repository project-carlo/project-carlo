## Holds Mass

Secondary mission church of Holy Spirit Parish with a weekend Mass. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-23) lists Saturday 5:30 p.m. and Sunday 8:00 a.m. 'A.B.V.M., Sykesville' on page 1 and tags the 24 and 25 October intentions 'at Assumption' on page 2; the parish home page and the diocese directory record give the same two Masses, plus Saturday confession 4:30-5:00 p.m. No weekday Mass.

This is the Latin-rite parish church. A Byzantine Catholic church (Holy Trinity) is also in Sykesville; it is a different jurisdiction and is not linked to this record.

## Name and address

GCatholic and the diocese directory agree on the name (the bulletin and site say 'Assumption' / 'ABVM') and on 20 Shaffer St., Sykesville, PA 15865; the two coordinate pairs (GCatholic Plus Code, directory locator pin) agree to about 10 m. Coordinates: gcatholic-plus-code.
