## Holds Mass

Yes, now. The 18 October 2026 bulletin of Our Lady of the Lake (Discover Mass no. 3527, read 2026-10-23) lists Saturday 24 October 4:00pm at St. Anthony of Padua Church and a Tuesday 12:10pm Mass (cancelled that week), matching the directory (Sat 4 p.m., Tue 12:10 p.m.). A weekend vigil makes it a secondary mission church in the decree's sense, so it is a church row under Our Lady of the Lake's bulletin. Merged into Our Lady of the Lake by the decree 'Merging St. Anthony (Cambridge Springs) into Our Lady of the Lake (Edinboro)' (read 2026-10-23), which says it 'would become a secondary mission church'.

## Name and address

Sources disagree on the street: the printable directory (Oct. 6, 2026) and GCatholic say 165 Beach Ave.; the older web detail page says 415 S. Main Street; the bulletin gives 417 S. Main Street for the 'St. Anthony Church Center' (a gathering space/hall). OSM maps "St. Anthony's Catholic Church" at the Beach Avenue location where GCatholic's Plus Code and the diocese pin (41.801009, -80.059469) both fall, and 415 S Main as a separate house about 90 m west. I followed 165 Beach Ave. (printable directory, GCatholic and OSM agree on the building); the Main Street addresses read as the Church Center next door or the web record's old address, which I inferred rather than read. Coordinates are the OSM church way; GCatholic's Plus Code is ~30 m away.
