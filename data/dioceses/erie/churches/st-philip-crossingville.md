## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Philip is a secondary mission of St. Lawrence the Martyr with a weekend Mass: the 11 October 2026 bulletin lists Sunday 8:00 am at St. Philip (reconciliation 7:30 am), and the parish homepage and diocese directory give the same.

## Name and address

The parish's own driving-directions page and the diocese directory both give 25797 State Highway 98, Edinboro, PA 16412 (the parish site calls it its 'mission church in Edinboro'); GCatholic gives 'Route 98', Crossingville 16412 (Crossingville is a village that mails as Edinboro, see city_aliases). The road and ZIP agree, so address_verified is true. Coordinates are in less agreement: GCatholic's Plus Code is 41.83119, -80.23744; the centre of the Google map the parish site embeds for that address is 41.83135, -80.24032 (about 250 m west); the directory pin is 41.83331, -80.23101 (about 650 m north-east). Nominatim could not geocode the address. I kept GCatholic's Plus Code (building-precise elsewhere in this diocese); the geocode stage or a map check of the Route 98 church could refine it.
