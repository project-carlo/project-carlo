## Holds Mass

Secondary church of St. Eulalia Parish with a recurring public Mass this season. The parish bulletin announces 'Daily Mass at St. Bibiana weekly on Thursday evenings at 6:00PM' starting 18 June 2026; the notice in the 18 and 25 October 2026 issues runs it 'through November 19th' with no Mass on 29 October or 12 November. Weekly intentions in the issues of 14 June to 13 September 2026 list '6:00PM—St. Bibiana' every Thursday. The 2025-26 issues also show the feast Mass on Tuesday 2 December 2025 at 6:00PM and Stations of the Cross on Sundays in Lent. There is no weekend Mass (the directory, the October 2023 plan and the bulletin schedule agree), so this is a seasonal weekday Mass: the secondary-church rule counts a recurring weekday or seasonal Mass as a church row. Whether the Thursday Mass resumes after 19 November 2026 is unknown; the church row stands while the bulletin lists it.

The directory (web record and 6 Oct 2026 printable) lists no Mass times here, so it lags the bulletin on this point; the bulletin wins.
