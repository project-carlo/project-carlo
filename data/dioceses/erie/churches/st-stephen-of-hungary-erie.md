## Holds Mass

Yes: a Sunday 12:00 pm Spanish Mass at San Esteban. The 25 October 2026 St. Jude bulletin (Discover Mass number 3515, read 2026-10-23) lists 'Sunday ... 12:00 pm San Esteban (Español)'; the parish's own site and the diocese directory (record 10314) give the same Sunday 12 p.m. Mass. That it is held at this address is inferred from the schedule label and the directory; the bulletin's Mass box doesn't repeat the street. This is a separate canonical parish partnered with St. Jude (decree effective 2024-07-09) with no bulletin of its own, so its church sits under the St. Jude row.

## Name and address

Name: the directory, GCatholic ('St. Stephen of Hungary', group 'Iglesia de San Esteban') and the parish site agree; the bulletin uses 'Parroquia de San Esteban'.
