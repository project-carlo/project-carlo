## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish bulletin of 25 October 2026 (rendered page 2) lists Saturday 4:00pm and Sunday 9:00am Masses and weekday Masses at St. Elizabeth; 18 October reads the same.

## Name and address

Name: bulletin, directory and GCatholic agree on St. Elizabeth of Hungary. Address: the bulletin masthead ('210 Main Street') and the diocese directory ('210 W. Main St.') give the church at 210 W. Main St.; GCatholic and the ParishesOnline org give 307 Franklin St., which the directory and bulletin call the mailing address (rectory). `address_verified` is left false because GCatholic's street differs; the coordinates (GCatholic Plus Code, building-precise) agree with the directory pin to within about 15 m, so the location is not in doubt. Settle by reading the street sign or the parish site's contact page.
