## Name and address

Sources disagree on the street number: the directory (physical address), the bulletin masthead and OpenStreetMap say 3750 West Lake Rd.; GCatholic and the directory's mailing address say 638 Roslyn Ave. The masthead settles it: '3750 WEST LAKE ROAD (RECTORY: 638 ROSLYN AVE.)', so 638 Roslyn is the rectory and the church is 3750 West Lake Road. OSM shows the church building at 3750 West Lake Road and a separate building at 638 Roslyn. I followed 3750 West Lake Rd. and set both flags true because the parish's bulletin, the directory and OSM agree on it and the GCatholic address is explained by the masthead. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 86JX3RWJ+V2 (42.0971875, -80.1699375), about 12 m from OSM's church way (42.0972205, -80.1700664) and the diocese pin.
