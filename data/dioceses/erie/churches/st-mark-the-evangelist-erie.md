## Holds Mass

Yes, now. The 25 October 2026 St. James bulletin (read 2026-10-23, image pages rendered) gives Sunday 9:30 at St. Mark in its weekend Mass list, a St. Mark Church box with the Smithson Avenue address, and a holy day Mass at St. Mark (5:30 PM); page 2 also shows the Sunday 1 November 9:30 intention in red (St. Mark's colour) and 'Holy Hours 11:00 at St. James & 6:30 at St. Mark' on Wednesday 28 October (a holy hour, not a Mass). The printable diocese directory (6 Oct 2026) lists it as a 'Secondary Mission' with Sunday 9:30 a.m. The parish merged into St. James by decree in July 2024; the church keeps a recurring Sunday Mass, so it is a church row under St. James.
