## Holds Mass

Yes, in term time: Sunday 6:30 pm Mass in Ford Memorial Chapel on the Allegheny College campus. The Epiphany of the Lord Parish bulletin lists it every week in its Mass Schedule (11, 18 and 25 October 2026 issues, read 2026-10-23: '6:30 pm Allegheny College: Mass at Ford Chapel'; also January 2026), so under the campus rule it is a church row under that parish. Discover Mass gives Sunday 6:30 pm 'if school is in session'. No chaplain or separate campus bulletin was found; the Epiphany bulletin is the only source with the weekly schedule. It is a college-term Mass, so summer weeks may be absent (not checked: the August 2026 issues have no text layer).

## Name and address

Name and address are from Discover Mass (campus page, 520 N Main St., Meadville 16335 is the college's address) and the bulletin ('Ford Chapel'); no second independent source gives the chapel's own street address, so both flags are false. Coordinates are Nominatim's point for Ford Memorial Chapel (OpenStreetMap), within about 100 m of the college's address point; the chapel is on campus one block south of the Campus Center.
