## Holds Mass

Yes, now: a weekday Mass, Thursday 12:10 pm (bulletin of 18 October 2026, no. 3527, read 2026-10-23; directory 'Daily: Thursday at 12:10 p.m.'). It has no Saturday or Sunday Mass in any source. Status disagreement: the web city list and the merger decree (28 Feb 2024 proposal) call it a 'secondary church without mission status', while the printable directory of Oct. 6, 2026 labels it 'Secondary Mission'. The decree's definition of a mission is a weekend Mass, and the bulletin and both directory forms show none, so the label is the printable directory's looser wording; I followed the schedule, not the label. It is a church row because a recurring public Mass is in the current bulletin (diocese rule for secondary churches).
