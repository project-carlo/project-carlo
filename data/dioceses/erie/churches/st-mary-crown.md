## Holds Mass

Yes. The 4 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, text layer) lists Mass at St. Mary Saturday 6:00pm (Oct 10), Sunday 8:00am (Oct 11) and weekdays 8:30am (Mon Oct 5, Wed Oct 7); the 18 October issue (rendered) lists Mon Oct 19 and Wed Oct 21 8:30am, Saturday Oct 24 6:00pm and Sunday Oct 25 8:00am. The directory's schedule matches.

## Name and address

Street address 117 Lencer Drive agrees in every source. The town and ZIP do not: the parish's own site says Leeper, PA 16233 (and the Clarion bulletin says Leeper); the diocese directory says Leeper with ZIP 16235 (Lucinda's, the PO box town; its mailing line gives 16233 in the printable directory); GCatholic says Crown, 16220. The street address is the same place in all of them, so this is one church; recorded as Leeper 16233 (the parish's own address) and `address_verified` is left false because the sources disagree on town and ZIP. The parish is canonically 'St. Mary (Crown)' and the roster alias Leeper-to-Crown covers the difference.
