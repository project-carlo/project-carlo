## Holds Mass

Yes, now. The 25 October 2026 St. John/Holy Rosary/Holy Trinity bulletin (read 2026-10-23) lists a Sunday 10:30 AM Mass at Holy Rosary and the intention '10:30 am HR For All Living & Deceased Parishioners' for 1 November. Holy Rosary is a 'Mission Church' of St. John the Baptist in the masthead and 'Secondary Mission' in the printable directory; the brief's phrase 'merged into St. John' describes that status, and the church still holds a weekly public Mass, so it is a church row.

## Name and address

Sources disagree on the address of the same church. 2701 East Ave. is given by GCatholic, OpenStreetMap (a place_of_worship at that address), the ParishesOnline partnered-parish organization and the diocese's printable directory (St. John block). 1002 East 28th St. is given by the bulletin masthead ('GPS Address ONLY'), the diocese web record and the printable directory's town listing. The two points are 40-50 m apart at the East Avenue / East 28th Street corner, which reads as two street addresses for one site rather than two buildings; I did not check a site photo. I followed 2701 East Ave. (four sources, one a building-level map) and noted 1002 East 28th St. as the parish's GPS address, and set both flags true on that basis. Coordinates are the OSM church node (the GCatholic Plus Code lands on the 1002 E 28th house, 45 m east).
