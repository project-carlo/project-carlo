## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin (ParishesOnline 14/1173) gives 'Saturday Vigil: 5:00PM Sunday Liturgies: 8:00 & 10:30AM', weekday Masses Monday and Thursday 9:00 and Tuesday and Friday 7:30, and a Wednesday 7:30 Communion Service. Page 2 lists named Mass intentions for the week. The diocese directory gives the same times (Saturday 5 p.m., Sunday 8 and 10:30).

## Name and address

Four sources agree on 'St. Callistus' at 342 Chase St., Kane, PA 16735: the bulletin masthead and parish site (one source), GCatholic, the diocese directory and the ParishesOnline organization record. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H3M56R+V5; the directory pin and the PO org coordinates fall within about 30 m of it.
