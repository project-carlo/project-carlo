## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 St. Mary / Sacred Heart bulletin (14/0966, read 2026-10-23) schedules Saturday 4:30 pm vigil, Sunday 9:00 am and Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday 8:30 am at St. Mary's, and Holy Day vigil 6:30 pm. The directory and the parish site agree.

## Name and address

Sources disagree on the street number. The parish site (thesmchurch.com) and the directory give 315 Church Street, 15857 (mailing 325 Church St.); GCatholic gives 139 Church St., which the bulletin attributes to the Benedictine Gift Shop. OpenStreetMap has an address point 315 Church Street and a place_of_worship 'Saint Mary's Church' within about 30 m of GCatholic's Plus Code, so GCatholic's coordinates are on the church and only its street number looks wrong. Followed 315 Church St. (parish site, directory, OSM) and kept GCatholic's Plus Code as the coordinates (the directory's locator pin is about 85 m away). Both flags true on the name and 315 address (parish site and bulletin count as one source; directory and OSM independent).
