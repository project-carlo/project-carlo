## Holds Mass

Yes: a weekly Sunday 9:00 am Mass. The 25 October 2026 St. Jude bulletin (Discover Mass number 3515, read 2026-10-23) lists 'Sunday ... 9:00 am Saint Andrew' in its combined schedule and names 'Saint Andrew Church, Secondary Mission of Saint Jude, 1116 W. 7th Street'. The merger decrees (2024; amended 2024-05-29 to keep it as a secondary mission church) merged the parish into St. Jude and did not close the building, and the bulletin confirms Mass. The directory's printed time (Sunday 9:15 a.m., Oct. 6, 2026 edition) and its web record (Sun 9 a.m.) differ; the bulletin's 9:00 am is followed. Its own domain, saintandrewerie.com, redirects to saintjude.church. Mass and events at this church are read from St. Jude's bulletin.
