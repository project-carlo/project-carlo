## Holds Mass

Yes, despite the 2024 merger of St. Teresa parish into St. Thomas. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (the parish of St. Thomas the Apostle "and its mission St. Teresa of Avila") lists Sunday 11:00 am, Monday 8:00 am, Saturday 31 October 4:30 pm and All Souls Monday 2 November 7:00 pm Masses at St. Teresa, Union City. Weekend Masses alternate: Sunday 8:00 am at Corry and 11:00 am at Union City every week; the Saturday vigil is at St. Teresa in October.

## Name and address

Bulletin masthead, GCatholic and the directory agree on St. Teresa of Avila, 9 Third Ave., Union City, PA 16438; both flags true. GCatholic and the directory's own site still point at st-teresa-unioncity.org, which posts the same bulletin files as St. Thomas (see the parish record). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
