## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Sunday Mass at St. Charles at 10:30 AM from 1 November (moved from 10 AM; the pastor's letter says 'St. Charles' Mass has been moved 30 minutes later, from 10 AM to 10:30 AM'), and a Mass intention for Sunday 1 Nov at 10:30 a.m. The directory and the parish site give Sunday 10 a.m. before the change. No daily Mass: the calendar shows 'No Mass' for Tuesday to Thursday and Monday. Friday 30 Oct reads 'Mass (St. Charles) - No Mass'.

## Name and address

201 Washington St., New Bethlehem 16242 agrees across GCatholic, the diocese directory, ParishesOnline (org st-charles-church-16242) and the bulletin. The printable directory's 'Secondary Church' label for it looks like a typo for the parish church (it is the parish's only parish church; the clergy list and the bulletin treat St. Charles as the parish church). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
