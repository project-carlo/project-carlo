## Holds Mass

Sacred Heart is the mission church of St. Eulalia Parish. The parish bulletin's Mass schedule (13 September 2026 issue) lists Sunday 8:30AM and Tuesday 6:00PM at Sacred Heart, and the cover of the 25 October 2026 issue is headed 'St. Eulalia Parish and Sacred Heart Mission'. The parish home page and the diocese directory give the same Sunday 8:30 a.m. Mass.

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic and the diocese directory agree on 'Sacred Heart' at 263 Main St., Genesee, PA 16923. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H4X4QJ+Q8; the directory pin is within about 20 m. The directory's locator names Rev. Joseph V. Dougherty as pastor here, which the clergy PDF does not support (Allen is pastor of St. Eulalia; Dougherty is not listed against it).
