## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/1167) prints Saturday 5:00 PM, Sunday 7:30 AM, 9:00 AM and 12:00 PM, weekdays Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 8:00 AM and Thursday 12:10 PM; the intentions page lists Masses on those days.

## Name and address

The bulletin (page 1 header and page 2 contact block), GCatholic and the diocese directory all give St. Gregory Church, 48 S. Pearl St., North East, PA 16428; both flags true. The parish office and Our Mother of Perpetual Help Chapel are at 136 W. Main St. (mailing address in the directory); that is not the church. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, agreeing with the directory pin.
