## Holds Mass

Yes, now. St. Paul was merged canonically into Sacred Heart but still holds the weekly Saturday 4:00 pm Mass: the 25 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-23) gives it in the page 1 Mass box and lists 'Saturday, October 31/4 pm at St. Paul' in the Mass intentions; the 18 October and 27 September 2026 issues carry the same masthead. Confessions 3:15-3:45 pm Saturday here. It is a mission under the shared bulletin and is the only weekend vigil of the partnership.
