## Publisher

One bulletin covers St. Charles, New Bethlehem, its mission St. Nicholas (Mayport/Crates), and the partnered parish St. Eusebius, East Brady with its mission St. Richard (Rimersburg). The 25 October 2026 issue (Diocesan.com edition, Discover Mass number 3195, read 2026-10-23) is headed 'St. Charles Parish / St. Nicholas Mission', gives Rev. Kevin O. Holland as pastor, announces the new Mass schedule from 31 October for all four churches, lists a Mass at St. Eusebius in its weekly calendar, and the pastor writes that he is 'the pastor of two parishes with four locations'. That is positive evidence that St. Eusebius and St. Richard are carried by this bulletin; the header and masthead name only the first two. No separate bulletin for East Brady was found (see `research`), so `st-eusebius-east-brady` has no row of its own; this is one issue read, so the mixed coverage should be re-read in a second issue.

The diocese's listing shows St. Eusebius 'Partnered with St. Charles Parish, New Bethlehem' (printable directory, 6 Oct 2026); the clergy list gives Holland both parishes.

## Worship sites

Four churches, all with Mass in the 25 Oct bulletin's schedule from 31 Oct 2026: St. Charles (Sunday 10:30 am; 10 am until then per the directory), St. Eusebius (Sunday 8:00 am from 1 Nov; 11:15 am before), St. Nicholas (Saturday vigil 5:30 pm, May-October) and St. Richard (Saturday vigil 5:30 pm, November-April; the directory still gives it Sunday 12:30 pm, which the bulletin's new schedule replaces). The directory's printable edition calls St. Charles 'Secondary Church' under New Bethlehem, which I take for a listing slip: the directory's web record 30904 and the clergy list ('1) St. Charles, New Bethlehem') treat it as the parish.
