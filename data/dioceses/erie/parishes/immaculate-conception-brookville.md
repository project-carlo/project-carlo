## Publisher

Immaculate Conception Parish, Brookville, publishes one weekly bulletin for itself and its secondary mission church St. Dominic, Sigel. The 18 October 2026 issue (read on 2026-10-23) has the masthead 'IMMACULATE CONCEPTION CHURCH', pastor Rev. William M. Laska, a Mass Schedule box for both Immaculate Conception and St. Dominic, and a page 2 headed 'THE CHURCHES OF IMMACULATE CONCEPTION AND ST. DOMINIC'. Pages 1-3 of both copies are scanned images, so the text could not be extracted; I rendered the pages and read them by eye. One pastor (clergy list 8/7/2026), one bulletin.

## Worship sites

Two churches: Immaculate Conception (129 Graham Ave., Brookville; Sat 5:15 pm, Sun 10:30 am, Tue-Fri 8:00 am) and St. Dominic (7017 Rt. 949, Sigel; Sunday 8:30 am). The bulletin lists St. Dominic's Sunday Mass in the weekly intentions, so it is a church row under this parish. Nothing else in the bulletin or the directory is a worship site of this parish.

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site icbrookville.com is a diocese-hosted template site whose weeklybulletin.php posts image-only PDFs, and ParishesOnline carries the same issue as container 14/1830 (org immaculate-conception-15825), which was chosen as the provider because it is the standard channel and posts the Sunday issue in advance. Both are the same bulletin by masthead and content (compared page 1 and page 2 by eye on 2026-10-23); the file bytes differ, so this is a judgement from the rendered pages, not a hash match. Because the pages are images, `fetch:check` identity may read `unknown`; the website's weeklybulletin.php is a fallback if the container stops.
