The masthead of the 25 October issue (rendered page 1; the page has no text layer) names both St. Elizabeth of Hungary, Smethport, and St. Gabriel the Archangel, Port Allegany, under Rev. Joseph Dougherty, with 'Mailing Address for both churches: 307 Franklin St.' The merger decree (St. Gabriel into St. Elizabeth, 2023) and this bulletin agree: Port Allegany is one parish with Smethport, so one row.
