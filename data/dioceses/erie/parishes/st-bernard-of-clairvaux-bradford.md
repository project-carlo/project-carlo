## Publisher

St. Bernard of Clairvaux Parish, Bradford, publishes one weekly bulletin, titled 'The Catholic Community of Bradford', on its diocese-hosted site stbernardcatholic.org (`weeklybulletin.php`, one hash-named PDF per issue, link text carries the date: newest 18 October 2026 on 2026-10-23, then 11 and 4 October). The 18 October 2026 issue names Fr. John Jacquel and Fr. Edward Walk and lists Masses at St. Bernard Church and the Divine Mercy Chapel only. The merged St. Francis of Assisi parish (merger decree 2022) and its secondary mission Our Mother of Perpetual Help, Lewis Run, are not separate publishers: St. Francis appears in the St. Bernard bulletin as a church of the same parish (see its exclusion record).

The same bulletin is also posted on ParishesOnline (container 14/1187) as an image-heavy ad edition, one issue ahead (25 October on 2026-10-23); all three ids are recorded in `publication`.

## Worship sites

St. Bernard Church, 95 E. Corydon St. (weekend Masses and the diocese's parish church), and the Divine Mercy Chapel, which the bulletin names but does not give an address for; the website says 'All masses are held at St. Bernard Church or The Divine Mercy Chapel'. The chapel is treated as part of the St. Bernard site at 95 E. Corydon St. (inference: no separate address is published; see Not yet confirmed). St. Francis of Assisi, 15 St. Francis Dr., has no Sunday Mass by decree (7 Aug 2025) and only its patronal feast Mass is in the issues read, so it is excluded as no-public-mass; Our Mother of Perpetual Help, Lewis Run, was relegated in 2023 and is excluded as closed.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`: the site is a diocese-hosted template with `weeklybulletin.php`. Bulletin text-layer PDFs on the site are preferred over the ParishesOnline ad edition.
