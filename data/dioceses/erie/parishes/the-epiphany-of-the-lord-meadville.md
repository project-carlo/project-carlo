## Publisher

The Epiphany of the Lord Parish, Meadville (merged 2021 from St. Agatha, St. Brigid and St. Mary of Grace) publishes one ParishesOnline/LPi bulletin, container 14/1183 (org `st-mary-of-grace-st-agatha-st-brigid-churches`, LPi 0018000000QxBk5AAF). Its parish site embeds that org. The issue dated 25 October 2026 (posted early, read 2026-10-23) carries the masthead 'The Epiphany of the Lord Parish' and names only this parish; its page 8 footer reads 'Epiphany of the Lord, Meadville, PA 14-1183'. Since August 2026 pages 1-7 of each issue are images (no text layer), so identity comes from the page 8 footer and from rendering pages 1-2; January-February 2026 issues have a text layer.

## Worship sites

St. Agatha (parish church, 353 Pine St), St. Mary of Grace (1085 Water St; Tuesday 12:05 pm and other weekday Masses marked 'St M') and a weekly Sunday 6:30 pm Mass at Ford Memorial Chapel, Allegheny College (campus rule: a campus Mass a parish bulletin lists every week is a church row under that parish). Ruled out: St. Brigid, 383 Arch St, relegated by decree effective 2026-02-02 with its final Mass on 2026-02-01 (excluded/st-brigid-meadville). Our Lady of Lourdes, Cochranton and St. Hippolyte, Guys Mills belong to the partner parish St. Hippolyte, which prints its own bulletin (see parishes/st-hippolyte-guys-mills). The Epiphany bulletin only prints 'For the Living and Deceased Parishioners of The Epiphany of the Lord and St. Hippolyte Parishes' as a Mass intention (Sunday 7:30 am and Saturday vigil); it carries no St. Hippolyte Mass times.

## Website and bulletin provider

ParishesOnline, read with `provider_id` = the org slug. The parish site only links to the ParishesOnline find page. The directory still lists catholic-meadville.com for St. Mary of Grace (dead DNS); the 2026 masthead prints staff e-mail addresses at catholic-meadville.org, so the old name survives only as mail.
