## Publisher

St. Mark, Emporium (pastor Rev. Paul S. Siebert) publishes one weekly bulletin, on ParishesOnline as container 14/1738 and as a print-ready copy on the parish site's `weeklybulletin.php`. The two channels carry the same issue (compared for 18 October 2026: same masthead, Mass intentions and attendance box). The 25 October 2026 issue is headed 'ST. JAMES, MISSION' on page 1 and prints the week's Mass intentions for St. Mark; the page 2 attendance box counts 'ST. JAMES 5:30 PM' separately from the St. Mark Masses. One bulletin therefore covers St. Mark and its mission St. James, Driftwood.

## Worship sites

- St. Mark, 235 E. Fourth St., Emporium: Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM, weekday Masses at 8:15 AM (Wednesday 4:15 PM), no Mass on Fridays in the October issues.
- St. James, Driftwood (mission, 7305 3rd St.): Saturday 5:30 PM in season. Each of 11 issues sampled between 12 April and 25 October 2026 lists 'ST. JAMES 5:30 PM'; the 18 October attendance box counts 26 and the 25 October box 37 for Saturday 17 October, and the 11 October issue announces 'The last Mass at St. James for 2026 will take place NEXT SAT., OCT. 17th @ 5:30 PM. Weekly Mass at St. James will resume in Spring of 2027', and the diocese directory says 'Saturday: 5:30 p.m. (April to Mid-October)'.

The diocese lists no other secondary church for this parish.

## Website and bulletin provider

The site stmarkemporium.org is a diocese-hosted template site (`weeklybulletin.php` shows 'October 18', 'October 11', 'October 4' and a monthly 'Growing in Faith'). Its PDFs are a week behind and mix in inserts, so ParishesOnline is the channel. The site's `weeklybulletin.php` shows a bulletin, so its 'No Bulletin found.' case (a playbook caution) does not apply.
