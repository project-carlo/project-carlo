## Publisher

Queen of the World Parish, St. Marys (pastor Rev. Jeffery Noble per the diocesan clergy list updated 8/7/2026) publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container 14/1220). The 25 October 2026 issue, read on 2026-10-23, is headed 'Queen of the World Church, 134 Queens Road St. Marys, PA' and prints one Mass schedule (Saturday 5:15 pm, Sunday 7:30 am and 10 am). It is a different document from the St. Mary / Sacred Heart bulletin (container 14/0966) of the same date, which prints the 7:00/9:00/10:30 Sunday rota and does not carry Queen of the World's schedule; Queen of the World is not part of the 2023 St. Mary / Sacred Heart partnership decree. The Queen of the World bulletin does mention the partner parishes (the 40 Days Rosary at St. Mary and Sacred Heart) as neighbours.

## Worship sites
