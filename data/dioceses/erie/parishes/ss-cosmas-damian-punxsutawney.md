## Worship sites

One church with public Mass: Ss. Cosmas & Damian, 616 W. Mahoning St. (Sat 4:30 pm; Sun 8:00 and 11:00 am; weekdays 8:00 am, per the 18 Oct bulletin and the directory, which gives 10 a.m. May-August and 11 a.m. September-April). The parish also had three secondary churches that the bulletins no longer list: St. Joseph, Anita and St. Adrian, DeLancey were relegated by decree (Anita effective 2020-01-26, DeLancey effective 2020-02-16), and St. Anthony of Padua, Walston by decree effective 2021-05-03. Each has an excluded record. A grep of the issues of 2 Nov and 14 Dec 2025, 15 Feb, 8 Mar, 7 and 14 Jun, 26 Jul, 23 Aug, 6 Sep and 18 Oct 2026 finds no Mass at Walston, DeLancey or Anita (the 14 Dec issue mentions a meeting 'in Walston'; the 18 Oct calendar has a 'Sewing Ministry - Walston Church Office', so a parish office or building still exists there, not Mass).
