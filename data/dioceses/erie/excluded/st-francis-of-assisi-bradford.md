## Why it is excluded

**St. Francis of Assisi, 15 St. Francis Dr., Bradford: no public Mass except the patronal feast (4 October).** The decree of 7 August 2025 made it 'a secondary church without mission status' where 'Masses are not to be celebrated ... on Sundays or holy days of obligation or the evenings preceding them', with Mass every year on the title solemnity, 4 October. The current St. Bernard bulletins (30 August-18 October 2026) list one Mass there, the 4 October feast at 11:30 am (that year a Sunday), and no weekly or monthly Mass; the directory's web record lists 'Sunday: None'. This is the settled secondary-church rule for this diocese: only the patronal feast Mass, stated by a current source, is `no-public-mass`. The church is not closed: the building stays open to the faithful, and the parish has begun relegation (the 11 October 2026 pastor's column says the Presbyteral Council recommended it on 16 September; no decree yet).

## What would change this

A bulletin listing a recurring Mass at St. Francis would make it a church row under St. Bernard's row. A relegation decree (expected after the bishop acts; check https://www.eriercd.org/bishop/decrees.html) would turn this into `closed`. Revisit at the 2027-10-04 feast, or sooner when the decree appears.
