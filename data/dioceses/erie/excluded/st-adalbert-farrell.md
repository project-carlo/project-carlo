## Why it is excluded

**St. Adalbert, 1035 Fruit Ave., Farrell - closed; relegated to profane use effective 2020-09-20.** The decree (read 2026-10-23) relegates this secondary church of St. Anthony of Padua parish, Sharon, 'to profane but not sordid use', and its history says regular Masses ceased in 2017 with only an annual feast-day Mass afterwards. A relegation decree states the building no longer serves for worship. `last_mass` 2020-09 is the decree's effective month: no closing Mass is dated. The 25 October 2026 Catholic Community of Sharon bulletin (Discover Mass number 3537, https://discovermass.com/church/st-joseph-sharon-pa/) lists Mass only at St. Joseph (79 Case Ave.) and St. Anthony of Padua (Sunday 8:30 am); a grep of its text for Adalbert, Stanislaus, Irvine, Spruce and Fruit Ave. found no Mass at this building (Sacred Heart appears only in a 'Sacred Heart Candle at Saint Anthony Church' line; 'Holy Trinity' and 'St. Stanislaus' appear in a collection note naming Erie-city churches). The Farrell parish's own bulletin (Our Lady of Fatima, 24 Oct 2026) lists no Mass here either.

The Discover Mass page for it persists but carries no bulletin and no schedule; it is not a channel.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the decree is final unless a later one reverses it.
