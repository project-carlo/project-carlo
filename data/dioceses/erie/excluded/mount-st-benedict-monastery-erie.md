## Why it is excluded

The Erie rule treats monastery chapels as `no-public-mass` unless a source invites the public to a scheduled Mass. The sisters' site (2026-10-23) invites the public to the Liturgy of the Hours and lists a 'Sunday Liturgy' at 9:30 a.m. but does not call it a Mass or say who may attend; I read the liturgy and pray-with-us pages, the spirituality overview, events and contact pages and found no use of 'Mass' for a public celebration. No bulletin or Discover Mass page was found.

The Sunday Liturgy may in fact be a Mass open to guests; that is an inference left unconfirmed.

## What would change this

The sisters (or a calendar) stating that the 9:30 a.m. Sunday Liturgy is a Mass open to the public would make it `no-bulletin` or, with a readable bulletin, a church row.
