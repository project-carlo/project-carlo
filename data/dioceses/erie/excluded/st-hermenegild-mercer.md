## Why it is excluded

**St. Hermenegild Church, 28 Church Road, Mercer (Pardoe): no public Mass on a schedule; its only public Mass is one a year.** The diocese directory (record 31120) and its printable edition of 6 Oct 2026 call it a 'Secondary Church' under Immaculate Heart of Mary, with every Mass field 'None'. The 25 Oct 2026 joint IHM / St. Columbkille bulletin lists no Mass there. The only Mass the parish announces is an annual August Mass and picnic: the 9 Aug 2026 bulletin says 'Join us for our annual St. Hermenegild Mass on Thursday, August 13, at 6:00 PM', open to 'All parishioners, families, and friends', and parish posts show the same event on 1 Aug 2024 and 7 Aug 2025 (and a 2022 post of the same title). Nine other issues (16 Aug - 25 Oct 2026; 6 and 13 Sept would not download) do not mention the church. Per the orchestrator's decision of 2026-10-23, a church with one Mass a year is treated like St. Walburga (Titusville), St. Augustine (Austin) and the DuBois feast-only churches: `no-public-mass`. (Originally recorded as a church row; reclassified the same day.)

## What would change this

A recurring schedule (a weekly, monthly or seasonal Mass in the IHM bulletin or the diocese directory) would make it a church row under Immaculate Heart of Mary, Mercer again. The next annual Mass should be announced in July 2027 on ihmercer.org; `revisit_after` is set for that.
