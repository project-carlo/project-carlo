## Why it is excluded

**Sacred Heart, 40 S. Irvine Ave., Sharon - closed; relegated to profane use effective 2018-03-25.** The decree (PDF above, read 2026-10-23) relegates this secondary church of St. Joseph Parish 'to profane but not sordid use'; a relegation decree states that the building no longer serves for worship. It also records that no Sunday Mass had been celebrated here since the parish merged into St. Joseph on 2017-02-13. `last_mass` 2018-03 is the decree's effective month: no closing Mass is dated, and the last regular Masses were earlier (merger, February 2017). The 25 October 2026 Catholic Community of Sharon bulletin (Discover Mass number 3537, https://discovermass.com/church/st-joseph-sharon-pa/) lists Mass only at St. Joseph (79 Case Ave.) and St. Anthony of Padua (Sunday 8:30 am); a grep of its text for Adalbert, Stanislaus, Irvine, Spruce and Fruit Ave. found no Mass at this building (Sacred Heart appears only in a 'Sacred Heart Candle at Saint Anthony Church' line; 'Holy Trinity' and 'St. Stanislaus' appear in a collection note naming Erie-city churches).

GCatholic still lists the church without a relegation date; the diocese directory no longer lists it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the decree is final unless a later one reverses it, or a current bulletin starts printing a Mass here.
