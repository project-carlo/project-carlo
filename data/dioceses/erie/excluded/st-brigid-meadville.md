## Why it is excluded

**St. Brigid, 383 Arch St., Meadville: closed, last Mass 2026-02-01.** The diocesan decree relegating the church to profane but not sordid use is effective 2 February 2026 (dated from the decree's text). The parish bulletin of 1 February 2026 announces 'The Final Mass at St Brigid Church will be at 12:30pm on February 1' and, on page 6, 'FINAL MASS AT ST. BRIGID CHURCH - February 1, 2026, 12:30 PM'; Friday 12:05 Masses were moved to St. Agatha; the 8 February issue refers to 'the last Mass at St. Brigid's last Sunday'. The current issues (11, 18 and 25 October 2026, read 2026-10-23) name only St. Agatha and St. Mary of Grace in their masthead and Mass schedule. `last_mass` is the closing Mass named in the bulletin, not the decree's effective date.

The directory's detail page 302s (inactive record) and GCatholic still lists the church; both lag the decree.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass at 383 Arch St. (the decree says the building no longer serves for worship). Only the cemetery (St. Brigid Cemetery) is still named in the parish bulletin.
