## Why it is excluded

**St. Adrian Martyr, 61 Church Ave., De Lancey - closed; relegated to profane use effective 2020-02-16.** The decree (given 7 February 2020) relegates the church 'to profane but not sordid use' and its history says regular Sunday Masses there ceased in 2006. A relegation decree states that the building no longer serves for worship. `last_mass` 2020-02 is the decree's effective month: no closing Mass is dated. The batch note and the survey's decree summary gave 2020-01-26 for this church; that is the Anita decree's date (the St. Adrian decree mentions it as the earlier relegation of St. Joseph), and the St. Adrian decree itself says February 16, 2020. The parish's current bulletin and nine other issues from the past year list no Mass at De Lancey (see the evidence).

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the decree is final unless a later one reverses it.
