## Why it is excluded

**St. Joseph, 20 Division St., Mount Jewett: closed (relegated 2025-03-10).** The relegation decree states that regularly scheduled Masses stopped in July 2023 and relegates the church 'to profane but not sordid use', effective 10 March 2025. `last_mass` is the decree's effective month (`2025-03`) per the diocese rule; the decree itself dates the last regularly scheduled Mass to July 2023 and mentions only the annual titular feast after that. The current parish bulletin (St. Elizabeth/St. Gabriel, 18 and 25 October 2026, rendered pages) lists no Mass at Mount Jewett. The directory files it under Smethport (record 20509, no address) and GCatholic still lists it; both lag the decree.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: a relegated church is not a worship site. A later bulletin listing Mass at 20 Division St. would.
