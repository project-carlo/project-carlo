## Why it is excluded

**St. Mary of the Immaculate Conception, 315 E. 9th St., Erie: closed 2015.** GCatholic (24540, history '2009') still lists it, but the parish was merged into St. Stanislaus effective July 31, 2015 (merger decree text, read 2026-10-23) and the church building closed that summer: the GoErie report of 4 October 2017 calls the building 'a closed Catholic church building' being taken over by a nondenominational ministry, and search snippets from YourErie (announcing a closing on July 26, 2015), the Columbus Dispatch (2015-06-25) and eriebuildings.info date the closing to June-July 2015. I could not open the YourErie or Dispatch pages (blocked), so the 2015-07 `last_mass` is approximate and rests on those snippets plus the decree; the 2017 GoErie lede is the one page I read. No relegation decree exists for it (the decrees page lists none), consistent with a building sold rather than relegated. The diocese's directory (printable, 2026-10-06) and the current St. Stanislaus bulletin do not list it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. A current Catholic Mass at 315 E. 9th St. would reopen it; the building is a different community's.
