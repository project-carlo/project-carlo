## Why it is excluded

**Mount Calvary, 2022 E Lake Rd, Erie: closed; Mass ended July 2024, church relegated effective 2025-03-10.** The decree relegating it (https://www.eriercd.org/images/sections/bishop/pdf/DECREE-Relegating-Mount-Calvary-church-Erie-to-profane-but-not-sordid-use.pdf, read 2026-10-23) decrees that the church is relegated to profane but not sordid use and states 'This decree is effective on March 10, 2025' (given 7 March 2025). Its history says Mount Calvary and St. Mark the Evangelist 'merged into Saint James Parish in Erie in July of 2024, at which time regularly scheduled Masses ceased at Mount Calvary church', that the church was given secondary-church status with no Sunday or holy day Masses, and that since then there has been one funeral and no other request for sacraments. Current schedule check: the 25 October 2026 St. James bulletin lists weekend and weekday Masses at St. James and St. Mark only, and names Mount Calvary only in the footer ('faith families of Mt. Calvary'); the diocese's printable directory of 6 Oct 2026 lists no Mount Calvary under St. James. `last_mass: 2024-07` is the decree's 'July of 2024' (no day, no dated closing Mass found); the relegation date is the later legal closure. The decree adds that the school building is leased to the R.B. Wiley Charter School, which intended to buy the complex and turn the church into an auditorium. GCatholic still lists it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; reopening would need a new decree. Its former parish merged into St. James, which keeps the St. James and St. Mark churches.
