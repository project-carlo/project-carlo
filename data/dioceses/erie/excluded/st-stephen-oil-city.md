## Why it is excluded

**St. Stephen, 21 State St., Oil City - closed; relegated to profane but not sordid use effective 2024-03-18.** The decree (given 11 March 2024) recounts that the building (dedicated 1907) needed over $3.4 million of repairs, that the City of Oil City cited it as a hazard and public nuisance on 19 January 2024, and that it stopped being a mission church in fall 2023 (no Sunday or holy-day Mass). A relegation decree states that the building no longer serves for worship. `last_mass` is the decree's effective month, per the rule settled for Erie; the last Sunday Mass was earlier (fall 2023), and no closing Mass is dated.

The current bulletin of St. Joseph Parish (12 issues, 2 August to 18 October 2026) lists no Mass here and treats the church's contents as salvage. GCatholic still lists the church (with the old website saint-stephens.org). The parish merged into St. Joseph effective 1 January 2020 after hierarchical recourse (the 10 July 2023 'Oil City Parishes' letter, an image-only PDF read here, reports the Apostolic Signatura's final rejection on 24 March 2023); St. Venantius, Rouseville had merged into St. Stephen in 2017.

## What would change this

A bulletin listing Mass at 21 State St. after March 2024.
