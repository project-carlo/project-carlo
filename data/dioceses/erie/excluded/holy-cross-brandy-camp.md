## Why it is excluded

**Holy Cross, Route 219, Brandy Camp: closed, regular Mass ended 2017-02, relegated 2020-07-26.** The relegation decree (effective 'July 26, 2020') says the 1970 church 'served as a mission church of Saint Boniface parish' after Holy Cross parish merged into St. Boniface, Kersey on 2017-02-13 ('February 13, 2017, Holy Cross church lost its parochial status for the last time'), that 'regularly scheduled Sunday Masses ceased there altogether when the parish merger took effect on February 13, 2017', and that since then 'only one baptism and one funeral have been celebrated'. The church is described as 'secondary church without mission status'.

`last_mass` is 2017-02, the decree's date for the end of regular Masses, rather than the decree's effective month (2020-07), which the orchestrator's rule uses when no closing Mass is dated: here the decree itself dates the end of regular Mass. The building's relegation is 2020-07-26.

The current St. Boniface bulletin (25 Oct 2026) and the printable directory (Oct. 6, 2026) list no Holy Cross. GCatholic still has it (lag).

## What would change this

A current bulletin or schedule that lists Mass at Brandy Camp. The relegation decree makes that very unlikely.
