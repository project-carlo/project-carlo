## Why it is excluded

**St. Bernard, 205 Taylor Ave., Falls Creek: a secondary church of Holy Spirit Parish whose only Mass in the current bulletins is the patronal feast; no regular public Mass. Open, not closed.** The 2023 decree made it a secondary church (not a mission); the parish's weekly schedule (18 October 2026 bulletin page 1, home page) lists Masses at St. Catherine, DuBois and Assumption, Sykesville only; the diocese's 22 October 2026 printable directory headed it 'Secondary Church' with no Mass schedule, and the web directory record gives 'Daily: None | Saturday: None | Sunday: None'.

What I read: all 27 Holy Spirit bulletins on the parish's Bulletin page, 19 April to 18 October 2026 (`pdftotext`; scratch/map-erie/scouts/16/pdfs), grepped for 'Bernard' and 'Falls Creek'. The only Mass at St. Bernard is the 20 August feast-day Mass at 5:15 p.m. (quoted in the evidence). The same pattern is in the diocese's own definition of a secondary church (no weekend Mass; Mass at least on the patronal feast) and in the relegation decree for St. Joseph, DuBois (d49), which says of that church: 'the only regularly scheduled Masses have been for the annual titular solemnity'. Under this diocese's settled rule (diocese.md, Secondary churches) a church whose only Masses are the patronal feast, funerals and weddings is `no-public-mass`. That is an inference from a one-year bulletin sample, not a statement by the parish that nothing else happens here.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule that lists a recurring Mass here (weekday, monthly, seasonal), or a feast-day Mass that the parish advertises beyond the patronal solemnity: then make it a church row under `holy-spirit-dubois`. The next patronal Mass is 20 August 2027 (St. Bernard). The church's old website stbernardfallscreek.org no longer resolves (DEAD-DNS, 2026-10-23). No closure or relegation decree exists for it (decrees page read 2026-10-23).
