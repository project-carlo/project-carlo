## Why it is excluded

**St. Mary, 607 E. Main St., Reynoldsville: a secondary church of Holy Spirit Parish whose only Mass in the current bulletins is the patronal feast; no regular public Mass. Open, not closed.** The parish's weekly schedule (18 October 2026 bulletin page 1; home page) lists St. Catherine and Assumption only; the diocese's 22 October 2026 printable directory headed St. Mary 'Secondary Church' with no Mass schedule, and the web record gives 'Daily: None | Saturday: None | Sunday: None'.

What I read: all 27 Holy Spirit bulletins on the parish's Bulletin page, 19 April to 18 October 2026, grepped for 'Mary’s', 'Reynoldsville'. The only Mass at St. Mary is the 5:15 p.m. weekday Mass on 8 September (Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary; quoted in the evidence, the church's feast). Everything else in the bulletins and on the home page for Reynoldsville is non-Mass: weekly rosary in the church or social hall (Tuesdays 6:30 p.m.), Sunday bingo in the social hall, a rosary-bead group and a scripture-sharing hour in the social hall. A search-engine snippet shows a funeral Mass here on 20 July 2026. Under this diocese's rule (diocese.md, Secondary churches: patronal feast and funerals only means `no-public-mass`) it is excluded on that basis; this is an inference from a six-month bulletin sample.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule that lists a recurring Mass at St. Mary (the rosary group and bingo suggest an active building); then make it a church row under `holy-spirit-dubois`. Next patronal Mass: about 8 September 2027. No closure or relegation decree exists for it (decrees page read 2026-10-23). The 1875 church is St. Mary Parish's, merged into Holy Spirit by the 2023 decree.
