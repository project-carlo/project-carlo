## Why it is excluded

**Holy Family, 913 Fulton St., Erie: closed by relegation, effective 2025-12-29.** The decree 'Effecting the Relegation of Holy Family Church (Erie) to Profane but Not Sordid Use' (https://www.eriercd.org/images/sections/bishop/pdf/DECREE-Relegating-Holy-Family-church-Erie.pdf, read 2026-10-23) decrees 'Holy Family church in Erie, Pennsylvania, is relegated to profane but not sordid use, according to the norm of can. 1222 §2. This decree is effective on December 29, 2025'. It gives the history (a Slovak parish divided from St. Stanislaus in 1907; merged into Our Mother of Sorrows 2009; since 2017 a 'secondary church without mission status', no Sunday Mass), the building's poor condition, and an offer from the R.B. Wiley Charter School to buy the complex. Its text says 'regularly scheduled Masses ceased there in July of 2017' and that 'the only scheduled Mass has been the annual celebration of its titular feast'. No current source lists any Mass there: the 25 October 2026 St. Stanislaus / Our Mother of Sorrows bulletin and the diocese's printable directory of 2026-10-06 both omit it, and the diocese's web record (10213) 302s to the home page. `last_mass: 2025-12` is the decree's effective month, not a dated closing Mass; the last Mass may have been the titular-feast Mass of an earlier year, or none since 2017 apart from that feast. GCatholic (24438, 'Last updated' not checked) and the city list still show it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. If the sale to the charter school falls through, the building stays relegated; reopening would need a new decree.
