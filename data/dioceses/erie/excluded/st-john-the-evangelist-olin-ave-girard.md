## Why it is excluded

GCatholic's St. John the Evangelist, Girard entry (24515) is at 101 Olin Ave., the 1946 church in the former school building. The bishop's decree of 9 June 2022 relegated it to profane but not sordid use, effective Sunday 17 July 2022, and moved all sacraments to the parish center at 1001 Main Street E. The decree adds that regularly scheduled Sunday Masses had not been celebrated in the Olin Avenue church since March 2020 (only weekday Masses continued there). The current parish bulletin (25 October 2026, read for 'Olin') does not list it; the parish's live church is st-john-the-evangelist-girard at 1001 Main Street E. `last_mass` is the decree's effective month: no bulletin or news item dating the last Mass was found.

The Plus Code decides which place the GCatholic entry is: 86JX2M2M+W9 is at Olin Avenue (about 1 km from the Main Street church by the ParishesOnline and Nominatim pins), so the roster entry is filed here, and directory:10308 is the Main Street church.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass at the Olin Avenue building (the decree states the building no longer serves for worship), or a decree reversal.
