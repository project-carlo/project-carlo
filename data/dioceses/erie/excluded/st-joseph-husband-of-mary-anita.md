## Why it is excluded

**St. Joseph, Husband of Mary, Anita - closed; relegated to profane use effective 2020-01-26.** No roster (GCatholic or the diocese directory) lists this church; it is recorded because the Walston and DeLancey decrees name it as the first of the parish's three secondary churches to be relegated, and so no later scout re-finds it. The decree says regular Sunday Masses ceased in 2006 and only an annual Mass on Our Lady of Mount Carmel's feast remained. `last_mass` 2020-01 is the decree's effective month. No street address was read; the town is Anita, near Punxsutawney.

Nothing expected.
