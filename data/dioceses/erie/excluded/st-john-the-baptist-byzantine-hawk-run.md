## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists this Ruthenian Catholic (Byzantine rite) church among the churches within the territory of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Erie, but its Jurisdiction line reads "Ruthenian Metropolitanate sui juris of Pittsburgh". It is not a Latin-church parish of the Diocese of Erie. As in every other diocese here, Eastern Catholic churches are excluded as `other-jurisdiction` (a scope decision, not a judgement about the church; Erie `diocese.md` *Rules settled*).

GCatholic address: 24 Fulton St., Hawk Run, Pennsylvania 16840.

Name check: this is not the Latin church in Hawk Run already recorded as Ss. Peter and Paul (open/ss-peter-and-paul-hawk-run); the two are different buildings under different jurisdictions.

A project decision to cover Eastern Catholic eparchies.
