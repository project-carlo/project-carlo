## Why it is excluded

**St. Elizabeth, 26 W. Pleasant St., Corry - closed; relegated to profane use effective 2019-12-01.** The decree (30 October 2019) says regular Sunday Masses ended when the parish merged into St. Thomas on 13 February 2017 and only the titular-feast Mass remained, and relegates the church effective December 1, 2019. A relegation decree states that the building no longer serves for worship. The 2025-2026 St. Thomas bulletins mention only the 'St. Elizabeth Parish Center' as a meeting hall. `last_mass` is the decree's effective month: no closing Mass is dated.

GCatholic still lists the church; the diocese directory does not.

## What would change this

A bulletin listing Mass in the St. Elizabeth church after December 2019.
