## Why it is excluded

**Holy Cross, 4672 Rasselas Rd., Wilcox (Rasselas): closed; no regular Mass since 2003, relegated 2017-09-07.** The relegation decree (effective 'September 7, 2017') says Sunday Masses were discontinued there in 2003, that only four funerals and two weddings have been celebrated since, that the last public Stations of the Cross were in Lent 2015, and that 'there is no regularly scheduled Mass at Holy Cross church in Rasselas'. The building was a secondary church of Holy Rosary, Johnsonburg. The decree also orders the sacred objects removed before any sale.

`last_mass` is 2003, the decree's date for the end of regular Mass (as for Holy Cross, Brandy Camp); the building's relegation is 2017-09-07, after the last occasional funerals and weddings. The roster entry is GCatholic's 'Holy Cross Devotional Chapel', Wilcox. The question whether devotional Masses continue: no source says so. The Oct. 6, 2026 diocese directory lists no Holy Cross under Holy Rosary's churches, Discover Mass shows 'No Masses', and the decree says the building lacks any regular Mass. Holy Rosary has no readable bulletin to grep (see `parishes/holy-rosary-johnsonburg`), so this is a schedule check, not a bulletin check.

A Holy Rosary bulletin or announcement scheduling a public Mass at Rasselas. Check the Holy Rosary bulletin if one becomes readable.
