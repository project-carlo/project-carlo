## Why it is excluded

Mercyhurst's Campus Ministry posts a Fall 2026 Mass schedule (read 2026-10-23): Sunday Mass at 7 p.m. in Christ the King Chapel on listed dates, with a few feast Masses. Discover Mass invites the public and also lists a 10:30 a.m. Sunday Mass at the Sisters of Mercy Motherhouse (a separate building on the campus; the site's older mass-times page said the Mercyhurst community may join the Sisters). No readable bulletin lists any of these (Discover Mass: no bulletins). Per the Erie campus-chapel rule: a Mass a source calls public with no readable bulletin is `no-bulletin`.

The Motherhouse chapel, if it is a public Mass site in its own right, is a separate place and is not recorded here; no source reviewed describes its address.

No roster entry covers this chapel (GCatholic and the directory omit it); it came from a lead, found on Discover Mass and Mercyhurst's site.

## What would change this

A bulletin listing the Masses weekly (Mercyhurst Campus Ministry or a parish) would make the chapel a church row. Revisit when the Spring 2027 schedule posts.
