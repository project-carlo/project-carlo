## Why it is excluded

**St. Stanislaus Kostka-Holy Trinity, 370 Spruce Ave., Sharon - closed; relegated to profane use effective 2019-06-09.** The decree (read 2026-10-23) relegates this secondary church of St. Anthony of Padua parish 'to profane but not sordid use'; a relegation decree states the building no longer serves for worship, and the decree adds that Masses had stopped in February 2017, after the merger into St. Anthony. `last_mass` 2019-06 is the decree's effective month (no closing Mass is dated; the last regular Mass was February 2017). The 25 October 2026 Catholic Community of Sharon bulletin (Discover Mass number 3537, https://discovermass.com/church/st-joseph-sharon-pa/) lists Mass only at St. Joseph (79 Case Ave.) and St. Anthony of Padua (Sunday 8:30 am); a grep of its text for Adalbert, Stanislaus, Irvine, Spruce and Fruit Ave. found no Mass at this building (Sacred Heart appears only in a 'Sacred Heart Candle at Saint Anthony Church' line; 'Holy Trinity' and 'St. Stanislaus' appear in a collection note naming Erie-city churches).

The Sharon church is a different building from St. Stanislaus (Our Mother of Sorrows partner) in Erie.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the decree is final unless a later one reverses it.
