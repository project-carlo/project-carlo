## Why it is excluded

St. Augustine, 33 Turner St., Austin is a secondary church of St. Eulalia Parish (the printable directory of 6 Oct 2026 says 'Secondary Church'; the web record lists no Mass on any day; the October 2023 plan removed its weekend Mass). A run of 53 weekly St. Eulalia bulletins (26 Oct 2025 to 25 Oct 2026) shows exactly two kinds of Mass there: the patronal feast Mass, Friday 28 August 2026 at 6:30 PM ('Past Parishoners and All Deceased Members of St. Augustine'), and a private funeral Mass noted in the 20 and 27 September issues. The parish's own weekly schedule lists no Mass at Austin. Under the Erie rule (a church whose only Masses are the patronal feast, funerals and weddings is `no-public-mass`), it is not a church row.

The feast Mass is public, but one day a year is the secondary-church pattern, and the schedule never lists a recurring Mass here. If a bulletin ever lists a recurring (monthly, seasonal) Mass, add it as a church row under St. Eulalia.

## What would change this

A bulletin or the parish site listing a recurring Mass at Austin (as St. Bibiana's Thursday Mass appeared in June 2026). Recheck the bulletins of late August 2027, and the parish's schedule after any new decree.
