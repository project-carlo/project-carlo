## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists this Ukrainian Catholic (Byzantine rite) church among the churches within the territory of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Erie, but its Jurisdiction line reads "Ukrainian Diocese of Saint Josaphat in Parma". It is not a Latin-church parish of the Diocese of Erie. As in every other diocese here, Eastern Catholic churches are excluded as `other-jurisdiction` (a scope decision, not a judgement about the church; Erie `diocese.md` *Rules settled*).

GCatholic address: Ramey, Pennsylvania 16671.

Name check: this is not the Latin church in Ramey already recorded as Holy Trinity (churches/holy-trinity-ramey, GCatholic 24442); the two are different buildings under different jurisdictions.

The batch roster note called this "Ukrainian: Archeparchy of Philadelphia"; GCatholic's page instead says the Ukrainian Diocese (Eparchy) of Saint Josaphat in Parma. Either way a non-Latin jurisdiction; the exclusion does not depend on which. GCatholic gives no street address for it.

## What would change this

A project decision to cover Eastern Catholic eparchies.
