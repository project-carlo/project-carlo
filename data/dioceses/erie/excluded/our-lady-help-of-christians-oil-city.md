## Why it is excluded

**Our Lady, Help of Christians, 564 Colbert Ave., Oil City - closed; relegated to profane but not sordid use effective 2020-07-19.** The decree (given 1 July 2020) says monthly public worship ceased in March 2019 and the church had been unusable since then because of mold and mildew. A relegation decree states that the building no longer serves for worship. `last_mass` is the decree's effective month, per the rule settled for Erie, because no closing Mass is dated (regular worship ended March 2019).

The current bulletin of St. Joseph Parish (12 issues, 2 August to 18 October 2026) lists no Mass here; GCatholic still lists the church. The parish merged into St. Joseph in 2017 (decree effective 13 February 2017).

## What would change this

A bulletin listing Mass at 564 Colbert Ave. after July 2020.
