## Why it is excluded

**Our Mother of Perpetual Help, 31 Lafayette Ave., Lewis Run: closed, last Mass 2023-02 (inferred).** The relegation decree (effective 1 July 2023) says that when St. Francis of Assisi Parish merged back into St. Bernard on 1 March 2023, 'permission for weekend Masses at Our Mother of Perpetual Help church was revoked' and the church 'has not been used for Mass since that date'; the decree relegates the church 'to profane but not sordid use'. `last_mass` is therefore the month before the 1 March 2023 change, inferred from the decree rather than read as a dated final Mass (no bulletin of that date was found). The current St. Bernard bulletin (18 October 2026, on the Bradford parish site) lists Masses at St. Bernard Church and the Divine Mercy Chapel only, and nothing in the eight 2026 issues read mentions Mass at Lewis Run. GCatholic still lists the church.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: a relegated church is not a worship site. A later bulletin listing Mass at 31 Lafayette Ave. would.
