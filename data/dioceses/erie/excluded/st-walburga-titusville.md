## Why it is excluded

**St. Walburga, 120 Brook St., Titusville: secondary church of St. Titus Parish, no recurring public Mass.** The diocese's 6 October 2026 printable directory lists it as 'Secondary Church' with no Mass times, and its web page shows 'Saturday: None | Sunday: None'. A text search of every St. Titus bulletin from 26 October 2025 to 25 October 2026 finds no weekly, monthly or seasonal Mass there; the building is used for faith formation (Faith Formation Center, Social Hall) and the parish cemetery. The only Mass the bulletins place there is a one-off: the 18 and 25 October 2026 issues invite the Catholic community to a special Mass 'in honor of the dedication of St. Walburga Church' on Tuesday 27 October 2026 at 6:00 PM (the church dedicated 17 August 1892), in place of that day's regular 6:30 PM Mass at St. Titus.

The 2016 merger decree that made St. Walburga a secondary church without mission status (effective 13 February 2017) forbids Sunday, holy-day and vigil Masses there and requires Mass 'every year on the solemnity of the anniversary of the church's dedication and on the solemnity of the church's title (February 25)'. The 27 October 2026 Mass is the dedication-anniversary Mass the decree requires, which is why it is read as the annual occasional Mass rather than a regular schedule.

This follows the rule settled for Erie secondary churches: a church whose only Mass is a once-a-year commemorative Mass stated by a current source is `no-public-mass`, not a church row. **Judgement call:** the 27 October Mass is advertised to the public, so a visitor could attend it; it is treated as the annual occasional Mass of a secondary church, not as a schedule. The title solemnity (25 February) is the other required Mass; the 22 February 2026 St. Titus issue lists nothing at St. Walburga that week: Wednesday 25 February is '8:30 AM at St. Joseph', and the Tuesday 6:30 PM intention for 'The Deceased Members of St. Walburga' is on the normal Tuesday St. Titus slot (location not stated). A bulletin listing a repeat of it in 2027, or any regular Mass, would turn this into a church row under `st-titus-titusville`.

No relegation decree for St. Walburga appears on the diocese's decrees page (74 PDFs read 2026-10-23), so the building is not closed.

## What would change this

A St. Titus bulletin showing a recurring Mass at St. Walburga (watch the late-October 2027 issues for a second anniversary Mass, and February 2027 for the feast on 25 February), or the parish saying the church is now a mission church.
