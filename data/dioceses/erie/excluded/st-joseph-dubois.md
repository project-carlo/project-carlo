## Why it is excluded

**St. Joseph, 301 S State St., DuBois: closed, relegated to profane but not sordid use effective 1 December 2019 (last Mass about then; the decree gives the effective date, no closing Mass is dated).** The relegation decree (given 23 October 2019, bishop Persico; text read 2026-10-23) says: 'This decree is effective on the First Sunday of Advent, December 1, 2019'. `last_mass` is therefore the decree month (2019-12), not a dated closing Mass; before it, the decree says the only regularly scheduled Masses since the 2017 merger into St. Michael had been the annual solemnity (19 March).

Current bulletin check (the settled rule requires one): Holy Spirit Parish, the successor of St. Michael, lists Masses only at St. Catherine and Assumption (18 October 2026 issue, page 1). I also grepped the 27 issues of 19 April-18 October 2026 for 'Joseph'; none places a Mass at the church. GCatholic still lists the building, with a website (stmikestjoe.com) that is dead.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: a relegation decree states the building no longer serves for worship. The point to verify if the building is ever re-dedicated is a bulletin listing Mass there.
