## Why it is excluded

**Our Lady of Fatima, 36198 Lake Rd. (Canadohta Lake), Union City - closed; regular Mass ended 2023; relegated to profane use effective 2024-08-14.** The decree says the church was a summer mission of St. Teresa of Avila (Mass "from the third Sunday of June until Labor Day" under a 1951 permission), that regular Masses ceased altogether in 2023, that there were no requests for weddings, funerals or other services in the year since, and relegates it effective August 14, 2024. A relegation decree states that the building no longer serves for worship. The current bulletin of St. Thomas the Apostle (25 October 2026, with the three prior October issues) lists no Mass there and reports its statues moved to St. Teresa Cemetery. `last_mass` is 2023 because the decree gives only the year; no closing Mass is dated.

The diocese directory keeps a stale record (10420, detail page 302s) and GCatholic still lists the church.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule listing Mass at Canadohta Lake after August 2024. Summer 2027 issues of the St. Thomas bulletin would show that.
