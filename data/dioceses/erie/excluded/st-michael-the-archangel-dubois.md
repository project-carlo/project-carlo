## Why it is excluded

**St. Michael the Archangel, 15 Robinson St., DuBois: a secondary church of Holy Spirit Parish whose only Mass in the current bulletins is the patronal feast; no regular public Mass. Open, not closed.** The parish's weekly schedule (18 October 2026 bulletin page 1; home page) lists St. Catherine and Assumption only; the diocese's 22 October 2026 printable directory headed St. Michael 'Secondary Church' with no schedule; the web record gives 'Daily: None | Saturday: None | Sunday: None'.

What I read: all 27 Holy Spirit bulletins on the parish's Bulletin page, 19 April to 18 October 2026, grepped for 'St. Michael'. Besides the 29 September feast-day Mass (5:15 p.m., Sts. Michael, Gabriel and Raphael; quoted in the evidence), the bulletins mention St. Michael's only as the Monday 9:00 a.m. meeting place of a rosary-bead-making group (not a Mass). Obituary snippets show funerals here (24 October 2026). Under this diocese's rule (diocese.md, Secondary churches) it is `no-public-mass`; this is an inference from a six-month sample.

Not to be confused with the parish St. Michael the Archangel, DuBois, which absorbed the closed St. Joseph (2017) and was itself merged into Holy Spirit in 2023; GCatholic still gives it the old website stmikestjoe.com (dead).

## What would change this

A bulletin listing a recurring Mass at St. Michael: then make it a church row under `holy-spirit-dubois`. Next patronal Mass about 29 September 2027. No closure or relegation decree exists for it (decrees page read 2026-10-23).
