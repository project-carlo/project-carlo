## Why it is excluded

**Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary, 7 Pulaski St., Oil City - closed; relegated to profane but not sordid use effective 2019-07-19.** The decree (given 15 July 2019) says regularly scheduled public worship there had already ceased in 2018, since the 13 February 2017 merger into St. Joseph made it a secondary church without mission status. A relegation decree states that the building no longer serves for worship. `last_mass` is the decree's effective month: no closing Mass is dated in a bulletin or news item I found, so the last occasional Mass is unknown (the decree only says regular worship ended in 2018).

The current bulletin of St. Joseph Parish (12 issues, 2 August to 18 October 2026) lists no Mass here; GCatholic still lists the church. A 2016 decree merged Assumption Parish into St. Joseph.

## What would change this

A St. Joseph bulletin listing Mass at 7 Pulaski St. after July 2019 (it would also mean the building was reopened for worship).
