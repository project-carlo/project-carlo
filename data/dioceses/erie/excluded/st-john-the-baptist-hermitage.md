## Why it is excluded

**St. John the Baptist, 3056 Motorfield Road, Hermitage - not a Catholic church.** No roster lists it; I found it on Discover Mass, whose page labels it 'Romanian Byzantin' with 'Saint George in Canton Rite: Romanian' (the Romanian Greek-Catholic eparchy) and gives the website stjohnacroc.org. That site (read 2026-10-23) is 'St Johns Orthodox Church' of the American Carpatho-Russian Orthodox Diocese of North America, not a Catholic eparchy's parish, so Discover Mass's jurisdiction label is wrong or stale. Excluded as `not-catholic` rather than `other-jurisdiction`, since the site identifies an Orthodox body. Even a Romanian Greek Catholic church would be outside the Diocese of Erie.

I did not find a separate Romanian Greek Catholic parish in Hermitage. The Ruthenian St. Michael, 2230 Highland Rd., Hermitage (GCatholic 17292) is a different church, on the GCatholic roster and outside this batch.

## What would change this

A source showing the Hermitage parish is in communion with Rome (for example a listing by the Romanian Catholic eparchy). Even then it would be `other-jurisdiction`, not an Erie parish.
