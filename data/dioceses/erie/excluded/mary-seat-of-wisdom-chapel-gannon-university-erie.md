## Why it is excluded

The university chapel holds Mass on a posted schedule (Sundays 10 a.m. and 8 p.m. during the academic year; weekdays 11:05/11:15 a.m.), read on Gannon's chapel page on 2026-10-23. Discover Mass invites the public ("We invite you to celebrate Mass with us at Gannon University"). No bulletin is readable: the Discover Mass page says no bulletins are available, and I found no parish bulletin that lists this chapel (I did not search every Erie bulletin for 'Gannon'). Per the Erie rule for campus chapels a Mass a source calls public but no readable bulletin lists is `no-bulletin`.

Address disagreement: GCatholic says 520 Peach St., 16051 (postal code looks wrong for the street); Discover Mass says 512 Peach St., 16541; Gannon's address is 109 University Square, 16541. Not reconciled; neither is recorded as verified.

## What would change this

A bulletin (Gannon campus ministry or a parish) that lists the chapel's Masses weekly would make it a church row. The Discover Mass listing includes a Bulletins tab: check it for a posted PDF.
