## Why it is excluded

Sts. Peter and Paul, Black Horse Pike and Cecil Rd., Williamstown is an Eastern Catholic church of the Ukrainian Greek Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Diocese of Camden. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives its jurisdiction and says it stands "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Camden"; it is in the Camden roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. **It is not on the archeparchy's own parish list** (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-29), so the eparchy's list does not confirm it; GCatholic's jurisdiction line is the only source, and it may be closed, a mission or a stale entry. GCatholic gives no website, phone or Mass times. Whatever its status, it is not a church of the Diocese of Camden. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md` for Eastern Catholic churches. Nothing found says it is open or closed.
