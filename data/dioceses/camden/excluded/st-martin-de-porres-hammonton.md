## Why it is excluded

**St. Martin de Porres, 129 Park Ave., Hammonton: closed, last services October 2013, sold 2016.** The parish's own page says the diocese's merger plan closed it ('Sunday Masses ended in January 2013 and all services at the church ended in October 2013. The property was listed for sale in June 2014 and sold in 2016'). GCatholic still lists it under the parish, so the roster entry is stale. The current bulletin schedule names only St. Joseph and St. Anthony of Padua.

`last_mass` is 2013-10, the month the parish page gives for the last services of any kind; the last Sunday Mass was January 2013.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass at Park Avenue. The 'St Martin de Porres Chapel' line in the Sanctuary Lights list of the 4 October 2026 issue is the only recent mention (absent from 1 November); it has no time or address.
