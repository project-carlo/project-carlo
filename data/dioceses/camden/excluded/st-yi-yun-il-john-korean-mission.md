## Why it is excluded

**St. Yi Yun Il John Korean Catholic Mission, 2001 Springdale Rd., Cherry Hill: open, no readable current bulletin.** The mission holds a public Sunday Mass in Korean at 1:00 pm (its own site, read 2026-10-29: '주일 미사 : 주일, 오후 1시'), in the building of The Catholic Church of St. Mary. It is not a separate place of worship, so it has no church row of its own: its building is St. Mary's church row (`st-mary-cherry-hill`). It cannot be a church row under St. Mary because St. Mary's current bulletin does not list its Mass (1 November 2026 issue, pages 1-2 rendered and read: only the English-language Masses; the parish Mass Schedule page also omits it). It cannot be a parish row because its own bulletin page (`chkcc.org/file-share`, '주보') ends at 9 June 2024 (folders for 2023 and 2024 and loose PDFs to `06.09.24`). The settled Camden rule for the Korean missions is a church row under the parish whose bulletin lists their Mass, a parish row if they publish a readable bulletin, otherwise `no-bulletin` (README Settled policy 4), so this is `no-bulletin`.

**Where it meets (the lead's question).** Two sources give two addresses. GCatholic gives St. Mary, 2001 Springdale Rd., Cherry Hill. The 2015 directory gives 1219 Delsea Dr., Westville (last updated 2015-03-18). The mission's own site settles it: it moved to St. Mary's on 1 July 2012 ('2012년 7월1일 ... 세인트 메리 성당으로 이전했습니다'), after earlier years at Holy Rosary, Cherry Hill and the Westville church, and gives St. Mary's address and phone 856.912.1355. The directory's Westville address is the pre-2012 place; GCatholic's is current. The directory's 1219 Delsea Dr. is where GCatholic puts Holy Redeemer, Westville Grove (gcatholic:18874, a church of Holy Angels Parish, Woodbury; batch `woodbury-paulsboro`), so the 'Westville church' the mission used before 2012 was probably Holy Redeemer; no source read says so. Either way the mission's own site says it no longer meets in Westville.

The mission is Latin and diocesan, not another jurisdiction: Rev. Francis D. Kim, its pastor, was ordained for the Diocese of Camden in 2012 (its site).

## What would change this

A 2025 or 2026 issue on `chkcc.org/file-share` would make it a parish row with that page as its recipe. A Korean Mass line on a page of St. Mary's bulletin (only pages 1-2 were read) would make it a church-level note under St. Mary, which already has its building as a row. The Sunday 1:00 pm Mass itself is on the mission's own site, so a live schedule exists; what is missing is a bulletin the pipeline could read.
