## Why it is excluded

**St. Monica, 108 N Pennsylvania Ave., Atlantic City: closed.** The Catholic Star Herald of 2024-11-21 says 'Although the original Saint Monica Church has been closed'. The 2015 merger plan (Catholic Star Herald, 2015-06-11) named only Our Lady Star of the Sea and St. Nicholas of Tolentine for worship, and the 1 November 2026 Parish of Saint Monica bulletin and the parish site list three churches, none on Pennsylvania Avenue. The summer guide omits it too. No source gives the closing date, so `last_mass` is blank; it was no later than the 2015 merger plan's statement of worship sites, which is inference, not a stated date.

The parish's current Mass schedule lists three churches; the only roster source for a fourth is GCatholic's undated 'parishes merged 2015' entry. The bulletin's giving appeal also speaks of 'our three church-' es, matching the three on the schedule.

## What would change this

A bulletin or parish page listing Mass at 108 N Pennsylvania Ave. Nothing expected. A closing date, if found (the diocese's 2015 merger decree is a scanned image on the Catholic Star Herald, `decree-of-the-merger-of-of-the-parishes-of-atlantic-city-and-establishment-of-the-parish-of-saint-monica`), would go in `last_mass`.
