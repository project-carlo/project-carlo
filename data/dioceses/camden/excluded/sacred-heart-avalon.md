## Why it is excluded

The 2010 decree consolidating Maris Stella and St. Paul does not name it. So it is recorded as a church with no public Mass, on the parish's own current schedules; the August check covers the summer season, and a June 2027 recheck is set because shore churches sometimes add a seasonal Mass. The reason is `no-public-mass` and not `closed`: nothing says the church closed.
