## Why it is excluded

**St. John Bosco, 2 Hillcrest Ave., Millville: closed, sold in 2020 or early 2021, last Mass date not found.** GCatholic (gcatholic:18932) still lists it under 'All Saints Parish'. The Catholic Star Herald says the former church 'was closed and sold in 2020' (2026-02-05) while its 2021-02-17 article says it 'is in the process of being sold to Hendrix House'. The two articles disagree by a few months on the sale; they agree it is no longer a church. The parish's current bulletin lists no Mass there, and its replacement is the Saint John Bosco Chapel, a different building on the parish campus at 621 Dock Street, with fixtures from the church (`churches/st-john-bosco-chapel-millville`). `last_mass` is left empty because no source dates the last Mass.

## What would change this

A source dating the last Mass would fill `last_mass`. Nothing here suggests the building will hold Mass again.
