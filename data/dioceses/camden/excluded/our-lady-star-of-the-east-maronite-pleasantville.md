## Why it is excluded

Our Lady Star of the East Maronite Church, 25 Black Horse Pike, Pleasantville is an Eastern Catholic church of the Maronite Eparchy of Saint Maron of Brooklyn, not of the Diocese of Camden. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives its jurisdiction and says it stands "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Camden"; it is in the Camden roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. The eparchy's own parish directory lists it at the same address (as '25 West Black Horse Pike'). Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md` for Eastern Catholic churches. GCatholic types it 'Mission Our Lady Star'. Whether it has Mass was not checked; its former website ourladystaroftheeast.org no longer resolves.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs to a dataset for its own eparchy, not Camden's.
