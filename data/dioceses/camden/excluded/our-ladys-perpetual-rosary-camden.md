## Why it is excluded

**Our Lady's Perpetual Rosary, the chapel of the Monastery of the Dominican Nuns of the Perpetual Rosary, 1500 Haddon Avenue, Camden: closed, last public Mass 9 December 2013.** GCatholic (gcatholic:37949, type Monastery/Shrine, website the lay fraternity's blog) still lists it. The Diocese of Camden's own news says 'the final public Mass in Camden at the Dominican Monastery on Haddon Avenue came to a close on Dec. 9' 2013, when the last four nuns left for Elmira, New York. The blog's 'daily mass 6:55am, Sunday 7:00am' predates that. None of the three batch bulletins lists Mass there, and the city has no parish of which it is a worship site. A June 2024 nj.com headline (snippet only, nj.com blocks the probe) says the building is to be demolished for a parking lot, which is consistent but not relied on.

## What would change this

A bulletin or diocesan source saying public Mass resumed at 1500 Haddon Avenue.
