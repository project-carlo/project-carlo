## Why it is excluded

**St. Lucy, Blue Anchor (Winslow Township) — closed, last Mass 2010-11-07.** Part of Blessed John XXIII Parish, with Sacred Heart, Cedar Brook, it was united into Christ the Redeemer Parish on 10 November 2010. The Catholic Star Herald (2015-02-05) says "On Nov. 7, 2010 St. Lucy closed after a final Mass", and the parish's own history says it "celebrated its final Mass and closed as a parish church" on the same date. Neither source gives a street address, and no roster (GCatholic, the diocese locator, the summer guide) lists it. The current Christ the Redeemer bulletin (24-25 October 2026) and site list no Mass at Blue Anchor; the parish keeps a St. Lucy's Chapel inside the Assumption church in Atco, which is part of that church's record, not a separate place.
