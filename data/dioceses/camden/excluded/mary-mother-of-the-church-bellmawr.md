## Why it is excluded

Closed. The church (20 Braisington Avenue, Bellmawr) was the seat of the Parish of St. Rita from 2010. On 30 June 2014 St. Rita was dismembered and the church was absorbed into St. Joachim Parish, whose pastor then stopped using it ('at first occasional and then later discontinued in favor of creating a one campus parish'). On 19 April 2021 the bishop relegated it to profane but not sordid use, with any sale proceeds going to St. Joachim. St. Joachim's current bulletin and website list Mass at Annunciation only.

No source read gives the date of the last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty: it was before 2021-04-19 and after the 2014 merger. The Catholic Star Herald and the diocese's site give no closing notice. GCatholic's group 'St. Rita of Cascia Parish' and its website theparishofsaintrita.org (no DNS) are stale, as the survey noted.
