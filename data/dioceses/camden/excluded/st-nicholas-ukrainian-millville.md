## Why it is excluded

St. Nicholas Ukrainian Catholic Church, Carmel Rd., Millville is an Eastern Catholic church of the Ukrainian Greek Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Diocese of Camden. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives its jurisdiction and says it stands "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Camden"; it is in the Camden roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. The archeparchy's own parish list names it, with the same phone number; its street number and zip differ from GCatholic's (801 Carmel Rd, 08332, against 373 Carmel Rd, 08832), the zip looking like a typo. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md` for Eastern Catholic churches. Whether it has Mass was not checked; its former website stnicholasmillville.com no longer resolves.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs to a dataset for its own eparchy, not Camden's.
