## Why it is excluded

**Our Lady of the Highway, Sea and Air, 1993 White Horse Pike, Pomona: a roadside shrine with no Mass found.** GCatholic lists it as a Shrine, a mission of Assumption, Galloway, and gives it OLPH's website. OLPH's current bulletin lists Mass only at Assumption and St. Nicholas and never mentions the shrine (text search, 18 October 2026 issue). A search-result snippet (Tripadvisor, page not opened) calls it 'a small Roman Catholic roadside shrine'. By Camden's settled rule for shrine chapels (a church row under a parish whose bulletin lists their public Mass, otherwise excluded) it is `no-public-mass`.

This rests on absence from the bulletin and a third-party description, not a source that says there is no Mass; hence the revisit. No bulletin of its own exists, so it is not a parish row either.
