## Why it is excluded

St. Michael Ukrainian Catholic Church, 675 Cooper Landing Rd., Cherry Hill is an Eastern Catholic church of the Ukrainian Greek Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Diocese of Camden. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives its jurisdiction and says it stands "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Camden"; it is in the Camden roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. The archeparchy's own parish list names it at the same address. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md` for Eastern Catholic churches. Whether it has Mass was not checked; its former website stmichaelscherryhill.com no longer resolves.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs to a dataset for its own eparchy, not Camden's.
