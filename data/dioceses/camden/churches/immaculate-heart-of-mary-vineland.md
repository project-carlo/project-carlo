## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Thursday 7:00pm Mass in Spanish (1 November 2026 bulletin page 1 and the parish Mass schedule, read 2026-10-29). It is the former Immaculate Heart of Mary parish church, merged with St. Francis of Assisi to form Divine Mercy Parish (Sacred Heart history page). It is in no roster (neither GCatholic nor the directory lists it), so it has no roster link; the Run's rule that a merged parish's churches come from its current masthead applies.

## Name and address

410 S. Eighth St., Vineland NJ 08360 in the bulletin; the parish page says 8th Street (410 S. 8th St.). A Nominatim search for the address returned nothing, so coordinates are null for `pnpm geocode`. Only the parish's own bulletin and site (one source) give it, so the flags stay false.
