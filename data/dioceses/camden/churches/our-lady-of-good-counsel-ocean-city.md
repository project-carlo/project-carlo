## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round, with one Sunday Mass off-season. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (image-only; page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) lists Sunday 7:30 AM only; the parish's 'Winter Schedule' page gives the same. The summer guide's Saturday 4 PM and Sunday 7:30, 9 and 11 AM are the summer additions, so the church is open for Mass all year and is not seasonal.

## Name and address

'Our Lady of Good Counsel' in GCatholic, the bulletin and the parish page; 'Our Lady of Good Counsel Church' in the summer guide. '40th & Asbury Ave.' (GCatholic and summer guide) and '40th Street & Asbury Ave.' (parish page): cross streets only, so the address is unverified. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code.
