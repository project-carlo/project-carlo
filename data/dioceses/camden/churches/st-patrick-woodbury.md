## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: the parochial church of Holy Angels Parish. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, read 2026-10-29) gives Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 7:30, 9:30, 11:30 AM and 6:00 PM, and Monday to Thursday 9:00 AM here, and the parish's home page gives the same times. 'St. Patrick's' is the only church it lists for Sunday Mass.

## Name and address

86 Cooper St., Woodbury, NJ 08096 in the bulletin masthead, the parish site and GCatholic, which names the building 'St. Patrick Holy Angels Parish'. Two older addresses disagree: the 2010 merger decree says '85 Euclid Street' and the diocese directory's parish entry (directory:22, last updated 2015) says 64 Cooper Street (the parish's old address; the parish office is now 81 Cooper St.). The current bulletin and GCatholic agree, so they are followed. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code.
