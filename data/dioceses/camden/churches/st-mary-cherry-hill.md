## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The parish's Mass Schedule page and the 1 November 2026 bulletin (image-only; page 1 rendered and read; both 2026-10-29) list Saturday 5:00pm, Sunday 8:30am and 10:30am and weekday Mass Monday to Friday 8:00am (in the Chapel, the same building).

The Korean mission of St. Yi Yun Il John also uses this building (Sunday 1:00 pm, per its own site) but it is not a second place: see `excluded/st-yi-yun-il-john-korean-mission`.
