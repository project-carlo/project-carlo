## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (image-only; page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) lists weekend Masses Saturday 4:00 PM and Sunday 8:00, 9:30 and 11:00 AM and weekday Mass Monday to Saturday 8:30 AM; the parish site shows the same. The summer guide's note '(June 29th to Labor Day Weekend)' does not make the Sunday schedule seasonal, since the same times are in force in November.

## Name and address

331 8th Street South, Brigantine, NJ 08203 in the bulletin masthead, the parish site, the diocese directory (2015) and the summer guide; GCatholic writes '331 8th St. S'. All agree. Names: 'Saint Thomas the Apostle Church' (bulletin), 'St. Thomas the Apostle' (GCatholic, summer guide). Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code (87F7CJ3J+FX); the directory's point is about 45 m away.
