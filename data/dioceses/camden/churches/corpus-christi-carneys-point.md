## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, read 2026-10-29) lists Saturday 4pm vigil, Sunday 10am and 12:30pm (Spanish) at Corpus Christi Church, and daily Mass at 8:30am in its chapel; the parish's Mass Times page agrees. It is the parish seat (diocese news, 2010-10-29).

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish site and the 2010 decree call it Corpus Christi; GCatholic (18853) and the diocese's Parish Locator (directory:71) list the same building at 369 Georgetown Rd. under the parish name 'St. Gabriel the Archangel'. The address agrees in the bulletin masthead (parish office), the Locator and GCatholic, so it is marked verified. The name is left unverified because the rosters carry the parish name, not the church's, and only the bulletin and parish site name the church. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6PG6P+FQ); the Locator's (39.712169, -75.462292) is about 40 m away.
