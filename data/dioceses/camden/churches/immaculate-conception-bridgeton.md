## Holds Mass

The parish's own church page (read in the browser 2026-10-29) lists: WEEKDAY MASSES Thursday 8:00 AM (English), Tuesday 6:30 PM (Spanish); WEEKEND MASSES Saturday 8:00 AM (English), Sunday 8:00 AM (English) and 7:00 PM (Spanish); confessions Saturday after the 8:00 AM Mass until 11:00 AM; adoration Thursday 9:00 AM-6:00 PM. This is the parish's website schedule; the bulletin PDF could not be read (see the parish record `holy-cross-bridgeton`), so the bulletin has not confirmed it.

The original Bridgeton church (the history on the same page says it served the whole city for almost 100 years before St. Teresa of Avila was built). The 2013 pantry article puts the parish convent at 64 North Street, Bridgeton (not a separate site).
