## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (image-only; page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) lists Saturday 4:30 PM and Sunday 10:30 AM, and the parish's 'Winter Mass Schedule' page gives the same. The summer guide's 'Sat 4:30 PM; Sun 7, 8:30, 10:30 AM' is the summer set, so the church is not seasonal.

## Name and address

'St. Ann' (GCatholic, bulletin) or 'St. Ann Church' (summer guide, parish site). 2901 Atlantic Avenue, Wildwood 08260 agrees across GCatholic, the summer guide, the bulletin cover and the parish site. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code. The directory's one entry for the whole parish is its office at 1500 Central Avenue, Suite 100 (North Wildwood); it is linked to this church for coverage only, a judgment recorded in the evidence, and says nothing about which church is the seat.
