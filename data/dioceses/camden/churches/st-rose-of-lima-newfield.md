## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: the parish's 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives a Saturday vigil at 4:00pm here, with confessions 3:15-3:45pm, and the parish's Mass Schedule page (read 2026-10-29) the same. It is the parish seat and its office.

## Name and address

GCatholic lists this building as 'Our Lady of the Blessed Sacrament' (the parish's name) at 104 Catawba Ave., Newfield, and the diocese directory gives the parish at the same address; the bulletin, the parish's churches page and the 2011 decree news all call the church **St. Rose of Lima**, which is followed. Address agrees in all four sources. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6GXWH+X9); the parish's churches page gives Plus Code GXXH+59, about 50 m away, and the directory (39.547876, -75.021529) about 20 m.
