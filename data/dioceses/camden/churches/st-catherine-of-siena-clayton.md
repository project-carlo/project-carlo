## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, read 2026-10-29) gives Sunday 8 AM, Monday and Thursday 9 AM and Saturday 7:30 AM here, with the Holy Day vigil at 7 PM; the parish's own schedule page agrees on Sunday 8a, Monday and Thursday 9a and the Holy Day vigil at 7p.

## Name and address

700 N. Delsea Dr., Clayton, NJ 08312, in the bulletin masthead and on the parish contact page (one source: the parish). No independent source gives this church by name: neither roster lists 'St. Catherine of Siena'. `name_verified` and `address_verified` stay false for that reason. Coordinates are left empty for the geocoder.

**Roster entries linked here, by inference.** GCatholic 18882 ('St. Michael the Archangel', 49 W North St., Clayton) and the diocese directory's entry 50 ('The Parish of St. Michael the Archangel', 49 W. North St., Clayton) name the parish, at its office address, which the parish's contact page puts 'right next to Saint Catherine of Siena Church'. Neither entry names a church, and the parish has two (this one and Nativity in Franklinville, which no roster has). I linked both entries here because the office adjoins this church and both are in Clayton; this is inference, not something either roster says. A reviewer could link them to the parish record instead. There was no church named St. Michael: the parish took that name at the 2010 merger of Nativity and St. Catherine of Siena.
