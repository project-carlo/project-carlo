## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, read 2026-10-29 from a rendering) lists Saturday vigil 4:00 pm and Sunday 7:30, 9 and 11 am, with weekday Mass 9:00 am Monday to Friday in Emmaus Chapel, which the bulletin lists beside the parish office as the chapel "Open Daily (Mon-Fri) 8:00 am - 2:00 pm". The summer guide does not cover Camden County.

## Name and address

"St. Charles Borromeo" in GCatholic, the directory, the bulletin and the parish site. The street is "Stagecoach Road" in the bulletin and the site, "Stage Coach" in GCatholic and the directory. The town differs: GCatholic and the directory say Washington Township (the municipality), the bulletin, the site and the 2026 priest assignments say Sicklerville; this record follows the bulletin and site, with the same ZIP 08081 in every source. The coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6PXR8+M8, 39.7417, -75.0342). The directory's own coordinates (39.7298, -75.0331) are about 1.3 km south of it and are probably a street-level geocode of the road; the Plus Code is building-precise, so the row follows it.
