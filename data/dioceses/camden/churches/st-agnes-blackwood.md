## Holds Mass

Yes, by inference. No source prints a Mass at St. Agnes under its own name: the 25 October 2026 bulletin (image-only; page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) gives one parish schedule of five weekend and eleven weekly Masses and assigns only the Spanish Masses (Saturday 6:00pm, Sunday 9:30am) to St. Jude. The parish's About page calls St. Agnes the main campus, with the church, chapel, rectory and parish office, and says it serves 4,537 families; the English Masses are not assigned to St. Jude, which the bulletin names only for the Spanish ones. The parish publishes no per-church schedule, so which Masses are at St. Agnes was not read. Next check: a bulletin or the parish's Mass page that names the location of each Mass.
