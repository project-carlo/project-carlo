## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: the parish's 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists Thursday and Friday 9:00 am daily Mass here, Tuesday 7:00 pm Spanish Mass, Thursday 7:00 pm Charismatic Mass and First Friday 7:00 pm Mass, and Eucharistic adoration every Thursday 9:30am-7:00pm and from First Friday 8:30pm; the parish's Mass Schedule page (read 2026-10-29) gives the Thursday and Friday 9am Mass. It is not in GCatholic or the directory, which list only the Newfield church for this parish.
