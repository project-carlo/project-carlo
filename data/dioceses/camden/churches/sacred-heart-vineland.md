## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Sunday 11:00 am at Sacred Heart Church (live-streamed), plus weekday 8:00 am Tuesday and Wednesday in the Sacred Heart High (School) Chapel, and a Spanish Mass on the first Sunday at 12:15pm (1 November 2026 bulletin page 1, read 2026-10-29). The chapel is on the Sacred Heart campus (the parish site calls it the Sacred Heart High School Chapel); it has no address of its own and no separate row.

## Name and address

**Sources disagree on the street number.** The 1 November 2026 bulletin gives Sacred Heart Church at 922 East Landis Avenue. The parish site footer and GCatholic give 1010 E. Landis Avenue, which may be the school or campus address. OpenStreetMap's Nominatim puts 922 and 1010 about 200 m apart on Landis Avenue (an interpolation, weak). The bulletin address is used here because it names the church itself; GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6FXPM+8W, about 80 m from Nominatim's 922) is used for coordinates. Both flags stay false until an on-site or postal source settles the number.
