## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (text layer, read 2026-10-29) gives Sunday 8:30 am English and 12pm Spanish and weekday Mass at 12:05pm Monday to Friday at the Cathedral; the parish site's Mass Schedule agrees on 8:30 and the weekday 12:05, and gives Sunday Spanish at 11:30 am (older than the bulletin's 12pm).
