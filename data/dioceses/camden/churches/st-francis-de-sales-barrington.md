## Holds Mass

Yes, but only monthly as far as found: the Igbo Catholic Apostolate of the Diocese of Camden has its Mass here at noon on the third Sunday of each month (Catholic Star Herald, 2026-07-23; the bishop celebrated it on 19 July 2026). The article calls it 'Saint Rose of Lima Parish's Saint Francis de Sales Church', so it is a church of the parish. The parish's 25 October 2026 bulletin and its home page Mass Times list the Haddon Heights church only, with no line for Barrington, so no weekly or weekday Mass here appears in the parish's own schedule. Whether the parish holds any other Mass here isn't known.

## Name and address

The church's name is St. Francis de Sales (the 2010 St. Rita decree notice and the 2026 Star Herald agree); GCatholic shortens it to 'St. Francis'. The only source for the address, 199 Willmont Ave., is GCatholic, so `address_verified` is false; the coordinates are its Plus Code. The Star Herald and the diocese give no street.

## Not yet confirmed

The bulletin does not list the monthly Igbo Mass, which the pipeline would not see from it. The row is kept on the Star Herald's account of a regular monthly Mass.
