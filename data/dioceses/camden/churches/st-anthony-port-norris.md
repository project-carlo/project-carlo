## Holds Mass

The parish's own church page (read in the browser 2026-10-29) lists: WEEKEND MASS Sunday 11:30 AM (2nd, 4th and alternating 5th Sunday); confessions by appointment. No weekday Mass is listed. This is the parish's website schedule; the bulletin PDF could not be read (see the parish record `holy-cross-bridgeton`), so the bulletin has not confirmed it.

The smallest church of the parish (its own page: 'the smallest church in our parish'). The 2010 decree calls it St. Michael's 'Mission' ('St. Michael, Cedarville and its Mission, St. Anthony in Pt. Norris'). Mass on only some Sundays, so it never overlaps with St. Michael's 1st/3rd Sunday Mass.
