## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round. The 25 October 2026 bulletin of the Parish of St. Maximilian Kolbe lists Daily Mass Tuesday to Friday 8:00 am, Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 8:00 am and 10:00 am, with confessions Saturday 3:00 pm. The summer guide's times are the same Saturday and Sunday ones, so nothing about this church is seasonal. This is the parish's seat.

## Name and address

'Church of the Resurrection' in the bulletin and the summer guide, 'Resurrection' in GCatholic; the diocese directory lists the parish 'St. Maximilian Kolbe The Parish of' at the same address. Address '200 Tuckahoe Road' in the bulletin and directory, '200 W Tuckahoe Rd.' in GCatholic and the summer guide, and ParishesOnline's organization record: the same building ('W' present in some). Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code; the directory's point is within about 15 m.
