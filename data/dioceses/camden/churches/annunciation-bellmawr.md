## Holds Mass

Yes: Saturday vigil 4 PM, Sunday 8 and 10 AM, Spanish Mass Sunday 12:00 PM, and Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 7:30 AM, per the 25 October 2026 bulletin and the parish's home page (both read 2026-10-29). Not seasonal, not closed. It is the parish's only worship site: the bulletin and the parish site give one address and one schedule.

## Name and address

The sources disagree on the name. The bulletin (page 6, 'St. Joachim Parish at Annunciation Church') and the diocese's 2021 relegation decree (the 'Annunciation church, located on the principle campus') call the building Annunciation Church. GCatholic and the diocese's 2015 directory give the parish's name, 'St. Joachim', for the same address. The row takes the building's name, as the two sources that describe the building do, and `name_verified` stays false because GCatholic differs and the parish's own pages never settle it. The address, 601 W. Browning Road, agrees across the bulletin, GCatholic and the directory (`address_verified` true). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.

The former Mary, Mother of the Church church, which the 2014 consolidation joined to this parish, is `excluded/mary-mother-of-the-church-bellmawr`.
