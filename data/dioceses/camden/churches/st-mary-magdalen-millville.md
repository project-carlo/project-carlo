## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly, as the parish's main church: the Parish of All Saints' 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday 4:00pm vigil and Sunday 8:00am and 10:30am at 621 Dock St., and the parish home page (read 2026-10-29) gives the same weekend times and 'Reconciliation ... Sunday 9:45AM to 10:15AM' in the church. The bulletin does not name the building for the weekend Masses; St. Mary Magdalen is the parish seat (diocese decree news, 2010-10-29) and the only church left at this address besides the chapel, so the weekend Masses are attributed to it. Weekday Mass is in the Saint John Bosco Chapel in the parish building next door (`st-john-bosco-chapel-millville`).

## Name and address

621 Dock St., Millville, NJ 08332 in GCatholic ('St. Mary Magdalen'), the diocese directory (entry 49, 'The Parish of All Saints', 621 Dock Street), the bulletin cover and the parish contact page. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6CX35+4Q); the directory's (39.403012, -75.041126) is about 55 m away. The directory entry is the parish's, linked here because this is its seat.
