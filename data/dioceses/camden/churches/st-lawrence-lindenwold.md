## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Sunday 8:30 am and 11:30 am (Misa en Español) and Thursday 7:00 pm (Spanish), per the 25 October 2026 bulletin and the parish's Mass Schedule page (both read 2026-10-29). Not closed and not seasonal.

## Name and address

The record's name keeps both. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code; the directory's point (39.820981, -74.994331) agrees within about 30 m.
