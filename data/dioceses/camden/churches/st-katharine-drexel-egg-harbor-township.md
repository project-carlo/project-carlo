## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, read 2026-10-29) lists Saturday 4:30pm Vigil, Sunday 8:30am, 10:30am and 5:00pm, weekdays 8:00am and Saturday 9:00am. The summer guide says all Masses are in Drexel Hall, and the bulletin's calendar puts parish liturgies (Mass of Remembrance, reconciliation) there. This is the one worship site of the parish; the bulletin names no church building beside Drexel Hall, and the summer guide's note is the only source saying the hall is where Mass is held. Whether a separate church building exists on the lot is not stated in anything read.
