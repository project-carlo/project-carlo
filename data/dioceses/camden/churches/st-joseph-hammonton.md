## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-29, page 1) lists Monday to Saturday 8:00 AM, Saturday vigil 5:00 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM, Wednesday 7:00 PM and Sunday 12:00 Noon in Spanish. It is the parish's base church.

## Name and address

226 French St. is the bulletin masthead's and the parish page's address, and the directory's (the directory's point is within a few metres of GCatholic's). GCatholic gives '220 3rd St.', which is presumably the same corner site's other frontage; the sources disagree on the street, so `address_verified` stays false. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code (87F7J5QV+PX). `name_verified`: the bulletin, the parish page and GCatholic all say St. Joseph.
