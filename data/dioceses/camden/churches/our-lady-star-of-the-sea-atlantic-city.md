## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 1 November 2026 Parish of Saint Monica bulletin (page 1, read 2026-10-29) lists Mass at Our Lady Star of the Sea Monday to Friday 7:00 AM and 5:15 PM, Tuesday 6:30 PM (Vietnamese), Wednesday 7:00 PM (Spanish), Saturday 4:15 PM and 6:00 PM (Spanish), Sunday 7:30 AM, 10:00 AM (Spanish) and 6:00 PM (Vietnamese). It is the parish's main church and office.

2651 Atlantic Avenue, Atlantic City, NJ 08401 in the bulletin, the parish site, GCatholic, the summer guide and the diocese directory (which gives the parish at this address); all agree.
