## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Sunday 10:15am Polish/English and Fridays 8:30am in the 'St. Joseph Polish Chapel' (25 October 2026 Cathedral bulletin, read 2026-10-29). The parish site gives the same times but Sunday 10:00. The 2017 merger article said 'all affected churches will remain open for normal worship, unless and until determined otherwise by the parishes themselves' (Catholic Star Herald, 2017-07-06); the current bulletin shows it did.

## Name and address

1010 Liberty Street, Camden, NJ 08104 on GCatholic and the Cathedral's site (which misprints the zip 08140); the bulletin gives no street address for it. Sources name it 'St. Joseph' (GCatholic), 'St. Joseph Polish' (bulletin) and 'St Joseph Polish Apostolate' (site); `name_verified` stays false because no source gives the church's own formal name, and the Pro-Cathedral is also St. Joseph in the same city, so 'St. Joseph (Polish)' keeps them apart. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code. GCatholic's stjoenj.net is JavaScript-rendered and was not read.
