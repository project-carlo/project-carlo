## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 1 November 2026 St. Simon Stock bulletin (read 2026-10-29) gives Monday to Saturday 8:00 am, Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 8:00, 10:00 am and 12:00 pm, and the parish site gives the same. Neither names the church by its former name. The parish has one address, 178 W. White Horse Pike, and the history page says the church there (Cedar Avenue and White Horse Pike, dedicated 1931) was Our Lady of Mt. Carmel's; GCatholic puts Our Lady of Mount Carmel at the same address.

## Name and address

178 W. White Horse Pike, Berlin, NJ 08009 in the bulletin, the parish site, GCatholic and the 2015 directory (all agree). The name is a naming difference: the diocese's directory lists the parish only ('St. Simon Stock Parish'), GCatholic lists the church 'Our Lady of Mount Carmel' in the group 'St. Simon Stock Parish', and the bulletin and site call it 'St. Simon Stock Parish'/'the Church'. Kept 'Our Lady of Mount Carmel' as the building's name on GCatholic's and the history page's authority; the bulletin never uses a church name for the Mass site. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code; the directory's point (39.803586, -74.949741) agrees within a few metres.
