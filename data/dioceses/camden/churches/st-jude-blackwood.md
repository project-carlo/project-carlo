Yes, weekly. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (image-only; page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) lists 'Misa en Espanol (St. Jude Church)' Saturday 6:00pm and Sunday 9:30am. The parish's single English schedule doesn't say which church holds each Mass, so any English Masses here were not read.
