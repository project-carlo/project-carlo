## Holds Mass

The parish's own church page (read in the browser 2026-10-29) lists: WEEKDAY MASSES Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday 8:00 AM (English); WEEKEND MASSES Saturday 5:00 PM and Sunday 9:30 AM (English); confessions Saturday 4:00-4:45 PM; adoration Wednesday 9:00 AM-3:00 PM. This is the parish's website schedule; the bulletin PDF could not be read (see the parish record `holy-cross-bridgeton`), so the bulletin has not confirmed it.

This is the former St. Mary, **Rosenhayn**: the 2010 decree names 'St. Mary, Rosenhayn' among the five uniting parishes, and the page's history says 'The new St. Mary's Church in Rosenhay[n]'. Both GCatholic and the parish site give a Bridgeton mailing address (732 Morton Ave., 08302), so the church is named for Bridgeton here to match them; the place is Rosenhayn, in Deerfield Township.
