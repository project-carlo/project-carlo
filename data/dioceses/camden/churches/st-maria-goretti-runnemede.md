## Holds Mass

Yes, but only once a week: the Sunday 9:00 am Mass (SMG) in the 13 September (text layer) and 25 October 2026 (image-only, rendered and read) bulletins, with Sunday 8:15-8:45 am confessions. Every weekday and the Saturday and other Sunday Masses are at St. Teresa. Not closed and not seasonal.
