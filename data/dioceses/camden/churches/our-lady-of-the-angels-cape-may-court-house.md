## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (pages 1-2 rendered and read 2026-10-29) gives the winter schedule: Monday to Friday 8:00AM, Saturday 5:00PM, Sunday 8:00AM and 10:30AM. The parish's site agrees and adds the summer set (Sunday 9:30AM and 11AM), the same as the 2026 summer guide. It is the parish's seat.

## Name and address

'Our Lady of the Angels' in the bulletin, GCatholic, the directory and the summer guide. 35 E. Mechanic Street is in the bulletin, GCatholic and the directory; the summer guide's 'East Mechanic St. & Garden State Parkway' is the same corner. Coordinates from GCatholic's building Plus Code; the directory's point is about 50 m away.
