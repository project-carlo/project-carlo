## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (masthead, page 2) lists St. Nicholas Church with Sunday 8:30 am (English) and 10:30 am (Spanish), weekday Masses Monday and Wednesday 7:00 am and a Wednesday 6:30 pm Spanish Mass. The summer guide gives the same Sunday times but puts the Spanish weekday Mass on Tuesday; the bulletin is the later and fuller source, so Wednesday stands, unconfirmed.

## Name and address

525 St. Louis Avenue, Egg Harbor City, NJ 08215 in the bulletin (which prints the town as 'Egg Harbor'), GCatholic and the summer guide. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code. Neither the 2015 directory nor the parish's own page lists it separately.
