## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round, and the parish's only church with daily Mass. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (image-only; page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) lists Saturday vigil 4:00 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM and Monday to Saturday 8:30 AM; the parish's 'Winter Schedule' page gives the same.

## Name and address

'St. Augustine' in GCatholic and the bulletin, 'St. Augustine Church' in the summer guide and the parish page. '13th & Wesley Ave.' (GCatholic), '13th and Wesley Ave.' (summer guide), '13th Street & Wesley Ave.' (parish page): the same cross streets, no street number, so the address stays unverified. The diocese's 2015 directory lists the parish (not a church) at '1310 Ocean Avenue' with coordinates 39.272552, -74.580503, about 60 m from this church's Plus Code point; it is linked here as the parish seat in the directory, but its street address is a different one (Ocean Avenue, not Wesley) and the parish office is now at 1337 Asbury Avenue.
