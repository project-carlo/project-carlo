## Holds Mass

The parish's own church page (read in the browser 2026-10-29) lists: WEEKEND MASSES Sunday 10:30 AM (English), 1:00 PM (Spanish). No weekday Mass is listed on the page. This is the parish's website schedule; the bulletin PDF could not be read (see the parish record `holy-cross-bridgeton`), so the bulletin has not confirmed it.

The seat of the parish: the 2010 decree says 'The seat of the parish will be St. Teresa of Avila Church', and the directory's address for the parish (46 Central Avenue) and the phone (856) 455-2323 are this church's. The Good Shepherd Dining Room soup kitchen is in its lower level (Catholic Star Herald, 2025-12-12).
