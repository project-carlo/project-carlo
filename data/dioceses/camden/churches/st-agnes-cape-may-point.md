## Holds Mass

In summer only. The parish's Mass page, headed 'Winter Mass Schedule Now in Effect', says 'NO MASS AT ST. AGNES', and the 1 November 2026 bulletin lists no Mass there; the Catholic Star Herald's 2026 summer guide gives 'Daily Wed 8:30 July 1st - Sept.; Sun 8:30 AM'. So St. Agnes is a seasonal mission church of Our Lady Star of the Sea: it keeps its church row with a revisit just past Memorial Day 2027 (the season's start isn't dated in any source read; the guide's Wednesday Mass begins July 1st). The guide's 'Sun 8:30 AM' has no stated end, and the winter page's 'NO MASS AT ST. AGNES' is the off-season proof.

## Name and address

'St. Agnes' in GCatholic, 'St Agnes Mission' in the summer guide, 'St. Agnes Mission Church' on the parish home page. 501 Cape Avenue, Cape May Point 08204 agrees across GCatholic, the summer guide and the parish home page. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code. Not the St. Agnes church in Blackwood (a different church of Our Lady of Hope's parish).
