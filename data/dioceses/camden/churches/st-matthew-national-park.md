## Holds Mass

Yes, one weekly Mass: Friday 9:00 AM (bulletin of 1 November 2026, page 1; the parish home page agrees), with Friday confessions at 9:30 AM and adoration 9:30 AM to 7:00 PM. The 2010 decree kept it as a worship site; it has no weekend Mass now.

## Name and address

The name is the same in every source. The address is not: the bulletin masthead has '307 Wesley Ave. / 4th & Monument Ave.', the parish's site has '331 Wesley Ave.', and GCatholic ('N 4th St. & Monument Ave.') and the 2010 decree ('4th Street and Monument Avenue') give the cross streets only. The building is at the corner of Wesley and Monument; I could not tell which house number is right, so `address_verified` is false and line1 follows GCatholic's cross streets.
