## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) lists Saturday 4:30pm, Sunday 9am and weekdays Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday 8am at Maris Stella Church. The 2026 summer guide's '(May 2 - October 1, 2026) Sat 5 PM; Sun 7 AM, 9 AM, 11 AM' and the 23 August bulletin are the longer summer set, so the church is not seasonal. It is the parish's seat.

## Name and address

'Maris Stella Church' in the bulletin, the 2010 decree and the summer guide; 'Maris Stella' in GCatholic. Address: the bulletin, the 2010 decree, the summer guide and the directory's parish entry give 5012 Dune Drive, which is followed here. GCatholic gives 4900 Dune Dr. and the parish's Places of Worship page gives 252 50th Street; the sources disagree, so `address_verified` stays false. Coordinates come from GCatholic's building Plus Code, about 100 m from the ParishesOnline and directory points (39.0827, -74.7341), which sit at the parish office.
