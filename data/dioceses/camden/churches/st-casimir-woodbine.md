## Holds Mass

Seasonally. The 25 October 2026 bulletin, an off-season issue, lists St. Casimir with 'Sunday, 9:00am' and the line 'Masses celebrated Memorial Day-Labor Day', so there is no Mass here at present (2026-10-29); the summer guide agrees ('Sun 9 AM, Memorial Day to Labor Day'). It is kept as a church with a revisit for the 2027 season (Rules settled: a seasonal shore church keeps its row). Memorial Day 2027 is 31 May. The parish also runs a St. Casimir Thrift Shop & Food Pantry (phone 609-861-5992), which is not a place of Mass.
