## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 1 November 2026 Parish of Saint Monica bulletin (page 1, read 2026-10-29) lists Saturday 8:00 AM and Sunday 9:30 AM, both English, at Saint Michael, 10 N. Mississippi Avenue, and its Mass intentions give 8:00 a.m. and 9:30 a.m. '(E/SM)' for 31 October and 1 November. The parish's Mass and Confessions page lists the church. This is a smaller schedule than the other two churches, but it is public weekend Mass.
