## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (image-only; page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) lists Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 AM and 6:00 PM, and Monday to Friday 11:00 AM, the same as the parish's 'Winter Mass Schedule Now in Effect'. The summer guide's extra Masses (Saturday 5:30 PM; Sunday 7, 8, 9:30 and 11 AM) are the summer additions, so the church itself is not seasonal.

## Name and address

'Our Lady Star of the Sea' in GCatholic and the bulletin. 525 Washington Street, Cape May 08204 agrees across GCatholic, the diocese's directory, the bulletin footer and the parish home page; the summer guide's 'Washington Ave.' is a slip. The parish office and mailing address, 520 Lafayette Street, is the former school building, not a church. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code, matching the directory's point. This is not Our Lady Star of the Sea, Atlantic City (a different church, merged into the Parish of St. Monica).
