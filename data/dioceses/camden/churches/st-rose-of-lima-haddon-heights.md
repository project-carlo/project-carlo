## Holds Mass

Yes: Saturday vigil 5:30 pm, Sunday 8:00, 9:30, 11:00 am and 5:30 pm (no 5:30 pm from Memorial Day to Labor Day weekends), Monday-Friday 6:30 and 8:30 am, Saturday 8:30 am, per the 25 October 2026 bulletin and the parish's home page (read 2026-10-29). The only worship site of the parish named in either source.

Weekday Masses are partly in the parish chapel, which stands on the same campus (the bulletin lists 'Spiritual Center Meeting Room (behind the Chapel)') and has no address of its own, so it is not a separate row. The bulletin puts both weekday morning Masses in the Chapel; the home page puts 8:30 am in the Church. The disagreement doesn't change the row.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish's site, GCatholic and the diocese's directory agree on St. Rose of Lima, 300 Kings Highway, Haddon Heights (the parish's rectory is 'Suite B'). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
