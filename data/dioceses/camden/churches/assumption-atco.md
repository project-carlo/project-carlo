## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 24-25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, read 2026-10-29) lists Saturday vigil 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am and 11:00 am, Monday to Friday 8:00 am, at 318 Carl Hasselhan Drive; the parish's General Information page gives the same times and calls this address "Assumption Church and Parish Office". The parish's own history ("The Parish Story") says the campus "centers around the former Church of the Assumption at 318 Carl Hasselhan Drive", the parish's "principal worship space"; it houses St. Lucy's Chapel. The building is the seat of the parish (diocese news, 2010-10-08).

## Name and address

The church is "Assumption" in GCatholic, "Assumption Church" on the parish's site and "Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary" in a 2019 Catholic Star Herald article; the bulletin calls it "Christ the Redeemer Catholic Church". The address, 318 Carl Hasselhan Drive, Atco, NJ 08004, is the same in GCatholic, the diocese (venue page and Parish Locator), the bulletin and the parish site.
