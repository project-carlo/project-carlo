## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (image-only; page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) lists Sunday Masses at 10:30 AM and 4:00 PM (Spanish) and Sunday confessions at 3:00 PM. The parish's 'Winter Schedule' page gives the same. The summer guide adds a Saturday 4:30 PM Mass and Sunday 8 AM for summer, so the Sunday Masses run all year. There is no weekday Mass here.

## Name and address

'St. Frances Cabrini' in GCatholic and the bulletin ('Worship Site at 2nd Street'); 'St. Frances Cabrini Church' in the summer guide. Address '2nd & Atlantic Ave.' in GCatholic, the summer guide and the parish Mass-schedule page ('2nd Street & Atlantic Ave.'): all three give the cross streets and no street number, so the address is left unverified.
