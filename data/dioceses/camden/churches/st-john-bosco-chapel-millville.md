## Holds Mass

Yes, daily: the bulletin for 25 October 2026 (page 1) gives Monday through Friday 8:00am 'in the chapel' with a chapel confessional after it, and the parish home page (read 2026-10-29) says the 8AM daily Mass 'is now being held in the Saint John Bosco Chapel' and that Thursday adoration runs 9AM to Benediction at 6PM. The chapel opened with its consecration Mass on 31 January 2026 (Catholic Star Herald, 2026-02-05).

## Name and address

The name is the parish's and the Star Herald's. It is a new chapel in the All Saints parish building (a converted school classroom, 'maroon awning', 'Enter through the doors under the awning that says "All Saints"'), not the former St. John Bosco Church at 2 Hillcrest Ave., which was sold in 2020 (`excluded/st-john-bosco-millville`). No source gives the building a street number of its own: the parish's only address is 621 Dock Street (contact page and bulletin), so that address and the St. Mary Magdalen church's coordinates (GCatholic Plus Code 87F6CX35+4Q) are used, and `address_verified` is false.
