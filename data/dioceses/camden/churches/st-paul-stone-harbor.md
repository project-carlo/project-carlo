## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) lists Sunday 7:30am and 10:15am and weekdays Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8am, with no Saturday Mass here in winter (Saturday vigil is at Maris Stella). The 23 August bulletin and the 2026 summer guide add Saturday 4pm and shift the Sunday times, so the church is not seasonal.

## Name and address

'St. Paul Church' in the bulletin, the 2010 decree and the summer guide; 'St. Paul' in GCatholic. All five sources give 9910 Third Avenue (3rd Ave.), Stone Harbor. GCatholic's ZIP is 08247 and the parish page's is 08202, which is Avalon's; the Stone Harbor ZIP 08247 is kept as it is GCatholic's and the town's. Coordinates from GCatholic's building Plus Code.
