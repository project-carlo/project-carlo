## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, read 2026-10-29) and the parish's Worship page put every Mullica Hill Mass at the Mullica Hill Parish Center: Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 10:30 AM, Monday, Wednesday and Friday 9:00 AM, plus Holy Day evening Mass at 7:00 PM.

## Name and address

GCatholic (18866) lists this campus as the church 'Holy Name of Jesus', 17 Earlington Ave.; the parish's History page says Holy Name of Jesus Church (dedicated 1901) stands beside the Parish Center, on adjoining land, and is 'still in use'. The bulletin and Worship page never name the church and give the Parish Center as the Mass location, so this record is named for where the bulletin puts Mass, and the Holy Name of Jesus roster entry is linked to it. Both share 17 Earlington Ave. The directory's parish entry (directory:11) is at the same address and is linked here too.
