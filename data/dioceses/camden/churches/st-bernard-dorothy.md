## Holds Mass

Yes, but only on a few dates a year. The summer guide marks it 'Currently not open', which is why the survey flagged it; the parish's own bulletins and site say otherwise. The 6 September 2026 bulletin lists the 2026 Masses: Sunday 6 September, 4 October (with a rosary procession), 29 November, all at 4 PM, and Christmas Day at 1 PM; the parish site's schedule image (read 2026-10-29) shows Sunday 1 November at 4 PM and Christmas Day at 1 PM. The 1 November bulletin's Mass intentions include a St. Bernard 4:00 pm Mass that day, and the 2 August issue lists 9 August. The 2 November 2025 bulletin listed 2 and 30 November 2025. The summer guide's 'Currently not open' is read as 'not open for the summer weekend schedule'; it is not a closure.

So this is a church with public Mass a few times a year, which counts as a church row. It has no weekly schedule to give; the parish's listing is the dated one above.

## Name and address

The parish site's page description and the summer guide give '20 Pennsylvania Avenue, Dorothy, NJ 08319'; GCatholic gives '42 Pennsylvania Ave., Mays Landing 08330' (Dorothy is an unincorporated community with a Mays Landing mailing area in some listings). The sources disagree on the number and the town, so `address_verified` is false and the parish's own address is recorded. No roster has coordinates; `pnpm geocode` fills them after promotion. `name_verified`: the parish site, bulletins and the 2010 decree say St. Bernard Mission (Church).
