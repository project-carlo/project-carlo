## Holds Mass

Summer only. Our Lady of the Angels' site gives 'St. Elizabeth Church - Route 47 - Goshen NJ: Sunday, 10:15 AM' in its Summer Schedule (May 9 - Sept 12, 2027) and says 'No Mass at St. Elizabeth from October through the end of April'; its 1 November 2026 bulletin's winter schedule lists Mass only at Our Lady of the Angels, with St. Elizabeth as 'Mission Church'. The 2026 summer guide (Sunday 10:15 AM) and the 2017 summer schedule give the same Mass. So the church holds public Mass at least in summer and keeps its row with a revisit for May 2027 (settled rule for seasonal shore churches). The summer guide gives no season dates; the 2027 summer dates are the parish's own.

## Name and address

'St. Elizabeth' (summer guide, 2017 summer schedule), 'St. Elizabeth Church' (parish site), 'St. Elizabeth's Church, 561 Rt. 47 N., Goshen' (2011 decree). The address is from the decree alone; the bulletin gives only 'Rte. 47, Goshen'. The ZIP (08270, Woodbine's) and coordinates come from OpenStreetMap's node for 'Saint Elizabeths Church' on North Delsea Drive (Route 47), Goshen; the node has no house number, so the decree's '561' is not independently confirmed and `address_verified` stays false.

## Which parish

The 2011 decree made this church (then a mission of St. Casimir, Woodbine) a worship site of the Parish of St. Maximilian Kolbe, Marmora. Kolbe's October 2026 bulletin lists only Resurrection and St. Casimir, while Our Lady of the Angels' bulletin and site name it as their mission church, and the 2017 summer guide put it under Our Lady of the Angels. It is recorded under Our Lady of the Angels, which publishes the Mass; the Kolbe batch is told in a lead.
