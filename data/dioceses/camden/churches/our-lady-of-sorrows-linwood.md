## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (image-only; page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) lists Sunday Masses at 8 a.m. and 10 a.m., the Saturday 5:00 p.m. Vigil, and weekday Mass at 7:45 a.m. (Saturday 8 a.m.). The weekend times match the summer guide's, so the church is not seasonal.

## Name and address

724 Maple Avenue, Linwood, NJ 08221 in the bulletin masthead, the diocese directory (2015) and GCatholic ('724 Maple Ave.'). Names: 'Our Lady of Sorrows Church' (bulletin), 'Our Lady of Sorrows' (GCatholic, summer guide). The summer guide alone gives the street as 'Wabash & Poplar Aves.', a cross-street description (or an error) that no other source repeats; the masthead's address was followed. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code (87F79C2J+X9); the directory's point (39.352596, -74.569522) is about 45 m away.
