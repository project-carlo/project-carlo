## Holds Mass

Yes, year-round. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (image-only; pages 1-2 rendered and read 2026-10-29) lists Monday to Friday 8:00 AM, Saturday 4:00 PM and Sunday 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM. The summer guide's '(Memorial Day to Labor Day) Sat 4 PM; Sun 7, 8:30, 10 & 11:30 AM' is the summer set, so the church is not seasonal.

## Name and address

'St. Joseph Church' (bulletin), 'St. Joseph' (GCatholic), 'St. Joseph Parish - Sea Isle' (directory), 'St. Joseph Parish' (summer guide). Address '126 44th Street' in the bulletin's rectory line, the directory and GCatholic; the summer guide prints '4308 Landis Avenue' (the church sits at the corner of 44th Street and Landis Avenue, so it is probably the same building under its other street; this is inferred, not read). The 126 44th Street address was followed because three sources give it. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code; the directory's point is about 20 m away.
