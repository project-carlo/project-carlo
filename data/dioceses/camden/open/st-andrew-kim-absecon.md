## What is known

**St. Andrew Kim Korean Catholic Mission, 702 South New Road, Absecon.** The 2015 diocesan directory lists it (Deanery 4, no website or pastor), GCatholic lists it as a Latin-rite Korean mission with the same address and phone, and a user-edited Korean church directory (last edited 2026-01-24) names a pastor, Fr. Sung Heum Kim, and 54 members. The mission is Latin and diocesan (Camden's settled rule), so it isn't other-jurisdiction.

## Why it is open, not excluded

Nothing found says when or whether it holds public Mass now. No source gives a Mass time; no website exists; neither Seton's nor OLPH's bulletin lists a Korean Mass (searched), and 702 South New Road is not either parish's address. Camden's rule for the Korean missions (church row under the parish whose bulletin lists their Mass, parish row if it publishes a readable bulletin, otherwise excluded `no-bulletin`) needs a confirmed public Mass first, and the exclusion needs a source that states it, so it stays here.

## What would settle it

A schedule or bulletin for the mission, or the diocese's confirmation that it has closed. See `research` and `revisit`.
