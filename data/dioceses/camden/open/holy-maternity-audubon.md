## What is known

Holy Maternity, Audubon (431 W Nicholson Road) joined Sacred Heart, Mount Ephraim in 2010 to form the Emmaus Catholic Community, which named Sacred Heart as its seat; Emmaus joined St. Rose of Lima, Haddon Heights on 2014-06-30. GCatholic still lists the church under 'Community of Emmaus'. St. Rose's current bulletin and home page give Masses at Haddon Heights only, and nothing read names Holy Maternity after the merger. No source read says it closed or was sold.

## Why it is not decided

The 2010 notice kept Sacred Heart as the seat but didn't say whether Holy Maternity stayed a worship site, and the 2014 notice says the church buildings 'will become the responsibility of the new parish communities' without saying which were kept. The merger rule needs a source that says it closed. Next: ask the parish office or find a bulletin from 2010-2016.
