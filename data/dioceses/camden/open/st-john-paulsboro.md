## What is known

St. John the Evangelist, Paulsboro (7th and Beacon Aves.) was one of the three parishes consolidated into St. Clare of Assisi on 29 September 2010. GCatholic still lists it as a church of the parish (637 Beacon Ave.). The 2010 decree notice named St. Michael as the seat and St. Joseph as a worship site and did not name St. John. The current bulletin (1 November 2026) and the parish's Mass Times page list Mass at Gibbstown and Swedesboro only, with confessions and adoration at those two; the bulletin masthead names two churches. A funeral notice for 8 November 2010 puts a Mass at St. John after the merger. No source read says the building closed or was sold, and the Catholic Star Herald search for 'Paulsboro' found no closure post.

## Why it is not decided

Absence from a Mass schedule is weak evidence, but a weekly Mass would be listed, and the rule here is that a merger ends a parish, not a church, so closing needs a source that says so. A church row needs a current Mass. Next: the parish office, or a 2010-2012 bulletin showing the last Mass.
