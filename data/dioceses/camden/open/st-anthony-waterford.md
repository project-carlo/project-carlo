## What is known

St. Anthony was one of the four churches merged into Christ the Redeemer Parish on 10 November 2010. Address: 436 Pennington Ave., Waterford (the parish site gives ZIP 08089, GCatholic 08084).

The roster (GCatholic) lists it under Christ the Redeemer Parish. Sources, all read 2026-10-29:

- The parish's current bulletin (24-25 October 2026) and its site give one Mass schedule at 318 Carl Hasselhan Drive, Atco, and every Mass intention and liturgical-minister line in the bulletin fits those three weekend times and the weekday 8:00 am, so no regular Mass is listed here.
- The parish's General Information page still lists this church's address next to Assumption's, with no Mass time.
- The Catholic Star Herald (2015-02-05) says the Assumption, Sacred Heart and St. Anthony parishes all ended in prayer services on 12 November 2010, and that St. Anthony's was 'kept opened as an active worship site' after being 'originally ... due to close'. A 2019 Catholic Star Herald article still says the parish 'includes the Sacred Heart Church in Cedar Brook, Saint Anthony in Waterford Works, and Assumption'.
- The parish's own history calls all four churches 'former' churches and keeps their statues and banners at the Atco campus.

## Why it is not decided

No source says the building is closed, sold or demolished, so it is not excluded as closed; and no current source gives a Mass there, so it is not a church row. It may be a building the parish still owns and uses occasionally, or an unused one. What would settle it: a bulletin line or the parish office (856-767-0719) saying whether Mass is ever celebrated here, or a sale, relegation or demolition notice (search the Catholic Star Herald for a decree). If a later bulletin lists Mass here, make it a church row under christ-the-redeemer; if a source says it closed, exclude it as closed with the date.
