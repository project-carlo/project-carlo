## What is known

Our Lady of Grace Chapel is in Lindenwold (the bulletin's page 4 line) and holds public Mass weekly: Monday and Wednesday 7:00 pm (bilingual) and the first Friday of the month at 7:00 pm, with Wednesday and Thursday adoration. It is entered 'through the old Spanish Office' (the parish's Hispanic Office). Its Mass is on the parish's own schedule, so it would be a church row, but no source read prints a street address, and nothing says whether it is a separate building or a room within the St. Lawrence complex (the parish's one Lindenwold address, 135 N White Horse Pike).

## What would settle it

An address in a bulletin, the parish site or a diocesan or GCatholic entry. Until then it is open, not excluded: it holds Mass and publishes in the parish's bulletin.
