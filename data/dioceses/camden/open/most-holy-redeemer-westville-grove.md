## What is known

Most Holy Redeemer, Westville Grove was merged into Holy Angels Parish on 21 April 2010. The decree notice said its church would be used for school liturgies, baptisms, weddings and funerals, not as a worship site for regular Mass (St. Patrick was the seat, St. Matthew a worship site). The 1 November 2026 bulletin and the parish's home page give no Mass, confession or adoration there, and the parish's own Most Holy Redeemer page is a 1987 history. GCatholic still lists the church. The Korean mission that the decree said it would host moved to St. Mary, Cherry Hill in 2012. Nothing read says whether the building still hosts any liturgy, or was closed or sold.

## Why it is not decided

The 2010 decree is a plan, and a use for sacraments is not a closure; a church row needs a current public Mass, which no source gives. Excluding it as 'closed' or 'no-public-mass' needs a source that says so. Next: the parish office, or older bulletins.
