## What is known

Sacred Heart, Mount Ephraim (7 W Kings Highway) was the seat of the Emmaus Catholic Community, formed 2010-06-09 from Sacred Heart and Holy Maternity, Audubon. On 2014-06-30 Emmaus was joined to St. Rose of Lima, Haddon Heights. GCatholic still lists it under 'Community of Emmaus'. St. Rose's current bulletin and home page give Masses at Haddon Heights only. No source read says Sacred Heart closed or was sold, and the diocese's 'Decree Emmaus to St. Rose' (linked from its 2014-05-08 notice) is a dead link.

## Why it is not decided

The rule here is that a merger ends a parish, not a church, so closing needs a source that says so; absence from the schedule is weak evidence but a weekly Mass would be listed. This church could still hold an occasional apostolate Mass (as St. Francis de Sales, Barrington does for the Igbo apostolate), which only a parish source would show. Next: ask the parish office or find the last Mass in a 2014-2016 bulletin.
