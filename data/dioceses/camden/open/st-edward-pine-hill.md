## What is known

GCatholic lists a St. Edward church at 500 Erial Rd., Pine Hill, under St. Simon Stock Parish (page last updated 2025-12-21). St. Edward's was a parish from 1953 (first a small chapel in 1919) until 4 November 2009, when it merged with Our Lady of Mt. Carmel, Berlin to form St. Simon Stock (the parish's history page and bulletin masthead, read 2026-10-29). The parish's bulletin (1 November 2026, and three earlier issues grepped) and its site give one set of Mass times and one church address (178 W. White Horse Pike, Berlin, `our-lady-of-mount-carmel-berlin`); none names Pine Hill, St. Edward or Erial Road as a place of Mass. No source read says the building closed or was sold.

Searches for a closure or sale notice found nothing: the Catholic Star Herald REST search for 'St. Edward Pine Hill' returned no relevant post; the web-search tools available in the sandbox (DuckDuckGo html, Bing) returned a bot check and irrelevant results.

## Why it is not decided

Absence from the bulletin's schedule is weak evidence: a church with one weekly Mass would have to be named, and none is, but the schedule doesn't say Mass is held at one site only. Calling it closed needs a source that says so (rule settled for this diocese: a merger decree ends a parish, not a church); a church row needs a current Mass.
