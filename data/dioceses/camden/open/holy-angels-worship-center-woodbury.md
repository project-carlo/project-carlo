## What is known

GCatholic lists a 'Worship Center of the Holy Angels' at 96 Green Ave., Woodbury, a chapel under Holy Angels Parish. The parish's own pages and the 1 November 2026 bulletin name no such place and schedule no Mass outside St. Patrick (86 Cooper St.) and St. Matthew. The parish history says St. Patrick's bought the Green estate in 1944 for its school and convent, so this may be a convent or school chapel, which is a guess.

## Why it is not decided

No source describes the place, and absence from the schedule alone doesn't prove it has no public Mass or doesn't exist. A church row needs a current Mass; an exclusion needs a source. Next: the parish office or a map of 96 Green Ave.
