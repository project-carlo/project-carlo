## What is known

GCatholic lists a 'Worship Center of the Holy Angels' at 96 Green Ave., Woodbury, a chapel under Holy Angels Parish. The parish history says St. Patrick's bought the Green estate in 1944 for its school and convent.

- In 2020 the place held a monthly public Mass: the Catholic Star Herald's list of special-needs Masses (2020-06-22) gives a '1 st Sunday Mass of Inclusion ... 6 p.m. Mass, Holy Angels Parish Worship Center, 96 Green Avenue, Woodbury'.
- In 2026 the parish calls the building its Parish Center: the 8 November 2026 bulletin (page 5) holds the school's bingo night at 'Holy Angels Parish Center - 96 Green Ave' (read 2026-11-07).
- The 1 and 8 November 2026 bulletins' Mass schedules list St. Patrick (86 Cooper St.) and St. Matthew only, and no page names a Mass at Green Ave.

## Why it is not decided

No current source says whether the monthly Mass of Inclusion still runs there, and absence from the weekly schedule alone doesn't prove it stopped: a once-a-month apostolate Mass can be left off a bulletin (as at St. Francis de Sales, Barrington). A church row needs a current Mass; an exclusion needs a source. Next: ask the parish office (856-845-0123) about the first-Sunday Mass of Inclusion.
