## Publisher

St. Joachim Parish publishes its own weekly bulletin, hosted as PDFs on its Weebly site (stjoachimparish.net), with the issue dated in the file name (`10.25.26.pdf.pdf`). The 25 October 2026 issue (page 1, text layer, read 2026-10-29) gives 601 West Browning Road, Bellmawr and names 'Reverend Allen Lovell ... Pastor'. It names this parish alone.

The John Patrick listing for the parish (id 508) and for Mary, Mother of the Church (543) exist on church-bulletin.org but list no `/bulletin/` editions, so neither is a bulletin; they are old per-parish codes. St. Joachim is not shared with St. Mary, Gloucester City (John Patrick 6049) or St. Rose of Lima, which print separately.

## Worship sites

One church, Annunciation at 601 W. Browning Road (`annunciation-bellmawr`; GCatholic lists it as 'St. Joachim'). The 2014 consolidation (Catholic Star Herald, 2014-05-08) joined Mary, Mother of the Church to this parish; the bishop's decree of 2021-04-19 relegated that church to profane use, after its use 'was at first occasional and then later discontinued', so it is `excluded/mary-mother-of-the-church-bellmawr`. The bulletin's sponsor-letter policy (page 5) refers to people who 'used to attend St. Annes/Mary Mother/St. Rita and so on when they were open'. St. Anne, Westville (relegated 2024, per the batch brief) is in no roster of this batch and the bulletin has no Westville line; the Korean Mass of St. Yi Yunil is on no St. Joachim bulletin.
