## Publisher

St. Brendan the Navigator Parish publishes its bulletin through ParishesOnline (LPi): org `st-brendan-the-navigator`, container 04/0697, issues weekly to 1 November 2026 (checked 2026-10-29). Its own site (stbrendanavalon.org, WeConnect/Duda) links the bulletin on /bulletin9ab2b387 through the ParishesOnline widget. The 1 November 2026 issue's page 1 names the parish and its pastor (Fr. Raymond P. Gormley) and gives one Mass schedule for two churches; no other parish is in it. The diocese's 2015 directory still names Fr. William Kelly as pastor.

## Worship sites

Two, each with its own church record: Maris Stella Church, Avalon (the seat; `maris-stella-avalon`) and St. Paul Church, Stone Harbor (`st-paul-stone-harbor`). The 2010 decree retains both. Both hold Mass year-round: the November bulletin and the parish's Mass Times page give Maris Stella Saturday 4:30pm and Sunday 9am, St. Paul Sunday 7:30am and 10:15am, with weekday Masses at both; the 23 August bulletin gives the longer summer set that the 2026 summer guide prints. The parish's Places of Worship page also names Sacred Heart Church, Avalon, which the bulletin lists only as open for private prayer (excluded `sacred-heart-avalon`).
