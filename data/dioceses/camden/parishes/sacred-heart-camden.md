## Publisher

John Patrick Publishing (page code 6039) prints the bulletin; the church-bulletin.org listing `sacred-heart-parish-parroquia-sagrado-corazon-camden-nj-08104` serves it (`church-bulletin.org/6039` redirects to it, checked 2026-10-29). The 1 November 2026 issue is image-only; page 1 (rendered and read) names Sacred Heart Parish and lists three churches with Mass: Sacred Heart, St. Bartholomew and St. Joan of Arc. It is one bulletin for the whole parish, so the former St. Josephine Bakhita parish (St. Bartholomew and St. Joan of Arc) publishes no bulletin of its own.
