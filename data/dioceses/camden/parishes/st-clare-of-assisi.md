## Publisher

St. Clare of Assisi Parish publishes its own weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site (id 23165; /bulletins lists current files.ecatholic.com/23165/bulletins/ PDFs). The 1 November 2026 issue (read 2026-10-29) names the parish alone, with St. Michael Church, Gibbstown and St. Joseph Church, Swedesboro as its two churches. The parish was established 29 September 2010 by consolidating St. John the Evangelist, Paulsboro, St. Joseph, Swedesboro and St. Michael, Gibbstown; the seat was St. Michael, and 'St. Joseph Church also will be used as a worship site' (camdendiocese.org, 'Decrees issued establishing two new parishes', 2010-08-27). That decree names no worship site at Paulsboro.

## Worship sites

`st-joseph-swedesboro` and `st-michael-gibbstown`, both with weekend and weekday Mass in the 1 November 2026 bulletin and on the site's Mass Times page. GCatholic and the rosters also list St. John, Paulsboro (637 Beacon Ave.) under this parish; no current source gives it a Mass, and none says it closed, so it is in `open/` as `st-john-paulsboro`. The diocese directory's entry (directory:68, 140 Broad Street) is the parish at St. Joseph's address and is linked to that church row.

## Website and bulletin provider

eCatholic, read from `website`; `publication` is ecatholic:23165. GCatholic's website for St. Joseph (stclareofassisiparish.org) has no DNS; stclarenj.org is the directory's and the current one.
