## Publisher

The parish publishes its own weekly bulletin through LPi / ParishesOnline (organization `our-lady-of-the-blessed-sacrament`, LPi id 0018000000Qc02AAAR, container 03/0188). The 25 October 2026 issue (page 1, read 2026-10-29) is headed with the parish's name in English and Spanish, names Rev. Ariel Hernandez as pastor and the parish office at 104 Catawba Avenue, Newfield, and lists four churches with Mass. It names no other parish. The directory gives the same pastor and address.

## Worship sites

Four churches, all with Mass in the 25 October bulletin and on the parish's Mass Schedule page: **St. Rose of Lima, Newfield** (the seat; Saturday vigil), **St. Michael, Minotola** (Sunday Masses, one in Spanish), **St. Mary, Malaga** (Monday to Wednesday) and **Our Lady of Victories, Landisville** (Thursday and Friday daily Mass, plus Tuesday, Thursday and first-Friday evening Masses and adoration). They are the four former churches of the 2011 merger of Queen of Angels (Landisville and Minotola), St. Mary, Malaga and St. Rose, Newfield (diocese news, 2011-07-27, which says St. Rose and St. Michael 'will be utilized for Sunday and daily Mass'; the bulletin has since added the other two).

GCatholic and the directory list only one church here, 'Our Lady of the Blessed Sacrament' at 104 Catawba Ave., which is St. Rose of Lima's address; neither roster has the other three, so those are new places (church records).
