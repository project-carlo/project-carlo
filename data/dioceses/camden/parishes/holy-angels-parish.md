## Publisher

Holy Angels Parish, Woodbury publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (code 635). The 1 November 2026 issue (page 1 rendered and read 2026-10-29) is headed 'Holy Angels Parish' and names two churches, St. Patrick (86 Cooper St., Woodbury) and St. Matthew (National Park), each with its Mass times. The parish was established 21 April 2010 by merging St. Patrick, Woodbury, Most Holy Redeemer, Westville Grove and St. Matthew, National Park (Catholic Star Herald decree, 2010-03-18). The pastor in the 1 November 2026 masthead is Rev. E. Joseph Byerley.

## Worship sites

Two churches have Mass in the current bulletin: `st-patrick-woodbury` (the parochial church; weekend and Monday to Thursday Masses) and `st-matthew-national-park` (Friday 9:00 AM). Two other places the rosters tie to this parish are not in the bulletin's schedule and are in `open/`: Most Holy Redeemer, 1219 Delsea Dr., Westville Grove (`most-holy-redeemer-westville-grove`, GCatholic 18874; the 2010 decree kept it for school liturgies, baptisms, weddings and funerals) and GCatholic's 'Worship Center of the Holy Angels', 96 Green Ave., Woodbury (`holy-angels-worship-center-woodbury`, GCatholic 18875). The diocese directory's entry (directory:22) is the parish at 64 Cooper Street, the old St. Patrick address; it is linked to St. Patrick's church row.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other` with `page-pdfs` on the church-bulletin.org listing, whose issues have uniform dated names. The bulletin is image-only (no text layer), so the pipeline has to read it as an image. The parish's own bulletin page (bulletin-archives.html) was not read: the listing is the John Patrick source and is current to 2026-11-01.
