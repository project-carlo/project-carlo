## Publisher

The parish prints its bulletin with Bon Venture Services, which posts it at `sponsors.bonventure.net/?page=4219` (checked 2026-10-29): the 1 November 2026 issue is there, 16 pages with a text layer. Page 1 names Rev. James King as pastor and lists Mass at St. Joseph Church (226 French St.) and St. Anthony of Padua Church (285 Rte. 206) only.

The parish's own eCatholic site (`smmcp.net`, ecatholic:33982) carries the same bulletin but lags: the `/bulletins` archive stops at 6 September 2026 and `/weekly-bulletin` shows 4 October 2026 (the file is `files.ecatholic.com/33982/documents/2026/10/October 4 2026 Bulletin.pdf`). Its pick by `fetch:check` would be 6 September, so the Bon Venture page is the row's source. The old `stmarymtcarmelhammonton.parishesonline.com` subdomain (GCatholic's website for St. Martin de Porres) answers 200 with no parish content and `probe po` finds no organization of that name: not a current publisher.

## Worship sites

Two: St. Joseph (`st-joseph-hammonton`) and St. Anthony of Padua (`st-anthony-of-padua-hammonton`). The parish was formed in 2010 from three Hammonton churches; St. Martin de Porres closed in 2013 and was sold in 2016 (`excluded/st-martin-de-porres-hammonton`).

## Website and bulletin provider

`other` with `page-pdfs` on the `http://` Bon Venture page (as for the other Bon Venture parishes in this diocese). `website` stays the parish's own site, which does not link the Bon Venture page.
