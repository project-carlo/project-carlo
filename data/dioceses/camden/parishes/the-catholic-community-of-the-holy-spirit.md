## Publisher

The Catholic Community of the Holy Spirit (Mullica Hill, Woodstown and Elmer) publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (code 6006). The 1 November 2026 issue (pages 1-2 read 2026-10-29, text layer) gives the parish office at 17 Earlington Avenue, Mullica Hill, names Rev. Nicholas Dudo as pastor and gives one Mass schedule across three places. The parish was established 23 June 2010 from Holy Name of Jesus (Mullica Hill), St. Joseph (Woodstown) and St. Ann (Elmer) (diocese news, 2010-05-21).

## Worship sites

Three places have Mass in the current bulletin and on the parish's Worship page: the Mullica Hill Parish Center (`mullica-hill-parish-center`, 17 Earlington Ave), the Woodstown Parish Center (`woodstown-parish-center`, 2 Lamplighter Dr, in no roster) and St. Joseph Church, Woodstown (`st-joseph-woodstown`). Holy Name of Jesus Church adjoins the Mullica Hill Parish Center at the same address; the bulletin and the Worship page say 'Mullica Hill PC' for every Mullica Hill Mass, so the Mass is recorded under the Parish Center and the roster's Holy Name of Jesus entry is linked to it (see that record). St. Ann, Elmer is not in the bulletin or the schedule and is excluded as closed (`excluded/st-ann-elmer`).
