## Publisher

Our Lady Star of the Sea Parish, Cape May, publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing as id 6023 (listing church-bulletin.org/church-of-our-lady-star-of-the-sea-cape-may-nj-08204) and linked from the Bulletins page of the parish's Zumu site, ladystarofthesea.org. The 1 November 2026 issue (image-only, 12 pages; pages 1-2 rendered and read 2026-10-29) is headed 'Our Lady Star of the Sea, Administered by the Oblates of St. Francis de Sales', names Rev. John J. Dolan, OSFS as pastor and gives one Mass schedule for the church at 525 Washington Street. It is no other parish's bulletin: St. John Neumann, North Cape May and Notre Dame de la Mer, the Wildwoods publish separately.

## Worship sites

Two, each with its own church record: Our Lady Star of the Sea (525 Washington Street) and, in summer only, St. Agnes Mission Church (501 Cape Avenue, Cape May Point). The parish's own Mass page says 'Winter Mass Schedule Now in Effect ... NO MASS AT ST. AGNES', and the bulletin's schedule lists no St. Agnes Mass, so St. Agnes is seasonal. The parish office (520 Lafayette Street, the former school) is not a church.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other` with a `page-pdfs` recipe on the John Patrick listing, whose uniform dated names keep the newest pick reliable. The parish site is a Zumu frameset: `probe url` sees no parish words and no links, and the rendered menu is onclick handlers, so the Bulletins page address (/olss/Bulletins/) was found from the browser-rendered HTML. The directory's website (http://www.ladystarofthesea.org) redirects to /olss/. The issues are image-only, so the pipeline has to read them as images.
