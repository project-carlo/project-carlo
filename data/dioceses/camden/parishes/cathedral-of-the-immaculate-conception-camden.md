## Publisher

John Patrick Publishing prints the bulletin (page code 606) and the church-bulletin.org listing serves it: the parish site links `jppc.net/onlinebulletins/606template.pdf`, which redirects (checked 2026-10-29) to the listing `the-cathedral-parish-of-the-immaculate-conception-camden-nj-08102`. The 25 October 2026 issue has a text layer. Its page 1 is headed 'The Parish of the Cathedral the Immaculate Conception / La Parroquia de la Catedral de la Inmaculada Concepcion / And St. Joseph's Polish Apostolate' and names Rev. David Rivera as Cathedral Parish Rector. It is one bulletin for the whole parish, so the former St. Joseph (Polish) parish publishes no bulletin of its own.

## Worship sites

Two, both in the bulletin's Mass schedule: the Cathedral, 642 Market Street (church record `cathedral-of-the-immaculate-conception-camden`) and St. Joseph (Polish), 1010 Liberty Street (`st-joseph-polish-camden`), which GCatholic lists as a separate parish (gcatholic:18840) and which merged into the Cathedral in 2017.

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory's camdencathedral.com (alive, Weebly) has a Bulletin page of its own PDFs (`/uploads/1/1/1/0/111029267/606_<month>_<day>_<year>.pdf`) whose page lists the whole year including weeks not yet issued, so the church-bulletin.org listing is the pinned source: uniform dated names, newest 2026-10-25. `other` with a `page-pdfs` recipe on the listing.
