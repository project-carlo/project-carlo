## Publisher

Notre Dame de la Mer Parish (the 2010 merger of the Wildwoods' parishes) publishes its own weekly bulletin. John Patrick Publishing prints it as id 650, listed at church-bulletin.org/notre-dame-de-la-mer-parish-north-wildwood-nj-08260, and the parish's WordPress Bulletin page mirrors the same PDFs a week late. The 1 November 2026 issue (image-only, 16 pages; pages 1-2 rendered and read 2026-10-29) is headed 'Notre Dame de la Mer Parish', names Rev. Cadmus D. Mazzarella as pastor and carries one Mass schedule for the two churches. It is no other parish's bulletin. Rev. Ernest Soprano, the directory's 2015 pastor of St. John Neumann, appears there only as 'Retired Weekend Assistant'; that is not a shared bulletin.

## Worship sites

Two, each with its own church record: St. Ann (2901 Atlantic Avenue, Wildwood) and Assumption (7100/7101 Seaview Avenue, Wildwood Crest). Both hold Mass year-round: the parish's winter schedule (first weekend in October to the weekend before Memorial Day) and the bulletin's cover list Mass at both. Only the extra Masses are summer-only. The parish office (1500 Central Avenue, Suite 100, North Wildwood) is not a church; the diocese's 2015 directory lists the parish there as 'Wildwood'.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other` with a `page-pdfs` recipe on the John Patrick listing, whose uniform dated names keep the newest pick reliable and which is a week ahead of the parish page. The directory's website path (/notredame/) answers 404; the site is notredamedelamer.org. The issues are image-only, so the pipeline has to read them as images.
