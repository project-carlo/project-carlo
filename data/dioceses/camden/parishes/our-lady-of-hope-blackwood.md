## Publisher

John Patrick Publishing (id 506) prints the bulletin and the church-bulletin.org listing serves it; the parish's Current Bulletin page links `jppc.net/onlinebulletins/506template.pdf`, which lands on the listing (checked 2026-10-29). The parish also posts each issue on its own year pages (`/2026-bulletins/`, WordPress uploads); the 25 October 2026 file is the same image-only issue. Pages 1-2 of it were rendered and read: the masthead gives the three campuses (St Agnes Church, the regional school, St Jude Church) and names Rev. Joseph T. Szolack as pastor.

## Worship sites

Two: St. Agnes, 701 Little Gloucester Rd. (`st-agnes-blackwood`) and St. Jude, 402 S. Black Horse Pike (`st-jude-blackwood`), merged as Our Lady of Hope on 12 May 2010 ('decreed by Bishop Joseph A. Galante', parish About page). The bulletin gives one Mass schedule for the parish and says only that the Spanish Masses are at St. Jude, so which of the two churches holds each English Mass is not printed there; see the church records.

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory and GCatholic give ourladyofhopenj.org (alive; the bulletin's own masthead says www.ourladyofhopenj.org). `other` with a `page-pdfs` recipe on the listing. The bulletin is image-only, so the pipeline will have to read it as an image.

## Not yet confirmed

Nothing blocking. The directory (2015) names Fr. Mark Cavagnaro as pastor; the 2026 bulletin names Rev. Joseph T. Szolack, with Rev. René Canales listed for Hispanic Ministry, who is also pastor of Our Lady of Guadalupe, Lindenwold (that parish's bulletin masthead, 25 October 2026). Each of the two parishes publishes its own bulletin (different publishers, masthead and Mass times), so no shared bulletin is claimed.
