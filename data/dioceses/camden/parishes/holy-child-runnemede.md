## Publisher

John Patrick Publishing (printer's code 698) prints the bulletin and the church-bulletin.org listing serves it. The parish's own site (`holychildparish.net`, a Duda site 'Powered by Our Sunday Visitor') links its own copies as `irp.cdn-website.com/c40bd8db/files/uploaded/698+<m-d-yy>+Bulletin.pdf`, but its Bulletin page stopped at 13 September 2026 when read on 2026-10-29, while the listing had the 25 October issue. The file prefix 698 is what led to `church-bulletin.org/?id=698`, which redirects to the listing. The 25 October issue is image-only; pages 1 and 3 were rendered and read.

## Worship sites

Two: St. Teresa of the Infant Jesus, 13 E. Evesham Road (`st-teresa-runnemede`) and St. Maria Goretti, 321 Orchard Avenue (`st-maria-goretti-runnemede`). The masthead says 'Two Beautiful churches, one GREAT parish.' and the Mass Intentions mark each Mass ST or SMG. St. Maria Goretti still has a weekly Sunday 9:00 am Mass (and Sunday 8:15 am confessions, 13 September issue); every other Mass listed is at St. Teresa. The Runnemede parish was formed from the two churches (GCatholic's group 'Holy Child Parish').
