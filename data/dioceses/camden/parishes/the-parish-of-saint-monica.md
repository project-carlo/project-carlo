## Publisher

Bon Venture Services prints the bulletin and posts it at `sponsors.bonventure.net/?page=2027`, which the parish's site links from its Parish Bulletin menu (checked 2026-10-29). The 1 November 2026 issue is headed 'Parish of Saint Monica' and gives one Mass schedule for three churches in Atlantic City; its pastor is Rev. Robert E. Hughes (page 2). The four Atlantic City parishes (Our Lady Star of the Sea, St. Michael, St. Monica, St. Nicholas of Tolentine) merged into this one on 2015-07-01.

## Worship sites

Three: Our Lady Star of the Sea (`our-lady-star-of-the-sea-atlantic-city`), St. Michael (`st-michael-atlantic-city`) and St. Nicholas of Tolentine (`st-nicholas-of-tolentine-atlantic-city`), each with Mass in the 1 November 2026 bulletin and on the parish's Mass-schedule page. The fourth former parish church, St. Monica, 108 N Pennsylvania Ave., is in neither and is excluded as closed (`excluded/st-monica-atlantic-city`). The summer guide's three Atlantic City entries match the bulletin's three, with the same times.
