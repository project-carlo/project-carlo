## Publisher

The Parish of St. Michael the Archangel (Clayton and Franklinville) publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (code 695). The 1 November 2026 issue (pages 1-2 rendered and read 2026-10-29) is headed 'Parish of Saint Michael the Archangel', pastor Rev. Michael J. Goyette, and gives one Mass schedule across two churches, St. Catherine of Siena (Clayton) and Church of Nativity (Franklinville). The parish's own site says it 'was established September 29, 2010 through the merger of two churches / parishes: the Church of the Nativity ... and Saint Catherine of Siena Church' (psmarectory.wixsite.com/psma/welcome, read 2026-10-29).

## Worship sites

Two churches, both with Mass in the current bulletin: `st-catherine-of-siena-clayton` and `church-of-the-nativity-franklinville`. Neither is in either roster by name. Both rosters carry the parish itself at 49 W. North St., Clayton (directory:50; GCatholic 18882 'St. Michael the Archangel'): that is the parish office, which the site's contact page puts 'right next to Saint Catherine of Siena Church'. It is not a third church. Both roster entries are linked to `st-catherine-of-siena-clayton`, with the reasoning in that record. The St. Michael the Archangel Regional School (51 W. North St.) is a school, not a worship site.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other` with `page-pdfs` on the church-bulletin.org listing, which has uniform dated names and is current to 2026-11-01. The bulletin is image-only. The directory's website (parishofstmichaelthearchangel.com) redirects to a spam site and GCatholic's (.org) has no DNS: neither is stored. The current domain, saintmichael-archangel.com, is a frameset around a Wix site (psmarectory.wixsite.com/psma) whose Bulletins menu links the church-bulletin.org 695 listing.
