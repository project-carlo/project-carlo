## Publisher

St. Damien Parish publishes its own weekly bulletin on its WordPress site (stdamienparish.com/bulletins/). The 25 October 2026 issue (image-only, 20 pages; pages 1-2 rendered and read 2026-10-29) is headed 'Saint Damien Parish' with the parish office at 1337 Asbury Avenue, names Msgr. Peter M. Joyce as pastor and carries one Mass schedule for three worship sites. It is no other parish's bulletin. The 2026 summer guide, GCatholic's group 'St. Damien Parish' and the bulletin agree on the three churches.

## Worship sites

Three, each with its own church record: St. Frances Cabrini (2nd Street & Atlantic Ave.), St. Augustine (13th Street & Wesley Ave.) and Our Lady of Good Counsel (40th Street & Asbury Ave.). The bulletin and the parish's 'Winter Schedule' page list Mass at all three in October, so none is summer-only (the summer guide adds Saturday and later Sunday Masses at Cabrini and Our Lady of Good Counsel). The parish office, 1337 Asbury Avenue, is not a church. The diocese's 2015 directory lists the parish at '1310 Ocean Avenue', whose coordinates are St. Augustine's; it is linked to that church. The directory still names Rev. Thomas Newton as pastor; the October 2026 bulletin names Msgr. Peter M. Joyce.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other` with a `page-pdfs` recipe on the parish's own Bulletins page, which lists the files newest first with the date in the name. The issues are image-only, so the pipeline has to read them as images. The directory's website (http://www.stdamienparish.com/) redirects to stdamienparish.com.
