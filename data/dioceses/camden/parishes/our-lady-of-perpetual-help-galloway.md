## Publisher

Our Lady of Perpetual Help, Galloway, publishes one bulletin for its two churches, Assumption (Galloway) and St. Nicholas (Egg Harbor City). The parish's Wix site (`olphparish-nj.org`, the directory's website; GCatholic's `olphparish-nj.org` is the same site) has a 'Weekly Bulletins' page, `/copy-of-news-events`, that links one PDF under `/_files/ugd/`, the current issue, replaced in place. Its cover reads '©2018 Bon Venture Services', so Bon Venture prints it, but the parish's Bon Venture sponsors page was not found (searches for it returned other parishes' accounts: Our Lady of Perpetual Help, Maple Shade is page 2214, not this one).

The issue linked on 2026-10-29 is the 18 October 2026 one (cover 'World Mission Sunday'; intentions 17-25 October), 11 days old, so the parish may lag; see Not yet confirmed.

Pitney Road, Galloway, and St. Nicholas Church, 525 St. Louis Avenue, Egg Harbor City (the bulletin says 'Egg Harbor, NJ 08215'; GCatholic and the summer guide say Egg Harbor City, 08215).
