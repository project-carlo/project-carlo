## Publisher

St. Elizabeth Ann Seton, Absecon, publishes its weekly bulletin through Bon Venture Services, account 2078 (`sponsors.bonventure.net/?page=2078`), which its own site (`setonabsecon.org`) links. The 1 November 2026 issue (text layer) is headed by the Mass schedule and `591 NEW JERSEY AVENUE | ABSECON, NJ 08201` and names the parish alone. The site's other bulletin link, `thekidsbulletin.com`, is a children's bulletin, not the parish's.

## Worship sites

One: 591 New Jersey Avenue. The bulletin and the site's Mass & Confession Schedule page agree (Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:00, 10:00 (livestream) and 11:30, weekdays 6:45 AM); the summer guide has the same weekend times. Neither mentions the St. Andrew Kim Korean mission, whose address (702 South New Road) is a different place; see `open/st-andrew-kim-absecon`.
