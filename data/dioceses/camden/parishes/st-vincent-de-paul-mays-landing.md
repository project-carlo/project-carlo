## Publisher

Bon Venture Services prints the bulletin and posts it at `sponsors.bonventure.net/?page=2080` (checked 2026-10-29); the parish's site links that page. The 1 November 2026 issue's page 1 gives the Mass schedule of St. Vincent de Paul Church (Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 10:45 am, weekdays Tuesday-Friday 7:00 am) and names Rev. John W. March as pastor. St. Bernard, Dorothy appears in the same issue's Mass intentions (Sunday 1 November, 4:00 pm) and its events list.

## Worship sites

Two: St. Vincent de Paul Church (`st-vincent-de-paul-mays-landing`) and St. Bernard Mission Church in Dorothy (`st-bernard-dorothy`), which has Mass a few Sundays a year. The two merged in 2010 under one pastor.

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory's `vincentdepaul.org` is alive, and so is the www form (the `svdpmayslanding.org` guess does not resolve). `other` with `page-pdfs` on the `http://` Bon Venture page, as for the other Bon Venture parishes here.
