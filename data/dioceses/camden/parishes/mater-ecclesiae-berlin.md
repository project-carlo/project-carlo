## Publisher

Mater Ecclesiae publishes its own weekly bulletin on its WordPress site (http://www.materecclesiae.org/bulletin/): 'The Mater Ecclesiae Bulletin is published weekly, and is updated on the site on Friday or Saturday of each week', with the two latest issues posted (25 and 18 October 2026, read 2026-10-29). The 25 October issue opens with 'The Feast of Our Lord Jesus Christ The King' and the week's Masses in the traditional Latin rite (Low Mass 8:00 AM, Choral High Mass 10:30 AM Sunday, anticipated Mass Saturday 5:00 PM), and names Rev. Robert C. Pasley as Spiritual Director; the site lists him as Rector. The diocese's directory calls it a 'Mission' with a Rector; the site's own header says 'Mater Ecclesiae Parish'. Either way it has its own bulletin, so it gets its own parish row (the diocese counts 62 parishes without it).
