## What is known

St. Maria Goretti, 1300 Woodtick Rd., Wolcott (GCatholic 22242), was a parish until 29 June 2017, when Archbishop Blair established St. Basil the Great "succeeding the older parishes of Saint Pius X and Saint Maria Goretti" (Town of Wolcott directory, read 14 November 2026); the Courant's 7 May 2017 list gives the same plan. St. Basil the Great's current bulletin (15 November 2026, shared with Mary, Mother of the Church) holds all of St. Basil's Masses "at St. Pius X Church, 525 Woodtick Rd" and does not mention St. Maria Goretti; the parish site's search finds nothing for "Goretti". The parish finder does not list it. Mass-times aggregators (catholicmasstime.org) mark it "[CLOSED]" without a date or source, and a YouTube description says the building "now serves new purposes".

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to closed, but no source with standing (decree, parish, archdiocese, dated news report) says when or that it closed, so it is not excluded. A dated report of its last Mass or a relegation decree would make it `closed`.
