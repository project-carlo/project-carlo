## What is known

St. Donato, 505 Lombard St in Fair Haven, was an Italian parish founded in 1915 (a 1965 Catholic Transcript item on its rededication, seen only as a search snippet from thecatholicnewsarchive.org). GCatholic (22146) still lists the church, with no website. It is in no current list or bulletin: the archdiocese's parish finder does not list it, the Hartford Courant's 7 May 2017 list of the pastoral plan names every New Haven parish of the time and not St. Donato (so its parish had already gone by 2017), the 2023 McGivney merger decree's eight sacred edifices omit it, and the 15 November 2026 McGivney bulletin names eight churches without it. Mass-time aggregators label it "St Donato - CLOSED" and a Facebook memory group says it closed (neither read as a source); newhavencatholic.org has no page mentioning it.

## Why it is not decided

The evidence is all absence: no source read states that St. Donato closed or when (see `research`). It stays open until one does. Nothing suggests a current Mass there.
