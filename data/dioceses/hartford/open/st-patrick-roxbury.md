## What is known

St. Patrick, 25 Church Street (Route 317), Roxbury, was the mission church of Our Lady of Perpetual Help, Washington Depot. On 3 June 2025 the Archdiocese of Hartford announced that it would close and that Our Lady of Perpetual Help would merge into St. Francis Xavier, New Milford on 1 January 2026 (CT Insider; WLAD: 'will close at the end of the year'). The merged parish's bulletin (container 03/1359) has no St. Patrick Mass or mention in any issue grepped: all pages of every issue from 22 June 2025 to 1 February 2026, page 1 of issues every eight weeks to July 2026, and every issue from 2 August to 20 September 2026 (where 'Patrick' matched only an intention for a Patrick J. Maguire and an advertiser). Its 15 November 2026 masthead and intentions key name only St. Francis Xavier, Our Lady of the Lakes and Our Lady of Perpetual Help. The archdiocese's parish finder (2026-11-13) no longer lists it; GCatholic does. Discover Mass still shows an old 'Sunday 8:30am' from MassTimes.org, undated.

The Town of Roxbury's facility listing gives 25 Church St, Roxbury 06783; GCatholic gives 'Route 317' with ZIP 06483 (Seymour's), which looks wrong. The record has no coordinates: GCatholic's Plus Code (87H8HM5V+GW) would put it at about 41.5588, -73.3052.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to closed, but what I found is the announced plan, not a dated report of a final Mass or a decree (this diocese's rule for plan closures). The Our Lady of Perpetual Help bulletins of 2025, which would say when Mass there ended, were on ourlady-stpatrick.org; that domain lapsed (GoDaddy auction page, 2026-11-14), the Wayback Machine has none of the 2025 issues (availability API, 2026-11-14), and Wayback answered 429 to the archived home pages of March 2026 and July 2025. A dated final-Mass report or decree makes this `excluded/` with `reason: closed`; a Mass there in a St. Francis Xavier bulletin makes it a church of that row.
