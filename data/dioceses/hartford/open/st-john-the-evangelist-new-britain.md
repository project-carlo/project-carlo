## What is known

St. John the Evangelist, 655 East Street, was one of four New Britain churches kept as parish churches from January 2022 (Hartford Courant, 1 December 2021). By December 2025 the St. Francis bulletin no longer names it among the parish's churches (the 25 October 2026 issue lists St. Jerome, St. Francis, St. Maurice, St. Joseph and St. Peter), and a commercial listing seen only as a search snippet offers 655 East St. as a religious facility (church and rectory) for sale. GCatholic still lists it; the parish finder does not.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to closed, but no source read says when Masses there ended or that it was sold, so it stays open rather than excluded. The listing pages (LoopNet, PropertyShark) answered 403 to the probe.
