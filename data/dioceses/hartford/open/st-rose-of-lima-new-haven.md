## What is known

St. Rose of Lima, 115 Blatchley Ave in Fair Haven, was one of the two churches of Our Lady of Guadalupe Parish, formed in 2017 from St. Francis and St. Rose of Lima (Hartford Courant, 7 May 2017; Blessed Michael McGivney Parish's St. Francis page: "In 2017, the church was merged with St. Rose of Lima Church to form our Lady of Guadalupe Parish"). The merger decree of 7 June 2023 (effective 1 July 2023) gives Blessed Michael McGivney Parish eight sacred edifices; Our Lady of Guadalupe contributes "Saint Francis" only. The parish site calls itself "uniting the eight Catholic churches of the Elm City", and the 15 November 2026 bulletin names the eight and schedules no Mass at St. Rose (English schedule; the Spanish pages of the image-only issue were not read). GCatholic (22153) still lists the church; mass-time aggregators mark "St. Rose (CLOSED)" (catholicmasstime.org, not a source). Other organisations (Apostle Immigrant Services, Springs Learning Center) give 115 Blatchley Ave as their address. The directory does not list it.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to St. Rose no longer holding Mass, but no source read says it closed or when: there is no relegation decree on the archdiocese's site and no dated report was found (see `research`). The decree's list of edifices is good evidence it was no longer a church of the parish in 2023, not a closure statement. Under the README's rule it stays open until a source states it.
