## What is known

GCatholic lists a St. Patrick church in Falls Village (22075) with no street address. The parish finder doesn't list it. St. Martin of Tours' history page calls it the area's first church, built in 1854, before Catholics moved to Lakeville. The current bulletin (7-15 November 2026) and the Mass Times page give Masses at Canaan, Lakeville and Norfolk only. A former pastor's oral-history interview names St. Patrick's in Falls Village as one of the parish's cemeteries.

## Why it is not decided

All of this is absence. No source found says the church closed, was sold or was relegated, and none says Mass is still held there. GCatholic's entry may be for the cemetery or a long-gone building, but nothing read states that.
