## What is known

The Church of the Assumption, 27 South Adams Street, Manchester, is one of the four churches of St. Teresa of Calcutta Parish (created 2021) and still appears in the parish bulletin's footer (15 November 2026), the parish finder and GCatholic (which gives 285 W Center St., presumably the other frontage). It has had no scheduled weekend Mass since 1 March 2025: the Journal Inquirer (22 January 2025) reported the new schedule dropped its Saturday 5 p.m. Mass, and the 15 November 2026 bulletin schedules Mass only at St. James, St. Bridget and St. Bartholomew. The pastor told the paper Assumption "will not close" and "will still hold special Masses, regular activities, weddings and funerals". The parish's Locations page lists it with "Bengali Community", weddings and funerals and no Mass times; the bulletin still lists memorial candles at its altars.

## Why it is not decided

No source says it closed, and no relegation decree was found; but no scheduled public Mass was found either. Occasional weddings, funerals and special Masses don't make a schedule. Whether the Bengali community has a recurring Mass here was not established (the site's search for "Bengali" returned only the Locations page). A bulletin or site listing a recurring Mass would make it a church of `st-teresa-of-calcutta-manchester`; a relegation decree or sale would make it `excluded` as closed.
