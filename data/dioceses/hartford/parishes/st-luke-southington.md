## Publisher

St. Luke Parish, Southington publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (org `st-luke-parish-southington`, container 03/1187). The 15 November 2026 issue names Saint Luke Parish on page 1 and gives its staff (Pastor Rev. James T. Gregory), Mass times and worship locations on page 2. The parish was formed by the decree of 26 April 2022 that merged Immaculate Conception, St. Dominic, Mary Our Queen and St. Aloysius into St. Thomas Parish under the name St. Luke (cited in the October 2025 Immaculate Conception relegation decree).

## Worship sites

Two, the only WORSHIP LOCATIONS on page 2 of the 15 November 2026 issue: Saint Dominic Church, 1050 Flanders Rd., Southington (Saturday Vigil 4:30 pm, Sunday 11:00 am, Monday-Saturday 8:00 am; `st-dominic-southington`) and Saint Aloysius Church, 254 Burritt St., Plantsville (Sunday 8:00 and 9:30 am; `st-aloysius-plantsville`).

Ruled out, each with its own `excluded/` record:

- Immaculate Conception, 130 Summer St. (directory 77, GCatholic 22184): closed after its heating failed in November 2024 (bulletins of 24 November and 1 and 8 December 2024), relegated to profane use by decree of 9 October 2025 effective 11 November 2025. `immaculate-conception-southington`.
- Mary Our Queen, 248 Savage St., Plantsville (directory 78, GCatholic 22169): "Last Mass at Mary Our Queen is 12-29-24" (29 December 2024 issue); relegated by decree of 5 June 2025. `mary-our-queen-plantsville`.
- St. Thomas, 99 Bristol St. (GCatholic 22186; not in the parish finder): closed 1 August 2021 and demolished in 2025; the site holds the parish office. `st-thomas-southington`.

The parish finder still lists Immaculate Conception and Mary Our Queen as St. Luke churches.
