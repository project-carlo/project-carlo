## Publisher

St. Maximilian Kolbe Parish (Thomaston and Terryville) publishes a weekly bulletin through church-bulletin.org (jppc.net, id 0931), and links each issue from its Bulletins page as a `parish.jppc.net/wp-content/churchuploads/931-BULL-<MM-DD-YYYY>.pdf` upload. The 15 November 2026 issue's page 1 heads "St. Thomas Church ~ Immaculate Conception Church ~ St. Casimir Church", with Rev. Nicholas P. Melo, Pastor.

## Worship sites

- St. Thomas Church, 1 East Main St., Thomaston (`st-thomas-thomaston`): Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 7:00 and 10:00 AM, weekdays
- St. Casimir Church, 17 Allen St., Terryville (`st-casimir-terryville`) and Immaculate Conception Church, 170 Main St., Terryville (`immaculate-conception-terryville`): the Sunday 8:30 AM Mass is "at St. Casimir Church or Immaculate Conception Church". The intentions put it at Immaculate Conception on 8 November and at St. Casimir on 15 November 2026.

The finder (76, 64, 65) and GCatholic (22192, 22191, 22190) list these three. St. Michael, Terryville is Ukrainian Catholic and belongs to the eastern batch.
