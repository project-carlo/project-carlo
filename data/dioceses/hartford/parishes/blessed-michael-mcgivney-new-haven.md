## Worship sites

Eight, all named on page 1 of the 15 November 2026 issue and all with Sunday Mass on page 3 ("Sunday Masses (English)", 14/15 November): St. Stanislaus (Sat 4:30 PM, daily 7:00 AM; `st-stanislaus-new-haven`), St. Anthony (8:00 AM; `st-anthony-new-haven`), St. Joseph (8:30 AM, weekdays 5:00 PM; `st-joseph-new-haven`), St. Aedan (9:00 AM; `st-aedan-new-haven`), St. Francis of Assisi (9:00 AM, Mon/Thu 9:00 AM; `st-francis-of-assisi-new-haven`), St. Michael (9:30 AM; `st-michael-new-haven`), St. Martin de Porres (10:00 AM; `st-martin-de-porres-new-haven`) and St. Mary (11:00 AM and 5:00 PM, weekdays 12:00 PM; `st-mary-new-haven`). Polish and Spanish Masses are on the bulletin's translated pages, which were not read. These are the directory's eight McGivney churches (139, 140, 142, 143, 144, 145, 149, 150) and the eight sacred edifices of the 7 June 2023 merger decree (effective 1 July 2023).

Not here, with their own records: St. Brendan, 455 Whalley Ave (Saints Aedan and Brendan's second church; last Mass 1 January 2017, sold 2017; `excluded/st-brendan-new-haven`) and St. Rose of Lima, 115 Blatchley Ave (Our Lady of Guadalupe's second church from 2017; not among the decree's eight edifices or in this bulletin, closure not dated; `open/st-rose-of-lima-new-haven`). St. Donato, 505 Lombard St (`open/st-donato-new-haven`) is in no 2017 or 2023 plan and in no bulletin. GCatholic's Knights of Columbus Museum, 1 State St, is a museum (`excluded/knights-of-columbus-museum-new-haven`). GCatholic files St. Mary as a Dominican priory with the Shrine of the Infant of Prague; the shrine is part of St. Mary's, not a separate row.
