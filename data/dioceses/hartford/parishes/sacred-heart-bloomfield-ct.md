## Publisher

Sacred Heart Parish, Bloomfield, Connecticut publishes its own weekly bulletin as PDFs on its diocesanweb site. The 8 November 2026 issue (`Bulletin-November-8th.pdf`, 4 pages, text layer) has the masthead on page 1: "Sacred Heart Parish A Roman Catholic Faith Community", Rev. Stuart Pinette, Pastor, "26 Wintonbury Ave., Bloomfield, CT". No ParishesOnline or other publisher id was found on the site. (The slug carries `-ct` because Newark has a `sacred-heart-bloomfield`.)

## Worship sites

One: Sacred Heart Church, 26 Wintonbury Ave (`sacred-heart-bloomfield-ct`). Page 1's Liturgical Schedule (Saturday 4:30 P.M.; Sunday 8:00 and 10:15 A.M.; daily 8:00 A.M. Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday) names no other place, and page 2's intentions are all at those times. The directory (3) and GCatholic (22040) list this church.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`, recipe `page-pdfs https://sacredheartbloomfieldct.com/bulletins/ filename=Bulletin-*.pdf`. The Bulletins page mixes the parish bulletin with the archdiocese's "Branches on the Vine" news PDFs and flyers; the filter keeps only the bulletins, which since September 2026 are named `Bulletin-<Month>-<Nth>.pdf` (earlier ones, e.g. `October-18th.pdf`, had no prefix). On Friday 13 November the newest bulletin was 8 November.
