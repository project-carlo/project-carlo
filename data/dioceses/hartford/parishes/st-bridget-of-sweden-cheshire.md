## Publisher

St. Bridget of Sweden Parish, Cheshire publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (org `st-bridget-of-sweden-parish`, LPi id 0018000000Qx9TKAAZ, container 03/1066), linked from cheshirecatholic.org. The 15 November 2026 issue's page 1 reads "SAINT BRIDGET OF SWEDEN PARISH ... Sunday, November 15, 2026"; page 2 (an image) gives the parish header and the Mass schedule.

## Worship sites

One: St. Bridget Church, 175 Main St (`st-bridget-cheshire`). Page 2 of the 15 November 2026 issue headed "Mass Schedule St. Bridget Church" lists every Mass there, and the parish's Mass Times page lists Saint Bridget Church alone. The "Epiphany House Adoration Chapel" in both is used for adoration sign-ups, not Mass.

Ruled out (the parish was formed on 29 June 2017 from St. Bridget, Epiphany and St. Thomas Becket, per its history page):

- Church of the Epiphany, 1750 Huckins Rd (GCatholic 22051): closed June 2017 and sold in 2019; `excluded/epiphany-cheshire`.
- St. Thomas Becket, 435 N Brooksvale Rd (GCatholic 22053): relegated by a decree read the weekend of 25-26 January 2025 and sold to the YMCA on 9 April 2025; `excluded/st-thomas-becket-cheshire`.

The Legion of Christ college at 475 Oak Ave (GCatholic 117386) is not a church of this parish; see its own record.
