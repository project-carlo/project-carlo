## Publisher

St. Martin of Tours Parish (Canaan, Lakeville, Norfolk) makes its own weekly bulletin and uploads it to its diocesanweb site. The issue headed "Saint Martin of Tours Parish, November 7th - November 15th" (`New-Version-Bulletin-Nov-7-Nov-15.pdf`, 4 pages, read 2026-11-14) lists the week's Masses by town: CANAAN Saturday 4:00 pm and Wednesday 6:00 pm, LAKEVILLE Sunday 9:00 am and Friday 8:00 am, NORFOLK Sunday 11:00 am and Thursday 8:00 am.

The pastor, Rev. Bruce Czapla, O.F.M., is also pastor of St. Joseph, Winsted (clergy directory, see `diocese.md` *Sources*), but this bulletin covers St. Martin's three churches only and Winsted publishes its own on Discover Mass (`st-joseph-winsted`), so these are two rows (*Rules settled*: a shared pastor makes one row only if a current bulletin covers both).

## Worship sites

- St. Joseph Church, 4 Main St., Canaan (`st-joseph-canaan`)
- St. Mary Church, 76 Sharon Rd., Lakeville (`st-mary-lakeville`)
- Immaculate Conception Church, 4 North St., Norfolk (`immaculate-conception-norfolk`)

The parish finder lists these three (directory 113, 124, 89). GCatholic also lists St. Patrick, Falls Village (22075), the area's first church, built 1854 (the parish's St. Mary history page). It has no Mass in the bulletin or on the Mass Times page, and no source found says it closed, so it is an `open/` record (`st-patrick-falls-village`).

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`, pinned to the bulletins page with `filename=New-Version-Bulletin-*.pdf` so the Branches on the Vine PDFs listed on the same page are never picked. The page lists issues newest first. Each issue is named by its weekend range ("Nov-7-Nov-15"), not by a single date.
