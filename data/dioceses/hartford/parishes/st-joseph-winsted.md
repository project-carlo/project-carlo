## Publisher

St. Joseph, Winsted publishes its own weekly bulletin through Discover Mass (church slug `st-joseph-winsted-ct`). The parish site's Bulletin page links the 15 November 2026 issue as the current one; its page 1 masthead reads "Saint Joseph Roman Catholic Church, 31 Oak St. Winsted, CT 06098 … The Franciscans of Immaculate Conception Province", and page 2's Mass intentions run Monday to Saturday 8:00am (Thursday 6:00pm), Saturday 4:00pm and Sunday 8:00am, 10:00am and 6:00pm (read 2026-11-14).

Its pastor, Rev. Bruce Czapla, O.F.M., also leads St. Martin of Tours, Canaan (clergy directory), but St. Martin publishes its own bulletin, which covers Canaan, Lakeville and Norfolk only (`st-martin-of-tours-canaan`), so the two stay separate rows.
