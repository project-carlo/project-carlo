## Publisher

St. John XXIII Parish, West Haven, publishes a weekly LPi bulletin: ParishesOnline org `st-john-xxiii-parish-06516`, container 03/0173. The 15 November 2026 issue has its masthead on page 1, "SAINT JOHN XXIII PARISH", with St. John Vianney Church, St. Louis Church and St. Lawrence School.

## Worship sites

Two, from the 15 November 2026 Mass schedule and the page 2 intentions (marked SL / SJV):

- St. Louis Church, 89 Bull Hill Lane (`st-louis-west-haven`): Sat 4:00 pm, Sun 9:00 am, 10:30 am and 12:30 (Español), Mon-Fri 12:00 pm, Wed 7:00 pm (Español).
- St. John Vianney Church, 300 Captain Thomas Blvd. (`st-john-vianney-west-haven`): Sat 4:30 pm, Sun 8:30 and 10:30 am.

History, from the New Haven Register (15 July 2017, 8 Dec 2021) and West Haven Voice (5 Feb 2020): in 2017 St. Lawrence, St. Louis and St. Paul formed St. John XXIII, and St. John Vianney merged into Our Lady of Victory; St. Paul and St. John Vianney churches closed to regular Mass. In 2020 Our Lady of Victory sold its Jones Hill Road campus and moved back into St. John Vianney Church; by December 2023 the St. John XXIII bulletin lists St. John Vianney as one of its locations (the two parishes have since been joined; no decree read).

Ruled out, each with an `excluded/` record: St. Lawrence Church, 207 Main St. (closing Mass 16 December 2023, relegated 27 December 2023; `st-lawrence-west-haven`), Our Lady of Victory, 600 Jones Hill Rd. (campus sold 2020; `our-lady-of-victory-west-haven`), and St. Paul, First Ave. (closed June 2017; `st-paul-west-haven`). GCatholic still lists all three. The old Our Lady of Victory site olov.org is stale (its newest link is a "2023 Bulletin Archive") and gives the same St. John Vianney times; it is not a separate current bulletin.
