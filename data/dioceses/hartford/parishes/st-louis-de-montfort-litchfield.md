## Publisher

St. Louis de Montfort Parish (Litchfield, Goshen, Bantam) publishes through ParishesOnline (org `st-louis-de-montfort-parish`, LPi id 0018000000Qc0ByAAJ, container 03/0572). The parish site's Bulletins page re-hosts the LPi issues as `<YYYYMMDD>B.pdf` uploads, but its newest is 25 October 2026; the container has the 15 November 2026 issue, whose page 1 masthead reads "Building Bridges to Christ, Saint Louis de Montfort Parish" and lists its three churches, with Pastor Rev. Matt Collins.

## Worship sites

- Saint Anthony of Padua Church, 49 South Street, Litchfield (`st-anthony-of-padua-litchfield`): Sunday 8:00am and 11:00am, Monday-Friday 8:00am
- Saint Thomas of Villanova Church, 71 North Street, Goshen (`st-thomas-of-villanova-goshen`): Sunday 9:15am
- Our Lady of Grace Church, 715 Bantam Road, Bantam (`our-lady-of-grace-bantam`): Saturday Vigil 4:00pm

The finder (82, 85, 93) and GCatholic list these three. GCatholic also lists the Lourdes shrine, 50 Montfort Rd., Litchfield (32365), run by the Montfort Missionaries. It holds a public Mass (Tuesday-Sunday 10:30am, Grotto Chapel, per shrinect.org), but the parish bulletin carries no schedule for it (it mentions only "The Living Nativity at Lourdes Shrine"), and the shrine publishes no bulletin: `excluded/lourdes-shrine-litchfield`, reason `no-bulletin`.
