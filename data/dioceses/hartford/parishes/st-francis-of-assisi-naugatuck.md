## Publisher

St. Francis of Assisi Parish, Naugatuck publishes its own weekly bulletin through church-bulletin.org (John Patrick Publishing, id 959). The 15 November 2026 issue's page 1 reads "Welcome To St. Francis of Assisi Church, 318 Church Street, Naugatuck", with Rev. John Kuzhikottayil, SDB, Pastor.

The clergy directory names Fr. Kuzhikottayil pastor of both St. Francis of Assisi and St. Vincent Ferrer, Naugatuck. The two still publish separate bulletins: St. Vincent Ferrer's 15 November 2026 issue (ParishesOnline container 04/0056) has its own masthead and Mass schedule, and this issue does not mention St. Vincent. Two rows sharing a `group` named for the pastor.

## Worship sites

One: St. Francis of Assisi Church, 318 Church Street (`st-francis-of-assisi-naugatuck`). Page 1's Schedule of Masses gives Monday-Friday 7:15 AM, Saturday 8 AM, Saturday Vigil 4 PM, Sunday 8 AM and 10:30 AM; the page 2 intentions list only these Masses.

GCatholic still lists St. Hedwig (32 Golden Hill St.) and St. Mary (338 N Main St.), Union City, which the 2017 plan merged into this parish. Both closed in June 2017 (`excluded/st-hedwig-naugatuck`, `excluded/st-mary-union-city-naugatuck`). St. Hedwig was used again from June 2018 while St. Francis was repaired (Patch, 1 June 2018); the 8 and 15 November 2026 issues name neither church.
