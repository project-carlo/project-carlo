## Publisher

St. Paul Parish, Kensington (Berlin), served by the Franciscan Friars Conventual, publishes a weekly LPi bulletin: ParishesOnline org `st-paul-church-06037`, container 03/0062. The 15 November 2026 issue (12 pages) has its masthead on page 1: "ST. PAUL CATHOLIC PARISH, Kensington, Connecticut", 467 Alling St., the Mass schedule, and both church addresses.

## Worship sites

One: St. Paul Church, 485 Alling Street (`st-paul-kensington`). The 15 November 2026 issue gives Saturday 4:00pm Vigil, Sunday 8:00, 10:00am and 12:00pm, and weekday Masses Monday, Wednesday and Friday 7:00am, Tuesday and Thursday 5:00pm, and says "All Masses are at St. Paul Church."

Ruled out: Sacred Heart Church, 48 Cottage Street, East Berlin (directory 71, GCatholic 22059). The bulletin still lists it, and it holds rosaries, but it has no Mass: its Sunday 9:30am Mass was suspended after 3 November 2024 for want of a priest (issues of 27 October and 10 November 2024). It is `excluded/sacred-heart-east-berlin`. Sacred Heart parish merged with St. Paul effective 1 March 2017 (Hartford Courant, 7 May 2017).
