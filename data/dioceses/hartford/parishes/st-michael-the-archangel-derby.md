## Publisher

St. Michael the Archangel Parish, Derby (Polish, served by the Vincentian Fathers) makes its own weekly bulletin and uploads it to its diocesanweb site as `Bulletin_<DD><Month><YYYY>.pdf`. Page 1 of the 15 November 2026 issue reads "ST. MICHAEL PARISH", pastor Rev. Marek Sadowski, CM, "75 DERBY AVENUE • DERBY, CONNECTICUT 06418". The issue has no LPi ad pages and the site links no ParishesOnline org, so it has no publication id beyond its URL.

One: St. Michael the Archangel, 75 Derby Ave. (`st-michael-the-archangel-derby`). Page 1 of the 15 November 2026 issue: Sunday Vigil Saturday 4:30 pm; Sunday 9:00 am, 11:00 am (Polish) and 5:30 pm; weekday Masses. The parish cemetery on Silver Hill Rd. is a cemetery, not a worship site.
