## Publisher

St. Jeanne Jugan Parish, Enfield publishes one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline (org `st-jeanne-jugan-parish`, container 03/0038). The 15 November 2026 issue (12 pages, text) has the masthead on page 1: "SAINT JEANNE JUGAN PARISH / SERVING THE ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCHES OF ENFIELD, CT", Rev. John Golas, Pastor, with offices at Holy Family, St. Bernard, St. Patrick and St. Martha. `probe po` on 2026-11-13 listed issues through 2026-11-15. The parish site also uploads each issue to diocesanweb (`20261115B.pdf`, the LPi file name), the same 12-page issue.

## Worship sites

Four, each with its own "Mass Times" box on page 1 of the 15 November 2026 issue:

- Holy Family, 23 Simon Rd (`holy-family-enfield`): Thu-Fri 9:00AM, Sat 8:30AM, Sat vigil 4:30PM, Sun 9:00AM.
- St. Bernard, 426 Hazard Ave (`st-bernard-enfield`): Mon-Wed 9:00AM, Sun 7:30AM and 11:00AM.
- St. Patrick, 64 Pearl St (`st-patrick-enfield`): Thu-Fri 8:00AM, Sat vigil 4:00PM, Sun 10:00AM.
- St. Martha, 214 Brainard Rd (`st-martha-enfield`): Mon-Wed 8:00AM, Wed 7:00PM (TLM), Sat vigil 4:00PM (Spanish), Sun 9:00AM and 11:00AM (TLM), first Friday 7:00PM and Sat 9:00AM (TLM).

The parish finder (directory 57, 72, 73, 59) lists the same four. GCatholic also lists St. Adalbert, 90 Alden Ave (22071): relegated to profane use by the Archbishop's decree of 15 March 2024, effective 22 April 2024, after a final Mass on 21 April 2024; it is `excluded/st-adalbert-enfield`. The bulletin mentions it only for "St. Adalbert's cemetery" (page 9).
