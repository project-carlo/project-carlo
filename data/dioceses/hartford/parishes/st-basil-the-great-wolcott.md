## Publisher

Two canonical parishes with one administrator publish one bulletin: St. Basil the Great (Wolcott) and Mary, Mother of the Church (Waterbury). Each parish has its own ParishesOnline org (`st-basil-the-great-mary-mother-of-the-church-st-leo`, LPi 0018000000Qc2PHAAZ, linked from st-basilparish.org; `mary-mother-of-the-church-parish-st-leo`, LPi 0018000000Qc07nAAB, linked from mmcparish.com), and both orgs serve the same container, 03/0349, with the same issue list. The 15 November 2026 issue is image-only; rendered, its page 1 masthead reads "ST. BASIL THE GREAT & MARY, MOTHER OF THE CHURCH CATHOLIC PARISHES" and gives a Mass schedule for each, and page 2 gives intentions for both, one office (525 Woodtick Road, Wolcott) and one administrator (Rev. Sean Yates). So one row with both parishes' churches. In September 2023 the same container carried St. Basil alone.
