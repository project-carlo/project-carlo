## Publisher

St. Damien of Molokai Parish, Windsor publishes one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline (container 03/0353). The 15 November 2026 issue (8 pages, page 1 an image cover) has the masthead on page 2: "St. Damien of Molokai Parish ... St. Gabriel and St. Joseph Churches", Rev. Joseph Moonnanappallil, Pastor; St. Gabriel Church & Parish Office, 379 Broad St, and St. Joseph Church & Adoration Chapel, 1747 Poquonock Ave, Windsor. `probe po` on 2026-11-13 listed issues through 2026-11-15.

## Worship sites

Two. The page 2 masthead gives St. Gabriel (`st-gabriel-windsor`) "Mass: Monday & Tuesday - 7:30am Saturday, 4:00pm, Sunday - 7:30am, 11:30am" and St. Joseph (`st-joseph-windsor`) "Mass: Wednesday & Friday - 7:30am" and "Sunday - 9:30am". The directory (16, 12) lists the same two; GCatholic lists St. Joseph under Poquonock (22171), the village of Windsor where it stands.

GCatholic also lists St. Gertrude, 550 Matianuck Ave, Windsor (22238). The parish's about page says St. Gertrude Parish was "subsumed into St. Damien parish" in October 2020; the 15 November 2026 bulletin and the site's Mass Times page give no Mass there (the bulletin's only "Gertrude" is the saint's feast on 16 November). No source found says the church closed, so it is an `open/` record (`st-gertrude-windsor`).
