Three, all in the 15 November 2026 page 1 schedule:

- Most Holy Trinity, 84 North Colony St, Wallingford (`most-holy-trinity-wallingford`): Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 10:30 AM and 12:15 PM (Spanish), weekdays.
