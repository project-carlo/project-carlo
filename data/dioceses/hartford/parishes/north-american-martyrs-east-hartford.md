## Publisher

North American Martyrs Parish, East Hartford publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (org `north-american-martyrs-parish-corporation-st-marys-and-st-rose-churches`, LPi id 0018000000Qc1NMAAZ, container 03/1253). The 15 November 2026 issue (8 pages, all text) carries the masthead on page 1: the Mass schedule, "N.A.M. Parish Office: 15 Maplewood Avenue, East Hartford", namct.org, and a footer naming ST. MARY'S CHURCH (1588 Main Street) and ST. CHRISTOPHER CHURCH (538 Brewer Street). The org's slug still names St. Rose; its title now reads "St. Mary's and St. Christopher Churches" (an account name, not the membership: the masthead decides).

## Worship sites

Two, both in page 1's schedule of the 15 November 2026 issue: St. Christopher Church, 538 Brewer St (Saturday 4:00pm Vigil, Sunday 7:30am and 4:30pm, weekdays 8:00am, Tuesday 7:00pm Spanish; `st-christopher-east-hartford`) and St. Mary's Church (Sunday 9:00am, 10:30am and 12:30pm Spanish; `st-mary-east-hartford`). These are the directory's 40 and 25 and GCatholic's 22062 and 22064.

Ruled out:
- St. Rose, 33 Church St (GCatholic 22065): the parish's own history page says it "closed on June 23, 2024", and the archdiocese relegated it to profane use effective 11 November 2025 (`excluded/st-rose-east-hartford`).
- Our Lady of Peace, 370 May Rd (GCatholic 22061): closed with the 1 July 2024 merger and relegated effective 28 April 2026 (`excluded/our-lady-of-peace-east-hartford`). The 2026 bulletins mention it only as a meeting place for youth groups.
- Blessed Sacrament, 15 Millbrook Dr (GCatholic 22060): closed in 2017 and sold to the town as a senior center (`excluded/blessed-sacrament-east-hartford`).
- St. Isaac Jogues church, 1 Community St: since 1 January 2019 the church of the St. Isaac Jogues Ghanaian Catholic Quasi Parish, a separate row (`st-isaac-jogues-ghanaian-east-hartford`). This bulletin names it only as the parking lot for a mobile food truck (page 6) and gives no Mass there. The namct.org page for it is a history page that the quasi-parish's directory entry still links.
