## Publisher

St. John Paul the Great Parish, Torrington publishes its weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (org `st-john-paul-the-great`, LPi id 0018000000Qc07yAAB, container 04/0982), embedded on the parish site's Bulletins page. The 15 November 2026 issue's page 1 lists "Our Worship Sites": St. Francis of Assisi Church, 160 Main St. and St. Peter Church, 99 East Main St., with Pastor Rev. Emmanuel I. Ihemedu; the ad pages carry "St John Paul the Great Parish Corporation | 04-0982".

## Worship sites

- St. Francis of Assisi Church, 160 Main St. (`st-francis-of-assisi-torrington`)
- St. Peter Church, 99 East Main St. (`st-peter-torrington`)

The week's calendar and Mass intentions (pages 2-4) name only these two. GCatholic still lists Sacred Heart (116 Grove St.) and St. Mary (85 Pulaski St.), the other two parishes merged into St. John Paul the Great in June 2017; both closed and were put up for sale (County Times, 23 January 2019), and are `excluded/` (`sacred-heart-torrington`, `st-mary-torrington`). St. Maron, Torrington is Maronite and belongs to the eastern batch.
