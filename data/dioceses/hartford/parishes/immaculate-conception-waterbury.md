## Publisher

Immaculate Conception Parish, Waterbury, publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (org `basilica-of-the-immaculate-conception`, LPi 0018000000Qx9TdAAJ, container 03/1341); the parish site's `/bulletins/` page embeds its widget. Page 1 of the 15 November 2026 issue is headed "Immaculate Conception" with the parish address "74 West Main Street | Waterbury, CT 06702", and page 2 lists the parish's four churches with their addresses.

## Worship sites

Four. Page 1's "MASS SCHEDULE by Church" (15 November 2026) gives the Basilica of the Immaculate Conception (Sat 4:15pm; Sun 6:30am, 8am, 10am, 12pm and 4pm; Mon-Sat 6:30am and 12:10pm; `basilica-of-the-immaculate-conception-waterbury`), Our Lady of Loreto (Sat 4pm, Sun 11am; `our-lady-of-loreto-waterbury`) and St. Michael (Sun 9am; `st-michael-waterbury`). Blessed Sacrament, 182 Robbins St. (`blessed-sacrament-waterbury`), is not in that schedule but has its own section in the page 3 Mass intentions with a Tuesday 10:00am Mass most weeks (17 November 2026; 10 November; 20 October; 16 September 2025; 12 November 2024 at 9:15am), paused in summer ("No Masses until the fall", 14 June 2026); so it is a worship site with a weekly Tuesday Mass.

The directory's four Immaculate Conception churches (94, 96, 83, 99) are these. A 16 November 12:10pm intention for "Parishioners of St Margaret of Scotland Parish" refers to a former parish; St. Margaret's church is decided in its own record.
