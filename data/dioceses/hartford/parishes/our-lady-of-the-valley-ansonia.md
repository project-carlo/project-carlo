## Publisher

Our Lady of the Valley Parish, Ansonia (the merged Assumption and Holy Rosary parishes) publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (org `our-lady-of-the-valley-06401`, LPi 0018000000Qbz2MAAR, container 04/1063). The 15 November 2026 issue, page 1, is headed "WINTER MASS SCHEDULE" with columns for ASSUMPTION CHURCH and HOLY ROSARY CHURCH and lists both under "CHURCH LOCATIONS"; page 2 names the pastor, Rev. Dominic Thomas, and welcomes visitors to "our two churches, the Church of the Assumption and Holy Rosary Church."

## Worship sites

Two, both with weekend Mass in the page 3 Mass intentions of the 15 November 2026 issue: Assumption, 61 North Cliff St. (Sat 4:00pm vigil, Sun 8:00am, Thu 8:00am in the chapel; `assumption-ansonia`) and Holy Rosary, 10 Father Salemi Dr. (Sun 10:00am and 12:00pm, Mon and Wed 8:00am in the chapel; `holy-rosary-ansonia`). Page 1's columns come out interleaved in the text layer; the intentions tie each time to its church.

Not here: St. Joseph, 32 Jewett St., Ansonia, which the directory lists as its own parish and which publishes its own bulletin (`st-joseph-ansonia`), and GCatholic's St. Anthony, 199 N Main St. (closed 29 November 2015, `excluded/st-anthony-ansonia`). Neither appears in this bulletin (grep of the 15 November issue for 'St. Anthony' and 'Joseph Church': no hits).
