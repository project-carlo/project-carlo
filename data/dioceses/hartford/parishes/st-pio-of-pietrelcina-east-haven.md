## Publisher

St. Pio of Pietrelcina Parish, East Haven publishes one weekly bulletin printed by LPi (container 03/0025, ParishesOnline org `st-pio-of-pietrelcina-parish`, LPi id 0018000000QbzzlAAB). The 15 November 2026 issue (12 pages) has the masthead on page 1: "ST PIO OF PIETRELCINA PARISH, NOVEMBER 15, 2026", parish office 355 Foxon Road, pastor Rev. Thomas Walsh. The ad pages' footer reads "St. Pio of Pietrelcina Parish and St. Bernadette Church | 03-0025".

## Worship sites

Three, all under "Our Churches and Mass Schedules" on page 1 of the 15 November 2026 issue: Our Lady of Pompeii, 355 Foxon Road, East Haven (Sat 4:00 pm, Sun 7:30 and 10:30 am, Mon-Thu 8:00 am; `our-lady-of-pompeii-east-haven`), Saint Vincent dePaul, 80 Taylor Avenue, East Haven (Sat 5:00 pm, Sun 11:45 am, Mon-Wed 8:45 am; `st-vincent-de-paul-east-haven`) and St Bernadette, 385 Townsend Avenue, New Haven (Sun 9:00 am, Thu and Fri 8:45 am; `st-bernadette-new-haven`). These are the directory's three St. Pio churches (138, 156, 158).

St. Clare, 234 Coe Ave, East Haven, which GCatholic still lists, closed in 2017 when its parish merged with Our Lady of Pompeii and St. Vincent de Paul to form St. Pio (`excluded/st-clare-east-haven`); a grep of the 15 November issue for "Clare" finds nothing.
