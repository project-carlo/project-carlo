One: St. Therese Church, 120 West Granby Road (`st-therese-granby`). Page 3's schedule (Saturday 5:00 PM; Sunday 8:15 and 10:30 AM; Monday to Thursday 8:30 AM) names no other place. The directory (8) and GCatholic (22081) list this church.
