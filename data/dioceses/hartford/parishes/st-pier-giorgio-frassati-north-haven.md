## Publisher

St. Pier Giorgio Frassati Parish (North Haven, North Branford, Northford), formed by the archdiocese's merger decree of 22 March 2026 from St. Ambrose and St. Elizabeth of the Trinity, publishes one weekly bulletin through LPi / ParishesOnline. It goes out under the older org `st-elizabeth-of-the-trinity-parish` (container 03/1511; the org's earlier container 03/0003 has had no issue since at least 30 August 2026). The 15 November 2026 issue's page 1 masthead reads "CATHOLIC PARISH St. Pier Giorgio Frassati, NOVEMBER 15, 2026" and names all four churches.

The two sites the survey found now lead to this one bulletin. stelizabethofthetrinity.org/bulletins embeds the same org's widget. nbcatholics.org (St. Ambrose) links a ParishesOnline org that no longer exists and a 2016 PDF. frassatiparish.org, the merged parish's site, links the org.

## Worship sites

Four, all in the page 1 Mass schedule of 15 November 2026 and in the merger decree's list of churches that "remain as sacred places for divine worship": St. Augustine, 30 Caputo Rd., North Branford (`st-augustine-north-branford`); St. Barnabas, 44 Washington Ave., North Haven (`st-barnabas-north-haven`); St. Monica, 1321 Middletown Ave., Northford (`st-monica-northford`); St. Therese, 555 Middletown Ave., North Haven (`st-therese-north-haven`). Directory 136, 121, 122, 127; GCatholic 22160, 22161, 22164, 22163.

Ruled out: St. Frances Cabrini, 57 Pond Hill Rd., North Haven (directory 119, GCatholic 22162). Its Sunday 8 AM Mass moved to St. Barnabas from 7 April 2024, leaving only funerals, meetings and weddings there, and it is not in the decree's list (`excluded/st-frances-cabrini-north-haven`). The directory's Latin Mass times for St. Augustine (Sunday 11:00, Tuesday 6:00 pm) are not in the 15 November 2026 bulletin (grep for "Latin" and "Extraordinary" found nothing); the bulletin's times were used.
