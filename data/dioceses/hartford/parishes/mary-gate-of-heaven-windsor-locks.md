## Publisher

Mary, Gate of Heaven Parish, Windsor Locks publishes one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline (container 03/0271, org `mary-gate-of-heaven-parish`). The 15 November 2026 issue (8 pages; page 4 an image) carries on page 5 the masthead: Rev. Patrick M. Kane, Administrator; "St. Mary Church 42 Spring Street, Windsor Locks" and "St. Robert Bellarmine Church 52 South Elm St., Windsor Locks"; parish offices at 45 Church Street. `probe po` on 2026-11-13 listed issues through 2026-11-15.

## Worship sites

Two, both in the bulletin's Mass schedule (page 2), where starred times are "* St. Robert Bellarmine Church": Saturday 4:30 PM vigil and Sunday 9:00 AM at St. Robert Bellarmine (`st-robert-bellarmine-windsor-locks`); Sunday 7:00 and 11:00 AM and weekday 7:30 AM at St. Mary (`st-mary-windsor-locks`). The directory (168, 169) and GCatholic (22239, 22240) list the same two churches. The Parish Center at 45 Church Street is an office.

## Website and bulletin provider

stmarystrobertwl.org (diocesanweb) is the directory's link. Its Bulletins page links the ParishesOnline org; the only PDF there is a June 2025 issue, so fetch reads the org. The homepage also links `Branches-on-the-Vine-2026.11.21.27.pdf`, which is "NEWS FROM THE ARCHDIOCESE OF HARTFORD", not this bulletin.
