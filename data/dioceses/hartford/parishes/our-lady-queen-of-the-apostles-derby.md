## Publisher

Our Lady Queen of the Apostles Parish, Derby (formed in 2017 from St. Mary the Immaculate Conception and St. Jude parishes) publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (org `our-lady-queen-of-the-apostles-parish`, LPi 0018000000Qc2FzAAJ, container 03/0607). Page 1 of the 15 November 2026 issue reads "Our Lady, Queen of the Apostles", "CHURCH OF ST. MARY THE IMMACULATE CONCEPTION, 212 ELIZABETH STREET, DERBY", pastor Fr. Jeffrey Gubbiotti.

## Worship sites

One: St. Mary the Immaculate Conception, 212 Elizabeth St. (`st-mary-the-immaculate-conception-derby`): Saturday Vigil 4 pm, Sunday 8 am, 9 am (Spanish) and 10:30 am, weekdays 8 am, per page 1 of the 15 November 2026 issue.

Not here: St. Jude, 71 Pleasant View Rd. (GCatholic 22056), the merged parish's second church, which held its last services at Christmas 2021 and was sold in December 2022 (`excluded/st-jude-derby`). The parish still keeps St. Jude's feast: the 25 October 2026 issue lists a "Feast of St. Jude Mass" at 6:30 pm on 28 October among its own Masses, with no other church named. A Facebook event for it gives 212 Elizabeth Street, St. Mary's address (search-result text only).
