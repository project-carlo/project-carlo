## Publisher

Our Lady Queen of Angels Parish, Meriden (formed in 2017 from five Meriden parishes, per its history page) publishes one weekly bulletin through church-bulletin.org (JPPC), linked from the Bulletins page of olqoa.org as `church-bulletin.org/0977`. The 15 November 2026 issue (scanned; page 1 read by rendering) has the masthead "Our Lady Queen of Angels Parish, Meriden, CT ... November 15, 2026".

## Worship sites

Four, from the 15 November 2026 page 1 schedule and footer:

- Our Lady of Mount Carmel, 109 Goodwill Ave (`our-lady-of-mount-carmel-meriden`): Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 AM.
- Holy Angels, 585 Main St, South Meriden (`holy-angels-meriden`): Sunday 10:30 AM.
- Our Lady Queen of Angels Chapel, 109 Goodwill Ave (`our-lady-queen-of-angels-chapel-meriden`): Monday to Friday 7:30 AM. The parish lists it as its own site beside Mount Carmel at the same street address; I kept it as a separate row because the bulletin and the Mass Times page schedule it separately. It is not in either roster.
- St. Joseph, 22 Goodwill Ave (`st-joseph-meriden`): in the footer of every issue but in no weekly schedule since at least 17 November 2024 (the oldest issue listed); the 15 March 2026 calendar and the 1 March 2026 notice give a "6 pm Mass at St. Joseph Church" on St. Joseph's Day, 19 March 2026. Kept as a church because it still holds that public Mass; see its record.

Ruled out: St. Laurent, 121 Camp St (`excluded/st-laurent-meriden`, closed 2017) and St. Mary, 5 Sherman Pl (`excluded/st-mary-meriden`, closed after the 2017 merger, sold), both still in GCatholic.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`, pinned to the church-bulletin.org parish page (newest first), per the diocese's church-bulletin.org lesson. Recent issues (from at least September 2026) are scanned images with no text layer, while issues to June 2026 had text, so the pipeline may have to read them as images.

## Not yet confirmed

Whether St. Joseph Church holds any other public Mass in the year (Christmas, funerals) beyond St. Joseph's Day: a scan of the readable issues from 17 November 2024 to 15 November 2026 for "Joseph Church" found only the March 2026 notices; 11 scanned issues in that range were not searched.
