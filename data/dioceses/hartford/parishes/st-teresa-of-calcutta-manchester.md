## Publisher

St. Teresa of Calcutta Parish, Manchester publishes one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline (org `st-teresa-of-calcutta-parish-06040`, container 03/0019), linked from saintteresacatholic.org. The 15 November 2026 issue (3 pages, text) has the masthead on page 1: "SAINT TERESA OF CALCUTTA ROMAN CATHOLIC PARISH", Father Marcin P. Pluciennik, Pastor, and a footer naming four churches: St. Bridget (80 Main St.), St. James (896 Main St.), St. Bartholomew (736 Middle Tpke. East) and the Church of the Assumption (27 Adams St. S). `probe po` on 2026-11-13 listed issues through 2026-11-15.

## Worship sites

Three. The page 1 schedule of the 15 November 2026 issue: "Saturday Vigil St. James 4:30 pm"; "Sunday St. Bridget 8 am; St. James 11 am / St. Bartholomew 9:30 am & 5 pm (except July and August)"; weekdays "St. James 8 am" and "St. Bridget 5 pm". Page 2's intentions use SJC, SBC and SBAR for the same three. So: St. James (`st-james-manchester`), St. Bridget (`st-bridget-manchester`), St. Bartholomew (`st-bartholomew-manchester`).

The Church of the Assumption, 27 Adams St. S (directory 58, GCatholic 22110), is in the footer but has no Mass in the schedule. The Journal Inquirer (22 January 2025) reported that the schedule effective 1 March 2025 dropped its Saturday 5 p.m. Mass, and that the pastor said it "will not close" and "will still hold special Masses, regular activities, weddings and funerals". The parish's Locations page lists it with no Mass times ("Bengali Community / Weddings / Funerals"), and the bulletin's page 3 still carries "Assumption Church Memorial Candles". No source says it closed, and no scheduled public Mass was found, so it is `open/church-of-the-assumption-manchester`.
