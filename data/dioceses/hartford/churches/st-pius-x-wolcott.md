## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the 15 November 2026 St. Basil the Great & Mary, Mother of the Church bulletin (container 03/0349, image-only, rendered) schedules St. Basil the Great's Masses "at St. Pius X Church, 525 Woodtick Rd, Wolcott": Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 9:30 AM, Monday and Tuesday 8:00 AM; page 2 lists intentions for each.

## Name and address

The bulletin and mmcparish.com call the building St. Pius X Church, 525 Woodtick Road, Wolcott. The parish finder (directory 81) lists it as 'St. Basil the Great Parish', 525 Woodtick Rd., 06716 (the parish, not the building), and GCatholic (22243) as 'St. Pius X', 525 Woodtick Rd., 06716-2898. The address agrees across all three; the name follows the bulletin. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H9H2MG+76, about 55 m from the directory pin.
