## Holds Mass

Yes. The 8 November 2026 St. Marianne Cope bulletin (page 1) gives St. Philip the Apostle Saturday vigil 4:00 p.m. and Sunday 10:30 a.m., with confession Saturday 3:00-3:30 p.m.

## Name and address

Sources disagree on the number. The bulletin's page 1 gives the church as 153 South Main Street and its page 2 the rectory as 150 South Main Street; the parish finder (150 S. Main St.) and GCatholic (150 S Main St.) give 150. Recorded 150, on which the diocese and GCatholic agree; 153 may be a typo or the church's own number across the lot (not checked on a map). Either finds the building. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; the finder pin (41.88967, -72.609057) is the same spot. The name follows the bulletin (St. Philip the Apostle); the rosters say St. Philip.
