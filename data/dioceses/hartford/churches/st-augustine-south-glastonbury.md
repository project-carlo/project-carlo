## Holds Mass

Yes, once a month. The 15 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, an image) lists Mass "at St. Augustine Church" on the first Saturday of the month at 7:30am, plus a Tuesday 7:00pm Rosary and Reconciliation on the first Monday. The parish finder gives only the Rosary. A monthly public Mass is enough for a church row.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish finder and GCatholic all give 55 Hopewell Road, South Glastonbury 06073. The bulletin calls it "St. Augustine Church". Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code, about 20 m from the finder's pin.
