## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 St. John XXIII bulletin (page 1) gives St. John Vianney Church: Saturday 4:30 pm (8:30 am on the first Saturday), Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 am; the page 2 intentions mark these SJV.

The New Haven Register of 15 July 2017 listed St. John Vianney among the churches closed at the end of June 2017. That closure was reversed: Our Lady of Victory parish sold its own campus in 2020 and moved its Masses here (New Haven Register, 8 December 2021), and the church has had weekend Masses in every St. John XXIII bulletin read since (10 December 2023, 15 November 2026).

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish finder and GCatholic all give 300 Captain Thomas Blvd., West Haven 06516. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within about 20 m of the finder pin.
