## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the 15 November 2026 St. Nicholas bulletin gives Saturday 4:30 pm vigil, Sunday 11:00 am and weekdays Monday to Thursday 9:00 am at the Church of the Good Shepherd.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish finder, GCatholic and the ParishesOnline org all give 135 Mountain Road. The points disagree: the finder's pin (41.38698, -73.10394) and the ParishesOnline org's (41.38699, -73.10395) are within a few metres of each other, while GCatholic's Plus Code decodes about 480 m north (41.39131, -73.10394). The finder's pin is used because two independent sources put it at the same spot; an OSM check of 135 Mountain Rd. would settle it. Name as the bulletin gives it.
