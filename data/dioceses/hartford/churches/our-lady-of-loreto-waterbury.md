## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 Immaculate Conception bulletin (container 03/1341) schedules Saturday 4pm and Sunday 11am Masses at Our Lady of Loreto, with intentions for both on page 3.

The church stands at the corner of the two streets; the address follows the parish and the directory. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H8HWCF+PG, about 70 m from the directory pin.
