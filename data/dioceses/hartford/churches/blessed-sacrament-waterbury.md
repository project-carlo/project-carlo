## Holds Mass

Yes, a weekly Tuesday Mass during the school year, open to the public. Blessed Sacrament is not in page 1's "MASS SCHEDULE by Church" of the Immaculate Conception bulletin (container 03/1341), but the page 3 Mass intentions have a 'Blessed Sacrament | BSC' section with a Tuesday Mass: 10:00am on 17 November 2026 (15 November issue), 10 November and 20 October 2026, 16 September 2025, and 9:15am on 12 November 2024. The 14 June 2026 issue says "No Masses until the fall" for BSC. The Catholic Academy of Waterbury's Blessed Sacrament Church page (read 14 November 2026) says Mass "is typically celebrated during the school year on Tuesdays at 10:00am" and that "Parishioners and friends of Blessed Sacrament are always welcome". The bulletin also lists overnight adoration at BSC on Saturdays.

WFSB reported on 17 August 2023, citing the archdiocese, that St. Joseph, Saints Peter and Paul and Blessed Sacrament would close on 30 September 2023, with the Blessed Sacrament campus kept for Catholic education. For Blessed Sacrament that was the end of the parish and its weekend Masses (merged into Immaculate Conception that day, per the Academy page), not of Mass in the church, which the bulletin shows continuing.

NBC Connecticut (18 August 2023) reported that the campus would be used "for Catholic education, weddings and funerals only. No regular masses will be held." The bulletins since November 2024 show a weekly school-year Mass anyway, listed in the parish's public intentions and open to parishioners per the Academy's page, so the church is kept as a worship site.
