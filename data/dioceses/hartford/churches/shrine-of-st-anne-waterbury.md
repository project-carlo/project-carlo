## Holds Mass

Yes. The 8 November 2026 All Saints - Todos los Santos bulletin (container 03/1242) schedules Saturday 6:00pm (Spanish), Sunday 8:30am and 12:30pm (Spanish) and 10:30am (English) Masses at the Shrine of St. Anne, a Tuesday 9:00am Mass in its chapel and Monday-Friday 6:30pm Spanish Masses; the page 3 intentions list them under 'SHRINE OF SAINT ANNE'.
