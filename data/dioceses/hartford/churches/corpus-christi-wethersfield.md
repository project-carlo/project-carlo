## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 Christ the King bulletin (page 1) gives Sunday 8:00 and 11:00 am and Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday 7:30 am at Corpus Christi Church.

## Name and address

The directory gives 601 Silas Deane Hwy; GCatholic gives 84 Somerset St. Both place the building at the same spot (directory pin and GCatholic Plus Code about 25 m apart), so the church stands on the corner lot with frontage on both streets; the parish site places Corpus Christi School next door at 581 Silas Deane Hwy. The diocese's address is recorded, as the official source. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code. The name is the bulletin's and the directory's.
