## Holds Mass

Yes, on Sundays. The quasi-parish's site (read 2026-11-13) gives Sunday Mass at 11:00 AM, and the parish finder gives "Sun 11:00 Twi". No bulletin was found (see the parish row), so this rests on the site and the finder.

## Name and address

The quasi-parish's site and the parish finder give 1 Community St, East Hartford 06108. These are two independent sources (the parish and the diocese), so the address is verified. GCatholic gives 73 Cannon Rd, but its Plus Code is about 10 m from the finder's pin, on the same building. The rectory is on Cannon Road (the history page says "A cannon Road apartment complex was refurbished as the new rectory"), and GCatholic may have used that address. The name "St. Isaac Jogues" appears in GCatholic and on both parish sites. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code.
