## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, scanned) and the parish's Mass Times page give Monday to Friday 7:30 AM Mass in the Our Lady Queen of Angels Chapel; Monday 6:00-7:00 PM adoration is there too. The 17 November 2024 issue already had the daily Mass there.

## Name and address

Only the parish (bulletin and site, one source) names this chapel, at 109 Goodwill Ave, the same street address as Our Lady of Mount Carmel Church; neither the directory nor GCatholic lists it, so neither field is verified. Which building on the Mount Carmel property holds it was not established. Coordinates are copied from Mount Carmel's GCatholic Plus Code (87H9G5RV+M6), hence `manual`.
