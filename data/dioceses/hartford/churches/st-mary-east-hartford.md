## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 North American Martyrs bulletin (page 1) gives Sunday 9:00am, 10:30am (Spanish) and 12:30pm (Spanish) at St. Mary's.

## Name and address

The bulletin footer gives the church as "ST. MARY'S CHURCH, 1588 Main Street, East Hartford 06108" and the parish office as 15 Maplewood Avenue. The parish finder (25) and GCatholic (22064) give 15 Maplewood Ave., which is the office address. The parish's history page puts the church "at the corner of North Main Street and Maplewood Avenue", so both addresses are on the same corner lot. I recorded the bulletin's church address, 1588 Main Street, because the other sources give the office. The address is not verified, because only the parish gives 1588 Main Street. The name is the bulletin's "St. Mary's Church" (finder: "St. Mary Church"). Coordinates are from GCatholic's Plus Code, which is on the corner lot and about 20 m from the finder's pin.
