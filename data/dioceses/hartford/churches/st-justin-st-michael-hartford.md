## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 bulletin (page 3) gives Sunday 8:30am Spanish, 10:00am English and 4:00pm Creole, and weekday Masses at 6:00pm in the parish chapel or Jubilee Hall (the church basement) on the same campus.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the directory and GCatholic all give 230 Blue Hills Ave., Hartford 06112. This is the former St. Justin church (GCatholic still calls it "St. Justin"); since the 2017 merger the parish and the directory use "St. Justin - St. Michael", and no source names the building itself, so `name_verified` is false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 40 m from the directory pin.
