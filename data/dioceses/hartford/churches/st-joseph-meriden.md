## Holds Mass

Occasionally. St. Joseph has no weekly Mass: the Holy Mass Schedule on page 1 of every Our Lady Queen of Angels issue from 17 November 2024 to 15 November 2026 names Mount Carmel, Holy Angels and the OLQA Chapel only, and the parish's Mass Times page (13 November 2026) omits it. But the parish still lists it in every issue's footer of its churches, and it held a public Mass there on St. Joseph's Day: the 1 March 2026 issue (page 7) invites all to a "6 pm Mass at St. Joseph Church" on 19 March 2026, and the 15 March calendar (page 5) lists it. A grep of the 94 readable issues in that range for "Joseph Church" found no other Mass there; 11 scanned issues were not searched. I kept it as a worship site because a public Mass is still celebrated there and nothing says it closed; whether a once-a-year feast-day Mass is enough is a judgement, made under *Rules settled* in `diocese.md` (a feast-day public Mass in a church with no listed weekly Mass shows it in use; reconcile 2026-11-14). See `revisit`.
