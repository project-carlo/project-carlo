## Why it is excluded

St. Thomas, 99 Bristol St., Southington is closed and demolished. Patch (23 September 2021) reports "The building closed on Aug. 1, with parishioners being directed to other local churches", with a decommissioning Mass scheduled for Sunday 26 September 2021; the Courant (28 September 2021) reports the planned demolition. A Southington preservation group's page says the church was "demolished 2025". St. Luke Parish's bulletins since July 2023 never list it, and the parish office is at 99 Bristol St. The October 2025 relegation decree for Immaculate Conception still counts "Saint Thomas Church in Southington" among St. Luke's sacred edifices, so no relegation decree is known; the building's demolition makes that moot. `last_mass` is 2021 because the decommissioning Mass is reported only as scheduled; regular Masses ended by 1 August 2021. GCatholic (22186) still lists it; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

A new church built on the site.
