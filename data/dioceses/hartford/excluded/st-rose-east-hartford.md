## Why it is excluded

St. Rose, 33 Church St, East Hartford is closed. North American Martyrs Parish's history page says "St. Rose Church closed on June 23, 2024", the weekend before the 1 July 2024 merger. The archdiocese then relegated the building to profane but not sordid use, by a decree of 9 October 2025 effective 11 November 2025 (public notice of 27 October 2025; the Our Lady of Peace decree of 11 March 2026 also cites it). The parish's bulletins of 19 October and 9 November 2025 and of 15 November 2026 hold no Mass there. `last_mass` is the parish's own closing date, which is earlier than the decree. GCatholic (22065) still lists the church; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a new church on the site: the building is no longer a sacred place.
