## Why it is excluded

Closed. Our Lady Queen of Angels' own history page says St. Mary "merged in 2017 and closed shortly after", in line with the 2017 plan (Courant, 7 May 2017: "St. Mary and St. Laurent buildings closing"). A Record-Journal Facebook post, seen only as a search snippet, reports the church and school sold to a local Pentecostal church after two years on the market. The 15 November 2026 Our Lady Queen of Angels bulletin lists no Mass there, and its footer names Mount Carmel, Holy Angels, St. Joseph and the OLQA Chapel only. No source read gives the date of the last Mass, so `last_mass` is left empty. GCatholic (22119) still lists it; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building has been sold to another denomination. A dated report of the final Mass would fill `last_mass` (see `revisit`).
