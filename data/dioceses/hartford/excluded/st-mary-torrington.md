## Why it is excluded

Closed in 2017. Preservation Connecticut's listing of 85 Pulaski Street (read 2026-11-14) says the building "operated as St. Mary’s Roman Catholic Church until its closure due to church consolidations in 2017", and the County Times of 23 January 2019 reports "The closed St. Mary of Czestochowa Roman Catholic Church in Torrington is for sale by the Archdiocese of Hartford." The parish merged into St. John Paul the Great in June 2017 (Wikipedia), whose 15 November 2026 bulletin names only St. Francis of Assisi and St. Peter as worship sites. GCatholic (22195) still lists it; the parish finder doesn't. No report found gives the day of the final Mass.

## What would change this

A St. John Paul the Great bulletin listing a Mass at St. Mary, or a report of the building returning to Catholic use.
