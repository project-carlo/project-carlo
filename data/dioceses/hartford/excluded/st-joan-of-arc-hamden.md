## Why it is excluded

Closed. The New Haven Register of 15 July 2017 lists St. Joan of Arc in Hamden among eight New Haven County churches "merged with other parishes and closed" at the end of June 2017, when it merged with Our Lady of Mount Carmel; the Hamden Historical Society says the same. No day for the last Mass was found, so `last_mass` is the month. St. Paul VI's 15 November 2026 bulletin does not name it. GCatholic (22087) still lists it; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

A St. Paul VI bulletin listing a Mass at St. Joan of Arc.
