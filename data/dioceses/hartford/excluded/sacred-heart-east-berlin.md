## Why it is excluded

St. Paul Parish suspended the 9:30am Sunday Mass at Sacred Heart, 48 Cottage Street, East Berlin, for want of a priest: the bulletin of 27 October 2024 announced it, and the 10 November 2024 issue says the Mass was "suspended after November 3rd", so the last scheduled Mass was Sunday 3 November 2024. No Mass has been scheduled there since. Every issue sampled from November 2024 to 15 November 2026 has none there; since August 2025 the masthead says "All Masses are at St. Paul Church." The parish site (13 November 2026) says "There are currently NO regularly scheduled Masses at Sacred Heart Church." The building still belongs to the parish and holds rosaries, so it is closed as a worship site, not sold or relegated.

## What would change this

A scheduled public Mass at Sacred Heart in the St. Paul bulletin (the suspension was put down to the lack of a friar) would make it a church of `st-paul-kensington` again.

Address: the parish bulletin and the finder give 48 Cottage Street; GCatholic gives 66 Cottage St. The parish's 48 is used.
