## Why it is excluded

Closed. The Hamden Historical Society's church-closings page (read 14 November 2026) says mold found in 2022 ended regular services and "a closing Mass was held on October 15, 2023"; St. Paul VI's own Facebook post announced that 2 pm Mass on Sunday 15 October 2023 at Church of the Ascension "as we formally close the church" (read only through its search-result text). The parish's 15 November 2026 bulletin still prints Ascension in its masthead, but its Mass schedule and the 14-22 November intentions name only Our Lady of Mt. Carmel, St. Rita and Blessed Sacrament, and the 10 May 2026 issue lists no Mass there even on Ascension Thursday. The parish homepage's Mass times also omit it. The directory (125) and GCatholic (22083) still list it. No relegation decree was found (search 14 November 2026).

## What would change this

A St. Paul VI bulletin listing a Mass at Ascension, or a reopening after repairs.
