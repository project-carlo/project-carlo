## Why it is excluded

Not a public worship site. GCatholic (42042) lists the Knights of Columbus Museum, 1 State St., New Haven, with type "Museum". Its website, kofcmuseum.org, now redirects to the Blessed Michael McGivney Pilgrimage Center at the same address, whose "Plan Your Visit" page (read 2026-11-14) describes exhibits on McGivney's life, guided tours, a gift shop and opening hours ("Wednesday through Sunday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m."), with no Mass schedule. Blessed Michael McGivney's own parish church, St. Mary's, 5 Hillhouse Ave, is a row of Blessed Michael McGivney Parish (`st-mary-new-haven`). The directory does not list the museum.

## What would change this

A regular public Mass at the center. Its Events and Novenas pages were not read; if one shows a recurring Mass in a chapel there, it becomes a church of whichever bulletin announces it.
