## Why it is excluded

St. Joseph, 140 S Main St, Suffield was consolidated with Sacred Heart in 2017 and has had no regular Mass since. The Suffield Observer (1 November 2022) says Sacred Heart "has controlled St. Joseph’s since the two churches were consolidated in 2017", that the 1952 church "served 65 years, plus a few more for special events", that it carried a "for sale" sign, and that a developer proposed apartments "now that the church is closed to normal use". The 8 November 2026 Sacred Heart bulletin (masthead "The United Parish Family of Sacred Heart and Saint Joseph") gives Masses only at Sacred Heart. The parish finder (2026-11-13) does not list St. Joseph; GCatholic (22188) does. `last_mass` is the year of the consolidation; the article gives no date and mentions special events for a few years after. Whether the sale went through was not checked. No relegation decree was found in the archdiocese's public notices.

## What would change this

A Sacred Heart bulletin listing a scheduled Mass at St. Joseph, 140 S Main St.
