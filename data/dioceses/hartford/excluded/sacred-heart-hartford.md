## Why it is excluded

Sacred Heart (Sagrado Corazón), 49 Winthrop Street, Hartford, the city's historic Puerto Rican church, closed under the 2017 pastoral plan. The Courant's list of 7 May 2017 gives "Sacred Heart building closing and merging with St. Peter", and its column of 23 June 2017 says the archdiocese "plans to close the doors" on 29 June, with parishioners moving to St. Peter's on Main Street. CT Insider (3 November 2023) calls it "Hartford's vacant Sacred Heart Church", empty since the archdiocese closed it, and reports the city's plan to make it a Puerto Rican cultural center. The parish finder omits it and no current Hartford bulletin names it.

The sources disagree on the year: CT Insider says 2015, the contemporaneous Courant pieces put the closing in June 2017. `last_mass` follows the Courant (2017-06); no source gives the day of the last Mass. St. Peter, where its parishioners went, also closed in 2017 (`excluded/st-peter-hartford`), so no current parish is recorded as its successor.

## What would change this

A current Hartford bulletin scheduling a Mass at 49 Winthrop Street.
