## Why it is excluded

The Connecticut Post of 16 January 2022 reports that St. Jude Church, Our Lady Queen of the Apostles' second church, "held its final services on Christmas" (2021) and was listed for sale on 3 January 2022. The Valley Independent Sentinel of 1 January 2023 reports that "The former St. Jude Church was recently sold for $3.1 million to a Milford-based Christian church", with a deed filed on 13 December 2022. The parish's 15 November 2026 bulletin names only St. Mary the Immaculate Conception. Its October feast-day "Feast of St. Jude Mass" (25 October 2026 issue) names no other church and cannot be at a building the parish sold. GCatholic still lists the church. `last_mass` is December 2021: the article gives "Christmas" without the exact day of the final service (Christmas Eve or Day).

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building belongs to another congregation.
