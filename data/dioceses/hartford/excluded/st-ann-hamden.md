## Why it is excluded

Closed. The New Haven Register of 15 July 2017 lists St. Ann's in Hamden among eight New Haven County churches "merged with other parishes and closed" at the end of June 2017 (with Ascension and Blessed Sacrament it formed Christ the Bread of Life), and reports the closing ceremony its last pastor held; on 21 January 2019 the Register reported the church at 930 Dixwell Ave. for sale. No day for the last Mass was found, so `last_mass` is the month. St. Paul VI's 15 November 2026 bulletin and homepage give no Mass there (the homepage names only a St. Ann Food Pantry). GCatholic (22086) still lists it; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

A St. Paul VI bulletin listing a Mass at St. Ann.
