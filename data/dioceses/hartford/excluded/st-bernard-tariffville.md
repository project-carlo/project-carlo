## Why it is excluded

St. Bernard, 7 Maple St, Tariffville (Simsbury) held its last regular Mass on Sunday 25 June 2017. The Hartford Courant that day: "On Sunday, the Rev. Collins I. Anaeche said Mass at St. Bernard’s for the last time. The church is merging with St. Therese parish in Granby", and "St. Bernard’s will not celebrate regular Mass, but will be open for weddings, funerals, special ministries and other events." The 15 November 2026 St. Therese bulletin gives Masses only at St. Therese and names St. Bernard only as a cemetery. The archdiocese's parish finder (2026-11-13) does not list it; GCatholic (22189) still does. No relegation decree was found in the archdiocese's public notices; `closed` here means no regular public Mass since 2017, on the Courant's report.

## What would change this

A St. Therese bulletin or site listing a scheduled Mass at St. Bernard, Tariffville.
