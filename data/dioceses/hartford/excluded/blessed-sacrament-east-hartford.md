## Why it is excluded

Blessed Sacrament, 15 Millbrook Dr, East Hartford is closed and sold. The Hartford Courant (14 February 2018) reports that the archdiocese "closed Blessed Sacrament last year" (2017) when it formed St. Edmund Campion Parish, and that the town council voted to buy "the former Blessed Sacrament Church" for a senior center. North American Martyrs Parish's history page says it "was closed and sold to the Town of East Hartford". `last_mass` is 2017, the year the Courant gives; no day was found. Only GCatholic (22060) still lists it. The successor parish's 15 November 2026 bulletin does not name it.

## What would change this

Nothing: the building belongs to the town.
