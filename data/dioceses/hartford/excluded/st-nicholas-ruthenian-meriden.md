## Why it is excluded

St. Nicholas, 89 Summer St., Meriden is an Eastern Catholic church. GCatholic's page for it (read 2026-11-14) gives "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic", "Rite: Ruthenian (Byzantine)" and "Location: within the territory of the Roman Metropolitan Archdiocese of Hartford". It belongs to that eparchy, not to the Archdiocese of Hartford, so it is `other-jurisdiction`, as *Rules settled* in `diocese.md` decided for the eleven Eastern Catholic churches GCatholic lists here. Its Divine Liturgy schedule and bulletin were not checked.

Nothing expected: it would be covered if the Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic were ever explored.
