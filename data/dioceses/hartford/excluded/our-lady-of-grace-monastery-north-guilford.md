## Why it is excluded

An open worship site with no bulletin. The Dominican nuns' homepage (read 14 November 2026) invites visitors "to join us for the holy sacrifice of the Mass & the Liturgy of the Hours", and their horarium gives Mass daily at 7:30 a.m., so the monastery chapel holds a public Mass. The site publishes a newsletter and event posts but no weekly bulletin or Mass schedule document the pipeline could read, and the nearest parish bulletin, St. George, Guilford (15 November 2026, page 1), lists only St. George Church. It was never a parish row, so it is `no-bulletin`, not a church of St. George.

## What would change this

A weekly bulletin or schedule document published by the monastery, or a Guilford-area parish bulletin printing its Mass times, would make it a church row.
