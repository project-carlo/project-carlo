## Why it is excluded

Closed and sold. The West Haven Voice of 5 February 2020 reports Our Lady of Victory parish selling its 10-acre Jones Hill Road site and moving to the St. John Vianney Church property; the New Haven Register of 8 December 2021 says the campus was sold in 2020 to the Christian Fellowship Church of God and the parish moved to St. John Vianney. No source found gives the day of the last Mass, so `last_mass` is the year. The current St. John XXIII bulletin (15 November 2026) schedules Masses only at St. John Vianney and St. Louis. GCatholic (22228) still lists it; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building belongs to another congregation. A dated Our Lady of Victory bulletin of 2020 announcing the last Mass would refine `last_mass`.
