## Why it is excluded

St. Thomas is the Syro-Malabar Catholic community of the Hartford area. GCatholic's page for it (read 2026-11-14) gives "Jurisdiction: Syro-Malabar Diocese of Saint Thomas the Apostle of Chicago", "Rite: Syro-Malabar" and "Location: within the territory of the Roman Metropolitan Archdiocese of Hartford". It belongs to the Chicago eparchy, not the Archdiocese of Hartford, so it is `other-jurisdiction`, as *Rules settled* in `diocese.md` decided for the eleven Eastern Catholic churches GCatholic lists here. Its liturgy schedule was not checked beyond its home page.

## Name and address

GCatholic gives 133 Woodland St. #10, Hartford 06105 (a suite address, probably an earlier meeting place). The community's own site, syromalabarct.org (read 2026-11-14), gives "30 Echo Ln, West Hartford CT 06107" with Sunday Holy Qurbana at 11:30 AM. That is the former St. Helena church, which `excluded/st-helena-west-hartford` already records as used by the Syro-Malabar community (We-Ha, 8 January 2021). The parish's own address is recorded here; the two records describe one building under two roster entries.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it would be covered if the Syro-Malabar eparchy of Chicago were ever explored.
