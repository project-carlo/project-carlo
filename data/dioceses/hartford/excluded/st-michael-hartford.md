## Why it is excluded

St. Michael, 7 Clark Street, Hartford, closed when its parish merged with St. Justin in 2017. The Hartford Courant of 9 April 2017 (Palm Sunday) reports that "After Mass on Sunday morning, more than 100 churchgoers left St. Michael Church on Clark Street for the final time" and processed to the merged St. Justin-St. Michael Parish on Blue Hills Avenue; the pastor said "this building will be closed". The merged parish's 15 November 2026 bulletin schedules every Mass at 230 Blue Hills Ave and does not name Clark Street. The parish finder omits it; only GCatholic still lists it. `last_mass` is that Palm Sunday Mass, 9 April 2017.

Not checked: whether a decree relegated the building or what it is used for now.

## What would change this

A St. Justin - St. Michael bulletin scheduling a Mass at 7 Clark Street.
