## Why it is excluded

Closed and sold. Divine Mercy Parish (St. Rita and St. Stephen, since merged into St. Paul VI) posted "the final Catholic Mass at St. Stephen Church in Hamden" on YouTube, uploaded 29 August 2020 (Pacific time), so the last Mass was in August 2020; the exact day is not stated. The Hamden Historical Society says the parish decided in 2020 to close St. Stephen and the building was sold to Virgin Mary & Archangel Michael Coptic Orthodox Church. St. Paul VI's 15 November 2026 bulletin does not name it. GCatholic (22089) still lists it; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building is no longer the archdiocese's.
