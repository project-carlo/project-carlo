## Why it is excluded

Closed. Wikipedia says the parish merged into All Saints in 2017 and "The building was closed for regularly scheduled worship and subsequently sold to a Pentecostal church" (its cited source is not given; a LoopNet sale listing for 86 E Farm St., 'Church Building and a 2,813 SF Rectory', answered 403). The 2017 plan (Courant list, 7 May 2017) closed the building, and the National Catholic Reporter (2018) describes the merged parishes' historic churches as "largely unused". All Saints' current bulletin (8 November 2026) schedules Masses only at Our Lady of Lourdes and the Shrine of St. Anne. GCatholic (22220) still lists it. No dated final-Mass report or sale record was read; `last_mass` is the year from the Wikipedia infobox ("closed 2017") and the 2017 plan.

## What would change this

An All Saints schedule listing a Mass there. A report of the final Mass or the sale would date it.
