## Why it is excluded

St. Luke, 66 Bolton Street in Hartford's South End, closed in June 2017 and its parish merged into Christ the King, Wethersfield (the former Corpus Christi). The Hartford Courant of 26 August 2017 says it was "closed in June by the Archdiocese of Hartford" and "had its final regular Mass on June 25"; it reports a special Mass that day by permission, and a wedding and a St. Lucy Mass planned for later in 2017, all one-off. `last_mass` is the last regular Mass, 25 June 2017. The parish finder omits it, and only GCatholic lists it.

## What would change this

A Christ the King (Wethersfield) bulletin scheduling a regular Mass at 66 Bolton Street.
