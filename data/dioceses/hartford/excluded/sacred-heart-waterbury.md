## Why it is excluded

Closed; sold to the City of Waterbury in June 2024. The Hartford Business Journal (2 July 2024) reports that the city acquired "the former Sacred Heart church property and four buildings at 13 Wolcott St." from All Saints Parish Corp. for $950,000 (deed filed 13 June 2024), for redevelopment as a shelter and re-entry campus (NBC Connecticut, 18 March 2024). The 2017 plan closed the building and merged the parish into All Saints - Todos los Santos (Courant list, 7 May 2017), but no dated final-Mass report was found, so `last_mass` is left empty. All Saints' current bulletin schedules no Mass here; GCatholic (22211, 'Sacred Heart', group 'Iglesia Sagrado Corazon') still lists it, and its website sacredheartsagradocorazon.org no longer resolves.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the city owns the building. A report of the final Mass would give its date.
