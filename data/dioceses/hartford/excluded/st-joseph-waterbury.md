## Why it is excluded

Closed; last Mass 24 September 2023. The Republican-American (reprinted 12 October 2023) reported "the last Mass of St. Joseph Catholic Church on Sunday morning, Sept. 24", a 9 a.m. Mass of about 200 people, after Rev. Frederick Aniello announced Archbishop Blair's decision on 20 August; WFSB (17 August 2023), citing the archdiocese, gave the closing date as 30 September 2023. The Save Our Lithuanian Parishes blog (27 July 2026) says the parish "closed by decree and was merged into Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish", that a Vatican appeal is pending with no decision made public, and that the church is unsold; until summer 2026 the merged parish listed it for weddings and funerals only. Our Lady of Mount Carmel's 15 November 2026 bulletin schedules no Mass here. GCatholic (22214) still lists it; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

A successful appeal reopening the church, or a Mass at St. Joseph in Our Lady of Mount Carmel's schedule.
