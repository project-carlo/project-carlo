## Why it is excluded

Closed in 2017. St. John the Baptist, on Dixwell Avenue in New Haven near the Hamden line, belonged to the Hamden group of parishes: the 2017 plan (Hartford Courant, 7 May 2017, under Hamden) closed the St. Ann and St. John the Baptist buildings and merged them with Blessed Sacrament and Ascension into Christ the Bread of Life (whose churches are now in the directory's St. Paul VI Parish, Hamden). The New Haven Register of 15 July 2017 reports it among the churches "merged with other parishes and closed" at the end of June 2017 and quotes a parishioner on the closing ceremony, at which the pastor pushed the door shut and said "St. John the Baptist is closed". The Register of 21 January 2019 says the church was for sale. `last_mass` is 2017-06 from these reports; none gives the day. GCatholic (22148) still lists it, at 782 Dixwell Ave; the 2019 article says 786 Dixwell Ave. The directory does not list it. No `parish` is set: its successor parish is in the Hamden batch.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. A dated report of the final Mass would give `last_mass` its day.
