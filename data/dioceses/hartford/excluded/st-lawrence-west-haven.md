## Why it is excluded

Closed. The pastor announced in December 2021 that St. Lawrence Church would close for want of costly repairs (New Haven Register, 8 December 2021). The St. John XXIII bulletin of 10 December 2023 (page 6) announces "A final, Closing Mass ... in St. Lawrence Church on Saturday, December 16, at 10 AM" and prints the decree notice of its relegation to profane but not sordid use (page 4); the New Haven Register of 16 December 2023 gives the decree's date, 27 December 2023. `last_mass` is the closing Mass. The 15 November 2026 bulletin lists only St. Lawrence School (231 Main St.), not the church. GCatholic (22230) still lists it; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a St. John XXIII bulletin giving a Mass in the church; the building was for sale (Patch reports it on the market at 211 Main St.).
