## Why it is excluded

The parish's own bulletin of 25 October - 7 November 2026 (page 2) still lists St. Joseph, 195 S. Main Street among its churches, footnoted "*St. Joseph and St. Peter Churches are no longer used for regular Masses.", and its schedule has no Mass there. The New Britain Herald reported on 5 April 2024 that St. Joseph "will stop offering Sunday masses". Closed, last regular Mass in 2024 (spring; no exact date found). The parish still uses St. Joseph School (29 Edson St.) for its food pantry and meetings.

## What would change this

A scheduled public Mass at St. Joseph in a St. Francis bulletin would make it a church of `st-francis-of-assisi-new-britain` again. Occasional weddings or funerals do not.
