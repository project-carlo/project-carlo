## Why it is excluded

Closed in 2017 and sold to the city in 2022. CT Insider (7 June 2026) reports that Waterbury bought the 24 Branch St. property in 2022 for $400,000, "five years after St. Lucy's Church closed due to a restructuring plan by the Archdiocese of Hartford", and plans a health center there; the National Catholic Reporter (2018) already called it "a church which no longer features regular Sunday Mass". The 2017 plan (Courant list, 7 May 2017) closed the building and merged the parish into All Saints - Todos los Santos. No dated final-Mass report was found. All Saints' current bulletin schedules no Mass here; GCatholic (22216) still lists it.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the city owns the building.
