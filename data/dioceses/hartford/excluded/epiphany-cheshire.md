## Why it is excluded

Closed. The New Haven Register of 21 August 2017 reports that the Church of the Epiphany "closed in June as part of a merger" forming St. Bridget of Sweden (effective 29 June 2017 per the parish's history page) and that Mass is no longer said there; the Register of 30 June 2019 reports the building sold to a Coptic Christian group. The church's Facebook page announced the last Mass "Wednesday at 9:15 a.m." with the church "closed as of June 29" (seen only as a search snippet), probably 28 June 2017; `last_mass` is recorded as 2017-06. The St. Bridget of Sweden bulletin of 15 November 2026 has no Mass at Epiphany; its "Epiphany House Adoration Chapel" is a parish adoration chapel, not this building. GCatholic (22051) still lists the church; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building is in non-Catholic hands. A dated source for the final Mass would refine `last_mass`.
