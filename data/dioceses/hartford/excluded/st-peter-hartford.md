## Why it is excluded

St. Peter, 160 Main Street, Hartford, is a closed and sold church. The Hartford Courant (21 July 2025) describes "the long-closed St. Peter's Church", "The vacant brownstone church with its soaring bell tower — closed in 2017", and says the church, school and convent were bought in 2018 for apartments and were headed to foreclosure. The parish finder omits it and no current Hartford bulletin names it; only GCatholic lists it. No source read gives the date of the last Mass, so `last_mass` is the year.

In the 2017 plan Sacred Heart was to merge into St. Peter (`excluded/sacred-heart-hartford`); where the merged parish went is not recorded in any source read for this batch.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building is in private hands.
