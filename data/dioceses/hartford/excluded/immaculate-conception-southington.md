## Why it is excluded

Immaculate Conception, 130 Summer St., Southington is closed. St. Luke Parish's bulletin of 24 November 2024 still listed daily Mass there at 8:00 am, but its pastor's column said the heating had failed and the church, already "slated to close and be readied for sale in January", would not reopen; the 1 December 2024 issue says "Immaculate Conception Church has been closed" and moves all daily Masses to St. Dominic. So the last Mass was in late November 2024 (no day given). The archdiocese then relegated the building to profane but not sordid use by decree of 9 October 2025, effective 11 November 2025 (public notice of 27 October 2025). The 15 November 2026 bulletin lists only St. Dominic and St. Aloysius. The parish finder (77) and GCatholic (22184) still list it.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a new church on the site: the building is no longer a sacred place.
