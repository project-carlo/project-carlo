## Why it is excluded

The Hartford Courant (1 December 2021) reported that from January 2022 members of St. Andrew (the Lithuanian church, 396 Church Street) were invited to Masses at the four remaining churches, with St. Andrew kept for weddings and funerals "for now" and likely to be sold. Save Our Lithuanian Parishes (July 2026) says "The archdiocese later sold both the 1911 church and the former Friendship Center." The St. Francis bulletin of 25 October 2026 no longer lists it among the parish's churches. Closed; last regular Mass at the start of 2022 (no exact date found), so `last_mass` is left empty.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building was sold. A dated sale record or a 'final Mass' report would give `last_mass`.
