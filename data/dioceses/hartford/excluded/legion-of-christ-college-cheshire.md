## Why it is excluded

A religious house: the Legionaries of Christ's novitiate and college of humanities at 475 Oak Ave, Cheshire, which GCatholic lists as a seminary (117386). Its own site (lccheshire.org, read 13 November 2026) gives Mass in the seminary chapel only for occasional events open to guests (the 15 August 2026 professions Mass at 10:30 am, a September vocation day), and no regular public Mass schedule. No parish bulletin read in this batch lists Mass there (St. Bridget of Sweden's 15 November 2026 schedule lists St. Bridget Church only). This is a judgement from what the site shows and omits, not a statement that the chapel is closed to the public.

## What would change this

A published weekly public Mass schedule at the seminary chapel (on lccheshire.org or in a Cheshire parish bulletin) would make it a worship site; it would still need a bulletin to be a parish row.
