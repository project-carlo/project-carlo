## Why it is excluded

Closed. The County Times of 23 January 2019 reports "The closed Sacred Heart Roman Catholic Church in Torrington will soon be put up for sale by the Archdiocese of Hartford." The Register Citizen of 19 May 2017 describes the plan that merged Sacred Heart, St. Mary, St. Francis and St. Peter into St. John Paul the Great; that is the plan, not a date for the last Mass, so `last_mass` is left empty (closed by January 2019). The 15 November 2026 St. John Paul the Great bulletin names only St. Francis of Assisi and St. Peter as worship sites. GCatholic (22193) still lists it; the parish finder doesn't. A local Facebook group post found by search speaks of the bell tower being demolished; it was not read.

## What would change this

A dated report of the final Mass (to fill `last_mass`), or a St. John Paul the Great bulletin listing a Mass at Sacred Heart.
