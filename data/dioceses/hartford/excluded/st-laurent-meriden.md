## Why it is excluded

Closed. Our Lady Queen of Angels' own history page says of St. Laurent: "In 2017, the church merged and closed the same year." A Record-Journal/CT Insider listing story of 6 September 2023 says the building closed in 2017, went up for sale in 2018 and was most recently the Sanul Presbyterian Church (it gives the address as 123 Camp St; GCatholic gives 121). A Facebook group post seen only as a search snippet says the church would close "at the end of June" (2017). The 15 November 2026 Our Lady Queen of Angels bulletin lists no Mass there and its footer names Mount Carmel, Holy Angels, St. Joseph and the OLQA Chapel only. `last_mass` is the history page's year, per the diocese's rule for history-page dates. GCatholic (22118) still lists it; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building has passed to another denomination. A dated report of the final Mass would refine `last_mass`.
