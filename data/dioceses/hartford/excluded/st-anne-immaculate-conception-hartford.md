## Why it is excluded

St. Anne-Immaculate Conception, 820 Park Street, Hartford, was closed in the 2017 pastoral plan: the Courant's list of 7 May 2017 gives "St. Anne-Immaculate Conception building closing and merging with St. Augustine", and Real Hartford (22 March 2021) says of the church "Today, the church is closed." St. Augustine's 15 November 2026 bulletin schedules all its Masses at 10 Campfield Ave. and does not name St. Anne; the parish finder omits it. The year of the last Mass is inferred from the 2017 plan and the 2021 report; no source read gives a date.

## What would change this

A St. Augustine bulletin scheduling a Mass at 820 Park Street.
