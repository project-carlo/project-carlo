## Why it is excluded

No Mass any more. CT Insider (25 June 2021) reports Christ the Redeemer parish merged into Precious Blood and quotes the parish: "Regularly scheduled Masses ceased as of June 1, and only weddings and funerals, as desired, will be held". The article says the last Sunday Mass came "earlier this month" while the quote dates the end to 1 June, so `last_mass` is the year, 2021. The building was not relegated (no decree found) and Precious Blood still uses it for retreats and its hall: twelve issues read from January 2025 to October 2026 mention it only as a venue, and the 15 November 2026 schedule names only St. Mary and St. Agnes. GCatholic (22123) lists it; the parish finder does not. The Courant's 7 May 2017 list had planned "Christ the Redeemer merging with St. Mary upon retirement".

Not to be confused with ctredeemer.org, which search results attach to it: that site and its LPi org (`christ-the-redeemer-catholic-church-48360`) are Christ the Redeemer, Lake Orion, Michigan.

## What would change this

A Precious Blood bulletin scheduling a Mass at Christ the Redeemer (a feast day or a regular Mass). Funerals and weddings alone don't make it a worship site.
