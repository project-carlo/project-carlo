## Why it is excluded

Mary Our Queen, 248 Savage St., Plantsville is closed. St. Luke Parish's bulletin of 29 December 2024 says "(Last Mass at Mary Our Queen is 12-29-24)" under its 9:30 am Sunday Mass; from 5 January 2025 the church is gone from the schedule, and the 12 January issue speaks of "divesting of Mary Our Queen and Immaculate Conception properties". The archdiocese's October 2025 decree relegating Immaculate Conception says Mary Our Queen was relegated to profane but not sordid use by decree dated 5 June 2025. The 15 November 2026 bulletin lists only St. Dominic and St. Aloysius. The parish finder (78) and GCatholic (22169) still list it.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a new church on the site: the building is no longer a sacred place.
