## Why it is excluded

Closed. A former parishioner quoted by the New Haven Register on 21 January 2019 says "Our last Mass there was June 25, 2017", and the article reports the church at 65 Burban Drive being sold after closing in June 2017; the Register's July 2017 report lists it among the churches "merged with other parishes and closed" when St. John Bosco was formed (established 29 June 2017). St. John Bosco's 15 November 2026 bulletin names St. Elizabeth only as one of the communities it unites; its Mass schedule lists St. Mary and St. Therese. GCatholic (22041) still lists the church; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

A St. John Bosco bulletin listing a Mass at St. Elizabeth, or news that the building was reopened for worship.
