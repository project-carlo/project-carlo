## Why it is excluded

Open, with public Mass, but no bulletin. The Montfort Missionaries' Shrine of Lourdes in Litchfield gives a "Fall Mass Schedule, Tuesday through Sunday 10:30am, Grotto Chapel" on its home page (shrinect.org, rendered 2026-11-14), and links no bulletin or newsletter. The St. Louis de Montfort parish bulletin (15 November 2026) mentions the shrine only for "The Living Nativity at Lourdes Shrine" and gives Masses at its own three churches. It is not in the parish finder; GCatholic lists it (32365) as a shrine.

## What would change this

A shrine newsletter or schedule document the pipeline could read, or the parish bulletin printing the shrine's Mass times (which would make it a church of that row).
