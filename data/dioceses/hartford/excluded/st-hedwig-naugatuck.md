## Why it is excluded

St. Hedwig, Union City (Naugatuck), closed under the 2017 pastoral plan, its parish merged into St. Francis of Assisi. Patch reported the final Mass on Thursday 29 June 2017 ("closed for the final time on Thursday evening", posted 30 June 2017), matching the date the parishes' bulletin had announced (Patch, 21 May 2017). GCatholic still lists it (22197); the parish finder does not.

St. Francis held all its Masses at St. Hedwig for a time from 14 June 2018 while St. Francis Church was repaired (Patch, 1 June 2018). That was temporary: the 8 and 15 November 2026 St. Francis bulletins give Masses at St. Francis of Assisi Church only and do not name St. Hedwig. `last_mass` is the 2017 final Mass; the end of the 2018 use is undated.

## What would change this

A St. Francis bulletin listing a Mass at St. Hedwig (for instance an annual Polish Mass).
