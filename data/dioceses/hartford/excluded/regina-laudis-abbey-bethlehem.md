## Why it is excluded

An open worship site with no bulletin. The abbey's Hours page (read 14 November 2026) says 'MASS is celebrated daily at 8:15 am in the Jesu Fili Mariae Church. All are welcome.' (its horarium on the same page gives 8:00 am), so the abbey church holds a public daily Mass. The site (probe url, 2026-11-14) links no bulletin or schedule document, and the neighbouring Prince of Peace bulletin (15 November 2026, page 1 schedule) does not list it. It was never a parish row, so it is `no-bulletin`, not a church of Prince of Peace.

The address is the abbey church's own, 15 Robert Leather Road, as the abbey's directions page and GCatholic give it; the abbey's postal address is 273 Flanders Road.

## What would change this

A weekly bulletin or schedule document from the abbey, or a Prince of Peace bulletin printing its Mass times, would make it a church row.
