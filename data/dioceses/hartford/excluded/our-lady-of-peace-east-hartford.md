## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Peace, 370 May Rd, East Hartford is closed. North American Martyrs Parish's history page says that after the 1 July 2024 merger "Our Lady of Peace Church and St. Rose Church were closed". The archdiocese's decree given 11 March 2026 (public notice 11 April 2026) relegated the building to profane but not sordid use "effective April 28, 2026". The parish's bulletins from October 2025 to April 2026 name the building only as a youth-group meeting place, and no Mass schedule lists it. `last_mass` is 2024, the year the parish gives for the closure. No source gives the day of the last Mass, so the date is earlier than the decree. GCatholic (22061) still lists the church; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a new church on the site: the building has been relegated to profane use.
