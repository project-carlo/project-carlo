## Why it is excluded

The Connecticut Post of 6 December 2015 reports that St. Anthony's, the Lithuanian church on North Main Street, "has closed": at the end of the Mass of 29 November 2015 its pastor read Archbishop Blair's decree suppressing the parish and announcing that the building was for sale. The article mentions "an occasional Wednesday Mass" only to keep tax-exempt status until the sale. A Lithuanian-parish history site says (search snippet only) that Abundant Life Fellowship Ministries bought the 195-199 North Main Street property. No Mass there appears in the current Our Lady of the Valley bulletin (15 November 2026), which names only Assumption and Holy Rosary. GCatholic still lists the church.

`last_mass` is the 29 November 2015 closing Mass the article reports; the occasional Wednesday Masses after it were not dated.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing a Mass at 199 North Main Street, which is unlikely if the building now belongs to another congregation.
