## Why it is excluded

St. Adalbert, 90 Alden Ave, Enfield was relegated to profane but not sordid use by the Archbishop of Hartford's decree given 15 March 2024, "effective April 22, 2024", at the request of St. Jeanne Jugan's pastor and trustees (5 December 2023). The Journal Inquirer (11 April 2024) reported "a final Mass at the church on April 21" and that the building was listed for sale. Under the diocese's settled rule this is closed, dated to the final Mass the press reports. The current St. Jeanne Jugan bulletin (15 November 2026) lists Mass at four other Enfield churches and none here. GCatholic (22071) still lists it; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

A St. Jeanne Jugan bulletin scheduling a Mass at 90 Alden Avenue (not expected: the building was put up for sale).
