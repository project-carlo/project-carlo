## Why it is excluded

Closed, 30 September 2023 (last Mass not dated to the day). WFSB reported on 17 August 2023, citing the Archdiocese of Hartford, that "St. Joseph, Saints Peter and Paul, and Blessed Sacrament will be closing September 30". Saints Peter and Paul, 67 Southmayd Rd., was a church of Mary, Mother of the Church Parish (a June 2023 obituary on legacy.com, read only in its search-result text, places a funeral at "Mary Mother of the Church Parish, SS Peter and Paul"). The parish's current bulletin (15 November 2026, shared with St. Basil the Great) and its Mass times page schedule every Mass at St. Leo's Church and none here. GCatholic (22210) still lists it, with Mary, Mother of the Church's phone number; the parish finder does not. No relegation decree or dated final-Mass report was found (DuckDuckGo searches, 14 November 2026); the parish's 2023 bulletins are not in container 03/0349, which then carried St. Basil alone.

## What would change this

A Mary, Mother of the Church schedule listing a Mass at Saints Peter and Paul. A report of the final Mass would give the day.
