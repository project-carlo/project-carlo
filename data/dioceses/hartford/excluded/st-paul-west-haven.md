## Why it is excluded

Closed. The New Haven Register of 15 July 2017 says St. Paul's, 898 First Ave., was "one of 26 church buildings to close at the end of June" 2017, when its parish joined St. Lawrence and St. Louis as St. John XXIII Parish; on 21 January 2019 it reported the closed church being sold to the University of New Haven. No day for the last Mass was found, so `last_mass` is the month. The current St. John XXIII bulletin (15 November 2026) schedules Masses only at St. John Vianney and St. Louis.

## Name and address

GCatholic (22232) gives 41 Alling St.; both Register articles give 898 First Ave. Recorded the Register's street address; Alling St. may be the side street or the old rectory.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the property was sold.
