## Why it is excluded

Regular Masses at St. Peter, 98 Franklin Square ended in 2022 (CT Insider, 30 April 2025: "regularly scheduled masses at St. Peter ended in 2022"; Hartford Courant, 25 March 2025). Funerals and baptisms continued until cracks from the 5 April 2024 earthquake were found; the city condemned the building in February 2025 and the archdiocese plans to demolish it (a lawsuit sought to stop that). The parish's 25 October - 7 November 2026 bulletin still lists it, footnoted "*St. Joseph and St. Peter Churches are no longer used for regular Masses." Closed, last regular Mass 2022.

## What would change this

Only a restored, reopened building with a scheduled Mass in the St. Francis bulletin. Whether it was demolished in 2025-2026 was not checked.
