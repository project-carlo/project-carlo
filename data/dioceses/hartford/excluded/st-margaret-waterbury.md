## Why it is excluded

Closed in 2017 and sold in 2018. Archbishop Blair's decree of 12 May 2017 slated St. Margaret of Scotland Church, 289 Willow St., for deconsecration (Hartford Courant, 2 June 2017; the decree itself was not read); its parish merged into All Saints - Todos los Santos when the plan took effect on 29 June 2017. The Republican-American reported on 10 April 2018 that, "An appeal to the Vatican defeated", the church and its buildings had been sold to the Brass City Charter School, and that "The church has since closed". The last Mass is not dated: the Courant's 2 June 2017 article says parishioners were still awaiting word on it, so it falls between then and the end of June 2017 rather than on the decree date. All Saints' current bulletin (8 November 2026) schedules no Mass here. GCatholic (22217) still lists it; the parish finder does not. Its Facebook page (last post June 2018) still shows old Mass times.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building belongs to a charter school. A report of the final Mass would give the day.
