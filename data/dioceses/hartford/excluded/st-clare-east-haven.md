## Why it is excluded

Closed in 2017. The 2017 pastoral plan (Hartford Courant, 7 May 2017) closed the St. Clare building and merged its parish with Our Lady of Pompeii and St. Vincent de Paul into St. Pio of Pietrelcina; Patch (9 May 2017) gives the closing date as 29 June 2017. The New Haven Register of 15 July 2017 reports the closing as done: St. Clare is among the eight New Haven County churches "merged with other parishes and closed" at the end of June, and describes the ceremony at which the oldest parishioner "was the last person to exit the church before the doors literally were locked". The Register of 21 January 2019 says the church, "closed in 2017", was being sold. `last_mass` is 2017-06, from the end-of-June closing in these reports; no source gives the day of the final Mass. The 15 November 2026 St. Pio bulletin lists three churches and does not mention St. Clare (grep for "Clare": no match). GCatholic (22067) still lists it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. A dated report of the final Mass would give `last_mass` its day.
