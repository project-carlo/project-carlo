## Why it is excluded

Holy Trinity, 53 Capitol Avenue, Hartford (the Lithuanian church; canonically a mission of the Cathedral parish, per its 9 February 2025 bulletin) no longer holds regular public Mass. Save Our Lithuanian Parishes (27 July 2026) reports that parishioners were told in April 2026 that "regular Masses would end on May 30", with the church kept for funerals and baptisms, and that "May 30, 2026. Holy Trinity held its last regularly scheduled Masses." Holy Trinity's own ParishesOnline container (03/0549) agrees: its last issue, 24 May 2026, schedules the 4:00 pm Saturday Mass on 30 May, and no issue exists for any Sunday from 31 May to 15 November 2026 (checked 2026-11-13, while the Cathedral's container answered). The Cathedral's 15 November 2026 bulletin names no Mass at Holy Trinity. Its site holytrinityhartford.org answers 404.

The archdiocese's parish finder still lists "Holy Trinity Mission" with a Saturday 4:00 pm Lithuanian-English Mass; that listing predates May 2026 and is not followed. The WTNH report the blog cites (April 2026) could not be read (human challenge). Funerals and baptisms are not public Mass, so the building counts as closed here, dated to the last regular Mass.

## What would change this

A Cathedral or archdiocesan schedule listing a regular Mass at Holy Trinity again, or the building reopened (the Lithuanian community's campaign seeks to keep it as a cultural centre).
