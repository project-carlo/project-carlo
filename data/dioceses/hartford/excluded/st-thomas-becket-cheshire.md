## Why it is excluded

Closed by relegation to profane use. The pastor's column in the 23 February 2025 St. Bridget of Sweden bulletin (page 2) says the parish "asked the Archbishop to relegate the property to profane but not sordid use", that the decree was read "on the weekend of January 25th and 26th", and speaks of "the official closing of Saint Thomas Becket Church". The 20 April 2025 bulletin (page 3) reports the property "officially been sold to the YMCA" on Wednesday 9 April 2025. No bulletin read gives a last Mass, so `last_mass` is dated to the decree's reading, 2025-01 (the decree's own date was not seen). The 15 November 2026 bulletin lists Masses at St. Bridget Church only. GCatholic (22053) still lists the church; the parish finder does not.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building has been sold. The decree itself, or a bulletin from 2024 naming the last Mass there, would refine `last_mass`.
