## Why it is excluded

St. Mary, 338 North Main Street, Union City (Naugatuck), closed under the 2017 pastoral plan with St. Hedwig, both parishes passing to St. Francis of Assisi on 1 July 2017. Patch (24 June 2017) printed St. Mary's final bulletin: "TUESDAY, JUNE 27TH ST. MARY 6:00PM FOR ALL PARISHIONERS", the Mass "ending 110 years of tradition". This is the scheduled final Mass as announced the weekend before; no report after the Mass was found. GCatholic still lists the church (22198); the parish finder does not. The 15 November 2026 St. Francis bulletin names no Mass there.

## What would change this

A St. Francis or St. Vincent Ferrer bulletin listing a Mass at St. Mary, Union City.
