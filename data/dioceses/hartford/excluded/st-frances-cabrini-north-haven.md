## Why it is excluded

Closed to regular Mass. The St. Elizabeth of the Trinity bulletin of 31 March 2024 announced that the 8 AM Sunday Mass would move from St. Frances Cabrini to St. Barnabas "starting April 7th", with "funerals, meetings and weddings only" at Cabrini afterwards. The 31 March 2024 issue was the last whose Mass schedule had a St. Frances Cabrini section, so the last regular public Mass was Easter Sunday, 31 March 2024 (inferred from the schedule; the bulletin gives no 'last Mass' wording). The building stayed in the masthead's church list until the 2026 merger. The 22 March 2026 merger decree does not name it among the churches that remain for worship, and the 15 November 2026 St. Pier Giorgio Frassati bulletin does not mention it. No relegation decree was found. The directory (119, with no Mass times) and GCatholic (22162) still list it.

## What would change this

A Frassati bulletin scheduling a public Mass at St. Frances Cabrini, as distinct from funerals and weddings.
