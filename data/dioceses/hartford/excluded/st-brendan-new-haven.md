## Why it is excluded

Closed, last Mass 1 January 2017. The New Haven Register of 16 July 2017 ("New Haven's St. Brendan Catholic Church sold to rabbinic school") reports that St. Brendan, 455 Whalley Ave, had been closed in winter for some years, that "The last Mass was celebrated on Jan. 1, the Solemnity of Mary", that the church was desacralized and that the property was sold to a yeshiva. Blessed Michael McGivney Parish's own St. Aedan history page says "the property and buildings of St. Brendan were sold in 2017". The 7 June 2023 merger decree's eight sacred edifices do not include it, and the 15 November 2026 McGivney bulletin names eight churches, not St. Brendan. GCatholic (22145) still lists it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building was sold out of Catholic use.
