## Why it is excluded

St. Maron, 613 Main St., Torrington is an Eastern Catholic church. GCatholic's page for it (read 2026-11-14) gives "Jurisdiction: Maronite Diocese of Saint Maron of Brooklyn", "Rite: Maronite (Antiochian)" and "Location: within the territory of the Roman Metropolitan Archdiocese of Hartford". It belongs to that eparchy, not to the Archdiocese of Hartford, so it is `other-jurisdiction`, as *Rules settled* in `diocese.md` decided for the eleven Eastern Catholic churches GCatholic lists here. Its Divine Liturgy schedule and bulletin were not checked.
