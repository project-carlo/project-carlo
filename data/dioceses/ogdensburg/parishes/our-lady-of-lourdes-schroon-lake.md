## Publisher

Our Lady of Lourdes, Schroon Lake publishes its own bulletin as a two-page PDF that the diocese hosts at `rcdony.org/images/ourladylourdes.pdf`. The copy read on 2026-09-30 is the issue for 27 September 2026 (header "OUR LADY OF LOURDES CATHOLIC CHURCH", Administrator Fernando V. Solomon, 1114 US Route 9, Schroon Lake, `schroonlakesec@rcdony.org`), last modified 25 September 2026, so it is current and weekly. It lists the week's Masses for all three churches of the Southern Adirondack Catholic Community, but is headed and kept by Schroon Lake.

The other two churches of the community, St. Mary's, Ticonderoga and Sacred Heart, Crown Point, publish through ParishesOnline (`southern-adirondack-catholic-community-ticonderoga`). That bulletin also lists Our Lady of Lourdes Masses, but because this separate channel is live, the rows are kept apart.

## Worship sites

- `our-lady-of-lourdes-schroon-lake`, 1114 US Route 9: Sunday 11:15 am, Tuesday 11:30 am, Wednesday 9:00 am. The community's website adds a Saturday 6:00 pm Mass that neither bulletin lists for the week of 27 September (it may be seasonal).

Nothing else in the rosters belongs to this row.

## Website and bulletin provider

There is no separate Our Lady of Lourdes website: the community site (southernadirondackcatholics.org) is used by all three, so the parish `website` is the PDF's URL (a website must be unique across rows), as other rows here do for a self-hosted PDF. `bulletin_provider: other`, publication `url:` of the PDF. The PDF sits at a fixed, undated URL and is replaced each week, so nothing can be archived from it; `recheck` quotes will drift with the issue.
