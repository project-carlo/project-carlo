## Publisher

Our Lady of the Sacred Heart (OLSH), Watertown publishes its own weekly bulletin, headed "THE
CHURCH OF OUR LADY OF THE SACRED HEART, 320 W. Lynde St." (27 September 2026 issue, ParishesOnline
container 06/0110, 4 pages, text layer; page 1 is a cover picture). Its pastor, Rev. Frank T. Natale,
M.S.C., is also pastor of St. Mary's, Evans Mills and St. Paul's, Black River, and page 3 has one
"Mass Schedule and Intentions" covering all three: OLSH Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:45 am and
Monday to Friday 8:15 am (28 September to 2 October); St. Mary's Sunday 8:00 am and Thursday 9:15 am;
St. Paul's Sunday 11:30 am. The 125th-anniversary notice for St. Paul's and the Blue Mass at St.
Paul's are on the same page.

**This is not one bulletin with River Catholics.** St. Mary's and St. Paul's also publish their own
bulletin (`river-catholics-evans-mills`, eCatholic files, masthead "River Catholics - Parishes of St.
Mary's and St. Paul's"), which lists OLSH's Sunday Masses in its schedule. Both documents are current,
have different mastheads and content, and come from different accounts, so they are two rows; the
churches are each attached to the bulletin that carries their own name (St. Mary's and St. Paul's to
River Catholics, OLSH here). A consumer that reads both bulletins will see St. Mary's and St. Paul's
Masses twice.

The diocese's directory lists OLSH as a single entry (`our-lady`, Fr. Natale, olshparish.org, no group).
It is not part of "Blessed Sacrament Parish" (Fr. Carrara), which publishes a separate bulletin (`blessed-sacrament-watertown`).
