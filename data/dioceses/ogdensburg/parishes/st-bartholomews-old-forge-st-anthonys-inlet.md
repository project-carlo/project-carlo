## Publisher

St. Bartholomew's, Old Forge and St. Anthony of Padua, Inlet (and its Raquette Lake oratory) share one pastor, Fr. Thomas Kornmeyer, one office (Old Forge) and one weekly bulletin, a two-page PDF the parish keeps at a fixed URL on fultonchaincatholics.org. The copy read on 2026-09-30 covers 26 September to 4 October 2026 and names both churches in its masthead and in a single Mass schedule ("(OF)" and "(Inlet)").

## Worship sites

- `st-bartholomews-old-forge`: Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 10:00 am, Tuesday and Thursday 8:00 am.
- `st-anthony-of-padua-inlet`: Saturday 5:30 pm (through 11 October 2026 only), Sunday 8:00 am, Wednesday 10:00 am.
- `open/st-williams-summer-chapel-raquette-lake`: the parish site calls it an "Oratory"; no Mass at it appears in the bulletin or on the website, so it is not decided.

## Website and bulletin provider

fultonchaincatholics.org is a self-hosted site (`probe url` 200, no ParishesOnline or eCatholic id); its "Bulletin" link is the PDF above. `bulletin_provider: other`, publication `url:` of the PDF. The diocesan directory's older addresses (fultonchaincatholic.rcdony.org, .dioogdensburg.org) were not tested. The bulletin has a complete text layer. Nothing on the site or in the PDF is dated, so the bulletin cannot be archived by its URL.
