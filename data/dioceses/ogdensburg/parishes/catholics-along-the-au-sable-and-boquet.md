## Publisher

One weekly eCatholic bulletin, site 35001 (files at `files.ecatholic.com/35001/bulletins/YYYYMMDD.pdf`, one per Sunday, newest 27 September 2026 read on 2026-09-30), is titled "Catholics Along the Au Sable and Boquet" and its masthead names three canonical communities: "Catholic Community of Holy Name/St. Matthew/St. Margaret", "Roman Catholic Community of Keeseville" and "Catholic Community of Willsboro/Essex". It pictures and addresses six churches and gives a single Mass schedule across them. The bulletin is image-only (scanned pages, no text layer) in every issue sampled (28 September 2025 to 27 September 2026), so the pipeline will need to read it as an image.

The diocese's directory splits the same churches into three groups (Holy Name; Keeseville's two churches; Willsboro and Essex) but gives all of them the website kawcatholics.org, the phone 518-647-8225 and the Au Sable Forks mailing address, and the masthead, the website's header ("Holy Name | St. Margaret's | St. John the Baptist | Immaculate Conception | St. Joseph | St. Phillip") and the single Mass-times page confirm **one bulletin for all**: one row. The diocese's Parish Bulletins page links "kawcatholics.org/bulletins" for Au Sable Forks and Essex and an old ParishesOnline account name for Keeseville; neither is a separate live bulletin.

## Worship sites

Five churches have Mass in the 27 September 2026 issue and on the website:

- `holy-name-au-sable-forks` (14203 Route 9N): Sunday 8:30 AM, Thursday 5:00 PM, Friday 8:30 AM.
- `st-john-the-baptist-keeseville` (1803 Main St.): Saturday 4:00 PM.
- `immaculate-conception-keeseville` (1660 Front St.): Monday and Tuesday 8:30 AM (rosary 8:00), Sunday adoration.
- `st-philip-of-jesus-willsboro` (3748 Main St. in the directory and GCatholic, 3746 in the bulletin): Sunday 11:00 AM, September to June.
- `st-joseph-essex` (2891 Essex Rd.): Sunday 11:00 AM, July and August only ("summer only"; the 26 July 2026 issue lists it, the 27 September and 16 August ones do not).

Not here, and why:

- **St. Margaret's, Wilmington** (5789 NY Route 86; GCatholic 18236): pictured and addressed in every issue's masthead, but in none of the five issues read (28 Sep and 28 Dec 2025, 28 Jun, 26 Jul, 16 Aug and 27 Sep 2026) does it have a Mass, and the website's Mass Times omit it. No source says it closed: `open/st-margaret-wilmington`.
- **St. Matthew's, Black Brook** (GCatholic 18235): still in the parish's canonical name and its give page, but the church was sold and is the Black Brook General Store (`excluded/st-matthew-black-brook`).
- Thursday 10:30 AM "Mass/Communion Service" at the Champlain Valley Senior Community, Willsboro is a residence; nothing in the bulletin says it is public, so no church record was made.
