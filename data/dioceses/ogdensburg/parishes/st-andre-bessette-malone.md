## Publisher

St. André Bessette Parish, Malone, publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline
(container 06/0038, organization `st-andre-bessette-parish-12953`, LPi id 0018000000Qbz9qAAB). I read the
27 September 2026 issue (6 pages, all with text). Its masthead reads "Welcome to St. André Bessette
Roman Catholic Parish, Notre Dame and St. Helen's Churches", with the office at 12 Homestead Park and
Father Scott Seymour as pastor; the cover box lists Sunday Masses at Notre Dame (Saturday 4:00 pm,
Sunday 8:00 and 11:00 am) and at St. Helen's (Sunday 9:30 am). I also extracted the 17 issues from
7 June to 27 September 2026: every one has the same two-church masthead and a Sunday "9:30 a.m.
(SH)" Mass intention for St. Helen's, so St. Helen's has Mass through the summer as well as the
rest of the year (the 27 September cover box says "9:30 A.M. at St. Helens").

The parish is the 2014 union of four Malone parishes (St. Joseph's, Notre Dame, St. Helen's and St.
John Bosco), per the parish website's history ("On February 28, 2014, Bishop Terry LaValley issued
the decree uniting the parishes as one in the new St. André Bessette Parish, effective July 1,
2014"). The diocese's directory groups it as "St. Andre Bessette Roman Catholic Parish" with worship
sites Notre Dame Church and St. Helen's Church - Chasm Falls. No other parish shares this bulletin.

## Worship sites

Two: `notre-dame-malone` (11 Church Place) and `st-helen-chasm-falls` (755 County Highway 41).
Both appear in the current masthead and schedule. GCatholic lists four more places under Malone
and Lake Titus that were formerly part of this parish and are not in any 2026 schedule:
`excluded/st-john-bosco-malone` (sold to another church), `excluded/st-joseph-malone` (306 W Main
St., closed) and `excluded/st-mary-lake-titus` (a former St. Helen's mission, no regular Mass since
2007). The parish site's history also names St. Joseph's, Owls Head (1923), another St. Helen's
mission, whose "last regularly scheduled Masses" were in 2007; it is in neither roster, and no bulletin
from June to September 2026 mentions Owls Head, so it has no record here. The Malone bulletin also
mentions St. Patrick's Church hall (a CCBC dinner) which is Chateaugay's church, another parish.
