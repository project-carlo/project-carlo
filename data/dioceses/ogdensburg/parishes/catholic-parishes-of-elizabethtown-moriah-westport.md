## Publisher

One weekly ParishesOnline bulletin (container `04/0733`, organization "The Catholic Parishes of Elizabethtown, Moriah, & Westport" at `catholic-community-of-moriah`) serves four churches in three canonical parishes joined into one "Pastoral Care Area" (Elizabethtown, Moriah, Westport), under Fr. Joseph Giroux. The 27 September 2026 issue (read 2026-09-30) has that title as masthead and names St. Elizabeth's, All Saints, St. Patrick's and St. Philip Neri on page 1. The diocese directory lists only the Moriah pair (St. Patrick's, Port Henry and All Saints, Mineville) as "The Catholic Community of Moriah"; Elizabethtown and Westport carry the same website and phone and are in the same bulletin, so they are one row with them. I also read the Mass-schedule pages of about forty issues of the container, sampled from January 2025 to September 2026, to see which churches have Mass when (see the churches and the All Saints record).

## Worship sites

- `st-patricks-port-henry`: Saturday 4:00 pm (Labor Day to Memorial Day), Sunday 11:00 am, weekday Mass.
- `st-elizabeths-elizabethtown`: Sunday 8:00 am, Friday 8:00 am.
- `st-philip-neri-westport`: Saturday 4:00 pm, Memorial Day to Labor Day only (summer).
- `open/all-saints-mineville`: still in the masthead and on the website's church list, but no Mass at it since 25 October 2025 and none in the schedule that took effect 23 May 2026; not decided (see that record).

## Website and bulletin provider

emwparishes.org is an eCatholic site (`ecatholic:36243`, found by visit.py) whose "Weekly Bulletins" menu item goes to the ParishesOnline organization; the site itself posts no bulletin files, so `bulletin_provider` is `parishes_online` and the eCatholic site id is not a publication. The bulletin's text layer is complete on all four pages.

## Not yet confirmed

- The Elizabethtown and Westport churches are not in any diocesan "family" grouping I could read; that they are one bulletin with Moriah is from the masthead, not from a diocese list.
- Whether All Saints, Mineville will hold any Mass at all (see its open record).
