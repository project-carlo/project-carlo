## Publisher

Blessed Sacrament Parish publishes **one** weekly bulletin for three Watertown churches: St.
Anthony's, Holy Family and St. Patrick's, with Fr. Christopher C. Carrara as pastor. The 27 September
2026 issue (ParishesOnline container 06/0117, 8 pages) is headed "Blessed Sacrament Parish, Watertown,
NY" over the three churches and their addresses, and its "Mass Intentions" page has a single schedule
coded HF, SA, SP and M. **Pages 1-6 are images** (no text layer; only the two advertising pages 7-8
are text), so I read the issue as rendered pictures. The diocese's directory lists the three
churches as separate entries with the group "Blessed Sacrament Parish", the same pastor, phone
315-782-1190 and the site catholicwatertown.org. The diocese's Parish Bulletins page groups them as
"Watertown St. Anthony's and St. Patrick's" (linked to the St. Patrick's ParishesOnline
organization) and lists "Watertown Church of the Holy Family" separately, but its link is the
same organization (st-patricks-): all three churches point to one bulletin.

Weekend and weekday schedule (site's Mass Times page and page 2 of the bulletin agree): Saturday 4:00 pm
St. Patrick's and 5:15 pm Holy Family; Sunday 8:00 am St. Anthony's, 9:00 am St. Patrick's, 11:00 am
and 5:15 pm Holy Family; weekdays 7:00 am St. Anthony's and noon at Holy Family (Mon, Fri) or St.
Patrick's (Tue-Thu); a Spanish Mass on the third Sunday at 1 pm at St. Anthony's (page 1).

## Worship sites

- `st-anthony-watertown`, 850 Arsenal Street (directory st-anthony, GCatholic 18345).
- `holy-family-watertown`, 129 Winthrop Street (directory holy-family, GCatholic 18343).
- `st-patrick-watertown`, 123 South Massey Street (directory st-patrick, GCatholic 18346).

The Precious Blood Monastery chapel, 400 Pratt Street, also appears in this bulletin's schedule
(daily 11:00 am Mass, "M") and in the 2027 Mass book, and GCatholic lists it (46337). I could not
confirm that its Mass is open to the public, so it is `open/precious-blood-monastery-watertown`
rather than a church row. The parish's page 3 also mentions "one rectory, two convents, the
Greystone Building, McCarthy Hall and the parish office building", none of which is a worship site.
Our Lady of the Sacred Heart, Watertown is a different parish with its own bulletin
(`our-lady-of-the-sacred-heart-watertown`).

## Website and bulletin provider

catholicwatertown.org is the parish's eCatholic site (behind Cloudflare; `probe url` gets 403, a
browser gets through). Its Bulletins link goes to the ParishesOnline organization "St. Patrick's
Church" (st-patricks-), whose website field is a dead sawatn.org: the organization is the parish's
bulletin account under an old name. The account's issues run weekly and the 27 September masthead
names all three churches, so provider `parishes_online`, provider_id `st-patricks-`, with the
three ids of that organization. The bulletin is image-only for the editorial pages, so the pipeline
will have to read it as images.
