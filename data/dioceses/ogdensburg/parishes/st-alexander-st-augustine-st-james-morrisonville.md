## Publisher

One weekly ParishesOnline (LPi) bulletin, container 04/0726, covers three churches. Page 1 of
the 27 September 2026 issue gives a Mass-times line for each of St. Alexander (Morrisonville),
St. Augustine (Peru) and St. James (Cadyville), and page 2's Mass intentions schedule Masses
at all three for the week of 28 September to 4 October. The pastor is Rev. Christopher J.
Looby; Fr. John K. Ojuok is parochial vicar. The website, the ParishesOnline organization,
the diocese's directory (same website and phone for all three) and the diocesan paper's
May 2026 assignment list ("St. Alexander and St. Joseph in Morrisonville, St. James Church
in Cadyville and St. Augustine's Church in Peru") all treat them as one community: **one row,
three churches**.

The name "St. Alexander and St. Joseph" in the directory and the diocese is older than the
bulletin: St. Joseph's, Treadwells Mills (1349 Military Turnpike, Plattsburgh) merged with St.
Alexander's and is now the St. Joseph's Community Outreach Center, which the bulletin lists
in its ministries (page 4: "ST. JOSEPH'S COMMUNITY OUTREACH 1349 Military Turnpike"). It has no
Mass (`excluded/st-joseph-plattsburgh-west`).

## Worship sites

- `st-alexander-morrisonville` (1 Church St.): Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 7:30 AM and 10:30 AM, weekday 9:00 AM.
- `st-augustine-peru` (3035 Main St.): Saturday 4:30 PM, Sunday 9:30 AM, Tuesday and Friday 8:00 AM.
- `st-james-cadyville` (26 Church St. in the directory, 26 Church Rd. in the bulletin and GCatholic): Sunday 9:00 AM.

Not here: St. Patrick's, West Peru (GCatholic 148975, Mother Cabrini Shrine), a mission of St.
Augustine's per Wikipedia, appears in no Mass schedule in the bulletin (`open/st-patrick-oratory-west-peru`).
