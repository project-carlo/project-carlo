## Publisher

The Catholic Community of Cape Vincent, Rosiere and Chaumont publishes one self-hosted
bulletin, a two-page PDF at a fixed URL (`www.cvrchparish.org/files/bulletin.pdf`) replaced
each week. The issue read on 2026-09-30 is dated 27 September 2026, the Twenty-Sixth Sunday
in Ordinary Time. Its masthead names the community, gives one pastor (Fr. Raymond Diesbourg,
MSC) and one office (P.O. Box 288, 139 N. Kanady St., Cape Vincent, 315-654-2662), and page
2 prints the Mass intentions for all three churches under three headings. The diocese's
directory groups the same three entries under "The Roman Catholic Community of Cape Vincent,
Rosiere and Chaumont". One bulletin, one row.

## Worship sites

Three, all with public Mass now (see each church's file): St. Vincent of Paul, Cape Vincent
(`st-vincent-of-paul-cape-vincent`), St. Vincent de Paul, Rosiere
(`st-vincent-de-paul-rosiere`, Saturday 4:00 pm only through October 2026) and All Saints,
Chaumont (`all-saints-chaumont`). The directory and GCatholic list the same three and
nothing else for this community. The two Vincent churches have near-identical names: Cape
Vincent's is spelled "of Paul" by the parish and the directory, Rosiere's "de Paul".

## Website and bulletin provider

`www.cvrchparish.org` is the directory's and GCatholic's link and the diocese's bulletin
page's link. Its "Bulletin" menu item is the fixed PDF, so the bulletin is `other` with
`url:http://www.cvrchparish.org/files/bulletin.pdf` as the publication. There is no
ParishesOnline, eCatholic or Discover Mass id on the site (`probe url` and visit.py both
found none; `probe url` reports the one bulletin link). Because the file name never
changes, the pipeline gets the current issue each time; there are no dated issues to cite.
