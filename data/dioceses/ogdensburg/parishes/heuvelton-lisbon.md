## Publisher

One bulletin for St. Raphael's, Heuvelton and Saints Philip and James, Lisbon, posted by the parish
office on the diocese's own website at a fixed URL, `rcdony.org/images/SR-SSPJbulletin.pdf`, which the
diocese's Parish Bulletins page links for both Heuvelton and Lisbon. The file read on 30 September
2026 has the masthead "St. Raphael's Church, Heuvelton / Saints Philip and James Church, Lisbon / 5
Clinton Street, PO Box 377 / Heuvelton, New York 13654 / Rectory: (315) 344-2383" and is dated
September 13, 2026 (Reverend Kevin O'Brien, Pastor; Deacon Richard Van Kirk; a director of faith
formation for each church). Its Mass list is one list for both churches, and the ministries and
stewardship sections are given per church ("St. Raphael's Ministries", "Saints Philip and James
Ministries"). Both directory entries give the same phone, e-mail (heuveltonsec@rcdony.org) and
pastor.

The URL is overwritten each week, so it is not a dated, permanent link; its HTTP `Last-Modified` on
30 September was 14 September 2026, i.e. the 20 and 27 September issues had not been posted by
then (two weeks stale). A search-engine snippet of the same URL showed a 3 May 2026 issue, which
confirms the overwrite.

## Worship sites

Two: `st-raphael-heuvelton` and `sts-philip-and-james-lisbon`. The diocese directory lists each with
the pastor shared; GCatholic lists both. There is no other site in the bulletin. A Discover Mass page
for St. Raphael (`discovermass.com/church/st-raphael-heuvelton-ny`) has no bulletins ("this church
does not publish through Discover Mass now") and an undated Mass-time listing (still carrying a
COVID-19 notice).

## Website and bulletin provider

Provider `other`: a self-hosted PDF on the diocese's site. `website` is the PDF itself, because no
parish site, ParishesOnline or eCatholic page exists for either church (the directory entries have no
website; `probe url` on the PDF says NO-PARISH-WORDS because it is not HTML). The publication id is
`url:` the PDF address. The PDF has a text layer; the first page holds the masthead and the Mass list.

Recipe `redirected-pdf`, pinned after `fetch:check` on 2026-10-17 picked the PDF itself, still the 13 September 2026 issue (Last-Modified 14 September; the diocese's bulletin page still links it for both towns) (`only-link`).
