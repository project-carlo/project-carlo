## Publisher

One weekly bulletin for the cathedral and Notre Dame, published through ParishesOnline (org
`st-mary-cathedral-and-notre-dame-church`, LPi `0018000000Qc2baAAB`, container 06/0092). The 27
September 2026 issue (page 1) names "St. Mary's Cathedral, 401 Hamilton Street" and "Notre Dame Church,
119 Ford Avenue" side by side under the date, gives the parish office (125 Ford Avenue) and one weekend
schedule for both churches: "4:00 p.m., Vigil Mass (SMC) / Sunday 8:00 a.m. (SMC); 9:30 a.m. (ND); 11:00
a.m. (SMC)", weekdays "Monday thru Friday 6:45 a.m. at Notre Dame Church and Noon at Saint Mary's
Cathedral". Page 2's intentions use the same (SM) and (ND) codes. The 4 October 2026 issue
(`20261004B.pdf`, posted by 30 September) is the same. The parish's own site says "The two churches
were canonically joined and incorporated as St. Mary's Cathedral Parish on July 1, 2017".

## Worship sites

Two: `st-marys-cathedral-ogdensburg` and `notre-dame-ogdensburg`. The bulletin also mentions St.
Mary's Academy ("SMDA", 315 Gate St., religious education classes), the Deacon Winter Chapel and
Brzana Hall inside the cathedral complex (adoration, meetings) and the Knights of Columbus hall;
none holds public Mass of its own. The diocese directory lists both churches under one entry ("other
sites": each names the other). GCatholic has Notre Dame (#18318, in the diocese roster) and the
cathedral (#2086, not in the roster list; it is filed as a special church).

## Website and bulletin provider

ParishesOnline is the bulletin; the parish site ogdensburgcatholics.org (eCatholic site 13689, behind
Cloudflare: `probe url` HTTP4XX, loaded by visit.py) embeds it on its Bulletin page ("Current and past
bulletins", linking the ParishesOnline org) and has no bulletin files of its own on files.ecatholic.com
(no Sunday issue found for 13689, so that id is not recorded as a publication). The 27 September issue
has a text layer. Page 3 announces that "Due to the discontinuation of the Publishers software ... the
bulletin will have a different format and appearance": expect the layout to change.
