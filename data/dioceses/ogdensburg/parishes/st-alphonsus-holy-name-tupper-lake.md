## Publisher

The Tupper Lake, Long Lake and Newcomb churches publish **one** bulletin on eCatholic, site
id 15094 (`files.ecatholic.com/15094/bulletins/<YYYYMMDD>.pdf`, dated by the Sunday). The 13
September 2026 issue (4 pages) has one staff list, one set of trustees per parish, the three
church addresses and one block of Mass intentions with a section for each town; page 1 is an
image of Liguori's "Good News!" insert (Sunday 13 September 2026), which is why the masthead
is only on page 2. The parish website's banner names "St. Alphonsus Holy Name of Jesus, 48
Wawbeek Ave, Tupper Lake", "St. Henry's Parish, Long Lake" and "St. Therese Parish Newcomb"
together. The diocese's directory lists the three churches as three entries (only Tupper
Lake has a `group`, "St. Alphonsus - Holy Name of Jesus Parish") but gives all three the same
website and phone (518-359-3405), and the bulletin confirms one publisher. It is one row.
The 20 and 27 September 2026 files were not posted on 2026-09-30 (403), so the read issue is
the newest on the parish's own page.

## Worship sites

Four with public Mass: St. Alphonsus, Tupper Lake (`st-alphonsus-tupper-lake`), St. Henry's,
Long Lake (`st-henrys-long-lake`), St. Therese, Newcomb (`st-therese-newcomb`) and the Holy Ghost
Chapel, 40 Marion St., Tupper Lake (`holy-ghost-chapel-tupper-lake`, in no roster: weekday
8:00 AM Mass Monday to Saturday, "HGC" in the bulletin's intentions). Ruled out:

- **Holy Name of Jesus, 115 Main St., Tupper Lake** (GCatholic 18341): merged into St.
  Alphonsus in 2011 and sold (`excluded/holy-name-tupper-lake`). The current name of the parish
  keeps "Holy Name" but the bulletin schedules no Mass there.
- **St. William's, Long Point, Raquette Lake** (GCatholic 38887, "Former Church", listed under
  Long Lake): non-denominational site, no Mass (`excluded/st-william-long-point-raquette-lake`).

The Mass Times page also lists "Tuesday 11:00 AM Mercy" (bulletin: "Tuesday 09/15 11:00am
Mercy People of the Parish"). It appears to be Mass at the Mercy nursing home in Tupper Lake; no
address or public-access statement was found, so it is not recorded as a church (see Not yet
confirmed).

## Website and bulletin provider

`tupperlakecatholics.org` is the directory's website for all three churches; `probe url`
answers 403 (Cloudflare) and the browser reads it (`ecatholic:15094` embedded on the home and
bulletin pages). Provider eCatholic, so the website has to be the eCatholic-hosted site:
it is. Page 1 of each issue read is an image (the Liguori insert); the rest has a text layer.
