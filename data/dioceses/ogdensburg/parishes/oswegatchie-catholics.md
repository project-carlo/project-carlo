## Publisher

The Catholic Community of the Oswegatchie publishes one weekly bulletin for Gouverneur, Edwards and Star
Lake. Page 1 of the 27 September 2026 issue (eCatholic site 25733) is headed "The Catholic Community
of the Oswegatchie", names Fr. James Seymour as pastor, gives two parish offices (164 E. Main Street,
Gouverneur; 1046 Oswegatchie Trail Road, Star Lake) and lists three churches with their Mass times:
St. James (Saturday 4:00pm, Sunday 11:00am), St. Hubert (Sunday 9:00am, Wednesday 8:30am) and Sacred
Heart, Edwards ("Saturday Only 5:30pm"). Page 2 is one week of Mass intentions across all three. The
parish website oswegatchiecatholics.org (same eCatholic site, title "Catholic Communities of the
Oswegatchie - Gouverneur, NY") lists the same three sites.

The diocese directory files Gouverneur (St. James, with Edwards as a second entry on the same
rcdony.org page) and Star Lake under separate pages but gives both the same website. No family or
group is named there. The bulletin shows they are one publisher.

## Worship sites

Three: `st-james-gouverneur`, `sacred-heart-edwards` (a "worship site" on the parish site; Saturday
5:30pm only) and `st-hubert-star-lake`. Nothing else in the bulletin or on the site: a "Clifton-Fine"
thank-you on page 1 refers to the area around Star Lake, not a church.
