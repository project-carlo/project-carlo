## Publisher

Four churches (Constableville, Lyons Falls, Port Leyden, West Leyden) share one pastor (Rev.
Daniel L. Chapin), one office (Port Leyden, 315-348-6104) and one bulletin, a two-page PDF
posted weekly on the parish's Weebly site. The 13 September 2026 issue names all four
churches on page 1 and gives one Mass list on page 2 with codes WL, LF, PL and Cville. The
directory lists each church separately (no group) but gives the same website, phone and pastor
to all four, and the diocese's bulletin page links this bulletin for three of them and
"None" for Constableville. One bulletin, one row.

## Worship sites

Four, each with its own weekend Mass: St. Mary's Nativity, West Leyden (Saturday 4 pm); St.
John's, Lyons Falls (Saturday 6 pm); St. Martin's, Port Leyden (Sunday 8:15 am); St. Mary's,
Constableville (Sunday 10 am). Weekday Masses are at 9 am, Monday, Wednesday and Friday in
the "St. Martin's Rectory Chapel" (Port Leyden) and Tuesday and Thursday at St. Mary's,
Constableville; the rectory chapel is a room of the Port Leyden rectory and is not recorded as a
separate church. The site's YouTube embed mentions "Sts. Peter & Paul Oratory" (Fish Creek), a
one-off Mass on 28 June 2026: see `open/sts-peter-and-paul-oratory-fish-creek`.

## Website and bulletin provider

`catholicparishesofslc.weebly.com` is the directory's link for all four churches and the
diocese's bulletin page's link. It hosts the bulletins itself as PDFs under
`/uploads/1/3/1/4/131484079/` (`probe url` and visit.py found no ParishesOnline, eCatholic or
Discover Mass id), so the provider is `other` and the publication `url:` of the bulletins page,
which lists one PDF per Sunday from July 2024. File names are irregular (bulletin_sept_13.pdf,
sept_6.pdf, august_23__2026.pdf), so the pipeline needs the page, not a pattern.
