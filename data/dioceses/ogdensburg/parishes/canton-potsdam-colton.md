## Publisher

One weekly bulletin, published for all three churches. The 27 September 2026 issue (ParishesOnline
container 04/0642, page 1) lists St. Mary’s Church - 20 Lawrence Ave, Potsdam; St. Patrick’s Church -
4897 State Highway 56, Colton; and St. Mary’s Church - 66 Court Street, Canton, above the heading
"THE PARISH COMMUNITIES OF ... Central St. Lawrence County" and the two sites
"cantoncatholics.com • stmarystpatrick.net". Page 2 is a single Mass and intention list for the week
covering all three churches, and page 3 (adoration and confession times, Knights of Columbus, men’s
meeting) speaks for "all three of our parishes". The pastor’s column is written from Potsdam
(Fr. Bryan Stitt is pastor of Canton, Potsdam and Colton in the diocesan directory). The 4 October
2026 issue (`20261004B.pdf`, already posted on 30 September) has the same layout.

The same issue is carried in four places, all one bulletin: ParishesOnline org
`st-marys-potsdam-st-patricks-colton-st-marys-canton` (Canton’s org, LPi `0018000000Qx7PRAAZ`),
ParishesOnline org `st-marys-church-13676` (Potsdam’s old org, LPi `0018000000Qc2bnAAB`; both serve
container 04/0642), Canton’s eCatholic site 11112 (a copy of the PDF under
`files.ecatholic.com/11112/bulletins/YYYYMMDD.pdf`), and the two parish websites. eCatholic site 3087
(stmarystpatrick.net) has no bulletin files. I recorded all the ids, so no other row can claim them.

## Worship sites

Three, all in the bulletin schedule: St. Mary, Canton (Sunday 9:00am and 5:00pm; weekday Masses
Monday/Wednesday/Friday/Saturday 8:15am and Tuesday/Thursday 5:15pm per cantoncatholics.com),
St. Mary, Potsdam (Sunday 9:00am Latin Mass and 11:00am; weekdays Tuesday 12:10pm, Thursday 5:15pm,
Friday 8:00am), and St. Patrick, Colton (Saturday vigil 5:00pm in summer time, 4:00pm from All
Saints to Easter; Monday and Wednesday 8:00am). Not recorded as churches:

- Canton’s site also lists monthly Masses at United Helpers (205 Canton-Madrid Rd) and Partridge
  Knoll senior living (30 Sullivan Dr). These are residential facilities, not public Mass, and no
  roster lists them.
- Canton’s Divine Mercy Chapel (confessions, morning prayer, open 24/7) is part of the St. Mary
  complex at Court Street; not a separate site.
- The bulletin has a line "Mass out of parish (no Mass in Canton)" for one Wednesday: a priest away,
  not a place.
