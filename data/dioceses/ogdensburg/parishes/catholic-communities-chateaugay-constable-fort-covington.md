## Publisher

One weekly bulletin on eCatholic (site id 18168; issues at
files.ecatholic.com/18168/bulletins/YYYYMMDD.pdf; newest 27 September 2026, read 2026-09-30). The
27 September page 1 masthead reads "Welcome to The Catholic Communities of St. Patrick's * St.
Francis of Assisi * St. Mary's of the Fort", with one pastor (Fr. Pasala), one central office
(5 Poplar St., Constable) and trustees listed for Chateaugay, Fort Covington and Constable. Page 2
gives one Mass schedule for the three churches, and the parish's Mass Times page lists the same
three.

The diocese's directory splits these into canonical parishes: "Catholic Community of Burke and
Chateaugay" (St. Patrick's, Chateaugay), "Catholic Community of Constable, Westville and Trout
River" (St. Francis of Assisi, Constable) and, with no group, St. Mary's of the Fort, Fort Covington.
All three entries carry the same website (ccfcatholics.com), phone (518-483-0486) and pastor.
Each canonical parish once had its own ParishesOnline account (`catholic-community-of-burke-and-
chateaugay`, `catholic-community-of-constable-westville-and-trout-river`), and each account's
website domain (ccbcparish.org, cwtrcatholics.org) is now DEAD-DNS and both accounts publish
nothing (`probe po`). Their old channels are silent and the eCatholic bulletin names all three, so
they are one row.

## Worship sites

Three churches hold Mass now: `st-patrick-chateaugay` (Saturday 4:00 pm, Thursday 8:30 am),
`st-francis-of-assisi-constable` (Sunday 8:30 am, Wednesday 8:30 am) and
`st-marys-of-the-fort-fort-covington` (Sunday 10:30 am, Tuesday 8:30 am, First Saturday 8:30 am).
The 27 September bulletin lists the weekday services as "Communion Service" rather than Mass, while
the Mass Times page calls them weekday Masses; the weekend Masses are Masses either way.

The rosters list six more places that belong to this area and hold no Mass in any 2026 bulletin
read (14 issues from 7 June to 27 September, 13 of them extracted in full, were searched for
Burke, St. George, Westville, Trout, Bridget and Bombay; those words appear only in fund-raising
notices, cemetery-care notices and the Burke thrift store's address, never in a Mass schedule):
- `open/st-george-burke` (797 Depot St., Burke): no Mass listed, a thrift store in the building,
  but no source states it is closed.
- `open/our-lady-of-fatima-westville` (4328 State Route 37): same situation.
- `excluded/st-joseph-bombay`: closed, final Mass 29 December 2019 (North Country Catholic).
- `excluded/st-bridget-trout-river`: "former oratory, currently up for sale" in the parishes' own 2018
  pastoral plan.
- `excluded/st-patrick-hogansburg`: "Sold" in the same plan.
- `excluded/st-regis-mission-akwesasne`: the Mohawk mission church at Akwesasne is a parish of the Diocese
  of Valleyfield (Quebec).

The 2018 pastoral plan (files.ecatholic.com/18168/documents/2018/2/Pastoral Plan form fillable Final
4th 6.pdf) says that with one priest "There will be three churches open to serve the faithful: They
are: St. Patrick's in Chateaugay, St. Mary's of the Fort, and St. Francis of Assisi in Constable",
which is the present schedule.

## Website and bulletin provider

`website` is https://ccfcatholics.com/, which every directory entry and the bulletin itself give.
It is an eCatholic site behind Cloudflare: `probe url` says HTTP4XX, and `visit.py --bulletin` loaded
it on the third try and reported `ecatholic:18168`. The /bulletins page lists 2026's issues from 4 January
and `issues.py ecatholic` reads them without a browser. GCatholic's websites for these churches
(ccbcparish.org, cccwtr.grainofwheat.net) are dead or a redirect to an unrelated site
(cccwtr.grainofwheat.net now redirects to facts.net).
