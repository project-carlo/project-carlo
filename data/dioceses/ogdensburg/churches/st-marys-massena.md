## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (container 06/0114, page 4) lists Sunday 8:00 am and 11:00 am and Tuesday 5:30 pm Masses at St. Mary's (SM), and the parish's Mass Times page agrees. Watertown Daily Times, 2 Sep 2026: from 1 November the 8 am Sunday Mass at St. Mary's is dropped and the 11 am continues, so the church keeps public Mass.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Mary", 9 Sycamore St.), the diocesan directory ("St. Mary's Church", 9 Sycamore Street, worship site of Sacred Heart) and the parish site footer ("St. Mary's, 9 Sycamore St., Massena") agree, and OSM has a place of worship on Sycamore Street (node 357641646, 44.93778, -74.88972) named "Saint Mary's And Saint Joseph's Church", the name from before the St. Mary and St. Joseph parishes joined; that is this building, not the closed St. Joseph on Bayley Rd. Both flags are true; the name follows the bulletin and the directory. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (44.93781, -74.88931), 30 m from the OSM node.
