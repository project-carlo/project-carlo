## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 2) lists Masses "(Canton)" on Sunday 9:00 am and
5:00 pm, and on weekdays Monday and Wednesday 8:15 am, Tuesday and Thursday 5:15 pm; the parish site
lists the same and adds Friday and Saturday 8:15 am. Canton’s Divine Mercy Chapel, used for morning
prayer and confession, is part of this complex.

## Name and address

Name: "St. Mary's Church" in the diocese directory, the bulletin and OSM ("Saint Mary's Roman Catholic
Church"), "St. Mary" in GCatholic, "The Roman Catholic Church of Saint Mary" on the site: flag true.

Address: two numbers on Court Street. **66 Court Street** is in the diocese directory, the bulletin
masthead, the ParishesOnline org and OSM. **68 Court St.** is in GCatholic, on the parish site’s map
embed and in a bulletin item about a church tour. The row follows 66 (four sources, one of them the
diocese). The flag stays false because the parish itself uses both. Coordinates are the OSM church
building (`osm-node`); GCatholic’s Plus Code (44.60094, -75.16956) is on the same building, about 60 m
away.
