## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly and most days: Saturday 4:00 pm (anticipated), Sunday 10:00 am, and on the 27
September 2026 bulletin's page 2 a 9:30 am Monday Communion service and Masses at 9:30 am
Tuesday, Thursday and Friday (Friday 2 October is the First Friday school Mass). The parish site's Mass Schedule
page gives the same weekend times.

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic "St. James Minor", directory "St. James Minor Church", OSM "Saint James Roman
Catholic Church", bulletin "St. James": name_verified true ("Minor" for the minor basilica
title is used by the directory and GCatholic, not the bulletin).

Address: the directory and OSM both say 327 West Street, Carthage 13619, and the bulletin
prints "St. James Rectory 327 West St." So 327 West Street; address_verified true.
**GCatholic's 317 West St. is a different building:** the same bulletin gives 317 West St. for
the Augustinian Academy (the school). The bulletin's cover caption puts the church on "State
Street"; the church presumably fronts State Street and shares the West Street lot with the
rectory, which I have not checked. Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code 87M6X9HR+9P, about 25 m
from the OSM way.
