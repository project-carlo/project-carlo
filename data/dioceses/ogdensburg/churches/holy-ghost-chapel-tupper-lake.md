## Holds Mass

Yes: weekday Mass at 8:00 AM Monday to Saturday in the chapel of the Holy Ghost Parish Center (the former Holy Ghost Academy). The 13 September
2026 bulletin (page 3) has "HGC" intentions each morning from 14 to 19 September, and the
parish's Mass Times page lists "8:00 AM Holy Ghost Chapel" every day but Sunday. The Sunday
Masses are at St. Alphonsus. It is a chapel inside the parish centre, not a separate parish
church; it counts because it holds public daily Mass. It appears in neither GCatholic nor the
directory.

## Name and address

The parish office and religious education centre is at 40 Marion St. (bulletin page 2), the
address OSM gives its "Holy Ghost Parish Center". The chapel's own street address is not given;
the parish centre's is used, and address_verified is true only for the building. The name
"Holy Ghost Chapel" is the parish site's and the bulletin's ("Holy Ghost Chapel, 40 Marion
St."), with "HGC" in the intentions; no second source names it, so `name_verified` is false.
Coordinates are Nominatim's for the centre (OSM way), not a Plus Code.
