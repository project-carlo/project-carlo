Probably weekly, **inferred**: the 13 September 2026 Heuvelton/Lisbon bulletin lists Sunday 9:00 am
Mass and the church has its own ministries and stewardship blocks, but the Mass list does not name
the church for any Mass. Discover Mass puts Heuvelton at Saturday 4:30 pm and Sunday 10:30 am, which
leaves the 9:00 am Sunday Mass for Lisbon. Nothing I could read states it. The diocese and GCatholic
both list the church as a parish church, and I found no closure news; search hits for "Sts. Philip and
James Lisbon" returned only the directory and GCatholic. A bulletin that says "9:00am, Ss. Philip and
James" would settle it.

keeps the diocese's street address and GCatholic's coordinates, which disagree with each other, and the

there. GCatholic's phone for the church (315-393-3796) differs from the directory's.
