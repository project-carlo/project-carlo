## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 Eastern Lake Ontario Catholics bulletin (page 2, key "QH = Queen of
Heaven in Henderson") lists Sunday 8:30 am and Wednesday 8:30 am (28 September to 2 October week),
and the same times for 3-4 October. Confessions 8:00-8:20 am on Sundays (parish site). The parish
site's "Normal Mass Schedule" agrees. Nothing says the Sunday Mass is seasonal; it is in the
September schedule.
