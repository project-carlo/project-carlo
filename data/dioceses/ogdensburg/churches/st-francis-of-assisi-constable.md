## Holds Mass

Yes, the parishes' 8:30 am Sunday Mass. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 2) has "(SF) 8:30AM"
Sunday Mass and the parish's Mass Times page says "Sunday Mass: 8:30AM at St. Francis of Assisi,
Constable". Wednesday 8:30 is a Communion Service in the bulletin.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Francis of Assisi", 5 Poplar St.), the diocese directory ("St. Francis of Assisi Church",
5 Poplar Street, Constable 12926) and the parish contact page ("St. Francis of Assisi ... 5 Poplar
Street, Constable, NY 12926") agree, so both flags are true. The same address is the parishes' central
office. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87P7WPH4+WV). OSM has only a "Saint Francis of Assisi
Cemetery" (way 1083978753) nearby, not the church building.
