## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 Eastern Lake Ontario Catholics bulletin (page 2, key "IC = Immac.
Conception in Brownville") lists Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am, Monday 8:30 am and Wednesday
5:30 pm, repeated for 3-4 October. The bulletin also announces a Rosary Rally at the statue of Mary
outside "Immaculate Conception Church" on 10 October. The parish site agrees.

## Name and address

**Disagreement on the street number.** The bulletin masthead, the diocese's directory ("Immaculate
Conception Church", 214 West Main Street, Brownville 13615) and an OpenStreetMap address point say
214 West Main Street. GCatholic says "119 W Main St.", but the bulletin's own contact block gives
119 West Main St. (PO Box 99) as the Brownville parish *office*, ParishesOnline puts the organization
at 119 W. Main St. too, and GCatholic's Plus Code point (44.0021875, -75.9865625) is 20 m from OSM's
214 point and about 200 m from 119. So the church is very probably at 214, GCatholic having copied the
office address; I followed the bulletin and the directory, kept GCatholic's building-precise
coordinates, and left `address_verified` false because one listed source disagrees. `name_verified`
is true (bulletin, directory, GCatholic and the parish site all say Immaculate Conception).
Nominatim's ZIP for the 214 point is 13634 (Dexter's); every other source says 13615.
