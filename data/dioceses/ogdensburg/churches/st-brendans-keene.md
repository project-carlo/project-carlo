## Holds Mass

Yes, one weekend Mass. The 20 September 2026 bulletin (page 2, text layer) and the 27
September issue (page 2, image) list Saturday 4:00 PM at St. Brendan's, Keene, with the rosary
and reconciliation at 3:30 PM; the same page lists Saturday 3 October. There is no Sunday Mass
in Keene; the pastor's Sunday Masses are at St. Agnes, Lake Placid. Eucharistic adoration
was held in Keene on Saturday 26 September 3:00 to 3:50 PM (Priesthood Sunday notice, 20
September issue).

## Name and address

The building is at **25 Church Street**: the directory, the bulletin and OpenStreetMap's
address point agree, so both flags are true. GCatholic says **17 Church St.**, a mistake: its
Plus Code lies on the 25 Church Street point, 55 m from OSM's 17 Church Street. The name
follows the bulletin and directory ("St. Brendan's Church"; GCatholic has "St. Brendan").
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
