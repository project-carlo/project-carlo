## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Sunday 8:00 am and Friday 8:00 am (followed by a Holy Hour with Adoration and Confessions at 8:30) in the 27 September 2026 bulletin, page 2; the parish's Mass Times page gives Sunday 8:00 am. Read 2026-09-30.

## Name and address

The name agrees across all sources. The address does not, so `address_verified` is false:

- Diocese directory: 7482 Court Street. The bulletin masthead gave 7482 Court Street in October 2025 and 7478 Court Street in September 2026.
- GCatholic: 8434 NYS Route 9N. OpenStreetMap's building named "St. Elizabeth Church" is numbered 8434 US Highway 9. Both look like the county's route-based numbering for the same building on the highway that is called Court Street in the village.
- The OSM house numbered 7482 Court Street is a different building about 170 m away.

I used the diocese's and bulletin's 7482 Court Street (the postal address the parish prints) and the OSM church building's coordinates. GCatholic's Plus Code point (44.21331, -73.59756) falls on a credit union at 7519 Court Street and was not used.
