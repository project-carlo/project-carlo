## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 2) has Sunday 4 October 9:45 am and Thursday and Friday
8:00 am Masses at St. Ann, and page 1 announces Friday Adoration at the church after Mass. The parish
website's Mass Times list Sunday 9:45 AM. (The North Country Catholic's Franklin events page still lists a
first-Saturday devotion there "Following the 4:30 anticipated Mass"; that is an old listing, and the
bulletin and the parish site show no Saturday Mass at St. Ann's.) Page 2 also notes heating-system work at St. Ann's
starting 5 October that "should take one week", which may move Masses for that week only.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Ann", 77 N Main St., St. Regis Falls 12980), the diocese directory ("St. Ann's Church",
77 North Main Street) and the bulletin ("St. Ann's Church, St. Regis Falls, NY") agree on name and
town, and the rosters agree on the address, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus
Code (87P7MFH3+4W).
