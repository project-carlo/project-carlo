## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 2, Mass intentions) has Saturday 3 October 4:00 pm at
St. Mary's and 9:00 am weekday Masses there on Monday, Wednesday and the First Saturday; the
parish website's Mass Times (read 2026-09-30) give "Saturday: 4 PM St. Mary's Church, Brushton".
It is the parish's only Saturday vigil.
