## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (Clayton / Alexandria community, ParishesOnline
06/0098) lists Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 9:30 am at St. Cyril's on page 1, and on page 2
Sunday 9:30 (SC), Monday to Friday 8:00 (SC) and the Saturday 4:00 (SC) vigil. Confessions
Saturdays 3:15-3:45 pm.

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic "St. Cyril", the directory "St. Cyril Church", OSM "Saint Cyril's Roman
Catholic Church" and the bulletin "St. Cyril’s" agree (name_verified true).

Address: **the sources disagree, so address_verified stays false.** The diocese directory
says 28 Walton Street, Alexandria Bay 13607; GCatholic says 17 Rock St. OSM puts the church on
Walton Street (no number) at 44.33664, -75.91607, and 2 Rock Street in the same block is the
old Methodist church, so 17 Rock St. looks like a neighbouring or older address. I followed
the directory (28 Walton Street), which agrees with OSM on the street. The GCatholic Plus
Code point (44.33681, -75.91631) is about 30 m from the OSM building, so the coordinates,
taken from the Plus Code, are on the church. What would settle it: a street number on the
church or a county parcel lookup.
