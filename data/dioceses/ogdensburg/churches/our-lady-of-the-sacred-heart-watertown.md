## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 OLSH bulletin (page 3, "Mass Schedule and Intentions") lists Saturday 4:00 pm,
Sunday 9:45 am and Mass at 8:15 am each weekday from Monday 28 September to Friday 2 October, with Saturday
3 October 4:00 pm and Sunday 4 October 9:45 am. The River Catholics bulletin of the same date also
lists "O.L.S.H. 4:00 PM (Sat. Ant.)" and "O.L.S.H. 9:45 AM" for Sunday. Confession Saturday 2:45-3:30 pm.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead ("The Church of Our Lady of the Sacred Heart, 320 W. Lynde St., Watertown, NY 13601"),
the diocese's directory (same name, 320 West Lynde Street), GCatholic ("Our Lady of the Sacred Heart",
320 Lynde St. W) and OpenStreetMap (address point and a church node) agree, so both flags are true. The
name follows the shortened form used in the schedule and by GCatholic. Coordinates are GCatholic's
Plus Code (87M6X3JQ+RV), within 10 m of OSM's address point.
