## Holds Mass

Yes, the parish's main church. The 27 September 2026 bulletin's cover box gives Saturday 4:00 pm and
Sunday 8:00 and 11:00 am Masses at Notre Dame, and page 2 lists weekday Masses coded (ND) at 7:00 am
and 12:05 pm; the parish website's Mass Times agree.

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic ("Notre Dame"), the directory ("Notre Dame Church") and the bulletin ("Notre Dame") agree,
so `name_verified` is true. Address: the directory says "11 Church Place, Malone 12953", GCatholic has the
street only ("Church Place") and ParishesOnline's organization record says "11 Church St."; the
number 11 is on two sources and the street name differs (Place vs St.), so `address_verified` is false
until OSM or the parish confirms the street type. The parish office and rectory are at 12 Homestead
Park, not a worship site. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87P7VP26+2W, 44.85006, -74.28769),
which matches ParishesOnline's point (44.84944, -74.28702) within about 90 m.
