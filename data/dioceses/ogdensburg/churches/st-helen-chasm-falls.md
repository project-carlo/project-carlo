## Holds Mass

Yes, one Sunday Mass a week at 9:30 am. The 27 September 2026 bulletin's cover box says "Sunday ...
9:30 A.M. at St. Helens" and page 2 has the Sunday 9:30 a.m. (SH) intention; the same is in every issue
read from 7 June to 27 September 2026, so it is not a summer-only Mass. The parish website says "Sunday
St. Helen's Church: 9:30 am" and confession before Mass on the first Sunday of the month.

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic ("St. Helen"), the directory ("St. Helen's Church") and the bulletin ("St. Helens" /
"St. Helen's Churches") agree, so `name_verified` is true. Address: GCatholic gives only "Chasm Falls,
NY 12953" (no street, no coordinates); the directory gives "755 County Highway 41, Chasm Falls 12953".
OpenStreetMap has a way "Saint Helens Church" on Fayette Road, Chasm Falls; whether Fayette Road is
also County Highway 41 is unchecked, so `address_verified` is false. Coordinates are that OSM way's
point (Nominatim). GCatholic's other entry for the same area, "St. Mary, 753 County Route 41, Lake
Titus" (`excluded/st-mary-lake-titus`), has an address two numbers from this church's and no
coordinates; it is treated as a different, closed mission chapel.
