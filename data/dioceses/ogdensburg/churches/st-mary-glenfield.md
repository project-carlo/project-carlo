## Holds Mass

Yes, **seasonally**: Saturday 5:30 pm. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 1 schedule and
page 2 "St. Mary's News") says the church closes for the winter after the 10 October Mass and
reopens in the spring; the 9 August issue lists the same Saturday 5:30 pm Mass. Say "summer
only" (roughly spring to Columbus Day). The parish's Sunday Masses are at St. Peter's and St.
Hedwig's, not here. Because the winter closure begins 11 October 2026, a reader after that
date should expect no Mass here until spring.
