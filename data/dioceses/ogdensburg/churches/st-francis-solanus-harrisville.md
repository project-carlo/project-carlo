## Holds Mass

Yes, on Sundays: the 27 September 2026 bulletin lists a Sunday Mass intention for 27 September and 4 October (and "Coffee hour ... after Mass" on 4 October), so Sunday Mass is current. The bulletin prints no time; Discover Mass (undated) says Sunday 10:30 am. No weekday Mass is listed.

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic "St. Francis Solanus", directory "St. Francis Solanus Church", bulletin letterhead and Discover Mass: name_verified true.

Address: **the sources disagree, so address_verified is false.** The diocese directory, the diocese's own page and Discover Mass say 14355 Maple Street, Harrisville 13648; GCatholic and the bulletin letterhead say 14361 Maple Street, which the diocese's page gives as the mailing address (with P.O. Box 208). They may be the church and the adjacent rectory, or one building under two numbers; no source says which. I followed the directory (14355). Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code 87P65M2H+VH.
