## Holds Mass

Yes. The tri-parish site's Mass Times page (read 2026-09-30) lists Sunday 8:30 AM and Tuesday and Thursday 8:30 AM at St. Patrick's, Brasher Falls, plus Wednesday and Friday adoration in its chapel. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (scanned page 1, read as an image) lists Sunday 4 October 8:30 am, Tuesday 29 September and Thursday 1 October 8:30 am Masses there. It is also the former parish of the merged St. Lawrence, North Lawrence (`excluded/st-lawrence-north-lawrence.md`).

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic ("St. Patrick"), the directory ("St. Patrick's Church"), the parish site and the bulletin all say St. Patrick's. Address: the directory, the parish site's contact page and the bulletin all say **834 State Highway 11C**; GCatholic says **836 State Hwy. 11C**. Three sources including the parish's own agree on 834, so this record uses 834 and both flags are true, but the GCatholic number is recorded as a disagreement (probably a transcription slip; the building is a single church on Route 11C). Nominatim/OSM found no entry for the church, so coordinates are left null for `pnpm geocode`; GCatholic gives no Plus Code for it either.
