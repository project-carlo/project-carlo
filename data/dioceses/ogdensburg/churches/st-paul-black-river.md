## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 River Catholics bulletin lists Sunday 11:30 am at St. Paul's (page 1
schedule; page 2 intentions for 27 September and 4 October), and the OLSH bulletin lists the same.
It is the only Mass at St. Paul's that week; weekday Masses are at OLSH or St. Mary's. On 27
September at 2:30 pm the Bishop celebrated the diocesan Blue Mass here (OLSH bulletin, page 3); the
125th anniversary Mass is Sunday 8 November at 11:30 am. Confessions first Sunday of the month
11:05-11:25 am.

## Name and address

**Disagreement on the street number.** The diocese's directory, GCatholic, OpenStreetMap, the Eastern Lake
Ontario Catholics bulletin and the heading of the OLSH bulletin's anniversary notice say 208 LeRay Street;
the parish's own contact page, its own Blue Mass notice and the OLSH bulletin's Blue Mass notice say 210
LeRay Street. OSM's address nodes for 208 and 210 are 60 m apart and its
church node and GCatholic's Plus Code are nearest 208. I used 208, kept GCatholic's coordinates and left
`address_verified` false. `name_verified` is true (bulletin, directory, GCatholic, OSM).
