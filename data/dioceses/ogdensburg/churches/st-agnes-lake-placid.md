## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 issue of the St. Agnes & St. Brendan's bulletin (page 2, image)
lists Saturday 5:30 PM (with reconciliation after Mass), Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 AM, and weekday
Masses Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday 8:30 AM and Tuesday 5:30 PM, with Tuesday
adoration 10:00 AM to 5:15 PM. The 20 September issue (text layer) lists the same pattern for
the week of 19-27 September.

## Name and address

The church is at **2338 Saranac Avenue**. The directory, the bulletin's Mass-intentions
heading ("ST. AGNES CHURCH 2338 Saranac Ave") and OpenStreetMap ("Saint Agnes Catholic
Church", 2338 Saranac Avenue) agree, and GCatholic's Plus Code is on that building, so both
flags are true. GCatholic's street address, **169 Hillcrest Ave.**, is the parish office and
rectory (bulletin cover and the Lake Placid News religious directory both give "169 Hillcrest"
as the parish office), one block behind the church; it is not a worship site. The bulletin
also names St. Agnes School at 2322 Saranac Ave (not a worship site). The name follows the
bulletin and directory ("St. Agnes Church"). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
