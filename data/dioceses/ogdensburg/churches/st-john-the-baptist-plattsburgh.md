## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday anticipated Mass 5:15 PM, Sunday 8:30 AM and weekday Mass at 8:00 AM on Monday, Wednesday and Friday; page 3 gives the same times for 3 and 4 October. The parish website's Mass Times block (read 2026-09-30) agrees ("5:15 PM at St. John's", "8:30 AM at St. John's", "M W F 8:00 AM at St. John's"), and adds Wednesday to Friday adoration at St. John's 9 AM to 9 PM.

## Name and address

Bulletin ("St. John's Church - 18 Broad St."), GCatholic ("St. John the Baptist", 18 Broad St.), the directory ("St. John the Baptist", 18 Broad Street) and OpenStreetMap (18 Broad Street, "Saint John the Baptist" and "Saint John's Roman Catholic Church") agree on the building and its number, so both flags are true; the parish's own short name is "St. John's" and the dedication St. John the Baptist.
