## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 issue of the parish bulletin (page 4, image) lists Saturday 4:30 PM
(anticipated) and Sunday 10:00 AM at St. Bernard's, Wednesday and Friday 8:30 AM, and Tuesday
and Thursday 12:00 PM, and gives intentions for each Mass from 26 September to 4 October.

Street, Saranac Lake 12983, so both flags are true. The name follows the directory and the
bulletin ("St. Bernard's Church"; GCatholic has "St. Bernard"; the parish's formal name is St.
Bernard of Montjoux). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 70 m from OSM's address point
on the same large parcel.
