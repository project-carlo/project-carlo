## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 issue of the Eastern Lake Ontario Catholics bulletin (page 2, "Liturgy
Schedule", key "SC= St. Cecilia's in Adams") lists Saturday 4:30 pm, Sunday 11:00 am, and weekday
Mass at 8:30 am on Tuesdays and Fridays (28 September to 2 October week: Tue 8:30 SC, Fri 8:30 SC);
the same times repeat for 3-4 October. The parish site's "Normal Mass Schedule" shows the same.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead ("St. Cecilia's, 17 Grove Street, Adams, NY 13605"), the diocese's directory
("St. Cecilia's Church", 17 Grove Street, Adams 13605), GCatholic ("St. Cecilia", 17 Grove St.) and
OpenStreetMap (a building tagged "Saint Cecelia Roman Catholic Church", 17 Grove Street) agree on
the address, so both flags are true. The name follows the diocese and the bulletin (OSM's "Cecelia" is
a misspelling). The parish office and Kehoe Parish Center are on the same site. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code (87M5RX4F+G5), which OSM's building agrees with to about 15 m.
