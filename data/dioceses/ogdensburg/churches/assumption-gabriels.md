## Holds Mass

Yes, one weekend Mass. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 4, image) lists Sunday 8:00 AM at the
Church of the Assumption, with intentions for 27 September and 4 October; there is no
Saturday or weekday Mass there.

## Name and address

Name: the directory and the bulletin say "Church of the Assumption"; GCatholic "Assumption".
Street address: 826 State Route 86, Gabriels agrees in the directory, GCatholic, the bulletin
masthead, the Historic Saranac Lake wiki and OSM's building. **The ZIP code disagrees**: the
directory says 12939 (Gabriels' own post office), GCatholic 12983 (Saranac Lake's) and OSM 12970
(Paul Smiths'). 12939 is used, so `address_verified` stays false until a postal source is
checked. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, which is on the building.
