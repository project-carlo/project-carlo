## Holds Mass

Yes, but in summer only: Saturday 4:00 pm from Memorial Day to Labor Day (Saturday Masses at Westport appear in the issues sampled from 24 May to 6 September 2026; the Saturday Mass is at Port Henry from 12 September), and the summer issues also show a weekday 8:00 am (SPN) Mass and Holy Hour some weeks. This is the schedule set at the 23 May 2026 change, and the parish's Mass Times page says "(Memorial Day-Labor Day)". Read 2026-09-30. Outside the season there is no Mass here, and the next season starts about 30 May 2027 (Memorial Day).

## Name and address

Name agrees (GCatholic, directory, bulletin), so `name_verified` is true. The address does not: the diocese directory and the October 2025 bulletin say 6599 Main Street; GCatholic says 6602 Main St.; the September 2026 bulletin says 6603 Main Street. All three numbers exist as buildings on Main Street within about 50 m of each other in OpenStreetMap, and none is tagged as a church. GCatholic's Plus Code point is 800 m east, on the Westport Federated Church, which is not a Catholic church, so it was not used. Coordinates are from Nominatim's geocode of 6599 Main Street (`nominatim`), so treat them as village-precise. What would settle it: the number on the church's own sign or a call to the office (518-546-7254).
