## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives St. Augustine "Mass Times: Saturdays - 4:30pm - Sundays 9:30am - Tuesdays & Fridays 8:00am", and page 2's intentions list Saturday 3 October 4:30 pm, Sunday 4 October 9:30 am and the Tuesday and Friday 8:00 am Masses (with adoration after the Friday Mass).

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic and OpenStreetMap give St. Augustine('s), 3035 Main Street, Peru 12972. The diocese's directory says 3029 Main Street (an adjoining address point in OSM); three sources beyond the directory agree on 3035, so that is used. Both flags are true on the strength of the bulletin, GCatholic and OSM; the directory's number is the one that disagrees. Coordinates: GCatholic's Plus Code.
