## Holds Mass

Yes, one Sunday Mass a week. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 2) lists Sunday 4 October
11:30 am at Holy Cross ("HC") and no weekday Mass there; the parish website's Mass Times give
"11:30 AM at Holy Cross Church, Hopkinton" on Sunday. The bulletin's page 1 gives the church its
own religious-education slot (Sunday mornings 9:30 to 11:00).

## Name and address

GCatholic ("Holy Cross", 2807 State Hwy. 11B, Hopkinton 12965), the diocese directory ("Church of
the Holy Cross", 2807 State Highway 11B) and the bulletin ("Holy Cross Church, Hopkinton, NY") agree
on name and town, and the two rosters agree on the address, so both flags are true; the name follows
the bulletin, which drops "Church of the". Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87P7M7QV+W5). No
street address appears in the bulletin.
