## Holds Mass

Yes. The 13 September 2026 bulletin (page 3) lists Wednesday 4:00 PM and Sunday 9:00 AM at St.
Therese (code ST); the parish's Mass Times page gives the same, and the Newcomb town website
lists "Sunday – 9:00am".

## Name and address

The directory, the bulletin (page 2) and OSM all give **14 Adams Lane**, so both flags are
true. GCatholic says "5 Adams Ln." and the parish website's header says "18 ADAMS LANE"; both
disagree with three sources and are not followed. No GCatholic coordinates exist, so the
coordinates are OSM's (Nominatim) for the building at 14 Adams Lane. The name follows the
directory (the bulletin says "St. Therese' Parish").
