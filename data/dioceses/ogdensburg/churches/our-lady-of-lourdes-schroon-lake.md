## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Sunday 11:15 am, Tuesday 11:30 am and Wednesday 9:00 am (the church's own bulletin of 27 September 2026, and the same in the community's website and in the ParishesOnline bulletin's Mass intentions, read 2026-09-30). The website also lists a Saturday 6:00 pm Mass, which neither bulletin shows for the weekend of 26 to 27 September and 3 to 4 October; it may be seasonal (not checked).

## Name and address

The bulletin and the community's website give 1114 US Route 9 (Route 9 is Main Street through the village; OSM names the road Main Street), the diocese directory has "1114 US Rt. 9", GCatholic has only "Main St." with no number. The directory and the bulletin agree on the number, so both flags are true. Coordinates: GCatholic's Plus Code point (`87M8R6PQ+J8`, 43.83656, -73.76169) lies about 200 m south-west of OpenStreetMap's church node (43.83822, -73.76079) and at the block of 1087 Main Street, so OSM's node is used (`coordinates_from: osm-node`); the two were not compared with imagery.
