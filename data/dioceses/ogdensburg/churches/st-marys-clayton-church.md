## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (ParishesOnline 06/0098, page 1 box and page 2)
lists Saturday 4:00 pm vigil, Sunday 9:30 am and weekday Masses at 9:00 am (Thursday a
Liturgy of the Word with Communion); the parish site's Worship page gives the same weekend
times.

## Name and address

Both flags true. GCatholic ("St. Mary", 521 James St.), the diocese directory ("St. Mary's
Church", 521 James Street, 13624), the parish's own bulletin masthead and site (521 James
Street) and OSM (James Street, at the same point) agree. Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code
87P56WQ7+CG. The parish office and the church are one address.
