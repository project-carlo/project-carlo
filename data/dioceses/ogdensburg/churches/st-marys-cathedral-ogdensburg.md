## Holds Mass

Yes, daily: Saturday 4:00 pm vigil, Sunday 8:00 am (broadcast on WQTK 92.7 FM) and 11:00 am, Monday to
Friday noon (27 September 2026 bulletin, page 1, and the parish Mass-times page). Confession Saturday
3:00 pm; adoration Wednesday in the Deacon Winter Chapel inside the cathedral.

## Name and address

Name: "St. Mary's Cathedral" everywhere (diocese, GCatholic "Cathedral of St. Mary", ParishesOnline,
bulletin, OSM): flag true.

Address: disagreement over the number. **401 Hamilton Street** is on the bulletin masthead, the parish
website and in OSM. **415 Hamilton St.** is in the diocese directory, GCatholic (#2086) and the
ParishesOnline org. The row follows the parish (401), which is also what OSM has on the building, and
keeps the flag false. Both numbers map to the same spot (GCatholic's Plus Code, the ParishesOnline
point and the OSM node are all within 50 m), so this changes nothing on a map; it is a label to settle
(e.g. by asking the cathedral office, 315-393-3930). The directory's mailing address for the parish is
125 Ford Avenue, the parish office beside Notre Dame. Coordinates: OSM node (`osm-node`); this church
is missing from the GCatholic roster, so `--from gcatholic` was not possible.
