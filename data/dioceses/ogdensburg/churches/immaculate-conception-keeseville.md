## Holds Mass

Yes, on weekdays only: Monday and Tuesday at 8:30 AM after the 8:00 rosary (27 September 2026 bulletin, page 1, an image; the website's Mass Times agree), plus Sunday adoration 1:00 to 4:00 PM. No weekend Mass is held here; Saturday Mass is at St. John the Baptist. The bulletins of December 2025 and June to September 2026 show the same.

## Name and address

The directory (Church of the Immaculate Conception, 1660 Front Street, Route 9, Keeseville), the bulletin and the parish website's church page agree on 1660 Front Street, and OSM has an address point with that number at Front Street, Keeseville. GCatholic has no separate entry: its one Keeseville entry (18288) is titled "St. John the Baptist-Immaculate Conception" at 1804 Main St. and is linked to St. John's, so it supports the name but not this address. The address is given by the directory, the parish (bulletin and church page) and an OSM address point, so address_verified is true; name_verified is true on the directory, the parish and GCatholic's title. Coordinates: the OSM address point for 1660 Front Street, not a building node.
