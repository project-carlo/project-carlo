## Holds Mass

Yes, **until 31 October 2026**. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (container 06/0114, page 4) has Saturday 6:00 pm (SL) Mass at St. Lawrence and the parish's Mass Times page lists "6:00 PM St. Lawrence Church"; the 4 October issue repeats it. Watertown Daily Times (2 Sep 2026): Fr. Mark Reilly says St. Lawrence in Louisville must become an oratory ("the building has been closed and does not have regular Masses scheduled", available for weddings and funerals, and opened for Mass on the August feast day) because from 1 November he also covers Madrid and Waddington. He asked for and got an extension to Saturday 31 October, "the eve of All Saints", and Bishop LaValley will offer the final Mass in October. So it is a worship site today with a known end date; see `revisit`. The same article says St. Joseph's, Massena, took the same path in 2017.
