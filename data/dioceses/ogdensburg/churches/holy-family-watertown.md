## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 Blessed Sacrament bulletin (page 2, an image; key "Holy Family - HF") has the
Saturday anticipated Mass at 5:15 pm, Sunday 11:00 am and 5:15 pm, and noon Mass on Mondays and Fridays.
The parish site's Mass Times page says the same ("Noon Monday and Friday (Holy Family Church - HF)",
"5:15 pm at HF" on Saturday, "11:00 am (HF) and 5:15 pm (HF)" on Sunday). Adoration Mondays 12:30-3:00 pm.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead ("Holy Family, 129 Winthrop St."), the diocese's directory ("Church of the Holy
Family", 129 Winthrop Street, Watertown 13601), GCatholic ("Holy Family", 129 Winthrop St.), the parish
site and OpenStreetMap (address point 129 Winthrop Street; node "Holy Family Roman Catholic Church")
agree, so both flags are true. The name follows the bulletin; the directory adds "Church of the".
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87M6X3CW+VJ), within 30 m of OSM's node. GCatholic's
website for it, holyfamilywatertown.org, was not checked and is not used.
