## Name and address

The directory, the bulletin and the parish website give St. John the Baptist Church, 1803 Main Street (Route 22), Keeseville 12944. GCatholic has one entry, "St. John the Baptist-Immaculate Conception", at **1804** Main St., with a Plus Code that lands at the 1803 point; its entry is linked here because Saturday Mass is at St. John's and no separate point for the Immaculate Conception building is given. So the number 1803 (directory, bulletin) is followed and 1804 (GCatholic) is a one-off difference. Because the sources disagree on the number and OSM has both, address_verified stays false; the name is verified (directory, bulletin and GCatholic). Coordinates: GCatholic's Plus Code.
