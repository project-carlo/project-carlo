## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 Blessed Sacrament bulletin (page 2, an image; key "St. Anthony - SA") has
Sunday 8:00 am (Christine McCargar) and a 7:00 am weekday Mass Monday to Friday, and page 1 announces a
Spanish Mass at 1 pm on the third Sunday of the month. The parish site's Mass Times page gives "Monday
through Friday at 7:00 am (St. Anthony's Church - SA)" and Sunday 8:00 am (SA); its adoration list also
has Tuesday 3:00-6:00 pm and first Fridays 6:00-7:00 pm at St. Anthony's.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the diocese's directory ("St. Anthony's Church", 850 Arsenal Street, Watertown
13601), GCatholic ("St. Anthony", 850 Arsenal St.), the parish site and OpenStreetMap (an address point
at 43.9769090, -75.9305195 and a church node "Saint Anthony's Roman Catholic Church") agree, so both
flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87M6X3G9+MQ), about 0 m from OSM's node.
