## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Sunday 11:00 am, plus weekday Masses at 8:30 am on Wednesday and Thursday, per
the 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 2) and the parish site's Mass Schedule.

## Name and address

Name: the parish site, the bulletin heading and the diocese directory all say "St. Vincent of
Paul"; GCatholic has the same ("St. Vincent of Paul"). name_verified true.

Address: **the sources disagree, so address_verified is false.** The parish site and the
diocese directory say 1626 Kanady Street (site: "corner of Kanady & Lake Street"), Cape
Vincent 13618. GCatholic says 139 N Kanady St., and the bulletin's masthead prints the parish
office as "139 N. Kanady St." Both are probably the same building under a village number and
a county 911 number, but no source says so. I followed the parish's own statement of the
church's address (1626 Kanady Street). Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code 87P54MF6+Q7, at the
building. What would settle it: OSM has no house number here; a county parcel lookup would.
