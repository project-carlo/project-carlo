## Holds Mass

Yes. The 13 September 2026 bulletin (page 3, Tupper Lake intentions) lists Saturday 4:00 PM
and Sunday 8:30 AM and 10:30 AM at St. Alphonsus (code SA), and the parish's Mass Times page
gives the same. Weekday Masses (Monday to Saturday 8:00 AM) are at the Holy Ghost Chapel, a
different building (`holy-ghost-chapel-tupper-lake`), not here.

## Name and address

GCatholic, the directory, the bulletin (page 2) and OSM's address point all say 48 Wawbeek
Avenue, so both flags are true. The name follows the directory ("St. Alphonsus Church"; the
parish's name is St. Alphonsus-Holy Name of Jesus). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
