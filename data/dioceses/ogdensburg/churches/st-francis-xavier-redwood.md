## Holds Mass

Yes, but seasonally: Sunday 8:00 am, starting Memorial Day (bulletin: "Sunday 8am Starts
Memorial Day"). It was still listed on 4 and 11 October 2026 in the 4 October issue of the
Clayton / Alexandria bulletin (page 2), so the season had not ended by 30 September; when it
stops for winter was not found. The 2021 Watertown Daily Times caption says it "will remain a
seasonal worship site open in the summers". Say "summer only" for *Holds Mass*.

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic "St. Francis Xavier", directory "St. Francis Xavier Church", OSM and the
bulletin agree  (name_verified true).

Address: **the sources disagree, address_verified false.** The directory gives 28685
Butterfield Lake Road, Redwood 13679; GCatholic gives 43596 Route 37. OSM places the church on
Butterfield Lake Road (no number) at 44.29883, -75.79971, which is within about 10 m of the
GCatholic Plus Code point (44.29881, -75.79981), so both are the same building and Route 37
is probably the mailing or corner address. I followed the directory. Coordinates: GCatholic
Plus Code 87P676X2+G3.
