## Holds Mass

Yes, the parishes' Saturday vigil. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 2) has Saturday 4:00 pm
Mass at St. Patrick ("SP"); the parish's Mass Times page says "Saturday Anticipated Mass: 4:00 PM
at St. Patrick's, Chateaugay" and Thursday 8:30AM there. The bulletin lists the weekday services at
St. Patrick's as Communion Services (Thursday and First Friday 8:30), so the Saturday vigil is the
Mass.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Patrick", 132 W Main St., Chateaugay 12920), the diocese directory ("St. Patrick's
Church", 132 West Main Street) and the parish's contact page ("Catholic Community of Burke and
Chateaugay, 132 W. Main Street, Chateaugay") agree on the address, and OSM has the church on West Main
Street at the same point, so both flags are true. The bulletin calls it "St. Patrick's" and the
site's Mass Times "St. Patrick's, Chateaugay". Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87P7WWG9+FG).
