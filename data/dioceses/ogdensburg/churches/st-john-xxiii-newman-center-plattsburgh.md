## Holds Mass

Yes, during the academic year: Sunday 5:00 PM. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists "5:00 PM Sunday" at the St. John XXIII Newman Center and page 3's intentions for Sunday 4 October include "5:00pm (NC)". Discover Mass qualifies the time "When College is in Session". The parish website's Mass Times block still ends "5:00 PM at Newman Center (Last Mass May 10)" (read 2026-09-30), which is the spring-semester note left up over the summer; the September bulletin shows Mass resumed. It is a campus (SUNY Plattsburgh) church with public Sunday Mass, so it counts; say "academic year only" if it is ever needed.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the directory (90 Broad Street) and Discover Mass (90 Broad St.) give St. John XXIII Newman Center at 90 Broad; OpenStreetMap has the address point 90 Broad Street and a node named "Blessed John XXIII Newman Center (SUNY Plattsburgh)". The address agrees in three sources beyond the parish's own, so address_verified is true. The name is not verified: the parish and the directory say "St. John XXIII", OSM says "Blessed John XXIII" (the pre-2014 style), and GCatholic has no entry. Coordinates: OSM node, there being no GCatholic point.
