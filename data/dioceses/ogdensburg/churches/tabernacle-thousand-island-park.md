## Holds Mass

Yes, **summer only**: Sunday 8:00 am from July 4th through Labor Day, in the Thousand Island
Park Tabernacle on Wellesley Island (the TIP Corporation newsletter puts a 10 am Protestant
service in the same building in the summer). The 27 September 2026 bulletin's "Weekend Masses" box says so, and its
9 August and 6 September issues list weekly (TIP) Masses at 8:00, and the 27 September and 4
October issues list none, as the summer season says. It is in
neither the directory nor GCatholic, so it moves no coverage number.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it "Tabernacle, Thousand Island Park". The only address source is OSM
(node 6823957893: 17221 Sunset Avenue, Thousand Island Park 13692), so `address_verified` and
`name_verified` stay false; the TIP Corporation's newsletter confirms the Mass is in "the
Tabernacle" but gives no street address. The coordinates are the OSM node's, since no roster
lists it. What would settle the address: the TIP Corporation office (tiparkcorp.com) or a county
parcel record.
