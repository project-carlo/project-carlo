Yes, the one Sunday Mass at 11:00 AM from September to June (27 September 2026 bulletin, page 1, an image; website Mass Times, read 2026-09-30). The 16 August 2026 issue, however, still shows Willsboro, so the switch may not be exact; the website's rule is what is followed.
