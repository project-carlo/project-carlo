## Holds Mass

Summer only: the 11:00 AM Sunday Mass is at St. Joseph's in July and August, and the rest of the year at St. Philip of Jesus, Willsboro (website Mass Times, read 2026-09-30; the 26 July 2026 bulletin lists "11:00am @ St. Joseph's Church, Essex (July-Aug)"). The 27 September 2026 bulletin has no Mass here. It is a church with public Mass for eight to nine Sundays a year.
