## Holds Mass

Yes. The 13 September 2026 bulletin (page 3) lists Wednesday 3:00 PM and Sunday 12:30 PM at
St. Henry's (code SH), and the parish's Mass Times page gives the same: Sunday 12:30 PM,
Wednesday 3:00 PM.

## Name and address

The directory, the bulletin and OSM say **1187 Main Street** (Route 30), Long Lake, so both
flags are true. Others differ: GCatholic has no number ("Main St. - Route 30"), and the parish
website's header says "1188 MAIN ST" (one off; not followed). GCatholic's website for it,
`www.sthenryll.com`, is now a squatted domain (an Indonesian football-prediction page on
2026-09-30) and is not used. The name follows the directory. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus
Code.
