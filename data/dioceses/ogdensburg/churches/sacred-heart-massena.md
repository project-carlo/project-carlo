## Holds Mass

Yes, and it is the parish's main church. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (container 06/0114, page 4) lists Saturday 4:00 pm vigil, Sunday 9:30 am and weekday Masses (Monday, Wednesday to Friday 12:05 pm, first Saturday 9 am) at Sacred Heart; the parish's Mass Times page says the same. The 4 October issue repeats the schedule. Fr. Reilly's announcement (Watertown Daily Times, 2 Sep 2026) keeps Sacred Heart as the Saturday 4 pm church from 1 November, with the Sunday Mass moving from 9:30 to 9 am.

## Name and address

Four sources agree on the building at 212 Main Street, Massena 13662: GCatholic ("Sacred Heart", 212 Main St.), the diocesan directory ("Sacred Heart Church", 212 Main Street), the bulletin masthead block ("212 Main Street Massena, NY 13662") and the ParishesOnline organization (same address). OSM has "Church of the Sacred Heart" on Main Street (way 682716848, 44.92611, -74.89177). Both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (44.92606, -74.89144), within 30 m of the OSM way.
