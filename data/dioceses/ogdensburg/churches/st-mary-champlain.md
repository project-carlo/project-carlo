## Holds Mass

Yes, three Masses a week: Saturday 4:00 PM anticipated, Thursday 9:00 AM and Friday 9:00 AM. The St. Mary's bulletin (20/27 September 2026 double issue, an image) and the Rouses Point bulletin of 27 September (page 1) both schedule them, and the church's website Mass Times agree (read 2026-09-30). There is no Sunday Mass here; the Sunday Masses are at St. Patrick's, Rouses Point.

## Name and address

The directory says 90 Church Street; GCatholic says 86 Church St.; Discover Mass (under the wrong name "St. Mary of the Lake Oratory") also says 86. OpenStreetMap has address points for both 86 and 90, 40 m apart, and GCatholic's Plus Code point is nearer to 90. 90 is followed (the diocese's), but the sources disagree, so address_verified is false. The name, "St. Mary's Church" / "The Church of St. Mary", agrees everywhere (name_verified true). Coordinates: GCatholic's Plus Code.
