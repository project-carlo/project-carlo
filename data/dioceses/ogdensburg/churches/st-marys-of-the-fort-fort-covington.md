## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 2) has "(SM) 10:30AM" Sunday Mass, repeated for the next
weekend, and the parish's Mass Times page adds Tuesday 8:30 am and a First Saturday Mass at 8:30 am
("FIRST SATURDAY'S: MASS 8:30AM at St. Mary's of the Fort"). The bulletin lists the Tuesday service and
the 3 October First Saturday as Communion Services; the Sunday Mass is a Mass either way.

## Name and address

The sources disagree on the address, so `address_verified` stays false.
- The diocese directory and GCatholic: "3 Burns-Holden Rd" / "3 Burns Holden Rd." (Fort Covington 12937).
- The parish's own contact page and OpenStreetMap: 2549 Chateaugay Street, Fort Covington 12937 (OSM
  names the building "St. Marys of the Fort Church").
- The parish's home page gives "3 Burns Holden Rd" as the address of Mary's Place Thrift Store, so the
  rosters' address is probably the parish hall or thrift store next to the church, not the church door.
GCatholic's Plus Code (87P7XGR6+46, 44.99031, -74.48944) falls on OSM's church building, so the
coordinates are the church whichever street name is right. I followed the parish and OSM (2549
Chateaugay Street). Two sources on each side; a site visit or the town's tax parcel would settle whether
3 Burns Holden Rd is a second entrance on the same lot.

The name follows the bulletin and the parish site ("St. Mary's of the Fort Church"); GCatholic drops
the apostrophe, the directory has "St. Mary's of the Fort Church", so `name_verified` is true.
