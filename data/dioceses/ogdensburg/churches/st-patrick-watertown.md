## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 Blessed Sacrament bulletin (page 2, an image; key "St.Patrick - SP") has the
Saturday anticipated Mass at 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am and noon Mass Tuesday to Thursday; page 1 lists
confessions Tuesday to Thursday 11:45 am and Saturday 3:00-3:30 pm at SP. The parish site's Mass Times
page says the same ("Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday at Noon (St. Patrick's Church - SP)", "4:00 pm at
SP", "9:00 am (SP)").

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the diocese's directory ("St. Patrick's Church", 123 S. Massey Street, Watertown
13601), GCatholic ("St. Patrick", 123 S Massey St.), the parish site (its Google map pin reads "123 S
Massey St, Watertown, NY 13601") and OpenStreetMap (address point 123 South Massey Street; node "Saint
Patricks Roman Catholic Church") agree, so both flags are true. The Pastoral Center (parish office) is
in the same building complex at the same street number. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code
(87M6X3GM+85), within 25 m of OSM's node.
