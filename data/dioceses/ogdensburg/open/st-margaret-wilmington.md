## What is known

St. Margaret's Church, 5789 NY Route 86, Wilmington, is pictured with its address in the masthead of every Catholics Along the Au Sable and Boquet bulletin read (28 September 2025 to 27 September 2026) and its name is in the parish's canonical name ("Catholic Community of Holy Name/St. Matthew/St. Margaret"), but none of the six issues read schedules a Mass there (including the 26 July and 16 August 2026 summer issues) and the website's Mass Times omit it. Discover Mass has an empty Mass-times listing. A third-party directory gives an undated "Sunday 7:30 AM". No source says it closed, was sold or has any Mass.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to no regular Mass, but the building is still presented by the parish as one of its churches and could have an occasional Mass (a feast, a funeral, a summer Sunday) that the sampled bulletins missed; "no longer has Mass" is an inference from absence in six issues. One phone call to the parish office (518-647-8225) or a dated closure would settle it. If Mass is held, it becomes a church under `catholics-along-the-au-sable-and-boquet`; if not, an exclusion (closed or no-public-mass).
