## What is known

The Catholic Parishes of Southern Lewis County announced in bulletins from 29 March 2026 a
"special Mass and Social" at Saints Peter and Paul Oratory, Fish Creek, on Sunday 28 June
2026 at 4:00 pm, celebrated by Fr. Sony Pulickal, and the 5 July bulletin reports it took place
with over 100 people and "renewed hope for the future of this historic church". The place is
in no roster and has no address in any source read. I searched all weekly bulletins on the
parish site from January 2025 to 13 September 2026 (text of about 55 issues; a few are image
or photo files and were not searchable) for "Fish Creek", "Oratory" and "Peter & Paul": the
only hits are the 2026 announcements (the 29 June 2025 hit is the feast's readings). A "Vigil
Mass ... at Sts. Peter & Paul Oratory" video is also listed on the parish home page.
OSM has a place node "Fish Creek" at 43.5203, -75.5332 (Nominatim), which I did not tie to
the oratory.

## Why it is not decided

This reads as a one-off commemorative Mass at an oratory, not a regular worship site: no
weekly or seasonal schedule lists it, and no 2025 bulletin mentions it. But one Mass a year
can still make it a public worship site (the README counts a building with Mass four times a
year), and it is unknown whether the June Mass will recur. It is not closed or sold, so
`excluded` would need a source stating no public Mass. The revisit asks for the June 2027
bulletins and the office.
