## What is known

All Saints Church, 23 Bartlett Pond Road, Mineville (directory and GCatholic agree; OSM has a building with that number and the church as a node next to it) belongs to the Moriah parish, which is one bulletin with Elizabethtown and Westport (`catholic-parishes-of-elizabethtown-moriah-westport`). Its Masses in the last year, from that bulletin (container 04/0733; about forty issues sampled between January 2025 and 27 September 2026, counting the Mass codes on each schedule):

- Summer and autumn 2025: Saturday 4:00 pm "(AS)" in each issue sampled from 11 May to 26 October 2025. The 26 October 2025 issue says the 25 October Mass "will be the last of the season"; from 1 November the Saturday Mass moved to St. Patrick's, Port Henry.
- 26 April 2026: Fr. Giroux proposes a Sunday schedule with no All Saints Mass, saying it "means Mass would no longer be regularly offered at All Saints Church" and "NO decisions have been made about the future of that building or property". The 10 May 2026 issue announces the schedule effective 23 May and writes that "Moriah is now on track to shutter a beloved house of worship in Mineville".
- Every issue from 23 May to 27 September 2026 has Saturday Masses at Westport (summer) or Port Henry, none at All Saints. The parish website's Mass Times page lists none, but its church list still names All Saints, as does the bulletin masthead.

## Why it is not decided

No source I read says the church is closed, sold or deconsecrated: the pastor calls it "on track to shutter" and says nothing has been decided about the building. That is a plan and a stop of regular Sunday Mass, not a closure. It is not a church row because no public Mass is scheduled there now; a Mass for All Saints' Day, a funeral or an anniversary might still be held and would not appear in a weekly schedule I read. If a closing announcement, a sale or a decree appears it becomes `excluded/` with `reason: closed` and `last_mass` 2025-10-25 (the last regular Mass); if All Saints returns to the schedule (for example a summer season) it becomes a church under the parish above.
