## What is known

St. Joseph's Oratory stands at Coopersville, between Plattsburgh and Rouses Point (Town of Champlain), and is an old mission church belonging to St. Patrick's, Rouses Point. The parish's cemetery page says funerals may be held in it with a $200 cleaning charge ("before it can be used"), which implies it is not in regular use. It is in neither roster (the diocese's directory and GCatholic) and in no bulletin read (Rouses Point, Champlain).

## Why it is not decided

No source gives a Mass schedule or a closure. A place that is open for occasional funerals may still hold an annual Mass; one call to the Rouses Point rectory (518-297-7361) would settle it. No street address was found (Coopersville is a hamlet on Route 9B/Coopersville Road).
