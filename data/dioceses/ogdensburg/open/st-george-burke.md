## What is known

St. George's, 797 Depot St., Burke was the second church of the "Catholic Community of Burke and
Chateaugay". It is not in the Mass schedule of the current parish bulletin, and I could find no source
that says it is closed.

- The 27 September 2026 bulletin and the 13 earlier ones read (7 June to 20 September) have Saturday
  4:00 pm at St. Patrick's, Chateaugay as the community's only Saturday Mass and never a Mass at Burke.
  The parish's Mass Times page lists Chateaugay, Constable and Fort Covington only.
- The 20 September 2026 bulletin advertises "St. George's Country Patch Store, 797 Depot St., Burke ...
  We are located in the body of the Church"; the home page repeats "Country Patch at St. George Church".
  The nave is a thrift store.
- The parishes' 2018 pastoral plan lists St. George's as a "worship site" and says that with one priest only
  St. Patrick's, St. Mary's of the Fort and St. Francis of Assisi would stay open, which is the present
  schedule.
- Old listings still show Mass: Discover Mass and catholicmasstime.org give a Saturday 4:00 PM "St.
  George's Church" and Thursday 8:30 AM (April to November) under a COVID-era note; both also name the
  dead ccbcparish.com and are stale.
- GCatholic (18257) and the diocese's own directory: the directory has no entry for Burke.

## Why it is not decided

Everything says no Mass is held in the building and a store occupies the nave, but no parish or diocesan
source says the church was closed or relegated, or when; the README asks for a source that states it. A
church that has an occasional Mass (for example an annual summer or feast-day Mass) would not show in a
weekly schedule read only from June to September. The next step is a call to the parish office
(518-483-0486) or a decree/notice, and then either `excluded/` with `reason: closed` and `last_mass` or,
if a Mass turns up, a church row under
`catholic-communities-chateaugay-constable-fort-covington`.
