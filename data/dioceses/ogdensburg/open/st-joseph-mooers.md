## What is known

GCatholic still lists St. Joseph, 83 Maple St., Mooers 12958 (phone and website of the St. Ann and Holy Angels parish). The diocese's directory has no entry for it. The parish's Mass schedule page and its 27 September 2026 bulletin schedule Masses at St. Ann's (Mooers Forks) and Holy Angels (Altona) only. Wikipedia's parish list says the St. Ann and St. Joseph parishes merged in 1992. The address exists in OpenStreetMap.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing read states whether the St. Joseph church building was closed, sold or is used for an occasional Mass; absence from one bulletin and one schedule page is weak evidence. A dated closure (the North Country Catholic, the parish office 518-236-5632, the parish's history page) would make it an exclusion.
