## What is known

St. Patrick's Church, 51 Patent Road, West Peru, is a small 1841 church beside the Mother Cabrini Shrine (1947), with cemetery, grounds and outdoor stations. GCatholic classes it as an oratory/shrine; Wikipedia's list calls it a mission of St. Augustine's, Peru. A Mass was celebrated at the shrine on 8 July 2012. The current Peru-community bulletin (27 September 2026) names neither the church nor the shrine and gives no Mass there. There is a Facebook page ("St Patrick's Church - Oratory" and "Mother Cabrini Shrine - Peru, NY") that needs a login and was not readable.

## Why it is not decided

A shrine or oratory may hold a Mass a few times a year (the July anniversary of the canonization, a summer Sunday); if it does, it is a church under the Morrisonville/Peru parish, otherwise an exclusion (no-public-mass or closed). No current schedule was found either way.
