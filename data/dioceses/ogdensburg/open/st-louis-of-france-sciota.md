## What is known

GCatholic lists St. Louis of France at 8965 Route 22 (Sciota, post office Chazy) with the phone number of the Altona parish, and Wikipedia calls it a mission of Holy Angels, Altona. The diocese's directory has no entry. Neither the Holy Angels and St. Ann parish's Mass page nor its 27 September 2026 bulletin schedules a Mass there. No search result gave a closure or a schedule, and OSM had no hit for the church by name.

## Why it is not decided
