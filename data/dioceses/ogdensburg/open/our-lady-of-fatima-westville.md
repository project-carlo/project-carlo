## What is known

Our Lady of Fatima (GCatholic: 4328 State Route 37, "Malone"; OSM: State Highway 37, Westville Center) is
the church of Westville, one of the three communities of the "Catholic Community of Constable,
Westville and Trout River". It is not in the current bulletin's Mass schedule and I could find no
source that says it is closed.

- The Mass Times page and the 27 September 2026 bulletin (and 13 earlier issues, 7 June to 20 September)
  list Chateaugay, Constable and Fort Covington only. "Westville" appears in the bulletins only in
  the community's name, a scholarship notice and the "Fort Covington Westville Bombay Adult Center".
- The parishes' 2018 pastoral plan lists Our Lady of Fatima as a "worship site" and, with one priest,
  names only St. Patrick's, St. Mary's of the Fort and St. Francis of Assisi as staying open. St.
  Bridget's oratory in Trout River is in the same plan as a "former oratory, currently up for sale".
- OpenStreetMap has the building as a church at Westville Center.

## Why it is not decided

The plan and the schedules point to no Mass, but no source says the church was closed, sold or
relegated, nor when, and an occasional Mass would not show in a June-September weekly schedule. The next
step is to ask the parish office (518-483-0486) or find the decree/notice; then `excluded/` with
`reason: closed`, or a church row under `catholic-communities-chateaugay-constable-fort-covington` if a
Mass is held.
