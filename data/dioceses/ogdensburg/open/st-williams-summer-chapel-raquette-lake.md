## What is known

The parish of St. Anthony of Padua in Inlet calls itself "of Inlet and Raquette Lake", and its website lists an "Oratory: St. William's, Raquette Lake". A Discover Mass page (managed by the same phone number) and an aggregator page name a "St. William's Summer Chapel, Main Street, Raquette Lake, NY 13436". No roster lists it: GCatholic's "St. William" (38887) is the separate 1890 church on Long Point, which is no longer Catholic (see `excluded/st-william-long-point-raquette-lake`). The non-denominational "Raquette Lake Chapel" (raquettelakechapel.org, worship Sundays 10 am in July, August and the first Sunday of September, communion first Sunday of the month) is a third building and is not Catholic.

Mass at the village chapel: Discover Mass says "No Masses are scheduled here regularly"; the aggregator lists many times (probably an old summer schedule); the parish website's Mass Times and the only bulletin I could read (26 September to 4 October 2026, out of season) show none. The Wayback Machine is blocked from this sandbox, so no summer 2026 bulletin was read.

## Why it is not decided

Whether public Mass is held there in summer (the name says "Summer Chapel") is not shown by any source I could read. A summer Mass would make it a church row with "summer only" under the Old Forge/Inlet parish; if none, it is an oratory without public Mass (`no-public-mass`). The street address is only "Main Street"; the building was not located in OpenStreetMap.
