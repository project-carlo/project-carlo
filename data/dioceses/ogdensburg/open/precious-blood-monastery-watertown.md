## What is known

The Precious Blood Monastery (Sisters of the Precious Blood, an enclosed contemplative community) is at
400 Pratt Street, Watertown, on the site's own footer; GCatholic lists its chapel with a Plus Code
(43.9649, -75.9222) but no street address. The Blessed Sacrament Parish bulletin of 27 September 2026
treats the monastery as one of its Mass sites: a code "M" (Precious Blood Monastery) in the Mass
Intentions page, with an 11:00 am Mass every day (Monday to Saturday and Sunday) carrying parishioners'
intentions, and the parish's 2027 Mass book is open for "the Precious Blood Monastery". The parish's Mass
Times web page (which lists the three churches) does not mention the monastery. The monastery's own site
lists no Mass times, and the 2020 North Country Catholic article describes an enclosed community with a
chapel but says nothing about public Mass.

## Why it is not decided

A daily Mass with parish intentions is being celebrated in the chapel, but no source says the public may
attend, and an enclosed monastery's chapel is often closed to the public or open only at set hours. If
the parish or the sisters confirm public attendance, this becomes a church row under
`blessed-sacrament-watertown` (the bulletin that lists the Masses) with the Mass times from the
bulletin's Mass Intentions page; if the Mass is for the sisters and their guests only it becomes an
exclusion with reason `no-public-mass`. Not a `closed` case: the Masses are current.
