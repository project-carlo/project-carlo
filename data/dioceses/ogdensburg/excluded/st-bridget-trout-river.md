## Why it is excluded

St. Bridget's oratory, Trout River Rd., Trout River is a former oratory. The Catholic Communities'
2018 pastoral plan says so in one line: "St. Bridget’s- former oratory, currently up for sale". No 2026
bulletin of `catholic-communities-chateaugay-constable-fort-covington` (which covers the Catholic Community of
Constable, Westville and Trout River) lists a Mass there: 13 issues from 7 June to 20 September and the 27
September issue mention Trout River only in the community's name. GCatholic still lists the "Oratory of
St. Bridget" with no address beyond the road. No date of the last Mass was found.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
