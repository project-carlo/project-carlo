## Why it is excluded

**St. Joseph's, 635 Church Road, Olmstedville holds public Mass but publishes no bulletin the pipeline can read (no-bulletin).** The parish's website lists Sunday 11:00 AM, Wednesday and Friday 8:30 AM (read 2026-09-30) and announces a Mass on 4 October 2026, but the bulletin page is empty and no other channel was found.

The parish's Facebook page (facebook.com/stjosephsolmstedville, linked from its site) may carry bulletins but it is behind a login wall and could not be read. The diocese's 2025 pastoral plan joined the three churches in a pastoral care area of the Adirondack Mountain Deanery, and the site is headed "St. Joseph's Catholic Church and St. Mary's and St. Paul's Parish", so they would be one row if a bulletin were found.

Address: the directory and OSM say 635 Church Road; GCatholic says 639, which OSM has as the next building. The Plus Code (`87M8Q3C9+3J`) falls between them. Not a church row, so no flags.
