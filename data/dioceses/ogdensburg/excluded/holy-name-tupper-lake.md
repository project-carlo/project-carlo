## Why it is excluded

**Holy Name of Jesus, 115 Main Street, Tupper Lake: closed; sold by March 2021, parish merged
into St. Alphonsus in 2011; last Mass date not found.** The Adirondack Daily Enterprise
(18 March 2021) says "The Holy Name of Jesus Church in Tupper Lake was recently sold after its
parish merged with the St. Alphonsus parish in 2011." The merged parish's own history page
describes "two parishes and two worship sites" restructured into one, and has an album "Holy
Name of Jesus 1904-2019", i.e. the building's use as a church ended by 2019 (that is the
album's title, not a stated closing date). The current bulletin (13 September 2026, page 3)
schedules Tupper Lake Masses only at St. Alphonsus and the Holy Ghost Chapel. GCatholic still
lists it as a church. No `last_mass` is recorded: no source dates the last Mass.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. If the parish or the paper dates the last Mass or the sale, add `last_mass`.
