## Why it is excluded

**St. Theresa of Avila, 318 Main Street, Theresa: closed as a church by decree of 5 March 2019; no
Mass since. Last Mass date not found.** The same chancery decree that merged St. Joseph's,
Philadelphia and St. Theresa's, Theresa into St. Mary's, Evans Mills (parish consolidation decree,
"As of March 5, 2019") declares "the churches of St. Joseph's Church, Philadelphia and St. Theresa's
in Theresa ... relegated to profane but not sordid use" and finds that "Parishioners of these churches
freely attend Mass at St. Mary's in Evans Mills". A July 2019 insurance agreement then insures the
building only for its demolition cost. The current schedules (27 September 2026) list Mass at St. Mary's,
St. Paul's and OLSH only.

A GCatholic listing survives, with St. Mary's Evans Mills street address (8408 S Main St.) copied in; the
real address (318 Main Street) is in the insurance agreement and matches OpenStreetMap's "St Theresa of
Avila Church" way. I did not find a source giving the date of the last Mass or a later sale, so
`last_mass` is empty. (A web-search summary called it a "mission" of St. Mary's; that is the pre-2019
oratory status from the parish's 2016 pastoral plan, not the present.)

## What would change this

Nothing expected. If a news item dates the last Mass, add it as `last_mass`.
