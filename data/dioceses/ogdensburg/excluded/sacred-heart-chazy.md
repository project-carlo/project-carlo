## Why it is excluded

**Sacred Heart, 31 Church St. (GCatholic: 27 Church St.), Chazy: open with public Mass, but no bulletin found (2026-09-30).** The diocese assigned a pastor (Fr. Todd E. Thibault, effective 22 May 2026, who also has St. Joseph's, West Chazy); Discover Mass's listing gives Mass Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 AM and Tuesday and Thursday 9:00 AM; and the September 2026 bulletins of two neighbouring parishes advertise its harvest dinner for 27 September. But the Discover Mass listing is undated and lists no bulletins, its ParishesOnline organization (`sacred-heart-church-12921`) publishes nothing, GCatholic's website chazysacredheart.org does not resolve, the directory has no website, the diocese's own bulletin page says "None", and its Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/SacredHeartChazy/) is behind a login that the browser tool could not pass. So there is no current bulletin to confirm the Mass times against, and the row cannot be a church with a parish row.

GCatholic's and the directory's street numbers differ (27 vs 31 Church Street); the Discover Mass listing says 31, and the mailing address is 27 Church Street, P.O. Box 459. Both are kept as recorded.

## What would change this

A bulletin: Facebook (Sacred Heart Parish of Chazy, St. Joseph's West Chazy, which share a pastor and probably a bulletin), a ParishesOnline or Discover Mass account, or a PDF on rcdony.org. Reading one would make this a church under a new parish row with St. Joseph's, West Chazy (`excluded/st-joseph-west-chazy`).
