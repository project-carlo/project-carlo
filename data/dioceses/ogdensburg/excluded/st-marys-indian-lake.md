## Why it is excluded

**St. Mary's, 6335 NYS Route 30, Indian Lake holds public Mass but publishes no bulletin the pipeline can read (no-bulletin).** The parish's website lists Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM and several weekday Masses at St. Mary's (read 2026-09-30); no bulletin was found.

The parish's Facebook page (facebook.com/stjosephsolmstedville, linked from its site) may carry bulletins but it is behind a login wall and could not be read. The diocese's 2025 pastoral plan joined the three churches in a pastoral care area of the Adirondack Mountain Deanery, and the site is headed "St. Joseph's Catholic Church and St. Mary's and St. Paul's Parish", so they would be one row if a bulletin were found.

Address: the directory and OpenStreetMap (a church building numbered 6335, road named Main Street, which is Route 30 in the village) agree; GCatholic says just 'Route 28' and places it 500 m away. Not a church row, so no flags.
