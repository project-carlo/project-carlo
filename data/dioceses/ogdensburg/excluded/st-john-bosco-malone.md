## Why it is excluded

St. John Bosco, 57 Rennie St., Malone was closed by St. André Bessette Parish and sold to Living Hope
Church. The evidence is in two parts, and only one is quotable. Living Hope's own website says "Join us
Sundays at 10:00 AM, 57 Rennie St Malone, NY", which is this church's address, and the parish's
27 September 2026 masthead names Notre Dame and St. Helen's only, with no Mass at Bosco in any issue
read from 7 June to 27 September. The closing itself, its bishop's decree of a 25 November and the sale
to Living Hope come from a Malone Telegram article whose text I saw only in a search summary (the page is
gone); see the evidence note. The date of the last Mass was not found.
