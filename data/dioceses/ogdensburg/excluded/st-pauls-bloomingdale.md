## Why it is excluded

**St. Paul's, 1640 State Route 3, Bloomingdale: closed; last Mass early 2016 (exact date not
found).** The Adirondack Daily Enterprise reported in May 2023 that "The last Mass at St.
Paul’s was held in early 2016, before it became an oratory. An oratory is a church building
that can be used for events such as weddings, funerals, fest days and baptisms, but not
regular Masses." A final liturgy in late April 2023 (Rev. Martin Cline, pastor of St.
Bernard's) decommissioned the building, sold to a local woman for a day care center ("Li'l
Tykes Daycare and Enrichment Center"). GCatholic still lists it as a church with St. Bernard's
phone number, and it is in no directory entry. The parish's current bulletin (27 September
2026, page 4) lists no Mass here. `last_mass` is therefore only the year.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building is sold and decommissioned. Not `no-public-mass`: it had
regular Mass until 2016.
