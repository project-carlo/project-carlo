## Why it is excluded

**St. John in the Wilderness, 6148 State Route 30, Lake Clear: closed; weekly Mass ended in
2015 (exact date not found), sold 2021.** The Adirondack Daily Enterprise of 18 March 2021
reported: "In 2015 the parish ended weekly Masses at St. John in the Wilderness and its status
was downgraded from a church to an oratory. Oratories do not hold regular services but are
available for events such as feast days, funerals and weddings." and that St. Bernard's parish
was under contract to sell it "this spring"; the Historic Saranac Lake wiki records it as
"closed, and sold to a private party in 2021". The parish's 27 September 2026 bulletin lists no
Mass here. The GCatholic listing is stale (no coordinates, still "Church"). `last_mass` is
therefore only the year.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: sold to a private party. Not `no-public-mass` because it had regular Mass
until 2015.
