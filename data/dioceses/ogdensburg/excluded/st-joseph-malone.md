## Why it is excluded

St. Joseph's, 306 W Main St., Malone (one of the four parishes united into St. André Bessette Parish in 2014)
was closed and sold by the diocese. A 2018 letter to a local paper says the bishop decided to close it despite
the diocesan planning committee's vote and asks "Why are we selling the church...". Since then the parish
has only ever named Notre Dame and St. Helen's as its churches: the parish website, the diocese's directory
entry ("Worship sites: St. Helen's Church - Chasm Falls") and every 2026 bulletin read (7 June to 27
September, 17 issues) show no Mass at St. Joseph. Discover Mass's page for it has no Mass times. OSM
still labels the building "Saint Joseph's Roman Catholic Church" at 308 West Main Street; a commercial
listing site shows 306 W Main St. "Office for Sale" (seen only as a search-result title). The date of the
last Mass was not found, and the letter is an opinion piece, so this is the weakest of the closure records.

## What would change this

A parish or diocesan notice with the closing date would fill in `last_mass`; a Mass at the building would
reverse the exclusion.
