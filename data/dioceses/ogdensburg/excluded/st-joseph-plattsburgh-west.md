## Why it is excluded

**St. Joseph's, 1349 Military Turnpike, Plattsburgh (Treadwells Mills, GCatholic: "Plattsburgh West"): closed as a church; now St. Joseph's Community Outreach Center (dedicated 27 June 2022).** The Press-Republican of 2 July 2022 reports the blessing and dedication of the former church "as St. Joseph's Outreach Center" by Bishop LaValley, led by "the Rev. Scott Seymour, the former St. Joseph's pastor", after the parishes of St. Alexander and St. Joseph merged; the sanctuary "has been transformed into a multi-purpose space". The 27 September 2026 bulletin of the community that absorbed it lists the center under its ministries (soup kitchen, food pantry) and schedules Mass at St. Alexander, St. Augustine and St. James only. The date of the last Mass is not known (the merger was before 2022; the article's paywalled text says the two parishes had merged four years before, about 2018), so `last_mass` is left out. GCatholic still lists it as a church.
