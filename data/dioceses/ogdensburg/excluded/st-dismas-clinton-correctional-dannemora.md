## Why it is excluded

**St. Dismas, the Good Thief, inside Clinton Correctional Facility, Cook St., Dannemora: a prison chapel with no public Mass.** GCatholic (founded 1939) and Wikipedia's parish list place it inside the state prison, a maximum-security facility that members of the public cannot enter for worship. It is in no parish bulletin or schedule read in this region (none of the Holy Cross, Morrisonville, Ellenburg, Rouses Point or Champlain bulletins, nor the Discover Mass listings for Dannemora and Redford), and the diocese's directory omits it. No source says it is open to the public.

## What would change this

A public Mass schedule (for example the diocese's prison ministry page) showing visitors admitted. Not expected.
