## Why it is excluded

St. Joseph, 22 County Route 4, Bombay closed with a final Mass on 29 December 2019. The North Country
Catholic's editor wrote on 8 January 2020 that "Bishop Terry R. LaValley was celebrating the final Mass
in the tiny country church" at St. Joseph's, Bombay "on Dec. 29", and that "St. Joseph's Church may have
closed". In 2018 the parishes' pastoral plan still called it a "worship site" with its own trustees, and
by 2026 it is absent from every Mass schedule of the Catholic Communities of Chateaugay, Constable and
Fort Covington (`catholic-communities-chateaugay-constable-fort-covington`); the 6 September 2026
bulletin mentions Bombay only as a place where the parish keeps a cemetery. GCatholic still lists it,
and lists St. Mary's of the Fort (Fort Covington) as its mission from before the merger.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. The building's disposition (sold, relegated) was not researched; it does not affect
the exclusion.
