## Why it is excluded

**St. Joseph's, 1 Garden Road, Philadelphia: closed as a church by decree of 5 March 2019; no Mass
since. Last Mass date not found.** The chancery's letter of 6 March 2019 to St. Mary's, Evans Mills
enclosed a Decree of Extinctive Union merging St. Mary's, St. Joseph's (Philadelphia) and St.
Theresa's (Theresa) into St. Mary's, and a second decree in which Bishop LaValley "hereby declare[s]
the churches of St. Joseph's Church, Philadelphia and St. Theresa's in Theresa be and hereby are
relegated to profane but not sordid use", finding that "Closure will not harm the good of souls" and
that parishioners "freely attend Mass at St. Mary's in Evans Mills". The parish (River Catholics)
still hosts the decrees on its "Parish Consolidations" page. A July 2019 insurance agreement then
covers the building only for demolition cost. The current schedules (27 September 2026) list Mass
at St. Mary's, St. Paul's and OLSH only.

The decree states that the building is no longer a church; I did not find a source giving the date of
the last Mass, or whether the building has since been sold, so `last_mass` is empty. GCatholic still
lists "St. Joseph" as a church, with St. Mary's Evans Mills street address (8408 S Main St.) copied
into it; the real address (1 Garden Road) comes from the insurance agreement. OSM's Garden Road
numbering has only "7 1/2", so the coordinates were not independently checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: a relegated church does not reopen for parish worship. If a news item dates the last
Mass, add it as `last_mass`.
