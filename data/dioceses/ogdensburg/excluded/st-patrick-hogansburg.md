## Why it is excluded

St. Patrick's, 1 Church St., Hogansburg was sold before 2018. The Catholic Communities' 2018
pastoral plan lists it among the churches of the area as "St. Patrick’s Hogansburg Sold", and the parish
website of the Malone parish dates the Hogansburg parish itself as 1827 to 2013. No 2026 bulletin of
the neighbouring Catholic Communities of Chateaugay, Constable and Fort Covington
(`catholic-communities-chateaugay-constable-fort-covington`) has a Mass at Hogansburg; the 6
September 2026 issue mentions only "our cemeteries in Fort Covington, Bombay and Hogansburg". The date
of the last Mass was not found.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. A dated closure notice would only fill in `last_mass`.
