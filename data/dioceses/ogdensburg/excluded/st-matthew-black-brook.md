## Why it is excluded

**St. Matthew's, 781 Silver Lake Rd., Black Brook (Clayburg): closed, about 2015 (no last Mass date found).** Adirondack Life (October 2022) reports that when the couple bought the church building in 2020 "it had been empty for five years, its dwindling congregation folded into the Church of the Holy Name in Au Sable Forks", and that it is now the Black Brook General Store. So it held no Mass from roughly 2015 and has been a shop since 2020. The parish that absorbed it still carries the name in its masthead, "Catholic Community of Holy Name/St. Matthew/St. Margaret", which is why GCatholic (no coordinates) and the old ParishesOnline account name ("...holy-name-st-matthew-st-margaret") still name it; its bulletin pictures six churches and St. Matthew's is not one of them.

The last Mass date is not known (only the year, inferred from "empty for five years" before 2020), so `last_mass` is left out.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; a dated closure notice in the North Country Catholic or the parish would add `last_mass`.
