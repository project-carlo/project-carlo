## Why it is excluded

**St. William's on Long Point, Raquette Lake: not a Catholic worship site now (other).** The
1890 church on Long Point is owned by Saint William’s on Long Point Inc., which states that it
is "a non-profit, non-denominational organization" and "not affiliated with or under the
jurisdiction of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Ogdensburg". Its 2026 summer schedule (read
2026-09-30) lists concerts and one ecumenical service, no Mass. GCatholic itself marks it
"Former Church". The catholicmasstime.org page "St. William's Summer Chapel, Main Street,
Raquette Lake" (Sunday 9:00, 8:00, 9:30 AM) is an aggregator listing at a village address, not
this boat-access site, and does not show Mass at Long Point; the village-side "Raquette Lake
Chapel" is another matter, for the region that owns St. Anthony of Padua's parish of Inlet and
Raquette Lake (see the final report).

## What would change this

A diocesan or parish schedule showing Mass at Long Point (none found). Reopened for Catholic
use: unlikely.
