## Why it is excluded

**St. John the Evangelist, 35923 NYS Route 180, La Fargeville: an oratory with no public Mass.**
The Clayton parish's own site still carries the header "The Catholic Churches of St. Mary's,
Clayton and St. John the Evangelist, Lafargeville" and a St. John's cemetery, but its Worship
page (read 2026-09-30) says, for summer and for the rest of the year, "No Mass at St. John's,
LaFargeville". The Watertown Daily Times of 7 June 2021 reported that the church "will become
an oratory, a space set aside for worship that does not see a regular celebration of Mass".
The 27 September 2026 bulletin of the parish (ParishesOnline 06/0098) lists Masses at
Clayton, Alexandria Bay, Redwood and Thousand Island Park and does not mention La Fargeville
in any of its six pages. So the building is standing and belongs to the parish, but there is
no public Mass, which is why the reason is `no-public-mass` rather than `closed` (no source
says it closed or was sold). GCatholic lists it (18260) with a phone number that is
Clayton's; the diocese directory does not list it at all.

## What would change this

A bulletin or the parish site listing a Mass at St. John's (even a seasonal one) would make it
a church under `st-marys-clayton`. Worth re-reading the Clayton Worship page after next
summer.
