## Why it is excluded

**St. Joseph's, 74 Mason Rd., Champlain: a cemetery, not a church.** The parish's own cemeteries page lists "St. Joseph's Cemetery 74 Mason Road, Champlain, NY" next to St. Patrick's Cemetery in Rouses Point, and GCatholic's entry for "St. Joseph" at the same address (with St. Patrick's and St. Mary's contact details) is this cemetery. No Mass schedule, bulletin or directory entry names a church there. The oratory the same page mentions, St. Joseph's in Coopersville, is a different place (`open/st-joseph-oratory-coopersville`).

## What would change this

A bulletin or the parish saying Mass is offered at a chapel on the cemetery; nothing suggests it.
