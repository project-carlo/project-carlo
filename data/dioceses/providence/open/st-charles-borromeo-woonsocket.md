## What is known

St. Charles Borromeo parish (Woonsocket's oldest, 190 North Main St.) was suppressed on 13 January 2020 and merged into All Saints, which merged into St. Joseph in 2022. The finder (entry 12257, read 2026-11-14) records that the bishop rescinded the relegation of the church building in December 2019, closed it "for an indefinite period" from 13 January 2020, and that the July 2020 Vatican decree kept it "as a place of divine worship to be used on an occasional basis". GCatholic (22474) still lists it as a church.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing shows a Mass there now and nothing says it was relegated or sold. Six St. Joseph bulletins (Christmas 2025, Easter 2026, November 2026) name no Mass at St. Charles; aggregator listings with weekly Masses are stale. A dated Mass in a St. Joseph bulletin would make it a church of that row.
