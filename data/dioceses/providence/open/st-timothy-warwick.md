## What is known

St. Timothy church, 1799 Warwick Ave., held Saturday 4:00 p.m., Sunday 7:00 and 9:00 a.m. and daily 8:00 a.m. Masses until October 2026 (18 October 2026 bulletin, page 1). The 25 October 2026 bulletin says its heating system is broken and that from 31 October all its Masses and confessions move to St. Rita Church; the 15 November issue confirms the move and says the Divine Mercy Adoration Chapel stays open at St. Timothy's on weekdays. No source gives an end date, and none says the church is closed. The finder (12236) and GCatholic (22454) still list it with its old times.

## Why it is not decided

The church has no public Mass now, so it is not a church row, but the move is described as a repair problem, not a closure, so it is not excluded either. The bulletin's 'MASSES AND DEVOTIONS' box will show when Mass returns; see `revisit`.
