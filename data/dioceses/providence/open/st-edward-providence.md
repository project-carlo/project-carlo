## What is known

St. Edward, 997 Branch Ave., Providence, was merged into St. Anthony, North Providence on 31 December 2022, with the diocese saying its building would "remain open and available" as a worship venue (finder 12207). St. Anthony's bulletin listed a Sunday 9 AM Mass there through the 24 May 2026 issue. The 31 May 2026 issue (page 3) cancelled it "until he returns", because the priest in residence, Fr. Stephano Kwak, had to go back to South Korea over a visa and would be "away until the end of August". Every issue sampled since, the newest of them the 15 November 2026 issue, gives St. Edward "No Mass Until Further Notice". The masthead still names "Saint Edward Church" and Fr. Kwak "In Residence", and the parish's St. Edward Food & Wellness Center and Kitchen carry on next door (1001 Branch Ave.). The finder still lists Sunday 9 AM there, which is out of date. It also lists Wednesday 6 PM Exposición, which is not a Mass; no bulletin checked mentions it.

## Why it is not decided

No Mass is held there now, so it is not a church row. Nothing says the church has closed, and the cancellation was announced as temporary, so it is not `excluded/` either. It becomes a church of st-anthony-north-providence if the Mass resumes, or a closed exclusion (last Mass 24 May 2026) if the parish announces a closure.
