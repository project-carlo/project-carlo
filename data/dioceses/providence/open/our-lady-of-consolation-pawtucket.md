## What is known

Our Lady of Consolation (211 Sabin St., Pawtucket, established 1895) was a church of Holy Family Parish, which merged into St. John Paul II Parish by the decrees announced in December 2025 (Rhode Island Catholic; the finder dates the merger 02-01-26). The finder's Holy Family entry (12179) still lists Sunday 9:00 AM at Our Lady of Consolation Church, but that entry appears unrevised: its Saturday 4:30 PM Mass clashes with St. John Paul II's own Saturday 4:30 PM Mass at St. Cecilia. GCatholic (gcatholic:22397) still lists the church.

## Why it is not decided

The four St. John Paul II bulletins posted (25 October to 15 November 2026) and its site schedule every Mass at St. Cecilia and name no Mass here; nothing says the church closed, was relegated or was sold. Holy Family's own site is gone and its Discover Mass page lists no bulletins. A dated Mass in a St. John Paul II bulletin (Christmas especially) would make it a church of that row; a closure notice an exclusion.
