## What is known

St. Mary of the Immaculate Conception (103 Pine St., Pawtucket, established 1829) merged into St. John the Baptist Parish by decree of 11 December 2025 (finder 12186 and 12184; Rhode Island Catholic, which says the parish reported 120 people at weekend Masses in 2024). The finder entry now carries St. John the Baptist's contacts and no Mass times. GCatholic (22403) still lists the church. St. Mary's own site (saintmaryri.org) no longer resolves, and its Discover Mass page lists no bulletins.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing shows a Mass there now and nothing says the church closed, was relegated or was sold. The St. John the Baptist bulletin (25 October 2026, the latest posted) and site list every Mass at 69 Quincy Ave. and name no St. Mary church. The decree texts read speak of the parish and its records, not the building. A dated Mass in a St. John the Baptist bulletin would make it a church of that row; a closure or sale notice an exclusion.
