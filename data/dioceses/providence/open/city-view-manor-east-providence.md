## What is known

Sacred Heart, East Providence lists a Saturday 3:30 pm Mass at "City View Manor" among its weekend Masses (15 November 2026 bulletin, page 2), and the diocesan finder's Sacred Heart entry lists the same Mass "Held at City View Manor". The name suggests a nursing or senior residence in East Providence; neither source gives its address.

## Why it is not decided

Neither source says whether the Mass is open to the public or for residents only, and I found no address. A Mass in a residence chapel for residents is `no-public-mass`; a public one makes the place a church on the Sacred Heart row. No roster lists it.
