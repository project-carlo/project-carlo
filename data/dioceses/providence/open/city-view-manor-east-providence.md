## What is known

Sacred Heart, East Providence lists a Saturday 3:30 pm Mass at "City View Manor" among its weekend Masses (15 November 2026 bulletin, page 2), and the diocesan finder's Sacred Heart entry lists the same Mass "Held at City View Manor". On 16 November 2026 the East Providence Housing Authority's public-housing page (https://ephousing.org/public-housing/) gave "City View Manor - 99 Goldsmith Avenue , East Providence, RI": a 150-unit, 11-story public-housing apartment building, joined to Goldsmith Manor (115 Goldsmith Ave.) by a community room. Several senior-living listing sites call it assisted living or skilled nursing, but the housing authority, which runs it, describes it as public housing. I recorded the housing authority's address. No roster lists the place.

## Why it is not decided

Neither source says whether the Mass is open to the public or for residents only, and I found no address. A Mass in a residence chapel for residents is `no-public-mass`; a public one makes the place a church on the Sacred Heart row. No roster lists it.
