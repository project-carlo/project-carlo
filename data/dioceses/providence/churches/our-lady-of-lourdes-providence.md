## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Adalbert's 15 November 2026 bulletin schedules "Saturday, November 21 4:00 pm (ENG) at OLL" and the Thanksgiving Mass "at 10:00 am at Our Lady of Lourdes Church"; St. Adalbert's Mass schedule page gives the Saturday 4:00 pm at "O. L. of Lourdes Church, 901 Atwells Ave". The finder (12196) also lists Sunday 6:30 AM, which no parish source mentions (see the parish record).

## Name and address

The St. Adalbert schedule page, the finder (12196) and GCatholic (22413) all give 901 Atwells Avenue, Providence 02909. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87HCRHG2+3W; the finder pin (41.82544, -71.44734) is within 40 m. Our Lady of Lourdes is canonically its own parish with the same pastor as St. Adalbert; it is recorded under St. Adalbert's row because that bulletin carries its Mass and no OLL bulletin was found.
