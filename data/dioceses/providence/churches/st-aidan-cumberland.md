## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 St. Aidan - St. Patrick bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday Vigil 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:30AM and 11:00 AM and daily Mass 8:00 AM Monday - Saturday, under the parish address 1460 Diamond Hill Road. The bulletin does not name the building, but it is the parish's only church: the St. Patrick building is closed (finder 12142), and the finder gives the same Mass times on entry 20485 at 1460 Diamond Hill Rd.

## Name and address

The finder (20485, 12138), GCatholic (22353), ParishesOnline and the bulletin all give 1460 Diamond Hill Rd., Cumberland, RI 02864. GCatholic and the finder's old entry 12138 call the church St. Aidan; the merged parish keeps both names. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87HCWHJW+GJ; the finder's pin (41.93163, -71.40301) is about 40 m away.
