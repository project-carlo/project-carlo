## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 Holy Trinity bulletin gives Saturday Vigil 4PM, Sunday 8AM, 10:30AM and 12 Noon (Spanish), Monday-Friday 7AM and Saturday 8AM; the page-2 intentions for 14-20 November match. The 21 December 2025 issue calls this building "OLQM Bldg.".

## Name and address

The former Our Lady Queen of Martyrs church, now Holy Trinity's main church. 1409 Park Ave.: the bulletin masthead, the finder's decree text ("primary worship site ... 1409 Park Ave.") and GCatholic agree; the finder's address for entries 12252 and 29694 is 1371 Park Ave., the parish office, so it wasn't used. The bulletin prints ZIP+4 02895-6957, GCatholic and the finder 02895-6597. Name kept as the parish calls it with the old name in brackets; only the parish's own sources use "Holy Trinity" for the building, so name_verified stays false.
