Yes. The 15 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, image) gives Saturday 4PM English, Sunday 8AM Português and 11AM English. The finder (12241) gives the Portuguese Mass at 9:00 AM; the bulletin's 8 AM is current (Christ the King's 15 November bulletin also says 8:00AM).

The finder, GCatholic and ParishesOnline all give 10 Sunset Ave., which is the rectory's number, so the masthead's church address is recorded (only one source gives it, so `address_verified` is false). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 60 m from the finder pin (41.714915, -71.525579); the ParishesOnline pin (41.688, -71.519) is 3 km off and was not used.
