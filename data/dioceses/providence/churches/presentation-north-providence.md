## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 St. Anthony Parish bulletin (page 2) lists the Church of the Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary: Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM, confessions Saturday 3:15-3:45 PM. The parish merged into St. Anthony on 31 December 2022; the diocese said the building would remain "open and available as additional worship venues of St. Anthony Parish" (finder 12176).

## Name and address

The finder's address field for 12176 is 5 Gibbs Street, which is St. Anthony's business office (bulletin page 2: "BUSINESS OFFICE 5 Gibbs Street"), not the church; its notes and the St. Anthony entry give the church at 1081 Mineral Spring Ave., as does GCatholic (22391). I recorded 1081 Mineral Spring Ave. The name is the bulletin's. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87HCVH9F+87; the finder pin (41.86846, -71.42623) is about 40 m away.
