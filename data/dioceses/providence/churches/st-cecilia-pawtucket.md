## Holds Mass

Yes. St. John Paul II's only church: the finder (12183) says "As of January 12, 2019 All Services are held at St. Cecilia Church building", and the 15 November 2026 bulletin lists weekend Masses Saturday 4:30pm and Sunday 8am, 10am and 12 noon, and daily Mass Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 5:15pm and Saturday 8:00am.

## Name and address

The church stands at the corner of Central and Newport Avenues. The finder's parish entry (12183) gives "St. Cecilia Church, 750 Central Ave."; its St. Cecilia entry (24231) and GCatholic (22395) give 1253 Newport Ave., the side with the adoration chapel entrance. 750 Central Ave. is recorded because the parish's own finder entry labels it the church's address; both find the building. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87HCVJQW+5G, on the church; the finder pin (41.887152, -71.355909) is on the school and office at 755 Central Ave.
