## Holds Mass

Yes, from October to Easter: the parish site's schedule (read 2026-11-15, in its fall season) gives "Spring Street Parish Center" daily Mass Monday-Thursday 8 am, Saturday 5 pm and Sunday 10 am for October-December, and daily Mass and Sunday 10 AM from January to the Easter Vigil. The finder (12168) gives Sunday 10:00 AM at the "Parish Center, Spring St.; October to May". No bulletin confirms individual weeks.

## Name and address

The parish site gives 22 Spring Street (contact page and capital campaign page); the finder (12168) gives only "Spring St." (and the rectory on Spring Street, Box 279). Name verified (parish site and finder both say Parish Center, Spring St.); the street number is the parish's alone, so address_verified is false. GCatholic has no entry for it. Coordinates are the finder's pin for 12168 (41.16222, -71.54981), which sits on Spring St. south of Old Harbor; it may be a street geocode rather than the building. The finder entry 12168 is linked here because its address and pin are the Spring Street site; GCatholic 22383 (Chapel St.) is linked to the church.
