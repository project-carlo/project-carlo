## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 bulletin lists Monday-Friday 8:00 am and Sunday 9:30 am (Polish) and 11:30 am (English); its Saturday 4:00 pm Mass is at Our Lady of Lourdes. The finder places the Saturday 4:00 PM here too; the parish's own schedule and bulletin put it at OLL.
