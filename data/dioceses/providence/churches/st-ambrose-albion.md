## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 St. Ambrose bulletin (page 1) lists "Saturday Vigil 5:15PM" and "Sunday 8:30AM & 11:15AM", with weekday Masses Monday, Wednesday and Friday 7:30AM and Tuesday 6:00PM. The St. James bulletin of the same date lists the same times in its "Mass Times at St. Ambrose Church" box.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the diocese's finder (12160) and GCatholic (22375) all give 191 School St., Albion (village of Lincoln), and call it St. Ambrose; the finder's ZIP+4 02802-0067 is the rectory's PO Box 67. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87HCXG2Q+FG; the finder's pin (41.95154, -71.46102) is within a few metres.
