## Holds Mass

The 15 November 2026 St. Joseph bulletin (page 1) lists "Our Lady of Victory Sunday 8:00 am"; the finder gives the same. The parish of Our Lady of Victory was merged into St. Joseph by decree, which keeps this church as "an additional worship site of St. Joseph Parish".
