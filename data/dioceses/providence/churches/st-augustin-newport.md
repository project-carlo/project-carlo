## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 bulletin (container 03/0246) lists "4:00 p.m.—St. Augustin" on Saturday and "9:30 a.m.—St. Augustin" on Sunday.

## Name and address

The bulletin and GCatholic (22385) give the corner of Carroll Ave. and Harrison Ave.; the finder (12170) gives 2 Eastnor Rd. as its address line but describes the church as "Corner of Carroll and Harrison Avenues (5 Harrison Avenue)". The corner is recorded (2 Eastnor Rd. is probably the rectory; 5 Harrison Ave. is the street number for the same corner). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87HCFMCM+HV.
