## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday 5PM, Sunday 10AM, 1PM and 6PM, and Monday-Friday 12PM; the page-2 intentions for 14-22 November match. The diocesan finder (2026-11-14) gives the same weekend times.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it the Cathedral of Saints Peter and Paul; the finder says "Cathedral of SS. Peter and Paul". Address: the bulletin and GCatholic give 30 Fenner St., Providence 02903; the finder gives only "Cathedral Square" (the plaza the church fronts). Recorded 30 Fenner St., which two sources agree on. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87HCRH9M+R8; the finder's pin (41.81965, -71.41732) is about 50 m west, on the square.
