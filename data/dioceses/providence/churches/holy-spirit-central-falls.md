## Holds Mass

Yes. The 15 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, rendered) lists Mass here Saturday 4:00 pm (English) and 6:00 pm (Spanish), Sunday 9:00 am (English) and 10:30 am (Spanish), and 6:30am Monday-Thursday in the chapel; page 2 gives the intentions for 14-22 November.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the finder (12123) and GCatholic (22339) all give 1030 Dexter St., Central Falls. The building was St. Matthew church until the 2007 unification; finder 24221 ("St. Matthew (now Holy Spirit Church)", same address) is this building, so it is linked here rather than excluded. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87HCVHQX+VH; the finder pin is within 30 m.
