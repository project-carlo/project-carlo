## Why it is excluded

The diocesan finder (entry 12185, read 2026-11-14) says "On November 20, 2016, the church building closed permanently" and "Building sold."; entry 12186 repeats the 20 November 2016 closure. Its records went to St. Mary, which has since merged into St. John the Baptist. GCatholic (22402) still lists the church at 155 Power Rd., the address recorded here. last_mass is the closure date the finder gives.

## What would change this

A source showing Catholic Mass at 155 Power Rd. after 2016; the building was sold, so this is unlikely.
