## Why it is excluded

The diocese's decree of 18 February 2026, posted on St. Joseph's site (https://stjosephww.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/sspp-relegation.pdf, read 2026-11-14), relegates the Phenix church to secular use: "Mass and any other public worship or liturgical act is now prohibited there. This means that the church building is closed permanently", effective 21-22 February 2026. It says weekend Masses stopped on 1 January 2025 and "there have been no public Masses or liturgies" since, so `last_mass` is December 2024 (the decree gives no day). The parish merged into St. Joseph, West Warwick on 8 November 2025 (finder 12245), and St. Joseph's 15 November 2026 bulletin lists no Mass here. GCatholic 22463 still lists the church.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building has been relegated to secular use.
