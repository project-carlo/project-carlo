## Why it is excluded

The diocese's decree of 18 February 2026, posted on St. Joseph's site (https://stjosephww.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/olgc-relegation.pdf, read 2026-11-14), relegates the church to secular use: "Mass and any other public worship or liturgical act is now prohibited there. This means that the church building is closed permanently", effective 21-22 February 2026. It records that regular weekend Masses stopped on 1 January 2025 and that "there have been only four funerals and no other public Masses or liturgies" since. The parish merged into St. Joseph, West Warwick on 8 November 2025 (finder 12239), and St. Joseph's 15 November 2026 bulletin lists no Mass here. `last_mass` is left empty: the decree dates neither the last weekend Mass (late December 2024) nor the last funeral. GCatholic 22457 still lists the church.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building has been relegated to secular use.
