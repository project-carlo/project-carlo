## Why it is excluded

An Eastern Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Stamford, not of the Latin Diocese of Providence. GCatholic (church 17151, read 2026-11-16) gives its jurisdiction as 'Ukrainian Diocese of Stamford', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Providence'; the diocesan finder (entry 12259, read 2026-11-14) names it 'St. Michael’s Ukrainian-Catholic Church, Diocese of Stamford'. The README's reason table puts Eastern Catholic eparchy churches under `other-jurisdiction`.

It is open: its own site (read 2026-11-16) says 'Saturday Vigil Liturgy begins at 4 PM and Sunday Divine Liturgy begins at 11 AM', matching the finder. No bulletin was read, since the row is out of scope.

Address: the finder, GCatholic and the parish site agree on 394 Blackstone St., Woonsocket.

## What would change this

A policy to map Eastern Catholic churches.
