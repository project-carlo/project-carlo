## Why it is excluded

The diocesan finder (read 2026-11-14) says decrees of 16 January 2022 merged All Saints Parish into St. Joseph Parish effective 7 February 2022, and "02-07-22: The church building closed permanently." The building at 323 Rathbun St. was St. Aloysius church until the 1998 merger that formed All Saints (finder 24224: "Closed 1998. This location is now All Saints Church."), so the St. Aloysius and All Saints entries are one building. GCatholic (22468) still lists it as a church. The 15 November 2026 St. Joseph bulletin names no Mass there. last_mass is the month of the closure; no source gives the final Mass's date.

## What would change this

A St. Joseph bulletin scheduling a Mass at 323 Rathbun St.
