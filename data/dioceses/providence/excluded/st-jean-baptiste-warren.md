## Why it is excluded

The diocese's parish finder (entry 24193, read 2026-11-14) says "St. Jean Baptiste Church closed permanently January 1, 2018.", that the parish merged with St. Mary of the Bay in 2010 and became its mission, and "Building sold." The St. Mary of the Bay bulletin of 15 November 2026 lists Mass only at St. Mary of the Bay and Saint Alexander. GCatholic (22443) still lists the church with no closure. No source gives the date of the last Mass, so `last_mass` is left empty.

## What would change this

A St. Mary of the Bay bulletin or diocesan notice listing Mass at 324 Main St. again (unlikely, as the building was sold).
