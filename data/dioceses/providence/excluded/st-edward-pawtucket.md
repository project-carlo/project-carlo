## Why it is excluded

The diocese's December 2025 decree text, in the finder (12186) and Rhode Island Catholic, calls it "the former Saint Edward Parish (merged into Saint Mary in 2014 and alienated and sold in 2021)". Finder 12182 still lists the building (58 Hancock St.) as "CLOSED - a mission of St. Mary", and GCatholic (22400) still lists it as a church; both are older than the sale. No source gives the date of the last Mass, so last_mass is empty; the sale was in 2021.

## What would change this

A source showing Catholic Mass at 58 Hancock St. after 2021; the building was sold, so this is unlikely.
