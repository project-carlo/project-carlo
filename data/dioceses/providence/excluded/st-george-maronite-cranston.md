## Why it is excluded

An Eastern Catholic church of the Maronite Eparchy of St. Maron of Brooklyn, not of the Latin Diocese of Providence. GCatholic (church 17053, read 2026-11-16) gives its jurisdiction as 'Maronite Diocese of Saint Maron of Brooklyn', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Providence'; the diocesan finder (entry 12132, read 2026-11-14) says '(Maronite Rite, Eparcy of St. Maron)'. The README's reason table puts Eastern Catholic eparchy churches under `other-jurisdiction`.

The building is the former Latin church of St. Ann, Cranston, so this record also carries the finder's 'St. Ann (CLOSED)' entry (24211): 'St. Ann Church closed in 2013 … (St. George Church is at the old St. Ann Church)'. The St. Ann parish closed in 2013, but the building is not closed: it holds public Mass now as St. George, so it is not filed under `closed`. The parish's site lists 'Sunday Morning: 8:30am - Latin Rite; 10:30am - Maronite' and Saturday 4:00pm Maronite; the Latin-rite Mass is the Maronite parish's own, under the eparchy, so the exclusion stands.

It publishes a weekly bulletin (https://www.stgeorgeri.com/bulletins.html, 15 November 2026 issue read); none was mapped, since the row is out of scope.

## Name and address

The 15 November 2026 bulletin's masthead ('1493 Cranston Street, Cranston, RI'), the parish site's title ('Cranston, RI') and both finder entries give 1493 Cranston St., Cranston. GCatholic alone gives 171 Twin River Rd., Lincoln (Plus Code in Lincoln), with the same telephone number; it is not followed, since the parish's own current bulletin and the diocese agree on Cranston, and the finder says the parish is 'Now located at the former St. Ann’s Church'. GCatholic's address is probably an older or mistaken location. The finder pins the building at 41.7908, -71.4623. The finder's other listed website for entry 24211 (stgeorgemaronitecatholicchurch.com, also GCatholic's) timed out on 2026-11-16; the parish now uses stgeorgeri.com.

## What would change this

A policy to map Eastern Catholic churches, or a Latin parish of the Diocese of Providence holding its own Mass in this building.
