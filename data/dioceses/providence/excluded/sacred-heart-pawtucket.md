## Why it is excluded

The finder (entries 12179 and 20656, read 2026-11-14) and Rhode Island Catholic (December 2025) say that when Holy Family Parish was formed in 2009, "The Sacred Heart church building and property were relegated and subsequently alienated and sold that same year." GCatholic lists no Sacred Heart church in Pawtucket. last_mass is the year of the relegation; no source gives the final Mass's date.

## What would change this

A source showing Catholic Mass at 38 Park St. after 2009; the building was sold, so this is unlikely.
