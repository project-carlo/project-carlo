## Why it is excluded

St. Agnes holds no Mass. The pastor's letter in St. Ann's 9 November 2025 bulletin (text layer) says the bishop decided "to begin the formal canonical process to close the parish, which has been inactive for over a year now"; the decree took effect on 8 November 2025, merging St. Agnes into St. Ann, with relegation of the church "to a secular, but not sordid use" and "ultimately the sale of the property" to follow. The finder (2026-11-14) marks it "merged into St. Ann" and gives no Mass times, and St. Ann's 15 November 2026 bulletin schedules nothing there. GCatholic still lists the church.

`last_mass` is left empty: the letter says only that the parish had been inactive for over a year by October-November 2025, so the last Mass was in 2024 or earlier, and no source gives the date.

## What would change this

A St. Ann bulletin scheduling Mass at 100 Chatham St. A relegation decree, if found, would date the formal end of worship there.
