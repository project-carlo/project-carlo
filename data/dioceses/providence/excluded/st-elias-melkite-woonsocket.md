## Why it is excluded

An Eastern Catholic church of the Melkite Greek Catholic Eparchy of Newton, not of the Latin Diocese of Providence: GCatholic (church 16553, read 2026-11-16) gives its jurisdiction as 'Greek-Melkite Diocese of Newton', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Providence'. The README's reason table puts Eastern Catholic eparchy churches under `other-jurisdiction`. The diocesan finder does not list it.

Whether it still holds liturgy was not settled, and doesn't change the exclusion: GCatholic gives it St. Basil the Great, Lincoln's telephone number, the Discover Mass listing (read 2026-11-16) gives St. Basil's old website and only a COVID-era notice, and St. Basil's 15 November 2026 bulletin does not name it (see research). It is inferred, not read, that St. Elias is served from St. Basil.

Address: GCatholic and Discover Mass both give 80 Hamilton St., Woonsocket (Plus Code 87HCXFVR+HC).
