## Why it is excluded

An Eastern Catholic church of the Melkite Greek Catholic Eparchy of Newton, not of the Latin Diocese of Providence. GCatholic (church 16548, read 2026-11-16) gives its jurisdiction as 'Greek-Melkite Diocese of Newton', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Providence'; the diocesan finder (entry 12161, read 2026-11-14) gives its legal title with '(Melkite Greek Catholic Eparchy of Newton)'; and the parish's own site (read 2026-11-16) says it 'is a local parish of the Melkite Eparchy of Newton'. The README's reason table puts Eastern Catholic eparchy churches in the territory under `other-jurisdiction`.

It is open: the site posts weekly bulletins (15 November 2026 issue, 'SUN., November 15, 10:30am - Divine Liturgy'), and the finder lists Sunday 9:00 AM (English) and 11:15 AM (Arabic and Greek). The 15 November issue does not name St. Elias, Woonsocket (grep 'Elias', 'Woonsocket': no matches in its 9 text pages of 16), though GCatholic gives St. Elias the same telephone number.

Address: the finder, GCatholic and the parish site all give 15 Skyview Dr., Lincoln. GCatholic's listed website (stbasilmelkitecatholic.org) does not resolve (DNS, 2026-11-16); the parish uses stbasilthegreatchurch.com.

## What would change this

A policy to map Eastern Catholic churches; the bulletin at stbasilthegreatchurch.com/bulletins-2026.html would then be its source.
