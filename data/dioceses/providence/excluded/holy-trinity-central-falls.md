## Why it is excluded

The diocesan finder (entry 24222, read 2026-11-14) says "Holy Trinity closed 2007" and "Building demolished." Holy Trinity was one of the three parishes unified into Holy Spirit Parish on 9 November 2007 (finder 12123). The 15 November 2026 Holy Spirit bulletin schedules all its Masses at 1030 Dexter St. GCatholic lists no Holy Trinity church in Central Falls. last_mass is the year of the closure; no source gives the final Mass's date.

## What would change this
