## Why it is excluded

The diocesan finder (entry 24220, read 2026-11-14) says "Notre Dame closed 2007. Building sold." Notre Dame was one of the three parishes unified into Holy Spirit Parish on 9 November 2007 (finder 12123). The 15 November 2026 Holy Spirit bulletin schedules all its Masses at 1030 Dexter St.; the Notre Dame Cemetery it names is at 359 Daggett Ave., Pawtucket. last_mass is the year of the closure; no source gives the final Mass's date.

## What would change this

A source showing Catholic Mass again at 668 Broad St.; the building was sold, so this is unlikely.
