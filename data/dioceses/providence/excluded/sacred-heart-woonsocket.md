## Why it is excluded

The 15 November 2026 Holy Trinity bulletin names no Mass there. GCatholic (22472) still lists it as a church. The last Mass date isn't given; the closure took effect 1 July 2018.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a Holy Trinity bulletin scheduling a Mass at 415 Olo St.; the building has been sold.
