## Why it is excluded

The diocesan finder (entry 12197, read 2026-11-14) says Our Lady of Mount Carmel parish was "Closed July 1, 2016; merged into Holy Ghost Parish, Providence" and "The church of Our Lady of Mt. Carmel closed permanently effective July 1, 2016. Building sold." The 15 November 2026 Holy Ghost bulletin schedules no Mass there. `last_mass` is the year only: the finder dates the closure (1 July 2016), not the last Mass. The finder's address, 472 Atwells Ave., is Holy Ghost's rectory, where the records are kept; GCatholic gives the church at 64 Brayton Ave., which is recorded here.

## What would change this

A current bulletin or diocesan source scheduling Mass at 64 Brayton Ave. GCatholic still lists the church without a closure note, which is lag, not evidence of Mass.
