## Publisher

St. Joseph, Hope Valley publishes one weekly LPi bulletin (container 03/0423) for its own church and the churches of Our Lady of Victory, Ashaway and St. Vincent de Paul, Bradford, under Rev. Francesco Francese (pastor of St. Joseph, administrator of the other two per the finder). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/03/0423/20261115B.pdf, page 1) is headed with all three churches and their addresses and "Mailing Address for ALL CHURCHES PO Box 388, Hope Valley, RI 02832"; its Mass times give St. Joseph Saturday 5:30 pm and Sunday 10:30 am plus weekdays, "St. Vincent de Paul Saturday 4:00 pm" and "Our Lady of Victory Sunday 8:00 am".

A decree on the parish site merges the Parish of Our Lady of Victory into St. Joseph Parish (unio extinctiva) and keeps the Our Lady of Victory church "as an additional worship site of St. Joseph Parish". It does not change the row: the bulletin was already one.

## Worship sites

Three churches, all in the page 1 masthead and Mass times: St. Joseph (1105 Main St., Hope Valley), Our Lady of Victory (169 Main St., Ashaway) and St. Vincent de Paul (5 Church St., Bradford). The finder lists them as 12154, 12153 and 12249, GCatholic as 22369, 22368 and 22467. The St. Clare, Westerly site and LPi footer still carry St. Vincent de Paul's name (container 03/1399 'St. Clare St Vincent de Paul'), but the St. Pius X and St. Clare masthead does not list it; this bulletin does.
