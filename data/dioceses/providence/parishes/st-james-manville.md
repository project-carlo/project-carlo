## Publisher

St. James, Manville (Lincoln) publishes its own weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site (`ecatholic:35051`). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://files.ecatholic.com/35051/bulletins/20261115.pdf, 2 pages) lists St. James's Mass intentions for the weekend (Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM) and weekdays (Tuesday and Thursday 8:30 AM, Monday 6:00 PM at St. James), its own weekly collection, and on page 2 "Parish Office: 45 Division Street ... Manville, RI 02838" and www.saintjamesmanville.org. It also lists the days' Masses at St. Ambrose ("7:30 AM Mass at St. Ambrose Church") and a box "Mass Times at St. Ambrose Church": those belong to the St. Ambrose row.

The finder names Rev. Robert E. Lacombe parochial administrator of both St. James and St. Ambrose, Albion, and his weekly reflection runs in both issues, but the two are separate bulletins on separate eCatholic sites with separate intentions and collections (see `publication_reviews` on st-ambrose-albion).

## Worship sites

One church: St. James, 33 Division St., Manville. The finder (12162) and GCatholic (22376) list only that building. The finder gives the convent of the Sisters of the Adoration of the Blessed Sacrament at the same address; no separate public Mass there is listed.
