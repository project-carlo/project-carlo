## Publisher

St. Michael the Archangel posts its own weekly bulletin as PDFs on its WordPress site. The 15 November 2026 issue (https://saintmichaelprovidence.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Parish-Bulletin-New-Version-11-15-2026-Final.pdf, 2 pages), page 1, is headed "St. Michael The Archangel Church", "239 Oxford St., Providence, RI 02905 / Tel. 401-781-7210", with its bilingual Mass schedule.

## Worship sites

One church, 239 Oxford St. The schedule lists Spanish Saturday 6:00 PM and Sunday 12:00 PM, English 10:00 AM on the 1st, 3rd and 4th Sundays (African community the 2nd), Haitian Creole Sunday 4:00 PM, and weekday Spanish Masses. The finder puts some in the "Lower Church" and some in the "Upper Church": two levels of the one building. Adoration is in the rectory chapel. The finder (12210) and GCatholic (22427) list only this church.

Its pastor, Rev. Jaime A. Garcia, is also pastor of St. Charles Borromeo, but the two parishes publish separate bulletins: this issue never mentions St. Charles, and St. Charles's own 15 November 2026 bulletin carries only St. Charles (see `publication_reviews`).

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`, recipe `page-pdfs` on the parish's Bulletins page, which lists the PDFs newest first. Some October issues were uploaded twice (`...-Final.pdf` and `...-Final-1.pdf`), and one file name carries a wrong year (`...08-31-2025-Final.pdf` among the 2026 issues).
