## Publisher

Our Lady of Grace publishes its own weekly LPi bulletin on ParishesOnline (container 03/1238). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/03/1238/20261115B.pdf) opens with the masthead "Church of Our Lady of Grace / Sanctuary of La Madonna della Difesa / 4 Lafayette Street Johnston, RI 02919", Pastor Rev. Richard A. Narciso, and is dated "Thirty Third Sunday in Ordinary Times November 15, 2026".

The same priest is pastor of St. Robert Bellarmine, Johnston (the finder calls him administrator here), and the two parishes alternate weekday Masses (Mon/Tue at Our Lady of Grace, Wed/Fri at St. Robert Bellarmine, per both bulletins). They are still two bulletins: St. Robert Bellarmine's 15 November 2026 issue is a different document on eCatholic (site 14377) with its own masthead and weekend schedule. See `publication_reviews`.

## Worship sites

One church, Our Lady of Grace, 4 Lafayette Street: Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 9:30 AM, Monday and Tuesday 8:00 AM (page 2 Mass intentions). The Wednesday and Friday weekday Masses this bulletin lists are at St. Robert Bellarmine, that parish's own church. The finder (12156) and GCatholic (22371) list only this church.
