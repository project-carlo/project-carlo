## Worship sites

One church, St. Ann (Hawkins and Russo Streets). Page 1 of the 15 November issue: Saturday 4:00 PM English, 6:00 PM Spanish; Sunday 10:00 AM English, 12:00 PM Spanish; Monday-Thursday 8:00 AM (lower church); Wednesday 7:00 PM Spanish Eucharist after adoration. The page-2 intentions for 14-22 November list only these Masses. The 15 November 2026 masthead and schedule don't name it. It has its own `excluded/` record.
