## Publisher

St. Bartholomew posts its own weekly bulletin as PDFs on its Weebly site. The 15 November 2026 issue (https://www.stbartschurchri.org/uploads/9/6/2/5/96251596/st_barts_bulletin_november_15_2026_eng_.pdf, 3 pages, text layer) is headed "Saint Bartholomew Parish ~ Providence"; page 3 is dated "33RD SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME ... NOVEMBER 15, 2026" and page 2 is a Spanish section dated "NOV 15 2026". Despite the `_eng_` file name it carries both languages; no separate Spanish file is posted.

## Worship sites

One church, 305 Laurel Hill Avenue. The issue lists English Masses Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 11:30 am (page 3 intentions), and Spanish Masses Monday, Wednesday and Friday 7:00 pm, Saturday 6:30 pm, Sunday 9:30 am and 7:00 pm (page 2). The finder (12204) and GCatholic (22421) list only this church.
