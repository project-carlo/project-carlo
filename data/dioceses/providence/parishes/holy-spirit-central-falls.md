## Publisher

Holy Spirit Parish publishes its own bilingual weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (id 998). The finder's website for the parish is a Discover Mass page that lists no issues but links https://church-bulletin.org/?id=998, which redirects to the church-bulletin.org page listing dated issues newest first. The 15 November 2026 issue is image-only; rendered, page 1 reads "Holy Spirit Parish, Parroquia Del Espiritu Santo, 1030 Dexter St. Central Falls, RI 02863" with "Pastor/Parroco Rev. Otoniel Gomez", and page 2 "33rd Sunday in Ordinary Time, November 15, 2026".

An earlier lead noted that Rev. Gildardo Suarez of Assumption, Providence is temporary administrator during the pastor's sabbatical (to 21 November 2026, per the finder). The masthead names Holy Spirit alone, so this is not a shared bulletin.

## Worship sites

One church, Holy Spirit (the former St. Matthew church), 1030 Dexter St. The 15 November 2026 page 1 schedule lists Saturday 4:00 pm English and 6:00 pm Spanish, Sunday 9:00 am English and 10:30 am Spanish, and weekdays 6:30am Monday-Thursday in the chapel (the finder: "Rectory Chapel (side door)" at the same address), with no other location. The finder's Sunday times (8:00, 10:00 and 11:00 AM) are older than the bulletin's; the bulletin is followed.

The 2007 unification's other two churches are excluded: Holy Trinity, 325 Cowden St. (finder 24222: "Holy Trinity closed 2007. ... Building demolished.") and Notre Dame, 668 Broad St. (finder 24220: "Notre Dame closed 2007. Building sold."). Notre Dame Cemetery (359 Daggett Ave., Pawtucket) is a cemetery. St. Joseph, Central Falls (Polish) is a separate parish (row st-joseph-central-falls).
