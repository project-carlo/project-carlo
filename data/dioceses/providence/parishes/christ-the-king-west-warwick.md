## Publisher

Christ the King, West Warwick publishes a weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing at church-bulletin.org (linked from www.ctkwwri.org as `church-bulletin.org/?id=965`). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://www.church-bulletin.org/bulletin/church-of-christ-the-king-west-warwick-ri-02893/2026-11-15.pdf) is image-only; rendered, page 1 is headed "Church of Christ the King / 124 Legris Ave., West Warwick, Rhode Island 02893 / Rev. Jairon Olmos, Pastor".

Christ the King and St. Anthony "yoked in July, 2022" (same page) and share a pastor (Rev. Jairon G. Olmos, finder entries 12238 and 12241), and this page prints both parishes' Mass times. But St. Anthony publishes its own LPi bulletin (container 03/0311) with a Saint Anthony Church masthead, and this page says "the schedule will be in each parish bulletin", so the two are separate rows (see `publication_reviews`).

## Worship sites

One church, Christ the King, 124 Legris Ave. (Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 9:30 AM per the 15 November 2026 issue). The St. Anthony Masses this issue also lists belong to the St. Anthony row. The finder (12238) and GCatholic (22456) list only this church for the parish.
