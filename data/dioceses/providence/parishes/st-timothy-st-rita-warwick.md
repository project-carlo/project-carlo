## Publisher

St. Timothy and St. Rita, Warwick, two parishes under one pastor (Rev. Dean P. Perri, finder 12234 and 12236), publish one weekly LPi bulletin under St. Timothy's account (container 03/0355). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/03/0355/20261115B.pdf) is headed "SAINT TIMOTHY CATHOLIC CHURCH", gives the joint website www.sstimothyandrita.com and the Facebook page "St. Timothy and St. Rita Parishes", and its Mass intentions on page 2 cover St. Rita's own Masses (Saturday 5:30 p.m., Sunday 10:30 a.m.) as well as St. Timothy's. The joint site's Bulletin page embeds this container, and no St. Rita org exists on ParishesOnline, so this row is both parishes' bulletin.

## Worship sites

One church now: St. Rita, 722 Oakland Beach Ave. Since 31 October 2026 all of St. Timothy's Masses and confessions are at St. Rita because St. Timothy's heating system is broken (25 October and 15 November 2026 issues). St. Timothy church (1799 Warwick Ave.) keeps its Divine Mercy Adoration Chapel open weekdays but has no Mass; it is in `open/` until the bulletin shows Mass there again or says it will not return. St. William (200 Pettaconsett Ave.), whose records are kept at St. Timothy, closed on 16 April 2017 and was sold (finder 12237); it has its own `excluded/` record.

`parishes_online`, org `st-timothy-church-02889`. The finder's homepages for both parishes are dead (a 'Coming Soon' page and 'Temporarily Unavailable'); the current site is https://www.sstimothyandrita.com.
