## Publisher

St. Mary of the Bay publishes a weekly ParishesOnline (LPi) bulletin, container 03/0705. The 15 November 2026 issue (https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/03/0705/20261115B.pdf), page 1, gives "Father Joseph Upton, Ph.D., Pastor", the parish office at 645 Main Street, Warren, and the Mass schedule: "Monday through Friday at 8am / Saturday at 4pm / Sunday at 8am & 10:30am / Sabbath@Sundown Sunday evening Mass at 6pm / Sunday at 9:15am (Saint Alexander)".

St. Alexander merged into St. Mary of the Bay in July 2023 (finder 12225) and St. Thomas the Apostle on 11 May 2026 (finder 12227). The 3 May 2026 pastor's letter (page 2 of that issue) says the merged parish keeps "three separate worship sites". St. Thomas, though, still publishes its own bulletin (container 03/0224, masthead "St. Thomas the Apostle ... A MISSION OF SAINT MARY OF THE BAY PARISH", with its own Mass schedule and intentions), and this bulletin's schedule does not list St. Thomas's Masses. So St. Thomas is its own row (st-thomas-the-apostle-warren); see `publication_reviews`.

## Worship sites

- St. Mary of the Bay, 645 Main St.: weekday 8am, Saturday 4pm, Sunday 8am, 10:30am and 6pm.
- St. Alexander, 218 Main St.: Sunday 9:15am ("Sunday at 9:15am (Saint Alexander)", every issue checked from 3 May to 15 November 2026).

Ruled out: St. Thomas the Apostle, 500 Metacom Ave. (own bulletin, own row) and St. Jean Baptiste, 324 Main St. (closed 1 January 2018 and sold, per finder 24193; `excluded/st-jean-baptiste-warren`).
