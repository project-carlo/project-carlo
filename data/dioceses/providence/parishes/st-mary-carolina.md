## Publisher

St. Mary, Carolina (with St. James Chapel, Charlestown) posts its own weekly bulletin as PDFs on its WordPress site. The 15 November 2026 issue (https://stmjparish.org/wp-content/uploads/20261115B.pdf, read in text and rendered) is headed "SAINT MARY CATHOLIC CHURCH" with "Sunday, November 15, 2026"; its Mass schedule lists St. Mary's Church (Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:30 am) and St. James Chapel (Saturday 5:30 pm, Sunday 10:00 am, weekdays Monday-Friday 8:30 am). Page 2: "Reverend Paul Desmarais", Pastor, and Mass intentions for "St. Mary's" and "St. James". Page 2 also announces that the Friday 8:30 AM Mass at St. James "will be discontinued effective November 27, 2026" (weekday Masses Monday-Thursday after that).
