## Publisher

St. Adalbert posts its own weekly one-page bilingual (English/Polish) bulletin on its site. The 15 November 2026 issue (https://www.stadalberts.us/bulletins/bulletin_2026_11_15.pdf) has no masthead as such; it is identified by "From Fr. Marek" (Rev. Marek S. Kupka, pastor of St. Adalbert and of Our Lady of Lourdes per the finder), the footer "Please remember St. Adalbert's in your Will and Estate", and the Mass intentions for 16-22 November 2026.

## Worship sites

Two churches. St. Adalbert, 866 Atwells Ave.: Sunday 9:30 am (Polish) and 11:30 am (English), weekdays 8:00 am. Our Lady of Lourdes, 901 Atwells Ave.: the bulletin's "Saturday, November 21 4:00 pm (ENG) at OLL" and confession "Saturdays (3:30 - 3:45 pm) (at OLL)", and the Thanksgiving Mass "at 10:00 am at Our Lady of Lourdes Church". The parish's Mass schedule page says the same: "Saturday at 4:00 pm (English, at O. L. of Lourdes Church, 901 Atwells Ave".

Our Lady of Lourdes is canonically a separate parish (finder 12196, French, est. 1904) with the same pastor, but it has no website (the finder's link is a masstimes.org map) and no bulletin of its own turned up (ParishesOnline slugs `our-lady-of-lourdes-church-02909` and `our-lady-of-lourdes-02909`: none; a DuckDuckGo search for "Our Lady of Lourdes" "901 Atwells" found only directory listings). Its finder Mass times are Saturday 4:00 PM, the same Mass St. Adalbert's bulletin places there, and Sunday 6:30 AM, which neither this bulletin nor the St. Adalbert site mentions; see *Not yet confirmed*. So its church is recorded here, as the place this bulletin's Saturday Mass is held.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`, `page-pdfs` on the "Parish Bulletin" page (`?q=parish-news`), which links the last two issues as `bulletins/bulletin_YYYY_MM_DD.pdf`, oldest first; the dated file names let `fetch` choose the newest.

## Not yet confirmed

The finder's Sunday 6:30 AM Mass at Our Lady of Lourdes: no parish source mentions it. A St. Adalbert bulletin or schedule listing a Sunday Mass at OLL would settle it; it does not affect the row.
