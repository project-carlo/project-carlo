## Publisher

No current bulletin found. St. Joseph's own site (https://www.stjosephprovidence.org/) lists self-hosted bulletin PDFs whose newest is "October 8, 2023"; nothing later is linked anywhere on the site. Its administrator pro tem, Rev. Edward A. Sousa, Jr., is pastor of St. Sebastian, and St. Sebastian's 15 November 2026 bulletin names him "Parochial Administrator St. Joseph Parish, 92 Hope St., Providence" in its masthead, but it carries no St. Joseph Mass times, intentions or news. So St. Joseph does not share it.

## Worship sites

One church, 92 Hope St. The parish site gives Sunday Mass 10:00 AM and daily Mass Monday-Thursday 8:00 AM in the chapel, and the finder (12208) gives the same. The finder and GCatholic (22425) list only this church.
