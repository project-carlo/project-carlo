## Publisher

St. Anthony Parish, North Providence publishes one weekly bulletin for its three churches through John Patrick Publishing (id 904), also posted on its eCatholic site (22385). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://files.ecatholic.com/22385/bulletins/20261115.pdf), page 1, is headed "Saint Anthony Parish North Providence", "Church of the Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary" and "Saint Edward Church", with Rev. Edward S. Cardente, Pastor. Page 2's "WEEKEND MASS CELEBRATIONS" box gives Saint Anthony Church (Monday-Wednesday 8:30 AM in Trinity Chapel; Sunday 8:00 AM and 11:30 AM), the Church of the Presentation (Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM) and Saint Edward Church ("No Mass Until Further Notice"). The John Patrick copy of the same issue is the same bulletin with ad pages (rendered pages 1-2 compared).

## Worship sites

Two churches: St. Anthony (1413 Mineral Spring Ave.; Trinity Chapel is inside it) and Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary (1081 Mineral Spring Ave.). Presentation and St. Edward, Providence were merged into St. Anthony effective 31 December 2022, the diocese saying both buildings "will remain open and available as additional worship venues" (finder entries 12176, 12207).

St. Edward (997 Branch Ave., Providence) is not a church row now: the 31 May 2026 issue (https://files.ecatholic.com/22385/bulletins/20260531.pdf, page 3) announced "CANCELLATION OF 9 AM SUNDAY MASS AT SAINT EDWARD CHURCH ... Because Father Stephano will be away until the end of August, the 9AM Mass on Sunday in Saint Edward Church will no longer be celebrated until he returns", and every issue sampled since (31 May to 15 November 2026) says "No Mass Until Further Notice". The 24 May 2026 and earlier issues listed "Sunday 9 AM" there. Nothing says the church closed, so it is an `open/` record (st-edward-providence) to revisit. The finder still lists a Sunday 9 AM Mass at St. Edward (out of date).

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`, `page-pdfs` on the John Patrick church-bulletin.org page, which lists dated issues (`2026-11-15.pdf` back to `2024-11-17.pdf`) newest first, following the diocese's John Patrick rule (no Cloudflare). The John Patrick issues are image-only; the eCatholic copies (files.ecatholic.com/22385/bulletins/YYYYMMDD.pdf) carry a text layer, so `ecatholic:22385` is recorded as a second publication id of this bulletin.
