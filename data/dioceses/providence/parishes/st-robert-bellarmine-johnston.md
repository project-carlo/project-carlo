## Publisher

St. Robert Bellarmine posts its own weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site (14377). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://files.ecatholic.com/14377/bulletins/20261115.pdf) is headed "ST. ROBERT BELLARMINE CHURCH", Pastor Fr. Richard A. Narciso, 1804 Atwood Avenue, and page 2 is dated "Thirty-third Sunday in Ordinary Time November 15, 2026".

Fr. Narciso also has charge of Our Lady of Grace, Johnston, and the two share weekday Masses, but Our Lady of Grace publishes a separate LPi bulletin (container 03/1238) with its own masthead and weekend schedule. See `publication_reviews`.

## Worship sites

One church, 1804 Atwood Avenue: Saturday 5:15 PM, Sunday 8:00 and 11:00 AM, Wednesday and Friday 8:00 AM ("@ SRB"). The Monday and Tuesday Masses in its intentions are "@ OLG", Our Lady of Grace's own church. The finder (12158) and GCatholic (22373) list only this church.

## Website and bulletin provider

`ecatholic`, site 14377, whose homepage answers 403 to `probe url` (Cloudflare) and loads in a browser. Issues are at `files.ecatholic.com/14377/bulletins/YYYYMMDD.pdf` with a text layer.
