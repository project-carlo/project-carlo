## Publisher

SS. Rose and Clement, Warwick publishes a weekly LPi bulletin (container 03/0323). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/03/0323/20261115B.pdf) is image-only on pages 1-6; page 1, rendered, has the masthead "Saints Rose and Clement Parish / 111 Long Street, Warwick, Rhode Island 02886", Rev. Carl Fisette, Pastor, and one Mass schedule (Saturday Vigil 4:00PM; Sunday 8:00AM & 10:30AM; Monday-Friday 8:00AM). It names no other church.

## Worship sites

One church, the St. Clement church building at 111 Long St. (finder 12235, GCatholic 22453). The finder says "All Masses are celebrated at St. Clement Church, 111 Long Street" in the entries of the two parishes merged in on 1 July 2021, St. Catherine (3252 Post Rd.) and St. Francis of Assisi (618 Jefferson Blvd.); the finder says both buildings were sold in 2023, and each has its own `excluded/` record. The old St. Rose church (Main Ave., merged 1998) is in neither roster and is not named in the bulletin; it was not researched further.
