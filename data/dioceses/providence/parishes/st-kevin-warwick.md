## Publisher

St. Kevin, Warwick publishes a weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing. The 15 November 2026 issue (https://www.church-bulletin.org/bulletin/saint-kevin-roman-catholic-church-warwick-ri-02889/2026-11-15.pdf): page 1 "Saint Kevin Parish / 333 Sandy Lane Warwick, RI 02889"; page 2 Rev. Robert L. Marciano, KCHS, Pastor, Rev. Roland Simoneau as "Pastor Emeritus, St. Benedict", and one Mass schedule (Saturday Vigil 4 p.m.; Sunday 8 and 10 a.m.; weekday 7:45 a.m.). It names no other church.

## Worship sites

One church, St. Kevin, 333 Sandy Lane (finder 12232, GCatholic 22450). St. Benedict (135 Beach Ave.) merged into St. Kevin and its church closed permanently on 17 November 2021 (finder 12228; diocesan news release); it has its own `excluded/` record. St. Kevin School (39 Cathedral Rd.) is a school.
