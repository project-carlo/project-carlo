## Publisher

St. Ambrose, Albion (Lincoln) publishes its own weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site (`ecatholic:14382`). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://files.ecatholic.com/14382/bulletins/20261115.pdf, 5 pages) has the masthead on page 1: "St. Ambrose Church - Established 1892", rectory 191 School Street, Albion, RI 02802, Rev. Robert E. Lacombe, Parochial Administrator, and its Masses (Saturday Vigil 5:15PM, Sunday 8:30AM & 11:15AM; weekdays Mon/Wed/Fri 7:30AM, Tuesday 6:00PM).

The diocese's finder names Fr. Lacombe parochial administrator of both St. Ambrose and St. James, Manville. The two parishes print separate bulletins: St. James's 15 November issue is a different 2-page PDF on its own eCatholic site (`ecatholic:35051`) with its own intentions and collection. Each bulletin carries a box with the other church's Mass times ("Mass Times at St. James Church" here), so the pipeline may see St. James's times in this issue too; they belong to the St. James row.

## Worship sites

One church: St. Ambrose, 191 School St., Albion. The finder (12160) and GCatholic (22375) list only that building for this parish. Page 1 also mentions "Adoration at Ave Maria Chapel" (Friday 9AM-4PM) under the St. James box; it is an adoration chapel with no Mass listed, so it is not recorded.

## Website and bulletin provider

eCatholic: https://www.stambrosechurchri.org/ is the eCatholic-hosted site (behind Cloudflare; `probe url` got 403, `visit.py` loaded it). Issues are at `files.ecatholic.com/14382/bulletins/YYYYMMDD.pdf`.
