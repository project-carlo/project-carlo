## Publisher

St. John Paul II Parish publishes its own weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site (ecatholic:14522; the site answers 403 to probe url and was read with visit.py). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://files.ecatholic.com/14522/bulletins/20261115.pdf), page 1, reads "November 15, 2026| 33 Sunday of Ordinary Time", "755 Central Avenue, Pawtucket R.I. 02861", "St. John Paul II Parish, Pawtucket, Rhode Island", with Rev. Stephen Battey, Pastor.

Holy Family Parish (St. Joseph and Our Lady of Consolation churches) merged into St. John Paul II: the finder dates it 02-01-26, the parish's history page "December of 2025", when the decrees were announced (Rhode Island Catholic). Holy Family publishes no bulletin of its own now: its site holyfamilypawtucket.org no longer resolves, its Discover Mass page lists no bulletins, and the finder gives saintjohnpaulri.com as its website. The St. John Paul II bulletin is the merged parish's only one.

## Worship sites

One church, St. Cecilia, Central Ave. at Newport Ave. The 15 November 2026 page 1 schedule ("Daily Mass: Mon, Tues, Thurs, Fri: 5:15pm, Sat: 8:00am; Weekend Mass: Sat: 4:30pm, Sun: 8am, 10am, 12 noon") names no building, and the finder says "As of January 12, 2019 All Services are held at St. Cecilia Church building". The St. Teresa Adoration Chapel is inside St. Cecilia church (Newport Avenue entrance), not a separate site.

Not here:
- St. Joseph, 195 Walcott St. and Our Lady of Consolation, 211 Sabin St., the Holy Family churches. The four issues posted (25 October to 15 November 2026) and the parish site name no Mass at either; the finder's Holy Family entry still lists Masses there (Saturday 4:30 and 5:30 PM Spanish and Sunday 11:00 AM at St. Joseph, Sunday 9:00 AM at Our Lady of Consolation), and those times clash with St. John Paul II's own 4:30 PM Saturday Mass, so the entry looks unrevised. No source says either church closed: `open/st-joseph-pawtucket` and `open/our-lady-of-consolation-pawtucket`. The bulletin still lists the Holy Family Food Pantry at 195 Walcott Street.
- St. Leo the Great, closed 6 January 2019 and sold (`excluded/st-leo-the-great-pawtucket`).
- Sacred Heart of Jesus, 38 Park St., relegated and sold in 2009 (`excluded/sacred-heart-pawtucket`).
- St. Cecilia School (755 Central Ave., also the parish office) and the Holy Union sisters' residence (760 Central Ave.) are not public worship sites.
