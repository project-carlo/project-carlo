## Publisher

St. Joseph, West Warwick (Natick) publishes its weekly bulletin through LPi (container 03/0824, org `st-joseph-church-02893`). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/03/0824/20261115B.pdf) is headed "SAINT JOSEPH CHURCH / 854 Providence Street-Natick / West Warwick, RI 02893" with Pastor "The Reverend Gregory P. Stowe" and "Saturday Vigil: 4:00 PM", Sunday 7:15, 8:30 and 11:00 AM, weekdays 7:00 AM.

Our Lady of Good Counsel and SS. Peter and Paul merged into St. Joseph on 8 November 2025 (finder entries 12239, 12245). Their churches were relegated to secular use and "closed permanently" by decrees of 18 February 2026 posted on stjosephww.org; both had had no regular weekend Masses since 1 January 2025. They have `excluded/` records.

Fr. Stowe is also administrator pro tem of Sacred Heart, West Warwick (finder 12240), next door at 820 Providence St. Sacred Heart publishes its own bulletin (ParishesOnline uploads, org `sacred-heart-church-02893`) with a Sacred Heart masthead and its own Mass times, so it is a separate row (see `publication_reviews`). The 15 November St. Joseph issue does not mention Sacred Heart.

## Worship sites

One church, St. Joseph, 854 Providence St. The bulletin's schedule and Mass intentions name no other building. Our Lady of Good Counsel (60 Pleasant St.) and SS. Peter and Paul (48 Highland St.), which the finder and GCatholic still list, are excluded as closed.
