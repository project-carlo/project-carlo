## Publisher

St. Charles Borromeo posts its own weekly bulletin on its GoDaddy-built site, in two editions: an English bulletin and a Spanish boletín, both PDFs on img1.wsimg.com. The 15 November 2026 English issue (https://img1.wsimg.com/blobby/go/adffab78-4c49-496f-aac4-3aa01157b2fe/downloads/ec0e4b87-eef8-45f2-8657-23af0be28843/English%20bulletin%20November%2015-2026.pdf?ver=1794596505612), page 1 (read rendered), is headed "Thirty-Third Sunday in Ordinary Time November 15th, 2026". It carries the week's Mass intentions and a flyer naming "St. Charles Church 178 Dexter St. Providence, RI 02907". The issue has no formal masthead naming the pastor.

## Worship sites

One church, 178 Dexter St. The 15 November intentions list Saturday 6:00 PM (Spanish), Sunday 8:00 AM (Spanish), 10:00 AM, 12:00 PM (Spanish) and 6:00 PM (Spanish), and weekday Masses, all with no other place named. The finder (12206) and GCatholic (22423) list only this church.

Its pastor, Rev. Jaime A. Garcia, is also pastor of St. Michael the Archangel, but each parish publishes its own bulletin: this one never mentions St. Michael, and St. Michael's own 15 November 2026 bulletin carries only St. Michael (see `publication_reviews`).

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`, recipe `page-pdfs` on the parish's bulletin page with `filename=English*.pdf`, so fetch takes the English edition and not the Spanish one. The page lists issues oldest first. `fetch:check` on 2026-11-15 printed `page-pdfs on https://stcharleschurchprovidence.com/bulletin%2Fboletines: current` and downloaded the 15 November English issue. It reported a date conflict because the issue quotes a "Medjugorje Message, October 25, 2026". Pages 2-3 and the masthead art are images.
