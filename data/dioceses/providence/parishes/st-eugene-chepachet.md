## Publisher

St. Eugene, Chepachet publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (id 927). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://www.church-bulletin.org/bulletin/saint-eugene-church-chepachet-ri-02814/2026-11-15.pdf) has an image cover ("Church / November 15, 2026"); page 2 is headed "St. Eugene Roman Catholic Church" and lists the week's Masses: Sunday 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM, Thursday and Friday 7:45 AM, Saturday 8:30 AM. Page 3 names Fr. Dandeneau and the St. Eugene Church Parish Office.

## Worship sites

One church, St. Eugene, 1247 Putnam Pike. The finder (12152) and GCatholic (22367) list only this church in Glocester. Rev. Stephen J. Dandeneau is pastor here and administrator of St. Joseph, Pascoag; the two share faith formation but publish separate bulletins (St. Joseph: John Patrick id 958, row st-joseph-pascoag), so they are separate rows (see publication_reviews).
