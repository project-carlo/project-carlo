## Publisher

Sacred Heart, East Providence publishes a weekly John Patrick Publishing bulletin on https://www.church-bulletin.org/sacred-heart-church-east-providence-ri-02914 (linked from sacredheartepri.com as church-bulletin.org/9025). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://www.church-bulletin.org/bulletin/sacred-heart-church-east-providence-ri-02914/2026-11-15.pdf, 8 pages, images; pages 1-2 read rendered) is headed "Sacred Heart Church / Served by Servants of Charity Fathers / 118 Taunton Avenue / East Providence, RI 02914". ANANDARAYAR, SdC Pastor" and gives "WEEKEND MASSES Saturday: 3:30 pm (City View Manor) & 5:00 pm / Sunday: 9:00 am & 11:30 am", daily Mass Monday-Friday 7:00 am and Saturday 8:00 am, holy days 9:00 am and 7:00 pm "(Church)".

## Worship sites

Sacred Heart church, Taunton Ave. The "Chapel" (Eucharistic adoration) and the "Senhor Santo Cristo Chapel" are in the parish's own buildings, not separate Mass sites. The Saturday 3:30 pm Mass at City View Manor, a nursing home, is listed among the weekend Masses; whether it is open to the public is not stated, so it has an `open/` record (city-view-manor-east-providence) rather than a church row. The finder (12145) and GCatholic (22360) list only the church; the finder also lists the 3:30 PM Mass "Held at City View Manor".
