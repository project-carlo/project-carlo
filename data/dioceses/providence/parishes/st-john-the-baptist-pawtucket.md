## Publisher

St. John the Baptist publishes its own bilingual (English/Spanish) bulletin, posted on its Wix site's "Message & Bulletin - Mensaje y Boletin" page as a single link, "Click here for this weeks bullentin". On 15 November 2026 the page carried the pastor's column for the 33rd Sunday (15 November), but the linked PDF is the issue of 25 October 2026: page 2 is the "Biblical Commentary for the 30th Sunday in Ordinary Time" and page 3's intentions run from "Saturday October 24" to "Sunday November 1". Its page 1 masthead reads "Saint John the Baptist Parish / Parroquia San Juan Bautista, 69 Quincy Ave., Pawtucket, RI 02860", Rev. Mauricio Romero Viquez, Pastor.

St. Mary of the Immaculate Conception merged into this parish by decree of 11 December 2025 (finder 12184 and 12186; Rhode Island Catholic). The bulletin names no St. Mary church or Pine Street (grepped "Mary" and "Pine": only intentions); St. Mary's own site (saintmaryri.org) no longer resolves and its Discover Mass page lists no bulletins, so there is no separate St. Mary bulletin.

## Worship sites

One church, St. John the Baptist, 69 Quincy Ave. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's page 1 and the site's Mass & Confession page (15 November 2026) list every Mass with no other location; the Saturday 8:00am Mass and confessions are in the chapel of the same church. The finder (12184) and GCatholic (22401) list only this church for the parish.

Ruled out here: St. Mary of the Immaculate Conception, 103 Pine St. (no Mass in the bulletin or on the site, but no source says it closed; `open/st-mary-pawtucket`), St. Edward, 58 Hancock St. (sold 2021; `excluded/st-edward-pawtucket`) and St. Maria Goretti, 155 Power Rd. (closed 2016; `excluded/st-maria-goretti-pawtucket`), both former St. Mary churches.
