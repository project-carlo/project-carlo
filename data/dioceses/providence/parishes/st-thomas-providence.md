## Publisher

St. Thomas, Providence publishes one weekly bulletin for itself and Holy Cross through John Patrick Publishing (id 948). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://www.church-bulletin.org/bulletin/saint-thomas-catholic-church-providence-ri-02909/2026-11-15.pdf) is image-only. Read rendered, page 1 is headed "St. Thomas Catholic Church", with "MASSES - St. Thomas Monday through Friday 7:30 a.m. Saturday Evening 4:00 p.m. | Sunday 10:30 a.m.", "MASS - Holy Cross Sunday 9:30 a.m.", "65 Fruit Hill Avenue Providence, Rhode Island 02909" and Rev. Przemyslaw "Shemek" Lepak, Pastor. Page 2 gives the intentions ("HC 9:30am" on 15 and 22 November) and separate collections for St. Thomas and "Holy Cross Church".

## Worship sites

Two churches: St. Thomas (65 Fruit Hill Ave.) and Holy Cross (645 Hartford Ave.). Holy Cross merged into St. Thomas in April 2018 as a mission church, and the finder (12193) says "Later 2018: Holy Cross parish closed" and labels it "(closed)". The parish is gone, but the building still has a Sunday 9:30 a.m. Mass in the current bulletin, so it is a church of this row.
