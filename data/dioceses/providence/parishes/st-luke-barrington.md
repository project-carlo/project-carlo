The 15 November 2026 issue (https://www.church-bulletin.org/bulletin/saint-luke-church-barrington-ri-02806/2026-11-15.pdf, 12 pages, every page an image; page 1 read rendered) is headed "St. Luke's Parish / 108 Washington Road / Barrington, Rhode Island 02806", "Pastor Fr. Marcel Taillon", "Assistant Pastor Fr. Mateusz Puzanowski".

Fr. Taillon is also administrator of Holy Angels, Barrington (finder entry 12114), and this masthead page prints Holy Angels' schedule beside St. Luke's ("Holy Angels Church / 341 Maple Avenue ... Saturday - 4:00 pm (Vigil) / Sunday - No Mass / Weekday Mass Tuesday, Thursday - 12:05 pm"; St. Luke's weekday Masses are "at Holy Angels" during St. Luke's restoration). Holy Angels nevertheless posts a bulletin of its own on its site (11 October 2026 issue: masthead "Holy Angels Parish", 4 pages, Holy Angels' Mass intentions only). Two documents with two mastheads: two rows (see `publication_reviews`). Holy Angels church is on the Holy Angels row, whose own bulletin covers it; that placement is my judgement, since St. Luke's issue also lists its times. If Holy Angels stops posting its own issue, merge it into this row (its record carries the revisit).
