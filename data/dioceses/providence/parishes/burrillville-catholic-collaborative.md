## Publisher

The Burrillville Catholic Collaborative publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (id 9012). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://www.church-bulletin.org/bulletin/the-burrillville-catholic-collaborative-burrillville-ri-02830/2026-11-15.pdf), page 1, is headed "The Burrillville Catholic Collaborative", "Three Churches, One Faith Community.", and lists "Our Lady of Good Help Church- 1063 Victory Highway, Mapleville 02839", "St. Theresa Church and Shrine- 35 Dion Drive, Harrisville 02830" and "St. Patrick Church- 45 Harrisville Main Street, Harrisville 02830", with Pastor Rev. Jose Parathanal, C.M.I. Page 2 gives the Mass intentions for all three churches.
