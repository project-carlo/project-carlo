## Publisher

St. Mary publishes its own weekly ParishesOnline (LPi) bulletin, container 03/0709. The 15 November 2026 issue (https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/03/0709/20261115B.pdf), page 1, is headed "ST. MARY'S CATHOLIC CHURCH / 330 Wood St., Bristol, RI", "Pastor: Rev. Henry P. Zinno, Jr.", "Weekday Mass 8:00 AM / Weekend Masses / Saturday: 4:00PM / Sunday: 8:30AM and 11:30AM". Page 2 has St. Mary's own Mass intentions.
