## Publisher

St. Joseph, Pascoag publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (id 958). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://www.church-bulletin.org/bulletin/saint-joseph-roman-catholic-church-pascoag-ri-02859/2026-11-15.pdf), page 1, ends with the masthead "Saint Joseph Roman Catholic Church / 183 Sayles Ave Pascoag RI 02859 / 401-568-2411 / November 14-15, 2026" and gives "HOLY EUCHARIST (Mass ) Saturday 4:00 P.M. & Sunday 9:15 A.M. Monday and Tuesday 7:45 A.M.", with Reverend Stephen J. Dandeneau as Parish Administrator.

## Worship sites

One church, St. Joseph, 183 Sayles Ave. The finder (12120) and GCatholic (22336) list only this church. Fr. Dandeneau is also pastor of St. Eugene, Chepachet, and the two share a faith formation program ("The year will start with classes at St Joseph and finish at St Eugene"), but St. Eugene publishes a separate bulletin (John Patrick id 927, row st-eugene-chepachet) with its own Mass intentions, so the two are separate rows (see publication_reviews). The finder's St. Joseph entry also gives a Sunday 8:00 AM Mass "At St. Eugene Church"; St. Eugene's own bulletin carries that Mass.
