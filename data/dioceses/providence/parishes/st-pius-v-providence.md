## Publisher

St. Pius V, Providence (Dominican friars) publishes its weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline (LPi container 03/0230). The 15 November 2026 issue (https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/03/0230/20261115B.pdf) has the masthead "St. Pius V Church, 240 Eaton Street., Providence, RI 02908". Page 2 names Rev. John Maria Devaney, O.P., and gives weekend Masses Saturday 4:30 p.m. (Vigil) and Sunday 8 a.m. and 10 a.m., daily Mass Monday-Friday at 12 Noon and Saturday at 8 a.m., and the Mass intentions for 14-22 November.

## Worship sites

One church. The bulletin names no other place, and the finder (12212) and GCatholic (22429) list only this church. The Dominican Sisters' convent (30 Elmhurst Ave.) and St. Pius V School are not public worship sites.
