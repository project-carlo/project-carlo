## Why it is excluded

Transfiguration closed with a final Mass of Thanksgiving at 5:30 pm on Monday 5 May 2025, and its
parish merged into St. John the Baptist the next day (Rome Sentinel, 2 and 6 May 2025, reporting
the bishop's decree). St. John's is now part of Sacred Heart Parish, whose 27 September 2026
bulletin lists four churches and not this one. The diocese's list marks it "CLOSED - See Sacred
Heart Parish".
