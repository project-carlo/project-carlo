## Why it is excluded

Closed and sold. St. Agnes merged with St. Michael (Central Square) in 2013 to form Divine Mercy, and
The Catholic Sun's report of the new church's dedication (13 October 2016) says "the church in
Brewerton" was sold. The diocese lists "St. Agnes, Brewerton (CLOSED - See Divine Mercy ...)", and
Divine Mercy's current bulletin (27 September 2026) schedules no Mass in Brewerton. No street
address or last-Mass date was found; GCatholic does not list it.

Nothing expected.
