## Why it is excluded

Relegated to profane use by decree. Bishop Lucia's decree of 20 June 2024 reduces "the church of Sacred
Heart of the Parish of Christ the Good Shepherd ... to profane but not sordid use" and opens the way to
its sale; the mission itself had been merged into Christ the Good Shepherd in 2021. A church reduced to
profane use cannot hold Mass. CTGS's current schedule (27 September 2026) has no Scriba Mass, and the
diocese lists it as "CLOSED". GCatholic still lists it as a mission at 3449 County Route 176 (the
address used here). No last-Mass date was found; it was before 20 June 2024.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
