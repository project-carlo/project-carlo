## Why it is excluded

A campus ministry with a weekly Sunday Mass: the 20 September 2026 bulletin of Holy Trinity
& Sacred Heart - St. Mary's invites college students "from Utica University, SUNY Poly, MVCC,
and all area colleges" to "Sunday Mass at 8:00 p.m. at the Francis A. Wilcox Intercultural &
Student Organization Center (the former Newman Center)", from 6 September; Mary, Mother of
Our Savior's bulletin lists the same event. It is in no roster. Like the Syracuse University
Catholic Center (`excluded/alibrandi-catholic-center-syracuse.md`) it holds Mass open to its
community but publishes nothing the pipeline can read: parish bulletins only advertise it,
and it has an Instagram account and an email address. So `no-bulletin`. It is a
semester-time Mass aimed at students; whether it counts as public Mass is a judgement, made
here the same way as for the Alibrandi Center.

The address is Utica University's main campus address (1600 Burrstone Road); Utica
University's page confirms the Wilcox Center is the former Newman Center building but gives
no street number.
