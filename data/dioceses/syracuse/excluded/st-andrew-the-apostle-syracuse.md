## Why it is excluded

Closed, merged into St. Lucy's around 2008-2009. St. Lucy's own history page gives "the
merger with the younger St. Andrew the Apostle parish in 2008"; the Catholic Sun (February
2010) says St. Andrew's "officially closed some months later" than Our Lady of Solace; the
diocese's list marks it "CLOSED - See St. Lucy". St. Lucy's current bulletin has Masses
only at 432 Gifford Street.

**Address not established.** The directory entry has none (its map pin is a generic
downtown point it also gives Holy Trinity and St. John the Evangelist). A ParishesOnline
organization `st-andrew-the-apostle-13205` gives 6136 S Salina St, but that is the address
of St. Andrew's **Anglican** Church (standrewscny.org/contact), so it may be a mix-up; it is
not used.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; a source for the former church's street address would complete it.
