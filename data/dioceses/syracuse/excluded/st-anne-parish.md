## Why it is excluded

Merged away in the early 2000s. The parish's own history says "St. Anne Mother of Mary was formed from
two parishes in the early 2000s, St. Anne's in Parish New York and St. Mary's Star of the Sea in Mexico"
and that its church "was consecrated as St. Mary's Star of the Sea", i.e. the Mexico building. The
current bulletin (27 September 2026) lists Masses only in Mexico and Pulaski, and the diocese lists
"St. Anne, Parish" as "CLOSED". Not in GCatholic. No address or date found; the town (Parish, NY) is
recorded.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
