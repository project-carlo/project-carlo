## Why it is excluded

St. Andrew's parish was merged with St. John the Evangelist to form the Parish of Saints John and Andrew, which worships at the former St. John the Evangelist church at 1263 Vestal Avenue. The diocese's parish list marks St. Andrew "CLOSED - See The Parish of Saints John & Andrew", the diocese keeps its records there, and the successor's 27 September 2026 bulletin has a single Mass schedule at 1263 Vestal Avenue with nothing at St. Andrew's. A search summary described the church as "St. Andrew church (Main St.), now permanently closed", but I could not read that page, so the street is not recorded, and I found no date for the last Mass.
