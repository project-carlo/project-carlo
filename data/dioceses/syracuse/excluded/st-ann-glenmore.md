## Why it is excluded

The diocese lists "St. Ann Mission, Glenmore/Annsville (CLOSED - See St. Patrick, Taberg)", and its page
for St. Patrick, Taberg says the mission's sacramental records are kept there. St. Patrick publishes no
bulletin (`excluded/st-patrick-taberg.md`); the only schedule found for it, on Discover Mass, lists
Masses at St. Patrick only. GCatholic does not list St. Ann. The ParishesOnline organization is still
named "St. Patrick / St. Anne", but account names accumulate old members and it publishes nothing.

This is the diocese's statement plus an absence from the successor's (second-hand) schedule; there is
no parish bulletin to confirm it against, and no date was found. OSM keeps an old GNIS point, "Saint
Anns Church", north of Taberg.

## What would change this

A St. Patrick bulletin or schedule listing a Mass at St. Ann's.
