## Why it is excluded

Closed and demolished. CTGS's history page says "St. Louis' celebrated its last Mass on December 31,
2000", and CNY Central reported on 25-26 July 2010 that the vacant former St. Louis Church at East 4th
Street and Route 104 (Bridge Street) partly collapsed and was demolished. The diocese lists it as
"CLOSED - See Christ the Good Shepherd". Not in GCatholic.

Nothing expected.
