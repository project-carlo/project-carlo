## Why it is excluded

Closed in 2008. Wikipedia (citing the Post-Standard of 20 June 2008, "St. Peter in
Syracuse to close") says St. Peter's Italian Church, 130 North State Street, merged with
Our Lady of Pompei in 2008; the diocese's list marks it "CLOSED - See Our Lady of
Pompei/St. Peter", and that parish's current schedule has Masses only at 301 Ash Street
(and at St. John the Baptist / Holy Trinity, its sister parish). No last-Mass date found.
