## Why it is excluded

The diocese's parish list marks St. Casimir "CLOSED - See Prince of Peace, Endicott", and Prince of Peace keeps its records. The 2026 decree forming Prince of Peace names four churches and not St. Casimir, and both Prince of Peace bulletins of 27 September 2026 (container 06/0139 and the North Endicott bulletin on Discover Mass) list Mass only at those four; the North Endicott staff list still names a trustee "(St. Casimirs)". The address is from a Mass-times listing that marks it closed; OSM has the building on the Witherill Street side. I found no date for the last Mass.
