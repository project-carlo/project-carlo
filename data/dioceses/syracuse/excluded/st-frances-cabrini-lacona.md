## Why it is excluded

Merged away in 2007. The parish's own history says "Christ Our Light was formed from two parishes in
2007, namely St. Frances Cabrini in Lacona and St. John the Evangelist in Pulaski", and Christ Our Light
worships in the Pulaski church. The current bulletin (27 September 2026) lists Masses only at Christ
Our Light and St. Anne, Mother of Mary; the diocese lists the Lacona parish as "CLOSED - See Christ Our
Light". Not in GCatholic. No address or last-Mass date found; the city is recorded.
