## Why it is excluded

The Polish parish of St. Stanislaus Kostka was merged into the Church of the Holy Trinity on 1 July 2003, and Holy Trinity's own history page dates the church's closing Mass to 30 May 2004. The diocese's parish list marks it "CLOSED - See Church of the Holy Trinity", and the current bulletin of Holy Trinity and SS. Cyril & Methodius (27 September 2026) lists Mass at those two churches only. The finder entry has no address and I did not find the church's street address; the history page says the church was dedicated on 31 October 1954.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
