## Why it is excluded

St. John the Evangelist in Bainbridge is an open parish, led by a deacon administrator with a
canonical pastor in Vestal (diocese page, read 2026-09-26), with Sunday Mass. It is excluded only
because no bulletin could be read anywhere:

- The site the diocese links (sites.google.com/saintjohn-saintagnes.org) has not been updated since
  2020, and its "Bulletin & Updates" page embeds a private Google Drive file (sign-in required).
- Its ParishesOnline organization (st-john-the-evangelist-13733) has no publications, and its
  Discover Mass listing says "There are no bulletins available".
- Web searches for its bulletin found only these and a Facebook page that needs a login.

Mass: Discover Mass (MassTimes data) lists Sunday 9:30 am; catholicmasstime.org says Sunday 11 am.
Neither is the parish's own current schedule, so the time is unsettled, but both, the diocese and
the parish's own old site treat it as an active church. It is not `closed`.

The diocese lists Fr. Serowik (Vestal, region G) as canonical pastor of Bainbridge and of Greene.
Greene's newsletter names only Greene. I did not read the Vestal parishes' bulletins; if one of them
prints Bainbridge's schedule, that would give this church a publisher (region G to check).

## What would change this

A readable bulletin (see `revisit`): then a parish row with this church.
