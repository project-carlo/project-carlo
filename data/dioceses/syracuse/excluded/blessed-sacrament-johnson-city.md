## Why it is excluded

Blessed Sacrament, Johnson City, was linked with St. Vincent de Paul in 2010 and merged into it on 1 January 2012; the successor's history page dates the "final prayer service at Blessed Sacrament and transfer of the Holy Eucharist" to 10 December 2011. That was a prayer service, not necessarily a Mass, so `last_mass` is left empty. The diocese's parish list marks it "CLOSED", and St. Vincent de Paul Blessed Sacrament's 27 September 2026 bulletin has one church, 465 Clubhouse Road, Vestal. OSM still has the building (Cenacle Plaza); I found no street number.

Nothing expected.
