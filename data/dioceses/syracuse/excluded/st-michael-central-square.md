## Why it is excluded

Closed as a church by 2016. St. Michael's and St. Agnes (Brewerton) merged in 2013 to form Divine
Mercy; the parish then built its new church onto the parish hall across Main Street and dedicated it
in October 2016. The Catholic Sun's report of the dedication calls the old building "the former St.
Michael's Church, which now serves as a residence for Pastor". The current bulletin (27 September
2026) schedules Mass only at Sacred Heart and Divine Mercy, the 2023 decree names Divine Mercy the
parish's sole church, and the diocese's finder lists "St. Michael (Closed)". No street address was
found (the diocese's finder gives none and GCatholic does not list it); it is on Main Street,
Central Square, facing Divine Mercy. No last-Mass date was found.
