## Why it is excluded

Closed in 2007. Holy Trinity's history says "St. Michael's Parish remained until June 24, 2007" and
that in the 2006 consolidation "St. Michael's closed, and its parishioners joined Holy Family"; Holy
Trinity was then formed in the Immaculate Conception church. The current Holy Trinity & St. Stephen
bulletin (27 September 2026) lists Masses only at Holy Trinity and St. Stephen, and the diocese lists
St. Michael as "CLOSED". `last_mass` is left empty: 24 June 2007 is the date the parish ended, which is
probably, but not stated to be, its last Mass. The address is OSM's.
