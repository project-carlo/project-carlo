## Why it is excluded

Christ the King and Our Lady of Angels merged in 2008 to form the Church of the Holy Family (GCatholic dates Holy Family to 2008; the diocese keeps both parishes' records there), which worships in the former Our Lady of Angels church at 3600 Phyllis Street. The diocese's parish list marks Christ the King "CLOSED - See Church of the Holy Family", and Holy Family's 27 September 2026 bulletin lists no Mass at Christ the King. Its old ParishesOnline organization (1501 Davis Ave) publishes nothing. I found no date for the last Mass.
