## Why it is excluded

No public Mass any more. St. Joseph (founded 1914 for Oswego's Italian Catholics; church dedicated 1958) was one of the four parishes merged into Christ the Good Shepherd
by the decree of 21 May 2020 (effective 31 May 2020), which made St. Paul's the parish church and kept
this church open only for Mass "on the anniversary of the dedication and on the patronal feast" and
for private worship, baptisms, weddings and funerals. It is now one of CTGS's "heritage churches":

- The current CTGS schedule (27 September 2026 bulletin) has Masses at four places, not this one.
- The 21 June 2026 bulletin announces "We are no longer able to celebrate baptisms, weddings or
  funerals at St. Peter's, St. Stephen's or St. Joseph's. Going forward all sacraments ... will be at
  CTGS @ St. Paul's" (in 2025 the bulletin still offered them there).
- No patronal-feast Mass here appears in the 2026 issues I grepped (15 and 22 March, around St. Joseph's day, 19 March).
- The diocese's list says "CLOSED - See Christ the Good Shepherd"; the directory marks it "(Closed)".

The building still stands and is owned by the parish, so this is "closed" in the sense of no Mass,
not demolished or sold. No last-Mass date was found.

## What would change this

A dedication-anniversary or patronal-feast Mass announced in the CTGS bulletin: then it becomes a
church row of `christ-the-good-shepherd-oswego` (the 2020 decree allows one).

Address: GCatholic's 178 W 2nd St.; OSM has the church as 240 West 1st Street and the parish uses
240 West Second Street for the parish center next door.
