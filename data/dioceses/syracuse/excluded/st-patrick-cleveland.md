## Why it is excluded

Closed. The diocese's parish list has "St. Patrick, Cleveland (CLOSED - See Divine Mercy Parish &
The Mission of St. Bernadette, Central Square)", a separate line from St. Mary of the Assumption,
Cleveland. Divine Mercy's current bulletin (27 September 2026) schedules no Mass in Cleveland, and
the 2023 decree names Divine Mercy the parish's sole church. No roster lists it, and I found no
address, date or other trace of the building; it is recorded so that the diocese's list is fully
accounted for. Whether it was a separate church building or only a canonical parish is not known.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. If a source shows it was the same building as St. Mary of the Assumption, merge
the two records.
