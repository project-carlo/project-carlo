## Why it is excluded

St. Mary's, Irish Ridge, has had no regular Mass since 2000. The parish's own page (read
2026-09-26) says "In the year 2000 the Dioceses of Syracuse, short of priests and funds, decided
to close St. Mary's", after which descendants and neighbours repaired it as "a spiritual gathering
place". The diocese now calls it a devotional chapel. The Spirit of Hope bulletin lists it only
among the parish's cemeteries (22 June 2025 and later issues), and no issue I read (eight from
June to September 2025, and the 27 September 2026 masthead) schedules a Mass there. The 2023
merger decree kept it as an auxiliary church with right of entry for worship, but did not reserve
the Eucharist there.

## What would change this

A Mass at the chapel (for example an annual cemetery or heritage Mass) in the bulletin. That
would make it a church row with occasional Mass.
