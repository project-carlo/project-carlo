## Why it is excluded

Syracuse University's Catholic center holds public Mass (Sunday 10:30 am at the center, 1 pm at
Hendricks Chapel, and two weekday Masses), so it is in scope under the rule that a place where
Mass happens counts whatever it is called. It is excluded only because it publishes nothing the
pipeline can read: all seven pages of sucatholic.org (Home, About, Worship, Gather, Serve, Grow,
Give) were fetched on 2026-09-25, none contains a bulletin, newsletter archive or PDF, and the
Mass times sit in the homepage text. This is `no-bulletin`, not `closed` and not
`no-public-mass`.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule document the pipeline could fetch (see `revisit`).
