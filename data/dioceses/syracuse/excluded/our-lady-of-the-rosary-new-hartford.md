## Why it is excluded

**Our Lady of the Rosary, 1736 Burrstone Rd, New Hartford: closed, last Mass 2026-07-16.**
The bishop's decree of 4 June 2026 (published 16 June, effective 16 July 2026) merged its
parish into Mary, Mother of Our Savior and reduced the church "to profane but not sordid
use", naming Our Lady of Lourdes "the sole church of the parish". The parish's 12 July
bulletin lists the "Final 5PM Sunday Mass @ Rosary" that evening and the closing "Mass of
Thanksgiving @ Rosary" with Bishop Lucia on Thursday 16 July, 5:30 pm; the 19 July masthead
has every Mass "at Lourdes" and looks back on the Mass of Thanksgiving; the 20 September
bulletin lists Lourdes only. The diocese's list marks it "CLOSED - See Mary, Mother of Our
Savior Parish". The property is to be sold.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
