## Why it is excluded

Closed in June 2010 and now a soup kitchen. Wikipedia (citing the Post-Standard's June
2010 report of its last Mass) says the church at 215 North State Street closed in June 2010
and merged with the Cathedral, and that since 2015 the building has housed the Samaritan
Center. The diocese's list marks it "CLOSED - See The Cathedral", and the cathedral's
current bulletin covers only the cathedral. The directory calls it "St. John the
Evangelist Mission (Closed)". No exact last-Mass date was read from a primary source.
