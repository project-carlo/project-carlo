## Why it is excluded

**St. Thomas the Apostle, 150 Clinton Rd, New Hartford: closed.** The bishop's decree of 26
June 2025 merged its parish into St. John the Evangelist and reduced the church "to profane
but not sordid use", effective immediately; the decree says worship had already moved to St.
John's when the parishes were linked in 2023. The diocese's list marks "St. Thomas Oratory,
New Hartford (CLOSED - See St. John the Evangelist)", and St. John's current schedule and 27
September 2026 bulletin name only the Oxford Road church. The last Mass date was not found
(some time in or after 2023; the decree does not say); `last_mass` is empty. GCatholic still
lists it, which is how it entered the roster.
