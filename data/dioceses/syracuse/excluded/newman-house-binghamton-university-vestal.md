## Why it is excluded

Binghamton University's Catholic community holds two Sunday Masses at Newman House, 400 Murray Hill Road, Vestal, during the academic year, open to anyone, so it is a worship site. No roster lists it. It publishes no bulletin: its site has the Mass times as page text and one Spring 2025 newsletter. St. Vincent de Paul Blessed Sacrament, whose church hosts the students' other Masses, does not print Newman House's schedule in its bulletin. So, like the Syracuse University center (`excluded/alibrandi-catholic-center-syracuse.md`), it is `no-bulletin`.

## What would change this

A bulletin or regular schedule document the pipeline could fetch.
