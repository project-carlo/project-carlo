## Why it is excluded

The bishop reduced the church of St. John, North Bay, to profane use by decree on 26 June 2025,
after a building survey recommended keeping it "out of service until serious foundational issues
are addressed"; the decree says Mass had been offered there only on request since 2015 and allows
the property to be sold. The diocese's list marks it "CLOSED", and the Spirit of Hope bulletin's
masthead (27 September 2026) does not list it. No date for the last Mass was found.

The diocese's list also has "Sts. Peter & Paul, North Bay (See Spirit of Hope ...)": that is the
same place under its earlier name ("St. John Oratory (formerly Sts. Peter & Paul), North Bay").
Discover Mass still shows a Friday 11:30am Mass for it, which is stale.

Nothing expected.
