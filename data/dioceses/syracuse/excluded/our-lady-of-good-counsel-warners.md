## Why it is excluded

Closed. The diocese's parish list reads "Our Lady of Good Counsel, Warners (CLOSED - See St. Joseph,
Camillus)", its finder "Our Lady of Good Counsel (Closed)", and St. Joseph's in Camillus keeps its
sacramental records. The successor's current bulletin (27 September 2026) schedules Mass only at
St. Joseph's. No address, closing date or last Mass was found (GCatholic does not list it; The
Catholic Sun's search is behind Cloudflare), so `last_mass` is empty and the city stands for the
address.

Lead not followed up: a Society of St. Pius X chapel ("Blessed Virgin Mary Church - NY - Warners",
fsspx.today) exists in Warners. Whether it uses this building is not known; it is not a diocesan
worship site either way.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. An address and a closing date would complete the record.
