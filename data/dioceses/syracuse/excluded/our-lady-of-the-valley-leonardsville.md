## Why it is excluded

The diocese's parish list marks Our Lady of the Valley Mission, Leonardsville, "CLOSED - See
St. Theresa of the Infant Jesus, New Berlin", and New Berlin's diocesan page keeps its records and
lists no church but its own. New Berlin publishes no bulletin (see
`excluded/st-theresa-of-the-infant-jesus-new-berlin.md`, another region's record), so the only
schedule to check is MassTimes.org's (via Discover Mass), which gives New Berlin a Sunday 11:00 am
Mass and nothing in Leonardsville. That is weaker than a bulletin, but two diocesan statements say
closed and nothing says otherwise. No date, street address or news source was found; it is not in
GCatholic.

## What would change this

A New Berlin schedule or bulletin that lists a Mass in Leonardsville.
