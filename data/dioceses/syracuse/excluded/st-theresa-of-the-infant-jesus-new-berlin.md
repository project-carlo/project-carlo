## Why it is excluded

St. Theresa of the Infant Jesus in New Berlin holds public Mass but publishes no bulletin the
pipeline can read now. Mass: St. Malachy's 13 September 2026 bulletin announces St. Theresa's 70th
anniversary "after the 11:00 am Mass" on Sunday 20 September, and St. Paul's 20 September bulletin
lists "5:30 PM Mass at St. Theresa's" on Monday 21 September; St. Bartholomew's homepage offers
confession at St. Theresa's after its Sunday 11 am, Monday 5:30 pm and Wednesday 9 am Masses.

Bulletin: the parish's own site (sttheresanewberlinny.org/bulletin) links weekly Google Drive PDFs,
but the newest is 24 August 2025. An older Google Sites bulletin page now demands a sign-in, Discover
Mass lists no issues, and neither of the sister parishes' bulletins (St. Paul's, St. Bartholomew's,
St. Malachy's) carries St. Theresa's schedule, so there is nothing to attach it to. Hence
`no-bulletin`, not `closed`. The Facebook page (facebook.com/sttheresanewberlinny) may post
bulletins, but it needs a login and I could not check it.

## What would change this

New issues on the parish's Bulletin page, or a readable bulletin on Facebook or Discover Mass (see
`revisit`).
