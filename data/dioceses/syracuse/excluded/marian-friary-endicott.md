## Why it is excluded

The friary of the Franciscans of the Immaculate at 120 Edson Road (Mount St. Francis Hermitage, in the Town of Maine with an Endicott address) holds Mass the public can attend: its order's page gives Sunday 9 am and daily 12:15 pm, with confessions, and invites anyone to its monthly Mass. So it is in scope as a worship site. It is excluded only because it publishes nothing the pipeline can read: the order's page has the schedule as page text and links no bulletin, and no ParishesOnline, Discover Mass or eCatholic bulletin turned up. This is `no-bulletin`, not `no-public-mass`.
