## Why it is excluded

St. Helena's last regular Mass was on Sunday 29 June 2025. The Spirit of Hope bulletin of 22 June
2025 announces "St. Helena's final Mass will be June 29th at 10:45am", its masthead that week
still lists "10:45AM at St. Helena", and from the 13 July 2025 issue on the masthead lists four
sites without it, as the 27 September 2026 issue still does. Discover Mass's listing says it
became an oratory effective 1 July 2025 and gives no Mass times; the parish's own St. Helena page
(read 2026-09-26) gives an address and no Mass time, where the other churches' pages give theirs.

The building is still used by the parish (a "Parish Center at St. Helena" and group meetings
appear in later bulletins), and as an oratory it could host an occasional Mass. It is excluded
because nothing schedules one.

Address: the parish site and Discover Mass say 204 Primo Ave.; the diocese says 202 and GCatholic
210. 204 is used.

## What would change this

A Mass scheduled here again in the Spirit of Hope bulletin.
