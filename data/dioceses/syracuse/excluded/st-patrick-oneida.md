## Why it is excluded

No Mass has been held here since the end of 2024. The Rome Sentinel reported on 31 December 2024
that St. Patrick's "has closed to the public because the ceiling is in danger of collapsing",
with repairs costing over $600,000 and its 10:45 am Sunday Mass moved to St. Joseph's. The
parish's own page still says (read 2026-09-26) "We are unable to hold Masses in the church at
this time", and the Spirit of Hope bulletin's masthead has not listed it since July 2025 (the
27 September 2026 issue names four sites, not this one).

It is `closed` in the sense this dataset uses (no Mass now), not a canonical closure: it is
still the parish's principal church under the 2023 decree, it has not been relegated, and the
parish is "weighing our options". The rectory next door is still used (the 27 September 2026
bulletin announces an animal blessing there). The last Mass date is not known exactly (late
December 2024), so `last_mass` is empty. The GCatholic address (370 Main St.) differs from the
parish's and the diocese's (347 Main St.); 347 is used.

## What would change this

Repairs and a return of Mass to the church; see `revisit`. If it reopens, it becomes a church
row of `spirit-of-hope-oneida` again.
