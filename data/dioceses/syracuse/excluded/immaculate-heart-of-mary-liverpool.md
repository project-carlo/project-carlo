## Why it is excluded

Closed; last Mass 23 September 2023. Immaculate Heart of Mary was the second worship site of
Epiphany Parish (formed in 2019 from IHM and St. Joseph the Worker). The bishop's decree, printed
in The Catholic Sun on 7 September 2023, reduces "the church of the Immaculate Heart of Mary,
located at 425 Beechwood Avenue" to profane use, effective 7 October 2023, and names St. Joseph the
Worker (1001 Tulip Street) the sole church of the parish. The Sun reported on 12 October 2023 that
"The last Mass was celebrated at the 4 p.m. Saturday vigil on Sept. 23". The parish's current
bulletin (Liverpool Catholic Community, 27 September 2026) schedules Mass only at Christ the King,
Epiphany (Tulip Street) and Pope John XXIII, and the diocese's finder lists the building as
"(Closed)". GCatholic still lists it as a church.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. The decree was issued so that the building could be sold ("alienated").
