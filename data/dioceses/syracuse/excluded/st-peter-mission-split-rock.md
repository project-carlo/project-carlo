## Why it is excluded

Closed. The diocese's list marks St. Peter Mission, Split Rock "CLOSED - See The Church of
St. Michael and St. Peter" (the merged parish whose name carries it), and that parish's 27
September 2026 bulletin schedules Mass only at 4791 West Seneca Turnpike and at Our Lady
of Hope. No address, date or current use of the mission building was found (GCatholic does
not list it; two web searches found nothing), so the city is all that is recorded.
