## Why it is excluded

The church closed in summer 2021: the Catholic Sun of 18 August 2021 reports its St. Therese
shrine moved to Oswego from "St. Therese Church in Munnsville, which closed earlier this summer".
The 2023 Spirit of Hope decree records the parish as "merged, relegated, and alienated". The
diocese's list marks it "CLOSED - See Spirit of Hope", and the Spirit of Hope bulletin
(27 September 2026 masthead) lists no Mass there. No exact last-Mass date found.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the building was alienated.
