## Why it is excluded

GCatholic lists it among the churches in the Diocese of Syracuse's territory but marks it with the Ruthenian rite: it belongs to an Eastern Catholic eparchy, not to the Roman Catholic diocese. As with `excluded/st-john-the-baptist-syracuse.md`, this is a scope decision (this exploration covers the Latin diocese), not a judgement about the church. Neither the diocese's finder nor its parish list includes it. GCatholic's website for it (holyspiritbyzantinecatholicchurch.webs.com) returned nothing readable on 2026-09-26; the rite field is the evidence.

## What would change this

A project decision to cover Eastern Catholic eparchies (here the Ruthenian Eparchy of Passaic).
