## Why it is excluded

The building closed when its parish merged into All Saints in 2008. The diocesan paper says so
outright ("The Our Lady of Solace Church building was closed", Catholic Sun, February 2010),
and All Saints' own site dates the merger to the Feast of All Saints 2008 and lists a single
church, the former St. Therese at 1340 Lancaster Avenue. No exact last-Mass date was found, so
`last_mass` is empty. It is not in the GCatholic roster; it is recorded because older
directories and Mass-time sites still carry it.

When All Saints is added, model the Lancaster Avenue church only. The same Catholic Sun
article says "neighboring St. Andrew's officially closed some months later"; it belongs here
too once its address is found.
