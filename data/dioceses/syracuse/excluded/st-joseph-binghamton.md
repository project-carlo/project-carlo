## Why it is excluded

The Lithuanian parish of St. Joseph was merged into the Church of the Holy Trinity on 1 July 2003; Holy Trinity's history page dates St. Joseph's closing Mass to 16 November 2003. The diocese's parish list marks it "CLOSED - See Church of the Holy Trinity", and the successor's 27 September 2026 bulletin lists no Mass there. The building (Glenwood and Judson Avenues) is still in OSM as "Saint Joseph Lithuanian Church"; I found no house number. This finder entry (directory:36270) is Binghamton's St. Joseph, not St. Joseph in Endicott, Deposit or Sanitaria Springs, which are church rows.
