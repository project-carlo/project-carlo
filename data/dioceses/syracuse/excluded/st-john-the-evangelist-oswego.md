## Why it is excluded

A parish closed before Christ the Good Shepherd was formed. The diocese's list says "St. John the
Evangelist, Oswego (CLOSED - See Christ the Good Shepherd, Oswego)", and CTGS's own homepage describes
the parish as formed from its predecessors "plus the closed parishes of St. John the Evangelist and
St. Louis". CTGS's current Mass schedule (27 September 2026 bulletin and homepage) has no St. John's.
GCatholic does not list it. No closing date or street address was found; OSM has an old GNIS point
named "Saint John's Roman Catholic Church" in Oswego (43.44917, -76.51000), not checked on the ground.

Nothing expected. A dated source for the closure (The Catholic Sun, the Palladium-Times) would fill
`last_mass`.
