## Why it is excluded

Closed; last Mass Saturday 15 July 2023. The bishop's decree of July 2023 (The Catholic Sun,
8 July 2023) merged the Mission of St. Bernadette into Divine Mercy and reduced "The church of Saint
Bernadette, located at 1667 NY-49, Constantia" to profane use, effective 16 July 2023, naming Divine
Mercy (592 South Main Street, Central Square) the parish's sole church. The Sun's report of 26 July
2023 says Mass "was celebrated for the final time" on the eve of 16 July. The current bulletin
(27 September 2026) schedules Mass only at Sacred Heart and Divine Mercy, and the diocese's finder
marks the mission "(Closed)". The parish keeps the old name "Divine Mercy Parish & The Mission of St.
Bernadette"; that is the canonical name, not a worship site. GCatholic still lists the mission.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the decree was issued so the property could be sold.
