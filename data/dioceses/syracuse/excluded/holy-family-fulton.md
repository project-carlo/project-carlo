## Why it is excluded

Merged away in 2008. Holy Trinity's own history says Holy Family (Fulton's west-side parish, church
built 1968) merged with Immaculate Conception "to form Holy Trinity Parish in March 2008, with
Immaculate Conception church chosen as the location". The current Holy Trinity & St. Stephen bulletin
(27 September 2026) has Masses only at Holy Trinity and St. Stephen, Phoenix, and the diocese lists
Holy Family as "CLOSED - See Church of the Holy Trinity". GCatholic does not list it. No last-Mass
date or reliable street address was found (a Yelp listing gives 45 W 4th St S, not checked); the city
is recorded.
