## Why it is excluded

**St. Patrick's Church, Clayville: closed in the 2009 merger.** The Catholic Sun (May 2010)
says the merger "meant closing St. Patrick's Church in Clayville while beginning the new
combined parish at the St. Anthony site" in Chadwicks; the diocese's list marks it "CLOSED -
See St. Patrick - St. Anthony, Chadwicks"; and the successor's 20 September 2026 bulletin has
Masses only at 3364 Oneida St., Chadwicks. No street address or last-Mass date was found
(GCatholic does not list it), so only the village is given.
