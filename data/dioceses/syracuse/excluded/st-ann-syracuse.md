## Why it is excluded

Closed, last Mass Sunday 29 January 2023. The Catholic Sun (7 February 2023, "'We've closed
the doors on the building, but not on our parish'") reports the final Mass at St. Ann's
Church on Onondaga Boulevard, opened in 1955 and merged with St. Charles in 2012; Fr.
Maloney said it had had no weekend Mass "since before COVID". The diocese's parish list
marks St. Ann "CLOSED - See St. Charles-St. Ann", and the linked parishes' current bulletin
has Masses only at St. Patrick's (and at St. Charles before its renovation).

GCatholic lists this site as "St. Charles–St. Ann, 4461 Onondaga Blvd." (history "1955");
that entry is linked here, not to the St. Charles church on South Orchard Road, because its
address, its founding year and its Plus Code (at 4461 Onondaga Blvd per Nominatim) are St.
Ann's. The parish still uses the "Onondaga Blvd. Campus" for Bible study and the school
building, not for Mass.

Nothing expected.
