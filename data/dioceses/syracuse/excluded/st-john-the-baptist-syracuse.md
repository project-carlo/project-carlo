## Why it is excluded

A Ukrainian Greek Catholic church. GCatholic lists it among the churches in the Diocese of
Syracuse's territory, marked with the Ukrainian rite, and its own site calls it a Ukrainian Catholic
church: it belongs to a Ukrainian Catholic eparchy, not to the Roman Catholic diocese. Eastern Catholic churches are Catholic
and their Divine Liturgy fulfils the obligation, so this is a scope decision, not a judgement
about the church: this exploration covers the Latin diocese. GCatholic marks eight such
churches here (Ruthenian, Ukrainian, Melkite, Maronite); this is the one recorded so far.

## What would change this

A project decision to cover Eastern Catholic eparchies, which would be explored as their own
jurisdictions.
