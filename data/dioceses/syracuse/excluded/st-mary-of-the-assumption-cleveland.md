## Why it is excluded

Closed; no date found. St. Mary's in Cleveland (148 State Route 49, per GCatholic) was the oratory
of Divine Mercy Parish (The Catholic Sun, 13 October 2016: "St. Mary's in Cleveland, which is the
oratory"). The diocese's parish list now reads "St. Mary of the Assumption, Cleveland (CLOSED - See
Divine Mercy Parish ...)" and its finder "St. Mary of the Assumption Oratory (Closed)". The July 2023
decree names Divine Mercy in Central Square "the sole church of the parish", and the parishes'
current bulletin (27 September 2026) and site schedule Mass only at Sacred Heart and Divine Mercy.
GCatholic still lists it as a church. No closing date or last Mass was found: The Catholic Sun's
search is behind Cloudflare, and web searches found nothing, so `last_mass` is empty.
