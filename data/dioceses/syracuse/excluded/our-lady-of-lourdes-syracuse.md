## Why it is excluded

Closed, last Mass Saturday 7 October 2017. Spectrum News (8 October 2017) reported that
"Our Lady of Lourdes Church held its final mass on Saturday" and that the church was for
sale; the parish merged with St. James into Our Lady of Hope, which took over St. James's
church at 4845 South Salina Street (Our Lady of Hope's own history page). The diocese's
parish list marks it "CLOSED - See Our Lady of Hope", and the successor's 27 September 2026
bulletin lists Masses only at Our Lady of Hope and St. Michael & St. Peter. GCatholic
still lists the Valley Drive church; its ParishesOnline organization
(`our-lady-of-lourdes-church-13207`) has no publications.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
