## Why it is excluded

The parish was merged and the church relegated to profane use and sold ("previously merged,
relegated, and alienated", Spirit of Hope merger decree, Catholic Sun, 7 June 2023; the decree
pairs St. Francis with 2021 and St. Therese with 2022, though the Sun reported St. Therese closing
in summer 2021, so the years may be swapped). The diocese's list marks it "CLOSED", its records are
at Spirit of Hope, and the Spirit of Hope bulletin (27 September 2026 masthead) lists no Mass there.
Its cemetery is still listed in the bulletin ("St. Francis Cemetery"). No last-Mass date found.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the building was alienated.
