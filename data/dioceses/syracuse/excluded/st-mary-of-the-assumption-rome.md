## Why it is excluded

St. Mary of the Assumption, 208 W. Liberty St., Rome, closed after its parish was folded into
St. Peter's (announced 2007), was sold by the diocese in 2022, and was slated for demolition after
the 16 July 2024 tornado tore off its steeple (Rome Sentinel, 27 August 2024). The diocese's list
marks it "CLOSED - See Sacred Heart Parish", and Sacred Heart's 27 September 2026 bulletin lists no
Mass there. The diocese's finder entry "St. Mary, Rome" has no address; it is linked here. (GCatholic's
"St. Mary–St. Peter" is the St. Peter's building, `churches/st-peter-rome.md`.) No last-Mass date found.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
