## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 3) lists Masses at COL on Saturday 4:00 pm and
Sunday 10:15 am, plus First Friday and First Saturday Masses; the joint site gives the same weekend
times.

## Name and address

GCatholic, the diocese directory and the bulletin all give "Christ Our Light" at 23 Niagara Street,
Pulaski, 13142: both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; the directory pin and the OSM
building are within about 20 m.

**The building is the former St. John the Evangelist church.** The parish's own history says Christ Our
Light "was formed from two parishes in 2007, namely St. Frances Cabrini in Lacona and St. John the
Evangelist in Pulaski", and OpenStreetMap still names the building at 23 Niagara Street "Saint John's
Roman Catholic Church". So the directory's "St. John the Evangelist (Closed), Pulaski" entry
(directory:33131, no address) is the old parish, whose church is this one, and it is linked here
rather than excluded. The link rests on OSM's name at the same address; nothing I read says in words
"Christ Our Light uses the St. John's church".
