## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 2) lists Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 10:30 am, and no weekday Mass that week.

This appears to be the former St. Catherine of Siena church: two church-listing sites put "St Catherine Of Sienna Parish" at 1031 Chenango St with St. Francis's phone number, and the diocese keeps St. Catherine's records here. On that basis the finder's "St. Catherine of Siena (Closed)" (directory:33104, no address) is linked to this record. It rests on listing sites, not a parish history; if a history shows St. Catherine's church stood elsewhere, move directory:33104 to an exclusion.
