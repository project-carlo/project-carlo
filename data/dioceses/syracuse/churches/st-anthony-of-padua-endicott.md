## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 27 September 2026 North Endicott bulletin (Discover Mass, page 2) lists Saturday 5:30 pm, Sunday 8:30 am and Monday 8:30 am here (STA), and page 3 a healing Mass on 8 October. The 2026 decree keeps it as an auxiliary church and puts the parish office here.

## Name and address

**GCatholic's number differs.** The diocese's finder and Prince of Peace page, the bulletin ("St. Anthony of Padua 300 Odell Ave."), Discover Mass and the 2026 decree ("Saint Anthony (300 Odell Avenue, Endicott, 13760)") give 300 Odell Avenue; GCatholic gives 306. I followed 300. OSM has the church ("Saint Anthony of Padua Roman Catholic Church", node 357631726) on the Jenkins Street side; the bulletin says the entrance is at 906 Jenkins St. Both flags are true.
