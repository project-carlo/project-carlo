## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. Its 27 September 2026 bulletin gives Sunday 8:30 am, a Saturday 4 pm vigil from October
to March (resuming 3 October, per its homepage), and Tuesday and Thursday 9 am (the homepage says
Thursday Mass is suspended "until further notice"). St. Paul's 20 September bulletin independently
lists the Sunday 8:30 am and Tuesday 9 am Masses at St. Bart's.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Bartholomew", 73 E Main St.), the diocese finder and page (73 East Main Street) and
the bulletin (73 East Main) agree, and OSM has "Saint Bartholomew's Roman Catholic Church" on East
Main Street, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within 10 m of the OSM
node.
