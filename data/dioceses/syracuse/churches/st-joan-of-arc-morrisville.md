## Holds Mass

**Not confirmed for 2026.** It is one of the four churches of Holy Spirit Parish, a mission church; missions count as worship sites. The
2020 merger decree closed none of them, and GCatholic (fetched 2026-09-25) still lists it. A
web-search summary attributed a weekend Mass here to a July 2025 Route 20 Catholic bulletin, but
that PDF now returns 404 and I could not read it, so it is not recorded as evidence. The
parish's `revisit` (read a current bulletin) settles this for all four churches.

## Name and address

GCatholic (6 Brookside Dr., listed as a Mission) and the Rome Sentinel ("St. Joan of Arc Mission, 6 Brookside Drive") agree, and OSM has an address point for 6 Brookside Drive about 20 m from GCatholic's Plus Code, so `address_verified` is true. The name flag waits for a current bulletin. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded.
