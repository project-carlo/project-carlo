## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly during the academic year at least. Christ the Good Shepherd's 27 September 2026 bulletin
lists a Sunday 6:30 pm Mass at the Hall Newman Center among the parish's weekend Masses, the parish
homepage lists it as "(Hall Newman Center- SUNY Oswego)", and the college's own page says "Weekly Mass
Sunday Evenings at 6:30pm". The June 2026 bulletins also list it (at 6:00 pm), so it is not
term-time only. It is SUNY Oswego's Catholic campus ministry, whose chapel Mass-time sites call the
"Chapel of the Holy Spirit"; its Mass is part of CTGS's published schedule, so it is a church of
that parish row.

## Name and address

Not in either roster: the diocese directory and GCatholic list no campus ministries here, so
`coverage` will report it under churches in no roster. The college's page and OSM (independent
sources) both give "Hall Newman Center" at 36 New Street, Oswego, 13126, so both flags are true.
Coordinates are the centre of the OSM building (way 408044058).
