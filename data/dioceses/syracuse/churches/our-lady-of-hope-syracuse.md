## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 St. Michael & St. Peter / Our Lady of Hope bulletin: Saturday 4
pm and Sunday 8 am ("OLOH").

## Name and address

The bulletin, the diocese page and directory, and GCatholic all give 4845 South Salina
Street, 13205, so `address_verified` is true. The name is not verified: GCatholic still
calls the building "St. James" (the parish's name before the 2017 merger with Our Lady of
Lourdes, which moved into St. James's church), and only the parish's own sources and the
diocese use "Our Lady of Hope". The directory's separate "St. James" entry (no address) is
that former parish and is linked here. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
