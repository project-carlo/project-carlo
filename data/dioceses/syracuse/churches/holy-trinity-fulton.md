## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 Holy Trinity & St. Stephen bulletin (page 2) lists Masses marked (HT) on
Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:30 am and Tuesday and Wednesday 9:00 am; the parishes' Mass page gives the
same.

## Name and address

Holy Trinity worships in the former **Immaculate Conception** church: the parish's own history says the
2008 merger chose "Immaculate Conception church ... as the location", and its welcome page calls it
"The Church of the Immaculate Conception (now the home of Holy Trinity)". OSM still names the building
"Immaculate Conception Roman Catholic Church". So the directory's "Immaculate Conception (Closed)"
entry (directory:33051, no address) is the old *parish*, and its building is this church; it is
linked here rather than excluded. The directory's "Holy Family (Closed)" and "St. Michael (Closed)"
are separate buildings, excluded.
