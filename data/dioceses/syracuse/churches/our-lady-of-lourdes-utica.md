## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 20 September 2026 Mary, Mother of Our Savior bulletin gives Saturday 4:00pm,
Sunday 8:15am, 10:30am and 5:00pm, and weekday Masses; the parish's only church since 16
July 2026.

## Name and address

**Two addresses.** GCatholic gives 2 Barton Ave., which is the parish office (the parish
site's contact block: "2 Barton Ave. Utica, NY 13502"). The bishop's decree ("2222 Genesee
Street"), the diocese's finder and the ParishesOnline org (2222 Genesee St) give the church.
The church stands at the corner of Genesee St and Barton Ave, so both are the same site; I
used 2222 Genesee Street for the church. Both flags true (the decree, the finder and
GCatholic's name "Our Lady of Lourdes"). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, which lies
on the same block (the finder's pin is about 10 m away).
