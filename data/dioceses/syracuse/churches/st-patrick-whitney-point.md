## Holds Mass

Yes, the community's main church. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (ParishesOnline container 03/1424,
page 1) lists Sunday 8:30 am and weekday Masses on Monday (6 pm), Tuesday and Thursday (10 am) at
St. Patrick, Whitney Point; the homepage and the "Our Churches" page give the same times.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Patrick", 59 Keibel Rd.), the diocese finder and page (59 Keibel Road, "Church & Office
Address - Whitney Point"), the ParishesOnline organization and the community's "Our Churches" page
("Saint Patrick's Church, 59 Keibel Rd") agree, so both flags are true. One disagreement: the bulletin
masthead gives the office as "P.O. Box 711, 51 Keibel Road"; that line is the mailing/office address
and I kept 59, which every source gives for the church. Whitney Point is in Broome County (Town of
Triangle), although the community is usually filed with Cortland County's Marathon. No OSM church
was found here. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
