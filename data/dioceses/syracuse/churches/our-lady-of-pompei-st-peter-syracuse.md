## Holds Mass

Yes. The 13 September 2026 bulletin and the parish homepage: Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 7:30 and
11:30 am, a Vietnamese Mass Saturday 8 am and Sunday 9 am, and daily Masses.

## Name and address

The diocese page and directory give 301 Ash Street, 13208, and GCatholic ("Our Lady of
Pompeii–St. Peter", 301 Ash St.) agrees; the bulletin gives only the parish office at 920
North McBride Street (the church stands at Ash and McBride). Both flags true on the diocese
and GCatholic. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
