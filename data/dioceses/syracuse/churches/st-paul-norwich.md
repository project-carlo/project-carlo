## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 20 September 2026 bulletin gives Sunday 11 am and, April–September, a Saturday
4 pm vigil, plus Wednesday 9 am. From 3 October the Saturday Norwich Mass moves to St. Bartholomew's
("Saturday Masses will be at St. Bartholomew's (instead of St. Paul's) starting on October 3rd", St.
Malachy's 13 September bulletin, page 3); St. Paul's keeps its Sunday Mass.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Paul", 30 Pleasant St.), the diocese finder and page (30 Pleasant Street) and the
bulletin masthead agree, and OSM has "Saint Paul's Roman Catholic Church" on Pleasant Street, so
both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within 10 m of the OSM node.
