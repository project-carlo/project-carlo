## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 Church of the Nativity bulletin gives Sunday Mass here at
11:30 a.m.; the parish site's St. Patrick's Mission page gives the same time.

13159, so `address_verified` is true. The town: GCatholic and the diocese say Otisco; the parish
site writes "1865 NY-80 Tully, NY 13159", which is the post office (the Tully ZIP), and OSM labels the point
"Otisco, Town of Tully". Modelled as Otisco, the place name the parish, the diocese and GCatholic
all use for the mission. The
