## Holds Mass

**Once a year, publicly and announced in the parish bulletin.** St. Mary of Mount Carmel stopped
regular services in 2003 and became a devotional chapel ("St. Mary Church in Florence will now serve as
a devotional chapel", parish timeline, July 2003). It still has an annual Mass: the Camden bulletin
announced "11:00 am Mass at St. Mary, Mt. Carmel, Florence" for Sunday 21 September 2025 (reported by the
Rome Sentinel as the church's annual Mass, 180th anniversary), and "MASS AT ST. MARYS, FLORENCE, 11:00
AM" for Sunday 20 September 2026. The Sentinel adds it is "available for weddings, funerals and special
Masses", and the bulletin masthead names it as the parish's oratory. One public Mass a year is thin, but
it is scheduled, public and published in the bulletin the pipeline reads, so it is a church row.

The diocese directory marks it "St. Mary Devotional Chapel (Closed)" (directory:33164); the diocese's
own alphabetical list does not say CLOSED ("St. Mary Devotional Chapel, Florence (See St. John the
Evangelist, Camden)"). The bulletin wins: Mass was held there on 20 September 2026.

## Name and address

Names: "St. Mary Devotional Chapel" (diocese), "St. Mary's Oratory" / "St. Mary Mount Carmel Oratory"
(bulletin), "St. Mary of Mount Carmel Catholic Church" (Rome Sentinel). Modelled as the bulletin's
"St. Mary of Mount Carmel Oratory"; `name_verified` false because the sources use different forms.

Address: no source gives a street number. GCatholic does not list it; the directory entry has no
address; Discover Mass's "Saint Mary" Florence listing gives "22 Church Street, Florence", which is
St. John's Camden address copied over and not used. The only location is OSM's GNIS node "Saint Marys
Church", which reverse-geocodes to Green Road, Town of Florence (43.44083, -75.74760); the line is
"Green Road" with Camden's ZIP 13316, which serves Florence. `address_verified` false. A look at the
building or the parish office would give the number.
