## Holds Mass

Yes. The 26-27 September 2026 bulletin: Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 9 am and 11:30 am.

## Name and address

The church is at 1340 Lancaster Avenue (GCatholic, the diocese page's "Physical Address",
the directory); the bulletin masthead and parish office use 1342, which the diocese page
gives as the mailing address. Both flags true. The building is the former St. Therese the
Little Flower church (All Saints was formed from Our Lady of Solace and St. Therese in
2008), so the directory's "St. Therese the Little Flower (Closed)" entry, which names the
closed parish, is linked here. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
