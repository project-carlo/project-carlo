## Holds Mass

Yes. The 20 September 2026 bulletin: Saturday Vigil 4pm, Sunday 11am, Tuesday and Thursday
9am, all at 3364 Oneida St.

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic (St. Patrick-St. Anthony, 3364 Oneida St., 13319) and the diocese's
finder agree; the ParishesOnline org says 3368 Oneida St (probably the parish center next
door; the bulletin's "Located at" is followed). Both flags true. This is the former St.
Anthony church (Catholic Sun, 2010), so the finder's "St. Anthony (Closed), Chadwicks"
(directory:33093) is linked here: the parish name ended, not the building. The diocese's list
agrees: "St. Anthony of Padua, Chadwicks (See St. Patrick - St. Anthony, Chadwicks)", with no
"CLOSED". Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
