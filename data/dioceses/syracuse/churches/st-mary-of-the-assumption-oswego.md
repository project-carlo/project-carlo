## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 20 September 2026 bulletin masthead gives Sunday 9:00 am Low Mass and 11:00 am High
Mass (Traditional Latin Mass, Institute of Christ the King), weekdays except Thursday 7:30 am,
Thursday 12:10 pm, and an extra 6:00 pm Mass on First Fridays. Discover Mass carries the same kind of
schedule.

## Name and address

The diocese directory calls it "St. Mary of the Assumption Parish & Shrine" and the bulletin
"Saint Mary of the Assumption Parish & Shrine"; GCatholic has "St. Mary of Assumption" (kind Shrine).
Same place, so `name_verified` is true, using the parish's own form. Address: 103 West 7th Street in
the bulletin, the directory, GCatholic and Discover Mass: `address_verified` true. OSM gives the
building 74 West 6th Street, the other side of the same block; recorded, not followed. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code, within about 20 m of the directory pin.

In June 2019 the diocese announced that St. Mary's would no longer be used for Mass after 1 July 2019
(Spectrum News, 16 May 2019); that plan was suspended by a decree of 7 October 2019 (recited on page 2 of the 2020 merger decree cited in `parishes/christ-the-good-shepherd-oswego.md`), and the 2020 decree left
St. Mary's out of the merger. The church holds Mass daily now.
