## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 bulletin of the Catholic Community of St. Stephen, St. Patrick &
Our Lady of Perpetual Help (ParishesOnline container 03/1424, page 1) lists "Saturday Vigil 5:00PM
St. Stephen, Marathon", and the community's homepage and "Our Churches" page give the same Saturday
5 pm Mass. It has no weekday Mass.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Stephen", 16 Academy St., Marathon 13803) and the diocese's parish finder and page
(16 Academy Street) agree, and the community's own "Our Churches" page names "Saint Stephen's Church"
on Academy St without a number. OpenStreetMap has "Saint Stephen's Catholic Church" on Academy Street.
Name and address each have two independent sources, so both flags are true. The diocese directory's
entry for this address (directory:33220) carries the community's name, not the church's, which is
why its name differs. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; the OSM building is about 5 m away.
