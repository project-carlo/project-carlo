## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 1) and the parish homepage give Saturday 4:30 pm,
Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 am, and Monday to Friday 10:00 am at Christ the Good Shepherd. It is the parish
church of CTGS under the 2020 merger decree.

## Name and address

This is the former St. Paul's church, renamed for the merged parish: the 2020 decree makes "the church
of Saint Paul" the parish church, the 2019 news (Spectrum, WRVO) says St. Paul's was chosen as the home
of Christ the Good Shepherd, and the parish now writes "CTGS @ St. Paul's". GCatholic still lists it as
"St. Paul" and OSM as "Saint Paul's Roman Catholic Church"; the diocese directory and the bulletin call
it Christ the Good Shepherd. The directory's separate "St. Paul (Closed)" entry (directory:36302, no
address) is the St. Paul *parish*, which the 2020 decree merged away; its church is this building, so
that entry is linked here rather than excluded. The name used is the parish's, with the old name in brackets so it can be
matched; the bulletin and the directory agree on "Christ the Good Shepherd", so `name_verified` is true.

**The address is disputed.** The church stands at the corner of East Fifth and East Mohawk streets:

- 134 East Fifth Street: the bulletin masthead, the parish homepage and the diocese directory.
- 50 East Mohawk Street: GCatholic, the 2020 decree, and the parish's own mailing address.
- 140 East 5th Street: OpenStreetMap.

Modelled at 134 East 5th Street, the address the parish uses for the church (it gives 50 E. Mohawk as
its mailing address). `address_verified` stays false because the diocese's own decree gives the
Mohawk Street address; a look at the building's number on East Fifth would settle it. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code (87M5FF4W+6X), which falls on the building OSM maps; the directory's pin
(43.45566, -76.50221) is on the same building.
