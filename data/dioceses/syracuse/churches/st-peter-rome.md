## Holds Mass

Yes: Sunday 10:30 am and 6:00 pm and every weekday, in the Sacred Heart Parish schedule of the
27 September 2026 bulletin (page 3). Sunday Masses here are livestreamed.

## Name and address

GCatholic lists this building as "St. Mary–St. Peter" (the pre-2025 parish of St. Mary-St. Peter,
formed when St. Mary of the Assumption was folded into St. Peter's) at **105 E Liberty St.**, ZIP
13442; the bulletin, the diocese's finder and the Rome Sentinel put St. Peter's at **200 N. James
Street**, 13440. They are one building on the corner: GCatholic's Plus Code is about 25 m from the
diocese's pin for 200 North James Street, and there is no other Catholic church on that block.
105 E Liberty St. with 13442 (Rome's PO-box ZIP) is probably the parish's former mailing address.
Modelled as the bulletin gives it; `address_verified` stays false because of the disagreement.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
