## Holds Mass

Yes, once a year. Spirit of Hope holds its parish All Souls' Day Mass here: Monday 3 November
2025 at 6 pm (19 October 2025 bulletin, page 3, with Bishop Lucia presiding) and Monday
2 November 2026 at 6 pm (25 October 2026 bulletin, page 3). Both notices invite the whole
parish, read the names of its deceased, take a collection and give parking directions, so the
Mass is public. There is no other regular Mass: the bulletins from June to September 2025 list
the chapel only among the parish's cemeteries, and the weekly schedule names four other churches.

This was `excluded/st-mary-irish-ridge.md` (reason `closed`) from 2026-09-26 to 2026-10-26. That
exclusion said "A Mass at the chapel (for example an annual cemetery or heritage Mass) in the
bulletin ... would make it a church row with occasional Mass", which is what the two All Souls'
notices show; the October 2025 issue had not been read. History, from the parish's own page:
"In the year 2000 the Dioceses of Syracuse, short of priests and funds, decided to close St.
Mary's", after which descendants and neighbours restored it as "a spiritual gathering place".
The diocese now calls it a devotional chapel, and the 2023 merger decree kept it as an auxiliary
church with right of entry for worship.

## Name and address

St. Mary, 6148 Irish Ridge Road, Irish Ridge 13054: GCatholic, the diocese's parish finder and
both bulletins agree, so both flags are true. The parish's page gives the postal town as
Durhamville; OSM places 6148 Irish Ridge Road in the hamlet of Paradise Hill, Town of Verona.
Coordinates are the diocese's finder's (OSM's address node for 6148 Irish Ridge Road is within
2 m).
