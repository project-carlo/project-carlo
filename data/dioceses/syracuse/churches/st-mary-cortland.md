## Holds Mass

Yes, the busiest of the Cortland churches. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (Discover Mass,
st-mary-cortland-ny) lists Saturday 4 pm and Sunday 8 am and 11:45 am at St. Mary's, and weekday
Masses there (Tuesday and Friday 12:10 pm in the 20 September issue).

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Mary", 46 N Main St.), the diocese finder (46 N. Main Street), the bulletin masthead
("St. Mary's Church 46 N. Main St., Cortland") and OpenStreetMap ("Saint Mary's Roman Catholic Church",
46 North Main Street) agree, so both flags are true. The parish office is across the street at the
Parish Center, 59 N. Main St.; the diocese's St. Mary page gives that office address, not the church's.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 10 m from the OSM node.
