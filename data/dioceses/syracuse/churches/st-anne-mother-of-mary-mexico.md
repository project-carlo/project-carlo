## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 3) lists SAMOM Masses on Saturday 5:30 pm and Sunday
8:30 am, and the joint site gives the same weekend times.

## Name and address

GCatholic, the diocese directory and the bulletin all give "St. Anne, Mother of Mary" at 3352 Main
Street, Mexico, 13114: both flags true. OSM has the building as 3354 Main Street, a neighbouring
number on the same lot, recorded here and not followed. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within
about 20 m of the directory pin and the OSM building.

The parish's history says St. Anne, Mother of Mary "was formed from two parishes in the early 2000s,
St. Anne's in Parish New York and St. Mary's Star of the Sea in Mexico" and that "the Church was
consecrated as St. Mary's Star of the Sea": this is that building. The diocese's list entry "St. Mary,
Star of the Sea, Mexico (CLOSED ...)" is the old parish, not this church; it is in no roster.
