## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 29/30 August 2026 Parish Update and the parish homepage (read 2026-09-26) give
Saturday Vigil 5:45 pm and Sunday 8:30 am; the update notes the Vigil Mass was suspended on 29
August and 5 September while the priest was away, resuming the weekend of 12/13 September.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Joseph", 3 Scott St.), the diocese finder and page (3 Scott Street, Oxford 13830) and
the parish's own update agree, and OSM has "Saint Joseph's Roman Catholic Church" on Scott Street, so
both flags are true. The homepage describes the site as "Corner of State and Scott St." Coordinates
are GCatholic's Plus Code.
