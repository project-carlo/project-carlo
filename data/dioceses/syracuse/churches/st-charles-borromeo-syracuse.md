## Holds Mass

**Not at present: closed for renovation since mid-August 2026, expected to reopen.** Until
the 9 August 2026 bulletin it had Saturday 5:15 pm and Sunday 10:30 am Masses ("SC"). The
23 August issue says renovations "will not allow for access to St. Charles-St. Ann's
Church" for the foreseeable future and all Masses move to St. Patrick's; the 30 August issue
reports the renovation has begun (pews out for refinishing, new kneelers ordered, asbestos
abatement); the 27 September issue has every Mass at St. Patrick's and notes a wedding
booked "prior to the renovation plans". It is kept as a church row, not excluded, because
the building is being renovated for reuse, not closed; the `revisit` is for when Masses
come back.

## Name and address

The parish site ("St. Charles Church, 501 South Orchard Rd."), the diocese page, the
ParishesOnline organization and OpenStreetMap (node "St. Charles Church", 501 South Orchard
Road) agree on **501 South Orchard Road**. GCatholic has the same building (its Plus Code
is within 25 m of the OSM node) but numbers it **417** S Orchard Rd. Because of that
disagreement `address_verified` stays false; the record follows 501. Note that GCatholic's
other entry, "St. Charles–St. Ann, 4461 Onondaga Blvd.", is the former St. Ann's church
(excluded), and the diocese directory's pin for 501 South Orchard Road actually sits at
that Onondaga Boulevard site, 1.2 km south. The directory's "St. Charles Borromeo" entry
(no address; the pre-merger parish) is linked here. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
