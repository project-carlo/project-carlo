## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 Spirit of Hope bulletin (ParishesOnline 04/0592, page-1
image) lists Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 10:45 am and Monday-Friday 8:30 am at St. Joseph, and the
13 July 2025 issue (quoted, text layer) and the parish's own St. Joseph page (read 2026-09-26)
give the same times. It is also the parish's central office, and since the end of 2024 it has
taken St. Patrick's Sunday Mass (`excluded/st-patrick-oneida.md`).

## Name and address

121 St. Joseph Place, Oneida, from GCatholic, the diocese's finder, the bulletin masthead and the
parish site, all agreeing; GCatholic and the diocese are independent of the parish, so
`address_verified` is true. The name is "St. Joseph" in the rosters and "St. Joseph's Church" on
the parish page; the flag stays false only because the forms differ slightly. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code, within about 10 m of the ParishesOnline organization's point.
