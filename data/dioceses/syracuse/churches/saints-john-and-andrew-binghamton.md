## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (container 06/0217, page 1) lists Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:30 am and Monday, Wednesday and Friday 6:30 am. The St. Vincent de Paul Blessed Sacrament bulletin of the same week prints the same times.

This is also the former St. John the Evangelist church: the parish office is at 7 Livingston Street and the church at 1263 Vestal Avenue, and church-listing sites still list "St John The Evangelist Parish" at 1263 Vestal Ave with the parish's phone number. The diocese's list says "St. John the Evangelist, Binghamton (See The Parish of Saints John and Andrew)" without "CLOSED", and keeps records only for St. Andrew here. So the finder's "St. John the Evangelist (Closed)" (directory:33135) is linked to this record; St. Andrew's church is the one that closed (`excluded/st-andrew-binghamton.md`).

## Name and address

GCatholic (1263 Vestal Ave.), the diocese's finder and parish page, the bulletin masthead ("1263 Vestal Avenue, Binghamton, NY 13903") and the ParishesOnline organization agree on the street address; GCatholic's postal code 13901 disagrees with everyone else's 13903, and I followed 13903. OSM has the building as "The Parish of Saints John and Andrew Catholic Church" (way 450684775). Both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded.
