## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 Church of the Nativity bulletin gives Sunday Mass here at
9:30 a.m., and confession and adoration on Mondays 6-7 p.m.; the parish site's St. Joseph's
page gives the same Sunday time.

## Name and address

The parish site, GCatholic and the diocese's finder all give 6104 US Route 20, LaFayette 13084,
so `address_verified` is true. OpenStreetMap has no church object here: a reverse lookup of the
Plus Code lands on a house at 5992 Cherry Valley Turnpike (Route 20), so OSM neither confirms
nor contradicts the number. The name is "St. Joseph’s" in the bulletin and "St. Joseph's
Church" on the parish site; GCatholic ("Nativity at St. Joseph") and the diocese ("Church of the
Nativity @ St. Joseph") prefix the parish's name. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded.
