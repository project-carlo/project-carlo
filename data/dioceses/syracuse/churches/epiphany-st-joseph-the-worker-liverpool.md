## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The Liverpool Catholic Community bulletin of 27 September 2026 lists Sunday Masses at
Epiphany (EP) at 8:00, 9:30 and 11:00 am, the Saturday 4:00 pm vigil, and 9:00 am weekday Masses.

## Name and address

One building, two names. The church was built as St. Joseph the Worker; since the 2019 union with
Immaculate Heart of Mary it is the church of Epiphany Parish, and the 2023 decree made it "the sole
church of the parish". GCatholic still calls it "St. Joseph the Worker"; the diocese's finder and
the bulletin call it "Epiphany Parish"; OSM calls it "St. Joseph the Worker Church"; the diocese's
Epiphany page says "Formerly St. Joseph the Worker." The record uses both so it can be found under
either, and `name_verified` stays false because the sources do not give one name.

The address is not disputed: GCatholic, the diocese (1001 Tulip Street, 13088), the bulletin and OSM
agree, so `address_verified` is true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded.
