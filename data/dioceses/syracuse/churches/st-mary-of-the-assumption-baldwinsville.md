## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (ParishesOnline 06/0074) gives Saturday Vigil 4 PM
and Sunday 9 AM here, and weekday Mass Monday 9 AM and Tuesday 7 PM "at St. Mary’s". The joint
Baldwinsville schedule (bvillecatholic.org, effective 30 August 2025) and St. Elizabeth Ann
Seton's 20 September bulletin list the same times.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, GCatholic and the diocese's finder all give 47 Syracuse Street,
Baldwinsville 13027, so `address_verified` is true. OpenStreetMap has the church building
("St. Mary's Roman Catholic Church") at number 49 Syracuse Street, a few metres from GCatholic's
Plus Code: the same building, with a neighbouring number, so 47 is kept. The name is "St. Mary of
the Assumption" in the bulletin and the diocese, "St. Mary of Assumption" in GCatholic; the
bulletin's form is used and `name_verified` is true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code,
decoded (on the OSM building); the ParishesOnline organization's point (43.1623, -76.3207) is
about 1 km off and was not used.
