## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 Church of the Nativity bulletin gives Sunday Mass here at
8 a.m.; the parish site's Immaculate Conception page gives the same time.

## Name and address

The parish site, GCatholic, and the diocese (finder and parish page) all give 7386 Academy
Street, Pompey 13138, so `address_verified` is true. OpenStreetMap has no church object; the
nearest address point to the Plus Code is 7396 Academy Street, which does not contradict it.
The name is "Immaculate Conception" everywhere, with GCatholic and the diocese prefixing the
parish's name ("Nativity at", "Church of the Nativity @"). The records of the closed St. Paul
Mission, Fabius are kept here (`excluded/st-paul-mission-fabius.md`). Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded.
