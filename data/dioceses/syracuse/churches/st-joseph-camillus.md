Yes, daily except Tuesday and Thursday. The 27 September 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 4:30pm,
Sunday 8:00am and 10:45am, and Monday, Wednesday and Friday 9:00am, and its page-2 liturgy
schedule lists those Masses for the week ahead.

`name_verified` is true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded.
