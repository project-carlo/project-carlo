## Holds Mass

Yes. The 20 September 2026 combined bulletin lists Saturday 4:00pm (Vigil) and Sunday 8:00am
and 11:00am at Sacred Heart - St. Mary's Church, with weekday Masses Monday to Wednesday
12:15pm, and a monthly Charismatic healing Mass there (6 September issue).

## Name and address

The church's own name, as in the diocese's finder and its July 2026 masthead, is "Church of
the Sacred Heart & St. Mary, Our Lady of Czestochowa"; GCatholic has "Sacred Heart and St.
Mary, Our Lady of Czestochowa". All three give 201 Main St., New York Mills 13417. Both flags
true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded. The former Sacred Heart parish of
Utica, whose records the diocese keeps here, had its own church (`excluded/sacred-heart-utica.md`).
