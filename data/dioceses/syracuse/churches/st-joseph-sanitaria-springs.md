## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 Eastern Broome bulletin (page 1) gives "St. Joseph's Sanitaria Springs Mass Time: Sunday 8:30 am", and page 3 the week's intentions.

## Name and address

GCatholic and the bulletin give 659 State Route 7B ("659 NY Rt. 7-B Sanitaria Springs, NY 13833"); the diocese's finder gives the same number and road but the town as Port Crane, and its parish list names it "St. Joseph, Sanitaria Springs/Port Crane". Sanitaria Springs is a hamlet next to Port Crane in the Town of Fenton; I followed the parish and GCatholic. OSM's nearest address point to GCatholic's Plus Code is 699 State Highway 7B, with no church node. Both flags are true (GCatholic and the parish agree).
