## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 bulletin lists Saturday Vigil 5:00 pm and Sunday 9:00 am here (SP), and Tuesday and Thursday 12:10 pm.

## Name and address

**The address is disputed.** GCatholic and the diocese's finder give 15 Doubleday St.; the diocese's St. Mary page gives 15 Doubleday Street as the two parishes' "Office & Mailing Address"; the bulletin masthead and Discover Mass give the church at "282 Chenango Street". OSM's nearest address point to GCatholic's Plus Code is 289 Chenango Street. So 15 Doubleday is most likely the office/rectory and the church fronts Chenango Street. I followed the bulletin (282 Chenango Street) and left `address_verified` false: only the parish's own sources give it. The name agrees in substance (`name_verified` true). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded.
