## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (Discover Mass, page 1) lists Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 11:00 am and Wednesday and Thursday 9:00 am here.

This building was Our Lady of Angels church: OSM still has a place_of_worship named "Our Lady of Angels Church" (node 357575689) about 10 m from the 3600 Phyllis Street address point and from GCatholic's Plus Code, and GCatholic gives the church's history as 2008, the year Our Lady of Angels and Christ the King merged into Holy Family. So the finder's "Our Lady of Angels (Closed)" (directory:33057) is linked here; Christ the King's church on Davis Avenue is the one excluded.

## Name and address

GCatholic, the diocese's finder and parish page, the bulletin masthead ("3600 Phyllis Street, Endwell, New York 13760") and Discover Mass agree, and OSM has an address point for 3600 Phyllis Street. Both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded.
