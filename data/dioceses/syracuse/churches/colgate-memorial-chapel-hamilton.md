## Holds Mass

Yes, during the academic year: Holy Spirit Parish's bulletin for 27 September 2026 lists
"8:30 pm Sunday at Colgate Chapel when in session", with Masses on 27 September and 4 October in
its intentions (page 4), and Colgate University's Catholic Community page gives "Memorial Chapel
Sunday, 8:30 p.m." (read 2026-09-26). A campus chapel with public Sunday Mass counts as a worship
site. It is in neither roster, so it moves no coverage number; it is recorded because the parish's
own schedule lists it.

## Name and address

The bulletin says "Colgate Chapel"; Colgate's page and OSM say "Memorial Chapel" / "Colgate
Memorial Chapel", so the name is verified. The chapel has no street address of its own: 13 Oak
Drive is the university's address (Colgate's page footer), and OSM tags the building "25 Campus
Footpaths". `address_verified` is false for that reason. Coordinates are the OSM building's.
The bulletin also names a Newman ministry at SUNY Morrisville, but lists no Mass there.
