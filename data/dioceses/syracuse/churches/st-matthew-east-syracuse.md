## Holds Mass

Yes. The One Catholic Family bulletin of 20 September 2026 schedules the Saturday 4:30 pm vigil and
Sunday 8 am Mass at St Matthew's, and Saturday confessions there.

## Name and address

**The street address is disputed.** The diocese gives the church as 100 Kinne Street and 229 West
Yates Street as the "Office & Mailing Address"; GCatholic gives the church as 229 W Yates St.; OSM
tags the church building itself "229 West Yates Street"; the bulletin gives 229 W Yates Street as the
parish office. GCatholic's Plus Code and the OSM building coincide, and the diocese's own map pin
(43.06478, -76.07629) is about 170 m south, near Kinne Street. So all sources agree on the building
but not on its number; it may sit on the corner with an address on each street, and the office may
be in or beside it. Modelled at 229 W Yates St. (GCatholic and OSM) with `address_verified` false.
The parish office or a look at the building would settle which street the church door is on. The
name is St. Matthew everywhere, so `name_verified` is true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code,
decoded, on the OSM building.
