## Holds Mass

**Not confirmed for 2026.** It is one of the four churches of Holy Spirit Parish, the parish's principal church under the 2020 decree. The
2020 merger decree closed none of them, and GCatholic (fetched 2026-09-25) still lists it. A
web-search summary attributed a weekend Mass here to a July 2025 Route 20 Catholic bulletin, but
that PDF now returns 404 and I could not read it, so it is not recorded as evidence. The
parish's `revisit` (read a current bulletin) settles this for all four churches.

## Name and address

GCatholic and the Rome Sentinel both give 16 Wylie St., and OSM has an address point for 16 Wylie Street about 30 m from GCatholic's Plus Code. The ParishesOnline organization for St. Mary's gives the same address. Two independent sources agree, so `address_verified` is true. The name is St. Mary in GCatholic and "St. Mary's Church" in the Sentinel; the flag stays false until a bulletin or the parish site confirms what the parish calls it. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded.
