## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 20 September 2026 bulletin masthead and the parish homepage give the same
schedule: weekdays 7 am and noon, Saturday 5:10 pm, Sunday 7:30 am, 9:45 am and 5:10 pm.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the homepage and the ParishesOnline organization all give 259 East
Onondaga Street, 13202; the name is the same everywhere. The masthead and homepage come from
the parish, and the ParishesOnline record from its publisher. OpenStreetMap, which is
independent of all three, has the church at the same name and address, so both flags are true.
Coordinates are the centre of that OSM building (way 219998565); the ParishesOnline point
(43.046708, -76.149843) is about 20 m away, on the same building.

The cathedral is **not in the GCatholic roster**: gcatholic.org/churches/local/syra0 lists the
diocese's churches without its cathedral, so `coverage` reports this row under
`churches_in_no_roster`. That is expected and is not a reason to doubt the row.
