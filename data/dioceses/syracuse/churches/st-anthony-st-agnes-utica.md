## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (Discover Mass) lists Saturday 4:30 p.m. and Sunday 9:30
a.m. at St. Anthony & St. Agnes, and Monday and Tuesday 8:30 a.m.; that weekend also had
extra Italian Masses for the Sts. Cosmas & Damian feast. Mount Carmel's bulletin lists the
same Saturday and Sunday Masses "at St. Anthony".

## Name and address

The bulletin (422 Tilden Ave), GCatholic (St. Anthony and St. Agnes, 422 Tilden Ave., 13501)
and the diocese's finder agree; both flags true. This is the former St. Anthony of Padua
church: the parishes merged in 2008 "with one worship site. St. Agnes church closed
permanently", so the finder's "St. Anthony of Padua (Closed)" (directory:33097) is the same
building and is linked here; the diocese's list says "St. Anthony of Padua, Utica (See St.
Anthony & St. Agnes, Utica)" without "CLOSED". Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
