## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 27 September 2026 bulletin gives the Saturday vigil at 4:15 pm, Sunday 9:15 am, and
weekday Mass Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday at 8:15 am (Hannon Chapel).

## Name and address

**The number and ZIP code are disputed.** The bulletin and GCatholic say 203 Roxboro Rd; the diocese's
finder and the ParishesOnline account say 200 Roxboro Road; OSM puts the church on Kirsch Drive
(100 Kirsch Drive), which runs behind it. The bulletin gives ZIP 13212; GCatholic, the diocese and
OSM give 13211. Modelled at 203 Roxboro Rd., 13211 (GCatholic, agreeing with the parish on the
number and with the diocese on the ZIP), with `address_verified` false. The name is St. Margaret
everywhere (the ParishesOnline account name "St. Margaret Mary" aside), so `name_verified` is true.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded, on the OSM building.
