## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 Christ the Good Shepherd bulletin (page 1) lists Sunday 10:15 am at
Our Lady of the Rosary, and its page-2 intentions have "10:15am - OLR" for 27 September and 4 October.
It is a mission served from Christ the Good Shepherd, Oswego, and shares its bulletin.

## Name and address

"Our Lady of the Rosary" in the bulletin and GCatholic, "Our Lady of the Rosary Mission" in the
directory: the same name, so `name_verified` is true. The number differs: the bulletin masthead and the
diocese directory give **931** Cayuga Street, GCatholic **923** Cayuga St. Modelled at 931, where the
parish and the diocese (independent of each other) agree; `address_verified` is true on that basis,
with GCatholic's 923 recorded here as the dissent. GCatholic's Plus Code and the directory pin are
about 25 m apart, on the same building; coordinates are the Plus Code.
