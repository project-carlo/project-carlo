## Holds Mass

Yes. The 20 September 2026 Holy Cross bulletin puts all Masses "on our Keyes Road (St.
Mark's) Campus" unless noted: Saturday 4:00pm, Sunday 10:00am, Monday 10:00am.

## Name and address

440 Keyes Rd., Utica 13502 in the bulletin, GCatholic and the diocese's finder ("Holy Cross
Parish @ St. Mark"): `address_verified` true. The building is St. Mark's; the parish is now
Holy Cross, and the bulletin masthead reads "Church of the Holy Cross". Whether the building
itself has been renamed Holy Cross is not settled by any source, so the row is named "St.
Mark's Church (Holy Cross Parish)" and `name_verified` is false. Coordinates are GCatholic's
Plus Code.
