## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (container 03/0543, page 1, read as an image) lists Sunday 8:45 am here and weekday Mass Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8:00 am.

This building was St. Ann's church. Holy Trinity's history page: "On November 9, 2003, the closing mass of St. Ann's Catholic Church was celebrated" and "The building that housed the former St. Ann's Church underwent extensive renovations from late 2003 through the spring of 2004. On June 12, 2004 the new sanctuary of the Church of the Holy Trinity was dedicated." St. Ann's was on Prospect Street ("the McNamara farm … on Prospect Street"). So the finder's "St. Anne (Closed)" (directory:33085, no address) is the same building and is linked here: the parish closed, the building still holds Mass.
