## Holds Mass

Yes, on Sundays during the academic year. SUNY Cortland's Catholic ministry page gives a Fall 2026
schedule of Sunday 4 pm Masses at the chapel from 30 August to 6 December (none on 18 October and
29 November), and the Cortland parishes' bulletin prints "4 PM Mass at The Newman Center" in its
weekend schedule from 12/13 September 2026. The Cortland clergy celebrate it (the diocese assigns the
parochial vicar "sacramental responsibilities, SUNY Cortland Newman Center"), so it is a church of the
Cortland row. Masses stop in the college's breaks; that is still a place with public Mass.

No roster lists it: the diocese's finder and GCatholic both omit it, so `coverage` shows it under
churches in no roster. That is expected.

## Name and address

The address is 8 Calvert Street, Cortland 13045 in the college's page and in OpenStreetMap, so
`address_verified` is true. The name varies: "O'Heron Newman Hall Catholic Chapel" (college),
"O'Heron Newman Catholic Center" (OSM), "O'Heron Newman Hall" (Discover Mass), "The Newman Center"
(bulletin). `name_verified` stays false; I used the college's name for the chapel. Coordinates are the
OSM node.
