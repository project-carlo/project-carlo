## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The Cortland and Homer parishes' bulletin (the 20 September 2026 issue; Discover Mass
`st-mary-cortland-ny`) prints the pastor's new weekend schedule, effective 12/13 September 2026, with
"5:15 PM Vigil Mass at St. Lawrence's" every Saturday. St. Lawrence is "a Mission of St. Margaret,
Homer" (the diocese's page), and the same pastor serves Cortland, Homer, DeRuyter and Truxton, so
its Mass is published in that bulletin. It is under region H's parish row `st-mary-cortland`; DeRuyter
has no bulletin or website of its own that I found (the directory gives none).

## Name and address

**The number is disputed.** The diocese (finder and page) and OSM give 1672 Cortland Street, and the
diocese's pin and the OSM node are within 10 m of each other; GCatholic gives 1976 Cortland St. and
its Plus Code is about 470 m west, on another part of the street. Modelled at 1672 with OSM's
coordinates, and `address_verified` false because GCatholic disagrees. The name is "St. Lawrence
Mission" (diocese), "St. Lawrence" (GCatholic), "St. Lawrence Church" (OSM) and "St. Lawrence's" (the
bulletin), so `name_verified` stays false.
