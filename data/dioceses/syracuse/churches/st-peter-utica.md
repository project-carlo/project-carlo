## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly but only on a weekday. The 20 September 2026 Holy Cross bulletin lists
"Wednesday, September 23, 10:00am (St. Peter's)" among the week's Masses (all others at St.
Mark's), and says baptisms, marriages and funerals remain available on both campuses "until
further notice". The parish's merger note chose Keyes Road as the parish's home, so this
church may close; see `revisit`.
