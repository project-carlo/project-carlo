## Holds Mass

Yes, daily except Monday (a communion service then). The parish's Mass Schedule page and its
27 September 2026 bulletin give Saturday vigil 4:30pm, Sunday 8:00am and 10:15am, and Tuesday to
Friday 8:15am. The Catholic Sun reports the 175th-anniversary Mass here on 13 September 2026.

## Name and address

**The address is disputed between two streets of one corner.** GCatholic gives 81 Jordan St.;
the diocese (finder and parish page), the parish site's footer and the bulletin's mailing address
give 10 West Austin Street. The Catholic Sun places "the brick church on the corner of Austin and
Jordan Streets", and OSM has address points for both numbers about 35 m apart on either side of
that corner, with GCatholic's Plus Code next to 81 Jordan Street. 10 West Austin is what the
parish calls its address (the bulletin labels it the mailing address), and 81 Jordan is probably
the church's own frontage, but no source says which door is the church. Modelled at 81 Jordan St.
(GCatholic, whose Plus Code is on the building) with `address_verified` false; the parish office
or a street-level look would settle it.

The name is "St. Mary's of the Lake" in the bulletin, the parish site and the diocese's finder,
and "St. Mary of the Lake" in GCatholic and the diocese's parish list: `name_verified` is true.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, decoded.
