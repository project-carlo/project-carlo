## Publisher

Christ Our Light (Pulaski) and St. Anne, Mother of Mary (Mexico) are two canonical parishes,
"canonically linked" under one administrator, that publish **one** weekly bulletin through eCatholic
(site 25311). I read the 27 September 2026 issue: page 1 is headed "CHRIST OUR LIGHT and ST. ANNE,
MOTHER OF MARY", page 2 gives both churches and offices, and page 3 lists every Mass as COL or SAMOM.
The diocese lists each parish separately, with the same website for both. One row, two churches.

## Worship sites

- `churches/christ-our-light-pulaski.md`: 23 Niagara Street, Pulaski (the former St. John the
  Evangelist church).
- `churches/st-anne-mother-of-mary-mexico.md`: 3352 Main Street, Mexico (consecrated as St. Mary Star
  of the Sea).

The bulletin and the joint site list no other place. Predecessor parishes the diocese lists as
closed: St. John the Evangelist, Pulaski (its building is Christ Our Light's church: linked there);
St. Francis Xavier Cabrini, Lacona and St. Mary of the Immaculate Conception Mission, Altmar
(`excluded/`, closed); St. Anne, Parish (`excluded/st-anne-parish.md`, closed). The diocese's list
also names St. Joseph Mission, Boylston; St. Anne, Colosse; St. Francis of Assisi Mission, Little
France; and St. Mary, Star of the Sea, Mexico: none is in a roster, and the last is the Mexico church
itself.

## Website and bulletin provider

stannechristourlight.com is the joint eCatholic-hosted site (the directory gives it for both
parishes); it embeds eCatholic site id 25311, whose weekly PDFs are at
files.ecatholic.com/25311/bulletins/YYYYMMDD.pdf. `visit.py` labels the site SPAM-SQUAT, which is a
false positive: the only "spam" term is "porn" inside the title of a Bishop Barron YouTube video
("The Curse of Pornography") in an embedded playlist. The site is the parish's.
