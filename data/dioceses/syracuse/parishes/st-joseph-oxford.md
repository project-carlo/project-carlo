## Publisher

St. Joseph's in Oxford publishes its own "Parish Update" as a PDF on its website. I read the file
current on 26 September 2026: the 29/30 August 2026 issue, covering two weekends. Its masthead names
St. Joseph Church, 3 Scott St., alone, with "Saturday Vigil Mass 5:45 PM, Sunday Mass 8:30 AM" and
notices that there were no Vigil Masses on 29 August and 5 September during Fr. Paul Machira's
vacation. St. Joseph's is linked with Immaculate Conception, Greene (same sacramental minister; they
share Holy Week services), but Greene publishes its own newsletter, so this is its own row.

## Website and bulletin provider

stjosephsoxfordny.com is the parish's own site (diocese and GCatholic agree). The homepage's
"This week..." link is resources/bulletin.pdf, a single file replaced with each issue, so the
publication id is that file's URL and the provider is `other`. The update appears to come out
every two weeks or less often, not weekly.
