## Publisher

St. Vincent de Paul Blessed Sacrament publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline,
container 03/1214. I read the 27 September 2026 issue: the masthead is "Saint Vincent de Paul
Blessed Sacrament Church", with the church at 465 Clubhouse Road and the office at 165 Clifton
Boulevard, and its Mass schedule is Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 10:15 am and Tuesday-Friday 9:00 am.

Page 3's weekly list also prints "Mass at Sts. John & Andrew" times, because Fr. Serowik is pastor
of both parishes. Sts. John & Andrew has its own current bulletin (container 06/0217), so it is a
separate row in the same group, not a church of this one.

## Worship sites

One: the church at 465 Clubhouse Road (`churches/st-vincent-de-paul-blessed-sacrament-vestal.md`).
Blessed Sacrament, Johnson City, merged into this parish on 1 January 2012 after its final prayer
service on 10 December 2011, per the parish's history page; it is excluded as closed
(`excluded/blessed-sacrament-johnson-city.md`). The diocese's list has "St. Vincent de Paul,
Vestal (See St. Vincent de Paul Blessed Sacrament)", which is this same church.
