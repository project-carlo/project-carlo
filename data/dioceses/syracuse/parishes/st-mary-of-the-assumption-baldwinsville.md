## Publisher

St. Mary of the Assumption publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (LPi),
container 06/0074. I read the 20 and 27 September 2026 issues. The page-1 masthead is St.
Mary's: its name, "47 Syracuse St, Baldwinsville", its website, its office hours, its
academy, and "St. Mary’s Weekend Masses Saturday Vigil: 4 PM Sunday: 9 AM". The same masthead
also prints the weekend Masses of the other two Baldwinsville parishes (St. Augustine's Sunday
10:30 AM; St. Elizabeth Ann Seton Saturday 5:30 PM, Sunday 7:30 and 10:30 AM) and the shared
weekday schedule, because the three share their clergy (Bishop Douglas Lucia is pastor of all
three, Rev. Benjamin Schrantz parochial vicar) and one Mass rota (bvillecatholic.org).

**Three rows, not one.** Each of the three publishes its own bulletin: St. Augustine's through
its own ParishesOnline container (04/0667, masthead "St. Augustine’s Church") and St. Elizabeth
Ann Seton on its own website (masthead "St. Elizabeth Ann Seton Parish Staff"). All three
channels are live for September 2026, so under "one live member channel means no merge" each
is its own row, grouped as "Baldwinsville Catholic Parishes". Each row carries only its own
church; the other parishes' Masses in this masthead are read from their own bulletins.
