## Publisher

St. Malachy's in Sherburne publishes its own weekly bulletin on its website. I read the 13 and 20
September 2026 issues: page 1 welcomes you to "St. Malachy of Armagh Roman Catholic Church, 29 East
State Street, Sherburne" and gives only its schedule (Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 8:30 am, Friday 9 am);
page 2's Mass schedule has only St. Malachy's Masses. It shares its pastor with St. Paul's and St.
Bartholomew's in Norwich and St. Theresa's in New Berlin, and prints news from them (St. Theresa's
70th anniversary, the Norwich Saturday Mass moving to St. Bartholomew's), but none of them is in its
schedule, so this is a one-church row.
