## Publisher

The Church of Saints John and Andrew publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline,
container 06/0217. I read the 27 September 2026 issue: the page-1 masthead is "The Church of
Saints John and Andrew", 1263 Vestal Avenue, with Mass Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:30 am and
Monday, Wednesday and Friday 6:30 am, all at the one church.

Its pastor, Fr. James Serowik, is also pastor of St. Vincent de Paul Blessed Sacrament in Vestal,
whose own bulletin (container 03/1214) prints Sts. John & Andrew's Mass times alongside its own.
Both containers publish every week, so they are two rows in one group; see
`parishes/st-vincent-de-paul-blessed-sacrament-vestal.md`.
