## Publisher

St. John the Evangelist, 66 Oxford Rd, New Hartford, publishes its own weekly bulletin on
eCatholic (site id 35905). The issues are scanned images with no text layer, so I read the
27 September 2026 issue as page images: every page is headed "St. John the Evangelist
Church", and it carries only this church's schedule changes. The weekend schedule (Saturday
5:30 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 11:00 am; weekdays Mon/Wed/Fri 6:45 am) is quoted from the parish
homepage.

The diocese names Rev. Matthew Rawson pastor of both St. John's and the Church of the
Annunciation, Clark Mills, and a joint OCIA flyer in the bulletin names four parishes, but
Annunciation's Masses are not in this bulletin, so Annunciation is not a church of this row
(it publishes nothing: `excluded/annunciation-clark-mills.md`).
