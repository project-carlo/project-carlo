## Publisher

The Church of the Nativity publishes one weekly bulletin for its four churches through
ParishesOnline (LPi). I read the 27 September 2026 issue in container 06/0037. Its page-1
masthead lists one Saturday Mass ("4:30 p.m. St. Leo’s, Tully") and three Sunday Masses
("8 a.m. Immaculate Conception, Pompey", "9:30 a.m. St. Joseph’s, LaFayette", "11:30 a.m.
St. Patrick’s, Otisco"), gives a separate office, phone and trustees for each of the four
churches, and names one pastor, Rev. Dennis Walker. Page 2's Mass intentions use the same four
churches, with weekday Mass at St. Leo's. The parish's homepage gives the same schedule.

The diocese lists the four as "Church of the Nativity @ St. Leo, Tully", "@ St. Joseph,
LaFayette", "@ Immaculate Conception, Pompey" and "St. Patrick Mission, Otisco", and its page for
Pompey names Fr. Walker "PASTOR, CHURCH OF THE NATIVITY AT ST. LEO, TULLY; ST. PATRICK MISSION,
OTISCO; ST. JOSEPH, LAFAYETTE; IMMACULATE CONCEPTION, POMPEY". Fr. Walker's August 2026 letter
still speaks of "our four parishes", so canonically there may be four parishes, but they publish
one bulletin: one row.

## Worship sites

The four churches the masthead lists: St. Leo (Tully), St. Joseph (LaFayette), Immaculate
Conception (Pompey) and St. Patrick Mission (Otisco).

Not here: **St. Paul Mission, Fabius**. The diocese's parish list has it as "CLOSED - See Church
of the Nativity @ Immaculate Conception, Pompey", and the bulletin's schedule has no Mass in
Fabius; see `excluded/st-paul-mission-fabius.md`. The diocese's list also sends **Corpus Christi
Oratory, Nedrow** here ("CLOSED - See Church of the Nativity @ St. Leo, Tully"). Nedrow is in
another region's batch; the Nativity bulletin has no Mass there either.

## Website and bulletin provider

nativityny.org is the parish's own site (an OSV/Duda site). Its Bulletin page says the new
bulletin "gets posted on Friday evenings" and links the ParishesOnline organization
`immaculate-conception-church-13138`, which the bulletin masthead also names. The organization
lookup gives LPi id 0018000000Qc1XXAAZ and container 06/0037, weekly through 27 September 2026.
The org is titled "St. Leo's Church" and its stored website is the old icpompey.org, an example
of account names accumulating. GCatholic still gives the old per-church sites
(stjosephslafayette.org, icpompey.org).
