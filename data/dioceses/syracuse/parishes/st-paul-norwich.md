## Publisher

St. Paul's in Norwich publishes its own weekly bulletin as a PDF on its website. I read the 20
September 2026 issue: its masthead is "Church of St. Paul", with St. Paul's schedule only (Saturday
4 pm vigil April–September, Sunday 11 am, Wednesday 9 am) and its own staff, finances and
intentions. The same pastor, Fr. Daniel Caughey, has St. Bartholomew (Norwich), St. Malachy
(Sherburne) and St. Theresa (New Berlin). The page-2 Mass list mentions his Masses at those churches
by name ("8:30 AM Mass at St. Bart's", "5:30 PM Mass at St. Theresa's", "9:00 AM Mass at St.
Malachy's"), but St. Bartholomew's and St. Malachy's publish their own bulletins, so each is its own
row. St. Theresa's publishes nothing current (`excluded/st-theresa-of-the-infant-jesus-new-berlin.md`).

GCatholic gives both Norwich churches the website stpaulstbart.com; that domain no longer resolves
(DNS failure, 2026-09-26), and there is no sign of a joint bulletin now.

## Worship sites

One: St. Paul's, 30 Pleasant Street (`churches/st-paul-norwich.md`). The diocese keeps the records of
the former St. Patrick parish, Norwich, here; no roster lists a St. Patrick church in Norwich and I
did not look for its building.
