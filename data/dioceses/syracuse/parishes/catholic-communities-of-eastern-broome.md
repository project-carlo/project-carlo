## Publisher

The Catholic Communities of Eastern Broome are four canonical parishes (each listed separately by
the diocese: Our Lady of Lourdes, Windsor; St. Joseph, Deposit; St. Joseph, Sanitaria Springs/Port
Crane; St. Mary, Kirkwood) run by a parish life director, Barbara Kane, from one office in
Kirkwood, and they publish **one** weekly bulletin. I read the 27 September 2026 issue: its page-1
masthead is "The Catholic Communities of Eastern Broome" and lists all four churches with their
Mass times (Our Lady of Lourdes Sunday 9:00 am; St. Joseph's, Deposit Sunday 11:00 am; St.
Joseph's, Sanitaria Springs Sunday 8:30 am; St. Mary's, Kirkwood Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday
10:30 am), and page 3 lists the week's intentions and collections at all four. One bulletin, so
one row.

## Worship sites

Four: `churches/our-lady-of-lourdes-windsor.md`, `churches/st-joseph-deposit.md`,
`churches/st-joseph-sanitaria-springs.md` and `churches/st-mary-kirkwood.md`.

## Website and bulletin provider

broomecatholics.org is the communities' site (the directory gives it for all four). Its Bulletin
page links a PDF per Sunday on irp.cdn-website.com, named by date (e.g. september27web2026.pdf,
but july262026.pdf without "web"), so the provider is `other`, read from the website. None of the
four has a ParishesOnline or Discover Mass bulletin (their Discover Mass listings have no issues).
