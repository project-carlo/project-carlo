## Publisher

St. Michael & St. Peter and Our Lady of Hope share a pastor (Fr. Michael Galuppi) and
publish one weekly bulletin. The 27 September 2026 issue's masthead names both churches
with their addresses, and its weekend schedule gives Masses at both, coded OLOH and SMSP.
Our Lady of Hope's own site (ourladyofhopesyr.org) carries no bulletin.

## Worship sites

Two: St. Michael & St. Peter, 4791 West Seneca Turnpike (`st-michael-st-peter-syracuse`),
and Our Lady of Hope, 4845 South Salina Street, the former St. James church
(`our-lady-of-hope-syracuse`). Closed and not here: Our Lady of Lourdes, 300 Valley Drive
(`excluded/our-lady-of-lourdes-syracuse.md`), and the former St. Peter Mission, Split Rock
(`excluded/st-peter-mission-split-rock.md`).
