## Publisher

Most Holy Rosary publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 03/1240. I
read the 27 and 20 September 2026 issues: page 1 is Most Holy Rosary's alone ("Most Holy Rosary
Church, 2596 Main St., Maine"), with Mass on Sunday 9:30 am and Tuesday and Friday 9:00 am, and
its own trustees and office staff.

It shares its pastor (Fr. Clarence Rumble), deacon, faith-formation staff and website with the
Church of the Holy Family, Endwell, whose Discover Mass bulletin lists MHR's weekday Masses but
not its Sunday Mass. Both bulletins are current, so this is a separate row in the same group; see
`parishes/holy-family-endwell.md`.
