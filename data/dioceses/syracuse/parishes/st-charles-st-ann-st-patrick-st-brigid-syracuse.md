## Publisher

St. Charles-St. Ann and St. Patrick-St. Brigid are "linked parishes" under one pastor,
Rev. Kevin Maloney, and publish **one** bulletin through ParishesOnline, container
06/0134 (LPi footer "St Charles/St Ann, Syracuse, NY 06-0134"). The 27 September 2026
masthead names both parishes and both offices. Both parishes' ParishesOnline organizations
point at the same container, and St. Patrick-St. Brigid's site posts the same issue, so this
is one row.

## Worship sites

Two churches, one of them currently without Mass:

- St. Patrick, 216 North Lowell Avenue (`st-patrick-syracuse`): every Mass of both parishes
  is there now.
- St. Charles, 501 South Orchard Road (`st-charles-borromeo-syracuse`): closed for
  renovation since mid-August 2026. The 9 August issue still has SC Masses (Sat 5:15 pm,
  Sun 10:30 am); from 23 August the bulletin says renovations "will not allow for access to
  St. Charles-St. Ann's Church" for the foreseeable future and all Masses move to St.
  Patrick's. Kept as a church row with a revisit because the parish is renovating it to
  return; see that file.

Closed and excluded: St. Ann's church on Onondaga Boulevard (last Mass 29 January 2023,
`excluded/st-ann-syracuse.md`) and St. Brigid & St. Joseph, 318 Herkimer Street
(`excluded/st-brigid-st-joseph-syracuse.md`).
