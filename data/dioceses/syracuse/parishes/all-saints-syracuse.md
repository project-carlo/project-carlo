## Publisher

All Saints publishes its own weekly bulletin. The 26-27 September 2026 issue
(Bulletin-for-9-2627FINAL.pdf) has a masthead for All Saints Church alone, and a liturgy
schedule of Saturday 4 pm and Sunday 9 am and 11:30 am. The same issue reports a diocesan
decision that from 1 January 2027 All Saints' pastor, Fr. Fred Daley, becomes administrator
of St. Lucy's as well (the "Costello Project"), with shared Mass times and buildings still
under discussion; St. Lucy's still publishes its own bulletin, so the two stay separate
rows for now.

## Worship sites

One: the church at 1340 Lancaster Avenue (`all-saints-syracuse`), the former St. Therese
the Little Flower church. All Saints was formed in 2008 from Our Lady of Solace and St.
Therese; Our Lady of Solace's building is in `excluded/our-lady-of-solace-syracuse.md`. The
directory's "St. Therese the Little Flower (Closed)" entry is linked to the Lancaster Avenue
church, because the closed thing is the parish, not the building.

## Website and bulletin provider

allsaintssyracuse.org (Squarespace). Its "Bulletins" link (/newsletters) lists one post per
weekend, and each post links the PDF, so provider `other` with the homepage as website.
The site also embeds eCatholic files (`ecatholic:32299`, e.g. a Hope Appeal PDF), but no
bulletin is published there: issues.py found no Sunday-dated issue in that account, so the
id is not recorded as a publication.
