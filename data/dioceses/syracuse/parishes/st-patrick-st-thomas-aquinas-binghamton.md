## Publisher

St. Patrick and St. Thomas Aquinas, two canonical parishes with one pastor (Msgr. John P.
Putano), publish **one** weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 06/0167. I read the 27
September 2026 issue; its first pages have no text layer, so I read page 1 as a rendered image.
The masthead is "The Linked Roman Catholic Communities of St. Patrick's and St. Thomas Aquinas",
and its Mass schedule covers both churches: Saturday 4:00 pm St. Thomas Aquinas and 5:15 pm St.
Patrick's; Sunday 9:00 am St. Patrick's, 11:00 am St. Thomas Aquinas, 4:00 pm St. Patrick's;
weekdays at both. Page 2's intentions list both churches ("SP", "STA") and both collections. The
text footer on pages 7-8 names both. One bulletin, so one row.

## Worship sites
