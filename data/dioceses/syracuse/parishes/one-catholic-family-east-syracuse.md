## Publisher

Syracuse) are three canonical parishes under one pastor, Rev. Stephen Wirkes, and they publish one
weekly bulletin as "One Catholic Family" through ParishesOnline. I read the 20 September 2026 issue
of container 06/0321: the page-1 masthead names "St Francis  St Mary  St Matthew" with one parish
office at 229 W Yates Street, East Syracuse, and page 5 gives one weekend schedule for all three
churches. The weekly collection report on page 2 lists all three too. One bulletin, one row.
