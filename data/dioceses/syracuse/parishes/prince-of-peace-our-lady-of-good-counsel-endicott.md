## Publisher

Prince of Peace Parish, Endicott, is one canonical parish, formed on 1 July 2026 when Bishop
Lucia's decree united St. Ambrose, St. Anthony, St. Joseph and Our Lady of Good Counsel (St.
Joseph is the principal church, the other three "auxiliary churches" where "faithful will
continue to have the right of entry ... for public worship"). Three months on, it publishes
**two** weekly bulletins. This row is the Our Lady of Good Counsel one: ParishesOnline container 06/0139. I read
the 27 September 2026 issue. Page 1 carries the Prince of Peace name and OLGC's address and
website; page 2's "OLGC DAILY MASSES & INTENTIONS" lists only OLGC's Masses (Saturday 9:00 am,
Sunday 7:00 and 10:00 am, Friday 8:00 am) and OLGC's office hours and collection.

The other three churches have their own bulletin on Discover Mass
(`parishes/prince-of-peace-north-endicott.md`), also current for 27 September 2026, whose
schedule covers St. Ambrose, St. Anthony and St. Joseph and not OLGC. Two live channels, so two
rows in one group. The diocese gives both websites for the parish.
