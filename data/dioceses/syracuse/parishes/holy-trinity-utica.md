## Publisher

Holy Trinity (Utica) and the Church of the Sacred Heart & St. Mary, Our Lady of Czestochowa
(New York Mills) share a pastor, Fr. Mark P. Kaminski, and since 6 September 2026 one
bulletin. The 6 September issue in ParishesOnline container 06/0050 says "We are excited to
introduce our new combined parish bulletin!"; the 20 and 27 September issues carry the same
joint masthead ("Holy Trinity • 1206 Lincoln Ave, Utica ... Sacred Heart & St. Mary's • 201
Main Street, New York Mills") and list the week's Masses at both churches.

Before that there were two bulletins: Holy Trinity self-hosted its own at
holytrinityutica.com/bulletins/, and container 06/0050 (an LPi account named "Holy Trinity
Church of Utica") carried Sacred Heart & St. Mary's bulletin (its masthead on 19 July, 9, 23
and 30 August 2026). Both old channels now carry the combined issue: the container, Holy
Trinity's own list (latest 13 Sept, the combined masthead), and Sacred Heart & St. Mary's
bulletin page ("2026.09.06-HT+SHSM-Bulletin.pdf" onward). No member channel is still
publishing separately, so this is one row. An example of the README's trap: the LPi account
name said Holy Trinity while its content was Sacred Heart & St. Mary's.

## Worship sites

Two: Holy Trinity, 1206 Lincoln Ave, Utica (`churches/holy-trinity-utica.md`) and Sacred
Heart & St. Mary, 201 Main St, New York Mills (`churches/sacred-heart-st-mary-new-york-mills.md`).
The diocese lists two closed parishes whose records went here: St. Stanislaus, Utica (merged
into Holy Trinity in 2009, `excluded/st-stanislaus-utica.md`) and Sacred Heart, Utica (see
Sacred Heart & St. Mary, `excluded/sacred-heart-utica.md`). Neither building appears in
the bulletin's schedule. The bulletin also promotes the Utica Newman Outreach Mass at Utica
University, which is not this parish's worship site (`excluded/utica-newman-outreach.md`).
