## Publisher

Sacred Heart Parish (Rome and Lee Center) was formed on 1 July 2025 by merging the Rome
parishes of St. John the Baptist, St. Mary-St. Peter and St. Paul with St. Joseph, Lee Center
(Rome Sentinel, 6 May 2025). It publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline, container
03/1453. I read the 27 September 2026 issue: the page-1 masthead, "Welcome to Sacred Heart
Parish", names St. John the Baptist, St. Joseph's (Lee Center), St. Paul's and St. Peter's with
their addresses, and the page-3 "SACRED HEART MASS SCHEDULE" covers exactly those four.

Before the merger St. Paul's and St. Joseph's shared their own bulletin in container 03/1272
("Welcome to Sacred Heart Parish St. Paul's Church and St. Joseph's Church", 6 July 2025). That
container has had no issue since (403 on every Sunday probed from August 2025 to September 2026,
while 03/1453 answered 200 on the same dates), so it is not a second publisher. It is not in
`publication` because it is a different, dead bulletin, not an id of this one.

## Worship sites

Four, all with weekend Mass in the 27 September 2026 schedule:

- St. Peter's, 200 N. James St. (`churches/st-peter-rome.md`): Sun 10:30 am and 6:00 pm, weekdays.
- St. John the Baptist, 210 E. Dominick St. (`churches/st-john-the-baptist-rome.md`): Sat 8:00 am, Sun 8:30 am.
- St. Paul's, 1807 Bedford St. (`churches/st-paul-rome.md`): Sat 4:00 pm.
- St. Joseph's, Lee Center (`churches/st-joseph-lee-center.md`): Sat 6:00 pm.

The diocese also sends two closed Rome parishes here: Transfiguration (final Mass 5 May 2025,
`excluded/transfiguration-rome.md`) and St. Mary of the Assumption (sold 2022, demolished after
the 2024 tornado, `excluded/st-mary-of-the-assumption-rome.md`). The Ukrainian Catholic church of
St. Michael in Rome is not part of this parish (`excluded/st-michael-ukrainian-rome.md`).

## Website and bulletin provider

stmarysstpeters.com is the former St. Mary-St. Peter site, now titled "Sacred Heart Parish"; the
diocese's page and the ParishesOnline organization both give it as the parish website. It
self-hosted some bulletins in 2025 (e.g. wp-content/uploads/2025/11/11-16-2025-Sacred-Heart-Bulletin.pdf),
but the ParishesOnline organization `sacred-heart-parish-13440` (LPi 001RP00000WuwzlYAB,
container 03/1453) is the current weekly channel, so the provider is ParishesOnline.
