## Publisher

St. Mary (Cortland), St. Anthony of Padua (Cortland) and St. Margaret (Homer) publish one weekly
bulletin, edited at the shared Parish Center (59 N. Main St.) and posted on Discover Mass under
St. Mary. I read the 27 September 2026 issue: its page-1 masthead lists the three churches with
their addresses and one staff (pastor Fr. Artur Krawczenko, parochial vicar Fr. Moses Arung, OP),
and page 2 gives the week's Masses at all three (St. Mary's Sat 4 pm, Sun 8 and 11:45 am; St.
Anthony's Sun 9:15 am; St. Margaret's Sun 10:30 am; weekday Masses rotating among the three). The
same bulletin file is what both St. Mary's and St. Anthony's websites link, so this is one row.

The pastor's new weekend schedule, printed from 30 August and in force from 12/13 September 2026,
also lists "5:15 PM Vigil Mass at St. Lawrence's" and "4 PM Mass at The Newman Center". The diocese
gives the same pastor to "St. Lawrence Mission, DeRuyter" and the "SUNY Cortland Newman Community",
and summer issues offer Masses "For the Parishioners of St Anthony's, St Margaret's, St Mary's & St
Lawrence's Parishes". So this bulletin also covers those two places.

## Worship sites

- St. Mary, St. Anthony of Padua and St. Margaret (Homer): in every issue's masthead and Mass list.
- O'Heron Newman Hall chapel at SUNY Cortland (`churches/oheron-newman-hall-cortland.md`): Sunday
  4 pm in the new schedule; in no roster.
- St. Lawrence Mission, DeRuyter (`churches/st-lawrence-deruyter.md`): Saturday 5:15 pm Vigil
  ("5:15 PM Vigil Mass at St. Lawrence's") in the new schedule printed in the 13 and 20 September
  2026 issues. It shares this pastor and the Truxton PO box, and no bulletin of its own was found.
- St. Patrick's Oratory, Truxton ("an Oratory of St. Margaret, Homer" per the diocese) is in the
  bulletin only as open for prayer on Thursday afternoons, never with a Mass:
  `excluded/st-patrick-truxton.md`.
- St. Bridget Mission, Solon, is listed by the diocese as closed with its records here; no roster
  entry, and the bulletin never mentions it. Not recorded.

## Website and bulletin provider

Discover Mass lists weekly issues for `st-mary-cortland-ny` (twelve issues, newest 27 September 2026);
its pages for St. Anthony and St. Margaret list none. The parishes' own sites link a single file,
saintanthonyofcortland.com/bulletins/bulletin.pdf, overwritten each week and a week behind Discover
Mass on 26 September. The row uses Discover Mass (`provider_id: st-mary-cortland-ny`) and records the
self-hosted file as a second publication id. The website is St. Mary's site, which links that file.
