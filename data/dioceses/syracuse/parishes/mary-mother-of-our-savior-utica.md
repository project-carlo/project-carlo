## Publisher

Mary, Mother of Our Savior Parish publishes one weekly bulletin. I read the 20 September 2026
issue (ParishesOnline container 06/0146; the same file is self-hosted on the parish site):
page 1 gives one schedule, "4:00PM Saturday Vigil Mass, 8:15AM, 10:30AM, 5:00PM Sunday Mass"
and weekday Masses, all at the Lourdes church since 19 July.

The parish has been one entity since 2021 ("functioning as one parochial entity called
'Mary, Mother of our Savior' (i.e., shared clergy, ... bulletin, etc.) with two worship sites
since 2021", per the decree), and the bishop's decree of 4 June 2026 merged Our Lady of Lourdes
(Utica) and Our Lady of the Rosary (New Hartford) canonically, effective 16 July 2026.

## Worship sites

One: Our Lady of Lourdes, 2222 Genesee St (`churches/our-lady-of-lourdes-utica.md`). Our
Lady of the Rosary, 1736 Burrstone Rd, New Hartford, was relegated by the same decree; its
last Mass was the Mass of Thanksgiving on 16 July 2026 (`excluded/our-lady-of-the-rosary-new-hartford.md`).
The parish's Mass Times web page also lists "Monday, Wednesday & Friday 6:45 am St. John the
Evangelist"; the bulletin does not, and those are St. John the Evangelist's own weekday Masses
(see that parish), so no church is added here for them.
