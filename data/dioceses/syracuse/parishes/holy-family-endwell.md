## Publisher

The Church of the Holy Family publishes its own weekly bulletin through Discover Mass. I read the
27 September 2026 issue: the page-1 masthead is "Church of the Holy Family, 3600 Phyllis Street,
Endwell", and its Mass schedule is Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 11:00 am and Wednesday and
Thursday 9:00 am at Holy Family, plus "Tuesday & Friday - Most Holy Rosary: 9:00 am".

Fr. Clarence Rumble is pastor of both Holy Family and Most Holy Rosary (Maine), and the two share
a website. But Most Holy Rosary publishes its own bulletin (ParishesOnline 03/1240, current, with
its own Sunday 9:30 am Mass that the Holy Family schedule does not list), so it is a separate row
in the same group; see `parishes/most-holy-rosary-maine.md`. Page 7 of this bulletin carries a
Most Holy Rosary notices section, but not its weekend Mass.

## Worship sites

One: `churches/holy-family-endwell.md`. The church at 3600 Phyllis Street is the former Our Lady
of Angels church (OSM still names the building "Our Lady of Angels Church"), so the finder's "Our
Lady of Angels (Closed)" is linked to it. Christ the King, the other parish merged into Holy
Family in 2008, had its own church on Davis Avenue, which is excluded as closed
(`excluded/christ-the-king-endwell.md`).

## Website and bulletin provider

holyfamilyendwell.com (the directory's site) stays behind Cloudflare, as the parish-site-access
skill records; the bulletin masthead now gives holyfamilyholyrosary.org, which was also blocked
(visit.py BLOCKED, 2026-09-26). It is the site the parish names, so it is the `website`. The
bulletin itself is on Discover Mass (`church-of-the-holy-family-endwell-ny`).
