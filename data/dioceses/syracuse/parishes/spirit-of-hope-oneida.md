## Publisher

Spirit of Hope Parish (Oneida) is one canonical parish, formed in June 2023 by merging eight
parishes of Oneida, Canastota, Sherrill, Vernon, Verona Beach, North Bay and Irish Ridge (the
Catholic Sun's text of the decree, 7 June 2023). It publishes one weekly bulletin through
ParishesOnline, container 04/0592. I read the 27 September 2026 issue. Its page-1 masthead is
"Spirit of Hope Parish, A Catholic Community", with the Central Office at 121 St. Joseph Pl.,
Oneida, and four worship sites: St. Agatha (Canastota), St. Mary of the Lake (Verona Beach),
St. Joseph (Oneida) and Holy Family (Vernon). The Mass schedule covers exactly those four. That
issue is scanned images with no text layer, so the quotable evidence is the 13 July 2025 issue,
which has a text layer and prints the same masthead and schedule.

## Worship sites

Four, from the September 2026 masthead:

- St. Joseph, Oneida (`churches/st-joseph-oneida.md`): Sat 4:00 pm, Sun 10:45 am, weekday Mass.
- Holy Family, Vernon (`churches/holy-family-vernon.md`): Sun 7:00 and 10:00 am.
- St. Agatha, Canastota (`churches/st-agatha-canastota.md`): Sun 8:45 am.
- St. Mary of the Lake, Verona Beach (`churches/st-mary-of-the-lake-verona-beach.md`): Sat 5:30 pm,
  Memorial Day to Columbus Day only.

The diocese and the parish site still list four more churches of the merged parish. None of them
has Mass now, and each has its own `excluded/` record:

- St. Patrick, Oneida (the canonical principal church): Masses moved to St. Joseph's at the end
  of 2024 because the ceiling is unsafe (`excluded/st-patrick-oneida.md`).
- St. Helena, Sherrill: final Mass 29 June 2025 (`excluded/st-helena-sherrill.md`).
- St. John, North Bay: relegated to profane use by decree, 26 June 2025 (`excluded/st-john-north-bay.md`).
- St. Mary, Irish Ridge: closed in 2000, now a devotional chapel and cemetery (`excluded/st-mary-irish-ridge.md`).

The diocese's list also sends three closed parishes here, recorded as exclusions: St. Francis
of Assisi (Durhamville), St. Therese of the Infant Jesus (Munnsville) and Holy Cross (New London).
Two more that it names, St. Agnes Mission (Vernon Center) and St. Catherine (Vernon), are in
neither roster and were not researched.

## Website and bulletin provider

spiritofhopecatholic.org is the parish's own site, but it links no bulletin. The bulletin
comes from the ParishesOnline organization `spirit-of-hope-catholic-community` (LPi id
0018000000Qc1XIAAZ, container 04/0592), which lists weekly issues through 27 September 2026
and gives the parish site as its website. All three ids are in `publication`. Discover Mass has
church pages for St. Joseph and St. Helena, but they list no bulletins.
