## Publisher

Immaculate Conception in Greene publishes its own weekly newsletter on its website. I read the 19/20
September 2026 issue: page 1 is headed "The Church of the Immaculate Conception", names no other
church, and schedules the weekend's 4 pm Vigil and 10 am Sunday Masses. The parish is "linked with
St. Josephs in Oxford; the same priest travels to both churches" (its Mass Times page), and the
diocese names Fr. Paul Machira sacramental minister of both, but St. Joseph's publishes its own
"Parish Update" (`parishes/st-joseph-oxford.md`), so they are two rows. The diocese also lists Fr.
James Serowik (Vestal/Binghamton, region G) as canonical pastor here and of St. John, Bainbridge;
that is a canonical arrangement, and neither shares this bulletin.
