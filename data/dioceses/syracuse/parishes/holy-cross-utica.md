## Publisher

Holy Cross Parish is the merger of St. Mark (440 Keyes Rd) and St. Peter (422 Coventry Ave),
North Utica. It publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 06/0233
(org `st-mark-church-13502`). The 20 September 2026 issue's masthead is "Church of the Holy
Cross", 440 Keyes Rd., with both old parish email addresses, and its Mass list says "Unless
otherwise indicated, all Masses are on our Keyes Road (St. Mark's) Campus": Saturday 4:00pm,
Sunday 10:00am, Monday 10:00am, plus "Wednesday, September 23, 10:00am (St. Peter's)".
St. Peter's own ParishesOnline account (`st-peters-church-13502`) has no issues, so one row.

Its pastor also serves St. Joseph & St. Patrick and Historic Old St. John's, which publish
their own bulletins (separate rows).

## Worship sites

Two. St. Mark (`churches/st-mark-utica.md`) holds the weekend Masses. St. Peter
(`churches/st-peter-utica.md`) is still used for a weekday Mass (Wednesday 10:00am in the 20
September issue) and, per the same issue, "Baptisms, Marriages, and Funerals are still
available on both campuses until further notice". The merger note on the parish site says
the Keyes Road property was chosen as the parish's home; St. Peter's future is open, see its
`revisit`.
