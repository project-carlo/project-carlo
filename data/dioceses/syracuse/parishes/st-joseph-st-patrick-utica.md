## Publisher

St. Joseph & St. Patrick Church, 702 Columbia St, Utica ("Home Parish of Saint Marianne
Cope"), publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 04/0809. The 20
September 2026 issue is the parish's alone: its masthead, Saturday 5:30 pm and Sunday 8:45 am
Masses, and "Unless otherwise indicated, Daily Liturgies are held in the Chapel. Weekend &
All Other Liturgies are held in Church" (the chapel is in the same complex, not a separate
site).

Its pastor, Fr. Thomas Servatius, is also pastor of Holy Cross and Historic Old St. John's,
but each of the three publishes its own bulletin in its own container (06/0233 and 06/0181),
so they are three rows (`group` records the shared pastor).
