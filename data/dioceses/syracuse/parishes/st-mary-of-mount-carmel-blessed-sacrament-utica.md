## Publisher

St. Mary of Mount Carmel / Blessed Sacrament Parish, 648 Jay St, Utica, publishes its own
weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container 06/0182). I read the 20 September 2026
issue: page 1 is the parish's own ("ABOUT THE PARISH ... Address: 648 Jay St."), pastor Rev.
Jim Cesta. Its Mass schedule reads "Saturday: 4:30 p.m. at St. Anthony & St. Agnes Parish;
Sunday: 8 and 11 a.m. at Mount Carmel; 9:30 a.m. at St. Anthony", with weekday Mass Thursday
and Friday "in our parish".

Fr. Cesta is also pastor of St. Anthony & St. Agnes, and each bulletin prints the other's
Masses, but St. Anthony & St. Agnes publishes its own bulletin (Discover Mass, its own
masthead and editor), so the two stay separate rows (`group` records the shared pastor).

## Worship sites

One: the Mount Carmel church at 648 Jay St (`churches/st-mary-of-mount-carmel-utica.md`).
The Saturday and 9:30 Sunday Masses it lists are at St. Anthony & St. Agnes's church, which
belongs to that parish's row. Blessed Sacrament, Utica, the parish merged into this one
("CLOSED" on the diocese's list), is not in the schedule (`excluded/blessed-sacrament-utica.md`).
