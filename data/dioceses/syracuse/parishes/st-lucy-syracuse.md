## Publisher

St. Lucy's publishes its own weekly bulletin. The 19-20 September 2026 issue, linked from
saintlucys.org/bulletin, has a page-1 masthead for St. Lucy's alone, 432 Gifford Street,
with Saturday 5:00 and Sunday 10:00 Masses. It names the Saint Kateri Tekakwitha Chapel
"located to the right the main altar", which is inside the church, not a separate site.

## Worship sites

One: the church at 432 Gifford Street (`st-lucy-syracuse`). St. Andrew the Apostle,
merged into St. Lucy's in 2008-2009, is `excluded/st-andrew-the-apostle-syracuse.md`; the
diocese also lists a closed "St. Joseph, Syracuse" with records here (not in this region's
rosters).

## Website and bulletin provider

saintlucys.org (Wix). `probe url` calls it SPAM-SQUAT, but that is a false positive: the
page is the parish's own (name, address, bulletin), and the trigger is Wix's feature-flag
text ("dynamicSlots"). Its "bulletin - directory" page links the current PDF, so provider
`other`, publication `url:` of that page. ParishesOnline has an old organization
`st-lucy-church-13204` with no publications.
