# Not parishes: Roman Catholic Diocese of Syracuse

Places found while exploring this diocese that looked like a parish but
should not be added, and why. Check here before adding a parish.
Managed with `pnpm diocese not-parish ...`; hand edits are fine if each entry
keeps its `## Name` heading and `- **Field:** value` lines.

## Alibrandi Catholic Center (St. Thomas More Chapel)
- **Slug:** alibrandi-catholic-center
- **Website:** https://www.sucatholic.org
- **Address:** 110 Walnut Place, Syracuse, NY 13210
- **Source:** https://syracusediocese.org/campus-ministries
- **Source:** https://discovermass.com/church/alibrandi-catholic-center-st-thomas-more-syracuse-ny/
- **Added:** 2026-09-25

Syracuse University's Catholic campus ministry. It has a chapel with public Masses and shows up in Mass-time directories (Discover Mass lists it as 'Alibrandi Catholic Center – St. Thomas More'), but the diocese lists it under college campus ministries, not parishes. Leave it out unless the project decides to cover campus chapels.

## SS. Peter and Paul Church (Orthodox Church in America)
- **Slug:** sts-peter-and-paul-oca-syracuse
- **Address:** Syracuse, NY
- **Source:** https://www.oca.org/parishes/oca-ny-syrspp
- **Added:** 2026-09-25

Came up in a search for the Diocese of Syracuse parish directory, but it is an Orthodox Church in America parish, not Roman Catholic. Same-named Orthodox, Episcopal and Lutheran churches are common in search results; check the denomination before adding.
