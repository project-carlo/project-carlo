## What is known

Transfiguration Monastery, 701 Route 79, Windsor, is a small contemplative Benedictine (Camaldolese) community that the Catholic Communities of Eastern Broome list on their website alongside their four churches. No roster lists it. Its own Worship Schedule page could not be read on 2026-09-26 (connection resets and HTTP 5xx), so I have no first-hand statement of whether Mass is celebrated there, how often, or whether the public may attend. A search-engine summary of that page says there has been no daily Mass since the chaplain's death in 2020 and that guests may attend Mass "whenever one is scheduled", and points to Our Lady of Lourdes, Windsor, for Sunday Mass. The Eastern Broome bulletin of 27 September 2026 does not mention it.
