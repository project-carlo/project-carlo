## Our Lady of Solace Church
- **Slug:** our-lady-of-solace-syracuse
- **Address:** Syracuse, NY (university neighborhood, southeast side)
- **Source:** https://thecatholicsun.com/two-churches-grow-through-closing-and-emerge-as-one/
- **Source:** https://www.allsaintssyracuse.org/about
- **Added:** 2026-09-25

Closed. Our Lady of Solace, St. Therese and St. Andrew's in Syracuse's university neighborhood combined into All Saints parish on November 1, 2008. The Our Lady of Solace building was closed and its records moved to the former St. Therese Church, which is now All Saints Church (1340 Lancaster Avenue). Old directories and Mass-time sites may still list it. When All Saints is added, record only the Lancaster Avenue church; St. Andrew's closed a few months later and belongs here too once confirmed.
