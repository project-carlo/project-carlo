## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 10:30 am, Thursday and Friday 9:00 am (27 September
2026 bulletin, page 2); the directory has the same weekend times.

## Name and address

Name: Our Lady of Hope everywhere: name flag true.

Address, three versions: the bulletin gives **9 Wheeler Avenue**; OSM has the building at
9 Wheeler *Street*; GCatholic says "Wheeler Ave. & Skene Ave." (a corner); the directory
gives **48 Skene Street**, probably the rectory/office on the other frontage. 9 Wheeler Ave.
(the bulletin, with OSM's number) is used; the address flag stays false because the
directory differs. Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code, within about 20 m of the OSM building.
