## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 St. Michael the Archangel bulletin (ParishesOnline 03/0901, page
1, an image) prints "ST. THERESE MISSION CHAPEL ... Mass Time: Sunday 9:30 am". The diocese
directory (662) also gives Sunday 9:30 am. The chapel's own Google Site, marked "under
construction", says Sunday 10 AM; the bulletin is current and wins.

## Name and address

GCatholic (18188, kind Chapel) and the diocese directory (662) both give "St. Therese",
1 Wilton Gansevoort Rd., Gansevoort 12831; the bulletin gives "Route 32 Gansevoort" (Route 32
is the village's main street, and St. Clement's history puts the chapel "on Gansevoort's
main street"). Two independent sources agree on name and street address, so both flags are
true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code. The chapel was built by St. Clement's,
Saratoga Springs (dedicated 18 September 1966), which is why GCatholic still gives St.
Clement's website; it is now a mission of St. Michael's, South Glens Falls.
