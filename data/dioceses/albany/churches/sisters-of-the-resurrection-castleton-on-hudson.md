## Holds Mass

Yes, weekdays. This is Sacred Heart's (Castleton-on-Hudson) weekday Mass, held at the
Sisters of the Resurrection's convent at 35 Boltwood Avenue. The 25 October 2026 bulletin
(container 03/1047, page 3, Sacred Heart's "Masses for the Week") says "Weekly Mass M - F 8am
Mass at the Sisters of the Resurrection", and page 5 lists an All Souls' Day Mass there at 8:00am
on 2 November. The same weekday line is in every issue from 27 September to 25 October 2026.
Sacred Heart's own home page and its Discover Mass listing give the weekday Mass as Tuesday,
Wednesday and Friday at 8:00 AM at this address. The days disagree (Monday to Friday in the
bulletin, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday on the site); the bulletin is newer and is followed for
whether Mass is held, but the days are for the seed to settle.

It is public: the parish publishes it as Sacred Heart's weekday Mass in its own schedule (home
page "Mass Schedule", bulletin, Discover Mass), and Sacred Heart church has Mass only on Sunday.
That makes it a worship site rather than a private convent Mass, which is why it was given a row
on 2026-10-26 (the parish record earlier called it a convent chapel Mass without a row).

## Name and address

35 Boltwood Avenue, Castleton-on-Hudson 12033: the parish's home page, Discover Mass and CMSWR's
listing of the congregation (independent of the parish) agree, so the address flag is true. The
name is mine: every source says "the Sisters of the Resurrection" (convent), none names the
chapel, so the name flag is false. No roster lists it. Coordinates are OSM's address node for 35
Boltwood Avenue (via Nominatim), not a tagged chapel building.
