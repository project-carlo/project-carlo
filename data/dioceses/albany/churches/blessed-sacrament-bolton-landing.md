## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Sunday 10:30 am (27 September 2026 bulletin, page 1) and Thursday 9:30 am
(page 2; the cluster's Mass Times page adds a Holy Hour after it on the first and third
Thursdays). The directory gives Sunday 10:30 am and Thursday 9:30 am too.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the directory and GCatholic give Blessed Sacrament, 7 Goodman Ave., Bolton
Landing 12814; OSM has the building as 7 Goodman *Street*. Name and number agree across three
independent sources, so both flags are true; "Avenue" (bulletin, diocese, GCatholic) is kept.
