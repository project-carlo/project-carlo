## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 shared bulletin lists Christ Our Light's Mass intentions for
Tuesday 9 am, Saturday 4 pm and Sunday 10 am, and names "the Saturday 4:00 PM Mass at Christ
Our Light". The diocese's page gives Sunday 10 am, Saturday 4 pm, Tuesday 9 am and
Wednesday 7 pm. It is also where the diocese held its marriage jubilee Mass on 27 September
2026 (announced in Our Lady of the Assumption, Latham's bulletin).

## Name and address

GCatholic ("Christ Our Light", 1 Maria Dr., Loudonville 12211), the diocese ("Christ Our
Light Roman Catholic Church", same address) and the bulletin ("Christ Our Light Catholic
Church, 1 Maria Drive, Loudonville, NY 12211") agree. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus
Code; the ParishesOnline point (42.7095, -73.7906) is on the same site.
