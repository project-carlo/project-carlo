## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin: Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 9 and 10:30 am at "St. Francis
(Del. Ave.)".

## Name and address

GCatholic (391 "Deleware" Ave., a misspelling), the directory (391 Delaware Ave., "At St.
James Place"), the bulletin (391 Delaware Avenue) and ParishesOnline agree; both flags true.
The building is the former St. James church (the institutional-records index sends St. James's
records to St. Francis, and the directory still says "At St. James Place"); GCatholic dates the
parish from 2010. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
