## Name and address

The street number disagrees. The parish itself (bulletin masthead and stlstb.org) and St.
Matthew's network page give **109 Grand St.**; GCatholic and the diocese's directory give
**113 Grand St.** The record keeps 113 (two independent listings) but leaves
`address_verified` false; 109 and 113 are probably the church and the rectory or office on
one lot. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, which places the point on the building
either way. The name agrees everywhere (GCatholic writes "St. Lucy–St. Bernadette").
