## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (image-only) lists Mass Saturday 5 pm and Sunday 8:30
and 11 am in the church, the same as the directory's schedule.

Church", 900 Madison Ave.) agree; both flags true. The bulletin cover names it "Church of St.
Vincent de Paul, Albany" (the name used here) but, being an image, its address was not
checked beyond page 2. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
