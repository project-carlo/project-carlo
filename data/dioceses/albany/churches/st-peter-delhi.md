## Holds Mass

Yes. The 20 September 2026 bulletin (page 2) lists Saturday 5:30 pm and Sunday 9:30 am at St.
Peter's, and weekday Masses Tuesday, Wednesday and First Friday at 8:00 am, with adoration after
Tuesday Mass.

## Name and address

The name agrees across GCatholic ("St. Peter"), the directory ("St. Peter's Church") and the
bulletin: name verified. The address does not: GCatholic gives "Cross St." with no number, OSM
has the church on Cross Street, and the directory, the parish site and the bulletin give "8
Franklin Street" (the bulletin as the parish office). The 8 Franklin Street address point is
about 25 m from OSM's church building, so the building is probably on the corner of Franklin
and Cross Streets. I followed the parish's own address and left `address_verified` false.
Coordinates are the OSM building's centre: GCatholic's Plus Code lands on the Presbyterian
church 150 m south.
