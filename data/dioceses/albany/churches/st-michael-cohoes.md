## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletins of both St. Michael's and Holy Trinity schedule Mass
at St. Michael's on Monday to Wednesday at 9 am, Thursday and Friday at 5:30 pm, the
Saturday vigil at 5:30 pm and Sunday at 9 am. The diocese's page has an older schedule
(Sunday 9 and 11, Saturday 4 pm).

## Name and address

The street number disagrees. GCatholic and the parish's own bulletin masthead give
**20 Page Ave.**; the diocese's directory gives **36 Page Ave.** (the same number in its
"Address" line). The record follows 20 (GCatholic plus the parish), but `address_verified`
stays false until the difference is explained: 36 may be the rectory or hall next door (the
bulletin gives a separate "Church Hall" phone line). The name agrees everywhere. Coordinates
are GCatholic's Plus Code, on the building.
