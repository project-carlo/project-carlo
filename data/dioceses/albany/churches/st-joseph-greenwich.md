## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 issue of the Catholic Community of the Holy Family bulletin
(Discover Mass, `st-joseph-greenwich-ny`, page 1) lists "St. Joseph's Church Sunday 10:30am"
and "Wed & Fri 9:00am", and the parish's own homepage (parishes.rcda.org/StJosephsChurch/,
read 2026-09-28) gives the same Sunday 10:30 am and Wednesday/Friday 9:00 am Masses, with the
Saturday Vigil at Notre Dame-Visitation, Schuylerville.

This place was first recorded as a `no-bulletin` exclusion by the Warren/Washington pass: its
own Bulletin page says "Bulletins are not available at this time" and its ParishesOnline
organization publishes nothing. The Saratoga pass then found the shared Holy Family bulletin
posted on Discover Mass under this church's own page, so it is a church of
`holy-family-schuylerville` (see that parish's *Publisher*). The exclusion was removed before
promotion.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Joseph", 35 Hill St., Greenwich 12834) and the diocese's Find A Parish list
("St. Joseph's Church", 35 Hill St.) agree on the name and the church's address, so both
flags are true. The bulletin's page-2 column and the ParishesOnline organization give **36
Bleecker Street**, which is taken to be the parish office/mailing address (not checked on a
map: Nominatim answered 429 on 2026-09-28). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code on the
building.
