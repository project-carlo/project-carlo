## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 Holy Family bulletin (Discover Mass, St. Joseph Greenwich page,
page 1): Saturday 4:00pm, Sunday 8:30am, Spanish Mass Sunday 3:00pm, Tuesday 6:00pm, Thursday
9:00am; page 2 reports attendance of 72, 87 and 71 at the three 12-13 September weekend
Masses. The parish's own homepage gives the same weekend times.

## Name and address

18 Pearl St., Schuylerville 12871 in GCatholic (18190, "Notre Dame-Visitation"), the diocese
directory (342) and the bulletin. Both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code. The
parish's site: "The 130 year old Notre Dame parish opened its worship to the parishioners of
Visitation Church when it was closed in 1987" (the institutional-records index sends both
Notre Dame de Lourdes and Our Lady of the Visitation records here).
