## Holds Mass

Yes: Saturday 5:30 pm and Sunday 10:30 am, per the 27 September 2026 bulletin (masthead and
page 4 ministry schedule).

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead calls the building "OUR LADY OF MT. CARMEL CHURCH, 39 Saint John St.";
GCatholic, OpenStreetMap and the diocese's directory give the canonical parish's name
instead ("St. Joseph–St. Michael the Archangel–Our Lady of Mount Carmel" / "The Parish of
Saint Joseph/Saint Michael/Our Lady of Mount Carmel"). The row follows the bulletin; the name
flag stays false because no independent source names the building itself. The address, 39
St. John St., 12010, is the same in all four, so the address flag is true. Coordinates:
GCatholic Plus Code (OSM's way centre is ~60 m away, same site).
