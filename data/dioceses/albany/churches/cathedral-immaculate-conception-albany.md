## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 Historic Albany Parishes bulletin: Sunday 11 am and 5 pm at the
Cathedral (weekday and Saturday Masses are at St. Mary's). The same issue says the Cathedral
is open for prayer daily and has Eucharistic Adoration Thursday to Sunday.

## Name and address

The directory, the bulletin masthead and ParishesOnline give 125 Eagle Street; both flags
true. GCatholic's church roster for the diocese has no entry for the cathedral, so there is
no Plus Code: the coordinates are ParishesOnline's point for the organization (42.64695,
-73.75945), not checked against the building. A geocode or OSM check can refine it (see
the Christ the King record for a ParishesOnline point that was 5 km off).
