## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 27 September 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 5:30 pm and Sunday 9:00 am here
(the Saturday Mass moves to 4:00 pm from 17 October 2026), and page 2 a Wednesday 9:30 am
daily Mass; the cluster's Mass Times page says the Wednesday Mass is in the Parish Center.

## Name and address

Name: St. Isaac Jogues everywhere (the cluster's page: "formally St. John the Baptist"; the
parish was formed in 2009, GCatholic).

Address, disagreement: GCatholic, the diocese's directory and OSM give **86 Riverside
Drive**; the bulletin masthead and the cluster's page give **6 Church St.** OSM puts the
church building at 86 Riverside Drive and a separate building at 6 Church Street about 40 m
away, so the church is probably on the corner with two frontages, or 6 Church St. is the
parish center. 86 Riverside Dr. is kept (three independent sources and the building's own
OSM tag); the name flag is true, the address flag false until the parish confirms which
frontage is the church's. Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code, on the OSM church building.
