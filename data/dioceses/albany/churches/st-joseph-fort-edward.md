## Name and address

Name: St. Joseph's everywhere. Address, small disagreement: GCatholic, the directory and OSM
give **166 Broadway**; the bulletin masthead and the ParishesOnline organization give **164
Broadway** (probably the rectory/office next door, or the same lot). 166 Broadway is kept
because OSM tags the church building itself with it; the name flag is true and the address
flag false until the parish settles it. Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code, about 30 m from the
OSM building.
