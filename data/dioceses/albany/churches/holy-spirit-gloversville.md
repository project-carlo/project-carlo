## Holds Mass

Yes: Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 11:30 am, Thursday and Friday 8:30 am, per the 27 September
2026 bulletin (page 1).

## Name and address

The name is the same everywhere. Street numbers differ: the bulletin masthead says **151**
South Main St. (and the offices are at 153), while the diocese's directory, GCatholic and
OpenStreetMap all say **149** S. Main St. Three independent sources agree on 149, which is
used; the address flag is true on that basis, with the bulletin's 151 noted here (the
building may span both numbers). Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code, on the OSM building.
