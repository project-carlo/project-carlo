## Holds Mass

Yes, seasonally. The 27 September 2026 All Saints on the Hudson bulletin (page 1) lists
"Sunday, 10:00 AM, St. Isaac Jogues, Saratoga Springs (May thru October)", and the 27
September issue's intentions include a 10:00 AM Mass that Sunday. The parish's Mass-times page
says "Seasonally Only (from Memorial Day until November)". Its own page calls it "The Chapel
on the Lake (our Mission Church)".

## Name and address

GCatholic (18185, "St. Isaac Jogues", Route 9p, Saratoga Lake 12866) and the diocese
directory (669, "St. Isaac Jogues Mission", Rt. 9P, Saratoga Lake 12866) agree; no street
number in either. Both flags true. "Saratoga Lake" is not a postal town (12866 is Saratoga
Springs, which the bulletin uses). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, the only
building-level location available.
