## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 St. Clare / Christ Our Light bulletin lists St. Clare's Mass
intentions for Wednesday 9 am, Saturday 4 pm and Sunday 9:30 am, and refers to "the Sunday
9:30 AM Mass at St. Clare's". The diocese's page gives the same (Sunday 9:30 am, Wednesday
8 am, Saturday 4 pm; its weekday time differs from the bulletin's 9 am).

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Clare", 1947 Central Ave., Colonie 12205) and the diocese ("St. Clare's
Church", 1947 Central Ave., Colonie 12205) agree, and the bulletin masthead has "Church of
Saint Clare, 1947 Central Avenue, Albany, NY 12205": the church is in the Town of Colonie
with an Albany mailing address. The name used is the bulletin's. Two independent sources
agree on name and street address, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus
Code; the ParishesOnline organization point (42.7401, -73.8575) is about 30 m away.
