## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 All Saints on the Hudson bulletin (eCatholic 20630, page 1):
Saturday 4:00 PM and Sunday 10:30 AM at "St. Paul's Church, Mechanicville"; confessions
Saturday 3:15-3:45 PM there.

## Name and address

GCatholic (18183, "St. Paul", group "Parish of All Saints on the Hudson") gives 121 N Main
St., Mechanicville 12118; Wikipedia's article on the church gives 121 North Main Street. Two
independent sources agree: both flags true. The diocese directory has no entry for the
church itself: its "Mechanicville Site" (768) carries the parish-center address, 52 William
St., and is linked to `parish-center-chapel-mechanicville`. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus
Code.
