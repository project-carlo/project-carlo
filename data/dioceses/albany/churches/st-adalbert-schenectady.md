Yes. The 27 September 2026 "Together in Unity" bulletin cover gives Sunday Mass 11 am here;
the church's homepage (2026-09-28) says "Sunday Mass - 11:00 AM" and also "Wednesday Mass -
8:00 AM", which St. Paul's schedule page contradicts (no weekday Masses).

directory ("550 Lansing St. - Crane St. Hill") and the county historical society's list all
