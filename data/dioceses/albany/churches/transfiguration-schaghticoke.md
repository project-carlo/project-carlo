## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 1, an image): Sunday 9:00 am, and a Tuesday 10:00 am
communion service. The directory lists "Sunday: 8:30 am Schaghticoke".

17 South Main Street, Schaghticoke 12154 in the bulletin and GCatholic ("Transfiguration"). The
building was the Church of the Holy Trinity (the 2001 merger of the village's St. John the
Baptist with St. Monica's and Our Lady of Good Counsel). Both flags true. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code. The directory has no separate entry for this site.
