## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 1): Saturday 4 pm vigil, Sunday 7:30 am, 9:30 am and
7 pm; weekday Mass Monday and Wednesday 9 am and Thursday 5:30 pm. Page 2 lists intentions for
each of the weekend's Masses.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("All Saints", 16 Homestead St.), the diocese's directory ("All Saints Catholic
Church", 16 Homestead St.) and the bulletin masthead ("ALL SAINTS CATHOLIC CHURCH, 16
Homestead Street") agree, so both flags are true; the name follows the bulletin. The parish
office is at 12 Rosemont Street, not a worship site. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
