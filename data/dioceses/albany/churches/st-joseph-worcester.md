## Name and address

Catholic Church"): verified. Address: GCatholic and OSM put the church at 205 Main Street; the
directory and the bulletin masthead give 201 Main Street, which OSM has as the next building
(probably the rectory and office). I kept 205 Main Street, the church building, and left
`address_verified` false. GCatholic has no coordinates, so they are OSM's building centre.
