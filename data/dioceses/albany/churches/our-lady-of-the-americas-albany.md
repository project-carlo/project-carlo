## Holds Mass

Yes. The 13 September 2026 bulletin (the newest posted on 2026-09-28) lists intentions for the
Sunday 9 am English Mass and 11 am Spanish Mass on 13 and 20 September. The directory adds a
First Friday 6:30 pm Mass.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("Our Lady of the Americas", 273 Central Ave.), the directory ("Shrine Church of Our
Lady of the Americas", 273 Central Ave.) and the shrine's site (273 Central Avenue) agree; both
flags true. The bulletin gives the mailing address and rear entrance, 274 Sherman Street, on
the street behind. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
