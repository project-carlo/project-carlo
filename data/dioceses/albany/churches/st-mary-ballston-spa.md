## Name and address

The sources disagree on the street number. GCatholic (18176) gives **167 Milton Ave.**;
the diocese directory (333), the ParishesOnline org and the bulletin masthead give **185
Milton Ave.**, which may be the parish office/rectory rather than the church door. 185 is
used (diocese and parish agree), but `address_verified` stays false until a map or the
county's records say which number is the church. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code,
which is placed on the building whatever its number. Name agrees: `name_verified` true.
