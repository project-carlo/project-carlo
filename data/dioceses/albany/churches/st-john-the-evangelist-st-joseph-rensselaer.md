## Name and address

and St. Joseph", the directory's "The Parish of St. John the Evangelist and St. Joseph"; name flag
true. Address sources disagree: the directory and the bulletin give 53 Herrick St. (the bulletin
adds "PO Box 256", so 53 may be the office), GCatholic gives "Herrick St. & Elm St.", and the
diocese's events calendar puts the church at 54 Herrick Street. Kept 53 Herrick St. (directory and
bulletin); address flag false until a map or the parish shows which number is the church.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, which is on the building.
