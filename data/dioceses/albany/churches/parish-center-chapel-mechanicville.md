## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly on a weekday. The 27 September 2026 All Saints on the Hudson bulletin (page 1)
lists "Thursday: 9:00 AM, Parish Center Chapel, Mechanicville", and the parish's Mass-times
page gives "Thursday Morning, 9:00am Parish Center Chapel 52 William St, Mechanicville". A
chapel with a public weekly Mass is a worship site under the dataset's rules, although no
weekend Mass is held here.

## Name and address

52 William Street, Mechanicville 12118: the parish center (office, chapel and the "Fr. Joe
Scerbo Ministry Center" named on the parish site), from the bulletin, the parish's
Mass-times page and the diocese directory, whose "All Saints on the Hudson Parish,
Mechanicville Site" (768) gives this address and is linked here. The bulletin and the parish
site are one source and the directory entry names the parish, not the chapel, so the name
flag stays false; the address flag is true (parish + diocese agree). GCatholic does not list
the chapel. No coordinates yet (geocode after promotion).
