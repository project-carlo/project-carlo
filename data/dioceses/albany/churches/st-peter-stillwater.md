## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 All Saints on the Hudson bulletin (page 1): Sunday 8:30 AM, and
weekdays Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 8:45 AM at "St. Peter's Church, Stillwater".

## Name and address

895 Hudson Avenue, Stillwater 12170 in GCatholic (18184), the diocese directory's "All
Saints on the Hudson Parish, Stillwater Site" (434) and the parish's Mass-times page: that
is the address used, and the name and address flags are true on those agreeing sources.
The directory also carries an older entry, "St. Peter the Apostle Church" (343), at "271 N.
Hudson Ave." with the parish's phone number and no website; it is linked here as the same
church under its pre-2011 parish name. The 271 number was not reconciled with 895 (possibly
an older numbering of the same road); OpenStreetMap or the county's address records would
settle it. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
