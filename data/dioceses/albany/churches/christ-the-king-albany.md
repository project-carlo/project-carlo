## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin: Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 8 and 11 am (livestreamed and ASL
interpreted at 11); daily Mass Tuesday to Thursday 9 am.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the directory and ParishesOnline give 20 Sumter Avenue, Albany 12203; GCatholic
spells it "Sumpter Ave." (same number). Both flags true, with the bulletin's spelling.

The church is in **Westmere, in the Town of Guilderland** (OpenStreetMap), not the City of
Albany; "Albany" is the 12203 postal town every source uses, and is kept as `city`. It is in
the albany-city region only because the region was drawn from the roster's city field.

Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 100 m from OpenStreetMap's church outline.
ParishesOnline's point for the organization is about 5 km east (near Albany's city line) and
is wrong for the building.
