## Holds Mass

Yes. The Holy Trinity bulletin of 27 September 2026 lists the Saturday 5:30 pm vigil coded
LNLTH (Linlithgo), and the diocese directory's schedule for the parish has "Saturday: 5:30 pm
(Vigil Mass) Linlithgo".

## Name and address

GCatholic lists it as the Chapel of the Nativity, "County Route 31 & County Route 10",
Linlithgo 12526 (a hamlet of Livingston). The institutional-records index names "Nativity"
at Linlithgo, with records at Holy Trinity. Neither the bulletin nor the directory gives a
name or address for the Linlithgo site, and OSM found nothing, so both flags false.
