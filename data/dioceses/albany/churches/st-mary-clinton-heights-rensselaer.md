pm, and First Saturday 8:00 am from October.

("Church of Saint Mary at Clinton Heights"). Clinton Heights is a hamlet in the town of East
Greenbush with a Rensselaer postal address. Both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus
Code.
