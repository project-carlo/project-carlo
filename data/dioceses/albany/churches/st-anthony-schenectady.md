## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Sunday Masses at 8:30 am, 11:45 am
(Spanish) and 4 pm, and no daily Mass.

## Name and address

historical society gives the same street number (with ZIP 12308, where the others give
12305; 12305 is followed). GCatholic gives the corner "Nott St. & Seward Pl.", which is
consistent with it rather than a different address. Both flags true. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code.
