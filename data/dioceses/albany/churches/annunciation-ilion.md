## Holds Mass

Yes: Sunday 11:30 am, Saturday 4 pm from November to April, Monday and Wednesday 7 am, per
the 27 September 2026 bulletin and the parish homepage.

## Name and address

Name: "Church of the Annunciation" in the bulletin and on OSM, "Annunciation" in GCatholic
and the diocese: flag true.

Address: three different numbers on West Street. The diocese's directory says **50 West
St.**, OpenStreetMap has the church building at **48 West Street**, and GCatholic says **109
West St.**, with a Plus Code that lands on an unnamed building at 109 West Street about 250 m
south of the church. The row follows the diocese (50), which is within one lot of OSM's 48,
and takes its coordinates from the OSM church building (`osm-node`) rather than GCatholic's
Plus Code, which looks like it is on another building (perhaps a former school or rectory).
The address flag stays false until someone confirms the number (the parish's own
publications give no street address for the church).
