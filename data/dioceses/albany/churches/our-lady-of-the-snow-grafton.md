Yes. The 27 September 2026 OLV bulletin: Sunday 11:00 am (page 1 schedule and page 5

## Name and address

The building was St. John Francis Regis Church (GCatholic still uses that name, under the "Parish
of Our Lady of the Snow"); since the 2009 merger with Sacred Heart, Berlin it is Our Lady of the
Snow, now a mission of OLV (OLV's site, the bulletin, the directory's "Mission Our Lady of the
Snow"). Name flag true on the current name (bulletin and directory). Address: 31 Owen Rd. in
GCatholic and the bulletin; the directory's "31 Owens Rd." is a spelling slip. Address flag true.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
