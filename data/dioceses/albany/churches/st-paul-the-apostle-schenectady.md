## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 "Together in Unity" bulletin cover gives Saturday Vigil 4 pm and
Sunday 8 am here, and the parish's Mass-schedule page (read 2026-09-28) agrees.

## Name and address

Name: St. Paul the Apostle everywhere (GCatholic, directory, bulletin); `name_verified` true.

Address disagreement: GCatholic and the diocese directory give 2733 Albany St.; the parish's
bulletin cover, its ParishesOnline organization and the Schenectady County Historical
Society's church list give 2777 Albany Street. St. Joseph's bulletin places "the old
St. Paul's School, 2777 Albany Street and Kings Road", so 2777 may be the campus or office
number and 2733 the church's own frontage. The parish's own number is followed; the flag stays
false until a street-level source (OSM, the county parcel map) settles it. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code, on the building, which does not depend on the number.
