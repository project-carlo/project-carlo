## What is known

GCatholic lists "Holy Mother and Child" at 329 Lake Ave., Lake Luzerne (founded 2009, phone
518-654-2113, which is the Corinth parish office's number). That is the old Church of the
Holy Infancy: Holy Mother and Child Parish was formed in 2009 from Holy Infancy, Lake Luzerne
and Immaculate Conception, Corinth, and the institutional-records index gives Holy Infancy's
records to "Holy Mother and Child Parish - Corinth". The parish's current bulletin (20
September 2026), its website and Discover Mass give Mass only at 405 Palmer Ave., Corinth
(Sunday 8:30 am, Wednesday noon). The diocese's Find A Parish list has only the Corinth entry.

Holy Mother and Child itself is in Saratoga County (the saratoga region); this record only
covers the Lake Luzerne building, which is in Warren County.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing read says the Lake Luzerne church closed or was sold; the evidence is its absence
from the parish's schedule. Web directories (Yelp, catholicmasstime.org) still list "Church of
Holy Infancy, 323 Lake Ave", which proves nothing either way. A parish statement, a news item
or a sale record would settle it (closed, with a date); a Lake Luzerne Mass in a bulletin
would make it a church row under the saratoga region's Holy Mother and Child parish row.
