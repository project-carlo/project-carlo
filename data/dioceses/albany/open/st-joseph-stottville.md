## What is known

St. Joseph's, 2824 Atlantic Ave., Stottville (formerly Holy Family), is still in GCatholic
("Affiliated with Church of St. Joseph, Stuyvesant", read 2026-09-28), but:

- The Evangelist (12 June 2024) describes the Catholic Community of Northern Columbia County
  as St. James, St. Joseph's (Stuyvesant Falls), St. John the Baptist "and at the time, St.
  Joseph's in Stottville", and says St. Joseph's "vacant rectory" in Stottville is leased to
  a maternity home.
- The CCNCC bulletin of 27 September 2026 counts "3 churches, 3 parishes" and schedules Mass
  only at Valatie, Chatham and Stuyvesant.
- The diocese's Find A Parish list has no Stottville entry, and its institutional-records
  index points Stottville's records to a deleted page named for this church.
- A web search result (not readable: the 2 October 2022 CCNCC issue now answers 403) had
  bulletin text about a recommended closure and a procession from Stottville to St. Joseph,
  Stuyvesant Falls after a final Mass.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to closed (probably late 2022), but no source that could be read states
the closure or a last Mass. Per the rules, that stays open rather than excluded.
