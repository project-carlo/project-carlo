## What is known

Sacred Heart of Jesus, 33 Walter Street, North Albany, lost its Sunday Mass in August 2025:
the Times Union (updated 1 December 2025) reports that the diocese "suppressed" the Sunday
Mass, with Bishop Scharfenberger celebrating the last one on 17 August 2025, and the parish's
own site says "our regular scheduled Sunday Mass will no longer be celebrated, we are still
open!". The building is open for prayer, a food pantry and a weekly rosary. The same Times
Union article (December 2025) reports a Monday rosary "followed by Mass at 6 p.m.". In
September 2026 the parish site and its Facebook page mention the Monday 5:30 pm rosary but
no Mass. Sacred Heart is now a "network parish" of St. Pius X, Loudonville (same pastor,
Rev. James Walsh), whose website lists only St. Pius X's Masses.

The directory's Vietnamese Apostolate (entry 444) is at this address with Sacred Heart's
email; the only schedule for it is the parish site's older pop-up ("Vietnamese mass: 3rd
Sunday of each month 10:30 am"), which also still lists the discontinued Sunday 9 am Mass,
so it is not current evidence. The directory also names St. Mary's on Capitol Hill as a
Sacred Heart worship site; St. Mary's is in the Historic Albany Parishes bulletin
(`cathedral-st-mary-albany`), which does not mention Sacred Heart.

No bulletin exists for Sacred Heart (Discover Mass lists it with no issues; neither the
Historic Albany Parishes nor the St. Francis bulletin mention it).

## Why it is not decided

It is neither a church row (no bulletin prints a Mass here) nor clearly closed: the building
is open and had a weekly Monday Mass as of December 2025, but no source from 2026 says
whether any Mass (Monday or Vietnamese) continues. That decides between `no-bulletin` and
`closed`/`no-public-mass`; see `revisit`. The Vietnamese Apostolate entry is linked here
because this building is its only known place; it should follow whatever this record
becomes.

Vietnamese Masses are printed elsewhere now, in churches that already have rows: Our Lady of
the Assumption, Latham's 27 September 2026 bulletin has "3:00 p.m. Vietnamese Mass 1st & 3rd
Sundays of Month" (`our-lady-of-the-assumption-latham`), and the directory puts a
first-Sunday Vietnamese Mass at St. Joseph's, Troy (`st-joseph-troy`). Neither names the
apostolate, so entry 444 stays linked to its listed address here (added in the cross-region
pass, 2026-09-28).
