## What is known

St. Mary's of the Lake, 3535 State Route 9L, Lake George, is the Paulist Fathers' summer house
and chapel. The Paulists' page says they "celebrate Mass at St. Mary's of the Lake Chapel from
Memorial Day to Labor Day", and in 2025 Sacred Heart, Lake George's bulletin listed it in its
"Local Daily Mass Schedule" as "Monday—Friday at 10am (June—August)", i.e. open to the public.
Sacred Heart's June and July 2026 bulletins drop it. A web-search summary (not a readable
source, so not quoted) reported a notice that St. Mary's would be "completely closed for the
Summer of 2026" for septic-system work, with no Masses. It publishes no bulletin of its own.

## Why it is not decided

It is a seasonal chapel whose only schedule appeared in another parish's bulletin, and in
2026 it apparently held no Masses at all. If the 2027 summer Masses resume and Sacred Heart
prints them, it could be a church row under `sacred-heart-lake-george` (the bulletin that
prints its schedule) or an exclusion with `reason: no-bulletin` (its own publisher prints
nothing); that is a policy call for the lead. If it does not reopen to the public, it becomes
`no-public-mass`. The address (3535 State Route 9L) is from the Paulists' page; GCatholic has
no street address.
