## What is known

St. Francis de Sales, 43 Elka Park Rd., Elka Park (1913 church, with cemetery) is in the
diocese's Find A Parish list with an address and no Mass schedule (directory:670). The
Sacred Heart-Immaculate Conception parish's own site calls it a mission church where "We hold
special Masses there, but not on a weekly basis". Its sacramental records now belong to
Sacred Heart-Immaculate Conception (institutional-records index). The HVVW network bulletin
of 27 September 2026 lists ten churches with Mass and not Elka Park; GCatholic does not list
it.

## Why it is not decided

It is not closed on any source's word, and occasional special Masses would make it a church,
but no current bulletin or schedule prints one, and the parish page that mentions them looks
several years old. Neither a church row nor an exclusion is supported yet.
