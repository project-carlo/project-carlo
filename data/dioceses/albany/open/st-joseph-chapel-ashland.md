## What is known

St. Joseph's Chapel, Route 23, Ashland: GCatholic lists it as a chapel (gcatholic:18133) and
the diocese's Find A Parish list has it with an address and no Mass schedule
(directory:667). St. Theresa's, Windham calls it "the mission church to St. Theresa's" and a
historic landmark. None of Windham's seven bulletins from August to mid-September 2026
mentions a Mass there, and the HVVW network bulletin's list of ten churches does not include
it.

## Why it is not decided

The parish still claims it as its mission, so it is not closed, but no source prints a Mass
there. A seasonal Mass (as its history suggests) would make it a church; its absence from the
August 2026 bulletins argues against one. Needs a summer bulletin or the parish office.
