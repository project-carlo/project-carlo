## What is known

The building at 9790 Graphite Mountain Rd. (Route 8), Hague is the old Blessed Sacrament
church, which OSM labels "Saint Isaac Jogues / Blessed Sacrament - Summer Chapel". The Hague,
Chestertown and Brant Lake parishes were merged into the Parish of St. Isaac Jogues (GCatholic:
2009); the institutional-records index lists Blessed Sacrament, Hague as an ended parish.

The Northern Points Cluster bulletins show a summer-only Sunday 9:00 am Mass "St. Isaac
Jogues, Hague" in 2022 and 2023, a 100th-anniversary celebration there in August 2023, and
"no 9:00 am St. Isaac Jogues, Hague" from 10 September 2023. The word "Hague" does not appear
in any of the cluster's June-September bulletins of 2024, 2025 or 2026 that were read (text of
about 45 issues searched on 2026-09-28; the 2024 "Summer Mass Schedule" has a single 9:00 am
St. Isaac Jogues Mass, i.e. Chestertown). The cluster's Mass Times page lists no Hague Mass.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to the summer chapel having stopped after the 2023 season, but no source
says it has closed, been sold or been deconsecrated: absence from three summers' schedules is
not a closure statement. If the parish or a news item confirms it, this becomes
`excluded/` with `reason: closed` and `last_mass` in early September 2023; if summer Masses
return, a church row under `northern-points-cluster-st-cecilia-chestertown`.
