## What is known

GCatholic (updated 2025-12-21) still lists "Church of the Sacred Heart", 27 Main St., Berlin, under
the "Parish of Our Lady of the Snow". OLV's own page says the Grafton parish "merged with Sacred
Heart in Berlin" in 2009 to form Our Lady of the Snow, now a mission of Our Lady of Victory. The
institutional-records index (rcda.org/parishes/institutional-records) sends both "Sacred Heart"
(Berlin) and "St. John Francis Regis" (Grafton) records to The Catholic Community of Our Lady of
Victory. The diocese's Find A Parish list has no Berlin entry. OLV's bulletin (which covers the
Grafton mission) has no Mass at Berlin in the September 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026 issues read.
ParishesOnline has an empty Sacred Heart, Berlin organization.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to no Mass at Berlin, but no source read says the church closed, when, or
whether the building was sold, so it cannot be an exclusion with `reason: closed` yet. See
`revisit`.
