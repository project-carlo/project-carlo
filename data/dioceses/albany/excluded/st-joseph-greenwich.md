## Why it is excluded

**St. Joseph's, 35 Hill St., Greenwich: open, with public Mass, but no bulletin found.** The
parish's own site gives Sunday 10:30 am and Wednesday and Friday 9:00 am Masses (and points to
a Saturday 4:00 pm Vigil at Notre Dame-Visitation, Schuylerville), and the diocese's directory
agrees (Sunday 10:30 am, livestreamed). But its Bulletin page says "Bulletins are not available
at this time" (2026-09-28), its ParishesOnline organization publishes nothing, a web search
found no Discover Mass or eCatholic listing, and a May 2025 Wayback copy of the Bulletin page
has no PDFs either.

It is not in a neighbour's bulletin that could be found: the Schuylerville parish's site
(ndv-schuylerville.weebly.com, saratoga region) links St. Clement's, Saratoga Springs'
Discover Mass bulletin as "bulletin", and the 27 September 2026 issue of that bulletin
mentions neither Greenwich nor Schuylerville.

## What would change this

A bulletin appearing on parishes.rcda.org/StJosephsChurch/bulletin.php or its Facebook page
(facebook.com/profile.php?id=61563658559499, not readable here), or Greenwich turning up in a
Schuylerville bulletin once the saratoga region settles Notre Dame-Visitation's publisher. Then
this becomes a church row under that parish.
