## Why it is excluded

St. Nicholas, 22 Pulaski St., Amsterdam is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church. GCatholic
(read 2026-09-28) lists it among the churches in the Albany diocese's territory but gives its
"Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Diocese of Stamford" and "Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)". It belongs to
the Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Stamford, not the Roman Catholic Diocese of Albany, and the
diocese's Find A Parish list does not include it. So `other-jurisdiction`; nothing was
checked about its Mass schedule.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it would be covered if the Eparchy of Stamford were ever explored.
