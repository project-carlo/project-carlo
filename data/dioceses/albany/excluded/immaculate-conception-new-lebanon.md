Monday and Wednesday 9:30 am, on the parish site and in the diocese directory, read
2026-09-28), so it is `no-bulletin`, not closed. The parish ("Immaculate Conception & Saint

separate Mass site. The parish's second church, St. Joseph's, 458 State Route 43,
Stephentown, is in the rensselaer region and left to that agent (told 2026-09-28).
