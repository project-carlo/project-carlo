## Why it is excluded

St. Joseph's, Stephentown is a worship site of Immaculate Conception & St. Joseph's (New Lebanon,
Columbia County). It appears open: the parish's diocese-hosted site lists "St. Joseph's Church
Stephentown ... Saturday: 4:00 PM ... Tuesday and Thursday: 11:00 AM" (undated), and the
diocesan directory still lists it. But nothing the pipeline could read is published: the site's
only bulletin (bulletin.pdf) is the 14 March 2020 issue; Discover Mass has a page for this church
with no bulletins; a web search found no other bulletin. The columbia-greene region excluded the
parish's main church, `immaculate-conception-new-lebanon`, as no-bulletin for the same reason, so
this is `no-bulletin` too (not `closed`: no source says Mass has stopped).

## What would change this

A current bulletin for Immaculate Conception & St. Joseph's (then both churches become a parish
row), or evidence that Mass at Stephentown has ended (then `closed`). See `revisit`.
