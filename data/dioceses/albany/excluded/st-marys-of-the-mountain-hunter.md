## Why it is excluded

St. Mary's of the Mountain, NY 23A, Hunter: closed. A 2013 article says "The Diocese of
Albany ... closed the church in 2002" and transferred the title to the Village of Hunter in
2011; Wikipedia says the building "was razed on 04/12/2017". GCatholic itself marks it
"Former Church (now not in use)". Its sacramental records went to the Sacred
Heart-Immaculate Conception parish (Haines Falls), now in the HVVW network. No current
bulletin names it: the HVVW network bulletin of 27 September 2026 lists ten churches and not
Hunter. No exact last-Mass date was found (2002 only).

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building is gone.
