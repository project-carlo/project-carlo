## Why it is excluded

**St. James, 239 Main St., North Creek: closed, last Mass 27 October 2024.** The Evangelist of
26 December 2024 reports "St. James Church in North Creek held its closing Mass on Oct. 27"
(roof repairs the parish could not afford), and the Northern Points Cluster bulletin of 3
November 2024 thanks those who "supported St. James' final Mass last Sunday". The cluster's
27 September 2026 bulletin schedules Mass only at St. Isaac Jogues, Chestertown and Blessed
Sacrament, Bolton Landing.

The diocese's directory still names "St. James Church North Creek" among the Other Worship
Sites of the Parish of St. Isaac Jogues, and its institutional-records index still links St.
James (as the holder of St. Pius X, Pottersville's records) to a now-trashed page: both are
stale. Its ParishesOnline organization publishes nothing.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; a reopening would appear in the Northern Points bulletin.
