## Why it is excluded

St. Francis de Sales, Church St., Lexington (1895, on the National Register since 1999):
GCatholic lists it as a "Former Church" and titles it "Church of St. Francis de Sales (now
secular)", i.e. no longer a church. It is not in the diocese's Find A Parish list, the
Hudson Valley Vicariate West network bulletin of 27 September 2026 lists ten churches with
Mass and not this one, and none of the seven Windham bulletins of August–September 2026 (the
nearest parish) names Lexington. The closure date was not found.

## What would change this

A bulletin or diocesan page listing a Mass at Lexington. Not to be confused with St. Francis
de Sales, Elka Park (`open/st-francis-de-sales-elka-park`).
