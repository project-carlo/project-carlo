## Why it is excluded

Closed in 2009. The Daily Gazette of Monday 29 June 2009 reported "the final Sunday service
of St. Mary's Catholic Church", closed under the diocese's Call to Be Church plan, and said
it had been a sister parish of St. John the Evangelist since 1997. `last_mass` 2009-06-28 is
inferred as the Sunday before the article (the article does not print the date). GCatholic now
lists the building as "Former Church of St. Mary (now secular)"; the Schenectady County
Historical Society's church list marks it "[CLOSED]" at 828 Eastern Avenue; the diocese's
institutional-records index places its records at St. John the Evangelist. Neither the diocese
directory nor any current Schenectady bulletin read on 2026-09-28 lists it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: GCatholic says the building is now secular.
