## Why it is excluded

Rosa Mystica Shrine (Rosa Mystica House of Prayer), 484 Angel Hill Road, Edmeston, holds public
Mass: its site's "Daily Mass Schedule" (read 2026-09-28) lists a "2026 season schedule" of
Monday to Saturday 12:00 pm Mass, a Saturday 4:00 pm Sunday vigil and Sunday 10:00 am. GCatholic
lists it as a shrine of the Diocese of Albany; the diocese's own directory does not list it.

It is excluded because it publishes nothing the pipeline could read (`no-bulletin`): the
schedule is plain page text, and the site's only PDFs (map, visitors' guide, places to eat,
group information) are not bulletins. No parish bulletin in the region prints its Masses
(Holy Cross, Morris, which holds Edmeston's old parish records, does not mention it).

## What would change this

A bulletin or dated schedule document the shrine starts posting, which would make it a parish
row with the shrine as its church. The schedule is a "season" one, so Mass may stop over the
winter; see `revisit`.
