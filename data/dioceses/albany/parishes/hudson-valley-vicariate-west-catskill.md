## Publisher

The Hudson Valley Vicariate West (HVVW) network is the Greene County churches plus St.
Patrick's, Ravena (Albany County), run from one main office at 66 William Street, Catskill.
It publishes a weekly network bulletin. The 27 September 2026 issue's cover is headed
"Hudson Valley Vicariate West ... Main Office: 66 William Street, Catskill NY 12414" and lists
all ten network churches with weekend Mass times (Catskill, Athens, Coxsackie, Ravena,
Windham, Haines Falls, Palenville, Cairo, Greenville, East Durham), with the three pastors
(Fr. Patrick Rice, Fr. Russell Bergman, and for Coxsackie/Ravena the new pastor Fr. Stephen
Langridge, arriving 11 October). Inner pages carry a St. Mary's Coxsackie section and
network news.

This row takes the churches whose schedule the network bulletin prints and that have no
live bulletin of their own:

- St. Mary's, Coxsackie: its own section (pages 3-5).
- St. Patrick's, Catskill and St. Patrick's, Athens (one "Community of Saint Patrick"): their
  old JPPC bulletin channel (church-bulletin.org, id 458) shows no issues, and their own site
  prints only the network Mass-times list.
- Immaculate Conception, Haines Falls and Sacred Heart, Palenville (the Sacred
  Heart-Immaculate Conception parish): their diocese-hosted site's Bulletin page says
  "Coming soon...". The Windham bulletin (`st-theresa-windham`, same pastor) prints their
  weekday Masses but not their Sunday Masses, which only this bulletin prints.

Not here, because they publish their own live bulletins: St. John the Baptist Greenville,
Sacred Heart Cairo and Our Lady of Knock (`st-john-the-baptist-greenville`) and St. Theresa's
Windham (`st-theresa-windham`). St. Patrick's Ravena is in the albany-county region and is
left to that record (the same cover lists it: Saturday 4:30 pm, Sunday 9:30 am).

## Website and bulletin provider

St. Mary's Coxsackie's WordPress site posts each issue at stmaryscoxsackie.com/bulletin/
(Bulletins Archive: one post per Sunday, e.g. /bulletin/september-27-26/, which redirects to
the PDF under /wp-content/uploads/). Provider `other`, publication `url:` of that archive.
The 20 September and 16 August 2026 issues have the same network cover; the June 2026 posts
are a different, older two-parish newsletter, so the network format dates from summer 2026.
