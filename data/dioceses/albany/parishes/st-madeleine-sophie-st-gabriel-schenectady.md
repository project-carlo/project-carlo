## Publisher

St. Madeleine Sophie (3500 Carman Road) and St. Gabriel the Archangel (3040 Hamburg Street)
publish one weekly bulletin through Discover Mass. The 27 September 2026 issue's page 1 is
headed "The Parishes of St. Madeleine Sophie & St. Gabriel the Archangel", with contact boxes
for both offices. Its "Network Mass Schedule" also lists Our Lady Queen of Peace and
St. Margaret of Cortona, which publish their own bulletin (`our-lady-queen-of-peace-rotterdam`,
27 September issue read); both channels are live, so they stay two rows.

## Worship sites

Two: `st-madeleine-sophie-schenectady` and `st-gabriel-the-archangel-rotterdam`. The diocese
lists them as separate entries, both pointing to smssgabparish.org.

## Website and bulletin provider

smssgabparish.org is the parishes' shared site (the diocese gives it for both; GCatholic's
smsparish.org is the older St. Madeleine Sophie domain). It embeds the Discover Mass church
`st-madeleine-sophie-schenectady-ny`, which lists weekly issues through 27 September 2026 with
a text layer, and also posts each issue as a PDF in its WordPress uploads.
