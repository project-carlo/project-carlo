## Publisher

One weekly bulletin for three canonical parishes and a mission chapel under one pastor (Fr.
Zachariah Chichester): Our Lady of Hope, Whitehall; St. Ann's, Fort Ann; St. Mary's,
Granville; and the Chapel of the Assumption, Huletts Landing (a mission chapel of Our Lady of
Hope). The 27 September 2026 cover reads "THE PARISHES OF ST. ANN'S, ST. MARY'S, OUR LADY OF
HOPE, & CHAPEL OF THE ASSUMPTION"; page 2 gives one Mass schedule for all four (Saturday 4 pm
Our Lady of Hope and 6 pm Chapel of the Assumption, summer schedule; Sunday 8:30 am St. Ann's,
10:30 am Our Lady of Hope, 12:30 pm St. Mary's), and later pages carry each parish's news and
collections. The advertising pages are by Pilot Bulletins ("Our Lady of Hope, Whitehall, NY &
Saint Mary, Granville, NY 11123/11189"), but the issue is published on eCatholic.

The diocese's directory already groups St. Ann's, Our Lady of Hope and St. Mary of the
Assumption as "Other Worship Sites" of one another; St. Mary's, Granville is listed on its own
there but is in this bulletin in full.

## Worship sites

Four: `our-lady-of-hope-whitehall`, `st-ann-fort-ann`, `st-mary-granville`,
`chapel-of-the-assumption-huletts-landing`.

Not here: the institutional-records index lists, as ended parishes whose records are held
here, Notre Dame des Victoires and Our Lady of Angels (Whitehall; Our Lady of Hope was formed
from them in 2000, GCatholic), the Mission Church of St. Phillip, Hartford (records at St.
Ann's), and All Saints and Our Lady of Mount Carmel, Granville / Middle Granville (records at
St. Mary's). None of those buildings is in either roster, and none is in this bulletin; they
were not researched further. Sts. Peter and Paul, Granville is a Ruthenian (Byzantine)
church: `excluded/sts-peter-and-paul-byzantine-granville.md`.
