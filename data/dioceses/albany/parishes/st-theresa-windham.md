## Publisher

St. Theresa of the Child Jesus, Windham publishes its own weekly bulletin on its Squarespace
site, dated by the Saturday Vigil. The 12 September 2026 issue: "Parish Bulletin September
12, 2026, 5:00 PM Vigil Mass", with the church's name and 5188 State Route 23 address on page
2 and pastor Fr. Pat Rice. It also lists the pastor's weekday Masses (9:00 am Tuesdays at
Immaculate Conception, Haines Falls; Wednesdays at Sacred Heart, Palenville; Thursdays at
St. Theresa's), set in letter-spaced type that text extraction splits into single letters.

own bulletin is live and separate, so it is its own row.
