## Publisher

Our Lady of the Assumption, Latham, publishes its own weekly bulletin through Discover Mass.
The 27 September 2026 issue carries "OUR LADY OF THE ASSUMPTION CHURCH, LATHAM, NEW YORK" on
its inner pages, pastor "Fr. Tom" (fr.tom@olalatham.org), and no other parish.

The schedule in that issue is not consistent with itself. The "THE EUCHARIST" box (page 7)
gives Saturday 4:30 pm and Sunday 8 and 10 am, with a Vietnamese Mass at 3 pm on the 1st
and 3rd Sundays; the "Masses for the Week" list (page 3) gives Saturday 3 October 4:30 pm
and Sunday 4 October 7:30 and 9 am, which is also what the diocese's page says. The page-7
box may be a seasonal template. It does not affect the rows: one church either way.
