## Publisher

St. Henry's publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 04/0926.
The 27 September 2026 issue is image-only on pages 1-5 (rendered with render.py): page 1 is
headed "Catholic Community of St. Henry's", page 2 names Rev. John Cronin as pastor and gives
the Mass schedule for St. Henry's alone (Saturday 4 pm; Sunday 8 and 10 am; weekdays 8:30 am),
and page 6's LPi footer reads "Catholic Community of St. Henry, Averill Park, NY 04-0926".

Fr. Cronin is also pastor of St. Mary's, Nassau, and the Tuesday 9:30 am weekday Mass is at
Nassau ("Tuesday 09:30am (St. Mary's, Nassau)"), but St. Mary's publishes its own bulletin
(`st-mary-nassau`, 20 September 2026 issue read), so they are two rows.
