## Publisher

St. Cecilia, Fonda and Sacred Heart, Tribes Hill publish one weekly bulletin through
ParishesOnline, container 03/1181. The 27 September 2026 issue's first page names both
("Sacred Heart Church, 111 Third Ave … Tribes Hill" and "Saint Cecilia Church, 26 Broadway …
Fonda") under one pastor, Fr. Nathaniel Resila, and page 2's Mass list codes every Mass (SH)
or (SC).

ParishesOnline has two organizations for them, `st-cecilia-church-12068` and
`sacred-heart-church-12177`, but both list the same container and issues: one bulletin, one
row. Both organizations' ids are recorded as publications so no second row can reuse them.

Fr. Resila is also pastor of St. Mary's, Amsterdam and St. Stephen's, Hagaman, which publish
their own bulletins (`st-mary-amsterdam`, `st-stephen-hagaman`); this bulletin's masthead
names neither.

## Worship sites

- `st-cecilia-fonda` (Sunday 9 am, weekdays Wed and Fri 8 am)
- `sacred-heart-tribes-hill` (Saturday 4 pm, Tue 8 am, Thu 6 pm)

The diocese's page for St. Cecilia still says "Sunday: 9:00 am Sunday: 5:00 pm"; the
27 September bulletin has no 5 pm Mass (grep for "5:00": none). The bulletin wins.

## Website and bulletin provider

parishes.rcda.org/StCeciliaSacredHeart is the parishes' own page (the diocese's directory
lists it for both churches) and its `bulletin.php` posts the same LPi bulletin, a week behind
ParishesOnline. `provider_id` is the St. Cecilia organization, which the parish page and the
PDF footer ("St. Cecilia Church, Fonda, NY 03-1181") both point to.

The copies on parishes.rcda.org are largely images (September 20: pages 1–3 without text);
the ParishesOnline copy of 27 September has text on every page.
