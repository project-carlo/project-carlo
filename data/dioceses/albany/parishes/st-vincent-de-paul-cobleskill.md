## Publisher

St. Vincent de Paul, Cobleskill publishes its own weekly bulletin through eCatholic (site
5247). I read the 27 September 2026 issue: masthead "138 Washington Avenue, Cobleskill, NY
12043", Masses Saturday vigil 5:15 pm, Sunday 8:30 am, Tuesday, Thursday and First Friday 9:00
am. The directory's "Saturday 3:45 pm" vigil is out of date (3:45 pm is Worcester's vigil).
Its sacramental minister also serves St. Joseph's, Worcester, which publishes separately.
