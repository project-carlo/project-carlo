## Publisher

Sacred Heart, Stamford and its mission St. Philip Neri, Grand Gorge publish one weekly bulletin.
I read the 27 September 2026 issue: page 1 "SACRED HEART ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH, 27 Harper
Street, Stamford, NY 12167", "PASTOR'S SERVANT: Rev. Paul J…"; its Mass schedule is "Sunday
12:30 pm – Sacred Heart Church; Sunday 11:00 am – St. Philip Neri Mission Church; Weekday Masses
at Sacred Heart Church: Monday at 9:00 am", and page 2 is headed "Sacred Heart Church/Stamford &
St. Philip Neri Church/Grand Gorge".

The same priest (Rev. Paul J. Mijas) serves Sacred Heart, Margaretville, whose bulletin uses the
same template but is a separate publication with its own masthead, office, collections and
schedule (see `parishes/sacred-heart-margaretville.md`). Two rows.

## Worship sites

- `churches/sacred-heart-stamford.md`: 27 Harper Street, Stamford.
- `churches/st-philip-neri-grand-gorge.md`: State Highway 30, Grand Gorge.

The directory groups the same two. Its Mass notes (Saturday 4:30 pm vigil, Sunday 8:00 am at
Grand Gorge) are out of date against the bulletin. The institutional-records index says the
records of Our Lady of Good Counsel, Roxbury are at "Sacred Heart Church" (probably this one,
Roxbury being next to Grand Gorge); that building is in no roster and the bulletin lists no
Mass in Roxbury.
