## Publisher

St. Matthew's publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container 03/0766).
The 27 September 2026 masthead is "Saint Matthew's Church, A Roman Catholic Community, 25
Mountainview St., Voorheesville", pastor Rev. Rick Lesser, with one schedule: Saturday
vigil 5:15 pm, Sunday 9:30 am, Wednesday and Friday 8:30 am.

Its site lists a "Network of Parishes": Christ the King, Albany (whose pastor, Fr. Rick
Lesser, is also St. Matthew's), St. Lucy / St. Bernadette and St. Thomas the Apostle. No
shared bulletin: this masthead names St. Matthew's alone, and St. Lucy / St. Bernadette
and St. Thomas each publish their own. Christ the King is in the albany-city region.
