## Publisher

Notre Dame-Visitation, Schuylerville and St. Joseph's, Greenwich publish **one** weekly
bulletin, "The Catholic Community of the Holy Family", posted on Discover Mass under St.
Joseph's church page (`st-joseph-greenwich-ny`; issues 13, 20 and 27 September 2026 listed
when read on 2026-09-28). Page 1 of the 27 September issue gives "The Catholic Community of
the Holy Family Mass & Reconciliation Times" for both churches (Notre Dame-Visitation:
Saturday 4:00pm, Sunday 8:30am, Spanish Mass 3:00pm, Tuesday 6:00pm, Thursday 9:00am, First
Saturday 9:00am; St. Joseph's: Sunday 10:30am, Wednesday and Friday 9:00am) and is footed
"Notre Dame-Visitation • St. Joseph's"; page 2 has one column per parish (18 Pearl Street,
Schuylerville; 36 Bleecker Street, Greenwich). Parochial Vicar Rev. Joseph Tuan Pham.

Discover Mass's own Notre Dame-Visitation page (`notre-dame-visitation-schuylerville-ny`)
lists no issues. NDV's website still says the community "includes St. Clements Church in
Saratoga Springs" and links St. Clement's bulletin page; St. Clement's current bulletin
names neither NDV nor Greenwich, so that is out of date.

## Worship sites

- `notre-dame-visitation-schuylerville` (18 Pearl Street), in this region.
- `st-joseph-greenwich` (35 Hill Street, Greenwich, Washington County): printed in this
  bulletin with Sunday 10:30am and weekday Masses. It had first been recorded as a
  `no-bulletin` exclusion (its own Bulletin page has none); that record was replaced by the
  church row once this bulletin was found.

## Website and bulletin provider

The bulletin is only on Discover Mass, so the website is the Discover Mass church page
(St. Joseph's own site, parishes.rcda.org/StJosephsChurch/bulletin.php, says "Bulletins are
not available at this time"; NDV's Weebly site has no bulletin). Provider `discover_mass`,
church slug `st-joseph-greenwich-ny`.
