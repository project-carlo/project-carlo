## Publisher

The Church of St. Peter publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container
03/1111; issues every Sunday, newest 27 September 2026 when read on 2026-09-28). Page 1 of the
27 September issue is St. Peter's own masthead ("241 Broadway Saratoga Springs, NY 12866",
Rev. James Ebert and staff, "THE EUCHARIST: Sunday Liturgy 7:30am and 9:00am, Anticipated on
Saturday 5:00pm, Tuesday and Friday 8:00am") and page 2 is headed "The Church of St. Peter
Saratoga Springs, New York September 27, 2026". It names no other church.

**Shared pastor with St. Clement's.** Fr. James Ebert is pastor of both St. Peter's and
St. Clement's, and St. Clement's own bulletin (Discover Mass,
`st-clement-saratoga-springs`) is now headed "The Catholic Community of Saratoga Springs"
and prints both churches' Mass times. St. Peter's own ParishesOnline channel is still live
and still carries its own masthead, so under "one live member channel means no merge" the
two stay separate rows and St. Peter's church stays here. If the ParishesOnline container
goes silent, fold this row into `st-clement-saratoga-springs` (see `revisit`).
