## Publisher

Historic St. Joseph's Church, 600 State Street, publishes its own weekly bulletin through
ParishesOnline (container 03/1107). The 27 September 2026 issue's page 1 gives the Mass
schedule (Saturday Vigil 5:30 pm, Sunday 9:30 am, weekdays 12:15 pm), pastor Rev. Dominic
P. Isopo, and "Historic St. Joseph's Church ... 600 State Street". Fr. Isopo is also pastor of
St. Luke's, which publishes its own bulletin (container 03/0382, 27 September 2026 issue read,
masthead "St. Luke's Church, 1241 State Street"), so they are separate rows.

## Worship sites

One: `st-joseph-schenectady`. The records index lists Sacred Heart and St. Columba's
(Schenectady) as closed parishes whose records are here; they are in no roster and not in this
bulletin. St. Joseph's Place (837 Albany Street) is a drop-in outreach center, not a worship
site.

## Website and bulletin provider

The website is the parish's page on the diocese's parish host, which both the directory and
the ParishesOnline organization give. Its "Bulletin" link is a fixed `bulletin.pdf` that is
replaced each week (so it is recorded as a `url:` publication too) and says to read the
bulletin on ParishesOnline. `bulletin_provider: parishes_online`, ids from `probe po`.
