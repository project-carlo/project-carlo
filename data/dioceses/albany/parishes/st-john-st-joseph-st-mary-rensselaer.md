## Publisher

The Church of St. Mary at Clinton Heights and the Parish of St. John the Evangelist and St.
Joseph (Rensselaer) share one weekly bulletin under one pastor, Rev. Stephen Yusko, published on
SJESJ's eCatholic site (id 21471). The 26-27 September 2026 issue is headed with both parishes'
names, has one "Mass Times" box covering both churches (Saturday 4 pm at St. John & St. Joseph;
Sunday 8 and 10:30 am at St. Mary; weekdays alternating), and one list of Mass intentions for
both. The same issue announces Fr. Yusko's installation as "pastor of our two" parishes on 1
October (the diocese's calendar: installation at St. John the Evangelist and St. Joseph, 54
Herrick Street, reception at St. Mary's).

## Worship sites

Two: St. John the Evangelist & St. Joseph, Herrick Street, Rensselaer
(`st-john-the-evangelist-st-joseph-rensselaer`) and St. Mary at Clinton Heights, 163 Columbia
Turnpike (`st-mary-clinton-heights-rensselaer`). The institutional-records index lists St. John
the Evangelist's and St. Joseph's (Rensselaer) as closed corporations whose records went to "The
Parish of St. John the Evangelist and St. Joseph"; neither roster lists a second Rensselaer
building, and the bulletin names one site for the merged parish, so no other building was
recorded.

## Website and bulletin provider

eCatholic, site 21471, at sjesj.org (the directory's link). St. Mary's own site
(stmaryny.org/multimedia/bulletin/) has a file-manager list of bulletin PDFs whose visible entries
run November 2025 to April 2026 ("LOAD MORE" hides the rest); it was not read further, since the
current issue on 21471 already carries St. Mary's full schedule.
