## Publisher

Transfiguration Parish publishes one weekly ParishesOnline bulletin (container 04/0527) for its
two churches. The 27 September 2026 issue's page 1 (an image) is headed "Transfiguration Parish"
and gives one liturgical schedule for both sites (Speigletown Saturday 4 pm; Schaghticoke Sunday
9 am; a communion service at each on a weekday), with a Parish Life Coordinator (Elizabeth
Thayer) and two priests (Fr. Tom Babiuch, Fr. Ed Kacerguis). Page 5's footer reads
"Transfiguration Parish, Troy, NY 04-0527".

## Worship sites

Two: the Speigletown church at 50 Hillview Drive, Troy (`transfiguration-speigletown-troy`, the
former St. Bonaventure's) and the Schaghticoke church at 17 South Main Street
(`transfiguration-schaghticoke`, the former Holy Trinity). The parish was formed in 2010 from Holy
Trinity (itself the 2001 merger of St. John the Baptist, Schaghticoke, St. Monica's, Johnsonville
and Our Lady of Good Counsel, Valley Falls) and St. Bonaventure. The records index sends St.
Monica's, Our Lady of Good Counsel and Holy Trinity / St. John the Baptist to Transfiguration.
The current bulletin has Mass at only the two sites; the Johnsonville and Valley Falls buildings
are in no roster and were not given records.

## Website and bulletin provider

transfigurationparish.net (the directory's link) is behind Cloudflare and stayed blocked for

gives the org, LPi id and container. The site's "Sunday Bulletin" page is
