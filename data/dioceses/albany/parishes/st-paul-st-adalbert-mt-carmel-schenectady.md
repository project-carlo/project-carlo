## Publisher

St. Paul the Apostle, St. Adalbert and Our Lady of Mount Carmel, three Schenectady parishes
under one pastor (Rev. Matthew H. Frisoni), publish one weekly bulletin through
ParishesOnline, titled "Together in Unity" (container 03/0820). The 27 September 2026
issue's cover (page 1) shows the three churches side by side with their addresses and
Mass times: Our Lady of Mt. Carmel, 1255 Pleasant Street (Sunday 9:30 am); Church of
St. Adalbert, 550 Lansing Street (Sunday 11 am); St. Paul the Apostle, 2777 Albany Street
(Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 8 am). The issue's pages 1-7 are images: they were read by rendering
them (parish-site-access `render.py`), so the evidence item for the cover is a note, not a
quote; only the LPi footer on page 8 ("St. Paul The Apostle, Schenectady, NY 03-0820") has
text.

## Worship sites

Three: `st-paul-the-apostle-schenectady`, `st-adalbert-schenectady`,
`our-lady-of-mount-carmel-schenectady`. The diocese lists the three as separate entries with
separate websites and no "Other Worship Sites", but all three sites link this one
ParishesOnline organization as their bulletin, and the bulletin cover names all three, so they
are one row. The diocese's records index lists St. Thomas the Apostle and Sts. Cyril &
Methodius (Schenectady) as closed parishes whose records are at Our Lady of Mount Carmel;
neither is in the rosters and neither appears in this bulletin (not given records here).

## Website and bulletin provider

www.spacny.org (St. Paul's) is the website the ParishesOnline organization itself gives; the
other two sites (churchofstadalbert.org, mountcarmelschdy.org) are Weebly sites by the same
builder whose bulletin pages link the same organization. `bulletin_provider: parishes_online`,
`provider_id` and the three `publication` ids come from `probe po` on 2026-09-28 (latest
issue 27 September 2026). The bulletin is image-only in its first seven pages: the pipeline
will need to read it as images.
