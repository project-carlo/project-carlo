## Publisher

The Parish of Our Lady of Hope, Fort Plain publishes a weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline
(container 04/0656). The 27 September 2026 masthead reads "OUR LADY OF HOPE CATHOLIC
COMMUNITY", parish office 115 Reid Street, Fort Plain, Saturday Vigil 4:00 pm and Sunday
9:30 am. It is led by a Parish Life Coordinator (Deacon Joseph Cechnicki); page 2 notes that
Bishop Mark celebrated the previous Saturday's 4:00 Vigil there.

Not to be confused with the *other* "Parish of Our Lady of Hope", Copake Falls (columbia-greene).

## Worship sites

One: `our-lady-of-hope-fort-plain`, 115 Reid St. The bulletin names no other building.

Our Lady of Hope was formed (GCatholic: "parish 2009") from the parishes of the western
Montgomery County villages. The Institutional Records index gives it the records of St.
James, Fort Plain, Sts. Peter and Paul, Canajoharie and St. Patrick's, St. Johnsville. None
of those buildings is in either roster or in this bulletin; they were not researched further
(see the region notes).
