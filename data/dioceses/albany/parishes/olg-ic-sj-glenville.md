## Publisher

Three parishes, Our Lady of Grace (Ballston Lake), Immaculate Conception (Glenville) and
St. Joseph's (Scotia), share a website (olgicsj.org, "OLG+IC+SJ", "The Tri-Parish Catholic
Communities") and one weekly bulletin, published through ParishesOnline under the
Immaculate Conception organization (container 04/0687). The 27 September 2026 issue's page 1
masthead reads "The Roman Catholic Communities of Immaculate Conception, 400 Saratoga Rd,
Glenville", lists the offices of all three, and its Mass box covers all three: Saturday 4 pm
SJ and 5 pm IC, Sunday 8 am SJ, 9 am IC, 11 am OLG, plus weekday Masses at each. Two pastors
serve (Rev. Anthony Onu for OLG and IC; Rev. Thomas Konopka for St. Joseph's), but the
bulletin is one.
