## Publisher

St. Joseph the Worker Parish (formed 2009 per GCatholic) has two churches, in Richfield
Springs (Otsego County) and West Winfield (Herkimer County), and publishes one weekly
bulletin on its eCatholic site (`ecatholic:30839`). The 27 September 2026 masthead names both
churches with their addresses, under one administrator (Fr. Brian Lehnert), and page 2's
Mass calendar codes each Mass WW or RS.

## Worship sites

- `st-joseph-the-worker-west-winfield`: Saturday 6:00 pm, Sunday 8:30 am, some weekdays.
- `st-joseph-the-worker-richfield-springs`: Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 10:30 am, some weekdays.

Richfield Springs is in the southern-tier region of this exploration; the church was created
here, under this parish, by the mohawk-valley agent (noted in the region notes). The
Institutional Records index lists "St. Joseph's" in both towns with records at this parish:
those are the two predecessor parishes of these same buildings.
