## Publisher

The Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception (125 Eagle Street) and Historic St. Mary's Church
on Capitol Hill (10 Lodge Street) publish **one** weekly bulletin, headed "HISTORIC ALBANY
PARISHES", with a masthead naming both. The 27 September 2026 issue (5 pages, text layer) has
one "Mass and Confession Schedule" for both: St. Mary's Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 8 and 10:30 am
and weekdays 12:05 pm; the Cathedral Sunday 11 am and 5 pm. The rector, Very Rev. Jay
Atherton, is listed by the directory as parochial vicar at St. Mary's.

LPi serves this bulletin from **two** containers, one per ParishesOnline organization: the
Cathedral's (03/0408) and St. Mary's (04/0782). Both had a 27 September issue on 2026-09-28
whose first two pages are word-for-word the same, so they are one publication recorded on
one row, with both organizations' ids.

The "Historic Albany Parish Network" (albanycatholic.org; an Evangelist article of 15 May
2026 names it for events at St. Francis of Assisi, the Cathedral and St. Mary's) also covers
St. Francis of Assisi, which is "currently being served by The Albany Parishes office" at 125
Eagle Street but publishes its **own** bulletin (container 04/0736, masthead "ST. FRANCIS OF
ASSISI"); it is a separate row, `st-francis-of-assisi-albany`, with the same `group`.

## Worship sites

- `cathedral-immaculate-conception-albany`, 125 Eagle Street (directory 256; GCatholic's
  roster has no entry for the cathedral).
- `st-mary-albany`, 10 Lodge Street (GCatholic 18084, directory 261).

The directory lists "St. Mary's Church on Capitol Hill" as the other worship site of Sacred
Heart of Jesus (North Albany). Sacred Heart is not in this bulletin (grep of the 27 September
issue for "Sacred Heart", "Walter" and "Vietnam" found nothing); it is in
`open/sacred-heart-of-jesus-albany.md`. The institutional-records index says the Cathedral
holds the records of Our Lady Help of Christians and St. Anthony's, and St. Mary's those of St.
Philip the Apostle Mission; none is in either roster.
