## Publisher

Our Lady of Victory publishes one weekly ParishesOnline bulletin (container 04/0923) for itself
and its mission, Our Lady of the Snow in Grafton. The 27 September 2026 issue's page 1 (an
image) is headed "Our Lady of Victory Church / Our Lady of the Snow Mission | Christ Sun of
Justice", with a Mass schedule for OLV (Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 9 am, Tuesday-Thursday 12:15 pm)
and OLS (Sunday 11 am); page 5 lists Mass intentions for both.

Christ Sun of Justice (RPI) shares the pastor and is named in the masthead, but its Masses are
not in this bulletin and it publishes its own (`christ-sun-of-justice-troy`), so it is a
separate row.

## Worship sites

Two: Our Lady of Victory, 55 N. Lake Ave. (`our-lady-of-victory-troy`) and Our Lady of the Snow
Mission, 31 Owen Rd, Grafton (`our-lady-of-the-snow-grafton`, the former St. John Francis Regis).
Sacred Heart, Berlin, the other church of the former Parish of Our Lady of the Snow, is in no
OLV bulletin schedule read (September 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026) and not in the directory; no source
found says it closed, so it is in `open/sacred-heart-berlin.md`. The records index also sends St.
Paul the Apostle and St. Francis de Sales (Troy) to OLV; neither is in any roster.
