## Publisher

Church of the Annunciation, Ilion and Our Lady Queen of Apostles, Frankfort are two parishes
with one pastor (Rev. Paul Catena) and combined offices (414 Frankfort St., Frankfort), and
they publish one weekly bulletin on their shared eCatholic site (`ecatholic:31047`). The
26/27 September 2026 issue's page 1 is headed by both church names with a Mass schedule for
each; page 2 gives the "COMBINED PARISH OFFICES".

## Worship sites

- `annunciation-ilion`: Sunday 11:30 am; Saturday 4 pm November–April; Monday and Wednesday 7 am.
- `our-lady-queen-of-apostles-frankfort`: Sunday 8:30 am; Saturday 4 pm May–October;
  Tuesday and Thursday 7 am.

The Institutional Records index gives OLQA the records of St. Mary's and Sts. Peter & Paul,
Frankfort; those buildings are in neither roster nor the bulletin, and were not researched.
