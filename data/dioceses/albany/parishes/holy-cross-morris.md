## Publisher

Holy Cross, Morris publishes its own two-page weekly bulletin on its diocese-hosted site. I
read the 20 September 2026 issue: its page 2 footer is "Holy Cross Parish, 96-98 Main Street,
PO Box 118, Morris NY 13808", and it gives "Mass: Sunday, 8:30 a.m." and "Liturgy of the
Word: Thursday, 10 a.m.", with Rev. Chris Welch as celebrant. Fr. Welch is pastor of St. Mary's,
Oneonta, whose bulletin is a separate ParishesOnline publication that does not print Holy
Cross's schedule, so this is its own row.

## Worship sites

- `churches/holy-cross-morris.md`: 98 Main Street, Morris.

The diocese's institutional-records index says the records of Nativity of the Blessed Virgin
Mary, Edmeston are held at Holy Cross; no roster lists a church in Edmeston other than the Rosa
Mystica shrine (`excluded/rosa-mystica-shrine-edmeston.md`), and the Holy Cross bulletin names
no second site.

## Website and bulletin provider

`parishes.rcda.org/holycross/` is the parish site the directory links; its `bulletin.php`
lists one PDF per Sunday at `bulletins/<year>/<Month>_<DD>.pdf`. Self-hosted, so
`bulletin_provider: other`, publication `url:` of that page.
