## Publisher

Holy Trinity publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container 03/0435).
The 27 September 2026 masthead (page 1) is "Church of the Holy Trinity, 122 Vliet Blvd.,
Cohoes", Father Kyle Gorenski, Pastor.

Fr. Gorenski is also pastor of St. Michael's, Cohoes, and page 1 prints a joint Cohoes
schedule ("TIME FOR PRAYER") covering both churches. But St. Michael's publishes its own,
different bulletin on its own site (masthead "ST. MICHAEL'S ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH, 20 Page
Avenue", with its own staff, office and Mass intentions; 27 September 2026 issue). Two live
bulletins, so two rows (`st-michael-cohoes`), marked with a common `group`.

## Worship sites

One: `holy-trinity-cohoes`. St. Michael's appears in this bulletin's schedule but belongs
to its own row. The diocese's institutional-records index lists seven former Cohoes
parishes whose records are at Holy Trinity (Sacred Heart, St. Agnes, St. Bernard's, St.
Joseph's, St. Marie's, St. Patrick's, St. Rita's). None of them is in either roster and
none appears in the bulletin, so none has a record here.

## Website and bulletin provider

holytrinitycohoes.org is the parish site named on the masthead and on the diocese's and
GCatholic's pages; its /bulletin page embeds the ParishesOnline container (visit.py,
2026-09-28). `probe po` gives the organization, LPi id and container.
