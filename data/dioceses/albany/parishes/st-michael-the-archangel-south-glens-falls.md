## Publisher

St. Michael the Archangel publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container
03/0901, newest issue 27 September 2026 when read on 2026-09-28). Pages 1-6 of that issue are
images; rendered (parish-site-access render.py), page 1 is the parish's masthead: "ST.
MICHAEL THE ARCHANGEL ... SEPTEMBER 27, 2026", Rev. Guy A. Childs, Pastor, 80 Saratoga
Avenue, South Glens Falls, the Mass schedule (Saturday 4:00 pm; Sunday 8:00, 11:00 am and 5:30
pm; Tuesday-Saturday 8:30 am), and a second panel for "ST. THERESE MISSION CHAPEL, Robert
O'Rourke, Administrator, Route 32 Gansevoort, NY, Mass Time: Sunday 9:30 am". The text
layer on pages 7-8 carries the LPi footer "St. Michael the Archangel Church, South Glen
