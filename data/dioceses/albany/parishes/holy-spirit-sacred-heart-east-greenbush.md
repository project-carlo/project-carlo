## Publisher

Church of the Holy Spirit (East Greenbush) and Sacred Heart Church (Castleton-on-Hudson) share one
weekly ParishesOnline bulletin, container 03/1047. The 27 September 2026 masthead (page 1) names
both churches with their addresses; page 3 has a "Masses for the Week" table for each, and page 4
a joint Mass schedule and staff list (one pastor, Rev. Quy N. Vo; a deacon, trustees and finance
manager for each parish). Both parishes' sites point to the same bulletin: hsceg.org/publications
lists the container's issues, and sacredheartcastleton.org/bulletin embeds the same LPi
organization (0018000000Qx7MxAAJ).

The same issue is Fr. Vo's farewell ("This is my last weekend celebrating Masses at both
parishes"); the directory already lists Rev. Thomas Krupa for Sacred Heart.

## Worship sites

Two: Church of the Holy Spirit, 667 Columbia Turnpike (`holy-spirit-east-greenbush`) and Sacred
Heart Church, 3 Catholic Way, Castleton-on-Hudson (`sacred-heart-castleton-on-hudson`). The
bulletin also lists a weekday "8am Mass at the Sisters of the Resurrection" (their convent in
Castleton); it is in no roster and was not given a row: a convent chapel Mass, not a parish
worship site.
