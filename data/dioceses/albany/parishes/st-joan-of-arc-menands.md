## Publisher

St. Joan of Arc Church, 76 Menand Road, Menands, and the diocese's Black Catholic Apostolate,
which worships there, publish **one** weekly bulletin, posted as a PDF on the parish's pages
on rcda.org. The 20 September 2026 issue (2 pages, text layer) lists both communities' Masses
in one masthead: "Black Apostolate Mass - Sunday, 10:15am" and "St. Joan of Arc Masses:
Saturday, 4:00pm, Sunday, 9:00am", daily Mass Monday to Friday 8 am. Pastor: Fr. Kofi
Ntsiful-Amissah. The 27 September issue had not been posted on 2026-09-28.

## Worship sites

One building, `st-joan-of-arc-menands` (GCatholic 18096, directory 268). The directory's
separate "Black Catholic Apostolate" entry (380) has the same address and lists St. Joan of
Arc Church as its other worship site; its 10:15 am Sunday Mass is in this church, so that
entry is linked to the church rather than recorded as a place of its own. The
institutional-records index says St. Joan of Arc holds St. George's records; St. George's is in
no roster.

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory's website for both entries is https://www.rcda.org/sjoa-bca.com, the parish's
section of the diocesan site, whose "Bulletins" page is used as `website` (provider `other`,
self-hosted PDFs under rcda.org/application/files/...). The old domain www.sjoa-bca.com,
still named by ParishesOnline, is a lapsed Wix domain (HTTP 404 "Reconnect Your Domain" in a
browser). The ParishesOnline organization's ids are recorded because they belong to this
publisher, though it publishes nothing there now; Discover Mass lists both St. Joan of Arc and
the apostolate with no bulletins.
