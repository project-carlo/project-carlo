## Publisher

The Parish of Our Lady of Hope, Copake Falls publishes its own weekly bulletin through
ParishesOnline (container 04/0813). The 27 September 2026 issue's masthead: "Parish of Our
Lady of Hope, 8074 State Route 22, Copake Falls", with "Eucharistic Liturgies: Saturday 5pm
- Sunday 10am" at one church. It is led by a Parish Life Director (Bruce Frishkoff) with a
sacramental minister. Not to be confused with the Parish of Our Lady of Hope in Fort Plain
(mohawk-valley region, `our-lady-of-hope-fort-plain`).

## Worship sites

One: `our-lady-of-hope-copake-falls`. The parish was formed in 2009 from St. Bridget's
(Copake Falls), St. John Vianney (Claverack), Holy Cross (West Taghkanic) and Sacred Heart
(Philmont); the institutional-records index sends all four to Our Lady of Hope. None of those
four is in either roster and the 27 September bulletin names no other worship site, so no
records were made for them.
