## Publisher

The Shrine Church of Our Lady of the Americas, 273 Central Avenue, Albany, a Spanish- and
English-speaking community that calls itself "A mission of Blessed Sacrament Parish",
publishes its own bilingual weekly bulletin on its website. The 13 September 2026 issue (4
pages, text layer) has a masthead on page 2 for the shrine alone ("274 Sherman Street ...
(Rear Entrance/Entrada Trasera)") and Mass intentions for the 9 am English Mass and the 11 am
Spanish Mass on Sundays 13 and 20 September.

Blessed Sacrament Church (same pastor, Fr. Daniel Quinn) publishes a different bulletin on
its own site whose masthead and schedule do not include the shrine, so the two are separate
rows; see `blessed-sacrament-albany`.

## Worship sites

One: the shrine church (`our-lady-of-the-americas-albany`, GCatholic 18079, directory 420).
The diocese's Hispanic Apostolate page (the directory's "Spanish Apostolate", entry 445, a
diocesan ministry with no address) lists the shrine first among its places for Mass in
Spanish, so that entry is linked to the shrine church; see the church file.

## Website and bulletin provider

ourladyoftheamericas.org is the directory's link (GCatholic's link is the dead
blessedsacramentchurchalbany.org). Its "Bulletins ~ Boletines" page lists one Wix-hosted PDF
per Sunday (`/_files/ugd/bafe59_<hash>.pdf`, file names not dated, so the date is the link
text), so provider `other` with that page as website and publication. The homepage also
links a one-page image-only PDF (`bafe59_61f14...pdf`), apparently a flyer.
