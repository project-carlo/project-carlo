## Publisher

The Family of the Holy Cross Pastoral Network (four parishes in Herkimer, Mohawk and
Newport, one pastor, Rev. Justin Thomas) publishes one weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site
(`ecatholic:36566`). The 4 October 2026 issue (posted by 28 September) opens with
"Sacramental Information for all of our Churches" under "FAMILY OF THE HOLY CROSS", and page 2
lists each church with its weekend Mass and trustees, followed by per-church Mass
intentions. The diocese's directory entries for all four churches point to the network's
site and say "Part of the Family of the Holy Cross Pastoral Network".

## Worship sites

- `sts-anthony-joseph-herkimer`: Saturday 4:00 pm (confessions 3:00–3:45)
- `blessed-sacrament-mohawk`: Saturday 5:30 pm
- `st-john-the-baptist-newport`: Sunday 8:00 am
- `st-francis-de-sales-herkimer`: Sunday 10:00 am

The Institutional Records index gives Sts. Anthony & Joseph the records of St. Anthony's and
St. Joseph's, Herkimer, and St. John the Baptist those of St. Mary of the Assumption,
Middleville. Those buildings are in neither roster nor the bulletin; not researched further.
