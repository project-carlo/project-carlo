## Publisher

St. Edward the Confessor publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline
(container 03/0292, newest 27 September 2026 when read on 2026-09-28). Pages 1-6 are images;
rendered, page 1 is St. Edward's masthead alone ("ST. EDWARD THE CONFESSOR CATHOLIC CHURCH,
SEPTEMBER 27, 2026"), 569 Clifton Park Center Road, Pastor Rev. Daniel McHale, and the Mass
schedule (Saturday 4:00 pm; Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 11:00 am; Tuesday-Wednesday and
Friday-Saturday 9:00 am). Page 2 adds a Spanish Mass ("The San Eduardo Community ... 1st &
3rd Saturday | 6:00pm (Chapel)") and "MERCY MONDAY - AT ST. MARY'S CHURCH, CRESCENT"
(reconciliation on first Mondays).

**Shared staff with St. Mary's, Crescent.** Fr. McHale and Fr. Jim O'Rourke serve both
parishes, but St. Mary's, Crescent publishes its own bulletin (ParishesOnline 03/1423, 27
September 2026, its own staff list and Mass intentions), so they are separate rows. St.
Edward's homepage says its Friday morning Mass is being held at St. Mary's (Crescent) until
the kitchen renovation ends in early January; the bulletin's page 1 still lists Friday 9:00
am without saying where.

## Worship sites

One: `st-edward-the-confessor-clifton-park` (569 Clifton Park Center Road). The "Chapel" of
the Spanish Mass is inside the parish buildings, not a separate site.
