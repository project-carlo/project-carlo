## Publisher

Sacred Heart, Lake George publishes its own weekly LPi bulletin (ParishesOnline container
03/1267). The 27 September 2026 masthead (page 1) is headed "LITURGY SCHEDULE SACRED HEART
CHURCH, LAKE GEORGE, NY": Saturday 5:30 pm (Vigil), Sunday 10:00 am, weekdays Wednesday and
Friday 8:30 am, office 50 Mohican Street. Below it the masthead also prints the weekend
times of St. Cecilia's, Warrensburg (Sun 10 am) and Our Lady of the Annunciation, Queensbury,
because the three share a pastor (Rev. Joseph G. Busch). Page 2's intentions are for Sacred
Heart only.

Those two neighbours are not part of this row: OLA publishes its own LPi container (03/0098,
live the same weeks: `our-lady-of-the-annunciation-queensbury`), and St. Cecilia's appears
in full in the Northern Points Cluster bulletin
(`northern-points-cluster-st-cecilia-chestertown`), which carries its intentions and
announcements.
