## Publisher

Blessed Sacrament Church, 607 Central Avenue, Albany publishes its own weekly bulletin on its
website. The 27 September 2026 issue (4 pages, text layer) has a masthead for "BLESSED
SACRAMENT CHURCH, 607 Central Ave., Albany" and a Mass schedule of Saturday vigil 4 pm and
Sunday 9 am and 6 pm (Urdu), with daily Masses Monday to Thursday. Pastor: Fr. Daniel Quinn.

The diocese's directory groups Blessed Sacrament with the Shrine Church of Our Lady of the
Americas (each lists the other under "Other Worship Sites"), and the shrine calls itself "A
mission of Blessed Sacrament Parish" with the same pastor. But the shrine publishes its own
bilingual bulletin on its own site (`our-lady-of-the-americas-albany`), and Blessed
Sacrament's bulletin schedule does not list the shrine. Two bulletins, so two rows (the
shared `group` records the diocesan grouping).
