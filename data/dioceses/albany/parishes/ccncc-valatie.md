## Publisher

The Catholic Community of Northern Columbia County (CCNCC) is three canonical parishes under
one pastor (Rev. Steve Matthews) that publish one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline
(container 03/0063). The 27 September 2026 issue says so itself on page 2: "the Roman
Catholic Community of Northern Columbia County (CCNCC), with 3 churches, 3 parishes, 2
languages, and one pastor". Page 1 lists the three churches with addresses and the week's
Masses at each: Saturday 4 pm at St. Joseph's (Stuyvesant), Sunday 8:30 am at St. John's
(Valatie), 10:30 am at St. James (Chatham) and a 12:30 pm Spanish Mass at St. Joseph's (San
José), plus weekday Masses at all three. The diocese's directory gives each church its own
entry (directory:277, 272, 1030), all with the website ccnccparishes.org, so one row.

## Worship sites

- `st-john-the-baptist-valatie` (1025 Kinderhook St., Valatie)
- `st-james-chatham` (117 Hudson Ave., Chatham)
- `st-joseph-stuyvesant` (1820 US Route 9, Stuyvesant / Stuyvesant Falls)

Not here: St. Joseph's, 2824 Atlantic Ave., Stottville (gcatholic:18109), which GCatholic
still lists as "Affiliated with Church of St. Joseph, Stuyvesant". The Evangelist (12 June
2024) says the CCNCC was made up of the three churches above "and at the time, St.
Joseph's in Stottville", and that its vacant rectory is now leased as a maternity home; the
current bulletin counts only 3 churches and names no Stottville Mass. No source found that
states the church closed and when, so it is in `open/st-joseph-stottville`, not excluded.
The diocese's institutional-records index sends Stottville's "Church of the Holy Family" and
Stuyvesant's "Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary" / Stuyvesant Falls' "Nativity/St. Mary's"
records to a trashed rcda.org page named church-saint-joseph-stottvile-ny (parish corporations
merged into St. Joseph's in 2009, per The Evangelist); those are corporations, not buildings.
