## Publisher

Our Lady of the Annunciation, Queensbury publishes its own weekly LPi bulletin (ParishesOnline
container 03/0098). Page 1 of the 27 September 2026 issue gives "Mass Schedule: Saturday 4 PM,
Sunday 8 AM Sunday 11:30 AM", the address 448 Aviation Road, Queensbury and Pastor Rev. Joseph
G. Busch; page 2 lists the week's Mass intentions (Mon. and Tue. 7:30 am) at this church only.

Fr. Busch is also pastor of Sacred Heart, Lake George (`sacred-heart-lake-george`) and St.
Cecilia's, Warrensburg, but each publishes separately: Sacred Heart has its own container
(03/1267) whose masthead only *lists* OLA's weekend times, and St. Cecilia's shares the
Northern Points Cluster bulletin (`northern-points-cluster-st-cecilia-chestertown`). The two
LPi containers are live the same weeks, so they are separate rows.
