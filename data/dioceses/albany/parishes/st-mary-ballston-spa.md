## Publisher

St. Mary's, Ballston Spa publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container
03/0817, newest 27 September 2026 when read on 2026-09-28) for itself and its Galway mission.
The 27 September masthead (page 1) reads "SAINT MARY'S ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH, BALLSTON SPA &
ST. MARY'S MISSION, GALWAY", Fr. Francis R. Vivacqua, Pastor, 185 Milton Ave., and "St.
Mary's Galway C/O St. Mary's Ballston Spa". Page 2's intentions give the Ballston Spa
schedule (Saturday 4:00 PM; Sunday 8:00, 10:00 AM and noon; weekdays 7:45 AM, Tuesday 5:30
PM) and page 5 "ST. MARY'S MISSION, GALWAY, NY SUNDAY MASS 9:30AM".

## Worship sites

Two: `st-mary-ballston-spa` (Milton Avenue) and `st-mary-galway` (2113 East Street). The
diocese directory groups the two (333 and 664, each other's "Other Worship Sites"; 664:
"St. Mary's in Galway is a mission site for St. Mary's Church in Ballston Spa"). The
institutional-records index splits Galway St. Mary's records among St. Joseph's, St.
Stephen's and St. Mary's, i.e. Galway's former parish territory went three ways; the church
itself is open as this parish's mission.
