## Publisher

Holy Mother and Child (Corinth) and St. Joseph's (Greenfield Center) with its St. Paul's
mission (Rock City Falls) publish **one** weekly bulletin. The 27 September 2026 issue
(ParishesOnline 03/0331, page 1) is headed "Welcome to the Holy Roman Catholic Community of
Holy Mother & Child St. Joseph's St. Paul's" and prints three columns: Holy Mother & Child,
405 Palmer Ave, Corinth (Sunday 8:30 am, Wednesday noon); St. Joseph's, 3159 Route 9N,
Greenfield Center (Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 10:30 am, Wednesday 9:00 am); St. Paul's, 771
Route 29, Rock City Falls (Sunday 12:30 pm, 9:00 am 2nd Mondays), under one Parish
Administrator, Rev. Simon Udemgba. Page 2 is the St. Joseph's & St. Paul's section.

The same bulletin reaches two channels: ParishesOnline under St. Joseph's org
(`st-joseph-church-12833`, weekly, newest 27 September) and Holy Mother and Child's own
bulletin page on parishes.rcda.org (weekly PDFs named by month and day, newest 20 September
when read; its 20 September page 1 is identical to ParishesOnline's 20 September issue). Both
ids are recorded on this one row.

## Worship sites

Three, all printed with Mass times on page 1:

- `holy-mother-and-child-corinth` (405 Palmer Avenue).
- `st-joseph-greenfield-center` (3159 Route 9N).
- `st-paul-rock-city-falls` (771 Route 29).

The diocese directory groups St. Joseph's and St. Paul's (337 and 672) but lists Holy Mother
and Child (335) alone; the bulletin shows the three together. GCatholic's Holy Mother and
Child at 329 Lake Ave., **Lake Luzerne** (18216, the former Holy Infancy church, Warren
County) is not in the bulletin's schedule; the warren-washington region holds it as
`open/holy-infancy-lake-luzerne`. The institutional-records index gives Corinth's
"Church of the Immaculate Conception" (Corinth/Palmer) records to Holy Mother and Child.
