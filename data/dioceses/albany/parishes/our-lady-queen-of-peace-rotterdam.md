## Publisher

Our Lady Queen of Peace (210 Princetown Road; in the Town of Rotterdam, mailing city
Schenectady) and its mission, St. Margaret of Cortona in Rotterdam Junction, publish one
weekly bulletin through Discover Mass. The 27 September 2026 issue's page 1 masthead names
"Our Lady Queen of Peace Parish, 210 Princetown Road" and "Mission Church: St. Margaret of
Cortona Church, 1228 Main Street, Rotterdam Junction", under the Very Rev. James Belogi,
parochial administrator.

The same page prints a "Network Mass Schedule" that includes St. Madeleine Sophie and
St. Gabriel the Archangel, but those two parishes publish their own bulletin (Discover Mass
`st-madeleine-sophie-schenectady-ny`, masthead "The Parishes of St. Madeleine Sophie &
St. Gabriel the Archangel", 27 September 2026 issue read). Both channels are live, so they are
two rows (`st-madeleine-sophie-st-gabriel-schenectady`), with the same `group`.

## Worship sites

Two: `our-lady-queen-of-peace-rotterdam` and `st-margaret-of-cortona-rotterdam-junction` (the
diocese lists St. Margaret under "Other Worship Sites" of this parish). The records index
lists Our Lady of the Assumption (Rotterdam) and Church of the Immaculate Conception
(Schenectady) as closed parishes whose records are here; neither is in the rosters or in this
bulletin.

## Website and bulletin provider

olqprotterdam.org is the parish site (the diocese directory has it; GCatholic's
"lqprotterdam.org" is a typo). Its Bulletins page links the Discover Mass church page, whose
slug (with the misspelling "schnectady") is the `provider_id`. `bulletin_provider:
discover_mass`; issues listed weekly through 27 September 2026, with a text layer.
