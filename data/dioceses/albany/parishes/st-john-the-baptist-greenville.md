## Publisher

St. John the Baptist, Greenville publishes a weekly bulletin on eCatholic (site 40212) that
covers the three churches of its pastor, Fr. Russell Bergman. The 27 September 2026 issue,
page 1: "Saturday Vigil 4:00 PM at Our Lady of Knock Shrine, Sunday 9:00 AM St. John the
Baptist, Greenville, NY, Sunday 11:00 AM Sacred Heart Church, Cairo, NY", and it signs off
"Fr. Russell Bergman, Pastor: Sacred Heart, Cairo; Our Lady of Knock Shrine, East Durham; St.
John the Baptist, Greenville". Weekday Masses in the same issue: Wednesday noon at St.
John's, Friday 6:30 pm at Our Lady of Knock.

These three are also members of the Hudson Valley Vicariate West network, whose network
bulletin (`hudson-valley-vicariate-west-catskill`) lists their weekend Mass times on its cover.
Greenville's own bulletin is a separate, live channel, so this is its own row and the three
churches sit here, where their full schedule is printed.
