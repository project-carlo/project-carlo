## Publisher

St. Francis of Assisi Parish, Albany publishes one weekly bulletin for its two communities:
the Delaware Avenue church (391 Delaware Avenue) and the South End community (93/95 Fourth
Avenue). The 27 September 2026 issue (ParishesOnline container 04/0736, 5 pages, text layer)
is headed "ST. FRANCIS OF ASSISI" with both addresses, and its schedule lists Saturday 4 pm
and Sunday 9 and 10:30 am at "St. Francis (Del. Ave.)" and Sunday 10:30 am at "St. Francis
(South End)", plus a Spanish Mass on Sundays at 10:30. Pastor: Fr. Daniel Vallejo.

The parish is served from "The Albany Parishes office" at 125 Eagle Street (the Cathedral) and
is part of the Historic Albany Parish Network, but its bulletin is its own (the Cathedral and
St. Mary's share a different one, `cathedral-st-mary-albany`), so it is its own row.

## Worship sites

- `st-francis-of-assisi-albany`: the Delaware Avenue church (GCatholic 18083, directory 752).
- `st-francis-of-assisi-south-end-albany`: the South End community's church, the former St.
  John-St. Ann (directory 379; Discover Mass lists it as "St. John-St. Ann (Now South End
  Community of St. Francis of Assisi Parish)").

The directory groups exactly these two ("Other Worship Sites"). The institutional-records
index says the South End community holds the records of St. Ann's, St. James, St. John's and
St. John's-St. Ann's; the only building of those still in a roster is the South End church.
