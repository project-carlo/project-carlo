## Publisher

St. Mary's, Nassau publishes its own weekly bulletin as a PDF on its diocese-hosted site
(parishes.rcda.org/St.MarysNassau). The 20 September 2026 issue (the newest posted on 28
September) has a cover for "St. Mary's Church Est. 1852, 26 Church St., Nassau" alone, and page
2 names Rev. John Cronin as pastor and Rev. John Close as sacramental minister, with Masses
Saturday 5:30 pm, Sunday 10 am, and Tuesday and Thursday 9:30 am.

Fr. Cronin is also pastor of St. Henry's, Averill Park, which publishes its own ParishesOnline
bulletin (`st-henry-averill-park`), so the two stay separate rows.

## Worship sites

One: St. Mary's Church, 26 Church St. (`st-mary-nassau`). The bulletin notes that in winter the
weekday morning Masses move to the rectory; that is the same parish, not a separate site.
