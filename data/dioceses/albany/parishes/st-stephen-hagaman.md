## Publisher

St. Stephen's Parish, Hagaman publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline
(container 03/1004). The 27 September 2026 masthead (page 1) is "ST. STEPHEN'S PARISH, 51
Pawling Street, Hagaman, NY 12086", pastor Fr. Nathaniel Resila; page 2 lists the Saturday
5:30 pm and Sunday 8:00 am Masses at St. Stephen's with their intentions.

Its "MASS SCHEDULES" box also prints St. Mary's, Amsterdam (Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 10 am and
12 noon Spanish), and weekday Masses are at St. Mary's: the two share a pastor. St. Mary's
publishes its own bulletin on eCatholic (33341) under its own masthead, so the two stay
separate rows (see `st-mary-amsterdam`).
