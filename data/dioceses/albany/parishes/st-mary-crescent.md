## Publisher

St. Mary's, Crescent publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (org
`st-mary-s-crescent`, container 03/1423, newest 27 September 2026 when read on 2026-09-28).
Page 1 is a cover; page 2 has the parish's staff (Pastor Fr. Dan McHale, Parish Life
Coordinator Deacon Andy Grebe, Parochial Vicar Fr. Jim O'Rourke, all at
stmaryscrescent.com addresses) and its Mass intentions: Saturday 4:30 pm, Sunday 9:00 am,
10:30 am and 7:00 pm, Monday, Thursday and Friday 9:00 am. Only St. Mary's is named.

Fr. McHale is also pastor of St. Edward the Confessor, Clifton Park, which publishes its own
ParishesOnline bulletin (03/0292): separate rows. The Friday 9:00 am Mass here is currently
St. Edward's Friday Mass (St. Edward's homepage: it "will remain at St. Mary's" until early
January); it is in this bulletin's schedule, so nothing changes for the rows.
