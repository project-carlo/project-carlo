## Publisher

Church of the Holy Spirit, Gloversville and Holy Trinity Church, Johnstown are **linked
parishes** with one pastor (Very Rev. Matthew B. Wetsel) and one office (153 S. Main St.,
Gloversville), and they publish one weekly bulletin. Page 1 of the 27 September 2026 issue
is a two-column masthead naming both churches, with "LINKED PARISH STAFF" and each
church's Mass times (Holy Spirit: Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 11:30 am, Thursday and Friday
8:30 am; Holy Trinity: Sunday 9:00 am, Tuesday and Wednesday 8:30 am).

Each parish's own site posts the same file: Holy Trinity's `bulletin.php` (current to 27
September) and Holy Spirit's `/bulletin` page (20 September was the newest there on
2026-09-28; its page 1 is the same joint masthead). Same bulletin, one row.

## Worship sites

- `holy-spirit-gloversville`
- `holy-trinity-johnstown`

The Institutional Records index gives Holy Spirit the records of the closed Gloversville
parishes (Our Lady of Mount Carmel, Sacred Heart, St. Mary of Mount Carmel, and St. Francis,
Caroga Lake) and Holy Trinity those of Immaculate Conception, St. Anthony's and St. Patrick,
Johnstown. None of these buildings is in either roster or in the bulletin; not researched
further (see the region notes).

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`: self-hosted PDFs. The `website` is Holy Trinity's `bulletin.php` on
parishes.rcda.org, which was the more current of the two copies on 2026-09-28 and loads
without Cloudflare. Holy Spirit's page is recorded as a second `url:` publication.

**Image-only:** every issue checked (20 and 27 September 2026, both hosts) is scanned images
with no text layer. The pipeline will need to read it as images.
