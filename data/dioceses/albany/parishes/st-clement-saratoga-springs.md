## Publisher

St. Clement's publishes a weekly bulletin through Discover Mass (church page
`st-clements-church-saratoga-springs-ny`, issues weekly, newest 27 September 2026 when read
on 2026-09-28); the parish's own Bulletin page links the same issues. The 27 September issue
(page 1, rendered: the PDF's text layer splits words) is headed "The Catholic Community of
Saratoga Springs" over "St. Clement's Parish • 231 Lake Avenue" and "The Church of St. Peter
• 241 Broadway", with Fr. James Ebert as pastor of both, and prints two Mass-time boxes: St.
Peter's (Tue/Fri 8am, Sat 5pm, Sun 7:30 and 9am) and St. Clement's (Mon-Fri 8am, Sat 4pm,
Sun 8, 10 and 11:30am). A notice on page 2 says the bulletin "may look a little different"
because the program used for years is being discontinued.

**Why St. Peter's is not under this row.** St. Peter's still publishes its own current
bulletin through ParishesOnline (container 03/1111, 27 September 2026, St. Peter's masthead
alone), so it keeps its own row (`st-peter-saratoga-springs`), and its church stays there.
This row's only church is St. Clement's. If St. Peter's container goes silent, merge that row
into this one.

**Not St. Therese, Gansevoort.** St. Clement's parish history page tells how St. Clement's
Redemptorists built the St. Therese chapel in Gansevoort in 1966, and GCatholic still gives
stclementschurch.com as the chapel's website, but the current bulletin does not mention it;
the chapel is now printed in St. Michael the Archangel, South Glens Falls' bulletin as its
"ST. THERESE MISSION CHAPEL" (see `st-therese-gansevoort`).

**Not Notre Dame-Visitation, Schuylerville.** NDV's own site still says it is "in The
Catholic Community of The Holy Family that includes St. Clements Church in Saratoga Springs
and St. Joseph's Church in Greenwich", and its "bulletin" button points at
stclementschurch.com/bulletin/. That is stale: the 27 September 2026 Holy Family bulletin
(Discover Mass, St. Joseph Greenwich) covers Notre Dame-Visitation and St. Joseph's only, and
St. Clement's bulletin names neither.
