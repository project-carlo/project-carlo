## Publisher

St. Mary's, Oneonta publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline. I read the 27
September 2026 issue: page 1 masthead "St. Mary's Catholic Church in the 'City of the Hills'",
39 Walnut Street; page 3 gives Rev. Christopher Welch as pastor and the Mass schedule for this
church alone (Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 10:30 am, Monday to Wednesday 8:30 am). Fr. Welch also
celebrates Holy Cross, Morris's Sunday Mass, but Holy Cross publishes its own bulletin
(`parishes/holy-cross-morris.md`).

## Worship sites

- `churches/st-mary-oneonta.md`: 39 Walnut Street, Oneonta.

The bulletin says the parish is "home to St. Mary's Newman Catholic Community" (campus ministry
for SUNY Oneonta and Hartwick College); no roster lists it and no separate Mass site is named,
so it has no record of its own. The institutional-records index lists "Former Missions of St.
Mary's, Oneonta in Otego" and St. Mary's, Laurens with records at St. Mary's; neither is in a
roster, and the bulletin lists no Mass there.
