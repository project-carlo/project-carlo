## Publisher

All Saints on the Hudson publishes one weekly bulletin through eCatholic (site 20630; issues
at files.ecatholic.com/20630/bulletins/YYYYMMDD.pdf, newest 27 September 2026 when read on
2026-09-28). Its page 1 masthead reads "ALL SAINTS ON THE HUDSON, A ROMAN CATHOLIC COMMUNITY
SERVING MECHANICVILLE, STILLWATER, AND SARATOGA SPRINGS" (52 William Street, Mechanicville;
Pastor Fr. George Fleming) and its schedule lists every worship site: weekday Mass Mon, Tue,
Wed, Fri 8:45 AM at St. Peter's, Stillwater and Thursday 9:00 AM at the Parish Center Chapel,
Mechanicville; weekend Saturday 4:00 PM St. Paul's, Mechanicville, Sunday 8:30 AM St.
Peter's, Stillwater, 10:00 AM St. Isaac Jogues (May through October) and 10:30 AM St.
Paul's.

The parish was formed in 2011 (GCatholic: "parish 2011.07") from Mechanicville's Assumption
and St. Paul the Apostle parishes and St. Peter the Apostle, Stillwater; the diocese's
institutional-records index sends all three parishes' records here. Fr. Fleming is also
pastor of Corpus Christi, Round Lake, which keeps its own ParishesOnline bulletin (separate
row).

## Worship sites

Four, all from the 27 September 2026 bulletin and the parish's Mass-times page:

- `st-paul-mechanicville` (121 North Main Street): weekend Masses.
- `st-peter-stillwater` (895 Hudson Avenue): Sunday and weekday Masses.
- `st-isaac-jogues-saratoga-lake` (Route 9P): seasonal Sunday Mass, May to October.
- `parish-center-chapel-mechanicville` (52 William Street): Thursday 9 AM Mass.

The directory's "All Saints on the Hudson Parish, Mechanicville Site" (768) gives 52 William
St., the parish center, and is linked to the chapel there; its "St. Peter the Apostle Church"
(343, 271 N. Hudson Ave., Stillwater, no website) is an older entry for the Stillwater church
and is linked to `st-peter-stillwater`. The former Church of the Assumption, Mechanicville
appears in no roster and in no current schedule; it was not researched further.
