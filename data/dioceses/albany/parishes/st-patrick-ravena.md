## Publisher

St. Patrick's, Ravena posts its own edition of the Hudson Valley Vicariate West network
bulletin on its own site. The 19-20 September 2026 issue ("Ravena Bulletin September
19-20.pdf", newest on 2026-09-28) opens with the network cover listing the Mass times of
all its churches (Catskill, Athens, Coxsackie, Ravena, Cairo, Greenville, East Durham,
Windham, Haines Falls, Palenville) and a network page; from page 3 on it is Ravena's own:
"St. Patrick's Ravena, (518) 756-3145, stpatricks-ravena.org, Mass Intentions", weekday
Mass, finances. It announces a new pastor, "Father Stephen" (Langridge), from 10-11
October.

**Why a separate row from the network.** The columbia-greene region's
`hudson-valley-vicariate-west-catskill` row publishes the edition at
stmaryscoxsackie.com/bulletin/. Its 20 September issue has the same cover but its page 3 is
St. Mary's, Coxsackie's own (Mass intentions for Coxsackie), with Ravena mentioned only in
shared news. The coordinator reported that its 27 September issue's inner pages cover
Ravena and Coxsackie together under Fr. Langridge. Ravena's own channel is live and carries
its own pages, so by the rule "one live member channel means no merge" it stays its own
row. Revisit once Fr. Langridge starts: if Ravena stops posting its own edition, or the
two editions become identical, fold this church into that row.

## Worship sites

One: `st-patrick-ravena`. The network cover's other churches are in the columbia-greene
region.

## Website and bulletin provider

The diocese and GCatholic give tcosp.org, which on 2026-09-28 failed a TLS handshake for
`probe url` and in the browser redirected to churchofsaintpatrick.wix.com (502). The
bulletin itself names stpatricks-ravena.org, which serves the same Wix site; its Bulletins
page only works at the wixsite address
(churchofsaintpatrick.wixsite.com/church-ravena/copy-of-event-calendar; the same path on
stpatricks-ravena.org answers 404). That page links one PDF at a time on Wix's file host
(filesusr.com/ugd/...pdf). Self-hosted: `other`.
