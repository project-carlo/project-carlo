## Publisher

St. Joseph's, Troy (served by the Carmelite friars since 1970) publishes its own weekly bulletin
as a PDF on its parish site. The 13 September 2026 issue, the newest posted on 28 September, is
headed "St. Joseph Church" with its Carmelite clergy and a schedule for this church alone: English
Novus Ordo, a Carmelite-rite Latin Mass (Sunday noon, Friday noon) and a Spanish Mass (Sunday 3
pm).

## Worship sites

One: St. Joseph's Church, 416 Third Street (`st-joseph-troy`). The records index sends Holy
Trinity, St. Jean Baptiste, St. Lawrence's and St. Mary's (Troy) to St. Joseph; none of them is
in any roster. The directory's first-Sunday Vietnamese Mass is held here and is not a separate
site.

## Website and bulletin provider

stjosephcatholicchurch-troy.org (the directory's link) has a "Bulletins" page listing the PDFs,
