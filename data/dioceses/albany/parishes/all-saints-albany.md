## Publisher

All Saints Catholic Church, 16 Homestead Street, Albany publishes its own weekly bulletin on
eCatholic. The 27 September 2026 issue (`files.ecatholic.com/33726/bulletins/20260927.pdf`,
7 pages, text layer) has a masthead for All Saints alone, Saturday 4 pm vigil and Sunday
7:30 am, 9:30 am and 7 pm Masses, and weekday Masses Monday and Wednesday 9 am and Thursday
5:30 pm. The next Sunday's file (20261004) answered 403 on 2026-09-28 (not yet posted).

All Saints shares its pastor, Fr. Charles Onyeneke, with the Parish of Mater Christi
(`mater-christi-albany`), but each publishes its own bulletin: Mater Christi's is a separate
ParishesOnline container (04/0808) whose 27 September masthead names Mater Christi alone.
So they are two rows. The bulletin's staff list also names "UAlbany Campus Ministry, Cathy
Reid Campus Minister" (see *Not yet confirmed*).

## Worship sites

One: the church at 16 Homestead Street (`all-saints-albany`). The directory (entry 254) and
GCatholic (18076) list only this building for All Saints. The parish office is at 12
Rosemont Street (per the bulletin: "please send all mail to this address"); it is not a
worship site. The institutional-records index (rcda.org/parishes/institutional-records) says All Saints
holds the records of the Church of St. Margaret Mary and Holy Cross Church, Albany; neither
building is in either roster, so neither is recorded here.

## Website and bulletin provider

allsaintscc.org is the directory's "Visit Our Website" link and is hosted on eCatholic
(`ecatholic:33726`, found by visit.py on the homepage, which links /bulletins). Issues are at
`files.ecatholic.com/33726/bulletins/<YYYYMMDD>.pdf`, dated by the Sunday, readable without a
browser (parish-site-access `issues.py ecatholic ecatholic:33726`). GCatholic's older link
(rcda.org/churches/AllSaints) is not used.
