## Publisher

Two Amsterdam parishes with one pastor (Rev. O. Robert DeMartinis) publish **one** weekly
bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 03/0895, under the organization of "The Parish of
Saint Joseph / Saint Michael / Our Lady of Mt Carmel". The 27 September 2026 issue's page 1
is a two-column masthead: "CHURCH OF ST. STANISLAUS, 50 Cornell St." (Saturday 4:00 pm,
Sunday 9:00 am) and "OUR LADY OF MT. CARMEL CHURCH, 39 Saint John St." (Saturday 5:30 pm,
Sunday 10:30 am). Pages 2–5 carry each church's news and Mass intentions under running
heads "ST. STANISLAUS CHURCH" and "OUR LADY OF MOUNT CARMEL".

The diocese lists them as separate parishes (directory 305 and 306) and the ParishesOnline
account is in Mt. Carmel's name only, but the masthead shows the one bulletin covers both.
St. Stanislaus's own site has no bulletin of its own (its bulletin link redirects home), so
there is no second channel.

Neighbouring St. Mary's (Amsterdam) and St. Stephen's (Hagaman) have a different pastor and
their own bulletins; the directory's note that weekday Mass "is celebrated at one location
for the four Amsterdam-area churches" is about Mass, not the bulletin.

## Worship sites

- `st-stanislaus-amsterdam`, 50 Cornell St.
- `our-lady-of-mount-carmel-amsterdam`, 39 Saint John St.

"The Parish of Saint Joseph/Saint Michael/Our Lady of Mount Carmel" is the canonical parish
formed from St. Joseph's, St. Michael the Archangel and Our Lady of Mt. Carmel; the
Institutional Records index gives it the records of all three. Only the Mt. Carmel building
appears in the current bulletin; the St. Joseph and St. Michael buildings are in neither
roster and were not researched (see the region notes).

## Website and bulletin provider

The diocese's directory gives the ParishesOnline organization itself as this parish's
website, and it is the only place the bulletin is posted, so it is the `website`. Fetching
goes by `provider_id`.
