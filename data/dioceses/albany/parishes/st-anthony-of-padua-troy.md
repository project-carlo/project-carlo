## Publisher

St. Anthony of Padua (Franciscan friars) publishes its own weekly bulletin as a PDF on its
diocese-hosted site. The 20 September 2026 issue, the newest posted, is image-only; rendered,
page 1 names St. Anthony of Padua Church alone, with its pastor Fr. Richard Donovan, OFM, and its
Mass times (Saturday 4:30 pm; Sunday 9 am and 12 pm; weekdays 7:15 am).

## Worship sites

One: St. Anthony of Padua Church, 28 State St. (`st-anthony-of-padua-troy`). The records index
sends St. Patrick's and St. Peter's (Troy) to St. Anthony; neither is in any roster.

parishes.rcda.org/stanthonyofpadua/ (the directory's link) has a Bulletin page listing the PDFs,
so provider `other` with that page as website and publication. GCatholic's
