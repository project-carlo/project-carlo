## Publisher

St. Joseph's, Worcester publishes its own weekly bulletin. I read the 20 September 2026 issue:
page 2 masthead "ST. JOSEPH'S CATHOLIC CHURCH, 201 Main Street, Worcester, NY 12197", weekend
Masses Saturday 3:45 pm and Sunday 10:30 am, weekday Masses Monday and Wednesday 9 am. Its
sacramental minister, Fr. Emmanuel Udoh, also serves St. Vincent de Paul, Cobleskill, and the
two share a bookkeeper and an Instagram name ("joevin138"), but Cobleskill publishes its own
eCatholic bulletin with a different masthead and schedule. Two rows.
