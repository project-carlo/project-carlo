## Publisher

St. Joseph's, Broadalbin and St. Francis of Assisi, Northville publish one weekly bulletin
on their shared page at parishes.rcda.org. The 20 September 2026 masthead reads "ST. JOSEPH
AND ST. FRANCIS OF ASSISI CHURCHES", pastor Rev. Jun Segura, with one Mass schedule for both
and a footer giving each church's address (St. Francis, 501 Bridge St, Northville; St.
Joseph's, PO Box 538, 7 North Street, Broadalbin). Page 2 has separate "ST. JOSEPH'S MASS
INTENTIONS" and "ST. FRANCIS MASS INTENTIONS".

## Worship sites

- `st-joseph-broadalbin`: Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 10:30 am, Monday and Friday 8:30 am.
- `st-francis-of-assisi-northville`: Sunday 8:30 am, Tuesday and Thursday 8:30 am, plus
  Saturday 6 pm in summer (July 4–Labor Day).
