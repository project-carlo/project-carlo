## Publisher

St. Jude the Apostle (Wynantskill) and St. Michael the Archangel (Troy, in the town of North
Greenbush) publish one weekly bulletin. The 27 September 2026 issue is headed "St. Jude the
Apostle & St. Michael the Archangel ... We are one parish family across two churches", with one
Mass schedule for both churches and one leadership list (Priest in Residence Fr. Joseph O'Brien,
Pastoral Associate Kelly Hession). It is posted on the joint eCatholic site (id 31038,
stjudestmichael.org) and the same issues are also posted on St. Jude's diocese-hosted site
(parishes.rcda.org/stjude); the directory still lists the two churches separately with their old
links.

## Worship sites

Two: St. Jude the Apostle, 43 Brookside Ave., Wynantskill (`st-jude-the-apostle-wynantskill`) and
St. Michael the Archangel, 175 Williams Rd. (`st-michael-the-archangel-troy`).

## Website and bulletin provider

eCatholic, site 31038, at stjudestmichael.org. Both publication forms are recorded: the eCatholic
id and St. Jude's rcda-hosted bulletin page, which carries the same issues.
