## What is known

Corpus Christi's own Mass page and its 25 October 2026 bulletin say the weekly (Wednesday 6:15 pm) and First Friday (7:00 pm) Traditional Latin Masses are celebrated in the conference room of St. Mark's Christian Academy, the former convent, at the corner of David and Kamm Avenue, South River, and not in the church. Both are published as parish Masses, so they appear open to the public. No roster lists this place.

## Why it is not decided

It is a room in a school building, with two Masses a month or week, and no street number was found. Whether it counts as a worship site of the same standing as a chapel or mission depends on the owner's view of such rooms; the parish presents the Masses publicly.
