## Name and address

The name St. James is given by the finder, GCatholic and the parish, so it is verified. The street address isn't settled. Three versions:

- The parish (masthead, home page, map embed): 149 Grove Street, which the finder gives as the *mailing* address. It is used for the office ('Parish Office Summer Hours', bulletin page 2), so it may be the rectory and not the church.
- The diocese's finder: 369 Amboy Avenue, '(Corner of Route 35 and Main Street)', a note that describes the church's corner. Amboy Avenue is Route 35.
- GCatholic: 148 Grenville St.

I followed the finder, the official source that describes the church building itself, and chose not to use the parish's address because it is the office address. The coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7HP39+72 (40.55319, -74.28244), within about 30 m of the finder's pin (40.55330, -74.28212), so both sources put the church on the same lot near Amboy Avenue and Main Street. The parish's map pin for 149 Grove St is about 90 m south. A street view or a Contact page that names the church's own address would settle it. address_verified stays false.
