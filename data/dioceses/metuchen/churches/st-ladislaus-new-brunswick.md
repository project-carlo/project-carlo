## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 Holy Family bulletin (page 1, read 2026-11-03) lists Sunday '8:00am (English, St. Ladislaus)' and '9:00am (Hungarian, St. Ladislaus)', and 'Weekdays: 7:00am Mon - Fri (St. Ladislaus)'.

## Name and address

Sources disagree on the street. The finder and the ParishesOnline organization give 215 Somerset Street; the parish's own Worship page gives 213 Somerset St.; GCatholic gives 40 Plum St. I recorded 215 Somerset Street, the diocese's address, which the LPi org repeats and which is two doors from the parish's own figure. GCatholic's Plum Street address is a side street of the same corner site: its Plus Code (40.49481, -74.45269) and the LPi org pin (40.49500, -74.45293) are about 30 m apart, so both mark one building.
