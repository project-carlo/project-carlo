The bulletin's directory (page 2) gives the cathedral as '548 Main St. Metuchen, NJ 08840', with the parish office at 45 Library Place. I recorded 548 Main Street, where the bulletin and OSM agree (address verified), with the OSM building's centre as coordinates.
