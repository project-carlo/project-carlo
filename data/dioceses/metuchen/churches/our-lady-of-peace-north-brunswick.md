## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (ParishesOnline, page 1, read 2026-10-30) lists Saturday vigil 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:30 and 11:00 am and daily Mass Monday-Friday 8:00 am, with the church and parish office at 1730 US Hwy 130. The parish's Mass Times page gives the same times. The finder adds 'First Saturday of the month: 8:00 am', which the bulletin doesn't print.

## Name and address

The finder, the bulletin masthead and the parish's Contact page give the church at 1730 Route 130 North, North Brunswick NJ 08902. GCatholic and ParishesOnline give 277 Washington Place, which the Contact page names as the rectory, so I followed the finder and the bulletin. Coordinates are the finder's; GCatholic's are about 25 m away. Address verification stays false: the bulletin and the parish's site are one source and the finder is the only other source that gives the same address, but I did not check it against a map or a second independent listing.
