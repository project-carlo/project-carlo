## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 Transfiguration of the Lord bulletin (ParishesOnline 04/1001, page 1, read 2026-11-03) lists St. Paul the Apostle Church with Sunday Mass at 9:30 a.m. and 12:30 p.m. (Spanish) and daily Mass Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 8:00 a.m.; page 2's intentions list Masses there on 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8 November.

## Name and address

The bulletin gives '23 South 5th Ave. (Access from corner of 6th & Raritan Ave.)', GCatholic gives 23 S 5th Ave., and the parish finder gives the parish at 23 South Fifth Avenue and the church at the 'Corner of Sixth and Raritan Avenues', which is the entrance the bulletin describes. I recorded 23 South Fifth Avenue (bulletin and GCatholic agree, two independent sources). The name agrees in the bulletin, GCatholic and the finder. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
