## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin and the parish home page (read 2026-11-02) give Saturday 4 pm vigil, Sunday 8, 9:30 and 11 am, and Monday-Friday 8 am.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead puts the church at '6 CHURCH STREET, HAMPTON' and the parish office at '32 Main Street P.O. Box 405'. The finder and GCatholic give 32 Main Street, which the bulletin calls the parish office mailing address. OSM's church building is on Church Street. I recorded 6 Church Street, the parish's own address for the church, which OSM's street agrees with; no second source gives the number, so the address is not marked verified. The finder pin, the GCatholic Plus Code and the OSM building are all within about 20 m of each other (the lot evidently runs between the two streets), so either address finds the building. Name 'St. Ann' in the finder and GCatholic; the parish calls it 'The Church of Saint Ann' (verified). Coordinates from the OSM building.
