## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 shrine bulletin (page 1, read 2026-11-02) lists Sunday 10:30 am (Latin), Monday through Friday 12:15 pm, First Fridays 7:00 pm and First Saturdays 9:00 am (Latin) at the Shrine. St. Ann's, St. Joseph's and Holy Trinity's bulletins of the same day print the same times.

## Name and address

GCatholic gives 50 W Somerset St.; the shrine's bulletin gives 'Shrine Chapel of the Blessed Sacrament, 50 West Somerset Street' and its offices (Clairvaux House) at 52 West Somerset Street, which is the address in the shrine website's footer. The record takes 50, the chapel's own address, on which GCatholic and the bulletin agree (verified). The name follows the shrine's masthead; GCatholic calls it 'Blessed Sacrament', so the full name rests on the shrine's own sources only and is not marked verified. The diocese's finder doesn't list the shrine. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7H987+VW (40.56719, -74.63519).
