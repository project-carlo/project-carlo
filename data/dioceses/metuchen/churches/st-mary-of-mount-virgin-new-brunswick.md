Yes. The 1 November 2026 Visitation bulletin (page 1, read 2026-11-03) gives Saturday 4:00 pm and 7:00 pm (Spanish); Sunday 8:30 am, 9:30 am (Spanish), 11:00 am, 1:00 pm (Spanish) and 6:00 pm; weekdays 8:00 am and 12:10 pm, with some Masses in the chapel. It is the parish's main church.
