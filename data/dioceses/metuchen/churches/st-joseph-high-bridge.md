## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish Mass Times page and the 1 November 2026 bulletin masthead (read 2026-11-02) give Saturday 4 pm vigil, Sunday 8 and 11 am, and Tuesday-Thursday 8 am in the chapel (the Madonna & Child Chapel behind the rectory, on the same campus).
