## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's Mass Times page (read 2026-10-30) lists Saturday vigil 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 AM, 10:00 AM and 12:00 PM, and weekday Mass Monday-Friday 8:30 AM.
