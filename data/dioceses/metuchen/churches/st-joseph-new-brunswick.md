## Name and address

The finder and GCatholic give only the corner, Somerset and Maple Streets. The parish's own Worship page gives 293 Somerset St.; the ParishesOnline organization (an old pre-merger listing) gives 15 Maple St, which may be the former rectory on the Maple Street side. I recorded 293 Somerset Street, the parish's street address, which lies on the corner the diocese names. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code (40.49281, -74.45681); the LPi org pin is about 70 m north. `address_verified` is false because no second source gives the street number.
