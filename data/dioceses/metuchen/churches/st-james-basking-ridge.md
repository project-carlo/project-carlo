## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin masthead and the parish home page (read 2026-11-02) give Saturday 5:30 PM, Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 10:30 AM and 12:00 noon, and weekdays 7:00 AM (Monday-Friday) and 8:00 AM (Tuesday-Saturday). During the sanctuary refurbishment the bulletin puts the Sunday 10:30 in the parish gym, on the same campus.
