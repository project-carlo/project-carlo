## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the 1 November 2026 Sts. Joseph and Luke bulletin (image, rendered) lists St. Luke Church with "HOLY MASS Saturday Vigil (5:00pm) Sunday (10:00am)"; the 25 October issue's text announces events "After 10:00am Mass at St. Luke". The parish of St. Luke was merged into Sts. Joseph and Luke on 1 July 2025; the decree says its parishioners "will continue to have opportunities to worship in the church", and the church still holds weekend Mass.
