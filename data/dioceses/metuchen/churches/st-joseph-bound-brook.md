## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday 5:00 pm English and Sunday 8:00 am English, plus Saturday 7:00 and Sunday 12:00 pm in Spanish, and weekday Masses Monday to Friday 8:30 am and Saturday 8:00 am; the 1 November issue (read 2026-10-30) repeats the schedule. Page 3 of the 1 November issue lists weekday Mass intentions at 8:30 am and Saturday 5:00 pm. The bulletin doesn't say which building the Masses are in; the parish has one church.
