## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:30 AM, 10:30 AM and 12 Noon (Polish), weekdays 8:00 AM (Mon., Tues., Wed., Fri.) and Thursday 6:30 PM; the parish's Mass Times page (read 2026-10-30) agrees. The bulletin says "{Weekday Masses are in St. Ann's Chapel}", and page 3 lists a sanctuary candle and altar candles for St. Ann's Chapel, and page 2 says a 10:30 AM Mass is in "the Msgr. Wujek Room or Chapel" on one occasion. The text read doesn't say whether St. Ann's Chapel is inside the church or a separate building; it is treated as part of this site and not recorded as a second church.
