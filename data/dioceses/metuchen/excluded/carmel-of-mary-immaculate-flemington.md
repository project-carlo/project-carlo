## Why it is excluded

The Discalced Carmelite monastery in Flemington holds public Mass in its chapel (home page, read 2026-11-02: 'Holy Mass times: Mon-Sat: 7:00 am Sun: 8:00 am', and 'The Monastery is open for anyone to come and pray in our chapel'), but it is not a parish and publishes no bulletin, and the bulletin of the parish it sits in, St. Magdalen de Pazzi, does not carry its Masses (no mention of the Carmel in the 1 November 2026 issue). Under the diocese's rule for a shrine or chapel with public Mass but no parish and no bulletin, it is excluded `no-bulletin`. It is in none of the rosters (finder, GCatholic, worship sites); I found it through a web search for St. Magdalen's bulletin. I recorded St. Magdalen de Pazzi as the related parish because the monastery is in Flemington; nothing states a formal tie.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule page the pipeline can read (for example St. Magdalen's bulletin starting to list the Carmel's Masses, or the monastery posting a PDF schedule) would make it a church of that row; a notice that the chapel Mass is no longer public would make it `no-public-mass`.
