## Why it is excluded

Holy Spirit, 580 Hazel Avenue has had no Mass since September 2015. A MyCentralJersey article of 29 October 2015 reports worship and activities there suspended 'effective Sept. 14' (2015), leaving Our Lady of the Most Holy Rosary as Good Shepherd's one operational worship site. Good Shepherd's Religious Education page (read 2026-11-03) now calls it 'the former Holy Spirit Church' and holds classes in its basement, and the 25 October 2026 bulletin's Mass schedule covers only 625 Florida Grove Road. GCatholic still lists it (gcatholic:18602), and Discover Mass and FindMyMass still show old MassTimes.org Mass times (Sunday 8:00 and 12:00), which match no current Good Shepherd schedule; the finder does not list it. The diocese called the 2015 step a suspension rather than a canonical closure; `closed` is used because no Mass is held. last_mass is given as the month (the last Sunday before 14 September 2015 was the 13th, but no source states the last Mass).

## What would change this

A Good Shepherd bulletin or Mass schedule naming Holy Spirit or 580 Hazel Avenue as a Mass site again.
