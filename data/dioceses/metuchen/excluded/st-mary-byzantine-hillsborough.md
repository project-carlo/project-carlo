## Why it is excluded

This is an Eastern Catholic church of the Byzantine Catholic Eparchy of Passaic (Ruthenian), not of the Latin Diocese of Metuchen: GCatholic (church 17176, read 2026-11-03) gives its jurisdiction as 'Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Metuchen', and the Eparchy of Passaic's Parishes page (read 2026-11-03) lists it. The settled rule for this diocese (survey-review, 2026-10-30) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as `other-jurisdiction`. No bulletin was looked for, since the row is out of scope.

Address: GCatholic gives 1900 Brooks Blvd., Manville; the eparchy's list gives 'Saint Mary Byzantine Catholic Church … 1900 Brooks Blvd, Hillsborough, NJ 08844-4299'. Same building and ZIP; the eparchy's town name (Hillsborough Township, Manville postal area) is used.

## What would change this

A policy to map Eastern Catholic churches, or a Latin parish of the Diocese of Metuchen holding Mass in this building.
