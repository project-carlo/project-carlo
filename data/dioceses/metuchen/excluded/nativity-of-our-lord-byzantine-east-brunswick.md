## Why it is excluded

This is an Eastern Catholic church of the Byzantine Catholic Eparchy of Passaic (Ruthenian), not of the Latin Diocese of Metuchen: GCatholic (church 17173, read 2026-11-03) gives its jurisdiction as 'Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Metuchen', and the Eparchy of Passaic's Parishes page (read 2026-11-03) lists it. The settled rule for this diocese (survey-review, 2026-10-30) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as `other-jurisdiction`. No bulletin was looked for, since the row is out of scope.

Address: GCatholic gives 700 Old Bridge Turnpike and the eparchy's list '700 Old Bridge Tpke, East Brunswick, NJ 08816-3869' (website nativitybyzantineeb.com).

## What would change this

A policy to map Eastern Catholic churches, or a Latin parish of the Diocese of Metuchen holding Mass in this building.
