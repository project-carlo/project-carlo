## Why it is excluded

St. Mary, 104 Center Street, has had no Mass since September 2015. St. John Paul II's own About page (read 2026-11-03) says 'On September 13, 2015, the church of St. Mary has been closed' and that the parish now has two churches, St. Stephen and Our Lady of the Rosary of Fatima. A MyCentralJersey article of 29 October 2015 reports worship there suspended 'effective Sept. 14' (2015), with Holy Spirit and La Asuncion, under Bishop Bootkoski's letter of 29 August. The 1 November 2026 bulletin's masthead names only the two other churches. GCatholic still lists it (gcatholic:18591); the finder does not. The diocese described the step as a suspension rather than a canonical closure; the reason here is `closed` because no Mass is held, and last_mass is the parish's own date, 13 September 2015.

## What would change this

A St. John Paul II bulletin or Mass schedule naming St. Mary or 104 Center Street again (La Asuncion, suspended in the same letter, holds Mass again under Most Holy Name of Jesus).
