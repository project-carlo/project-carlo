## Why it is excluded

The building no longer serves the Latin-rite diocese. Divine Mercy's own Parish History page (read 2026-11-02) says weekend Masses moved to Holy Family from January 2015, and that Malabar-rite Catholics bought Sacred Heart of Jesus Church in a sale 'finalized on May 30, 2018'. It is now Christ the King Knanaya Catholic Church: the Knanaya Region's page (read 2026-11-02) gives 'Church Address: 67 Fitch St., Carteret' with Sunday Masses in English (10 A.M.) and Malayalam (11 A.M.), and the parish's own Contact page gives the same address. So Mass is still celebrated there, but under the Syro-Malabar Eparchy of Chicago, which the settled rule for Eastern Catholic churches in the territory excludes as `other-jurisdiction`. The 1 November 2026 Divine Mercy bulletin's schedule is 'All at Holy Family Church'. GCatholic's Latin entry (18599) is out of date.

## What would change this

A Divine Mercy schedule listing Latin Masses at 67 Fitch Street again, or a policy to map Eastern Catholic churches.
