## Why it is excluded

Closed for Mass, last Mass 31 May 2026. The Visitation bulletin of 31 May 2026 (page 2) heads that day's 9:00 am intention 'Sunday, May 31, 2026 - Last Mass at St. John'. Until then every issue checked (one a quarter from June 2025, then 19 April, 3, 17, 24 and 31 May 2026) gave St. John the Baptist a Sunday 9:00 am Mass and, most weeks, a Tuesday 7:00 pm Mass. From the 14 June 2026 issue the schedule has no St. John entry, and from 21 June the masthead's church list captions it 'Elijah's Promise Soup Kitchen' instead of giving its address. The 1 November 2026 issue still lists weekend Masses only at St. Mary of Mount Virgin and St. Theresa. The parish website still names a 'St. John Location' and St. John's CCD classes, which is use of the building, not Mass. The finder and GCatholic (18657) still list it as a worship site, which is out of date.

What I inferred: the soup-kitchen caption suggests the building (or its hall) is now used by Elijah's Promise, but I found no notice of a sale or a decree. The exclusion rests on the bulletin's 'Last Mass' line and the schedule since.

## What would change this

A Visitation bulletin listing a Mass at St. John the Baptist again (an occasional feast-day Mass, for example around 24 June) would make it a worship site again.
