## Why it is excluded

This is an Eastern Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Latin Diocese of Metuchen: GCatholic (church 17484, read 2026-11-03) gives its jurisdiction as 'Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Metuchen', and the Archeparchy of Philadelphia's Parishes page (read 2026-11-03) lists it. The settled rule for this diocese (survey-review, 2026-10-30) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as `other-jurisdiction`. No bulletin was looked for, since the row is out of scope.

Address: GCatholic gives only 'Rt. 46, Great Meadows'; the archeparchy's list gives '329 Route 46, P.O. Box 162, Great Meadows, NJ 07838', whose street number is used.

## What would change this

A policy to map Eastern Catholic churches, or a Latin parish of the Diocese of Metuchen holding Mass in this building.
