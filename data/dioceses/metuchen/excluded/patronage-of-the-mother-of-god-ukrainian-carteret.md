## Why it is excluded

GCatholic marks this entry 'Former Church' with 'History: destroyed by fire 2018.11.27'. The parish is still active: its own site (read 2026-11-03) cancels 'this Saturday October 31, 2026' Divine Liturgy at 5:00 PM, implying a regular one, and the archeparchy still lists it at 719 Roosevelt Ave. Whether the liturgy is in a rebuilt church or another building at that address was not checked; it does not change the exclusion, which rests on jurisdiction, not on closure. It is not recorded `closed` because a Ukrainian Catholic parish still worships here.

This is an Eastern Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Latin Diocese of Metuchen: GCatholic (church 17481, read 2026-11-03) gives its jurisdiction as 'Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Metuchen', and the Archeparchy of Philadelphia's Parishes page (read 2026-11-03) lists it. The settled rule for this diocese (survey-review, 2026-10-30) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as `other-jurisdiction`. No bulletin was looked for, since the row is out of scope.

Address: GCatholic, the archeparchy's list ('719 Roosevelt Ave, Carteret, NJ 07008') and the parish site agree on the address.

## What would change this

A policy to map Eastern Catholic churches, or a Latin parish of the Diocese of Metuchen holding Mass in this building.
