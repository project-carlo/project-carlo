## Why it is excluded

This is an Eastern Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Latin Diocese of Metuchen: GCatholic (church 17485, read 2026-11-03) gives its jurisdiction as 'Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Metuchen', and the Archeparchy of Philadelphia's Parishes page (read 2026-11-03) lists it. The settled rule for this diocese (survey-review, 2026-10-30) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as `other-jurisdiction`. No bulletin was looked for, since the row is out of scope.

Address: GCatholic gives 1700 Brooks Blvd., Manville; the archeparchy's list and the parish site give 1700 Brooks Blvd, Hillsborough, NJ 08844. Same building and ZIP; the parish's town name is used.

## What would change this

A policy to map Eastern Catholic churches, or a Latin parish of the Diocese of Metuchen holding Mass in this building.
