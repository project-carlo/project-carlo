## Why it is excluded

This is an Eastern Catholic church of the Byzantine Catholic Eparchy of Passaic (Ruthenian), not of the Latin Diocese of Metuchen: GCatholic (church 17182, read 2026-11-03) gives its jurisdiction as 'Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Metuchen', and the Eparchy of Passaic's Parishes page (read 2026-11-03) lists it. The settled rule for this diocese (survey-review, 2026-10-30) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as `other-jurisdiction`. No bulletin was looked for, since the row is out of scope.

Address: GCatholic gives 320 Washington St.; the eparchy's list gives only 'PO Box 1030, Perth Amboy, NJ 08862' for Saint Nicholas Byzantine Catholic Church, so GCatholic's street address is used. The finder website stnicholaspa.org answered HTTP 502 on 2026-11-03.

## What would change this

A policy to map Eastern Catholic churches, or a Latin parish of the Diocese of Metuchen holding Mass in this building.
