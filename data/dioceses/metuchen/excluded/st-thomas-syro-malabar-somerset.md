## Why it is excluded

This is an Eastern Catholic church of the Syro-Malabar Eparchy of St. Thomas the Apostle of Chicago, not of the Latin Diocese of Metuchen: GCatholic (church 16976, read 2026-11-03) gives its jurisdiction as 'Syro-Malabar Diocese of Saint Thomas the Apostle of Chicago', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Metuchen', and the parish's own site (read 2026-11-03) names it a Syro-Malabar Catholic Forane Church. The settled rule for this diocese (survey-review, 2026-10-30) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as `other-jurisdiction`. No bulletin was looked for, since the row is out of scope.

Address: GCatholic (16976) gives 44 Livingston Ave., Somerset; the parish's own site (read 2026-11-03) gives 508 Elizabeth Ave, Somerset, NJ 08873 and says its new church was built in 2015. The parish's address is used, since GCatholic's appears to predate the 2015 church.

## What would change this

A policy to map Eastern Catholic churches, or a Latin parish of the Diocese of Metuchen holding Mass in this building.
