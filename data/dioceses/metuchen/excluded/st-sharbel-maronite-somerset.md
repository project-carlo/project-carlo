## Why it is excluded

This is an Eastern Catholic church of the Maronite Eparchy of Saint Maron of Brooklyn, not of the Latin Diocese of Metuchen: GCatholic (church 17034, read 2026-11-03) gives its jurisdiction as 'Maronite Diocese of Saint Maron of Brooklyn', 'within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Metuchen', and the Eparchy of Saint Maron of Brooklyn's Parishes page (read 2026-11-03) lists it. The settled rule for this diocese (survey-review, 2026-10-30) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as `other-jurisdiction`. No bulletin was looked for, since the row is out of scope.

Address: GCatholic (17034) and the eparchy's list give 7 Reeve St.; the parish's own Contact page (saintsharbelnj.org/contact-1, read 2026-11-03) gives 14 Reeve Street. The parish's own current address is used; the two are on the same short street and the parish is running a building project, which may explain the difference. The finder website saintsharbel.net failed to connect (proxy 502) on 2026-11-03; saintsharbelnj.org is the site the eparchy links.

## What would change this

A policy to map Eastern Catholic churches, or a Latin parish of the Diocese of Metuchen holding Mass in this building.
