## Why it is excluded

Christ the King, 211 Louis Street, Manville, a worship site of Christ the Redeemer Parish, no longer has Mass. Christ the Redeemer's 5 July 2020 bulletin says 'As of now there will be no Masses scheduled at Christ the King Church' (the 5 January 2020 issue still had weekday 7:00 am Masses marked 'CTK'). Six years later the parish's own Mass page (read 2026-11-02) puts every weekend, weekday and holy day Mass at Sacred Heart, and the 1 November 2026 masthead names Sacred Heart as the 'Worship Site' and Christ the King only as a 'Parish Center' for religious education. An undated listing on masstimesusa.com calls it 'Christ the King - CLOSED'. The last Mass was in 2020: the 5 January 2020 issue still lists Masses there and the 5 July 2020 issue says none are scheduled. No source read gives the day, so `last_mass` is `2020`.

The diocese's finder still lists it as a worship site with holy-day Masses at 7 am and 6 pm, and GCatholic (18595) lists it as a church. Both conflict with the parish's own schedule, which I followed. Whether the building was sold or is used only for the parish center isn't settled by these sources. The masthead's parish center is at 99 North 13th Avenue, while the finder and GCatholic give the church as 211 Louis Street; they may be one property with two frontages (not checked).

## What would change this

A Christ the Redeemer bulletin or Mass page listing a Mass at Christ the King (for example the holy-day Masses the finder gives) would make it a church of `christ-the-redeemer-manville` again.
