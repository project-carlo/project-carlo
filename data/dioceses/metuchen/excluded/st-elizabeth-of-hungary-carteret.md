## Why it is excluded

Closed. Divine Mercy's own Parish History page (read 2026-11-02) says: 'A Decree of Closure was issued by Bishop Checchio in April of 2018 for St. Elizabeth of Hungry Church and, after being relegated to profane but not sordid use, was sold the Borough of Carteret in September, 2018.' Regular weekend Masses had ended earlier: from January 2015 they were 'all ... celebrated in Holy Family Church', with funerals and sacraments still possible at St. Elizabeth on request. The exact last Mass is not given, so `last_mass` is left empty; it was before the April 2018 decree. The 1 November 2026 bulletin's schedule is 'All at Holy Family Church'. GCatholic's entry (18600) is out of date.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building was relegated to profane use and sold to the Borough of Carteret.
