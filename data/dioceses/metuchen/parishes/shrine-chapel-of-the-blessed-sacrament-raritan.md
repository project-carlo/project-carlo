## Publisher

The Shrine Chapel of the Blessed Sacrament, 50 West Somerset Street, Raritan, publishes its own weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site (26980). The 1 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-02) has the masthead 'SHRINE CHAPEL OF THE BLESSED SACRAMENT', with 'Very Rev. Jeffrey M. Calia, C.O., Provost, Shrine Rector' and 'Rev. Steven J. Bolton, C.O., Vice Rector', the shrine office's own phone and email, and its own Mass intentions (page 2).

## Why a parish row

The shrine is not a parish (GCatholic lists it as a shrine chapel, the diocese's finder doesn't list it), and the batch's lead suggested recording it as a church of St. Ann or St. Joseph if their bulletin listed its Masses. Their bulletins do list its Masses, but so do Holy Trinity's and the shrine's own: all four print one combined Raritan Oratory schedule. The shrine publishes its own weekly bulletin, so under the settled rule ('one that publishes a bulletin … gets a parish row', survey-review 2026-10-30) it is its own row with one church, not a church of another row.

## Shared bulletin with the Oratory parishes?

No. St. Ann (eCatholic 22511, Bon Venture G60661), St. Joseph (22569) and Holy Trinity (22534, G60610) each post their own weekly issues with their own mastheads. See `publication_reviews`.
