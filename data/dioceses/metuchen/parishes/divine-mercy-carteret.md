## Publisher

John Patrick Publishing (church-bulletin.org id 612). The 1 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-02) has the masthead 'Divine Mercy Parish', pastor Rev. Edmund J. Shallow, rectory 213 Pershing Avenue, and its Mass schedule reads 'All at Holy Family Church': Saturday 4:00pm, Sunday 7:30 and 10:45am, weekdays 8:00am. The parish's own site (divinemercycarteret.org/dmc/) has the same Mass times and its Bulletin card links church-bulletin.org/?id=612; the site hosts no bulletin files itself, so there is only one publication.

## Worship sites

One church: Holy Family, 140 Emerson Street (directory:24477, gcatholic:18598, worship-sites:divine-mercy-holy-family-church), the only place in the bulletin's schedule and the finder's only worship site.

GCatholic still lists the parish's two other former churches. Both are ruled out with their own records, from the parish's own Parish History page (read 2026-11-02): from January 2015 all weekend Masses moved to Holy Family.
- Sacred Heart of Jesus, 67 Fitch St (gcatholic:18599), was sold to Malabar-rite Catholics in May 2018 and is now Christ the King Knanaya Catholic Church (Syro-Malabar): `excluded/sacred-heart-of-jesus-carteret`, other-jurisdiction.
- St. Elizabeth of Hungary, 119 Washington Ave (gcatholic:18600), was closed by decree in April 2018 and sold to the Borough: `excluded/st-elizabeth-of-hungary-carteret`, closed.

St. Joseph, Carteret is a separate parish with its own bulletin (`parishes/st-joseph-carteret`).
