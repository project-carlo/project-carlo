## Publisher

St. Joseph, 16 East Somerset Street, Raritan, publishes its own weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site (22569), printed by Bon Venture. The 1 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-02) has the masthead 'The Roman Catholic Community of Saint Joseph, 16 East Somerset Street, Raritan, NJ 08869', names 'Rev. Kevin P. Kelly, C.O.' as Administrator (as the finder does) under 'Very Rev. Jeffrey Calia, C.O., Provost', and gives the parish's own phone, email and sjraritan.org.

## Shared bulletin with the other Oratory parishes?

No. St. Ann (eCatholic 22511, Bon Venture G60661), Holy Trinity (22534, G60610) and the Shrine Chapel of the Blessed Sacrament (26980) each post their own weekly issues with their own mastheads. All four print the same combined Raritan Oratory Mass schedule, so the schedule doesn't tell them apart; the mastheads and contact details do. See `publication_reviews`.
