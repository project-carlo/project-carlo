## Publisher

The Church of Saint James, Basking Ridge, publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (id 6027) and posted both on John Patrick's church-bulletin.org and on the parish's eCatholic site (id 22520). I read the 1 November 2026 issue on both (2026-11-02): each has 8 pages, and the page-1 renders are the same masthead, 'St James Church, 184 South Finley Avenue, P. O. Box 310, Basking Ridge, NJ 07920', pastor Rev. Msgr. Sylvester J. Cronin (matching the finder), with the same Mass schedule. So they are one publication. Under *Rules settled* (one bulletin posted on the parish's eCatholic site and on a publisher's) I recorded the parish-hosted eCatholic copy, listing both ids. The eCatholic archive is current, with 1 November as its newest issue, the same as the John Patrick listing.

## Worship sites

One church, 184 South Finley Avenue (directory:24467, gcatholic:18653). The masthead's schedule puts the Sunday 10:30 AM Mass in the 'GYM', and the home page describes a 'Sanctuary Refurbishment Project'. The gym belongs to this parish campus (St. James School is next door at 200 S. Finley Ave.), so I took it as a temporary room for one Mass during the works, not a separate worship site. The chapel named for Saturday confession and Thursday adoration is also on the campus. No other church is named.
