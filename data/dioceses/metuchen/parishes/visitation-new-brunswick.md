## Publisher

The Parish of the Visitation, New Brunswick (parish office 192 Sandford Street), publishes one weekly bulletin through Bon Venture, account G60662, listed at `sponsors.bonventure.net/?page=6555`. The parish's eCatholic site (23109) links only that listing from its Bulletins page. The 1 November 2026 issue (page 1, read 2026-11-03) names Rev. Michael Fragoso as Pastor and Rev. Frank Fellrath and Rev. Randy J. Gamboa Espinoza as parochial vicars. The finder lists different parochial vicars (Rev. Edmund Luciano, Rev. Virgil Tolentino); I followed the masthead.

## Worship sites

The 1 November 2026 masthead gives Masses at two churches:

- St. Mary of Mount Virgin, 190 Sandford Street (`st-mary-of-mount-virgin-new-brunswick`): Saturday 4:00 and 7:00 pm (Spanish); Sunday 8:30, 9:30 (Spanish), 11:00 am, 1:00 pm (Spanish) and 6:00 pm; weekdays. GCatholic's 'St. Lucy Filippini' National Shrine (18676), at 190 Sanford St. and grouped under 'Church of St. Mary of Mount Virgin', is this church under its shrine title, so it is linked here rather than given a record of its own.
- St. Theresa of the Infant Jesus, 15 Fox Road, Edison (`st-theresa-of-the-infant-jesus-edison`): Saturday 5:30 pm.

Ruled out: **St. John the Baptist, 29 Abeel Street** (`excluded/st-john-the-baptist-new-brunswick`). The 31 May 2026 issue lists 'Sunday, May 31, 2026 - Last Mass at St. John'; issues up to 31 May gave it Sunday 9:00 am and Tuesday 7:00 pm, from 14 June it has no Mass times, and since 21 June the masthead captions it 'Elijah's Promise Soup Kitchen' in place of its address. The finder and GCatholic (18657) still list it.
