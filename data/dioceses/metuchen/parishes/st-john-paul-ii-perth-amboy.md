## Publisher

St. John Paul II, Perth Amboy publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by Bon Venture and posted on Bon Venture's sponsor page 2153, which the parish site's Bulletin page links (the parish hosts no copy). The 1 November 2026 masthead (page 1, read 2026-11-03) names 'Saint John Paul II', 'PARISH IN PERTH AMBOY', Fr. Sławomir Romanowski, CSsR as pastor, and two churches: St. Stephen's, 490 State Street, and Our Lady of the Rosary of Fatima, 188 Wayne Street. The finder calls Fr. Romanowski administrator; the masthead says pastor.

## Worship sites

Two churches, as the masthead lists: St. Stephen (the main schedule) and Our Lady of the Rosary of Fatima ('OLRF': Sunday 9:30 AM and Thursday 7:30 PM, Portuguese). The finder's two worship sites agree.

GCatholic also lists St. Mary, 104 Center St. (gcatholic:18591) under the parish. The parish's About page says 'On September 13, 2015, the church of St. Mary has been closed' and 'Currently, the parish has two churches'; a 29 October 2015 MyCentralJersey article dates the suspension of worship there from 14 September 2015; the 1 November 2026 bulletin's text pages do not name it. It is excluded as closed (`excluded/st-mary-perth-amboy`).

Our Lady of Fatima, 380 Smith Street is a separate parish (`our-lady-of-fatima-perth-amboy`), not this parish's Our Lady of the Rosary of Fatima, 188 Wayne Street.

## Website and bulletin provider

Provider `other`, reading the Bon Venture listing with `page-pdfs http://sponsors.bonventure.net/?page=2153`; website is the parish's own site, johnpaulsecond.com (probe url said DEAD-DNS, but probe get loaded it). Discover Mass has a church page for Our Lady of the Rosary of Fatima with no bulletins, so it is not recorded as a publication.
