## Publisher

The parish posts its own bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (account 6053), on its Catholic Web Company site at `https://spsj.org/event` ("Events & Bulletin"), as `/uploads/docs/bulletins/<YYYYMMDD>.pdf`. The 1 November 2026 issue's masthead (page 1, text layer) reads "St. Philip & St. James CATHOLIC CHURCH, 430 SOUTH MAIN STREET • PHILLIPSBURG, NJ 08865" and lists "Fr. Antony Arockiadoss Pastor" with parochial vicars Fr. Alex Carles and Fr. Joseph Ban Thien Ho. Its last page carries the code line "6053 St Philip & St James, Phillipsburg, NJ (B)" and "John Patrick Publishing Company".

The same issue is on church-bulletin.org (`?id=6053` redirects to the St. Philip & St. James listing). Its 2026-11-01.pdf has the same 12 pages, and its `pdftotext` output matched the parish copy line for line, so it is one publication (rule settled for parish-hosted plus publisher copies). Both URLs are listed in `publication`. The newest issue on both was 1 November 2026 when checked.

The finder's clergy line ("Pastor: Rev. John Barbella") is out of date; the masthead is followed.

## Worship sites

One church, 430 South Main Street (directory:24527, gcatholic:18685). The masthead's schedule covers that church alone. The "Holy Family Chapel" (adoration and Monday novena) is a chapel used by the parish, not a separate Mass site in the schedule. The bulletin also mentions Masses at local nursing homes (not public) and an All Souls Mass at the cemetery (a one-off). No other church in Phillipsburg or Alpha is named.
