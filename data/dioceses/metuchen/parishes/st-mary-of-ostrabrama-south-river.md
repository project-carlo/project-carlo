## Publisher

St. Mary of Ostrabrama publishes through eCatholic (site id 11802, embedded on www.stmarysr.org). Its issues are at `files.ecatholic.com/11802/bulletins/<YYYYMMDD>.pdf`. The 25 October 2026 issue (6 pages, read 2026-10-30) gives the parish's contact details, mission statement and Mass schedule on page 1 and names "Very Rev. Michael J. Gromadzki, Pastor". Greps of the issue for "Stephen" and "Corpus" found nothing, so it isn't shared with St. Stephen Protomartyr or Corpus Christi.
