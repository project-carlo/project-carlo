## Publisher

Our Lady of the Mount, Warren publishes its own weekly bulletin. The parish posts it on its eCatholic site (14065), and Discover Mass carries a copy. The 1 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-02) has the masthead 'OUR LADY OF THE MOUNT ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH … 170 MT BETHEL RD| WARREN, NJ', names 'Rev. Sean W. Kenney, OFS, Pastor' (as the finder does) and no other church.

## Worship sites

One church, 170 Mount Bethel Road (directory:24551, gcatholic:18626). The bulletin's schedule places weekend Masses in the 'Church' (Saturday Vigil 5:00 pm; Sunday 8:00, 10:00, 11:30 am) and daily and holy-day Masses in the 'Chapel', which I take to be the chapel on the same campus (the parish site has a 'Chapel' photo album); inferred, not read from an address. No separate worship site.

Website https://ourladyofthemount.org, eCatholic site 14065. GCatholic's www.olmwarren.org is an old domain (staff emails still use @olmwarren.org) that didn't resolve through the probes; the finder and the masthead give ourladyofthemount.org.
