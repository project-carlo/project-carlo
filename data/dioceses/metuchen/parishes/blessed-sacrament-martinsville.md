Blessed Sacrament, Martinsville publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by Bon Venture. The parish posts each issue on its eCatholic site (23107), and Bon Venture's sponsor page 1569 carries the same file. The 1 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-02) names 'REV. BRIAN J. NOLAN, D. MIN PASTOR' (the finder's pastor) and gives '1890 Washington Valley Road - Physical Address' and '852 Newmans Lane - Mailing Address'; page 2 is headed 'Blessed Sacrament Church'. It names no other church.

## Worship sites

One church (directory:24505, gcatholic:18593, worship-sites:blessed-sacrament-church). The finder lists one worship site at 1890 Washington Valley Road while its pin and GCatholic use 852 Newmans Lane; the bulletin calls the first the physical address and the second the mailing address, and OSM puts both house numbers on one campus about 230 m apart with the church building between them, so these are one place, not two. Masses (1 November 2026 bulletin): Saturday 4 pm; Sunday 7:30 am, 10 am and 12 noon; Monday to Saturday 9 am; Wednesday 12 pm. Page 3 notes 'We are no longer holding 12:10 pm weekday Masses'.
