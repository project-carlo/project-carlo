## Building identity

GCatholic 37951 identifies St. Francis at 1 Little Flower Square, Garrison. The building-specific checks and remaining public-access/publication question are below; no local scratch file is needed.

## October 10, 2026 resolved checks

The actual Weekly Mass and Prayer Times page was read, not an invented /st-francis-chapel URL. Sunday 11 AM is expressly at Our Lady of the Atonement Chapel, 40 Franciscan Way. The monthly prayer service in a Sisters' St. Francis Chapel is expressly a Holy Hour, not Mass. The 2026 jubilee page separately describes the 1911 Friars' St. Francis Chapel and invites parish groups to arrange Mass there. It supplies positive current chapel-use evidence but no date open to individual members of the public and no weekly chapel bulletin. These three chapels/services cannot be conflated.
