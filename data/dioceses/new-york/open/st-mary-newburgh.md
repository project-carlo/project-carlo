## What is known

St. Mary, 180 South St., Newburgh (GCatholic gcatholic:16329, listed as a mission of St. Patrick) was the subject of the archdiocese's 2015 closures: a news report reposted by BishopAccountability on July 27, 2015 says it 'will be closed on August 1' and that parishioners had appealed to the Congregation for the Clergy. No source found gives the appeal's outcome, a last Mass or a sale.

The merged parish, St. Patrick's-St. Mary's & Our Lady of the Lake, publishes one bulletin; its November 8, 2026 issue gives Masses only under 'St. Patrick-St. Mary's' (55 Grand Street) and Our Lady of the Lake (433 South Plank Rd.), both already church records of [the parish row](../parishes/st-patrick-st-mary-and-our-lady-of-the-lake-newburgh.md). The joined name refers to the merged parish at Grand Street, not to Masses at South Street.

## Why it is not decided

The 2015 source is a planned closure under appeal, not a dated last Mass, so it can't by itself support a `closed` exclusion. A source dating the last Mass (or the appeal's rejection and a sale) would exclude it as closed with `last_mass` 2015; a South Street Mass in the bulletin would make it a church of the parish row.
