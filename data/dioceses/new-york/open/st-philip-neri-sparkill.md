## What is known

GCatholic 60865 is the New York Oratory of St. Philip Neri, 175 Route 340, Sparkill: a house of Oratorian priests on the Dominican Convent campus (its site gives that address; it grew out of Hallel communications, founded 1967, and received its decree of foundation in 2009). The Oratorians' public ministry is at their apostolate parishes, St. Paul and St. Ann (Congers) and Our Lady of the Sacred Heart (Tappan), whose bulletins are separate rows. Read on 8 November 2026, the Oratory's homepage and calendar give no Mass at Sparkill; the homepage's Mass items are livestreams from St. John's, Piermont and SPaSA parish, and its news concerns St. Paul, Congers and St. Ann's Church. The healing ministry at philipneri.org (read October 10) is a charity at the same address.

The campus's other GCatholic chapel, Sacred Heart (60864), is the Dominican Sisters' chapel and is [excluded](../excluded/sacred-heart-sparkill.md).

## Why it is not decided

A priests' community has a chapel and says Mass daily, but no source says whether visitors may attend at Sparkill, and none says they may not. The next check is the Oratory's site, around its patronal feast (26 May).
