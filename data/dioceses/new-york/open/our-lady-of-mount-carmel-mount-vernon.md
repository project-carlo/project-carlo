## What is known

Our Lady of Mount Carmel, 10 South 10th Avenue, Mount Vernon, was merged into St. Mary in 2015; the merged parish, [St. Mary-Our Lady of Mount Carmel](../parishes/st-mary-and-our-lady-of-mount-carmel-mount-vernon.md), publishes a readable eCatholic bulletin. Its masthead on 20 September and 1 November 2026 gives one address, 23 South High Street, and one Mass schedule (Saturday 5:00 and 7:00 PM Spanish; Sunday 8:00, 9:30 Spanish, 11:30 AM; daily 9:00 AM; Monday and Wednesday 7:00 PM Spanish). The parish homepage (read October 10) gives the same schedule with no second site.

The Wikipedia list of closed churches in the archdiocese (read 8 November 2026) names the old Chapel of St. Anthony, once attached to Mount Carmel, as closed in 1949, but not Mount Carmel church itself. A web search found no sale, relegation or demolition of the 10 South 10th Avenue building.

## Why it is not decided

No source dates a public Mass at 10 South 10th Avenue now, and none says the building closed. A merged parish's old church can keep occasional Masses (its patronal feast, 16 July) that the regular schedule omits. The next check is the parish's bulletins around the July 2027 feast and the archdiocese's decree index.
