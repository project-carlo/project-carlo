## What is known

St. James, 12 Main St., Milton (GCatholic gcatholic:16427) was merged with St. Mary, Marlboro in August 2015 (GCatholic; a 2015 Poughkeepsie Journal report read by the October session). The merged parish's church in Marlboro is now called Saint Mary-Saint James Church. As of 2026-11-08 its bulletin (Bon Venture 1601, November 1 and 8 issues) puts every Mass at Saint Mary-Saint James in Marlboro or Our Lady of Mercy Chapel in Roseton and never mentions Milton. Web searches found no dated sale, closure or reuse of the Milton building.

## Why it is not decided

No current source gives Mass in Milton and no source dates the building's closure; a merger is not a closure source, and the merged church's name keeps 'St. James' for the Marlboro building. A dated sale or closure record would exclude it as closed; a Milton Mass in the bulletin would make it a church of [St. Mary and St. James](../parishes/st-mary-and-st-james-marlboro.md).
