## What is known

This record now covers both former open records for Sts. Peter and Paul, Melrose: GCatholic 16187 gives 833 St. Ann's Avenue and the archdiocesan map (directory:4760) gives 824 Brook Avenue, with the same phone, 718-665-3924, in every source. OSM's church building is at 833 St. Ann's Avenue, about 120 m from the directory pin; the Bronx Catholic blog titles the parish 'Brook Avenue' and Wikipedia gives 833 St. Ann's Avenue, so the two addresses are one parish complex fronting both streets, not two churches. The address recorded is OSM's and GCatholic's for the church building; the directory's may be the rectory or office.

The archdiocesan page (9 November 2026) lists the church with no Mass times, website or bulletin. Two third-party listings give conflicting, undated schedules (Discover Mass/MassTimes.org: Saturday 5:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 and 11:30 AM Spanish, with a COVID-era caveat; catholicmasstime.org: Sunday 8:30, 10:00, 11:15 AM). No parish website, bulletin, Discover Mass bulletin or ParishesOnline org was found.

## Why it is not decided

The archdiocese still lists it as a church, but no dated source shows public Mass there now and none says it stopped. See `revisit`.
