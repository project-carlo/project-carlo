## Exact institution identified

This is the Catholic Trinity Retreat at 1 Pryer Manor Road, Larchmont, not a Montfort chapel, not the non-denominational Trinity Church on Boston Post Road, and not the Episcopal Trinity Retreat Center in Connecticut. The original GCatholic record names the Franciscan Friars of the Renewal and supplies location 87G8W68V+VH. The historical mirror of the retreat's own information page supplies the Pryer Manor Road address and links its original franciscanfriars.com/trinityretreat/ information/schedule pages.

## What the historical information does and does not prove

The information page describes a 16-person retreat house and services for clergy, brothers, seminarians and deacons. It permits arranged group activities and historical guest visits; that does not establish a present publicly advertised Eucharist. Its named directors/staff and archived access policy must not be represented as current October 2026 staffing or admission rules. The listed 914-235-6839 and 914-632-3743 numbers are historical leads, not confirmed working current contacts. The current CFR friary list provides no Trinity public Mass entry. Neither that omission nor a generic map label is an explicit closure source.
