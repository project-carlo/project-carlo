## What is known

This is the Catholic Trinity Retreat, 1 Pryer Manor Road, Larchmont (GCatholic 113150, Plus Code 87G8W68V+VH), a 16-person retreat house for priests founded by Fr. Benedict Groeschel and listed by GCatholic as served by the Franciscan Friars of the Renewal. It is not the Episcopal Trinity Retreat Center in West Cornwall, Connecticut, nor the Catholic Community of Larchmont's churches. The only description of the house is a historical mirror of its own page (bishop-accountability.org, read October 10), which describes retreats for clergy, not a public Mass. The Friars' current friary list (read October 10) has no Larchmont entry, and a web search on 8 November 2026 found nothing about the house from 2024 to 2026.

## Why it is not decided

No source says whether the house still operates, whether its chapel has a Mass open to the public, or that it closed; the Friars' omission of it is absence, not a closure statement. The next check is the Friars' friary list, GCatholic and local news for the property's current use.
