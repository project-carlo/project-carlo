## What is known

Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary, 209 Hoyt Street, Port Ewen (GCatholic gcatholic:16441, directory:4602), is one of the churches of the parish that publishes the St. Mary-St. Peter & Sacred Heart/Presentation bulletin ([its parish row](../parishes/presentation-of-the-blessed-virgin-mary-sacred-heart-esopus.md)). As of 2026-11-08 neither that bulletin (November 1 issue, schedule of Masses and the week's intentions) nor the parish Mass Times page gives any Mass here: weekend Masses are at St. Mary-St. Peter, Kingston, and the Latin Mass at Sacred Heart, Esopus. The parish site's footer still prints the church's address, and the archdiocese reported an Election-eve adoration service here on November 4, 2024 (the newest dated activity found).

Address: the parish site gives 209 Hoyt Street; GCatholic and the archdiocese's map give Father Kelley Dr. (the same block). Recorded 209 Hoyt Street, the parish's own.
