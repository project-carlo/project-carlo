## What is known

Santa Maria, 2352 St. Raymond Avenue (GCatholic 16142), is a parish entrusted to the Idente Missionaries since 1994; the order's current location page (read 8 November 2026) still lists it, with phone 718-828-2380 and the account name santamariachurchbronx, but no Mass times. The archdiocese's official parish map (October 2026) has no entry for it, and archny.org/places/santa-maria answers 404. A third-party listing, catholicmasstime.org, says 'No regular Masses as of 9/17/2023'. The old domain santamariaparish.us served a software-download page and is not a parish source. The Cluster 31 site (cluster31bx.org) carries only history and 2017 schedules. The Instagram profile is unavailable, and the 8 November bulletin of Our Lady of Solace - St. Dominic, the other Idente parish nearby, does not mention Santa Maria.

## Why it is not decided

No dated source says Mass is celebrated there now, and none official says it stopped: the 2023 listing is third-party and the absence from the archdiocesan map is only absence. See `revisit` for what would settle it.
