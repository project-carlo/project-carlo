## What is known

Assumption, 211 Homestead Ave., Maybrook, belongs to the Holy Name of Mary-Assumption parish (GCatholic gcatholic:16337: a mission of Holy Name of Mary, Montgomery; the parish history marks the church's 110th anniversary in 2017-18). The parish site says it serves Montgomery and Maybrook.

As of 2026-11-08, no source read gives a Mass at this building. The November 1 bulletin (image-only) has one unlabelled schedule; the homepage and the archdiocese's place page print that schedule with the single address 89 Union Street, Montgomery, and the archdiocese's map has no Maybrook location. The schedule is recorded under [Holy Name of Mary](../churches/holy-name-of-mary-montgomery.md).

## Why it is not decided

Nothing shows Mass here now, and nothing says the church closed; absence from a schedule is not a closure source. A Maybrook-labelled Mass in a bulletin (Christmas, the August 15 feast) would make it a church of [Holy Name of Mary-Assumption](../parishes/holy-name-of-mary-assumption.md); a dated closure or sale notice would exclude it.
