## What is known

St. Veronica, 149 Christopher Street, lost its regular Masses in 2017; a Vatican court re-sanctified it as a church in 2020 (amNY, October 18, 2023), and from then the responsible parish, Our Lady of Guadalupe at St. Bernard (328 West 14th Street), held two Masses a year there: the June 7 dedication Mass and the St. Veronica feast-day Mass, July 12, 2024 at noon (The Village Sun, July 11, 2024). Village Preservation reported on August 26, 2025 that the archdiocese had issued a decree of relegation earlier that month, meaning "they intend to end use of the property, setting it up for sale", and had curtailed services and access.

## Why it is not decided

No source found dates a last Mass or a sale after the 2025 decree. In 2026 the responsible parish held the St. Veronica feast-day Mass in its own church: the 5 July 2026 Guadalupe bulletin (page 6) announces "St. Veronica's Feast Day Mass at Our Lady of Guadalupe at St Bernard Sunday July 12th, 2026 @ 10:30am", and its 31 May and 7 June issues announce no Mass at St. Veronica's; the 8 November 2026 issue doesn't mention it. That points to no public Mass at 149 Christopher Street in 2026, but no source says so, and a decree is not a dated closure (the 2018 one was overturned in 2020). A dated statement that the church is closed or sold would make it `excluded/` closed; a Mass there would make it a church under Our Lady of Guadalupe at St. Bernard (see revisit).
