## What is known

Most Sacred Heart, 12 McAllister St., Port Jervis (GCatholic gcatholic:16345) was the second Catholic parish in the city; the October session found 2007 regional-restructuring announcements that planned its parish's closure into Immaculate Conception-St. Mary. As of 2026-11-08 the St. Mary's Mass Times page puts every Mass at the Ball Street church or its convent chapel and names no Mass or event at Sacred Heart. Web searches found no dated closure, sale, demolition or reuse of the McAllister Street building (results were for other Sacred Heart churches and an unrelated Port Jervis Reformed church).

## Why it is not decided

No current source gives Mass here and no source dates the building's closure; a 2007 plan is not a closure source. A dated sale or reuse record, or a closure notice, would exclude it as closed; a Mass or event in the St. Mary schedule would make it a church of [Immaculate Conception-St. Mary](../parishes/immaculate-conception-st-mary-port-jervis.md).
