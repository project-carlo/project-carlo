## What is known

The official directory names Sts. Peter and Paul at 824 Brook Avenue, phone 718-665-3924. Its detail page supplies no public Mass schedule. GCatholic 16187 instead names 833 St. Anns Avenue; that assigned building has its own open record.

## Why it is not decided

A same-name same-city entry is not sufficient evidence that these are the same actual worship site. Verify site identity and current public Mass/bulletin before associating the rosters or excluding either.
