## What is known

GCatholic (16167) lists St. John Nam, a Korean Catholic church, at 3663 White Plains Road, Williamsbridge; a 2012 Bronx Catholic blog post says the community moved in 2007 into the former St. Mary church there. The archdiocese's official parish map (October 2026) has no entry for it. A September 2024 New York State housing board book (PDF page 57) describes an eight-story, 136-unit project at the same address, without saying worship there has ended or the building has been demolished. Discover Mass lists the church with no bulletins; OSM has only a house number at the address; English and Korean web searches on 8 November 2026 found no current Mass schedule, bulletin, closure notice or new venue.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing dated shows public Mass there now, and nothing says it stopped: a housing financing plan is not a completed closure. See `revisit` for what would settle it.
