## What is known

St. Sylvia, 104 Broadway, Tivoli (GCatholic gcatholic:16219) merged into St. Christopher, Red Hook, in 2015; the archdiocese's November 2014 announcement (as reported by Wikipedia) said weekly Masses and sacraments would end there in August 2015 but the church could be used on special occasions. A February 2020 HVNY article reported Brice and Helen Marden's plan to preserve the property as a community and cultural space, with a village hearing set for March 4, 2020; no source found records a completed sale or conversion. The St. Christopher / Good Shepherd bulletin of November 1, 2026 does not mention St. Sylvia or Tivoli.

## Why it is not decided

The 2015 change ended weekly Mass but kept the church for occasional use, and the 2020 sale was a plan. A dated sale or secular reuse record would exclude it as closed (`last_mass` unknown, weekly Mass ended August 2015); an occasional Mass in the Red Hook bulletin would make it a church of [St. Christopher / Good Shepherd](../parishes/st-christopher-good-shepherd.md).
