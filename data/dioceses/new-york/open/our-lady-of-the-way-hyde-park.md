## What is known

Our Lady of the Way is the chapel of the former St. Andrew-on-Hudson Jesuit property, now the Culinary Institute of America, 1946 Campus Drive, Hyde Park (GCatholic gcatholic:38955). The St. Peter's Parish (Poughkeepsie) bulletin of November 8, 2026 still lists it among 'The Chapels of St. Peter's Parish' ('Our Lady of the Way, 1903, at the Culinary Institute'), but the Mass Schedule on the same page has Masses only in the Parish Auditorium and Our Lady of the Rosary Chapel.

Other sources are old: a Discover Mass / MassTimes listing gives 'Wednesday 10:00am' under a COVID-19 notice; 2018-19 blog posts announced a Sunday 10 AM Traditional Latin Mass there. The chapel's listed website, olwuacia.org, no longer resolves (2026-11-08).

## Why it is not decided

No current source gives a Mass at the chapel, and none says Mass ended there or that it is closed to the public. A dated current notice of a Mass (or of its end) settles it; if Mass is listed in St. Peter's bulletin it becomes a church of [St. Peter](../parishes/st-peter-poughkeepsie.md).
