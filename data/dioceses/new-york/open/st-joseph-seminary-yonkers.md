## What is known

St. Joseph's Seminary (Dunwoodie), 201 Seminary Avenue, Yonkers, is the archdiocese's major seminary, distinct from St. Joseph Church, 141 Ashburton Avenue (GCatholic lists them separately). Its site (read 8 November 2026) describes the Main Chapel as an event venue ("Liturgical Services: NO FEE", 164 stalls), offers the Main and Deacon chapels for hosted events, and keeps its calendar behind a password for internal members. It publishes an academic catalogue, not a bulletin, and announces no Mass schedule open to visitors. On October 8 the Visitors and Researchers page was read; it covers the Corrigan Library, not the chapel.

## Why it is not decided

The seminary community certainly celebrates Mass daily, but no source says whether the public may attend, and none says they may not. Without a public schedule there is nothing the site could list either way. The next check is the seminary's news and events for a publicly announced Mass, such as on the patronal feast, 19 March.
