## What is known — October 10, 2026

The community's replacement primary website is https://www.saintmichaels.nyc/, not the old stmichaelruscath.org generic church blog. Its official history, rendered October 10, identifies the founding chapel at 266 Mulberry Street and says the rector after 2016 was appointed by the Archdiocese of New York. Russian Byzantine rite alone therefore does not justify other-jurisdiction exclusion. Its current Directions explicitly says the community is now in residence at St. Catherine of Siena, 411 East 68th Street; its Service Times gives Sunday noon Divine Liturgy and Saturday 6 PM Great Vespers. Those are services at the current residence, not proof of worship at the original Mulberry building. This replaces the earlier generic search/website blocker with a documented relocation.
