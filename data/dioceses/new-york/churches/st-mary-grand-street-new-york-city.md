## Holds Mass

The 11 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, read November 8) prints the "Mass Schedule October 10 - 17, 2026": Saturday 5 P.M., Sunday 9 A.M. and 11 A.M., with intentions, and says the church is now open every day 8:15 a.m. to 3 p.m. The homepage on October 8 listed a Saturday 5 PM vigil, Sunday Masses and a daily 9 AM bilingual Mass.

## Name and address

The parish's homepage gives the church at 440 Grand Street, New York, NY 10002 (its office is at 213 Stanton Street), and the archdiocese's map lists "St. Mary Church", 440 Grand Street: two sources agree, so both flags are true. GCatholic gives 28 Attorney Street, a side-street address next to the Grand Street block; the parish and the archdiocese are followed. Coordinates are the archdiocese map's pin. The parish calls itself Saint Mary; the map's "St. Mary Church" stays in the roster link.
