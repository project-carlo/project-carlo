## Holds Mass

The St. Aloysius (Livingston Manor) bulletin of November 8, 2026 lists, in its weekend Mass schedule, Sunday "9:30am - Monastery of Sisters of Bethlehem, Livingston Manor. All are welcome." Every St. Aloysius and St. Peter issue from October 4 to November 8 carries the same line, so this is a standing public Sunday Mass, not a one-off. It is filed under St. Aloysius, the Livingston Manor publisher whose bulletin lists it (St. Peter's Liberty bulletin prints the same shared schedule; the Woodbourne one does not). The monastery's own site invites guests to share the sisters' liturgy; a search result summarising it gives other daily Mass times (7:40 AM, Monday 5 PM), which were not read on the site itself and are not used here.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it the Monastery of Sisters of Bethlehem, Livingston Manor; the monastery's own site (read 2026-11-08) gives Monastery of Bethlehem, 393 Our Lady of Lourdes Camp Road, Livingston Manor, NY 12758. Only that one source gives the street address, so both verification flags stay false. Neither roster lists it (the archdiocese's map and GCatholic were searched for Bethlehem, Monastery and Livingston Manor); it is added on the bulletin's schedule under Settled policy 3. Coordinates are left unset for the geocode stage rather than guessed.
