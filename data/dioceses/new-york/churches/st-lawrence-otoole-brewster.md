## Holds Mass

Yes, every weekend and every weekday. The 27 September 2026 bulletin, page 1, lists Saturday
Vigil 5:00 pm and 7:30 pm (Spanish), Sunday 8:00, 9:30 and 11:00 am, 12:30 pm (Spanish) and
5:30 pm, and Monday–Friday 8:30 am, plus confessions Monday and Friday from 4:00 to 7:00 pm.
The archdiocese place page lists the same hours.

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic and the archdiocese all use St. Lawrence O'Toole, and all three give
31 Prospect Street, 10509. The bulletin and GCatholic say Brewster. The archdiocese says
"Town of Southeast", the town the Village of Brewster is in, at the same street and ZIP. I
stored Brewster, the postal city the bulletin and GCatholic agree on. That agreement is why
both flags are true; the town name is recorded rather than treated as a second building.
Coordinates are the centre of GCatholic's Plus Code 87H899XM+87.
