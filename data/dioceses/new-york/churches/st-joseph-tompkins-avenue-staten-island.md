## Holds Mass

The October 4, 2026 St. Joseph and St. Mary Immaculate bulletin explicitly schedules Saturday 5 PM English / 6:30 PM Spanish and Sunday 10 AM / noon English / 1:30 PM Spanish at St. Joseph.
