## Current public Mass

The 1 November 2026 bulletin (Bon Venture, read 31 October) names this church at 221 East Lake Boulevard and lists its Masses for 31 October to 8 November: Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 10:15, 11:30 AM and 5:30 PM, weekday noon, and a Saturday 7:30 PM Spanish Mass "(Chapel)". The October 11 issue (Flocknote attachment, read October 10) gave the same weekend pattern; its accompanying note cancelled only the October 11 Polish Mass. The public diocesan entry corroborates the weekend church schedule.

## Address and chapel distinction

Address chosen: 221 East Lake Boulevard. The parish's bulletin masthead (1 and 8 November 2026 issues) and the archdiocese's place page (read October 10) both give 221, two independent sources, so `address_verified` is true; GCatholic and Discover Mass give 225 East Lake (GCatholic Plus Code 87H897JF+64), the same complex a few doors off, not used. The name St. John the Evangelist is the bulletin's and the archdiocese's, and GCatholic's "Church of St. John the Evangelist" agrees. Coordinates are left for the geocode stage at the chosen address. Weekday noon and Saturday 7:30 PM Spanish services are labelled "(Chapel)" in the bulletin; they are taken as a chapel within the parish complex, and no separately located chapel record is made.
