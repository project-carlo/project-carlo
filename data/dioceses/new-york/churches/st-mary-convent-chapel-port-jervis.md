## Holds Mass

St. Mary's, Port Jervis, holds all its weekday Masses (Monday to Thursday 9:00am) in the convent chapel, 'entrance in parking lot', plus a first-Saturday 12pm Latin Low Mass and a first-Sunday 12pm Hungarian Mass there (Mass Times page, read 2026-11-08). The main church's weekend Masses are recorded under [St. Mary Church - Immaculate Conception](immaculate-conception-port-jervis.md). The publisher is [Immaculate Conception-St. Mary](../parishes/immaculate-conception-st-mary-port-jervis.md).

## Name and address

The parish calls it the convent chapel and gives no separate address; the chapel is on the St. Mary's campus, reached from the church parking lot. Recorded the campus address the parish prints, 50 Ball Street (the main church record uses the directory's 48 Ball Street; GCatholic gives 46). Neither roster lists the chapel separately. Coordinates are left for the geocode stage rather than borrowing the main church's pin for a different building.
