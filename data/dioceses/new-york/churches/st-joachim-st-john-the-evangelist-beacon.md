## Holds public Mass

This compound-named roster entry describes the St. John the Evangelist building, not both Beacon churches at the parish office. Page 1 of the October 4, 2026 Bon Venture 1521 issue identifies it at 35 Willow Street and assigns Sunday 7:30/10:30 AM/noon Spanish and weekdays 8 AM. Saint Joachim is separately mapped at 51 Leonard Street.

GCatholic uses the parish-office address 2 Oak Street, the official directory uses 31 Willow Street, and the current bulletin uses 35 Willow Street. The existing building-precise Plus Code is retained; no office coordinate is invented and address verification remains false.
