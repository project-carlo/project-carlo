## Name and address

The parish source gives 130 Cedar Grove Avenue, Staten Island, NY 10306. Neither roster lists this worship venue separately, so it is intentionally unlinked. On October 8, 2026 an actual Nominatim search returned the named Our Lady of Lourdes Chapel building, OSM way 248242951, at 40.5613937, -74.0999488. The footprint is sourced, not a guessed street point. OSM calls it 128 Cedar Grove Avenue; the parish calls it 130.
