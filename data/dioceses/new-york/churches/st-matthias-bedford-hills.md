## Holds Mass

The parish's Mass page (read 8 November 2026) lists "St. Matthias Church Mass, 109 Babbitt Rd, Bedford Hills" on Saturday at 4:00 PM, and the parish's 8 November bulletin lists Saturday 4:00 PM Masses with intentions on 7 and 14 November ("4:00 PM Monsignor John Davis", "4:00 PM Frank Monteleone"). The bulletin's intentions don't name the church; the Mass page is the only 4:00 PM slot the parish has. It is the parish's mission chapel, listed by the archdiocese as "St. Mary of the Assumption: St. Matthias Mission Chapel".
