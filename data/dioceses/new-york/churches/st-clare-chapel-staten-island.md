## Holds Mass

The 8 November 2026 St. Clare bulletin (Bon Venture publisher 6686), page 1, schedules weekday Mass at 7:00, 8:15 and 9:00 AM and Saturday Mass at 8:15 and 9:00 AM "in the chapel"; Sunday and holy-day Masses are in the church. The 25 October and 4 October issues say the same. The parish's Architectural Highlights page (read 31 October) identifies the chapel as Saint Clare Chapel, the original 1921 wooden church rolled down Nelson Avenue in 1957 and "still in daily use", one of six separate buildings on the campus. It is not the private convent chapel. Public Mass six days a week in a separate building makes it a church of `st-clare-staten-island`.

## Name and address

Neither roster lists the chapel, so it has no roster link (Settled policy 3). The parish names it Saint Clare Chapel; no second source does, so `name_verified` is false. No source gives the chapel its own street number. The bulletin masthead's parish address, 110 Nelson Avenue (the rectory on the property diagram), is stored with a line-2 note, because the diagram puts the chapel on Nelson Avenue just west of the rectory, on the same campus. `address_verified` is false. Coordinates are left unset rather than copied from the main church: OpenStreetMap has only the main Church of Saint Clare (way 248316591, tagged 92 Nelson Avenue) and the school on this campus, and Nominatim searches for the chapel returned nothing (31 October). Geocoding 110 Nelson will place it within the campus, tens of metres from the building; a mapped footprint of the chapel would make the point exact.
