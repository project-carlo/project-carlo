## Holds Mass

Yes. The shrine's Mass Times page, read 9 November 2026, gives Saturday 9:00 AM (Latin), 4:00 PM (English vigil) and 5:30 PM (Spanish vigil); Sunday 8:00 AM (Spanish), 9:15 AM (English), 10:30 AM (Latin), 12:00 noon (Spanish), 1:00 PM (English) and 2:30 PM (Polish); weekdays 7:00 AM (English) and 9:00 AM (Latin); a Haitian Mass every third Saturday at 10:00 AM. The 1 February 2026 bulletin's intentions list the same Masses.

## Name and address

GCatholic (16234), the archdiocesan map (directory:4688), the shrine's site and its 1 February 2026 bulletin all give 448 East 116th Street, New York 10029. (The October research note about an East 115th Street church address had no source; every source read gives East 116th Street.) The parish calls itself the Pontifical Shrine of Our Lady of Mount Carmel; the church is recorded as Our Lady of Mount Carmel. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
