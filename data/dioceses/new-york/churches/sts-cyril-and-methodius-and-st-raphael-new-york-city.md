## Holds Mass

The parish homepage (read November 8, 2026) gives its standing schedule, every Sunday at 9:00 and 11:00 in Croatian, and lists its Christmas Eve and Christmas Day 2025 Masses; the archdiocese's place page (same day) gives Saturday 8:00 AM and Sunday 9:00 and 11:00 AM in Croatian. The newest bulletin found (6 April 2025) also printed Sunday 9:00 and 11:00 a.m.

## Name and address

The archdiocese map and place page give 502 West 41st Street with ZIP 10018; GCatholic gives 502 W 41st St., 10036, and the April 2025 bulletin masthead names the parish at 502 West 41st Street. The street address agrees across sources, so both flags are true. The ZIP is 10036, the ZIP of West 41st Street west of Tenth Avenue, as GCatholic gives; the archdiocese's 10018 was not followed. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code on the building.
