## Public Mass and publisher

The October 4 St. Mary-Saint James bulletin explicitly lists Our Lady of Mercy Chapel Sunday Mass at 9:30 am. The official directory separately locates this chapel at 977 River Road in Roseton, Town of Newburgh. One existing current masthead serves both places; no new parish row is needed.

## Identity

This is directory-only location 114825, not Our Lady of Mercy in the Bronx or Port Chester. Its coordinates are from the official directory, and exact two-source name/address verification is not claimed.
