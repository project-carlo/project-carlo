## Holds Mass

St. Peter's Parish holds its Saturday Vigil Mass at 4:00 pm in the Parish Auditorium (bulletin of November 8, 2026, page 2), which its website calls the School Gym ('Saturday (Vigil) School Gym 4:00pm' on the Mass Times page; 'Sunday Vigil (St. Peter's School Gym)' on the homepage, read 2026-11-08). Sunday and weekday Masses are at [Our Lady of the Rosary Chapel](our-lady-of-the-rosary-chapel-poughkeepsie.md).

This record merges two October 2026 open questions: the 'parish auditorium' venue (no address) and GCatholic's 'St. Peter, 6 Father Cody Plaza' (gcatholic:16217), which that session could not tie to a building. They are one place: the parish's own history (summarised on Wikipedia) says the parish moved to its Hyde Park campus in 1965, built a school and convent there and held parish Masses in the school chapel and auditorium; the bulletin puts the parish's mailing address at 6 Fr. Cody Plaza, the convent at 16 Fr. Cody Plaza and a chapel 'at St. Peter's School'. The school itself closed in 2020; the gym is still the vigil venue.
