## Holds Mass

Sacred Heart of Jesus, Highland Falls, lists a weekly Sunday 8:00 am Mass at Blessed Sacrament Chapel on its Mass Times page (read 2026-11-08, carrying the 32nd Sunday readings), and its Blessed Sacrament Chapel page says the chapel, on 9W in Fort Montgomery, is the parish's mission church, with Sunday Mass at 8 am and baptisms, funerals and weddings. The October 2026 session had seen only the wedding notice on the homepage and had not opened these two pages (the site then answered 403). The November 1, 2026 bulletin (eCatholic 6794, text read) does not name the chapel; its publisher is [Sacred Heart of Jesus](../parishes/sacred-heart-of-jesus-highland-falls.md), whose own site gives this schedule.

## Name and address

The archdiocese's directory (directory:107068) gives Blessed Sacrament Chapel, 794 United States Route 9W, Town of Highlands 10928; the parish site gives 'on 9W in Fort Montgomery' without a number. Fort Montgomery is the hamlet in the Town of Highlands. The street and name agree; the house number has a single source, so address_verified stays false. Coordinates are the directory's pin. GCatholic has no entry for the chapel.
