## Holds Mass

The October 11, 2026 bulletin, published before and read on October 8, page 1 gives Sunday 10 AM English/noon Spanish, Tuesday 7:15 PM and Friday noon Spanish at 119 Avenue B. Page 3 dates actual intentions October 11-16.

## Name and address

The masthead says The Parish of Saint Brigid, 119 Avenue B, New York, NY 10009. This is the physical St. Brigid church, not the former St. Emeric building on Avenue D. The directory's combined St. Brigid-St. Emeric title describes the canonical parish; use St. Brigid as the building name. No building-precise coordinates have been verified for this directory-only record.
