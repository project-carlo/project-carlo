## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish home page, read 8 November 2026, gives Sunday 9:30 AM (English), 11:00 AM (Spanish) and 12:30 PM (English), and daily Masses Monday to Saturday.

## Name and address

The sources give three numbers on one block of Tinton Avenue: 685 (the parish site header and the archdiocesan map, directory:3094), 683 (OpenStreetMap's church building) and 673 (GCatholic 16145). The parish and the diocese agree on 685, so that is recorded; the church and parish office share the block, and any of the three finds the building. Coordinates are the OSM church building, about 35 m from GCatholic's Plus Code.
