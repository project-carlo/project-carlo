## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's own bulletin for 25 October 2026 (eCatholic 32546, read 31 October) lists that Sunday's 9:30am, 11:30am and 1:00pm Mass intentions, 7:00AM Masses Monday to Friday and 9:00AM Saturday. The parish home page, read 8 October, gives the same Sunday times (11:30 Spanish, 1:00 Igbo). The 2015 canonical merger into Holy Rosary did not end Mass here.

## Name and address

GCatholic (16196) and the parish's own site both give 1531 East 233rd Street, Bronx 10466, and the name Nativity of Our Blessed Lady, so both flags are set. The official archdiocesan map has no separate entry for this building; its 'Holy Rosary - Nativity of the Blessed Lady' entry (directory:3918) is at 1510 Adee Avenue, which is Holy Rosary.
