## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish home page, read 9 November 2026, gives Saturday 6:30 PM (Spanish), Sunday 9 AM (Spanish), 11 AM (English) and 12:30 PM (Spanish), weekday Masses, and monthly special Masses with dates in November and December 2026.

## Name and address

GCatholic (16172) and the archdiocesan map (directory:4758) give 1914 Morris Avenue, but the parish's own site calls that the 'office door' and puts the 'main church door on Tremont Ave.'; ParishesOnline's org for the church gives 110 East Tremont Avenue. The church is on the corner, so 110 E Tremont Ave is recorded as the church entrance. Only ParishesOnline gives that number, so `address_verified` stays false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code on the building.
