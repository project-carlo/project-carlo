Yes. ## Name and address

The archdiocesan map (directory:4704, "St. Cecilia - Holy Agony Church") and Wikipedia's landmark article give 120 East 106th Street; GCatholic (16252) gives 125 East 105th Street, the other side of the same block. The church fronts East 106th Street, so 120 East 106th Street is recorded. Two independent sources agree on it, so `address_verified` is true.
