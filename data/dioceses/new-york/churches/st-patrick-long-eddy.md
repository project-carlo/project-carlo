## Holds Mass

The November 8, 2026 joint Holy Cross, St. Patrick, St. Mary, St. George and St. Francis bulletin (eCatholic 10462, page 1, read 2026-11-08 from a rendering because the schedule box is an image) gives Sunday 8:00 AM Mass at St. Patrick. Holy Cross's Mass Times page gave Sunday 8 AM at St. Patrick, Long Eddy on October 8, 2026. The bulletin's masthead lists St. Patrick Church among its five churches, and Holy Cross's Bulletins page now links this bulletin.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead gives St. Patrick Church, 18 Church St., Long Eddy; the archdiocese's directory (directory:100886) gives 20 Church Street, Town of Fremont (Long Eddy is the hamlet, ZIP 12760). Recorded the bulletin's 18, the parish's current own figure; the numbers are two doors apart and either finds the building. With the sources split, address_verified stays false. GCatholic has no separate entry for this church. Coordinates are the directory's pin, which is in Long Eddy.
