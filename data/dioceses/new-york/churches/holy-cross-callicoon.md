## Holds Mass

The November 8, 2026 joint bulletin (eCatholic 10462, page 1, read 2026-11-08 from a rendering because the schedule box is an image) gives Sunday 10:00 AM Mass at Holy Cross with confession at 9:30, Monday and Tuesday 8:30am at Holy Cross Church, and First Saturday 8:30 AM Mass at Holy Cross. Holy Cross's own Mass Times page gave the same Sunday 10 AM and weekday assignments on October 8, 2026.

Its publisher is the joint Holy Cross, St. Patrick, St. Mary, St. George and St. Francis bulletin: the masthead names all five churches and Holy Cross's own Bulletins page now lists that bulletin's issues. Holy Cross's old eCatholic 17760 archive stopped at June 14, 2026 and its issues already named the same five churches.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead gives Holy Cross Church, 9719 State Route 97, Callicoon; the archdiocese's directory (directory:4316) gives 9719 State Highway 97, Town of Delaware, the same road and number. GCatholic (gcatholic:16419) gives Route 97, Callicoon. The directory's pin (41.7911, -75.0737) sits about 2.5 km north of Callicoon; the coordinates kept are GCatholic's Plus Code, which falls in the hamlet.
