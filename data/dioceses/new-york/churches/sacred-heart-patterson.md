## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin, page 1, lists Sunday 10:30 am at Sacred Heart, 414
Haviland Drive, Patterson, NY 12563. That is the only Mass on the schedule for this church.

## Name and address

The bulletin says Sacred Heart at 414 Haviland Drive, Patterson, NY 12563. The parish site
footer says Sacred Heart Roman Catholic Church at that address. GCatholic says Church of the
Sacred Heart at 414 Haviland Dr., Patterson, New York 12563. The street, city and ZIP agree,
so both flags are true. I did not find an archdiocese place page that states this address;
the St. Lawrence page is titled for the pair and only prints the Brewster address.
