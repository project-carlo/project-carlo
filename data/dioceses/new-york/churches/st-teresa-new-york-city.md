## Holds Mass

The parish's bulletin sheet (https://stteresany.org/documents/2026/10/Bulletin.pdf, uploaded October 2026, read November 8) prints Saturday vigil 5:00 p.m. English; Sunday 10:00 a.m. English and 12:00 p.m. Español; Tuesday-Friday 8:30 a.m. Español; First Saturday 8:30 a.m. bilingual. The homepage on October 8 gave the same weekly times and a patronal Mass on October 15, 2026.

## Name and address

The parish sheet gives "Church of St. Teresa", 141 Henry Street, New York, NY 10002; the archdiocese map lists St. Teresa Church at 141 Henry Street, and GCatholic lists 141 Henry St. Sources agree, so both flags are true. Coordinates are the archdiocese map's pin.
