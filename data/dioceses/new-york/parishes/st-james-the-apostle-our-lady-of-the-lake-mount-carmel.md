## Publisher and current issue

The October 4, 2026 cover identifies one St. James the Apostle and Our Lady of the Lake/Mount Carmel issue with two separate building schedules. St. James is printed at 14 Gleneida Avenue, Carmel, Saturday 5 PM, Sunday 8/10 AM/noon. Our Lady of the Lake/Mount Carmel is printed at 1 Doherty Drive, Lake Carmel (Fair Street to Noonan Drive), Saturday 4 PM, Sunday 9/11 AM. Current official Mass-times page independently labels these times by church. Roster addresses 16 Gleneida Avenue and 27 Noonan Drive differ from the current printed addresses; current publication addresses are used without setting address_verified. Weekday St. James liturgies are explicitly in Chapel of Life, not automatically the main church.
