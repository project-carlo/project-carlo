## October 8, 2026 last-Mass audit

Read the signed four-page decree at https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/lx9z76st97pgk2oa9570i/Saint-Monica-Saint-Elizabeth-of-Hungary-Saint-Stephen-of-Hungary.pdf?rlkey=voz85kwzjpnv9nlom5n05lhf6&dl=1, linked by https://www.archny.org/parish-planning under Decrees 6/30/17. Page 3 reduces this specific building to non-sacred use, corroborating the existing explicit closed statement independently of the corporation merger. Page 4 dates the decree June 30, 2017 and makes it effective immediately; neither it nor the other pages identifies the last celebration of Mass. The decree consistently identifies this church, not St. Joseph Yorkville.

Exact final-Mass evidence: independently read https://www.cbsnews.com/newyork/news/final-mass-churches-merge/ on October 8, 2026. CBS New York published Stressful Times For New York Catholics As Churches Close, Parishes Merge on July 31, 2015 at 6:28 PM EDT. The second paragraph reports Joan Romanelli leaving the final Mass at St. Elizabeth of Hungary on East 83rd Street Friday morning. This is a report of an actual final celebration on the publication day, July 31, 2015, not the next-day merger or the 2017 relegation. last_mass is therefore 2015-07-31, superseding the October 7 unknown.
