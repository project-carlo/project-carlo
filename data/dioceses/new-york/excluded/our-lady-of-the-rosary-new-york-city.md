## Why it is excluded

The church of Our Lady of the Rosary, the Elizabeth Ann Seton shrine at 7 State Street, has been in the custody of the Sisters of Life since April 2023 (the responsible parish's own page). A visitor's report of 31 August 2025 (Society of St. Hugh of Cluny) says "the Seton Shrine is at the present time a private chapel of the Sisters of Life. No religious services open to the general public are provided", and The Tablet (2 June 2026) says the shrine "is open only on a limited basis" as the base of the Sisters' Visitation pregnancy mission. The responsible parish's Mass schedule and its 1 and 8 November 2026 bulletins list Masses only at St. Peter's and Our Lady of Victory. It is a religious community's private chapel, so `no-public-mass`; no closing date is known.

## What would change this

A public Mass at 7 State Street announced by the St. Peter-Our Lady of Victory bulletin or schedule, or by the Sisters of Life.
