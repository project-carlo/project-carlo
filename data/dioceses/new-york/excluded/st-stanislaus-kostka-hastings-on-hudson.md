## Physical church closure

The successor parish?s own St. Stanislaus history says the church building closed after roof damage in October 2005, that parishioners attended Mass at St. Matthew while awaiting reopening, and that the January 2007 decision closed the church and suppressed the parish. It then describes the altar, tabernacle and other furnishings transferred to St. Matthew and states ?Even though the church is no more?. This is explicit building evidence, not an inference from the merger or a missing timetable.

The history does not give the exact last Mass day; no last_mass date is invented. The former 52 Main Street roster identity is retained. The active St. Matthew/Our Lady of Perpetual Help publisher is not changed by this exclusion.
