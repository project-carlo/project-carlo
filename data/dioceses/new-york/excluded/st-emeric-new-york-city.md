## Physical closure and current worship

The church-operated page is entitled A History of the Former Church of Saint Emeric and heads its history Saint Emeric Church (1949 - 2013). Read October 8, 2026, this identifies the building as former and dates the end of its service; the year is not an exact last-Mass date. The November 4, 2020 relegation decree at https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/v6hlw62n5cuk0rpcne2af/Saint-Emeric-Church-New-York.pdf?rlkey=32z5y61syh3fm8ntdjwl0c1ie&st=94zuzqut&dl=1, visually read in all four pages October 8, subsequently reduced Saint Emeric to profane use effective immediately (page 4, clause 1). The actual October 11, 2026 Saint Brigid bulletin at https://files.ecatholic.com/34038/bulletins/20261011.pdf, already available October 8, page 1 identifies 119 Avenue B and prints its Sunday 10 AM English/noon Spanish schedule. Taken together, the former-building history, completed relegation and present community worship establish closure of the Avenue D venue, rather than deriving it from the combined canonical name or a decree alone.
