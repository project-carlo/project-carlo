## Why excluded

The building-specific May 10, 2023 decree, page 4, explicitly relegates Saint Margaret Mary Church and takes effect immediately. Its exact last Mass is unknown. Closure is not inferred from the merger or the current schedule.

## Why the bulletin still names it

Current October 4 and October 11, 2026 issues and historical October 12 and October 19, 2025 issues retain the joint parish identity. September 27, 2026 mentions the centennial of Saint Margaret Mary Parish and a trip to Saint Patrick's Cathedral, not a Mass at 560 Lincoln Avenue. These references do not revoke the church relegation. One shared current bulletin remains the publisher record; St. Christopher remains its public-Mass venue.
