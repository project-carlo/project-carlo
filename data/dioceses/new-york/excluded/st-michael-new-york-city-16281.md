## Why it is excluded

The Russian Byzantine Catholic chapel of St. Michael, a ground-floor room in the former chancery building at 266 Mulberry Street (behind Old St. Patrick's), held its last liturgy in February 2019. The Society of St. Hugh of Cluny's report "The Last Liturgy at St. Michael's", posted 5 February 2019 by someone who attended, describes the final liturgy on "The Sunday after the Feast of the Presentation (Candlemas) – Zacchaeus Sunday" (3 February 2019) and says "By the last Sunday many of the icons already had been taken down and packed." The community's own site (read November 8, 2026) says it "is currently in residence at the Church of St. Catherine of Siena, which is located at 411 East 68th Street", with Sunday Divine Liturgy at noon there, and its Christmas 2025 services were held there. The 8 November 2026 bulletin of St. Patrick's Old Cathedral, next door, does not mention St. Michael's or a Byzantine liturgy. `last_mass` is the month, 2019-02; the day (3 February) comes from the liturgical date in the report.

## What would change this

A return of the community or any public liturgy to 266 Mulberry Street. The community itself worships in St. Catherine of Siena's church building, which is a separate place.
