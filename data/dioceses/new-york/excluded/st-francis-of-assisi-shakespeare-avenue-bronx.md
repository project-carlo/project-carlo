## Decision

This former Shakespeare Avenue church is distinct from the Baychester Avenue Francis Chapel. The October 4, 2026 Sacred Heart-Saint Francis bulletin retains the canonical title but gives the Sacred Heart church at 1253 Shakespeare Avenue and rectory at 1257. Its religious-education office at 1255 does not establish Mass at the former Francis church.

The archdiocesan decree linked from https://www.archny.org/parish-planning was visually read October 8, 2026. PDF page 4, operative paragraph 1 explicitly reduces this physical church to profane but not sordid use. The same page says the decree is effective immediately and is signed 2018-11-06. No reopening evidence was found in the inspected current successor bulletin. This is a completed building-specific relegation, not inference from a canonical merger.

The exact final Mass is unknown; the effective decree date is not used as last_mass. Original roster/address evidence is retained. No bulletin publishers are merged or otherwise redefined by this exclusion.
