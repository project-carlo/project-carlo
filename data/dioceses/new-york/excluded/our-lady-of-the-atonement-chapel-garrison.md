## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of the Atonement Chapel, 40 Franciscan Way, Graymoor, Garrison, is the Franciscan Friars of the Atonement's chapel, with a public Mass every Sunday at 11:00 AM (the Friars' Mass page, read 8 November 2026: "Join the Friars for Mass at Our Lady of the Atonement Chapel"). So it has public Mass, but it publishes no bulletin: the Friars' site links no bulletin or newsletter, only an impact report, a Holy Week 2026 event list, a novena calendar and maps. It was never a parish row, and the nearest parish, Our Lady of Loretto in Cold Spring, does not list Graymoor in its bulletin. It is in neither roster; it was found while settling the St. Francis Chapel (GCatholic 37951), which is [excluded with no public Mass](st-francis-garrison.md).

## What would change this

A bulletin or weekly schedule that the pipeline could read: on the Friars' site, or a parish bulletin that starts listing the Graymoor Mass.
