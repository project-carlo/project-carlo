## October 8, 2026 follow-up

The primary Yonkers IDA application dated March 22, 2024, PDF page 15, explicitly identifies the historic Ethan Flagg House and Blessed Sacrament Monastery at 21-23 Park Avenue as the two buildings proposed for residential rehabilitation. Its exact words include "Currently, the Site's existing buildings are predominately vacant and unused" (the PDF's apostrophe has an extraction-encoding artifact). Source: https://yonkersida.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Abbey-on-the-Park-Application-Redacted-3-22-2024.pdf, read October 8, 2026. The IDA's project page, https://yonkersida.com/21-23-park-ave-the-abbey/, reports "As of May 2025, 0% Complete"; this is a dated progress statement, not a claim of completion in 2026. This strengthens the building-specific secular-use identification and corrects any impression that GCatholic's older medical-center description establishes the present tenant. Neither source identifies the final public Mass. The warning remains deliberately undated rather than borrowing a housing approval or construction date.
