## Physical site and closure

The responsible parish history explains that the Elizabeth Street church was sold after falling into disrepair; worship moved to the Holy Name Centre chapel at 18 Bleecker Street. Both the centre and Loreto parish closed in 2011. Nearby parishes welcomed remaining worshippers. The property reopened in 2015 as the Sheen Center arts venue, not a Loreto Mass site. Records are at Most Holy Redeemer-Nativity. This is the Manhattan mission, not the Brooklyn church. No exact last-Mass date is supplied.
