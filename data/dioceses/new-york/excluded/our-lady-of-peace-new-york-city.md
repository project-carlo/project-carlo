## Present use, not physical closure

The present occupying community identifies itself at https://www.stmarystmarkmanhattan.com/ as St. Mary & St. Mark Coptic Orthodox Church. Its Location & Schedule page, read October 8, 2026, places liturgical worship at 241 East 62nd Street and gives its donation address as 237 East 62nd Street, matching the former Our Lady of Peace property range. It describes Divine Liturgy, service books, an agape meal and its current priest. The building remains a place of worship, but not a Catholic venue. Therefore reason is not-catholic rather than closed or other-jurisdiction. The Holy Family-St. John bulletin retains Our Lady of Peace in its canonical masthead; that historical name does not make this Orthodox building a Catholic Mass site. No date of the last Catholic Mass is invented.
