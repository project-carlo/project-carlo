## Why excluded

The November 30, 2017 primary decree, pages 3-4, explicitly relegates the church of Saint Mary of the Assumption, effective immediately. The exact last-Mass date is unknown. This is affirmative physical-building evidence, not an inference from a merger, address or schedule omission.

## Distinguishing the decrees

The official Parish Planning index has another similarly named PDF dated May 26, 2018. Its operative paragraph concerns the chapel of Christ the King, not Saint Mary of the Assumption; it must not be substituted as this church's closure source. Likewise the January 4, 2020 Saint Mary decree concerns Bay Street, a different church. The current October 4, 2026 joint bulletin does not establish reopening at 2230 Richmond Terrace.
