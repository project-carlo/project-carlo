## Decision

GCatholic explicitly labels this a Former Church and states closed 2017.06.30. This is not inferred from a canonical merger or directory omission; June 30, 2017 is the reported closure date, not an independently established last-Mass day.

## Recheck

Obtain an explicit current physical-site reopening statement if later sources differ. The continued use of a saint's name in a combined parish is not evidence that this building reopened.

## October 8, 2026 last-Mass audit

Read the signed four-page decree at https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/d14me3mgf7tn0jf1o3qnl/Holy-Name-of-Jesus-Saint-Gregory-the-Great.pdf?rlkey=o3jxr62hwchq3o4d980s1hde9&dl=1, linked by https://www.archny.org/parish-planning under Decrees 6/30/17. Page 3 reduces this specific building to non-sacred use, corroborating the existing explicit closed statement independently of the corporation merger. Page 4 dates the decree June 30, 2017 and makes it effective immediately; neither it nor the other pages identifies the last celebration of Mass. last_mass remains unknown, not the relegation date. Its pastor requested relegation June 16 and the council considered it June 19, 2017. Neither event is a Mass date.

Follow-up final-Mass search on October 8 used St. Gregory the Great, Manhattan, and last Mass. Results for the Downside monastic community are a different place. The successor parish history at https://hnj.nyc/history explicitly says St. Gregory the Great closed in 2017, but supplies no final-Mass day. Keep last_mass unresolved; retain the already-read specific-building decree rather than assigning a school closure, merger, or relegation date.
