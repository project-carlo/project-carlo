## Why it is excluded

The responsible parish explicitly says on its official history page, read October 10, 2026, under "2017 to Today": "Due to the changing demographics and shortage of clergy, the churches of St. James (32 James St.) and St. Joseph (5 Monroe St.) were closed and merged with Transfiguration." This is affirmative physical-building closure evidence, not a deduction from the merger or an omitted schedule. Its current Mass page identifies 29 Mott Street and lists Saturday 6 PM and Sunday 9 AM, 10:30 AM, noon and 1:30 PM. Neither former church is assigned as a present worship site.

## Date and publisher

The history groups the change under "2017 to Today" but supplies no exact final Mass date; last_mass is deliberately unset. The related publisher is Transfiguration, not an invented independent bulletin for this closed building.

## What would change this

A later responsible-parish announcement of resumed public Mass at this exact building, including occasional liturgies, would reopen the classification.
