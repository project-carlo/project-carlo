## Completed demolition

The former parishioner campaign site reports that Our Lady of Vilnius on Broome Street was demolished in 2015. Its April 25, 2015 post announced a final gathering in front of the condemned building for April 26 and now explicitly records completed demolition. This is physical-site evidence, not a canonical merger or a presumption from a dead website. No exact last-Mass day is inferred from the February 26, 2007 lockout date.
