## Decision

This is the former Bathgate Avenue church, not the current Saint Simon Stock venue.

On October 8, 2026, the actual archdiocesan decree linked from https://www.archny.org/parish-planning was visually read. PDF page 3, operative paragraph 1 begins with the quoted church name and explicitly reduces that named physical church to profane but not sordid use. The final page states that the decree is effective immediately. This building-specific cessation of sacred use is stronger than a canonical merger or absence from a schedule. No reopening evidence was found in the inspected current parish channels.

The retained roster identity/address evidence distinguishes this building from the surviving parish worship site. The decree date is not an exact final-Mass date; last_mass remains unset. Access failures were not used as closure evidence. Publisher records are not merged by this decision.
