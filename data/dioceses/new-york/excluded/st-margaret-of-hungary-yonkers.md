## Explicit completed closure, not a proposed merger

The February 11, 2007 New York Times report expressly says St. Margaret of Hungary in Yonkers was already closed and that most parishioners had moved to nearby St. Matthew. It distinguishes that completed fact from other Westchester churches merely scheduled to close. The March 28, 2006 archdiocesan proposal supplies historical context but is not the dispositive evidence.

The earlier GCatholic/Discover Mass identity points to 76 Locust Hill Avenue. The surviving Discover Mass listing has no Mass times or issues; neither its generic welcome sentence nor its continued existence overturns the explicit completed-closure reporting. No exact last-Mass date is given, and February 11, 2007 is not fabricated as the last Mass day. No unverified housing-development claim is relied upon. Earlier October 7 evidence dates are retained.
