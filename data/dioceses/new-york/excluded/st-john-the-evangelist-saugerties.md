## Why it is excluded

The current official St. John the Evangelist site explicitly says NO MASSES AT THIS CHURCH LOCATION and points readers to St. Mary of the Snow/St. Joseph/St. John for current bulletins. Its church-location panel identifies St. John at 915 Route 212 and repeats NO MASSES AT THIS LOCATION. This is not a closure inferred from the merger or an archive ending July 7, 2024.

The current shared schedule instead assigns services to St. Mary of the Snow at 36 Cedar Street and St. Joseph at 38 Glasco Turnpike. The record says no-public-mass because the site does not establish sale, demolition, permanent relegation or a last Mass date. The historical 2016-2024 St. John issues do not override the explicit present notice.

## What would change this

A new official notice reopening public Mass at 915 Route 212 would remove this exclusion.
