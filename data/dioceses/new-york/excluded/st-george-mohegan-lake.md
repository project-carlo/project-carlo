## October 8, 2026 follow-up

Rob Yasinsac's building history, published April 25, 2012 and read October 8, 2026, explicitly says "St. George's Church was last used for services in 1983" and describes its sale by St. Elizabeth Ann Seton parish and eventual winery conversion: https://www.hudsonvalleyruins.org/rob/?p=909. This is a building historian's account, not an exact final-Mass register; the year does not supply a day for `last_mass`.

The old "now winery" description is historical, not a verified current business. Daily Voice's January 30, 2025 report, https://dailyvoice.com/article/last-call-brewery-in-century-old-church-announces-closure-in-mohegan-lake/, read October 8, identifies Argonne Rose Brewing Company at 1715 East Main Street inside the former St. George's Church and reports its planned February 9, 2025 final day of business. A business shutdown does not mean resumed worship, and is not a final-Mass date. An attempted old brewery domain redirected to unrelated content; that redirect is not used as parish evidence. The affirmative church-to-commercial conversion remains the reason for exclusion; no newer tenant or precise last liturgy is asserted.
