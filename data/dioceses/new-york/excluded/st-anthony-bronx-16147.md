## Decision

The October 4 Frances bulletin names Anthony but assigns worship to Frances and Francis Chapel. The separate operative building-relegation decree settles Richardson Avenue, not the parish merger or missing Mass box. This does not affect Saint Anthony on Commonwealth Avenue.

The archdiocesan decree linked from https://www.archny.org/parish-planning was visually read October 8, 2026. PDF page 4, operative paragraph 1 explicitly reduces this physical church to profane but not sordid use. The same page says the decree is effective immediately and is signed 2018-05-26. No reopening evidence was found in the inspected current successor bulletin. This is a completed building-specific relegation, not inference from a canonical merger.

The exact final Mass is unknown; the effective decree date is not used as last_mass. Original roster/address evidence is retained. No bulletin publishers are merged or otherwise redefined by this exclusion.
