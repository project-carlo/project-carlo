## Why excluded from this archdiocese

The building at 199 North Broadway is currently St. John Paul II Maronite Catholic Church at the Immaculate Conception. The official Eparchy of Saint Maron directory and the parish independently identify this exact address and jurisdiction. It holds public Maronite liturgy and Roman Rite Masses, so it is NOT closed or without public Mass. It belongs in other-jurisdiction, not as an undecided St. Teresa member.

## Source disagreement

On October 8 the parish site gives traditional Latin Mass Sunday 4 PM; the eparchial directory gives 2 PM. No preferred time is silently chosen, and that discrepancy does not affect the independently agreed building and jurisdiction. The parish lists ordinary-form Saturday 5 PM/Sunday 10 AM and Maronite Sunday 11:30 AM.

## What would change this

An official jurisdictional reassignment of this physical building. A Roman Rite liturgy occurring here does not itself transfer the Maronite parish into the Archdiocese of New York.
