## Why it is excluded

The archdiocese relegated St. Mary Church, 1101 Bay Street, to profane use: the decree signed January 4, 2020 (page 4, paragraph 1) says the church "is hereby reduced to profane but not sordid use", effective immediately. A relegated building is no longer a church, so it has no Latin public Mass; the parish's current bulletin (4 October 2026) schedules only St. Joseph and Immaculate Conception. The decree gives no last-Mass date, so `last_mass` is left empty rather than set to the decree date.

## Eastern mission

The 8 October session kept this open because GCatholic once listed the Syro-Malabar Blessed Kunjachan mission at 1101 Bay Street. Its current public notices place it at 463 (office) and 466 Tompkins Avenue (its 11 October 2026 feast at St. Joseph). Even if it still met at Bay Street, an Eastern Catholic community is outside this Latin dataset and is already recorded as `excluded/blessed-kunjachan-mission-staten-island` (other-jurisdiction). So the building's possible Eastern use doesn't change this record.

## What would change this

A later decree restoring the building to sacred use, or a Latin parish bulletin scheduling Mass at 1101 Bay Street.
