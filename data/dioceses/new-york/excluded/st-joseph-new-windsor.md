## Why it is excluded

On October 8, 2026 the official St. Marianne Cope Mass/Confession Schedule explicitly states, in its St. Joseph Church panel, that Masses are not currently being celebrated at St. Joseph Church. This is positive building-specific evidence, not absence from a timetable or the 2015 parish merger.

The site gives 4 Saint Joseph Place; GCatholic uses 6. The October 4, 2026 bulletin still prints St. Joseph as a contact/center alongside St. Thomas, but assigns its public Mass schedule to St. Thomas. The finding is present no-public-mass, not a claim that the St. Joseph building is sold, demolished or permanently relegated. No last Mass date is supplied.

## What would change this

A parish announcement restarting public Mass at the St. Joseph building would remove this exclusion; the current parish schedule is the concrete check.
