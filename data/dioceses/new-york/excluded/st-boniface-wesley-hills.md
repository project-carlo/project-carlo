## Decision and current checks

The physical building at 5 Willow Tree Road is now Holy Family Syro-Malabar Catholic Church, an active parish of the St. Thomas Syro-Malabar Catholic Eparchy of Chicago. The current parish homepage gives this exact address and Sunday 10 AM Malayalam Mass; the eparchial directory independently lists it. Official history expressly connects the former St. Boniface building to the move, purchase and September 8, 2020 erection of the Holy Family parish in that eparchy. Therefore this old New York roster entry is excluded as other-jurisdiction, NOT closed or no-public-mass. The historical Latin community merger is not the reason for exclusion; current named jurisdiction at the building is. No last-Mass date is asserted, and this does not purport to prove whether an occasional Latin liturgy occurs.
