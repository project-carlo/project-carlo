## Why it is excluded

The October 11, 2026 Christ in the City bulletin was already published and downloaded October 8. Page 3 explicitly states under ST. JOHN THE BAPTIST that the church will remain temporarily closed until further notice for safety, and that construction has begun. This is a building-specific current cessation statement, not an inference from its absence in a schedule.

The same issue still prints the usual St. John/Holy Cross schedule on page 1. The specific current suspension overrides that unchanged template: it would be misleading to map St. John as presently holding Mass. The existing Holy Cross-St. John publisher remains active through Holy Cross. This exclusion is temporary and does not assert sale, demolition, permanent canonical closure or a last-Mass date.

## What would change this

A dated reopening notice from Christ in the City or the parish office, 212-564-9070, specifically resuming public Mass in the St. John building, would restore its church record under the existing shared publisher. Recheck the next current bulletin for an affirmative reopening; do not restore it merely because the standing schedule is reprinted.

On 2026-11-09 the November 8 issue (and those of October 18, October 25 and November 1) still printed "Is still temporarily closed *until further notice* as your safety is being considered" under ST. JOHN THE BAPTIST, so the exclusion stands. Next reading after 2026-12-07.
