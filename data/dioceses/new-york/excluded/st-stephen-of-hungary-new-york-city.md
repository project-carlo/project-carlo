## Why it is excluded

St. Stephen of Hungary, 414 East 82nd Street, closed as a church in 2015, when its parish was merged into St. Monica. Wikipedia's article (read November 8, 2026) says the High Mass for the Feast of St. Stephen on August 23, 2015 was the parish's last major event and "The final Mass was held on August 30."; a June 14, 2025 interview in Hungarian Conservative with the lay chairman of the Hungarian Catholic community calls it "St. Stephen's Roman Catholic church, which was closed in 2015" and says the community now meets at St. Joseph's in Yorkville. The merged parish's current bulletin (8 November 2026, ParishesOnline 03/4032, page 2) keeps the St. Stephen name in its title but says "Our church is located at 413 E. 79th St" and lists Masses only there. Its history page (October 10) says St. Stephen's legacy continues through St. Stephen of Hungary School. The last Mass date, 2015-08-30, is Wikipedia's, and the 2025 interview agrees on the year.

## What would change this

A St. Monica-St. Elizabeth-St. Stephen bulletin or schedule listing a Mass at the East 82nd Street church open to the public (school Masses alone would not count).
