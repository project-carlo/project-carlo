## Why it is excluded

St. Andrew's, 20 Cardinal Hayes Place, was given to the Sisters of Life as a residence in 2018 (Aleteia, 8 May 2018: "the new residence at the Church of St. Andrew"), and Wikipedia says the church "was closed to the public when the Sisters of Life were given the building". A Mass-times listing gives "No public Mass", and the responsible parish, St. Peter-Our Lady of Victory, schedules every Mass at St. Peter's or Our Lady of Victory: its schedule page and its 1 and 8 November 2026 bulletins never mention St. Andrew. No source gives a final public Mass, so `last_mass` is empty. Whether the Sisters still live there is not known (a 2025 visitor's report on the Seton Shrine says the Sisters had been moved between several archdiocesan buildings); either way no public Mass is offered.

## What would change this

A Mass at St. Andrew announced in the St. Peter-Our Lady of Victory bulletin or on spcolr.org/mass-times.
