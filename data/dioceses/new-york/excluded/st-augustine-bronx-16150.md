## Decision

The former Saint Augustine church at 1183 Franklin Avenue was demolished for Saint Augustine Terrace. On October 8, 2026, the completed-project accounts of the building architect and landscape architect were read. Terrain explicitly says the church was demolished, identifies the former Catholic church site, names the Archdiocese as client and marks the project completed. Magnusson Architecture and Planning describes the existing 112-unit housing on the former church site.

This explicit completed demolition settles the physical building, unlike the surviving Saint Augustine-Our Lady of Victory parish title or current Our Lady of Victory Mass schedule. No publisher boundary is inferred from the parish merger. No exact last-Mass date is given by these sources, so last_mass remains unset. Original roster evidence distinguishes Franklin Avenue from the active Our Lady of Victory church on Webster Avenue.
