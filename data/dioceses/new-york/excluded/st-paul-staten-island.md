## Why it is excluded

No current source shows public Mass at the St. Paul chapel. The 2016 amended decree merging St. Paul into St. Peter (signed July 26, 2016, page 2) keeps it "open for public and private worship" with only dedication-anniversary and patronal-feast Masses and others at the pastor's discretion. Every weekly parish bulletin from 28 December 2025 to 8 November 2026 (45 readable issues; eCatholic 6605) was searched for "Paul" and "Clinton": St. Paul appears only in the parish's name and footer, in homilies about the Apostle and in people's names. The 25 January 2026 issue, on the Conversion of St. Paul, notes the Sunday liturgy takes precedence and schedules no Mass at the chapel; the 28 June issue's weekday Masses for the feast of Sts. Peter and Paul are the parish's usual ones. The parish schedule covers Our Lady of Good Counsel and St. Peter only; the 11 October 2026 rededication was at St. Peter. The official archdiocesan map does not list the chapel. Discover Mass has a stale listing with no Mass times.

## Judgment

This is a judgment call, not a closure: no source dates a last Mass or relegates the building, and the decree's allowance for occasional Mass still stands (the separate Assumption church was relegated in 2024; St. Paul was not). Reason `other` is used rather than `closed`. The dataset tracks places with public Mass now, and a building with no Mass announced across ten months, including its patronal feast, is not one. GCatholic lists it as a chapel; Wikipedia says St. Paul's services moved to the school's auditorium on Clinton Avenue after the old church was demolished in 1963, which fits a chapel inside the school complex. No `last_mass` is set because no source gives one.

## What would change this

A bulletin notice of a public Mass at St. Paul or 145 Clinton Avenue (the January 2027 issues around the 25 January feast are the next test) makes it a church of `our-lady-of-good-counsel-st-peter-st-paul-assumption-staten-island`; a relegation decree or dated closure notice changes the reason to `closed`.
