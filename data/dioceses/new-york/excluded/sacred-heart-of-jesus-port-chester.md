## Physical closure and demolition

The August 16, 2025 Greenwich Time report explicitly locates the demolished church at 229 Willett Avenue. It states that Sacred Heart closed in 2015, was deconsecrated and consolidated in 2017, subsequently sold to developers, and demolished in summer 2025. The record?s older Willet spelling denotes this same roster address; the source spells Willett. This physical demolition evidence settles the former building even if an old MassTimes/Discover Mass listing still survives. No exact last Mass date is supplied. Do not confuse it with an unrelated Sacred Heart adoration chapel or attach St. John Bosco?s current Our Lady of Mercy/Corpus Christi services here.
