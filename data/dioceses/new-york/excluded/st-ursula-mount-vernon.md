## Decision

GCatholic explicitly labels this a Former Church and states closed 2017.06.30. This is not inferred from a canonical merger or directory omission; June 30, 2017 is the reported closure date, not an independently established last-Mass day.

## Recheck

Obtain an explicit current physical-site reopening statement if later sources differ. The continued use of a saint's name in a combined parish is not evidence that this building reopened.

## October 8, 2026 last-Mass audit

Read the four-page decree at https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/x44yjk5fj8nyptr3667t5/Saint-Peter-and-Paul-Saint-Ursula.pdf?rlkey=3d0yy0050v1fqh0c1b9tziid7&dl=1, linked from https://www.archny.org/parish-planning under Decrees 6/30/17. Page 3 orders the specific worship building reduced to non-sacred use, rather than merely merging a parish corporation; this corroborates the prior explicit closed statement. Page 4 dates the instrument June 30, 2017 and makes it effective immediately. None of its pages dates a final celebration of Mass; last_mass remains unknown, not the instrument date. The decree identifies St. Ursula in Mount Vernon, not the surviving Saints Peter and Paul building.

Follow-up final-Mass search on October 8 used St. Ursula, Mount Vernon, and final Mass/July 2015. Daily Voice's July 27, 2015 account explicitly dates a final Mass at St. Denis in Yonkers, but lists St. Ursula only among parish mergers; it does not date a St. Ursula liturgy. The accessible July 31 News 12 Mount Vernon final-Mass item did not identify the church. Neither is assigned to this building. last_mass remains unresolved; the existing St. Ursula decree supplies primary building-specific corroboration.
