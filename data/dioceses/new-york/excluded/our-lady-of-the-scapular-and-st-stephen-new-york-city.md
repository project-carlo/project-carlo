## Why it is excluded

City Limits (31 December 2018) reports that "St. Stephen's was closed by the Archdiocese of New York as part of its Making All Things New restructuring initiative", that the building has "sat in silence ... since the parish merged with another in 2015", and that the archdiocese has relegated it to "profane use but not sordid use". The parish merged into Our Saviour on 1 August 2015 (GCatholic); `last_mass` is the year the sources give. Today the responsible parish's home page says Masses are celebrated at Our Saviour and the Sacred Hearts chapel only, and its 8 November 2026 bulletin does not mention St. Stephen. The Renwick church runs through the block from East 28th Street (149 East 28th, per Wikipedia) to East 29th Street (the roster's 142 East 29th, the 1865 extension); the Epiphany School uses part of the property.

## What would change this

A Mass at St. Stephen announced by Our Saviour's bulletin or schedule.
