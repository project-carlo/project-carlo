## Why it is excluded

GCatholic, read October 7, 2026, explicitly says "History: parish merged with St. Brendan 2015.08.01; closed 2017.06.30" for 3519 Bainbridge Avenue. The closure statement, not merger, supports exclusion. Brendan retains Ann in its parish title but schedules Mass at Brendan. The October 8 audit below corroborates the building closure with the archdiocesan decree and records July 26, 2015 as the reported closing Mass from Norwood News, with the later-exception caveat retained. This date is not inferred from the closure date.
