## Why it is excluded

The Friars of the Atonement's 1911 St. Francis Chapel at Graymoor, 1 Little Flower Square, has no public Mass schedule. The Friars' Mass page (read 8 November 2026) lists a single public Mass, "every Sunday at 11:00am" at Our Lady of the Atonement Chapel, 40 Franciscan Way. Their Jubilee Year of St. Francis page (read 8 November) invites visitors to pray in the St. Francis Chapel and offers groups a Mass they arrange: "We are happy to help you arrange a Mass for your group in one of our chapels, including our St. Francis Chapel." A Mass booked by a visiting group is not a public Mass the site could list. The monthly Prayers for Unity in the Franciscan Sisters' St. Francis Chapel is a different chapel and a prayer service, not Mass.

Our Lady of the Atonement Chapel, which does hold the public Sunday Mass, is recorded separately as [an exclusion with no bulletin](our-lady-of-the-atonement-chapel-garrison.md).

## What would change this

A scheduled public Mass in the St. Francis Chapel on the Friars' Mass page (for example, if the Sunday Mass moves there).
