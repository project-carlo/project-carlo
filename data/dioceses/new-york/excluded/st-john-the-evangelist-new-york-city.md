## Why it is excluded

St. John the Evangelist was the church inside the New York Catholic Center, 1011 First Avenue (entrance 348 East 55th Street). The parish was merged into Holy Family by decree of 20 May 2024 and the church relegated to profane use in August 2024, to stay open for worship only until the building's sale closed or the archdiocese moved out, whichever came later (St. Hugh of Cluny, quoting the decree). The building was sold in October 2024; the archdiocese held a final Mass for its staff "one final time at St. John the Evangelist Church" on Wednesday 28 May 2025 and moved to 488 Madison Avenue on 9 June 2025 (The Good Newsroom, 2 June 2025). By 28 July 2025 the door carried a "Please join us in our new home" notice and the facade's Catholic symbols had been removed. The joint parish's 8 November 2026 bulletin gives a single schedule at Holy Family. `last_mass` is 28 May 2025, the last Mass a source records there.

## What would change this

Nothing likely: the building has been sold and vacated. The stjohnevannyc.com site still shows the 348 East 55th Street header, but it is the joint parish's site and lists no Mass there.
