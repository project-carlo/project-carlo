## Why it is excluded

The actual Church of Saint Peter, Kingston, relegation decree explicitly removes this church from sacred use. PDF page 4 states that the church is reduced to profane but not sordid use, effective immediately, and bears November 4, 2020 as its signing date. That is a building-specific relegation, not the 2026 canonical merger.

The September 16, 2020 pastor request on page 3 cites the repair burden. The decree authorizes sale negotiations but does not prove a sale occurred, and it does not give the last Mass date; no last_mass is invented. The current October 4, 2026 St. Mary/Sacred Heart timetable is consistent with this finding but is not used as closure evidence. St. Peter Rosendale belongs to a different active publisher and building.

## What would change this

An official reversal of the November 4, 2020 relegation and a current building-specific public Mass announcement at 93 Wurts Street would warrant reopening the record.
