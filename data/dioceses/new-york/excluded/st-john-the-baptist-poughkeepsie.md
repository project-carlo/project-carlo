## Affirmative reuse, not missing schedule

The National Trust's New York historic-tax-credit report, downloaded and read October 10, 2026, PDF page 35, explicitly identifies the Church of St. John the Baptist at 3 Grand Street in Poughkeepsie as a 2025 Commercial rehabilitation. This is building-specific secular-reuse evidence, not a decision inferred from omission in another parish's bulletin or from the 2007 consolidation plan. The original GCatholic street-only Union Street address is retained; the newer report identifies the former church by dedication, city and numbered Grand Street address in that historic neighborhood.

No exact final-Mass day is supplied by that report. Historical web search leads disagree between 1966 and 2007, and a 2007 closing announcement alone cannot establish when the final liturgy occurred. No speculative last_mass is stored. A current Catholic reopening or actual building-specific public-Mass notice would require reconsideration; none was established. This decision concerns this Poughkeepsie building, not Manhattan St. John the Baptist's temporary suspension.
