## Why it is excluded

St. John Neumann has no public Mass that any current source shows. The 2017 decree that merged it into St. Joseph-St. Thomas (signed April 29, 2017, page 4) keeps the building "open for public and private worship" but gives it no regular Mass, only Mass on its dedication anniversary and patronal feast and others at the pastor's discretion. Every weekly parish bulletin from 4 January to 8 November 2026 (45 issues; eCatholic 6604) was searched: "Neumann" appears only in the parish's name, footer and headings. The week of its patronal feast, Monday 5 January 2026, the bulletin lists Monday Masses at St. Thomas (7 and 8 am) and St. Joseph (9 am) and none at Neumann. The official archdiocesan parish map lists the parish's St. Joseph and St. Thomas churches and no Neumann location, and the parish homepage's 10-11 October 2026 celebrant list (8 October session) names only St. Joseph and St. Thomas. A third-party Mass-times site lists it as "St. John Neumann - CLOSED".

## Judgment

This is a judgment call, not a closure: no source dates a last Mass or relegates the building, and the decree's allowance for occasional Mass still stands. Reason `other` is used rather than `closed` for that reason. The dataset tracks places with public Mass now; a building whose only permitted Masses were not announced on the one feast we could check, and that appears in no 2026 schedule, is not one. No `last_mass` is set because no source gives one.

## What would change this

A bulletin notice of a public Mass at St. John Neumann or 1380 Arthur Kill Road (the January 2027 issues around the 5 January feast are the next test), which would make it a church of `st-joseph-st-thomas-st-john-neumann-staten-island`; or a relegation decree or dated closure notice, which would change the reason to `closed`.
