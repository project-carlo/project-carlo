## Why it is excluded

GCatholic 60864, the "Chapel of the Sacred Heart" at Plus Code 87H823Q9+F3, is the Dominican Sisters of Sparkill's 1914 Sacred Heart Chapel on the Dominican Convent grounds, 175 Route 340 (the Sisters' centenary journal and site name it so). The Sisters' page for it, read 8 November 2026, says: "This sacred space is currently unavailable for public use." It was their Motherhouse chapel from 1914 to 1967; their daily Eucharist is now in Our Lady of the Rosary Chapel, a community chapel beside the convent. Since 30 August 2026 the chapel has been lent to St. Thekla Malankara Orthodox Church, which holds its Sunday Holy Qurbana there (Sisters' news item, read 8 November). So no Catholic Mass is celebrated publicly there.

Our Lady of the Rosary Chapel is described only as the Sisters' community chapel, with no public Mass times, and gets no record of its own. The New York Oratory (GCatholic 60865) on the same campus is a separate open question, [St. Philip Neri](../open/st-philip-neri-sparkill.md).

## What would change this

The Sisters reopening Sacred Heart Chapel for a public Catholic Mass.
