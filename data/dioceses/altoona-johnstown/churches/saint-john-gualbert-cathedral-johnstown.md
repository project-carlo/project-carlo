## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the 18 October 2026 bulletin lists the cathedral's Mass times (Saturday 6PM;
Sunday 7AM, 11AM, 5PM; weekdays 7AM, and 12:05PM Tuesday to Friday; Saturday 8AM). The
diocese directory's times agree (Saturday 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM; Sunday 7:00 AM, 11:00 AM and
5:00 PM).

the parish site footer and OpenStreetMap all give 117 Clinton Street, Johnstown 15901.
