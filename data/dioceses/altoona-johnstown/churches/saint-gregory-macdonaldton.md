## Name and address

The directory gives "247 High Street, Meyersdale 15552" as the address of Saint Gregory Parish, MacDonaldton. That is the Meyersdale rectory (the same address as Saints Philip and James), not the church: GCatholic 23861 places the church in Macdonaldton PA 15530 with no street, and the Discover Mass page says 15530. The street address 137 Church St, Macdonaldton, PA 15530 comes from a Catholic Index snippet (single source, not fetchable now) and Nominatim has a house node at that number in Macdonaldton. The Discover Mass page's "137 426, Berlin, PA 15530" is the same number garbled ("Berlin" is the mailing town of 15530) and supports 137. Coordinates are the Nominatim address point (GCatholic has none): about the right building, to be confirmed. `address_verified` false for lack of a second independent source; name agrees across sources.
