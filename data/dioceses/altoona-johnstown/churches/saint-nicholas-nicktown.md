## Holds Mass

The 25 Oct 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-21) lists daily Mass here: weekdays 8:00 AM (Wednesday 7:00 PM), Saturday 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM Vigil, Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 AM. The diocese directory matches except for an extra Sunday 6:00 AM that the bulletin does not show.

Nicktown PA 15762). Coordinates: GCatholic Plus Code 87G3J57V+GW. (GCatholic's website for it, saintnicholasparish.org, is the wrong TLD; the parish's is .com.)
