## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-21) lists Sunday Masses at 8:00AM and 10:00AM, the Saturday 4PM vigil and weekday Masses Monday and Tuesday 7:30AM, Wednesday 6:00PM and Friday 8:45AM (none on 19-23 Oct, rector away). The directory's Mass times agree.

## Name and address

Name: the directory, GCatholic, OSM and the bulletin all say Basilica of St. Michael the Archangel. Address 321 St. Mary Street (bulletin, directory; GCatholic writes "321 St. Mary's St."); OSM numbers the building 319 Saint Mary Street, a one-number difference, same street and ZIP 15940. The name is verified; the address is left unverified because OSM says 319 (the bulletin and directory are the parish's own and the diocese's, GCatholic agrees on 321, so this is likely an OSM numbering quirk). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 35 m from the OSM way.
