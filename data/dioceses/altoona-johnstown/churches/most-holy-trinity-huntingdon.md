## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 newsletter (read 2026-10-21) lists the Saturday 24 October 4:00 PM
anticipated Mass and the Sunday 25 October 10:30 AM Mass, with 11:00 AM weekday Masses; the
parish site shows the same weekend times. The directory adds Sunday 6:00 PM "at Juniata
College", which is a different place (open/juniata-college-tussey-hall-huntingdon).

site and the directory; GCatholic lists it as "Holy Trinity" and the prefilled name was
corrected to the bulletin's. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code for the building.
