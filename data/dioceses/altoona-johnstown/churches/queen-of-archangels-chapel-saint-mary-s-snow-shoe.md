## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's Mass Times page and the 18 October 2026 bulletin list a regular Sunday 7:30 AM Mass at the Snow Shoe Chapel, and a 7:00 PM holy-day vigil there. Read 2026-10-21. It is not in the GCatholic roster (checked by city: no Snow Shoe entry), so its only roster id is the directory's.

## Name and address

The diocese directory calls it "Queen of Archangels Chapel/Saint Mary's"; the parish calls it "Snow Shoe Chapel"; OSM names the building "Saint Mary Catholic Church". The name evidently reflects a former parish church (St. Mary) now a chapel of Queen of Archangels, but no source I read says so, so `name_verified` stays false and I followed the directory's name. The address 204 S. Fourth Street, Snow Shoe, PA 16874 agrees across the bulletin, directory and OSM (`address_verified` true; a 16871 ZIP on the parish site's map link is a typo). Coordinates are the OSM building (way 895101393).
