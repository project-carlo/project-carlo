## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-21) gives "Weekend Masses: Saturday at 4 PM, Sunday, 8 AM and 10:30 AM" and "Mass is celebrated at 8 AM Monday through Friday"; the parish site's Mass Schedule says the same. This is the parish's only church (the other Portage buildings are closed, see the parish record).

## Name and address

GCatholic (`23878`) lists the building as "St. Joseph", 509 Caldwell Ave.; the diocese's directory and the parish's own site call it Holy Family, 509 Caldwell Avenue 15946, and the parish history says "Saint Joseph Church was renamed Holy Family Church" in 2016. The address agrees across GCatholic, the directory and the site footer; the name difference is the renaming. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code `87G398PJ+Q2`.
