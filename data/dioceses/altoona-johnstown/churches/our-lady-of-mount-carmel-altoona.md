## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (Discover Mass, read 2026-10-21) gives the parish's schedule as
"Saturday 4:00 p.m., Sunday 8:00 a.m. and 11:00 a.m., Monday-Friday 7:00 a.m., Tuesday/First
Friday 7:00 p.m." and its intentions list for 17-25 October puts the Saturday 4:00 p.m., the
Sunday 8:00 a.m. and 11:00 a.m. and the weekday Masses under "MT. CARMEL" (the Sunday 9:30 a.m.
is under Assumption Chapel, a separate church record). The directory's times for this church are
Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM and the weekday 7:00 AM / Tuesday 7:00 PM Masses; it omits
the Sunday 8:00 AM that the bulletin prints under Mount Carmel.
