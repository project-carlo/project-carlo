## Holds Mass

Weekend and weekday Masses are listed by the diocese directory (read 2026-10-21): Sat 5:00 PM, Sun 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, Mon/Wed/Thu/Fri 7:30 AM; the parish's Facebook page (via findglocal's mirror, posts to 23 Sep 2026) gives "Saturday at 5 PM; Sunday at 8 AM and 10 AM", and the Carrolltown bulletin of 18 Oct 2026 announces a dinner "St. Bernard Church, Hastings". No bulletin lists the Mass schedule (the parish has none the pipeline can read), so this is the directory and the parish's own page, not a bulletin.

## Name and address

Name agrees everywhere ("St. Bernard", "Saint Bernard"). The street disagrees: the diocese directory, Discover Mass and the Facebook page say 147 Seventh Avenue; GCatholic says 148 7th Ave.; OSM and the old parish page say Beaver Street (147 Beaver St in OSM; "148 Beaver St." on the old WebParish stub), so it is a corner building. I followed the directory (147 Seventh Avenue, three sources) but left `address_verified` false, with the house number unsettled between 147 and 148.
