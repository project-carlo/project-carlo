## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish site's schedule (omostoday.com/our-mother-of-sorrows/, read 2026-10-21) lists
Saturday vigil 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:30 am and 11:00 am, and weekday Masses (Mon, Tue, Fri 8:00
am; Wed 6:00 pm; Thu 8:00 am). The directory's times are the same. The 18 October 2026
bulletin is a scan, so I did not read its schedule page as text.
