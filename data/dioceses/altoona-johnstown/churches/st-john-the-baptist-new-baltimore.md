## Holds Mass

The 11 October 2026 bulletin (https://newbaltimorecatholic.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/11-x-17-Bulletin-10.11.26.pdf)
lists "Sat: 6:00 PM (Vigil) Sun: 8:00 AM & 10:30 AM" and weekday Masses "held in the chapel". The
parish schedule page says the same weekend times. The weekday chapel and the "small, private
chapel" of the retreat center are inside this one campus; there is no separate public Mass site
for the retreat center (see the parish record).
