## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin, page 1, lists "Mass Times: Saturday 4PM Sunday 9AM
Thursday 8AM" under "Saint Patrick Church". The directory gives Saturday 4:00 PM and Sunday
9:00 AM, with confession Saturday 3:00 PM (it omits the Thursday 8AM Mass).

## Name and address

Directory "Saint Patrick", GCatholic "St. Patrick", bulletin "Saint Patrick Church", OSM
"Saint Patrick Catholic Church": all at 609 Park Avenue, Johnstown 15902 (also the parish
site footer). Coordinates are the GCatholic Plus Code point (OSM within 10 m).
