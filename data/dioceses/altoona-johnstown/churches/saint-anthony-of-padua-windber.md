## Holds Mass

Yes. The St. Anthony bulletin of 18 October 2026 (posted on the parish's Web Parish Bulletin page, read 2026-10-21) lists Masses "at St. Anthony of Padua" on Monday 9:00am, Tuesday 12:00pm and Saturday 4:00pm Vigil, and Sunday "10:00am (SA)"; the 25 October issue of the sister parish's bulletin lists the same slots. These match the diocese directory (Sat 4:00 PM, Sun 10:00 AM, Mon 9:00 AM, Tue 12:00 PM). The Sunday 10:00 AM Mass is the main Windber St. Anthony Mass; the 2 Nov All Souls Mass at 10AM is also here.
