## Holds Mass

Yes, on a reduced rota. The parish page (read 2026-10-21) gives Sunday 9:00 am on first
Sundays (not in June to August) and Saturday 6:00 pm on the second, third and fourth
Saturdays. The 27 September bulletin lists 6:00 p.m. Saturday Masses at St. Michael's. The
directory lists Saturday 6:00 PM and Sunday 9:45 AM, which disagrees with the parish page on
the Sunday time and frequency; the parish's own page is followed. The bulletin's Mass times
are not readable as text in the 18 October scan.

## Name and address

Directory "Saint Michael", GCatholic "St. Michael", bulletin "ST MICHAEL CATHOLIC CHURCH",
OSM "Saint Michael Catholic Church": all give 180 Gilbert Street, Johnstown. The ZIP differs: the
bulletin (27 September cover, "180 Gilbert St., Johnstown, PA 15906") and one OSM lookup say
15906; the directory, GCatholic and another OSM lookup say 15905. I used the bulletin's 15906
(the bulletin is the parish's own address line). The GCatholic Plus Code point is within 40 m
of OSM's, so the coordinates are the building.
