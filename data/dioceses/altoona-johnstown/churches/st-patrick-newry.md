## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (ParishesOnline 14/0551) gives the weekly schedule at 704 Patrick
Lane: weekdays 8:00 AM, Saturday anticipated 4:30 PM, Sunday 7:00 AM and 11:00 AM, with a named
Mass intention for each. It lists a Mass for 25 October (7:00 and 11:00 AM) with intentions.
The parish home page gives the same times.
