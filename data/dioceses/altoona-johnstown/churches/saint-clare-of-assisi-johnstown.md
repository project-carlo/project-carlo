## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday 4:00 PM and Monday 6:30 PM at St.
Clare of Assisi; the parish's own site (Mass Time Schedule page, 2026-10-21) and the directory
(Saturday 4:00 PM, Monday 6:30 PM, adoration after the second-Monday Mass) agree.

Avenue" and the bulletin "St. Clare of Assisi Church 124 Maple Avenue, Johnstown, Pa. 15901" agree. The
parish office is separate, at 110 Maple Avenue. Coordinates are the GCatholic Plus Code point.
