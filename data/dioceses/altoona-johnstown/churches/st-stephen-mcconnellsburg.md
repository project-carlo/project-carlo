## Holds Mass

Yes. The St. Stephen bulletin of 11 October 2026 gives Saturday 4:00 p.m. and Sunday noon. The
diocese directory has the same times (Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM). It is the only Mass: no
weekday Masses are listed.

## Name and address

Bulletin masthead, Discover Mass and GCatholic 23862 ("303 Lincoln Way E") say 303 Lincoln Way East;
the diocese directory says "303 Lincoln Way West", apparently a slip. The address follows the bulletin, but
`address_verified` stays false because the directory disagrees and I did not check a map. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; "Mcconnellsburg" in
GCatholic's spelling is corrected to McConnellsburg (bulletin, directory).
