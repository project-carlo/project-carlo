## Holds Mass

Yes: the 18 Oct 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-21) lists "Sunday: 8:00am (Chapel)" every weekend, and the diocese directory lists the chapel with "Sunday: 8:00 AM" (and confession 7:30 AM). The bulletin is the parish's one bulletin for "Church & Chapel" (see the parish record).

## Name and address

Names disagree in form only: the diocese directory and the parish call it "Holy Cross Chapel"; GCatholic 23872 lists it as a chapel named "Prince of Peace" (same address). The parish's own page prints "Holy Cross Chapel" (my reading: that is the name; the bulletin calls it just "the Chapel"). Address 2410 Campbell Avenue, Northern Cambria, PA 15714 is the same in the directory, GCatholic and the parish's Mass Schedules page. Coordinates are from Nominatim (an address point for 2410 Campbell Avenue), because GCatholic has none for this entry.
