## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (a .docx on the parish's Weebly site, read 2026-10-21) lists Masses Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 am and 10:30 am, and daily Mass Tuesday to Friday 9:00 am, "No Mass on Monday". The diocese's directory gives the same weekend times (and a Monday Mass the bulletin denies). The parish is in the Franciscan (OFM Conv.) pilgrimage-site list in a May 2026 diocesan letter on the Portage site, which names "St. Michael Church, 751 Locust Street, St. Michael, PA 15951".

## Name and address

GCatholic (`23884`), the diocese's directory and the bulletin masthead all give St. Michael, 751 Locust Street, St. Michael, PA 15951. The directory and GCatholic agree on the name and address, which the parish's own bulletin repeats. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code `87G386JP+WH`.
