## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-21) lists the Saturday vigil at 4:00 p.m.,
Sunday at 9:00 a.m. and a weekday Mass at 8:00 a.m. Monday to Friday, with intentions for each; the
parish site shows the same times. The diocese directory (scraped 2026-10-21) says Saturday 5:00 PM
and Confession Saturday 4:30 PM, which differs from the bulletin and site; the bulletin is newer
and the parish's own, so 4:00 PM is the time to follow.
