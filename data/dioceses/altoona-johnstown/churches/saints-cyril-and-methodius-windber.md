## Holds Mass

Yes. The Seton bulletin of 25 October 2026 and St. Anthony's of 18 October 2026 (read 2026-10-21) both list Sunday 12:00 PM, Wednesday 9:00 AM, Thursday 12:00 PM and Friday 9:00 AM Masses at Sts. Cyril and Methodius. These match the directory (Sun 12:00 PM, Wed 9:00 AM, Thu 12:00 PM, Fri 9:00 AM; adoration after the First Friday Mass). St. Anthony's Mass page also says "First Friday - 9:00 AM at SS Cyril & Methodius".

## Name and address

The directory, GCatholic (23895) and the parish Web Parish stub all give 604 Graham Avenue, Windber PA 15963 (a stale page giving Fr. Misurda as pastor). OSM confirms the address but not the name; the bulletins confirm the name only as "Ss. Cyril & Methodius" and "(SSCM)". Coordinates from the GCatholic Plus Code.
