## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 shared bulletin (Discover Mass, read 2026-10-21) schedules Sunday 8 am Mass at the Sacred Heart site (it calls the building "OLQA Church"), and the parish home page lists "Sunday: 8AM (Sacred Heart)" and weekday noon Masses here from the Monday after Pentecost to Christ the King (St. John's has them the rest of the year).
