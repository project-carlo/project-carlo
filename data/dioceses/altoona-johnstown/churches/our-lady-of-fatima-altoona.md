## Holds Mass

The 18 October 2026 issue of "The Marian" (St. Mary's bulletin, page 1) lists "Weekend Mass at
Our Lady of Fatima Church on Sunday 11:30 AM", and the parish site's Mass & Confession
Schedule page says "Sunday Morning Mass at Our Lady of Fatima - 11:30 AM". The directory
says Sunday 11:30 AM and Holy Days 6:00 PM, and Fatima's own January 2026 bulletin says
"Eucharist: Sunday: 11:30 AM". Only the Sunday Mass is confirmed for now.

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic, the directory, ParishesOnline ("Our Lady of Fatima Church") and the bulletin
agree. Address: the sources disagree on the number. GCatholic (23805), the diocese directory
and ParishesOnline give 2010 12th Ave., Altoona 16601; Discover Mass and Fatima's own January
2026 bulletin masthead (olf_01-18-2026.pdf, "Our Lady of Fatima Parish 2000 12th Avenue
Altoona, PA 16601") give 2000 12th Avenue. I kept 2010, which three sources give and the
GCatholic Plus Code (87G3GH6Q+CF) locates on the building, but left `address_verified` false
because the parish's own materials say 2000. Name is verified; the street number is not.
