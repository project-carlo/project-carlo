## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's own site (revised October 2026, read 2026-10-21) gives "Masses: Saturday evenings
at 5:30 pm, and Sunday mornings at 7:30 am in the Church", and Tuesday Mass at 6:30 pm after
Adoration. The diocese directory (scraped 2026-10-21) agrees on Saturday 5:30 PM and Sunday 7:30 AM
but lists no Tuesday Mass. No bulletin was read.

## Name and address

Street and ZIP agree everywhere: 20896 Croghan Pike, 17243 (the parish site "Orbisonia, PA
17243-9000", the diocese directory "Orbisonia", GCatholic "Shade Gap, Pennsylvania 17243-9000").
The post-office town differs: the site and directory say Orbisonia, GCatholic says Shade Gap, and
the old WebParish stub gives a Shade Gap address ("HC 62 Box 696", ZIP 17255). The record
follows the parish's own site and the directory (Orbisonia). The parish name is "Saint Mary Parish,
Shade Gap" in the directory and "St. Mary's Catholic Church in Shade Valley" on its site.
`address_verified` is left false because of the town disagreement. Coordinates are GCatholic's
Plus Code.
