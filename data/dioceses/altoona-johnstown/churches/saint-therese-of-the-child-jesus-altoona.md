## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (eCatholic file 20261017.pdf, read 2026-10-21) prints "Weekend
Mass Schedule: Saturday Evening 5:00 PM, Sunday Morning 8:00 AM & 10:30 AM", weekday Masses
at 7:45 AM (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri) and 8:30 AM (Wed) and Saturday at 8:00 AM, and an intentions
table for 24-25 October with Saturday 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM and Sunday 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM. The
directory's times agree (Sat 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM; Sun 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM; weekdays 7:45 AM,
Wed 9:00 AM).

## Name and address

The name is the same everywhere ("Saint Therese of the Child Jesus" in the directory, "St.
Therese of the Child Jesus" in GCatholic and OSM). **The address sources disagree**: GCatholic
(id 23814) and OSM, which names the church, say 2301 5th Street, Altoona 16601; the diocese
directory and the parish site's footer say 411 Wopsononock Avenue; the parish office is 417 25th
Avenue. The three addresses geocode within about 100 m of each other (see the evidence), so they
are one campus; which one is the church's street number is not settled, so `address_verified`
stays false. I kept GCatholic's 2301 5th St., because OSM also puts the named church there. The
coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G3GHHV+57. What would settle it: the parish's own
statement of the church's address, or its Mass-times page.
