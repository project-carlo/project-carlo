## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 Oct 2026 St. Anne bulletin (read 2026-10-21) lists Mass intentions Monday-Friday 7:00AM (including Thursday 22 Oct), Saturday 5:30PM and Sunday 9:00AM. The parish site lists daily Mass on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday only; the directory says Monday-Friday; the bulletin shows a Thursday Mass.

## Name and address

Name agrees across GCatholic ("St. Anne"), the directory ("Saint Anne") and the bulletin ("ST. ANNE'S CHURCH"). Street number: GCatholic and the bulletin say 533 Woodland Avenue; the diocesan directory says 531. I followed 533 (two sources, one of them the parish's own); `address_verified` stays false because the directory disagrees and no map source has been checked. Coordinates are the GCatholic Plus Code point.
