## Holds Mass

Yes. The Bedford bulletin of 18 October 2026 (page 4) schedules Saturday 5:00 p.m. and Sunday 7:00 a.m.
and 9:30 a.m. in the church, and page 1 lists weekday Masses at 8:00 AM (Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri; none
Tuesday). The parish home page and the diocese directory give the same weekend times.
