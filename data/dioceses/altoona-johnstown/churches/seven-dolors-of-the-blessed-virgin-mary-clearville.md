## Holds Mass

Yes, a Sunday noon Mass, by three sources dated 2026-10-21 (none is a bulletin that lists it):
the diocese directory (Sunday 12:00 PM; confession following), the Discover Mass page (Sunday
12:00pm, mass-times feed) and the Bedford parish site, whose home page says "Fr. Rich also has a
noon Mass at his mission parish, Seven Dolors of the Blessed Virgin in Beans Cove, PA". The Bedford bulletin of
18 October 2026 names it ("ST. THOMAS AND SEVEN DOLORS" under the 25 October 7:00 AM intention)
but its Mass schedule lists Bedford only. No bulletin of its own exists: Discover Mass says "no
bulletins available", the directory gives no homepage, and no site turned up in a web search.
So the church sits under the Bedford row (see parishes/saint-thomas-the-apostle-bedford).

## Name and address

**One building, not two.** GCatholic has two entries: 23816 "Seven Dolors of the Blessed Virgin
Mary", 2174 Beans Cove Rd., Clearville (Plus Code 87F3QCVC+28), and 148968 "Our Lady of Seven
Dolors", Bean's Cove, no street (87F3QCG2+C6, on its Jubilee 2025 churches list). They are 2.5 km
apart. OpenStreetMap has one church, way 1412535592, "Seven Sorrows of the Blessed Virgin Mary
Catholic Church", housenumber 2174 Beans Cove Road, at 39.77603, -78.59927: within 20 m of
148968's Plus Code, and 2.5 km from 23816's, whose point reverse-geocodes to an unrelated house
(3081 Beans Cove Road). The directory, Discover Mass and the Bedford site name one church at
2174 Beans Cove Road. So 23816 has the right address and a wrong coordinate, and 148968 the
right coordinate and no address: duplicates of one place. Coordinates follow 148968 (building
precise); the address follows the directory, GCatholic 23816 and OSM, which agree. Both GCatholic ids are in `roster`.
"Seven Dolors" (directory, GCatholic, Discover Mass) and "Seven Sorrows" (OSM) are the same title.
