## Holds Mass

The 18 October 2026 issue of "The Marian" (page 1) lists "Weekend Mass Schedule at St. Mary's
Church: Saturday, October 24, 2026 4:00 PM ... Sunday, October 25, 2026 8:00 AM" and weekday
Mass at 9:00 AM on Thursday 22 and Friday 23 October (none Monday to Wednesday that week,
though the parish site's Mass & Confession Schedule page gives Monday, Wednesday, Thursday
and Friday at 9:00 AM as the usual weekday pattern). The directory's times are the same.

## Name and address

GCatholic (23812) says "St. Mary", the directory "Saint Mary", ParishesOnline "St. Mary
(Immaculate Conception)", Discover Mass "St. Mary Immaculate Conception", and the parish site
"Saint Mary's Immaculate Conception Roman Catholic Church"; all give 1405 5th Avenue, Altoona
16602 (the parish site's header and the bulletin's rectory phone 814-942-2416 match
GCatholic's phone). "St. Mary" is the common short name; the full name is Immaculate
Conception. Coordinates come from the GCatholic Plus Code 87G3GJ53+X5.
