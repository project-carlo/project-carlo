## Holds Mass

The 18 October 2026 bulletin (image, page 1) prints the weekly schedule: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday
and Friday 8:30 AM, Wednesday 6:00 PM, Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM. The
parish site's home page (2026-10-21) gives the same Saturday time and the 11:00 AM second Sunday
Mass. The diocese directory's times match.

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic, the diocese directory and the bulletin all say St. John the Evangelist.

Address: **the sources disagree.** The bulletin masthead, the parish home page and GCatholic say
309 Lotz Avenue (Lakemont), Altoona 16602. The diocese directory says 344 Baynton Avenue,
Altoona 16602. I followed the bulletin, the parish site and GCatholic, and kept the directory's
address only here. 344 Baynton Avenue may be the rectory or a mailing address (not checked), so
`address_verified` stays false until a map source settles it. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus
Code 87G3FJ74+P7, which sits on the Lotz Avenue address.
