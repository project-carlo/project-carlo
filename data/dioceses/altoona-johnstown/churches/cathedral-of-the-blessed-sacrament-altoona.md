## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin, page 2, gives the Mass times block (Sunday 8:00 AM,
10:00 AM, 5:00 PM; Saturday 5:00 PM vigil and 12:00 noon; weekdays 12:00 noon; the extraction
order is scrambled, so the exact assignment is read together with the directory) and
announces the cathedral's anniversary Mass. The diocese directory's Mass times:
Saturday 12:00 PM and 5:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 AM, 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM, weekdays 12:00 PM.

## Name and address

Cathedral of the Blessed Sacrament, One Cathedral Square, Altoona, PA 16601. **Sources
disagree on the street.** The bulletin masthead ("ONE CATHEDRAL SQUARE, Altoona,
Pennsylvania 16601-3315") and GCatholic (page 2439: "1 Cathedral Square, Altoona,
Pennsylvania 16601") agree; the diocese directory says "1405 13th Avenue, Altoona, PA
16603". I followed the bulletin and GCatholic, which are the cathedral's own and an
independent source; the directory address is probably a rectory or office, not checked. The
name agrees in all three, so `name_verified` is true. `address_verified` stays false because the
directory disagrees (recorded above). Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code `87G3GH8W+9H`, decoded (40.5159375, -78.4035625).
