## Holds Mass

The 18 October 2026 bulletin (Discover Mass download, page 3 "St. Rose Liturgy Schedule") lists
Weekends: Saturday 9:30 AM and 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM; weekdays: Monday 7:00 PM,
Tuesday-Friday 8:30 AM. Page 4 gives the week's Mass intentions (19-25 October) at those times.
The directory has the same times.

## Name and address

GCatholic (23813), the diocese directory and the bulletin masthead ("5514 Roselawn Avenue
• Altoona, PA 16602") agree on 5514 Roselawn Ave., Altoona 16602, and ParishesOnline's
organization for the church gives the same street address. Discover Mass's church page says
"5519 6th Avenue, Altoona, PA 16602" (its directions say "Church on 764 (Sixth Ave and 56th
to 55th Sts)"): a different street description of the same block, or an old address; the
parish's own masthead and two other sources outvote it. Coordinates come from the GCatholic
Plus Code 87G3FHFH+G5.
