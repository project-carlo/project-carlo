## Holds Mass

Yes, on the strength of two sources; no bulletin was read. The parish's Facebook page (mirrored
on findglocal.com, newest post 5 September 2026, read 2026-10-21) gives "Weekend Mass: Sat 4pm,
Sun 9am" and Confessions Saturday 3pm-3:45pm. The diocese directory (scraped 2026-10-21) gives
a different schedule: Saturday 6:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 AM, Wednesday 6:00 PM, Thursday 11:00 AM,
Confession Wednesday 5:30 PM. The Facebook version is newer and the parish's own, so it is
the one to trust, but the two disagree on every time and nothing settles which is current.
The church holds public Mass either way.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("1416 Dudley Rd., Dudley, Pennsylvania 16634"), the diocese directory ("1416 Dudley
Road") and the parish Facebook mirror ("1416 Dudley Road, Dudley, PA") agree. The old WebParish
stub gives "Washington St Ext. PO Box 188", an older mailing address. Coordinates are
GCatholic's Plus Code.
