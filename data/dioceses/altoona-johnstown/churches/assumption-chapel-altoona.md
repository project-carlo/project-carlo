## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 Our Lady of Mount Carmel bulletin (Discover Mass, read 2026-10-21)
prints a Mass-intentions list for 17-25 October with "SUNDAY—MT. CARMEL 8 :00 A.M. ... 11:00
A.M." followed by its own heading "ASSUMPTION CHAPEL 9 :30 A.M. Mary Jane Feigh (Husband,
John)". The diocese directory gives the chapel "Sunday: 8:00 AM, 9:30 AM" with Confession
Sunday 9:00 AM; the bulletin's 8:00 a.m. intention is listed under Mt. Carmel, so the bulletin
supports only the 9:30 a.m. here. That is a conflict over the 8:00 a.m. between the directory
and the bulletin; the bulletin wins, and it is the parish's own, so the Sunday 9:30 a.m. is the
only Mass recorded here.
