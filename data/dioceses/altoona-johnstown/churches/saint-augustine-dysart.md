## Holds Mass

Yes, by sources other than a bulletin (none exists, see parishes/saint-augustine-dysart). The parish Facebook post of 27 March 2026 schedules the Palm Sunday vigil, Holy Week and Easter Masses at St. Augustine; funeral Masses with Fr. Bob Reese are reported there for 24 January, 20 July and 22 July 2026 (obituary snippets); the directory gives Saturday 6:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 AM, Tuesday and Thursday 9:00 AM, Friday 8:00 AM. Third-party aggregators also list a weekday Mass at "Saint Monica, Chest Springs" for Dysart's Monday to Wednesday slots; they disagree with each other and the directory, and are not used.
