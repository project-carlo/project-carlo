## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin of the shared Cresson row (read 2026-10-21) lists SA Mass on Thursday 22 Oct at 8:00am and Sunday 25 Oct at 8:00am; the weekday Masses on 19-21 Oct were cancelled for the clergy conference. The diocese's directory lists Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM, Tuesday and Thursday 8:00 AM, which lags the bulletin (the Sunday Mass at SA is at 8:00, and the 4:00 and 11:00 vigil and Sunday Masses are at St. Francis Xavier); the bulletin wins.

## Name and address

GCatholic 23826, the diocese directory, the bulletin masthead and OSM all agree on St. Aloysius, 7911 Admiral Peary Highway, Cresson PA 16630. The bulletin is the same source as the parish's own site and ParishesOnline's org is another, so the name and address are verified by GCatholic, the directory and OSM independently. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within about 20 m of the OSM way and 30 m of the PO org point.
