## Publisher

Lange, O.S.B.) publishes its own bulletin on Discover Mass (`st-nicholas-nicktown-pa`), which its eCatholic homepage links as its "Bulletins" menu. The 25 Oct 2026 issue (read 2026-10-21) is this parish's alone: it lists Nicktown's own Mass intentions, Saint Nicholas Catholic School and Northern Cambria Catholic School contacts. It does not list Carrolltown or Hastings. The three north-Cambria Benedictine parishes therefore have three bulletins and three pastors (Gabler, Rettger, Lange); no shared bulletin.
