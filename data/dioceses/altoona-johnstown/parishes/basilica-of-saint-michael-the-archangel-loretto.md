## Publisher

The Basilica of Saint Michael the Archangel Parish, Loretto (rector Very Rev. John David Byrnes) publishes its own weekly bulletin as a PDF on its Weebly site, https://www.basilicasm-loretto.org/bulletins.html (files `/uploads/4/5/8/1/45811561/<M-D-YY>.pdf`). The 18 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-21) names the Basilica alone and has one Mass-intention list and schedule. It is a one-parish, one-church row.

## Worship sites

The Basilica church, 321 St. Mary Street. The summer schedule in the same bulletin adds "Sunday Masses: 8:00AM and 7:00PM (Shrine)" between Memorial Day and Labor Day: the Shrine of Our Lady of the Alleghenies on the Basilica's grounds, seasonal and out of season now. It is recorded in `open/shrine-of-our-lady-of-the-alleghenies-loretto` to be settled next summer. The Prince Gallitzin Chapel House (a trust named in the masthead) lists no Mass in this bulletin. Saint Francis University's Immaculate Conception Chapel is a separate campus chapel: see `open/immaculate-conception-chapel-saint-francis-university-loretto`.
