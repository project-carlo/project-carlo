## Publisher

Prince of Peace Parish, Northern Cambria (Rev. Mark E. Groeger, pastor; the diocese calls him Administrator) publishes one weekly bulletin for both its buildings, posted as dated PDFs on its own Weebly site. The 18 Oct 2026 masthead reads "PRINCE OF PEACE ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH & CHAPEL" and its Mass list gives the Saturday and 10:30 Sunday Masses at the Church and the 8:00am Sunday Mass at the Chapel. One bulletin, so one row with two churches.

## Worship sites

Prince of Peace Church, 811 Chestnut Avenue (GCatholic 23871), and Holy Cross Chapel, 2410 Campbell Avenue (GCatholic 23872, listed there as a chapel named "Prince of Peace"). Not here: the two Eastern Catholic churches in Northern Cambria (GCatholic 17407 Immaculate Conception, Ukrainian; 17307 St. John the Baptist, Ruthenian), left to the Eastern-rite batch.
