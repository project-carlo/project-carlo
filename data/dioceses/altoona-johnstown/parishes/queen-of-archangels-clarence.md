## Publisher

Queen of Archangels Parish, Clarence (pastor Rev. Matthew Laffey, O.S.B.) publishes its own weekly bulletin. The 18 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-21) is headed "Queen of Archangels Roman Catholic Parish" and one Mass schedule covers the Clarence church and the Snow Shoe chapel. The directory lists the two as the parish's two worship sites. One parish, two churches, one bulletin.

## Worship sites

Queen of Archangels Church, 102 Church Street, Clarence (Saturday 4:30 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM, Tue 7 PM, Wed-Fri 8 AM) and Queen of Archangels Chapel / Saint Mary's, 204 S. Fourth Street, Snow Shoe (Sunday 7:30 AM; holy-day vigil 7:00 PM), both from the parish site's Mass Times page and the bulletin. GCatholic 17276 (Dormition of the Mother of God, Clarence) and the ParishesOnline "St. Mary Byzantine Church" are Eastern Catholic, a separate Byzantine parish about 100 m away (ParishesOnline puts it at 103 Byzantine Ln), not part of this row.
