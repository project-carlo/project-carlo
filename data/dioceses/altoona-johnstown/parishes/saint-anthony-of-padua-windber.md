## Publisher

St. Anthony of Padua publishes its own weekly bulletin, one PDF a week named `MMDDYY.pdf` on webparish.com, listed on the Bulletin page of its Web Parish site (`webparish.com/aj/stanthonyswindber/index.php?PG=upcomingevents`). On 2026-10-21 the newest issue was 18 October 2026 (`101826.pdf`); the list reaches back to 20 September. The pastor is Rev. Roderick N. Soha, T.O.R., who also serves St. Elizabeth Ann Seton and Sts. Cyril and Methodius, Windber (directory, 2026-10-21). The issue has no masthead; it is St. Anthony's by where it is posted and by its text (parish office 467-7292, Children's Christmas Party "at St. Anthony of Padua", All Souls Mass "at St. Anthony of Padua Church").

## Worship sites

One church here: St. Anthony of Padua, 2201 Graham Avenue. The bulletin's Mass list covers all three Windber churches, but this is not a merged bulletin: St. Elizabeth Ann Seton publishes a different PDF every week (`SEAS2026-10-18Bulletin.pdf`, a different layout and text) from its own site, so the two parishes stay separate rows, each with its own live channel. Sts. Cyril and Methodius has no bulletin channel of its own (its webparish page is a stale stub naming Rev. Matthew Misurda as pastor); it is filed under St. Elizabeth Ann Seton's row, whose weekday Masses are said there ("SEAS at SSCM"). Its Masses also appear in this bulletin. See `saint-elizabeth-ann-seton-windber`. GCatholic's St. Mary, Windber (gcatholic:17326) is Ruthenian: not recorded here.
