## Publisher

St. Thomas the Apostle Parish, Bedford publishes its own weekly bulletin as PDFs on its WebParish site
(webparish.com/aj/thomas/). The newest issue read on 2026-10-21 is
`http://www.webparish.com/pdfs/Bulletin-10-18-2026.pdf`, dated 18 October 2026, with the masthead
"SAINT THOMAS THE APOSTLE CATHOLIC CHURCH, 215 East Penn Street, Bedford" and "Rev. Richard B. Tomkosky,
Pastor".

**Bedford and Beans Cove: one bulletin.** The lead was that Bedford and Seven Dolors of the BVM,
Beans Cove share a pastor (Fr. Tomkosky). The Bedford home page calls Seven Dolors his "mission
parish" with a noon Mass, and the Bedford bulletin names it in the 25 October intention ("ST. THOMAS AND
SEVEN DOLORS"). Seven Dolors has no bulletin, site or Discover Mass bulletin of its own (its Discover Mass page says "no
bulletins available"; the directory gives no homepage), so there is no second channel to be a separate row. Absence evidence:
I searched the Discover Mass sitemap list (st-thomas and seven-dolors slugs only), the directory entry
and a DuckDuckGo search for "Seven Dolors Beans Cove" (only mass-time aggregators). The bulletin itself does not print the noon Mass
(page 4 lists Bedford Masses only), so the Seven Dolors time rests on the home page, the directory and Discover Mass.
