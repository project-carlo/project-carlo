## Publisher

Our Lady of Lourdes publishes its own weekly bulletin, "The Lourdian", through its eCatholic
site (id 29859). The 18 October 2026 issue (2 pages) is headed "Our Lady of Lourdes,
www.ourladyoflourdesaltoona.com, 2716 Broad Ave. Altoona, PA 16601", gives its own pastor
(Fr. James Dugan) and lists only this church's Masses. The diocese directory also gives the
parish its own homepage, pastor and Mass times, so there is no sharing with another parish.
