## Publisher

Two canonical parishes, Church of the Transfiguration (Conemaugh) and Saint John Vianney (Mundys Corner), share one pastor (Fr. Timothy J. Harris, T.O.R.), one website (sjvcc.com) and one weekly bulletin, printed by LPi and carried on ParishesOnline as container 14/1788 (org `st-john-vianney-church-15909`). The 18 Oct 2026 issue (read 2026-10-21) is headed "Twinned Parishes of the DIOCESE OF ALTOONA-JOHNSTOWN" and carries both churches' Mass intentions, offerings and sanctuary-lamp intentions. So this is one row with two churches, not the two rows the roster lists. Neither Transfiguration ParishesOnline organization (340 2nd St; "Sacred Heart Church", 219 Oak St) publishes anything, and no Transfiguration website of its own exists (the site's header links a Facebook page, `facebook.com/TransfigurationConemaugh`, not read).

The 2 Aug 2026 issue (container 14/1788, 20260802B.pdf, read 2026-10-21) has the same "Twinned Parishes" masthead and "T.C.O.T. Weekly Mass Intentions", so the joint bulletin is not new. Container 14/1788 is the only live channel found for either church.
