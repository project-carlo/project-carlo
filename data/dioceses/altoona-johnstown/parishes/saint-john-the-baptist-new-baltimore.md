## Publisher

St. John the Baptist Parish, New Baltimore publishes its own weekly bulletin on its WordPress
site (newbaltimorecatholic.com, titled "Church and Retreat Center"). The 11 October 2026 issue
(`.../2026/10/11-x-17-Bulletin-10.11.26.pdf`, 4 pages) gives the week's Masses, "Sat: 6:00 PM
(Vigil) Sun: 8:00 AM & 10:30 AM", and is signed Fr. Mark Pattock, OFMcap, Pastor. It names this
church alone (no Meyersdale or MacDonaldton in its text). The pastor is a Capuchin of the St.
Augustine Province, and the parish site says the friars staff the church and the retreat center.

## Worship sites

One church, St. John the Baptist (churches/st-john-the-baptist-new-baltimore). The question was
whether the retreat center is a separate public Mass site. It is not: the retreat center is the
1890 Carmelite novitiate building at the same address (101 Findley St, shared phone and
website), with "a small, private chapel" (facilities page). The parish schedule page puts the
weekday Masses (Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri 8:00 AM; Tue 7:00 PM) "in the Chapel" and the weekend Masses in the
church; the bulletin says "all daily masses will be held in the chapel". That is one campus
and one bulletin, so one church row. Nothing found lists a separate retreat-center Mass
schedule, site, Discover Mass page or bulletin.
