## Publisher

Queen of Peace Parish, Patton (pastor Rev. Ananias Buccicone, O.S.B., a monk of Saint Vincent Archabbey) publishes its own weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site, queenofpeacepatton.org (site id 19026). I read the 18 October 2026 issue (`https://files.ecatholic.com/19026/bulletins/20261018.pdf`, 2 pages) on 2026-10-21: the masthead is "QUEEN OF PEACE ... Patton, PA. 16668 ... 907 Sixth Avenue", the contact block names "Fr. Ananias, O.S.B., Pastor", and the Mass intentions list only Patton's Masses. The text never names Colver, Chest Springs, Ashville, St. Monica, St. Thomas Aquinas or Holy Family (grepped), so it is not a joint bulletin with those neighbours.
