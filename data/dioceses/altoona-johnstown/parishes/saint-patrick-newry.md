## Publisher

St. Patrick Parish, Newry publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline / LPi
(container 14/0551, organization `st-patrick-church-16665`). The newest issue read on
2026-10-21 is `https://container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/14/0551/20261018B.pdf`, dated
"October 18, 2026", with a masthead for 704 Patrick Lane, Newry, "Rev. Allen P. Zeth" as
administrator, St. Patrick Parish Education Center (the school), and a Mass schedule for St.
Patrick alone.

**Newry and Roaring Spring: two parishes, this bulletin is Newry's only.** The lead was that
Newry and St. Thomas More, Roaring Spring share an administrator (Rev. Allen P. Zeth). The
Newry home page says so ("Sunday at St. Thomas More, Roaring Spring: 9:00 AM"), and the
ParishesOnline organization for St. Thomas More (825 Williams Street) lists "Fr. Allen Zeth -
Pastor/Administrator 814-695-3413", Newry's rectory number. But the Newry bulletin carries no
Roaring Spring schedule, intentions or staff list: four issues (18 Oct, 27 Sep, 6 Sep, 5 Jul
2026) have no "Roaring" hit, and the one "Thomas More" hit is a thank-you for a donation. The
Roaring Spring parish has no bulletin I could find (see parishes/saint-thomas-more-roaring-spring),
so it is not folded into this one. One shared pastor is not a shared bulletin.
