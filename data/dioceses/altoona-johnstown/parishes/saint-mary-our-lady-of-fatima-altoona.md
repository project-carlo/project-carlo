## Publisher

Fr. Mariusz Gorazd is pastor of St. Mary and administrator of Our Lady of Fatima (the
diocese directory lists him for both), and the two parishes publish **one** bulletin, "The
Marian", on the St. Mary website. The 18 October 2026 issue (4 pages) lists St. Mary's
Masses and "Weekend Mass at Our Lady of Fatima Church on Sunday 11:30 AM", and its weekday
intention prays for the members of both parishes. Fatima's own site published bulletins until
18 January 2026 and has not been updated since; I found no newer Fatima bulletin on that site,
on Discover Mass or on ParishesOnline. Both rows would otherwise have no current channel, so
this is one row; Fatima's old channel is silent and the St. Mary one is current.

## Worship sites

Two churches, both with Mass now: `saint-mary-altoona` (Immaculate Conception, 1405 5th
Avenue) and `our-lady-of-fatima-altoona` (2010 12th Avenue; the parish's own papers say 2000).
Nothing else is listed in the bulletin or the Mass schedule page. The St. Mary site has an
"Our Parish Buildings" page that I did not open; the bulletin's Mass list names only these two.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other` with `provider_id` `page-pdfs`: the St. Mary site is a Wix site whose home page links
"Weekly Bulletin" to a PDF on `filesusr.com/ugd/...`. The file name changes with each weekly
upload, so the resolver reads the page. `fetch:check` on 2026-10-21 resolved "page-pdfs on
https://stmaryschurchaltoona.wixsite.com/saintmarys: titled" to the 18 October 2026 issue, which
is the current one (336,840 bytes). The home page also links "Upcoming Events for 2026", another
PDF, which the resolver did not pick. The URL is found through the Discover Mass page's "Visit
Website" link for St. Mary, since the diocese directory gives no homepage for either church.
