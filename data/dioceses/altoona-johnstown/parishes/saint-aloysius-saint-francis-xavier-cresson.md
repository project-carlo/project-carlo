## Publisher

St. Aloysius Parish and St. Francis Xavier Parish, both in Cresson, share a pastor, Rev. Leo F. Arnone, and publish **one** weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (org `parishes-of-st-aloysius-st-francis-xavier`, LPi `0018000000QxAlrAAF`, container 14/1078). The 18 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-21, 5 pages, `container.parishesonline.com/bulletins/14/1078/20261018B.pdf`) names both parishes' addresses on page 1, is headed "Cresson Catholic Community of St. Aloysius Parish, St. Francis Xavier Parish & All Saints Catholic School", and carries one Mass schedule coded SA and FX, one ministries schedule and sanctuary lamps for both churches. Issues of 11 Oct, 4 Oct and 27 Sep are in the same container (probe po).

Both parishes' own homepages (Weebly) point at this same bulletin: the St. Aloysius site's "Weekly Bulletin" link goes to the PO org, and the St. Francis Xavier site embeds container 14/1078. Neither has a separate bulletin channel, so the two roster entries (and the two homepages) are **one** row, settling the batch lead.

## Worship sites

Two churches, both with Mass in the 18 Oct schedule: St. Aloysius (SA, 7911 Admiral Peary Highway) and St. Francis Xavier (FX, 211 Powell Avenue). Neither the directory nor GCatholic lists anything else in Cresson for these parishes. (Mount Aloysius College's chapel is separate: see `open/mount-aloysius-college-chapel-cresson`.)
