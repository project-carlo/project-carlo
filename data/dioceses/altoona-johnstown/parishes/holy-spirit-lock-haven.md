## Publisher

Holy Spirit Parish, Lock Haven (pastor Rev. Joseph T. Orr) publishes one weekly bulletin on its own WordPress site, https://holyspiritlockhaven.org/parish-news/sunday-bulletins/. The 18 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-21, 4 pages) is headed "HOLY SPIRIT PARISH STAFF" and its Mass schedule lists Immaculate Conception Church (310 West Water Street) and Saint Agnes Church (3 East Walnut Street). It is one parish, two churches, one bulletin.

Fr. Orr is also administrator of Saint Joseph Parish, Renovo (diocese clergy page). That does **not** make one bulletin: Renovo posts its own weekly bulletin on stjosephchurchrenovopa.org (e.g. /s/10-4-26.pdf, a different document with its own Mass intentions, lectors and announcements). The Lock Haven bulletin only prints Renovo's noon Sunday Mass as one line of its Mass schedule. Renovo stays its own row (rule: one live member channel means no merge).

## Worship sites

Immaculate Conception (Sunday 8:00 AM; Tue/Thu 8:30 AM) and Saint Agnes (Saturday 5:00 PM; Sunday 9:30 AM; Mon/Wed/Fri 8:30 AM), both from the 18 Oct 2026 bulletin and matching the diocese directory. St. Joseph, Renovo is a different parish row. Lock Haven University's Newman Center (445 W Main St; ParishesOnline org newman-center-17745, empty) appears in the bulletin only as "Campus Ministry Chris Klopp ... newmanlockhaven@gmail.com": no Newman Mass is in the bulletin schedule or the directory entries (I did not find a Newman page), so under the campus-Mass rule there is no record for it. The Tuesday "LHCS Mass 8:30 AM – IC" in the bulletin's events is the school Mass at Immaculate Conception, not a separate site.
