## Publisher

Four canonical parishes with one pastor, Rev. Joseph C. Nale (St. John the Baptist, Summerhill; Immaculate Conception, New Germany; Most Holy Trinity, South Fork; St. Bartholomew, Wilmore), publish **one** weekly bulletin, posted on the Summerhill/New Germany site stjic.org (WordPress; PDFs at `www.stjic.org/wp-content/uploads/<YYYY>/<MM>/<Month>-<D>-<YYYY>.pdf`, listed on https://www.stjic.org/bulletins/). The 18 October 2026 issue (2 pages, read 2026-10-21) is headed "Fr. Joseph C. Nale, Pastor", with the contact block for all four churches ("ST. JOHN THE BAPTIST 540 Main St. Summerhill, Pa ... IMMACULATE CONCEPTION 1640 New Germany Rd. New Germany, Pa ... MOST HOLY TRINITY 550 Main St. South Fork, Pa ... ST. BARTHOLOMEW 185 Church Hill, Wilmore, Pa") and one "MASSES FOR THE WEEK" list coded SJ, IC, MHT and SB, plus one liturgical-minister schedule, one offertory table and one Catholic Ministry Drive table covering all four. The 11 October issue has the identical masthead and a rota for all four. So there is one bulletin and one row, not four.

None of the other three has a separate channel: the old homepage of Most Holy Trinity (GCatholic's mostholytrinitychurch.com) now redirects to a domain-sale page, and the Discover Mass pages for Most Holy Trinity (`most-holy-trinity-south-fork-pa`), St. Bartholomew (`st-bartholomew-wilmore-pa`) and St. John the Baptist (`st-john-the-baptist-summerhill-pa`) all say "Shucks! There are no bulletins available" (read 2026-10-21). The diocese's directory gives no homepage for South Fork and Wilmore. Their Facebook pages are named in the bulletin ("Saint Bartholomew Catholic Church", "Most Holy Trinity Catholic Church"); not opened (login wall).

## Worship sites

Four churches, all in the bulletin's Mass list: St. John the Baptist (Summerhill), Immaculate Conception (New Germany, which the diocese's directory files under Summerhill), Most Holy Trinity (South Fork) and St. Bartholomew (Wilmore). Nothing the directory or GCatholic lists for these four parishes is left out. The Byzantine St. Michael the Archangel, South Fork (Discover Mass `st-michael-the-archangel-byzantine-south-fork-pa`) is Eastern Catholic and not part of this row.
