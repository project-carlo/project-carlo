## Publisher

Saint Joseph Parish, Renovo publishes its own weekly bulletin on its Squarespace site, http://www.stjosephchurchrenovopa.org/announcements (PDFs at /s/<M-D-YY>.pdf). The 4 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-21) is headed "CHURCH OF SAINT JOSEPH, 925 Huron Avenue, Renovo" and has its own Mass intentions and schedule. One parish, one church.

Its administrator Fr. Joseph T. Orr is also pastor of Holy Spirit Parish, Lock Haven. The Lock Haven bulletin (18 Oct 2026) lists "Sunday, 12:00 Noon – St. Joseph Church, Renovo" among its Mass times, but is a different document with Lock Haven's own intentions and events. Renovo's channel is live and distinct, so there is no merge: two rows.

## Worship sites

One church: St. Joseph, 925 Huron Avenue (Sunday noon Mass). The bulletin's candle list mentions a "Chapel Sanctuary" beside the "Church Sanctuary"; no separate chapel address or Mass is given, so I treated it as part of the same building. Hyner Cemetery (parish cemetery) is not a Mass site.
