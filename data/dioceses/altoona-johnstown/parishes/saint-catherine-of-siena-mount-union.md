## Publisher

St. Catherine of Siena Parish, Mount Union publishes its own weekly bulletin as a PDF on its own
site, www.scosmu.org (Homestead site builder). The issue read on 2026-10-21,
`http://www.scosmu.org/October_25.pdf` (Sunday 25 October 2026), is headed with the Bishop,
"Fr. Antony Sudherson, Administrator", Deacon Dr. John Roth and the Mount Union parish office
(203 West Market Street), and lists only Mount Union's Masses and ministers.

**Mount Union and Shade Gap: two bulletins.** The lead said the two share an administrator (Fr.
Sudherson, HGN). Both sites name him. But the Mount Union bulletin does not mention Shade Gap or
St. Mary at all (searched "Shade", "Orbison", "St. Mary", "Saint Mary" in the 18 and 25 October
issues), and Shade Gap has no bulletin of its own online (parishes/saint-mary-shade-gap). A
shared administrator does not make a shared bulletin, so Shade Gap is not folded in.

## Worship sites

One church, St. Catherine of Siena (churches/st-catherine-of-siena-mount-union). The
parish's old pages describe Shade Gap as a former mission (independent since 1962 or 1969,
the site gives both); it now has its own row.
