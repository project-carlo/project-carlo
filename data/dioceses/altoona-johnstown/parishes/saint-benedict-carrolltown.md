## Publisher

Saint Benedict Parish, Carrolltown (pastor Fr. Michael J. Gabler, O.S.B., St. Vincent Archabbey) publishes its own weekly bulletin, carried on its Discover Mass page `st-benedict-carrolltown-pa` and as PDFs on its own site. I read the 25 Oct 2026 issue (PDF on the parish site): the masthead and every Mass line name this parish only. Its only mention of any neighbour is an announcement "From other parishes" of a St. Bernard, Hastings dinner, so this is not a shared bulletin.

Batch lead was to check whether the three Benedictine parishes of north Cambria share a bulletin. They do not: Carrolltown (Gabler), Hastings (Fr. Thaddeus Rettger, O.S.B., per the diocese directory) and Nicktown (Fr. Jeremiah G. Lange, O.S.B., read from its own 25 Oct bulletin cover) have three pastors and the Carrolltown and Nicktown bulletins each name only their own parish.

## Worship sites

Two places: St. Benedict Church, 100 Main Street (weekly Masses), and St. Joseph Mission Church, Strip Road (Mass a few times a year; see its record). GCatholic lists the latter as "St. Joseph, Carrolltown" (gcatholic:148967, a mission of St. Benedict); the diocese directory does not. Not here: St. Benedict's cemeteries (St. Benedict, Sacred Heart, St. Joseph), which the bulletin names as the Cemetery Association's.
