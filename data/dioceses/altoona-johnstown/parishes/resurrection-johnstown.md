## Publisher

Resurrection Parish publishes one weekly bulletin on its Weebly site, and the same issue is the
bulletin of St. Clare of Assisi Parish, which has none of its own. The 18 October 2026 issue
(read 2026-10-21, page 1) has a single cover that gives the offices, church addresses and Mass
schedules of both parishes under "Rev. George M. Gulash, Pastor/Administrator", with directors
"for both Parishes". Its weekly Mass table lists Resurrection Church, Resurrection Oratory and St. Clare of Assisi, and page 2 prints both
parishes' collections. St. Clare's own WebParish site has no bulletin: it lists the joint Mass
times and sends "the lastest news" to the Resurrection site. One bulletin, so one row. This
follows the Johnstown partner-bulletin pattern (see diocese.md).

## Worship sites

Two churches with public Mass: Resurrection (324 Chestnut Street) and St. Clare of Assisi (124
Maple Avenue). The Resurrection Oratory (721 Chestnut Street) has only a weekday Rosary (not
Mass) in the bulletin and on the site, so it is an exclusion (`resurrection-oratory-johnstown`).
