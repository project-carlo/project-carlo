## Publisher

No bulletin found. Saints Gregory and Barnabas Parish (pastor Rev. Callistus Elue) has no website of its own: the directory lists none, the old ssgregbar.org does not resolve, its WebParish page (webparish.com/aj/GregoryandBarnabas) is a stub with no bulletin, its Discover Mass page (`st-gregory-johnstown-pa`) says "no bulletins available", and the ParishesOnline organizations for the parish and for St. Barnabas are empty. Its only public online presence found is a Facebook page, behind a login wall here.

Searched on 2026-10-21: `probe url` on the old and WebParish sites; `probe po` on both ParishesOnline organizations; Discover Mass for Gregory, Anne, Therese and Visitation (the Johnstown neighbours without homepages); DuckDuckGo searches for the parish, its pastor and its two street addresses (they return only listing sites such as catholicmasstime.org and the directory pages); and the latest bulletins of the neighbouring Johnstown parishes read in this and earlier batches (St. Therese, St. Anne and Visitation read now; St. Benedict and Resurrection from batch 13's text copies), none of which carries a Gregory and Barnabas schedule. Whether one of the other Johnstown bulletins I did not read (St. Clement, St. Francis of Assisi, the cathedral) carries it is not settled, but nothing links them to this pastor.

## Worship sites

Two: St. Gregory, 120 Boltz Street, and St. Barnabas, 408 Luzon Avenue (Bon Air). Both appear in the directory, GCatholic, the Discover Mass page and third-party Mass-time listings (catholicmasstime.org: "St. Gregory: -Saturday 4:00 PM and Sunday 10:00 AM ... St. Barnabas: -Sunday - 8 AM", weekdays "Monday 5:30 PM & Tuesday 8:00 AM" and "Thursday 5:30 PM & Friday 8:00 AM", agreeing with the directory). Mass times are therefore from the directory only, not from a bulletin.
