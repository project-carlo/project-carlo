## Publisher

St. Therese of the Child Jesus publishes its own weekly bulletin on the shared parish WordPress site sttheresejohnstown.com. The 18 Oct 2026 issue (`STTHERESESBULLETIN-Oct-18-2026-BP.pdf`, read 2026-10-21) is headed "ST. THERESE OF THE CHILD JESUS / THE CHURCH OF THE FRANCISCANS / FR. BERNARD KARMANOCKY, OFM, PASTOR" and lists Mass intentions only for St. Therese (Mon-Fri 7:50AM, Sat 4:00PM, Sun 7:30AM and 10:30AM). No other church's Masses appear in it.

**One bulletin or two?** The pastor and the website are shared with St. Anne (Fr. Karmanocky is pastor of both), which suggested one bulletin. It is two: the site publishes a separate PDF for each church every week (`STTHERESESBULLETIN-<Mon>-<D>-2026-BP.pdf` and `St-Annes-Bulletin-<Mon>-<D>.pdf`, Aug through Oct 2026 on the Bulletins page). The 18 Oct St. Anne issue is headed "ST. ANNE'S CHURCH", with its own intentions (Sat 5:30PM, Sun 9:00AM, weekdays 7:00AM), lectors and notes. The two issues share the Paluch reflection and some announcements, but each is a separate file naming its own church, so by the rules they are separate rows (this one and `saint-anne-johnstown`).
