## Publisher

Our Mother of Sorrows (OMOS) publishes one weekly bulletin on its WordPress site, and the
bulletin is shared with St. Michael Parish. The cover of the 18 October 2026 issue (scanned
image, page 1 rendered and read) is titled "OUR MOTHER OF SORROWS & ST. MICHAEL THE
ARCHANGEL" and gives the St. Michael church address (180 Gilbert St.). The 27 September
text-layer issue shows the same title block. St. Michael has no website of its own: omostoday.com
has an "St. Michael's" page with its Mass schedule. The directory gives it no homepage. One
bulletin, so one row.

## Worship sites

Two: Our Mother of Sorrows (407 Tioga Street, Westmont) and St. Michael (180 Gilbert Street,
Brownstown), both with current Masses on the site's schedule pages and in the bulletin.
