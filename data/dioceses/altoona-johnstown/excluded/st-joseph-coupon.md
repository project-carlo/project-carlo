## Why it is excluded

**St. Joseph, Coupon (3236 Coupon-Gallitzin Rd) -- no Mass since 2009.** The Tribune-Democrat reported that fire destroyed the century-old church on Sunday 21 September 2008 and that about 300 parishioners "will begin attending Mass in the nearby social hall"; the diocese would not discuss rebuilding. By its 18 October 2009 article the pastor, Rev. Brian Saylor, had been transferred, no priest was named, and members were "urged to attend Mass at St. Demetrius Catholic Church in Gallitzin and St. Thomas Aquinas Catholic Church, Ashville" (some went to St. Monica, Chest Springs): "We still have everything going, except we don't have Mass." So the last Mass in the social hall was in 2009 (no day given; `last_mass` is the year).

What is not sourced: a formal suppression decree. The diocesan spokesman said in 2009 there was "no formal decision about the future of the parish". Seventeen years on, the diocese's directory (87 parishes), GCatholic and the Discover Mass site list no Coupon church, and the ParishesOnline organization st-joseph-church-16629 is empty; that is absence, and the closure rests on the 2009 reports of Mass having ceased. No current source was found showing Mass at Coupon (the old WebParish stub, webparish.com/aj/Joseph-Coupon, has only an address and phone). Aggregators (catholicchurch.directory) still list "St. Joseph Parish, Coupon" with no times.

This is not a mission of any roster row: it is in neither roster, and the Ashville (St. Thomas Aquinas) row's own bulletin search turned up only this empty ParishesOnline organization.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule listing Mass at Coupon (the social hall may still be used occasionally), or the diocese saying the parish is still active.
