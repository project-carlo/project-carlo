## Why it is excluded

Holy Cross, 534 Woodland Ave, Johnstown (ParishesOnline lists it as "Holy Cross National Catholic" next to St. Anne) is a Polish National Catholic Church parish, not part of the Roman Catholic Church and not of this diocese. It is a real, current church: MapQuest's listing (read 2026-10-21) says it "offers regular Sunday Mass at 11 am" and a Facebook page snippet dated 5 Apr 2026 names its administrator; a January 2024 post says its new priest would say Mass at 10:00 AM. The name "National Catholic" is the PNCC's own. Its Masses are therefore not Roman Catholic Masses. Not in the diocese's directory or GCatholic's list for the diocese (checked 2026-10-21).

What was inferred: that it is PNCC comes from the listings' wording ("Polish National Catholic Church") and the Facebook page title; the parish's own site (holycrosspncc.org appeared in a search result) was not opened.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; the PNCC is not in communion with Rome.
