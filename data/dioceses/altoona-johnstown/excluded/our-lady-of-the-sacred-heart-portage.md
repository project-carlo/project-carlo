## Why it is excluded

**Our Lady of the Sacred Heart (former Sacred Heart of Jesus), 601 Mountain Ave., Portage: closed, building demolished April 2021; no exact last-Mass date found.** The parish of that name merged with St. Joseph Parish on 17 August 2015 to form Holy Family, which now worships only at 509 Caldwell Ave. (the former St. Joseph). The parish's own history page says so; the Observer-Reporter of 12 March 2017 reports "Mass is now held in the former St. Joseph building on Caldwell Avenue" and "Both Sacred Heart parish church buildings remain vacant and are listed for sale"; a Tribune-Democrat story dated 15 April 2021 (via Yahoo) says demolition crews "began working this week at the former Sacred Heart of Jesus Church on Mountain Avenue". Holy Family's 18 Oct 2026 bulletin lists Masses only at the one church (no Mountain Ave. site). GCatholic still lists it as a church (`23877`); it lags. (The Sacred Heart Ushers Club still named in the bulletin is a parish club, not a place of Mass.) The other former Portage church, Assumption of the BVM on Hammer St., is not in any roster; the 2017 article shows it vacant too.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building is gone. The last Mass date is somewhere between the merger (August 2015) and the 2017 article; the parish office (814-736-4279) could date it.
