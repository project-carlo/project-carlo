## Why it is excluded

The Resurrection Oratory, 721 Chestnut Street, is a parish chapel beside the parish center. The 18
October 2026 bulletin and St. Clare's Mass Time Schedule page (both read 2026-10-21) list it only
for a public Rosary on Mondays and Fridays at noon; every Mass of the week is at Resurrection
Church or St. Clare. It is a sanctuary (the bulletin carries an Oratory sanctuary light) but no
public Mass is listed there. This is evidence from one week's bulletin plus the schedule page.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule that lists a Mass at the Oratory (for example a weekday or holy day Mass
when the church is in use). Then add it as a church of `resurrection-johnstown`.
