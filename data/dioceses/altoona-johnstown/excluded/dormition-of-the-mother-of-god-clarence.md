## Why it is excluded

Dormition of the Mother of God, 104 Byzantine Ln., Clarence, Pennsylvania 16829-0304, is an Eastern Catholic church under the Ruthenian (Byzantine) Catholic Metropolitan Church of Pittsburgh (GCatholic: "Ruthenian Metropolitanate sui juris of Pittsburgh"), rite Ruthenian (Byzantine), not the Roman Catholic Diocese of Altoona-Johnstown, although it stands in the diocese's territory. The GCatholic page (read 2026-10-21) gives "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Metropolitanate sui juris of Pittsburgh" and places it "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Altoona-Johnstown". It is not in the diocese's own parish directory.

## What would change this

Nothing; it could be added under its own eparchy if the dataset covers Eastern Catholic churches.
