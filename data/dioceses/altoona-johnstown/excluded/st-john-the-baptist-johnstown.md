## Why it is excluded

St. John the Baptist, 606 Maple St., Johnstown, Pennsylvania 15901, is an Eastern Catholic church under the Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Saint Josaphat in Parma (GCatholic: "Ukrainian Diocese of Saint Josaphat in Parma"), rite Ukrainian (Byzantine), not the Roman Catholic Diocese of Altoona-Johnstown, although it stands in the diocese's territory. The GCatholic page (read 2026-10-21) gives "Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Diocese of Saint Josaphat in Parma" and places it "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Altoona-Johnstown". It is not in the diocese's own parish directory.

## What would change this

Nothing; it could be added under its own eparchy if the dataset covers Eastern Catholic churches.
