## Why it is excluded

**Carmel of St. Therese of Lisieux (Discalced Carmelite nuns), Loretto -- open public Mass, no bulletin.** The monastery's own site (read 2026-10-21) lists "Sunday and Daily Mass: 7:45 am" and "All services are open to the public", with Sunday Benediction at 4:00 pm. A Saint Francis University page (lead from an earlier batch, not re-read here) also says "Mass everyday at 7:45am"; a findmymass.com listing (search snippet) gives Sunday 7:45. So this is a public Mass site, not `no-public-mass`. It is under no parish row: it is a monastery chapel, not a worship site of the Basilica of St. Michael, Loretto, whose bulletin (page-pdfs of basilicasm-loretto.org/bulletins.html) was not found to list it. Nothing else publishes its schedule as a bulletin; the monastery has a news and novena page and a Facebook page only.

The street address was not read (the site's contact page returned 403 to curl); the record has the town and ZIP only. The Mass time comes from the schedule page and could differ on Holy Days.

## What would change this

A bulletin that lists the Carmel's Masses (the Basilica's, or a monastery bulletin) would make it a church record under that row. The address and coordinates (GCatholic, OSM or the contact page) are needed first.
