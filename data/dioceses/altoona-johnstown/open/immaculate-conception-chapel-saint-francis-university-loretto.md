## What is known

Saint Francis University (Franciscan Friars T.O.R., Loretto) offers daily Mass in the Immaculate Conception Chapel, Sunday 10 am and 8 pm in term (SFU Campus Ministry pages, read 2026-10-21; the two pages differ slightly on weekday times). The Basilica's 18 Oct 2026 bulletin only mentions Fr. Stephen Waruszewski, TOR, "Director of Campus Ministry at St. Francis University" as a speaker; it does not list a campus Mass. The friary has no separate listing.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing read says the public may attend, no bulletin lists the Masses and the chapel has no street number of its own (OpenStreetMap, read 2026-10-21 through Nominatim, maps it as way 1363839974, "Immaculate Conception Chapel, Franciscan Way, Loretto, 15940", at 40.50389, -78.63693). Same treatment as Juniata College's campus Mass. Asking Campus Ministry would settle it.
