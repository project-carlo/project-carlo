## What is known

The diocese directory's entry for Most Holy Trinity, Huntingdon lists a Sunday 6:00 PM Mass held "at
Juniata College". The college's own Catholic Campus Ministry page (read 2026-10-21) says Mass is on
campus at 6 pm Sundays "in the Global Commons of Tussey Hall", and its worship page (a search result,
not opened) says "during the academic year". The ministry is an office of the diocese. Neither Most
Holy Trinity's 18 October 2026 newsletter nor its site lists this Mass, so it is not in the parish's
bulletin schedule.

## Why it is not decided

It is a campus Mass in a student common room, in term time only. The policy counts campus chapels and
any place with public Mass, but nothing read says non-students may attend, and the place has no
street address of its own, only the college's (Tussey Hall is a campus building). Asking the campus
minister would settle it; if public, add a church under most-holy-trinity-huntingdon with the
college's address.
