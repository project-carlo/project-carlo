## What is known

The Basilica's summer schedule (18 Oct 2026 issue, read 2026-10-21) lists a Sunday 7:00 PM Mass "(Shrine)" between Memorial Day and Labor Day: the Shrine of Our Lady of the Alleghenies, which the bulletin masthead names as part of the Basilica's home. It is out of season now. The bulletin does not give the shrine's address; whether it is a separate building from the Basilica is not read.

## Why it is not decided

A seasonal Mass at a place that may be on the Basilica's grounds. If it is a separate worship site, it counts (four Masses a year is enough), but no source read gives its address or says where the Mass is held. Read the June 2027 bulletin and the Basilica site; if separate, add a church under `basilica-of-saint-michael-the-archangel-loretto`. The Prince Gallitzin Chapel House also named in the masthead lists no Mass in this issue.
