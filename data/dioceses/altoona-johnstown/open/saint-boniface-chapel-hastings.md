## What is known

GCatholic lists a "Chapel of St. Boniface" at 1278 Main St., Hastings, PA 16646 (id 23837), a mission of St. Bernard, Hastings; the point is in the village of Saint Boniface, about 2.4 km east of St. Bernard Church, where there is also a St. Boniface Cemetery. The diocese's directory lists only St. Bernard for the parish. Saint Bernard Parish has no bulletin the pipeline can read (see its `no_bulletin` record), and the Carrolltown and Nicktown bulletins do not mention the chapel. The searches (Discover Mass, ParishesOnline, WebParish stub, DuckDuckGo, findglocal's mirror of the Facebook page) show no Mass time, event or closure for it. Nothing found says it closed either.

## Why it is not decided

No source states a Mass at the chapel, so it cannot be a church row; no source states that it closed, so it cannot be excluded (absence from the directory is weak: the directory also omits St. Joseph Mission, Carrolltown, which does hold Mass a few times a year). It may be a cemetery chapel with an occasional Mass.
