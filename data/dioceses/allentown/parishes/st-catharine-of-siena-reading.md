## Publisher

St. Catharine of Siena Parish (now on Boyertown Pike, Exeter Township, Reading) publishes
one weekly bulletin, posted to Discover Mass and, as a 6-page PDF, to its own WordPress
site. I read the 18 October 2026 issue in both forms. It is headed "St. Catharine of Siena
Parish, Reading, Pennsylvania, October 18, 2026" and covers two worship sites: the parish
church (weekday 7:15 a.m. Mass; the weekend schedule is on the parish's Mass-schedule page:
Saturday 4:30 pm, Sunday 7:30, 9:30 and 11:30 am) and St. Mary's, 12th Street, Reading,
whose own box on page 2 ("St. Mary's Masses") lists Saturday 3:30 pm and Sunday 10:30 am
Masses with intentions for 17/18 and 24/25 October. The diocesan map lists St. Mary as
"St. Mary Church Worship Site for St. Catharine of Siena Parish" with the same times.

## Worship sites

- St. Catharine of Siena, 4975 Boyertown Pike, Reading (`st-catharine-of-siena-reading`).
- St. Mary, 248 South 12th Street, Reading (`st-mary-reading`): the former Polish parish,
  a worship site of St. Catharine's. It also hosts a monthly Sunday 12:30 pm Traditional
  Latin Mass (Berks TLM newsletter, August 2026).

GCatholic's St. Catharine entry (23651) still has the parish's earlier address, 2427
Perkiomen Avenue, Mount Penn; it is linked to the current church (see that file).
