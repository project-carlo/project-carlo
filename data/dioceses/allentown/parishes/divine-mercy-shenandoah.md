## Publisher

Divine Mercy Parish, Shenandoah (formed 28 July 2014 from seven parishes in Shenandoah and
Lost Creek) publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (church-bulletin.org
id 218). The 18 October 2026 issue (8 pages, all images) has the parish's masthead (108 West
Cherry St., Msgr. Ronald C. Bocian, pastor) and a Mass schedule in which every Mass is coded
"DM": weekdays 7:00 am, Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am and 12:30 pm (Spanish). The schedule
on page 1 is for the coming week (the 18 October issue covers 19–25 October).

## Worship sites

- `divine-mercy-shenandoah`: Divine Mercy Church, the former Annunciation B.V.M. church on
  West Cherry Street ("the new Divine Mercy Parish at the former Annunciation Church
  building", 2014). Mass every day.
- `st-casimir-shenandoah` (229 N. Jardin St.): a **seasonal** worship site. The 2014 decree
  retained it "for sacred worship"; the bulletins put the Sunday morning Masses there in the
  warm months ("SC": 7:30 and 10:00 am in the 21 June, 19 July and 6 September 2026 issues;
  9:00 am on 20 and 27 September) and move them to Divine Mercy Church for the winter (the
  19 October 2025 issue: "Beginning Monday, October 20th, all Masses will be held at Divine
  Mercy Church due to high cost of oil"; in 2026 the move came on 4 October). Its last Mass so
  far was 27 September 2026.

Ruled out: St. Stephen, St. Stanislaus and Our Lady of Mount Carmel (shuttered in 2014), St.
George (closed) are in `excluded/`; St. Mary Magdalen, Lost Creek, is in `open/` (no source
yet says the building closed, though no current schedule lists it). GCatholic's St. George
entry shares the Annunciation building's address and Plus Code. The "Father Walter J. Ciszek
Chapel" next door has Wednesday adoration only in the bulletin (no Mass), so it is no row.
St. Michael (Ukrainian Catholic, 114 S. Chestnut St.) is another jurisdiction.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`. dmparish.com (a John Patrick Publishing WordPress site, the diocese map's website)
links "Current Bulletin" to http://www.jppc.net/onlinebulletins/218template.pdf, which
redirects to church-bulletin.org's dated listing; its own /bulletin-archive/ page holds 2025
PDFs (`Bulletin-JP-0218-25_<MMDD>-pdf.pdf`). `fetch_check_rows.py` on 2026-10-18 picked
2026-10-18.pdf ("page-pdfs on http://www.jppc.net/onlinebulletins/218template.pdf: current"),
this week's issue, so the recipe is pinned. **Since 30 August 2026 the issues are image-only**
(the issues sampled from October 2025 to 23 August 2026 have a text layer; 30 August onward are 8 pages of pictures), so
the pipeline must read them as images.
