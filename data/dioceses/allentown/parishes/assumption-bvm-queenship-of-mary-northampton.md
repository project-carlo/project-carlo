## Publisher

Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary and Queenship of Mary, the two Northampton (borough)
parishes, share one pastor (Rev. Patrick H. Lamb, per the diocesan directory and the
bulletin) and **one weekly bulletin**. I read the 18 October 2026 issue in ParishesOnline
container 04/0555: its page-1 masthead lists both churches with their rectories, staff and two
Mass-time boxes, and page 2 has a Mass-intentions column for each church. The diocese lists
them as two parishes (`/places/assumption-bvm-northampton` and `/places/queenship-of-mary`).

The same issue is published twice: in ParishesOnline (org
`queenship-of-mary-assumption-of-the-blessed-virgin-mary-church`, linked from
queenshipofmary.org) and as `files.ecatholic.com/16004/bulletins/20261018.pdf` on Assumption's
eCatholic site, bvm-northampton.com. Both PDFs have the same masthead, Mass times and length,
so they are one bulletin and one row; both ids are in `publication`.

## Worship sites

- Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary, Washington Avenue at 22nd Street, Northampton
  (`assumption-bvm-northampton`): Saturday 5:30 PM, Sunday 8:00 AM.
- Queenship of Mary, 1308 Newport Avenue, Northampton (`queenship-of-mary-northampton`):
  Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 10:30 AM.

The bulletin's two Mass-time boxes are not labelled with the church names in the text layer;
I paired them with the churches by the intentions columns (Queenship's column has the Friday
9:15 school Mass, Good Shepherd School being at 1300 Newport Avenue) and by the diocesan
directory, which gives Assumption "Saturday 5:30 p.m., Sunday 8 a.m." and Queenship
"Saturday 3:30 p.m., Sunday 10:30 a.m." (its Saturday time is out of date against the
bulletin's 4:00 PM).
