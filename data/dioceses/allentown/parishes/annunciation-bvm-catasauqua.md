## Publisher

Annunciation of the Blessed Virgin Mary ("St. Mary's"), 122 Union Street, Catasauqua,
publishes a weekly bulletin as Google Drive PDFs linked from its homepage. St. Mary's and
St. John Fisher, North Catasauqua, are two canonical parishes with one pastor (the
diocesan map names Fr. Eric N. Tolentino for both; the bulletin's Pastor's Corner is now
signed Fr. Gruber) and one Mass schedule: the homepage's "Mass Schedule for St. Mary's
And St. John Fisher: Effective August 3, 2026", and the 18 October 2026 bulletin's Mass
intentions, which mark every Mass St. Mary's or St. John Fisher.

The bulletin is St. Mary's own (its financial report, mission statement and events are
St. Mary's), but it carries St. John Fisher's whole Mass schedule. St. John Fisher has no
bulletin of its own that could be found: no website in the diocesan map, its old domain
is squatted by spam, Discover Mass has "no bulletins", and no ParishesOnline organization
under its name was found. So St. John Fisher's church is listed here, under the bulletin
that schedules it. If St. John Fisher turns out to publish its own bulletin, it becomes a
row of its own.

## Worship sites

- `annunciation-bvm-catasauqua` (St. Mary's), 122 Union Street, Catasauqua: Tuesday,
  Thursday and Saturday 8 AM, Saturday 6 PM, Sunday 8 AM.
- `st-john-fisher-north-catasauqua`, Third Street, North Catasauqua (the former St.
  Andrew's church): Monday and Friday 8 AM, Wednesday 6 PM, Saturday 4 PM, Sunday 10:30
  AM.

The former St. Lawrence the Martyr church, Catasauqua, closed on 28 October 2022
(`excluded/st-lawrence-the-martyr-catasauqua`).
