## Publisher

The Cathedral Parish of Saint Catharine of Siena publishes its own weekly bulletin through
John Patrick Publishing (church-bulletin.org, parish number 0226). The 18 October 2026
issue, the newest on 18 October, has the cathedral's masthead on page 1 (1825 West Turner
St., Msgr. Donald W. Cieniewicz, Rector) and on page 2 one Mass schedule: daily Monday to
Friday 6:30 and 8 AM, Wednesday 12:15 PM, Saturday 8 AM, Saturday vigil 4:15 PM, Sunday
7:30, 9:30 and 11:30 AM. It names no other worship site.

The cathedral shares its religious-education program with St. Francis of Assisi (the St.
Francis bulletin says PREP "has been combined with the Cathedral"), but St. Francis
publishes its own bulletin (`st-francis-of-assisi-allentown`), so they are separate rows.
