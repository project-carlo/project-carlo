## Publisher

St. Peter the Apostle Parish, Reading, publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick
Publishing for its two churches. I read the 18 October 2026 issue: the masthead is "Saint
Peter the Apostle Roman Catholic Parish … SS. Cyril & Methodius Church, worship site", and
the Mass schedule places the Sunday 10:00 am English Mass at "C&M, 451 S. 6th St." and the
rest (Saturday 4:00 pm; Sunday 8:00 am, noon and 6:30 pm Spanish; weekdays) at St. Peter.
