## Publisher

Our Lady Help of Christians, 444 N. Jasper Street, Allentown, publishes its own weekly
bulletin through Discover Mass (J.S. Paluch). The 18 October 2026 issue has its own
masthead, Mass intentions and financial report; page 1 says it is "Twinned with Ss. Simon
& Jude Church" in Bethlehem, which has the same pastor (Msgr. William F. Baver), deacons
and office. Its Mass schedule is the twinned one: at OLHC, Sunday 10:30 AM and Monday and
Wednesday 8 AM; the other Masses it prints (Saturday 5 PM, Sunday 9 AM, Tuesday,
Thursday and Saturday 8 AM) are at Ss. Simon and Jude.

Ss. Simon and Jude publishes a separate Discover Mass bulletin with its own masthead and
finances (row `ss-simon-and-jude-bethlehem`, Northampton region). Two bulletins, two
rows. The same 10:30 OLHC Mass is printed in both.
