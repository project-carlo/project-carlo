## Publisher

No bulletin found. St. Teresa of Calcutta Parish, Mahanoy City (formerly Blessed Teresa of
Calcutta) has no website in the diocese's map, and its page there refers readers to a bulletin
("Holy Days: Consult bulletin"), so one probably exists on paper. Checked on 2026-10-18:
Discover Mass (listing under the old name: "There are currently no bulletins available"),
ParishesOnline (`st-teresa-of-calcutta-parish` is Collingswood, NJ; Mahanoy City slug guesses
don't exist), web searches for the parish's bulletin (nothing), and stteresacalcutta.com
(St. Teresa of Calcutta, Limerick, Archdiocese of Philadelphia, not this parish). The row's
`website` is the diocese's page for the parish, because the parish has none.

## Worship sites

- `st-teresa-of-calcutta-mahanoy-city`: the parish church on West Mahanoy Avenue, with
  weekend Masses Saturday 4 pm, Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 am per the diocese's page.

The Missionaries of Charity convent named for St. Teresa of Calcutta in Mahanoy City closed
(its stained glass was sold in 2019) and is in no roster. St. Mary (Ruthenian Byzantine, 621
W. Mahanoy Ave.) is another jurisdiction (`excluded/`). The St. Mary/St. Joseph (Ringtown)
bulletin of October 2026 mentions a Knights of Columbus holy hour "in Mahanoy City, St.
Canicus"; St. Canicus is a name for a building in this parish (possibly the parish church
itself: the parish's rectory is at 22 S. Catawissa St., an address MapQuest gives for "St
Canicus Catholic Church"), not settled here.
