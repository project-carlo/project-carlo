## Publisher

Holy Ghost, 417 Carlton Avenue, Bethlehem, is the diocese's parish for the traditional Latin
Mass, run by the Priestly Fraternity of St. Peter (FSSP). It publishes one weekly bulletin
through John Patrick Publishing (church-bulletin.org). I read the 18 October 2026 issue: the
page-1 masthead is "HOLY GHOST PARISH, Roman Catholic Church of the Tridentine Rite", with
Sunday Masses at 7:00, 8:30 and 10:30 AM and daily Masses at 7:00 and 8:00 AM.
