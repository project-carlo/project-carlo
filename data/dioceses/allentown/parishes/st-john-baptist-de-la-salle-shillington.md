## Publisher

St. John Baptist de la Salle Parish, Shillington, publishes its own weekly bulletin through
Bon Venture Services. I read the 18 October 2026 issue: page 1 (an image) is the parish's
masthead with one schedule (Saturday Vigil 4:00 pm; Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 am), and page 3's
Mass intentions name no other place.

St. Anthony of Padua, Millmont (Reading), consolidated into this parish in 2015 and its
building passed to the Ukrainian Catholic parish (see
`excluded/st-anthony-of-padua-reading.md`).
