St. John the Baptist, 3024 S. Ruch Street, Whitehall (Stiles), publishes its own weekly
bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (number 208). The 18 October 2026 masthead names
this church alone; its only weekend Mass is Sunday 8 AM, and weekday Masses are at 8 AM,
some of them "at St. Peter" (Coplay). St. Peter has the same pastor but its own bulletin
(`st-peter-coplay`). Two rows.
