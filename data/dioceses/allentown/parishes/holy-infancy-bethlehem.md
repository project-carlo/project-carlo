## Publisher

Holy Infancy, 312 East Fourth Street, South Bethlehem, publishes its own weekly bulletin
through LPi / ParishesOnline (container 03/1407). I read the 18 October 2026 issue (pages 1-5
are images; read rendered): the page-1 masthead is "HOLY INFANCY CATHOLIC CHURCH" alone, and
page 2's Schedule of Masses gives Sunday 7:00 (English), 8:15 (Portuguese), 10:00 (Spanish)
and 12:00 (English).

Its pastor, Rev. Andrew Gehringer, is also pastor of Incarnation of Our Lord, 617 Pierce
Street, and this bulletin points its readers to Incarnation's Saturday 4:15 PM Vigil. But
Incarnation publishes its **own** bulletin on its own site (see
`incarnation-of-our-lord-bethlehem`, latest issue "October 3rd – 11th, 2026", with a
different masthead, secretary and collection report), so the two are separate rows. The
Saturday Vigil is Incarnation's Mass and is recorded under that row.
