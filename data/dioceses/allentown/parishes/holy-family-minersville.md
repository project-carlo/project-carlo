## Publisher

Holy Family Parish of Minersville publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline
(container 03/1413). I read the 18 October 2026 issue. The page-1 masthead is "HOLY FAMILY
PARISH OF MINERSVILLE", with the parish office at 538 Sunbury Street. Its Mass schedule
covers two churches: Saturday 4:00pm at St. Matthew and 5:30pm at St. Michael; Sunday 8:30am
at St. Michael and 10:00am at St. Matthew; weekdays alternate between them. The parish was
formed on 1 July 2020 from St. Matthew the Evangelist and St. Michael the Archangel. AD Today
announced it on 21 June 2020: "Both the St. Matthew and the St. Michael church buildings will
remain open". St. Matthew's old ParishesOnline org has no publications, so this is the only
bulletin. One row.

## Worship sites

- St. Matthew the Evangelist, Spruce St. (`st-matthew-minersville`).
- St. Michael the Archangel, 541 Sunbury St. (`st-michael-minersville`).

The diocesan parish map has one entry for the parish ("Holy Family Parish", 538 Sunbury
Street, the parish office), and its page lists both churches with their weekend Masses. That
entry is linked to St. Michael, whose building is across the street from the office and next
to the map's pin. GCatholic still lists the two churches under their own names.

Ruled out: **St. Francis of Assisi church, Minersville** (ParishesOnline lists a "St. Francis
Church" at 320 Spruce St with no publications). St. Matthew's committees recommended closing
it in June 2020, saying it had been open "only for one Mass per year". The 18 October 2026
bulletin doesn't mention it. It's in neither roster, and I found no source that confirms the
closure, so it has no record of its own. The Ukrainian St. Nicholas (Minersville and its
"St. Nicholas Hall, Primrose", mentioned in page-5 community news) and the Ruthenian Sts.
Peter and Paul are Eastern Catholic and excluded as `other-jurisdiction`.
