## Publisher

Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish (Roseto; mailing address 560 N. 6th Street, Bangor) publishes
one weekly bulletin on its own WordPress site, olmcroseto.org, numbered issue by issue
(`<n>-Mount-Carmel-Bulletin-<Mon>-<D>-<YYYY>.doc-pdf.pdf`). I read issues 142 (11 October
2026) and 143 (18 October 2026): the masthead is "OUR LADY OF MOUNT CARMEL CHURCH" with Rev.
Daniel Kravatz as pastor, and the schedule lists Saturday 4:30 pm and Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 am
at the one church.

The Slate Belt parishes of Our Lady of Good Counsel (Bangor) and Our Lady of Mount Carmel
(Roseto) merged on 31 January 2023, keeping both churches and closing the Chapel of St. Vincent
de Paul in Portland (Lehigh Valley Public Media, 15 January 2023). Until February 2026 the
bulletin was headed "OUR LADY OF GOOD COUNSEL AND OUR LADY OF MOUNT CARMEL"; Good Counsel's
final Mass was Saturday 7 February 2026, and from the 15 February issue the masthead names
Mount Carmel alone.
