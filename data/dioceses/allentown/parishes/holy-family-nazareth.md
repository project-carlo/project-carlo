## Publisher

Holy Family Parish, Nazareth publishes its own weekly bulletin through LPi / ParishesOnline
(container 03/1198). I read the 18 October 2026 issue: page 1 names "Holy Family Catholic
Church, 23 Forest Drive, Nazareth PA 18064" (the rectory/office address) with Rev. Jason
Stokes as pastor, and page 3's Mass intentions give Saturday 5:00 PM and Sunday 7:00, 9:00
and 11:00 AM, weekdays 8:30 AM. No other parish appears in the masthead. The diocese does not
group it with any other parish (its `/places/holy-family` page lists it alone).
