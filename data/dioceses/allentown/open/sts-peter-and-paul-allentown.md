## What is known

Ss. Peter and Paul Parish, Fullerton Avenue, Allentown, was consolidated into St.
Elizabeth of Hungary, Whitehall, effective 25 April 2021 (AD Today, 22 March 2021). The
announcement said the church building would stay open for parishioners' funerals and for
Mass on 29 June and on the anniversary of the church's dedication, and St. Elizabeth's
history calls it "now a worship center". GCatholic still lists it (1061 Fullerton Ave.;
AD Today says 1065). The diocesan map does not list it. St. Elizabeth's 2026 bulletins
read here show no Mass there, not even on 29 June 2026, and its old domain is for sale.

## Why it is not decided

Two Masses a year would make it a church, but no 2026 source shows one; equally, no
source says it closed or was sold. Absence from St. Elizabeth's bulletins is weak
evidence. See `revisit`.
