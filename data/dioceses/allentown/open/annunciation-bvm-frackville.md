## What is known

Annunciation B.V.M. (Lithuanian), Frackville is one of the three parishes merged into St.
Joseph the Worker (the diocese's map: "St. Joseph is the repository for records of
Annunciation B.V.M. and St. Ann, Frackville"). GCatholic places its church at 7 S. Broad
Mountain Ave., which the diocese's map now gives as St. Joseph the Worker's business office;
Discover Mass has an old listing at 37 South Broad Mountain Avenue. The St. Joseph the Worker
bulletin of 18 October 2026 lists every Mass as "SJWP" without naming a second church, and its
"St. Joseph Complex Locations" are 49 North Line Street and 33 South Broad Mountain Avenue. The
parish's home page still says "Churches of St. Ann, Annunciation B.V.M, St. Joseph" under the
parish office, and its About page welcomes visitors to "any of our churches". Unlike St. Joseph,
Frackville, Annunciation is not among the closed churches whose glass the diocese sold in 2019.

## Why it is not decided

No source says the Annunciation church closed, and the parish's own pages still speak of
several churches; but the current schedule lists only one place. It may be used occasionally
(or 33 S. Broad Mountain Ave. may be part of it). See `revisit`.
