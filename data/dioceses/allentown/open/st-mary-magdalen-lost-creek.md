## What is known

St. Mary Magdalen, Route 54, Lost Creek (GCatholic 23683) was a parish until 28 July 2014,
when it merged with six Shenandoah parishes into Divine Mercy Parish "at the former
Annunciation Church building", with "St. Casimir Church ... also ... retained and used for
sacred worship" (the bishop's letter as reported in 2014). Divine Mercy's history lists "St.
Mary Magdalene (founded 1881)" among its former parishes. No current Divine Mercy bulletin
(October 2025 to October 2026) lists a Mass in Lost Creek, and the diocese's parish map has no
entry for it. The Shenandoah Sentinel's 2019 report lists St. Mary Magdalen among the merged
parishes but not among the churches "shuttered" or whose glass was sold.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to the building no longer being used, but no source read says that it
closed, was sold or demolished, or when: the merger letter only says which churches were
kept. Absence from the schedules is not enough for `closed`. See `revisit`.
