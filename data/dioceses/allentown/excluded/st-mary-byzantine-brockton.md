## Why it is excluded

St. Mary, Green St., Brockton is a Ruthenian (Byzantine) Catholic church. Its GCatholic page (read 2026-10-18) gives "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic",
"within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Allentown", "Rite: Ruthenian (Byzantine)". It is in the
Allentown roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the diocese's
parish map does not list it. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in
`diocese.md` for Eastern Catholic churches; whether it is open was not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it would belong to an Eparchy of Passaic dataset, not Allentown's.
