## Why it is excluded

St. Joseph, 211 W. Main St., Girardville: closed. Merged into St. Charles Borromeo,
Ashland in 2015; the diocese listed "Girardville St. Joseph" among the "closed churches" whose
stained glass it sold in October 2019 (Shenandoah Sentinel). After St. Vincent de Paul's last
Mass on 5 January 2026 the Sentinel described the end of "the Catholic Church’s brick-and-mortar
presence in the community". The St. Charles Borromeo bulletin of 18 October 2026 lists no Mass
outside Ashland. No date of the last Mass found.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass in this building (none of the parish bulletins read in October 2026 does).
