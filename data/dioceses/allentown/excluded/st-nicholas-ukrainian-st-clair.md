## Why it is excluded

St. Nicholas, N Morris St., St. Clair is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church. Its GCatholic page (read 2026-10-18) gives "Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia",
"within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Allentown", "Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)". It is in the
Allentown roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the diocese's
parish map does not list it. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in
`diocese.md` for Eastern Catholic churches; whether it is open was not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it would belong to an Archeparchy of Philadelphia dataset, not Allentown's.
