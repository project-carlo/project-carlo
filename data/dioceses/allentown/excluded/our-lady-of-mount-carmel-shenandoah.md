## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Mount Carmel, Washington & Diamond Sts., Shenandoah: closed in 2014. The
Shenandoah Sentinel (5 October 2019) names it among the churches "shuttered" in 2014 at the
Divine Mercy merger, and in July 2026 calls it "the former Our Lady parish" (the annual
procession now starts at St. Casimir and stops outside it). The Divine Mercy bulletin of 18
October 2026 lists no Mass here. No date of the last Mass found.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass in this building (none of the parish bulletins read in October 2026 does).
