## Why it is excluded

Open with public Mass, but no bulletin. The National Centre for Padre Pio (Our Lady of
Grace Chapel), 111 Barto Road, Barto, is a shrine whose own Mass & Confession Schedule
page (read 2026-10-18) gives a public weekday Mass at 10:30 AM on Mondays, Tuesdays,
Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, with Sunday Mass only for pilgrimage groups on request.
It publishes no weekly bulletin: its site has a Mass-schedule page, an event calendar and
a yearly calendar PDF, nothing the pipeline reads. It belongs to no parish row (Most
Blessed Sacrament, Bally, the nearest parish, does not list it in its bulletin).

## What would change this

If the pipeline gains a way to read a fixed Mass-schedule page, this becomes a church with
its own row; or if a parish bulletin starts listing its Masses.
