## Why it is excluded

The former St. Jerome church, 266 W. Broad St. (at Swatara St.), Tamaqua: closed in May 2018
and sold. The Times News (18 March 2018): "Effective Pentecost Sunday, May 20, 2018, all
services will be held at the former SS. Peter and Paul Church ... The former St. Jerome Church
building will close." The diocese sold the church, school and rectory on 21 January 2022
(Times News, 15 February 2022). St. John XXIII Parish's own history says the same.
`last_mass` is 2018-05: the last Mass was before Pentecost (20 May 2018); its exact date was
not found.

The diocese's map still gives 266 West Broad Street as St. John XXIII's address; its church is
307 Pine St. (`churches/st-john-xxiii-tamaqua`).

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building has been sold.
