## Why it is excluded

St. Michael the Archangel, 17 E Blaine St., McAdoo is a Ruthenian (Byzantine) Catholic church. Its GCatholic page (read
2026-10-18) gives "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic", "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of
Allentown", "Rite: Ruthenian (Byzantine)". It is in the Allentown roster only because GCatholic lists
Eastern churches by territory; the diocese's parish map does not list it. Excluded as
`other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md` for Eastern Catholic churches;
whether it is open was not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs to the Byzantine Catholic Eparchy of Passaic, not to the Diocese of Allentown.
