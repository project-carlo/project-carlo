## Why it is excluded

St. Stanislaus, 108 W. Cherry St., Shenandoah: closed in 2014. The Shenandoah Sentinel (5
October 2019) names it with St. Stephen and Our Lady of Mount Carmel as "shuttered" in 2014
when the parishes merged into Divine Mercy; the 2014 merger letter retained only the former
Annunciation church and St. Casimir "for sacred worship". The Divine Mercy bulletin of 18
October 2026 lists no Mass here. 108 W. Cherry St. is now Divine Mercy's parish office and
rectory (the address GCatholic gives for St. Stanislaus), so the parish's buildings are still
in use, but not the church for Mass. No date of the last Mass found.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass in this building (none of the parish bulletins read in October 2026 does).
