## Why it is excluded

St. Mauritius, 802 Pine St., Ashland: closed. One of the parishes merged into St. Charles
Borromeo in 2015; the diocese listed "Ashland St. Mauritius" among the "closed churches" whose
stained glass it sold in October 2019 (Shenandoah Sentinel). The St. Charles Borromeo bulletin
of 18 October 2026 lists Mass only at St. Charles Borromeo. No date of the last Mass found.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass in this building (none of the parish bulletins read in October 2026 does).
