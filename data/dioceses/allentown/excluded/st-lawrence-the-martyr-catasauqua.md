## Why it is excluded

Closed on 28 October 2022. The diocese's AD Today reported on 31 October 2022 that "the
former Saint Lawrence the Martyr church building in Catasauqua, now being maintained by
Saint John Fisher Parish", had been closed "because of declining use and rising
maintenance costs"; since the 2014 merger that formed St. John Fisher it had had Mass
one day a year and funerals, and "The church building closed effective October 28." The
article gives no date for the last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty. St. John Fisher's
current schedule (St. Mary's 18 October 2026 bulletin and homepage) has Masses only at
St. John Fisher and St. Mary's.

GCatholic still lists it (1001 2nd St.); Discover Mass's stale listing gives 1003 Second
Street.

## What would change this

A reopening announced by St. John Fisher or the diocese. Nothing expected.
