## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Good Counsel, 436 South 2nd Street, Bangor, closed with a final Mass on Saturday
7 February 2026 at 4:00 pm. The parish's own bulletin of 8 February 2026 (issue 107, page 4)
says "The final Mass at Our Lady of Good Counsel Church will be on Sat., February 7, 2026 at
4:00pm", and Lehigh Valley Public Media reported on 19 January 2026 that the diocese would
close the building ("The final Mass at Our Lady of Good Counsel at 436 S. Second St. will be
Saturday, Feb. 7"), for the cost of repairs (roof, trusses, plumbing, HVAC) and declining
attendance, inviting its congregation to Our Lady of Mount Carmel, Roseto. From the 15
February 2026 issue the bulletin's masthead and schedule name Mount Carmel alone, and the 18
October 2026 issue lists Masses there only.

Good Counsel had been the Bangor church of the merged Our Lady of Good Counsel and Our Lady of
Mount Carmel parish (merged 31 January 2023), so the related parish row is
`our-lady-of-mount-carmel-roseto`. The diocesan parish map still lists it ("Worship Site for
Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish", "Saturday 4 p.m.") on 18 October 2026, eight months after
the closing Mass; GCatholic lists it too.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. The building may be sold; a reopening would show in the Mount Carmel
bulletin.
