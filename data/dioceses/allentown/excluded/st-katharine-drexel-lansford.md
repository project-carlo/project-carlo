## Why it is excluded

Closed, last Mass Sunday 26 June 2016. St. Katharine Drexel Church, 124 E. Abbott St.,
Lansford, was the former St. Michael the Archangel church, used by St. Katharine Drexel
Parish (formed in the 2008 consolidation of six Lansford and Coaldale parishes). The diocese
announced in April 2016 the merger of St. Katharine Drexel and St. Francis of Assisi into
St. Joseph, Summit Hill (Times News, 23 April 2016). The Times News of 26 June 2016 reports
"its final Mass before consolidating with St. Joseph Parish in Summit Hill"; St. Joseph's own
history page says the church served "until closing in June of 2016"; parishioners' appeal to
Rome failed, and the Times News of 13 February 2021 reports the church and Our Lady of the
Angels Academy sold to Catholic Worker. The current (18 October 2026) St. Joseph bulletin
lists no Lansford church. GCatholic (23676) still lists it.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building is privately owned since 2021.
