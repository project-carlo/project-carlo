## Why it is excluded

Closed in June 2016, sold in 2021. After the 2008 consolidation, St. Francis of Assisi
Parish, Nesquehoning, worshipped in the former Immaculate Conception church on West Mill
Street (Sacred Heart, West High Street, was slated for demolition in 2010; Our Lady of Mount Carmel became the diocesan
shrine). St. Joseph Parish of the Panther Valley's history page says St. Francis "occupied
the former Immaculate Conception Church building until closing in June of 2016"; the Times
News of 8 July 2016 reports the diocese "officially closed ... St. Francis of Assisi in
Nesquehoning, merging them into St. Joseph's". Family Promise of Carbon County bought "the
former St. Francis of Assisi Church and rectory, located at 140 W. Mill St." (Times News, 24
October 2019) and completed the purchase in July 2021 (Times News, 1 December 2021). No
day for the last Mass was found, so `last_mass` is the month.

This building is in no roster under its own entry: GCatholic's "St. Francis of Assisi"
(23658) carries the shrine's Plus Code and "Shrine of St. Therese of Lisieux", so it is
linked to `churches/shrine-of-st-therese-nesquehoning`. This record exists so the next person
does not mistake that entry for an open parish church.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building is owned by Family Promise.
