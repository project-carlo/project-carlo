## Why it is excluded

St. Stephen, 18 E. Oak St., Shenandoah: closed in 2014. The Shenandoah Sentinel (5 October
2019) says the church was "shuttered" in 2014 when the Shenandoah parishes merged into Divine
Mercy Parish; by then its statue, cross and stained glass had been removed, the diocese had "an
agreement of sale for the building", and the diocese listed it among "closed churches" whose
glass it sold (1 October 2019). The 2014 merger kept only the Annunciation building and St.
Casimir for worship, and the Divine Mercy bulletin of 18 October 2026 lists Mass at those two
only. No source gives the date of the last Mass; `last_mass` is the year.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass in this building (none of the parish bulletins read in October 2026 does).
