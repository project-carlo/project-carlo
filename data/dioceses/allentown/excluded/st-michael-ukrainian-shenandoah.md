## Why it is excluded

St. Michael the Archangel, 114 S Chestnut St., Shenandoah is a Ukrainian (Byzantine) Catholic church. Its GCatholic page (read
2026-10-18) gives "Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia", "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of
Allentown", "Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)". It is in the Allentown roster only because GCatholic lists
Eastern churches by territory; the diocese's parish map does not list it. Excluded as
`other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md` for Eastern Catholic churches;
whether it is open was not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs to the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not to the Diocese of Allentown.
