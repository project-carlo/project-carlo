## Why it is excluded

Closed as a Roman Catholic church. St. Anthony of Padua, 504 Summit Avenue (Millmont,
Reading), was consolidated into St. John Baptist de la Salle, Shillington, in April 2015;
the GoReadingBerks parish history says it "held one final Mass" on Sunday 28 June 2015 and
that the building "was acquired by the nearby Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary Ukrainian
Catholic Church". GCatholic still lists it (23647), with a website that no longer resolves;
the diocesan map does not. The St. John Baptist de la Salle bulletin of 18 October 2026
names only its own church.

The building's present Ukrainian Catholic use, if any, belongs to the eparchy (see
`nativity-bvm-ukrainian-reading.md`), not to this dataset.
