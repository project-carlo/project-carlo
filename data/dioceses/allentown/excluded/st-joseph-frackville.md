## Why it is excluded

St. Joseph, 237 W. Frack St., Frackville: closed. Merged into St. Joseph the Worker,
Frackville; the diocese listed "Frackville St. Joseph" among the "closed churches" whose
stained glass it sold in October 2019 (Shenandoah Sentinel). The St. Joseph the Worker bulletin
of 18 October 2026 lists Mass only at "SJWP" and names its locations as 49 North Line Street
and 33 South Broad Mountain Avenue. No date of the last Mass found.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass in this building (none of the parish bulletins read in October 2026 does).
