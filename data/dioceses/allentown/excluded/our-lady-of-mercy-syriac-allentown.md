## Why it is excluded

An Eastern Catholic (Syriac) community under the Syriac Catholic Eparchy of Our Lady of
Deliverance, not the Diocese of Allentown. GCatholic says it uses the building of the
National Shrine of Our Lady of Guadalupe, i.e. Immaculate Conception BVM, 501 Ridge
Avenue, which is a church of its own here (`immaculate-conception-bvm-allentown`). By
this diocese's rule, Eastern Catholic churches are `other-jurisdiction`.

## What would change this

Nothing; the Latin-rite church in the same building is already recorded.
