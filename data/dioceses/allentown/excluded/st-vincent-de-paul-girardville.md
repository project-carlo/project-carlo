## Why it is excluded

St. Vincent de Paul, 260 N. 2nd St. (2nd and C Sts.), Girardville: closed, last Mass Monday 5
January 2026 at 6 pm. Kept as a worship site of St. Charles Borromeo after the 2015 merger;
"Regular weekend Masses ended in 2021" (the diocese, December 2025), and the diocese announced
it would close in January 2026 for "financial and structural challenges". The Shenandoah
Sentinel reported the final Mass on 5 January 2026, after which "the lights went out for the
final time". The St. Charles Borromeo bulletin of 18 October 2026 lists no Mass here.

The diocese's map still has the entry (no Mass times, a "-portland" page address and a
610 phone number, so the entry looks partly mixed up with a St. Vincent de Paul elsewhere); it
lags the closure.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: the building is closed and the parish says all its liturgies are now in Ashland.
