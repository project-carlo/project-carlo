## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Lebanon, 54 S 4th St., Easton is a Maronite Catholic church. Its GCatholic page (read 2026-10-18) gives "Jurisdiction: Maronite Diocese of Saint Maron of Brooklyn", "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Allentown". It is in the Allentown roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the diocesan parish map does not list it. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule in `diocese.md` for Eastern Catholic churches; whether it is open was not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs to an eparchy's dataset, not the Diocese of Allentown's.
