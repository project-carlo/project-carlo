## Why it is excluded

St. George (Lithuanian), Shenandoah: closed. The diocese listed "Shenandoah St. George" among
the "closed churches" whose stained glass it sold in October 2019 (Shenandoah Sentinel), and
the 2014 merger into Divine Mercy kept only the Annunciation building and St. Casimir for
worship. GCatholic gives St. George the Annunciation building's address (218 W. Cherry St.)
and Plus Code and says the two share a church building; that building is today's Divine Mercy
Church (`churches/divine-mercy-shenandoah`), so this record is the St. George church and
parish as a separate place, which no current source lists for Mass. No date of the last Mass
found; GCatholic's street address for St. George is not its own.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass in this building (none of the parish bulletins read in October 2026 does).
