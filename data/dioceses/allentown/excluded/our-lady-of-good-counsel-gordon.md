## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Good Counsel, 220 W. Biddle St., Gordon: closed. Merged into St. Charles
Borromeo, Ashland in 2015; the diocese listed it among the "closed churches" whose stained
glass it sold in October 2019 (Shenandoah Sentinel). The St. Charles Borromeo bulletin of 18
October 2026 lists Mass only in Ashland. No date of the last Mass found.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass in this building (none of the parish bulletins read in October 2026 does).
