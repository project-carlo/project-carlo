## Why it is excluded

The Chapel of St. Vincent de Paul, 720 Delaware Avenue, Portland (Northampton County), a
mission of Our Lady of Good Counsel, Bangor, was closed in the January 2023 Slate Belt
merger: Lehigh Valley Public Media reported on 15 January 2023 that "the Diocese of Allentown
is closing the Chapel of St. Vincent de Paul", with the changes effective 31 January 2023
(before then it had Saturday 6 p.m. and Sunday 11 a.m. Masses). The merged parish's 18
October 2026 bulletin lists Masses only at Our Lady of Mount Carmel.

No source found gives the day of its last Mass; `last_mass` is the month the closure took
effect (31 January 2023, per the article), so the last Mass was in January 2023 at the latest. It is in no roster: neither GCatholic nor the diocesan
parish map lists it. The ZIP code 18351 is Portland's; the street address is the news
article's.

Nothing expected.
