## Why it is excluded

Other jurisdiction. Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary, Reading, is a Ukrainian Catholic
parish (GCatholic 17530 marks it `rite: Ukrainian`), under the Ukrainian Catholic
Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not the Diocese of Allentown. Rule settled for this diocese:
Eastern Catholic churches are excluded as `other-jurisdiction`.

GCatholic gives 630 Laurel St. and a ParishesOnline website; the GoReadingBerks history of
St. Anthony of Padua says the Ukrainian parish acquired the former St. Anthony church (504
Summit Avenue) after 2015. Which building it worships in now does not matter for this
exclusion and was not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
