## Why it is excluded

St. Mary, 141 W High St., Nesquehoning, is an Eastern Catholic church: GCatholic (church 17225, read 2026-10-18) gives its
jurisdiction as the Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic, "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Allentown", rite Ruthenian
(Byzantine). It belongs to that jurisdiction, not to the Diocese of Allentown, so it is
`other-jurisdiction` (diocese.md: Eastern Catholic churches are excluded, as in Scranton,
Syracuse and Rochester). Whether it still holds services was not checked; that doesn't
change the call.
