## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin (page 2, image) announces Masses for 19–25 October, including Saturday 4:00 p.m. and Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 10:30 a.m. and 12:00 p.m.

## Name and address

GCatholic gives "2810 St. Alabans Drive, Sinking Spring" (a typo for Albans); the diocesan
map ("2810 Saint Albans Drive, Reading 19608") and the bulletin ("2810 ST. ALBANS DRIVE ~
READING, PA 19608") agree on 2810 St. Albans Drive, Reading. The church is in Spring
Township near Sinking Spring; Reading is its postal town. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G58XPW+R3), which falls on the building; the diocesan map pin agrees to within a few metres.
