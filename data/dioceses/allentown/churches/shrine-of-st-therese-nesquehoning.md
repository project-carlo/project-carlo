## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly but not on Sunday. The St. Joseph Parish of the Panther Valley bulletin of 18
October 2026 lists "Sat.: 9:00 AM - Shrine of St. Therese" in its daily schedule and a
"Shrine -Daily Mass- 9am" on Saturday 24 October; the shrine's own site says "Weekly Mass,
Saturday 9:00 AM, at the Shrine"; the diocesan map gives "Weekday Mass: Saturday 9 a.m.".
A weekly public Mass makes it a worship site of the parish, even though (as the Times News
noted in 2017) it does not satisfy the Sunday obligation.

## Name and address

The building is the former Our Lady of Mount Carmel church, closed as a parish church in the
2008 consolidation and opened as a diocesan shrine to St. Therese (diocese announcement
reported 22 December 2012). Name: the diocesan map ("Diocesan Shrine of St. Therese of
Lisieux"), the parish bulletin ("The Shrine of Saint Therese of Lisieux") and the shrine's
site agree; GCatholic's entry 23658 is headed "Church of St. Francis of Assisi" with
"Shrine of St. Therese of Lisieux" and type Shrine, a leftover of the 2008-2016 parish
name. The former St. Francis of Assisi parish church was a different building (the old
Immaculate Conception, 140 W. Mill St.; `excluded/st-francis-of-assisi-nesquehoning`).
I linked GCatholic 23658 here because its Plus Code is on this building and it calls it a
shrine. `name_verified` true on the map and the bulletin.

Address: the map and the bulletin give 1 E. Garibaldi Ave.; GCatholic has 15 E Garibaldi
Ave. Followed the map and bulletin; `address_verified`
true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G6V59M+JQ), within 10 m of the map's pin.
