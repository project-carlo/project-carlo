## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 Immaculate Conception bulletin: Saturday 4 PM, Sunday 9 AM
(English) and 10:30 AM (Polish), daily 8 AM, and Monday 8 AM Mass and novena at the
Shrine of Our Lady of Guadalupe in this church.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("Immaculate Conception BVM Church", 501 Ridge Avenue, 18102) and the
diocesan map ("Immaculate Conception BVM", 501 Ridge Ave) agree. GCatholic knows the
building as the "National Shrine of Our Lady of Guadalupe, Mother of the Americas", but
that entry is not in the GCatholic roster (only its Syriac "Our Lady of Mercy", which uses
the building, is; excluded as `other-jurisdiction`). Coordinates are the diocesan map's
pin.
