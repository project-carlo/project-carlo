## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 All Saints Parish bulletin gives Sunday 10:30 am at Our Lady of Lourdes
Church, and its Mass intentions list "Sun 10/25/26 10:30 AM Our Lady Of Lourdes". The
diocese's map ("Our Lady of Lourdes Church Worship Site for All Saints Parish": "Weekend Mass:
Sunday 10:30 a.m.") agrees. Weatherly is in Carbon County; the church belongs to All Saints,
McAdoo (Schuylkill County), whose bulletin covers it.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead ("Our Lady of Lourdes Church, 318 Plane St., Weatherly, PA 18255"), the
diocese's map (318 Plane Street) and GCatholic (318 Plane St.) agree. Both flags true.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G6W5V7+GR); the diocese's pin is about 100 m north.
