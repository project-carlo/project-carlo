## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 St. Charles Borromeo bulletin gives Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 9:00
am, and Tuesday and Friday 8:30 am (in St. Anne Chapel on the church's lower level); the
parish's home page and the diocese's map agree.

## Name and address

The parish's site calls it "St. Charles Borromeo Church, 1115 Walnut St., Ashland", and the
diocese's map "St. Charles Borromeo"; name flag true. GCatholic has no St. Charles Borromeo:
its "St. Joseph", 1115 Walnut St., Ashland (Plus Code 87G5QMJ3+9X) sits where the diocese
pins St. Charles Borromeo (about 25 m apart), so it is taken to be this building under its
pre-2015 name (St. Joseph, Ashland was one of the merged parishes, and St. Joseph, Ashland is
not among the closed churches the diocese sold glass from in 2019). That is an inference from
the address and coordinates, not something a source says.

Street numbers disagree: 1115 Walnut St. (parish site, GCatholic; also the bulletin's parish
office address) against 1104 Walnut Street (the diocese's map). Followed 1115; address flag
false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
