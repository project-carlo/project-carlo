## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 bulletin (the newest posted on 18 October) lists Sunday 10:45 am
and Monday and Thursday 8:00 am at Immaculate Conception (ICC), plus first, third and fifth
Wednesday 6 pm per the site's schedule. The site's standing schedule also has the Saturday 4
pm vigil here, but the October bulletins move it to St. Joseph for now ("Location change").

GCatholic ("Immaculate Conception", 178 W Broadway) and the diocesan map ("Immaculate
Conception", 178 West Broadway, with the rectory at 180 W. Broadway) agree; the bulletin
masthead gives the parish office's 180 W. Broadway. Both flags true for 178 W Broadway.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G6V772+CF), about 100 m from the map's pin.
