## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 5 pm, Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 am,
Monday 7 pm and Tuesday to Thursday 8:30 am, and page 2 lists that week's Masses; the
parish site and the diocesan map give the same weekend times.

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic ("Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary"), the diocesan map ("Assumption
BVM") and the bulletin ("Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary Catholic Church") agree, so
`name_verified` is true.

Address: the sources disagree on the number. GCatholic has 633 W Washington St.; the
diocesan map puts the church at 649 West Washington Street with "Mailing Address: 633 W.
Washington St."; the bulletin masthead spans both ("633-649 W. Washington St.") and the
parish site gives 633 as the rectory. A web search turned up "Assumption BVM Social Hall,
649 W Washington St." in another parish's bulletin, so 649 may be the hall rather than the
church. I kept GCatholic's 633 and left `address_verified` false; a look at the building
(or the parcel map) would settle it. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G6P9WM+9G),
about 50 m from the map's pin, so either number puts the pin on the site.
