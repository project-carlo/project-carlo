## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 Most Blessed Trinity bulletin (page 1, an image) lists "Chapel:
Sunday 9:00 AM" and a holy-day vigil at 7:00 PM. The parish homepage ("9:00 AM - Mass
(Chapel - Tower City)") and the diocesan map's Most Blessed Trinity page ("Weekend Mass
Tower City: Sunday 9 a.m.") agree.

## Name and address

The parish site calls it "Ss. Peter & Paul Chapel, 840 E. Grand Avenue, Tower City", the
former Ss. Peter & Paul parish church (parish history). OSM node 5797307889 is "Saints Peter
& Paul Roman Catholic Church" at 840 East Grand Avenue. The two independent sources agree on
name and address, so both flags are true. Coordinates are from the OSM node. Neither roster
lists the chapel on its own (see the parish's *Worship sites*).
