## Holds Mass

Yes, seasonally. Divine Mercy Parish holds its Sunday morning Masses here in the warm months:
the bulletins list "SC" Masses on Sundays from at least 21 June to 27 September 2026 (7:30 and
10:00 am through mid-September, then 9:00 am), and the Sentinel reports an 8 am Mass here before
the 19 July 2026 procession. For the winter the parish moves every Mass to Divine Mercy Church
"due to high cost of oil" (announced in the 19 October 2025 issue; in 2026 the Sunday Mass
moved on 4 October), so the 18 October 2026 bulletin lists no Mass here. The 2014 merger
decree retained the church "for sacred worship", and the bulletin keeps a separate list of
"Candles for St. Casimir" all year. Kept as a church on that basis; `revisit` checks that the
summer Masses return in 2027.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("St. Casimir Church, 229 North Jardin Street"), the diocese's map ("St. Casimir
Church Worship Site of Divine Mercy", 229 North Jardin Street) and GCatholic (229 N Jardin St.)
agree. Both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G5RQFW+4P).
