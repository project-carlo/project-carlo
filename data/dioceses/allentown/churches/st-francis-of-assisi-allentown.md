## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 St. Francis bulletin (newest posted on 18 October) gives
Saturday 4:30 PM, Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 AM.

## Name and address

The name agrees everywhere. The address does not: GCatholic and OpenStreetMap put the
church at 825 N. 11th Street; the parish site's footer and the diocesan map give 1046 W.
Cedar Street, which is probably the parish center on the same block (the Plus Code, OSM
and the diocesan pin are within about 100 m of each other). This record follows 825 N.
11th Street for the building; `address_verified` stays false until the parish's own
material names the church's street address. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code
(87G6JG67+RV).
