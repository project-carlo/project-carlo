## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin (Discover Mass) gives Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday
9:00 and 11:00 am and Tuesday to Thursday 8:00 am, and page 2 lists that week's Masses.
The parish site and the diocesan map give the same weekend times.

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic, the map, OSM and the bulletin all say St. Peter the Fisherman.

Address: the bulletin and parish site ("33 Trinity Circle Rd."), the diocesan map ("Rectory:
33 Trinity Circle Rd.", P.O. Box 237) and OpenStreetMap (a place-of-worship way at 33 Trinity
Circle Road) agree. GCatholic has "2111 N Lake Drive, Lake Harmony, Pennsylvania 19624-0237"
(the ZIP is a typo for 18624; -0237 is the PO box) and a Plus Code (87H63C65+P5) about 1.7
km north-west of the church, so I did not use its coordinates. Both flags true for 33 Trinity
Circle Road. Coordinates are OSM's (the same point as the diocesan map's pin).
