## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 St. Catharine of Siena bulletin (page 2, an image) has a box
"St. Mary's Masses" listing Saturday 17 October 3:30 p.m., Sunday 18 October 10:30 a.m.,
Saturday 24 October 3:30 p.m. and Sunday 25 October 10:30 a.m., each with an intention. The
diocesan map gives the same times. The Berks TLM newsletter also announces a monthly
Sunday 12:30 pm Traditional Latin Mass here (latest post read: 6 August 2026).

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Mary", 248 S 12th St.) and the diocesan map ("St. Mary Church Worship Site
for St. Catharine of Siena Parish", 248 South 12th Street) give number 248; St. Catharine's
Mass-schedule page and the TLM newsletter give 250 South 12th Street (probably the rectory
or the church's other frontage at 12th and Spruce). I followed the two rosters, so
address_verified is false until the number is settled. GCatholic's website for it
(stmarystjoseph.net) is the Ringtown/Sheppton parishes' site, a GCatholic error; the
parish's former site stmaryrdg.com is now a domain auction. Coordinates are GCatholic's
Plus Code (87G683JP+85).
