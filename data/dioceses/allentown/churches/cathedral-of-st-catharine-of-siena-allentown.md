## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 cathedral bulletin gives Saturday 4:15 PM, Sunday 7:30, 9:30
and 11:30 AM, and daily Masses (Monday to Friday 6:30 and 8 AM, Wednesday 12:15 PM,
Saturday 8 AM).

## Name and address

The bulletin ("Cathedral Parish of Saint Catharine of Siena, 1825 West Turner St") and the
diocesan map ("Cathedral of St. Catharine of Siena", 1825 W Turner Street, 18104) agree.
GCatholic's Allentown church list omits the cathedral. Coordinates are the diocesan map's
pin; there is no Plus Code.
