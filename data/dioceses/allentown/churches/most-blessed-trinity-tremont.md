Saturday Vigil 4:00 PM and Sunday 10:30 AM, plus daily Masses at 9:00 AM (Tuesday 6:00 PM)

Blessed Trinity" (the building is the former Immaculate Conception church). I followed the
parish. The names differ only by "Most", so `name_verified` is true. Address: GCatholic (113
Cherry St.), the diocesan map (113 Cherry Street) and the bulletin (113 Cherry Street) agree,
so `address_verified` is true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G5JJJ7+9H).
