## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The Divine Mercy Parish bulletin of 18 October 2026 codes every Mass of the week
"DM": Monday–Friday 7:00 am, Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am and 12:30 pm (Spanish). In the
cold months (October 2025 to spring 2026, and again from 4 October 2026) the Sunday morning
Masses are here too; in summer they move to St. Casimir (see the parish row).

## Name and address

The church is the former Annunciation B.V.M. church, which the 2014 merger made the seat of
Divine Mercy Parish ("at the former Annunciation Church building"). The bulletin calls it
"Divine Mercy Church", as does Discover Mass; the diocese's map calls the place "Divine
Mercy". Name flag true.

Addresses disagree: the diocese's map gives 224 West Cherry Street, and Discover Mass's
directions end "to 224 West Cherry"; GCatholic gives 218 W Cherry St. for Annunciation (and
the same address and Plus Code for St. George, which it says shares the building); a 2020
obituary was reported to give 232 W. Cherry St. (not read). The 19 October 2025 bulletin
places the parish's new "Prayer League Center at 218 West Cherry Street", so 218 may be the
neighbouring building. Followed the diocese (224); address flag false. The parish office and
rectory are at 108 W. Cherry St. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G5RQ9W+6F), on the
Annunciation building; the diocese's pin for 224 W. Cherry is about 60 m east.
