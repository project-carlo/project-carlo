## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 bulletin lists Saturday 4:00 pm (moved here from Immaculate
Conception for now), Sunday 8:30 am, Tuesday and Friday 8:00 am and second and fourth
Wednesday 6:00 pm at St. Joseph Church (SJC). The site's standing schedule agrees on Sunday
8:30 and the weekdays. St. Joseph Parish was consolidated into Immaculate Conception in June
2020 with both buildings kept open (Times News, 22 June 2020; see the parish row).

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Joseph", 526 North St.), the diocesan map ("St. Joseph", 526 North Street,
"Worship Site for Immaculate Conception Parish") and the bulletin masthead ("Saint Joseph
Church 526 North Street") agree. Both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code
(87G6V7G9+22).
