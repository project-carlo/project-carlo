## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's Mass schedule page (read 2026-10-18) gives Saturday 4:30 PM and Sunday 7:30, 9:30 and 11:30 AM in the Church, and the 18 October 2026 bulletin announces the weekday 7:15 a.m. Mass and the 40 Hours Devotion in the church on 25–27 October.

## Name and address

The parish's site gives the church at 4975 Boyertown Pike, Reading 19606 ("Church Get
Directions 4975 Boyertown Pike"), and the diocesan map lists St. Catharine of Siena at the
same address. GCatholic (23651) still gives 2427 Perkiomen Ave. (Mount Penn) with a Plus
Code there (87G684H6+68, about 5 km west), which I take to be the parish's earlier home in
Mount Penn (inferred; not read anywhere); the parish school is at 2330 Perkiomen Ave. No source read says what the
2427 Perkiomen Avenue building is now, and no current schedule mentions it, so GCatholic's
entry is linked here as a stale address for this church rather than given its own record.
Name and current address are verified by the parish site and the diocesan map. Coordinates
are the diocesan map's pin (40.323051, -75.832911), not GCatholic's.
