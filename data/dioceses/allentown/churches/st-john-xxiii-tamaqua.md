## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am, Wednesday and
Thursday 9:00 am at St. John XXIII ("SJ").

## Name and address

The church is the former SS. Peter and Paul church: the parish's history says St. Jerome's
was closed and the parish united "into one building at SS. Peter & Paul Church", and the Times
News (March 2018) that from Pentecost 2018 all services are "at the former SS. Peter and Paul
Church, which will become the seat of St. John XXIII Parish". The bulletin and site call it
"Church of St. John XXIII, 307 Pine Street"; GCatholic lists "Sts. Peter and Paul", 307 Pine St.
Name flag true (bulletin and the diocese's map, "St. John XXII[I]").

The diocese's map gives 266 West Broad Street, which is the former St. Jerome church (sold
2022), so it disagrees; followed the bulletin and GCatholic (307 Pine St.). Address flag false
until the diocese's entry is corrected. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G6R22J+FH)
for SS. Peter and Paul; the diocese's pin is on St. Jerome.
