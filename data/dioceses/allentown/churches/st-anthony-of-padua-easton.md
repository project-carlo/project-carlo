## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 joint Easton bulletin (image) gives St. Anthony Saturday 4:30 pm, Sunday 10:30 am, and Tuesday and Thursday 8:00 am.

## Name and address

The name agrees everywhere. The address does not: the directory and the bulletin give 900 Washington Street (the parish office and, per the directory, "Mailing Address and Parish Office"); GCatholic (23648) gives "9th St. & Lehigh St."; OpenStreetMap puts the church building at 841 Washington Street. I kept 900 Washington Street (directory and bulletin) as the address the parish uses; address_verified stays false until a source states the church's own number. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G6MQQH+2Q), within 20 m of OSM's building.
