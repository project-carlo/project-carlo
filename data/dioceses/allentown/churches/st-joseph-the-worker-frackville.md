## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 St. Joseph the Worker bulletin lists Saturday 5:30 pm, Sunday 11:00
am, Monday 5:30 pm and Thursday 8:30 am, all "SJWP"; the parish site's Mass Times page and the
diocese's map agree on the weekend Masses. The bulletin names no other church for Mass.

## Name and address

The diocese's map places the parish ("St. Joseph the Worker") at 49 North Line Street, and the
bulletin's "St. Joseph Complex Locations" begin with 49 North Line Street; GCatholic lists its
St. Ann church at 49 N Line St. (Plus Code 87G5QQPC+VV, which the diocese's pin matches within
40 m). Address flag true. The name is not settled: GCatholic calls the building St. Ann, the
diocese names only the parish, and no source read calls the building "St. Joseph the Worker
Church"; name flag false. The rectory/office is at 99 N. Line St.; the diocese's map gives the
business office as 7 S. Broad Mountain Ave. (GCatholic's Annunciation address).
