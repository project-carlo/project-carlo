## Holds Mass

Yes. The Holy Family bulletin of 18 October 2026 (container 03/1413, page 1) lists Saturday
4:00pm and Sunday 10:00am at St. Matthew, plus weekday Masses on Monday and Wednesday at
8:00am and on alternate Thursdays at 5:30pm. The week of 22 October has "5:30 pm Mass and
Adoration at St. Matthew".

## Name and address

Name: the bulletin and the diocesan parish map say "St. Matthew the Evangelist Church", and
GCatholic says "St. Matthew". They agree, so `name_verified` is true.

Address: the sources disagree on the street number. GCatholic gives 135 Spruce St. The
diocesan parish map's Holy Family page gives "St. Matthew the Evangelist Church: 139 Spruce
St." and "Rectory: 139 Spruce St.", and St. Matthew's old ParishesOnline org gives 139 Spruce
Street. 139 may be the rectory's number, used for both. I kept GCatholic's 135, which comes
with a Plus Code on the building (87G5MPQQ+PP), and left `address_verified` false. A look at
the building or OSM would settle it. Coordinates are from the Plus Code.
