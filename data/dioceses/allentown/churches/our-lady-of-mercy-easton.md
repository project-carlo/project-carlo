## Holds Mass

Yes. The 18 October 2026 joint Easton bulletin (image) gives Our Lady of Mercy Sunday 8:00 am (English) and 12:00 pm (Spanish), and weekday Masses Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

## Name and address

The name agrees everywhere. The address: GCatholic (23627) and OpenStreetMap put the church at 129 Davis Street, South Easton; the directory and the bulletin give 132 S. 5th Street, which the directory labels "Mailing Address and Parish Office" and which OSM shows as a separate downtown building 700 m north. I followed GCatholic and OSM (two independent sources on the building), so both flags are true; the directory's pin is on the office. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G6MQJP+WQ), within 30 m of OSM's building.
