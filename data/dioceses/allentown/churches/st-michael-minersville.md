5:30pm and Sunday 8:30am at St. Michael, plus weekday Masses on Tuesday and Friday at 8:00am.

## Name and address

GCatholic ("St. Michael the Archangel", 541 Sunbury St.) and the diocesan parish map's Holy
Family page ("St. Michael the Archangel Church: 541 Sunbury St.") agree on the name and
address, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G5MPRM+R3).

The map's only Minersville entry, `directory:29233` ("Holy Family Parish", 538 Sunbury
Street), is the parish office across the street. Its pin (40.69201, -76.26736) is within a
few metres of this church, so the entry is linked here. Its page lists both St. Michael and
St. Matthew (`st-matthew-minersville`).
