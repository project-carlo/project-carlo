## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 St. Clare of Assisi bulletin (page 1, "MASS TIMES") lists Saturday
5:30 PM, Sunday 10:45 AM, Tuesday 6:00 PM and Wednesday 8:30 AM at Holy Cross. The
diocesan map's Holy Cross page agrees on the weekend ("Saturday 5:30 p.m., Sunday 10:45
a.m.").

## Name and address

Name: "Holy Cross" in GCatholic, on the diocesan map and in the bulletin, so
`name_verified` is true.

Address: the sources disagree on the number. The diocesan map places the church at "101
Valley Street" and gives "Mailing Address, Parish Office and Rectory: 99 Valley St.".
GCatholic and Holy Cross's old ParishesOnline org give 99 Valley St. I followed the map,
which tells church and rectory apart, so `line1` is 101 Valley St., and left
`address_verified` false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G5PV9M+G7), within a few
metres of the map's pin.
