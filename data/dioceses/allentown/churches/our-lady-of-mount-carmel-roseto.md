## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin (issue 143) lists Saturday 4:30 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 am, and weekday 7:30 am Masses (no Mass Tuesday) at this, its only church since Our Lady of Good Counsel closed on 7 February 2026.

## Name and address

The name agrees everywhere (GCatholic, directory, bulletin, OSM). The address does not:

- the bulletin: "560 N. 6th Street, Bangor, Pennsylvania 18013", adding "While the church has an address in Bangor, the altar and sacristy reside in Roseto";
- GCatholic (23629): "560 N 6th Ave., Roseto";
- OpenStreetMap: "560 Garibaldi Avenue, Roseto" on the church building;
- the diocesan directory: "80 Ridge Street, Roseto" (its pin is a street centroid on Ridge Street, Uttsville; the directory gives 560 N. Sixth St., Bangor as the rectory).

I followed the bulletin and GCatholic ("560 North 6th Street", Roseto, 18013); the building straddles the Bangor/Roseto line and the street seems to carry both names. address_verified stays false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G6VQFP+V9), within 40 m of OSM's building.
