## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Mary's 18 October 2026 bulletin schedules Masses here on Saturday 24 October at
4 PM and Sunday 25 October at 10:30 AM, and on weekdays (Monday and Friday 8 AM,
Wednesday 6 PM); the St. Mary's homepage gives the same schedule, effective 3 August
2026. It is listed under `annunciation-bvm-catasauqua` because that bulletin is the only
one found that schedules it (see the parish row).

## Name and address

The building is the former St. Andrew's church; GCatholic still calls it "St. Andrew"
(3rd St. & Cypress St., Catasauqua). The street number disagrees across sources: the St.
Mary's bulletin says "1229 Third Street, N. Catasauqua", the diocesan map lists the
parish at 1237 Third Street with mailing address 1229 Third St., and OpenStreetMap
numbers the church 1239. This record follows the bulletin (1229), so `address_verified`
is false. The borough is North Catasauqua (Northampton County, just across the line from
Catasauqua), as the bulletin and OSM say; the diocesan map and GCatholic say Catasauqua.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G6MG69+9Q), within 30 m of the OSM node.
