## Holds Mass

Yes. Holy Ghost Parish (FSSP, Bethlehem) offers its 7 AM Masses here: the 18 October 2026
Holy Ghost bulletin marks the Sunday and weekday 7:00 AM Masses "Offered at St. Stephen
of Hungary Oratory" (Traditional Latin Mass). The diocesan map's oratory entry lists Holy
Ghost's website and phone. So the oratory is a church of the Holy Ghost row
(`holy-ghost-bethlehem`, Northampton region), although it stands in Allentown.
