## Publisher

One bulletin, published by Catholic Community Buffalo North (Family #20), covering 5 worship site(s). PR 14 modelled this as `catholic-community-buffalo-north`.

## Worship sites

St. Rose of Lima Church; Assumption Church; St. Mark Church; St. Margaret Church; Holy Spirit Church. Each church record gives the Mass evidence. Churches of the family that no longer hold Mass are in excluded/ or open/.

Not a separate worship site: the "Central Office Chapel" at the CCBN Central Office, 16 Peter
Street. Until 9 October 2026 the bulletin's weekday schedule had "8:30 a.m. at Central Office
Chapel" Monday to Friday (27 September and 4 October issues, page 7). From 12 October the new
weekday schedule moved those Masses to St. Margaret ("Monday through Friday 8:00am Mass at St.
Margaret", 18 October issue, page 5), and the 25 October schedule no longer names the chapel.
The Central Office is on Assumption's grounds: the bulletin sends people to "Assumption Church |
CCBN Central Office, 16 Peter St." and "the Peter Street lot", and the NYS tax-parcel layer puts
16 Peter St and Assumption Church (435 Amherst St) in one parcel owned by Assumption. So the
chapel's earlier Masses belong to `assumption-buffalo` (a chapel on a listed church's grounds),
and it gets no row of its own; with no Mass there now, there is nothing to add either way.
