## Publisher

One bulletin, published by Catholic Family of South Buffalo (Family #31), covering 5 worship site(s). PR 14 modelled this as `catholic-family-south-buffalo`.

## Worship sites

St. Teresa Church; St. Martin of Tours Church; St. Ambrose Church; Holy Family Church; Our Lady of Perpetual Help Church. Each church record gives the Mass evidence. Churches of the family that no longer hold Mass are in excluded/ or open/.

Not a worship site here: the chapel of Mercy Hospital of Buffalo (565 Abbott Road). Every issue
from 20 September to 25 October 2026 lists, among the week's Mass intentions on page 8, "12:00pm •
Mercy Hospital Chapel" Monday to Saturday and "10:00am" on Sunday, all for "All Souls
Intentions". I read them as the hospital chaplaincy's Masses (an inference): the family's own
Mass Times page (read 2026-10-26) lists only its five churches, and neither the bulletin nor Catholic Health's
spiritual-care page says the public may attend. Following the rule other dioceses here apply
(Ogdensburg and Harrisburg: hospital and care-home Masses are not churches without a statement
that the public may attend), it gets no row. A notice inviting the public to these Masses
would change that.
