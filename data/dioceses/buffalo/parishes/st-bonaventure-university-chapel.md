## Publisher

St. Bonaventure University's University Ministries publishes no bulletin. Its page on the university's site gives the campus Mass schedule. It is a `website_schedule` row: its schedule is a source seed drafted from that page (docs/source-schedules-plan.md).

It was in open/ from 16 November 2026 under the owner's rule that a place whose Mass is published online but which publishes no bulletin waits for this feature (diocese.md, Rules).

## Worship sites

The University Chapel in Doyle Hall (church `st-bonaventure-university-chapel`). The weekday Mass is in the Great Room of the McGinley-Carney Center for Franciscan Ministries, a meeting room in another building; it is not a church row here, so the source seed leaves that Mass out.

## Website and bulletin provider

https://www.sbu.edu/life-at-sbu/university-ministries. Provider `website_schedule`.

## Why the schedule looks current

The page is maintained (its news items are dated November 2026, read 24 November 2026), and its Mass Schedule box names the rooms in use now. The box itself is undated, and the page says Masses are offered "during the academic year", so the times may not hold in summer; Discover Mass and catholicmasstime.org give the same times for the school year.

Whether the Sunday Masses run in summer and during breaks.
