## Publisher

Canisius University's Campus Ministry publishes no bulletin for Christ the King Chapel. The chapel's page on the university's site gives its schedule and a calendar of liturgies, and the Masses are public ("All are welcome"). It is a `website_schedule` row: its schedule is a source seed drafted from that page (docs/source-schedules-plan.md). The Campus Ministry Family bulletin (`st-joseph-university-buffalo`; 22 November 2026 issue read) covers Buffalo State's and UB's Newman Centers and St. Joseph University, not Canisius.

It was in open/ from 16 November 2026 under the owner's rule that a place whose Mass is published online but which publishes no bulletin waits for this feature (diocese.md, Rules).

## Worship sites

Christ the King Chapel on the Canisius campus (church `canisius-christ-the-king-chapel`). The summer Sunday-morning Mass at Riverfest Park, 249 Ohio St., is outdoors and not a church row.

## Website and bulletin provider

https://www.canisius.edu/student-experience/faith-service/campus-ministry/christ-king-chapel, the chapel's page on the university's site. Provider `website_schedule`.

## Why the schedule looks current

The chapel page's Calendar of Events covers this autumn and winter: 4 October (the 800th anniversary of St. Francis's death, which falls in 2026), Thanksgiving on 26 November, the First Sunday of Advent on 29 November, and "January 1, 2027" (read 24 November 2026). The Campus Ministry page's heading "2025-26 Mass Schedule" is stale, but its times match the chapel page's.
