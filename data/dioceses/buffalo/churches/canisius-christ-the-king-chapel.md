## Holds Mass

Yes, daily: Sunday 9:30 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. and weekdays 12:05 p.m., public ("All are welcome"; the Campus Ministry and chapel pages, read 24 November 2026). In summer the Sunday-morning Mass moves outdoors to Riverfest Park.

## Name and address

The address is the university's, 2001 Main St., Buffalo 14208; the chapel stands on the campus, on Hughes Avenue in OpenStreetMap, which gives it no house number, so `address_verified` is false. The coordinates are the OSM chapel's. It is on no roster: neither GCatholic nor the diocese's finder or Mass-times pages list it; the diocese's Campus Ministry page links Canisius.
