## Holds Mass

Yes, at least once: the Roman Catholic Community of the Tonawandas' 25 October 2026 bulletin
(page 9) announces "Monday, November 2nd Mass at 12:00 PM St. Francis of Assisi Chapel" for All
Souls' Day, as a parish-wide notice, so the Mass is public. The bulletin's weekly Mass schedule
(page 1) does not include the chapel, and no other Mass there has been found; see `revisit`.
The family's home page lists the chapel first among its sites ("St. Francis of Assisi Chapel 71
Adam St, Tonawanda, NY 14150"), which is why it is a row and not a one-off venue.

This is a separate building from the former St. Francis of Assisi church at 150 Broad Street,
which is `open/st-francis-of-assisi-tonawanda` (no Mass found there). Added 2026-10-26 after a
seed draft flagged the All Souls' Mass; the draft guessed it was in the former church.

## Name and address

Name and address are the family's home page's; the bulletin gives the same name without an
address. OSM has an unnamed building tagged as a chapel at 71 Adam Street, on the old St. Francis
campus between the former church (150 Broad St.) and the school (70 Adam St.). No roster lists the
chapel, and only one source names it, so both flags are false. Coordinates are that OSM building's.
