## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly: Sunday at 11 a.m., public ("We welcome everyone to join us for Mass each Sunday at 11 a.m. held in Holy Peace Chapel.", its own site, read 24 November 2026).

## Name and address

The site names the chapel Holy Peace Chapel (its Mass event and its Visit Us page), and so does OpenStreetMap. The address is the site's, 3621 Roberts Rd., Friendship 14739 (its event listing); OSM tags the chapel building 3701 Roberts Road. The site's is kept as the place's own, so `address_verified` is false. The coordinates are the OSM chapel building's. It is on no roster: neither GCatholic nor the diocese's lists include it.
