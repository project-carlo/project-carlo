## Diocesan Mass-times pages

2026-11-16: the diocese's weekend Mass-times pages list 'St. John the Baptist, Lockport': Sat. 5:30 p.m., Sun. 7 a.m., Sun. 10:30 a.m., Sun. 12:30 p.m. (Every 3rd. Sun. Spanish). They differ from the site, but the 15 November 2026 Central Niagara bulletin (an image) gives a Saturday 4:00 p.m. vigil that is at St. John the Baptist for the second half of the year and at All Saints from January to June, Sunday 7:00 and 10:30 a.m., and a Spanish Mass at 12:30 p.m. on the third Sunday. The site stores all of these, the vigil with its July-December season. The diocese's 'Sat. 5:30 p.m.' is wrong.
