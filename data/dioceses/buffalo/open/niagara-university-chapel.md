## What is known

Niagara University (Vincentian) holds public Mass on campus. Its Campus Ministry page (read 16 November 2026) lists, under "Alumni Chapel", Sunday Mass at 11 a.m. and weekday Mass at 12:15 p.m. during the academic year. GCatholic lists the university's "Chapel of Our Lady of the Angels" at 5795 Lewiston Rd.; whether that is the same room as the Alumni Chapel wasn't checked. No bulletin was found: the Campus Ministry page links none.
