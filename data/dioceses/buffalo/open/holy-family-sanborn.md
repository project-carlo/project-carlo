## What is known

Holy Family, 5180 Chew Rd. on the Tuscarora Nation (Sanborn), holds public Sunday Mass: the diocese's weekend Mass-times pages list it at 10 a.m. from September to May and 9:30 a.m. from June to August. It has no website or bulletin of its own (the Barnabites' site has no parish page), so there is nothing to extract. The diocesan parish finder lists it as "Holy Family [1953]", Tuscarora Reservation.

## Mass times published

- https://www.buffalodiocese.org/sunday-morning-mass-times/ (seen 2026-11-16): "Holy Family Sanborn/Tuscarora Nation Sun. 9:30 a.m. (June 1 – August 31)"; "Holy Family Sanborn/Tuscarora Nation Sun. 10 a.m. (Sept. 1 – May 31)".

## Why it is not decided

Under the owner's rule of 16 November 2026, a place whose public Mass is listed online but which publishes no bulletin is kept open here, not excluded as `no-bulletin`, so a coming feature can show it from the published times. `open/` is the closest status the CLI has: it keeps the place off the site and on the work list. It was excluded as `no-bulletin` from 26 September to 16 November 2026 and was reopened under that rule.
