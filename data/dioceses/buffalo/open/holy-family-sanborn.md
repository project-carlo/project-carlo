## What is known

Holy Family, 5180 Chew Rd. on the Tuscarora Nation (Sanborn), holds public Sunday Mass: the diocese's weekend Mass-times pages list it at 10 a.m. from September to May and 9:30 a.m. from June to August. It has no website or bulletin of its own (the Barnabites' site has no parish page), so there is nothing to extract. The diocesan parish finder lists it as "Holy Family [1953]", Tuscarora Reservation.
