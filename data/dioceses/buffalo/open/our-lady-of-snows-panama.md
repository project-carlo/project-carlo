## What is known

WRFA reported on 12 September 2024 that the diocese had decided to close and sell Our Lady of Snows along with three other south-county churches. The current Catholic Neighbors in Faith bulletin has no Mass at Panama, but its parishes page lists only Loreto and Victory as closed.

## Why it is not decided

The WRFA story reports a decision, not a closing. Of the four churches it named, SS. Peter and Paul still holds Mass.
