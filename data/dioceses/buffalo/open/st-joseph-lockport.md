## What is known

The September 2024 summary lists the St. Joseph site under "merging and closing". The Central Niagara bulletin's Mass schedule does not include it, but the April 2026 masterlist still carries it.

## Why it is not decided

No source seen states that it closed.

2026-11-16: The 2024-2025 bulletins call 391 Market St. the "St. Joseph Oratory", the family's faith-formation office and classrooms (Carra Center), and in November 2025 still had an SJO code in the Mass-intentions legend, though no week sampled had a Mass there. By 22 November 2026 the legend has dropped SJO. That is consistent with its closing between those dates, but no source seen says it closed, and the 2026 issues are images, so a closing notice in them could only be found by rendering each one. Still open.
