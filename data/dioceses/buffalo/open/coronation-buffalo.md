## What is known

Coronation, 348 Dewitt St., Buffalo was the home of Western New York's Vietnamese Catholic community. Wikipedia still lists it as a current church with Mass "in both Vietnamese and English". But it isn't on the diocese's parish finder, Family of Parishes masterlist or weekend Mass-times pages, those pages now give a Vietnamese Mass at Our Lady Help of Christians, Cheektowaga, and news reports (WIVB, Audacy, 2024) put it on the Road to Renewal closure list.

## Mass times published

None found current. Wikipedia's undated sentence that Mass is held in Vietnamese and English is the only Mass claim.

Under the owner's rule of 16 November 2026, a place whose public Mass is listed online but which publishes no bulletin is kept open here, not excluded as `no-bulletin`, so a coming feature can show it from the published times. `open/` is the closest status the CLI has: it keeps the place off the site and on the work list. Here the place's status itself is unsettled: the evidence points to closure but no source states it, so it stays open.
