## What is known

Mt. Irenaeus, a Franciscan friary and retreat at 3621 Roberts Rd., Friendship (Allegany County), invites the public to Sunday Mass at 11 a.m. (its site, read 16 November 2026). A search result names the chapel Holy Peace Chapel; the pages read don't. It publishes no bulletin, and it is on no roster. The older domain mountirenaeus.org no longer resolves; the site is mountainonline.org.
