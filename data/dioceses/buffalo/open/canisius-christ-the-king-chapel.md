## What is known

Canisius University's Christ the King Chapel holds public Masses all year: Sunday 9:30 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. and weekdays 12:05 p.m. during the academic year, "All are welcome" (Campus Ministry, read 16 November 2026). The chapel page says its Faith Community liturgies "are not limited to the Academic schedule"; in summer the Sunday morning Mass is held outdoors at Riverfest Park. The diocese's Campus Ministry page links Canisius among its campus ministries. No bulletin was found. It is on no roster: neither GCatholic nor the diocese's finder or Mass-times pages list it. The street address is the university's (2001 Main St.), not checked against a second source.

## Mass times published

- https://www.canisius.edu/student-experience/faith-service/campus-ministry (seen 2026-11-16): "Sunday Mass on campus is at 9:30am and 7:00pm. We also have Weekday Mass on M-F at 12:05pm in Christ the King Chapel."
- https://www.canisius.edu/student-experience/faith-service/campus-ministry/christ-king-chapel (seen 2026-11-16): summer Sunday morning Mass at 9:00 a.m. at Riverfest Park, 249 Ohio St. (28 June to 23 August: "our morning Mass will be at 9:00 a.m. at Riverfest Park"); Sunday 7:00 p.m. "year-round, Christ the King Chapel"; weekdays Monday-Friday 12:05 p.m.
