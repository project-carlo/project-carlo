## What is known

The September 2024 summary lists Divine Mercy under "merging and closing". The Niagara Falls R.C. Family bulletin's Mass schedule names Prince of Peace, St. Leo, St. John de LaSalle, St. Joseph and St. Mary of the Cataract, not Divine Mercy.

## Why it is not decided

A plan plus absence from one schedule is not a stated closure.
