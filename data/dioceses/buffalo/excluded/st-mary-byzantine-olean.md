## Why it is excluded

St. Mary Byzantine Catholic Church, 331 Fountain St., Olean, New York 14760 is a Ruthenian (Byzantine) Catholic church. GCatholic (read 2026-11-16) lists it among the churches in the territory of the Roman Diocese of Buffalo but gives its "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic" and "Rite: Ruthenian (Byzantine)". It belongs to the Byzantine Catholic Eparchy of Passaic, not the Roman Catholic Diocese of Buffalo, and the diocese's parish finder and weekend Mass-times pages don't include it. So `other-jurisdiction`; nothing was checked about its Divine Liturgy schedule.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it would be covered if the Byzantine Catholic Eparchy of Passaic were ever explored.
