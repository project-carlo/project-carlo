## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Mount Carmel, 2499 Independence Ave. (27th St.), a worship site of Holy Family of Jesus, Mary and Joseph, closed by the bishop's decree effective 5 April 2025 (Niagara Falls R.C. Family bulletin, 30 March 2025: "Fr. Mario received the decree from Bishop Michael Fisher for Our Lady of Mt. Carmel church to close, effective April 5, 2025."). It had a Sunday 10:30 a.m. Mass in November 2023 and none in the family schedule from 30 March 2025. The last Mass isn't dated, so `last_mass` is empty. It isn't on the diocese's parish finder, Family of Parishes masterlist or weekend Mass-times pages (rosters fetched 26 September 2026; finder and Mass-times pages re-read 14 November 2026).
