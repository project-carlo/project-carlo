## Why it is excluded

St. Anthony, 306 Ingham Ave., Lackawanna held its closing Mass at noon on Sunday 27 April 2025 (Our Lady of the Sacred Heart family bulletin, 13 April 2025: "April 27th at 12:00 pm will be our closing mass", and "Saint Anthony's as they prepare for their final mass on April 27th"). The parish merged into Our Lady of the Sacred Heart, Orchard Park. It isn't on the diocese's parish finder, Family of Parishes masterlist or weekend Mass-times pages (rosters fetched 26 September 2026; finder and Mass-times pages re-read 14 November 2026).
