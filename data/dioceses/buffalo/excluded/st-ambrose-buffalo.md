## Why it is excluded

The Catholic Family of South Buffalo bulletin of 18 October 2026 announced "Final Mass at St. Ambrose, November 1", the 9:30 a.m. Sunday Mass on All Saints' Day. The 1 and 8 November issues speak of "the closing of St. Ambrose Church" and move its Saturday 4:00 p.m., Sunday 9:30 a.m. and daily Mass intentions to Holy Family. From the 8 November issue the masthead lists only Holy Family and St. Martin of Tours as Our Lady of Charity's worship sites, and no Mass list (8 and 15 November) has a St. Ambrose line. `last_mass` is the announced date; no later issue reports on the Mass itself.

The diocese's parish finder and weekend Mass-times pages still listed St. Ambrose with Saturday 4:00 p.m. and Sunday 9:30 a.m. on 14 November 2026: they lag the bulletin, as they did for Queen of Angels.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. The family's central office stays at the St. Ambrose site, 260 Okell St., so the name will keep appearing in the bulletin's contact lines; that is not Mass. No relegation decree or sale was looked for.
