## Why it is excluded

The bishop reduced the church to profane use. His decree of 6 January 2025, posted by the Catholic Neighbors in Faith family, orders "that the church of Our Lady of the Snows, located at 36 E. Main Street, Panama, New York 14767, be reduced to profane but not sordid use", effective Monday 24 March 2025, with a dispensation allowing the property to be sold. So no Mass has been possible there since 24 March 2025. The last Mass isn't dated in any source found, so `last_mass` is empty. WRFA (12 September 2024) and the Post-Journal (September 2024) reported the decision to close and sell it, the family's current bulletin has no Mass at Panama, and the diocese's Mass-times pages don't list it. The diocesan parish finder still does.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
