## Why it is excluded

The bishop reduced the church to profane use. His decree of 5 March 2025 ("Decree reducing the church of St. Joseph, Lockport and its associated property to profane but not sordid use") orders "that the church of St. Joseph, located at 391 Market Street, Lockport, New York 14094, be reduced to profane but not sordid use", with its altars losing their consecration, effective Monday 2 June 2025. Page 1 gives its history: the church at Adam and Market Streets, dedicated in 1956, was an oratory of All Saints Parish from the 2008 merger. So no Mass has been possible there since 2 June 2025; no source found gives the date of the last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty.

This agrees with the bulletins. Central Niagara issues from September 2024 to November 2025 call 391 Market St. the "St. Joseph Oratory", the faith-formation office and classrooms (Carra Center), with no Mass there in any week sampled. The 22 November 2026 Mass-intentions legend (an image page) no longer has an SJO code. The April 2026 Family of Parishes masterlist still lists St. Joseph, Lockport as a worship site, and that list lags on closures.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
