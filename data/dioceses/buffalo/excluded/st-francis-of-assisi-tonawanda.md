## Why it is excluded

The church at 150 Broad St., Tonawanda held its last Mass on Sunday 27 April 2025, a noon Mass of Thanksgiving. The Tonawandas bulletin of 13 April 2025 (container 14/1840, page 3) announces "Mass of Thanksgiving Sunday, April 27, 2025 at 12:00pm St. Francis of Assisi Church" as one of "the final Masses of St. Francis of Assisi Church and St. Jude the Apostle Parish", and the pastor's letter of 27 April says "This weekend is the final Mass for St. Francis of Assisi in the city of Tonawanda." The family's own St. Francis page (read 16 November 2026) confirms "the final Mass was said in the church in April 2025" and that the church is being converted into the RCCT Outreach Center, Matthew 25 Ministries.

The St. Francis of Assisi Chapel at 71 Adam St., a separate building about 50 m north, still holds occasional Masses and has its own church row (`churches/st-francis-of-assisi-chapel-tonawanda.md`); this exclusion is the church only.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
