## Why it is excluded

The church at 496 Terrace Blvd., Depew (St. James, latterly the church of Blessed / St. Mother Teresa of Calcutta parish) held its last Mass on Sunday 8 June 2025 at 8:30 am. The Beloved Disciples family bulletin of 1 June 2025 (container 14/1888, page 4) announces "SMT - Final Mass 6/8 at 8:30 am St. Mother Teresa of Calcutta"; that issue's weekend schedule still has Sat. 4:00 pm and Sun. 8:30 am there. From the 15 June 2025 issue the masthead drops St. Mother Teresa and the schedule has no Mass there; monthly issues to 22 November 2026 have none either.

The current bulletin (22 November 2026, page 9) still prints "ST. JAMES CHURCH (SJC)" and its address as part of St. Mary of the Assumption parish, and an SJC code in its abbreviation list, but no Mass at SJC appears in its Mass schedule and intentions (page 3). That reads as the building and its parishioners' records being kept, not as Mass there. It is also absent from the diocese's weekend Mass-times pages (re-read 14 November 2026).

## What would change this

A Mass at SJC in the Beloved Disciples Mass schedule.
