## Why it is excluded

Immaculate Conception, 88 N. Main St., Cassadaga merged into St. Anthony, Fredonia in 2008 and "used for weekend masses until it closed in 2014" (Wikipedia's list of the diocese's churches; that row cites nothing, so it is the weakest source in this record). Everything else agrees: the sacramental-records index ends the parish in 2008; The Lord's Vineyard bulletins from September 2024 to November 2026 never mention Cassadaga; the family's churches page, which dates its other closings, doesn't name it; and the diocese's weekend Mass-times pages don't list it. The diocesan parish finder still lists it as a St. Anthony worship site, though St. Anthony itself closed on 31 August 2026.

## What would change this

A source showing Mass at Cassadaga after 2014; a St. Anthony bulletin from 2014 would date the last Mass.
