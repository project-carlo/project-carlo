## Why it is excluded

St. Joseph Maronite Catholic Church, 1102 Walnut St., Olean, New York 14760-1497 is a Maronite Catholic church. GCatholic (read 2026-11-16) lists it among the churches in the territory of the Roman Diocese of Buffalo but gives its "Jurisdiction: Maronite Diocese of Saint Maron of Brooklyn" and "Rite: Maronite (Antiochian)". It belongs to the Maronite Eparchy of Saint Maron of Brooklyn, not the Roman Catholic Diocese of Buffalo, and the diocese's parish finder and weekend Mass-times pages don't include it. So `other-jurisdiction`; nothing was checked about its Divine Liturgy schedule. GCatholic notes it was "formerly Oratory of the Transfiguration", a Roman-rite oratory; it is now a Maronite church, so the Olean Roman Catholic churches are unaffected.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it would be covered if the Maronite Eparchy of Saint Maron of Brooklyn were ever explored.
