## Why it is excluded

Protection of the Blessed Virgin Mary Ukrainian Catholic Church, 2715 Ferry Ave., Niagara Falls, New York 14301 is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church. GCatholic (read 2026-11-16) lists it among the churches in the territory of the Roman Diocese of Buffalo but gives its "Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Diocese of Stamford" and "Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)". It belongs to the Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Stamford, not the Roman Catholic Diocese of Buffalo, and the diocese's parish finder and weekend Mass-times pages don't include it. So `other-jurisdiction`; nothing was checked about its Divine Liturgy schedule.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it would be covered if the Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Stamford were ever explored.
