## Why it is excluded

Closed on 31 March 2025 and sold. The community's own notice board says so: on 27 January 2025, that the church had been sold to O'Hara High School next door and had to be vacated by the end of May, with Masses until the end of February; on 1 April 2025, that the church "officially closed as of yesterday (31 March 2025)". Its September 2025 anniversary notice says "the Buffalo Diocese closed St. Andrew Kim Korean Catholic Church". The last Mass in the building is not stated; a farewell Mass was planned for 24 February 2025, so `last_mass` is left empty.

It was a live church (and its own `no_bulletin` parish row, `st-andrew-kim-tonawanda`) until 7 October 2026, on the strength of the home page's "주일미사 : 일요일 오전 11시" (Sunday Mass 11 am), which is stale. The community now gathers only for occasional Masses elsewhere (18 October 2026 at the Sisters of St. Mary of Namur, Buffalo), which is no place to list. The parish row was deleted with this exclusion: it had no other church and no bulletin, and `promote` had already taken it off the site.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. A regular Korean Mass at a new place would be a new church record (and a parish row only if it publishes a bulletin).
