## Why it is excluded

St. Agatha, South Buffalo merged with Holy Family and St. Ambrose into Our Lady of Charity in 2010, and the parish later sold the building to developer Sam Savarino (Wikipedia's list of the diocese's churches; its Our Lady of Charity entry adds that the St. Agatha complex "was sold to a private developer in 2018"). The South Buffalo family's bulletin lists only Holy Family and St. Martin of Tours. GCatholic's address is 54 Alamo Place; Wikipedia gives 65 Abbott Rd. for the same complex. It isn't on the diocese's parish finder, Family of Parishes masterlist or weekend Mass-times pages (rosters fetched 26 September 2026; finder and Mass-times pages re-read 14 November 2026).
