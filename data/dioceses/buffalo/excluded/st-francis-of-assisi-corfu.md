## Why it is excluded

St. Francis of Assisi, 18 W. Main St., Corfu, the Corfu site of St. Maximilian Kolbe Parish, closed in 2024 when that parish merged into Resurrection, Batavia. The September 2024 Road to Renewal summary lists "St. Maximilian Kolbe, Corfu site" under "Parishes/sites merged and closed", which this diocese reads as a closed building for "site" entries (diocese.md), and Wikipedia's list gives it as closed in 2024. It isn't on the diocese's parish finder, Family of Parishes masterlist or weekend Mass-times pages (rosters fetched 26 September 2026; finder and Mass-times pages re-read 14 November 2026).

Nothing expected.
