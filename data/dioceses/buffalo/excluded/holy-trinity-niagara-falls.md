## Why it is excluded

Holy Trinity, 1419 Falls St., Niagara Falls merged into Divine Mercy in 2008; "The last Mass at Holy Trinity was held on Easter Sunday of 2008" (23 March 2008), and the building was bought by Niagara Heritage of Hope and Service in 2010 and runs as a historic site (Wikipedia's list of the diocese's churches). GCatholic lists it as a former church. It isn't on the diocese's parish finder, Family of Parishes masterlist or weekend Mass-times pages (rosters fetched 26 September 2026; finder and Mass-times pages re-read 14 November 2026).
