## Why it is excluded

St. Augustine, 425 Penora St., Depew closed in 2008 when it merged with St. James into Blessed Mother Teresa of Calcutta Parish, and was sold to a Wesleyan church (Wikipedia's list of the diocese's churches); GCatholic gives it as "now The Vine Wesleyan Church". It isn't on the diocese's parish finder, Family of Parishes masterlist or weekend Mass-times pages (rosters fetched 26 September 2026; finder and Mass-times pages re-read 14 November 2026).
