## Why it is excluded

The Olean Times Herald of 21 August 2025 reports "A final Mass was celebrated this past summer in the Oratory of the Sacred Heart of Portville. The church building is now up for sale." The family's own page for the building (emcatholic.org, read 16 November 2026) is a sale listing ("Make an offer") with no Mass times, and the family bulletin advertised it for sale on 27 September 2026 (page 6). The Enchanted Mountains Mass schedule has no Portville Mass and the diocese's weekend Mass-times pages don't list it.

The newspaper gives only "this past summer", so `last_mass` is the year, 2025. A search summary said 1 July 2025, but that wasn't read in the article itself, so it isn't recorded as the date.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; a family bulletin from June or July 2025 would give the exact date.
