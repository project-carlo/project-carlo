## Why it is excluded

Divine Mercy's own website, as archived on 4 April 2025, says "Divine Mercy RC Church is now closed Holy Trinity Cemetery remains open", with a farewell to its parishioners. The diocese's sacramental-records index gives the parish as "Divine Mercy, Niagara Falls (2008-2024)", its records now at Holy Family of Jesus, Mary & Joseph. In November 2023 it still had a Sunday 11 a.m. Mass (WNY Papers, the family's combined schedule); the family bulletin from its first issue in container 14/1887 (30 March 2025) to 22 November 2026 never schedules one there, and the diocese's weekend Mass-times pages (re-read 14 November 2026) don't list it.

No source found gives the date of the last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty; the evidence puts it between 25 November 2023 and 4 April 2025, most likely late 2024 when the parish ended. The diocesan parish finder still lists it at 2437 Niagara St.

## What would change this

A Mass at 2437 Niagara St. in the Niagara Falls family bulletin. A bulletin or news story giving the final Mass would add the `last_mass` date.
