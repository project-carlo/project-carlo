## Why it is excluded

The diocese's September 2024 summary lists the Strykersville site under "Parishes/sites merged and closed", and the current Fields of Grace bulletin gives Mass times for five other sites and none here. That heading also lists whole parishes whose churches still hold Mass (St. George, St. Brigid, Ascension), so only its entries marked "site" are read as closed buildings.
