## Why it is excluded

St. Francis Xavier, 157 East St., Buffalo (Black Rock) is closed and now a museum (GCatholic: "closed; now museum"); Wikipedia's list of the diocese's churches gives the parish as defunct in 2007 and says its congregation moved to Assumption on that dissolution, which is where `last_mass` (2007) comes from. It isn't on the diocese's parish finder, Family of Parishes masterlist or weekend Mass-times pages (rosters fetched 26 September 2026; finder and Mass-times pages re-read 14 November 2026).
