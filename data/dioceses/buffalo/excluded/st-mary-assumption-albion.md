## Why it is excluded

St. Mary of the Assumption, 47 Brown St., Albion merged into Holy Family, Albion in 2007, was used as an oratory for a time, and was sold in 2011 to Light of Victory Church (Wikipedia's list of the diocese's churches). Wikipedia's "Closed" year is 2007 but it also says the oratory was used afterwards, so `last_mass` is empty. It isn't on the diocese's parish finder, Family of Parishes masterlist or weekend Mass-times pages (rosters fetched 26 September 2026; finder and Mass-times pages re-read 14 November 2026).
