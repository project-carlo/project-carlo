## Why it is excluded

The St. John Neumann Shrine, at St. John the Baptist, Kenmore (Light of Christ), is a chapel used for Eucharistic Adoration, not for scheduled Mass. The 22 November 2026 Light of Christ bulletin (page 2) says "The Saint John Neumann Shrine is open for Eucharistic Adoration Monday through Friday from 2 to 4pm. Holy Hour with Vespers is offered Sunday from 3 - 4pm." and its Mass schedules list no Mass there; the 25 October issue is the same. GCatholic lists it as a shrine and mission of St. John the Baptist, with no street address. It isn't on the diocese's finder or Mass-times pages. So `no-public-mass`: open for prayer, with no public Mass found.

## What would change this

A Mass scheduled at the shrine in the Light of Christ bulletin.
