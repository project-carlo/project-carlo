## Why it is excluded

The Dominican nuns of the Perpetual Rosary left their monastery at 335 Doat St., Buffalo for Ohio in August 2020 and sold it (Western New York Catholic, 1 September 2021: the community "moved from Buffalo’s East Side to Newark, Ohio, last August" and "sold the Our Lady of the Rosary monastery last year"). Their federation now describes the monastery as "formerly of Buffalo, NY/Heath, OH". `last_mass` is the year they left. It isn't on the diocese's parish finder, Family of Parishes masterlist or weekend Mass-times pages (rosters fetched 26 September 2026; finder and Mass-times pages re-read 14 November 2026).
