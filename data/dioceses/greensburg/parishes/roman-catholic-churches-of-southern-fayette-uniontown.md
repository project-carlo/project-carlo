## Publisher

The four Uniontown parishes, St. John the Evangelist (directory 283), St. Joseph (285), St. Mary (Nativity) (287) and St. Therese, Little Flower of Jesus (289), share one pastor (Very Rev. Anthony J. Klimko), one office at 61 N. Mount Vernon Avenue and one weekly bulletin, "The Roman Catholic Churches of Southern Fayette", on ParishesOnline container 14/1769. The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-22; pages 1-13 are images, so pages 1-2 were rendered and read) gives one box per church with address and Mass times. The same bulletin also covers three parishes outside the city with the same pastor: Ss. Cyril & Methodius (Fairchance, directory 097), St. Hubert (Point Marion, 245) and St. Joan of Arc (Farmington, 099); the masthead's office box lists "Ss. Cyril & Methodius/St. Hubert Parish" and "St. Joan of Arc Parish" offices beside the Uniontown one. The 4 January 2026 masthead has the same seven churches. The 12 November 2023 issue's Mass-intention key has six (no St. Joan of Arc), so Farmington joined between then and January 2026.

None of the three has a channel of its own now. Fairchance and Point Marion published "THE PARTNER PARISHES OF Ss. Cyril & Methodius AND St. Hubert" on container 14/1792 until 22 October 2023 (its last issue; both of its LPi orgs list nothing newer and no 2026 issue answers), and the first 14/1769 issue checked, 12 November 2023, already carries their codes SCM and SH. Their website cmhparishes.org and St. Joan of Arc's sjoafarmington.org both redirect to faysouth.org (headed Chrome, 2026-10-22), whose "BULLETINS" link is this row's ParishesOnline org. No LPi org or Discover Mass page exists for St. Joan of Arc. The old 14/1792 ids are recorded in `publication` as this bulletin's predecessor so no other row claims them.

The directory gives each Uniontown parish its own website and no partners, but all four domains now redirect to the shared site faysouth.org, which has one Mass schedule for all seven churches and no separate bulletins. The per-parish LPi organizations for St. John, St. Joseph and St. Therese exist but publish nothing (empty orgs, not other channels). No Uniontown parish has a channel of its own, so the four are one row.

## Worship sites

Seven churches, the seven Mass boxes on page 1 of the 25 October 2026 issue and the seven on faysouth.org's schedule: St. John the Evangelist (50 Jefferson St.), St. Joseph (180 Old Walnut Hill Rd.), St. Mary Nativity (61 N. Mount Vernon Ave.) and St. Therese (61 Mill St.) in Uniontown, and Ss. Cyril & Methodius (50 N. Morgantown St., Fairchance), St. Hubert (9 Sadler St., Point Marion) and St. Joan of Arc (3523 National Pike, Farmington). No chapel or mission appears in either. St. Francis of Assisi (Masontown and Footedale) shares the pastor and vicars but publishes its own bulletin (container 14/0702) and is its own row, `st-francis-of-assisi-masontown`. faysouth.org also has pages for "Our Lady of the Mountains" (an outdoor prayer shrine) and "Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary" (a cemetery, plots through the St. Joan of Arc office); both belong to the Farmington parish, neither is a Mass site in the bulletin, and no roster lists either, so neither has a record (the roster's "Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary" is the Mount Pleasant parish). The Former Churches Directory also sends St. Helen, Shoaf (closed 2007) to Ss. Cyril & Methodius and Visitation BVM, Elliottsville (1962) to St. Joan of Arc; no roster lists either as open. The Former Churches Directory sends St. Cecilia, Lemont Furnace (closed 2006) to St. Mary (Nativity), and Holy Cross, Uniontown and St. Callistus, Republic (both 1915) to St. Therese; no roster lists them as open.
