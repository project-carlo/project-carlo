## Publisher

The four Uniontown parishes, St. John the Evangelist (directory 283), St. Joseph (285), St. Mary (Nativity) (287) and St. Therese, Little Flower of Jesus (289), share one pastor (Very Rev. Anthony J. Klimko), one office at 61 N. Mount Vernon Avenue and one weekly bulletin, "The Roman Catholic Churches of Southern Fayette", on ParishesOnline container 14/1769. The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-22; pages 1-13 are images, so pages 1-2 were rendered and read) gives one box per church with address and Mass times. The same bulletin also covers three parishes outside the city with the same pastor: Ss. Cyril & Methodius (Fairchance), St. Hubert (Point Marion) and St. Joan of Arc (Farmington). Those are in another batch and are not modelled here yet (see *Not yet confirmed*). The 4 January 2026 masthead has the same seven churches.

The directory gives each Uniontown parish its own website and no partners, but all four domains now redirect to the shared site faysouth.org, which has one Mass schedule for all seven churches and no separate bulletins. The per-parish LPi organizations for St. John, St. Joseph and St. Therese exist but publish nothing (empty orgs, not other channels). No Uniontown parish has a channel of its own, so the four are one row.

## Worship sites

St. John the Evangelist (50 Jefferson St.), St. Joseph (180 Old Walnut Hill Rd.), St. Mary Nativity (61 N. Mount Vernon Ave.) and St. Therese (61 Mill St.): the four Uniontown churches in the bulletin's Mass boxes and on faysouth.org's schedule. No Uniontown chapel or mission appears in either. faysouth.org also has pages for "Our Lady of the Mountains" (an outdoor prayer shrine) and "Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary" (a cemetery, plots through the St. Joan of Arc office); both belong to the Farmington parish and neither is a Mass site in the bulletin. The Former Churches Directory sends St. Cecilia, Lemont Furnace (closed 2006) to St. Mary (Nativity), and Holy Cross, Uniontown and St. Callistus, Republic (both 1915) to St. Therese; no roster lists them as open.
