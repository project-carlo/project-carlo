## Publisher

They publish **one** weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 14/4007, LPi organization `christ-the-king-st-gertrude-churches`. The 25 October 2026 issue (the latest on 2026-10-22) is headed "Partner Parishes of the Diocese of Greensburg and Pennsylvania Charitable Trusts ST. GERTRUDE PARISH CHRIST THE KING PARISH", with both addresses and both websites. Page 2 has one joint Mass schedule for the two churches. The LPi footer reads "Christ the King & St. Gertrude Churches, Leechburg, PA 14-4007".

Both parish websites repost this container's issue, with the same file name (`20261018B.pdf`) in one "Weekly Bulletin for <date>" post per week. These are copies, not separate bulletins. St. Gertrude's own LPi organization `st-gertrude-church-15690` listed no publications: it is empty, not a channel.

## Worship sites

Two churches, the two in the masthead and the Mass times. Christ the King, 125 Park Road, Leechburg, has Saturday 5:30pm and Sunday 9:30am, plus 8:30am on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. St. Gertrude, 303 Franklin Ave., Vandergrift, has Saturday 4:00pm and Sunday 8:00am and 11:00am, plus 8:30am on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. GCatholic (24095, 24131) and the directory list no other site for either parish. St. Michael, West Leechburg (GCatholic 17412) is a Byzantine church and is left to the Eastern-churches task.
