## Publisher

The Epiphany of Our Lord Parish, Monessen publishes its own weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline (container
14/1319). The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-22, page 1) gives "OUR LOCATION Epiphany of Our Lord Catholic
Church 44 Pennsylvania Blvd., Monessen" with Rev. Anthony Onoko as pastor, and a Mass schedule for Epiphany alone:
Saturday 4:00 p.m., Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 a.m., Monday and Thursday 8:00 a.m.

Fr. Onoko is also pastor of St. Sebastian, Belle Vernon and St. John the Baptist, Perryopolis, and the three run one
weekday rota (page 3: "8:00 AM Mass at St. Sebastian", "9:00 AM Mass at St. John the Baptist"; page 2 speaks of
"our three partner parishes"). The directory lists no Partner Parishes for any of them. Each publishes its own
issue for 25 October 2026: St. Sebastian in LPi container 14/1696, St. John the Baptist on Discover Mass. Three
live channels, so three rows (st-sebastian-belle-vernon, st-john-the-baptist-perryopolis).

## Worship sites

One: Epiphany of Our Lord church, 44 Pennsylvania Blvd., Monessen, the only place the bulletin names, and the
directory's address.

GCatholic lists a second "Parish of the Epiphany of Our Lord" at 618 Knox Ave., Monessen (24169), as the parish
seat with 44 Pennsylvania Blvd. (24178) as its mission. The parish site still has an orphaned page "Main Church -
Knox Ave" (618 Knox Ave.) and its homepage says the parish has "two consecrated sites for worship", but its menu's
Site Locations lists only "Epiphany Church Pennsylvania Blvd", and neither the 25 October 2026 issue nor the
December 2025 and June 2024 issues mention Knox Ave. No source found says the Knox Ave building closed, so it is in
`open/` (epiphany-knox-ave-monessen) rather than excluded. The Former Churches Directory lists five Monessen parishes
(St. Anthony, St. Cajetan, Most Holy Name of Jesus, St. Hyacinth, St. Leonard) closed in 1991 into Epiphany.
