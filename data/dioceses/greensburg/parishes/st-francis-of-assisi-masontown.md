## Publisher

Saint Francis of Assisi Parish (Western Fayette County) publishes its own weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline container 14/0702. The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-22) has the masthead "Saint Francis of Assisi / Western Fayette County / ROMAN CATHOLIC PARISH / 101 West Church Avenue, Masontown" and two Mass boxes, "Masses in Masontown" and "Masses in Footedale" (page 1 rendered to read the box headings, which are not in the text layer); its LPi footer reads "St. Francis of Assisi, Masontown, PA 14-0702".

Its administrator, Very Rev. Anthony J. Klimko, is also pastor of the seven churches of The Roman Catholic Churches of Southern Fayette (container 14/1769), and the same four parochial vicars serve both. The two bulletins are separate: St. Francis is not in the 14/1769 masthead, and 14/0702 names only St. Francis. Two live channels, so two rows (group `Southern Fayette (Fr. Klimko)` records the shared clergy).

## Worship sites

Two churches, the two Mass boxes on page 1: Masontown (101 West Church Avenue; Saturday 6:00 pm, Sunday 11:30 am, Monday and Friday 8:00 am) and Footedale (528 Footedale Road, New Salem; Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:30 am, Tuesday and Thursday 8:00 am). The bulletin also mentions "the social hall in Footedale" (a hall, not a Mass site). The Former Churches Directory sends nine closed parishes here (All Saints, Masontown; St. Thomas, Footedale; St. Procopius, New Salem; Holy Rosary, Republic; Our Lady of Perpetual Help, Leckrone; St. Mary, Cardale, all 2013; St. Francis de Sales, McClellandtown and St. Albert, Palmer, 2008; St. Mary, Gates Mine, 1985). No roster lists any of them as open, so none has a record; which former building each site occupies was not checked. The directory's Masontown line still has a Sunday 8:00 a.m. Mass that the bulletin no longer lists; the bulletin is followed.
