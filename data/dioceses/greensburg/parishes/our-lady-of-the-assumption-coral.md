## Publisher

Our Lady of the Assumption Parish, Coral and Lucernemines, posts its own weekly bulletin on its diocese-template site, one post per issue under /bulletins/. The 25 October 2026 issue (OCTOBER-25-2026-1.pdf, posted 21 Oct) is headed on page 1 with the Mass times for Lucernemines and Coral, "P.O. Box G 2434 Neal Road Coral, PA 15731" and "Pastor - The Very Reverend John A. Pavlik, OFM Cap, V.F.". Posts run weekly back through at least 30 Aug 2026.

It is not LPi-printed now: its two LPi orgs are empty and the old container 14/1598 (a 7 Aug 2022 repost on the site carries that number) has no current issues. It does not share a bulletin with Church of the Resurrection (Clymer), which has another pastor (Fr. Craig Alexander) and its own container 14/1395; the directory lists no Partner Parishes for either.

The parish was formed in 2008: the Former Churches Directory lists St. Francis (Coral/Graceton) and St. Louis (Lucernemines) as closed in 2008 into Our Lady of the Assumption (and St. Bonaventure, Black Lick, in 2002; Seven Dolors, Waterman, in 1961).

These match the directory's site pages 070CS and 070LS and GCatholic 24109 and 24176. Nothing else is listed.
