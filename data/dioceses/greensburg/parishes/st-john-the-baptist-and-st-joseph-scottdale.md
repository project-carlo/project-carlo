## Publisher

St. John the Baptist Parish, Scottdale (directory 257) and St. Joseph Parish, Everson (directory 093) are diocesan partner parishes with one pastor (the Very Rev. Paul A. Lisik, V.F., who also has the two Mount Pleasant parishes and the Connellsville/Dunbar parishes), one office at 416 S. Broadway Ave., Scottdale, and one website, stjohnsandstjosephs.org. They publish one weekly bulletin, printed by LPi in ParishesOnline container 14/0732 (org st-john-the-baptist-and-st-joseph-parishes). The 25 October 2026 issue (8 pages, read 2026-10-22 with `probe pdf`) names both churches with their addresses in its page-1 masthead and gives one combined schedule, coding the churches SJB and SJE. One bulletin, so one row.

It is separate from the Mount Pleasant bulletin (`st-pius-x-and-visitation-mount-pleasant`, self-hosted) despite the shared pastor and parochial vicars: different masthead, office and staff, and neither prints the other's Mass schedule.

## Worship sites

St. John the Baptist (416 S. Broadway, Scottdale) and St. Joseph (201 Painter St., Everson): the only two places in the 25 Oct liturgical schedule (Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 10:30 am SJB, Sunday 8:30 am SJE, weekday Masses split between them). GCatholic and the directory list no other Roman Catholic site in Scottdale or Everson. The Byzantine St. John the Baptist at 525 Porter Ave., Scottdale (gcatholic 17317) is another jurisdiction and not part of this parish. St. Joseph Hall / school hall in the bulletin is a hall.
