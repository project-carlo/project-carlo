## Publisher

The Yough Catholic Community of Connellsville & Dunbar publishes one weekly bulletin for four diocesan parishes: Immaculate Conception, St. John the Evangelist and St. Rita in Connellsville (directory 065, 067, 069, listed as three-way partners) and St. Aloysius in Dunbar (directory 083, listed with no partner but sharing the pastor and website). The 17-18 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-22 with `probe pdf`) names all four in its page-1 masthead: "THE PARISHES OF IMMACULATE CONCEPTION, ST. ALOYSIUS GONZAGA, ST. JOHN THE EVANGELIST, & ST. RITA OF CASCIA", with a Connellsville office (116 S. 2nd St.) and a Dunbar office (459 Ranch Rd.). The latest issue, 24-25 October 2026, has the same masthead (rendered page 1). One bulletin, so one row. So St. Aloysius, missing from the directory's partner list, is in this row too.

The pastor (Very Rev. Paul Lisik) also has the Mount Pleasant and Scottdale/Everson parishes, but those publish their own bulletins (`st-pius-x-and-visitation-mount-pleasant`, `st-john-the-baptist-and-st-joseph-scottdale`), and batch 9 found that neither prints a Connellsville or Dunbar schedule. This bulletin's announcements name only the four churches here, apart from events in Mount Pleasant and Uniontown.

## Worship sites

Immaculate Conception (148 E. Crawford Ave.), St. John the Evangelist (908 W. Crawford Ave.) and St. Rita (116 S. 2nd St.), Connellsville, and St. Aloysius (459 Ranch Rd.), Dunbar. These are the four places on the site's Mass Times page (read headed 2026-10-22: Saturday 4:00 pm St. Rita, 5:00 pm St. Aloysius, 6:00 pm Immaculate Conception; Sunday 8:00 am St. Rita, 9:00 am St. Aloysius, 9:30 am Immaculate Conception, 11:00 am St. John the Evangelist), and the four in the 24-25 Oct issue's All Souls Day schedule (page 6, image). The bulletin prints no weekend grid. The weekend times agree with the diocesan directory's lines for 065/067/069/083.

GCatholic and the directory list no other Roman Catholic church in Connellsville or Dunbar. The Former Churches Directory lists Holy Trinity (closed 2008), Our Lady of Mount Carmel (1913) and St. Emory (1970), Connellsville; Sacred Heart, Dawson (2007); St. Vincent de Paul, Leisenring (2008) and St. Polycarp, West Leisenring (1997), with their records at these parishes. No roster lists them as open, so they have no records. An empty LPi org `holy-trinity-church-15425` (109 N. 7th St.) survives. GCatholic's St. Stephen, Leisenring (17297) is Byzantine Ruthenian, another jurisdiction, and not part of this parish.
