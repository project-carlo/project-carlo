## Publisher

Ascension Parish (directory 147) and Sacred Heart Parish (directory 149), Jeannette, are diocesan partner parishes with one administrator (Deacon William D. Wilson), one office at 615 Division Street and one website, ascensionsacredheartchurches.org. They publish one weekly bulletin as the "Jeannette Catholic Community", self-hosted on that site. The 18 October 2026 issue (12 pages, read 2026-10-22 with `probe pdf`) names both churches in its page-1 masthead ("Ascension Church 615 Division Street", "Sacred Heart Church 310 N 7th Street") and gives one combined Mass schedule for both. One bulletin, so one row.

## Worship sites

Ascension (615 Division St.) and Sacred Heart (310 N. 7th St.): the only two places in the 18 Oct Mass box (Saturday 4:30 pm Sacred Heart, Sunday 8:30 am Ascension, 10:30 am Sacred Heart, 6:00 pm Spanish Mass at Ascension, weekday Masses split between the two). The directory's seasonal note (Saturday vigil at Ascension April-September, at Sacred Heart October-March) matches the October issue having it at Sacred Heart. GCatholic and the directory list no other site. St. Benedict Hall and Mersinger Hall in the masthead are parish halls, not worship sites. The Byzantine St. Demetrius in Jeannette (gcatholic 17400) is another jurisdiction and not part of this parish.
