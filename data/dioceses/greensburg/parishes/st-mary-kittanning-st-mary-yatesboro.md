## Publisher

St. Mary, Our Lady of Guadalupe Parish, Kittanning (directory 155) and St. Mary, Mother of God Parish, Yatesboro (directory 305) are listed as each other's "Partner Parishes" in the diocese directory and publish one weekly bulletin. Each parish's site posts it. stmarykittanning.org's file for 25 October 2026 is named "BULLETIN-OCTOBER-25-2026-St.-Mary-Kittanning-and-St.-Mary-Yatesboro.pdf". Its page 1 carries one Mass schedule for both churches ("Saturday Vigil 4:00 pm Kittanning, 6:00 pm Yatesboro ... Sunday 9:00 am Kittanning, 11:00 am Yatesboro") and the closing title "St. Mary, Our Lady of Guadalupe and St. Mary, Mother of God". saintmaryyatesboro.org's file for the same week (2026-10-25.pdf) has the same masthead and pages, plus two Diocesan/Discover Mass ad pages. Discover Mass shows the issue under both church slugs with the same bulletin number, 3024. One bulletin, so one row.

Pastor Dinga also has Christ, Prince of Peace & St. Lawrence (Ford City/Cadogan), which publishes its own bulletin (Discover Mass issue 3395, row `christ-prince-of-peace-st-lawrence-ford-city`). He is also administrator of St. Patrick, Brady's Bend, which posts its own issue 3396 (`stpatrickbradysbend.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/10182026-3396.pdf`, batch 17). Neither appears in this issue, so both are separate rows.

## Worship sites

- St. Mary, Our Lady of Guadalupe, 101 West High Street, Kittanning: Sat 4:00 pm, Sun 9:00 am, Mon and Fri 8:00 am, Wed 6:00 pm (25 Oct 2026 issue).
- St. Mary, Mother of God, 109 2nd Street, Yatesboro: Sat 6:00 pm, Sun 11:00 am, Tue and Thu 8:30 am.

The issue's Mass schedule lists no other place. GCatholic (24154, 24153) and the directory list no other site for either parish. Fr. Kodavatikanti is "Chaplain at ACMH" (Armstrong County Memorial Hospital). No public Mass there appears in the bulletin.
