## Publisher

Church of the Resurrection, Northern Indiana County, publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 14/1395 (LPi org church-of-the-resurrection-15728, lpi:0018000000QxC0JAAV). The 25 October 2026 issue (page 1) is headed "Church of the Resurrection / A Parish of the Diocese of Greensburg", "Pastoral Center - 349 Morris St. Clymer, PA 15728", "Reverend Craig Alexander, Pastor". The container has an issue for every Sunday from 3 August 2025 to 25 October 2026 (checked one by one on 2026-10-23).

The parish was formed on 21 June 1995 from six parishes and four missions of northern Indiana County (parish site, /about/history/: "a unique single parish within which five church buildings are used as places of worship"). The Former Churches Directory lists the predecessors: SS. Peter and Paul (Arcadia), St. Patrick (Cameron's Bottom), St. Anthony (Clymer), Sacred Heart (Dixonville), Assumption (Ernest), St. Michael (Glen Campbell), St. John the Baptist (Heilwood), St. Francis (Rossiter), St. Elizabeth (Starford), all "closed" 1995 into Church of the Resurrection. Those are closed parish corporations; the buildings that still hold Mass are below.

Our Lady of the Assumption, Coral (the other multi-site parish in this part of the county) has a different pastor (Fr. John Pavlik) and its own self-hosted bulletin; the two do not share a bulletin.

## Worship sites

The 25 October 2026 Mass Schedule box lists five sites, all with weekend and weekday Mass: Clymer, Ernest, Glen Campbell, Heilwood and Rossiter, matching the directory's five site pages (060CS..060RS) and GCatholic 24098, 24171-24174.

A sixth site, Historic Saint Patrick's Church at Cameron's Bottom (near Heilwood), has occasional Masses that replace a Heilwood weekend Mass: the bulletin announced "Mass will be celebrated: Saturday, April 25th at 4 PM (no PM mass in Heilwood)" (19 Apr 2026), and its intentions list Cameron's Bottom Masses on Saturday 25 Oct 2025 (4:00 PM), Saturday 25 Apr, Sunday 17 May (9:00 AM), Saturday 18 Jul and Sunday 16 Aug (9 AM, "NO 9 AM Heilwood Mass") 2026, and Saturday 31 Oct 2026 (4:00 pm). It is a church row (`st-patrick-camerons-bottom`); GCatholic lists it as "Resurrection", 596 Pineton Rd. (24175), and no diocesan page lists it.
