## Publisher

St. John the Baptist Parish, Perryopolis publishes its own weekly bulletin through J.S. Paluch's Discover Mass. The
25 October 2026 issue (listed as "Current Bulletin Oct 25, 2026" on its Discover Mass page, downloaded once on
2026-10-22) has the masthead "Saint John the Baptist Roman Catholic Church", 3332 Pittsburgh Road, Perryopolis,
with "Weekend Masses: Sat. – 4:00 PM; Sun. – 9:30 AM" and weekday Masses Monday to Thursday at 9 AM, and Fr.
Anthony Onoko as pastor.

Fr. Onoko is also pastor of Epiphany of Our Lord, Monessen and St. Sebastian, Belle Vernon; the Discover Mass page
calls the three "Partner parishes" (the diocese directory lists no partners). This issue prints the other two
parishes' Mass schedules in boxes on page 3, but each of them publishes its own issue for the same Sunday, with its
own masthead, in its own LPi container (Epiphany 14/1319, St. Sebastian 14/1696), and neither of those carries St.
John's schedule. Three live channels, so three rows, and each church sits under its own parish's row
(epiphany-of-our-lord-monessen, st-sebastian-belle-vernon).

## Worship sites

One: St. John the Baptist church, 3332 Pittsburgh Road, Perryopolis. The Friday "8:00am Mass at St. Sebastian" and
the partner schedule boxes belong to the other two rows. The Former Churches Directory lists St. Hedwig, Brier Hill
(closed 1977), St. Casimir, Keisterville (2001) and St. Hedwig, Smock (2013) with records at St. John the Baptist;
no roster lists them as churches and the bulletin names none of them. GCatholic's St. Nicholas, 102 Railroad St.,
Perryopolis (17309) is a Byzantine Catholic church, not part of this parish.
