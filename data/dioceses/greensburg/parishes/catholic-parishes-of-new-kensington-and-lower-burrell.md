## Publisher

Four canonical parishes with one pastor (Fr. Daniel L. Blout) publish one weekly bulletin, *The Valley Messenger*, "a bulletin for the Catholic parishes of New Kensington & Lower Burrell", on ParishesOnline container 14/2000: St. Joseph (directory 223) and St. Mary of Czestochowa (225), which the directory lists as partners; Mount St. Peter (227), which the directory lists with no partner but on the same website; and St. Margaret Mary, Lower Burrell (179), which has its own site (smmlb.org) and no listed partner. The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-22; page 1 is an image, rendered and read) gives a Mass box for each of the four churches and two offices (New Kensington at 100 Freeport Road, Lower Burrell at 3055 Leechburg Road).

## Worship sites

The four churches in the page-1 Mass box: St. Margaret Mary (Lower Burrell), Mount St. Peter, St. Joseph and St. Mary of Czestochowa (New Kensington). They are the four directory entries and GCatholic 24147, 24107, 24144, 24150. The Former Churches Directory lists All Saints, Arnold (closed 2008, records to St. Joseph) and St. John Bosco, Braeburn (closed 1999, to St. Margaret Mary); no current roster lists either, so they get no record. St. Vladimir, Arnold (GCatholic 17397) is Ukrainian Catholic, left for the Eastern-churches task.

## Website and bulletin provider

`website` is newkensingtoncatholic.org, the site the directory gives for three of the four parishes and the one the LPi org names; its Bulletin page embeds the org's widget. smmlb.org embeds the same widget, so it is not a separate channel. Provider `parishes_online`, org slug `roman-catholic-parishes-of-lower-burrell-new-kensington` (the org whose title names both towns). The second org carrying 14/2000 is recorded too. The three single-parish LPi orgs are empty and were not recorded as publications. The old domains: mountsaintpeter.org is taken by a gambling spam site; stjosephnk.org and stmarynk.org answered 503 on 2026-10-22.
