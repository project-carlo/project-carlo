## Publisher

St. Peter Parish, Brownsville (directory 041) and St. Cecilia Parish, Grindstone (directory 125) are two canonical parishes in a diocesan partnership with one pastor (Fr. Efren C. Ambre), one office (118 Church Street, Brownsville) and one website, stpeterstcecilia.org. They publish one weekly LPi bulletin, ParishesOnline container 14/1809 (org `church-of-st-peter-st-cecilia`). The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-22 with `probe pdf`) has its masthead on page 1: "CHURCHES OF ST. PETER AND ST. CECILIA", both church addresses, and one Mass schedule keyed "C=St. Cecilia P=St. Peter". The page 12 LPi footer reads "Church of St. Peter & St. Cecilia, Brownsville, PA 14-1809". The 20 September 2026 masthead is the same. Two canonical parishes, one bulletin, so one row.

## Worship sites

St. Peter (Shaffner Ave, Brownsville) and St. Cecilia (Grindstone Rd, Grindstone) are the only places in the bulletin's schedule. Weekend Masses are Saturday 4:00 pm at St. Peter and Sunday 9:00 am at St. Cecilia and 11:00 am at St. Peter. GCatholic and the diocesan directory list no other site for either parish. The Former Churches Directory lists St. Mary (Brownsville, closed 2005), St. Julian (Isabella, 2008), St. James (Maxwell, 2008) and St. Theresa (Allison, 1976) as merged into St. Peter. None of them is on a current roster, so none gets a record. "St. Mary Hall" in the bulletin is the parish hall, not a worship site. St. Nicholas, Brownsville (GCatholic 17272) is Byzantine Ruthenian and is left to the Eastern-churches task.

## Website and bulletin provider

The website is the partners' shared diocese-template site, whose "Bulletins" menu link goes to the ParishesOnline org page. The site hosts no copies of the bulletin.
