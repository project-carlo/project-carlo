## Publisher

St. Bernard of Clairvaux Parish (directory 135) and St. Thomas More University Parish (directory 137, the IUP campus parish) are two canonical parishes in Indiana with one pastor (Fr. Richard N. Owens, OFM Cap), one joint office at St. Thomas More and the shared name "Indiana Catholic Community". The directory lists no partner for either, but they publish one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline container 14/1162 (LPi footer "Saint Thomas More University and St. Bernard of Clairvaux 14-1162"). The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-23) gives page 2 to St. Bernard and page 3 to St. Thomas More, each with its own Mass schedule, and a joint directory on page 10. St. Bernard's earlier container 14/1819 (org st-bernard-of-clairvaux) stopped after 29 December 2024, and both parish sites now embed the joint org's widget. Both orgs' ids are recorded here.

## Worship sites

St. Bernard of Clairvaux church (200 Clairvaux Drive) and St. Thomas More church (1200 Oakland Avenue): the only two places in the bulletin's Mass schedules. GCatholic (24123, 24167) and the directory list no other Latin-rite site for either parish. Homer City's Holy Protection of Mary (GCatholic 17293) is Byzantine and is left to the Eastern-churches task.
