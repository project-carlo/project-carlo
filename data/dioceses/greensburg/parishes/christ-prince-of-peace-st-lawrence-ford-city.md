## Publisher

Christ, Prince of Peace Parish, Ford City (directory 114) and St. Lawrence Parish, Cadogan (directory 047) are listed as each other's "Partner Parishes" in the diocese directory, share the website fccatholic.org and publish one weekly bulletin on Discover Mass under the church slug `christ-prince-of-peace-ford-city-pa`. The 25 October 2026 issue (current on 2026-10-22, downloaded once) is headed "Partner Parishes of Christ, Prince of Peace & St. Lawrence ... October 25, 2026 ... Partner Parish Office 718 4th Avenue Ford City, PA 16226". St. Lawrence's own Discover Mass page (`st-lawrence-cadogan-pa`) says "Shucks! There are no bulletins available", and fccatholic.org's Bulletins page links only the Christ, Prince of Peace page. One bulletin, so one row.

The same pastor, the Very Rev. Eric J. Dinga, also has St. Mary, Our Lady of Guadalupe (Kittanning) and St. Mary, Mother of God (Yatesboro), and is administrator of St. Patrick, Brady's Bend. The masthead box says so ("Pastor Christ, Prince of Peace, St. Lawrence, St. Mary, Our Lady of Guadalupe, & St. Mary, Mother of God ... Administrator, St. Patrick, Brady's Bend"). Those parishes publish separately. The two St. Marys share one bulletin, issue 3024 on Discover Mass, row `st-mary-kittanning-st-mary-yatesboro`, whose 25 Oct masthead names only the two St. Marys. St. Patrick posts its own issue 3396 at stpatrickbradysbend.org. Neither Kittanning, Yatesboro, St. Patrick nor Brady's Bend appears in this issue's Mass intentions, so this is a separate row.
