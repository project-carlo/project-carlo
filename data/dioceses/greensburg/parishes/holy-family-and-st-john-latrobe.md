## Publisher

Holy Family Parish and St. John the Evangelist Parish, Latrobe (two canonical parishes, one pastor,
Fr. E. George Saletrik) publish one weekly bulletin through LPi / ParishesOnline, container 14/1543.
The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-22) opens with the masthead "THE PARTNER PARISHES OF HOLY
FAMILY & SAINT JOHN THE EVANGELIST", one Mass schedule for both churches and contact blocks for both
parish offices. Both parishes' own websites carry this same issue (St. John's posts it as
`14-1543-October-25-2026.pdf`; Holy Family's Wix page hosts a copy of the 18 October issue with the same
masthead), so there is one bulletin and one row. The diocese directory lists the two separately with
no "Partner Parishes" entry; the bulletin itself calls them partner parishes.

## Worship sites

Two: Holy Family church, 1200 Ligonier Street, and St. John the Evangelist church, 306 St. John Drive,
the only two buildings in the bulletin's weekend and weekday Mass schedule. Eucharistic adoration is in
the "Holy Family Eucharistic Chapel", which is part of the Holy Family building and not a separate
worship site. The schedule credits "Weekend Assistance: Benedictine Fathers of St. Vincent Archabbey";
the archabbey's basilica is a separate parish row (st-vincent-basilica-parish-latrobe).

## Website and bulletin provider

`website` is Holy Family's own site, which lists the bulletins. Provider `parishes_online`, org slug
`holy-family-church-st-john-the-evangelist-parish`: its container 14/1543 has the 25 October issue up
three days early. The `url:` publications are the two parish sites' bulletin pages, both of which serve
the container's issues. An older, empty LPi organization for St. John
(`st-john-the-evangelist-st-rose`, named for a former pairing with St. Rose) carries nothing and is
not recorded as a publication; St. Rose now publishes its own bulletin (st-rose-latrobe).
