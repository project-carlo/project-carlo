## Publisher

St. Vincent Basilica Parish, Latrobe (served by the Benedictines of Saint Vincent Archabbey) publishes
its own weekly bulletin through J.S. Paluch's Discover Mass. The 25 October 2026 issue (listed on the
Discover Mass church page, read 2026-10-22) has the masthead "SAINT VINCENT BASILICA PARISH ... A
PARISH OF THE ROMAN CATHOLIC DIOCESE OF GREENSBURG", 300 Fraser Purchase Road, and names no other
parish. The directory gives it no partner parish.

## Worship sites

One: the Saint Vincent Archabbey Basilica, where the bulletin's Mass intentions (Sunday 7:30, 9:30,
11:30 a.m.; Saturday 5:00 p.m. vigil; weekday 6:50 and 8:00 a.m.; the Wednesday 5:00 p.m. community
Mass) and the parish's schedule page put every Mass. The directory's weekend line lists only the 9:30
and 11:30 Sunday Masses with the Saturday vigil; the bulletin and the parish page also list a 7:30 a.m.
Sunday Mass (with the archabbey's intention), and the college's page adds a 7 p.m. Sunday Mass.

Other places on the Saint Vincent campus, not in this row:

- GCatholic 40409 "St. Vincent Seminary" (Plus Code 87G27HVX+8H, about 100 m from the basilica) is the
  seminary, not the basilica: GCatholic lists the basilica separately as church 772. Its chapel (Saint
  Gregory Chapel, Roderick Center) has adoration in this bulletin but no public Mass: excluded,
  `st-vincent-seminary-latrobe`.
- Mary, Mother of Wisdom Chapel, the Saint Vincent College chapel, has a daily and weekend Mass schedule
  on the college's site, but this bulletin does not mention it: excluded as `no-bulletin`,
  `mary-mother-of-wisdom-chapel-latrobe`.
