## Publisher

One weekly bulletin for three canonical parishes with one pastor (Msgr. Michael Begolly): St. John Baptist de La Salle, Delmont (directory 075), St. Mary (Our Lady of Lourdes), Export (095) and Mother of Sorrows, Murrysville (215). The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-10-22) is image-only (Canva) apart from the LPi ad pages, so page 1 was rendered and read: a joint masthead with one column per parish, each with its address and weekend Masses, and a shared sacraments box ("Reconciliation: Saturdays at 9:00 AM at MOS; Saturdays at 12:00 NN at St. John; Tuesdays at 5:00 PM at St. Mary").

LPi prints it in two ad editions: container 14/0550 (org "St. John Baptist de La Salle & St. Mary Our Lady of Lourdes Parishes, Export, PA 14-0550", office 5900 Kennedy Ave, Export) and container 14/1783 (org "Mother of Sorrows Church, Murrysville, PA 14-1783"). Both 25 Oct issues have 16 pages; page 1 carries the same three-parish masthead and page 2 renders pixel-identical. Only the advertiser pages (14-16) differ. The 3 May 2026 issue of each container already had the three-parish masthead. Both parish websites repost the same issue (stjohnstmary.org's SJSM102526.pdf, motherofsorrowschurch.org's "10-25-26 30 OT.pdf"). Every channel carries the one bulletin, so this is one row. No member channel is a separate bulletin, so this is not a merge decision.

The diocesan directory lists only 075 and 095 as partners; Mother of Sorrows has no partner field there but the same pastor. St. Sylvester, Slickville (265), also Begolly's, publishes its own bulletin (row `st-sylvester-slickville`; batch 4 found no Sylvester or Slickville in 14/0550).
