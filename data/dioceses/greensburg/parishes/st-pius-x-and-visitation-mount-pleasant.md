## Publisher

St. Pius X Parish (directory 208) and Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary Parish (directory 213), Mount Pleasant, are diocesan partner parishes with one pastor (the Very Rev. Paul A. Lisik, V.F., who also has St. John the Baptist, Scottdale, St. Joseph, Everson, and the Connellsville/Dunbar parishes), one office at 740 W. Walnut Street and one website, mpcatholicchurches.org. They publish one weekly bulletin, self-hosted on that site. The 25 October 2026 issue (8 pages, read 2026-10-22 with `probe pdf`) names "St. Pius X Parish" and "Visitation Parish" in its page-1 masthead with both church addresses ("224 Summit Street ◊ Mt. Pleasant, Pennsylvania ◊ 740 West Walnut Street") and gives one combined Mass schedule. One bulletin, so one row.

The bulletin is separate from the Scottdale/Everson one (`st-john-the-baptist-and-st-joseph-scottdale`, LPi container 14/0732) despite the shared pastor and parochial vicars: different masthead, different office, different staff, and neither names the other's churches in its Mass schedule.

## Worship sites

St. Pius X (Summit St. at S. Silver St.) and Visitation (740 W. Walnut St.): the only two places in the 25 Oct Mass schedule (Saturday 4:00 pm Visitation, 6:00 pm St. Pius X; Sunday 8:30 am Visitation, 10:30 am St. Pius X; weekday 7:00 am St. Pius X). The directory and GCatholic list no other Roman Catholic site in Mount Pleasant. The Byzantine St. Mary Holy Protection (4480 Route 981, gcatholic 17296) is another jurisdiction and not part of this parish. The St. Pius X Social Hall mentioned in the bulletin is a hall.
