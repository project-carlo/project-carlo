## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 Church of the Resurrection bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/1395, page 1, "Mass Schedule") lists Heilwood: Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am, Wednesday 8:00 am; the page 2 intentions for 26 Oct - 1 Nov 2026 list Masses at Heilwood too. The directory site page 060HS gives the weekend line "Saturday: 4:00 p.m.; Sunday: 9:00 a.m.". On some weekends the Heilwood Mass moves to Historic St. Patrick, Cameron's Bottom (19 Apr 2026: "Mass will be celebrated: Saturday, April 25th at 4 PM (no PM mass in Heilwood)"; 26 Jul 2026 calendar: "9 AM Mass at Cameron's Bottom", "*NO 9 AM Heilwood Mass" on 16 Aug), so the 31 Oct 2026 Saturday intentions list Cameron's Bottom in place of Heilwood.

## Name and address

GCatholic 24173 ("Resurrection", Heilwood) and the diocesan directory (060HS, "Church of the Resurrection Parish, Heilwood Site") agree on the name and on 265 1st Ave. (directory: 265 First Avenue). The bulletin calls the site only "Heilwood". The record name adds the town to tell the parish's five churches apart. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G3J3FM+3F; the directory has no map pin for this site.
