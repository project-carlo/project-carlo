## Holds Mass

The 18 October 2026 bulletin of St. Ambrose and St. Matthew (self-hosted on partnerparishesmas.org, read 2026-10-22, page 1) gives "Saturday Vigil: 5:00 pm at St. Ambrose", and its week of intentions has Masses here on Saturday 10/17 and 10/24 at 5:00 pm and Monday 10/19 at 8:30 am. The diocesan directory agrees ("Saturday: 5:00 p.m.; Sunday: no Mass"); the Sunday Mass is at St. Matthew, Saltsburg.

## Name and address

Name: "St. Ambrose" in GCatholic (24118), "St. Ambrose Parish" in the diocesan directory (LocationID=021) and "St. Ambrose Church" in the bulletin masthead, so name_verified is true.

Address: 505 Cambria Ave(nue), Avonmore, PA 15618 in GCatholic, the directory, the bulletin masthead and the LPi organization st-ambrose-church-15618 ("505 Cambria Ave, Avonmore"). Independent sources agree, so address_verified is true. The directory's mailing address is P.O. Box 617, Avonmore, the parish office's box, not the church. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (40.52869, -79.46531), about 20 m from the directory's map pin (40.52885, -79.46542).
