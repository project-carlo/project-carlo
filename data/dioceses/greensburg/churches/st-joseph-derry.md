## Holds Mass

## Name and address

Address: the sources split on the street number. GCatholic (24142) and the LPi organization st-martin-st-joseph-churches give **117 S Ligonier St.**; the diocesan directory (LocationID=077), the parish site's contact block ("St. Joseph Parish 125 South Ligonier Street") and the bulletin masthead (as the "Partner Parish Office") give **125 South Ligonier Street**. 125 is most likely the rectory/office next to the church and 117 the church itself (inferred: the bulletin uses 125 only for the office, and the LPi organization is titled "Churches"); not confirmed. This record follows 117 and leaves address_verified false. A look at the building (Street View, or a page naming the church's own number) would settle it. OSM (Nominatim reverse at the Plus Code) only returns an interpolated "191, South Ligonier Street", which does not help.
