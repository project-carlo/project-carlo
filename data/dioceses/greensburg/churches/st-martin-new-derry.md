## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin of the Partner Parishes of St. Martin and St. Joseph (self-hosted on stmartinstjoseph.org, read 2026-10-22, page 2) schedules Sunday Mass here at 9:00 AM, plus Monday 6:00 PM and Friday 8:00 AM; there is no Saturday vigil at St. Martin (the vigil is at St. Joseph). The parish site's home page (headed Chrome, 2026-10-22: "9:00 a.m. – St. Martin", "Monday 6:00 p.m. – St. Martin") and the diocesan directory ("Saturday: no Mass; Sunday: 9:00 a.m.") agree.

## Name and address

Name: "St. Martin" in GCatholic (24148), the diocesan directory ("St. Martin Parish", LocationID=219) and the bulletin: name_verified true.

Address: 5684 Route 982 in GCatholic, the directory and the parish site's contact block ("St. Martin Parish 5684 Route 982 Derry, PA 15627 (Mailing address same as St. Joseph)"); the LPi organization st-martin-church-15671 gives "5684 Route 982, New Derry, Pennsylvania, 15671-1008". The street address agrees across independent sources, so address_verified is true. The town differs only in naming: the directory says "Derry" with ZIP 15671, the site's footer "Derry, PA 15627" (the mailing ZIP of the St. Joseph office), and GCatholic, LPi and the bulletin masthead (which pairs "Derry, Pa. 15627" with "New Derry, Pa. 15671") say New Derry 15671. The church is in the New Derry CDP of Derry Township (Nominatim reverse at the Plus Code: "5684, PA 982, New Derry, Derry Township"), so this record uses New Derry 15671.
