## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, rendered image) lists Sunday Masses at 7:30 AM and 10:00 AM and weekday Masses Tuesday to Thursday at 7:00 AM. faysouth.org's schedule (read 2026-10-22) has "7:30 AM at St. Joseph" and "10:00 AM at St. Joseph" on Sunday, and the directory's weekend line agrees.

## Name and address

GCatholic (24145) "St. Joseph, 180 Old Walnut Hill Rd., Uniontown", the diocesan directory (285) "St. Joseph Parish, 180 Old Walnut Hill Road" and the bulletin agree, so both flags are true. The building is in South Union Township with a Uniontown mailing address. OSM puts the named way on Saint James Drive, presumably the church's lot road off Old Walnut Hill Road (inferred, not checked).

The coordinates disagree. Those two independent sources agree within metres, so the record uses the OSM point (coordinates_from `osm-node`; it is a way's centre) rather than the Plus Code.
