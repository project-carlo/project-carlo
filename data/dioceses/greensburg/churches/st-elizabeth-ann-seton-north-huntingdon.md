Yes. The St. Elizabeth Ann Seton bulletin of 25 October 2026 (container 14/1083, page 1) lists Mass intentions for Saturday 24 Oct at 4 pm, Sunday 25 Oct at 8:30 and 10:30 am, and 8:30 am each weekday. (The directory's Sunday line says 8:30 and 11:30 a.m.; the bulletin is current.)
