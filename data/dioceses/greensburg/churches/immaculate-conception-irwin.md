## Holds Mass

Yes. The Immaculate Conception bulletin of 25 October 2026 (container 14/1204, page 3) reports attendance at the 18 October weekend Masses: "4:30 pm – 301, 8:00 am – 247, 10:00 am – 312, 11:30 am - 210". The directory gives the same schedule (Saturday 4:30 p.m.; Sunday 8:00, 10:00, 11:30 a.m.).

## Name and address

GCatholic (24105) gives "Immaculate Conception", 302 2nd St., Irwin, PA 15642; the diocesan directory (141) gives "Immaculate Conception Parish", 302 Second Street. They agree, so both flags are true. The bulletin and the LPi org give the parish office next door, 308 Second Street, not the church. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G287MR+C6 (40.33356, -79.70944), about 80 m from the directory's map pin (40.33341, -79.70859).
