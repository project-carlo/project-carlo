## Holds Mass

Yes. In the 25 October 2026 bulletin of the Churches of St. Peter and St. Cecilia (container 14/1809, page 1), St. Peter ("P") has the Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 11:00 am weekend Masses, weekday Mass on Tuesday and Thursday at 8:00 am, and the 1st-Friday holy hour. Page 2 adds a 5:00 pm All Souls vigil Mass on 1 November 2026. The diocesan directory (LocationID=041) gives the same weekend times.

## Name and address

All three sources name it St. Peter: the bulletin, GCatholic 24158 and the diocesan directory ("St. Peter Parish"). The street number disagrees. The bulletin masthead and the parish's LPi org both give **300** Shaffner Ave. The directory and GCatholic give **304** Shaffner Avenue, and GCatholic's entry links the diocesan page, so it is probably copied from the directory rather than independent. I followed the bulletin (300) because it is the parish's own current statement. `address_verified` stays false until a third source settles it (a county parcel record or the church's signage). The parish office and mailing address is a separate building, 118 Church Street. The coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G224FF+33. The directory map pin (40.02283, -79.87668) is about 55 m east of it, and the LPi org point (40.02224, -79.87741) about 50 m south.
