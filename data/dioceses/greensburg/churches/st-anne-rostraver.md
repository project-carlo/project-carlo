## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 issue of the joint St. Anne and Holy Family bulletin (container 14/1743, read
2026-10-22) gives St. Anne's Masses as Saturday 4:30 p.m., Sunday 8:00 and 11:00 a.m. and Monday, Thursday and
Friday 8:00 a.m., and page 2 lists intentions coded "SA" at those times from 24 October to 1 November. The
directory's line agrees ("Saturday: 4:30 p.m.; Sunday: 8:00 a.m., 11:00 a.m.").

## Name and address

GCatholic (24119: "St. Anne", 1870 Rostraver Rd., city "Rostraver"), the diocese directory (250: "St. Anne
Parish", 1870 Rostraver Road, Rostraver Township) and the bulletin ("Church of Saint Anne, 1870 Rostraver Road,
Rostraver Township, PA 15012") agree on the name and address: both flags true. The city follows the directory and
bulletin ("Rostraver Township"; GCatholic shortens it to "Rostraver"; 15012 is the Belle Vernon post office).
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (40.18819, -79.79606); the directory's pin (40.18794, -79.79583) is about
35 m away. The LPi organization's point (40.1447, -79.8319) is some 5.7 km off and not used.
