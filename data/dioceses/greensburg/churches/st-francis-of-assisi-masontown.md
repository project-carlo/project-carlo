## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (container 14/0702, page 1) lists "Masses in Masontown": Saturday 6:00 pm, Sunday 11:30 am, and Monday and Friday 8:00 am. The diocesan directory (190MS) still lists Saturday 6:00 p.m. and Sunday 8:00 a.m. and 11:30 a.m.; the bulletin, which no longer has the 8:00 a.m. Sunday Mass, is followed.

## Name and address

GCatholic 24130 ("St. Francis of Assisi", 101 W Church Ave., Masontown, PA 15461) and the diocesan directory (190MS, 101 West Church Avenue) agree, and the bulletin masthead gives the same street address for the parish (which the bulletin does not separate from the church).
