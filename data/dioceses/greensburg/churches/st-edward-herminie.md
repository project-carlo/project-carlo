## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-22, page 1) gives St. Edward's schedule as Saturday
Vigil 6:00 p.m., Sunday 9:00 a.m. and Tuesday and Thursday 9:00 a.m., and page 2 lists intentions at those
times from 17 to 25 October. The diocese directory's Mass line agrees ("Saturday: 6:00 p.m.; Sunday: 9:00 a.m.").

## Name and address

GCatholic (24127: "St. Edward", 120 St Edward Ln.) and the diocese directory (129: "St. Edward Parish",
120 Saint Edward Lane) agree on name and address, so both flags are true. The bulletin and the LPi
organization spell the street "St. Edwards Lane"/"St Edwards Ln" (same number, same building); the
GCatholic/directory spelling is kept. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code on the building
(40.25719, -79.70994); the directory's map pin (40.25737, -79.70988) is about 20 m away.
