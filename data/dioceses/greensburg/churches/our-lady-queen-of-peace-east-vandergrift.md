## Holds Mass

The parishes' homepage (read headed 2026-10-22) lists "Our Lady, Queen of Peace Church Sunday Mass, 11 a.m." and Saturday confessions from 12 to 12:30 p.m. The 25 October 2026 shared bulletin names the church in its masthead and reports the week's offering for it. The directory has "Saturday: no Mass; Sunday: 11:00 a.m.", and Discover Mass adds "Thursday 12:00pm".

## Name and address

Name: "Our Lady, Queen of Peace" in GCatholic (24110) and in the directory (086), so name_verified is true. **The address is disputed.** GCatholic, the directory and the empty LPi organization all give "400 Kennedy Avenue". The current bulletin masthead and Discover Mass give "420 McKinley Avenue". This record follows the bulletin, because it is the parish's own current statement and because the GCatholic Plus Code point (87G2HCXP+6Q) reverse-geocodes to "452, McKinley Avenue, East Vandergrift" in OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, 2026-10-22). The church may stand on a corner lot, or 400 Kennedy Avenue may be the rectory or hall: not checked. address_verified is false until a second independent source settles it, for example the county parcel record or a street view of the church door. Coordinates come from the GCatholic Plus Code, about 90 m from the directory pin.
