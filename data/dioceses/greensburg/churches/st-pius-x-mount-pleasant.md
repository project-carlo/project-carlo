## Holds Mass

Yes. The St. Pius X and Visitation bulletin of 25 October 2026 (page 1) schedules the Saturday 6:00 pm vigil and Sunday 10:30 am Mass at St. Pius X, and the daily weekday Mass at 7:00 am; the page-2 intentions list St. Pius X Masses Monday to Friday 26-30 October. The directory gives the same weekend times ("Saturday: 6:00 p.m.; Sunday: 10:30 a.m.").

## Name and address

Name: "St. Pius X" in GCatholic (24159), "St. Pius X Parish" in the diocesan directory (208) and the bulletin masthead; they agree. Address: GCatholic gives "Summit St. & S Silver St.", the directory "Summit and South Silver Street"; they agree, so both flags are true. The bulletin masthead gives a street number, "224 Summit Street", which fits the corner but is from that one source, so line1 keeps the intersection. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G24FW6+CQ (40.14606, -79.53806), about 27 m from the directory's map pin (40.14597, -79.53836).
