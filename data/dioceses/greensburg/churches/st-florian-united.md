## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin (read 2026-10-22) gives Saturday Vigil 5:00 pm and Sunday 9:00 and 11:00
am, with daily Mass at 8:00 am; page 2 lists intentions for the weekends of 17-18 and 24-25 October, and page 5
reports 293 at the three Masses of 10-11 October. The directory's Mass line agrees ("Saturday: 5:00 p.m.; Sunday:
9:00 a.m., 11:00 a.m.").

## Name and address

Name: GCatholic (24129: "St. Florian") and the diocese directory (291: "St. Florian Parish") agree, so
`name_verified` is true.

Address: the sources disagree on the number and the town. GCatholic gives 4261 Route 981, United, PA 15689; the
diocese directory gives 4263 Route 981, Mount Pleasant, PA 15666 (with the mailing address P.O. Box 187, United, PA
15689); the bulletin gives the office's mailing address "PO Box 187, 4263 Route 981, United PA 15689". 4263 is
probably the parish office/rectory and 4261 the church next to it, but no source says which building has which
number. GCatholic's 4261 / United 15689 is kept because it describes the church; The town difference looks like naming
only (United is a village; the directory uses the Mount Pleasant 15666 ZIP), an inference not checked against USPS. `address_verified` stays false until a second source gives the church's own number.
Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G26GC9+35 (40.22019, -79.48206); the directory's map pin (40.22001,
-79.48162) is about 40 m away.
