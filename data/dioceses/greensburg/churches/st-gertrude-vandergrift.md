## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin of Christ the King and St. Gertrude (container 14/4007) lists Masses here on Saturday at 4:00pm, Sunday at 8:00am and 11:00am, and 8:30am on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. The directory gives "Saturday: 4:00 p.m.; Sunday: 8:00 a.m., 10:30 a.m.". It disagrees with the bulletin on the second Sunday Mass, and this record follows the bulletin.
