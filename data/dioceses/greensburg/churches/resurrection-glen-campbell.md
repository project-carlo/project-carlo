## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 Church of the Resurrection bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/1395, page 1, "Mass Schedule") lists Glen Campbell: Saturday 4:00 pm, Monday 8:00 am; the page 2 intentions for 26 Oct - 1 Nov 2026 list Masses at Glen Campbell too. The directory site page 060GC gives the weekend line "Saturday: 4:00 p.m.; Sunday: no Mass".

## Name and address

GCatholic 24172 ("Resurrection", Glen Campbell) and the diocesan directory (060GC, "Church of the Resurrection Parish, Glen Campbell Site") agree on the name and on 157 1st Ave. (directory: 157 First Avenue). The bulletin calls the site only "Glen Campbell". The record name adds the town to tell the parish's five churches apart. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G3R58C+FW; the directory has no map pin for this site.
