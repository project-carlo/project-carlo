## Holds Mass

Yes. The Jeannette Catholic Community bulletin of 18 October 2026 (page 1) schedules the Saturday 4:30 pm vigil (October-March, per the directory) and Sunday 10:30 am Mass at Sacred Heart, with weekday Masses Monday and Friday at 8:00 am.

## Name and address

Name: "Sacred Heart Church" in the bulletin, "Sacred Heart" in GCatholic (24111), "Sacred Heart Parish" in the directory (149): agreed, so `name_verified` is true.

Address: the sources disagree. The parish's own bulletin masthead (18 Oct 2026) gives "310 N 7th Street"; GCatholic, the diocesan directory and the empty LPi org sacred-heart-church-15644 give 504 Cowan Avenue. The parish website's directions page warns "Do not leave envelopes at Sacred Heart old office location", which suggests 504 Cowan Ave. is the former office/rectory address the older lists carry (inferred, not stated). OSM's church building lies between N. 7th St. and Cowan Ave. on Beno Way, and Nominatim places 310 N. 7th St. about 50 m from it. I followed the bulletin (310 N. 7th St.) because it is the current source naming the church itself; `address_verified` stays false until a second source gives that number. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G289JM+Q7 (40.33194, -79.61681), which falls on the OSM church building.
