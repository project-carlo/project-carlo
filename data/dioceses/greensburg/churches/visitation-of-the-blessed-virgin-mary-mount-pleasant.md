## Holds Mass

Yes. The St. Pius X and Visitation bulletin of 25 October 2026 (page 1) schedules the Saturday 4:00 pm vigil and Sunday 8:30 am Mass at Visitation; the page-2 intentions list "Saturday, 10/31 Visitation 4:00 p.m." and the All Saints Day Mass there at 8:30 am on 1 November. The directory gives the same weekend times ("Saturday: 4:00 p.m.; Sunday: 8:30 a.m.").

## Name and address

Name: "Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary" in GCatholic (24170) and the diocesan directory (213, "... Parish"); the bulletin says "Visitation Parish". Address: 740 Walnut St. in GCatholic and the directory; the bulletin masthead and office line say 740 West Walnut Street, which line1 follows (the same building; the sources differ only in the "W."). Both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G24FX2+HJ (40.14894, -79.54844), about 22 m from the directory's map pin (40.14909, -79.54827).
