## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin of the Delmont/Export/Murrysville parishes (container 14/0550, read 2026-10-22; page 1 rendered, as the issue is image-only) gives "Sunday Mass 9:00 AM" under Saint Mary, Our Lady of Lourdes, 5900 Kennedy Avenue, Export, and confessions "Tuesdays at 5:00 PM at St. Mary". The diocesan directory agrees ("Saturday: no Mass; Sunday: 9:00 a.m.").

## Name and address

Name: the bulletin masthead says "Saint Mary, Our Lady of Lourdes", the LPi account "St. Mary Our Lady of Lourdes", and the diocese's faith-formation region page "St. Mary, Our Lady of Lourdes Parish" (Export, Murrysville Region). GCatholic (24151) and the directory (LocationID=095) say only "St. Mary" / "St. Mary Parish". The full title is used to tell it from the other St. Marys in the diocese; the bulletin and the diocese's region page give it, so name_verified is true.

Address: 5900 Kennedy Ave., Export, PA 15632 in GCatholic, the directory, the bulletin masthead and the LPi organization (5900 Kennedy Ave, Export): address_verified true. It is also the parish office for St. John (Delmont) and St. Sylvester (Slickville). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (40.41731, -79.62344), about 30 m from the directory's map pin (40.41740, -79.62312).
