## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, rendered image) lists Saturday 4:00 PM and Sunday 11:00 AM, with weekday Masses Monday and Friday at 7:00 AM and Wednesday at 6:00 PM. faysouth.org's schedule (read 2026-10-22) has "4:00 PM at St. Therese" and "11:00 AM at St. Therese", and the directory's weekend line agrees.

## Name and address

GCatholic (24166) and the diocesan directory (289) both have "St. Therese, Little Flower of Jesus" at 61 Mill St., Uniontown, so both flags are true. The bulletin calls it "St. Therese de Lisieux" at 61 Mill Street, the same church. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87F2V7X9+7V; the directory map pin is about 15 m away.
