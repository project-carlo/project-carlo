## Holds Mass

Yes. The St. John the Baptist and St. Joseph bulletin of 25 October 2026 (container 14/0732, page 1) schedules the Saturday 4:00 pm vigil and Sunday 10:30 am Mass at SJB, plus weekday Masses Thursday and Friday at 8:30 am; the page-2 intentions list "SJB 4:00 pm" on 24 and 31 October. The directory gives the same weekend times ("Saturday: 4:00 p.m.; Sunday: 10:30 a.m.").

## Name and address

Name: "St. John the Baptist" in GCatholic (24138), the diocesan directory (257, "... Parish") and the bulletin masthead; they agree. Address: GCatholic "416 S Broadway", directory "416 South Broadway", bulletin "416 Broadway Ave." (masthead) and "416 S. Broadway Ave." (office, page 2): the same number and street, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G23CW5+FF (40.09619, -79.59131), about 17 m from the directory's map pin (40.09621, -79.59151). Not to be confused with the Byzantine St. John the Baptist at 525 Porter Ave., Scottdale (gcatholic 17317), another jurisdiction.
