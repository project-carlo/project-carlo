## Holds Mass

The parishes' homepage (read headed 2026-10-22) lists "Saint James the Greater Church Saturday Vigil Mass, 4:30 p.m. Sunday Mass, 9 a.m.". The 25 October 2026 bulletin names the church in its masthead, invites people to pray the Rosary "prior to the Saturday 4:30 p.m. and Sunday 9 a.m. Masses", and reports the week's offering for "Saint James the Greater Church". The directory gives the same times ("Saturday: 4:30 p.m.; Sunday: 9:00 a.m.").

## Name and address

Name: "St. James the Greater" in GCatholic (24134), "St. James the Greater Parish" in the directory (013), and "Saint James the Greater Church" in the bulletin, so name_verified is true. Address: the number 109 agrees everywhere, but the street suffix does not. GCatholic has "109 Owens View Rd." and the directory "109 Owens View Road". The bulletin masthead and Discover Mass have "109 Owens View Avenue", the directory's own mailing line for the partner parish has "c/o 109 Owens View Avenue", and OpenStreetMap names the street at the Plus Code point "Owens View Avenue" (Nominatim reverse, 2026-10-22). This record follows the bulletin and OSM ("Ave."). address_verified is left false because the sources disagree on the suffix. Coordinates come from the GCatholic Plus Code 87G2HCJV+RP, about 45 m from the directory pin. The church is in Kiskiminetas Township, Armstrong County, with an Apollo postal address.
