## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 Holy Trinity bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/1188, read 2026-10-23, page 1) gives "Saturday: 5:30 p.m.", "Sunday: 8:00 a.m. & 11:00 a.m." and weekday Masses Monday through Friday at 8:00 a.m.; the 18 Oct issue on the parish site has the same schedule. The diocesan directory agrees ("Saturday: 5:30 p.m.; Sunday: 8:00 a.m., 11:00 a.m.").

## Name and address

Name: "Holy Trinity" in GCatholic (24103), "Holy Trinity Parish" in the directory (LocationID=177) and the bulletin masthead, so name_verified is true.

Address: the sources disagree on the street number. The bulletin masthead, the directory and the LPi organization give 342 West Main Street; GCatholic gives "332 W Main St.". 342 is followed (two independent sources, and it is the parish's own masthead), but the masthead address may be the parish office rather than the church door, so address_verified stays false. A look at the building's number (street view, or OSM if it carries one) would settle it. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G26QW5+VH (40.24719, -79.24106), about 60 m from the directory's map pin (40.24760, -79.24061); LPi's point (40.24639, -79.24252) is about 150 m off and was not used.
