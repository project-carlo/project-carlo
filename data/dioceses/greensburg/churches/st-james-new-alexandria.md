## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 St. James bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/1661, read 2026-10-22, page 1) gives weekend Masses "Saturday | 4:30 p.m. Anticipated Mass" and "Sunday | 9:30 a.m. | Livestreamed", and weekday Masses Tuesday to Friday at 8:00 a.m. (the week of 26 October has "No Morning Mass" each weekday). The diocesan directory agrees ("Saturday: 4:30 p.m.; Sunday: 9:30 a.m.").

## Name and address

Name: "St. James" in GCatholic (24133), "St. James Parish" in the diocesan directory (LocationID=217) and "Saint James Catholic Church" in the bulletin masthead, so name_verified is true.

Address: 306 St. James Lane, New Alexandria, PA 15670 in GCatholic ("306 St James Ln."), the directory, the bulletin ("306 Saint James Lane") and the LPi organization. Independent sources agree, so address_verified is true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (40.39394, -79.43881), about 10 m from the directory's map pin (40.39390, -79.43869). LPi's organization coordinates (40.2594, -79.3723) are about 15 km south-east and were not used.
