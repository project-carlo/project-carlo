## Holds Mass

Yes. In the 25 October 2026 bulletin of the Churches of St. Peter and St. Cecilia (container 14/1809, page 1), St. Cecilia ("C") has the Sunday 9:00 am Mass and weekday Mass on Monday and Wednesday at 8:00 am. It has no Saturday Mass. The diocesan directory (LocationID=125) gives "Saturday: no Mass; Sunday: 9:00 a.m.".

## Name and address

All three sources name it St. Cecilia: the bulletin, GCatholic 24125 and the diocesan directory ("St. Cecilia Parish"). The bulletin masthead and the diocesan directory give **1571** Grindstone Road, and those two are independent. GCatholic gives **571** Grindstone Rd., probably a dropped digit. I followed the bulletin and the directory. `address_verified` stays false because of GCatholic's disagreement. The coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G22579+CX, which lies about 20 m from the directory map pin (40.01366, -79.83024), so the building is the same even though GCatholic's number is not.
