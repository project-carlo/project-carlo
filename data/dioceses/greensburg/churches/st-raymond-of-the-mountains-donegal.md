## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 St. Raymond of the Mountains bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/1384, read 2026-10-23, page 1) gives "Saturday (Vigil): 4:30 p.m." and "Sunday: 8:00 and 10:30 a.m.", with a revised weekday schedule announced on page 2 ("Weekday 8:00 a.m. Mass will only be held on the ..."). The diocesan directory agrees ("Saturday: 4:30 p.m.; Sunday: 8:00 a.m., 10:30 a.m.").

## Name and address

Name: "St. Raymond of the Mountains" in GCatholic (24160), the directory (LocationID=082, "St. Raymond of the Mountains Parish") and the bulletin, so name_verified is true.

Address: 170 School House Lane, Donegal, PA 15628 in GCatholic ("170 School House Ln."), the directory and the bulletin masthead; address_verified is true. The LPi organization gives 164 School House Ln, which none of the other sources uses. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G24J2V+8J (40.10081, -79.35594), about 60 m from the directory's map pin (40.1006, -79.355236). LPi's point (40.1066, -79.3752) is about 1.8 km off and was not used.
