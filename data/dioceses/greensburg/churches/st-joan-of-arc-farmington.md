## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 Southern Fayette bulletin (container 14/1769, page 1, rendered image) lists Saturday 5:30 PM and Sunday 9:30 AM, and weekday Masses Monday and Friday at 9:00 AM. faysouth.org's schedule (read 2026-10-22) has "9:30 AM at St. Joan of Arc" on Sunday and 5:30 PM on Saturday, and the diocesan directory (099) agrees: "Saturday: 5:30 p.m.; Sunday: 9:30 a.m."

## Name and address

GCatholic 24135 ("St. Joan of Arc", 3523 National Pike, Farmington, PA 15437), the diocesan directory (099, 3523 National Pike; mailing address P.O. Box 92) and the bulletin ("3523 National Pike/P O Box 92") agree. Name and address verified on GCatholic plus the directory. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87F2RC89+R2 (39.8170625, -79.5824375), about 25 m from the directory's map pin (39.8172331, -79.5822484).

The parish's Our Lady of the Mountains shrine (an outdoor prayer shrine) and Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary cemetery have pages on faysouth.org but no Mass in the bulletin; no roster lists either, so neither has a record. (The roster's "Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary" is the Mount Pleasant parish.) The Former Churches Directory sends Visitation BVM, Elliottsville (closed 1962) to St. Joan of Arc.
