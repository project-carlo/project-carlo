## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin of the two St. Marys (current on 2026-10-22) has the Saturday Vigil at 4:00 pm and Sunday Mass at 9:00 am in Kittanning, and weekday Mass on Monday 8:00 am, Wednesday 6:00 pm and Friday 8:00 am. The directory says "Saturday: 4:00 p.m.; Sunday: 9:00 a.m.".

## Name and address

Name: "St. Mary, Our Lady of Guadalupe" in GCatholic (24154), the directory (155, "... Parish") and the masthead. They agree. Address: "101 W High St." in GCatholic, "101 West High Street" in the directory and "101 West High Street Kittanning, PA 16201" in the masthead. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 40 m from the directory's map pin (40.817766, -79.524842). The two independent sources agree, so both flags are true.
