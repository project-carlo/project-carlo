October 4:00 pm, Sunday 18 October 9:30 am, weekday Masses (8:30 am; Wednesday 6:00 pm) and Saturday
24 October 4:00 pm. The diocese directory gives the same weekend times (Saturday 4:00 p.m., Sunday
9:30 a.m.).
