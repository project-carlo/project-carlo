## Holds Mass

Yes. The St. John the Baptist and St. Joseph bulletin of 25 October 2026 (container 14/0732, page 1) schedules the Sunday 8:30 am Mass at SJE (St. Joseph, Everson), weekday Masses Monday to Wednesday at 8:30 am and the holy-day vigil at 6:00 pm; page 3 announces 24-hour Adoration at Saint Joseph Church on 27-28 October. No Saturday vigil here, matching the directory ("Saturday: no Mass; Sunday: 8:30 a.m.").
