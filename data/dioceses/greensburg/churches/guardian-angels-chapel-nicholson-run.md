## Holds Mass

Yes, occasionally. This is the former Guardian Angels church at Nicholson Run. Its parish closed in 1994 and passed to St. Lawrence, Cadogan, according to the diocese's Former Churches Directory. The building is kept as a historic chapel by the Christ, Prince of Peace & St. Lawrence partner parishes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin announces "Mass will also be celebrated on All Souls Day at 9:00 AM at Guardian Angels Chapel" (Monday 2 November 2026). The 27 September issue says "This year, Mass will be celebrated there on All Souls Day (November 2)". A Leader Times article of 15 October 2025 quotes the parishes' office: "Mass is currently celebrated there twice a year", on the Feast of the Guardian Angels (2 October) and on Memorial Day.

This is a public Mass announced in the bulletin, held once or twice a year. It is included as a worship site under the README's rule that occasional Masses still count. The orchestrator may rule otherwise.

## Name and address

"Guardian Angels Chapel" is the name in the bulletin and the Leader Times. The diocese's Former Churches Directory calls it "Church of the Guardian Angels Parish", Nicholson's Run. GCatholic and the diocese's parish directory do not list it. The only address found is "Cadogan Slate Lick Road in Nicholson Run" (Leader Times), with no house number. The postal code 16212 (Cadogan) is inferred: Nominatim places Cadogan Slate Lick Road in North Buffalo Township, 16212. OpenStreetMap has no node for the chapel that Nominatim could find.
