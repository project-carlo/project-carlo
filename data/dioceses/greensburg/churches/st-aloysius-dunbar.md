## Holds Mass

Yes. The Yough Catholic Community bulletin of 17-18 October 2026 (page 5) announces an event "after 5pm Mass" at St. Aloysius on Saturday 24 October. The 24-25 October issue announces a pie sale after Mass on 21/22 November and schedules an 8:30 am All Souls Day Mass here on 2 November (page 6). The community's Mass Times page (read 2026-10-22) gives Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am and Monday/Tuesday/Thursday/Friday 8:30 am, matching the directory ("Saturday: 5:00 p.m.; Sunday: 9:00 a.m."). The directory lists no partner for St. Aloysius, but it is in the four-parish bulletin's masthead, so it belongs to this row.

## Name and address

Name: "St. Aloysius" in GCatholic (24117) and the directory (083, "St. Aloysius Parish"); the bulletin and the site say "St. Aloysius Gonzaga". The same patron, so the name flag is true; "St. Aloysius" is kept. Address: 459 Ranch Rd. in GCatholic, "459 Ranch Road" in the directory and the bulletin's Dunbar office line; both flags true. **Coordinates disagree:** GCatholic's Plus Code 87F2X9F9+3H decodes to 39.97269, -79.63106, while the directory's map pin (39.97447, -79.63021) and the empty LPi org (39.97416, -79.62971) are about 180-210 m north-east, near Ranch Road itself (Nominatim reverse of the Plus Code point gives Ranch Road, Mahoning, Dunbar Township). The Plus Code is kept, as it is building-precise elsewhere in this diocese, but here it is unconfirmed. Comparing the building footprint on satellite imagery or OSM would settle it.
