## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 Church of the Resurrection bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/1395, page 1, "Mass Schedule") lists Clymer: Saturday 6:00 pm, Sunday 11:00 am, Thursday 8:00 am; the page 2 intentions for 26 Oct - 1 Nov 2026 list Masses at Clymer too. The directory site page 060CS gives the weekend line "Saturday: 6:00 p.m.; Sunday: 11:00 a.m.".

## Name and address

GCatholic 24098 ("Resurrection", Clymer) and the diocesan directory (060CS, "Church of the Resurrection Parish, Clymer Site") agree on the name and on 349 Morris St.. The bulletin calls the site only "Clymer". The record name adds the town to tell the parish's five churches apart. The directory site page 060CS has a map pin (40.6698923, -79.0077741), about 70 m from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G2MXCV+52 (40.6704375, -79.0074375); the Plus Code is used. 349 Morris St. is also the bulletin's Pastoral Center address.
