## Holds Mass

Yes, occasionally. The Church of the Resurrection bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/1395) announced "Historic Saint Patrick’s Church, Cameron’s Bottom, PA. Mass will be celebrated: Saturday, April 25th at 4 PM (no PM mass in Heilwood)" (19 Apr 2026). Reading every Sunday's issue from 3 Aug 2025 to 25 Oct 2026 (grep "Cameron", 2026-10-23), its Mass intentions or calendar list Masses here on Saturday 25 Oct 2025 (4:00 PM), Saturday 25 Apr 2026 (4:00 PM), Sunday 17 May (9:00 AM), Saturday 18 Jul (4:00 PM), Sunday 16 Aug (9 AM, "*NO 9 AM Heilwood Mass") and Saturday 31 Oct 2026 (4:00 pm, in the 25 Oct issue). About six Masses a year, each replacing a Heilwood weekend Mass, so it is a worship site, not a closed church. It is not in the page 1 Mass Schedule box.

St. Patrick Parish, Cameron's Bottom (founded 1832) is in the diocese's Former Churches Directory as closed in 1995 into Church of the Resurrection: that is the parish corporation; the building is still used. The parish history page says Catholic presence here goes back to a 1776 land grant.
