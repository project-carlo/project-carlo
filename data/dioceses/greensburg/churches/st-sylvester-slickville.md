## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 St. Sylvester bulletin (self-hosted on stsylvester.org, read 2026-10-22, page 1) gives "Sunday Mass 8:30 AM" and lists Masses for October 25 and November 1, plus All Souls on November 2 at 6:30 PM. The diocesan directory agrees ("Saturday: no Mass; Sunday: 8:30 a.m.").

## Name and address

Name: "St. Sylvester" in GCatholic (24165), "St. Sylvester Parish" in the diocesan directory (LocationID=265) and in the bulletin masthead, so name_verified is true.

Address: 3028 State Route 819, Slickville, PA 15684 in GCatholic and the directory; the bulletin masthead and the site footer say "3028 Route 819", and the LPi organization st-sylvester-church-15684 has "3028 State Route 819, Slickville, Pennsylvania, 15684-0307". Independent sources agree, so address_verified is true. The directory's mailing address ("c/o P.O. Box 617, Avonmore") is the former partner office. The site footer gives "Box 307", and the bulletin gives the office at 5900 Kennedy Avenue, Export. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (40.45831, -79.52219), within about 15 m of the directory's map pin (40.45845, -79.52210).
