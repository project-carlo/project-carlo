## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 25 October 2026 parish bulletin (Discover Mass, page 2, read 2026-10-22) lists Masses
every day from 25 October to 1 November, and the parish's schedules page and Saint Vincent College's
event-times page give the same schedule in the Archabbey Basilica (Saturday 5 p.m.; Sunday 7:30, 9:30,
11:30 a.m., plus 7 p.m. per the college; weekdays 6:50 and 8:00 a.m.).

## Name and address

The building is the "Saint Vincent Archabbey Basilica" (GCatholic 772; the college's event-times page);
the parish that worships in it is "St. Vincent Basilica Parish" (directory 163, bulletin masthead). Name
flag true on GCatholic and the college. Address: GCatholic "300 Fraser Purchase Rd.", the directory and
the bulletin "300 Fraser Purchase Road": agree, flag true. Coordinates are GCatholic 772's Plus Code
87G27HVX+3W (40.29269, -79.40019), decoded with the repo's `decode_plus_code`; the directory's map pin
(40.29225, -79.40118) is about 95 m away, toward the campus centre. GCatholic 40409 (the seminary,
87G27HVX+8H) is a different building and is excluded separately.
