## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin (container 14/1162, read 2026-10-23), page 3, gives St. Thomas More's Mass schedule: Saturday Vigil 6:00 pm, Sunday 9:30 am and 6:00 pm, Tuesday to Friday 12:10 pm, with weekend attendance of 453 for 17/18 October. The directory's weekend line agrees ("Saturday: 6:00 p.m.; Sunday: 9:30 a.m., 6:00 p.m. (Regional Mass)"). It is the university parish for Indiana University of Pennsylvania; the bulletin names no separate campus chapel.
