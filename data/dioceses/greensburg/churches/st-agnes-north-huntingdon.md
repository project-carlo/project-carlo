## Holds Mass

Yes. The St. Agnes bulletin of 25 October 2026 (container 14/1865, page 1) gives the Mass schedule: Saturday 8:00 AM and 4:30 PM vigil, Sunday 9:00 and 11:15 AM, Monday to Friday 7:30 AM; page 2 lists intentions for the 24-25 Oct weekend Masses.

## Name and address

GCatholic (24116) gives "St. Agnes", 11400 St Agnes Ln., North Huntingdon, PA 15642; the diocesan directory (234) and the bulletin masthead give 11400 St. Agnes Lane. They agree, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G28779+F5 (40.31369, -79.73206), within 10 m of the directory's map pin (40.31394, -79.73213). The LPi org's point (40.32753, -79.71702) is about 2 km off and was not used.
