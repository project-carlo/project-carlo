## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin of Christ the King and St. Gertrude (container 14/4007) lists Masses here on Saturday at 5:30pm, Sunday at 9:30am, and 8:30am on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. The directory agrees on the weekend ("Saturday: 5:30 p.m.; Sunday: 9:30 a.m.").

## Name and address

"Christ the King" at "125 Park Rd., Leechburg" in GCatholic (24095), "Christ the King Parish, 125 Park Road, Leechburg" in the directory (168), and "CHRIST THE KING PARISH 125 Park Road Leechburg, PA 15656" in the bulletin: all agree, so both flags are true. Coordinates come from the GCatholic Plus Code 87G2J9XR+M2, about 20 m from the directory pin. OpenStreetMap places the point on Park Road, Gilpin Township, Armstrong County (Nominatim reverse, 2026-10-22). The LPi organization's point (40.6230, -79.6175) is about 3 km off and was not used.
