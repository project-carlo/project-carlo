## Holds Mass

Yes. The Yough Catholic Community bulletin of 10-11 October 2026 (page 5) announces a meeting after the 8 am Sunday Mass at St. Rita, and the 24-25 October issue (page 6) schedules the 8:00 am All Souls Day Mass at St. Rita on 2 November. The community's Mass Times page (read 2026-10-22) gives Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 am and Monday/Wednesday/Friday 8:00 am here, matching the directory ("Saturday: 4:00 p.m.; Sunday: 8:00 a.m."). The St. Rita Holy Name and Christian Mothers societies are active (24-25 Oct issue, page 5).

## Name and address

Name: "St. Rita" in GCatholic (24162) and the directory (069, "St. Rita Parish"); the bulletin masthead says "St. Rita of Cascia". The same patron, so the name flag is true; "St. Rita" is kept. Address: 116 S 2nd St. in GCatholic and "116 South Second Street" in the directory. That is also the Connellsville parish office in the bulletin, so the church and office share a lot. Both flags true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G22C84+J5 (40.01656, -79.59456), about 15 m from the directory's map pin (40.01649, -79.59471).
