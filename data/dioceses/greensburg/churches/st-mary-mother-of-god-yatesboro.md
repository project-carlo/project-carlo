## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin of the two St. Marys (current on 2026-10-22) has the Saturday Vigil at 6:00 pm and Sunday Mass at 11:00 am in Yatesboro, and weekday Mass on Tuesday and Thursday at 8:30 am. The directory says "Saturday: 6:00 p.m.; Sunday: 11:00 a.m.".

## Name and address

Name: "St. Mary, Mother of God" in GCatholic (24153), the directory (305, "... Parish") and the masthead. They agree. Address: "109 2nd St." in GCatholic, "109 Second Street" in the directory and "P.O. Box 327, 109 2nd Street Yatesboro, PA 16263" in the masthead. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 25 m from the directory's map pin (40.800705, -79.334718). The two independent sources agree, so both flags are true.
