## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 Church of the Resurrection bulletin (ParishesOnline container 14/1395, page 1, "Mass Schedule") lists Rossiter: Sunday 8:30 am, Friday 8:00 am; the page 2 intentions for 26 Oct - 1 Nov 2026 list Masses at Rossiter too. The directory site page 060RS gives the weekend line "Saturday: no Mass; Sunday: 8:30 a.m.".

## Name and address

GCatholic 24174 ("Resurrection", Rossiter) and the diocesan directory (060RS, "Church of the Resurrection Parish, Rossiter Site") agree on the name and on 215 2nd St. (directory: 215 Second Street). The bulletin calls the site only "Rossiter". The record name adds the town to tell the parish's five churches apart.
