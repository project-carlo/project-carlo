## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 25 October 2026 bulletin (Discover Mass `st-patrick-east-brady-pa`, read 2026-10-22) and its 18 October 2026 issue (parish site, `10182026-3396.pdf`) list "Saturday Vigil ... 5:00 pm" and "Sunday ... 9:30 am". The 25 Oct "Masses For The Week" has 9:00 am Masses Tuesday to Friday, the 5:00 pm All Saints vigil and Sunday 9:30 am. The directory's line agrees: "Saturday: 5:00 p.m.; Sunday: 9:30 a.m.".

## Name and address

GCatholic (24156: "St. Patrick", 915 State Route 68, Brady’s Bend, PA 16028) and the diocese directory (033: "St. Patrick Parish", 915 State Route 68, Brady's Bend) agree, and the bulletin masthead gives the same street address. Both flags are set on those two independent sources. The bulletin, Discover Mass and the LPi organization give the postal town as East Brady (ZIP 16028). The church stands in Brady's Bend Township, which is the name the parish and the diocese use, so the city is kept as Brady's Bend. The coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G2X9V9+3W (40.99269, -79.63019). The directory's map pin (40.99270, -79.63043) is about 20 m away.
