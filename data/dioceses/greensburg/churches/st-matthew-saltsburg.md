## Holds Mass

The 18 October 2026 bulletin of St. Ambrose and St. Matthew (self-hosted on partnerparishesmas.org, read 2026-10-22, page 1) gives "Sunday Mass: 11:00 am at St. Matthew", and its week of intentions has Masses here on Sunday 10/18 and 10/25 at 11:00 am and Friday 10/23 at 8:30 am. The diocesan directory agrees ("Saturday: no Mass; Sunday: 11:00 a.m."); the Saturday vigil is at St. Ambrose, Avonmore.

## Name and address

Name: "St. Matthew" in GCatholic (24155), "St. Matthew Parish" in the diocesan directory (LocationID=253) and "St. Matthew Church" in the bulletin masthead, so name_verified is true.

Address: 501 Cathedral St(reet), Saltsburg, PA 15681 in GCatholic, the directory, the bulletin masthead and the LPi organization st-matthew ("501 Cathedral St, Saltsburg, Pennsylvania, 15681-1103"). Independent sources agree, so address_verified is true. The directory's mailing address is "c/o P.O. Box 617, Avonmore", the shared office. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (40.48731, -79.44744). The directory's map pin (40.48864, -79.44814) is about 160 m north-west of it; the Plus Code was kept because GCatholic places it on the building.
