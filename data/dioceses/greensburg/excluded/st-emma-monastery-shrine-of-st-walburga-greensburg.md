## Why it is excluded

GCatholic's "Shrine of St. Walburga" (39112) is St. Emma Monastery, the Benedictine nuns' house at 1001
Harvey Ave., Greensburg. The shrine itself is a star-shaped chapel on the grounds, built in 1974 from the
windows of the former St. Walburga Church in Pittsburgh and "open to the public as a place of peace and
a place of prayer" (stemma.org, read 2026-10-23). The monastery has public Mass: its prayer schedule
lists "7:00 a.m. - Eucharist (Mass)" daily, and its homepage says "You are welcome to join us for ...
our daily Mass" (also live-streamed). Which room the daily Mass is in (the monastery chapel or the
shrine) is not stated; the record covers the monastery as one place.

It publishes nothing the pipeline can read: the monastery's eCatholic site has a Bulletin page with an
empty archive, and the 25 Oct 2026 bulletins of Blessed Sacrament Cathedral and Our Lady of Grace,
Greensburg do not mention "Emma" or "Walburg" (St. Bruno/St. Paul's issue is image-only and was not
read). An open place with public Mass and no bulletin, so `no-bulletin`, as with Saint Vincent's
Mary, Mother of Wisdom Chapel. It is not in the diocese's parish directory.

stemma.org answers 403 to curl and `probe url`; the quotes were read in headed Chrome, so `recheck`
may not be able to confirm them.

## What would change this

A bulletin or newsletter on stemma.org carrying the monastery's Mass times, or a parish bulletin that
prints them.
