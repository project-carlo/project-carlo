## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Perpetual Help, 500 W Main St., Uniontown, Pennsylvania 15401, is an Eastern Catholic church (Ruthenian (Byzantine) rite) of the Byzantine Catholic (Ruthenian) Archeparchy of Pittsburgh, not of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Greensburg, although it stands in the diocese's territory. Its GCatholic page (read 2026-10-23) gives "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Metropolitanate sui juris of Pittsburgh" and "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Greensburg". The diocese's parish directory (rosters/directory.json) has no entry at this address. GCatholic marks no closure. GCatholic lists it as a shrine (website sistersofstbasil.org, the Sisters of St. Basil the Great at Mount St. Macrina).

## What would change this

Nothing expected; it could be added under its own eparchy if the dataset ever covers Eastern Catholic churches.
