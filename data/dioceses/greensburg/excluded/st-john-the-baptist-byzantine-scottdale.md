## Why it is excluded

St. John the Baptist, 525 Porter Ave., Scottdale, is a Byzantine Ruthenian church of the
Ruthenian Metropolitanate of Pittsburgh, not of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Greensburg,
although it stands in the diocese's territory. Its GCatholic page (read 2026-10-23) gives
"Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Metropolitanate sui juris of Pittsburgh" and "within the territory of
the Roman Diocese of Greensburg". It is a different building from the Roman Catholic St. John
the Baptist, 416 S. Broadway, Scottdale (`churches/st-john-the-baptist-scottdale`), which
`coverage` would otherwise match by name; hence the slug. GCatholic marks no closure.

## What would change this

Nothing expected; it could be added under its own jurisdiction if the dataset ever covers
Eastern Catholic churches.
