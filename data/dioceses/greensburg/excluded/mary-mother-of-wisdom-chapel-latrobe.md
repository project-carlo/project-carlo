## Why it is excluded

Mary, Mother of Wisdom Chapel is the Saint Vincent College campus chapel, on the archabbey campus at 300
Fraser Purchase Road. Its Mass schedule is open and current: the college's event-times page (read
2026-10-22) lists Masses Monday to Friday at 12:05 p.m., Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday at 4:15
p.m., Saturday at 4:30 p.m. and Sunday at 7:00 and 9:30 p.m., and posts changes "on the Chapel doors".
The college's campus ministry does not publish a bulletin, and the St. Vincent Basilica Parish
bulletin, the only bulletin on the campus, does not mention the chapel (25 October 2026 issue grepped
for "Wisdom" and "Chapel"). It is an open place with public Mass and nothing the pipeline can read, so
`no-bulletin`. No roster lists it. A catholicchurchtimes.com listing (seen in a 2026-10-22 web search)
also describes it as the college's chapel, with daily Mass.

The address is the campus address; the chapel's own building and coordinates were not checked.

## What would change this

A bulletin or regular newsletter from Saint Vincent College campus ministry with the chapel's Masses,
or the basilica parish's bulletin starting to carry them (then it becomes a church of
st-vincent-basilica-parish-latrobe).
