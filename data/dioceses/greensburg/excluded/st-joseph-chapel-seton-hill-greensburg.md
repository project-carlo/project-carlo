## Why it is excluded

St. Joseph Chapel is the Seton Hill University campus chapel at 1 Seton Hill Drive, Greensburg (the
university founded by the Sisters of Charity). The university's Campus Ministry page (read 2026-10-23)
gives a current, public Mass schedule: "Masses are held in Saint Joseph Chapel when classes are in
session from late August to early December and from mid-January through early May. All are welcome!",
Sunday at 7:00 p.m., Monday and Wednesday at 12:00 p.m. and Tuesday at 8:30 p.m., with no Mass in summer,
winter or spring breaks. A catholicchurchtimes.com listing (reported by the orchestrator, not read here)
gives the same Sunday-to-Wednesday pattern.

Campus Ministry publishes no bulletin or newsletter the pipeline could read, and neither the Blessed
Sacrament Cathedral nor the Our Lady of Grace bulletin of 25 October 2026 mentions Seton Hill. An open
place with public Mass and nothing to read, so `no-bulletin`, like Saint Vincent College's Mary, Mother
of Wisdom Chapel. No roster lists it, and no parish is related.

The address is the university's address; the chapel building's own coordinates were not checked.

## What would change this

A Campus Ministry bulletin or regular schedule newsletter, or a Greensburg parish bulletin starting to
print the chapel's Masses (then it becomes a church of that parish).
