## Why it is excluded

GCatholic 40409 ("St. Vincent", type Seminary, 300 Fraser Purchase Rd.) is Saint Vincent Seminary, not
the Archabbey Basilica: GCatholic lists the basilica separately (church 772, about 100 m away), and
the basilica is the church of st-vincent-basilica-parish-latrobe. The seminary's own chapel is Saint
Gregory (the Great) Chapel in the Roderick Center, built in 1997. On 2026-10-22 I checked the three
current schedules for the Saint Vincent campus: the basilica parish's 25 October 2026 bulletin, the
parish's schedules page and Saint Vincent College's event-times page. None lists a public Mass in the
seminary chapel. The bulletin uses it only for Friday Eucharistic exposition, and the seminary joins
the Wednesday community Mass in the basilica. It is a seminary chapel for its own community, so
`no-public-mass`. This rests on its absence from those schedules; no source says in so many words that
the chapel is closed to the public. A 2017 web listing shows a one-off public Mass there for the feast
of Blessed Karl.

saintvincentseminary.edu answers 403 to curl, and its "daily-mass-livestream" page returned 404 in
headed Chrome, so the seminary's own site was not read.

## What would change this

A regular public Mass schedule for Saint Gregory Chapel (on the seminary's site or in the basilica
bulletin) would make it a church of the basilica parish row.
