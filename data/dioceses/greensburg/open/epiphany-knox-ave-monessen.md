## What is known

GCatholic has two entries for Epiphany of Our Lord, Monessen: 24169 at 618 Knox Ave. (with the parish phone, and
listing the other as its mission) and 24178 at 44 Pennsylvania Blvd. (page last updated 2025-12-21). The
parish's own site still serves an orphaned page "Main Church - Knox Ave" giving directions "to the main church
site", 618 Knox Ave., and its homepage says the parish has "two consecrated sites for worship" in a paragraph whose
Mass and confession times are out of date. Third-party listings (catholicchurch.directory, joinmychurch.com) also
give 618 Knox Ave.

Everything current points at 44 Pennsylvania Blvd. only: the 25 October 2026 bulletin (container 14/1319) gives
that as "OUR LOCATION" and names no other site; "Knox" appears in none of the issues checked (25 Oct 2026, 7 Dec
2025, 2 Jun 2024); the parish site's Site Locations menu lists only "Epiphany Church Pennsylvania Blvd"; the
diocese directory lists only 44 Pennsylvania Boulevard; the 2022 annual report and a 2024 diocesan events list
(search snippets) also use 44 Pennsylvania Boulevard.

Which historic church stood at 618 Knox Ave. was not established (the five parishes merged in 1991 were St.
Anthony, St. Cajetan, Most Holy Name of Jesus, St. Hyacinth and St. Leonard).

## Why it is not decided

All the evidence that the Knox Ave. building no longer holds Mass is absence (the bulletin, menu and directory
don't name it). No source found says it closed, was sold or was demolished, so it can't be excluded as `closed`;
and nothing current shows Mass there, so it isn't a church row. Web searches for its closure (2026-10-22) found
nothing. A dated closure notice, a news item, or the parish office would settle it.
