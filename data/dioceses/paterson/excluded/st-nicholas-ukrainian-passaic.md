## Why it is excluded

St. Nicholas Ukrainian Catholic Church, Passaic belongs to the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia: GCatholic 17491 (read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia", and the parish's own site calls it "St. Nicholas Ukrainian Catholic Church", with Sunday Divine Liturgy at 8:30 (English) and 10:30 (Ukrainian). It is not a Paterson parish: `other-jurisdiction`. It is a different church from the Latin St. Nicholas, Passaic (153 Washington Place, finder pin directory:14090), which belongs to Our Lady of Fatima & St. Nicholas.

Address: the parish site gives "217 President St., Passaic, NJ 07055" as its main address; GCatholic gives 216 President St. I followed the parish's own site.

## What would change this

Nothing short of the church passing to the Diocese of Paterson; the settled rule (diocese.md, *Rules settled for this diocese*, accepted by survey-review 2026-11-04) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as other-jurisdiction.
