## Why it is excluded

Holy Wisdom, 197 Emmans Rd., Flanders is a Ruthenian (Byzantine) Catholic church of the Eparchy of Passaic: GCatholic 17175 (read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic", located "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Paterson". It is not a parish of the Diocese of Paterson, so it is `other-jurisdiction`. The address is GCatholic's (no other source read; probe get on a guessed holywisdomnj.org failed at the proxy). Its Divine Liturgy schedule was not read, since the exclusion doesn't depend on it.

## What would change this

Nothing short of the church passing to the Diocese of Paterson; the settled rule (diocese.md, *Rules settled for this diocese*, accepted by survey-review 2026-11-04) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as other-jurisdiction.
