## Why it is excluded

St. Michael's Chapel, 415 Lackawanna Ave., Woodland Park is the Woodland Park chapel of the Byzantine Catholic Cathedral of St. Michael the Archangel, Passaic. The cathedral's site (read 2026-11-05) says "We are the Byzantine Catholic Cathedral Church of Bishop Kurt Burnette of the Eparchy of Passaic" and lists "St. Michael's Chapel 415 Lackwanna Ave. Woodland Park, NJ" with an 11am Sunday Divine Liturgy there. GCatholic 17191 gives "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic". It holds public liturgy but under the Eparchy of Passaic, not the Diocese of Paterson: `other-jurisdiction`. Address: GCatholic's street number with the site's street (the site spells it "Lackwanna"; GCatholic's "Lackawanna" is the street's name).

## What would change this

Nothing short of the church passing to the Diocese of Paterson; the settled rule (diocese.md, *Rules settled for this diocese*, accepted by survey-review 2026-11-04) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as other-jurisdiction.
