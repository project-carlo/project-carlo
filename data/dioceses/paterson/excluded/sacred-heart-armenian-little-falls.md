## Why it is excluded

Sacred Heart, 155 Long Hill Rd., Little Falls is an Armenian Catholic church: GCatholic 15216 (read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Armenian Diocese of Our Lady of Nareg in Glendale", "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Paterson". It is not a Paterson parish: `other-jurisdiction`. The eparchy page GCatholic links (armenianeparchy.org/sacredheart/) timed out on 2026-11-05; the address is GCatholic's alone.

## What would change this

Nothing short of the church passing to the Diocese of Paterson; the settled rule (diocese.md, *Rules settled for this diocese*, accepted by survey-review 2026-11-04) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as other-jurisdiction.
