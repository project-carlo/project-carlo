## Why it is excluded

St. John the Baptist, 60 N Jefferson Rd., Whippany is a Ukrainian Catholic church: GCatholic 17496 (read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia", "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Paterson". It is not a Paterson parish: `other-jurisdiction`. The parish site GCatholic links (sjucc.com) only frames sjuccwhippany.com, which refused the browser on 2026-11-05; the address is GCatholic's alone.

## What would change this

Nothing short of the church passing to the Diocese of Paterson; the settled rule (diocese.md, *Rules settled for this diocese*, accepted by survey-review 2026-11-04) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as other-jurisdiction.
