## Why it is excluded

St. George's church, 408 Getty Ave., Paterson is no longer a church of the Diocese of Paterson. The diocese's own history of its parishes (rcdop.org, read 2026-11-05) says: "1897 Saint George, Paterson merged with Saint Brendan in 2011; church taken over by Saint George Syro-Malabar Parish". The Syro-Malabar parishes of the US belong to the Syro-Malabar Eparchy of St. Thomas of Chicago, (that eparchy is not named on the page; it is the only Syro-Malabar jurisdiction in the US), so the building is `other-jurisdiction` under the diocese's settled rule. The Latin parish's successor, St. Brendan & St. George, Clifton (`st-brendan-st-george-clifton`), has a 1 November 2026 bulletin naming only its Clifton church.

GCatholic 19003 (updated 2025-12-21) still lists St. George as a Latin church of the Diocese of Paterson; I followed the diocese's own page, which is the official source and names the 2011 merger and the handover. The Syro-Malabar parish's own site was not found (guessed domains did not answer, and a Beacon search for Syro-Malabar found nothing about it), so its Mass schedule is not recorded; the exclusion doesn't depend on it. The address is GCatholic's.

## What would change this

Nothing short of the church passing to the Diocese of Paterson; the settled rule (diocese.md, *Rules settled for this diocese*, accepted by survey-review 2026-11-04) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as other-jurisdiction.
