## Why it is excluded

Saint Luke Academy is St. Luke parish's preschool, not a worship site. St. Luke's 8 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, rendered) lists "St. Luke Academy Preschool, Jeannie Soltes, Preschool Director, (908) 876-3515 ext. 200, www.stlukeacademylv.org", using the parish's phone number; the diocese's finder pins it with no address and no Mass times, and its site (read 2026-11-04) has a parent handbook and no Mass schedule. The address recorded is the parish's (inferred from the shared phone number; no source gives the school's own street address). Mass for the parish is at `st-luke-long-valley`.
