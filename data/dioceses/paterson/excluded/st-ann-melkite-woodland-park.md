## Why it is excluded

St. Ann, 802 Rifle Camp Rd., Woodland Park (also the Shrine of Our Lady of America) is a Melkite Greek Catholic church: GCatholic 16545 (read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Greek-Melkite Diocese of Newton", "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Paterson". It is not a Paterson parish: `other-jurisdiction`. Its site saintannmelkite.org showed only a hosting provider's default page on 2026-11-05; the address is GCatholic's alone.

## What would change this

Nothing short of the church passing to the Diocese of Paterson; the settled rule (diocese.md, *Rules settled for this diocese*, accepted by survey-review 2026-11-04) excludes Eastern Catholic churches in the territory as other-jurisdiction.
