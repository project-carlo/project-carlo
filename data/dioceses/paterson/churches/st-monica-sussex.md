## Name and address

The bulletin masthead gives "Saint Monica's Church Sussex, New Jersey" and "RECTORY 33 Unionville Avenue"; the finder (directory:12096) gives 33 Unionville Avenue too, the rectory's address. GCatholic (19030) gives the church as "35 Unionville Ave.", so 35 is recorded: the official 33 is labelled the rectory, the building next door. Only GCatholic gives 35, so the address is not verified; the name is (bulletin, finder, GCatholic). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H7696W+X8. OSM has no building or place of worship mapped here (Nominatim reverse returns only the street, interpolated as number 47), so it can't confirm the Plus Code; the finder pin lies about 100 m south-west, where Nominatim interpolates 33-35, and may be a street geocode.
