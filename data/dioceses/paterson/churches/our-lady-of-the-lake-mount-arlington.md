## Holds Mass

Page 1 of the 1 November 2026 bulletin (text layer, read 2026-11-04) gives the "PARISH WORSHIP Mass Schedule": Saturday Evening 5:00 pm; Sunday 8:00 & 11:00 am; Monday - Friday 9:00 am. The diocese's finder (read 2026-11-04) gives the same times.

## Name and address

The masthead lists "CHURCH Windemere Avenue" and "RECTORY 1 Park Avenue", with "1 Park Avenue Mt. Arlington 07856" as the parish office. The finder and GCatholic both give 1 Park Ave., Mount Arlington 07856, which is the rectory, so the church's street is taken from the bulletin. OpenStreetMap has the church building ('Our Lady of the Lake Church', way 311861307) at 8 Windermere Avenue; the bulletin's "Windemere" is a misspelling of the street OSM names Windermere Avenue. The house number 8 comes from OSM alone (the bulletin gives none), so `address_verified` stays false. Name: the masthead's "Our Lady of the Lake", which the finder and GCatholic share. Coordinates are the OSM building's (40.9286301, -74.6359321). GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7W9H7+FH is about 12 m from it and the finder pin about 35 m, so all three are on the same site; the church and rectory are on adjoining lots.
