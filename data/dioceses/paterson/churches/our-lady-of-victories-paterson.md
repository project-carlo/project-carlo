## Name and address

The sources disagree. The parish home page says "Church address: 169 Broadway (corner of Paterson St. & Memorial Dr.)" and "Entrance to our two parking lots by 100 Fair St. (back of the Church)". The finder, GCatholic, OSM and the site's own embedded Google map give 100 Fair Street. I followed the parish's explicit church address, since it says Fair Street is the back of the lot; 100 Fair Street would also find the building. address_verified is false because only the parish gives 169 Broadway. OSM's "169 Broadway" house nodes (ways 1331228181 and 468488464) lie about 100 m and 250 m from the church, so OSM's numbering doesn't confirm either version. Name as the site's header.

Coordinates are the centre of the OSM church building (way 379442268), within about 30 m of the finder pin and the Google map's pin. GCatholic's Plus Code (87G7WR9M+HG) is not used: it lands on New Shiloh Missionary Baptist Church at 209 Broadway.
