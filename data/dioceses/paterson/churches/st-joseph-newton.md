## Name and address

The bulletin masthead and the parish home page give "24 Halsted Street", Newton. The finder (directory:11734) gives 17 Elm Street and GCatholic (19029) 15 Elm St.; the church evidently stands at the Halsted/Elm block, and the Elm Street numbers may be an office or the side street. 24 Halsted Street is recorded, the parish's own address, and OSM puts house 24 Halsted beside the church. Only the parish gives it, so the address is not verified; the name is (bulletin, finder, GCatholic).
