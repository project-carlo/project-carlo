## Holds Mass

Page 2 of the 1 November 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 5:00 PM; Sunday 8:00, 9:30, 11:30 AM and 6:00 PM; a Spanish Mass Sunday at 2:00 PM; daily Mass 8:15 AM Monday to Friday. Its Mass intentions list the same times for 7-8 November. The diocese's finder (read 2026-11-04) agrees.

## Name and address

The bulletin cover (page 1, an image), the parish site's footer, the diocese's finder and GCatholic all give 427 Stanhope Road, Sparta, NJ 07871. The bulletin calls the parish Saint Kateri Tekakwitha; the finder and GCatholic say St. Kateri Tekakwitha. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H7282W+6G. A Nominatim reverse lookup there returned only Stanhope Road (no place_of_worship mapped), but the finder pin (41.000936, -74.653451) and the Google Maps embed on the parish site (41.0007334, -74.6534969, typed "Building") are both within about 40 m.
