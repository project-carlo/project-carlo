## Holds Mass

The Padre Pio Parish bulletin of 1 November 2026 (page 1, rendered 2026-11-04) lists at Our Lady of Pompei Church Sunday 7:30 am, 10:30 am and 12:00 pm Español, Weds & Thurs 8:00 am and Wednesday 7:00 pm Español; page 2's intentions list "7:30 am - Our Lady of Pompei Church (English)" for 1 and 8 November. The Beacon (16 April 2026) says of the merger that "both churches are remaining open".

## Name and address

The sources disagree on the street address. The merged parish's masthead gives "Our Lady of Pompei Church, 154 Caldwell Avenue" and the parish mailing address "70 Murray Avenue"; the finder (directory:22616) and GCatholic 19049 give 70 Murray Avenue. I recorded 154 Caldwell Avenue: the masthead names it as the church's address and 70 Murray as the mailing address, and Nominatim places 154 Caldwell about 30 m from the OSM church building and 70 Murray about 40 m north-east of it (probably the rectory). Either would find the building, so `address_verified` is false. Name: the masthead's "Our Lady of Pompei Church"; GCatholic and OSM spell it "Pompeii". Coordinates are the OSM church building (way 379239857), about 20 m from the GCatholic Plus Code.
