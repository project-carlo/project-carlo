## Holds Mass

The 1 November 2026 bulletin (read 2026-11-04) lists Saturday Vigil 4:00pm, Sunday 9:00 am & 10:30 am, and daily Mass Monday - Saturday at 8:30 am and Monday at 6:30pm; page 2 gives intentions for each of those Masses from 31 October to 8 November. The diocese's finder gives the same weekend times.

## Name and address

The bulletin separates the two addresses: "Church Address: 22 St. Bernard Rd., Wharton, NJ 07885" and "Parish Center/Office: 446 Mount Hope Rd.". OSM tags Saint Bernard's Roman Catholic Church at 22 St Bernards Road. The diocese's finder (446 Mt. Hope Road, its pin at 40.917723, -74.537011) and GCatholic (446 Mount Hope Rd., Plus Code 87G7WF97+G5 at 40.9188, -74.5371) both give the office, about 1 km east of the church, so I recorded the church address from the bulletin, which OSM confirms (address_verified), and the OSM building's coordinates rather than the Plus Code. Name: the bulletin's "Saint Bernard of Clairvaux Parish" and GCatholic's "St. Bernard of Clairvaux" agree; the finder has "Saint Bernard, Rockaway Township, (Mt. Hope)".
