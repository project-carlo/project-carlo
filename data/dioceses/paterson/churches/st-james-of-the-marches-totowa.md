## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 St. James of the Marches bulletin (page 1, rendered; read 2026-11-04) gives "Vigil Mass 5:30 pm (Saturday) 7:30, 10:15 am & 12:00 noon", Monday-Wednesday and Friday 8:00 am and 12:05 pm, Thursday 8:15 am and 12:05 pm, Saturdays 8:30 am. The 8 November 2026 issue schedules ministers for the same four weekend Masses. The finder pin (read 2026-11-04) gives the same weekend times.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead gives "CHURCH OF ST. JAMES OF THE MARCHES 410 TOTOWA ROAD, TOTOWA, NEW JERSEY 07512-2098" and lists "Parish House 32 St. James Place" separately. The finder (11368) and GCatholic (18962) give 32 St. James Place, the parish house. I followed the masthead, as the other address is the office. OSM's church way 1346094517 lies between St. James Place and Totowa Road, and OSM's "410, Totowa Road" address point is at its south edge, which agrees with the masthead, but OSM tags the church itself on St James Place; address_verified is left false. The name is verified (bulletin and GCatholic). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7WQ3Q+X3, about 30 m from the centre of the OSM church way; the finder pin is about 60 m away.
