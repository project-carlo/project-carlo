## Holds Mass

The Padre Pio Parish bulletin of 1 November 2026 (page 1, rendered 2026-11-04) lists at "St. Bonaventure Church & St. Anthony Shrine for the Sick" the Saturday Vigil 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am and 5:30 pm Español, Mon, Tues & Fri 11:30 am and First Saturday 11:30 am Mass; page 2's intentions list "9:00 am - St. Bonaventure Church (English)" for 1 and 8 November. The Beacon (16 April 2026) says of the merger that "both churches are remaining open".

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead gives "174 Ramsey Street, Paterson, NJ"; the finder (directory:11320, 174 Ramsey Street, 07501) and GCatholic 19001 (174 Ramsey St., 07501) agree. The parish office is at the same address. Name: the masthead's "St. Bonaventure Church"; GCatholic and the finder say "St. Bonaventure". Coordinates are the OSM church building (way 379239861, 40.906604, -74.1886191), which fronts Danforth Avenue behind the Ramsey Street parish hall; the GCatholic Plus Code and finder pin sit about 60 m east, on Ramsey Street, so the OSM building was used.
