## Holds Mass

The 8 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday 4:30 and 7:00 pm, Sunday 8:00, 9:15, 10:45 am, 12:15 pm and 5:00 pm (Vietnamese) Masses and weekday Masses at Saint Cecilia, Rockaway. The diocese's finder (read 2026-11-04) gives the same weekend times.

## Name and address

The bulletin and the diocese's finder (directory:6224) give 90 Church Street, Rockaway 07866; GCatholic (18975) gives 70 Church St. The official address is followed, as two independent sources agree on it. The finder also lists the convent at 90 Church St., so 90 may be the parish's postal number for the whole property; either number finds the same block. OSM has no church building here, only interpolated house numbers (70 and 90 about 50 m apart). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7WF3Q+GF, between the two and about 25 m from the finder pin (40.90362, -74.51121).
