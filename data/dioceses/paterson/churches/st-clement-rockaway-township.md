## Holds Mass

The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday 4:30 pm and 7:00 pm (Spanish), Sunday 8:30, 10:30 am and 12:30 pm (Spanish) Masses and weekday Masses; the 1 November issue's schedule (page 2) adds a Sunday 5:30 pm Spanish liturgy.

## Name and address

The bulletin and the parish site ("154 Mt. Pleasant Avenue"), the diocese's finder (directory:11657) and GCatholic (18976) agree on 154 Mount Pleasant Avenue. The bulletin's postal town is Dover, NJ 07801; the municipality is Rockaway Township, as the finder and GCatholic give it, with the same ZIP. Name: the site's "St. Clement Pope & Martyr Church" (the bulletin masthead shortens it to "St. Clement Church"). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7WF27+5X, about 20 m from the finder pin (40.90056, -74.53490); OSM has no place-of-worship entry found by Nominatim here (searched 2026-11-04).
