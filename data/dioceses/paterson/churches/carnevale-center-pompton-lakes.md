## Holds Mass

The 8 November 2026 bulletin's MASS SCHEDULE (page 2, read 2026-11-04) lists "9:00 AM—English (Carnevale Center)" every Sunday, and the issue announces sensory-friendly Masses on 15 November and 13 December "at 11:00am in the Carnevale Center". The parish home page's schedule (read 2026-11-04) also lists "9:00 AM Carnevale Center". It is a weekly public Sunday Mass in a building apart from the church, so it is a worship site of its own. Neither the finder nor GCatholic lists it.

## Name and address

The bulletin names the "Carnevale Center" but gives no address. OSM has a building of that name (way 228771175, tagged building=church) at 10 Lenox Avenue, about 100 m from the church at 17 Pompton Avenue; the name agrees with the bulletin (name verified), the address rests on OSM alone (not verified). Coordinates are OSM's.
