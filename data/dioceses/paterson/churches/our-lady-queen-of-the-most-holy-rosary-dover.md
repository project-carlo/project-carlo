## Holds Mass

The 1 November 2026 bulletin (read 2026-11-04) lists on page 1 "Domingos 8:00 am (Santo Rosario)", and its page 2 Mass Intentions give "8:00am (S) Holy Rosary" for Sunday 1 November: one Spanish Sunday Mass a week. Perpetual adoration is also "Held at Our Lady Queen of the Most Holy Rosary". The parish site's Mass times list "HOLY ROSARY PARISH / IGLESIA SANTO ROSARIO" Sunday 8:00 AM Español.

## Name and address
