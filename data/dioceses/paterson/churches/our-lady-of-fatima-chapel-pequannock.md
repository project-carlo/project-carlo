## Holds Mass

Page 1 of the chapel's 1 November 2026 bulletin (read 2026-11-04) lists Mass intentions for Sunday 1 November at 7:00, 9:00 and 10:30 a.m., 1:30 and 5:00 p.m., and for Masses at 7:00 and 9:00 a.m. (and 7:00 p.m. on some days) from 2 to 5 November. These are Traditional Latin Masses of the Priestly Fraternity of St. Peter, open to the public.
