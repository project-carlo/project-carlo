## Holds Mass

Page 2 of the 1 November 2026 bulletin (read 2026-11-04) lists "MASSES FOR the WEEK" with intentions: Sunday 8:30 A.M., 10:30 AM and 12 Noon; weekdays 8:00 A.M.; Saturday 7 November 5:00P.M. The diocese's finder (read 2026-11-04) gives the same weekend schedule.

## Name and address

The parish's own site (footer, read 2026-11-04) and GCatholic both give 237 Espanong Rd, Lake Hopatcong, NJ 07849, so the address is verified by two independent sources. The diocese's finder gives 204 Espanong Road, a few doors along the same road (OSM's address points for the two numbers are about 80 m apart); 237 is recorded because the parish and GCatholic agree on it. The bulletin gives no street address. Name: the masthead's "OUR LADY STAR OF THE SEA CHURCH", which the finder and GCatholic share. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7X92M+QM (40.9519375, -74.6158125). OSM has no church building here to confirm it, but the Plus Code is about 35 m from OSM's 237 Espanong Road address point and about 60 m from the site's map marker, while the finder pin is about 45 m away.
