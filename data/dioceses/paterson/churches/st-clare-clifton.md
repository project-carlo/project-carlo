## Holds Mass

The 8 November 2026 St. Clare bulletin (read 2026-11-05) gives the Saturday Vigil Mass at 4:00 PM, Sunday Mass at 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM, and weekday Mass at 8:30 AM. The finder (read 2026-11-04) gives the same weekend times.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead and the site header give 69 Allwood Road, and OSM's place_of_worship way 277926166 'St. Clare Catholic Church' is addressed 69 Allwood Road. The finder (11620) and GCatholic (18944) give 31 Allwood Road instead. I followed the parish's own 69, which OSM's building agrees with (two independent sources, so verified); 31 is probably an older or office address, and the finder pin (40.832367, -74.141126) sits about 120 m east of the church building. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7RVM5+43, which falls on OSM's church way (centre about 29 m south-west). The name is the masthead's "Saint Clare Church"; GCatholic and OSM agree on St. Clare.
