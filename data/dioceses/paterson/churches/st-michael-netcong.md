## Holds Mass

Page 2 of the 1 November 2026 bulletin (read 2026-11-04) lists Masses on Saturday at 10am and 5pm, Sunday at 7am, 9:30am, 11:30am and 1:30pm (Spanish) and weekdays at 10am, through 8 November. The diocese's finder (read 2026-11-04) gives the same weekend times. (The finder's older notes text, "Sunday - 7, 8:45, 10:30 a.m., noon", is out of date; the bulletin and the finder's schedule agree.)

The 25 October 2026 masthead gives "4 CHURCH STREET • NETCONG, NJ 07857" (the 1 November issue has a special cover with no address).
