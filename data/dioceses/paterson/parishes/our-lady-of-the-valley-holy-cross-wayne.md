## Publisher

Our Lady of the Valley and Holy Cross, Wayne publish one weekly bulletin, posted on their shared eCatholic site (id 11969) and printed by Bon Venture (footer "182A - Our Lady of the Valley & Holy Cross, Wayne, NJ"). The 1 November 2026 issue (`https://files.ecatholic.com/11969/bulletins/20261101.pdf`, read 2026-11-04) heads both churches on page 1, Holy Cross at "Van Duyne Avenue & Holy Cross Way" and Our Lady of the Valley at "630 Valley Road", with "Our Parishes are Served by: Pastor, Rev. Peter VB. Wells", trustees for each church, and a Mass schedule for each.

## Worship sites

- `our-lady-of-the-valley-wayne`: page 1, "Daily Mass: 9:00 a.m. (Monday through Friday) Saturday: 5:00 p.m. Sunday: 8:00 a.m., 10:00 a.m., and 12:00 Noon".
- `holy-cross-wayne`: page 1, "Saturday: 5:00 p.m. Sunday: 10:00 a.m."; page 2 lists the 7-8 November intentions and presiders under "Holy Cross" at those times, and 24-25 October attendance "Holy Cross 98". Holy Cross is still a worship site with weekend Mass.

The finder has one pin for both (directory:11969); GCatholic lists Our Lady of the Valley (18965) and Holy Cross (18960) separately; the diocese's deanery list still names the two parishes separately (diocese.md, *Sources*). Whether they are one canonical parish or two under one pastor doesn't change the row: one bulletin, two churches. No source read gives a merger date.
