## Worship sites

- `st-margaret-of-scotland-morristown`: the only church. Page 2's Mass Schedule (Saturday 5 PM English, 7 PM Español; Sunday 7:30 and 10:30 AM English, 8:45 AM and 12:15 PM Español; weekdays 8 AM English Mon-Sat, 6:30 PM Español Mon-Fri) names no other place.

The bulletin also names a "Perpetual Adoration Chapel / Capilla de Adoración Perpetua · 12 Columba Street" (page 3), beside the parish office; the parish site asks adorers to register and "confirm access arrangements". Nothing in the issue schedules Mass there (the Mass intentions on page 2 list only the church's times), so it is an adoration chapel, not a worship site, and gets no record. The diocese's finder (directory:17644) and GCatholic (19019) list the one church.
