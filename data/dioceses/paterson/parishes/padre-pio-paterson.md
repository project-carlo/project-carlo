## Publisher

Padre Pio Parish, Paterson (formed 12 April 2026 from St. Bonaventure and Our Lady of Pompei, per The Beacon, 16 April 2026) publishes one weekly bilingual bulletin on its eCatholic site, site id 39965. The 1 November 2026 issue (`https://files.ecatholic.com/39965/bulletins/20261101.pdf`, read 2026-11-04) has a broken-font text layer, so page 1 was rendered: "PADRE PIO PARISH", "www.piopaterson.org", "Mailing Address: 70 Murray Avenue, Paterson, New Jersey 07501", with "St. Bonaventure Church & St. Anthony Shrine for the Sick, 174 Ramsey Street" and "Our Lady of Pompei Church, 154 Caldwell Avenue" either side and "Two Churches, One Family in Christ". Page 2 gives Mass intentions for November 1 - 8, 2026.

Before the merger the site carried both parishes' bulletins in one archive (Saturday-dated "(OLP)" files to 4 April 2026, Sunday "(BON)" files); since 12 April only one issue a week is posted, still labelled "(BON)", and its masthead is the merged parish's. The two former parishes' own eCatholic sites (11320 `ozoka.sites.ecatholic.com`, 22616 `63ovna.sites.ecatholic.com`, still the finder websites of pins 11320 and 22616) both stop at 5 April 2026, so neither publishes separately now; their ids are not this bulletin's and are not recorded in `publication`.
