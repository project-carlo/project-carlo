## Publisher

The 1 November 2026 issue (`https://files.ecatholic.com/25510/bulletins/20261101.pdf`, read 2026-11-04 with probe pdf) has on page 1 "SACRED HEART & HOLY ROSARY PARISH / PARROQUIA DEL SAGRADO CORAZON & SANTO ROSARIO", "RECTORY/RECTORIA: 4 RICHARDS AVENUE • DOVER, NEW JERSEY", "Fr. ## Worship sites

Both former parish churches still have Mass:

- `sacred-heart-dover` (4 Richards Avenue): the page 1 schedule's "Domingos 9:30 am, 1:30 & 5:00 pm (Sagrado Corazón)"; page 2's Mass Intentions mark every Mass "SH" (Sacred Heart) except one, including the English Sunday 8:00, 11:30 am and 7:00 pm and all weekday Masses.
- `our-lady-queen-of-the-most-holy-rosary-dover` (40 Myrtle Avenue): page 1 "Domingos 8:00 am (Santo Rosario)"; page 2 "8:00am (S) Holy Rosary" for Sunday 1 November; perpetual adoration is "Held at Our Lady Queen of the Most Holy Rosary". The home page gives "40 Myrtle Ave Dover, NJ 07801 (Misa Dominical 8:00 a.m.)".

So this is one row with both churches, as the playbook's rule for merged parishes whose bulletin lists Mass at both churches says. The diocese's finder has one entry (directory:5701, 4 Richards Avenue); GCatholic has both churches (18980 Sacred Heart, 18979 Our Lady of the Holy Rosary). A grep of the 1 November and 25 October issues for chapel, Capilla, Myrtle and Blackwell found no other worship site.
