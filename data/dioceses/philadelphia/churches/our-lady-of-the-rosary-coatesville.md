## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (parish row `our-lady-of-the-rosary-coatesville`) gives the Saturday vigil at 5:00 PM, Sunday Masses at 8:30 AM, 10:30 AM and 6:00 PM, and weekday Masses (Monday and Wednesday 6:00 PM; Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday 8:00 AM). It is the parish's only church now; St. Cecilia was relegated in 2016.
