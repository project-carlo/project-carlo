## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Mass box lists Saturday vigil 4:30 PM, Sunday 7:30, 9:30 and 11:30 AM and weekday Masses Monday-Friday 8:15 AM; the parish website's contact page gives the same times plus a 4th-Sunday Young Adult Mass at 7 PM. It is the only church in the bulletin.

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic and the directory all say St. Matthew. The address is given only as the intersection 3rd Ave. and Fayette St. (directory, GCatholic); the bulletin, site and directory give 219 Fayette Street as the parish office and rectory, and 205 Fayette Street as the parish center, so no street number for the church itself is known. `address_verified` is false for that reason.
