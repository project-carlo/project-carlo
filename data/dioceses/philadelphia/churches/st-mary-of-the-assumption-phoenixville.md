Yes. The 24-25 October 2026 bulletin (page 2, image) lists St. Mary Church for Sunday 8:15 AM and 10:45 AM ("STM"), and the directory agrees. Saturday vigil and weekday Masses are at Holy Trinity Church, the parish's other worship site.
