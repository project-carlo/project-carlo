## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Mass Schedule (page 1): Monday-Friday 12:05 PM; Sunday 9:00 AM, 11:00 AM and 7:00 PM. The directory gives the same times.

## Name and address

The bulletin and the directory both give the church's GPS address as 250 N. 4th Street (the directory: 'on 4th St. south of Vine St.'); GCatholic gives the intersection 4th St. & New St., which is the same block. 243 N. Lawrence St. is the parish office and rectory behind the church, not the church. The pin is GCatholic's Plus Code. The bulletin calls it the National Shrine of Santo Niño de Cebú; that is a devotion at this church, not a second place.
