## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Ann Church is one of the parish's two places with public Mass: the 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 6, image) lists Sunday 7:00 AM, 9:30 AM (Spanish) and 4:00 PM, Friday and Saturday 8:30 AM, and the Thursday, Vietnamese, Portuguese and Tamil Masses "in Church". The directory also names "St. Ann Church" for the Spanish and monthly Vietnamese Masses. The other site is St. Ann Chapel.

## Name and address

The Decree of Relegation for Sacred Heart gives "Saint Ann Memorial Church (500 S Main Street)"; OSM places "Saint Ann Roman Catholic Church" at 500 S Main Street; GCatholic and the directory give the intersection 3rd Ave. and Main St. Name: the bulletin and directory both say "St. Ann Church". Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, matching the OSM node. GCatholic's ZIP 19475 is a disagreement with the other sources (19460), recorded, not adopted.
