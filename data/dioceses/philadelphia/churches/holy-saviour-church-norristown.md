## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Mass box lists Saturday vigil 5:15 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM, Sunday 7:30, 9:30 and 11:30 AM and weekdays 7:30 AM at Holy Saviour Church, and page 2 lists the week's intentions at those times. It is the parish's main church; its second church is Our Lady of Mount Carmel, Plymouth Township (own record).
