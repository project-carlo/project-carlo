## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Saturday 5:00 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM (Spanish) and 11:00 AM, and Monday and Thursday 7:00 PM (Spanish); the 4th-Sunday 1:00 PM Spanish Mass names no other place.

## Name and address

Name follows the bulletin ('Annunciation BVM Church'); GCatholic and the directory agree on the dedication. Address: the bulletin footer, the directory's rectory line and OSM all say 1511 S. 10th St., while the directory and GCatholic locate the church at the corner of 10th and Dickinson; the number may be the rectory or friary beside it (St. Rita's directory page gives the Our Mother of Good Counsel Friary at 1511 S. 10th St.). Coordinates from the OSM node, 25 m from GCatholic's pin. `address_verified` is false for that reason; a map or the parish office would settle which number is the church door.

St. Rita of Cascia, merged into this parish in 2016, is not a site of this row: its shrine publishes separately (see national-shrine-of-st-rita-of-cascia-philadelphia).
