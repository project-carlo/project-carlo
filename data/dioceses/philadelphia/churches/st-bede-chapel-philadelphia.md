## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists daily Mass in 'the chapel' Monday-Friday 12:05pm and Tuesday-Friday 5:30pm, and Saturday vigils (2nd Spanish, 3rd Korean, 4th Intercultural) at 5:30pm or 4:30pm in the chapel. The parish site places that chapel on the second floor of the Newman Center. As a campus Mass the parish bulletin lists every week, it gets a church row under the parish, as the diocese's settled rule says for the Penn Newman Center.

## Name and address

GCatholic is the only source for the name 'St. Bede' ('Chapel of St. Bede, Newman Center', Chestnut St., no number); the bulletin and parish site say only 'the chapel' or 'Newman Chapel', so `name_verified` is false. The address 111 S. 38th St. is the Newman Center's (bulletin, directory rectory line, OSM); GCatholic's Chestnut St. is the side of the same block, with coordinates 45 m from the OSM Newman Center point. `address_verified` stays false because no source gives the chapel's own door.
