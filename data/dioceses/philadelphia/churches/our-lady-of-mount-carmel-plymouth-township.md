## Holds Mass

Yes. Holy Saviour's 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 2) lists the Traditional Latin Mass at Our Lady of Mount Carmel on Sunday 25 October at 11:30 AM and on 1 November at 11:30 AM, plus a 10:10 AM Mass for the Sodality on 1 November (the page-1 Mass box says 'First Sunday 10:00 AM'). The Latin Mass is every Sunday; the first-Sunday Mass is the directory's 'October to May' Mass. No other bulletin in the batch lists a Mass here.
