## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, image, rendered) gives Saturday vigil 5:00pm, Sunday 9:30 and 11:30am, Monday to Friday 6:30 and 8:00am and Saturday 8:00am. This is the parish's only church. The directory page (updated two years ago) also lists a Sunday 8 AM Mass the bulletin does not.

## Name and address

GCatholic and the directory both give the church only as the corner of Lancaster and Aberdeen Avenues, Wayne 19087; the bulletin gives the rectory (104 S. Aberdeen Avenue), the school and the convent but no number for the church. No source states a street number, so I used the corner as `line1` and left `address_verified` false.
