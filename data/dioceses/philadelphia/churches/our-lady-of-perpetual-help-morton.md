## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (text layer) lists Saturday 5:00 PM; Sunday 8:00 AM, 10:00 AM and 12:00 PM; and Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday 9:00 AM (Wednesday intention: 'No Mass'). Daily Eucharistic adoration is in the church's chapel; it isn't a separate Mass site.

## Name and address

The directory (rectory and church corner), GCatholic and OSM agree on 2130 Franklin Avenue, Morton 19070; the ParishesOnline organization record says 2139, which is the odd one out and is recorded here as such. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87F6WM5H+36; OSM's node is within about 25 m.
