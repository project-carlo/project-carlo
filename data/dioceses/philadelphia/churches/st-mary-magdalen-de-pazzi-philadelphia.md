## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Paul's 25 October 2026 bulletin lists St. Mary Magdalen de Pazzi Church, 714 Montrose Street, with Sunday Mass at 10:00 AM and a Vigil of Holy Days at 5:00 PM, and its Mass intentions give celebrants for the 10:00 AM Sunday Masses of 25 October and 1 November. A worship site of St. Paul, not a parish of its own (closed as a parish in 2000).

The bulletin and the directory's closed-parish page agree on the name and 714 Montrose St., 19147. OSM has no church node; the coordinates are the address point from Nominatim.
