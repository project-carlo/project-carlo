## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists three Sunday Masses (8:00 low, 10:00 high, 1:00 PM low) and Masses every weekday and Saturday (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday 7:15 AM; Wednesday 7:15 AM and 12:05 PM; Friday 12:05 and 6:30 PM; Saturday 7:15 and 9:00 AM), all in the Extraordinary Form.

## Name and address

This is GCatholic's 'St. Mary, W Elm St. & Oak St.' as well as the directory's quasi-parish, not two buildings: GCatholic's Plus Code is about 30 m from OSM's 'St. Mary Catholic Church' way, which carries 'Oak Street', and the parish's own bulletin, footer and phone give 140 West Hector Street, which the directory repeats. The old Polish parish's closed-parish page gives W. Elm and Oak Sts., consistent with a property fronting Oak Street and W. Hector Street at the corner. The sources disagree on the street name only (Hector in the parish's and directory's text, Oak in OSM, Elm and Oak in GCatholic and the old directory page); I follow the parish's own address and leave `address_verified` false. A site visit or the county parcel record would settle it. Coordinates are OSM's way centre; the name is confirmed by the bulletin, OSM and GCatholic.
