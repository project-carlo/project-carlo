## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday vigil 4:30pm, Sunday 8am, 10:30am and 12noon, weekdays 8am, and lists daily intentions at 8:00 am for 26-31 October. The directory (page updated 8 months ago) gives the Saturday vigil as 5 PM; the bulletin is current and was followed.

## Name and address

GCatholic, the directory, OSM and the bulletin all say Our Lady Help of Christians at 1500 Marian Rd., Abington 19001 (church and rectory share the address, per the bulletin). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, which OSM agrees with. This is the Abington church dedicated in 1963, not the Our Lady Help of Christians church relegated in 2018 elsewhere in the archdiocese.
