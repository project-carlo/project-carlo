## Holds Mass

Yes, on Sundays. The parish's 25 October 2026 bulletin lists a weekly 11:15 am Mass at the Schneider Parish Center (the directory says 11 AM). It is the parish hall, not a church, and no roster lists it; under the diocese's rule a place with public Mass is a church row.
