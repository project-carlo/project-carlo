## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, image, rendered) lists 'Villanova Church Sunday: 7:30am, 10:30am', and its box gives Daily Mass Mon-Fri 12:05pm (the bulletin does not say where it is said: see Not yet confirmed). Our Mother of Good Counsel's bulletin of the same date adds University-led Masses at 5:00, 7:00 and 9:00 PM at Villanova Church while the university is in session. This is the university church GCatholic files under 'Villanova University'; the campus Masses are in the same building, so there is no separate campus church row.

## Name and address

GCatholic (19264) gives 'Villanova, Pennsylvania 19085' with no street, the parish site's header gives 'Villanova Church | 800 Lancaster Ave, Villanova, PA', and John Patrick's university listing gives 800 East Lancaster Avenue, Villanova 19085. I used the last two; the directory gives no ZIP and only the parish office (1229 E. Lancaster Ave.). `address_verified` stays false because the number comes from the parish site and the university listing, not an independent church source. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G62MP4+8M), which falls on the campus. GCatholic's other entry, 41992, is the parish office and Rosemont Chapel at 1229 E Lancaster Ave. (see that church record).
