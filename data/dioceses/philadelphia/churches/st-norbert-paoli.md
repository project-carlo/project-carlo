Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, read from the rendered image) gives Saturday 7:30 am and 5:00 pm Vigil, Sunday 9:00 am, 11:30 am, 6:00 pm and 1:30 pm in Spanish, and daily liturgy at 7:30am, and lists the week's Mass intentions at these times. This is the parish's only church.
