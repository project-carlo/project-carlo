## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin and the parish site both give the Saturday 4:30 PM vigil, Sunday 10:00 AM and weekday 8:00 AM Masses (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday). The directory's Sundays 9 and 11 AM are older than both.

## Name and address

3000 Keenwood Rd. is the parish's address on its bulletin, its site and OSM, and the same campus as the directory's and GCatholic's intersection 'Norriton Dr. & Keenwood Rd.'; GCatholic's Plus Code lies about 15 m from OSM's church node. The bulletin and site say East Norriton (the township), where the directory and GCatholic say Norristown; the post town for 19403 is Norristown, so the row keeps Norristown.
