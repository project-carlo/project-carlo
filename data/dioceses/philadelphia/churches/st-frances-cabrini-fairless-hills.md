## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's schedule (image, rendered) and the parish site: Saturday vigil 5:30 PM; Sunday 7:00, 9:00 and 11:00 AM; Monday to Saturday 9:30 AM; holy days 6:30 AM, 12:00 Noon, 7:00 PM.
