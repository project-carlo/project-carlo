## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists the Saturday 4:30 PM vigil, Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 AM and a daily 8:00 AM Mass, with intentions for each.

## Name and address

The bulletin and parish site give 2007 New Hope Street as the rectory and parish address; the directory and GCatholic give the church at the corner of Johnson Highway and New Hope Street (post town Norristown in the directory, East Norriton in the bulletin). The two are probably the same campus on the corner, but no source states it: OSM's search found no St. Paul Catholic church, so the address is not verified by two independent sources and the coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
