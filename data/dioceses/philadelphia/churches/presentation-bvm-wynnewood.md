## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin and the parish site both give Saturday 5:00 PM vigil, Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 AM and weekday 8:00 AM Mass at Presentation BVM Church. The directory (page updated 8 months ago) still lists Sundays 8, 9:45 and 11:30 AM, which is stale.

## Name and address

GCatholic gives 240 Haverford Rd.; the bulletin masthead, the site footer and the directory give 204 Haverford Rd., which GCatholic's neighbouring numbers and the Plus Code suggest is the parish center or rectory next to the church. I used GCatholic's 240 for the church and its Plus Code for coordinates, and left `address_verified` false because no second source names the church's own street number. 'Our Lady's Chapel' (adoration Monday to Friday 10 AM to 3 PM) is in the same complex and lists no Mass.
