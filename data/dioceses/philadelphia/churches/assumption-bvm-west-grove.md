## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (eCatholic 3092, and John Patrick listing 229) gives Saturday 4:30 pm, Sunday 8:00 am and 10:00 am, and 8:15 am Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday. The directory's older times (Noon on Sunday, 6:30 AM weekdays) are not in the bulletin; the bulletin was followed.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the directory and GCatholic agree on 300 State Rd., West Grove 19390, and on the name (the bulletin masthead says 'Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary Parish'). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6R58J+JQ). GCatholic's 1873 history date matches the build date of the original church (inferred; GCatholic doesn't say), which the 2016 decree says the current church replaced in 2001 on this site, 1.1 miles away.
