## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (eCatholic file, byte-identical to the John Patrick listing 61 issue) gives one Mass schedule: Saturday vigil 5:00 PM (livestreamed), Sunday 7:30 and 10:30 AM, weekdays 6:30 AM (Monday, Wednesday, Friday) and 8:30 AM (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday). It lists no other church or chapel.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the directory and GCatholic all give the church at the corner of Easton Road and Fairhill Avenue, Glenside 19038; none gives a church street number. The rectory and office are 2316 Fairhill Avenue, the number OSM puts on the church node, so the address is kept as the intersection. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, within about 30 m of OSM's node.
