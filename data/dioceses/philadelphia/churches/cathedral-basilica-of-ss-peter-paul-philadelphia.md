## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists five Sunday Masses (Saturday 5:15 PM anticipated; Sunday 8:00, 9:30 and 11:00 AM and 6:30 PM in the Basilica), a 12:30 PM Spanish Mass and weekday and Saturday noon Masses in the Cathedral Chapel.

## Name and address

The cathedral is also the Shrine of St. Katharine Drexel, so one row carries both names. The directory gives only the intersection 18th St. and the Benjamin Franklin Pkwy.; 1723 Race St. is the parish office and also OSM's address for the cathedral way (the cathedral fronts Logan Square between Race and Cherry). GCatholic omits the cathedral. `address_verified` stays false: the street number is the office's, and two sources do not give a building number for the church itself. The pin is the OSM way's centre.
