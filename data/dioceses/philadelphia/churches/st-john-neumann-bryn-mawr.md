## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Mass schedule lists Saturday 5:15 pm vigil, Sunday 7:30 am, 9:00 am, 10:30 am and 12:00 pm, weekdays 7:30 am and Saturday 8:00 am, with weekly Mass intentions at those times. The church is being renovated, but no other location is named for Mass.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish site and the directory give 380 Highland Lane, Bryn Mawr 19010; GCatholic's address is the same and its Plus Code lies about 25 m from the OSM node. OSM files the building under the post town Havertown (Haverford Township); the parish and the directory say Bryn Mawr, which is followed. Coordinates from the OSM node.
