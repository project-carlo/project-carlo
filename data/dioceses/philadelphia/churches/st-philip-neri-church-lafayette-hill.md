## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Parish Mass Schedule gives Saturday vigil 5 pm, Sunday 7:30, 9:30 and 11:00 am, and Monday to Saturday 8:30 am; the directory agrees.

## Name and address

437 Ridge Pike, Lafayette Hill 19444 is given by the directory, GCatholic and OSM (the bulletin cover prints no street). OSM's node and GCatholic's Plus Code are about 20 m apart. The school (3015 Chestnut Street, OSM) is a separate building with no Mass listed.
