## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin of Our Lady of Peace Parish (text layer) lists Notre Dame de Lourdes as a worship site with Sunday 9:30 AM, Saturday 8:30 AM and Friday 8:30 AM Masses, and announces the Tuesday 10 November Mass there. The parish merged into Our Lady of Peace in 2014 (directory). The church has its own John Patrick listing (12, 'Notre Dame de Lourdes Church', Swarthmore 19081) that has no editions at all, so it publishes nothing of its own; its Masses are in the Our Lady of Peace bulletin.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the directory and GCatholic agree on 1000 Fairview Road, Swarthmore 19081 (the school is at 990 Fairview Road). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87F6VMQ3+83; OSM has only a house node at that address, about 40 m away.
