## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Mass Schedule box gives Sunday Mass at 9am, 11:30am and 5:30pm 'in church', and the 1st-Saturday Filipino vigil at 7pm in the church. The parish site gives the same Sunday times (9am, 11:30am, 5:30pm).

## Name and address

The name agrees across the bulletin, directory and GCatholic. The address is an intersection (38th and Chestnut) in the directory and GCatholic; the bulletin's 111 S. 38th Street is the Newman Center and parish office next door (its own church row: st-bede-chapel-philadelphia). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; OSM has an unnamed building at 3728 Chestnut Street nearby, which would be the street number but no source ties it to the church, so `address_verified` stays false.
