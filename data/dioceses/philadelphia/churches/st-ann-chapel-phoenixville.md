## Holds Mass

Yes. The chapel is where most of the parish's Masses are held: the 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 6, image) gives Monday-Thursday 7:30 AM, the Saturday 4:30 PM vigil and Sunday 9:30 AM (English) at the Chapel. It has no GCatholic or directory-roster entry of its own; the directory names it in the parish's "Chapel:" line and Mass times.

## Name and address

"Saint Ann Chapel (604 S. Main Street)" is the Decree of Relegation's wording; the directory has "Chapel: 604 Main St."; the bulletin says "Chapel". Coordinates are OSM's node for Saint Ann Chapel (Nominatim), which sits on Main/Buttonwood Streets just south of the church (500 S. Main).
