## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday vigils at 4:00 PM (English) and 7:00 PM (Spanish), Sunday 8:00, 9:30 and 11:00 AM, and daily Mass Monday to Friday 8:45 AM. The directory's times agree. No other site appears.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the directory and OSM give 215 East County Line Road, in Warminster (ZIP 18974), although the parish and GCatholic call it Hatboro. GCatholic gives 235 East County Line Rd.; I followed the three that agree and noted the difference. Its Plus Code is about 35 m from OSM's node, so the pin is the same building.
