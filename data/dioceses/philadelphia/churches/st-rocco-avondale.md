## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 18 October 2026 bulletin of St. Rocco Parish (row `st-rocco-avondale`) lists a Saturday vigil at 6:00 PM, five Sunday Masses and weekday Masses at noon and 6:30 PM, with the weekday Masses 'en la Iglesia'. It is the parish's only church; the Sunday 2:00 PM Mass at Sacred Heart, Oxford belongs to that church's own row.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead and GCatholic say 313 Sunny Dell Road; the directory says 333 Sunny Dell Rd. (and the parish website text says 333). I followed GCatholic and the bulletin, which agree, and left `address_verified` false because the parish's own site and the directory disagree with them; reading the street on a map would settle it. The mailing address is the parish center, 9016 Gap Newport Pike, not the church. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6R725+95). Name: the bulletin says 'San Roque'/'Saint Rocco Parish', the directory 'St. Rocco (Hispanic)'; the church row takes GCatholic's name plus 'Church'.
