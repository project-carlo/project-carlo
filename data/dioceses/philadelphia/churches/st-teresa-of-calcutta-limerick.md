## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, an image) lists a full weekend and weekday schedule at the Church and Ministry Center, 256 Swamp Pike; the directory's schedule agrees on the weekend (Saturday 5 PM; Sunday 7:30, 9, 10:30 AM, Noon, 6 PM). This is the parish's only church.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the John Patrick listing and the directory give 256 Swamp Pike, Schwenksville, ZIP 19473; GCatholic gives the same street address with ZIP 19468. The street and number agree, the ZIP does not; the parish's ZIP was followed (it is also the rectory's ZIP at 284 Swamp Pk.), and `address_verified` stays false because of the disagreement. The directory's "Limerick" is the township, not the post town. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (building-precise).
