## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's page-1 Mass box (read from the rendered image): 'Vigil Mass (Saturday Evening): 5:30 pm; Sunday Masses: 7:30, 9:30, 11:00 am, 12:30 pm; Weekday Masses (Monday through Friday): 6:30 & 9:00 am; Saturday Mass & Miraculous Medal Novena: 8:30 am'. The masthead names it an 'Archdiocesan Shrine of St. Padre Pio'; the shrine is in this church.

## Perpetual Adoration Chapel

The same box lists a 'Perpetual Adoration Chapel: 7:00 am to 9:00 pm (open to all); 9:00 pm to 7:00 am' (the Adoration Society, with a door code at night), and the site calls it the Saint Katharine Drexel Adoration Chapel. No Mass is listed there, so it is part of this church's campus and not a church or excluded row under the diocese's rule for adoration chapels (the directory's 'Open 24 hrs/day' is the same chapel). If a later bulletin lists Mass in the chapel, add it as a church row.

## Name and address

Name and address agree across the bulletin masthead, the directory and GCatholic (1071 Holland Road, Holland 18966), and OSM places the church on Holland Road beside the GCatholic pin.
