## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 2) lists the Saturday vigil at 4:00 PM, Sundays 7:30 AM and 10:00 AM, weekdays Monday to Friday 8AM 'in the Saint Anne Chapel', and a First Friday Mass at 9:00 AM in the church. The directory's Saturday 5 PM and weekdays Tuesday to Friday are older; the bulletin wins. The weekday and Adoration chapel is described without an address, so its Masses are carried on this row (see the parish record).

## Name and address

The bulletin gives 'Church & Rectory Address: 1325 Klinerd Rd; Pennsburg 18073', the directory gives the rectory at 1325 Klinerd Rd., and OSM has the church at 1325 Klinerd Road; GCatholic omits the number but its Plus Code is about 50 m from the OSM node. The name follows OSM and the parish site ('Saint Philip Neri Catholic Church'). Coordinates from the OSM node. This is the replacement church of 1968; the original church in East Greenville, two miles away, was relegated in 2023 (`excluded/st-philip-neri-east-greenville`), and GCatholic's 1919 date for this entry is that church's, not this building's.
