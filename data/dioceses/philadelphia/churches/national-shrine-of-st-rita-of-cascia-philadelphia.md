## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 25 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday Vigil 5:00 p.m., Sunday 10:00 a.m., weekdays 8:00 a.m. and Noon, a Healing Mass at noon on the third Sunday September to May, and the directory (page updated 2 years ago) gives the same times. This is the church of the former St. Rita of Cascia parish (1907-2016, merged into Annunciation B.V.M.), and the directory calls it 'Open to the Public'.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the directory and the shrine's site agree on the name and on 1166 S. Broad St. 19146 (the directory also gives 'Broad and Ellsworth Sts.'). Coordinates are Nominatim's building match for that address (no OSM church node, no GCatholic entry), so treat the pin as address-level.

Roster: the closed-parish worship-sites entry for St. Rita of Cascia. The shrine's bulletin is its own (parish row national-shrine-of-st-rita-of-cascia-philadelphia), not Annunciation B.V.M.'s.
