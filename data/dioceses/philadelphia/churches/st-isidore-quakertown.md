The bulletin masthead, the directory and GCatholic all give 2545 W. Pumping Station Rd., Quakertown 18951. OSM (Nominatim searches for the name and the street address, 2026-10-29) returned nothing for it, so the coordinates are GCatholic's building Plus Code. The parish office is a separate address (603 W Broad Street); it is not the church.
