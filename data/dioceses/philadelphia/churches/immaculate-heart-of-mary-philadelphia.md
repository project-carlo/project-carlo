## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 and 25 October 2026 bulletins (John Patrick listing 237) give the Saturday 5:15 pm vigil and Sunday 7:30, 9:30 and 11:30 am Masses in the church, and weekday Masses (Monday to Friday 8:30 am, Saturday 8:00 am) in the Sacred Heart Chapel, which neither issue gives a separate address (one campus, 819 E. Cathedral Road, with the school at 815); school-day Wednesdays are in the church. Those weekday Masses are therefore on this row.

## Name and address
