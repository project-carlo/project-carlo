## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday vigil 5:15 PM, Sunday 8:30 and 11:30 AM and weekday Mass at 6:30 AM (Monday, Wednesday, Friday) and 8:00 AM (Tuesday, Thursday), the weekday Masses in the Adoration Chapel. The directory (updated 4 months ago) gives the same times, with the weekday Masses 'in the chapel' and Saturday 8 AM, which the bulletin does not list. The chapel is part of the church building, with no address of its own, so there is no second church row.

## Name and address

Sources disagree on the street: the bulletin masthead and the directory's rectory line say 100 Old Soldiers Road; GCatholic and the directory's church line say 'Old Soldiers Rd. & Woodland Ave.' (an intersection, no number). I used 100 Old Soldiers Road, the parish campus address, and OSM's named place-of-worship node for coordinates; `address_verified` stays false because no source gives the church door's own number. The name agrees across GCatholic, the directory and the bulletin.
