## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Weekend Mass Schedule and daily schedule (page 2) list Masses at the parish; its Mass intentions list 6:30, 8:00, 9:30, 11:30 am Sunday and 8:30 am and 5:30 pm weekdays. The Sacre Coeur adoration chapel on the same campus is a separate place without Mass: see its excluded record.

## Name and address

The bulletin and parish site give 2401 St. Denis Lane (the directory calls it the rectory address); GCatholic and the directory give the corner 'Eagle Rd. & St. Denis Ln.'; OSM's place_of_worship node is at 2401 St Denis Lane and within 20 m of GCatholic's Plus Code. All agree on the site. Coordinates from the OSM node.
