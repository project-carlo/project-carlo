## Holds Mass

Sunday Masses at 10:00 AM and 7:00 PM, read in the parish's 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1 Mass box, 'SUNDAY MASS 10AM & 7PM') and on its home page (seen 2026-10-29). Confession is 'AT SAINT MALACHY CHURCH' on Sundays 9:00-9:45 AM. The directory's 'Saturday, 9 AM (September - May)' is not in the bulletin's Mass box. The weekday Masses are at the Temple Newman Center (`churches/temple-newman-center-philadelphia.md`), not here.

## Name and address

Name: bulletin, home page, directory, GCatholic and OSM agree. Address: the bulletin, home page and directory give 1429 N. 11th St., which the directory labels the rectory; OSM puts the church at 1419 N. 11th St., and GCatholic gives only the intersection 'N 11th St. & W Master St.'. I used OSM's 1419 for the church and left `address_verified` false: the number is one source's, and the bulletin never says which of 1419 or 1429 is the church door. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6XRFW+QP), within 15 m of OSM's way.
