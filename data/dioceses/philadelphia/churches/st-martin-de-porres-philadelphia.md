## Holds Mass

Sunday Mass at 10 am; weekday Masses in the chapel Wednesday and Thursday 9 am; first Saturday of each month 9 am in the chapel (25 October 2026 bulletin, page 1). The directory's Saturday 5 PM vigil and weekday 7 AM are older and not in the bulletin; the bulletin is followed. The Temple Newman Center Masses in the same schedule are the other row (`churches/temple-newman-center-philadelphia.md`).

## Name and address

The bulletin, directory, GCatholic and OSM agree on the name. The bulletin's and directory's 2340 W. Lehigh Ave. is both the rectory and the church (same block at 24th St.; OSM puts the church node 3 m from GCatholic's Plus Code and the house number beside it). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code. Two sources beyond the parish's own (directory, OSM) agree on the street number.
