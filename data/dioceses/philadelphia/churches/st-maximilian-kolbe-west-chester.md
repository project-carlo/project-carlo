## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, image) lists daily Mass Monday to Saturday 8:00 AM, Saturday vigil 4:00 PM and Sunday 7:30, 9:00 and 10:30 AM, and the parish Mass page (read 2026-10-28) gives the same times. It is the parish's only church; the bulletin's sanctuary-lamp list also names a "Chapel" (page 4), but no schedule lists a Mass there.

## Name and address

GCatholic and the directory's church line give the intersection "Rt. 202 & E Pleasant Grove Rd."; the directory's rectory line, the parish's Mass page and the bulletin give "15 E. Pleasant Grove Rd., West Chester, PA 19382". The record follows the parish's own street address; `address_verified` is false because the street number rests on the parish's own pages (one source) and the directory's rectory line, and no outside source gives a number for the church itself.
