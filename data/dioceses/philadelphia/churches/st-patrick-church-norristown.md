## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish site's home page lists weekend Masses (Saturday 4 PM English and 6 PM Spanish; Sunday 8 AM and 12 PM Spanish, 10 AM English) and weekday Masses, and the 25 October 2026 bulletin announces a Mass on 28 October. The pages of the bulletin read (1-2) carry no Mass box, so the schedule is the site's and the directory's, which agree.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead gives 714 DeKalb St., the directory's rectory address and the ParishesOnline organization's address; the directory puts the church at DeKalb and Chestnut Sts. and GCatholic agrees; OSM's node carries 703 Green Street, the parish offices. No source gives a street number for the church building itself, so the intersection is kept and `address_verified` is false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, confirmed within about 12 m by the OSM node.
