## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish Mass page (read 2026-10-28) lists weekend and daily Mass, and the 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, image) gives its Forty Hours Masses "in Church" at 6 PM on Sunday and at 6:45 AM, 9 AM and 7 PM on the following days. This is the parish's only church.

## Name and address

GCatholic and the directory give the church only as the intersection of Route 3 and Route 352, West Chester 19382, which is why `line1` is the intersection. The bulletin masthead gives "8 Cavanaugh Court", which the directory assigns to the Meehan Center (the rectory is 6 Cavanaugh Court), so it is a campus address, not clearly the church's. `address_verified` is false: no source gives a street number for the church itself. `pnpm geocode` is not needed; coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6XF8G+CP), building-precise.
