## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (rendered page 1) lists Saturday vigil, two Sunday Masses and weekday Masses at 8:30 AM; the parish site's Mass and Confession Times page shows the same (Sat 4:30 PM; Sun 9:00 and 11:00 AM; Mon-Fri 8:30 AM, in the chapel). The weekday Mass is in the parish chapel at 126 Walnut Ave. (the rectory address), which is the parish's own property and not a separate church row.
