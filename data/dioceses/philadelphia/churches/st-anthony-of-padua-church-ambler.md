## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 joint bulletin's page-2 schedule gives St. Anthony (SA) Sunday 9:00 AM, 11:00 AM and 5:00 PM, a Spanish 1:00 PM Mass on the second, fourth and fifth Sundays, and Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8:15 AM; the Mass-intentions list for 25 October to 1 November tags the same times SA. The directory agrees (Sunday 9, 11, 5; weekdays Monday, Wednesday, Friday 8:15).

## Name and address

259 Forest Avenue, Ambler 19002 is given by the bulletin, the directory, GCatholic and OSM. The bulletin calls the parish 'St. Anthony of Padua Parish'; OSM and GCatholic name the church after the saint, so the church row is 'St. Anthony of Padua Church'. GCatholic's Plus Code lies about 20 m from OSM's node.
