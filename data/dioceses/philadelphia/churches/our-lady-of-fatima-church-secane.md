## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Saturday vigil 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 AM, and weekday Mass 8:30 AM Monday - Saturday. The directory's Sunday times (7:30, 10, 11:30) are stale. A St. John Neumann Chapel on the grounds hosts adoration and devotions; the bulletin gives no Mass there.

## Name and address

Name: bulletin, site, directory and GCatholic agree. Address: bulletin, site and directory give 1 Fatima Drive, Secane 19018, and GCatholic matches it. The directory and bulletin call it the rectory address, and the site gives the same one for the church. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (building-precise); OSM has no named node.
