## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Andrew's own site, current to at least September 2026, lists a Sunday 9:00 AM English Mass 'at the Saint Casimir Worship Site', and the directory's St. Casimir page gives Sundays 9 AM. St. Casimir's parish closed in 2011 but the building is still a worship site of St. Andrew's; this is not a closure (see the Rules settled on worship sites of merged parishes). No bulletin lists the Mass, so the church sits under the `no_bulletin` St. Andrew row and stays off the site.

## Name and address

The directory and OSM give 324 Wharton St.; the site gives 4th St. & Wharton St., 19147 (the ZIP the directory page lacks). GCatholic lists no St. Casimir. The pin is the OSM way.
