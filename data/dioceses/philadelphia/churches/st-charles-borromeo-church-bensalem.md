## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (rendered page 1) and the parish site's Mass schedule list Saturday, Sunday and weekday Masses (above); the site says 'St. Charles Borromeo Church will be open for private prayer', i.e. the schedule is this church's.

## Name and address

Name: the bulletin, the parish site, the directory and GCatholic agree on St. Charles Borromeo. Address: the directory, GCatholic and CatholicPhilly (2014) give only the intersection of Bristol Pike and Hulmeville Rd.; the bulletin and parish site print 1731 Hulmeville Road, which the directory gives as the rectory address, so it may be the office rather than the church door. The intersection is what the sources agree on, so `address_verified` stays false; no street number for the church building was found. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (40.0756,-74.9584).
