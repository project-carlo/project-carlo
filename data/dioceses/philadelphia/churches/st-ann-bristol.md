## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Mark's 25 October 2026 bulletin lists the St. Ann Worship Site with Sunday Mass at 10:45 am and a holy-day Mass at 9:00 am; the parish home page shows 'St. Ann Worship Site' beside St. Mark. The directory's Sunday 10:30 and Tuesday 7 PM Masses (and CatholicPhilly's 2019 report of the same) are not in the current bulletin box, which is followed. This is the Italian national church (Trinitarians served it until the 2014 merger).

## Name and address

Both the bulletin and the directory give only the intersection, Dorrance & Pond Streets, and GCatholic the same with ZIP 19007; no source read states a street number, so `address_verified` stays false. The pin is GCatholic's Plus Code. The directory's `worship-sites` entry and GCatholic 19157 are the same building.
