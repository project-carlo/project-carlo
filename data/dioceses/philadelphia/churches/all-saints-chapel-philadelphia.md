## Holds Mass

Yes, on weekdays. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (John Patrick listing 58) prints 'Parish Center: All Saints’ Chapel: Weekdays at 7:00 AM' and gives the office and chapel the address 146 Rector St., apart from the church at 119 Rector St.; the Masses for the week (Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM) are the chapel's.

## Name and address

Name: 'All Saints’ Chapel' in the bulletin, 'All Saints Rectory Chapel' in the directory; verified by those two. Address 146 Rector St.: the bulletin ('Office & Chapel at 146 Rector St.') and the directory's rectory address agree. No coordinates: OSM has two address points for 146 Rector Street (above), and none is a chapel node; `geocode` fills them after promotion.
