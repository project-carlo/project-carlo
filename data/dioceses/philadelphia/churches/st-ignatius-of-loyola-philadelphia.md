## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Sunday Mass at 10:00 AM, Holy Days at 10 AM and 7 PM, and a Ghanaian Mass at 2:00 PM on the last Sunday of the month (the line does not say where, taken as this church). It lists no weekday Mass; the directory's weekday 9:30 AM is at a nursing-home chapel (see the parish record).

## Name and address

Name agrees across the bulletin, directory and GCatholic. The bulletin and OSM give 636 N. 43rd St., which the directory calls the rectory; the directory and GCatholic place the church at 43rd and Wallace Sts., and GCatholic's pin is about 12 m from OSM's point at 636, so church and office are one site. address_verified is left false because the sources differ in form (a number against an intersection).
