## Holds Mass

Daily and weekend Mass: Saturday 5:30 PM vigil, Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 AM, Monday through Saturday 8:00 AM, holy days 8:00 AM, 10:00 AM and 7:30 PM (1 November 2026 bulletin, page 1; the parish home page, seen 2026-10-29, agrees). The directory's times (Sunday 8 and 10:30, weekdays 8 AM) match.

## Name and address

The parish and its Oratory are one church. The bulletin and site call it 'St. Francis Xavier Parish - The Oratory'; GCatholic calls the building an Oratory ('St. Francis Xavier'); the directory 'St. Francis Xavier – The Oratory'. Address: OSM puts the church at 2319 Green Street, which is the site footer's address and the directory's 'Parish House: 2319 Green St.'; GCatholic gives the corner 24th & Green. The church and parish house share the number in OSM and the site, so I record 2319 Green St. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 25 m from OSM's way.
