## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists a Saturday 5:15 PM vigil, Sunday 8:30, 10:30, 12:30 and 6:00 PM in the upper church, and weekday Masses at 7:45, 12:05 and 5:15 in the lower church. The directory adds a weekday 8:30 AM and 1:05 PM that the bulletin does not.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the directory and OSM agree on 21 S. 13th St., 19107. GCatholic omits this church, so there is no roster entry other than the directory's.
