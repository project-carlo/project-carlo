## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, an image) lists Saturday Vigil 5:00 PM, Sunday 7:30 AM, 10:15 AM and 11:30 AM (Spanish) and weekday Masses; the parish home page (read 2026-10-28) gives the same times. This is the parish's only church; Holy Trinity, listed on the directory page as an alternative worship site, is excluded (see `excluded/holy-trinity-pottstown`).

## Name and address

The bulletin gives the parish's physical address as 223 Beech Street, which the directory shows as the rectory and the site's footer as the church address; the directory's own parish address, 844 N. Keim Street, is the parish office and school (the bulletin calls it the mailing address). GCatholic places the church at Beech St. & Hanover St. with a Plus Code, and the coordinates are that Plus Code's. The street (Beech) agrees but GCatholic gives only an intersection, so `address_verified` stays false: the number 223 comes from the parish's own materials alone. The name agrees across the bulletin, directory and GCatholic.
