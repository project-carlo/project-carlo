## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (text layer, page 7) gives daily Mass at 9:00AM, Saturday Vigil 5:00PM and Sunday Mass 7:30, 9:30 and 11:30AM, and the parish Mass page (read 2026-10-28) adds weekday 6:30 AM (school year) and Saturday 9:00 AM. This is the parish's only church.

## Name and address

Sources disagree on the building's street address. GCatholic: 126 Woodland Ave. (pin 40.03556, -75.51206). The parish site's footer and OSM: 104 Channing Avenue (OSM node 40.03519, -75.51299, about 80 m from GCatholic's pin). The directory and the bulletin masthead give 131 Channing Avenue, which the parish's own contact page lists as the Parish Office and Religious Education office, not the church (the rectory is 108 Woodland Ave. and the school 115 Channing Ave.). I followed the parish site and OSM, which agree; `address_verified` stays false because GCatholic differs and the pins are 80 m apart. Coordinates are the OSM node. Name: "St. Patrick" in the directory and GCatholic, "Saint Patrick Roman Catholic Church" in OSM; the dataset name adds 'Church'.
