## Holds Mass

Yes. The cover of the 25 October 2026 bulletin (image only) lists daily Mass 8 AM Monday-Friday, Saturday vigil 4 PM, Sunday 8:30 AM, 11 AM and 5 PM. The directory (updated about four months earlier) lacks the Sunday 5 PM Mass. The cover names one church.

## Name and address

The directory gives the church as 400 Morton Ave.; GCatholic gives 'Penn St. & Morton Ave.' and OpenStreetMap places the named church on Penn Street at the same point as GCatholic's Plus Code. The bulletin and John Patrick listing give only the rectory, 110 Park Street. The street number 400 Morton Ave. rests on the directory alone, so the address flag stays false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
