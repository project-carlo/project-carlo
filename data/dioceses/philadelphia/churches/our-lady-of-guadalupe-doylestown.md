## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's cover gives Saturday Vigil 5 p.m., Sunday 9 a.m. and 11 a.m., and Tuesday through Saturday 8 a.m.; the 8 a.m. weekday and 9 a.m. Sunday Masses are livestreamed.

## Name and address

5194 Cold Spring Creamery Road, ZIP 18902, is the same in the bulletin, the listing, the directory and GCatholic. The sources split on the post town: the bulletin's address block and the directory say Doylestown, the listing, the bulletin's date band and GCatholic say Buckingham (the township). Doylestown is used, as the bulletin's own address block and the diocese's `city_aliases` do. `address_verified` stays false because the bulletin, listing and directory all repeat the parish's own mailing address; the pin is GCatholic's Plus Code, not checked against OSM.
