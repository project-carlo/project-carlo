## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists a Saturday 4:00 PM vigil, four Sunday Masses (7:45 AM, 10:00 AM, noon, 2:00 PM Spanish) and weekday Masses Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 8:00 AM and Tuesday, Thursday at 6:15 PM. The directory's older times (vigil 5 PM, Sunday 8 AM, Tuesday 7 PM) differ; the bulletin was followed. St. Rocco's bulletin also lists the Sunday 2:00 PM Mass here.
