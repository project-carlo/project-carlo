## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 joint bulletin's page-2 schedule gives St. Joseph (SJ) a Saturday vigil at 4:00 PM, Sunday 7:30 AM, a Spanish 1:00 PM Mass on the first and third Sundays, weekday Masses Tuesday and Thursday 6:30 AM and communion services Monday, Wednesday and Friday 6:30 AM. The directory and St. Joseph's own site list the same weekend times. It is a worship site of the joint St. Anthony and St. Joseph row because that one bulletin lists it; St. Joseph has no bulletin of its own.

## Name and address

16 S. Spring Garden Street, Ambler 19002 is given by the bulletin, the site, the directory, GCatholic and OSM; GCatholic's Plus Code lies within about 10 m of OSM's node.
