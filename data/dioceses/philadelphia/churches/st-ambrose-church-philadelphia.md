## Holds Mass

Yes: Saturday 4:00 PM and Sunday 9:00 AM (English) and 10:30 AM (Spanish); weekdays Monday to Friday 8:05 AM, in the 25 October 2026 bulletin's schedule box (rendered image).

## Our Lady of Fatima Chapel

The weekday Masses are in 'Our Lady of Fatima Chapel'. The box gives no separate address, so it is read as a chapel of the same campus (diocese rule on a chapel named in a weekday Mass line) and has no church row. A later source giving it another address would change that.

## Name and address

The bulletin cover, the listing, the directory's rectory and OSM give 401 E. Roosevelt Blvd.; GCatholic and the directory give the church's corner as C St. and Roosevelt Blvd. The pin is GCatholic's Plus Code, within 20 m of OSM's node.
