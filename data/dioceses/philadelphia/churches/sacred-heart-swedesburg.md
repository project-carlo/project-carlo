## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's masthead panel and the parish website both give Saturday anticipated Mass 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 and 10:15 AM and weekday Mass 8:00 AM, all at the parish church; the site adds that Mass is held 'at our parish: 120 Jefferson St'.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the parish website, the directory and GCatholic agree on Sacred Heart at 120 Jefferson Street, 19405. The post town differs by source: the bulletin and the directory's church line say Swedesburg, the website and the directory's rectory line say Bridgeport; both are 19405. I use the bulletin's 'Swedesburg'. The bulletin, the directory and GCatholic are independent of each other, so the address is verified.
