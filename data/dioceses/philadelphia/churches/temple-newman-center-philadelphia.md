## Holds Mass

Weekday Mass on the Temple University campus: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 12:10 PM, Thursday 5:00 PM (St. Malachy's 1 November 2026 Mass box); St. Martin de Porres's 25 October 2026 Mass schedule gives Monday and Friday 12:10 pm and Thursday 5 pm, 'except winter & summer break'. It is a campus Mass the bulletin lists every week, so it gets a church row under the Rules settled for this diocese; I filed it under St. Malachy because that bulletin's Mass box lists it first and with the fullest schedule, and its page 4 names 'the Temple Catholic Newman Center' beside the two parishes. Which parish staffs it is not stated in either bulletin: a Newman Apostolate office exists in the archdiocese, and no Newman Center bulletin of its own was found.

## Name and address

St. Malachy's bulletin says 'Temple Catholic Newman Center' and St. Martin de Porres's 'Temple Newman Center'; the directory says 'Temple University Newman Center'. I used the bulletin's full form and left `name_verified` false for lack of a source with the official name. Address: the directory's '2129 N. Broad St.' and OSM way 192992780 agree, and OSM gives the coordinates. No GCatholic entry exists.
