## Holds Mass

The directory (page updated about four months before 2026-10-28) gives Sunday Masses at 8:30 AM (English) and 11:45 AM (Spanish) and Monday-Saturday 8 AM (Spanish) at St. Veronica; Discover Mass lists the same times. No bulletin was found (see the parish record), so the times are the directory's, undated beyond that. CatholicPhilly's 13 November 2025 photo feature names Rev. Roberto Gurrola, I.V.E., as St. Veronica's pastor.

Tioga St. is the directory's rectory, and ParishesOnline puts the 'St. Veronica Church' organization there. I kept the intersection as given and left `address_verified` false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, about 55 m from the ParishesOnline pin; OSM returned no named St. Veronica node.
