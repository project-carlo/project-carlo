## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists two Saturday vigils, four Sunday Masses (Vietnamese, English, Indonesian, Spanish) and weekday Mass at 8:30 AM in the chapel. The chapel and the Aquinas Center are rooms of the same campus with no address of their own in the Mass box, so they stay on this row.

## Name and address

The bulletin, OSM, the directory's rectory line and the listing's Google Maps address agree on 1719 Morris Street. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6WRJG+3H).
