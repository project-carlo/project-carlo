## Holds Mass

Yes, currently, on Sundays: the 25 October 2026 St. Richard bulletin puts the Sunday 10:00 AM Mass at Holy Spirit. The weekday and other weekend Masses are at St. Richard.

## Name and address

GCatholic, OSM and the directory give 1845 Hartranft Street; the bulletin masthead prints 1857 Hartranft Street. I followed the sources that agree (GCatholic, OSM, directory) but left address_verified false because the parish's own bulletin disagrees.
