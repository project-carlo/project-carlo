## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday vigil Mass at 4:30 pm on the first and third Saturdays, Sunday 9:00 am and Monday to Friday 8:30 am (intentions listed for 26 to 30 October). The directory agrees.

## Name and address

'120 Allison Road' is the bulletin masthead's address and the directory's rectory address; the directory and GCatholic give the church only as the corner of Ulmer Avenue and Allison Road, and OSM has the church and the rectory as separate nodes about 50 m apart with no house number. The masthead street may therefore be the parish office rather than the church door, so `address_verified` stays false; coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (the OSM church node is about 40 m away).
