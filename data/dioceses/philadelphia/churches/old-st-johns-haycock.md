## Holds Mass

Yes, weekly. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) and the parish site's Mass Schedule both put the Thursday 9:00am weekday Mass at old St. John's Church (the site: 'Thurs. 9am Mass at Old St. John Church, Haycock'). Neither gives any other Mass there. It is a church of St. John the Baptist Parish, Ottsville, and its Mass appears in that parish's bulletin.

## Name and address

The parish history calls the 1855 church 'Old St. John's', at the base of Haycock Mountain, and says the parish owns it and the cemetery around it. The address 453 Haycock Run Rd., Kintnersville 18930 is the directory's cemetery line, and a third-party business listing gives the same address for 'Old St. John's Catholic Church'. No bulletin, parish page or map I could read gives the church's own number, so the address is a lead: `address_verified` and `name_verified` are false, the coordinates are null (Nominatim found nothing for the church or the street), and `pnpm geocode` or an OSM look-up can fill them after promotion. GCatholic and the directory's church list do not carry it (the directory names it nowhere; GCatholic's St. John the Baptist is the Durham Road church).
