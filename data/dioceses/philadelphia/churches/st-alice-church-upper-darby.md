## Holds Mass

Yes, one Mass a week. The 25 October 2026 St. Laurence bulletin (image only, page 1 rendered) lists 'St. Alice, Sunday 10:00AM in Vietnamese' in its Mass schedule; St. Laurence's site footer and the directory's page for St. Laurence give the same 10:00 AM ('Vietnamese only'). The site's upper Mass Times block says 10:30 AM; the bulletin's 10:00 AM is followed. No weekday or other Mass is listed there. St. Alice parish (1922-2013) merged into St. Laurence; GCatholic and the closed-parish page say nothing of a closure, and the closed page's 'Formerly located at Copley Rd. and Walnut St.' is only an address line.

## Name and address

Name: bulletin, directory, GCatholic and the John Patrick listing agree on St. Alice. Address: **the sources disagree.** GCatholic and the closed-parish page give 'Copley Rd. & Walnut St.'; John Patrick listing 38 gives 150 Hampden Road, Upper Darby 19082, with the phone (610) 352-1431 that GCatholic also gives. I stored 150 Hampden Rd because it is a street address and its phone ties it to the same church; Nominatim places 150 Hampden Road about 110 m from GCatholic's Plus Code point, so the two are probably the same corner, but I did not confirm it from a bulletin or the site. Coordinates are Nominatim's house node at 150 Hampden Road (no OSM place_of_worship found). `address_verified` stays false; a St. Laurence bulletin page naming the St. Alice address would settle it.
