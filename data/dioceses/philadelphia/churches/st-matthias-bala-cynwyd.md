## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Saturday 5:00 PM vigil, Sunday 8:30 AM, 10:30 AM and 7:00 PM, weekdays 7:30 AM and Saturday 8:00 AM, matching the directory.

## Name and address

The church stands at Bryn Mawr and Highland Avenues. The bulletin's mailing address, the directory's rectory and the OSM node all say 128 Bryn Mawr Avenue; OSM files the church building under that number, so the street may be the rectory's number with the church beside it. I used it with OSM's coordinates, and left `address_verified` false because the number is the parish's mailing address, not a source naming the church door.

## Not yet confirmed

Whether 128 Bryn Mawr Ave. is the church or the rectory next to it; the directory and bulletin disagree on the parish center's address (120 Bryn Mawr Ave. vs 108 N. Highland Ave.), which is not a worship site.
