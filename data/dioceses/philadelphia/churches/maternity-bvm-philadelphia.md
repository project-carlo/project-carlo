## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Mass box (page 1, rendered): Saturday vigil 4:30 PM; Sunday 8:00 AM, 9:30 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:30 PM and 7:00 PM; weekday Masses Monday-Thursday 6:30 and 8:00 AM, Friday 6:30 and 8:30 AM, Saturday 8:00 AM; every line marked '(Lower Chapel)' while the church roof is replaced.

## Name and address

The bulletin footer, the directory and GCatholic all give 9220 Old Bustleton Avenue, Philadelphia 19115 (the directory and GCatholic abbreviate 'Ave.'). The site's own pages say 'Maternity of the Blessed Virgin Mary Catholic Church'. The pin is GCatholic's Plus Code. GCatholic gives '1870.10' as the church's first-recorded date; the parish's history page says ground was broken for a new church on 12 July 1964, with Masses until then at an interim church, the auditorium and 'the Old Church in the hollow' (inferred: the 1870 date belongs to that Old Church).

## Not a separate row

The directory's monthly 'Old Church' Mass (10:30 AM) is not on this row's evidence as a separate place: no bulletin or page read gives the Old Church an address of its own, and the parish record's `research` keeps the question open.
