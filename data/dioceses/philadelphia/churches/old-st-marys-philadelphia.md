## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin: Saturday vigil 4:30 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM, Monday-Friday 7:30 AM (page 3: 'DAILY MASS IN OSM CHURCH'), Saturday confession 4:00 PM.

## Name and address

The directory gives the church as 248 S. 4th St. and the rectory as 252 S. 4th St.; the bulletin footer gives 252 S. 4th St. (the parish office). OSM's way for the church is at 248 S. 4th St. GCatholic does not list this church (it omits it). `address_verified` stays false: two sources agree on the street address (directory and OSM) but the bulletin gives the rectory number, and OSM's ZIP (19147) differs from 19106. The pin is the OSM way's centre.

The directory's weekday Mass 'in chapel of former convent, 5th and Locust' is stale; the current bulletin puts the weekday Mass in the church.
