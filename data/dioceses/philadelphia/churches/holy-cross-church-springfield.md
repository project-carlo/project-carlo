## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Saturday vigil 4:30 PM, Sunday 8:30 AM and 11:00 AM, and daily Mass Tuesday - Friday 8:30 AM (no Mass Monday or Saturday morning, as the directory says). The adoration chapel at the back of the rectory is adoration only.

## Name and address

Name: bulletin, directory, GCatholic and OSM agree ('Holy Cross'). Address: the bulletin masthead gives 651 E. Springfield Road and OSM puts the place_of_worship at that address; GCatholic and the directory give the cross-street corner (Bishop Rd. and Springfield Rd.), within 20 m of the OSM way, so the sources agree. Coordinates are the OSM way's centre.
