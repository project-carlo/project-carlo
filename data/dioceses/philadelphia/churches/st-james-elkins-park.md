## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin gives a Saturday 5:00 evening Mass, Sunday 9:30 AM and 12:30 PM (Filipino Mass, bilingual on the 1st and 3rd Sundays), and Monday to Friday 8:00 AM. The directory's Sundays (8:30, 10:30 AM, 12:30 PM), from a page updated two years ago, are out of date.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, GCatholic, the directory's rectory line and OSM agree on 8320 Brookside Road, Elkins Park 19027. The bulletin and OSM call it 'St. James the Great Church' and 'St James Church'; GCatholic and the directory 'St. James'. Coordinates are OSM's place-of-worship node, which matches the GCatholic Plus Code.
