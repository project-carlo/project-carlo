## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's page-3 'Weekly Mass Schedule' gives Saturday 5:00 PM Vigil, Sunday 7:00 AM, 9:00 AM, 10:30 AM and 12:00 Noon, and Monday-Friday 8:00 AM. The Sunday noon Mass on the fourth Sunday of the month is the 'Sensory Friendly Mass'.

## Name and address

235 East State Street, Doylestown 18901 is the bulletin's, the listing's and the directory's rectory address; GCatholic gives 'E State St. & Maple Ave.' with a Plus Code, used for the pin. The number is the parish's mailing address, which may be the rectory beside the church, so `address_verified` stays false. The name is the one the bulletin, listing and directory use.
