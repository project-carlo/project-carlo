## Holds Mass

Yes. The church's own site and St. John the Evangelist's 25 October 2026 bulletin both give Sunday Masses at 10:00 AM (Cantonese), 11:15 AM (English) and 12:30 PM (Mandarin). The directory adds Holydays 6 PM, and the site a Wednesday 8:30 AM school Mass in the school year.

## Name and address

The directory, GCatholic, the church's footer and the St. John's bulletin all give 915 Vine St.; the ZIP is 19107 on the site and GCatholic (ParishesOnline's 19145 is its own). Two independent sources agree, so the address is verified. The pin is GCatholic's Plus Code. GCatholic's group name is the Chinese name of the church.
