## Holds Mass

Yes, once a month. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Mass box lists 'Worship Site: St. Josaphat Church (124 Cotton St.): First Saturday Mass at 8:30 AM; confessions at 8:00 AM', and announces the next one for 'FIRST SATURDAY—NOV 7'. The directory's page for the closed parish gives the same 8:30 AM first-Saturday Mass. A monthly Mass counts as a worship site.

## Name and address

The bulletin writes 'St. Josaphat Church (124 Cotton St.)'; the directory names the parish 'St. Josaphat (Polish)' at 'Cotton and Silverwood Sts.'; OSM spells it 'Saint Josephat's Church' at 124 Cotton Street. Name and the 124 Cotton St. address are given by the bulletin and OSM, so both flags are true. GCatholic has no entry (its St. Josaphat is the Ukrainian church on Longshore Ave.). Coordinates are the OSM node.
