## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Parish Site schedule: Saturday vigil 5:00 pm; Sunday 7:30 and 9:00 am; Monday to Friday 8:00 am; holy days 8:00 am, Noon and 6:30 pm. The directory also lists a Spanish Mass at 9 AM and a 10:45 AM Sunday Mass at St. Mark; the bulletin puts the 10:45 am at the St. Ann worship site.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, GCatholic and the directory all give 1025 Radcliffe Street, Bristol 19007 (the parish office is at 1024). The pin is GCatholic's Plus Code.
