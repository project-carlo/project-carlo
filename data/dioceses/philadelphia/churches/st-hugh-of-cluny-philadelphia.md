## Holds Mass

A worship site of St. Veronica's parish (merged 2013). The directory's St. Veronica page lists a Saturday 5 PM bilingual vigil, a Sunday 10 AM Spanish Mass and Wednesday and Friday 5:30 PM Spanish Masses at St. Hugh, plus Saturday evening adoration; the Archbishop said Mass in the church on 2 November 2025 (CatholicPhilly, 13 November 2025). No bulletin was found for St. Veronica, so the weekly schedule rests on the directory page (updated about four months before 2026-10-28). The closed-parish page's 'through 2011' is the records line and says nothing about the building. The `revisit` on the parish record covers finding a bulletin that dates the schedule.

## Name and address

145 W. Tioga St. is given by the directory, ParishesOnline (145 W Tioga St) and OSM (house 145 West Tioga Street); GCatholic and the closed-parish page give the corner of Howard and Tioga, the same place (ParishesOnline's pin is 3 m from GCatholic's Plus Code). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code. The name matches in all of them.
