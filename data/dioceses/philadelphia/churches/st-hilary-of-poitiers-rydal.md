## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 2 of the 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Mass intentions at the Saturday-evening 5:00pm vigil (31 October), Sunday 8:30am and 10:30am, and 8:00am each weekday. The directory (updated 2 months ago) agrees: Saturdays 5 PM vigil, Sundays 8:30 and 10:30 AM, weekdays 8 AM.

## Name and address

Name: 'St. Hilary of Poitiers' (GCatholic, directory), 'Saint Hilary of Poitiers Church' (OSM), 'St. Hilary of Poitiers Parish' (bulletin): the same church. **Address disagrees.** The bulletin cover, the directory's rectory line and OSM's church node all give 820 Susquehanna Road; GCatholic gives 920 Susquehanna Rd., which OSM assigns to St. Hilary of Poitiers School. I followed 820 (three sources, one of them the parish's own bulletin, against GCatholic's one) and left `address_verified` false because the 920 entry is GCatholic's and the two buildings are on one campus. What would settle it: the parish's contact page or a parcel record. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (40.09931, -75.09719), about 55 m from OSM's church node (40.09880, -75.09710): the same campus.
