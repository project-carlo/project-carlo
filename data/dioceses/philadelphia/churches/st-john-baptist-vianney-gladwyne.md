## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (image, page 1 rendered) lists Saturday 5:00 PM vigil, Sunday 8:30 and 10:30 AM, weekdays 7:30 AM, Saturday 8:00 AM and holy day Masses 7:30 AM and 5:30 PM. The directory (page 2 years old) adds Noon and 7:30 PM holy day Masses that the bulletin does not.

## Name and address

The church is called St. John Vianney by the bulletin and the parish site; the directory and GCatholic say 'St. John Baptist Vianney'. The bulletin and the directory give 350 Conshohocken State Road; GCatholic and OSM put the church at the Youngsford Road and Route 23 (Conshohocken State Road) corner, consistent with that. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, 10 m from OSM's node. `address_verified` is false: the number comes from the parish's own materials, and OSM carries no number.
