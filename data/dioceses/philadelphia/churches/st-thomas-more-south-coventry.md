## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, an image) lists Saturday Vigil 4:30 p.m., Sunday 8 a.m. and 11 a.m., and weekday Mass at 8:30 a.m. (7 a.m. Tuesday and Thursday). The directory's Sunday 9:30 AM is older.

## Name and address

The sources give three forms of the same place. The bulletin gives 2101 Pottstown Pike, Pottstown 19465. The directory puts the church at "Rt. 100 North of Rt. 23, South Coventry Township" and gives 2101 Pottstown Pk. only as the rectory address. GCatholic gives Rt. 100 & Rt. 23, South Coventry, with a Plus Code. Pottstown Pike is Route 100, so they plausibly agree and the parish's own street number was followed with its Pottstown post town (the township name is the roster's); that the church stands at 2101 and not beside it rests on the bulletin and the directory's rectory line, so `address_verified` stays false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (building-precise).
