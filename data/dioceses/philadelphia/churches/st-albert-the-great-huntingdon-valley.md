## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 2) gives Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 AM and 12:00 PM, weekdays 8:00 AM; the parish site's schedule agrees and adds 'No Vigil Mass on October 31'. The directory (updated 12 months ago) agrees on the weekend times and adds Saturday 8 AM.

A Syro-Malabar mission also uses this building (GCatholic 16982, in the other-eastern batch). It is not a second church row: the building is this one church, with its Latin parish Masses listed here.
