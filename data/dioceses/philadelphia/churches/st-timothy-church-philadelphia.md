## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin: Saturday 4:00 pm vigil, Sunday 8:00 and 11:30 am, daily Tuesday to Saturday 8:30 am, Holy Days 8:30 am and 6:00 pm; Chinese Mass 3 pm on the first Sunday. The directory's times (6:30 AM, 9:15 AM) are older.

## Name and address

The bulletin and directory give the parish office as 3001 Levick Street; the church stands at Levick and Battersby Streets (directory, GCatholic). `address_verified` stays false because no source gives the church's own street number, only the office's; the pin is GCatholic's Plus Code. The upper and lower church are one building.
