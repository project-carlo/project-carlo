## Holds Mass

Yes. The current bulletin (24-25 October 2026) names Holy Trinity Church among its Worship Sites and holds the Saturday 4:00 PM vigil and Monday-Thursday 8:00 AM Masses there. This answers the directory-lead question: the former Holy Trinity (Polish) church still holds Mass. It is not the Holy Trinity, Philadelphia (6th and Spruce) that was relegated in 2023; this is a different building.

## Name and address

Bulletin: Holy Trinity Church, 219 Dayton Street; the (stale) directory page says 217 Dayton St.; the parish's own bulletin also puts the Parish Center at 219 Dayton St. and the Young Adult Holy Hour at "212 Dayton St." (which is St. Mary's rectory). `address_verified` is false because the sources disagree on the number; the coordinates are OSM's Holy Trinity Church node on Dayton Street.
