## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Mass box lists St. Monica Saturday 8:30 AM and 5 PM, Sunday 8:30, 10 and 11:30 AM, weekdays 6:30 AM (during school year) and 8:30 AM.

## Name and address

The name agrees in the directory, GCatholic and the bulletin. The address follows the bulletin masthead (2422 South 17th Street, also the directory's rectory address); GCatholic gives only the corner of 17th and Ritner and OSM puts the church at 1700 Ritner Street, so the street number is not confirmed by two independent sources and `address_verified` stays false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87F6WRCF+FP).
