## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, image, rendered) lists Rosemont Chapel Saturday 9:00am, Vigil Mass 4:30pm and Sunday 9:00am; its Daily Mass at 12:05pm Mon-Fri is probably here too (Our Mother of Good Counsel's bulletin lists 'Monday - Friday 12:05 P.M. & Saturday 9:00 A.M.' under 'STOV Rosemont Chapel').

## Name and address

The directory, the bulletin masthead, the parish site and GCatholic (41992) agree on 1229 E Lancaster Ave., ZIP 19010; the post town is Rosemont in the directory and bulletin, Bryn Mawr on the parish site, Villanova in GCatholic. I used Rosemont. The name 'Rosemont Chapel' comes from the directory, the bulletin and the site; GCatholic lists the place only as 'St. Thomas of Villanova'. This is the second GCatholic entry for the parish (the first, 19264, is Villanova Church on the campus). The directory's parish entry is linked here because its address is 1229 E. Lancaster. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G62MGC+GV), building-precise.
