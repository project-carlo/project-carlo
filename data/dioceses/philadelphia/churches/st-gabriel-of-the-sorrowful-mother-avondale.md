## Holds Mass

Yes, daily. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, an image, rendered) lists a Saturday 4:15 PM vigil, Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 AM, a Sensory-friendly Mass at 8:00 AM on third Sundays, weekday Mass at 9:00 AM Monday to Thursday, and Holy Day Masses at 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM. The directory's list agrees.

The ZIP disagrees: the bulletin, the John Patrick listing and the parish site say 19311 (the parish's PO Box 709 is also Avondale 19311); GCatholic says 19350. 19311 is followed because it comes from the parish itself, but `address_verified` stays false until a postal source settles it.
