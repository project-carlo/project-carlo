## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1, image) gives Masses on Sunday at 12:00 (Solemn Opening Mass of the Forty Hours) and 5:15 pm and weekday Mass at 8:00 am, and the parish Mass page (read 2026-10-28) lists a regular weekend schedule of Saturday 5:15 PM and Sunday 7:00, 9:00, 10:30 AM, 12:00 PM (Spanish) and 5:15 PM, with daily Mass at 6:30 and 8:00 AM. This is the parish's only church.

## Name and address

GCatholic, the archdiocesan directory and the parish's own pages agree on St. Agnes at 231 W. Gay St., West Chester, PA 19380 (the directory shows the rectory next door at 233 W. Gay St.).
