## What is known

St. Stanislaus (Polish), 242 Fitzwater St., closed as a parish in 2006 and became a worship site of St. Philip Neri on 1 July 2006. The parish's history page says it 'remains a place where Mass is celebrated', and the 25 October 2026 bulletin cover and mission text count it among the parish's 'two churches'. No source read gives a Mass day or time there: the Mass Times page and the directory put every Mass at St. Philip Neri.

## What would settle it

A Mass line naming St. Stanislaus in a bulletin or on the site makes it a church row under st-philip-neri-philadelphia; a closure source makes it an exclusion. The open record keeps it off the dataset until then.
