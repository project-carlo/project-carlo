## What is known

Our Lady of Fatima, Street Rd., Bensalem merged into St. Charles Borromeo on 1 July 2014 and the archdiocese's directory still calls it a worship site. In 2014 it hosted the parish's Spanish Masses (Sat 7 PM, Sun noon) and an English Sunday 10 AM. In 2026 the St. Charles bulletin and website give a single Mass schedule with no place named (Sat 8:00, 5:00, 7:00 Spanish; Sun 7:30, 10:00, 12:00 Spanish), the site speaks only of St. Charles Borromeo Church, and the bulletin masthead keeps the line 'Our Lady of Fatima Worship Site' above St. Charles's own address. The Street Road property is in use: the bulletin's Fatima Catholic Outreach Center is at 2913 Street Rd., the thrift store in the former Fatima school at 2915, and GCatholic (2025-12-21) says a Ukrainian Catholic mission shares the church building (GCatholic 17498, in the Ukrainian batch). The GCatholic address (2913 Street Rd.) is the Outreach Center's address; the directory gives 'Street Rd. and Mechanicsville Rd.'.

## Why it is not decided

No source read says Mass is celebrated here for St. Charles, none says the church closed, and the Spanish Masses are not placed at either building. A Roman Catholic Mass in the church would make it a church row of St. Charles; a Ukrainian-only liturgy would make it an `other-jurisdiction` exclusion; the parish office can settle which. The Ukrainian mission's entry was left to the Ukrainian batch.
