## What is known

St. Charles Borromeo Seminary moved from Overbrook to a new campus at 1400 Evans Road, Ambler (Lower Gwynedd Township) in August 2024; the Overbrook campus's records are in `excluded/st-charles-borromeo-seminary-wynnewood` and `excluded/immaculate-conception-chapel-wynnewood`. The new campus has three chapels, the largest being Immaculate Conception Chapel (consecrated 8 September 2024; seats more than 200). The seminary's 'Seminarian Life' page (read 2026-10-28) gives Mass there daily at 7:25 a.m., Saturday 8:00 a.m., Sunday 10:00 a.m. and a community Mass at 4:30 pm. The two Ambler-area bulletins read for this batch (St. Anthony & St. Joseph; St. Alphonsus) do not list it. No roster entry covers it: GCatholic still lists only the Overbrook campus.

## Why it is not decided

The settled rule for seminary chapels is a church row when a parish bulletin lists the public Mass, a parish row if the house publishes its own bulletin, and otherwise an exclusion as `no-public-mass` (community-only Mass) or `no-bulletin` (public Mass, no bulletin). No source read says whether the public may attend these Masses: the seminary's page describes them as the seminarians' Masses and is silent on visitors, and the only 'not open to the public' wording is for the 2024 dedication. Absence of a public-Mass statement is not a statement that Mass is private, so the place stays open. Checked: scs.edu 'Seminarian Life', its livestream-events page, its safety-and-security page, the CatholicPhilly 2024-2026 articles above and the two bulletins. Not checked: a phone call to the seminary, masstimes.org and Google listings.
