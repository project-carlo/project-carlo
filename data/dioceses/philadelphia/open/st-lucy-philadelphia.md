## What is known

St. Lucy (Italian) was a parish founded in 1927 at 140 Green La., Manayunk, merged into Holy Family on 1 July 2012; the decree said the church 'will remain open as a worship site for Holy Family Parish'. The directory still names it a worship site on Holy Family's page and on its own closed-parish page (updated about ten years ago). Holy Family's current evidence names no Mass there: the 25 October 2026 bulletin and three older 2026 issues never mention St. Lucy and print one Mass schedule, the parish's home page and directory Mass times list only Holy Family's own times, and the parish's pages have no St. Lucy history or address. The archdiocese's relegation-decree list (read 2026-10-28) has no St. Lucy decree and CatholicPhilly searches for St. Lucy, Manayunk and Green Lane found no story on it. OSM tags the Green Lane address as an apartment building.

## Why it is not decided

No source states that St. Lucy closed, so it cannot be excluded as `closed`, and no source shows a Mass there, so it cannot be a church row. The absence is from one parish's bulletins and site; the parish office (215-482-0450) or a later decree would settle it. If a decree or story dates the closing, exclude it `closed`; if a Mass is printed, add a church row under holy-family-philadelphia.
