## What is known

St. Donato (Italian national parish, 1910) merged into Our Lady of Lourdes in 2013 and its church was to remain a worship site. Lourdes' directory page still calls it one, and St. Donato's own directory page says only 'Holydays, holidays, special occasions, see parish bulletin for upcoming times'. Lourdes' current bulletin and home page list no Mass there, and the archdiocese's planning page lists no relegation decree for it. Nothing read says it has closed and nothing says Mass is held there, so it is neither a church row nor excluded. The roster entry `worship-sites:st-donato-philadelphia` and `gcatholic:19118` are this place.

## What would settle it

A Lourdes bulletin or notice announcing a Mass at St. Donato (a feast day, for example), a closure notice, or the parish office (215-473-1669).
