## What is known

St. Elizabeth Ann Seton, Park Ave., Bensalem merged into St. Thomas Aquinas, Croydon on 1 July 2014. In April 2016 CatholicPhilly said the church 'still functions as a worship site' with the pastor saying the 9 a.m. Sunday Mass there, in addition to the weekend Masses at St. Thomas. In October 2026 the parish's bulletin and site give one address (601 Bristol Pike) and one Mass schedule, Sat 4:30 PM and Sun 9:00 and 11:00 AM, with no place named except for the weekday Mass in the chapel at 126 Walnut Ave.; Seton appears only in the masthead's merger line. The directory's page and GCatholic still list the church, and no relegation decree for it appears in the archdiocese's list (diocese.md).

## Why it is not decided

Absence of Park Avenue from the schedule is weak, because a schedule with no places could still mean the Sunday 9:00 AM Mass is at Seton, as in 2016. No source says the church closed, and none places a Mass there now, so neither a church row nor a closed exclusion is supported. The parish office can settle it.
