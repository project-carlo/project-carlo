## What is known

St. Margaret Mary Alacoque, Essington merged into St. Gabriel, Norwood in 2014. The directory's closed-parish page (about ten years old) calls it a worship site of St. Gabriel and GCatholic still lists the church, but St. Gabriel's current directory page, its 25 October 2026 bulletin and its website's Mass page give one church only. The archdiocese's relegation decree list (diocese.md) has no entry for it, and CatholicPhilly's search finds no closure report. The parish home page still refers to a 'Margaret Mary Parking Lot', so the property is in parish use.

## Why it is not decided

No source says it is closed and none lists a Mass, so neither a church row nor a 'closed' exclusion is supported. The next step is a later St. Gabriel bulletin or a call to the parish office.
