## What is known

St. Joachim parish (1845-2013) merged into Holy Innocents in July 2013. The Mater Dolorosa decree (2016) names 'Saint Joachim Church' among Holy Innocents' churches, but the parish's current Mass Schedule, its 25 October 2026 bulletin and its history page list worship at the parish church and St. Joan of Arc only, and its St. Joachim page covers only the cemetery behind the church (records kept at Holy Innocents). OSM and GCatholic still show a church building at Church and Griscom Sts. CatholicPhilly announced a one-off healing Mass with adoration and a concert there on 29 August 2026, with no parish named.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing says the church is closed or relegated (no decree on the parish-planning page), and nothing puts a regular Mass there: the one 2026 Mass is a single event whose organizer is unnamed. Under the diocese rule on worship sites of merged parishes a church row needs a bulletin or schedule listing Mass there, and a closure needs a source that states it, so no row or exclusion is written. The parish's own silence is evidence it is not a worship site, not that the church is closed.
