## What is known

The directory lists 'Sacred Heart of Jesus' as an open parish but gives it no pastor, church address, Mass times, website or deanery, and its page sends the reader to St. Mark, Bristol for the spiritual records. It carries rectory phone 215-788-2128, which GCatholic gives St. Ann, Bristol. St. Mark's directory page names 'Sacred Heart of Jesus Church, Tullytown' an 'Additional Worship Site' but lists Mass only at St. Mark and the St. Ann Worship Site. So it is not a parish with a bulletin of its own: no John Patrick listing, no site and no pastor were found, and no row is made for it. GCatholic has a church at 610 Main St., Tullytown with no phone or Mass; OSM finds only a house at that number. St. Mark's 25 October 2026 bulletin and the earlier issues read, and five text-layer St. Michael the Archangel issues (its neighbour, 500 m from GCatholic's pin), mention no Mass or building at Tullytown. CatholicPhilly has no report and the archdiocese's relegation list has no decree for it.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing read says Mass is held there, so no church row is supported, and nothing says it closed, so a 'closed' exclusion is not supported either. The directory's 'Additional Worship Site' line is a lead, not evidence of current Mass (playbook rule), and its page is two years old. The building's address is also unconfirmed: GCatholic 610 Main St. (pin about 300 m from OSM's 610 Main St.), Church Finder 357 Dorrance St., Bristol. The next step is the St. Mark parish office or a later bulletin.
