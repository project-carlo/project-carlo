## What is known

All Saints parish (Bridesburg, 1860-2013) merged into St. John Cantius in 2013. Its directory page says 'Formerly located at Buckius and E. Thompson Sts.' and sends records to St. John Cantius. GCatholic still lists the church. St. John Cantius's current bulletin and website list Mass only at its own church and chapel (Thompson St. above Orthodox St., 410 m away) and never mention All Saints as a worship site. An undated aggregator calls the church closed.

## Why it is not decided

Every source says the parish ended; none says the building closed or gives a last Mass, and the only 'closed' text is a third-party listing derived from the parish label. Under the diocese rule on worship sites of merged parishes, a church row needs a bulletin or schedule that lists Mass there, and a closure needs a source that states it. Neither exists yet, so no exclusion is written. The St. John Cantius bulletin's silence is evidence that the church is not a worship site of that parish, but not that it closed.
