## Why it is excluded

St. Mary (Ruthenian), 88 Gap Rd., Coatesville is a Byzantine (Ruthenian) Catholic church of the Eparchy of Passaic, not of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Philadelphia. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives 'Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic' and 'Rite: Ruthenian (Byzantine)'; the eparchy's own parish list and the church's site (read 2026-10-29) carry it as 'Blessed Virgin Mary Byzantine Catholic Church' at 88 Gap Road, so GCatholic's 'St. Mary' is a naming artefact (the Latin St. Joseph, Coatesville is 640 m away and a different church). It is in this diocese's roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the archdiocese's directory lists none. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs to its own eparchy's dataset, not this archdiocese's.
