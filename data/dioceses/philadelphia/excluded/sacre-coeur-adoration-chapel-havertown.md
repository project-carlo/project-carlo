## Why it is excluded

The Sacre Coeur Perpetual Adoration Chapel, Havertown (dedicated 2026-06-12) is **St. Denis's, not Sacred Heart's**. The batch notes had it 'attached to Sacred Heart by its name only'. GCatholic says 'Mission of: Church of St. Denis' and pins it 110 m from St. Denis (2.2 km from Sacred Heart); CatholicPhilly (2026-06-05) places it 'behind St. Denis Church', at the edge of its cemetery, built by parishioners of St. John Neumann on a former maintenance-building site, and says the French name was chosen 'to help distinguish the chapel from nearby Sacred Heart Parish'. The related parish is `st-denis-havertown`.

It is a 30-seat chapel for perpetual adoration, not a place with public Mass: the dedication Mass was in St. Denis's church, then a procession to the chapel for benediction and exposition; no source describes or schedules Mass inside it, and St. Denis's 25 October 2026 bulletin schedule lists Masses only at the parish church. So no church row; `no-public-mass`. It has no street address of its own in any source (GCatholic: 'Havertown, Pennsylvania 19083').

## What would change this

A St. Denis bulletin or the chapel's own page listing a regular Mass in the chapel would make it a church row under `st-denis-havertown`. The bulletin was read on pages 1-2 only (no text layer), so an announcement on a later page was not looked for.
