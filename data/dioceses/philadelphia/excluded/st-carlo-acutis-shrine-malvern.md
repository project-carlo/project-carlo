## Why it is excluded

The shrine at Malvern Retreat House (315 S. Warren Avenue, Malvern) is open to the public and holds a public Mass on the first Friday (7:30am) and a First Saturday Youth Mass (9:00-11:00am) each month, per the retreat house's shrine page and its November event page (read 2026-10-28). That is public Mass at a place, but nothing lists it: St. Patrick, Malvern's 25 October 2026 bulletin never names the retreat house, the retreat house publishes no bulletin that was found (a newsletter and 'Malvern Minute' only; no listing in the John Patrick scan), and GCatholic gives no address. By the diocese's settled rule for retreat-house shrines it is excluded `no-bulletin`: open, public Mass, no bulletin that lists it. It is not a St. Patrick site: the parish's schedule is St. Patrick Church alone.

## What would change this

A bulletin that lists the shrine's Masses (the retreat house's own or St. Patrick's), or a weekly Mass there. The monthly Masses and the address above are what a row would use. GCatholic's pin (87G62FJW+G2) is at the retreat house's campus, about 1.5 km from St. Patrick Church.
