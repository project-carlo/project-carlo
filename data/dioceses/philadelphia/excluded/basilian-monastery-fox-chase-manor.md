## Why it is excluded

Basilian Monastery (Sisters of the Order of St. Basil the Great), 710 Fox Chase Rd., Fox Chase Manor is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Philadelphia. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives 'Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia' and 'Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)'; the archeparchy's own parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-29) carries it as 'Sisters of the Order of Saint Basil the Great 710 Fox Chase Road'. It is in this diocese's roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the archdiocese's directory lists none. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`. The archeparchy lists it as the Sisters of the Order of Saint Basil the Great, 710 Fox Chase Road, with a daily Divine Liturgy at 8:00 AM; its Chapel of the Holy Trinity is a community chapel of a Ukrainian religious province, and no Roman Catholic bulletin lists Mass there.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: a Ukrainian Catholic church belongs to the Archeparchy of Philadelphia's own dataset, not to this archdiocese's.
