## Why it is excluded

**St. Cyril of Alexandria, 153 Penn Blvd, East Lansdowne: closed, no regular Mass since Christmas 2018, relegated effective 2021-01-11.** The archbishop's decree (dated 2 December 2020, effective 11 January 2021) says the church 'has not been used as a regular alternate church for worship for Saint Philomena Parish since Christmas 2018' and that since then it has held only services for the children of its mission school, three in the last year. St. Philomena Parish (merged with St. Cyril 2013) now lists only St. Philomena Church in its 25 October 2026 bulletin. `last_mass` is `2018-12` because the decree gives Christmas 2018 as the last regular use and no day; the building's street address comes from ParishesOnline's organization record, the decree names no street.

## What would change this

A St. Philomena bulletin listing a Mass at East Lansdowne, or a notice that the building was reopened. The directory merely notes 'Merged with St. Cyril of Alexandria, East Lansdowne: 2013' on St. Philomena's page and GCatholic still lists the church; both lag the decree.
