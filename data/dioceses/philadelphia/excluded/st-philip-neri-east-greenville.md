## Why it is excluded

The original St. Philip Neri Church, East Greenville (built 1919) is closed. The archdiocese's decree of 9 December 2022 (effective 23 January 2023) states that it 'has not been used for divine worship of any kind since 1968', the year the new parish church was built in Pennsburg, and relegates it to profane but not sordid use. So the decree itself gives 1968 as the last use for worship; the parish's current bulletin lists no Mass there. The church's street address is not given in the decree; the directory lists the parish's Religious Education Center and Catholic Family Life Center at 26 E. 6th St., East Greenville, on the same campus, which hold no Mass. No roster lists this church (GCatholic's 19179 is the Pennsburg church, with the 1919 date of this one). The batch note recorded it as relegated in 2023; the decree dates that to 23 January 2023.

## What would change this

A bulletin or archdiocesan notice announcing public Mass at the East Greenville campus.
