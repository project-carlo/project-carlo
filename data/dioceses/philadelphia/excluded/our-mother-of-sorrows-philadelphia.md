## Why it is excluded

Our Mother of Sorrows (48th St. and Lancaster Ave., West Philadelphia) merged into St. Ignatius of Loyola in 2013 and stayed a worship site. CatholicPhilly's story of 12 September 2017 says the church 'has been formally closed by the Archdiocese of Philadelphia, effective October 9, 2017', and relegated to profane use. `last_mass` is that effective date, per the diocese's rule when no bulletin dates the last Mass. St. Ignatius's 25 October 2026 bulletin names no Mass there (see its parish record).

The church is in no roster; this record answers the St. Ignatius directory page's 'Merged with Our Mother of Sorrows: 2013' line. No street number was read.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a reopening; a relegated church does not return to worship without a new decree.
