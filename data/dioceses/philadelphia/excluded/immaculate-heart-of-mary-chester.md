## Why excluded

Immaculate Heart of Mary, Chester, a church of St. Katharine Drexel Parish since the parish's suppression in 1993, was relegated to profane but not sordid use by decree effective 1 March 2024. It is in no roster (neither the directory's open list nor GCatholic), so this record is the only place it is accounted for. `last_mass` is the decree's effective date; no source read gives the final Mass. The address is the directory's 'Formerly located at 2nd and Norris Sts.' and carries no ZIP.
