## Why it is excluded

St. Madeleine Sophie Church (Greene St. & Upsal St., 19119) was relegated to profane but not sordid use by decree of Archbishop Chaput dated 7 December 2016, effective 11 January 2017 (page 2 of the signed decree, scanned and read from a render). It had been a worship site of Holy Cross since the July 2013 merger. The current Holy Cross bulletin (25 October 2026) lists Mass at one church only. `last_mass` is 2017-01: the decree gives no last-Mass date, so this is an upper bound from the effective date. GCatholic still lists the church; its phone is Holy Cross's.

## What would change this

A Holy Cross bulletin or the archdiocese announcing Mass in the building again, or a sale and conversion report that gives a different last-Mass date.
