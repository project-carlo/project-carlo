## Why it is excluded

Sts. Peter and Paul (Ukrainian), 301 Fairview St., Phoenixville is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Philadelphia. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives 'Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia' and 'Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)'; the archeparchy's own parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-29) carries it as 'Sts. Peter and Paul Ukrainian Catholic Church, Phoenixville 301 Fairview St, Phoenixville, PA 19460'. It is in this diocese's roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the archdiocese's directory lists none. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`. GCatholic's address (472 Emmett St.) is the one the archeparchy's page gives for the pastor, Rev. Ihor Royik; the church is at 301 Fairview St. (also shown on the parish's own site). Rev. Royik also serves St. Michael, Pottstown.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: a Ukrainian Catholic church belongs to the Archeparchy of Philadelphia's own dataset, not to this archdiocese's.
