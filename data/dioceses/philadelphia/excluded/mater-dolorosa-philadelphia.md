## Why it is excluded

**Mater Dolorosa Church, Paul and Ruan Sts. (1676 Ruan St.), Philadelphia 19124: closed, relegated effective 2016-09-16.** The parish merged into Holy Innocents in July 2013 and its church stayed a worship site until the Archbishop's decree of 12 August 2016 (a scan, read from renders), which relegates it to profane but not sordid use effective 16 September 2016 because Holy Innocents no longer needed it. Holy Innocents' parish site says the church 'was closed in 2016', and its 25 October 2026 bulletin lists no Mass there.

`last_mass` is the decree's effective date, 2016-09-16, as the diocese rules settle when no bulletin gives a date; the decree states no last Mass, so the real one may be earlier. GCatholic still lists the church without a closure.

## What would change this

A current Holy Innocents bulletin listing a Mass at the old church (a relegated building is not necessarily gone, but one-off services would not make it a worship site).
