## Why it is excluded

**Immaculate Conception Church, Front and Allen Sts. (directory: 1020 N. Front St.), Philadelphia: closed, relegated effective 2021-01-11.** The parish (1869-2011) merged into St. Michael in 2011 and the church stayed a worship site; the decree of 2 December 2020 (a scan, read from renders) says Mass was suspended in winter 2017 and all use of the building as a church stopped in August 2018, and relegates it to profane but not sordid use effective 11 January 2021. St. Michael's current bulletin lists no Mass there.

`last_mass` is the decree's effective date, as the diocese's rules settle when no bulletin gives one; the decree itself shows no Mass since 2018, probably earlier. The batch note asked which Immaculate Conception the 2020 relegation was: it is this church (Front and Allen Sts.), the one the directory gives as St. Michael's worship site.

## What would change this

A St. Michael bulletin listing a Mass at the old church.
