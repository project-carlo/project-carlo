## Why it is excluded

**Holy Trinity Church, 6th & Spruce Sts.: closed; relegated to profane but not sordid use effective 2023-01-23.** The church had merged into Old St. Mary Parish in 2009, was 'little used' for the decade before 2019, and the 2022 decree (CatholicPhilly, 2022-12-12) says it 'will no longer serve as a worship site of Old Saint Mary Parish and will close as a Roman Catholic church'. GCatholic lists it as a Former Church. Old St. Mary's current bulletin lists no Mass there.

**Why the name lingers.** Old St. Mary's John Patrick listing (167) is still called 'Old Saint Mary Church & Holy Trinity Church', and the bulletin masthead keeps a Holy Trinity blurb; that is a stale account name, not a worship site. See `parishes/old-st-marys-philadelphia.md`.

`last_mass` is the decree's effective date (the settled rule when no source gives a last Mass); the real last regular Mass was earlier, probably before the 2019 first decree, and no source read dates it.

## What would change this

An Old St. Mary's bulletin or an archdiocesan notice listing a Mass at 6th & Spruce after 2023-01-23.
