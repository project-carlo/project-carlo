## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Perpetual Help (Ruthenian), 1787 Woodburne Rd., Levittown is a Byzantine (Ruthenian) Catholic church of the Eparchy of Passaic, not of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Philadelphia. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives 'Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic' and 'Rite: Ruthenian (Byzantine)'; the eparchy's own parish list (read 2026-10-29) lists it under its Pennsylvania parishes with Olphbyzpa.org as its site. Do not confuse it with the Latin Immaculate Conception, Levittown (relegated 2015) or Queen of the Universe, Levittown, 1.3 km away. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs to its own eparchy's dataset, not this archdiocese's.
