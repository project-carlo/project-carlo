## Why it is excluded

Annunciation of the Blessed Virgin Mary (Ukrainian), 1206 Valley Rd., Melrose Park is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Philadelphia. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives 'Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia' and 'Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)'; the archeparchy's own parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-29) carries it as 'Annunciation of the Blessed Virgin Mary Ukrainian Catholic Church Melrose Park 1206 Valley Rd, Melrose Park, PA 19027'. It is in this diocese's roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the archdiocese's directory lists none. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: a Ukrainian Catholic church belongs to the Archeparchy of Philadelphia's own dataset, not to this archdiocese's.
