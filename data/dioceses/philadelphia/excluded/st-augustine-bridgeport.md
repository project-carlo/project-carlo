## Why it is excluded

St. Augustine Church (464 Ford Street per GCatholic, 'Ford St.' per the directory), Bridgeport, merged into Sacred Heart in July 2014 and served as a worship site until December 2015. The relegation decree (given 7 December 2016, effective 11 January 2017) states that Sunday Masses were discontinued there in January 2015, funerals stopped in December 2015 and 'no divine worship of any kind' has taken place since, because of mold. `last_mass` is therefore 2015-12 (the last funerals; the last Sunday Mass was in 2014). Sacred Heart's current bulletin and Mass page list only its own church. The directory's closed page and Sacred Heart's directory entry still tie the two together; they lag the decree. The cemetery named St. Augustine, which Sacred Heart runs, is a cemetery and not a worship site.

## What would change this

A current Sacred Heart bulletin listing Mass at Ford Street.
