## Why it is excluded

Christ the King (Ukrainian), 1629 W Cayuga St., Philadelphia is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Philadelphia. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives 'Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia' and 'Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)'; the archeparchy's own parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-29) carries it as 'Christ the King Ukrainian Catholic Church, Philadelphia 1629 W Cayuga St, Philadelphia, PA 19140'. It is in this diocese's roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the archdiocese's directory lists none. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`. The archeparchy gives 1629 W Cayuga St. where GCatholic has 1623. It is not the archdiocese's Christ the King parish, which is a separate Roman Catholic parish with its own record.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: a Ukrainian Catholic church belongs to the Archeparchy of Philadelphia's own dataset, not to this archdiocese's.
