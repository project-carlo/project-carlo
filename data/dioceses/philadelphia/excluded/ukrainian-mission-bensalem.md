## Why it is excluded

Ukrainian Catholic Mission, Bensalem: GCatholic 17498 (read 2026-10-29) lists a Ukrainian Catholic mission 'Using the building: Church of Our Lady of Fatima, Bensalem', address 2913 Street Rd. It has no name of its own, and the archeparchy's parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-29) has no Bensalem entry. A mission of the Archeparchy of Philadelphia is other-jurisdiction whatever building it uses. **This record is not a worship site of Our Lady of Fatima or of St. Charles Borromeo, Bensalem:** whether Roman Catholic Mass is celebrated in the Fatima building is a separate open question in `open/our-lady-of-fatima-bensalem.md`, and this exclusion must not be taken as evidence on it either way.

## What would change this

Nothing expected for the Roman archdiocese. An archeparchy listing or a Ukrainian Catholic bulletin would only say whether the community still exists, not change its jurisdiction.
