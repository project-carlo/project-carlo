## Why it is excluded

GCatholic's 'St. Michael the Archangel' at E Mount Airy Ave. & Chew Ave. is an Anglican Personal Ordinariate congregation (Jurisdiction: Anglican Personal Ordinariate of The Chair of Saint Peter) listed as 'using the building' of Holy Cross, Mount Airy. It is not a separate building: its address is Holy Cross's own corner. Under this diocese's rule that Ordinariate churches are `other-jurisdiction` (see Our Lady of Mount Carmel, Bridgeport), it is excluded. A second, independent reason: CatholicPhilly (2016-12-15) says St. Michael the Archangel merged with Blessed John Henry Newman, Wayne, to form St. John the Baptist, which worships in Bridgeport, and GCatholic's website for it, ordinariatephiladelphia.org, no longer resolves. I found no source for any current Mass of this congregation in Mount Airy; Holy Cross's bulletin lists none. That is absence from one bulletin and one dead website, so the record rests on the Ordinariate rule and the 2016 merger report, not on a closure statement.

## What would change this

A Holy Cross bulletin or Ordinariate site listing a public Mass at Holy Cross by St. Michael's community, and a decision to cover Ordinariate parishes.
