## Why it is excluded

Sts. Peter and Paul (Ukrainian), 519 Union Ave., Bridgeport is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Philadelphia. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives 'Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia' and 'Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)'; the archeparchy's own parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-29) carries it as 'Sts. Peter and Paul Church, Bridgeport P.O. Box 126, 519 Union Ave, Bridgeport, PA 19405'. It is in this diocese's roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the archdiocese's directory lists none. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: a Ukrainian Catholic church belongs to the Archeparchy of Philadelphia's own dataset, not to this archdiocese's.
