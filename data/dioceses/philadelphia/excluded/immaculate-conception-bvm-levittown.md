## Why excluded

Immaculate Conception B.V.M., Levittown closed: Archbishop Chaput's decree of 2 December 2015 relegated the church to profane but not sordid use effective 1 January 2016, after it had been a worship site of Queen of the Universe since the 2014 merger. `last_mass` is the decree's effective date; no source read gives the final Mass. Queen of the Universe's current bulletin lists Mass at its own church only, so the building is not a worship site of the row `queen-of-the-universe-levittown`. GCatholic's listing is stale. The ZIP 19057 is GCatholic's; the directory's closed page says 5201 'Bristol Emilie Rd.'.

## What would change this

A current Queen of the Universe bulletin that lists Mass at Emilie Road, or a later decree reversing the relegation (none seen in the archdiocese's parish-planning list).
