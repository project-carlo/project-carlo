## Why it is excluded

St. Therese of the Child Jesus Church (E. Upsal St. & Ardleigh St., 19119) was relegated to profane but not sordid use by decree dated 7 December 2016, effective 11 January 2017 (the second decree in the PDF shared with St. Madeleine Sophie, read from a render). It merged into Holy Cross in July 2013 and was a Holy Cross worship site until then. Holy Cross's current bulletin lists one church. `last_mass` is 2017-01, an upper bound from the effective date, because the decree gives none.

## What would change this

A bulletin listing Mass there, or a dated closing report that gives an earlier last Mass.
