## Why it is excluded

Holy Myrrhbearers (900 Fairview Road, Swarthmore) is a parish of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia: the archeparchy's own parish page (read 2026-10-29) lists it with its pastor and a daily Divine Liturgy schedule. Eastern Catholic churches are excluded `other-jurisdiction` under this diocese's settled rule. It is in no roster (neither the directory nor GCatholic lists it); the record exists because the darby-morton batch found it next door to Notre Dame de Lourdes (1000 Fairview Rd., church `notre-dame-de-lourdes-swarthmore` under Our Lady of Peace).

Whether it worships in a building of its own or in a Latin building was not established; either way it is not a worship site of Our Lady of Peace, whose bulletin lists only Latin Masses, and Notre Dame de Lourdes keeps its own church row.

## What would change this

Nothing in this diocese's data: Ukrainian Catholic churches belong to the archeparchy.
