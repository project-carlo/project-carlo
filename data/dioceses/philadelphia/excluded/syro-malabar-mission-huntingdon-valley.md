## Why it is excluded

A Syro-Malabar mission (GCatholic 16982, read 2026-10-29: 'Using the building: Church of St. Albert the Great, Huntingdon Valley', 'History: 1999') meets in St. Albert the Great's church, 212 Welsh Rd. A mission of the Syro-Malabar Eparchy of Chicago is other-jurisdiction whatever building it uses. **This record is not a second church row for the building:** St. Albert the Great keeps its own row (`churches/st-albert-the-great-huntingdon-valley`), whose bulletin lists only Latin Masses, and this exclusion must not be read as evidence about them. The eparchy's directory does not list the mission (it may have been merged into St. Thomas, Philadelphia, 1.3 km away); GCatholic's entry, last updated 2025.12.21, is the only source.

## What would change this

Nothing expected for the Roman archdiocese. An eparchy listing would only say whether the mission still exists.
