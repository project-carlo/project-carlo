## Why excluded

St. Joseph the Worker, Fallsington closed: Archbishop Chaput's decree (29 September 2015) relegated the church to profane but not sordid use effective 1 November 2015, after it had been a worship site of St. Frances Cabrini since 1 July 2014. `last_mass` is the decree's effective date; no source read gives the final Mass. Cabrini's current bulletin and site list Mass only at the Fairless Hills church, so the building is not a worship site of the row `st-frances-cabrini-fairless-hills`. GCatholic's listing is a stale entry.
