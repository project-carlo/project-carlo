## Why it is excluded

**St. Leo Church, Keystone and Unruh Sts., Philadelphia 19135: closed, relegated effective 2019-01-07.** The directory still names it 'Worship Site: St. Leo Church' on Our Lady of Consolation's page (merged 2013), and GCatholic still lists it. The Archbishop's decree of 29 November 2018 (a scan, read from renders) says the church 'has not been used as a regular church for worship since January 1, 2018', gives the few uses in 2018, and relegates it to profane but not sordid use effective 7 January 2019. Our Lady of Consolation's 25 October 2026 bulletin lists no Mass there.

`last_mass` is the decree's effective date, 2019-01-07, as the diocese rules settle when no bulletin gives a date. No Mass is dated: the decree says regular worship had stopped by 1 January 2018 and mentions only funerals and a renewal of vows after that, so the true last Mass was probably earlier, in 2017 or before. The directory page's Mass times (page updated 10 years ago) are stale.

## What would change this

A current Our Lady of Consolation bulletin listing a Mass at St. Leo (the building was relegated, not necessarily demolished; one-off services would not make it a worship site).
