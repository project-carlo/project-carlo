## Why it is excluded

St. Cecilia Church (6th and Chestnut Sts., Coatesville) was relegated to profane but not sordid use by a decree given 12 August 2016, effective 16 September 2016 (page 2 of the decree). The decree says it had been a worship site of Our Lady of the Rosary since 2013, that it was no longer needed, and that the only recent use was a funeral liturgy. The directory's closed-parish page still calls it a 'Worship site of Our Lady of the Rosary Church', but that page is six years old and lags the decree. Our Lady of the Rosary's 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Mass at its own church only. `last_mass` is the decree's effective date, the closure date available; no bulletin dating a last Mass was read, so the real last Mass was on or before that day.

## What would change this

A current bulletin or schedule listing Mass at 6th and Chestnut Sts. would reopen it, as a church row under whichever parish publishes it.
