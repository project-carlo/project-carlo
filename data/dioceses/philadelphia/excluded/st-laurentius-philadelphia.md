## Why it is excluded

**St. Laurentius (Polish) Church, Memphis and E. Berks Sts., Philadelphia 19125: closed since March 2014.** The parish merged into Holy Name of Jesus in 2013 and the church stayed a worship site until the archdiocese closed it on 28 March 2014 as in danger of collapse; all parish Masses moved to Holy Name. The archdiocese announced its demolition in March 2015, a court fight followed, and the building was sold in January 2020. Holy Name's current bulletin and website list no Mass there.

`last_mass` is the date of the closure announcement, 2014-03-28 (it says 'closed immediately'; the real last Mass was that Sunday or earlier). The archdiocese's planning page, which lists relegation decrees from 2015, has none for this church, so the closure rests on CatholicPhilly. The directory page and GCatholic both lag it.

## What would change this

A Holy Name bulletin or the owner's reuse listing a regular Mass at the old church; none is plausible.
