## Why it is excluded

**St. John of the Cross, 2741 Woodland Rd., Roslyn: closed, relegated to profane but not sordid use effective 2016-09-16.** The archdiocese's decree of 12 August 2016 (scanned; rendered and read 2026-10-29) says the church 'has served as a worship site of Queen of Peace Parish, Ardsley since 2014', 'is no longer needed by the parish' and 'is relegated to profane but not sordid use' as of 16 September 2016. No bulletin gives a last Mass, so `last_mass` is the decree's effective date. Queen of Peace's current (25 October 2026) bulletin and directory page list Mass only at 820 North Hills Avenue, Ardsley, and its text never mentions Roslyn, John of the Cross or Woodland Road. GCatholic still lists the church (19308); that is a stale list, not a worship site. OSM's only nearby feature is 'Saint John of the Cross School', Woodland Road (a school, 40.13483, -75.14017), not a church.

## What would change this

A Queen of Peace or Archdiocese notice that Mass has resumed at Woodland Road, or a current bulletin naming a Roslyn site in its Mass schedule.
