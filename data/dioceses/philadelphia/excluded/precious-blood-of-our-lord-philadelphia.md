## Why it is excluded

Most Precious Blood of Our Lord, 2800 Diamond St., closed with the 1993 North Philadelphia mergers. CatholicPhilly (11 May 2018): 'Most Precious Blood, founded in 1907 also merged into St. Martin De Porres.' GCatholic lists the church as a 'Former Church', '1907; closed 1993'. The directory's closed entry gives 'Formerly located at 28th and Diamond Sts.' and sends records to St. Martin de Porres, whose current bulletin (25 October 2026) lists Masses at its own church and chapel and at the Temple Newman Center, none at Diamond St.

The year 1993 is the parish's closure; neither source gives a month or a last Mass, and no decree of relegation exists for it (the archdiocese's list starts in 2015). Treated as closed because two sources date the closure and no current schedule lists the place.

## What would change this

A bulletin or site listing Mass at 2800 Diamond St.
