## Why it is excluded

**Holy Spirit Church, Sharon Ave. & School St., Sharon Hill: relegated to profane but not sordid use effective 2021-11-15.** The directory's closed-parish page for Holy Spirit still says 'Worship site of St. George Church, Glenolden' (a page about ten years old), and GCatholic lists the church without a note. The Archbishop's decree of 30 September 2021, linked from the archdiocese's parish-planning page, relegated the church effective 15 November 2021 so that all parishioners would worship at St. George; the current St. George bulletin (25 October 2026) lists no Mass there, and St. George's own directory page now names no worship site. The decree gives no last-Mass date, so `last_mass` is the effective date.

## What would change this

A St. George bulletin or archdiocesan notice listing a Mass at Holy Spirit after 2021. Not searched: a CatholicPhilly report of the last Mass (a 2026-10-28 REST search for 'Holy Spirit Sharon Hill' found only an obituary of its pastor emeritus).
