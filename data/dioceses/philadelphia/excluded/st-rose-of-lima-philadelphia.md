## Why it is excluded

**St. Rose of Lima Church, 1535 N. 59th St.: closed; relegated to profane but not sordid use effective 2019-09-03.** The Archbishop's decree of 25 July 2019 says the parish merged into St. Barbara on 1 July 2013, that the church had not been used as a regular church for worship since October 2013 (monthly school Masses only), and relegates it. GCatholic lists it as a Former Church. St. Barbara's current bulletin names Masses at St. Barbara only.

`last_mass` is the decree's effective date, as the settled rule has it when no source gives a last Mass; the decree says regular worship ended in October 2013 and only monthly school Masses followed, so the real last Mass is earlier than 2019-09-03 and undated by anything read. The directory's closed-parish page for St. Rose of Lima (1921-2013) says 'Formerly located at 1535 N. 59th St.'; that is its address line, not a closure statement.

## What would change this

A St. Barbara bulletin or archdiocesan notice listing a Mass at the old church after 2019.
