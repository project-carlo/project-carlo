## Why it is excluded

GCatholic's St. Charles Borromeo Seminary entry is the Overbrook campus in Wynnewood. The seminary left it: CatholicPhilly reports that 'after 153 years at its Wynnewood location' the seminary opened its 2024-25 year on 21 August 2024 at a new campus in Lower Gwynedd (1400 Evans Road, Ambler), and that the Overbrook campus was sold to Main Line Health in 2019. The seminary's website now gives only the Ambler address. No source gives a day for the last Mass at Overbrook, so `last_mass` is the year of the move (2024) and means 'no later than August 2024'. No parish bulletin read here lists a Mass at the old campus (St. Matthias's 25 October 2026 bulletin mentions the seminary only as an appeal weekend).

## What would change this

The seminary's chapels at the new Lower Gwynedd campus (three, per CatholicPhilly; one is Immaculate Conception) are in a different batch's territory and in no roster entry; see the handoff lead. Whether they hold public Mass is not decided here.
