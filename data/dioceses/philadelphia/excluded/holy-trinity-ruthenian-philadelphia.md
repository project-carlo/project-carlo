## Why it is excluded

Holy Trinity (Ruthenian), 6801 N 10th St., Philadelphia is a Byzantine (Ruthenian) Catholic church of the Eparchy of Passaic, not of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Philadelphia. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives 'Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic'; the eparchy's list gives the same address. **It is not the Latin Holy Trinity, 6th and Spruce Sts. (Old City, relegated from 2023-01-23, `excluded/holy-trinity-philadelphia`)**, although `coverage --link` attached `gcatholic:17229` to that record by name: this exclusion is the one that owns the roster id. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`.
