## Why it is excluded

St. Callistus Church, Overbrook, merged into Our Lady of Lourdes in 2013 and was kept as a worship site. CatholicPhilly's story of 30 September 2014 says it and St. Laurentius 'officially will close Wednesday, Oct. 1', after a year as worship sites 'with limited use for liturgies', and that the church 'faces a dangerous mold problem'. `last_mass` is the closing date the story gives (2014-10-01), following the diocese's rule of using the effective date when no bulletin dates the last Mass; the last Mass itself was on or before that day. Our Lady of Lourdes's 25 October 2026 bulletin and site name no Mass there (see its parish record).

The church is in no roster (the directory and GCatholic list neither it nor its parish). The record exists so the next researcher doesn't reopen it from the Lourdes directory page's 'Merged with St. Callistus' line.

**Address disagreement:** the 2013 story gives '6700 Lansdowne Ave.', the 2014 story '6700 Lancaster Avenue'. The street was not checked on a map; Lansdowne is used here because the 2013 story's form matches the parish's historic location at 67th and Lansdowne, which is an inference.

## What would change this

A current Lourdes bulletin or other source listing Mass at St. Callistus.
