## Why it is excluded

St. Mary of the Assumption (German), 172 Conarroe St., Manayunk, merged into St. John the Baptist Parish in July 2012 and, per CatholicPhilly on 26 October 2015, 'will officially close Nov. 1' of that year, ceasing to be a worship site. St. John's 25 October 2026 bulletin lists no Mass there, and the directory's page for the old parish is a closed-parish page with records at St. John's. The last Mass is not given; `last_mass` records the month of the closing date.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule printing a Mass at 172 Conarroe St. A decree of relegation, which the archdiocese's list (starting 2015) does not show for this church, would date it better.
