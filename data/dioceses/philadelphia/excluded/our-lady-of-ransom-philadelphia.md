## Why it is excluded

**Our Lady of Ransom Church, Roosevelt Blvd. & Unruh St.: closed; relegated to profane but not sordid use effective 2019-09-03.** The Archbishop's decree (25 July 2019) says the parish merged into Resurrection of Our Lord on 19 June 2017 and the church has not been a regular church for worship since, with one funeral in summer 2017. GCatholic lists it as a Former Church. Resurrection's current bulletin lists Masses at its own church only.

`last_mass` is the decree's effective date, as the diocese's settled rule has it when no bulletin gives a last Mass; the decree itself says regular worship ended with the 2017 merger, so the real last Mass was earlier (probably summer 2017, not dated by any source read).

## What would change this

A Resurrection bulletin or archdiocesan notice listing a Mass at the old church after 2019.
