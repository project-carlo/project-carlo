## Why it is excluded

St. Nicholas (Ukrainian), 871 N 24th St., Philadelphia is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Philadelphia. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives 'Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia' and 'Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)'; the archeparchy's own parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-29) carries it as 'St. Nicholas Ukrainian Catholic Church, Philadelphia 871 N 24th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19130'. It is in this diocese's roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the archdiocese's directory lists none. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`. GCatholic gives the intersection N 24th St. & Poplar St.; the archeparchy gives 871 N 24th Street (Fairmount).

## What would change this

Nothing expected: a Ukrainian Catholic church belongs to the Archeparchy of Philadelphia's own dataset, not to this archdiocese's.
