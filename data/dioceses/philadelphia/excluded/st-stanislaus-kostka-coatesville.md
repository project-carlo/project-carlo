## Why it is excluded

St. Stanislaus Kostka Church (201 W. Lincoln Hwy., Coatesville; the archdiocese's list says 'Chester County, PPA 240') was merged into St. Joseph Parish on 1 July 2012 and relegated to profane but not sordid use by a decree given 29 November 2018, effective 7 January 2019. The decree dates the end of Mass precisely: 'The sacraments have not been celebrated in Saint Stanislaus Kostka Church since July 2, 2016', after which only a monthly exposition took place until May 2018. St. Joseph's 25 October 2026 bulletin names no Mass there. It is in no roster; the directory carries it as a closed parish only.

## What would change this

A current bulletin listing Mass at this address.
