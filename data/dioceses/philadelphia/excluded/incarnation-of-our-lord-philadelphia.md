## Why excluded

Closed. The relegation decree (given 2015-06-08, effective 2015-07-15) records that the church had only a monthly Sunday Mass in its last year and its people already attended St. Helena. St. Helena's current bulletin lists no Mass there. `last_mass` is the decree's effective month: the decree gives no last-Mass date, so this is an upper bound, not the day of the last Mass.

The parish merged into St. Helena in 2013 (directory: 1900-2013). GCatholic's 5th St. & Lindley Ave. entry is the church building, not a worship site of St. Helena.
