## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Mount Carmel (Italian), 500 Ford St., Bridgeport, merged into Sacred Heart in July 2014, and Sunday Masses there stopped in January 2015 (St. Augustine's decree). No relegation decree was found for it: the archdiocese's parish-planning page lists only St. Augustine's for Bridgeport. Its property was acquired by the Personal Ordinariate of the Chair of St. Peter, which holds public Masses there as St. John the Baptist Catholic Church (502 Ford Street per its own site; GCatholic's 500 Ford St. page, with 'Jurisdiction: Anglican Personal Ordinariate', is the same building). It is not a Sacred Heart worship site: Sacred Heart's bulletin and website list its own church only. It is also not a closed building, which is why this record isn't `closed`: the church holds Mass, in another jurisdiction (the Ordinariate, a personal diocese-equivalent whose parishes the Rochester and Scranton records also treat as `other-jurisdiction`). The earlier lead (an 'unclosed' Mount Carmel) is resolved: the parish is closed, the building is in use by the Ordinariate.

## What would change this

A decision to cover Ordinariate parishes (St. John the Baptist publishes no bulletin that was found; its Mass times are on the site), or a Sacred Heart bulletin listing Mass at Ford Street.
