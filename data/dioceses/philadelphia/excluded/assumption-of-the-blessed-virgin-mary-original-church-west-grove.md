## Why it is excluded

The original Assumption BVM church (built 1873) was replaced in 2001 by the present church at 300 State Rd. and, in the decree's words, 'has not been used for sacred worship since the winter of 2014' (it closed for safety reasons). Archbishop Chaput relegated it to profane but not sordid use by a decree of 7 December 2016, effective 11 January 2017 (page 2). `last_mass` is the decree's 'winter of 2014': no bulletin giving the day was read. The decree gives no street address (it says the church is 1.1 miles from the current one, with a former convent and the rectory on the same property), and no source read here gives one, so `line1` is empty. The parish's current bulletin lists Mass at 300 State Road only.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a bulletin listing Mass at 'Saint Mary's Chapel'.
