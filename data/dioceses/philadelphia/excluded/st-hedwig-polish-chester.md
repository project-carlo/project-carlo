## Why it is excluded

**St. Hedwig Church, 2623 W. 4th St., Chester - closed; Sunday worship suspended since 2023-12-31, relegated effective 2026-01-01.** The Archbishop's decree (posted on Sacred Heart's site; pages 1-2 rendered) states 'This Decree is effective as of January 1, 2026' and that 'As of December 31, 2023, regular Sunday worship at St. Hedwig's has been suspended'. CatholicPhilly (2025-11-18) reports the church 'will be formally relegated to profane but not sordid use effective Jan. 1, 2026' and 'no longer serve as a Roman Catholic worship site'. Sacred Heart Parish, Clifton Heights, whose worship site it was, lists no Mass there in its 25 October 2026 bulletin (pages 1-2). `last_mass` is 2023-12 because the sources say Sunday worship was suspended from 2023-12-31, not that a particular Mass was last; neither source says no Mass was ever said afterwards (a priest's re-interment is posted on Sacred Heart's site: 'Reinternment of priest remains from St. Hedwig', not read).

Sacred Heart's directory page (updated 1 year ago) still says 'Mass at St. Hedwig Chapel, Sundays, 9:15 AM (Bilingual - Polish and English)': that line is stale. The decree, the news article and the current bulletin outrank it.

## What would change this

A current Sacred Heart bulletin listing a Mass at St. Hedwig (the building was relegated, not demolished; a one-off Mass would not make it a worship site).
