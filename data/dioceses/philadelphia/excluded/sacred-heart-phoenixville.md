## Why excluded

Sacred Heart (Slovak), merged into St. Ann on 2012-07-01, was an alternate church of St. Ann Parish. Weekend parish Mass there ended in 2018 and all liturgies, including weddings and funerals, stopped in March 2020. The archbishop's decree relegated the building to profane but not sordid use effective 2023-01-23. The directory page still calls it a worship site of St. Ann, but it is a stale lead: the current St. Ann bulletin lists no Mass there.

`last_mass` is March 2020 (the decree's "no religious services since March 2020"); the last regular weekend Mass was in 2018. The building's street address is not given in the decree or on the directory page, so none is recorded.
