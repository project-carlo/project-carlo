## Why it is excluded

Sts. Cosmas and Damian (Italian), 5th Ave. and Maple St., Conshohocken, was a worship site of St. Matthew Parish from the 2014 merger. Archbishop Chaput relegated it to profane but not sordid use by a decree given 16 February 2016, effective 16 March 2016 (page 2 of the decree), saying it was 'no longer needed by the parish' and noting that the Coptic Orthodox Church had been using it for worship and wanted to buy it. OSM now shows a Coptic Orthodox church at 209 West 5th Avenue at the same spot, which is consistent with that sale, though no source read says the sale closed. No bulletin gives a last Mass, so `last_mass` is the decree's effective date. St. Matthew's current bulletin lists no Mass there. GCatholic still lists it as a church of this archdiocese, and the directory's closed page gives only 'Formerly located at'.

## What would change this

A current bulletin or schedule of a Catholic parish listing Mass at 5th Ave. and Maple St. The Coptic Orthodox Church's use is not Catholic Mass, so it would not.
