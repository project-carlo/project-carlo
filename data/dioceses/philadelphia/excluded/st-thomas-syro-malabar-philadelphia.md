## Why it is excluded

St. Thomas (Syro-Malabar), 608 Welsh Rd., Philadelphia is a Syro-Malabar Catholic forane church of the Syro-Malabar Eparchy of St. Thomas the Apostle of Chicago, not of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Philadelphia. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives 'Jurisdiction: Syro-Malabar Diocese of Saint Thomas the Apostle of Chicago' and 'Rite: Syro-Malabar'; the eparchy's own parish directory (read 2026-10-29) lists it at the same address. It has a building of its own, 1.3 km from the Latin St. Albert the Great, Huntingdon Valley (a different church). Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs to its own eparchy's dataset, not this archdiocese's.
