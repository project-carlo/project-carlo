## Why it is excluded

Presentation of Our Lord (Ukrainian), 1564 Allentown Rd., Lansdale is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia, not of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Philadelphia. GCatholic (read 2026-10-29) gives 'Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia' and 'Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)'; the archeparchy's own parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-29) carries it as 'Presentation of Our Lord Ukrainian Catholic Church, Lansdale 1564 Allentown Rd, Lansdale, PA 19446-0627'. It is in this diocese's roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory; the archdiocese's directory lists none. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule settled in `diocese.md`.
