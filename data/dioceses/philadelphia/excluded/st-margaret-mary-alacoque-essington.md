## Why it is excluded

St. Margaret Mary Alacoque, Essington (Wanamaker Ave.) merged into St. Gabriel, Norwood in 2014 and was kept as a worship site. CatholicPhilly's story of 9 May 2017 says the worship-site designation would cease and the church building 'will be relegated to profane but not sordid use effective June 12' (2017). `last_mass` is that effective date, per the diocese's rule when no bulletin gives the last Mass. This agrees with what the map batch read on 2026-10-28 and left open for want of a closure source: St. Gabriel's 25 October 2026 bulletin and Mass page list only the Norwood church, and the parish still uses the 'Margaret Mary Parking Lot' (the property, not the church). The directory's closed-parish page ('Worship site of St. Gabriel Church, Norwood') is about ten years old and stale. Found by reconcile on 2026-10-29 with CatholicPhilly's site search ('st margaret mary alacoque essington'); moved from `open/`.

Addresses: the directory gives 'Wanamaker Ave. & Governor Printz Blvd.', GCatholic 'Wanamaker Ave. & Industrial Hwy.'.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a new decree; a relegated church does not return to worship.
