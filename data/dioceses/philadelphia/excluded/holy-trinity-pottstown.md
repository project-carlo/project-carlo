## Why it is excluded

**Holy Trinity Church (Slovak), 366 South St., Pottstown: closed, last Mass 2014-05.** The St. Aloysius directory page still names it as an "Alternative Worship Site". CatholicPhilly reported on 2014-05-12 that the archdiocese closed the church on 11 May 2014 for unsafe, unstable roof trusses and told its regular worshippers (a single Sunday 10 a.m. Mass since the 2004 merger) to attend St. Aloysius. The current St. Aloysius bulletin (25 October 2026) and home page list no Mass at it. The closure date and reason come from the 2014 article; that no Mass has resumed in the twelve years since rests on those two current sources listing none, not on a later statement. It is not on the diocese's list of relegation decrees (diocese.md), and GCatholic's roster has no entry for it.

## What would change this

A later St. Aloysius bulletin or archdiocesan notice listing a Mass at Holy Trinity, 366 South St., or a decree of relegation or demolition (which would only add a date). Searches made 2026-10-28: CatholicPhilly's REST search for "Pottstown Holy Trinity" (found only the 2014 article), OSM Nominatim (366 South Street is a house; the Pottstown "Holy Trinity" it names is an Orthodox church on Juniper St.).
