## Why it is excluded

Immaculate Conception Chapel is the main chapel of St. Charles Borromeo Seminary's campus at 1400 Evans Road, Ambler (Lower Gwynedd Township), where the seminary moved from Overbrook in August 2024 (the Overbrook records are `excluded/st-charles-borromeo-seminary-wynnewood` and `excluded/immaculate-conception-chapel-wynnewood`). Its Masses are the seminary community's: the 'Seminarian Life' page (read 2026-10-28 and 2026-11-07) gives the seminarians' weekday Mass at 7:25 a.m., a Wednesday community Mass at 4:30 pm, Saturday 8:00 a.m. and Sunday 10:00 a.m., all in Immaculate Conception Chapel, as part of the seminarians' day, and says nothing of visitors. The campus is not open to the public: the seminary's ID Cards and Key Access policy (read 2026-11-07, under a Safety and Security page that names the Lower Gwynedd police, so it is the current campus's) requires every unescorted person to check in at the gatehouse, and 'if the person does not have an appointment, the Campus member will be contacted to determine if the visitor will be admitted to the Campus'. A Mass that a visitor can attend only by appointment or a host's leave is not public, so under this diocese's rule for seminary chapels (community-only Mass) it is excluded `no-public-mass`.

What was read, not inferred: the Mass schedule and the access policy. Inferred: that the policy applies on Sunday mornings as on any day; neither page makes an exception for Mass. No Ambler-area bulletin lists a Mass here (St. Anthony & St. Joseph, St. Alphonsus, 25 October 2026), the dedication on 8 September 2024 was 'NOT open to the public', and the seminary publishes no bulletin. No roster lists it: GCatholic still has only the Overbrook campus.

## What would change this

A seminary page, parish bulletin or archdiocesan listing that invites the public to Mass in the chapel (for example a Sunday Mass open to visitors) would make it a church row under the parish that lists it, or an exclusion `no-bulletin` if no bulletin lists it (the rule for seminary chapels in `diocese.md`).
