## Why it is excluded

**Our Lady Help of Christians Church, E Allegheny Ave. & Gaul St. (3160 Gaul St.): closed; relegated to profane but not sordid use effective 2018-06-04.** The parish merged into Nativity BVM on 1 July 2016. The decree (26 April 2018) says the last funeral Mass was 'in the early fall of 2016' and 'no other acts of divine worship have been celebrated there since', after a 2017 facade inspection found the church unsafe. The St. John Paul II directory schedule lists no Mass there.

`last_mass` is 2016-09, the decree's 'early fall of 2016' for a funeral: an approximate month, not a day. The decree's effective date is 2018-06-04.

## What would change this

A parish bulletin or archdiocesan notice listing a Mass at the church after the decree.
