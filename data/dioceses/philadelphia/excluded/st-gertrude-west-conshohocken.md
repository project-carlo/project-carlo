## Why it is excluded

St. Gertrude, 200 Bullock and Merion Aves., West Conshohocken, was a worship site of St. Matthew Parish from the 2014 merger and was relegated to profane but not sordid use by a decree given 2 December 2015, effective 1 January 2016 (page 2 of the decree). The decree says it was 'no longer needed by the parish' and had 'few requests' for public worship. No bulletin gives a last Mass, so `last_mass` is the decree's effective date. St. Matthew's 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Mass at the parish church only. GCatholic still lists the church (phone +1 (610) 828-0268, Plus Code 87G63MCM+HV) and John Patrick listing 65 'Saint Gertrude Church' has no editions.

## What would change this

A current Catholic bulletin listing Mass at Bullock and Merion Aves.
