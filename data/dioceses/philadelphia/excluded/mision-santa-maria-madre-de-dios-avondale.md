## Why it is excluded

The directory lists 'Mision Santa Maria, Madre De Dios' as a parish with no Mass times; its page says its spiritual records are kept at St. Rocco and gives only a 'Donation Center' at 29 Gap Newport Pike. St. Rocco's 18 October 2026 bulletin prints the same address as 'Centro de Las Donaciones: Misión Santa María, Madre de Dios, "The Avondale Center"', and uses 'el Centro' in its weekly calendar for catechism, RCIA and prayer meetings, never for Mass. Weekday Masses are 'en la Iglesia' and the only other location with Mass is Sacred Heart, Oxford. So the mission is a donation and parish center of St. Rocco, not a worship site: `no-public-mass`. This is read from one bulletin (the 18 October issue); the 'Centro' of the calendar could also mean the Saint Rocco Center at 9016 Gap Newport Pike, which doesn't change the conclusion.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule listing Mass at 29 Gap Newport Pike (or the mission's own site) would make it a church row under St. Rocco.
