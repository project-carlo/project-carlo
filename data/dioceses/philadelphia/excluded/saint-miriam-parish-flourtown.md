## Why excluded

Saint Miriam Parish & School, 654 Bethlehem Pike, Flourtown, publishes a current John Patrick bulletin (listing 286, 25 October 2026) and holds Sunday Mass, but it is not a Roman Catholic parish. Its own site says it belongs to the Old Catholic Communion and 'declare[d] our independence from Rome' (Who We Are, read 2026-10-28); its bulletin names a pastor (Monsignor James St. George, OSF) and a 'Bishop in Residence' (Most Reverend Kenneth Denski, OCR) and runs a school and the historic Union Cemetery of Whitemarsh. It is in neither the Archdiocese of Philadelphia's directory nor GCatholic's list, which is why no roster has it. The place is in the Whitemarsh/Springfield area near St. Genevieve, but the two bulletins are unrelated: St. Genevieve's masthead is the Roman Catholic parish at 1225 Bethlehem Pike.

The reason `not-catholic` is the nearest of the standard reasons ('a same-named Orthodox, Episcopal, Lutheran... church'): the community calls itself Catholic but is outside the Catholic Church. If the data owner wants Old Catholic and independent Catholic communities treated otherwise, this is the place to say so.
