## Why it is excluded

This is the old seminary chapel at Overbrook (1875). The seminary moved out in August 2024 and the campus is owned by Main Line Health, so no Mass is said here. The seminary's 'Seminarian Life' page now puts its daily and Sunday Masses in an Immaculate Conception Chapel at the new Lower Gwynedd campus, whose dedication was closed to the public; that new chapel is a different place and not one of this batch's roster entries. `last_mass` is the year of the move (no source gives a day).
