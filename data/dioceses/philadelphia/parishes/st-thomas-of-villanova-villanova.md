## Publisher

St. Thomas of Villanova Parish is an Augustinian parish that publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 180 ("Saint Thomas of Villanova Church", filed under Rosemont, PA 19010, the parish office's post town). The 25 October 2026 issue (image only, rendered) has the masthead "SAINT THOMAS OF VILLANOVA CHURCH" and a Mass schedule for Villanova Church and Rosemont Chapel together.

## Parish and campus: one bulletin

The university church is the parish's own church. GCatholic lists it twice (19264, under the group 'Villanova University', ZIP 19085, no street; and 41992, 1229 E Lancaster Ave., 19010), but those are the parish's two buildings: Villanova Church, on the campus at 800 E Lancaster Ave., and Rosemont Chapel at the parish office, 1229 E Lancaster Ave. The parish's bulletin lists Masses at both. Villanova University's own John Patrick listing (233) has no editions, so no separate campus bulletin exists to merge or keep; the 'University led Masses' at 5, 7 and 9 PM are said in Villanova Church (Our Mother of Good Counsel's bulletin says so) and are not in this parish's own bulletin. Neither GCatholic entry is a different parish row.

## Worship sites

Two churches under this row: Villanova Church and Rosemont Chapel (see their church records). The 'Our Mother of Good Counsel' schedule box in this bulletin is another parish's (Bryn Mawr, its own listing 277 and its own 25 October issue, a different file), so it is not a worship site of this row and is left to its own batch. The Bryn Mawr parish's bulletin in turn prints this parish's schedule, so the pair carry each other's Masses without sharing a file or a masthead.

## Website and bulletin provider

stthomasofvillanova.org (Weebly) links the John Patrick listing and hosts no bulletin PDFs. The row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing. John Patrick PDFs have no text layer, so `fetch:check` reports publication identity unverified by design; the masthead was read by rendering page 1.
