## Publisher

St. Joseph Parish, 7631 Waters Rd., Cheltenham, publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 7 ('Saint Joseph Roman Catholic Church'). The 25 October 2026 issue (4 pages, text layer) carries the masthead 'SAINT JOSEPH PARISH CENTER' and the footer '007 St. Joseph - Cheltenham'. Rev. Dominic Ishaq, J.C.D. is pastor of St. Joseph and of Presentation BVM, Cheltenham (Clerical Appointments, May 2026; the directory says he lives at Presentation).

## One bulletin or two with Presentation BVM, Cheltenham?

Two. The two parishes share a pastor, a printer and each other's schedules, but not a bulletin. St. Joseph's cover prints Presentation's rectory, school and Mass times beside its own, and Presentation's cover prints 'St. Joseph: 10 AM' lines, as St. Helena and St. Titus do. The files differ (md5 54bcc045... against 47bcb0d9...), have different page counts (4 against 6), different mastheads, and their own Mass-intention lists and footer codes ('007 St. Joseph - Cheltenham', '015 p. 2'). A shared pastor is not a shared bulletin (settled rule), and no joint masthead names both parishes, so each keeps its own row.

This is not Presentation BVM, Wynnewood (listing 177, md5 0dcd20e5...): Presentation BVM, Cheltenham is a separate parish at 100 Old Soldiers Rd., 19012. The Wynnewood record's remark that its pastor also leads St. Joseph, Cheltenham, refers to this Cheltenham parish; see the lead in the handoff.

## Worship sites

One church: St. Joseph Church, 7631 Waters Rd. The bulletin's Mass box lists Sunday, Wednesday and Friday 10 AM there. The directory and GCatholic list no other site. Presentation BVM's Masses, printed on this cover, belong to that parish's own row.

## Website and bulletin provider

stjoescheltenham.org (WordPress) links the listing's `jppc.net/onlinebulletins/007template.pdf` from its Bulletin page and hosts no PDFs, so the listing is the channel: `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing.
