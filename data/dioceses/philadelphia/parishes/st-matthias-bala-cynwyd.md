## Worship sites

One church: St. Matthias Church, Bryn Mawr and Highland Aves. The Mass schedule lists one location. The 'Parish Center' at 108 N. Highland Ave. and the mailing address at 128 Bryn Mawr Ave. are offices; no Mass is listed there. The bulletin mentions St. Charles Borromeo Seminary only as an appeal weekend, not as a Mass site.
