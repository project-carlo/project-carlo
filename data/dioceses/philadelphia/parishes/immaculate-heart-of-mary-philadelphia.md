## Publisher

Immaculate Heart of Mary Parish, 819 E. Cathedral Rd. (Roxborough/Andorra), publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 237. The 25 October 2026 issue has no text layer; page 1, rendered, has the masthead 'IMMACULATE HEART OF MARY ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH, 819 E. Cathedral Road', pastor Rev. Edward J. Casey, and a Mass box for one church: Saturday vigil 5:15 pm, Sunday 7:30, 9:30 and 11:30 am, weekdays in Sacred Heart Chapel. The 11 October issue has a text layer and the same page 1.

## Worship sites

One church row: Immaculate Heart of Mary Church. The weekday Mass line 'WEEKDAYS: IN SACRED HEART CHAPEL' names a chapel with no address of its own anywhere in the issue (the bulletin and the directory give one campus, 819 E. Cathedral Road, next to the school at 815), so under the diocese's rule it is a room of the campus and its Masses sit on the church row. The directory and GCatholic name no other site.

## Website and bulletin provider

The row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the John Patrick listing, the channel with the latest issue (25 October 2026). The parish's own site ihmphila.org links the listing and keeps a self-hosted archive that stops at 27 September 2026.
