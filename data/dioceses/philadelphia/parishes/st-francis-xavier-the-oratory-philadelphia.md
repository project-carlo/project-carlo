## Publisher

St. Francis Xavier Parish - The Oratory (the Philadelphia Oratory of St. Philip Neri), 2319 Green St., publishes its own weekly bulletin, posted on the Announcements page of philadelphiaoratory.org (a Squarespace site; the directory lists no website, GCatholic's sfxoratory.org redirects here). The 1 November 2026 issue's page 1 names the parish and its priest, Fr. Paul C. Convery, C.O., and the Mass times; page 2 lists the week's Mass intentions. John Patrick Publishing was not checked beyond the survey's scan (no listing in `jppc-directory.json` for this parish).

## Worship sites

One: St. Francis Xavier Church at 24th and Green Sts. (`churches/st-francis-xavier-philadelphia.md`). The bulletin names no other place of Mass; a Memorial Day Mass in Ward Park (24th and Aspen) is on the parish's site as a once-a-year event, not a worship site.
