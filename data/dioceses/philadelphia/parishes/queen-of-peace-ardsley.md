## Publisher

Queen of Peace, Ardsley (Rev. James R. DeGrassa, pastor) publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 149. The 25 October 2026 issue's masthead names 'Queen of Peace Parish, 820 North Hills Avenue, Ardsley' alone, with one Masses box (Saturday 4:30 pm; Sunday 7:30, 9:30, 11:00 am; daily Monday to Saturday 8:00 am). It is the parish that absorbed St. John of the Cross, Roslyn in the 2014 merger.

## Worship sites

One: Queen of Peace church (churches/queen-of-peace-ardsley.md). St. John of the Cross, Roslyn was a worship site of Queen of Peace from 2014 until its relegation to profane use effective 2016-09-16 (decree), so it is excluded as closed (excluded/st-john-of-the-cross-roslyn.md); neither the bulletin nor the parish's Mass times mention it. Queen of Peace shares Good Shepherd Catholic Regional School with neighbouring parishes but the school is not a worship site.
