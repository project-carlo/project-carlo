## Publisher

Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary Parish, Cheltenham (100 Old Soldiers Rd., 19012), publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 15. The 25 October 2026 issue is 6 pages of images; its masthead reads 'PRESENTATION BVM, 100 Old Soldiers Road, Cheltenham, PA 19012' and the page-2 footer '015 p. 2'. Rev. Dominic Ishaq is pastor here and of St. Joseph, Cheltenham, and the parishes call themselves 'Twinned' (page 2: 'Parishioners of the Twinned Parishes of Presentation BVM and St. Joseph').

## One bulletin or two with St. Joseph, Cheltenham?

Two; see st-joseph-cheltenham. Presentation's file (md5 47bcb0d9...) differs from St. Joseph's (54bcc045...), has 6 pages against 4, and its own masthead, intentions and footer. Each prints the other's Mass times. A shared pastor is not a shared bulletin (settled rule).

This is not Presentation BVM, Wynnewood (listing 177, 204 Haverford Rd., md5 0dcd20e5...), a different parish with its own pastor and bulletin.

## Worship sites

One church: Presentation BVM Church, Old Soldiers Rd. and Woodland Ave. The weekday Masses 'in the chapel' on the directory page and 'Adoration Chapel' in the Mass box are the church's own chapel (the Monday adoration is 'in the Presentation BVM Adoration Chapel'), not a separate address. St. Joseph's Masses printed in this bulletin belong to St. Joseph's own row.

## Website and bulletin provider

presentationbvm.org is eCatholic (site 39000), but its Bulletin page only links the John Patrick listing and hosts no issue, so the listing is the channel: `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing.
