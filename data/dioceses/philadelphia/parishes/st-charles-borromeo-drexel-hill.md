## Publisher

St. Charles Borromeo Parish publishes a weekly bulletin printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 220). The 25 October 2026 issue (image only, rendered) is headed 'Saint Charles Borromeo Church, 3422 Dennison Ave Drexel Hill, PA 19026' and names Rev. Msgr. George A. Majoros as Pastor and Rev. Michael Davis as Parochial Vicar.

**One bulletin or two with Sacred Heart, Clifton Heights?** Two. Msgr. Majoros is Pastor of both ('partnering parishes' since 2016 per the St. Hedwig relegation decree, item 2) and the directory says he resides at Sacred Heart. Each parish still prints its own issue: the 25 October 2026 files are different PDFs (6 pages each, different md5) with their own mastheads and their own page 2 (this one a Pennsylvania Catholic Conference voting insert; Sacred Heart's is its Masses for the Week and Mass Intentions), and each parish's website links only its own listing (scbdh.org -> 220, sacredheart-cliftonheights.net -> 92). Both covers print the same combined 'Partnering Parishes' Mass schedule. See `sacred-heart-clifton-heights` and diocese.md's rule that a shared pastor is not a shared bulletin.
