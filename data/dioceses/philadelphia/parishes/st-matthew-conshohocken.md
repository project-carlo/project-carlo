## Publisher

St. Matthew Parish, Conshohocken (pastor Rev. Joseph Devlin) is distributed by John Patrick Publishing as listing 244. The issue of 25 October 2026 (12 pages, text layer) has the masthead 'St. Matthew Parish', the parish's own Mass intentions and Mass box, and Fr. Devlin's pastor's column. The parish's eCatholic site (38442) links the listing from its home page and its 'Bulletin Archive' page carries no files, so the listing is the only channel found. The row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing, as for the other John Patrick parishes in this diocese; an eCatholic id alone is not a publication, so it is not in `publication`.

## Worship sites

One church, St. Matthew, at 3rd Ave. and Fayette St. The 25 October 2026 Mass box lists Saturday 4:30 PM, Sunday 7:30, 9:30 and 11:30 AM and weekdays 8:15 AM with no second location. The directory says the parish absorbed three more parishes in 2014:

- **Sts. Cosmas and Damian (Italian), 5th Ave. and Maple St.** and **St. Gertrude, West Conshohocken, 200 Bullock and Merion Aves.** were both worship sites of St. Matthew and were relegated to profane but not sordid use by decrees of 16 February 2016 (effective 16 March 2016) and 2 December 2015 (effective 1 January 2016). Each has an `excluded/` record. The bulletin mentions 'the Cosmas & Damian Room in the Parish Center', a meeting room named for the old parish, not a church.
- **St. Mary (Polish), W. Elm and Oak Sts.**, is not a St. Matthew worship site now. Its building is the church of St. Mary's Quasi-Parish (FSSP Latin Mass), which publishes its own bulletin (see `st-mary-quasi-parish-conshohocken`). The Cosmas and Damian decree says St. Matthew 'has responsibility for the upkeep of Saint Mary Church', and the quasi-parish's 25 October 2026 bulletin asks for prayers that 'St. Mary will be able to purchase the property of St. Mary from St. Matthew': St. Matthew owns the building, but the Masses there are the quasi-parish's and do not appear in St. Matthew's bulletin.

The pastor and the other staff named are not shared with any other directory parish.
