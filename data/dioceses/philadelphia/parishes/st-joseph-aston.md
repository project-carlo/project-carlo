## Publisher

St. Joseph, Aston publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 107, 'Church of Saint Joseph Parish'). The 25 October 2026 issue (image only, 12 pages; page 1 rendered) is headed 'Church of St. Joseph Parish, 3255 Concord Road, Aston' with Fr. Henry Graebe as Parochial Administrator.

**Link to Lenni.** The batch notes Lenni's previous administrator was St. Joseph's pastor (Msgr. Brian Bransfield, per CatholicPhilly's 2025-12-05 profile of Mark Griswold). Lenni now has a Parish Life Director and its own bulletin; Aston's issue lists only its own Masses. Separate: see `publication_reviews` and `st-francis-de-sales-lenni`.

## Worship sites

One: the church at 3255 Concord Road (see `churches/st-joseph-aston`). The weekday 6:30 am Mass is in 'Mary's Chapel', which the bulletin's box sets beside 'Church' for Friday and Saturday; it is read here as a chapel on the parish campus, not a separate worship site (see the church record).

## Website and bulletin provider

churchofstjosephaston.org is an eCatholic site (ecatholic:5594) whose own archive stopped in March 2025; the row follows the listing: recipe `page-pdfs https://www.church-bulletin.org/church-of-saint-joseph-parish-aston-pa-19014`. The issues are image-only.
