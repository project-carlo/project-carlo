## Publisher

Holy Cross Parish, Springfield, publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 24). The parish site is John Patrick hosted and its 'most recent weekly bulletin' link is the listing's template, so the listing is the channel. The 25 October 2026 issue is image-only; page 1 is headed 'Holy Cross Church, 651 E. Springfield Road' and names David Winter as Parish Life Director.

**Parish Life Director change.** CatholicPhilly (2026-10-19) appoints Mr. David Winter Parish Life Director effective 2026-10-12. The 25 October bulletin, the first full issue after the change, still comes from John Patrick on the same listing, so the publisher is unchanged. The parish's own WordPress archive stopped in 2025, which is no sign of a move.

**One bulletin or several?** One bulletin for Holy Cross alone. St. Francis of Assisi and St. Kevin, the other Springfield parishes, each publish their own file with their own masthead (see `publication_reviews`). The directory names no shared pastor for this parish.
