## Publisher

St. Thomas the Apostle, Glen Mills publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 97). The 25 October 2026 issue (image only, 16 pages; pages 1-2 rendered) has a cover headed 'St. Thomas the Apostle Catholic Church' with the Mass times, and page 2 lists the week's Mass intentions.

## Worship sites

One: St. Thomas the Apostle Church, 430 Valleybrook Road (see `churches/st-thomas-the-apostle-glen-mills`). Page 2 marks events as in the church (C), the chapel (Ch), the social hall or the parish office, and 'Sanctuary Lamp Intentions' name a 'Church' and a 'Chapel'. The Masses on the page (Sunday 7:30, 9:30, 11:30 AM, weekdays 8 or 9 AM and the Monday 7:30 PM Mass of Healing) are all marked (C) or unmarked, and the chapel entries are the Chaplet of Divine Mercy, rosary, chapel tours and the perpetual adoration chapel, with no Mass. The chapel is therefore not a separate church row.
