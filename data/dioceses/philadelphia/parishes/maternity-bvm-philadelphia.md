## Publisher

Maternity B.V.M. Parish publishes its own weekly bulletin. The parish's eCatholic site (id 13740) archives each issue at `files.ecatholic.com/13740/bulletins/<yyyymmdd>.pdf`, and John Patrick Publishing's listing 90 carries the same file (the 25 October 2026 issue has the same md5 on both), so the eCatholic archive is the channel. The 25 October issue is image-only; page 1 is headed 'Maternity BVM Catholic Church' and page 2 carries the Mass intentions and Fr. Maloney's column.

**One bulletin or several?** One bulletin for this parish alone. The directory names no shared pastor and no other parish's name appears on pages 1-2.
