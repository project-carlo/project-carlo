## Publisher

St. John the Baptist Parish, Manayunk, publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 58. The 25 October 2026 issue (8 pages, text layer) has the masthead 'Saint John the Baptist Parish, Manayunk, 146 Rector Street' (the parish office; 'Church is located at 119 Rector Street'), Parochial Administrator Rev. Andrew J. Auletta, and a Mass box with the main church, a worship site and the All Saints' Chapel.

## Worship sites

Three church rows. St. John the Baptist Church, 119 Rector St., holds the weekend and Holy Day Masses. The All Saints' Chapel in the Parish Center, 146 Rector St., holds the weekday 7:00 AM Mass ('Parish Center: All Saints’ Chapel: Weekdays at 7:00 AM'); the bulletin gives the office and chapel the address 146 Rector St., not the church's, so it is its own row. St. Josaphat Church, 124 Cotton St., is printed in the Mass box as 'Worship Site: St. Josaphat Church (124 Cotton St.): First Saturday Mass at 8:30 AM; confessions at 8:00 AM', and again on 25 October as 'FIRST SATURDAY—NOV 7 First Saturday Mass 8:30AM & Novena at St. Josaphat Church'. The directory's closed-parish page for St. Josaphat (updated 7 years before) gives the same '8:30 AM ... on the 1st Saturday of the month'.

Not a row: St. Mary of the Assumption (German), 172 Conarroe St., merged in 2012 and was closed as a worship site on 1 November 2015 (excluded/st-mary-of-the-assumption-philadelphia).

## Website and bulletin provider

The row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the John Patrick listing, which has the latest issue (25 October 2026) and is the only bulletin link on the parish's own site, which hosts no PDFs.

## Not yet confirmed

Nothing open for the channel. The Mass box prints only the first-Saturday Mass at St. Josaphat; whether other Masses (feast days, Polish-language Masses) are held there is not given.
