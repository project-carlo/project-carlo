## Publisher

Annunciation B.V.M. (10th and Dickinson Sts., South Philadelphia) publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 127. The 25 October 2026 issue (8 pages, text layer) is headed 'Annunciation BVM Church / Iglesia de la Anunciación', in English and Spanish, with Rev. Jonathan Rice as Parochial Administrator and a page-2 'Parish Mass & Confession Schedule': Saturday 5:00 PM (English); Sunday 9:00 AM (Spanish), 11:00 AM (English), and 1:00 PM (Spanish) on the 4th Sunday; Monday and Thursday 7:00 PM (Spanish).

**St. Rita of Cascia and the National Shrine.** St. Rita's parish merged into Annunciation in 2016 (directory: 'Merged with St. Rita of Cascia: 2016'; St. Rita's page: 'Spiritual records are kept at Annunciation B.V.M. Church'). Its church at Broad and Ellsworth is the National Shrine of St. Rita of Cascia, run by the Augustinians with its own Executive Director, and it publishes its own bulletin (John Patrick listing 241, and saintritashrine.org). Annunciation's bulletin lists no Mass there, so the shrine is a parish row of its own under the settled shrine rule, not a church row here; see national-shrine-of-st-rita-of-cascia-philadelphia.
