## Publisher

Sacred Heart Parish (Polish and Territorial), Clifton Heights, publishes its own weekly bulletin printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 92, the template file is `092` on the parish's own archive). The 25 October 2026 issue (image only, rendered) is headed 'Sacred Heart Church, Partnering Parishes with St. Charles Borromeo (Drexel Hill)', names Rev. Msgr. George A. Majoros as Pastor and Rev. Michael Davis as Parochial Vicar, and its Masses for the Week lists Sacred Heart intentions.

**One bulletin or two with St. Charles Borromeo?** Two: see `st-charles-borromeo-drexel-hill`, which records the side-by-side comparison of the 25 October 2026 issues (different files, mastheads and page 2s). Both covers print the same combined Mass schedule, so each bulletin lists both churches' Masses; each church is a row under its own parish.

## Worship sites

One with public Mass: Sacred Heart Church, 316 East Broadway Avenue. The directory also names St. Hedwig Chapel, Chester ('Mass at St. Hedwig Chapel, Sundays, 9:15 AM'), but the Archbishop's decree (copy on this parish's own site, https://sacredheart-cliftonheights.net/wp-content/uploads/Decree-of-the-Relegation-of-St.-Hedwig-Chester.pdf, page 2) relegates it to profane but not sordid use effective 1 January 2026 and states 'As of December 31, 2023, regular Sunday worship at St. Hedwig's has been suspended'; the 25 October 2026 schedule lists no Mass there. See `excluded/st-hedwig-chester`.

## Website and bulletin provider

sacredheart-cliftonheights.net (WordPress). The site's 'Area Parishes' sidebar links St. Andrew's template (037) by name, which is a neighbour link, not this parish's bulletin.
