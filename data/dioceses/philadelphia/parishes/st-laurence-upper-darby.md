## Worship sites

Two. (1) St. Laurence Church, 8245 West Chester Pike: Monday-Saturday 8:30 AM, Saturday vigil 4:30 PM and 7:00 PM (Spanish), Sunday 7:30 AM, 11:30 AM and 9:00 AM (Spanish). (2) **St. Alice Church**, Upper Darby: the 25 October 2026 Mass Schedule box lists 'St. Alice, Sunday 10:00AM in Vietnamese'. St. Alice parish merged into St. Laurence in 2013 (records are kept at St. Laurence per the directory's closed-parish page; address line 'Formerly located at Copley Rd. and Walnut St.', which is an address, not a closure), and the building still has a weekly Sunday Mass in the current bulletin, so it keeps a church row here (`st-alice-church-upper-darby`).
