## Publisher

St. Francis de Sales (47th St. and Springfield Ave., West Philadelphia) publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 187 ('Saint Francis de Sales Parish United By The Most Blessed Sacrament', Philadelphia PA 19143, latest edition 25 October 2026). The long listing name comes from the parish's own site title; the directory lists one parish, St. Francis de Sales. The 25 October 2026 issue (8 pages, text layer) names Fr. Robert Ngageno, Parochial Administrator, and lists Masses at one church.

The parish was listed in the survey as a possible tie to Most Blessed Sacrament: John Patrick listing 134 ('Most Blessed Sacrament Church', 19143) has no editions and is a merged church's readings page; the bulletin lists no Mass there.

## Worship sites

One: the church at 47th and Springfield (rectory and office 4625 Springfield Ave.). The directory names no worship site, and the bulletin lists no other place.

## Website and bulletin provider

desalesphilly.com is hosted by John Patrick Publishing and links only the template for listing 187, so the listing is the `page-pdfs` channel.
