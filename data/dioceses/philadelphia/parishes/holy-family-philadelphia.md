## Publisher

Holy Family Parish, 234 Hermitage St., Manayunk, publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 166. The 25 October 2026 issue (6 pages, text layer) has the masthead 'The Church of The Holy Family, 234 Hermitage Street Philadelphia, PA 19127', pastor Fr. Timothy F. O’Sullivan, and a Mass box for one church: Saturday 4:00pm, Sunday 9:00am & 11:00am, weekdays 9:00am.

## Worship sites

One church row: Holy Family Church. The directory's page says 'Merged with St. Lucy (Italian): 2012' and 'Worship Site: St. Lucy Church', and the 11 April 2012 merger decree (archphila.org Announcements2012.pdf, p. 8) says 'Saint Lucy Church will remain open as a worship site for Holy Family Parish'. Neither the 25 October 2026 bulletin, the parish's home page nor its older issues (30 August, 20 September, 11 October 2026) mention St. Lucy or Green Lane, and the Mass box lists no second place. No relegation decree or news story for St. Lucy was found, so it is not excluded as closed: it is an `open` record (open/st-lucy-philadelphia) with the evidence of absence listed there.

## Website and bulletin provider

The row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the John Patrick listing, the channel with the latest issue (25 October 2026). The parish's own WordPress archive at holyfamilychurch.org/bulletins/ stopped at 12 October 2025, so it is not the channel.
