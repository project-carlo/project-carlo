## Publisher

St. James the Great Church, Elkins Park (8320 Brookside Rd., 19027) publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 56. The 25 October 2026 issue is 6 pages of images; the masthead reads 'SAINT JAMES THE GREAT CHURCH, 8320 Brookside Road, Elkins Park, PA 19027' and gives Rev. Andrew Labatorio as Parochial Administrator, with Bishop Efren V. Esmilla in residence.

## One bulletin or two with Our Lady of Hope, Philadelphia?

Two; see our-lady-of-hope-philadelphia. Father Labatorio is administrator of both (the directory says he lives at Our Lady of Hope). Each parish has its own bulletin: this one has the listing 56 file and footer '056saintjameselkinsparkpa'; Our Lady of Hope posts its own PDFs on a Google Site (olhcatholic.org/bulletins), headed 'OUR LADY OF HOPE CATHOLIC PARISH, 5200 North Broad Street', with different Mass times, a different layout and no mention of Elkins Park. A shared pastor is not a shared bulletin (settled rule).

## Worship sites

One church: St. James the Great Church, 8320 Brookside Rd. The bulletin's Mass box names no other site.

## Website and bulletin provider

stjameselkinspark.com forwards to a Wix site whose Parish Bulletin page links the listing (`church-bulletin.org/?id=056`), so the listing is the channel: `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing.
