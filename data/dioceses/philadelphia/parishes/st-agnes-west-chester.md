## Publisher

St. Agnes Parish, 231 W. Gay St., West Chester, publishes its own weekly bulletin. The 25 October 2026 issue (12 pages, image-only; page 1 rendered and read) is headed "Saint Agnes Parish / In historic West Chester, PA" with the parish's phone and website, and its Forty Hours schedule lists Masses at the church only. The directory gives the pastor as Rev. Louis P. Bellopede; no other West Chester parish names him, so there is no shared-pastor lead. The three other West Chester parishes' bulletins (checked in this batch) have their own mastheads and different PDFs.

## Worship sites

One: St. Agnes Church (see its church record). The directory page lists no worship site or mission, and the parish's Mass page lists no Mass anywhere else. It does mention a "lower chapel", used for Wednesday adoration with no Mass listed, in the same building. GCatholic lists one St. Agnes church (19215).

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site is eCatholic (site 2676) and its Bulletin page links each issue as files.ecatholic.com/2676/bulletins/<yyyymmdd>.pdf. John Patrick Publishing (listing 171, linked from the same page on John Patrick's side) serves the same file, so the two are one bulletin; `ecatholic` is used because it reads the parish's own site. The PDF has no text layer: the pipeline will need to read it as images.
