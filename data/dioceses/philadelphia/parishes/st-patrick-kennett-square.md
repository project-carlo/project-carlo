## Publisher

St. Patrick Parish, Kennett Square, publishes one weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 236, 'Saint Patrick Parish Roman Catholic Church', Kennett Square, PA 19348). The newest issue on 2026-10-28 is 25 October 2026. The PDF is an image with no readable text layer, so page 1 was rendered and read: the masthead names 'St. Patrick Parish, Roman Catholic Church, 212 Meredith Street, Kennett Square' and gives its own Mass Schedule (Saturday vigil 5:00 PM; Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 11:00 AM; weekday Masses). The parish's own WordPress site posts the same file: its 2026-10-25.pdf is byte-identical to the listing's, so both channels are current. The row uses the listing because the site's file names vary by month (01-04-2026.pdf, 02.01.2026.pdf, 2026-10-25.pdf) and a `page-pdfs` collector is steadier on the listing's dated names.

## Worship sites

One church, 212 Meredith St. The bulletin's Mass Schedule lists St. Patrick's own Masses only. 'Buffington House, 227 Lafayette Street' on the cover is a parish house, and the directory's parish offices at 205 Lafayette St. are offices; neither is given a Mass. The directory page lists no worship site or chapel.

`stpatrickkennettsquare.org` is a self-hosted WordPress site (`probe url`: ok); its Bulletins page links the John Patrick listing id 236 as well as its own PDFs.
