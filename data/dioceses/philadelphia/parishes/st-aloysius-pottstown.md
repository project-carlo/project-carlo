## Publisher

St. Aloysius Parish, Pottstown, publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 142). The 25 October 2026 issue (page 1 rendered; the PDF has no text layer) is headed "Saint Aloysius Parish" with its Beech Street and Keim Street addresses and gives one Mass schedule. The directory names Rev. Wesly L. Taveras Medina as Parochial Administrator; no other parish names him, so there is no shared-pastor lead. The three neighbouring parishes in this batch (St. Gabriel, Stowe; St. Thomas More; St. Teresa of Calcutta) have their own bulletins with their own mastheads.

## Worship sites

One: St. Aloysius Church, 223 Beech Street (see its church record). The directory's page for this parish still lists "Alternative Worship Site: Holy Trinity Church, 366 South St., Pottstown" (page updated about four months before 2026-10-28). Neither the 25 October 2026 bulletin nor the parish home page lists any Mass there, and CatholicPhilly reported on 2014-05-12 that Holy Trinity was closed for unsafe structure with its Mass moved to St. Aloysius. It is recorded in `excluded/holy-trinity-pottstown`. The directory also lists Begley Hall (Beech and Penn Sts.) and cemeteries, which are not worship sites.

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site is a John Patrick Publishing WordPress site whose "Current Bulletin" page leads to the John Patrick listing; `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing reads the dated PDFs (the slug was read from the listing's redirect of ?id=142). The PDF has no text layer, so `fetch:check` reports `publication_identity` unverified by design and the pipeline must read the pages as images.
