## Publisher

St. Monica publishes weekly through John Patrick Publishing, listing 155 ('Saint Monica Parish', Philadelphia PA 19145), whose newest issue is dated 25 October 2026. The parish site (saintmonicaparish.net) links the listing's template and hosts no bulletin files, so the row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing. The 25 October issue has a text layer; its masthead names the parish alone, with both churches' addresses over one Mass schedule.

## Worship sites

Two churches: St. Monica (2422 South 17th Street) and St. Edmond (2130 South 21st Street), the directory's 'Worship Site' since the 2014 merger. The bulletin's Mass box gives St. Edmond its own Saturday 4 PM and Sunday 10 AM Masses and a 10 AM holy day Mass, so it still holds public Mass. GCatholic lists St. Edmond (19119) and St. Monica (19136) as separate churches, matching.
