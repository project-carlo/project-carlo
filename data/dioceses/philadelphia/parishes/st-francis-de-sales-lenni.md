## Publisher

St. Francis de Sales, Lenni publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 105). The 25 October 2026 issue (image only, 8 pages; pages 1-2 rendered) has a cover headed 'St. Francis de Sales ... From the little church on the hillside of Lenni' and lists the parish's own Masses for the week. Page 2 is the parish office page and names Mr. Mark Griswold as Parish Life Director.

**One bulletin or two with St. George, Glenolden?** Two. Mr. Griswold is Parish Life Director of both (directory pages of both parishes, read 2026-10-28; CatholicPhilly's profile of 2025-12-05 describes him as Parish Life Director at St. Francis de Sales since July 2025), but the two churches are about 10 km apart and publish separate issues: different John Patrick listings (105 and 112), different files (md5 5a738c8c... and f6949703...), different mastheads, and different Mass schedules (Lenni Saturday 5:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 AM, weekdays 8:30 AM; Glenolden Saturday 4:30 PM, Sunday 8:00 and 10:30 AM, weekdays 4:00 PM). Neither bulletin lists the other's Masses. See the `publication_reviews`.

**And with St. Joseph, Aston?** Also separate. The CatholicPhilly profile says St. Joseph's pastor, Msgr. Brian Bransfield, had been Lenni's parochial administrator before he was reassigned to St. Eleanor, Collegeville; Aston now has its own Parochial Administrator and its own 12-page issue.

## Worship sites

One: St. Francis de Sales Church, 33 New Road (see `churches/st-francis-de-sales-lenni`). The bulletin lists no other Mass location, and the directory page names no worship site. The parish campus also has a cemetery, a convent and a former gym used as a warehouse (CatholicPhilly 2025-12-05), none a place of Mass.

## Website and bulletin provider

sfdslenni.org is an eCatholic site (ecatholic:2405). Its home page links the John Patrick listing, and its own /bulletins archive stops at 16 March 2025, so the row follows the listing with recipe `page-pdfs https://www.church-bulletin.org/saint-francis-de-sales-church-lenni-pa-19052`.
