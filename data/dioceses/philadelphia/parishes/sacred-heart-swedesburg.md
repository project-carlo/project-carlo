## Publisher

Sacred Heart Parish (pastor Rev. Peter J. DiMaria) takes its weekly bulletin from John Patrick Publishing, listing 143; its site `unitedinthesacredheart.com` links the listing's dated PDFs and the `jppc.net/onlinebulletins/143template.pdf` template from its Bulletins page. The 25 October 2026 issue is image-only (no text layer), so `fetch:check` can't verify its identity from text, by design; pages 1-2 were rendered and read for the masthead and Mass panel. The site's own `/images/` uploads stop in early 2025, so the listing is the current channel, as `page-pdfs` on the listing. The row is `other`.

## Worship sites

One church: Sacred Heart, 120 Jefferson Street. The 25 October 2026 masthead panel, the site's Mass & Confession Times page and the About page all give Masses at that church only. The directory says the parish absorbed two more in 2014, and the closed-parish pages for both still read 'Worship site of Sacred Heart Church, Swedesburg', but neither holds Mass:

- **St. Augustine, Ford St., Bridgeport:** the 7 December 2016 relegation decree (effective 11 January 2017) says it served as a worship site of Sacred Heart from July 2014 until December 2015, 'beginning in January 2015, Sunday Masses were discontinued at both worship sites', and 'no divine worship of any kind has taken place at Saint Augustine Church' since December 2015 (mold). Excluded `closed`; the parish keeps the St. Augustine Cemetery, 175 W. DeKalb Pike, King of Prussia (a cemetery, not a worship site).
- **Our Lady of Mount Carmel (Italian), 500 Ford St., Bridgeport:** the same decree says Sunday Masses were discontinued there in January 2015. The property was then acquired by the Personal Ordinariate of the Chair of St. Peter: St. John the Baptist Catholic Church, 502 Ford Street, 'formed in 2015 after acquiring the property of the former Our Lady of Mount Carmel parish'. It is not a Sacred Heart site and not in this archdiocese's jurisdiction; excluded `other-jurisdiction`.

The parish's directory 'Founded 1907 (Polish parish)' is the Swedesburg church's own history (GCatholic: 1907.01, Language: Polish).
