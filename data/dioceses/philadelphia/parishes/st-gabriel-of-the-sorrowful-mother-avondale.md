## Publisher

St. Gabriel of the Sorrowful Mother Parish, Avondale, is printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 247, 'The Church of Saint Gabriel of the Sorrowful Mother', Avondale, PA 19311); the newest issue on 2026-10-28 is 25 October 2026. The PDF is an image with no text layer; page 1 was rendered and read. It carries the parish name and Gap Newport Pike address, the staff list and its own Mass Times box (Saturday vigil 4:15 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 am, weekday 9:00 am, Holy Days 9:00 am and 7:00 pm). The parish site's 'Current Bulletin' link goes to the listing and the site hosts no PDFs, so the listing is the only channel. The parish of the same patron in Stowe (Montgomery County) is a different parish with its own listing (85); this row is not that one.

## Worship sites

One church, 8910 Gap Newport Pike. The bulletin's Mass Times box lists St. Gabriel Church alone. 'Mision Santa Maria, Madre de Dios', 29 Gap Newport Pike, Avondale, which the directory lists as a separate parish (a donation center of St. Rocco), is not mentioned on this parish's bulletin and is not a worship site here (it is recorded under St. Rocco, Avondale).
