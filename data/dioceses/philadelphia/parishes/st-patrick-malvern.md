## Publisher

St. Patrick Parish, Malvern, hosts its own weekly bulletin as dated PDFs on its WordPress site (`/bulletin/`, files `<yyyymmdd>B.pdf`), newest first. The 25 October 2026 issue has a text layer: page 1 is headed "St. Patrick Catholic Church, 131 Channing Avenue, Malvern", the Liturgy Schedule lists Masses at one place. It names this parish alone. The pastor, Msgr. Joseph P. Duncan, is named by no other parish. The directory lists no John Patrick listing for it; none was found for Malvern in the 1-1300 scan.
