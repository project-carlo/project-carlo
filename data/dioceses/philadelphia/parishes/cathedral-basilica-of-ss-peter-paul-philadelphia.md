## Publisher

The cathedral parish publishes through John Patrick Publishing: listing 62 carries a weekly dated PDF with a text layer, and the 25 October 2026 issue is the newest. The row reads the listing with `page-pdfs`. The parish's own site (cathedralphila.org) is the `website`.

## One bulletin or several?

One, for the cathedral alone. Father Gill is also Parochial Administrator of St. Agnes-St. John Nepomucene (Slovak) and St. Andrew (Lithuanian), whose directory pages put their rectory "c/o Cathedral Parish Office, 1723 Race Street". The cathedral's 25 October masthead, Mass schedule and intentions name only the cathedral, shrine and Cathedral Chapel, and nothing in the 8 pages mentions either ethnic parish or St. Casimir. A shared pastor does not make a shared bulletin (Rules settled), so the two ethnic parishes are separate rows (`parishes/st-agnes-st-john-nepomucene-philadelphia.md`, `parishes/st-andrew-philadelphia.md`).

## Worship sites

One: the Cathedral Basilica, which is also the Shrine of St. Katharine Drexel (a shrine in a parish church is that church's row). The "Cathedral Chapel" where the Spanish Mass and weekday Masses are said is a room of the same building (`churches/cathedral-basilica-of-ss-peter-paul-philadelphia.md`).
