## Publisher

The parish publishes its own bulletin: the eCatholic archive of `www.assumptionbvmwestgrove.org` (site 3092) carries the 25 October 2026 issue, and John Patrick Publishing's listing 229 carries the same issue. The two share date, Mass schedule and staff, so the row is `ecatholic` with the listing as a second `url:` publication id. The directory-named pastor of St. Rocco, Msgr. Depman, is 'In Residence' in this bulletin's staff block only; it is a separate bulletin from St. Rocco's.

## Worship sites

One church: Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary, 300 State Road. The bulletin's Mass times (Saturday 4:30 pm; Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 am; weekdays 8:15 am) name no other place. The original church of 1873, 'Saint Mary's Chapel', was relegated to profane use effective 11 January 2017 and is excluded (`excluded/assumption-of-the-blessed-virgin-mary-original-church-west-grove`).
