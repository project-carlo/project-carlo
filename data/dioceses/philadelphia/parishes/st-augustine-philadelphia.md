## Publisher

St. Augustine Parish (Augustinian Friars, in Old City since 1796) posts its own weekly bulletin on its Squarespace site, `/bulletins`, newest first, as `/s/210-Bulletin_<Month>-<day>-<year>.pdf`. John Patrick Publishing's listing 210 carries the same 25 October 2026 issue in another layout, so either channel is current. The row reads the parish's own page (`page-pdfs`) and records the listing as the second publication id.

**One bulletin or several?** One, for this parish alone. The masthead names St. Augustine only and the Mass box lists one church. The directory names no shared pastor (Fr. Drennen, O.S.A., is Parochial Administrator here only).

## Worship sites

One: St. Augustine Church, 4th St. south of Vine St. (the bulletin's GPS address is 250 N. 4th Street; 243 N. Lawrence Street is the office and rectory). See `churches/st-augustine-philadelphia.md`.

## Website and bulletin provider

st-augustinechurch.com is a Squarespace site; `probe url` reads it (HTTP 200). Its `/bulletins` page lists every issue back to July 2015, newest first.
