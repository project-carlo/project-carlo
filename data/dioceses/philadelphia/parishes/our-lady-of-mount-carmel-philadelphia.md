## Publisher

Our Lady of Mount Carmel publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 128. The 25 October 2026 issue (image only) has a cover headed 'Our Lady of Mount Carmel, 2319 South 3rd Street', its pastor and the Mass times (Saturday 4PM, Sunday 8AM and 10:30AM, weekdays Mon-Fri 7:30AM, holy days 6:30AM and 7:30AM 'Church'), which match the directory. It names no other parish; the school it lists (Our Lady of Hope Regional School) is a regional school.

## Worship sites

One. The holy-day line's '(Church)' is the one building at 3rd and Wolf, not a second site. This parish is not the Bridgeport Our Lady of Mount Carmel (500 Ford St., excluded as an Ordinariate church).
