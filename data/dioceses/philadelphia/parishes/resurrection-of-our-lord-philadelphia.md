## Publisher

Resurrection of Our Lord Parish publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 268). The parish site links the listing's `jppc.net/onlinebulletins/268template.pdf` and hosts no PDFs, so the listing is the channel. The 25 October 2026 issue is image-only; page 1 is the masthead 'Resurrection of Our Lord Church, Under the Protection of Our Lady of Ransom', with the Mass schedule and staff.

**One bulletin or several?** One bulletin. It covers the merged parish (Our Lady of Ransom merged into Resurrection on 19 June 2017); the masthead's subtitle names Our Lady of Ransom as patron, not a Mass site. Our Lady of Ransom has no bulletin of its own and its church was relegated in 2019 (excluded record `our-lady-of-ransom-philadelphia`).

## Worship sites

One: Resurrection of Our Lord Church, Castor Ave. and Vista St., with an Upper Church and a Lower Church in the same building (page 2 names both for the week's lamps and bread and wine). The 'Blessed Sacrament' chapel at the Shelmire ramp entrance is adoration only (Monday-Friday 7:00 AM-5:00 PM); no Mass is listed there.
