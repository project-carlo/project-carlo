## Publisher

Immaculate Conception B.V.M. Parish, 602 West Ave., Jenkintown, publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 145. The 25 October 2026 issue (12 pages, image-only) has the masthead 'IMMACULATE CONCEPTION BVM' with the parish office at 606 West Avenue and a Mass box for this parish alone (Saturday vigil 4:30 PM; Sunday 7:30, 9:30, 11:30 AM and 4:30 PM). The pastor on the cover is Reverend Joseph E. Howarth.

## One bulletin or several?

One row, one church. No other parish shares the masthead or the Mass box, the directory names no worship site or chapel, and GCatholic has a single church for the parish.
