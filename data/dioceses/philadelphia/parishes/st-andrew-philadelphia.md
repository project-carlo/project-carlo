## Publisher

The parish keeps its own website, saintandrewlithuanianparish.org, which gives the Sunday Mass schedule, contacts and a parish-hall and school notice but no bulletin: no link, PDF or image. It has no John Patrick listing, and the cathedral's 25 October 2026 bulletin does not mention it. The row is `no_bulletin` (`bulletin_status: not-found`), kept in the records and left off the site until a readable bulletin appears (Settled policy 4).

## One bulletin or several?

Separate from the cathedral and from St. Agnes-St. John Nepomucene. Father Gerald Dennis Gill administers all three and their rectory is the Cathedral Parish Office, but a shared pastor is not a shared bulletin (Rules settled), and the cathedral's masthead, Mass schedule and intentions name only the cathedral. The site says the Holy Day Masses are 'available at your local parishes', not at the cathedral by name.

## Worship sites

Two: St. Andrew Church, 19th and Wallace Sts. (`churches/st-andrew-philadelphia.md`), with the 11:00 AM Mass in Lithuanian and English, and St. Casimir Church, 324 Wharton St., a worship site of this parish with the 9:00 AM English Mass (`churches/st-casimir-philadelphia.md`). St. Casimir's own parish closed in 2011 (directory: 'Closed parish: 1893-2011'), but the site still shows Mass there.
