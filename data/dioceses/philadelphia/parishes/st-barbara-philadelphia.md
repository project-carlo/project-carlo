## Publisher

St. Barbara publishes its own weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site (id 21577): `https://stbarbphila.org/bulletins` links one PDF per Sunday, newest `files.ecatholic.com/21577/bulletins/20261025.pdf`. The 25 October 2026 issue is two pages with St. Barbara's own masthead (5359 Lebanon Avenue, office 215-473-1044) and its own Mass box. John Patrick listing 152 for this parish has no editions, so the eCatholic archive is the channel.

## One bulletin with St. Cyprian?

No. The same priest, Msgr. Federico A. Britto, is Parochial Administrator here and Pastor of St. Cyprian (directory pages of both, 2026-10-28), but the two parishes print separate issues on the same Sunday: this 2-page issue, and St. Cyprian's 8-page `bulletin_10-25.pdf` on saintcyprianchurch.org, each with its own address, staff list and Mass schedule (St. Cyprian's lists 8:00 and 11:00 AM English, 1:00 PM Igbo, 3:30 PM Francophone). They are separate rows; see `publication_reviews` in st-cyprian-philadelphia. St. Ignatius of Loyola, also under Msgr. Britto, publishes a third bulletin.
