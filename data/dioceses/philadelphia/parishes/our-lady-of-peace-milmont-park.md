## Publisher

Our Lady of Peace Parish, Milmont Park, publishes its own weekly bulletin printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 109). Its 25 October 2026 issue (text layer) names Our Lady of Peace Catholic Parish, 208 Milmont Avenue, and its Mass schedule box covers both Our Lady of Peace and the Notre Dame de Lourdes worship site.

## Worship sites

Two with public Mass in the 25 October 2026 schedule: Our Lady of Peace Church (208 Milmont Avenue) and Notre Dame de Lourdes, 1000 Fairview Road, Swarthmore (Sunday 9:30 AM, Saturday and Friday 8:30 AM; the bulletin also says the Tuesday 10 November Mass will be at Notre Dame de Lourdes). Notre Dame de Lourdes also has its own John Patrick listing, 12 (church-bulletin.org/notre-dame-de-lourdes-church-swarthmore-pa-19081), with no editions at all, so it is not a separate publisher. See `churches/notre-dame-de-lourdes-swarthmore`.
