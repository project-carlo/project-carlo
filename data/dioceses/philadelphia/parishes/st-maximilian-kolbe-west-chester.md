## Publisher

St. Maximilian Kolbe Church, 15 E. Pleasant Grove Rd., West Chester, publishes its own weekly bulletin on its eCatholic site (id 2560). The 25 October 2026 issue (12 pages, mostly images; page 1 rendered and read) is headed "St. Maximilian Kolbe Church / October 25, 2026" and lists the Mass times. The directory gives the pastor as Rev. Christopher J. Papa; no other West Chester parish names him.

## Worship sites

One: the church (see its church record). The directory page names no worship site or mission. The bulletin's sanctuary-lamp list names a "CHAPEL" as well as the "CHURCH", but no schedule lists a Mass there; the parish's Mass page (stmax.org/mass-confession-times, read 2026-10-28) gives one set of times with one church address. Treated as a chapel within the same campus, not a separate site.

## Website and bulletin provider

The John Patrick Publishing listing the survey flagged (id 124, "Saint Maximilian Kolbe Church") has no editions, only a daily-readings page. The parish publishes on its own eCatholic site instead: the Bulletin page lists every Sunday issue to 25 October 2026. Provider `ecatholic`, id 2560. The PDF is largely image pages.
