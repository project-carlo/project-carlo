## Publisher

Saints Peter and Paul Parish, 1325 Boot Rd., West Chester, publishes its own weekly bulletin. The 25 October 2026 issue (12 pages, image-only) has a cover headed "Saints Peter and Paul Parish / October 25, 2026 / Thirtieth Sunday in Ordinary Time" with the Boot Road address, and page 2 gives the parish's Mass schedule (Saturday vigil 4:30 PM; Sunday 7:30, 9:30, 11:30 AM; weekdays and Saturday 8:30 AM), which agrees with the directory's mass_times. The directory gives the pastor as Rev. Michael J. Reilly; no other West Chester parish names him, so there is no shared-pastor lead.

## Worship sites

One: Sts. Peter and Paul Church (see its church record). The directory page lists no worship site, and the Mass schedule on page 2 lists no other place. GCatholic lists one church (19217).
