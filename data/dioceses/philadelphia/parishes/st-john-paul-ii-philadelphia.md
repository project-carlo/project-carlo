## Publisher

St. John Paul II Parish (Rev. James P. Olson, Pastor; rectory 2645 E. Allegheny Ave., 19134) is the 2019 merger of Nativity BVM, St. Adalbert, St. George and Mother of Divine Grace (Our Lady Help of Christians merged into Nativity in 2016). The directory lists no website and no bulletin. The parish did publish one bulletin for all its churches: the 20 April 2025 issue on stadalbert.org carries a single Mass-intentions list tagged SA, SG, N and MDG and is payable to 'St. John Paul II Parish'. That page stops there, so no current issue was found. One row, `no_bulletin`, `bulletin_status: not-found`: the row stays in the records, off the site, until a current channel turns up.

## Worship sites

Four churches hold weekly Mass by the directory's schedule (updated about June 2026) and by the April 2025 bulletin: St. Adalbert (primary site, rectory and Polish apostolate), Nativity BVM, St. George and Mother of Divine Grace (MDG); each has its own church record. St. Adalbert's page and Facebook (a live broadcast on 2026-10-29, 'St. John Paul II Parish - St. Adalbert Church') are current; the other three rest on the directory and the 2025 bulletin. Our Lady Help of Christians was relegated to profane use effective 2018-06-04 (excluded record). The directory's schedule is the only 2026 source for St. George, Nativity BVM and MDG.

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish has no site. `website` points at the Bulletin page under the 'St. John Paul Parish' menu of the Mother of Divine Grace school's eCatholic site (motherdivinegrace.com), which is where the parish's own pages (history, weekly Mass videos, donations) live; its archive is empty. stadalbert.org is the St. Adalbert church site and carried the bulletin until April 2025. nativity-bvm.org is Nativity BVM in Media, a different parish. No John Patrick, ParishesOnline or Bon Venture channel exists (research above).
