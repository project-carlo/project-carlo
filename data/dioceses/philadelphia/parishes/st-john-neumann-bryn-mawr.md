## Publisher

St. John Neumann Parish, Bryn Mawr, publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 153). The 25 October 2026 issue has a text layer and is headed 'St. John Neumann Parish'; page 1 gives the parish office (380 Highland Lane), staff (Pastor Rev. Msgr. Michael J. Matz) and the Mass schedule (Saturday vigil 5:15 pm; Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 10:30 am and 12:00 pm; weekdays 7:30 am, Saturday 8:00 am). It names this parish alone. Its pastor is named by no other parish.

**Not shared with Our Mother of Good Counsel**, the other Bryn Mawr parish in this batch: different pastor (Rev. Joseph S. Mostardi, O.S.A.), different mastheads and listings (153 and 277), each parish's site linking only its own. See `our-mother-of-good-counsel-bryn-mawr`.

## Worship sites

One: St. John Neumann Church. The church is under renovation ('Celebrating Our Faith, Embracing Our Future' campaign; HVAC overbuild, floor and heating work inside the church, a new school building), and the bulletin refers to 'temporary spaces' only for the livestream equipment, but its Mass schedule names no other location and the weekly Mass intentions run unchanged. The Sunday 5:15 pm Mass was suspended from July 2026 (parish site). The 'Gathering Room Chapel' holds Tuesday confessions and the Monday rosary, not Mass.

## Website and bulletin provider

sjnparish.org (WordPress). Its Bulletin Library links the John Patrick template for 153 and posts its own PDFs a week late. Recipe `page-pdfs https://www.church-bulletin.org/saint-john-neumann-church-bryn-mawr-pa-19010`.
