## Publisher

St. Malachy (1429 N. 11th St., North Philadelphia) publishes its own weekly bulletin on its WordPress site's Bulletins page. The 1 November 2026 issue (page 1) is headed 'SAINT MALACHY PARISH, Rev. Matthew Biedrzycki, Pastor' with the parish's own address and Mass box. The page lists the newest issue embedded and dated links below it, newest first.

The John Patrick Publishing listing 211 for the parish has no editions, so the parish site is the channel. St. Martin de Porres shares a priest's residence with St. Malachy (the directory says its pro tem pastor 'resides at Saint Malachy Philadelphia') and the two bulletins mention each other's events (a joint Forty Hours opening, 1-3 November), but each has its own masthead and Mass schedule: see `publication_reviews`.

## Worship sites

Two, taken from the 1 November 2026 Mass box rather than the directory: St. Malachy Church (`churches/st-malachy-philadelphia.md`) with Sunday Masses at 10AM and 7PM, and the Temple Catholic Newman Center, 2129 N. Broad St., with the weekday Masses the box lists (`churches/temple-newman-center-philadelphia.md`). Not recorded: the directory's 'Saturday, 9 AM (September - May)', which the bulletin's Mass box does not print; and the Gesu church (merged into St. Malachy in 1993, 'its church remains as a chapel for St. Joseph's Preparatory School', CatholicPhilly 2018-05-11), which no bulletin or site lists as a Mass site here.
