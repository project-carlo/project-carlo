## Publisher

St. Ann Parish publishes its own weekly bulletin. The 25 October 2026 issue (8 pages, image-only; pages 1, 2 and 6 rendered and read) is headed "Saint Ann Roman Catholic Church" with the parish office at 502 Main Street and phone 610-933-3732. Its Mass Times (page 6) list the Chapel and the Church of the parish; no other parish is named. The directory gives the pastor as Rev. Daniel J. Arechabala; no other parish in this batch names him. The other three bulletins of the batch (St. Mary of the Assumption, St. Basil the Great, St. Joseph) have their own mastheads, so the four rows are separate.

## Worship sites

Two places with public Mass: the Chapel (604 S. Main St.), where most Masses are held, and St. Ann Church (500 S. Main St.), used for the Sunday 7:00 AM, 9:30 AM Spanish and 4:00 PM Masses, weekday Friday and Saturday Mass and the monthly language Masses. Sacred Heart (Slovak) is no longer a worship site: the Decree of Relegation (effective 2023-01-23) says no Mass has been held there since March 2020; see its excluded record. The bulletin's Mass schedule lists no other site.
