## Publisher

St. Denis Parish, Havertown, publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 53). The 25 October 2026 issue is headed 'Church of Saint Denis' (page 1, image, rendered) and page 2 gives the parish's clergy, office hours and Weekend Mass Schedule (Saturday Vigil 5:00 pm; Sunday 6:30, 8:00, 9:30, 11:30 am; daily Mon-Sat 8:30 am, Mon-Fri 5:30 pm Sept.-June).

**One bulletin or two with Annunciation BVM?** Two. Rev. Kevin J. Gallagher is Pastor of both and lives at St. Denis (directory: 'In residence at Saint Denis'), and both parishes' pages 2 name him Pastor. But the two issues of 25 October 2026 are different files (8 pages each, md5 differs) with their own mastheads, Mass schedules, Mass intentions and sick lists, and each parish's own site links only its own listing (039 and 053). See `annunciation-bvm-havertown` for the other side and diocese.md's rule that a shared pastor is not a shared bulletin.

## Worship sites

One with public Mass: St. Denis Church. The Sacre Coeur Perpetual Adoration Chapel (dedicated 2026-06-12, GCatholic 153921 'Mission of: Church of St. Denis') is on this parish's campus, but holds adoration, not Mass: see `excluded/sacre-coeur-adoration-chapel-havertown`. The bulletin's schedule lists Masses at the church only.
