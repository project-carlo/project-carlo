## Publisher

Annunciation of the Blessed Virgin Mary Parish, Havertown, publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 39). The 25 October 2026 issue (page 1, an image) is headed 'Annunciation of the Blessed Virgin Mary, 401 Brookline Blvd., Havertown, PA 19083', and page 2 gives its own Mass Schedule (Saturday Vigil 4:30 PM; Sunday 7:30, 10:30 AM; weekdays 7:30 AM Mon/Wed/Fri, 6:30 AM Tue/Thu). The cover carries the same image as St. Denis's (the printer's shared weekly artwork, 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself', Matthew 22:39), but the masthead, schedule and contents are this parish's.

**One bulletin or two with St. Denis?** Two. Rev. Kevin J. Gallagher is Pastor of both (page 2 of each bulletin names him Pastor; the directory says 'In residence at Saint Denis'; the 2026 clerical appointments say he moved to Annunciation 'while continuing as Pastor, Saint Denis Parish'). His email domain appears on this parish's page 2 (pastor@stdenishavertown.org). But each parish has its own John Patrick listing (39 and 53), its own masthead, Mass schedule and intentions, and the files differ. The README's shared-pastor rule (diocese.md *Rules settled*) applies: one pastor does not make one bulletin.

## Worship sites

One: Annunciation Church (see its church record). The bulletin's Mass Schedule lists no other place. St. Denis's Sacre Coeur adoration chapel is St. Denis's (see its excluded record), not a site here.
