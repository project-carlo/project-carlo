## Publisher

Holy Cross, Mount Airy publishes weekly through John Patrick Publishing, listing 3 ('The Roman Catholic Community of Holy Cross', Philadelphia PA 19119). Its home page links `church-bulletin.org/?id=003`. The 25 October 2026 issue is image-only (8 pages, no text layer), so I rendered pages 1-2: the masthead reads 'Holy Cross Catholic Church, Our Catholic community in Mount Airy', and the Mass box lists one church, 140 East Mount Airy Ave. The page-2 footer code '02-003' names the listing. The listing's own current issue is the channel; the parish site hosts no PDFs.

## Worship sites

One church, Holy Cross. The 25 October Mass box lists Sunday 9:00 and 11:00 AM and 6:00 PM, Saturday Vigil 5:00 PM and weekday Mass Tuesday-Friday 8:00 AM, 'in chapel for summer (148 E Mt Airy Ave)': a room beside the church, not a second address, so it is on Holy Cross's row. The bulletin names none of the churches Holy Cross absorbed in July 2013: St. Madeleine Sophie and St. Therese of the Child Jesus were relegated to profane use effective 2017-01-11 (see their `excluded/` records). GCatholic's 'St. Michael the Archangel' at E Mount Airy Ave. & Chew Ave. is an Ordinariate congregation listed 'using the building' of Holy Cross; it is excluded as `other-jurisdiction` (`excluded/st-michael-the-archangel-philadelphia.md`).

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site (holycrossphl.org) links the John Patrick listing and hosts no bulletin files, so the row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing, as for the other John Patrick parishes here. `fetch:check` is recorded in the manifest.

## Not yet confirmed

The bulletin's attendance table has a 10 AM row where its Mass box says 11:00 am and records no 6 PM count; the parish site's home page gives Sunday 9am, 11am and 6pm, so I took the Mass box as right and the table as a slip. The weekday 'chapel for summer' location (convent chapel per the site, 148 E Mt Airy Ave per the bulletin) could change in winter.
