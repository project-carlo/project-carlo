## Publisher

St. Christopher Parish publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by Bon Venture Services (sponsor page 1904). The parish's eCatholic site (id 16547) has no bulletin archive of its own: its 'Weekly Parish Bulletins' menu entry and 'Latest Bulletin' button go to `http://sponsors.bonventure.net/?page=1904`, which lists dated PDFs (`pdfs/1904/<yyyy-mm-dd>.pdf`), next Sunday's early. The 25 October 2026 issue has a text layer and names Rev. Philip G. Bochanski, Pastor.

**One bulletin or several?** One bulletin for St. Christopher alone: the issue names no other parish and the directory names no shared pastor.
