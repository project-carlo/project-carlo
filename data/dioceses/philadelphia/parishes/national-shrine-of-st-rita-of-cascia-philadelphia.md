## Publisher

The National Shrine of St. Rita of Cascia (1166 S. Broad St., 19146) publishes its own bulletin: John Patrick listing 241, issue of 25 October 2026 (4 pages, image only). Its cover names the shrine, the Augustinian Province of St. Thomas of Villanova, an Executive Director and Augustinian friars, and gives its own Mass schedule; nothing in it names Annunciation B.V.M. The directory calls the site 'the National Shrine of St. Rita of Cascia (Open to the Public)' with its own Shrine Director and website.

**Parish row, not a church row under Annunciation.** St. Rita's parish merged into Annunciation B.V.M. in 2016 and its records are kept there, but Annunciation's bulletin (listing 127) lists no Mass at the shrine and its masthead is its own. A shrine in a former parish church whose own bulletin lists its Masses is a parish row (settled shrine rule, diocese.md); see publication_reviews.

## Worship sites

One: the shrine church (see national-shrine-of-st-rita-of-cascia-philadelphia). The cover's 'Lower Shrine' (Healing Mass confessions, Thursday adoration chapel) is the same building.

## Website and bulletin provider

saintritashrine.org (Squarespace) posts only the inside pages of each issue as /s/241_<mmddyy>.pdf (2 pages for 25 October); the cover with the Mass schedule is only on the John Patrick listing. Recipe `page-pdfs` on the listing.
