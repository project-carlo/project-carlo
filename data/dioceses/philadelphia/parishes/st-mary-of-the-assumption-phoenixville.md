## Publisher

St. Mary of the Assumption Parish publishes its own weekly bulletin, on its own site and through John Patrick Publishing (listing 18). The 24-25 October 2026 issue (image-only; pages 1 and 2 rendered and read) is headed "St. Mary of the Assumption, 212 Dayton Street" and its page 2 gives the clergy (Deacon Mark Nowakowski, Parish Life Director, since 2025 per the directory), the Worship Sites and the Mass Schedule. The other three bulletins in the batch name their own parishes.

## Worship sites

Two churches, both listed in the bulletin's Worship Sites: St. Mary Church (100 St. Mary's Street; Sunday 8:15 and 10:45 AM) and Holy Trinity Church (the former Holy Trinity (Polish) parish, 219 Dayton Street; Saturday 4:00 PM vigil and Monday-Thursday 8:00 AM, also Wednesday confession and adoration). The directory's "Worship Site: Holy Trinity Church" is confirmed by the current bulletin.
