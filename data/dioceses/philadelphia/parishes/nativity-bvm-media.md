The 25 October 2026 issue (image only, 12 pages; pages 1-2 rendered) has a cover headed 'Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary Church' with the pastor, Rev. Edward H. Bell, and the parish's own Mass and worship schedule.

## Worship sites
