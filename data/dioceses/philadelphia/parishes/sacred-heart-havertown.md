## Publisher

Sacred Heart Parish ('God's Family in Manoa'), Havertown, publishes its own weekly bulletin printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 169). The 25 October 2026 issue (page 1, image, rendered) is headed 'Sacred Heart Church, Havertown, PA' with its own staff list (Rev John B. Flanagan, Pastor), Mass Schedule and contact address, 105 Wilson Ave. It names this parish alone. Its pastor is named by no other parish.

## Worship sites

One: Sacred Heart Church (see its church record). The cover also lists a 'Eucharistic Adoration Chapel' (Mon-Fri 9-7, Sat 9-3:30) and a Divine Mercy Chaplet: the parish's own adoration, inside its campus, no separate Mass. **Sacre Coeur is not Sacred Heart's.** GCatholic lists 'Sacre Coeur' (153921) next to this parish only by the name; GCatholic itself says 'Mission of: Church of St. Denis, Havertown' and its pin is 110 m from St. Denis and 2.2 km from Sacred Heart, and CatholicPhilly says the name was chosen 'to help distinguish the chapel from nearby Sacred Heart Parish' (2026-06-05).
