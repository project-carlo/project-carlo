## Publisher

St. Philip Neri publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 51; the parish site's Bulletin page only links the listing's template. The 25 October 2026 issue is image only: its cover names St. Philip Neri (218 Queen St.) and, in the drawing's captions, St. Stanislaus (240 Fitzwater St.) under Rev. Edward P. Kuczynski; page 2 gives the week's Masses.

## Worship sites

One with Mass: St. Philip Neri Church, 218 Queen St. Page 2 of the bulletin gives times without naming a church, but the parish site's Mass Times page puts every Mass (weekday 7:30am, Saturday 8:00am, vigil 4:30pm, Sunday 10:00am) 'at St. Philip Neri', and the directory says 'All Masses at St. Philip Neri.' The bulletin's mission text calls St. Stanislaus one of 'our two churches', but no source gives it a Mass time; see `open/st-stanislaus-philadelphia`. The Saint John Neumann Chapel named under 'Sanctuary Lamps' is inside the main church, not a second site.
