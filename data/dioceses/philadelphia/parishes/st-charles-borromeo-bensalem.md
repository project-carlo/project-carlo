## Publisher

St. Charles Borromeo Parish, Bensalem (Rev. Philip M. Forlano, Pastor) prints with John Patrick Publishing: listing 278 has an issue for 25 October 2026. That PDF is image-only; rendered, page 1's masthead reads 'St. Charles Borromeo Parish / Our Lady of Fatima Worship Site / 1731 Hulmeville Road, Bensalem, PA 19020' beside one Mass schedule, and page 2 is the parish office and school page. The masthead names this parish alone (the 'Worship Site' line is the merged parish's, below).

## Worship sites

One church row: St. Charles Borromeo Church (Bristol Pike and Hulmeville Rd.). The 2014 merger made Our Lady of Fatima (Street Rd.) a worship site: CatholicPhilly's 14 September 2014 report says Spanish Masses 'will be offered at both St. Charles and at Our Lady of Fatima Church' (Sat 7 PM, Sun noon, Sun 10 AM English). The 2026 bulletins keep 'Our Lady of Fatima Worship Site' in the masthead but place no Mass at Street Road: the schedule has no location column and the parish site's Mass schedule speaks only of St. Charles Borromeo Church. At 2913 Street Rd. the bulletin lists the 'Fatima Catholic Outreach Center' (English classes, legal clinics, 'St. Charles Thrift Store' in the former Our Lady of Fatima school at 2915 Street Rd.), and GCatholic (2025-12-21) says a Ukrainian Catholic mission uses the Fatima church building. Nothing read says Mass is still celebrated at Fatima for this parish, so that building is an open record (`our-lady-of-fatima-bensalem`), not a church row of this one.
