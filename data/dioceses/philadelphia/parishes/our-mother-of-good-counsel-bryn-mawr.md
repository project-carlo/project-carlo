## Publisher

Our Mother of Good Counsel Parish, Bryn Mawr, publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 277). The 25 October 2026 issue has no text layer; page 1 (rendered) is headed 'Our Mother of Good Counsel, Parish Community' with the office at 31 Pennswood Road and Fr. Joseph S. Mostardi, OSA, Pastor. It names this parish alone. The Sunday and weekday Mass schedule box has OMGC's Masses and, below them, St. Thomas of Villanova Parish's (Rosemont Chapel 4:30 pm vigil, 9:00 am and weekday Masses; Villanova Church 7:30 and 10:30 am): the two parishes print each other's box. Those are STOV's worship sites; see `st-thomas-of-villanova-villanova`.

**Not shared with St. John Neumann**, the other Bryn Mawr parish: see `st-john-neumann-bryn-mawr`.

## Worship sites

One: Our Mother of Good Counsel Church, 31 Pennswood Road. The Rosemont Chapel and Villanova Church in the cover box belong to St. Thomas of Villanova.
