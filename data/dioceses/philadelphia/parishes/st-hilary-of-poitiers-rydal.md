## Publisher

St. Hilary of Poitiers Parish, 820 Susquehanna Road, Rydal, publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 160. The 25 October 2026 issue (8 pages, image-only) has the cover 'St. Hilary of Poitiers Parish, 820 Susquehanna Road, Rydal, PA 19046', and page 2's weekly intentions give Saturday 5:00pm vigil, Sunday 8:30am and 10:30am, weekday 8:00am. Msgr. Fairbanks signs the page-2 reflection.

## One bulletin or several?

One row, one church. No other parish or church is named on the cover or among the intentions, and the directory and GCatholic name no worship site.

## Worship sites

One church: St. Hilary of Poitiers Church, 820 Susquehanna Rd. GCatholic gives 920 Susquehanna Rd.; OSM puts the school at 920 and the church at 820 (see the church record).

## Website and bulletin provider

sthilarypoitiers.org is a Wix site. Its Bulletins page links the listing's template and also hosts the same issues under `_files/ugd/` hashes, oldest first. The listing is the newest-first channel (the Wix page's first file is January 2026), so the row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing; the Wix copy of 25 October is byte-identical.
