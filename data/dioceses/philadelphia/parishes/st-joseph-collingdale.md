## Publisher

St. Joseph Parish, Collingdale, publishes its own weekly bulletin printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 50). The 25 October 2026 issue has a text layer; its page 1 masthead names Saint Joseph Church, 500 Woodlawn Avenue, Collingdale and Rev. Thomas M. Sodano as pastor. It mentions Blessed Virgin Mary only as a 31 October intention at BVM (a neighbouring parish's Mass), so it is not shared with BVM, which has its own ParishesOnline bulletin (04/0756).
