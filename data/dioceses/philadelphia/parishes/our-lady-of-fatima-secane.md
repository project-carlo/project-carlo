## Publisher

Our Lady of Fatima Parish, Secane, publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 83). The 25 October 2026 issue has a text layer and a masthead naming Our Lady of Fatima Parish, 1 Fatima Drive, and Rev. Amalraj Sawarimuthu, SdC, Administrator. The parish site links only the listing's template.

**One bulletin or several?** One bulletin for Fatima alone. The directory names no shared pastor, and Fatima is in a different deanery from the Springfield parishes.

## Worship sites

One: Our Lady of Fatima Church, 1 Fatima Drive. The bulletin and site mention a chapel (Saturday 8:00 AM Reconciliation 'in Chapel'; St. John Neumann Chapel for Thursday adoration, Flame of Love and Sodality devotions), but give no Mass there. The weekday 8:30 AM Mass is listed without a location. The chapel is on the same grounds as far as the bulletin shows, and is not a separate church row.
