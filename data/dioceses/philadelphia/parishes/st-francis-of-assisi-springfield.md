## Publisher

St. Francis of Assisi Parish, Springfield, publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 175). The 25 October 2026 issue has a text layer; its masthead names St. Francis of Assisi Roman Catholic Church and Rev. Matthew J. Tralies as Pastor. The parish site's /bulletins/ page links the listing's template and hosts only single-page inserts, so the listing is the channel.

**One bulletin or several?** One bulletin for St. Francis alone. Holy Cross and St. Kevin, the other Springfield parishes, publish their own files (see `holy-cross-springfield`'s `publication_reviews`). The directory names no shared pastor.

## Worship sites

One: St. Francis of Assisi Church, Saxer Avenue. The bulletin's Mass schedule (Sunday 7:30, 9:30, 11:30 AM; Saturday vigil 4:00 PM; weekdays 8:00 AM) lists no other location.
