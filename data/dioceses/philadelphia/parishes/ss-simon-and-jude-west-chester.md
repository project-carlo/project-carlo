## Publisher

Saints Simon and Jude Parish, Route 3 and Route 352 (8 Cavanaugh Court), West Chester, publishes its bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 94). The 25 October 2026 issue (12 pages, image-only; page 1 rendered and read) is headed "SAINTS SIMON AND JUDE ... 8 Cavanaugh Court, West Chester, PA". The directory gives the pastor as Rev. Michael J. Gerlach; no other West Chester parish names him. The directory also lists the Meehan Center (8 Cavanaugh Court, www.meehancenter.org), the parish's hall and school building, with no Mass.
