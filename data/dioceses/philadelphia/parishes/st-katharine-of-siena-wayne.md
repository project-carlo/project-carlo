## Publisher

St. Katharine of Siena publishes its own weekly bulletin, which John Patrick Publishing lists as id 213 ("Saint Katharine of Siena Parish", Wayne, PA 19087). The 25 October 2026 issue (8 pages, image only) was rendered and page 1 read: masthead "ST. KATHARINE OF SIENA", Pastor Rev. Msgr. Hans A. L. Brouwers, a Mass box and the rectory address, 104 S. Aberdeen Avenue. It names this parish alone.

## Worship sites

One: St. Katharine of Siena Church (see its church record). The directory names no worship site or mission, and the bulletin box lists Mass at one place.

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory's website (sksparish.org) redirects to www.stkatharineofsiena.org, a WordPress site that posts each issue on its own post page and hosts the same PDF the John Patrick listing does (byte-identical for 25 October 2026). The post pages carry no direct PDF links on the /parish-bulletin/ index, so the row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the John Patrick listing, which has the file at a stable URL. John Patrick PDFs have no text layer, so `fetch:check` reports publication identity unverified by design.

## Not yet confirmed

Nothing outstanding for the bulletin. The directory page (updated two years ago) lists a Sunday 8 AM Mass the 25 October bulletin does not.
