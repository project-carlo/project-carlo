## Publisher

Holy Saviour Parish, Norristown (the Italian national parish founded 1903; pastor Msgr. Charles L. Sangermano) is distributed by John Patrick Publishing as listing 172. The issue of 25 October 2026 (8 pages, text layer) has the masthead 'Holy Saviour Parish', the parish's own Mass intentions and a Mass box for both of its churches. The parish's own site, holysaviour.org, links only the listing's `172template.pdf` and posts no files, so the listing is the channel. The row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing, as for the other John Patrick parishes in this diocese.

## Worship sites

Two churches, both listed in the 25 October 2026 bulletin's page-1 Mass box and page-2 intentions:

- **Holy Saviour Church, 409 E. Main St., Norristown**: Saturday vigil 5:15 PM, Sunday 7:30, 9:30 and 11:30 AM, weekdays 7:30 AM, Saturday 9:00 AM.
- **Our Lady of Mount Carmel Church (OLMC), 454 Fairfield Rd., Plymouth Township** (the bulletin says 'Blackhorse'; GCatholic 38959 files it under Plymouth Meeting 19462): Traditional Latin Mass every Sunday 11:30 AM and a 10:00 AM Mass (10:10 in the intentions) on the first Sunday. The directory calls it a 'Chapel of Holy Saviour Parish' and says the first-Sunday Mass runs October to May; the bulletin shows the Latin Mass weekly.

The pastor, bulletin, intentions and mailing address are Holy Saviour's, so OLMC is a worship site of this row and not a separate publisher. The Latin Mass at OLMC is on this parish's bulletin and no other bulletin in the batch (St. Patrick, St. Francis and Epiphany) mentions it.
