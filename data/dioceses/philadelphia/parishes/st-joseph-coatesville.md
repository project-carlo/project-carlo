## Publisher

St. Joseph Parish, Coatesville, publishes through eCatholic (site 37050): its own `/bulletins` page lists dated PDFs `files.ecatholic.com/37050/bulletins/<yyyymmdd>.pdf`, newest 25 October 2026, with a text layer. John Patrick Publishing's listing 239 (the survey's lead) carries an issue of the same date with the same page 1; the files differ byte for byte, so the listing is kept as a second publication id, not the channel.

The directory names Rev. Komlan D. Gnangba, F.M. for both this parish and Our Lady of the Rosary (each 'in residence at' the other), but the two bulletins are separate: each masthead names its own parish alone with its own Mass schedule (see `publication_reviews`).

## Worship sites

One: the church at 400 Charles St. The 25 October 2026 bulletin names no other site. The former St. Stanislaus Kostka church (201 W. Lincoln Hwy.), a worship site of this parish from 2012, was relegated effective 7 January 2019 and had no Mass since 2 July 2016 (see `excluded/st-stanislaus-kostka-coatesville`). Directory times (Saturday 5 and 7 PM Spanish, Sunday 8, 10, Noon, weekdays 8:30 and 7 PM) differ in detail from the bulletin; the bulletin wins.

## Website and bulletin provider

`www.stjosephcoatesville.org` is eCatholic-hosted (`probe url`: ecatholic:37050, links /bulletins); files are under `/bulletins/`.
