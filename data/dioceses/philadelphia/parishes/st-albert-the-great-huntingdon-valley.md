## Publisher

St. Albert the Great Parish, 212 Welsh Road, Huntingdon Valley, publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 139. The 25 October 2026 issue (12 pages, image-only) has the cover 'St. Albert the Great Church' and, on page 2, the clergy, Mass times (Saturday vigil 4:00 PM; Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 AM, 12:00 PM; weekdays 8:00 AM) and the 212 Welsh Road contact block.

## One bulletin or several?

One row, one church. No other parish shares the cover or Mass box.

## Worship sites

One church: St. Albert the Great Church, 212 Welsh Rd. A Syro-Malabar mission also uses this building (GCatholic 16982, the other-eastern batch, treated as `other-jurisdiction` per the diocese rule). That mission is not a Latin worship site of this parish and its Masses are not in this bulletin's pages 1-2, so this record holds only the parish's own church; the Latin church keeps its row either way.

## Website and bulletin provider

saintalthegreat.org is a John Patrick-hosted site; its bulletin link is the listing's template, so it hosts no PDFs. The row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing.

## Not yet confirmed

The Syro-Malabar mission's hours were not found in this bulletin's pages 1-2 or on the parish site (pages 3-12 are images and were not read). Nothing here depends on it.
