## Publisher

St. Patrick's weekly bulletin is on John Patrick Publishing listing 159 (church-bulletin.org), dated PDFs newest first. The parish's own Weebly site links the listing from its home page and keeps its archive in a Google Drive folder; the listing is current to 25 October 2026, so the row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing.

## One bulletin or several?
