## Publisher

St. Mary's Quasi-Parish (FSSP, Traditional Latin Mass; canon 516 quasi-parish established by Archbishop Chaput's decree of 15 March, per the parish's About page, which gives no year) publishes its own weekly bulletin, hosted on its Squarespace site at `https://www.stmarylatinmass.com/bulletin`. The 25 October 2026 issue (Christ the King) names Fr. Gismondi, FSSP and the parish's own Mass schedule and groups. It is not John Patrick's and is not shared with St. Matthew, whose own bulletin (listing 244) lists no Latin Mass; see `publication_reviews` on this row's peer, st-matthew-conshohocken.

## Worship sites

One church: St. Mary Church, the former Polish national parish church, which GCatholic files as 'St. Mary, W Elm St. & Oak St.' (Latin, served by the Priestly Fraternity of Saint Peter) and the directory as 140 W. Hector Street. They are the same place: OSM has a place_of_worship 'St. Mary Catholic Church' at 140 Oak Street, Conshohocken, 40.07415, -75.30886, which is GCatholic's Plus Code (87G63MFR+H9, about 40.0739, -75.3091), and the parish's own bulletin, site footer and phone give 140 West Hector Street as its address (the Hector Street and Oak Street frontages of one property, with the rectory). The GCatholic row therefore belongs to this church and not to a separate building. The About page lists the physical property as 'the church, rectory, garage/barn, parking lot and fields'. The 25 October bulletin lists no Mass outside St. Mary.

St. Matthew Parish owns the property: the Cosmas and Damian relegation decree (16 February 2016) says St. Matthew 'has responsibility for the upkeep of Saint Mary Church', and the bulletin prays for St. Mary 'to purchase the property of St. Mary from St. Matthew'. Ownership does not make the Masses St. Matthew's: its bulletin does not list them.

## Website and bulletin provider

Self-hosted Squarespace; `page-pdfs` on the `/bulletin` page, newest first, reads the `/s/<n>-<name>.pdf` files. The files have no dates in the names (numbered), so check each week that the first link is the newest; recipe recorded from `fetch:check` below.
