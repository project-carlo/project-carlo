## Publisher

St. Isaac Jogues publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 30 ("Saint Isaac Jogues Parish", Wayne, PA 19087). The 25 October 2026 issue (image only, so rendered) has the masthead "ST. ISAAC JOGUES" with Rev. Msgr. Albin J. Grous, 50 West Walker Road, and a Mass Schedule for this parish alone. It is a different file from the neighbouring parishes' issues and has a different masthead and schedule (see publication_reviews).

## Worship sites

One: St. Isaac Jogues Church (see its church record). The directory names no worship site, mission or chapel and the bulletin's Mass Schedule lists Mass at one place; its note that 'The Chapel will open at 8:00am with Exposition' refers to the adoration chapel in the same complex, not a separate Mass site (Daily Mass is listed once, 12:05pm).

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory's website (stisaac.org, Squarespace) has a Bulletins page, but its newest entry is July 17, 2022 (files /s/030template*.pdf, copies of John Patrick id 030 issues), so it is not the current channel; the home page links the John Patrick listing. The row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing. John Patrick PDFs have no text layer, so `fetch:check` reports publication identity unverified by design; the masthead was read by rendering page 1.
