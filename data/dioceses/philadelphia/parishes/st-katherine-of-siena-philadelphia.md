## Publisher

St. Katherine of Siena Parish publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 266). The parish site (stkatherineofsiena.org, built by Solutio) links the listing from its menu and its footer and hosts no PDFs, so the listing is the channel. The 25 October 2026 issue is image-only; page 1 is the masthead and Mass schedule, naming Rev. Joseph S. Zaleski, Pastor.

**One bulletin or several?** One bulletin for this parish alone: it names no other parish, and the directory names no shared pastor.

## Worship sites

One: St. Katherine of Siena Church, 9700 Frankford Avenue. The site has an 'Adoration Chapel' page; the bulletin's Mass schedule lists no Mass there, so it is not a church row.

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory's website, skschurch.com, redirects to an old Wix site (copyright 2025, 'under construction' notice) that links the same listing; `probe url` calls that REDIRECT-OFFSITE because the Wix tab title reads 'New Faith Gospel Church'. The current site, stkatherineofsiena.org, answers HTTP 403 to `probe url` and loads in the browser (`visit.py`). Recipe: `page-pdfs https://www.church-bulletin.org/saint-katherine-of-siena-philadelphia-pa-19114`.
