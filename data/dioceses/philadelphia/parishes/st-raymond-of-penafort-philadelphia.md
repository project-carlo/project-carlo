## Publisher

St. Raymond of Penafort publishes weekly through John Patrick Publishing, listing 21, which the Prayer & Worship page of its Wix site links as 'Weekly Church Bulletin' (jppc template 021). The 25 October 2026 issue is image-only, so I rendered pages 1-2: the masthead names St. Raymond of Penafort alone, with the parish office at 1350 Vernon Road, and the page-2 footer reads '021 Saint Raymond'.

## Worship sites

One church. The Mass lines (Saturday 5:00 PM vigil, Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 AM, weekdays 8:00 AM) are all at the one site; the site and directory agree and name no worship site or mission.
