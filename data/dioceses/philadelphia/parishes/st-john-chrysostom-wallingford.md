## Publisher

St. John Chrysostom, Wallingford publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 78). The 25 October 2026 issue is an 8-page PDF with a text layer, headed with the parish name and Rev. Edward J. Hallinan, Pastor.

## Worship sites

One: St. John Chrysostom Church, 615 S. Providence Road (see `churches/st-john-chrysostom-wallingford`). The weekly calendar lists Masses without a second location; its events mention a back church parking lot, a Stevenson Center, a Lower Church Hall and a gym, none a place of Mass.

## Website and bulletin provider

sjcparish.org is an eCatholic site (ecatholic:5277) whose bulletin page links the John Patrick template, so an eCatholic id is not the publication; the row follows the listing with recipe `page-pdfs https://www.church-bulletin.org/saint-john-chrysostom-parish-wallingford-pa-19086`.
