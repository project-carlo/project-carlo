## Worship sites

St. Benedict is the closed parish of 1922-2013 that the directory names a worship site; the current bulletin's Mass box confirms the Sunday Mass, so it is a live church (roster `worship-sites:st-benedict-philadelphia`, `gcatholic:19061`).
