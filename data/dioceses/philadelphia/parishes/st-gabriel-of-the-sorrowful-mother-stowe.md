## Publisher

St. Gabriel of the Sorrowful Mother Parish, Stowe, publishes its own bulletin: John Patrick Publishing listing 85 and the parish's own archive. The 25 October 2026 issue (page 1 rendered; no text layer) is headed with the parish name and its Stowe address and gives its own Mass times. The pastor shared with St. Thomas More (Rev. Matthew R. VanSmoorenburg, L.C., Parochial Administrator here since the July 2026 appointments) is named on the cover ("Pastor - St. Thomas More Parish"), but the two parishes publish separate bulletins with separate mastheads, Mass schedules and files, so they are two rows (see publication_reviews).

## Worship sites

One: St. Gabriel Church, 127 E. Howard St. (see its church record). The directory lists a Church Hall at the same address; no other worship site, and the bulletin Masses box names none.

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site is self-hosted WordPress; its archive runs a week behind the John Patrick listing, so the row reads the listing (`page-pdfs`). The PDF has no text layer, so `fetch:check` reports `publication_identity` unverified by design.
