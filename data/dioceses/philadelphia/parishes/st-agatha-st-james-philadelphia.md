## Publisher

St. Agatha-St. James (38th and Chestnut Sts., University City) and its Newman Center for Penn and Drexel publish one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 186 ('Saint Agatha - Saint James & Newman Center', Philadelphia PA 19104, latest edition 25 October 2026). The 25 October 2026 issue (4 pages, text layer) is headed 'St. Agatha St. James & Newman Center', 111 S. 38th Street, and its Mass Schedule box lists the Sunday Masses in the church and the daily and Saturday-vigil Masses in the chapel. It names no other parish.

## Worship sites

Two: the parish church at 38th and Chestnut Sts. (Sunday 9am, 11:30am, 5:30pm; Filipino vigil 1st Saturday 7pm) and the Newman Chapel on the second floor of the Newman Center, 111 S. 38th St. (weekday 12:05pm and 5:30pm, other Saturday vigils 5:30pm). GCatholic lists the chapel as 'Chapel of St. Bede, Newman Center' (id 19128, no address beyond Chestnut St.); neither the bulletin nor the parish site uses the name St. Bede, so the chapel row follows the Mass schedule and records the GCatholic name as the only source for it. GCatholic's 'Mission of: St. Agnes-St. John Nepomucene' on that entry is not supported by anything here (the bulletin and directory tie the Newman chapel to St. Agatha-St. James) and is probably a GCatholic error. See the church records.

## Website and bulletin provider

saintsaj.org (Squarespace) answered probe url with HTTP 200 and links its Bulletin page, which holds one PDF with a fixed name posted a week early; the record uses the listing as the `page-pdfs` channel.

## Not yet confirmed

The chapel's name (St. Bede) rests on GCatholic alone. Fr. Gokie also serves St. Francis de Sales as parochial vicar; the two parishes' bulletins are different issues, so they are separate rows.
