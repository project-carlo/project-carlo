## Publisher

St. George, Glenolden publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 112, 'Saint George Parish Community'). The 25 October 2026 issue (image only, 8 pages; pages 1-2 rendered) is headed 'St. George Church, 22 E. Cooke Avenue, Glenolden', gives its Mass schedule on page 2 and lists Mark Griswold as Parish Life Director with Rev. Edward Brady as parish priest.

**One bulletin or two with St. Francis de Sales, Lenni?** Two: see `st-francis-de-sales-lenni`, which records the comparison. Same Parish Life Director, different bulletins (listings 105 and 112, different files and mastheads, different Mass schedules).

## Worship sites

One: St. George Church (see `churches/st-george-glenolden`). The directory's closed-parish page for Holy Spirit, Sharon Hill still reads 'Worship site of St. George Church, Glenolden', but the Archbishop's decree relegated Holy Spirit Church to profane but not sordid use effective 15 November 2021, and the 25 October 2026 bulletin lists no Mass there. See `excluded/holy-spirit-sharon-hill`. St. George's own directory page now names no worship site.
