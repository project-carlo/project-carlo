## Publisher

Old St. Mary Parish publishes through John Patrick Publishing, whose listing 167 carries a weekly dated PDF (text layer) and hosts the parish's own site (oldstmary.com, whose 'Current Bulletin' link is the listing's template). The row reads the listing with `page-pdfs`.

**The listing's name is stale.** It reads 'Old Saint Mary Church & Holy Trinity Church' and the bulletin masthead still shows 'Holy Trinity Church' with a history blurb. Holy Trinity (6th & Spruce) was merged into Old St. Mary in 2009 and then relegated to profane but not sordid use effective 2023-01-23, so it no longer serves as a worship site (CatholicPhilly 2022-12-12, quoted in `excluded/holy-trinity-philadelphia.md`). The current bulletin's Mass schedule lists Old St. Mary Church only, so the row has one church.

**One bulletin or several?** One, for this parish alone. Nothing in the issue names another parish, and the directory gives no shared pastor.

## Worship sites

One: Old St. Mary Church, 248 S. 4th St. (`churches/old-st-marys-philadelphia.md`). Holy Trinity is excluded as closed.
