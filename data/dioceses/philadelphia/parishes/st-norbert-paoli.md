## Publisher

St. Norbert Parish, Paoli, is served by the Norbertines of Daylesford Abbey (the directory gives its pastor and parochial vicar as 'In residence at Daylesford Abbey'; the abbey's own site says Norbertines "are responsible for two parishes in the Archdiocese": St. Norbert, Paoli, and St. Gabriel, South Philadelphia). The parish publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing, listing 271 ("Saint Norbert Catholic Community"). The 25 October 2026 issue (8 pages, image only) was rendered and page 1 read: masthead "St. Norbert Catholic Community", the parish's staff and a Mass & Worship Schedule. It names this parish alone. The abbey is not named in the schedule and no Daylesford Mass is listed (see the Daylesford Abbey exclusion).

## Worship sites

One: St. Norbert Church (see its church record). The directory names no worship site or mission, and the 25 October bulletin's schedule and intentions list Mass at one place. GCatholic lists St. Norbert (19205) and, separately, Daylesford Abbey (40344), a monastery whose church is open to the public: it is not part of this bulletin's schedule, so it is excluded `no-bulletin` rather than made a church of this row.

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory's website, parish.stnorbert.org, is a parish page whose only bulletin link is John Patrick listing 271 (stnorbert.org is a shell linking the parish and school pages). John Patrick PDFs have no text layer, so `fetch:check` reports `publication_identity` unverified by design; the 25 October masthead was read by rendering page 1. The row uses `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing.
