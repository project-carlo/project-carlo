## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Masses on weekdays at 12:05 PM, Saturday at 4:00 PM, and Sunday at 8:30 AM (Vietnamese), 10:30 AM and 6:30 PM, with the St. Jude Novena on Tuesday after the 12:05 PM Mass. The parish homepage gives the same times.

## Name and address

The masthead, the directory (88) and GCatholic (18445) all give 252 Ninth (9th) Street, Jersey City 07302; the ParishesOnline org gives zip 07303, which I did not follow. GCatholic lists it as a shrine (Shrine of St. Jude). Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7PXH4+97; the directory pin (40.72867, -74.04420) is about 30 m away.
