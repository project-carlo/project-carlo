## Holds Mass

Yes. The November 2026 monthly bulletin (issue dated 1 November) lists Sunday Masses at 8:00 am, 10:00 am and 12:00 pm and weekday Masses at 12:15 pm or 7:00 pm, marked "Mass Celebrated in the Church".

## Name and address

The sources give three addresses:

- The bulletin masthead gives the church at "600 Myrtle Street" and the office at 717 Polonia Avenue.
- OSM's church building (way 1147252185) is at 600 Myrtle Street.
- The directory (38) gives 717 Polonia Avenue, which the bulletin calls the parish office.
- GCatholic (18394) gives 716 Clarkson Ave.

I recorded 600 Myrtle Street, where the bulletin and OSM agree; the Clarkson address is probably another side of the same lot. Coordinates are the OSM building centroid. GCatholic's Plus Code (40.64444, -74.21519) is about 25 m away, and the directory pin (40.64469, -74.21387), probably the office, is about 140 m away.
