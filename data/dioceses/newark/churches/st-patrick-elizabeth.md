## Holds Mass

Yes. The 8 November 2026 bulletin, whose masthead gives 215 Court Street, schedules Monday-Friday 7:00pm, Saturday vigil 6:00pm and Sunday 8:30am, 10:00am and 12:30pm. The schedule names no building; that it is this church is inferred from the masthead and from Jersey Catholic's 2024 report that the parish's "current church opened in 1899" (St. Patrick's).
