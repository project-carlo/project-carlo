## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 2 of the 1 November 2026 bulletin lists intentions for Saturday 4:30pm, Sunday 9:00am (Portuguese), 11:00am, 2:00pm Chinese Mass (except the second Sunday of the month) and 7:00pm (Spanish), and weekdays 12:10pm; the parish site's 'Holy Cross Schedule' gives the same times.
