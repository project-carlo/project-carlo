## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the 1 November 2026 bulletin (image): Saturday 4pm and 5pm; Sunday 7am, 8am (Latin), 9:30am, 10:45am, 12pm, 1pm (Spanish) and 6pm. Page 2 adds a Saturday 14 November 10AM memorial Mass and adoration Tuesday and Thursday.

## Name and address

The archdiocese directory and GCatholic give 81 Lynn Street, which the bulletin names as the OLV Rectory. The bulletin masthead and the parish site's map give the church at 150 Harriot Avenue. I followed the parish: the directory's address is the rectory, not the church. All the pins (site map 40.98562, -73.98165; GCatholic Plus Code; OSM building way) fall within about 15 m of one another, on the building OSM names Our Lady of Victories Church (tagged Lynn Street; inferred to be a corner lot with a Harriot Avenue address), so the coordinates are the OSM building's centre. Address not marked verified: only the parish's own bulletin and site give 150 Harriot Avenue. Name as the masthead has it, which GCatholic and the directory agree on.
