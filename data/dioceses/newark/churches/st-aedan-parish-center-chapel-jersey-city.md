## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin's Mass Times box puts the Saturday 9:00 AM Mass in the 'Chapel, 39 Tuers Ave' and the Friday 7:00 PM Spanish Mass 'en el Centro Parroquial'; the parish homepage (read 3 November 2026) says both are at the Parish Center Chapel. Public parish Masses, so a church row, as for Holy Trinity, Hackensack's parish center chapel.

## Name and address

The masthead and homepage give the Parish Center at 39 Tuers Avenue, Jersey City, NJ 07306, behind the church (the bulletin: 'Church Parking Lot: located on Tuers Avenue (behind the Church)'). The name is descriptive, from the homepage's 'Parish Center Chapel'. Neither the directory nor GCatholic lists the chapel, so name and address rest on the parish alone (unverified). Coordinates are the Google Maps pin the homepage links for the Parish Center (manual).
