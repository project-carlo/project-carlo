## Name and address

The parish's bulletin gives the church at 212 Lafayette St. (office at 202 Lafayette St.); GCatholic, the LPi org and newarkhistory.com give 114 Prospect Street; Wikipedia's list of churches gives 202 Lafayette St. The building stands on the corner of Prospect and Lafayette Streets, so both are the same place. Recorded the parish's own 212 Lafayette Street; no second source gives that number, so `address_verified` is false. Coordinates are the centre of OSM's church building (way 436652103, on Lafayette Street), about 35 m from GCatholic's Plus Code. The name agrees in the bulletin and GCatholic.
