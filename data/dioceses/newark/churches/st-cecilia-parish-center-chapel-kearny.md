## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Cecilia's 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists 'Wednesday: 7:30 PM Português | Chapel' and 'First Saturday of every month: 10:00 AM Español | Chapel', and the parish website gives the same two Masses in the Chapel, open for prayer during office hours.

## Name and address

The parish website says 'The Chapel is located in our Parish Center 119 Chestnut Street Kearny, New Jersey 07032', and the bulletin gives the Parish Center as 119 Chestnut St. One source (the parish's own site and bulletin), so name and address are not verified. The name is descriptive: the parish calls it 'the Chapel'. Neither the directory nor GCatholic lists it. No coordinates yet: `pnpm geocode` fills them after promotion.
