## Holds Mass

The 1 November 2026 bulletin (container 04/1091, page 1) gives Saturday 9:00 a.m. and 5:30 p.m. (bilingual), Sunday 7:30, 9:00 and 11:30 a.m. in Portuguese and 10:15 a.m. in English, and weekdays at 9:00 a.m. and 7:30 p.m.

## Name and address

The directory (133) and GCatholic (18493) give 82 Congress Street. The bulletin and the website give "82-84 Congress St., Newark, NJ. 07105-1802", and the site labels it the parish office. ParishesOnline's org address is 79 Jefferson St, which the bulletin lists along with 85 Jefferson St as other parish buildings. I recorded 82 Congress Street, where the directory, GCatholic and the bulletin agree, so the address is verified. The coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7PRJQ+57. The directory pin is about 25 m away and the ParishesOnline point about 60 m away, so the address and the Jefferson Street buildings seem to share one block.
