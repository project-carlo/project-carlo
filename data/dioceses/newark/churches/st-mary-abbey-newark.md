## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (Canva, page 3) gives Sunday 7:30 AM, 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM, Saturday 8:45 AM and Monday-Friday 12:15 PM; page 4 says the 12:15 weekday Mass is now the abbey's Conventual Mass and 'All are welcome to attend!'.

## Name and address

The sources split between 520 and 528 Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd. The current bulletin (25 October 2026, page 3), GCatholic (18509) and the ParishesOnline org give 528; the directory (276) and the parish's Wix site header and footer give 520. I chose 528: it is the address the current bulletin gives for the church's Mass schedule, and GCatholic's Plus Code puts the building there; 520 may be the parish office or an older address. Either finds the abbey church. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7PRP9+3X; the directory pin (40.73578, -74.17973) is about 70 m away. Name as the bulletin and site give it, 'St. Mary's Church'; the directory and GCatholic call it St. Mary (of the Immaculate Conception).
