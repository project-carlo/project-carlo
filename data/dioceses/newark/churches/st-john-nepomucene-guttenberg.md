## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 Holy Redeemer bulletin (page 1, image) lists weekday Mass here at 7:30 am and Saturday 8:00 am, Saturday vigils at 4:30 pm (English) and 7:00 pm (Spanish), and Sunday Masses at 9:00 am, 10:15 am (Croatian) and 2:00 pm (Spanish). The parish of St. John Nepomucene was merged into Holy Redeemer (archdiocese records table, 2025-05); the church stayed in use.

## Name and address

The masthead, GCatholic (18414) and OSM all call it St. John Nepomucene. Holy Redeemer's homepage gives 7001 Polk Street, GCatholic 7007 Polk St.; OSM puts the church building at 70th Street and Polk Street. I followed the parish's own 7001 (official source; the two numbers are a few doors apart on the same block, and either finds the building), so the address is not verified. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7QXWQ+R2, about 15 m from the OSM building centre.
