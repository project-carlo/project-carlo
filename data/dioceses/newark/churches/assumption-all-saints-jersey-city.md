## Holds Mass

Yes, weekdays only, as of the newest source found. The 5 April 2026 bulletin and the parish's Liturgical Schedule page both say all weekday Masses, Monday through Saturday at 9:00 AM, are celebrated at Assumption/All Saints Church, followed by a Holy Hour. Sunday Masses are at St. Patrick. No source after April 2026 was found (see the parish's `research`).
