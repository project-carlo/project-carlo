## Holds Mass

Yes. The English bulletin for 1 November 2026 (page 1, an image) gives a Saturday 8:00 pm vigil, Sunday 8:00 am, 10:30 am and 11:30 am, and Monday-Saturday 9:30 am. The Masses are in the 'Main Church' and the 'Augustine Chapel' (and a monthly youth Mass in a 'Small Chapel'); the bulletin gives one address, 280 Parker Ave., for the parish, and I take the chapels to be rooms on the same property. GCatholic and the directory list one place there. This is one church row.
