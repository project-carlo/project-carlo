## Name and address

The parish calls it Saint Joseph Roman Catholic Church (masthead), ParishesOnline "St. Joseph Church" and GCatholic "St. Joseph": the parish and GCatholic agree on the name. The sources give different addresses for the parish campus, which straddles the Oradell/New Milford line:

- The parish's own homepage: "the church building is 300 Elm Street in Oradell", and the office/rectory, "directly across from the church", is 105 Harrison Street, New Milford.
- The archdiocese directory and ParishesOnline: 105 Harrison Street, New Milford 07646 (the office).
- GCatholic: "Elm St., Oradell, New Jersey 07649", no number.
- OSM: the church node is on Grand Street, Oradell, postcode 07646.

I recorded the parish's own address for the building, 300 Elm Street, Oradell, with GCatholic's 07649 for Oradell; the directory's address is the office across the street. Only the parish site gives the number, so `address_verified` is false. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G7XX2G+84), about 20 m from the OSM church node; the directory pin is about 80 m away and ParishesOnline's (40.928, -74.022) is a street geocode more than 2 km south.
