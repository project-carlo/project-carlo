## Holds Mass

The Salvatorians' November 2026 newsletter and their homepage (read 2026-11-03, in Polish) give public Masses at Divine Mercy Hill: Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 11:00 am, Monday-Friday 8:00 am, and a first-Friday vigil Mass at 7:00 pm. The homepage invites everyone ('zapraszamy wszystkich') to its first-Thursday holy hour and to the 7 November 2026 anniversary of the 'Wielka Pokuta Polonii Amerykańskiej' held on the hill, so I took the Masses to be public; GCatholic calls the place a monastery, but these are scheduled community Masses, not a private convent chapel.

## Name and address

The newsletter, the site and GCatholic (47767) all give 23 Crestmont Road, Verona 07044. Name as GCatholic and the newsletter give it ('Wzgórze Miłosierdzia', Divine Mercy Hill). The archdiocese's parish directory doesn't list it. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7RQHG+XP.
