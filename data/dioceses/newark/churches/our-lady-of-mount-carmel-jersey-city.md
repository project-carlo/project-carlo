## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday Vigil 5 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM and 11:15 AM (Spanish), daily 8:30 AM, and Tuesday and Thursday 6:30 PM (Spanish). Page 2 lists intentions for each of these from 31 October to 8 November. Since 10 May 2026 these Masses have been held in the parish hall while the church building is repaired (page 3 notice). The hall belongs to the same parish at the same address, so the place is unchanged.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the directory (90) and GCatholic (18441) all give 99 Broadway, Jersey City, NJ 07306. The name is the masthead's. Coordinates come from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7PWPG+FV. The directory pin (40.73604, -74.07283) is about 20 m away.
