## Holds Mass

Yes. The 11 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday 5:00PM (Spanish) and Sunday 9:00AM and 12:00PM (English), 10:30AM and 1:15PM (Spanish), and page 2 lists intentions for each. Weekday Mass is at the monastery chapel on Central Avenue (`st-nicholas-of-tolentine-chapel-union-city`), not here.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the directory (198) and GCatholic (18565) all give 530 35th Street. The parish site's header prints '350 35th Street', a transposition: its own map link and the bulletin give 530. OSM's 'Holy Family Church' building (way 592169989) is on 35th Street about 10 m from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7QXFC+Q7, used for the coordinates.
