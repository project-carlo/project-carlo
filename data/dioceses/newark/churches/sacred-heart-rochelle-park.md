## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday 4 PM and 5:30 PM, Sunday 10 AM and 12 PM, and weekday Masses in the chapel Monday to Friday at 8 AM.

## Name and address

The bulletin (Church, 12 Terrace Ave, Rochelle Park), the archdiocese directory (12 Terrace Avenue) and GCatholic (12 Terrace Ave.) agree, as does the ParishesOnline org. The parish center is at 15 Forest Place, a separate building with no Mass listed. The coordinates come from GCatholic's Plus Code (87G7WW4C+3V).
