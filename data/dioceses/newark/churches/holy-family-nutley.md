## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the 1 November 2026 bulletin gives Saturday 5:30pm and 7:00pm Italian (during DST), Sunday 8:00, 10:00am and noon, and daily 8:45am. The parish site says the main church is under restoration and weekday Masses are temporarily in the Mother Cabrini Chapel (see the parish record); weekend Masses are in the main church.
