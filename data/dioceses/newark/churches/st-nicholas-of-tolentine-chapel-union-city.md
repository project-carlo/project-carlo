## Holds Mass

Yes, on weekdays. Holy Family's 11 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists 'Weekdays: 8AM English (Monastery- Central Ave)' as the parish's weekday Mass, and its homepage (read 2026-11-03) gives '8:00 AM in the Monastery 3201 Central Ave.'. Page 2 and the homepage also announce a public novena of evening Masses there, 14-23 October 2026, at the 'Capilla del Monasterio SAN NICOLAS DE TOLENTINO'. A parish weekday Mass announced to all parishioners is public, so under the settled rule for monastery chapels this is a church row. No weekend Mass is listed here.

## Name and address

The name is the parish's ('Capilla del Monasterio San Nicolás de Tolentino', 'Capilla de San Nicolás'), from the bulletin and the parish site, one source; no second source names it, so `name_verified` is false. The address, 3201 Central Avenue, is in both the bulletin and the site, and OSM has an address point there, which places the coordinates (OSM way 1280445641) but does not name the building, so `address_verified` is false too. It is the Augustinian Recollect community house; the order's site lists Holy Family and St. Augustine as the parishes the community serves. Neither the directory nor GCatholic lists the chapel.
