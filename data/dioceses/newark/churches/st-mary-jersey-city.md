## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Masses on weekdays at 7:00 AM, Tuesday and Thursday 7:00 PM (Spanish), Saturday 9:00 AM and vigil 5:30 PM, and Sunday 8:00 AM, 9:00 AM Tagalog (first Sunday), 10:00 AM Spanish and 12:00 PM English. Page 2 lists the intentions for each of these Masses for 2-8 November 2026.

## Name and address

The parish calls itself St. Mary’s; the directory (80) gives the full title St. Mary of the Immaculate Conception, which the parish's about page also uses. Three addresses: the directory gives 209 Third Street, which the masthead labels the Parish Office; GCatholic gives 219 3rd St.; the masthead gives 254 Second Street as a second address, and the about page says the church 'dominates Erie Street on the block between Second and Third streets'. I recorded 254 Second Street as the church's own address in the parish's bulletin (the others are the office and a neighbouring number on the same block). Not verified: no second source gives 254 Second Street. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7PXF4+29, on the Erie Street block; the directory pin (40.72296, -74.04341) is about 50 m away.
