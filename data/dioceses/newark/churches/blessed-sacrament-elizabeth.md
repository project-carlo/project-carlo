## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the 8 November 2026 bulletin lists Saturday 7:00 PM, Sunday 8:30 AM, 10:00 AM and 12:00 Noon, and weekday Masses Monday-Saturday 8:30 AM and Tuesday-Thursday 7:00 PM.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead gives the church at 747 Madison Ave., Elizabeth 07201, and separately a 'Mailing Address: 1096 North Ave.'. The directory (32), GCatholic (18386) and the ParishesOnline org all give 1096 North Ave., which the masthead calls the mailing address (the office). I followed the masthead, since it distinguishes the two; the buildings share the North Avenue/Madison Avenue block, and only the bulletin gives 747 Madison, so `address_verified` is false. Name as the masthead and directory agree, Blessed Sacrament. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7MQHW+MJ; the directory pin (40.67906, -74.20312) is about 20 m away.
