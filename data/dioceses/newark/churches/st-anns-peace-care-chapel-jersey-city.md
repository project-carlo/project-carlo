## Holds Mass

Yes: St. Paul the Apostle's 1 November 2026 bulletin lists, among its Sunday Masses, '11AM at St. Ann's Peace Care Chapel'. Peace Care's pastoral care page says 'Catholic Mass celebrated multiple times per week' in its 'on-site chapels'. It is a row because the parish lists the Mass in its public Mass Times with no restriction. Whether visitors may attend is not stated anywhere I read; it is a nursing-home chapel, which the README would exclude as `no-public-mass` if the Mass were for residents only. That question is flagged to the owner (advisory) and in `revisit`.

## Name and address

The name is the bulletin's ('St. Ann's Peace Care Chapel'); the facility calls itself 'Peace Care St. Ann's'. The address, 198 Old Bergen Road, Jersey City, NJ 07305, comes from Peace Care's site only; neither the directory nor GCatholic lists the chapel, so name and address are not verified by a second source. Coordinates are OSM's for house 198 Old Bergen Road (way 275405027), the building, about 60 m from St. Paul's church.
