## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 2 of the 1 November 2026 bulletin lists weekend Masses Saturday 4:00 pm (English) and 6:30 pm (Spanish), Sunday 8:30 am (Portuguese), 10:30 am (Spanish) and 12:30 pm (English), with intentions for each day of 31 October to 7 November. Weekday Masses (Mon-Fri 7:30 am English, Wednesday 7:00 PM Portuguese) are said in the 'Pastoral Center'; the Spanish Mon/Tue/Fri 7:00 pm Masses have no place given.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead gives the church as '1000 North Broad St., Hillside' and '19 King St.' as the mailing address. The directory (65) and GCatholic (18426) both give 19 King Street, which the bulletin calls the mailing address (probably the rectory or office on the same corner lot). I recorded 1000 North Broad Street because the parish's own bulletin labels it as the church (that the two addresses are one lot is my inference, not read). Only one source gives it, so `address_verified` is false. GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7MQPM+QX and the directory pin (40.68698, -74.21540) are about 30 m apart. I used the Plus Code; that the pin is on the church building and not the rectory is unconfirmed. Name as the masthead gives it ('Saint Catherine of Siena Church'), which GCatholic and the directory agree with.
