## Name and address

The parish's own bulletin and site give the church at 470 Broad Avenue, Leonia, and its site names 235 Harrison Street as the rectory's mailing address. The directory (98), GCatholic (18462) and the ParishesOnline org give 235 Harrison Street, the office. I followed the parish: the church is the building at 470 Broad Avenue, where OSM also puts a place of worship, 'Saint Johns' (two independent sources, so address verified). Coordinates from that OSM node (40.86634, -73.98506); GCatholic's Plus Code (40.86719, -73.98569) is about 110 m north, toward Harrison Street, and is probably on the rectory. Name as the masthead uses it.
