## Holds Mass

Yes. The 8 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives weekdays 8:30 AM, Saturday Vigil 5:00 PM and Sunday 8:30, 10:15 (Polish) and 11:30 AM.

## Name and address

The bulletin puts the church at the "Corner of Ridge Rd. and Page Ave." and gives 624 Page Ave. for the rectory and office; the archdiocese directory, GCatholic and the ParishesOnline org all give 624 Page Ave(nue), which I recorded since it is the address every source uses and sits on the corner lot. The coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G7RV3H+H7), about 70 m from the directory and ParishesOnline pins. The name is the bulletin masthead's.
