## Holds Mass

The 18 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives 'Sunday Masses: 8 a.m. and 10 a.m.; Igbo Mass: 12:30 p.m.' and daily Masses Monday-Saturday at 8 a.m. (virtual) and 6:30 pm in the convent chapel; first Friday and third Thursday Mass at 6:30 pm with adoration 'in the church'. It is the only church the bulletin names.

## Name and address

The church stands on the corner of Clinton Avenue and Van Ness Place (parish history: property 'at Van Ness Pl. and Clinton Ave., as the site for a new church', first Mass 5 October 1913). The bulletin masthead gives '610 Clinton Avenue, Newark, NJ 07108' with the 'Parish Center, 19 Van Ness Place'; the archdiocese directory (144) and GCatholic (18489) give 15 Van Ness Place, probably the rectory/office on the side street. I recorded the parish's own 610 Clinton Avenue; either finds the building. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7PQFV+2G, which sits on the corner building. Name from the masthead ('Blessed Sacrament St. Charles Borromeo'), which GCatholic matches ('Blessed Sacrament–St. Charles Borromeo'); address not verified because the sources differ.
