## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the 1 November 2026 Divine Mercy bulletin (image) gives weekdays 12:00pm, Tuesday 7:00pm in Spanish, Saturday 9:00am and 4:00pm vigil, Sunday 8:00, 9:30, 11:00am and 12:30pm in Spanish, at 232 Central Avenue, the parish's only church.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the archdiocese directory (172) and GCatholic (18540) all give 232 Central Avenue, Rahway, NJ 07065. The building is the former St. Mary's Church (parish history); GCatholic calls it the Church of Divine Mercy, and the parish uses only the parish name, so it is recorded as Divine Mercy Church. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7JP5C+XJ; the directory pin (40.60970, -74.27850) is about 30 m away.
