## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 am, 9:30 am (Spanish), 11:00 am and 12:30 pm (Spanish), and Monday-Friday 9:00 am in the Divine Mercy Chapel.

## Name and address

The archdiocese directory and GCatholic both give 8016 Kennedy Boulevard; the parish's bulletin masthead, its homepage footer and its ParishesOnline org give 8101 Kennedy Blvd. The pins of all four sources fall on the same spot (GCatholic 40.80519,-74.01069; directory 40.80530,-74.01086; LPi 40.80530,-74.01097), so either number finds the building. I recorded 8016, on which two independent sources agree; 8101 may be the parish office's number. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7RX4Q+3P.
