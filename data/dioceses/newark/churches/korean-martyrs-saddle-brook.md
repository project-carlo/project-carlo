## Holds Mass

Yes, as of July 2026. The parish homepage, in the Wayback copy of 13 July 2026, gives Saturday vigil 5:00 PM (Korean), Sunday 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM (Korean) and 12:00 PM (English), and daily Masses (Korean) Monday to Saturday. The live site was down on 3 November 2026, so I have no newer schedule. The archdiocese's 2026 directory still lists the parish. Nothing I found says it closed or stopped Masses.

## Name and address

The parish site's July 2026 snapshot (585 Saddle River Road, Saddle Brook, NJ 07663), the archdiocese directory (585 Saddle River Road) and GCatholic (585 Saddle River Rd.) agree, as does the ParishesOnline org. The name is the directory's and GCatholic's; the site's Korean title is 성 백삼위 한인천주교회 (Korean Martyrs Catholic Church). The coordinates come from GCatholic's Plus Code (87G7WW64+89).
