## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, image) lists daily Mass at 8:30 am (English) and 7:00 pm (Spanish), Saturday 9:00 am, 5:00 pm (English) and 6:30 pm (Spanish), and Sunday Masses at 8:30 am, 10:30 am in both the upper and lower church, 12:00 pm (English and Spanish), 1:30 pm and 5:00 pm.

## Name and address

The masthead gives 'St. Joseph of the Palisades Church, 6401 Palisade Ave', the directory (209) 6401 Palisade Avenue and GCatholic (18580) 6401 Palisade Ave.; they agree. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7QXRQ+RF, about 40 m from the directory pin. The 'Iglesia Arriba' and 'Iglesia Abajo' of the Spanish schedule are the upper and lower church of this building, not separate places.
