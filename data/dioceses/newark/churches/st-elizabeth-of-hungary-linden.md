## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the bulletin for 8 and 15 November 2026 lists weekend Masses Saturday 4 PM and 7:30 PM and Sunday 8 AM, 10 AM and 12:00 PM; the parish site (2026-11-05) adds Saturday 12:05 PM and weekday Masses at 8:00 AM and 12:05 PM, with the 7:30 PM vigil in Spanish.

## Name and address

The bulletin gives 220 E. Blancke Street; the directory (99) gives 220 East Blanke Street (a misspelling of the same street); GCatholic (18464) gives E Blancke St. with no number. Bulletin and directory agree on the number, so `address_verified`. The rectory and the ParishesOnline org address are 179 Hussa Street, the office. Name as the masthead gives it ('St. Elizabeth of Hungary Roman Catholic Church'), matching GCatholic and the directory. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7JPMW+JJ; the directory pin (40.63388, -74.25301) is about 50 m away.
