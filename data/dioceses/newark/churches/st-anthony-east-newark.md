## Holds Mass

Yes, until 23 November 2026. Both Holy Cross websites (parishoftheholycross.org and harrisoncatholic.com, read 4 November 2026) give St. Anthony's schedule as Sunday 12:30 pm in Spanish, and the 1 November 2026 bulletin's intentions list a 12:30pm (Sp.) Mass on 1 and 8 November without naming the church. On 10 October 2026 the archdiocese announced a decree closing the church: it 'will close permanently for worship on Nov. 23, 2026', with 'a final Mass in November. Details will be announced.' Following the diocese's rule for decreed closures, it stays a church row until that date has passed and a source confirms it; `revisit_after` is 2026-11-24.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead gives 'St. Anthony’s 409 N. 2nd St. E. Newark NJ'; the parish site 'St. Anthony Church 409 North 2nd St. E. Newark, NJ 07029'; the directory (23) 409 N 2nd Street, East Newark, NJ 07029; GCatholic (18380) 409 N 2nd St., East Newark. All agree.
