## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the 8 November 2026 bulletin lists Saturday (anticipated) 5:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 AM, 10:30 AM and 12:00 PM, and Monday-Saturday 8:00 AM; the parish homepage (2026-11-05) gives the same weekend times.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead and the directory (181) both give 113 Coolidge Place, Roselle Park 07204; GCatholic (18549) gives 113 Chiego Place, and OSM puts the church on Roosevelt Street with a Chiego Place beside it and no Coolidge Place in Roselle Park (Nominatim, 2026-11-05). The street may have been renamed or carry an honorary name; nothing I read says which. I followed the parish's and the diocese's own sources, which agree. Name as the masthead gives it, 'Church of the Assumption'. Coordinates from OSM's place-of-worship node; GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7MP6G+55 (40.66044, -74.27456) and the directory pin are about 50 m south of it.
