## Holds Mass

Yes. The Latin Mass Community of Jersey City's homepage, headed 8 November 2026, gives 'Sundays: Masses at 9:00am at Our Lady of Sorrows', and rcan.org appointed a chaplain to that community from 20 April 2026. The parish's own schedule is known only from the June 2025 Wayback copy of olsnj.org (Sunday 9:00 Latin, 11:00 English, 1:00 PM Spanish; Tuesday 7:00 PM Spanish; Saturday 8:00 AM bilingual), since the parish domain is lost and no bulletin was found; the current English and Spanish times are unconfirmed.

## Name and address

The directory (76) gives 93-95 Clerk Street, GCatholic (18442) 93 Clerk St., and the ParishesOnline org 93 Clerk St # 95, all Jersey City 07305. The Latin Mass Community's site gives '73 Claremont Avenue', with parking 'behind school on Clerk Street'; the church probably stands at the Clerk Street and Claremont Avenue corner, with two street addresses. I followed the directory and GCatholic (93 Clerk Street). Coordinates come from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7PW5F+36; the directory pin (40.70732, -74.07678) is about 40 m south.
