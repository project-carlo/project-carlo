## Holds Mass

Yes. Page 1 of the 1 November 2026 bulletin: weekdays 8:30am; Saturday vigil 5:00pm; Sunday 8:00am, 10am, 12:00pm and 6:30pm.

## Name and address

The parish site's header and GCatholic give 102 Park Street; the archdiocese directory gives 100 Park Street, which the bulletin names as the rectory (102 is the Parish Center). I followed the parish site, which puts the church itself at 102; the two numbers are neighbouring buildings on one campus at Park Street and St. Nicholas Avenue. Name as the masthead has it. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (87G8X255+R8), about 35 m from OSM's church node on St. Nicholas Avenue.
