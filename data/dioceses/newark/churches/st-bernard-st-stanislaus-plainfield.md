## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin gives the schedule Saturday 5:00 PM English, Sunday 9:00 AM English, 10:30 AM Polish and 12:30 PM Spanish, and Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM, and page 2 lists intentions for each of those Masses from 31 October to 8 November 2026.

## Name and address

The parish (bulletin masthead and site footer) gives the church address as 1235 George Street, Plainfield, NJ 07062, with offices at 1232 George Street. GCatholic (18537) and the archdiocese directory (170) give 368 Sumner Avenue, 07062. OSM's 'Saint Bernard's Church' node is on George Street between the geocodes of the two addresses (about 50 m from each), so this looks like one corner building with an address on each street. I recorded the parish's own George Street address; address_verified is false because no second source gives it. Name as the masthead and GCatholic give it (St. Bernard of Clairvaux and St. Stanislaus Kostka); the history page calls the building the former St. Bernard church. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7JHMW+5G, about 30 m from the OSM node.
