## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 Holy Redeemer bulletin (page 1, image) lists daily Mass at Our Lady Help of Christians (8:00 am English, 7:00 pm Spanish), a Saturday 6:00 pm vigil and Sunday Masses at 8:00 and 11:00 am (English) and 9:00 am, 12:30 pm and 6:00 pm (Spanish) at 'St. Mary', the name the schedule uses for this church; the parish's Mass Times page lists the same at 'St. Marys'.

## Name and address

The bulletin's masthead names 'Our Lady Help of Christians' first among the parish's churches, and GCatholic (18578) uses the same name; the schedules call it St. Mary. The masthead gives 569-65th Street, the directory (208) 569 - 65th Street and GCatholic 569 65th St.; they agree. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7QXVM+QV, about 90 m from the directory pin.
