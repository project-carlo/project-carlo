## Holds Mass

Probably, but the newest schedule I could read is from June 2023: the Wayback copy of the parish site (9 June 2023) gives Saturday 9:00am and a 5:00pm vigil, and Sunday 8:00am, 9:30am (Spanish) and 12 noon. The parish's site has been down since mid-2024. The archdiocese's 2026 directory still lists the parish (English and Spanish), and its school's site (stjosepheo.com, with a 2025 handbook) is active. Nothing I found says the church closed.

## Name and address

The archdiocesan directory, GCatholic and ParishesOnline give 110 Telford Street, East Orange, NJ 07018, and the name St. Joseph. The coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7QQ2F+J6, within 50 m of the directory's pin.
