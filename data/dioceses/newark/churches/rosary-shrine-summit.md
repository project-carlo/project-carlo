## Holds Mass

Yes, publicly by its own page. The monastery's 'Pray With Us' page (read 5 November 2026) gives 'Sunday Mass - 8:00 a.m.' and 'Weekday (Mon-Sat) - 7:30 a.m.', with the chapel open for adoration 6 a.m. to 7 p.m.; only Compline is marked 'Closed to the public.' The schedule is a standing page, not a dated issue.

## Name and address

The nuns' site gives 543 Springfield Ave., Summit, NJ 07901-4498; GCatholic (33105) gives 543 Springfield Ave., Summit, New Jersey 07901. They agree. Name: GCatholic calls it 'Rosary' (shrine, in the Monastery of Our Lady of the Rosary); the nuns call the community the Dominican Monastery of Our Lady of the Rosary, and the chapel is known as the Rosary Shrine. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7PJ8P+P2.
