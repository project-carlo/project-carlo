## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (John Patrick 655, image-only page 1) gives weekend Masses Saturday 4:00 (English) and 7:00 p.m. (Portuguese) and Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 10:45 a.m., 12:00 and 6:00 p.m., plus Wednesday and Thursday evening Masses. The parish site's schedule agrees.

## Name and address

The directory (130 and 142) and the LPi org give 142 Jefferson Street; GCatholic and the bulletin give 143 Madison St, but the bulletin prints it in its Parish Office box, so it is the office on the next street (the church stands between Jefferson and Madison). OSM's building is on Jefferson Street. Recorded 142 Jefferson Street (directory, LPi, OSM agree). Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code, on the building (OSM's centre is about 25 m away). The name St. James is the same in the bulletin, GCatholic and the directory.
