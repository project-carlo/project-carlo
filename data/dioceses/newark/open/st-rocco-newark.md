## What is known

St. Rocco's (212 Hunterdon Street) was the second church of the Parish of the Transfiguration when St. Ann's and St. Rocco's merged on 1 July 2005; the parish's about page says the archbishop 'allowed us to continue with the two worship sites' and made St. Ann's the parish church. GCatholic (18511) still lists it, and the parish's John Patrick listing (church-bulletin.org/585) still gives its address and phone as the contact.

## Why it is not decided

No current source shows Mass at St. Rocco's and none says it closed. The parish website's one 'Church Schedule' names no building and sits under St. Ann's address (103 16th Avenue); the parish posts no bulletin; the rcan.org records table reports the parish merger, not the building's status. Checked 4 November 2026: the parish site (home, about, sacraments, staff, links, blog), church-bulletin.org/585, Wikipedia and the rcan.org records table.
