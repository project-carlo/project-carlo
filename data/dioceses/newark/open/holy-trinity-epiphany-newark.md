## What is known

Holy Trinity-Epiphany is a working parish. The archdiocese appointed Fr. Paulo dos Santos Frade pastor of Our Lady of Mount Carmel and Holy Trinity-Epiphany from 8 September 2025, and Fr. Alan De Almeida Barreto parochial vicar of both from 13 June 2026 (rcan.org clergy appointment posts). The archdiocese's 2025 workbook for the parish says it was formed in 2002 by merging Holy Trinity and Epiphany parishes, has over 145 registered families, and had "3 Masses on a weekend" in 2024 in a church seating 150. The directory (148) and GCatholic (18490) both give 207 Adams Street, Newark 07105, and neither gives a website.
