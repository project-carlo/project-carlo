## What is known

GCatholic (18388) lists an Immaculate Heart of Mary church at 205 E Jersey St., Elizabeth, with the merged parish's phone number. The merged parish of Immaculate Heart of Mary and St. Patrick prints one bulletin (John Patrick 6020) whose 8 November 2026 masthead gives 215 Court Street and whose one Mass schedule names no building; Jersey Catholic's 2024 article says the parish's "current church opened in 1899", which is St. Patrick's on Court Street. The only source giving Mass at 205 East Jersey Street is an undated catholicchurch.directory listing from before the merger.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing I read shows a Mass at the E Jersey St. church now, and nothing says it closed or was relegated (the archdiocese's closed/merged records table has no Immaculate Heart of Mary, Elizabeth entry). Absence from the bulletin is not a source for a closure. What would settle it is in `revisit`.
