## Worship sites

- St. Mary, Erie Street between Second and Third Streets (`st-mary-jersey-city`): Saturday 9:00 AM and vigil 5:30 PM; Sunday 8:00 AM, 9:00 AM Tagalog (first Sunday), 10:00 AM Spanish, 12:00 PM; weekdays 7:00 AM, Tuesday and Thursday 7:00 PM Spanish.

The parish was part of Resurrection Parish (1997-2014) with St. Boniface, St. Bridget, St. Michael and St. Peter; its sacramental-records page says the cluster was suppressed in February 2014 and St. Mary and St. Michael became separate parishes again. GCatholic still lists St. Mary (18446) and St. Bridget (18444) under 'Parish of the Resurrection'. St. Bridget, 372 Montgomery Street, is excluded as closed (`excluded/st-bridget-jersey-city`): the same page lists 'St. Bridget's Church (Closed, 2015)', and the 1 November 2026 bulletin uses 'St Bridget’s' and 'St Bridget’s Library' only as meeting rooms, with no Mass there. The bulletin also names a 'St. Boniface Chapel' for adoration and prayer groups (Monday silent adoration, Tuesday prayer group after the 7 PM Mass); St. Boniface Church itself is listed as 'Closed, 2006' on the same page, and no Mass is scheduled in the chapel, so it has no row. St. Michael has its own row (`st-michael-jersey-city`).
