## Publisher

John Patrick Publishing prints the bulletin (church-bulletin.org listing 6040, 'Saint John Paul II Parish Bulletin'). I read the 1 November 2026 issue, which is image-only (rendered pages 1-2): masthead "Saint John Paul II Parish / Church of Our Lady of Mount Carmel / SUNDAY, November 1, 2026 - All Saints", served by the Salvatorians, pastor Rev. Tadeusz Majcher SDS, rectory 39 East 22nd Street.

## Worship sites

One church, `our-lady-of-mount-carmel-bayonne`. The page-1 schedule gives Saturday Vigil 4:30PM, Sunday 7:30, 8:30 (Italian), 9:45, 11:00 (Polish) and 12:30, and weekday Masses, all under the masthead's one church; page 2's Mass intentions (31 October-8 November) give the same times and no other place. The parish's site names only the Church of Our Lady of Mount Carmel too.

The parish was formed in 2016 from Our Lady of Mount Carmel, Our Lady of the Assumption and St. Michael & St. Joseph (Wikipedia, 'Our Lady of Mt. Carmel (Bayonne, New Jersey)'; the archdiocese's records table, rcan.org/sacramental-records, lists Mount Carmel, Assumption, St. Michael and St. Joseph as 'Merged' into St. John Paul II). GCatholic still lists Our Lady of the Assumption (18354, 91 W 23rd St.): it is in no current schedule, but I found no source saying it closed, so it is `open/our-lady-of-the-assumption-bayonne`. No roster lists a St. Michael church; GCatholic's nearby 'St. Joseph Syriac Former Cathedral' is Syriac Catholic, outside this row.

The parish is one of the four Bayonne Parish Mission Partners pilot parishes (June 2026, `group`); the others publish their own bulletins with other publishers.
