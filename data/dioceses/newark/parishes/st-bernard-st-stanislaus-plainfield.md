## Publisher

The parish publishes its own bulletin as a PDF linked from its homepage (`/downloads/<yyyy>_<mm>_<dd>.pdf`). The 1 November 2026 issue (6 pages, text layer, no publisher's footer) has the masthead 'The Church of St. Bernard of Clairvaux & St. Stanislaus Kostka', 'November 1, 2026', church address 1235 George Street, Plainfield, NJ 07062, and the Mass schedule Saturday 5:00 PM English, Sunday 9:00 AM English, 10:30 AM Polish, 12:30 PM Spanish, Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM. Page 2 lists the week's Mass intentions at those times (plus a Friday 7:00 PM Polish Mass on 6 November).

## Worship sites

One church, the former St. Bernard church (`st-bernard-st-stanislaus-plainfield`). The parish history says St. Stanislaus Kostka merged into St. Bernard of Clairvaux on 20 November 2005 and that 'The church building that was St. Bernard and is now the parish of St. Bernard and St. Stanislaus' is the parish church. The current bulletin, the site's Mass schedule page and the site footer give one church address and one schedule, with the Polish Mass at 10:30 on Sunday; a 6 May 2006 Wayback copy of the Mass schedule also shows one schedule. GCatholic (18537) and the archdiocese directory (170) each list one church for the parish, and the archdiocese's closed/merged records table lists both former parishes as 'Merged' into the new parish. So no source shows Mass at the former St. Stanislaus Kostka church now. Its building's address was not found (see research), and no roster lists it, so it has no record.
