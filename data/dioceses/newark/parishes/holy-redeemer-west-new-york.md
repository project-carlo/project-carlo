## Publisher

The 1 November 2026 issue (church-bulletin.org listing for id 6041) is images only; page 1, rendered, has the masthead 'HOLY REDEEMER PARISH Serving Our Lady Help of Christians, St. John Nepomucene & Our Lady of Libera Churches, 569-65th Street, West New York' and the code '6041 November 2026'. ## Worship sites

- Our Lady Help of Christians ('St. Mary' in the schedule), 569 65th Street, West New York (`our-lady-help-of-christians-west-new-york`).
- St. John Nepomucene, Polk Street, Guttenberg (`st-john-nepomucene-guttenberg`), including a Sunday Croatian Mass.
- Our Lady of Libera, 5808 Kennedy Boulevard, West New York (`our-lady-of-libera-west-new-york`).

The masthead names these three churches and the page 1 schedule has weekend Masses at each. The archdiocese's closed/merged records table (2025-05) lists Saint John Nepomucene, Guttenberg, as merged into Holy Redeemer; its church still holds Mass. The directory (208) lists the parish once; GCatholic lists the three churches (18578, 18414, 18579).
