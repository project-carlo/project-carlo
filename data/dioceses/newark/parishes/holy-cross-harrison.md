I read the 1 November 2026 issue (6 pages, text layer): the page 1 masthead names two churches, 'Holy Cross 16 Church Square Harrison NJ' and 'St. Anthony’s 409 N. 2nd St. E. Mass without a place; both websites put the 12:30 pm Spanish Mass at St. Anthony.
