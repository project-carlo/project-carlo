## Publisher

None found. The cloistered Dominican nuns of the Monastery of Our Lady of the Rosary, 543 Springfield Avenue, Summit, keep a public chapel (the Rosary Shrine) and publish only a twice-yearly newsletter (archive on https://www.summitdominicans.org/newsletter, newest listed 'Fall 2025 Newsletter'), which is not a Mass-schedule bulletin. Their schedule is a standing web page, 'Pray With Us' (read 5 November 2026): 'Sunday Mass - 8:00 a.m. Weekday (Mon-Sat) - 7:30 a.m.', chapel open 6 a.m. to 7 p.m.

This row exists because the diocese's rule (convent or monastery chapels are church rows when a current schedule shows Mass open to the public) makes the chapel a church row, and a church row needs a parish row. It is `no_bulletin`, so promote leaves it and its church off the site (Settled policy 4).
