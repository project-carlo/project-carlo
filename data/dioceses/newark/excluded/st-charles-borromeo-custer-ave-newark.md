## Why it is excluded

St. Charles Borromeo parish was merged with Blessed Sacrament on 10 May 1999 ('Blessed Sacrament and St. Charles Borromeo were merged on May 10, 1999', bsscb.org/parish-history, read 2026-11-04). The merged parish's 18 October 2026 bulletin gives its address as 610 Clinton Avenue (the Blessed Sacrament church) and a Mass schedule with no Mass at Custer Avenue. The building at 84 Custer Avenue is now listed as Greater Friendship Baptist Church (MapQuest and other listings found by a 2026-11-04 search), so no Catholic Mass is held there. GCatholic 18503 still lists the church, with no schedule. No source gives the date of the last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty.

Inferred, not read: that the building was sold to the Baptist congregation. The listings give its address, not a sale date.

## What would change this

A BSSCB bulletin or archdiocesan source naming a Mass at St. Charles, 84 Custer Avenue.
