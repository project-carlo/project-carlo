## Why it is excluded

Stella Maris Chapel, 170 Corbin Street in Port Newark, is the port's Apostleship of the Sea chapel, kept by Fr. John Corbett. Its own site (read 2026-11-04) gives "Mass schedule: Sunday 10:30 am" and "Daily Mass Monday 12:00 noon" through Friday, and invites visitors: "We would love to meet you and celebrate Mass together." The Mass is public, so it would be a church row. But no bulletin covers it. The site links none, and its Discover Mass listing says "There are currently no bulletins available". The bulletins of the Ironbound parishes around it list only their own churches: Our Lady of Mount Carmel (John Patrick 6017, 1 November 2026) and Our Lady of Fatima (04/1091, 1 November 2026). It was never a parish row, so it is excluded as `no-bulletin`. No `parish` is set: the chapel answers to no parish whose bulletin I read.

The schedule's date is weak. The site's footer says "© 2021 Father John Corbett", and the rcan.org posts I searched mention neither the chapel nor Fr. Corbett. I took the live site's schedule as current because nothing says Mass stopped.
