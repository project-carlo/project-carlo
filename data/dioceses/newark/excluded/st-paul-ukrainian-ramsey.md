## Why it is excluded

St. Paul, Cherry Ln. & Darlington Ave., Ramsey, NJ is an Eastern Catholic church of the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia. Its GCatholic page (https://gcatholic.org/churches/usa-03/17493, read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia", "Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)" and the address "Cherry Ln. & Darlington Ave., Ramsey, New Jersey 07446". It is in the Newark roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule in `diocese.md` (*Rules settled*) for Eastern Catholic churches. Its Divine Liturgy schedule and bulletin were not checked.

Not to be confused with the Roman Catholic St. Paul, Ramsey, which GCatholic lists 950 m away.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs in its eparchy's dataset, not the Archdiocese of Newark's.
