## Why it is excluded

St. Thomas Syro-Malankara Mission, Rahway, NJ is an Eastern Catholic church of the Syro-Malankara Eparchy of St. Mary, Queen of Peace. Its GCatholic page (https://gcatholic.org/churches/usa-03/17006, read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Syro-Malankara Diocese of Saint Mary, Queen of Peace, of the United States of America and Canada", "Rite: Syro-Malankara (Antiochian)" and the address "St. Mark Church, 287 Hamilton St., Rahway, New Jersey 07065". It is in the Newark roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule in `diocese.md` (*Rules settled*) for Eastern Catholic churches. Its Divine Liturgy schedule and bulletin were not checked.

GCatholic places the mission (history: 'mission 1996') at St. Mark Church, 287 Hamilton St., Rahway. That church was demolished in August 2018 (https://www.rahwayrising.com/st-marks-church-demolished/, read 2026-11-05; recorded as `st-mark-rahway`, excluded closed), and GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7MR23+3J lies next to Sts. Peter and Paul in Elizabeth, not on Hamilton Street. So I recorded the town without a street; the mission's current meeting place was not checked. It is excluded on jurisdiction, which doesn't depend on where it meets. Not to be confused with St. Thomas the Apostle (Ruthenian), 1410 Church St., Rahway (`st-thomas-the-apostle-byzantine-rahway`).

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs in its eparchy's dataset, not the Archdiocese of Newark's.
