## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Sorrows parish, Garfield, merged into Our Lady of Mount Virgin on 1 July 2016. We the Italians / Il Regno (posted 9 March 2017): "On July 1, 2016 the Church of Our Lady of Sorrows in Garfield, New Jersey, was merged with Our Lady of Mount Virgin and ceased to exist", calling it "the recently closed Our Lady of Sorrows Roman Catholic Church" and saying daily Mass continued only "in the 'old' chapel around the corner from the closed site" (that chapel is `our-lady-of-sorrows-chapel-garfield`, also excluded). The Record (30 June 2016; paywalled, read from its search snippet) dates the last Sunday Mass to 26 June 2016, so `last_mass` is 2016-06. The archdiocese's closed-or-merged records table lists it as merged into Our Lady of Mount Virgin, whose 1 November 2026 bulletin and Mass times page name only the MacArthur Avenue church.

GCatholic (18410) still lists the church at 69 Market St. with no status. I didn't find what became of the building.

## What would change this

A current Our Lady of Mount Virgin bulletin or archdiocesan listing showing Mass at 69 Market St.
