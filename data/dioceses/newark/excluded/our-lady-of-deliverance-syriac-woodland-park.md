## Why it is excluded

Our Lady of Deliverance, 220 Browertown Rd., Woodland Park, NJ is an Eastern Catholic church of the Syriac Catholic Eparchy of Our Lady of Deliverance. Its GCatholic page (https://gcatholic.org/churches/usa-03/25653, read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Syriac Diocese of Our Lady of Deliverance in the United States", "Rite: Syriac (Antiochian)" and the address "Holy Cross Polish National Catholic Church, 220 Browertown Rd., Woodland Park, New Jersey 07002". It is in the Newark roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule in `diocese.md` (*Rules settled*) for Eastern Catholic churches. Its Divine Liturgy schedule and bulletin were not checked.

GCatholic lists it as a parish meeting in Holy Cross Polish National Catholic Church, 220 Browertown Rd., Woodland Park, and says it is 'within the territory of the Roman Metropolitan Archdiocese of Newark'. Woodland Park is in Passaic County, which is in the Diocese of Paterson, not the Archdiocese of Newark, so GCatholic's territory is wrong; either way the parish belongs to the Syriac eparchy. GCatholic's zip 07002 is Bayonne's; Woodland Park's is 07424, which I recorded. Its website http://www.ourladyofdeliverancechurch.net does not resolve (`probe url` DEAD-DNS by the proxy's resolver, 2026-11-05); whether it still meets there was not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs in its eparchy's dataset, not the Archdiocese of Newark's.
