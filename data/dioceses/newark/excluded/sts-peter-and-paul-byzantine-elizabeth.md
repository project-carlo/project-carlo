## Why it is excluded

Sts. Peter and Paul, 316 1st Ave., Elizabeth, NJ is a Byzantine (Ruthenian) Catholic church. Its GCatholic page (https://gcatholic.org/churches/usa-03/17174, read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic", "within the territory of the Roman Metropolitan Archdiocese of Newark", "Rite: Ruthenian (Byzantine)". It is in the Newark roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule in `diocese.md` (*Rules settled*) for Eastern Catholic churches. That holds whether or not the church is still open.

## Whether it still exists

This was **not** confirmed. The Eparchy of Passaic listed "Ss. Peter and Paul", Elizabeth, NJ on its parish list in a Wayback capture of 2017-05-01. But its parish directory of 2019-08-18, its /parishes captures of 2020-09-19 and 2026-06-15, and the live list read 2026-11-05 have no Elizabeth entry. GCatholic's phone number for it, +1 (908) 486-6500, is the rectory number the eparchy gives for St. George, 417 McCandless St, Linden (also excluded here, `st-george-byzantine-linden`). That suggests, but doesn't show, that the Elizabeth parish closed or was folded into Linden around 2018–2019. A DuckDuckGo search for the church and for "316 First Avenue" Elizabeth found only the GCatholic page. No source states a closure, so it is not recorded as `closed`.

## What would change this

Nothing would move it into the Newark dataset. Whether it is closed belongs to an Eparchy of Passaic dataset; see `research` for the next check.
