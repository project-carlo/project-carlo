## Why it is excluded

Its GCatholic page (https://gcatholic.org/churches/usa-03/17490, read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Metropolitan Archdiocese of Philadelphia", "Rite: Ukrainian (Byzantine)" and the address "733 Sanford Ave., Irvington, New Jersey 07106". It is in the Newark roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule in `diocese.md` (*Rules settled*) for Eastern Catholic churches. Its Divine Liturgy schedule and bulletin were not checked.

Its website http://www.stjohn-nj.com answered `probe url` on 2026-11-05 (status 200, title 'St. John The Baptist Ukrainian Catholic Church in Newark NJ'): the church is open, and its site says Newark where GCatholic says Irvington (Sanford Ave. runs along the town line). I kept GCatholic's town.
