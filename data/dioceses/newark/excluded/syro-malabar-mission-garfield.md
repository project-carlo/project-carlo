## Why it is excluded

Syro-Malabar Mission, Garfield, Garfield, NJ is an Eastern Catholic church of the Syro-Malabar Eparchy of St. Thomas the Apostle, Chicago. Its GCatholic page (https://gcatholic.org/churches/usa-03/16962, read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Syro-Malabar Diocese of Saint Thomas the Apostle of Chicago", "Rite: Syro-Malabar" and the address "Our Lady of Sorrows Church, 30 Madonna Place, Garfield, New Jersey 07026". It is in the Newark roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule in `diocese.md` (*Rules settled*) for Eastern Catholic churches. Its Divine Liturgy schedule and bulletin were not checked.

GCatholic names the mission only '(Mission)' and places it at Our Lady of Sorrows Church, 30 Madonna Place, Garfield (history: 2000). That Roman church is itself excluded here as closed: Our Lady of Sorrows parish merged into Our Lady of Mount Virgin on 1 July 2016 (`our-lady-of-sorrows-garfield`, `our-lady-of-sorrows-chapel-garfield`). So the mission probably no longer meets at that address; I recorded the town without a street. GCatholic's website for it, http://www.syromalabarmissiongarfield.com, does not resolve (`probe url` DEAD-DNS by the proxy's resolver, 2026-11-05), and a DuckDuckGo search through `visit.py` met a bot check. Where the mission meets now was not found; it doesn't change the exclusion, since it belongs to the Syro-Malabar eparchy wherever it meets.

Nothing expected: it belongs in its eparchy's dataset, not the Archdiocese of Newark's.
