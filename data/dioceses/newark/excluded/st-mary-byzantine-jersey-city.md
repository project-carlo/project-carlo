## Why it is excluded

St. Mary, 231 Pacific Ave., Jersey City, NJ is a Byzantine (Ruthenian) Catholic church. Its GCatholic page (https://gcatholic.org/churches/usa-03/17177, read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic", "within the territory of the Roman Metropolitan Archdiocese of Newark", "Rite: Ruthenian (Byzantine)". The Eparchy of Passaic's own parish list (https://www.eparchyofpassaic.com/parishes, New Jersey tab, read 2026-11-05 in the browser) still lists it: 231 Pacific Ave; administered by the same priest as St. John the Baptist, Bayonne (Rev. Joseph Bertha, rectory 201-339-1840). So the church exists and belongs to the eparchy, not the Archdiocese of Newark.

It is in the Newark roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule in `diocese.md` (*Rules settled*) for Eastern Catholic churches. Its Mass (Divine Liturgy) schedule and bulletin were not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs in an Eparchy of Passaic dataset, not the Archdiocese of Newark's.
