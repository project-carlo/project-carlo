## Why it is excluded

St. Bridget, 372 Montgomery Street, was one of the five churches of Resurrection Parish (1997-2014). St. Mary's parish, which holds Resurrection Parish's records, lists 'St. Bridget's Church (Closed, 2015)' on its sacramental-records page (read 2026-11-03), and the archdiocese's table of closed or merged parishes (modified 2025-05-22) lists 'Saint Bridget, Jersey City' as Closed. The 1 November 2026 St. Mary's bulletin names 'St Bridget’s' and 'St Bridget’s Library' only as rooms where the Catholic Action of Mary and the Knights of Columbus meet; its Mass schedule is for St. Mary's alone. GCatholic still lists the church (18444) under 'Parish of the Resurrection', a parish suppressed in February 2014, with a website that no longer resolves. `last_mass` is the year St. Mary's gives; no source gives the day.

## What would change this

A Mass scheduled at St. Bridget's in St. Mary's bulletin or on an archdiocesan page.
