## Why it is excluded

The archdiocese's records list (rcan.org/sacramental-records/, modified 22 May 2025) gives 'Queen of Angels Parish, Newark' as 'Closed', with its records at the Archdiocesan Archives. Wikipedia (read 4 November 2026) says 'The school and parish closed in 2012' and that the church was 'demolished in 2016'. The year of closure (2012) is Wikipedia's; neither source gives the date of the last Mass. GCatholic (18496) still lists the church; diocese.md's *Sources* notes GCatholic keeps closed churches. The directory has no entry for it, and the neighbouring St. Stanislaus's 11 October 2026 bulletin does not mention it.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a new church on the site: the building was demolished in 2016.
