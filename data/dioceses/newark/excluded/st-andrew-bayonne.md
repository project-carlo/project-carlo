## Why it is excluded

The archdiocese's announcement of 28 May 2025 says a decree permanently closes "St. Andrew the Apostle Church, located at 10 West 4th Street in Bayonne", relegating it to profane but not sordid use, and that "Since September 22, 2024, St. Andrew's Church has been closed for divine worship", when a final Mass of Thanksgiving was celebrated; the parish's worship continues at St. Mary, Star of the Sea. The 1 November 2026 Blessed Miriam Teresa Demjanovich bulletin names only St. Mary, Star of the Sea. `last_mass` 2024-09-22.

Address: the decree gives 10 West 4th Street; GCatholic (18356, which still lists the church) gives 125 Broadway, probably the same corner building's other frontage. I followed the archdiocese.

## What would change this

A Blessed Miriam Teresa Demjanovich bulletin listing Mass at St. Andrew.
