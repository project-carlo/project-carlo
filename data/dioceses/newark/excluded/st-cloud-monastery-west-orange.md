## Why it is excluded

St. Cloud Monastery, 29 Ridgeway Avenue, West Orange, is a house of the Augustinian Recollects: GCatholic (39184, last updated 2025-12-21) lists it as type 'Monastery', 'Served by: Augustinian Recollects', and the order's own page of provinces (recoletos.org, read 2026-11-03) gives 'Saint Cloud Monastery, 29 Ridgeway Ave West Orange NJ 07052-3217' as an address of its US province. A recoletos.org news item describes a Christmas gathering of the order's northeastern communities, including 'St. Cloud Monastery en West Orange', at prayer in its chapel.

The diocese's rule for convent and monastery chapels (diocese.md *Rules settled*, accepted by survey-review on 2026-11-03, which names St. Cloud) makes such a chapel a church row only when a current schedule shows Mass open to the public. I found none. What I checked on 2026-11-03: the GCatholic page (no Mass times, no website); the order's WordPress search on recoletos.org for 'West Orange', 'Cloud' and 'Ridgeway' (news items and the provinces page, no public schedule); the 1 November 2026 bulletin of St. Joseph, West Orange, the parish a few blocks away (no mention of 'Cloud'); rcan.org's search; and guessed domains (stcloudmonastery.org and oarsaintaugustine.org do not resolve; augustinianrecollects.com is a parked page). The directory does not list it. So this is a judgement from absence under the settled rule, not a source saying Mass there is private.

## What would change this

A current schedule (the order's site, a parish bulletin, a sign or a diocesan listing) showing a Mass at St. Cloud open to the public: then it becomes a church row, under the parish whose bulletin lists it or a row of its own.
