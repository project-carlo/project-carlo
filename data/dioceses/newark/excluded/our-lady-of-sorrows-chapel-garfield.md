## Why it is excluded

The original 1917 chapel of Our Lady of Sorrows, around the corner from the 69 Market St. church, kept a daily Mass after the parish merged into Our Lady of Mount Virgin in 2016 (Il Regno, March 2017: "still has a daily mass in the 'old' chapel around the corner from the closed site"). Since 12 May 2023 the archdiocese has listed it for sale as "Our Lady of Sorrows Former Chapel – Garfield", 113 Jewell Street, possession immediate, still 'Available' on 2026-11-03. The 1 November 2026 Our Lady of Mount Virgin bulletin and Mass times page list no Mass there. No source gives the date of its last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty; the archdiocese calling it a "Former Chapel" and offering immediate possession is the statement that it no longer holds Mass.

No roster lists it. Web listings (chamberofcommerce.com) give '111 Jewell St'; I followed the archdiocese's 113.

## What would change this

A Mount Virgin bulletin listing Mass at the Jewell Street chapel.
