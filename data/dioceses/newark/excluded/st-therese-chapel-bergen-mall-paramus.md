## Why it is excluded

GCatholic (18535) still lists 'St. Therese', a Carmelite chapel at 'Bergen Mall, Paramus, New Jersey 07652' (phone +1 (201) 845-6115, Plus Code 87G7WW8R+59). It closed in 2017. The Catholic Register (CNA/EWTN News, 3 March 2017): "St. Therese's Chapel at the Bergen Town Center in Paramus, N.J. ended its 47 years of service with a final Mass on Ash Wednesday. The chapel, also called the Chapel on the Mall, was run by Carmelite priests." The mall's management had ended the lease. Ash Wednesday 2017 was 1 March, hence `last_mass: 2017-03-01`. The New York Times ('After Nearly 50 Years, a Mall's Chapel Offers Its Final Prayers', 28 February 2017) and Fox News, CBCP News and the Bergen Record syndication ('Mall chapel congregants looking for a new home in Paramus', 1 March 2017) report the same.

Checked 2026-11-03 for a successor: a web search for the chapel at a new location found nothing; the article says new sites had been scouted but rent might be prohibitive. Neither the Annunciation nor the Our Lady of the Visitation (Paramus) 1 November 2026 bulletin mentions a St. Therese or Carmelite chapel. The diocese's directory (rcan.org/parishes) has no entry for it. The survey-review rule on convent and monastery chapels (church row only with a current public schedule) does not apply: the chapel is gone, not private.

## What would change this

A current schedule showing the Carmelites' Chapel on the Mall (St. Therese) reopened somewhere in Paramus with public Mass; it would then be a church row (and possibly its own parish row if it publishes a bulletin).
