## Why it is excluded

GCatholic (125251) lists Redemptoris Mater at 672 Passaic Ave., Kearny, as type 'Seminary'. The seminary's own site (rmnewark.org, read 2026-11-04) describes 'The Redemptoris Mater Archdiocesan Missionary Seminary of Newark ... established on October 29, 1990, in Kearny', a house of priestly formation, and gives no Mass schedule: the homepage never mentions Mass and the site's search for 'Mass', 'schedule' and 'Eucharist' returns nothing. The bulletin of St. Stephen, the nearby Kearny parish, does not mention it either. This is a seminary's house chapel with no public schedule found, so `no-public-mass`, as the diocese's rule treats convent and monastery chapels. The evidence that no Mass is public is absence, listed above.

`parish` points to St. Stephen, Kearny, only because the batch grouped them by location; the seminary is not part of that parish.

## What would change this

A current schedule showing a Mass at the seminary open to the public (for example a Neocatechumenal community's Sunday Eucharist advertised in a parish bulletin) would make it a church row of the parish whose bulletin carries it.
