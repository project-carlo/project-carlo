## Why it is excluded

St. Mark's, 287 Hamilton Street, merged with St. Mary's into Divine Mercy Parish in 2010 and the building is gone. Rahway Rising, 9 August 2018: 'St. Mark’s Church on Hamilton Street finally met the wrecking ball on Wednesday, eight years after the parish merged with nearby St. Mary’s', after standing 'largely vacant' since the merger. Divine Mercy's parish history calls St. Mary's 'our current church edifice' and says St. Mark's bells were placed in front of the rectory 'as reminder of the previous church edifice'. The 1 November 2026 Divine Mercy bulletin gives one Mass schedule, at 232 Central Avenue. No source gives the date of the last Mass, so `last_mass` is left empty; it was between the 2010 merger and the August 2018 demolition.

GCatholic still lists the church (59526) and gives 'St. Mark Church, 287 Hamilton St.' as the address of the Syro-Malankara St. Thomas mission (17006). Neither shows Mass in a building that was demolished in 2018; the mission is settled in its own batch.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a new church on the site: the building was demolished in 2018.
