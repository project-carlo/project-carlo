## Why it is excluded

GCatholic (153721) lists Most Blessed Sacrament at 375 13th Ave., Newark as a friary chapel. It is the Franciscan Friars of the Renewal's friary; the order's friaries page (read 4 November 2026) gives only its address, and no source read shows a Mass there open to the public. St. Antoninus, the nearby parish whose events the friars sponsor, lists its Masses only at 337 South Orange Avenue (23 November 2025 bulletin; Mass page). Under the diocese's settled rule, a convent or monastery chapel is a church row only when a current schedule shows public Mass, so this is `no-public-mass`. This rests on absence: the sources checked are the GCatholic page, the friaries page, and St. Antoninus's bulletin and Mass page.

## What would change this

A current schedule showing a public Mass in the friary chapel would make it a church row (of the parish whose bulletin lists it).
