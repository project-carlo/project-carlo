## Why it is excluded

Holy Spirit, Island St. & Church St., Mahwah, NJ is a Byzantine (Ruthenian) Catholic church. Its GCatholic page (https://gcatholic.org/churches/usa-03/17179, read 2026-11-05) gives "Jurisdiction: Ruthenian Diocese of Passaic", "within the territory of the Roman Metropolitan Archdiocese of Newark", "Rite: Ruthenian (Byzantine)". The Eparchy of Passaic's own parish list (https://www.eparchyofpassaic.com/parishes, New Jersey tab, read 2026-11-05 in the browser) still lists it: Island & Church Streets, Mahwah, NJ 07430; website Holyspiritmahwah.com. So the church exists and belongs to the eparchy, not the Archdiocese of Newark.

It is in the Newark roster only because GCatholic lists Eastern churches by territory. Excluded as `other-jurisdiction` under the rule in `diocese.md` (*Rules settled*) for Eastern Catholic churches. Its Mass (Divine Liturgy) schedule and bulletin were not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing expected: it belongs in an Eparchy of Passaic dataset, not the Archdiocese of Newark's.
