## What is known

GCatholic 16803 (read 2026-11-12) lists the Chapel of the Resurrection, Coptic (Alexandrian) rite, founded 1985, at 328 14th St., Brooklyn NY 11215, and gives its jurisdiction as "Jurisdiction: Diocese of Brooklyn". The Coptic Catholic Church has no eparchy in the United States, which is presumably why GCatholic places it under the Latin diocese; that reasoning is mine, not a source's. OSM (Nominatim, read 2026-11-12) names the building "Resurrection Coptic Catholic Church" (way 250424858). The Tablet of 2022-11-15 reports a solemn Mass at "Resurrection Coptic Catholic Church" in Park Slope for its pastor, Msgr. Francis Fayez, pastor since 2013; its 2022-12-09 article on Christmas among the diocese's apostolates also came up for 'Resurrection Coptic' but was not read. No current liturgy schedule and no bulletin were read: the church is not in the Brooklyn Mass finder or directory rosters, and Yahoo search answered HTTP 500.

## Why it is not decided

The diocese's settled rule excludes Eastern Catholic and Coptic churches as other-jurisdiction "each with a source naming the eparchy". For this church the source names the Diocese of Brooklyn, so the rule's premise fails and an other-jurisdiction exclusion would have no source. Whether to map it as a Brooklyn worship site (a parish row if it has a bulletin, otherwise a no-bulletin exclusion) or to extend the rule to it is the owner's decision; the eastern-catholic batch flagged it. See `revisit` for both paths.
