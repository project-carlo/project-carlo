## What is known

The chapel at 103-56 52nd Avenue belongs to St. Leo the Great, Corona: GCatholic 16796 gives St. Leo's phone number for it, and St. Leo's bulletin and site list "Our Lady of Mount Carmel Chapel" as one of the parish's locations. Both use it for Eucharistic exposition every Thursday 5-7 pm. St. Leo's Mass schedule (bulletins of 12 and 19 July and 30 October - 1 November 2026, and /mass-times, all read 2026-11-05) names no place for its Masses and none at the chapel, even in the week of the 16 July Mount Carmel feast. The diocese's parish and deanery directories and its Mass finder don't list the chapel.

## Why it is not decided

The evidence is absence: the schedule lists the chapel for adoration and lists no Mass there, but no source says Mass is never said there or that it is only an adoration chapel. So it is neither a church row nor an exclusion. Under which parish: St. Leo (st-leo-corona), settled by its bulletin and site.
