## What is known

Our Lady of Lourdes, Bushwick (founded 1872) merged with St. Martin of Tours in 2007 (diocesan archives List of Parishes, read 2026-11-07). Its church building at 89 Furman Avenue (Broadway and Aberdeen Street) is in OpenStreetMap as a place of worship (way 250422915).

The Mass finder (place 579, read 2026-11-07) lists "Our Lady Of Lourdes Chapel" at 89 Furman Ave. with a Sunday 11:00am "OLOL Chapel - Bilingual" Mass; the Mass finder is not schedule evidence in this diocese. The parish's own sources show no Mass there: the 8 November 2026 bulletin's schedule (Sunday 9:00, 10:30, 12:00) and the Mass Times page name no other place and no 11:00 Mass, and the 31 May and 13 September 2026 issues never mention the church.

GCatholic 16742, "Chapel of Our Lady of Lourdes" at 1288 Hancock St., is not this building: its address, Plus Code, phone and 1906 founding year are St. Martin of Tours', and it is linked to that church record. So of the two Our Lady of Lourdes listings, the real building is the one at 89 Furman Ave.

## Why it is not decided

No parish source shows Mass at 89 Furman Ave, but no source says the church closed, was relegated or was sold (The Tablet API and the survey's decrees page found nothing). A closure without a source can't be recorded, and a church row needs a parish schedule. See research for the next step.
