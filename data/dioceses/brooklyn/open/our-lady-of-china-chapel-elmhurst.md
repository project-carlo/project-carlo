## What is known

GCatholic (16801) lists Our Lady of China as a Chinese-language chapel founded in 1978 at 54-09 92nd St., Elmhurst (Plus Code 87G8P4QH+3R). The diocesan archives' List of Parishes (page modified 2025-05-23, read 2026-11-06) still lists "Our Lady of China Chapel 54-09 92nd Street Elmhurst, NY 11373" among its chapels, with a phone, email and website and no closure note. The website it gives, www.olc.faithweb.com, is a disabled FreeServers page. OSM has an untagged building at 54-09 92nd Street (40.73776, -73.87034).

## Why it is not decided

No source read on 2026-11-06 shows Mass there now, and none says it closed. It is not in the diocese's Mass finder list, has no Discover Mass page, is not named on the diocese's Chinese Apostolate page, and is not in the bulletins of the nearby parishes mapped so far (St. Bartholomew, Elmhurst, whose Chinese Masses are in its own Ithaca Street chapel; Ascension, Elmhurst). No bulletin found names it, so it has no parish. These are all absences, which are weak evidence; the revisit says what would settle it.
