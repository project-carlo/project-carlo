## What is known

Epiphany church, 96 South 9th Street, Williamsburg (built for a parish founded 1905), belongs to Ss. Peter and Paul - Epiphany parish since the 2007 merger (diocesan archives' List of Parishes: "Merged with SS. Peter & Paul, 2007"). The Tablet of 10 February 2016 described the merged parish as "one parish with two worship sites". GCatholic (16672) still lists Epiphany as a church, and OpenStreetMap still tags the building as a Catholic place of worship.

No current parish source mentions Epiphany as a Mass site: on 2026-11-07 the parish's Contact page named one church ("Ss. Peter & Paul - Epiphany Parish Church, 288 Berry Street") and the rectory (71 South 3rd Street), its /mass-times page gave a single schedule naming no building, and the 8 November 2026 bulletin and four other 2026 issues never mention South 9th Street. The diocese's directory, deanery list and Mass finder list only the Berry Street/South 3rd Street parish. Aggregator sites (catholicmasstime.org, masstime.us, catholicchurch.directory) still list "Epiphany" at 96 South Ninth Street, some with the parish's current schedule and some with older times; they are not sources.

## Why it is not decided

The evidence is absence: nothing shows Mass at Epiphany now, and nothing says it closed, was reduced to profane use or was sold. The parish office or a news or property record would settle it (see revisit).
