## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish site's Mass Times page (read 2026-11-06) gives Monday-Friday 9 a.m., Saturday 5 p.m. (Vigil), Sunday 10 a.m. and 1 p.m. (Haitian Creole), and the newest posted bulletin (28 June 2026) lists intentions at those times. No bulletin since June has been found (see the parish row), so the schedule is from the site, not a current bulletin. GCatholic records the church destroyed on 2019-06-16 and the new church dedicated on 2023-03-26.
