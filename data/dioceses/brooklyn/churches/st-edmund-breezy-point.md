## Holds Mass

Open all year, not a summer chapel: the 1 November 2026 Blessed Trinity bulletin lists a Sunday 10:30 am Mass at St. Edmund on 1 and 8 November, and the parish homepage (read 2026-11-06) lists Sunday 10:30 AM at St. Edmund. The site's History page says the parish library was housed in St. Edmund's from 1996.

## Name and address

The bulletin and site say St. Edmund, as does GCatholic 16805, so the name is verified. Only GCatholic gives an address: Clinton Walk, Breezy Point 11697, with no house number; the diocese directory and Mass finder do not list the church, and OSM had no match for it (Nominatim search 2026-11-06).
