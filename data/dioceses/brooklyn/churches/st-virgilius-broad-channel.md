## Holds Mass

Open, not closed: the 8 November 2026 St. Camillus-St. Virgilius bulletin (page 1) lists a Saturday Evening Vigil Mass at 4 pm and Sundays 10:00 am at St. Virgilius Church, with confessions before the Sunday Mass and a rosary "at St. Virgilius Church … before the 4 pm Vigil".

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic 16692 and OSM call it St. Virgilius. The bulletin prints the church at 14 Noel Road and the parish office at 16 Noel Road; GCatholic, OSM and the Mass finder (place 1189) give 16 Noel Road. I chose the bulletin's 14 Noel Road for the church, since 16 is the office the bulletin names separately; the two are neighbouring lots and either finds the building. The diocese directory does not list the church separately. Coordinates are the centre of the OSM church building (way 285046923), 15 m from GCatholic's Plus Code.
