## Name and address

The bulletin masthead calls it "Saint Sebastian Roman Catholic Church" at 58–02 Roosevelt Ave, with the rectory at 39–63 57th Street; the ParishesOnline org gives 58-02 Roosevelt Avenue too. The diocese's directory, deanery list, Mass finder and GCatholic all give 39-63 57th Street, which the bulletin itself calls the rectory. I recorded the church's own address, 58-02 Roosevelt Avenue (the church fronts Roosevelt Ave on the same block as the rectory). Only the parish's own sources (bulletin and its LPi org, one source) give it, so `address_verified` is false. The name St. Sebastian is common to every source.

Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8P3WV+FG (40.7461875, -73.9061875), on the block between Roosevelt Ave and 39th Ave and 57th-58th Streets; the ParishesOnline pin (40.744993, -73.906423) sits on Roosevelt Ave at the front of the same block. Either finds the building.
