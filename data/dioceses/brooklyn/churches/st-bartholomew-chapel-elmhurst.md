Yes, current. ## Name and address

The bulletin calls it the Chapel on Ithaca St; the parish history calls it "the present chapel on Ithaca St.", the 1910 church. No source gives the chapel its own number. I recorded the parish's Ithaca Street address, 43-22, which the bulletin uses for the rectory office on the same street; the chapel is next to it. No roster lists the chapel separately. Coordinates are left for the geocode stage: no source pins the chapel building itself.
