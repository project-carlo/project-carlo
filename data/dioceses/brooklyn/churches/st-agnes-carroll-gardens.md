Yes. The 4 October 2026 bulletin, the newest posted when read on 2026-11-06, lists Sunday Masses at Saint Agnes at 10:30 AM in English ("NEW TIME! As of OCT 04") and 12:15 PM in Spanish. The issue is five weeks old (see the parish's *Not yet confirmed*).
