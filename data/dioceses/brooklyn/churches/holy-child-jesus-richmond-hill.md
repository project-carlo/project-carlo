## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 8 November 2026 parish bulletin (page 6, read 2026-11-06) gives Mass at Holy Child Jesus on Saturday at 5:00 pm (Vigil) and 6:30 pm (Spanish) and on Sunday at 8:30 am, 10:30 am (Spanish), 12:30 pm and 5:00 pm. Weekday Masses (8:30 am Monday-Friday, Wednesday 7:30 pm Spanish) are listed under "Weekday: Leonard Center", the parish building with a chapel ("la Capilla del Leonard Centro").

## Name and address

The bulletin gives "Holy Child Jesus, 111-11 86th Ave, Richmond Hill, NY 11418". The diocese directory, deanery list and Mass finder 820 (all for the merged parish, at 111-11 86th Avenue) and GCatholic 16605 (111-11 86th Ave) agree, so name and address are verified. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8M5X6+HX; Mass finder 820's pin (40.69909, -73.83766) is within about 20 m.

The Leonard Center's address is not in the bulletin. It is taken here as part of the parish grounds (the parish office is at 86-13 112th Street, by the church), which is an inference: see `revisit`.
