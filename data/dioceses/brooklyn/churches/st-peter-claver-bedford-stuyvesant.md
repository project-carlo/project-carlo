## Holds Mass

Yes. The 8 November 2026 St. Martin de Porres bulletin (image-only; page 1 read 2026-11-07 by rendering) lists "St. Peter Claver Church, 29 Claver Place, Brooklyn, NY 11238": Saturday 5pm, Sunday 11:30am, Monday and Wednesday 12noon, first Fridays 12noon. Saturday confessions are at St. Peter Claver from 4:00pm to 4:45pm.

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic 16765 and the Mass finder's place 1152 all call it St. Peter Claver (name verified). The bulletin and GCatholic give 29 Claver Place, Brooklyn 11238 (address verified). The Mass finder gives 21 Claver Pl., a few doors away, which I did not follow because two other sources agree on 29. The coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8M2JV+V2.
