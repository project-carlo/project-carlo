## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 1 November 2026 Mary, Mother of the Church bulletin (page 1, read 2026-11-07) gives St. John Cantius its own schedule: Sunday 9:30 AM (English) and 11:00 AM (Spanish), Thursday 7:00 PM (Spanish), with confessions Thursday 6:15-6:45 PM. Its parish was merged with St. Gabriel's into Mary, Mother of the Church in 2007 (batch plan, archives list), but the church was not closed.

## Name and address

The bulletin and GCatholic 16723 both give St. John Cantius, 479 New Jersey Avenue, Brooklyn, NY 11207, so name and address are verified. The diocese directory, deanery list and Mass finder in this batch give only the Linwood Street church. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8M494+4G.
