## Holds Mass

Yes. The 8 November 2026 St. Brigid bulletin (read 2026-11-07) lists Saturday 5:00pm and 7:00pm, Sunday 7:30am, 9:00am, 9:00am Español "Iglesia de Abajo" (the lower church), 11:00am and 1:00pm, Monday-Saturday 8:45am and Thursday 7:00pm, under the masthead address 409 Linden Street. The lower church is taken to be in this building: the bulletin gives no other address.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("Saint Brigid Roman Catholic Church"), GCatholic 16690 and the diocese's directories all call it St. Brigid and give 409 Linden Street, Brooklyn 11237 (name and address verified). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8P32Q+J8; the Mass finder pin (place 138) is about 60 m away on the same block.
