## Holds Mass

Yes, current. Page 2 of the 1 November 2026 bulletin (a scanned image, read 2026-11-06) lists Monday-Friday 8:30 a.m., Saturday 8:30 a.m. and 5:00 p.m., and Sunday 8:30, 10:30 a.m. and 12:00 p.m. (Spanish). The same page's Mass intentions run from 31 October to 8 November.

## Name and address

The bulletin (rectory 111-50 115th Street), the diocese's directory and deanery list, GCatholic 16645 and Mass finder 1136 all give 111-50 115th Street, 11420. The deanery list carries the parish under both Q8 and B8 (Brooklyn West), with the same name and address; both entries are linked here. B8 is presumably a listing error, since South Ozone Park is in Queens. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code position; the Mass finder pin (40.678182, -73.824458) is within 30 m.
