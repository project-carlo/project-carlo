## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The parish site's schedule page (read 2026-11-05) lists Sunday 8:30am English, 10:00am and 12:30 Spanish, Saturday 5:30pm and 8:00pm, and daily 8:30am; the 6 September 2026 bulletin, the only one posted, gives nearly the same schedule (Saturday 8:30 pm Neocatechumenal) and announces new Sunday times from 20 September.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead ("26-26 98th street & Astoria BLVD"), the diocese's parish and deanery directories, GCatholic 16714 and Mass finder 785 all give 26-26 98th Street, East Elmhurst 11369. The parish calls itself St. Gabriel of the Sorrowful Mother; the record keeps the directory's short name, which the site also uses ("St. Gabriel Parish"). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code position; the Mass finder pin (40.76144, -73.87135) is about 55 m south.
