## Holds Mass

Yes. The 8 November 2026 St. Martin of Tours bulletin (read 2026-11-07) lists Saturday 6:30 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM, 10:30 AM and 12:00 PM, and weekday 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM Masses, under the rectory address 1288 Hancock Street; the site's Mass Times page agrees.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("SAINT MARTIN OF TOURS PARISH") and OSM ("Saint Martin of Tours Roman Catholic Church") give the name (verified). The bulletin's rectory address, the diocese's directories and GCatholic 16742 give 1288 Hancock Street, 11221 (verified); OSM places the building on Knickerbocker Avenue at the Hancock Street corner with postcode 11207, the same building. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8M3VR+88, 25 m from the OSM way.

GCatholic 16742 is linked here although it is titled "Chapel of Our Lady of Lourdes": its address, Plus Code, phone and founding year (1906, St. Martin of Tours' year per the diocesan archives; Our Lady of Lourdes dates from 1872) are all this church's. The Our Lady of Lourdes building at 89 Furman Avenue has its own `open/` record.
