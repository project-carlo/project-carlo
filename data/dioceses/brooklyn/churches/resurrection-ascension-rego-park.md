## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 1 November 2026 Resurrection Ascension - Our Lady of the Angelus bulletin (page 1, read 2026-11-06) lists for the R.A. Roman Catholic Church Mass Monday-Saturday 9am, Saturday vigil 4:00pm, and Sunday 9:30am, 11:00am (English) and 12:30pm (Spanish); page 12 adds an Indonesian Mass on the second Sunday at 2 pm and a Filipino Mass on the third Sunday at 3 pm.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it "R.A. Roman Catholic Church" under the parish heading "Resurrection Ascension"; GCatholic and the diocese call it Resurrection-Ascension, which I used. The bulletin (page 1) and GCatholic 16660 both give the church at 85-25 61st Road, Rego Park 11374, so the address is verified. The diocese directory, deanery list and Mass finder 1174 give 61-11 85th Street, which the bulletin's page 13 names as the rectory office (it writes "Middle Village, NY 11379" there; the directory says Rego Park 11374): that is the office around the corner, not the church, so I followed the bulletin and GCatholic. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8P4HH+67; the Mass finder pin (40.727705, -73.872647, the rectory address) is about 80 m away.
