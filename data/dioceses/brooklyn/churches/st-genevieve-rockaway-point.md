## Holds Mass

Open all year, not a summer chapel: the 1 November 2026 Blessed Trinity bulletin lists Sunday 9:15 am Masses at St. Genevieve on 1 and 8 November, a Saturday 4:00 pm Mass and a 10:00 am VFW Veterans Mass on 7 November, and a Friday 6 November 11am Mass with Exposition. The parish homepage (read 2026-11-06) lists Saturday 4:00 PM and Sunday 9:15 AM at St. Genevieve.

## Name and address

The bulletin and parish site say St. Genevieve; GCatholic and OSM agree on the name. Neither the bulletin nor the site gives the church's address, and the diocese directory and Mass finder do not list it. GCatholic 16715 gives 6 Beach 178th St.; OSM node 8424644115 gives 26 Church Avenue, Roxbury. Both put it on the same spot (25 m apart). I chose OSM's 26 Church Avenue because it is an address tag on the building's own node, while GCatholic's street address is unsourced; either finds the building. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code. Address unverified: no official source gives one.
