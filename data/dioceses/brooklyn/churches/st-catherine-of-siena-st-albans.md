## Holds Mass

Yes, current. Page 1 of the 1 November 2026 Our Lady of Light bulletin (read and rendered 2026-11-06) lists St. Catherine of Sienna, Riverton St. and Baisley Blvd., with Mass Sunday 8am and Monday to Saturday 8am.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it St. Catherine of Sienna and gives its office at 118-22 Riverton Street, St. Albans, NY 11412 (church at Riverton St. and Baisley Blvd.). GCatholic 16636 (St. Catherine of Siena, 118-22 Riverton St.), the diocese directory and the deanery list (Our Lady of Light, 118-22 Riverton Street) agree, so name and address are verified. The record uses the usual spelling Siena; the bulletin spells it Sienna. Mass finder 381 names St. Catherine of Siena but gives St. Pascal's street address (112-43 198th St.); its pin is at Riverton Street, about 10 m from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8M6RP+2M, which the coordinates use. The directory and deanery entries are for the merged parish at this address, so they are linked here.
