## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The November 8, 2026 St. Bartholomew bulletin (container 03/1400, read 2026-11-05) lists Masses at the "CHURCH - WHITNEY AV": Mon-Fri 11:15 AM (English) and 6:30 PM (Spanish), Saturday 9 AM, 5 PM and 6:30 PM, Sunday 8 AM, 10 AM, 11:30 AM, 1 PM, 5 PM and 6:30 PM.

## Name and address

The bulletin places the church on Whitney Avenue without a number, and the parish history says the main church was built on Whitney Ave. and dedicated in 1930. The diocese's directory, deanery list and Mass finder (place 1185), GCatholic 16684 and ParishesOnline all give the parish address, 43-22 Ithaca Street, which the bulletin calls the rectory office; OSM tags the 43-22 building as 12.7 m high, a house-sized building. Discover Mass gives 87-98 Whitney Ave., which I recorded as the only street number any source gives for a church on Whitney Avenue; one source, so address_verified is false. The bulletin's other Whitney address, 87-34 Whitney Av (corner of Judge St), sits with the religious-education email and is probably that office. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, which falls on the tall building at the Whitney Avenue end of the parish block (OSM way 284687002, 29.4 m, tagged 43-02 Ithaca Street), probably the church; the Mass finder pin (40.74318, -73.87654) is on the 43-22 Ithaca Street building. See revisit for the street number.
