## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, a scanned image, read 2026-11-06) lists Sunday 9:00 am in English and 10:30 am in Spanish at St. Teresa, plus Monday and Tuesday 8:30 am. The 25 October 2026 issue (text) gives the same schedule.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("St. Teresa Church, 109-26 130th Street", also the rectory), the diocese directory and deanery list (as the parish address), Mass finder 1166 and GCatholic 16785 ("St. Theresa of Avila") all give 109-26 130th Street, 11420. The name follows the parish's own spelling. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code position; the Mass finder pin is about 40 m away. Mass finder 1166's roster name carries "(St. Anthony of Padua)", but its address and pin are St. Teresa's, so it is linked here.
