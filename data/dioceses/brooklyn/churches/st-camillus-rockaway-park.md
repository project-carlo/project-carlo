## Holds Mass

The 8 November 2026 St. Camillus-St. Virgilius bulletin (page 1) lists a Sunday 11:30 am Mass at St. Camillus Church and a first-Saturday 12 noon Mass; the week's intentions list "St. Camillus … Sun. 11:30 AM".

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic 16691 and OSM call it St. Camillus (Church). The bulletin prints the church at 99-21 Rockaway Beach Blvd.; the diocese directory, deanery list, GCatholic and OSM give 99-15 Rockaway Beach Blvd. I chose the bulletin's 99-21, the parish's own address for the church building: the parish office is now at 16 Noel Road, so the directory's 99-15 is probably the former rectory or parish address on the same block. Either finds the building. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, on the OSM building. The Mass finder's place 1189 is named '(St. Camillus)' and pinned here although its street address is St. Virgilius's office (16 Noel Road), so it is linked here.
