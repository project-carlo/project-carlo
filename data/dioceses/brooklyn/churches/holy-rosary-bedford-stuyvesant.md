## Holds Mass

Yes. The 8 November 2026 St. Martin de Porres bulletin (image-only; page 1 read 2026-11-07 by rendering) lists "Holy Rosary Church, 139 Chauncey Street / Brooklyn, NY 11233": Sundays 9am, Tuesdays 11am. Sunday confessions are at Holy Rosary Church from 8:15am to 8:45am.

## Name and address

The bulletin calls it Holy Rosary Church, as GCatholic 16743 does (name verified). The address sources differ by one number: the bulletin gives 139 Chauncey Street and GCatholic 141 Chauncey St. I recorded the parish's own 139. The two are probably one building's frontage or the church and its neighbour. Either finds it, but with no second source for 139 the address is not marked verified. The coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8M3JC+52. Holy Rosary is not in the diocese's directories (they list the merged parish at 583 Throop Avenue) or the Mass finder.
