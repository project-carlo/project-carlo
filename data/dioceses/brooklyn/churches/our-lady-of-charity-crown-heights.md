## Holds Mass

The 8 November 2026 St. Matthew Parish bulletin (read 2026-11-09) lists Our Lady Of Charity, 1669 Dean St., on page 1 with a Sunday 10am Mass (Rosary 9:00am, Praise & Worship 9:30am), and page 3 carries its Mass intentions. The parish merged with St. Matthew in 2011 (diocesan archives); the church keeps its Sunday Mass under St. Matthew's bulletin.
