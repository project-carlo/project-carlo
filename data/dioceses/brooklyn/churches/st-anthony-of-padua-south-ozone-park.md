## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, a scanned image, read 2026-11-06) lists the Saturday 5:00 pm English Vigil and the Sunday 12:30 pm Spanish Mass at St. Anthony, plus Thursday and Friday 8:30 am. The 25 October 2026 issue (text) gives the same schedule and the same address.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("St. Anthony Church, 127-17 135th Avenue") and the diocese's Mass finder place 1166 give 127-17 135th Avenue. GCatholic 16679 gives 133-25 128th St. Queens numbering puts that on 128th Street near 133rd-135th Avenue, so it is probably the side-street address of the same lot. That is inferred from the numbering, not checked on a map. The parish's and the diocese's address is recorded. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code position (40.66906, -73.81194), near 135th Avenue and 128th Street. The name follows GCatholic and the Mass finder; the bulletin calls it "St. Anthony Church". The parish directory and the deanery list don't list it separately; they give the merged parish once, at St. Teresa's address.
