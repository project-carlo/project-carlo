## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 All Saints-Pompeii bulletin (read 2026-11-07) lists Sunday 9:00 am English and 11:30 am Spanish, weekday Masses (in the chapel Monday, Wednesday, Thursday; in the church Friday and Tuesday 7:30 pm) and a Saturday 8:00 pm Neocatechumenal Way Mass, under the masthead address 115 Throop Ave. The weekday chapel is taken to be in this building: the bulletin gives no other address.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("All SAINTS –POMPEII CATHOLIC CHURCH") and GCatholic 16591 call it All Saints (name verified). The bulletin, the directory, the deanery list and GCatholic all give 115 Throop Ave. (address verified). Mass finder 794 gives "225 Seigel Street" (Pompeii's street) but pins 40.700955, -73.94463, this building; following the settled rule, the entry is linked here and Pompeii has its own record from the page's address. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8P324+85.
