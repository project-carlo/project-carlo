## Holds Mass

The 1 November 2026 Blessed Trinity bulletin lists Masses at "St. Thomas" every day (weekdays 8:30 am, Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 am, 11:45 am and 7:00 pm), and the parish homepage's Mass Times (read 2026-11-06) give the same at St. Thomas More. It is the parish's main church, beside the rectory and office.

## Name and address

The bulletin and site call it St. Thomas More (Church); GCatholic 16788 the same. 204-25 Rockaway Point Blvd. is the address the diocese directory, deanery list and Mass finder give for Blessed Trinity, and GCatholic gives it for St. Thomas More (with town Far Rockaway; the directory's Rockaway Point is used). The bulletin gives it as the parish office. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, 30 m from the Mass finder pin. The directory and Mass finder name the parish, Blessed Trinity, for this place; their entries are linked here as the parish's main church.
