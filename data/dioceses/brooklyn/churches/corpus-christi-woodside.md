## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 8 November 2026 bulletin (container 03/9001, read 2026-11-06) lists Sunday 8:45 am, 11:00 am (Spanish), 12:30 pm and 3:00 pm Filipino (4th Sunday), Monday to Saturday 8:00 am, and page 2 gives intentions for the 8:00 AM Masses of 9-14 November; both this issue and St. Sebastian's name the celebrants for its 15 November Masses.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the deanery directory, the Mass finder and GCatholic all give Corpus Christi, 31-30 61st Street, Woodside 11377 (ParishesOnline writes 3130 61st St); name and address agree across independent sources. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G8Q34X+F2; the Mass finder pin is about 50 m east.

The diocese's parish directory has no Corpus Christi entry of its own; its merged "St. Sebastian – Corpus Christi" entry is linked to the St. Sebastian church, whose address it gives.
