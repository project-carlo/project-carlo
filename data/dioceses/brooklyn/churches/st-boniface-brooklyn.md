## Holds Mass

Yes, current. The 5 November 2026 Oratory newsletter (read 2026-11-06) lists St. Boniface Masses on 8 November 11:15am and 6:00pm and 9-13 November 12:10pm, and the site's /mass-times page gives Sunday 11:15am and 6:00pm and Monday-Friday 12:10pm at St. Boniface.

## Name and address

The newsletter, the diocese's directory and Mass finder 211 give 190 Duffield Street; the deanery list and GCatholic 16688 give 109 Willoughby Street. Willoughby Street meets Duffield Street at the church, so either address finds it; recorded 190 Duffield, the parish's own address for the church. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code position on the building. Mass finder 211's pin is at Assumption (about 1 km north-west), so it is not used. Mass finder 211 is one place covering both Oratory churches; it is linked here because it carries St. Boniface's name and street.
