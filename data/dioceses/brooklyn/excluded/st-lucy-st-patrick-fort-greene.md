## Why it is excluded

Closed. The Tablet (18 July 2022) says St. Lucy-St. Patrick, 285 Willoughby Ave., "has been shuttered since its last Mass in late Fall 2021" and that Bishop Brennan's decree that it "will no longer be used for divine worship" took effect on 1 July 2022. The Tablet of 7 April 2026 again says the parish "closed in 2021". Mary of Nazareth, into which it merged on 31 January 2011, now says "All Masses are at Sacred Heart Church at 32 Clermont Ave." (read 2026-11-07). `last_mass` is 2021 ("late Fall 2021", no day given).

GCatholic still lists it (16736) as a church; the directory, the deanery list and the Mass finder do not.

## What would change this

A Mary of Nazareth bulletin or a diocesan notice scheduling Mass at 285 Willoughby Ave. again.
