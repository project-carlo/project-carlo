## Why it is excluded

This is Our Lady of Victory, 2 Floral Parkway, Floral Park NY 11001, which GCatholic 16843 (read 2026-11-07) places under "Jurisdiction: Diocese of Rockville Centre". It is not a Brooklyn church. It appears here only because the Brooklyn Mass finder's place 1152, "St. Martin de Porres (Our Lady of Victory)", confused it with St. Martin de Porres's Our Lady of Victory Church at 583 Throop Avenue, Bedford-Stuyvesant: the page lists this Floral Park address as a worship site and is pinned here. Following the settled rule (link a finder entry to the church at its pin), massfinder:1152 is recorded on this exclusion. The Bedford-Stuyvesant church is `churches/our-lady-of-victory-bedford-stuyvesant`. `parish` names St. Martin de Porres as the related parish only because of this mix-up.

## What would change this

Nothing about the jurisdiction. If the Mass finder corrects place 1152's address and pin to 583 Throop Avenue, move massfinder:1152 to the Bedford-Stuyvesant church.
