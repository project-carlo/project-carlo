## Why it is excluded

The former Holy Family (Slovak) Church holds public Mass as San Damiano Mission but publishes no bulletin. Its own site (read 2026-11-07, page modified 2026-11-03) says "Our church building, also known as Holy Family Catholic Church, was founded in 1905 by Slovak immigrants and was renamed San Damiano Mission in 2015", that it is "part of the Diocese of Brooklyn" and run since October 2020 by the Shalom Catholic Community, and lists Mass on Thursday and Friday 6:15 PM, Saturday Vigil 4:00 PM and Sunday 12:15 PM. The Tablet records the conversion in February 2015 (Conventual Franciscans first, 2016-08-31 article) and a priest assigned to reside at the mission from 1 July 2026.

The archives list says Holy Family, Greenpoint "Merged with St. Anthony St. Alphonsus, 2011", but the mission is not in that parish's bulletin: the 1 November 2026 St. Anthony – St. Alphonsus issue has one schedule for 862 Manhattan Avenue and no mention of Holy Family, Nassau Avenue, San Damiano or Shalom. The mission's site links no bulletin, newsletter or PDF (WordPress media API: none), and ParishesOnline has no organization for it under the slugs tried. So it is open, with public Mass, and nothing the pipeline can read: `no-bulletin`, not `closed`.

Address: the mission's site gives 85 North 15th St.; GCatholic and The Tablet place Holy Family on Nassau Avenue (GCatholic: 21 Nassau Ave, Plus Code 87G8P2FW+69, 40.72306, -73.95406), the same corner building. I follow the mission's own address. No diocesan directory or Mass finder entry lists the mission.

## What would change this

A bulletin or newsletter posted on sandamianomission.org (or its Facebook page), or the St. Anthony – St. Alphonsus bulletin starting to print the mission's Masses, would make it a worship site of a parish row. See research for the recheck.
