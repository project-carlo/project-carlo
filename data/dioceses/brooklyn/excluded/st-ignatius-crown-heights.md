## Why it is excluded

The church was demolished. The Tablet of 1 November 2017 says the Church of St. Ignatius merged with St. Francis of Assisi-St. Blaise in 2011 and that the Jesuits now want to reach "the five-story homeless shelter built on the former site of St. Ignatius Church". The diocesan archives give the 2011 merger, and the 8 November 2026 St. Francis of Assisi-St. Blaise bulletin lists Mass only at its church on Maple Street (no mention of Ignatius or Carroll Street). GCatholic still lists it at 1101 Carroll St.; that listing is out of date. `last_mass` 2011 is the merger year; no source gives the date of the last Mass.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a new church on the site; the former rectory houses the Carroll Street Jesuit Community (a residence, not a listed public Mass site).
