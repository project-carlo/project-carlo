## Why it is excluded

Closed as a worship site. The Tablet (7 April 2026) says St. Michael and St. Edward were "two other Fort Greene parishes that merged in 1942 but later closed and consolidated with Sacred Heart Parish in 2008", and Mary of Nazareth's history page says "In 2008, the Church of St. Michael-St. Edward was consolidated into Sacred Heart Church". Mary of Nazareth's Mass Times page (read 2026-11-07) says "All Masses are at Sacred Heart Church", and its 1 November 2026 bulletin names no other church. `last_mass` 2008 is inferred from the consolidation year; no source gives the last Mass.

The building at 108 St. Edward's St. still stands (OSM way 250366829); GCatholic 16756 still lists it as a church. Its current use was not found: The Tablet's API (searches 'St. Michael-St. Edward', 'St. Edward Street') returned no article on a reuse or sale, and DuckDuckGo returned a bot challenge.

## What would change this

A source showing public Mass at 108 St. Edward's St. now (for instance a reuse by another Catholic community), which would make it a worship site of whichever bulletin lists it.
