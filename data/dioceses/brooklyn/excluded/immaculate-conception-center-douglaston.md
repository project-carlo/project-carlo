## Why it is excluded

The Immaculate Conception Center at 7200 Douglaston Parkway is the diocese's pastoral center, not a parish. On the deanery directory (read 2026-11-06) it appears only in the page's "Contact Us" footer, next to the 310 Prospect Park West offices; the roster entry `deaneries:q10-immaculate-conception-center` is that footer read as part of Deanery Q10. The diocese describes it as the home of the Bishop Mugavero Residence for Senior Priests, "a senior living community for retired priests" with "a recently renovated Chapel", and as the place where diaconate aspirancy sessions are held. Its "Main Chapel" hosted a one-off public Mass by Bishop Brennan on 7 May 2025 for the start of the conclave (press release of 6 May 2025).

No regular public Mass was found: the Mass finder lists no place at this address, and the diocese site search turned up no schedule. The judgement that the chapels serve the residents and diocesan events rather than the public is inferred from those descriptions and that absence; no source says in so many words that the public cannot attend. (immaculateconceptioncenter.org is a different retreat house, in Putnam, Connecticut.)

GCatholic 17009 puts St. Basil (Syro-Malankara, mission 1998, parish 2012) at the same address; it belongs to its eparchy and is handled in the eastern-catholic batch, not here.

## What would change this

A published regular Mass open to the public at the ICC (a Mass finder place, a diocesan page or a Tablet notice with weekly times) would make the Main Chapel a church row; it would then need a bulletin or go to excluded as no-bulletin.
