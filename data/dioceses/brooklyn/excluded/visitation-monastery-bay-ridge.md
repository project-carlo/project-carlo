## Why it is excluded

The Sisters of the Visitation announced in February 2024 that they would close their Bay Ridge monastery and Visitation Academy (The Tablet, 8 February 2024: "The closure of both the academy and the monastery was also confirmed in a letter ... sent to parents on Feb. 5"). The Tablet of 10 December 2024 reports a Mass at the monastery on 8 December 2024 at the last Christmas Nativity lighting. On 17 August 2026 The Tablet reported the property at 8902 Ridge Blvd, including the monastery chapel, "under contract to be sold" with the Attorney General's approval of 10 August 2026. The monastery's website now says only "To contact the Visitation Sisters, visit the website of the Rockville, VA Visitation Monastery."

No source states the date of the last Mass in the chapel, so `last_mass` is left empty; the latest Mass found is the one of 8 December 2024. Before its closure the chapel was a cloistered monastery chapel, and no source read here gave it a public Mass schedule. No parish bulletin of the batch (Our Lady of Angels, St. Andrew, St. Anselm, St. Patrick, 8 November 2026 issues) mentions it.

## What would change this

A buyer or a religious community reopening the chapel with public Mass; the sale's completion would settle it the other way. Recheck The Tablet for the sale's outcome.
