## Why it is excluded

Closed. A fire broke out during Easter Mass on 31 March 2024 and its Sunday Mass was "relocated to All Saints for now" (The Tablet, 8 April 2024). Bishop Brennan's decree of 27 May 2025 (diocesan decrees page, scanned PDF read from a render on 2026-11-07) relegates "The Roman Catholic Church of Our Lady of the Rosary located at 225 Seigel Street" to profane use, as the cost of restoring it after the fire was prohibitive, and says it "will not be restored and used for divine worship." The parish's 25 October 2026 bulletin lists Mass only at All Saints, 115 Throop Ave.

`last_mass` is 2024-03-31, the interrupted Easter Mass; no later Mass there was found. GCatholic 16653 and the Mass finder (place 794) still list it.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a new decree; the relegation is final unless revoked.
