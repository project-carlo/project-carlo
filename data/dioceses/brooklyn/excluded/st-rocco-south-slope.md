## Why it is excluded

The merged parish of St. John the Evangelist - St. Rocco holds no Mass here. Its Mass Times page (read 2026-11-06) says "All Masses take place at 250 21st Street, between 5th & 6th Avenues.", and its 1 November 2026 bulletin lists one Mass schedule with only the 21st Street address. The parish history dates the merger to 30 June 2011. GCatholic 16773 still lists the church at 216 27th Street with no status. No source I read gives the last Mass at St. Rocco or says what became of the building, so `last_mass` is empty; that the building no longer holds Mass rests on the parish's statement that all Masses are at 21st Street.

## What would change this

A bulletin or parish page listing a Mass at 216 27th Street (for example a feast-day Mass for St. Rocco in August). The parish's history page or a Tablet article dating the last Mass would fill `last_mass`.
