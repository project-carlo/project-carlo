## Why it is excluded

Closed. Bishop DiMarzio's decree of 2 May 2017 (diocesan decrees page, read 2026-11-06) relegated "the Church of Saint Rosalia, located at 6301 14th Avenue", in the territory of the Parish of the Basilica of Regina Pacis, "TO THE PROFANE BUT NOT SORDID USE", effective thirty days after publication, after which it "will no longer be used for divine worship". The decree gives no day for the last Mass; by its terms worship ended about June 2017, so `last_mass` is 2017. The parish's 8 November 2026 bulletin lists Masses only at the Basilica of Regina Pacis and its lower-level St. Joseph Chapel, and does not mention St. Rosalia. The priests' directory still uses the parish's older name "St. Rosalia - Regina Pacis", which is not evidence of Mass there. No roster in this batch lists the building.

## What would change this

A current Regina Pacis bulletin or diocesan source listing Mass at 6301 14th Avenue. The address is the decree's only; the building's current use was not checked.
