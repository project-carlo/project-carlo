## Why it is excluded

It belongs to that eparchy, not to the Diocese of Brooklyn, so it is `other-jurisdiction` under the diocese's settled rule for Eastern Catholic churches GCatholic lists in Brooklyn and Queens. It uses the address of the Immaculate Conception Center (7200 Douglaston Parkway), which is recorded separately as `excluded/immaculate-conception-center-douglaston` (no-public-mass). The website GCatholic gives, stbasilqueens.org, was DEAD-DNS to `probe url` on 2026-11-06 (lead from an earlier job). Whether and when it holds services was not checked; that is for its eparchy's mapping.
