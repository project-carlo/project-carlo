## Why it is excluded

310 Prospect Park West, Brooklyn, is the Diocese of Brooklyn's office building, not a parish or a church. On the deanery directory (read 2026-11-12) it appears only in the page's "Contact Us" footer, "Diocese of Brooklyn 310 Prospect Park West Brooklyn, NY 11215 (718) 965-7300", followed by the Immaculate Conception Center (excluded separately as `immaculate-conception-center-douglaston`). The roster entry `deaneries:q10-diocese-of-brooklyn` is that footer read as the last item of Deanery Q10. The Mass finder (read 2026-11-05) has no place at this address. No public Mass here was looked for beyond the finder; none is advertised on the pages read.

## What would change this

A diocesan or Mass finder listing of a regular public Mass in a chapel at 310 Prospect Park West would make it a church record.
