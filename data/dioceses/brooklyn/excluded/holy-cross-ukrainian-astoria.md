## Why it is excluded

GCatholic 17136 (read 2026-11-12) lists this Ukrainian (Byzantine) church at 31-12 30th St., Long Island City, New York 11106 under "Jurisdiction: Ukrainian Diocese of Stamford", "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Brooklyn". It belongs to that eparchy, not to the Diocese of Brooklyn, so it is `other-jurisdiction` under the diocese's settled rule for Eastern Catholic churches GCatholic lists in Brooklyn and Queens. GCatholic adds "Served by: Basilian Order of Saint Josaphat". Its own site (https://www.holycrossukrainian.org/, read 2026-11-12, Wix) is titled "Holy Cross Ukrainian Catholic Church, Astoria New York" and lists Divine Liturgy times; its bulletin pages were not read, since the church is not mapped under Brooklyn. The Romanian St. Mary mission uses this building (`excluded/st-mary-romanian-astoria`). Whether and when it holds services was not checked; that is for its eparchy's mapping.

## What would change this

Nothing about the jurisdiction short of GCatholic or the eparchy moving it to the Roman diocese. If the pipeline later maps the Ukrainian Diocese of Stamford, this record is where to find the church.
