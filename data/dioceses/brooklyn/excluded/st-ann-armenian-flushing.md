## Why it is excluded

GCatholic 15214 (read 2026-11-12) lists this Armenian church at 142-30 58th Ave, Flushing, New York 11355 under "Jurisdiction: Armenian Diocese of Our Lady of Nareg in Glendale", "within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Brooklyn". It belongs to that eparchy, not to the Diocese of Brooklyn, so it is `other-jurisdiction` under the diocese's settled rule for Eastern Catholic churches GCatholic lists in Brooklyn and Queens. GCatholic says it is "Using the building: Church of St. Ann , Flushing", the Latin St. Ann (`churches/st-ann-flushing`, recorded at 58-02 146th Street, the corner of 58th Avenue; GCatholic 15214 gives 142-30 58th Ave). `parish` names Mary's Nativity - St. Ann only because the building is theirs; the Armenian community's liturgies are not part of that bulletin row. Whether and when it holds services was not checked; that is for its eparchy's mapping.

## What would change this

Nothing about the jurisdiction short of GCatholic or the eparchy moving it to the Roman diocese. If the pipeline later maps the Armenian Diocese of Our Lady of Nareg in Glendale, this record is where to find the church.
