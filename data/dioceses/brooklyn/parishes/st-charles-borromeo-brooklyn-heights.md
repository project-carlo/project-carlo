## Publisher

St. Charles Borromeo publishes its own weekly LPi bulletin, ParishesOnline org `st-charles-borromeo-church-11201` (container 04/0287). The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06) has on page 1 "St. Charles Borromeo", "A Roman Catholic community in the heart of Brooklyn Heights", 31 Sidney Place, and on page 2 its weekly schedule, Mass intentions and "Very Rev. Joseph R. Gibino, Pastor".

## Worship sites

One church, 31 Sidney Place. Page 2 of the 8 November issue gives Sunday 9 AM and 7 PM Mass and weekday Mass Monday and Thursday at 12:10 PM (Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday are a Liturgy of the Word with Communion). The directory, deanery list, GCatholic 16696 and Mass finder 1190 list only this church.

Fr. Gibino is also Administrator (Rector) of the Cathedral Basilica of St. James. The two parishes do not share a bulletin: the cathedral posts its own issue on its Tilma site, whose 8 November masthead names the cathedral alone with its own Mass times and a letter "From the Rector at Saint James Cathedral Basilica"; St. Charles's 8 November issue does not mention the cathedral. Recorded as a `separate` review with `cathedral-basilica-of-st-james-brooklyn`.
