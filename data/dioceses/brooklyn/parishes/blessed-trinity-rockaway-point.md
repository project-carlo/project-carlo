## Worship sites

Three churches. The 1 November Mass intentions list Masses at St. Thomas (More) daily and on the weekend, at St. Genevieve (Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:15 am; Saturday 7 November 10:00 am VFW Veterans Mass) and at St. Edmund (Sunday 10:30 am), and the mission statement names "a trinity of churches: Saint Edmund, Saint Genevieve and Saint Thomas More". The homepage's Mass Times (read 2026-11-06) give the same schedule. So St. Edmund and St. Genevieve, which GCatholic lists as churches of this parish, are not summer-only chapels: both have Sunday Mass in November 2026. The site's History page lists St. Genevieve (founded 1924) and St. Edmund (founded 1937) as former missions of St. Francis de Sales, and its pastor lists for them as separate parishes end "Closed"; that refers to their parishes, merged into St. Thomas More-St. Edmund (1957) and now Blessed Trinity, not the buildings.

The diocese directory and deanery list (Q10) list Blessed Trinity once at 204-25 Rockaway Point Blvd; the Mass finder lists one place (1113) there.
