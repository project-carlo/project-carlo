## Publisher

St. Agatha's bulletin is printed by LPi (ParishesOnline org `st-agatha-church-11220`, container 03/9993), weekly from 4 October 2026. The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06) has on page 1 "ST. AGATHA'S R.C. CHURCH", "IGLESIA CATÓLICA SAN AGATHA", 702 48th St., Brooklyn, NY 11220, and Pastor Very Rev. Fr. Fulgencio Gutierrez.

Fr. Gutierrez is also pastor of St. Michael, Sunset Park. The two parishes share clergy and print each other's Mass intentions (page 2), and joint notices (the 26 November Thanksgiving Mass at each church, page 7; St. Michael's 4 October issue announces that "The parishes of St. Michael and St. Agatha will be CLOSED on Tuesday, October 12"). But each masthead names only its own church and prints only its own Mass schedule, in separate issues: St. Agatha's here, St. Michael's on eCatholic (row `st-michael-sunset-park`). Recorded as a `separate` review.
