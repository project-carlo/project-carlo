## Publisher

All Saints-Our Lady of the Rosary of Pompeii publishes its own weekly bulletin, a self-made 12-page PDF (no LPi, John Patrick or Church Bulletin Inc. footer) posted on its Tilma site. The 25 October 2026 issue (read 2026-11-07) has the masthead "Parish of All Saints– Pompeii", 115 Throop Ave, Brooklyn 11206, Fr. Vincenzo Cardilicchia, Pastor, and "All Saints-Our Lady of the Rosary of Pompeii" at the foot of page 1.

## Worship sites

One: All Saints Church, 115 Throop Ave. The page 1 schedule lists Saturday 8:00 pm (Neocatechumenal Way), Sunday 9:00 am English and 11:30 am Spanish, and weekday Masses "In the chapel" or "IN THE CHURCH" (a chapel of the same building, as no other address is given). The site's Mass Times page gives the same Sunday and weekday times without naming a church.

Our Lady of the Rosary of Pompeii (225 Siegel St.; GCatholic 16653) merged with All Saints in June 2019, was damaged by fire during Easter Mass on 31 March 2024 (The Tablet, 8 April 2024), and was relegated to profane use by the bishop's decree of 27 May 2025 ("will not be restored and used for divine worship"); see its `excluded/` record. The Mass finder (place 794) still lists Pompeii Masses; under the settled rule it is not evidence of a schedule.
