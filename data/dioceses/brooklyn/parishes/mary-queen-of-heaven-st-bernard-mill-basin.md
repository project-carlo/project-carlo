## Publisher

Mary Queen of Heaven-St. Bernard parish publishes one weekly LPi bulletin (ParishesOnline org `mary-queen-of-heaven-church-11234`, container 03/4054). The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06, 12 pages) has the header "MQH-SBC PARISH" on every page, one clergy list under Rev. John J. Maduri, Pastor, and one weekend Mass schedule giving each Mass's location at Mary Queen of Heaven or St. Bernard of Clairvaux. The St. Bernard site's copy of the same issue carries the masthead "Mary Queen of Heaven-St. Bernard Parish".

## Worship sites

Two churches, both in the page 1 schedule: Mary Queen of Heaven, 1395 East 56th Street (`churches/mary-queen-of-heaven-mill-basin`), and St. Bernard of Clairvaux, 2055 East 69th Street (`churches/st-bernard-of-clairvaux-bergen-beach`). The deanery list and the Mass finder still list Mary Queen of Heaven and St. Bernard separately and the diocese's directory lists the merged parish at St. Bernard's address; all are this one bulletin, so each roster entry is linked to its church. The schedule also names a monthly Spanish Mass "at St. Thomas Aquinas", a church of another parish, not of this row.
