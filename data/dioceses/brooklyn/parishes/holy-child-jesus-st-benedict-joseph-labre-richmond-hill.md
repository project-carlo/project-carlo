## Worship sites

Two, from the page 6 Mass schedule, which has a column for each church. Holy Child Jesus: Saturday 5:00 pm (Vigil) and 6:30 pm (Vigilia, Spanish), Sunday 8:30 am, 10:30 am (Spanish), 12:30 pm and 5:00 pm; weekday Masses at the Leonard Center, 8:30 am Monday-Friday and Wednesday 7:30 pm (Spanish). St. Benedict Joseph Labre: Sunday 10:00 am and 11:30am (Spanish), weekday 9:30 am Monday-Friday. The directory, the deanery list and Mass finder 820 list only the merged parish at 111-11 86th Avenue; GCatholic still lists both churches (16605 and 16685), and the bulletin confirms both hold Mass.

The Leonard Center (with a chapel, "la Capilla del Leonard Centro") is the parish building where weekday Masses are held. It is recorded as part of Holy Child Jesus, not as its own church; see *Not yet confirmed*.
