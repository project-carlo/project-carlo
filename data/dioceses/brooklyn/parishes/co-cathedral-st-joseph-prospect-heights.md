## Publisher

The parish of the Co-Cathedral of St. Joseph - St. Teresa of Avila publishes one weekly bulletin, a self-made 2-page PDF (no LPi, John Patrick or Church Bulletin Inc. footer) posted on its Tilma site. The 1 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-07) is headed "THE CO-CATHEDRAL OF SAINT JOSEPH", gives the parish office at 563 Sterling Place, Brooklyn 11238, and the rector, The Very Rev. Patrick J. Keating.

## Worship sites

Two, both in that issue's page 1 schedule and in the site's Mass Times page:

- the Co-Cathedral of St. Joseph, 856 Pacific Street (Sunday 9:00 AM Creole, 11:00 AM English, 1:30 PM Spanish, 6:00 PM English; weekdays 8:00, 9:00, 10:00 AM);
- St. Teresa of Avila, 777 Classon Ave (Sunday 9:30 AM English, 11:00 AM on the first and second Sundays; Monday 8:00 AM).

Page 1's text layer prints the headings out of order. I matched each schedule to its church from page 2: the intentions mark the St. Teresa Masses "(St. Teresa)", and the attendance table lists 9:00, 11:00, 1:30 and 6:00 under ST. JOSEPH and 9:30 AM under ST. TERESA. The Mass Times page gives the same split. One collection and one set of intentions cover both churches, so this is one row.

The Mass finder's place 137 lists a different schedule (Saturday 7:30 pm Spanish, Sunday 7:00 am Spanish and others). Under the settled rule it is not evidence for Masses, and I followed the parish's own bulletin. Mass finder place 1165 ('St. Teresa') lists 777 Classon Avenue together with St. Teresa, Woodside, and is pinned at Woodside. Its times are not this parish's. See the St. Teresa of Avila church record.
