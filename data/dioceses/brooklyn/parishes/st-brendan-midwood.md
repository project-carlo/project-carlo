## Publisher

St. Brendan publishes its own weekly bulletin, typeset by the parish (ads sold through The Church Bulletin Inc., no LPi or John Patrick footer) and posted as Google Drive files on the parish's Google Site. I read the 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06, Drive id 1nSiKF0P-xtdSkIWczg78KeYjYHor0XFZ): page 1 masthead "THE ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH OF SAINT BRENDAN, Est. 1907", "Sunday, November 8, 2026", rectory 1525 East 12th Street, Brooklyn 11230, Rev. Lukasz P. Trocha, Parish Administrator.

Rev. Trocha is also Pastor of St. Edmund (st-edmund-homecrest), and St. Brendan's site links St. Edmund's. The two still publish separate bulletins: St. Edmund's 8 November issue is a John Patrick Publishing bulletin (#433) with its own masthead and schedule (Sunday 8:30 and 11:30 a.m.), while St. Brendan's has Saturday 5 and 7 p.m. and Sunday 10 a.m. and its own intentions.
