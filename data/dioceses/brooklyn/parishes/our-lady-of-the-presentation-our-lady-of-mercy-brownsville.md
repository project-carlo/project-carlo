## Publisher

The merged parish of Our Lady of the Presentation - Our Lady of Mercy publishes one short weekly bulletin, self-made (no LPi, John Patrick or Church Bulletin Inc. marks in the text layer), posted as PDFs on its Tilma site. The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-07, 2 pages, file uploaded 2026-11-05) has no name masthead: page 1 is parish notices and sponsors, page 2 is headed "THIRTY SECOND SUNDAY OF ORDINARY TIME ... NOVEMBER 8, 2026" and lists the Mass intentions of both churches, with the parish email o.l.presentationmercy@gmail.com. The issue is identified by its hosting site and by intentions that match the site's Mass Times page.

## Worship sites

Two, both in the current issue and on the site's Mass Times page (read 2026-11-07):

- Our Lady of the Presentation, 1677 St. Marks Avenue: intentions at 9:00 AM and 12:00 PM on 8 November; site gives Sunday 9:00 a.m. English and Tuesday/Thursday weekday Mass.
- Our Lady of Mercy, 680 Mother Gaston Blvd. (the site calls it "Our Lady of Mercy Chapel"): intentions at 10:30 AM and 1:00 PM; site gives Sunday 10:30 a.m. Spanish and 1:00 p.m. English and Monday/Friday weekday Mass.

The batch note that the merged parish kept only Our Lady of Mercy's church is not borne out by the current issue: Presentation still has Sunday and weekday Mass. The directory and deanery list give one address (1677 St. Marks Avenue, the Presentation rectory); GCatholic lists both churches (16652, 16640); Mass finder 795 is pinned on Our Lady of Mercy.
