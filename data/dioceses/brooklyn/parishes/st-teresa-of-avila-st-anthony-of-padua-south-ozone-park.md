## Publisher

The parish of St. Teresa of Avila - St. Anthony of Padua publishes one weekly bulletin for both churches, printed by The Church Bulletin Inc. (West Babylon; ad-page footer "#190"). The current issue, dated November 1, 2026 (file `2026-11-03-Bulletin-1.pdf`, read 2026-11-06), is scanned images; its page 1 masthead reads "The Parish of St. Teresa of Avila - St. Anthony of Padua", Rectory 109-26 130th Street, with Very Rev. Thomas W. Ahern as pastor. The 25 October 2026 issue in the parish's Drive folder is a text PDF with the same masthead and schedule.

Fr. Ahern is also pastor of Our Lady of Perpetual Help (South Ozone Park), so the two could share a bulletin. They don't: this bulletin's masthead and Mass schedule name only St. Anthony and St. Teresa. OLPH appears only in an ad for a seminar at its church. OLPH has its own bulletin; see its row and the `publication_reviews` there.

## Worship sites

Two, from the masthead's "Worship Sites" and the Mass schedule (1 November 2026): St. Anthony Church, 127-17 135th Avenue (Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 12:30 pm Spanish, Thursday and Friday 8:30 am), and St. Teresa Church, 109-26 130th Street (Sunday 9:00 am English and 10:30 am Spanish, Monday and Tuesday 8:30 am). There is no Mass on Wednesdays. The directory and deanery list give the parish once, at St. Teresa's address. GCatholic lists both churches (16785, 16679). Mass finder place 1166 is the parish, pinned at St. Teresa, and its page lists both churches.

The Holy Hours are in St. Joseph's Chapel in the convent (page 3). That is a devotional chapel with no Mass listed, so it is not a church row.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`, recipe `page-pdfs https://www.stasap.org/parish-news/bulletin/`. The WordPress bulletin page embeds a 3D FlipBook (post 1903) over the current issue's upload, `/wp-content/uploads/<yyyy>/<mm>/<date>-Bulletin*.pdf`, and `fetch:check` resolved it to the 1 November issue on 2026-11-06. The same page links a Google Drive folder that holds every issue since November 2020. That is a second copy, not used here, because a Drive folder page serves only its first 50 files in name order, which would be the 2020-2021 issues. There is no publication id: The Church Bulletin Inc. has no online container. Current issues are image-only, so the pipeline needs OCR to read Mass times; older issues such as 25 October had text.
