## Publisher

St. Josaphat, Bayside is an oratory of the Institute of Christ the King Sovereign Priest and publishes its own weekly bulletin, self-produced (no LPi footer or container) and posted on its Tilma site. The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06) has on page 1 "INSTITUTE OF CHRIST THE KING SOVEREIGN PRIEST", "Saint Josaphat Oratory", Rev. Canon Andreas Hellmann, Rector, 34-32 210th Street, Bayside, "November 8, 2026 — 24th Sunday after Pentecost".

## Worship sites

One church. Page 1's Regular Mass Times (Sunday 9:30 am; Monday-Saturday 7:00 am, Thursday and First Friday 7:00 pm) and page 2's calendar of the week's Masses name no other building. The diocese's directory and deanery list (both as St. Josaphat's Roman Catholic Church), GCatholic 16727 and Mass finder 822 list only this church. The diocese lists it among its parishes; it is mapped under Brooklyn as an oratory the diocese entrusts to the Institute, not as another jurisdiction.

## Website and bulletin provider

`other`, with a `page-pdfs` recipe on the Tilma /bulletins page of stjosaphat-queens.org (the directory's link). The site is a Tilma site on its own domain serving PDFs from its own /system/resources/ path; recent files are named `Bulletin YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`. The bulletin also names the Institute's page https://institute-christ-king.org/queens-home. No publication id: the bulletin is not on ParishesOnline.
