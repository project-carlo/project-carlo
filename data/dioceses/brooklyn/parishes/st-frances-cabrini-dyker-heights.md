## Publisher

St. Frances Cabrini publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by The Church Bulletin Inc. (West Babylon; ad-page footer "#167") and posted as PDFs on the parish's Tilma site. The 1 November 2026 issue (FrCabBklyn11-1.pdf, read 2026-11-06, 6 pages) has on page 1 the masthead "Saint Frances Cabrini Roman Catholic Church, 1562 86th St. Brooklyn, NY 11228 (Corner of 86th St and Bay 11th St)", with Rev. Jeremy Canna (Pastor). Page 1's text layer is in a shifted font (greps miss it); it was read from a render.

## Worship sites

One. The page 1 Mass list gives Saturday Vigil 4 p.m., Sunday 9:30 a.m. (English and Italian) and 12:30 p.m. (Spanish), Monday-Friday 9 a.m., with no other church or chapel; page 2's intentions agree. The directory, the deanery list, GCatholic 16706 and Mass finder 902 all give the one church at 1562 86th Street.

**Shared pastor, separate bulletins.** Rev. Jeremy Canna is pastor of both St. Frances Cabrini and St. Bernadette (Dyker Heights). Their current bulletins are separate: Cabrini's (Church Bulletin Inc. #167, Tilma) names Cabrini alone with its own Masses, intentions and collections; St. Bernadette's (LPi 03/4063, 8 November 2026) names the Shrine Church of Saint Bernadette alone with its own. Page 2 of the Cabrini 1 November issue does carry a stray box "Saturday Vigil Mass at 5:30p.m. / Sunday: 8:30a.m. and 11:00a.m. / Monday through Saturday: 8a.m." that matches St. Bernadette's schedule, not Cabrini's; it names no church and is outweighed by the masthead and intentions, so it is read (inference) as layout text shared by the two offices. Guardian Angel's bulletin pairing "St. Finbarr and St. Frances Cabrini" is not a shared bulletin either: St. Finbar's 1 November 2026 bulletin names St. Finbar alone, with a different pastor (Rev. Pedro Angucho) and printer number (#117). Reviews are in `publication_reviews`.
