## Publisher

St. Jude Shrine, Canarsie, publishes its own weekly LPi bulletin (ParishesOnline org `shrine-church-of-st-jude`, container 03/0439; page 4 footer "Shrine Church of St. Jude | 03-0439"). The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06, 4 pages) has the masthead "ST. JUDE SHRINE R.C. CHURCH 1677 Canarsie Road, Brooklyn New York 11236" on page 1, with Pastor Rev. Ikenna Okagbue.

It is one of the three "Canarsie Cluster" parishes whose weekend Masses Our Lady of Miracles prints in a page 1 box; this bulletin names neither Our Lady of Miracles nor Holy Family (grepped 2026-11-06), so those are separate rows.

## Worship sites

One: the shrine church at 1677 Canarsie Road. The page 1 schedule lists Saturday 5:30 pm, Sunday 8:30 am, 10:30 am and 12:30 pm, Monday-Friday 8:10 am, Holy Days 8:30 am, and no other church or chapel (page 2 mentions an "Adoration Chapel", a room for adoration, not a separate site). The directory, the deanery list, GCatholic 16732 (Shrine) and Mass finder 1148 all give this one church.
