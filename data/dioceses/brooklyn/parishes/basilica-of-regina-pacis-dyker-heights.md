## Publisher

The Basilica of Regina Pacis publishes a weekly LPi bulletin (ParishesOnline org `the-basilica-of-regina-pacis`, container 03/1436). The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06, 8 pages) has the masthead "Basilica of Regina Pacis", 1230 65th Street, Brooklyn NY 11219, on page 1, with Rev. Sebastian Tarcisio Andro, Pastor, and the footer "The Basilica of Regina Pacis | 03-1436" on page 8. The priests' directory calls the parish St. Rosalia - Regina Pacis (Fr. Andro, Pastor, "St. Rosalia - Regina Pacis, Dyker Heights"); the bulletin and the diocese's parish directory call it The Basilica of Regina Pacis.

## Worship sites

One building. The page 1 schedule gives the Basilica Saturday 5:00pm, Sunday 9:00am (Spanish), 10:30am, 12:00pm (English/Italian) and 1:30pm (Chinese), and the St. Joseph Chapel Monday-Saturday 8:30am; page 7 places the "St. Joseph Chapel & Columbarium" in the "Lower Level of the Basilica", so it is part of the basilica's church record.

The former second church, St. Rosalia (6301 14th Avenue), was relegated to profane use by Bishop DiMarzio's decree of 2 May 2017, and the 8 November 2026 bulletin does not mention it (grep "Rosalia": none); see `excluded/st-rosalia-bensonhurst`.
