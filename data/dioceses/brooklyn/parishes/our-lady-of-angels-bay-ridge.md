## Publisher

Our Lady of Angels publishes a weekly LPi bulletin (ParishesOnline org `our-lady-of-angels-church-11209`, container 03/4061). The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06, 8 pages) has on page 1 the masthead "In partnership with Our Lady of Angels Church / Saint Anselm Church", both rectories' contacts, and OLA's own schedule: Saturday 8:30am, Vigil 5:00pm; Sunday 9:00am, 10:30am (Spanish), 12:00pm; Monday-Friday 6:45am & 11:45am. Page 2 names Rev. Kevin P. Abels, Pastor; page 8 has the footer "Our Lady of Angels Church | 03-4061".

Fr. Abels is pastor of Our Lady of Angels, St. Andrew the Apostle and St. Anselm (priests' directory, 2026-11-05), but each of the three publishes its own LPi bulletin with its own masthead, schedule and intentions: St. Anselm 03/4053 (`st-anselm-bay-ridge`), St. Andrew 03/0335 (`st-andrew-the-apostle-bay-ridge`). The OLA issue only mentions St. Anselm for joint events ("Partnership Events", e.g. an All Souls Mass on 2 November at St. Anselm), and St. Andrew's bulletin mentions a Tuesday 7pm Spanish Mass "at OLA". The reviews in `publication_reviews` record this.

## Worship sites

One church, Our Lady of Angels, 7320 4th Avenue. The Adoration Chapel and the Msgr. Cavanagh Room named in the bulletin are in the parish complex; no other building has Mass in its schedule.
