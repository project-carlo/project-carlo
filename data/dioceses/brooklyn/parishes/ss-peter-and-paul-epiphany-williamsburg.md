## Publisher

Ss. Peter and Paul - Epiphany (Williamsburg; merged with Epiphany in 2007) publishes its own weekly bulletin as a PDF on its Tilma site, https://peterandpaul-brooklyn.org/bulletins, newest first. The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-07, uploaded 4 November per its resource token) has the masthead "SAINTS PETER AND PAUL - EPIPHANY ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH, 71 South 3rd Street Brooklyn, New York 11249" (page 1, an image with a text layer). No LPi, John Patrick or Church Bulletin Inc. footer: self-made.

The issues carry no Mass schedule: page 2 gives adoration, confessions ("After 9:30am Mass", "After 6:00pm Mass"), baptisms and weddings. The Mass times are on the site's /mass-times page (below).

## Worship sites

One church: Ss. Peter & Paul - Epiphany Parish Church, 288 Berry Street (the renovated McCaddin Hall at Berry and South 3rd Streets, dedicated as the parish church on 29 June 2021 per The Tablet, 30 June 2021). The site's Contact page (2026-11-07) names it as the parish church and 71 South 3rd Street as the "Rectory Office"; the /mass-times page gives one schedule with no other building: weekdays (Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat) 9:30am Spanish, Thursday 6:00pm Spanish, Tuesday and Friday 6:00pm English, Saturday 6:00pm Spanish, Sunday 9:00am English, 10:30am and 12:00pm Spanish. The Mass finder (place 760) and Discover Mass also list only 288 Berry St.

Ruled out or not settled:

- 71 South 3rd Street (directory, deanery list, GCatholic 16666 "Sts. Peter and Paul") is the rectory office, not a separate church; GCatholic 16666's pin is about 40 m from 288 Berry St, so it is linked to the church at 288 Berry St.
- The provisional church on South Second Street (1963) had its last Mass on Divine Mercy Sunday, 3 April 2016, and was reduced to profane use and leased (The Tablet, 6 April 2016). No roster lists it, so it has no record.
- Epiphany, 96 South 9th Street (GCatholic 16672 only): The Tablet (10 February 2016) called the merged parish "one parish with two worship sites", but no current parish source names Epiphany as a Mass site and none says it closed. It is an `open/` record (`epiphany-williamsburg`) until the parish settles it.
