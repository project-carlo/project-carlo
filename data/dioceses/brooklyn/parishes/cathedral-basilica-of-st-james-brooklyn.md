## Publisher

The cathedral publishes its own weekly bulletin, "The Saint James Experience", posted on its Tilma site. The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06) has on page 1 "The Cathedral Basilica of Saint James", its Mass times and "250 Cathedral Place, Brooklyn, NY 11201", and on page 2 a letter from Fr. Joseph R. Gibino, "From the Rector at Saint James Cathedral Basilica".

## Worship sites

One church, the cathedral at 250 Cathedral Place. Page 1 of the 8 November issue gives Sunday 11:00 AM and Monday-Friday 12:00 Noon ("Saturday : Closed"). The directory, deanery list and Mass finder 648 list only this church; GCatholic has no entry for it in this batch's roster.

Fr. Gibino is also pastor of St. Charles Borromeo, which publishes its own LPi bulletin (row `st-charles-borromeo-brooklyn-heights`); its 8 November masthead names St. Charles alone, with its own Mass times, and the cathedral's issue names St. Charles only as the venue of a 12 December prayer vigil (page 5). Recorded as a `separate` review.
