## Publisher

St. John the Baptist parish (which absorbed Our Lady of Good Counsel) publishes one weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing and listed at church-bulletin.org/4050. The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-07, 4 pages) has the masthead "Saint John The Baptist / Our Lady of Good Counsel / Roman Catholic Church", "SJB - 333 Hart St. Brooklyn, N.Y. 11206 and OLGC- 915 Putnam Ave. Brooklyn, N.Y. 11221", Rev. Juan Luxama (Administrator).

## Worship sites

Two, both in the page 1 Mass schedule: St. John the Baptist, 333 Hart St. (Sunday 10:00 A.M. Spanish, 12:00 P.M. English) and Our Lady of Good Counsel, 915 Putnam Ave. (Sunday 8:30 A.M. and Thursday 7:00 P.M., bilingual; the Saturday 5:00 PM Mass there has been dropped). The directory and deanery list give the parish at 333 Hart Street; GCatholic lists only St. John the Baptist (16724, at the 75 Lewis Ave. parish center); Mass finder 1145 lists both churches and pins Good Counsel.
