## Publisher

Holy Spirit, Borough Park publishes its own weekly bulletin, laid out by the parish (no LPi, John Patrick or Church Bulletin Inc. footer) and posted as PDFs on its WordPress site. The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06, 8 pages) has the masthead "Holy Spirit Roman Catholic Church, 1712 45th ST. Brooklyn, N.Y. 11204" on page 1, with Rev. Fr. Rony Mendes, Administrator, and Rev. Fr. Joseph Bassey, "Parochial Vicar from St. Rose of Lima Church".

## Worship sites

One. The page 1 schedule lists Monday-Saturday 8:30 A.M. "In the Chapel", Saturday 7:00 P.M. (Spanish), Sunday 9:00 A.M. (Bilingual), 10:30 AM (Korean) and 12:00 PM (Spanish). "The Chapel" is read as a chapel of this church at the same address (Friday Exposition runs "until the Celebration of the morning mass at 8:30AM in the Chapel"); no separate address is given, and no roster lists a second place. The directory, the deanery list, GCatholic 16613 and Mass finder 1119 all give the one church at 1712 45th Street.

The parochial vicar is shared with St. Rose of Lima (`st-rose-of-lima-kensington`), but the two parishes publish separate bulletins: this one's masthead names Holy Spirit alone; see `publication_reviews` on that row.
