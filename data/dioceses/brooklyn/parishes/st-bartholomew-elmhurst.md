Two, both in the November 8 schedule. Weekday and weekend Masses are listed for the "CHURCH - WHITNEY AV" (Mon-Fri 11:15 AM English, 6:30 PM Spanish; Saturday 9 AM, 5 PM, 6:30 PM; Sunday 8 AM, 10 AM, 11:30 AM, 1 PM, 5 PM, 6:30 PM) and for the "CHAPEL - ITHACA ST" (Sunday 8:30 AM English, 10 AM Spanish, 11:30 AM English, 1:30 PM Chinese; Tuesday 6:30 PM Chinese).
