## Publisher

St. Anselm publishes a weekly LPi bulletin (ParishesOnline org `st-anselm-church-11209`, container 03/4053). The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06, 8 pages) has on page 1 the masthead "Saint Anselm Church / In partnership with / Our Lady of Angels Church", St. Anselm's own Mass schedule, both rectories' contacts and Rev. Kevin P. Abels, Pastor; the intentions are for St. Anselm's Masses; the footer on pages 7-8 is "St. Anselm Church | 03-4053".

Fr. Abels also pastors Our Lady of Angels and St. Andrew the Apostle, but each publishes its own LPi bulletin: Our Lady of Angels 03/4061 (`our-lady-of-angels-bay-ridge`), with its own schedule and intentions, and St. Andrew 03/0335 (`st-andrew-the-apostle-bay-ridge`). See `publication_reviews`.
