## Publisher

Holy Family publishes its own weekly LPi bulletin on ParishesOnline (org `holy-family-church-11366`, container 04/0458). The 1 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-05) has on page 1 "Holy Family Roman Catholic Church", "Fresh Meadows, New York", "175-20 74th Avenue, Fresh Meadows, NY 11366", Rev. Sean M. Suckiel, Pastor. The parish's own Tilma site posts the same file at holyfamilyfm.org/bulletins.

## Worship sites

One church. Page 1 gives Vigil 5:00pm, Sunday 7:30am, 10:30am, 12:00pm and 5:00pm, Monday-Saturday 8:30am; the page 2 intentions are at those times. The diocese directory, deanery list, GCatholic 16610 and Mass finder 143 list only this church.

Fr. Suckiel is also pastor of American Martyrs, Bayside, and page 4 prints "Our New Combined Mass Schedule" for both churches. That is a shared schedule, not a shared bulletin: American Martyrs publishes its own eCatholic issue the same week with its own masthead and collection (row `american-martyrs-bayside`), recorded as a `separate` review.
