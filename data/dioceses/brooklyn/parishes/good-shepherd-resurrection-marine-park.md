## Publisher

Good Shepherd-Resurrection parish publishes one weekly LPi bulletin (ParishesOnline org `good-shepherd-church-11229`, container 03/4056). The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06) has the masthead "GOOD SHEPHERD-RESURRECTION CHURCH, BROOKLYN, NEW YORK" on page 1 with separate Mass schedules for Good Shepherd and Resurrection.
