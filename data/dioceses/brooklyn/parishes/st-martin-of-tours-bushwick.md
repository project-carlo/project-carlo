## Publisher

St. Martin of Tours publishes its own weekly bulletin, a self-made 3-page PDF (no LPi, John Patrick or Church Bulletin Inc. footer) posted on its Tilma site. The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-07) has the masthead "SAINT MARTIN OF TOURS PARISH - R.C.C.", rectory 1288 Hancock Street, Brooklyn 11221, Rev. Juan D. Ruiz, Pastor.

## Worship sites

One: St. Martin of Tours Church, 1288 Hancock Street. The page 1 schedule lists weekday, Saturday and Sunday Masses without naming any other place; confessions are "San Martin de Tours: Sábado 5:00". The site's Mass Times page agrees.

Our Lady of Lourdes (Bushwick, 1872) merged into this parish in 2007 (diocesan archives). Its church building at 89 Furman Avenue is in OpenStreetMap, and the Mass finder (place 579) lists it as "Our Lady Of Lourdes Chapel" with a Sunday 11:00am "OLOL Chapel - Bilingual" Mass, but the Mass finder is not schedule evidence here, and none of the bulletins read (31 May, 13 September, 8 November 2026) nor the Mass Times page mention it. Neither closure nor Mass is shown, so it has an `open/` record (our-lady-of-lourdes-chapel-bushwick). GCatholic 16742, "Chapel of Our Lady of Lourdes, St. Martin of Tours Parish", gives 1288 Hancock St., a Plus Code on the St. Martin of Tours church, its phone and its founding year (1906): it describes this church, and is linked to the St. Martin of Tours church record.
