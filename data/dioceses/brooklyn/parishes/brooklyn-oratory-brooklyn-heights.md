## Publisher

The Brooklyn Oratory (the Congregation of the Oratory serving Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary and St. Boniface) publishes one weekly bulletin for both parishes as a Constant Contact email newsletter, "The Oratory Connection: The Newsletter for the Brooklyn Oratory Parishes". The 5 November 2026 issue (Thirty-Second Sunday in Ordinary Time, read 2026-11-06 at myemail.constantcontact.com, linked from the homepage's "Bulletins & News" card via conta.cc/47Ys2o8) carries both churches' news, a "Weekly Mass Intention Schedule" with an ASSUMPTION list and a ST. BONIFACE list, and a footer naming "Church of the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary, 55 Cranberry St." and "Church of St. Boniface, 190 Duffield St.", with "THE BROOKLYN ORATORY PARISHES OFFICE | 64 MIDDAGH STREET" and 718-875-2096.

That phone is the shared office's, which is why the directory gives it to Assumption and the deanery list to St. Boniface; the directory links both parishes to oratorychurch.org.

## Worship sites

Two churches, both in the newsletter's intention schedule and on the site's /mass-times page (read 2026-11-06):

- Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary, 55 Cranberry St.: Saturday 5:00pm Vigil, Sunday 9:30am, Monday-Friday 8:00am, first Saturday 9:00am.
- St. Boniface, 190 Duffield St.: Sunday 11:15am and 6:00pm, Monday-Friday 12:10pm.

These are two canonical parishes with one bulletin, so one row (the model's rule; the playbook's merger rule asks for one row only when the current bulletin covers both, which it does). The directory, deanery list, GCatholic and Mass finder list no other church for them.
