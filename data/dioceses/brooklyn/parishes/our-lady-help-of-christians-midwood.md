## Publisher

Our Lady Help of Christians, Midwood publishes its own weekly bulletin, a self-made 4-page PDF (no printer footer, no LPi or Church Bulletin Inc. marks) posted on its Tilma site. The 1 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06) has the masthead "Our Lady Help of Christians Roman Catholic Church, The Heart & Soul of Midwood, Brooklyn, 1315 East 28th Street, Brooklyn, NY 11210" on page 1; page 2 names Very Rev. Dwayne D. Davis, V.F., Administrator.

Fr. Davis is also Pastor of St. Thomas Aquinas, Flatlands, and the two parishes share parochial vicars and staff, but each prints its own bulletin: this one's schedule and intentions are OLHC's alone and it does not mention St. Thomas, while St. Thomas's 8 November 2026 issue is a Church Bulletin Inc. (#243) bulletin with its own schedule. See `publication_reviews`.

## Worship sites

One. The page 2 Mass schedule lists Monday-Friday 8:30 am, Saturday Vigil Mass 4:00 pm, Sunday 10:00 am, Holy Days 8:30 am and a Filipino Ministry Mass on the first Sunday at 2:00 pm, all at the one church; no other church or chapel is named. The directory, the deanery list, GCatholic 16626 and Mass finder 927 all give the one church at 1315 East 28th Street.
