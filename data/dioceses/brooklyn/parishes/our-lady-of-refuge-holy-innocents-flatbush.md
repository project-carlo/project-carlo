## Publisher

Our Lady of Refuge – Holy Innocents publishes one weekly bulletin for both churches, printed by John Patrick Publishing (job 404; 1 November 2026 page 8 footer "404 Our Lady of Refuge-Holy Innocents, Brooklyn, NY"). The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06 from church-bulletin.org) has the page 1 masthead "Our Lady of Refuge – Holy Innocents R.C. Church" with both church addresses and Pastor Rev. Jean Yvon Pierre. The 15 February 2026 issue's Pastoral Message on the Merger says the diocese united the two parishes "by decree and effective January 1, 2026" under the name Our Lady of Refuge – Holy Innocents Parish, main office 279 East 17th Street.
