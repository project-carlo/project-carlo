## Publisher

Holy Family - St. Laurence, the merged parish of Holy Family (Canarsie) and St. Laurence (East New York), publishes one weekly bulletin for both churches, printed by The Church Bulletin Inc. (West Babylon; page 8 footer "137") and posted on the parish's Tilma site. The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06, 8 pages) has the masthead "HOLY FAMILY CHURCH ST. LAURENCE CHURCH" on page 1, with each church's address, phone and office hours and Administrator Rev. Saint-Charles Borno. The 1 November issue has the same masthead.

It is one of the three "Canarsie Cluster" parishes whose weekend Masses Our Lady of Miracles prints in a page 1 box. This bulletin does not mention Our Lady of Miracles or St. Jude (grepped 2026-11-06), so those are separate rows.

## Worship sites

Two, both in the page 1 Mass schedule:

- Holy Family Church, 9719 Flatlands Avenue: Monday-Saturday 9:00am; Saturday Vigil 5:00pm; Sunday 8:00am, 9:15am (French/Creole), 11:15am (Family Mass), 12:45pm.
- St. Laurence Church, "122-65 Flatlands Avenue" (12265): Monday-Wednesday and Friday 9:00am; Thursday 6:30pm (Spanish); Sunday 9:30am, 12:00pm (Spanish).

GCatholic still lists St. Laurence (16734) at 1020 Van Siclen Ave; the parish site calls that the "St. Laurence - Parish Office" and the church "St. Laurence Worship Site - Church, 122-65 Flatlands Ave." The two are one block (OSM buildings at both numbers sit side by side at Van Siclen and Flatlands Avenues), and GCatholic's pin is on the church. St. Laurence is open, not closed; see its church record.
