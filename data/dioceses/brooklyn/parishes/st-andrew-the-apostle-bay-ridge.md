## Publisher

St. Andrew the Apostle publishes a weekly LPi bulletin (ParishesOnline org `st-andrew-the-apostle-church-11220`, container 03/0335). The 8 November 2026 issue (read 2026-11-06, 6 pages) has on page 1 the masthead "Saint Andrew The Apostle Roman Catholic Church", Parish House 6713 Ridge Boulevard, and the parish's own schedule; page 2 names Rev. Kevin P. Abels, Pastor, and gives intentions for St. Andrew's Masses; page 6 has the footer "St. Andrew the Apostle Church | 03-0335".

Fr. Abels also pastors Our Lady of Angels and St. Anselm, which publish their own LPi bulletins (03/4061, `our-lady-of-angels-bay-ridge`; 03/4053, `st-anselm-bay-ridge`). St. Andrew's masthead names no other parish; see `publication_reviews`.

## Worship sites
