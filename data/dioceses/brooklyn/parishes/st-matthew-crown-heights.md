## Worship sites

Three churches, all with their own Mass schedule on page 1 of the 8 November 2026 issue:

- St. Matthew, 1123 Eastern Pkwy. (daily and Sunday Masses in English, Spanish and Kreyol): `churches/st-matthew-crown-heights`.
- St. Gregory the Great, 224 Brooklyn Ave. (Sunday 11:30 A.M., First Friday 10:00am): `churches/st-gregory-the-great-crown-heights`. GCatholic lists it as a church of its own (gcatholic:16718); the diocesan archives say it merged with St. Matthew in 2011, and the bulletin shows it still has Sunday Mass.

Page 3 carries separate Mass intentions for each of the three churches, so this one bulletin covers all three.
