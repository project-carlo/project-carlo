## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 Saint Anne's Catholic Community bulletin (03/0123, page 1, an image) lists Saturday 5:00 PM and Sunday 9:00 AM; page 2's priest and lector schedules cover both Masses for November 14-15 and 21-22.

## Name and address

All sources give 11 Baltic Road. The bulletin and GCatholic place it in Occum (a village of Norwich; GCatholic gives ZIP 06360); the diocese's list says "Occum, Norwich" with a mailing address at P.O. Box 256, Versailles 06383, which the locator map uses as the town ("Versailles"); the bulletin's mailing address is P.O. Box 208, Taftville. The bulletin's "Occum" and GCatholic's ZIP 06360 are recorded, since Versailles and Taftville are mailing addresses. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87H9HWWX+F8.
