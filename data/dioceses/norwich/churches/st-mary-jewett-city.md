## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 Holy Spirit Catholic Community bulletin (container 03/0945, page 2) lists Saturday 4:00pm, Sunday 8:00am and Tuesday 8:00am Masses at St. Mary, and a 4:30pm Mass of Remembrance there on November 22.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the diocese's List of Parishes, the locator map and GCatholic all give 34 North Main Street, Jewett City 06351. The church office is in the same building. The full name 'St. Mary, Our Lady of the Rosary' is GCatholic's and the locator map's, and the bulletin's page 1 uses it; the diocese's list says St. Mary. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87HCJ259+4P.
