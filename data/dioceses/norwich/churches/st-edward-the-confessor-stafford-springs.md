## Holds Mass

## Name and address

The sources give three streets. The bulletin, the diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) and the ParishesOnline org give 27 Church Street; GCatholic gives 55 High St.; the locator map gives 6 Benton Street. I followed the parish's and the diocese's 27 Church Street. It may be the office's address rather than the church's, but GCatholic's Plus Code (87H9XM3R+RQ, 41.95456, -72.30806) and the ParishesOnline pin for 27 Church Street (41.95548, -72.30832) are about 100 m apart, so they mark the same church block.
