## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 St. Lawrence bulletin (container 04/0920, page 1) has a Sunday Masses box (Vigil Saturday, Sunday two morning Masses) and a Daily Masses box (Monday no Mass, Tuesday evening, Wednesday to Saturday morning).
