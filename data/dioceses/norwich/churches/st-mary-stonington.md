## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 Holy Cross Community bulletin (container 04/0504, page 4) lists Saturday 5:30 pm and Sunday 8:00 am Masses at St. Mary, and 8:00 am Monday to Friday except Thursday. Page 3's intentions name St. Mary at the 8:00 am weekday Masses.

## Name and address

The sources disagree. The bulletin (page 4) and the diocese's Pawcatuck listing give 22 Broad Street, Stonington. The diocese's own St. Mary entry, GCatholic and the locator map give 95 Main St., 06378. In Stonington Borough, Broad Street runs off Main Street, so both probably describe the one corner building; 95 Main St. is recorded because three sources, including the diocese's entry for this church, give it. The Plus Code wasn't checked against OSM (Nominatim answered 429). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87HC83PV+QF.
