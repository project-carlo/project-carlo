## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 Holy Spirit Catholic Community bulletin (container 03/0945, page 2) lists Sunday 10:00 and Friday 8:00am Masses at St. Thomas.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the diocese's List of Parishes, the locator map and GCatholic all give 61 Preston City Rd., Voluntown 06384. The name follows the bulletin's masthead ('SS. Thomas and Anne') and the locator map. The diocese writes 'St. Thomas and St. Anne', and GCatholic gives the church's older title 'St. Thomas the Apostle' (the parish took St. Anne's name when St. Anne, Glasgo closed). The bulletin's schedule calls it 'St. Thomas'. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87HCH4FP+59.
