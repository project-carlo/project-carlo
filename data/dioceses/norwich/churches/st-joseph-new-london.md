## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 St. Brendan bulletin (container 03/1228) lists Saturday 4:00pm Vigil and Sunday 8:00am and 10:45am at St. Joseph (SJ), with weekday Masses, and page 2's week table has a St. Joseph column.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("149 Montaulk Avenue", a misspelling), the diocese's List of Parishes and the ParishesOnline org give 149 Montauk Ave.; the diocese gives 37 Squire St. as the office, which the locator map uses, and GCatholic gives 17 Squire St. Squire Street runs beside the church, so these are probably one block; 149 Montauk Ave. (official, two sources) is recorded. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H98VVV+PX (41.34431, -72.10506), within 30 m of the ParishesOnline org point for 149 Montauk Ave. (41.34433, -72.10538).
