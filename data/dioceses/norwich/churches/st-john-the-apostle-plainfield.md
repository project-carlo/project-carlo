## Holds Mass

The November 14 & 15, 2026 St. Andre Bessette bulletin (LPi 04/0778, page 1) lists Sunday 10:30 a.m., Wednesday 6:00 p.m. and Friday 8:00 am in Plainfield; page 3 lists Plainfield Mass intentions.

## Name and address

The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026), GCatholic and the locator map give 10 Railroad Ave., which the bulletin gives as the parish's mailing address. The bulletin also gives 15 Railroad Ave. on page 1 and says the parish office at 15 Railroad Avenue is "located in the church basement", so I recorded 15 as the church's own address (address_verified false: only the parish gives it). The two numbers face each other on one short street. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code (87HCM3HM+X4); it was not checked which side of the street it falls on.
