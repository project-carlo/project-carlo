## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 Divine Mercy bulletin (page 1) lists Saturday Vigil 4:00 pm, Sunday 11:00 am, and Monday and Thursday 7:30 am and Wednesday 12:05 pm at SJE.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the diocese's List of Parishes and the locator map give 22 Maple Ave., Uncasville 06382; GCatholic gives the same street with the town name Montville (Uncasville is a village of Montville). The name is the bulletin's ("Saint John the Evangelist Church"); the diocese and GCatholic say "St. John the Evangelist". Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H9CVPQ+96.
