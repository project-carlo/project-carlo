## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 St. Edmund's Retreat bulletin (files.ecatholic.com/32297, page 2) gives "MASS Daily 9:00am", with a Mass intention for each day from Sunday, November 15 to Saturday, November 21, and a first-Saturday devotion after the 9:00 a.m. Mass. The retreat's home page invites visitors to pray in the chapel. The chapel is in no roster (coverage lists it as unrostered): the diocese lists St. Edmund's Retreat only in its group directory.

## Name and address

The bulletin names the chapel "The Chapel of Our Lady of the Assumption" and gives 1 Enders Island, Mystic 06355. The diocese's group directory gives Enders Island, Mystic 06355-0399 (with the PO box). They agree on the place; the name rests on the retreat's own bulletin alone, so `name_verified` is false. No source with a building pin was read, so coordinates are left for the geocode stage.
