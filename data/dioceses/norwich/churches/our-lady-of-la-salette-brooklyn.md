## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 bulletin (LPi 04/0499, page 1, an image) lists Saturday Vigil 4:00 PM and Sunday 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM at La Salette; the page-2 text lists the week's Masses with intentions, weekdays Tuesday-Friday 8:00 am.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026, street address under a P.O. Box 211 mailing address) and the locator map give 25 Providence Road; GCatholic and the ParishesOnline org give 21 Providence Rd. I followed the parish's and the diocese's 25. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code (87HCQ3Q2+9F), which the ParishesOnline pin for 21 (41.78904, -71.94874) matches within 20 m, so both numbers point at the same church.
