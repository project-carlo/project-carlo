## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 St. Therese of Lisieux bulletin (LPi 03/0332, page 1) lists St. Joseph Church, N. Grosvenordale: Saturday 4:00PM, Sunday 10:00AM, with weekly adoration in its chapel.

## Name and address

The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026), GCatholic and the locator map give 12 Main St.; the bulletin gives 18 Main Street. I followed 12 Main St, which the diocese and two other sources agree on; the bulletin's 18 may be a typo or the building's other frontage (the list puts St. Joseph's thrift shop at 17 and food pantry at 20 Main St, across the road). Either finds the church. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code (87HCX3MX+8H).
