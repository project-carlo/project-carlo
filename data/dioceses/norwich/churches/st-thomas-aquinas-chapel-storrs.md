## Holds Mass

Page 1 of the November 15, 2026 Our Lady Queen of Peace bulletin (container 04/0008, an image) lists Sunday 8:30 am and Saturday 8:30 am Masses at St. Thomas Aquinas on the UConn campus, with Sunday 12:30 and 7:00 pm and weekday Masses when UConn is in session.

## Name and address

The bulletin and the parish site call it St. Thomas Aquinas Chapel; the diocese's list and the locator map say St. Thomas Aquinas Church, GCatholic St. Thomas Aquinas. All give 46 North Eagleville Rd., Storrs. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H9RP6W+PF, close to the parish site's map pin (41.81217, -72.254).
