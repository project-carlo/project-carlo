## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 Our Lady of Mercy bulletin (container 03/0204) lists Sunday Masses at 8:00 and 10:00 AM at St. Colman (page 1 MASS TIMES; page 2 "Sunday 8 AM and 10:00 AM are being celebrated at St. Colman Church").

## Name and address

The bulletin, the diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) and the locator map give 170 Hubbard St., Middlefield 06455; GCatholic gives 145 Hubbard St. The parish's and the diocese's 170 is recorded (two independent sources agree). Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87H9G8C3+Q8, which GCatholic places on the church; not checked against the building on a map, and if 145 and 170 are on opposite sides of the street the point is still within a few dozen metres.
