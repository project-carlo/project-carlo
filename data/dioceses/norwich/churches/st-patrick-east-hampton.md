## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 St. John Paul II bulletin (container 03/1195) gives St. Patrick's schedule on page 1 (Saturday Vigil 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am, weekday Masses Wednesday and Thursday 8:00 am), and its week's Mass list marks the Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 9:00 am Masses "(SP)", the key for St. Patrick. The diocese's March 2026 list gives older times (Saturday 4:00 p.m.; Sunday 8:30, 10:00 a.m., 7:00 p.m.); the bulletin is the current source.
