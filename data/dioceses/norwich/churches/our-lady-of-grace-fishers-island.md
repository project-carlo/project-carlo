## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 St. Brendan bulletin (container 03/1228) names Our Lady of Grace on page 1 and gives it (OLOG) a Saturday 5:30 PM Mass on November 14 and 21 in page 2's week table. The church's own site says "June - August, Saturday Vigil Mass at 5:30 pm, and Sunday Mass at 10:00 am".

## Name and address

Fishers Island is in New York (Town of Southold, Suffolk County) with the Connecticut-routed ZIP 06390, but it belongs to the Diocese of Norwich: the diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) gives Our Lady of Grace as merged into St. Brendan the Navigator. So the state is NY and the record is in this diocese, not other-jurisdiction. The bulletin, the diocese and the locator map give Alpine Ave. with no number (the diocese adds P.O. Box 425); GCatholic gives Alpine Ave. but labels the state Connecticut, which is wrong. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87H97X6M+VR.
