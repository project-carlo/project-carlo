## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 St. Pio bulletin (container 03/0172, page 1) gives St. John's schedule: Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 7:30, 9:00 and 11:00 am, daily Monday, Wednesday and Thursday 7:15 am. These match the diocese's March 2026 times for St. John (Saturday 4:00 p.m.; 7:30, 9:00, 11:00 a.m.), which is how the masthead's two unlabelled schedule columns are assigned.

## Name and address

The bulletin ("St. John The Evangelist", 161 Main Street), the diocese's List of Parishes (St. John Church, 161 Main St.), the locator map, GCatholic and the ParishesOnline org agree on 161 Main St., Old Saybrook 06475. The name is the bulletin's; the diocese and GCatholic say only "St. John", so `name_verified` is false. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87H97JRF+M9, about 150 m from the ParishesOnline org point (41.29136, -72.37856).
