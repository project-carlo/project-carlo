## Holds Mass

The November 15, 2026 Our Lady of La Salette and Our Lady of Lourdes bulletin (LPi 04/0499, page 1, an image) lists Sunday Mass 9:30 AM at Lourdes. The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) gives the mission's Sunday Mass as 9:30 a.m. too.

GCatholic and the diocese's locator map give 40 Cedar Swamp Rd.; the bulletin's page 1 gives 41 Cedar Swamp Rd; the List of Parishes gives no street.
