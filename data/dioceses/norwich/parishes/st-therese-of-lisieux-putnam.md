## Publisher

St. Therese of Lisieux Parish, Putnam (the merged St. Mary, Putnam; Most Holy Trinity, Pomfret; St. Joseph, North Grosvenordale; and St. Stephen, Quinebaug) publishes one LPi bulletin, container 03/0332, under the ParishesOnline org `st-mary-church-06260` (an old account name). The November 15, 2026 issue (read 2026-11-14) is headed "St Therese of Lisieux / Most Holy Trinity • St. Joseph • St Mary • St. Stephen" on page 1, and page 2 is footed "ST. THERESE OF LISIEUX, PUTNAM, NOVEMBER 15, 2026".

## Worship sites

Page 1 gives a Mass schedule for each of the four churches: Most Holy Trinity (Sunday 11:00AM, Monday, Thursday, Friday 8:00AM), St. Joseph (Saturday 4:00PM, Sunday 10:00AM), St. Mary (Saturday 4:00PM, Sunday 9:00AM) and St. Stephen (Sunday 8:30AM, Monday-Wednesday 8:00AM). Page 2 lists Mass intentions for each of the four. The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) gives the same four and marks Sacred Heart, West Thompson, a former mission of St. Joseph, closed; a grep of the issue for "Thompson" finds nothing (only "Sacred Heart Memorial Plaques" in an intention), so it has its own `excluded/` record. The Daughters of the Holy Spirit and the Immaculate Conception Convent (Putnam) and the Marian Fathers (Thompson), which the diocese's Full Group Directory lists, are not in the bulletin's schedule; each has an `excluded/` record.
