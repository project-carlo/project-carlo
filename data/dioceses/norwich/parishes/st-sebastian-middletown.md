## Publisher

St. Sebastian, Middletown publishes its own LPi bulletin, ParishesOnline org `st-sebastian-st-john` (container 03/0082, lpi:0018000000Qc2SJAAZ; `probe po` gives the title "St Sebastian" and 155 Washington St, Middletown). The November 15, 2026 issue (read 2026-11-14) has on page 1 "SAINT SEBASTIAN CATHOLIC CHURCH", "155 Washington Street Middletown, CT 06457" and "Rev. James Thaikoottathil, J.C.D., Pastor". The org slug names St. John too, but the masthead names St. Sebastian alone (an account name is not a membership list).

## Worship sites

The page 1 schedule lists Masses at the one church only: Saturday Vigil 5:00pm; Sunday 8:00am, 11:00am & 5:00pm; weekdays 7:30am Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday; First Friday Mass 7:00pm. No mission or chapel is named.

Wesleyan University (Middletown) has no campus Mass of its own that could be found: its Catholic chaplaincy page (read 2026-11-14) says "St. Sebastian Roman Catholic Church is on the edge of the Wesleyan campus and only a short walk" and points to St. Sebastian's Masses. No roster lists a Wesleyan chapel, so no record was made for it.

## Yoked with St. John: separate bulletins

The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) yokes St. Sebastian with St. John, Middletown. The November 15, 2026 issues are separate: this one (03/0082) has no 'St. John' or 'Saint John', and St. John's (03/1513) names St. John alone, with another pastor (Rev. Gregory Galvin). See `st-john-middletown` and the `separate` review there.

## Website and bulletin provider

The list's www.stsebastianmiddletownct.weebly.com fails TLS (`probe url` UNREACHABLE, 2026-11-14) and GCatholic's stsebastianct.org does not resolve (DEAD-DNS). The masthead's saintsebastianmiddletownct.weebly.com answers and embeds the org, so it is the `website`. fetch reads the bulletin by `provider_id`.
