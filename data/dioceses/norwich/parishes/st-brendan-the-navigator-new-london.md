## Publisher

St. Brendan the Navigator Catholic Community, the parish the diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) formed from St. Joseph and St. Mary, Star of the Sea (New London), St. Paul (Waterford) and Our Lady of Grace (Fishers Island, NY), publishes one LPi bulletin: ParishesOnline org `st-brendan-the-navigator-catholic-community-06320`, container 03/1228, lpi:0018000000QxAEWAA3. The November 15, 2026 issue (read 2026-11-14) has on page 1 "ST. BRENDAN THE NAVIGATOR ... CATHOLIC COMMUNITY", 37 Squire Street, www.sbnccct.com, and the four churches with their addresses.

## Worship sites

Four churches, all in the November 15, 2026 bulletin. Page 1's schedule: Saturday 4:00pm (SJ), 4:30pm (SP) Vigil; Sunday 8:00am (SJ), 9:15am (SMSS), 10:00am (SP), 10:45am (SJ), 12:00pm (SMSS, Spanish); weekday Masses at all three mainland churches. Page 2's week table has a column for each of St. Joseph, St. Mary, St. Paul and OLOG; OLOG (Our Lady of Grace, Fishers Island) has a Saturday 5:30 PM Mass.

Fishers Island prints no bulletin of its own: ourladyofgracefi.org (read 2026-11-14) gives its Mass times and the St. Brendan office phone and links no bulletin, and the St. Brendan bulletin carries its Masses. Our Lady of Grace is in New York (Fishers Island is part of Southold, Suffolk County) but in the Diocese of Norwich: the diocese's list gives it as a merged church of St. Brendan, so it is recorded here with state NY, not as other-jurisdiction.

The diocese lists no other church in this parish.
