## Publisher

St. Pio Parish (the merger of St. John, Old Saybrook and St. Mark, Westbrook) publishes one LPi bulletin, ParishesOnline org `st-pio-parish` (container 03/0172, lpi:0018000000Qc1P6AAJ). The November 15, 2026 issue (read 2026-11-14) names on page 1 both churches with their addresses, "St. Pio Parish" and "Rev. Grzegorz P. Brozonowicz, Pastor"; inside it reads "Churches of St. John, Old Saybrook and St. Mark, Westbrook".

## Worship sites

Page 1 gives two schedules: Saturday 4:00 pm; Sunday 7:30, 9:00 and 11:00 am; daily Monday, Wednesday, Thursday 7:15 am, and Saturday 5:00 pm; Sunday 8:00 am, 10:00 am and 7:00 pm; daily Tuesday and Friday 7:15 am. The columns are unlabelled in the text layer; they are assigned to St. John and St. Mark by the diocese's March 2026 times (St. John Saturday 4:00 p.m. and 7:30, 9:00, 11:00 a.m.; St. Mark Saturday 5:00 p.m.). Page 2 has separate "At St. John Church" and "At St. Mark Church" Mass lists. Two churches; the diocese lists no other in Old Saybrook or Westbrook.
