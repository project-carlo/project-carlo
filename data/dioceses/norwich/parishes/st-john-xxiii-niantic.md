## Publisher

St. Agnes, Niantic and St. Matthias, East Lyme, yoked under one pastor (the diocese's List of Parishes, March 6, 2026), publish one LPi bulletin as the St. John XXIII Catholic Community: ParishesOnline org `st-agnes-and-st-matthias-catholic-church`, container 03/1158, lpi:0018000000QxAEbAAN. The November 15, 2026 issue (read 2026-11-14) has on page 1 "ST . JOHN XXIII CATHOLIC COMMUNITY", one parish office and pastor, and a Mass schedule for each church.

## Worship sites

Two churches. Page 1 of the November 15, 2026 issue: St. Agnes, 22 Haigh Avenue, Niantic (Saturday Vigil 4 p.m., Sunday 7:30 and 11:00 a.m., Wednesday noon, Friday 9 a.m.); St. Matthias, 317 Chesterfield Road, East Lyme (Saturday Vigil 5:30 p.m., Sunday 9:00 a.m., Thursday 9 a.m.). Page 2's intentions have one column for each.

The diocese also lists St. Francis Chapel, Crescent Beach as a seasonal summer mission of St. Agnes, and the parish site has a history page for it, but no 2025 or 2026 summer bulletin read mentions it (see research), and the site's Masses page lists only the two churches. It is an open record (st-francis-chapel-niantic) until a summer schedule settles it.
