## Publisher

Our Lady of Mercy Parish (the merger of Notre Dame, Durham and St. Colman, Middlefield) publishes one LPi bulletin, ParishesOnline org `notre-dame-church-06422` (container 03/0204, lpi:0018000000Qbz7VAAR; the org's title is "Our Lady of Mercy Parish Community", 272 Main St, Durham). The November 15, 2026 issue (read 2026-11-14) has on page 1 "OUR LADY OF MERCY PARISH", both churches with their addresses, and "PASTOR: Fr. Jan Swiderski". The org slug keeps Notre Dame's old name; the masthead, not the slug, shows whom it serves.

## Worship sites

Page 1 MASS TIMES: Saturday Vigil 4:00 PM (Notre Dame), Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 AM (St. Colman), weekday Masses Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 8:00 AM (Notre Dame). Page 2 repeats: "Saturday Vigil 4 PM, Notre Dame Church" and Sunday 8 AM and 10:00 AM "celebrated at St. Colman Church". So two churches: Notre Dame, Durham and St. Colman, Middlefield. The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) gives the same two and nothing else in Durham or Middlefield.
