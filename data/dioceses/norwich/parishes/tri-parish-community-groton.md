## Publisher

The three yoked Groton parishes (Sacred Heart, St. Mary Mother of the Redeemer, Our Lady of Lourdes Gales Ferry; the diocese's List of Parishes, March 6, 2026, gives each as 'Yoked with' the other two under Very Rev. Brian J. Converse) publish one LPi bulletin, container 03/0770. The November 15, 2026 issue (read 2026-11-14) has on page 1 one pastoral staff and the Mass schedules of all three churches ("SACRED HEART CHURCH - WWW.SACREDHEARTGROTON.ORG", "OUR LADY OF LOURDES - WWW.OLOLGF.ORG", "ST. MARY, MOTHER OF THE REDEEMER - WWW.STMARYSGROTON.ORG"), and page 3 a "Tri-Parish Calendar".

Two ParishesOnline accounts point at that container: `tri-parish-community-of-sacred-heart-our-lady-of-lourdes-st-mary` (lpi 0018000000Qc2DvAAJ, titled 'St. Mary Church') and `our-lady-of-lourdes-roman-catholic-church` (lpi 0018000000QbzyVAAR). Both list the same issues, so both ids are recorded here as one bulletin.

## Worship sites

Three churches, from page 1 of the November 15, 2026 issue: Sacred Heart (Saturday 7:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM, Friday 9:00 AM), Our Lady of Lourdes (Saturday 5:30 PM, Sunday 8:30 AM and 5:00 PM, Monday and Tuesday 9:00 AM) and St. Mary, Mother of the Redeemer (Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 7:00 AM and 11:30 AM, Wednesday and Thursday 9:00 AM). The diocese lists no other church for these parishes. Sacred Heart Catholic School (50 Sacred Heart Dr.) appears in the bulletin with no Mass of its own.
