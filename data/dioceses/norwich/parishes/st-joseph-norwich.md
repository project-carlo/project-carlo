## Publisher

St. Joseph (Polish), Norwich publishes its own bulletin on its own site. The November 15, 2026 issue (read 2026-11-14 with probe pdf) is headed "WWW.SAINTJOSEPHNORWICH.COM ... NOVEMBER 15 TH , 2026 — 33 RD SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME", with Msgr. Leszek T. Janik, Pastor, and Fr. Ray Introvigne, Assisting Priest. Page 2 carries "STEWARDSHIP AT ST. JOSEPH" and St. Joseph's Mass intentions.

St. Joseph is yoked with Sacred Heart, Norwichtown under Msgr. Janik (diocese List of Parishes, March 6, 2026), and page 1 prints Sacred Heart's schedule beside St. Joseph's, but Sacred Heart publishes its own LPi bulletin (container 03/0472, masthead "SACRED HEART CHURCH & SAINT JOHN MISSION", with its own intentions and offertory), so the two are separate rows (see `publication_reviews`).

## Worship sites

The St. Joseph box on page 1 lists 120 Cliff Street, Norwich: Vigil Mass Sat. 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 am (English & Polish); the page 2 intentions are for those two Masses only. Sacred Heart and St. John Mission, Fitchville, whose times page 1 also prints, are churches of the Sacred Heart row, whose own bulletin covers them. The diocese's locator map links St. Joseph to jnccfaith.org and Fr. Robert Washabaugh (the Joint Norwich Catholic Cluster); the March 6, 2026 list and this bulletin both put it with Msgr. Janik, and the bulletin is newer.

## Website and bulletin provider

No ParishesOnline id appears, so the row is `other`. The folder name changes with the liturgical year (2025-2026YearA); the index page is the stable entry.
