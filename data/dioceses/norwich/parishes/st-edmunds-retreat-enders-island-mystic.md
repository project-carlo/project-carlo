## Publisher

St. Edmund's Retreat, the Edmundite retreat ministry on Enders Island, Mystic, publishes its own weekly bulletin, "ISLAND REFLECTIONS", on its eCatholic site (id 32297). The November 15, 2026 issue (read 2026-11-14) has on page 1 "THE CHAPEL OF OUR LADY OF THE ASSUMPTION ... WEEKLY BULLETIN" and "1 ENDERS ISLAND, P.O. BOX 399, MYSTIC, CT 06355". It is in no roster: the diocese's Full Group Directory lists it as an institution, not a parish.

## Worship sites

One: the Chapel of Our Lady of the Assumption. Page 2 gives "MASS Daily 9:00am" with an intention for each day, Sunday included. The home page invites visitors to "Come pray in our Chapel", and the site has Mass Times and live-stream pages, so the Mass is treated as public. The site also names a Seaside Chapel, with no Mass times found for it, so it is not recorded.

eCatholic, read from `website` (the eCatholic-hosted endersisland.org). The diocese's directory gives www.endersisland.com, which redirects there. `probe url` gets 403; visit.py reads it.
