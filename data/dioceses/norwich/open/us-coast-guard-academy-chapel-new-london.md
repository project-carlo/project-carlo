## What is known

The diocese's Full Group Directory (read 2026-11-14) lists the U.S. Coast Guard Academy, 15 Mohegan Avenue, New London, with a Catholic priest of the Navy Chaplain Corps (Rev. Keith J. Shuley, C.H.C., U.S.N.) as contact. The Academy's Chaplains page describes a religious program for cadets in the nondenominational Coast Guard Memorial Chapel, which "welcomes visitors from sunrise to sunset", and a Catholic choir, but publishes no Mass times. No roster lists it as a church, and no parish bulletin of this batch (St. Brendan, New London) mentions a Mass there.

## Why it is not decided

A Catholic Mass for cadets is likely, but no source read says when it is or whether the public may attend; and no source read says whether the ministry belongs to the Diocese of Norwich or to the Archdiocese for the Military Services. So it is neither a church row nor an exclusion yet. The chaplain's office would settle both.
