## Why it is excluded

The Holy Family Motherhouse of the Sisters of Charity of Our Lady, Mother of the Church, 54 West Main Street, Baltic, with the Academy of the Holy Family (a girls' high school) at the same address (the diocese's Full Group Directory, read 2026-11-14), has a chapel where Mass is said: the Sisters' Mass-enrollment form promises "three Masses each month at the Motherhouse Chapel". Nothing read gives a public Mass there: the Sisters' site and the Academy's site have no Mass schedule, the directory gives none, and the local Saint Anne's Catholic Community bulletin of November 15, 2026 lists only its three parish churches. This is inferred from absence on those sources: a convent chapel with Masses for the community and school, not shown to be open to the public.

## What would change this

A published schedule of Masses open to the public at the motherhouse chapel (the Sisters' site, the Academy's site or a parish bulletin) would make it a church of the Saint Anne's Catholic Community row if that bulletin lists it, or a `no-bulletin` exclusion if none does.
