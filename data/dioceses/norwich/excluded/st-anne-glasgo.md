## Why it is excluded

The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) gives "St. Anne (Est. 1891), Route 201, Glasgo ... Closed – For inquiries or records, contact St. Thomas & St. Anne, Voluntown". The successor's current bulletin (Holy Spirit Catholic Community, container 03/0945, November 15, 2026) lists Mass only at St. Mary, St. Catherine and St. Thomas. This applies the diocese's accepted rule for churches the list marks closed. GCatholic and the locator map don't list the church at all. No source dates the last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty.

## What would change this

A Holy Spirit Catholic Community bulletin scheduling Mass at St. Anne, Glasgo.
