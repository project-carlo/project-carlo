## Why it is excluded

The friary church is open with public Mass. Its Schedule page (read 2026-11-14) gives "Holy Mass Monday to Saturday – 7:00 am, Sunday – 9:30 am", and the diocese's Full Group Directory gives the same times. It publishes no bulletin the pipeline could read: figuadalupe.com links none and embeds no publisher id, and the neighbouring Holy Spirit Catholic Community bulletin (Jewett City, container 03/0945, November 15, 2026) doesn't carry it. It was never a parish row, so it is excluded as no-bulletin (not closed). Its Facebook page, facebook.com/figriswold, was not checked.

## What would change this

A weekly bulletin or newsletter from the friary (on its site or posted on its Facebook page) would make it a parish row with this church. See research.
