## Why it is excluded

The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) gives "Sacred Heart (Est. 1870), 620 Wauregan Road, Wauregan. Closed: For inquiries or records, contact All Hallows, Moosup." The successor's current bulletin (St. Andre Bessette, container 04/0778, November 14 & 15, 2026) lists Mass only at Canterbury, Moosup and Plainfield. GCatholic still lists the church, without a closure. This applies the diocese's accepted rule for churches the list marks closed. No source dates the last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty.
