## Why it is excluded

Sacred Heart on the Lake is a retreat centre of the Apostles of the Sacred Heart of Jesus, at 194 Candlewood Hill Rd., Higganum (the diocese's Full Group Directory, read 2026-11-14; the sisters' house is listed separately at 529 Brainard Hill Rd.). It has a chapel ("The Center features a chapel with the Blessed Sacrament", its facility page), but nothing read gives a public Mass there: the centre's site lists retreat days, study groups and talks and no Mass times; the diocese's directory gives none; and the November 15, 2026 bulletins of St. Peter, Higganum and St. Lawrence, Killingworth mention it only for an interfaith service and retreats, with no Mass. This is absence across those four sources, not a statement that no Mass is held; a Mass for a retreat group would be a private one.

## What would change this

A published schedule of Masses open to the public at the centre (its site, its Facebook page, or a parish bulletin) would make it a church of St. Peter, Higganum's row, or a `no-bulletin` exclusion if no bulletin lists it.
