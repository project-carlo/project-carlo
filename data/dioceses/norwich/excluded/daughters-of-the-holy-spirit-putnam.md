## Why it is excluded

The Daughters of the Holy Spirit's US province is in Putnam: the diocese's Full Group Directory (read 2026-11-14) gives 72 Church Street, and the congregation's site gives PO Box 419, 508B Pomfret Street. Neither gives a chapel or Mass times, and the St. Therese of Lisieux bulletin of November 15, 2026, the local parish, schedules Mass only at its four churches. This is inferred from absence on those sources: a religious house, not shown to have a public Mass. Which of the two addresses is the house now was not settled; the directory's is recorded.

## What would change this

A published Mass schedule open to the public at the Sisters' Putnam chapel, on their site or in the parish bulletin.
