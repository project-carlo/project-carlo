## Why it is excluded

The Chapel of Our Lady Queen of the Apostles at Holy Apostles College and Seminary, 33 Prospect Hill Rd., Cromwell, holds public weekday Mass: the college's chapel page (read 2026-11-14) says "During the Fall semester, the public is welcome to join us for Mass at: 8:30 AM Tuesday thru Thursday, 11 AM Friday", and that Holy Day times vary. So it is open, not closed. It publishes no bulletin: holyapostles.edu links none and embeds no publisher id (`probe url`, 2026-11-14), and the November 15, 2026 bulletin of St. John, Cromwell (container 03/0934) does not carry its schedule (no 'Holy Apostles' or 'Seminary' on pages 1-3). It was never a parish, so it is excluded with reason no-bulletin rather than given a parish row. It has no weekend Mass and its schedule follows the semester.

## What would change this

A parish bulletin (St. John, Cromwell, or another) printing the chapel's Mass times, or the college publishing a bulletin `fetch` can read.
