## Why it is excluded

The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) gives St. Joseph, Rte. 19, Staffordville as "Closed – For inquiries or records, contact St. Edward the Confessor, Stafford Springs." The successor's current bulletin, St. Edward's November 15, 2026 issue (LPi 03/1293), schedules Mass only at St. Edward and does not mention Staffordville. Excluded as closed under the rule accepted for this diocese (survey-review, 2026-11-14). GCatholic still lists it as a church, with St. Edward's website and fax; that listing is older than the diocese's. No source read dates the last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty.

## What would change this

A St. Edward bulletin or the diocese listing a Mass at St. Joseph, Staffordville (for example a seasonal or feast-day Mass).
