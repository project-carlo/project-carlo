## Why it is excluded

The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) gives "Church of the Sacred Heart (Est. 1880), 11 Sacred Heart Drive. Closed – For inquiries or records, contact St. Joseph, N. Grosvenordale", and under St. Joseph "Mission: Sacred Heart, W. Thompson, -Closed-". The successor's current bulletin (St. Therese of Lisieux, container 03/0332, November 15, 2026) lists Mass only at its four churches. This applies the diocese's accepted rule for churches the list marks closed. GCatholic and the locator map don't list the church. No source dates the last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty.

## What would change this

A St. Therese of Lisieux bulletin scheduling Mass at Sacred Heart, West Thompson.
