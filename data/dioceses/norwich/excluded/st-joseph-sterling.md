## Why it is excluded

The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) gives "St. Joseph (Est. 1909), 06377, Closed – For inquiries or records, contact All Hallows, Moosup" and, under St. Andre Bessette, "Mission: St. Joseph, Sterling – Closed". The successor's current bulletin (St. Andre Bessette, container 04/0778, November 14 & 15, 2026) lists Mass only at its three churches. This applies the diocese's accepted rule for churches the list marks closed. GCatholic and the locator map don't list the church, and no source gives its street. No source dates the last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty.

## What would change this

A St. Andre Bessette bulletin scheduling Mass in Sterling.
