## Why it is excluded

The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) gives 'St. Margaret (Est. 1945), Rte. 14, 06264 Closed – For inquiries or records, contact St. Joseph, Willimantic.' St. Joseph is now one of Corpus Christi Parish's churches, and that parish's November 15, 2026 bulletin (container 03/0033, read 2026-11-14) lists Mass only at its three Willimantic/Windham churches; St. Margaret is not mentioned. This follows the diocese's accepted rule for churches the list marks Closed. No source dates the last Mass, so last_mass is empty. GCatholic still lists the church (22298); the locator map does not.

## What would change this

A Corpus Christi bulletin or the diocese listing a Mass at St. Margaret, Scotland.
