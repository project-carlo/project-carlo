## Why it is excluded

The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026) gives 'St. Joseph (Est. 1935), 887 Stafford Rd., Rte. 32. Mailing Address: 1600 Main St., Coventry 06238. Closed – For inquiries or records, contact St. Mary, Coventry.' St. Mary's parish, Our Lady Queen of Peace, schedules Mass on page 1 of its November 15, 2026 bulletin (container 04/0008, read 2026-11-14) only at St. Mary, Coventry and St. Thomas Aquinas, Storrs. This follows the diocese's accepted rule for churches the list marks Closed. No source dates the last Mass. GCatholic still lists it (22304) at 88 Stafford Rd.; the diocese's 887 is recorded. The locator map omits it.

## What would change this

An Our Lady Queen of Peace bulletin or the diocese listing a Mass at St. Joseph, Eagleville.
