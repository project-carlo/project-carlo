## Why it is excluded

St. Ann, Waterford, is an Eastern Catholic church (Greek-Melkite rite). GCatholic (read 2026-11-14) lists it among the churches in the Norwich territory, but under the "Greek-Melkite Diocese of Newton" ("Jurisdiction: Greek-Melkite Diocese of Newton Location: within the territory of the Roman Diocese of Norwich"). It belongs to the Melkite Greek Catholic Eparchy of Newton, not to the Roman Catholic Diocese of Norwich. The diocese's List of Parishes (March 6, 2026, scraped into `rosters/directory.json`) and the parish locator map (`rosters/locator-map.json`) list no church at this address, and neither mentions any Ukrainian or Melkite church (grep for the street, "Ukrain" and "Melkite", 2026-11-14). The survey-review job (002-survey-review, 2026-11-14) accepted the rule that these three GCatholic Eastern Catholic churches are excluded as `other-jurisdiction`. Whether it holds services, and whether it publishes a bulletin, was not checked.

## What would change this

Nothing within this diocese. If the dataset takes in the Melkite Greek Catholic Eparchy of Newton, the church would be recorded there.
