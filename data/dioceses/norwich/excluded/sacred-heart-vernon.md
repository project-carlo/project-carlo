## Why it is excluded

The successor's current bulletin, Blessed Sacrament Parish's November 15, 2026 issue (LPi 03/0263, page 1), lists Mass only at St. Bernard, Rockville and St. Matthew, Tolland. Excluded as closed under the rule accepted for this diocese (survey-review, 2026-11-14). GCatholic still lists it as a church (with sacredheartchurch.net); that listing is older than the diocese's. No source read gives the date of the last Mass, so `last_mass` is empty.
