## Holds Mass

Yes, and it is the only place in the parish that does. The parish's Mass page (read 2 October 2026) says "ALL MASSES ARE HELD AT GUARDIAN ANGELS", with Saturday 4:30pm, Sunday 8:00 and 10:30am and weekday Masses; the 27 September 2026 bulletin's intentions use the same times.

## Name and address

"Guardian Angels Church", 2061 East Henrietta Road, Rochester 14623, in the parish site, the bulletin cover, GCatholic, the diocesan directory and the Parish Locator. OpenStreetMap labels the building 2059 East Henrietta Road; I treat that as an OSM numbering difference. Both flags true. The diocese's directory also joins this church and St. Joseph, Rush in one garbled address (`parish-directory:st-marianne-cope-2`), linked here. The coordinates (geocode stage) are 55 m from GCatholic's Plus Code point and 98 m from the OSM outline; left alone.
