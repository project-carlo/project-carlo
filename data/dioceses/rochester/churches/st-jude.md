## Holds Mass

Yes, in summer only (Father's Day to the second Sunday in September, Sunday 8:00am). Nothing is scheduled on 2 October 2026. The 4 October bulletin's giving table shows St. Jude attendance of 53 for the weekend of 13 September 2026, so the 2026 season ran, and the parish page and Mass page describe the season. This is a seasonal Mass, about 13 Sundays a year.
