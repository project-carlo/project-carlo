## Holds Mass

Yes: Sunday 8:30 am, and Tuesday and Friday 11:00 am after adoration (parish home page, 2 October 2026; 27 September 2026 bulletin). Older sources (diocese directory, Locator) say Sunday 8 am, and the Locator adds summer Saturdays at 4:00 pm.

## Name and address

78 Shethar Street, Hammondsport 14840: the bulletin, parish site, diocese directory, GCatholic and the Locator agree on number 78 (spelling "Shethar", "Shether"); OSM tags the same church building 78 Church Street, at the live point (the church is on the corner). Name "St. Gabriel's Church" (bulletin, diocese, OSM). Both flags true. The live row spells the street "Shethar" as the bulletin and diocese do. The diocese's second listing (`parish-directory:st-john-vianney-2`, 78 Shether Street) is this church.
