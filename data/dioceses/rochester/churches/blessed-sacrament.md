## Holds Mass

Yes. The 4 October 2026 cluster bulletin lists Sunday 9:00 am at Blessed Sacrament Church and weekday Masses Monday and Wednesday 8:00 am; the diocesan directory lists "9:30 AM Blessed Sacrament".

## Name and address

The church stands at the corner of Oxford and Rutgers Streets. GCatholic, the locator and the directory's address field say 534 Oxford St.; the bulletin and the directory's text say 259 Rutgers Street, a second street address for the same corner (OSM has a house node at 259 Rutgers Street 70 m from the stored point and a "Blessed Sacrament School" on Rutgers). The stored coordinates are within 22 m of GCatholic's Plus Code. Both flags true: the sources agree on the place and differ only in which street name is used; recorded here.
