## Holds Mass

Yes, but only on weekdays, and not at the weekend: Thursday 8:00 am at St. Juan Diego Community (parish home page, 2 October 2026; the 27 September 2026 bulletin shows it as "(SJDC)" on Thursday 1 October). The diocese directory adds Friday 8am (the parish site and bulletin list Thursday only). The weekend schedule has no Mass here. The Parish Locator's "St. Thomas Aquinas, Leicester" entry says "Sat: 4pm at St. Lucys, Retsof", i.e. it points people elsewhere. The parish masthead heads it "Diocese of Rochester Migrant Ministry", so it serves the migrant farmworker community.

## Name and address

The parish site names it "St. Juan Diego Community (formerly St. Thomas Aquinas)" and the diocese directory says the same; GCatholic and the Locator still call it St. Thomas Aquinas, so the old name accounts for those entries (linked here: `gcatholic:17814`, `directory:ind-st-thomas-aquinas`, and `directory:344-st-thomas-aquinas`, the Locator's St. Luke-group entry, which is placed at "Geneseo" with no address and "No Mass"; that city is the parish seat, not the building). Address 175 Main Street, Leicester 14481: parish site, bulletin, GCatholic and an OSM building agree. Both flags true. Coordinates are the OSM building, 12 m from GCatholic's Plus Code.
