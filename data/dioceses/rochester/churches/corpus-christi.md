## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's FAQ page and the diocesan directory both list the Sunday 8:30 am English and 10 am Spanish Masses here, and the 5-6 September bulletin lists them ("8:30 am MASS at Corpus Christi").

## Name and address

The parish and the diocese call it Corpus Christi; GCatholic and the locator also know it as Our Lady of the Americas (that parish merged into Cabrini; the sacramental index lists "Our Lady of the Americas, Rochester" under Cabrini). Same building, 864 E Main St in every source, so both flags are true and the alias is recorded. `directory:358-corpus-christi-our-lady-of-the-americas` is linked. The locator's "Corpus Christi School" at 546 Oxford St is a separate school (excluded).
