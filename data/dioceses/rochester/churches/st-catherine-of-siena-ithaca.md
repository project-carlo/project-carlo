## Holds Mass

Yes. The 4 October 2026 bulletin (image-only pages, read by rendering page 2) schedules Saturday 5:00 pm and Sunday 9:00 am weekend Masses and Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 9:00 am. The Directory of Parishes gives Sunday 10:00 a.m. and the Locator Sunday 9 and 11:30; the current bulletin wins, and the three disagree on the Sunday time only.

## Name and address

**Address disagreement.** The bulletin masthead (page 1) and the diocese Directory of Parishes (updated 1/27/25) say 309 Siena Drive, Ithaca 14850, which is the live row's address. GCatholic and the older Parish Locator say 302 St. Catherines Cir. (presumably the same property under another street name; not checked on a map). I followed the bulletin and the Directory of Parishes, two independent sources, so both flags stay true. Coordinates: geocode stage, 21 m from GCatholic's Plus Code.
