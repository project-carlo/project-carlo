## Holds Mass

Yes: Saturday 4:30 pm, Sunday 6:45 am and 10:45 am, and Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 12:10 pm (the parish's 4 October 2026 bulletin and its Mass-times page, 2 October 2026; the diocese directory agrees).

## Name and address

27 Erie Avenue, Hornell 14843 is the parish office and campus address on the bulletin masthead, the parish site and GCatholic, and is one of the diocese directory's two entries. The directory's description gives St. Ann's as 31 Erie Avenue (OSM has a house there). The Courier places the church at the corner of Erie Avenue and Elm Street, and OSM puts the church on Elm Street 60 m from the live point. I take 27 Erie as the church's address (three sources, plus the directory's own entry) and the live point is within 60 to 90 m of OSM's and GCatholic's, so it stays. Name "St. Ann's Church" (site: "St. Ann’s Church in Hornell"), "St. Ann" in the bulletin and GCatholic. Both flags true; the 31 Erie variant is recorded.
