## Holds Mass

Yes. The 27 September 2026 bulletin of The Parish of the Holy Family (pages 1 and 2) lists Saturday 4:00 PM and 8:00 AM (Vietnamese), Sunday 9:00 AM, 11:30 AM and 5:00 PM, and weekday Masses at 9:00 AM; the parish home page (read in a browser) gives the same. All of them are at 4100 Lyell Road, the parish's only campus (its Location Key lists a church, a Parish Life Center and an office building).

## Name and address

The building is **St. Jude the Apostle Church**: GCatholic (17809), the Parish Locator ("St. Jude") and OpenStreetMap ("Saint Jude the Apostle Roman Catholic Church") all name it so, at 4100 Lyell Road. I renamed the live church from the parish name "The Parish of the Holy Family" (the slug `holy-family` stays) because this row is a building and the parish name is on the parish row. The bulletin and parish site never name the building, so the name rests on GCatholic and OpenStreetMap, independent of each other and of the parish: `name_verified` true. Address 4100 Lyell Road, 14606 (the locator says ZIP 14624): the bulletin, the diocesan directory, GCatholic, the locator and OSM agree: `address_verified` true. Coordinates: geocode stage, 41 m from GCatholic's Plus Code and 16 m from the OSM outline; left alone. Two former sites of the old cluster, Holy Ghost and St. Helen, are `excluded/holy-ghost-gates.md` and `excluded/st-helen-gates.md`.
