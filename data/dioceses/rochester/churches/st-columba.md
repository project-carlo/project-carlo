## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish site lists Saturday vigil 4:00 pm at St. Columba, and the weekday Masses Wednesday 6 pm and Friday 12 pm (directory and locator); the 27 September 2026 bulletin cover (image) gives "Mass Saturday 4:00 pm" under St. Columba.

## Name and address

198 North Street, Caledonia 14423: the bulletin cover, parish site, ParishesOnline record, diocesan directory, locator and GCatholic agree. Name: the bulletin and parish site say "St. Columba", OSM "Saint Columba's"; the locator and diocese directory say "St. Columba/St. Patrick" and GCatholic "St. Columba and St. Patrick", because St. Patrick (Mumford) merged into it (sacramental-records index: "St. Patrick, Mumford" records at "St. Columba, Caledonia"). The one building is St. Columba's; the live name stays. Both flags true. Coordinates (geocode stage) are 25 m from GCatholic and 15 m from OSM; left alone.
