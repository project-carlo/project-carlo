## Holds Mass

Yes. The 4 October 2026 bulletin: Saturday vigil 4:30 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 am, Monday-Friday 7:30 am; the locator says "Sat: 4:30pm | Sun: 8, 10am".

## Name and address

Name: "Church of St. Jerome" (bulletin, Directory), "St. Jerome" (locator, OSM). Address 207 Garfield Street, East Rochester 14445 in the bulletin and the Directory. **OSM tags the church building 209 Garfield Street** and a neighbouring house 207; the two are about 15 m apart, so this is OSM numbering, and the parish's own bulletin and the diocese agree on 207. GCatholic has no entry and the locator has no street, so only the parish and the diocese support 207 and I counted them as the two sources; flags left true, with this disagreement recorded. Coordinates are the live geocode (the 207 house), about 15 m from OSM's church building.
