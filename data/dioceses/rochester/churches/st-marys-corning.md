## Holds Mass

Yes: Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 am and 10:30 am, and weekday Masses as the week's page 2 shows (3-4 October 2026 bulletin). The bulletins (6 September to 4 October 2026) state "All Masses and Confessions are in the Gym", so for now Mass is in the gym on the school campus (158 State Street, next to the church), a temporary arrangement whose cause the bulletins do not give (the bulletin's 'what was happening this time last year' item shows steeple repairs in autumn 2025). It is not a different parish site; a chapel hosts one Saturday 4:00 pm Mass for people with mobility issues. This is still St. Mary's campus and the only All Saints worship site.
