## Holds Mass

Yes, in the school year. The 4 October 2026 bulletin (page 3) lists Sunday 8:00am Mass ("8:00am (ST) - Bob & Jean Grunow") and confession 7:20 to 7:50. The parish's Mass page gives Sunday 8:00 here in the fall, winter and spring; in summer the Mass is at St. Jude, Fair Haven.

## Name and address

Name true. Address 6927 Main Street, Red Creek 13143: the bulletin, the parish page, the Locator and OSM's address point agree. The diocese directory says 6291 Main Street and GCatholic has no number. Three sources outvote the directory's single number, but the directory is a diocesan source, so the flag stays **false** (a disagreement, not resolved by a second independent survey). The GCatholic entry 17874 ("St. Thomas the Apostle", Route 104A, Red Creek) is the same church and is now linked. Coordinates are the OSM address point, 14 m from GCatholic's Plus Code.
