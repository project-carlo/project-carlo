## Holds Mass

Yes, during the school year. The 4 October 2026 bulletin (page 5) lists a Sunday 4:30pm Mass at "St. John's Chapel" on 4 and 11 October (page 6: "(Except summer)"). This is the Hobart and William Smith campus Mass, in the parish bulletin; it is not in the Locator or GCatholic.

## Name and address

Name true: the bulletin and the diocese directory both call it St. John's Chapel. Address true: both say 630 South Main Street, Geneva 14456, which OpenStreetMap also has as an address point on the HWS campus (tagged "Chaplain's house"). The chapel is in neither GCatholic nor the Locator, so no roster is linked. Coordinates are that OSM address point from the geocode stage; the chapel building itself is not separately mapped.
