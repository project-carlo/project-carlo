## What is known

The Catholic Newman Community at SUNY Brockport is a ministry of the Nativity / St. Elizabeth Ann Seton pastor (parish `nativity-seas`). GCatholic (17778) lists a "Newman Oratory of SUNY Brockport" at 101 Kenyon St., Brockport 14420, and the diocese's Parish Locator lists a "Newman Center", Brockport, with "Sun: 11:30 am" (a stale entry; the locator's link, an `acs.brockport.edu/~newman` page, answers 403 to curl). The parish's Campus Ministry page lists "Newman Mass scheduled at SUNY Brockport at 8:00 p.m." on November 2 and 9, 2025 only, with no 2026-27 dates. The parish's home-page Mass times do not include the campus. I did not find a source that gives a campus address for those Masses or any Mass this semester.

## Why it is not decided

A church row needs public Mass now, and no current source says there is one. The last dated Masses are from last November, a different semester.
