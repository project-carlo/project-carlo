## What is known

St. Casimir Church (1000 Davis St., Elmira; the Polish parish, church built 1912) had one weekend Mass until
the pandemic. In November 2021 the Catholic Courier reported it "currently is unavailable for worship due to a
faulty ceiling", not included in the decree that relegated Ss. Peter and Paul and Our Lady of Lourdes, with
Masses unable to resume until the ceiling was replaced. Since then it has been missing from every Mass
schedule I read: the 4 October 2026 bulletin and the parish homepage list Mass only at St. Mary and St. Patrick
and name only those two as the parish's churches, and the 2023 to 2026 bulletins' "Our Churches" box omits it.
The diocese Directory of Parishes (1/27/25) still lists it as "St. Casimir Church & Faith Formation Center".
GCatholic lists it under "Christ the Redeemer Parish" and the Parish Locator shows "Mass: Sun: 7:30am"
(both stale). I found no bulletin, article or decree that says it was closed or relegated.

## Why it is not decided

Everything points to no Mass for five years, but the README asks for a source that states a closure, and absence
from schedules is weak. The Courier's "unavailable for worship" is a 2021 statement, and the church was
explicitly not part of the relegation decree. A church row is out (no Mass now), and a `closed` exclusion needs
a source. Next step: ask the parish office (607-733-3484) whether St. Casimir is relegated or sold, or find the
decree; then move it to `excluded/` with `closed` and the date. It has no effect on the live data either way
(there is no live row for it). Revisit 2027-01-15.
