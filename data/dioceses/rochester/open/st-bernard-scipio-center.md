## What is known

- Good Shepherd's own pages say the parish has four worship sites (Moravia, Aurora, Union Springs, King Ferry), and the 4 October 2026 bulletin schedules Masses only there.
- The Locator lists St. Bernard under "Individual" and sends readers to Aurora for Mass times. GCatholic lists it as an open church (2841 Center Rd., Scipio Center).
- The sacramental-records index moves St. Bernard's records to Good Shepherd, Aurora.

## Why it is not decided

Every source points to no Mass, but none says the church closed or when (the records index says a corporation ended, and the parish page never mentions the building). The step that settles it is a call to the Good Shepherd office (315-364-7197) or a dated closure notice; then exclude it as closed. If an occasional Mass (a funeral or cemetery Mass) is all there is, it is still not a public Mass site.
