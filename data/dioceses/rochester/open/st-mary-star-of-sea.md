## What is known

St. Rose of Lima, Sodus Point (slug kept from the old "St. Mary Star of the Sea" row) is a summer
chapel: the Wayne County Historic Sites entry says it is "open for services June through
August", and DiscoverMass's 2015 listing had a seasonal Saturday 6:15 pm Mass. No 2026 Mass was
found: the 26 July 2026 Maximilian Kolbe / Katharine Drexel bulletin (in the middle of the
season) and the cluster's website list Masses at Macedon, Palmyra, Ontario and Sodus (Epiphany)
only, and the 4 October issue names none at Sodus Point. Details are under *When it was a church
row* below.

## Why it is not decided

Nothing says the chapel closed, so it is not excluded. But a summer chapel with no Mass in its
own summer is not a place with public Mass now: on 7 October 2026 it was one of the churches
showing on the site with no Mass at all, so it was moved here from `churches/` (an audit of
churches with no listings). `promote` takes it off the site; the revisit is set for the 2027
season.
