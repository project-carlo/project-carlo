## What is known

The old diocesan locator lists a "St. Ann Community" at 1500 Portland Ave, Irondequoit with Masses Saturday 3:45 pm and Sunday 10 am. It is not in GCatholic or the 2025 diocesan directory, and not the parish "St. Anne" on Mount Hope Avenue (which is `churches/st-anne.md`). The address is that of a senior-living and care campus (St. Ann's Community), which suggests a chapel whose Masses are mainly for residents, but I found no page of the campus saying so, nor one saying Mass is open to the public.

## Why it is not decided

No current source states a schedule, and a residents-only chapel would be `no-public-mass`, while an open Mass would be a church row needing a bulletin or schedule. Next step: find the campus's own site (the stannscommunity.org domain is parked) or phone the pastoral-care office, then decide.
