## What is known

- The Locator lists "Mission of Our Lady of Guadalupe (St. Gregory)", Marion, under the group "St. Benedict" (a Canandaigua parish; wrong), Mass "Sun: 2PM Spanish", no address, no website.
- GCatholic (updated 2025-12-21) lists St. Gregory (under St. Katharine Drexel Parish) and the Mission of Our Lady of Guadalupe at 3799 Union St., Marion, sharing one building.
- A 2014 blog post says the church reopened as a Spanish-language mission after Mass had stopped for a while.
- The sacramental-records index shows St. Gregory's records at St. Katharine Drexel, Macedon, so the parish corporation ended.
- The cluster's website Mass list and the 4 October 2026 bulletin (the whole issue) say nothing about Marion, Our Lady of Guadalupe or a Spanish Mass. The diocese's Directory of Parishes entry for the two parishes lists four churches (Macedon, Palmyra, Ontario, Sodus), not Marion.

## Why it is not decided

Absence from the cluster's Mass list is weak evidence, and the Locator and GCatholic still show a Sunday Spanish Mass. Nothing states that Mass has ended or continues. The step that settles it is a call to the cluster office (315-524-2611 or 315-538-8242) or the diocese's Hispanic ministry.
