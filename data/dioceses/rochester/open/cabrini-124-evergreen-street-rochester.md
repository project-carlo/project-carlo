## What is known

The old locator gives Cabrini Parish's own entry, and its "St. Michaels" entry, the address 124 Evergreen St, Rochester 14605. That is not any of the three churches (1760 Norton, 864 E Main, 869 N Clinton) nor the current parish office (80 Prince Street, per the diocesan directory). It is probably a former parish office or rectory (possibly of the former St. Michael or Holy Redeemer parishes). The locator's `St. Michaels` entry is linked to `churches/st-michael.md` by name; this entry (the parish-level one) is parked here.
