## What is known

A privately owned chapel (Saint Padre Pio Institute of Rochester, Inc.) in the Town of Gates, open to the public for prayer; GCatholic lists it under the Diocese of Rochester. The chapel website lists blessings and a few evening prayer services, and its board includes a priest (Fr. Daniel Condon), but no Mass schedule. The diocesan directory and the locator do not list it. The diocese's directory of parishes has no entry for it.
