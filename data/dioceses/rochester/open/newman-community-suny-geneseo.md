## What is known

SUNY Geneseo's Newman page says there is a "weekly Mass and free pasta dinner every Thursday @ 5:15 at the Interfaith Center", 11 Franklin Street. OSM has a place of worship there ("Interfaith Center, 11 Franklin Street", 42.80032, -77.81945) and GCatholic lists it as "SUNY Geneseo", Newman Parish, with Plus Code 87J4R52J+57 (42.80044, -77.81931). The 27 September 2026 St. Luke the Evangelist bulletin masthead lists the Newman Community at that address as one of the parish's sites, as a ministry rather than a Mass time.

## Why it is not decided

The Newman page is demonstrably out of date elsewhere (St. Mary's Sunday times and house number), and neither St. Luke's site, its bulletin schedule nor the diocese directory lists a Thursday Mass at the Interfaith Center. Whether it is still held, and whether non-students may attend, is not stated by any current source. If it is, this becomes a church under `st-luke-the-evangelist` (address 11 Franklin Street, Geneseo 14454; GCatholic 17815 and the locator's Newman entry link to it). Ask the parish office (585-243-1100) or the Newman liaison named on the parish site.
