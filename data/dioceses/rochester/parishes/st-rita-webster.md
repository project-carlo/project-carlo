## Publisher

St. Rita publishes its own weekly bulletin. The 4 October 2026 issue is headed "ST. RITA CHURCH
OCTOBER 4, 2026" and lists weekend Masses Saturday 5:00 pm and Sunday 8:00 am and 10:30 am,
with the church's address 1008 Maple Dr., Webster. It covers St. Rita alone. The diocese groups
it with no other parish.

## Worship sites

One: `st-rita`, 1008 Maple Drive. The parish also runs a school on the same site (the site
title is "Saint Rita Catholic Church and School"); the school is not a worship site.

the PDFs, which are stored on an S3 bucket, so provider `other`. The publication is that
page. The diocese's Parish Locator links the same site.
