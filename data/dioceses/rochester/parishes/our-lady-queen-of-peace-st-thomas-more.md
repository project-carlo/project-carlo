## Publisher

Our Lady Queen of Peace (OLQP) and St. Thomas More (STM) in Brighton publish one weekly bulletin on eCatholic (site id 17932). Its Mass intentions are labelled OLQP and STM throughout, and page 3 ends with a donate box for each. It is separate from the Our Lady of Lourdes / St. Anne bulletin in the neighbouring cluster (`our-lady-of-lourdes-st-anne`), which mentions OLQP and STM only in a shared faith-formation block, and whose own parish finance office sits at St. Thomas More (the OLOL cluster directory entry says "Parish Finance Office: St. Thomas More, 2617 East Avenue"). Pastor Fr. Joseph Hart (OLQP/STM); the OLOL cluster's pastor is Fr. Gary Tyman. Not a shared pastor, so I left `group` empty.

## Worship sites

Two: `our-lady-queen-of-peace` (601 Edgewood Avenue) and `st-thomas-more` (2617 East Avenue). Both appear in the bulletin's weekly Mass list and in the diocesan directory.

## Website and bulletin provider

`olqpstm.com` is the parish's eCatholic site (Cloudflare 403 to `probe url`; loads in a browser) and embeds `ecatholic:17932`, so `ecatholic` with the website is right. Issues are at `files.ecatholic.com/17932/bulletins/<date>.pdf` (the 4 October issue is up; the weekly files are dated by Sunday).
