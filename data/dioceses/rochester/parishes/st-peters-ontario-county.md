## Publisher

St. Peter's Roman Catholic Parish publishes its own weekly bulletin as a PDF on its website (The Church Co hosting). The 4 October 2026 issue is headed "St. Peter's Roman Catholic Parish at the Churches of St. Dominic, St. Felix, and St. Francis" (page 2) and its attendance table (page 3) shows one weekend of Masses at all three: St. Dominic Sat 4:00 and Sun 11:30, St. Felix Sun 8:00, St. Francis Sun 9:30. One bulletin, one row. The Locator's group "St. Peter" has the same three churches.

## Worship sites

St. Felix, Clifton Springs; St. Francis, Phelps; St. Dominic, Shortsville. Nothing else is listed for the parish by the diocese, GCatholic or the Locator. The Locator also has an entry "St. Peter" (Clifton Springs) with no address, which is the parish itself, linked to `st-felix` (the parish office is at 12 Hibbard Ave).

## Website and bulletin provider

`website` https://www.stpeterparish.us links the bulletin page `/bulletins` (and the newest PDF on the home page). The provider is `other` (self-hosted PDFs on the church-co asset host) with no provider_id. The publication id is the bulletin page. The old domain GCatholic carries for the parish (stdominicchurch-shortsville.com) is dead.
