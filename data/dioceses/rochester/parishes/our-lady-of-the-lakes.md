## Publisher

Our Lady of the Lakes Catholic Community (merged 2010 from six parishes) publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 06/0100. The 4 October 2026 issue says on page 1 "Our Lady of the Lakes Catholic Community" with the weekend Masses at St. Januarius, St. Michael and St. Patrick, and its pastor refers to "each of the three churches". One row; the Locator groups the same three under "Our Lady of the Lakes".

## Worship sites

Three churches have Mass: St. Januarius, Naples (Sun 8:30; Tue and Thu weekday Mass), St. Michael, Penn Yan (Sat 5:30, Sun 10:30; Mon and Wed weekday) and St. Patrick, Prattsburgh (Sat 3:30). Ruled out: St. Mary, Rushville (closed 1 Jan 2011, `excluded/st-mary-rushville`), St. Theresa, Stanley (closed 30 June 2019, `excluded/st-theresa-stanley`), both still listed by GCatholic (Stanley and Rushville as Our Lady of the Lake parish churches), and the parish centre at 210 Keuka St, Penn Yan (`excluded/olol-parish-center-penn-yan`, an office the directory lists as an address). St. Andrew, Dundee (closed 28 June 2009 per the same history page) is in no roster. GCatholic puts St. Januarius under "Our Lady of the Grapes Shrine" (a naming quirk).
