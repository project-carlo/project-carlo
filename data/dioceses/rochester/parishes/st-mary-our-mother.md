## Publisher

St. Mary Our Mother, Horseheads, publishes its own weekly bulletin: the 27 September 2026 issue (and the
4 October issue in the ParishesOnline container) is headed "St. Mary Our Mother, Horseheads, New York",
816 West Broad Street, (607) 739-3817. Fr. Christopher E. Linsler is pastor (page 4). It names one church
and one chapel inside it ("Holy Hour & Rosary for Life ... in Chapel", the church's own chapel). It is not
grouped with any other parish.

## Worship sites

One church: `churches/st-mary-our-mother.md`. The parish also runs a school at 811 Westlake Street and a
cemetery; neither is a worship site.

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site is `stmaryourmother.com` (the live row's `other` provider is right): its Bulletins page
lists self-hosted PDFs under /Bulletins/2026/. The same bulletin is also in ParishesOnline container 06/0149
(found from the LPi footer "St. Mary Our Mother, Horseheads, NY 06-0149"), recorded as a publication id.
The diocese Directory of Parishes gives the address as "816 East Broad Street, Rochester" for this
parish, an error (the parish and its bulletin say 816 West Broad Street, Horseheads).
