## Publisher

One bulletin serves both canonical parishes. The 4 October 2026 issue (ParishesOnline container 06/0230) reports giving and attendance for "St. Katharine Drexel" and "St. Maximilian Kolbe" side by side on page 4 and has one Mass-intention list (page 3) for St. Mary's (Ontario), Epiphany (Sodus), St. Patrick (Macedon) and St. Anne (Palmyra). The 26 July 2026 issue's masthead (an image; I read it by rendering page 1) is "Catholic Parishes St. Maximilian Kolbe & St. Katharine Drexel" over those four churches. The diocese's two directory listings carry identical churches and schedules. So one row.

## Worship sites

Four churches have Mass in the bulletin and the cluster site's schedule: Macedon `st-patricks-macedon` (Sat 4:30, Sun 10:00), Palmyra `st-annes` (Sun 8:30), Ontario `st-marys-of-lake-ontario` (Sat 4:30, Sun 8:30) and Sodus `epiphany` (Sun 10:30). Not here: Sodus Point's St. Rose of Lima (summer chapel, `open/st-mary-star-of-sea`: no 2026 Mass was found, so on 2026-10-07 it was moved to open/ with a revisit for the 2027 season) and Marion's St. Gregory / Our Lady of Guadalupe mission (`open/st-gregory-marion`). The Locator files the Marion mission under "St. Benedict" (a Canandaigua parish), which is wrong; GCatholic and the records index put St. Gregory with St. Katharine Drexel.

`website` https://www.catholicparisheswwc.org is the cluster's own site and links the bulletin.
