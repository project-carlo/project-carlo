## Publisher

The Southeast Rochester Catholic Community cluster publishes one weekly bulletin on eCatholic (site id 19887). The 4 October 2026 issue (`files.ecatholic.com/19887/bulletins/20261004.pdf`, five pages) has the cluster masthead on page 1 and a single weekend Mass box for all three churches: Saturday vigil 4:30 pm at St. Mary's; Sunday 8:00 am St. Boniface, 9:00 am Blessed Sacrament, 11:15 am St. Mary's. The diocesan directory says the same three churches "comprise the Southeast Rochester Catholic Community Cluster". The page 1 footer of the bulletin gives the cluster address as 259 Rutgers Street (Blessed Sacrament's street address) for all three.

## Worship sites

Three churches: `blessed-sacrament`, `st-boniface`, `st-marys-rochester`. The directory's other listings for the cluster are the same three buildings (it carries one entry per address, all named "Blessed Sacrament"). Saturday vigil alternates between St. Boniface (January to June) and St. Mary's (July to December) per the directory; the October issue puts it at St. Mary's, consistent with that. No other building is listed by the bulletin, the directory, the locator or GCatholic.

## Website and bulletin provider

`southeastrochestercatholics.org` is the cluster's eCatholic site and it embeds `ecatholic:19887`; the pipeline reads eCatholic bulletins from the website, so no change. I read the issue straight from `files.ecatholic.com`, which needs no browser.
