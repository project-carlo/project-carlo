## Publisher

St. Louis publishes its own ParishesOnline bulletin (container 06/0026). The 4 October 2026
issue's page 1 gives "64 S. Main St., Pittsford, NY 14534", weekend Masses Saturday 4:30 pm
and Sunday 8:00 am, 10:30 am and 5:00 pm, and weekday Mass at 11:00 am. It is St. Louis
alone, with its school ("St. Louis School Principal") and "Multi-parish finance director" noted
among staff. The diocese lists it as its own group.

## Worship sites

One: `st-louis`, 64 South Main Street, Pittsford.

## Website and bulletin provider

The website `stlouischurch.org` is an eCatholic site behind Cloudflare, but no bulletin is
posted in that eCatholic account, so the bulletin is the ParishesOnline one that the live row
already has as `provider_id: st-louis-church`. The Directory of Parishes puts this listing's
city as "Fairport"; the street, ZIP 14534 and every other source say Pittsford.
