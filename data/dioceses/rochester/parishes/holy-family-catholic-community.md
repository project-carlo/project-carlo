## Publisher

Holy Family Catholic Community publishes one weekly bulletin on ParishesOnline (container 06/0327) for its four churches. The 4 October 2026 issue is headed "HOLY FAMILY CATHOLIC COMMUNITY" (page 1) and its page 3 says "Donation boxes are available in all 4 church sites." The diocese's name for it ("Community of the Holy Family" in GCatholic, "Holy Family" in the Locator) is the same unit; no other row shares this bulletin.

## Worship sites

Four, all in the 4 October bulletin and on the parish schedule: `st-mary-dansville` (Saturday 4:00 pm; weekday Masses in the annex chapel), `st-joseph-wayland` (Sunday 7:30 am, Monday and Wednesday 9:00 am), `sacred-heart-of-jesus` (Sunday 9:15 am, Perkinsville) and `st-pius-v` (Sunday 11:00 am, Cohocton). The sacramental-records index also lists St. Matthias (Atlanta) and St. Patrick (Dansville) as merged into Holy Family; neither is in the schedule, and I did not look for a closure source for them.
