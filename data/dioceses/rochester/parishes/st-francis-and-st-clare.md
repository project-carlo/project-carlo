## Publisher

St. Francis & St. Clare Parish (St. Mary's, Waterloo and St. Patrick's, Seneca Falls) publishes one self-hosted PDF bulletin. The 26-27 September 2026 issue (the newest posted on 2 October) is headed "St. Francis & St. Clare Parish" with the office at 35 Center Street, Waterloo, and "Seneca Falls & Waterloo, New York"; it prints one block per church with its Mass times. One bulletin, one row; the Locator groups the two churches as "St. Francis & St. Clare".

## Worship sites

St. Mary's, Waterloo (Sat 5:00 PM, Sun 9:30 AM) and St. Patrick's, Seneca Falls (Sun 8:00 and 11:00 AM). Nothing else is listed by the diocese, GCatholic or the Locator. The Locator's "St. Francis & St. Clare" entry (25 Center St., Waterloo) is the parish itself and is linked to `st-marys-waterloo`; the Locator's "St. Patricks" (Seneca Falls) is `st-patricks-seneca-falls`. The bulletin's street numbers differ from GCatholic and the directory for both churches (see the church records).

## Website and bulletin provider

`website` https://sfscrcc.org (the home page links `bulletin.htm`, which lists the PDFs). Provider `other` with no provider_id, since fetch reads the page; the publication is the bulletin page. The PDFs are named by timestamp (1790326230.pdf), not date, so fetch must read the page, not guess names. The old GCatholic domain (smsprcc.org) is dead.
