## Publisher

St. Benedict Parish publishes one eCatholic bulletin for its two churches. The 4 October 2026
masthead reads "ST. BENEDICT PARISH / ST. BRIDGET'S CHURCH · ST. MARY'S CHURCH · ST. MARY'S
SCHOOL, CALVARY CEMETERY · ST. BRIDGET'S CEMETERY", with "Our churches" St. Bridget's, 15
Church St, Bloomfield, and St. Mary's, 95 N. Main St, Canandaigua. The intentions list Masses at
both ("SM" and "SB").

## Worship sites

Two: `st-marys-canandaigua` and `st-bridgets`. **St. Bridget, East Bloomfield (the lead)**: the
locator's "St. Bridget, East Bloomfield" (`directory:365-st-bridget`, no street address, county
wrongly Monroe) and GCatholic's "St. Bridget-St. Joseph, 15 Church St., East Bloomfield"
(`gcatholic:17791`) are this church. The village post office and ZIP are Bloomfield 14469
(bulletin, Directory, OSM); "East Bloomfield" is the town and the locator's hamlet. They are
now linked to `st-bridgets`. GCatholic's double name reflects St. Joseph, West Bloomfield,
whose parish merged away (sacramental-records index: records at St. Benedict). No source in
this audit lists a building or Mass at West Bloomfield, and the bulletin names only two
churches, so no separate record was made. Likewise Our Lady of Lebanon (Canandaigua), listed
in the index, has no roster entry and is not in the bulletin.

**The locator's "Mission of Our Lady of Guadalupe (St. Gregory), Marion"** sits under St.
Benedict there but belongs to region D (St. Katharine Drexel; the index puts St. Gregory,
Marion at St. Katharine Drexel, Macedon). Not touched.
