## Publisher

Peace of Christ is a multi-site parish (St. Ambrose, St. James, St. John the Evangelist) with one weekly bulletin on Discover Mass. The bulletin is listed under two Discover Mass church pages, "Peace of Christ Parish at St. Ambrose" and "...at St. James", with the same issues and the same page-2 staff list ("AM- St. Ambrose, JA- St. James, JO- St. John, PO - Parish Office"). The issues of 27 September and 4 October 2026 are posted. Because the PDF's text layer is broken the Mass schedule can't be quoted from it; the diocesan directory gives it: Saturday St. James 4:30 pm; Sunday St. James 7:45 am, St. John 9:00 and 11:00 am, St. Ambrose 9:45 am and 5:00 pm; weekdays at all three, with a Friday 7:00 am Traditional Latin Mass at St. John.

## Worship sites

Three: `st-ambrose` (25 Empire Boulevard), `st-james-rochester` (130 Brett Road), `st-john-evangelist-humboldt` (553 Humboldt Street). The locator's parish-level entry "Peace of Christ Parish" (`directory:348-peace-of-christ-parish`) is linked to St. Ambrose. St. Ambrose Academy (31 Empire Blvd) is a school.

## Website and bulletin provider

`peaceofchristparish.org` redirects to `www.` and embeds both Discover Mass slugs. `provider_id` is the St. Ambrose slug (kept as in the live row); the St. James slug is the same bulletin and is recorded in `publication` so no other row can claim it. Fetch reads Discover Mass by `provider_id`, so either works.
