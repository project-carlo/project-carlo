## Publisher

All Saints Parish, Corning, publishes one weekly ParishesOnline bulletin (container 06/0144). The 3-4 October 2026 masthead is "ALL SAINTS PARISH, 158 State Street, Corning, NY 14830". One row, one church.

## Worship sites

One: `st-marys-corning`. The parish's own history page says the community is "back to one family and one church, St. Mary's". GCatholic's St. Vincent de Paul (222 Dodge Ave., Corning) and Immaculate Heart of Mary (115 E High St., Painted Post) are excluded as closed, with last Masses dated from the Catholic Courier (30 June 2018 and 7 September 2019): `excluded/st-vincent-de-paul-corning`, `excluded/immaculate-heart-of-mary-painted-post`. St. Patrick, Corning closed in 2001 (Courier, 23 September 2019) and is in no roster of mine. The current bulletins put every Mass "in the Gym" (the school campus at 158 State Street), a temporary arrangement; see the church record.

## Website and bulletin provider

allsaintsparish.org is live and links the ParishesOnline organization `all-saints-parish` (the live provider_id, correct); the three publication forms come from `probe po`. The PDFs have text layers.
