## Publisher

Our Lady of Peace Parish, Geneva (the 2020s merger of St. Francis de Sales and St. Stephen) publishes one bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 06/0090. The 4 October 2026 issue covers both churches and the chapel: page 5 lists Masses "(SS)" at St. Stephen, "(SF)" at St. Francis and "4:30 P.M. (St. John's Chapel)". Its own parish listing in the diocese directory names the same three sites. One row.

## Worship sites

St. Francis de Sales (130 Exchange St), St. Stephen (48 Pulteney St) and St. John's Chapel (630 South Main St, the Hobart and William Smith campus chapel, Sunday 4:30 pm except in summer). The Locator's St. John's Chapel is in no roster, but the bulletin and the directory both name it, so it is a church row (`st-johns-chapel`). The Locator's "Our Lady of Peace | Geneva" entry has no address and is the parish itself; it is linked to `st-francis-desales`, where the parish office is.
