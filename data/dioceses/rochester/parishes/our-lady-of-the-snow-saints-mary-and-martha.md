## Publisher

St. Mary's Parish publishes one weekly bulletin through ParishesOnline, container 06/0329, for three canonical parishes under one pastor: St. Mary's (Auburn), Ss. Mary & Martha (St. Francis of Assisi and St. Hyacinth) and Our Lady of the Snow (St. Joseph, Weedsport and St. Patrick, Cato). The 4 October 2026 page-1 masthead reads "The Catholic Parishes of St. Mary's, Ss. Mary & Martha, Our Lady of the Snow", and the Sunday Mass list on that page runs across all five churches. So **Our Lady of the Snow is in the same bulletin as Ss. Mary & Martha**: the lead's hunch that the Locator and ps.dor treat them as separate "Our Lady of the Snow" and "Ss. Mary and Martha" listings is only a listing split, and their old own sites are dead (marymarthaauburn.org is for sale) or stubs (ourladyofthesnow.org says "We have moved!" to stmaryauburn.org). One row. It is not the Sacred Heart cluster's bulletin (a different row).

## Worship sites

Five churches with Mass: St. Mary's (three Sunday Masses), St. Francis of Assisi and St. Hyacinth (Auburn), St. Joseph (Weedsport) and St. Patrick (Cato). **St. John, Port Byron** (the third Our Lady of the Snow church) closed in June 2020 (`excluded/st-john-port-byron`). The `st-ann` church (Owasco) the lead asked about belongs to the **Sacred Heart cluster** (the Locator also files it under "Holy Family"), not here. Ss. Peter and Paul, Auburn (136 Washington St) is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic church, not Rochester (`excluded/ss-peter-and-paul-ukrainian-auburn`).
