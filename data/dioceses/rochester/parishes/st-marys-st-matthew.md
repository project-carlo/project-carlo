## Publisher

One bulletin for the two-church cluster. The 27 September 2026 issue is headed "Sts. Mary &
Matthew Faith Community", gives both churches' addresses and offices, and its calendar lists
all Masses (Saturday 4:30 pm at St. Matthew; Sunday 8:30 am at St. Mary's and 10:30 am at St.
Matthew; weekday Masses alternating). The 4 October issue is image-only but has the same
masthead.

## Worship sites

Two: `st-marys-honeoye` (8961 West Main Street, Honeoye, Ontario County) and `st-matthew`
(6591 Richmond Mills Road, Livonia, Livingston County). The locator's `directory:339-st-matthew`
(address garbled "6591 Richmond Mills RdLivonia") is linked to `st-matthew`. The Directory of
Parishes lists both listings at the Livonia address, an error for St. Mary's. The
sacramental-records index shows St. Joseph (Livonia) and St. Margaret (Conesus Lake) records at
St. Matthew and St. Michael (Livonia Center) at St. Mary, Honeoye; those are ended parishes, and
neither bulletin nor roster lists a building or Mass for them.

## Website and bulletin provider

`stsmaryandmatthew.com` is a Wix site; its `/bulletin` page lists PDFs under `/_files/ugd/`,
so provider `other` with the home page as website.
