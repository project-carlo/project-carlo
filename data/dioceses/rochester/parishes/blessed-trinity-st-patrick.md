## Publisher

Blessed Trinity Parish (Waverly, Newark Valley, Apalachin) and St. Patrick Parish (Owego) publish **one**
bulletin, not two. The 4 October 2026 masthead (page 1, an image I rendered) reads "Blessed Trinity Parish &
Saint Patrick Parish", with one parish office (309 Front Street, Owego) and the four churches' addresses and
Mass times in one column. Page 2 is one Mass schedule for all four churches. The ParishesOnline footer
"Blessed Trinity & St. Patrick, Owego, NY 06-0156" names both. There is one website, one bulletin archive and
one container; the diocese lists them as a single cluster (Directory of Parishes: "Blessed Trinity a cluster
community"), although it has a second page, "St. Patrick's" (st-patrick-2), with the identical text.

## Worship sites

`churches/st-james-waverly.md`, `churches/st-john-evangelist-newark-valley.md`,
`churches/st-margaret-mary-apalachin.md`, `churches/st-patrick-owego.md`. The Parish Locator marks St. John and
St. Margaret Mary "No Mass"; the bulletin and the Directory of Parishes show Sunday and weekday Masses at both.
The sacramental-records index also lists St. Francis of Assisi (Catatonk) and St. Pius X (Van Etten) as having
joined Blessed Trinity, Owego, but no source here shows Mass at either now: neither is in the rosters or the
bulletin (not researched further).

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site `blessed-trinity-parish.org` (WordPress) links each Sunday's PDF (probe url finds the
2026-09-27 file on the homepage), so the provider stays `other`. The container id comes from the footer, and
`po-container:06/0156` has a 4 October issue. I found no ParishesOnline organization slug.
