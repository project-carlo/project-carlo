## Publisher

Good Shepherd Catholic Community (a 2006 merger) publishes one ParishesOnline bulletin, container 06/0198. The 4 October 2026 issue's page 1 shows the four church names and the parish office (299 Main Street, Aurora), and page 2 schedules Masses at all four. One row; the Locator groups the same four.

## Worship sites

St. Michael's (Union Springs, Sat 4:00 and Thu 4:00), St. Patrick's (Moravia, Sun 8:30), St. Patrick's (Aurora, Sun 9:00) and Our Lady of the Lake (King Ferry, Sun 10:30; a Friday 9:00 Mass from 9 October). The parish calls these "four historic worship sites". **St. Bernard, Scipio Center** is listed by the Locator (under "Individual") and GCatholic but by no parish page or Mass list: it is `open/st-bernard-scipio-center`. The Aurora church's live coordinates were Moravia's (23 km off) and are corrected in its record.
