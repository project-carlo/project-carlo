## Publisher

One ParishesOnline bulletin (container 06/0132, org `our-lady-of-lourdes-saint-anne-church`, LPi id 0018000000Qc0EGAAZ) serves the Our Lady of Lourdes and St. Anne cluster. In the 27 September 2026 issue the masthead is "Our Lady of Lourdes † Saint Anne" and the Mass intentions on page 2 are for Saint Anne (SA) and Our Lady of Lourdes (OLOL) only. Page 3 has a faith-formation block headed "Our Lady Queen of Peace, Our Lady of Lourdes, St. Anne & St. Thomas More" (shared classes at OLQP and St. Thomas More), but those two churches' Masses are not in this bulletin and they publish their own on eCatholic. So the two Brighton clusters are two rows.

## Worship sites

Two: `our-lady-of-lourdes` (150 Varinna Drive) and `st-anne` (1600 Mount Hope Avenue). The directory also lists "St. George Lithuanian Church" at 150 Varinna Drive: the same address as OLOL, with a Lithuanian Mass at "Our Lady of Lourdes Church, Sunday 12:30" in the directory text. It is the Lithuanian community that uses the OLOL building, not a separate building; the locator's "St. George R. C. Lithuanian" entry (`directory:35-st-george-r-c-lithuanian`, no address) is linked to the OLOL church record. Seton Catholic School (165 Rhinecliff Drive) is a school.
