## Why it is excluded

Closed 15 November 2021, by the same decree of relegation as Our Lady of Lourdes (sources above). The roster addresses disagree: GCatholic and the Locator say 556 St. Joseph Blvd, the Directory of Parishes' structured address says 161 High Street; the Courier only says the church is across from St. Joseph's Hospital. The exclusion keeps the GCatholic/Locator address. The 2026 bulletin lists only St. Mary and St. Patrick. GCatholic still lists it under "Blessed Sacrament Parish".

## What would change this

Nothing expected. If the parish center on the same block hosts a Mass, that would be a new place with its own record.
