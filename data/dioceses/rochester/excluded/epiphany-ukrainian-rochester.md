## Why it is excluded

**Church of the Epiphany (Ukrainian), 202 Carter St., Rochester: other jurisdiction.** GCatholic lists it under the Ukrainian Catholic Eparchy of Stamford ("Ukrainian Diocese of Stamford", rite Ukrainian (Byzantine)), within the territory of the Roman diocese. As with the Eastern churches in other dioceses here (see `syracuse/excluded/holy-spirit-byzantine-binghamton.md`), this is a scope decision, not a judgement about the church.
