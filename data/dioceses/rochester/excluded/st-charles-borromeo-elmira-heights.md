## Why it is excluded

Closed in early 2020. The Catholic Courier's November 2021 article lists St. Charles Borromeo, Elmira Heights, among the Elmira churches that have closed ("in early 2020"). In 2018 it had already lost its weekend Mass. No last-Mass date; `last_mass` is blank. The name is the Elmira Heights parish, not St. Charles Borromeo in Greece (another region). The 2026 parish site and bulletin do not list it, and the Locator's own entry for it says "no mass".
