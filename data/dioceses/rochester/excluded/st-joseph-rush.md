## Why it is excluded

**St. Joseph, Rush: closed; last Mass (outdoors) 27 April 2024.** The parish's history page says the church could no longer be used for Mass from fall 2023 for structural reasons, that it was decommissioned on 16 January 2024, and that the last Mass was celebrated outside on the grounds on Saturday 27 April 2024. The parish's Mass page (2 October 2026) says "ALL MASSES ARE HELD AT GUARDIAN ANGELS", and the 27 September 2026 bulletin lists no Rush Mass. The diocese's directory (ps.dor.org, 1/27/25) wrongly says "All Masses will be held at our St. Joseph Worship Site"; the parish's own newer page contradicts it. The Parish Locator's listing "Mass: Sun, Tue, Thu: 8am" is stale. The sacramental-records index lists St. Joseph, Rush with records at St. Marianne Cope.

**Address disagreement** (moot, but recorded): GCatholic 1209 Rush Lima Rd.; the Locator and the directory 6105 Rush Lima Rd. Not resolved; neither matters for a closed church.

## What would change this

If the church is repaired and reopened for Mass the parish would say so on its Mass page.
