## Why it is excluded

**Carmelite Monastery of Rochester, 1931 West Jefferson Road, Pittsford: open with public Mass, but no bulletin.** The monastery's own site (Contact page, 2 October 2026) says "You are welcomed to join us for Mass: Monday-Friday at 7:30AM, Saturday-Sunday at 8:00AM", and the locator carries the same times ("Sun: & Sat: 8am | Mon-Fri 7:30am"). So this is a public worship site, answering the lead. It is not a parish and publishes no weekly bulletin: the site (Squarespace) has "Schedule a Mass" intention pages, a prayer-request form and vocation pages, but nothing that lists Masses by week, and no bulletin PDF or provider id turned up. Filed as `no-bulletin`, not `closed` or `no-public-mass`.

GCatholic lists it as "Our Lady and St. Joseph" (a Carmelite Monastery, "1930; moved 1956") at the same address, with a Plus Code on the building (43.0870625, -77.5661875); that entry and the locator's "Carmelite Monastery" (Pittsford, no street) are the two roster entries it covers.

## What would change this

A weekly bulletin or Mass schedule page the pipeline could read would turn this into a church (it would need a parish row for the monastery). Revisit if the site gains one.
