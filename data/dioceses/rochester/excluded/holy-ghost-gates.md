## Why it is excluded

**Holy Ghost, 250 Coldwater Rd, Gates: closed 30 June 2020.** WHAM reported that the Parish of the Holy Family announced the closing of St. Helen and Holy Ghost, "consolidate the ministry and close the two locations, while keeping St. Jude Church", and "The two locations will close on June 30, 2020" (see the dated URLs above). I have not found a source naming the last Mass itself, so `last_mass` is the announced closing date, 30 June 2020. The Parish Locator's own row still lists the church under The Parish of the Holy Family but with "Mass: closed". Nothing current lists a Mass here: the parish's 27 September 2026 bulletin and its homepage Mass times, and the diocese's directory, all use 4100 Lyell Road only. GCatholic (17805) still lists the church with the old cluster website `gatescatholiccommunity.org`, which no longer resolves. The parish website has a "Holy Ghost Cemetery" page; a cemetery is not a Mass site.

The parish's absence from the current schedule alone would be weak evidence; the WHAM stories are what state the closure.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. If the cemetery chapel were ever found to hold public Mass, it would need its own record.
