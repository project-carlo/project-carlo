## Why it is excluded

**Catholic Newman Community, University of Rochester: public Mass, no bulletin the pipeline can read (`no-bulletin`).** The community holds Mass on campus every week of the academic year (Sundays 10:30 am and 7 pm; weekdays Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Wednesday night), at the Mack Catholic Center, 610 Faculty Rd (the new Catholic Center and Carpenter Family Catholic Chapel); all three rosters (GCatholic, the locator and the diocesan directory) still place it at the Interfaith Chapel on Wilson Blvd., which the site no longer uses. It publishes its schedule as page text; the "Bulletin" page's newest PDF is from February 2025. Campus chapels count as worship sites, so this is not `no-public-mass`; it is `no-bulletin`.

## What would change this

A current weekly bulletin or schedule document the pipeline could read (the site's Bulletin page used to post weekly PDFs).
