## Why it is excluded

**Our Lady of the Lakes Parish Center, 210 Keuka Street, Penn Yan: parish office, no public Mass.** The diocese's Directory of Parishes gives this address as an entry of the Our Lady of the Lakes listing ("Parish Center, 210 Keuka Street"), alongside the three churches. The 4 October 2026 bulletin names it as the parish office and lists no Mass there; the week's schedule has Masses only at St. Januarius (Naples), St. Michael (Penn Yan, 312 Liberty St) and St. Patrick (Prattsburgh). The roster entry exists only because the directory makes one entry per address.

Nothing expected.
