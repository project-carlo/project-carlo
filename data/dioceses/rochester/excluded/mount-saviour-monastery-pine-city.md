## Why it is excluded

Public Mass, no bulletin. The monastery celebrates Mass daily (9:00 AM, 7:00 on Mondays) and invites the public to it (Discover Mass listing). It publishes nothing the pipeline could read: its site has monthly news letters, Discover Mass has no issues, and the Parish Locator says "Mass: Sun: 9am". So this is `no-bulletin`, not closed. Not a parish; the Locator lists it as "Individual".

## What would change this

A bulletin or a Mass-times page in a format the pipeline can read (the Discover Mass listing could be used for Mass times if the data owner wants monasteries without bulletins).
