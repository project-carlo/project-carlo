## Why it is excluded

**St. Joachim, West Main Street, Canisteo: closed 2005 (last Mass not dated).** The Catholic Courier of 17 April 2023 says plainly: "However, St. Joachim closed in 2005", that the building later housed Steuben County Rural Ministry until 2019, and that its statue was moved to St. Ann, Hornell in 2022 because the property was not in use. Our Lady of the Valley's own site and its 27 September and 4 October 2026 bulletins schedule Masses only at St. Ann and St. Mary. GCatholic lists it as "Mission of: Church of St. Ann" with only "Canisteo, New York 14823" as its address.
