## Why it is excluded

An adoration chapel, not a place of public Mass. This was a live church row (parish most-holy-name-of-jesus). The evidence is absence, so I list what I checked: the 4 October 2026 bulletin (every Mass and its place), the parish homepage Mass schedule (Saturday 4:00 PM St. Mary; Sunday 7:30 AM and 10:30 AM St. Patrick, 9:00 AM St. Mary; weekdays at those two churches) and the Directory of Parishes (same schedule; the chapel is called "Perpetual Adoration Chapel"). None lists a Mass at 1012 Davis St. The bulletin does not say "no Mass is celebrated there", so this is an inference from a complete schedule.

## What would change this

A Mass listed at 1012 Davis St. (for example on the parish's Mass Times page), or a bulletin saying the chapel holds Mass. Revisit with the next diocesan Directory of Parishes update.
