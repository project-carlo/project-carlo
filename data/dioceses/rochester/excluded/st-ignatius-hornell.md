## Why it is excluded

**St. Ignatius Loyola, 343 Canisteo Street, Hornell: closed (parish ended 2007; last Mass not dated).** No source I found gives a last-Mass date. The evidence that it holds no Mass now is direct and current: Our Lady of the Valley's own About page says the parish "includes the churches of St. Ann and St. Mary's" and that Masses are celebrated at those two; its Mass-times page and the 27 September and 4 October 2026 bulletins list every Mass at St. Ann or St. Mary; the Catholic Courier (2023) calls it "the former St. Ignatius Loyola". This is stronger than absence alone, but no source says in so many words "the building is shut". GCatholic says only "Mission of: Church of St. Ann" and the diocese's sacramental index says the corporation ended.

The GCatholic address (343 Canisteo St.) and the third-party site's (W Pine St & Grand St) differ; neither matters while there is no Mass.

## What would change this

A bulletin that schedules Mass there. Revisit if the OLV schedule changes.
