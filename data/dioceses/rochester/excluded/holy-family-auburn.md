## Why it is excluded

**Holy Family, 85 North St, Auburn: closed, last Mass 2024-06-16.** The Catholic Courier of 17 June 2024 reports the pastor's letter: "the Holy Family campus will be closed as of Sunday, June 16, 2024" (church, rectory and school, for roof, window and mold damage; repairs estimated at $2.5 million). Parishioners were sent to St. Alphonsus, Sacred Heart and St. Ann, the other three churches of the cluster. The diocese's own directory carries the listing "Holy Family - CLOSED" with "HOLY FAMILY, AUBURN CLOSED JUNE 2024". The Citizen's report (auburnpub.com, paywalled, not quoted) is headed "Holy Family celebrates final Mass" and shows "the final morning Mass at Holy Family Church ... on Sunday", consistent with 16 June; a search snippet gave 23 June, which I could not read, so the date follows the Courier.

The current evidence agrees: the 27 September 2026 Sacred Heart bulletin and the cluster's Mass-times page (both read 2 Oct 2026) list Masses at Sacred Heart, St. Alphonsus and St. Ann and none at Holy Family, though the bulletin masthead and church list still print the Holy Family name and address. The Locator (Sat 4pm, Sun 9am) and GCatholic (17767) still list it as open; both lag.

## What would change this

A reopening or a Mass at the building. The group "H.O.P.E." has sought landmark status for the closed building (auburnpub.com); that does not change the Mass status. Nothing expected.

The live row had this name, address 85 North St. (bulletin, cluster site) against GCatholic's 5 North St., and coordinates from the geocode stage (29 m from GCatholic's Plus Code). It was in the cluster `sacred-heart-auburn-cluster`; promote will retire the live row.
