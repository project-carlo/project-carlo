## Why it is excluded

**St. John, 36 Rochester St, Port Byron: closed June 2020 (day not stated).** The parish's history page says "With the closing of St. John's in Port Byron in June 2020, Our Lady of the Snow Parish now consists of St. Joseph Church in Weedsport and St. Patrick Church in Cato." The 4 October 2026 bulletin (container 06/0329) lists Sunday Masses at St. Patrick (Cato) and St. Joseph (Weedsport) only, and the diocese's directory lists the same two. The Locator (Sat 5pm), GCatholic and the directory's address block still carry the Port Byron address. The sacramental-records index sends St. John's records to Our Lady of the Snow, Weedsport. `last_mass` is left empty because no source gives the day.
