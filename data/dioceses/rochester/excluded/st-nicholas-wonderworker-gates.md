## Why it is excluded

**St. Nicholas the Wonderworker, 1492 Spencerport Road (Gates, mailing city Rochester): Melkite Greek Catholic, other jurisdiction.** GCatholic lists it as "(Greek-Melkite)" under the Greek-Melkite Diocese (Eparchy) of Newton, inside the territory of the Diocese of Rochester. Eastern Catholic churches are outside the Latin diocese's parishes and bulletins, and the diocese's own Parish Locator lists it only as an "Individual" entry (Sunday 10:00am). Its own website was down when I tried (HTTP 500), so I did not read a bulletin; the Eparchy placement is GCatholic's.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. The project's other-jurisdiction policy for Eastern Catholic churches applies.
