## Why it is excluded

**St. Mary, Rushville: closed 1 January 2011.** The Our Lady of the Lakes history page lists the sites closed after the six parishes merged in 2010: "St. Andrew's in Dundee was the first to close on 6/28/2009. ... St. Mary's, Rushville closed on 1/1/2011. ... Closed on 6/30/2019: St. Theresa's, Stanley." The page gives a closing date, not a last-Mass date; `last_mass` holds that date. The current bulletin (4 Oct 2026) lists Masses at St. Patrick (Prattsburgh), St. Michael (Penn Yan) and St. Januarius (Naples) only. GCatholic still lists the church (no address); the Locator does not.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. St. Andrew, Dundee (closed 28 June 2009 per the same page) is in no roster, so it has no record.
