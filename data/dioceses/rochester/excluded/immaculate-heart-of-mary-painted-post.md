## Why it is excluded

**Immaculate Heart of Mary, 115 E. High Street, Painted Post: closed, last Mass 2019-09-07.** The Catholic Courier of 23 September 2019 reports the final liturgy on 7 September 2019 and says "The lone remaining worship site for All Saints is St. Mary Church, 155 State St., Corning." Sunday Masses had stopped there before 2009 and daily Masses ceased in late August 2019.

Seen again on 2 October 2026: the All Saints history page says the community is "back to one family and one church, St. Mary's", and the 3-4 October 2026 bulletin schedules every Mass at St. Mary. GCatholic and the diocese's sacramental-records index (which lists the parish as merged into All Saints, Corning) do not state the closure; the Courier does.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
