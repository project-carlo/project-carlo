## Why it is excluded

**Holy Name of Jesus, 15 Saint Martin's Way, Greece: closed, last regular Mass 21 September 2014.** Three things say so. (1) The diocese's own sacramental-records index shows Holy Name's records at St. Charles Borromeo, Greece. That alone says a corporation ended, not that a building shut, so it is a lead only. (2) The "Churches of Rochester" site, which cites Bp. Matano's decree of 23 June 2014 amalgamating the parish into St. Charles, gives the final regularly scheduled Mass as 21 September 2014 and says the church was kept for funerals, weddings and special occasions until sold; that is a third-party summary, not a diocesan source. (3) Nothing current lists Mass at this address: the Roc Catholics Northwest site lists seven parishes without Holy Name; St. Charles's own 27 September 2026 bulletin lists Masses at 3003 Dewey Avenue only; the diocese directory (1/27/25) has no entry; the domain `holyname-greece.org` that GCatholic (17822) lists is dead. GCatholic (17822) lists the church with no closure note.

## What would change this

If the church is still used for occasional public Mass (the third-party page says special occasions only), it would be a worship site; the St. Charles parish office could say. Nothing currently suggests it. I did not find a sale record.
