## Why it is excluded

**St. Alban's (Ordinariate), meeting at St. Cecilia, Irondequoit: other jurisdiction.** GCatholic files it as a fellowship of the Personal Ordinariate of the Chair of Saint Peter, not of the Diocese of Rochester (it lists the community in Henrietta, using the Church of the Good Shepherd building, an older address). The diocese's own directory and the locator place its Sunday 11:00 am Mass in the St. Cecilia church building, 2732 Culver Road, which is already a church record of the Kateri parish (`churches/st-cecilia.md`). So the building is in the data; the community is a different jurisdiction's. Scope decision, like the Eastern churches.

## What would change this

A project decision to cover the Ordinariate.
