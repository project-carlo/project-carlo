## Why it is excluded

**St. Vincent de Paul, 222 Dodge Avenue, Corning: closed, last Mass 2018-06-30.** The Catholic Courier of 1 August 2018 reports that a ceiling board fell during the 5:30 pm Saturday Mass on 30 June 2018, and that the parochial administrator, after a diocesan inspection on 1 July, decided "to permanently discontinue Masses at St. Vincent de Paul effective immediately" (his 5 July letter to parishioners). The church had already been slated for closure. All weekend Masses moved to St. Mary, Corning.

Today (2 October 2026) All Saints' own history page says the community is "back to one family and one church, St. Mary's", and the 3-4 October 2026 bulletin lists every Mass at St. Mary. GCatholic (page last updated 2025-12-21) still lists St. Vincent de Paul under All Saints Parish with no status, and the diocese's sacramental-records index lists the parish as merged into All Saints. Neither of those states a closure of the building; the Courier article is the source that does.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. The 2018 article says Providence Housing Development was to redevelop the property as apartments and a community centre.
