## Why it is excluded

Closed 15 November 2021. The bishop's decree of relegation took effect that day (WETM, 25 Nov 2021: "The decree goes into effect November 15, 2021"; Catholic Courier: "closed Nov. 15"). Relegation means the church "is no longer to be used for worship" (canon 1222, as the Courier explains it); the Courier says the church and former convent were being put up for sale. Both the parish notice and the Courier were produced for that announcement, so this is a stated closure, not an absence. No last-Mass date was found, so `last_mass` is blank. The parish had used it "only sparingly" since March 2020. GCatholic (listed under the old "Christ the Redeemer Parish"), the Locator and the Directory of Parishes still list it; the 2026 bulletin and parish site do not.

## What would change this

Nothing expected. Not to be confused with the old parish; the building is for sale.
