## Why it is excluded

**Holy Redeemer, Rochester 14621 (no street address): closed in 1985.** GCatholic's page for the building (a "Former Church", dedicated 1867) says "closed 1985; now Northside Church Of Christ", i.e. the building is a Protestant church now. The diocesan directory says that of the former churches that formed Cabrini Parish (Holy Redeemer among them) "only Annunciation, Corpus Christi and Saint Michael's remain as Catholic Churches". Cabrini's own Mass schedule (FAQ page, 2 October 2026) lists only those three. `last_mass` is left empty: the only date, 1985, is a year.
