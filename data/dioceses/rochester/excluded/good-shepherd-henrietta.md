## Why it is excluded

**Good Shepherd, 3318 East Henrietta Road, Henrietta: closed, last Masses 19-20 October 2019.** The St. Marianne Cope parish's own history page (quoted above) says the campus was sold to the Rush-Henrietta Central School District in 2019, the last Masses were celebrated the weekend of 19-20 October 2019 (the same page also says a last Mass on Monday 21 October), and the church became a town performing-arts hall in 2024. Today the parish site says "ALL MASSES ARE HELD AT GUARDIAN ANGELS". The diocese's Parish Locator lists the entry with "Mass: Closed"; the sacramental-records index lists Good Shepherd, Henrietta, with records at St. Marianne Cope. The diocese's directory (ps.dor.org, 1/27/25) still gives this address as the parish's office ("St. Marianne Cope Roman Catholic Parish 3318 E. Henrietta Rd"); it is stale, and the 27 September 2026 bulletin prints 2061 East Henrietta Rd as the parish address. That directory entry (`parish-directory:st-marianne-cope-1`) is linked here.

## What would change this

Nothing expected.
