## Why it is excluded

**Corpus Christi School, 546 Oxford St, Rochester: a school, not a worship site.** The old locator lists a "Corpus Christi School" under Cabrini Parish and, in place of a Mass schedule, the pointer "Mass: (See Our Lady of the Americas)". Corpus Christi's church is at 864 East Main Street (`churches/corpus-christi.md`, which GCatholic and the locator call Our Lady of the Americas). Cabrini's current Mass schedule (parish site and diocesan directory) lists no Mass on Oxford Street. The evidence is the locator's own pointer plus absence in those two lists; the street is also not close to the church (546 Oxford Street is the street of Blessed Sacrament, 534 Oxford, in the southeast).

## What would change this

A source showing Mass at 546 Oxford Street.
