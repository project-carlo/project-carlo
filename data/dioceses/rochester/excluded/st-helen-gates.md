## Why it is excluded

**St. Helen, 310 Hinchey Rd, Gates: closed at the end of June 2020.** WHAM reported the Parish of the Holy Family's decision "to consolidate the ministry and close the two locations, while keeping St. Jude Church", with closing on 30 June 2020. The Locator lists the church with "Mass: closed". The Vietnamese community that used St. Helen's now has its Masses at 4100 Lyell Road (the Locator's St. Jude entry lists the Vietnamese Masses; the 27 September 2026 bulletin lists "Vietnamese Mass" times at the one address). `last_mass` is the announced closing date, 30 June 2020 (no source I read names the last Mass itself). GCatholic (17808) still lists it with the dead `gatescatholiccommunity.org`.
