## What is known

Jesus the Lord Church, 118 Broad Street, Keyport, was a parish merged with St. Joseph in the 2017 plan (in effect 2019) to form Our Lady of Fatima Parish. The merged parish's bulletin cover (five issues, 27 September to 1 November 2026) and homepage still name it as one of the two churches, and its building exists (OSM way 157112758, a place of worship at 118 Broad Street). The bulletin lists a prayer group at "Iglesia Jesús el Señor" and a Cursillo at its parish house. Nothing read says Mass is, or is not, celebrated there: the parish's Mass schedules name no church. GCatholic's 123 Broad St. is the same building under another number.

## Why it is not decided

A merger ends a parish, not a church (Rules settled), and no source says this one closed, so it can't be excluded as closed. It can't be a church row either: no source reads as public Mass there now. The prayer-group listing places a congregation in the building but isn't Mass. The step that would settle it is a per-church Mass schedule from the parish (see `revisit`).
