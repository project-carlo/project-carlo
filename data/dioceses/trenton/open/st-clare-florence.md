## What is known

St. Clare was the Florence-area church of St. Clare Parish, which merged in 2008 with Holy Assumption Parish, Roebling as Sts. Francis and Clare Parish (the Monitor obituary of Fr. Felix Venza, 2025-05-08). That parish merged with St. Mary, Bordentown in 2018 into Mary, Mother of the Church (the Monitor, 2025-07-11, which says the parish 'has two worship sites – Holy Assumption, Roebling, and St. Mary Church, Bordentown'). GCatholic still lists St. Clare (242 E Front St, Florence; the aggregators say 232) under the old parish name. The parish's 1 November 2026 bulletin and its homepage list Mass at St. Mary and Holy Assumption only. OSM shows only unnamed houses at both numbers.

## Why it is not decided

Every source I found is the absence of St. Clare from a schedule, a worship-site sentence that leaves it out, or a stale directory. None says the building closed, was sold or was reused, and nothing says when. A closed exclusion needs such a source, and a church row needs a Mass held there. The Monitor's REST search (St. Clare Florence; Sts. Francis and Clare; Florence Township St. Clare church sold) and two web searches found nothing on the building. The parish office, a 2008-2018 bulletin or the county deed would settle it; until then it stays off both lists. The address also needs a check (242 against 232 E Front St).
