## What is known

GCatholic still lists Our Lady of Peace, Normandy Beach under St. Pio of Pietrelcina Parish, and The Monitor says its parish merged with St. Bonaventure in 2008. Nothing the parish now publishes places a Mass there: the August 2026 and November 2026 bulletins' schedules, and the parish's own Mass page, list only the church and chapel in Lavallette and Sacred Heart in Bay Head. That is strong evidence it holds no public Mass now, but no source says it closed, was sold or was relegated, and an exclusion needs one (README, *The model*), so it stays here.
