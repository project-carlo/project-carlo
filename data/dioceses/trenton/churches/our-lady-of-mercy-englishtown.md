## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Thomas More's 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 2) gives Sunday 7:30am and daily Mass Monday to Friday 8:30am, with First Friday Benediction after it. This church is not a parish of its own on the diocese's finder or in the July 2024 cohort list; it is a second church of the Parish of St. Thomas More and shares that parish's bulletin.

## Name and address

The bulletin and site agree with GCatholic on 58 Main St, Englishtown, NJ 07726 and the name Our Lady of Mercy, so both flags are true (the bulletin and parish site count as one source, GCatholic the other). The finder has no pin for it. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code.
