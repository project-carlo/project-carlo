## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October and 1 November 2026 bulletins (page 2) list Masses on Saturday at 5:00 p.m., Sunday at 7:30, 9:00 and 11:00 a.m., and weekdays at 8:30 a.m.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the finder, GCatholic and OpenStreetMap agree on 406 Forman Avenue, Point Pleasant Beach, NJ 08742. The bulletin says 'Saint Peter Catholic Church'; the finder says 'St. Peter Parish'. GCatholic's website saintpetersonline.org is the older domain; the finder's is stpetersbytheshore.org. Coordinates follow GCatholic's Plus Code, with the finder's pin about 20 m and OpenStreetMap's house point about 8 m away.
