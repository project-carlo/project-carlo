## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Michael Church is a worship site now and is scheduled in the St. Ann bulletin. The 1 November 2026 issue (page 4) lists, under "St. Michael's Church", Sunday 8:30 AM (English), 10:00 AM (Slovak) and 12:30 PM (Korean), Thursday 10:00 AM (Korean) and Friday 8:00 PM (Korean). The Monitor's 19 October 2026 story on the St. Jude novena says the same and puts it at 1130 Brunswick Ave. The church was St. Michael Parish (the novena began there in 1939); it is now "part of St. Ann Parish, Lawrenceville".

## Where the Korean community worships

The Church of the Korean Martyrs is a Korean-language community with its own pastor (Father Dae Hyeok Son, The Monitor 2025-05-19) that worships in this building: the finder's "Korean Martyrs Parish" pin and GCatholic's "Korean Martyrs" and "St. Michael" entries all sit at 1130 Brunswick Ave, with the same Plus Code. It is one worship site, so the three roster entries are linked to this one church. The July 2024 cohort list shows it in parentheses under St. Ann, Lawrenceville (Cohort 7). Its three Korean Masses are the Sunday 12:30 PM, Thursday 10:00 AM and Friday 8:00 PM ones in the St. Ann bulletin.

## What the community publishes

The community's own site, princetonsd.org, has a Korean-language weekly bulletin board. Each issue is a post linking one Google Drive file, so no folder or page recipe collects them; the file read (27 September 2026) is a two-page Korean PDF. Given that, and that the St. Ann bulletin already lists every Mass here, this is a church under the St. Ann row rather than a parish row. See `research`.

## Name and address

The Monitor (1130 Brunswick Ave.), GCatholic and the diocese's finder agree on the address, so address_verified is true. The name follows the bulletin and The Monitor ("St. Michael Church"); the Korean community's name, the Church of the Korean Martyrs, is in the title. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G767R3+C6; the finder's pin (40.24089, -74.74677) is about 25 m away. GCatholic's website for both entries, stmichaelstrenton.com, has no DNS.
