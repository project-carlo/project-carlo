## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 Our Lady of Hope bulletin (page 2) lists St. Jerome's Saturday 4 PM vigil, Sunday 7:30 AM and 11 AM and Thursday-Saturday 8:30 AM Masses, and page 3 gives its intentions for 31 October-8 November. The 2017 decision's merger with St. Mary, Deal ended the parish, not this church (see `our-lady-of-hope-west-long-branch`).

## Name and address

Sources disagree on the number: the bulletin's schedule page says the church is at 250 Wall Street; the parish's masthead address, the finder (directory:14613) and GCatholic say 254 Wall Street (the parish office, apparently next door). Followed the bulletin's 250 for the church and recorded 254 as the office; `address_verified` stays false until the two numbers are matched (OSM has no house number for the church). The name is St. Jerome Church in the bulletin, GCatholic and OSM, so `name_verified` is true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, 40 m from OSM's node.
