## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 2, text layer) lists Mass daily at 8:15am (Monday-Saturday), Saturday 5:30pm and Sunday 8:30am, 10:00am, 11:30am, 5:00pm and 1:00pm en español, plus a 12:10pm weekday Mass. It is the parish's only church.

## Name and address

The parish prints 216 Nassau Street (both the 25 October and 1 November covers); the finder and GCatholic (which agree, probably one copying the other) say 214 Nassau Street, and OSM puts the church at 216 and a plain house at 214. The row follows 216 (the parish's own address, matched by OSM's place of worship), so `address_verified` is false: the street number is a disagreement between the parish and OSM on one side and the directory and GCatholic on the other, and the same building is meant (all within 50 m). Name: 'St. Paul' in the finder and GCatholic, 'St. Paul's Catholic Church' in OSM, 'St. Paul Parish' in the bulletin; `name_verified` true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code point (40.35156, -74.65419), 40 m from OSM's node and 20 m from the finder's pin.
