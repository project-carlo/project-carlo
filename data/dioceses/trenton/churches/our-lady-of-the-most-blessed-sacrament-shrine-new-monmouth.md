## Holds Mass

Yes, on Saturday mornings. The 25 October 2026 bulletin's Mass Schedule (page 4) lists, after the church's Masses, 'Our Lady of the Most Blessed Sacrament Shrine Saturday 8:15 a.m.', and the Mass intentions page prints 'Shrine:' intentions beside 'Church:'. The parish site calls it the Diocesan Adoration Shrine, with Eucharistic adoration around the clock since May 1997; the Saturday 8:15 Mass is the only Mass the bulletin assigns to it. Its Mass is a public one in the parish schedule; the building is a chapel rather than a parish church.
