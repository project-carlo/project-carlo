## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) lists weekday Masses at 6:45 and 9:00 am, Saturday vigil 5:00 pm and Sunday 7:00, 9:30, 11:30 am and 5:00 pm, with Monday-evening adoration 'in the church'. The school and rectory share the campus; no other building is named for Mass.
