## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, read from a render) lists daily Mass Monday through Friday 8:30am, Saturday 5:00pm and Sunday 7:00am, 8:30am and 10:30am, and says the church is open for daily prayer Monday through Friday 8:00am to 4:00pm. It is the parish's only church.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish site's footer, GCatholic (18729) and the diocese's finder (14638) agree on 935 Bennetts Mills Road, Jackson, NJ 08527 (GCatholic and the finder abbreviate 'Rd'). The name follows the bulletin masthead, 'Saint Aloysius Church'; the finder says 'St. Aloysius Parish', GCatholic 'St. Aloysius'. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G74PJG+9X (40.13094, -74.27256); the finder's pin (40.13080, -74.27154) is about 90 m away, on the same campus. Both flags are true: the parish's own bulletin and site, GCatholic and the diocese's finder are independent sources that agree.
