Yes. The 25 October and 1 November 2026 bulletins list the parish's Masses at 555 Yardville Allentown Road: Saturday vigil 4:00 PM, Sunday 7:30, 9:30, 11:30 AM and 4:00 PM, weekdays 9:00 AM (Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday) and 4:00 PM Wednesday.

The street is 'Yardville Allentown Road' in the bulletin and 'Allentown Rd' in GCatholic and the finder, same number 555. The bulletin's town is Yardville, a section of Hamilton Township; the rosters say Hamilton, which I followed for the city. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, matching OSM's house point to about 10 m (the finder's pin is about 150 m off).
