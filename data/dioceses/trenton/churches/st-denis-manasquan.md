## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) and the parish's own Mass page give Saturday 4:30 PM, Sunday 8:00 and 9:30 AM, and weekday Masses at 7:30 AM (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday) and Saturday 8:30 AM.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish site, the finder and GCatholic agree on 90 Union Avenue, Manasquan, NJ 08736. Coordinates follow GCatholic's Plus Code; the finder's pin is about 30 m and OpenStreetMap's house point about 85 m from it, all on the same block. The parish styles itself "Church of St. Denis" and "St. Denis Church" (bulletin); the finder says "St. Denis Parish".
