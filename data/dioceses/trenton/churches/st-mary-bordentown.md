## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin schedules Saturday Vigil 5:30 PM, Sunday 9:45 AM and 11:30 AM, and weekday Mass Monday to Friday 12:30 PM at St. Mary, Bordentown.

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic and the diocese finder agree on the name and on 45 Crosswicks Street, Bordentown 08505 (the finder lists it as the parish's seat). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; the finder's pin is about 70 m away. The 'Chapel of Saints' the bulletin mentions is entered through this church's side door and has no separate address, so it is part of this church, not a second row. GCatholic's website for it, stmarysbordentown.org, is not the parish's (mmotcp.org). The merger of St. Mary Parish with Sts. Francis and Clare Parish into Mary, Mother of the Church (2018) ended the parish, not the church.
