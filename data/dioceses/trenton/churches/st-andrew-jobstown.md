## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, rendered) lists the St. Andrew Worship Site, 2489 Monmouth Road, Jobstown, with Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 9:30 am; the parish homepage gives the same, plus Saturday 3:30 pm Reconciliation.

## Name and address

The bulletin and the parish site (one source) and GCatholic agree on 2489 Monmouth Road, Jobstown (GCatholic adds ZIP+4 08041-2014). The diocese's finder has no pin here: its single pin for the merged parish is the office at 76 Evergreen Road. OSM has only a house node at the number, about 20 m from GCatholic's Plus Code point; coordinates are the Plus Code's. The church was St. Andrew's Parish until the merger into St. Isidore the Farmer (2017 decisions, Cohort 4, item 2). Both flags are true: bulletin or site, GCatholic and OSM agree.
