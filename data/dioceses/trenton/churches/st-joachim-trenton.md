Yes: the parish's 25 October 2026 bulletin and its site list the weekday, Saturday 4:00 PM and Sunday 11:00 AM Masses at St. Joachim.

## Name and address

The site and OSM give 20 Butler Street; GCatholic gives '20-56 Butler St.', the same block (OSM's 20 Butler is the church; the range is the church-and-school complex). I used 20 Butler. `address_verified` stays false only for that numbering difference. The finder's pin for the parish (19 Bayard St, 40.21341, -74.75297) is the parish office, 21-23 Bayard Street by the site, about 50 m north of the church: I linked directory:14578 here because it is the parish seat beside this church, with no church of its own at that address.
