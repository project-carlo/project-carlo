## Holds Mass

Yes, in two rooms on one campus. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives the Church (Sunday 7:00 am and 9:30 am, weekdays 8:30 am, Sunday 5:00 pm Vietnamese) and the Chapel (Saturday vigil 4:00 pm and 7:00 pm Spanish, Sunday 11:00 am, 12:30 pm and Spanish 7:00 am and 9:00 am, Wednesday and Friday 7:00 pm Spanish).

## Name and address

Bulletin, GCatholic and the diocese's finder agree on 16 McLean St, Freehold, NJ 07728, so both flags are true. The Chapel is described only as between the back of the church and the rectory, with no separate address, so it is not a second row. The "Old Church" on the lawn (bulletin page 3) is not in any Mass schedule. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; the finder's (40.25679, -74.27466) are about 50 m away.
