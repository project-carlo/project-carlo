## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, read from a render) lists Weekend Mass Saturday Vigil 5:30pm and Sunday 7:30am, 9:00am, 10:30am and 12:00pm, and Daily Mass Mon-Fri 7:15am and Mon-Sat 9:00am (Fridays at School). It is the parish's only church.
