## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday vigil 5:00 pm, Sunday 10:30 am and Tuesday-Friday 7:00 pm, all in Spanish; the parish site lists the same, with Eucharistic adoration Monday-Saturday 5:00-6:50 pm.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish site's schedule, the finder's merged-parish pin and GCatholic agree on St. Anthony Claret at 780 Ocean Avenue, Lakewood 08701. The parish (St. Anthony Claret, Divine Word Missionaries) merged with St. Mary of the Lake on 1 July 2020. Coordinates follow GCatholic's Plus Code, within about 40 m of one OpenStreetMap point.
