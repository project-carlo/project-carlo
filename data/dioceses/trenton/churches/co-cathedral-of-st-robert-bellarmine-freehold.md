## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 2) gives Saturday 4:30PM and Sunday 8AM, 10AM and 12PM, and daily Mass Monday to Friday 7AM and 9AM and Saturday 9AM; the parish homepage lists the same.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the parish site and the diocese's finder agree on 61 Georgia Road, Freehold, NJ 07728, so both flags are true. The name follows the bulletin and site ("Co-Cathedral of St. Robert Bellarmine"); the finder styles it "St. Robert Bellarmine Co-Cathedral". GCatholic omits this church, so it has no Plus Code and the coordinates are the finder's (40.21476, -74.29253). An OpenStreetMap lookup (Nominatim, 2026-10-30, node 1028660800 'Saint Robert Bellarmine Roman Catholic Church, 61 Georgia Road, Freehold') gives 40.21515, -74.29286, about 45 m away, so the finder's pin is on the right site.
