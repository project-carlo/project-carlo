## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin schedules Sunday 10:30 AM and daily 8:00 AM at St. Paul; the 25 October issue has Sunday 10:30 AM here too (with daily Mass at All Saints that week).

## Name and address

The name agrees across the bulletin, GCatholic and the site. The number does not: the bulletin and the finder print 223 E. Union Street (also the parish office's number), GCatholic 226, and the parish site's 2024 note 233. OSM's 223 and 226 house points are 20 m apart and neither names a church, so I could not tell which is the church door. I used the bulletin's 223 and GCatholic's Plus Code (40.07969, -74.85394, nearest OSM's 226 point), and left `address_verified` false. A visit or the county parcel record would settle it. 'Burlington Township' in GCatholic's address is the same town as 'Burlington' in the bulletin.
