## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, read from a render) lists Saturday 5pm, Sunday 8am, 10am (main church, and a Family Mass in the chapel) and 12pm, and daily Mass 9am. The chapel is the Crypt Chapel under the church (page 2: 'Reconciliation: 3:30 PM Saturdays in the Crypt Chapel'; sanctuary lamp for 'Church' and 'Chapel'), the same building, so it is not a second church row.
