## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (listing 696, page 2, rendered from an image-only issue) gives the Weekly Schedule: Saturday vigil 5:30 pm; Sunday 7:30 am, 9:00 am (Traditional Latin Mass), 11:00 am; weekday Mon 7:00 pm, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat 8:00 am, Fri 12:00 pm.

## Name and address

Name: the masthead's 'Saint Catherine Labouré Church', the finder's 'The Church of St Catherine Laboure'' and GCatholic's 'St. Catherine Laboure' agree. Address: the finder and GCatholic say 130 Bray Ave (North Middletown 07748-5419), the printer's listing 696 says 110 Bray Avenue, the bulletin prints the parish office at 20 Shore Acres Ave, and the frozen stcathek.org says '5 Shore Acres Avenue'. OSM has house nodes for both 110 Bray Avenue (40.43863, -74.11188) and 130 Bray Avenue (40.43842, -74.11188), about 25 m apart, with no place_of_worship node. All of these are on one block, so the building is not in doubt, but the street number is, so address_verified stays false. Coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code 87G7CVQQ+H7 (40.43894, -74.11181).
