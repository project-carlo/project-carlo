## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 St. Teresa of Calcutta bulletin (page 2, read from a render) lists St. Elizabeth Church's weekend Masses (Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 9:00 and 10:30 am) and daily Mass Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at 8:00 am. GCatholic lists it as a separate parish's church; the 2017 merger made it a second church of St. Teresa of Calcutta (`st-teresa-of-calcutta-bradley-beach`), and the bulletin's cover calls the parish 'One Parish Family with Two Churches'.

## Name and address

The bulletin (page 1 and page 2), GCatholic and OSM agree on 424 Lincoln Avenue, Avon-by-the-Sea 07717 (the bulletin writes 'Avon By the Sea'). The bulletin names it 'St. Elizabeth Church', GCatholic 'St. Elizabeth'. Coordinates follow GCatholic's Plus Code, about 100 m from OSM's house node.
