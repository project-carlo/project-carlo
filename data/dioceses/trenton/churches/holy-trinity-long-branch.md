## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin lists Sunday 8:00, 9:30 and 11:30 am and Monday-Friday 12:10 pm Masses at Holy Trinity (page 1), and page 2 gives each Mass of 1-8 November with its celebrant, plus a Monday 2 November Mass at Mt. Carmel Cemetery. It is the parish's second church (see `christ-the-king-long-branch`).

## Name and address

The masthead and GCatholic both give Holy Trinity Church, 408 Prospect St., Long Branch 07740, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; the street resolves in OSM within 30 m. The finder has no pin of its own for this church (its single Christ the King pin, directory:14590, is plotted 130 m from here but is the parish office, linked to Our Lady Star of the Sea).
