## Holds Mass

Yes: the parish's daily Mass, Monday to Saturday 8:30 am, is said here ('in the Chapel': bulletins of 2 August and 1 November 2026, the parish's Mass page; 'used for daily Mass', The Monitor, 13 February 2025). The chapel has its own street address on the parish's contact page, so it is a church row by the settled rule for a chapel with its own address and daily Mass (`diocese.md`, *Rules settled*).

## Name and address

**The name disagrees:** The Monitor and the parish's photos call it St. Bonaventure Chapel; the site's footer says 'St. Pio's Chapel'. No other source gives the chapel's address or name: GCatholic lists only the church at 1100 Grand Central Avenue as 'St. Bonaventure' (roster gcatholic:18791, see `st-pio-of-pietrelcina-church-lavallette`), so this place has no roster entry. Both flags are false (one source for the address). Coordinates are null for the geocoder: OpenStreetMap's text lookup for 1206 Grand Central Avenue returned only the road, and GCatholic's point for 1100 is about 100 m away.
