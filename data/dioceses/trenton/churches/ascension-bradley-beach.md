## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 St. Teresa of Calcutta bulletin (page 2, read from a render) lists Ascension Church's weekend Masses (Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 and 10:00 am) and daily Mass Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 8:00 am.

## Name and address

The bulletin (page 1 masthead and page 2), GCatholic, the finder's address line and OSM agree on 501 Brinley Avenue, Bradley Beach 07720; this is also the parish office's address. The bulletin names it 'Ascension Church', GCatholic 'Ascension'. Coordinates follow GCatholic's Plus Code, 90 m from OSM's house node; the finder's pin for the parish sits at St. Elizabeth in Avon, not here.
