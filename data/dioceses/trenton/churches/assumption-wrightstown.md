## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, rendered) lists the Assumption Worship Site, 28 Monmouth Road, Wrightstown, with Saturday 5:30 pm and Sunday 8 and 11 am; the parish homepage gives the same.

## Name and address

The bulletin and the parish site (one source) and GCatholic agree on 28 Monmouth Road, Wrightstown 08562; GCatholic's phone is St. Isidore's office number. The July 2024 cohort list calls the parish 'St. Isidore the Farmer, Wrightstown' and the finder places its seat at 76 Evergreen Road, New Egypt, so the 'New Egypt' in the 2017 decisions' 'Assumption, New Egypt' is the parish's mail address, not this church's town. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; OSM has no point to compare, so only the bulletin or site and GCatholic back the address, which are two sources, and both flags are true.
