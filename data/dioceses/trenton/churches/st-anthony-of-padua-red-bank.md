## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 2) and the Oratory's Mass Times page give daily Mass at 7:30am (Monday-Friday) and 12:15pm in Latin (Monday-Wednesday, Friday), Saturday vigils at 4pm and 7pm (Spanish), and Sunday Masses at 7:30am, 9am, 10:30am, 12pm (Latin), 1:30pm (Spanish) and 6pm.

## Name and address

The finder and GCatholic give 121 Bridge Avenue, Red Bank, NJ 07701-1201, and OpenStreetMap has an address point there (within 20 m of GCatholic's coordinates), so both flags are true. The parish's own site footer gives a different address, 27 Oratory Way, which its staff page explains is the parish office and center across the street from the church, so this is not a disagreement about the church's location, but the site does not itself state the church's street number (the church address rests on the finder, GCatholic and OSM). Coordinates follow GCatholic's Plus Code. The name follows the finder and the bulletin ("The Oratory Church of Saint Anthony of Padua"); GCatholic says "St. Anthony of Padua" and records it as an oratory since 2016-05-29.
