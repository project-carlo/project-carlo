## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin schedules Saturday 4:00 pm, Sunday 7:30 am and 11:00 am, Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8:00 am, and Holy Day Masses at 7:00 pm, all '@ Incarnation'.

## Name and address

Sources disagree on the number. The bulletin prints the church at 1555 Pennington Road and the parish office at 1545; GCatholic and the finder give the parish's address as 1545 Pennington Road (GCatholic's entry is the parish's, 'Incarnation–Saint James'). I followed the bulletin's 1555 for the church. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (40.25256, -74.77756), which is nearer OSM's 1555 point (about 60 m) than OSM's 1545 point (about 190 m): so GCatholic's building point sits at the church, whatever number it prints. Both flags stay false: the name 'Incarnation Church' rests on the parish's own bulletin and site ('The Church of the Incarnation') alone, and the number is disputed. A visit or the county's parcel record would settle 1555 against 1545.
