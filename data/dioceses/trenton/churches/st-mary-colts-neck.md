## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 2, read from a render) lists Saturday 31 October 4 PM; Sunday 1 November 8 AM, 10 AM, 12 PM; Monday-Wednesday 9 AM at St. Mary; Thursday-Saturday 9 AM at St. Catharine, Holmdel (that church has its own record); Saturday 7 November 4 PM and Sunday 8 November 8 AM, 10 AM, 12 PM. The bulletin mentions a chapel on the St. Mary site as well as the church; it is the same address and not a separate row.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, GCatholic and the diocese's finder agree on 1 Phalanx Road, Colts Neck, NJ 07722, so both flags are true. The name "Church of St. Mary" is the church's own form; the bulletin says "The Parish of Saint Mary", GCatholic "St. Mary" and the finder "St. Mary Parish". Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; the finder's (40.30072, -74.17497) is within 20 m.
