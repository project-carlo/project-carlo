## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin lists daily Mass Monday-Friday 8:00am, Saturday 4:00pm and Sunday 9:00am and 11:00am; the intentions page names the same times.

## Name and address

The parish site's footer and GCatholic both give 11 South Sunset Road, Willingboro; the bulletin and finder give the rectory (63 Sylvan Lane) as the parish address. Both flags are true on that agreement (site, GCatholic and the bulletin's 'Corpus Christi Parish' name). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code. Nominatim's search for '11 South Sunset Road' returned two house points (40.04379, -74.90025 and 40.04128, -74.90269), 120-190 m either side of the Plus Code, so the Plus Code is kept.
