## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish site (read 2026-10-30) lists the weekend Masses Saturday vigil 5:00pm and Sunday 8:30am and 10:30am, and daily Mass at 9:00am Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. The 1 November 2026 bulletin's cover and letter (rendered pages 1-2) place the parish at this address and speak of Mass 'here in our Worship Space'; the pages read in that image-only issue carry no schedule. It is the parish's only church.

## Name and address

The bulletin cover, GCatholic (18703) and the diocese's finder (14550) agree on 520 Medford Lakes Road, Tabernacle, NJ 08088 (GCatholic and the finder abbreviate 'Rd.' and add +4 ZIP 8590). The name follows the bulletin, 'Church of the Holy Eucharist'; GCatholic says 'Holy Eucharist'. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87F7R7VP+RJ (39.84456, -74.71344); the finder's pin (39.84500, -74.71368) is about 55 m away on the same campus. Both flags are true: the bulletin and parish site, GCatholic and the diocese's finder are independent sources that agree.
