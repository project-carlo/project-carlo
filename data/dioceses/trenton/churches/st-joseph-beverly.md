## Holds Mass

Yes. It is the parish's only church; the 25 October 2026 bulletin and the parish site list the weekend and daily Masses at 805 Warren Street.

## Name and address

The bulletin and site never print the church's name (the cover says 'The Parish of Jesus, the Good Shepherd'). 'St. Joseph Church, Beverly' comes from The Monitor and GCatholic, which agree. The address is the parish's on its site, bulletin, finder and GCatholic; OSM and GCatholic's Plus Code agree to about 90 m, so both flags are true. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code. The finder's pin is St. Peter's old site in Riverside, not here.
