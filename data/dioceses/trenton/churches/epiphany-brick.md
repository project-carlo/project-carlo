## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin gives Saturday vigil 4:00 pm and Sunday 8:00 am and 10:30 am, with daily Mass at 9:00 am (page 2 intentions, Monday-Saturday); the 1 November issue has the same schedule.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish site's footer, the finder and GCatholic agree on 615 Thiele Road, Brick, NJ 08724 (the site's header gives the rectory at 641 Thiele Road). The parish styles itself 'The Church of Epiphany'; the finder says 'Church of Epiphany Parish'. Coordinates follow GCatholic's Plus Code; the finder's pin is about 8 m and OpenStreetMap's house point about 120 m away.
