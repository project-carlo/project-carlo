## Holds Mass

Yes. The parish's Mass Schedule page and its 29 October 2026 weekly email give Saturday 5:00 pm, Sunday 8:00 am, 10:00 am and 12:00 pm, and weekday Masses Thursday, Friday and Saturday 9:00 am. St. Mary, Colts Neck's 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 2) also lists the Thursday-Saturday 9 AM Masses "AT ST. CATHARINE, HOLMDEL".

## Name and address

The parish's own footer, GCatholic and the diocese's finder agree on 108 Middletown Road, Holmdel, NJ 07733 (the finder and GCatholic add the P.O. box ZIP+4 07733-0655), so both flags are true. The coordinates disagree: GCatholic's Plus Code and OpenStreetMap's address point agree within 150 m, while the finder's pin is about 3.5 km away, so the coordinates follow GCatholic. The name follows the parish's footer ("Church of St. Catharine"); GCatholic says "St. Catharine".
