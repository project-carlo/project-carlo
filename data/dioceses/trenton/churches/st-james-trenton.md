## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 Incarnation-St. James bulletin schedules Sunday 9:30 am and Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday 8:00 am at St. James, and a Holy Day 8:00 am.

## Name and address

No roster lists this church: GCatholic and the finder carry only the parish at 1545 Pennington Rd (see `incarnation-ewing`), so the place is established by the bulletin and the parish site. Sources disagree on the street number: the bulletin prints 29 East Paul Avenue, which OSM gives to 'Holy Trinity Novitiate' (the Trinitarians' house, 45 m from the church), while OSM's named St. James church is at 11 East Paul Avenue. I used the bulletin's address and OSM's church node for coordinates; both flags stay false. The church and the Trinitarian house are neighbours on East Paul Avenue, so 29 may be the community's mailing address. A parcel lookup would settle which number is the church's.
