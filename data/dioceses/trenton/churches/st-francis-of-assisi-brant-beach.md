## Holds Mass

Yes, all year. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 5) schedules Mass here 9:00 AM Monday-Saturday, Saturday 5:00 PM and Sunday 8:30 AM, 10:00 AM and 11:30 AM. It is the parish seat and the only one of the four churches open in the Oct 17/18-Dec 19/20 schedule.

## Name and address

The bulletin footer, GCatholic, the finder and OSM agree on 4700 Long Beach Boulevard, 08008. The bulletin and OSM say Brant Beach; GCatholic and the finder say Long Beach Township (the same place). Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code, 25 m from the OSM point.
