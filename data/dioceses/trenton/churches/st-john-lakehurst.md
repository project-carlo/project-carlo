## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 2, read from a render) lists Sunday Masses Saturday 4 PM and 6 PM and Sunday 9 AM and 11 AM, and daily Mass Monday to Saturday 8 AM under 'Chapel Schedule'; the parish homepage gives the same times. The chapel is a second room on the same campus (no street address of its own in either source), so it adds no church row.

## Name and address

The bulletin (page 1), GCatholic (18767) and the finder (14648) agree on 619 Chestnut Street, Lakehurst, NJ 08733 (they abbreviate 'St'). The name follows the bulletin masthead, 'St. John's Catholic Church'; the finder says 'St. John R.C. Church', GCatholic 'St. John'. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87G72M9F+5M (40.01794, -74.32581); the finder's pin is about 12 m away. Both flags are true: the parish's own bulletin and site, GCatholic and the diocese's finder are independent sources that agree.
