## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 4) gives Saturday 5:00pm, Sunday 8:00 and 10:00am, and weekday Mass Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday at 9:00am in the Chapel; Wednesday is an Eucharistic Service. The community site lists the same.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the community site, the finder and GCatholic agree on 115 E. Delaware Avenue, Pennington, NJ 08534, so both flags are true. The name follows the bulletin and site ("St. James"); the finder styles it "St. James Church". Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code point (40.32981, -74.78881). The finder's pin is about 135 m from it and OSM's house point for 115 E Delaware (40.32848, -74.78726) about 200 m, so the three disagree by a few hundred metres at most, all in the same part of Pennington; the Plus Code is the building-precise one.

## St. James Chapel

The masthead lists "St. James Chapel, 6 Eglantine Avenue" as a fourth address, and weekday Mass (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri 9:00am) is said "in the Chapel". The map batch first kept it within this row. Reconcile (2026-10-30) made it a row of its own, `st-james-chapel-pennington`, under the diocese's settled rule for chapels with their own street address and daily Mass. This church keeps the weekend Masses (Saturday 5:00pm, Sunday 8:00 and 10:00am).
