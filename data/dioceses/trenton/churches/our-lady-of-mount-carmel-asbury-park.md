## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 Mother of Mercy bulletin (page 1) names this church as the parish's one 'Worship Site' and gives its Mass schedule: Monday-Friday 9:00 AM; Saturday 4:00 PM English and 7:00 PM Spanish; Sunday 7:30, 9:00 (English), 10:30 (bilingual), 12:00 Noon (Spanish), 2:00 PM (Creole) and 4:00 PM (Spanish). The count agrees with the Monitor's 2020 plan to move Holy Spirit's Masses here after its closing.

## Name and address

The bulletin and the finder give 1201 Asbury Avenue; GCatholic, ParishesOnline and the Monitor's 'Pine Street' give 805 Pine St. ParishesOnline's 805 Pine St and catholicmasses.org's 1201 Asbury Ave carry the same ZIP+4 (07712-5793; GCatholic's is 07712-5769), and GCatholic's Plus Code and OSM's 805 Pine Street house node are 16 m apart, so I take them to be the corner building's two addresses (Asbury Ave and Pine St). That is an inference, so `address_verified` stays false. The row prints the bulletin's address and takes coordinates from GCatholic's Plus Code (the finder's pin is about 90 m north). The bulletin and GCatholic agree on the name, 'Our Lady of Mt. Carmel' / 'Our Lady of Mount Carmel'; the parish school, Our Lady of Mt. Carmel School, shares the campus.
