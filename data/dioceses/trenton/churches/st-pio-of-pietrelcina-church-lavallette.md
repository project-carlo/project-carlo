## Holds Mass

Yes: the parish's weekend Masses are said 'in the Church' (parish Mass page and bulletin), with the daily Mass in the chapel next door (`st-bonaventure-chapel-lavallette`). The 2 August 2026 and 1 November 2026 bulletins list the same churches, so the church is open all year; only the times shift (Sunday 7:30 in summer, 7:15 from 1 November).

## Name and address

**The name disagrees.** The parish's site and bulletin call it St. Pio of Pietrelcina (the church of the merged parish); GCatholic's entry for the same address is 'St. Bonaventure', the name of the pre-2008 parish whose church it was (The Monitor, 13 February 2025: the worship site 'became part of St. Pio Parish' on the 2008 merger with Our Lady of Peace, Normandy Beach). I used the parish's name and left `name_verified` false. The street address, 1100 Grand Central Avenue, agrees between the parish's contact page and GCatholic, so `address_verified` is true; the bulletin masthead's 103 Washington Avenue is the parish center and office, one block south. The point is GCatholic's Plus Code; OpenStreetMap's text lookup returned only Grand Central Avenue (a road, 220 m away), so no second point.
