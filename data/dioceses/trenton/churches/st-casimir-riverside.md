## Holds Mass

Yes. St. Casimir still holds Mass: the 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, rendered) lists Sunday 9:00 AM, Tuesday and Thursday 12pm and Saturday 9:00 AM at St. Casimir, Riverside, and the parish site's homepage lists the same. It is the Sunday 9:00 AM church of Resurrection Parish (the 2007 merger of Holy Name, Delran and St. Casimir, Riverside, per resurrection2.org/our-history).
