## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, text layer) gives Masses Monday to Thursday 9 am, Saturday 4 pm and Sunday 9 am and 11 am, and page 2 lists dated Mass intentions for the following week at those times (for example Saturday 31 October 4:00 pm, Sunday 1 November 9:00 am and 11:00 am). The 2017 decisions put this parish's status under periodic review from July 2018; the current bulletin is the evidence it still holds Mass.
