## Holds Mass

Yes, monthly. St. Joseph's 25 October 2026 bulletin and the parish site give Mass on the second Sunday of the month at 9:00 am, and every Sunday at 9:00 am in the summer months. The site's calendar lists the next at 9:00 am on 8 November 2026. It is a mission of St. Joseph, Toms River: GCatholic carries St. Joseph's website for it, which is why the survey flagged it.

## Name and address

The parish site and the bulletin (name only) and GCatholic (address 103 Central Ave., Island Heights 08732) agree; OSM names the same place 'Saint Gertrude's Mission Church' on Ocean Avenue at the same corner, 40 m from the Plus Code point. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code point. Both flags are true: the parish's own site and GCatholic are independent sources that agree on name and address.
