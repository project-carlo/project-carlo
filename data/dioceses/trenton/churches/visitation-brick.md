## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 2) gives Saturday 4:00 PM (vigil), Sunday 9:00 and 11:00 AM and daily Mass Monday-Friday 7:30 AM and Saturday 8:15 AM, all "(Church)"; the 4:00 PM and 11:00 AM Masses are with the congregation, 9:00 AM is also live-streamed.

## Name and address

The bulletin styles it "Church of the Visitation"; the finder says "Visitation Parish" and GCatholic "Visitation". Addresses disagree: the bulletin prints Church Address 755 Mantoloking Rd. and Rectory Address 730 Lynnwood Ave.; the finder, GCatholic and the John Patrick listing give only 730 Lynnwood Ave. (the rectory/office). The record follows the bulletin's church address, as the playbook rule for a masthead that prints the church apart from the office. OpenStreetMap has both numbers as house points about 130 m apart; GCatholic's building-precise Plus Code lies nearer 755 Mantoloking Rd. (about 45 m), so it is used for the coordinates. `address_verified` stays false because the bulletin's church number is not confirmed by a second source.
