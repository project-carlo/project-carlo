## Holds Mass

Yes. Sacred Heart's 1 November 2026 bulletin lists Holy Cross Church as its second church, with Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM (the Gospel Mass, per the parish's Worship Schedule page) and 12:00 Noon in Polish. The Monitor's 'Trenton faithful look ahead to a new day' (2018 event, REST date the 2019 migration) says Sacred Heart's merger with Divine Mercy 'resulted in Sacred Heart Parish having two worship sites ... Sacred Heart and Holy Cross Church' and that the Gospel Mass would be celebrated 'in Sacred Heart's Holy Cross Church at 10 a.m.'.

## Name and address

Holy Cross is the church of the former Divine Mercy Parish (formed 2005 from St. Stanislaus, Holy Cross and Sts. Peter and Paul, per its old bulletin header), so GCatholic's 'Divine Mercy' (18700) is this church: the Discover Mass listing is named 'Divine Mercy (Holy Cross)', and GCatholic's phone (609-393-4826) is the one the old Divine Mercy bulletin and Sacred Heart's current bulletin (Fr. Charles Muorah, ext. 108) print. I inferred the identity from the name, phone and street; no source says 'GCatholic 18700 is Holy Cross' in so many words.

The address disagrees: the bulletin, Discover Mass and the old bulletin say 233 Adeline Street; GCatholic says 201 Adeline Street (OSM has both numbers, 60 m apart, on the same block). I used 233, with GCatholic's Plus Code for coordinates (30 m from OSM's 233). `address_verified` stays false because of the 201 against 233 conflict; GCatholic's number is probably the former rectory or hall.

Other Divine Mercy churches (St. Stanislaus, Sts. Peter and Paul) are named only in the sacramental-records list of the current bulletin ("Saint Stanislaus Church, Holy Cross Church") and in no Mass schedule; no roster lists them.
