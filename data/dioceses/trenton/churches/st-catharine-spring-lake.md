## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin's schedule (page 1) and the parish's Mass Times page give Saturday 5:00 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM and 12:00 PM, and weekday 12:00 PM Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. Wednesday's 9:00 AM and noon Masses are at "St. Catharine Chapel", listed under this church's address; no separate address for it turned up, so it is treated as part of this site (see the parish record).

## Name and address

The bulletin, the finder, GCatholic and OpenStreetMap agree on the church at 215 Essex Avenue (GCatholic gives the cross streets '3rd Ave. & Essex Ave.'). Coordinates follow GCatholic's Plus Code, within about 15 m of OpenStreetMap's point.
