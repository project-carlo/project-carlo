## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 2) lists daily Mass Monday-Saturday at 8am and weekend Masses Saturday 4:30pm, Sunday 7:30am, 9:30am and 11:30am; the parish's homepage places the 2 November Memorial Mass "in the Church".

## Name and address

The bulletin footer, the parish's site header, the finder, GCatholic and OpenStreetMap all give 180 Ridge Road, Fair Haven, NJ 07704, so both flags are true. The coordinates disagree: GCatholic's Plus Code (40.35381, -74.02906) and the finder's pin (40.35469, -74.03002, about 130 m away) agree, while OpenStreetMap's node for the church is about 750 m west (40.35301, -74.03773). The coordinates follow GCatholic and the finder; OpenStreetMap's point is the outlier (its road geometry may be offset), and the church's address is not in doubt. The parish styles itself "The Church of the Nativity"; GCatholic says "Nativity".
