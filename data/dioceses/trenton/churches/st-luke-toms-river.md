## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 St. Luke bulletin (Bon Venture 4167, page 1) lists Saturday vigil 4:00 pm, Sunday 7:30, 10:00 am and 12:00 pm, and weekday Mass every day (7:30 am; Wednesday also 12:30 pm, Friday also 11:00 am).

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the finder pin and GCatholic agree on St. Luke, 1674 Old Freehold Road, Toms River 08755. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code point, 15 m from the finder's pin. OSM has only a house node 200 m away, not used. Both flags are true: the bulletin (with the parish site) and GCatholic/the finder are independent sources that agree.
