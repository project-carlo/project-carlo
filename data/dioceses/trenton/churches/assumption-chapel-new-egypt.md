## Holds Mass

Yes, on weekdays. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1, rendered) lists 'Daily Masses, Assumption Chapel, 76 Evergreen Road, New Egypt, Monday - Friday 8:00 am'; the parish homepage specifies Mass Monday through Thursday and a Communion Service on Friday. No weekend Mass is held here; weekend Masses are at St. Andrew and Assumption.

## Name and address

The chapel has the parish office's street address, 76 Evergreen Road, which the finder lists as the parish seat. The parish site's Locations page lists it as its own place ('Assumption Chapel') apart from the Assumption Worship Site at 28 Monmouth Road, Wrightstown, and says it is the 'Parish Office and Chapel Address'. It is a separate church row because it has its own street address and public daily Mass, unlike a chapel with no address of its own on a church's campus (settled rule). Whether it is a former church of the Assumption parish or a chapel in the office is not stated; the name suggests an Assumption connection, an inference. Coordinates are OSM's house node (nominatim) for 76 Evergreen Road, about 100 m from the finder's pin; there is no GCatholic Plus Code. Flags stay false: the sources agree on the address (bulletin, site and finder) but the name 'Assumption Chapel' rests on the parish's own words alone.
