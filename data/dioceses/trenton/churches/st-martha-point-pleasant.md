## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday vigil 4:30 pm, Sunday 7:30 am, 10:00 am and 12:00 noon, and Tuesday-Friday 9:00 am; page 2 lists the intentions for those Masses.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the finder and GCatholic agree on 3800 Herbertsville Road, Point Pleasant (NJ 08742); the finder and GCatholic file it under 'Point Pleasant Borough'/'Point Pleasant Boro' and the bulletin and site under 'Point Pleasant'. The parish styles itself 'The Church of St. Martha' (site, finder 'The Church of St Martha') and 'St. Martha Church' (bulletin). Coordinates follow GCatholic's Plus Code; the finder's pin is about 50 m from it.
