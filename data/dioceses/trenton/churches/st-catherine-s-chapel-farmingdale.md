## Holds Mass

Yes, on weekdays. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 2) says "St. Catherine's Chapel Weekday Mass Now Open" with a 9:00 AM Mass and 8:30 AM Rosary Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, and its Mass intentions (page 4) name the chapel for the 26, 27, 29 and 30 October Masses. Weekend Masses are at the church on Asbury Road (`st-catherine-of-siena-farmingdale`). GCatholic lists the chapel as a separate entry, which is why it was checked.

## Name and address

The parish calls it 'St. Catherine's Chapel' (bulletin) and 'St. Catherine of Siena Chapel' (site page); GCatholic says 'St. Catherine', kind Chapel. The address disagrees between the parish's own pages: the home page prints '1 West Main St.', while its directions link points at '5 W Main St'; GCatholic has only 'W Main St. & N Main St.'. I took 1 West Main Street from the parish's printed text and left `address_verified` false. The coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code (40.19931, -74.17081), which sits within about 20 m of the Google link's pin (40.199325, -74.170845), so the point is the same building whichever number is right. A street-level lookup would settle 1 vs 5.
