## Holds Mass

Yes. The 25 October 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Saturday 4:00 PM, Sunday 7:30, 9:00, 10:30 AM, 5:00 PM and 12:00 Noon (Traditional Latin Mass), and weekday Mass Monday-Friday 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM, Saturday 8:00 AM.

## Name and address

The bulletin masthead, the parish site's footer, the finder and GCatholic agree on 250 Old Squan Road, Brick, NJ 08724 (the finder and GCatholic add "Ste 1" for the rectory office). GCatholic styles the church "St. Dominic", a Shrine (Diocesan Shrine of Saint Carlo Acutis, dedicated 2023-10-01): the shrine is this church, so the two GCatholic/finder entries are one place. Coordinates follow GCatholic's Plus Code; the finder's pin is about 20 m and OpenStreetMap's school node about 40 m away.
