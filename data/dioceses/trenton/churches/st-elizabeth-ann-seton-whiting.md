## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (pages 1-2, text layer) and the parish's Mass Times page give weekend Masses Saturday 4:00 pm and Sunday 8:00 am, 10:00 am and 12:00 pm, and daily Mass Monday to Friday 8:00 am and 12:00 pm plus First Saturday 8:00 am, 'in All Saints Chapel unless otherwise specified'. All Saints Chapel (dedicated 2 May 1998; daily Mass, Adoration, columbarium, per seaswhiting.org/about-us/chapel-columbarium) is on the parish's single address, so it adds no church row.

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish site, GCatholic (18752) and the finder (14643) agree on 30 Schoolhouse Road, Whiting, NJ 08759. The name follows the bulletin ('St. Elizabeth Ann Seton'); the finder calls the parish 'St. Elizabeth Ann Seton Parish'. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code 87F7WMP2+XG (39.93744, -74.34869); the finder's pin (39.93697, -74.34721) is about 135 m away, within the campus. Both flags are true: the parish's own bulletin and site, GCatholic and the diocese's finder are independent sources that agree.
