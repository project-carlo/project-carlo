## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 1) gives Sunday 9:30 am (Polish) and 11:30 am (English), daily 9:00 am Monday-Saturday and 7:30 pm Thursday (Polish); the parish site's Mass and Confession Schedule lists the same. It is also the parish office (43 Madison Avenue).

## Name and address

The bulletin, the parish site, the finder and GCatholic (as "St. Mary", group St. Mary of the Lake Parish) agree on 43 Madison Avenue, Lakewood 08701. The old parish, St. Mary of the Lake, merged into Our Lady of Guadalupe on 1 July 2020 (The Monitor, 26 June 2020); the finder still carries it as a separate pin, and GCatholic's two Lakewood entries "St. Mary of the Lake" (18818) and "St. Mary" (18779) are two different places: 18779 is this church, 18818 is the Holy Family site on East County Line Road. Coordinates follow GCatholic's Plus Code; the finder's pin is about 30 m away.
