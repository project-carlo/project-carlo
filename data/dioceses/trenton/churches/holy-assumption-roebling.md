## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin schedules Saturday Vigil 4:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 AM, and weekday Mass Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 9:30 AM at Holy Assumption, Roebling. It was the church of the pre-merger Holy Assumption Parish, which merged in 2008 with St. Clare Parish to form Sts. Francis and Clare Parish (the Monitor, 2025-05-08 obituary of Fr. Felix Venza) and in 2018 into Mary, Mother of the Church.

## Name and address

The bulletin, GCatholic and OSM agree on 1290 Hornberger Avenue, Roebling 08554; the church is named in the bulletin as 'Holy Assumption' and 'Holy Assumption Church'. Coordinates are GCatholic's Plus Code; OSM's house point is about 30 m away. The finder has no pin here (the parish's pin is at St. Mary). The two Eastern Catholic churches in Roebling (St. Mary, St. Nicholas) are other parishes in batch `eastern-catholic`.
