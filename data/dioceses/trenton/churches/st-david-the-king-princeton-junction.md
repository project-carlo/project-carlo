Yes. The parish's Mass Schedule page and page 2 of the 1 November 2026 bulletin (an image, read from a render) list Saturday 4:00 and 5:30 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM, 10:30 AM and 7:00 PM, and weekday 9 AM Masses (First Friday, First Saturday). It is the parish's only church.
