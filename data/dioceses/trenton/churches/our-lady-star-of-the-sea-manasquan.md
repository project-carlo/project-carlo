## Holds Mass

Yes: a public Sunday 10:00 AM Mass. St. Denis's bulletins of 11 January, 1 February, 1 March and 25 October 2026 all list "Sunday: 10:00 AM - Our Lady Star of the Sea Chapel", and the parish's Mass page does too, with no season attached. GCatholic lists the place as a chapel; the question was whether it held public Mass, and the schedule answers it. Not seasonal on that evidence (a summer-only Mass would have dropped out of the January-March issues). Weekday Masses are all at St. Denis Church.

## Name and address

The parish says "Our Lady Star of the Sea Beach Chapel" (site) and "Our Lady Star of the Sea Chapel" (bulletin); GCatholic says "Star of the Sea". **The street number disagrees:** the bulletin and the parish site print 554 East Main Street, GCatholic 544 E Main St. I followed the parish (its own printed address, in four issues and the site), left `address_verified` false, and used GCatholic's Plus Code for the point (40.11306, -74.03531; the shore end of East Main Street). OpenStreetMap had no entry for either number. A street-level lookup of 544 vs 554 would settle it.
