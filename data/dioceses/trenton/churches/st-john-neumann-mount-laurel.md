## Holds Mass

Yes. The 1 November 2026 bulletin (page 2 'Weekly Liturgical Schedule', text layer) lists Monday and Thursday and Friday 9:00am, Wednesday 7:00pm, Saturday 4:00pm and Sunday 8:00am and 10:00am (Tuesday: no Masses). It is the parish's only church; the Our Lady of Sorrows Adoration Chapel has adoration hours, not Mass.

The bulletin (560 Walton Ave., Mt. Laurel, NJ 08054), the finder (560 Walton Ave, Mount Laurel, 08054-9582) and GCatholic (same, Plus Code 87F7X427+C4, 39.95106, -74.88719) agree. Both flags are true: three independent sources agree.
