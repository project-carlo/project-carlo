## Publisher

Our Lady of Good Counsel (Moorestown) publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 676 on church-bulletin.org), which its own site links as its Bulletin. The 1 November 2026 issue is image-only; rendered page 1 is headed 'Our Lady of Good Counsel Parish' with Very Rev. Jim Grogan, Sr., V.F. as pastor and gives one Mass schedule. It is the only Cohort 26 parish with no link to the other two in the 2017 decisions; its masthead, pastor and listing are its own.

## Worship sites

One church, 42 W. Main Street (see `our-lady-of-good-counsel-moorestown`). The Sunday 10am 'Family Mass in Chapel' and the Saturday confessions 'in the Crypt Chapel' are in the same building (page 2 sanctuary lamp lists 'Church' and 'Chapel'), so the chapel is not a second church row; GCatholic (18713) and the finder (14552) list the one site.

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory's and GCatholic's site (olgcnj.org) answers 403 to `probe url` and loads in the browser; its Bulletin link goes to John Patrick listing 676, so the row records the listing.
