## Publisher

Church of the Holy Eucharist (Tabernacle) publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 602 on church-bulletin.org). The 1 November 2026 issue is image-only; its rendered cover names the church and gives 520 Medford Lakes Road, and page 2 is signed by Fr. McClane. St. Mary of the Lakes (Medford) and St. Joan of Arc (Marlton), the other Cohort 3 parishes, publish under their own mastheads, pastors and Mass times (listings 556 and 520), so the cohort is not a shared bulletin.

## Worship sites

One church, Holy Eucharist, 520 Medford Lakes Road (see `holy-eucharist-tabernacle`). The parish site's schedule lists Masses there only; it mentions a Blessed Sacrament Chapel and a daily chapel inside the same building for prayer and adoration, which are not separate sites. GCatholic (18703) and the diocese's finder (14550) list the same single site.

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory's site (holyeucharist.org) is a Clover site with no bulletin archive of its own; its Bulletin link goes to the John Patrick template for listing 602, so the row reads the listing.

## Not yet confirmed

The pastor's name comes from the signature 'Fr. McClane' on page 2; no masthead listing staff was read in an image-only issue.
