## Publisher

Jesus the Good Shepherd Parish, Beverly (Cohort 1 with Corpus Christi, Willingboro). Formed in 2008 by merging St. Peter, Riverside and St. Joseph, Beverly; the bulletin's footer still reads 'Jesus The Good Shepherd, Riverside, NJ' and the site's title 'Riverside, NJ', both left from the St. Peter days. The parish publishes one bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 511); the parish's own site archive is stale (January 2025) and links that listing as the current bulletin.

One worship site now: St. Joseph Church, 805 Warren Street, Beverly. St. Peter, Riverside suspended operations in June 2017 (see excluded/st-peter-riverside). Cohort 1 is not a shared bulletin: Corpus Christi publishes its own (listing 6035).
