## Publisher

St. Thomas More publishes one bulletin, on a Google Sites page that embeds a Google Drive folder. The 1 November 2026 issue's page 2 is headed "Parish of St. Thomas More" and lists two churches under its Mass Times: St. Thomas More, 186 Gordons Corner Rd., Manalapan, and Our Lady of Mercy, 58 Main St., Englishtown, with the note "Please contact St Thomas More for all business matters." So the bulletin covers Our Lady of Mercy, which GCatholic lists separately (gcatholic:18823) under the same website.

## Worship sites

Two churches: St. Thomas More (`st-thomas-more-manalapan`) and Our Lady of Mercy (`our-lady-of-mercy-englishtown`). The directory's finder has a single pin for the parish at the St. Thomas More address. The bulletin also mentions a Eucharistic Chapel (novena on the third Monday at 7:00 pm), which is inside St. Thomas More's campus and not a second Mass site.

## Website and bulletin provider

The bulletins page (www.moremercy.org/bulletins) links "Open Drive Folder, Bulletins in new window" and embeds the folder. The issues are Google Drive files, so the row is `google_drive` pinned to the folder. Note the Drive folder lists the 'August 23, 2023 bulletin' among the issues; order is not strictly newest-first, so check which issue the fetch picks.
