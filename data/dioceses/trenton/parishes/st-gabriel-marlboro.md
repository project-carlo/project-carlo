## Publisher

St. Gabriel's publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 570 on church-bulletin.org). The 1 November 2026 issue's masthead (page 2) names "St. Gabriel's Roman Catholic Church, 100 North Main Street, Marlboro, New Jersey" alone, with Pastor Rev. Brian T. Butch, and lists Saturday 4:30PM and Sunday 8:00AM, 9:30AM, 11:00AM and 5:00PM. Nothing in it names St. Mary, Colts Neck, St. Catharine, Holmdel or St. Leo the Great, the other parishes in Cohort 13, so this is a single-parish row.

## Worship sites

One church, 100 N Main St, Marlboro (see `st-gabriel-marlboro`). The bulletin gives the daily Masses as Monday 9:00AM, Tuesday 10:00AM and 7:00PM, Wednesday 9:00AM, Thursday 10:00AM and Friday 9:00AM "in the Main Church". The parish website's history page (stgabrielsparish.org/about-our-parish) says the original church "served as a meeting place as well as a church where Mass is still offered Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10:00am"; that sentence dates from the 1990s pastorate it describes, and no current source I read shows a second building with public Mass (see *Not yet confirmed*). The Cemetery & Chapel Mausoleums (stgabrielsmausoleums.com) is a burial site; its page mentions no Mass.

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site is Duda (irp.cdn-website.com). Its "CURRENT BULLETIN" button is the John Patrick link. The listing's PDFs are image-only (`probe pdf` reports no extractable text for the 25 October and 1 November issues), so the pipeline reads them as images or with OCR; this does not affect which bulletin is the parish's.

## Not yet confirmed
