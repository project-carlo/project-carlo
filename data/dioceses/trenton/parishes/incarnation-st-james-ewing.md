## Publisher

Incarnation-St. James Parish, Ewing (Cohort 9, July 2024 list), two churches in one parish, publishing one ParishesOnline bulletin (org `incarnation-st-james`, container 04/0399). The 1 November 2026 issue's front page lists the whole Mass schedule at both churches and the parish office (quoted in the evidence). The pastor, Fr. Stanley DeBoe, O.SS.T., is a Trinitarian ("Administered by the Trinitarians"); the bulletin names no other parish.

The 2017 decisions put Incarnation-St. James and Our Lady of Good Counsel on a "Collaborative Model", not a linkage; Good Counsel publishes its own bulletin (John Patrick 533) with its own pastor (see `our-lady-of-good-counsel-west-trenton`).

## Worship sites

Two churches with public Mass in this bulletin: Incarnation (1555 Pennington Road, Ewing) and St. James (29 East Paul Avenue, Trenton 08638, the former St. James Parish's church; the site's history page says it began as Holy Family mission in 1919). GCatholic and the finder list only Incarnation-Saint James at the parish office, 1545 Pennington Rd; neither lists the St. James church, so the St. James church row comes from the bulletin and parish site alone.

## Website and bulletin provider

The finder's `IncarnationStJames.com` redirects to the current `incarnationstjames.org` (a WeConnect site) whose Publications page lists the ParishesOnline container's issues. Provider is `parishes_online`.
