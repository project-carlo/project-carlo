## Publisher

St. Aloysius Parish (Jackson) publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 640 on church-bulletin.org) and mirrors each issue on its own WordPress site. The 1 November 2026 issue is image-only; its rendered page 1 is headed "SAINT ALOYSIUS CHURCH" with Very Rev. John P. Bambrick as pastor and gives one Mass schedule for the one church at 935 Bennetts Mills Road. St. Monica (Jackson), St. John (Lakehurst) and St. Elizabeth Ann Seton (Whiting), the other Cohort 23 parishes, each publish under their own masthead and pastor (listings 649 and 6022; Bon Venture 2166), so the cohort is not a shared bulletin.

## Worship sites

One church, St. Aloysius, 935 Bennetts Mills Road (see `st-aloysius-jackson`). The bulletin's Mass schedule lists no other building; "The Church is open for daily prayer" is not a Mass. GCatholic (18729) and the diocese's finder (14638) list the same single site.

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory's site (saintaloysiusonline.org) is live and carries the bulletin as dated posts, each linking a PDF named like the John Patrick template. The 25 October PDF is byte-identical on both channels, and the listing already has 1 November, so the row reads the John Patrick listing (the Run's rule: the channel with the latest issue).
