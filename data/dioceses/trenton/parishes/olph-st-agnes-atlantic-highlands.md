## Publisher

OLPH-St. Agnes publishes one bulletin for the merged parish, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 599). The 1 November 2026 masthead (page 1) names the parish and both churches, Our Lady of Perpetual Help (Highlands) and St. Agnes (Atlantic Highlands), with Rev. Jarlath Quinn as pastor. It is a single publisher for two churches, so one row.

## Worship sites

Two: St. Agnes at 103 Center Ave, Atlantic Highlands (`st-agnes-atlantic-highlands`; the parish office and the finder's pin) and Our Lady of Perpetual Help at 141 Navesink Ave, Highlands (`our-lady-of-perpetual-help-highlands`; GCatholic 18718). The bulletin's schedule assigns Masses to each by name.

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site (olphstagnes.org, Squarespace) only links the printer's listing, so the recipe is `page-pdfs` on the listing. GCatholic's site for St. Agnes (stagnesnj.com) is dead and for OLPH (jewelbythesea.org) is dead.

## Not yet confirmed

Nothing outstanding. The 1 November bulletin notes 'Our program for creating the bulletin will be discontinued on October the 1st. Please be patient while we transition to another program', so the printer may change; recheck if the listing stops updating.
