## Publisher

St. Anthony of Padua publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 568 on church-bulletin.org, linked from the parish's site as jppc.net/onlinebulletins/568template.pdf). The 1 November 2026 issue is image-only; the cover and page 2 name the parish alone, with its pastor and one Mass schedule.

## Worship sites

One church, 251 Franklin St. (see `st-anthony-of-padua-hightstown`). The Mass schedule lists no other place.
