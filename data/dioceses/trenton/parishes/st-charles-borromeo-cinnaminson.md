## Publisher

The 1 November 2026 issue's masthead names the parish alone with Rev. Daniel E. Kirk as pastor and gives one Mass schedule. The other Cohort 2 parishes publish separately (Sacred Heart: ParishesOnline; Our Lady of Perpetual Help: Bon Venture 2214; Resurrection: John Patrick 542).
