## Publisher

St. Charles Borromeo, Cinnaminson publishes its own weekly bulletin through Bon Venture (sponsor page 2203), which its own homepage links. The 1 November 2026 issue's masthead names the parish alone with Rev. Daniel E. Kirk as pastor and gives one Mass schedule. The other Cohort 2 parishes publish separately (Sacred Heart: ParishesOnline; Our Lady of Perpetual Help: Bon Venture 2214; Resurrection: John Patrick 542).

## Worship sites

One church, 2500 Branch Pike (see `st-charles-borromeo-cinnaminson`). The 'Adoration Chapel' the bulletin lists (open 8AM-10PM weekdays) has no address of its own and no Mass, so it is not a church row. GCatholic (18746) and the finder (14559) list the same one site.
