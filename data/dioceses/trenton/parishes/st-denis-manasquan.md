## Publisher

The Church of St. Denis, Manasquan publishes its own weekly bulletin through Bon Venture (sponsor page 2095). The 25 October 2026 issue's text layer opens with Rev. William J. P. Lago as pastor and a Mass schedule for St. Denis Church and Our Lady Star of the Sea Chapel. It names no other parish. The other Cohort 29 parishes publish under John Patrick (513, 613), so there is no shared bulletin.

## Worship sites

Two: St. Denis Church, 90 Union Avenue, and Our Lady Star of the Sea Beach Chapel, 554 East Main Street (see `st-denis-manasquan`, `our-lady-star-of-the-sea-manasquan`). The chapel holds a Sunday 10:00 AM Mass year-round on the evidence read: the same line is in the 11 January, 1 February, 1 March and 25 October 2026 bulletins' page 1, and the parish's Mass page lists it without a season.
