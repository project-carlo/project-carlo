## Publisher

St. Rose of Lima publishes its own weekly bulletin through LPi/ParishesOnline (org `st-rose-of-lima-church-7728`, container 04/1243). The 25 October 2026 issue's masthead (page 1) reads "16 McLean Street, Freehold, New Jersey 07728", names Rev. Joel Wilson as pastor, and gives the Mass schedule below. Nothing in it names the Co-Cathedral, St. Joseph or St. Thomas More, the other Cohort 12 parishes, so this is a single-parish row. Rev. ## Worship sites

One campus at 16 McLean St (see `st-rose-of-lima-freehold`): the Church (Sunday 7:00 am, 9:30 am; weekdays 8:30 am; 5:00 pm Vietnamese) and the Chapel, "located between the back of the church and the rectory" (page 8), which holds the Saturday 4:00 pm vigil, Spanish Masses and the 11:00 am and 12:30 pm Sunday Masses. The bulletin and site give one street address for both, so they are one church row. The "Old Church" on the lawn (page 3, Christmas tree notice) appears nowhere in the Mass schedule.
