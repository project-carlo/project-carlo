## Publisher

St. Dominic Parish, Brick (Cohort 22 with Visitation, Brick and Our Lady of Guadalupe, Lakewood). It publishes one bulletin, printed by Bon Venture (sponsor page 4139) and linked as "Weekly Bulletin" from the parish homepage. The 25 October 2026 issue's page 1 names the Church of Saint Dominic and the Diocesan Shrine of Saint Carlo Acutis, 250 Old Squan Road, with the pastor Rev. Brian Patrick Woodrow and the Mass schedule for this parish alone. The 1 November 2026 issue is on the sponsor page too, but its pages 1-16 have no text layer, so the 25 October issue (14 readable pages) was the one read.

## Worship sites

One church, St. Dominic, 250 Old Squan Road. GCatholic 18750 lists it as "St. Dominic", type Shrine, group "Shrine of St. Carlo Acutis" ("shrine ded. 2023.10.01"): the shrine is the parish church itself, not a second building (the bulletin masthead reads "Church of Saint Dominic / Diocesan Shrine of Saint Carlo Acutis" at the one address). St. Joseph's Chapel, which the parish site describes (renovated October 2019), is on the same campus with no address of its own; the bulletin mentions it only for a monthly Rosary and prayer hour, with no Mass line, so no church row.

## Website and bulletin provider

stdominicsparish.com is an eCatholic site (id 40144) whose bulletin archive is empty; the printer's Bon Venture page is the row's website. John Patrick listing 571 (Saint Dominic's Church) has no editions.
