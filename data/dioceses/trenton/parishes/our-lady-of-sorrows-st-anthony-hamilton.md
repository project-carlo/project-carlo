## Publisher

Our Lady of Sorrows-St. Anthony Parish, Hamilton (Cohort 6, "Lumen Christi", with St. Gregory the Great and St. Raphael-Holy Angels, July 2024 cohort list). The parish publishes one bulletin, printed by Bon Venture (sponsor page 2163). The 1 November 2026 issue names both churches in its Mass schedule ("Churches: Our Lady of Sorrows (OLS); St. Anthony (SA)") and has one pastor, Rev. Msgr. Thomas Gervasio. The Lumen Christi cohort is a collaboration group, not a shared bulletin: St. Gregory and St. Raphael-Holy Angels publish their own (publication_reviews).

## Worship sites

Two churches, both with weekend and weekday Mass in the 1 November 2026 schedule: Our Lady of Sorrows (3816 East State Street Ext., where the office is) and St. Anthony (626 South Olden Avenue; the Creole Masses are held there). GCatholic lists them as two entries (18719, 18805); the diocese's finder has one pin for the parish at the Our Lady of Sorrows address. Nothing the diocese lists is left out.
