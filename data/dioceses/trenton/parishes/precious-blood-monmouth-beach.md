## Publisher

Precious Blood publishes its own weekly bulletin from the parish site's /bulletin page (PDFs on becdn.net, named Bulletin-PBC-...). The 25 October 2026 masthead gives 72 Riverdale Avenue, Monmouth Beach and names Rev. Michael Sullivan as pastor and Rev. Andrés Serna as parochial vicar, the same two priests as St. Dorothea's bulletin. That is the linkage the January 2017 decisions planned (St. Dorothea with Precious Blood by 1 July 2019). It is a shared pastor, not a shared bulletin: each parish prints its own file, with its own masthead, Mass intentions and schedule, so the rows stay separate (see the settled rule on cohorts and linkages). Page 3 of both prints a combined table of the two parishes' Sunday and weekday Masses under the heading 'OUR LINKED PARISHES'; it adds no church to either parish.
