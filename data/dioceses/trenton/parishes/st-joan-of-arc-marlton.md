## Publisher

St. Joan of Arc Parish (Marlton) publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 520 on church-bulletin.org) and posts issues on its own WordPress site. The 1 November 2026 issue is image-only; its rendered page 1 is headed 'Church of St. Joan of Arc' and page 2 names Fr. Richard D. LaVerghetta as pastor and gives one Mass schedule. St. Mary of the Lakes (Medford) and Holy Eucharist (Tabernacle), the other Cohort 3 parishes, publish under their own mastheads (listings 556 and 602). St. Isaac Jogues, Marlton (listing 637) is a different parish with its own listing and belongs to another batch.

## Worship sites

One church, St. Joan of Arc, 100 Willow Bend Road (see `st-joan-of-arc-marlton`). The bulletin's schedule lists no other building; the parish school and convent share the campus. GCatholic (18764) and the diocese's finder (14560) list the same single site.

## Website and bulletin provider

The directory's site (stjoans.org) is live; its dated bulletin list was a week or more behind the John Patrick listing on 2026-10-30 (newest dated 2026-10-18, listing 2026-11-01), so the row reads the listing (the Run's rule: the channel with the latest issue).
