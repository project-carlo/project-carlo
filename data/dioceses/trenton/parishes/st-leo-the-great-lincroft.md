## Publisher

St. Leo the Great publishes its own weekly bulletin, hosted by Bon Venture Services on sponsors.bonventure.net, account 2156. The 1 November 2026 issue (page 2) names Pastor Reverend Daniel F. Swift, the Church of St. Leo the Great and its school; nothing in the text layer names St. Catharine, Holmdel, St. Gabriel, Marlboro or St. Mary, Colts Neck, the other Cohort 13 parishes, so this is a single-parish row.

## Worship sites

One church campus, 50 Hurleys Ln, Lincroft (see `st-leo-the-great-lincroft`). The site's Mass Times list Saturday 5:30 PM and Sunday 7:00, 8:30, 10:00 and 11:30 AM in the Church and the Monday-Saturday 9:00 AM Mass in the "CHAPEL - Parish Center" (in the Church during Lent). Both are on the one campus at the one address, so the chapel is not a separate church row.

## Website and bulletin provider

The parish site is eCatholic (14276) but its bulletin is not: the site's "Bulletin" menu link goes to the Bon Venture page, and the eCatholic /bulletins archive stops in August 2017. The Bon Venture listing has the latest issue (1 November 2026) with a text layer. No other channel (John Patrick listing, own-site PDFs) was found: the Run's John Patrick scan has no Lincroft listing.
