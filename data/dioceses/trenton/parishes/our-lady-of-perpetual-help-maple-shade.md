## Publisher

The 1 November 2026 issue is headed with the parish's site and street address and gives Fr. Fernando Monsalve as pastor and one Mass schedule. The parish's Solutio site (olphparish.com) lists the same Sundays by number (188 = 1 November) but its /file PDFs answer 403, so the row reads Bon Venture.
