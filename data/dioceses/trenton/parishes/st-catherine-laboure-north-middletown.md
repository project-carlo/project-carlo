## Publisher

St. Catherine Laboure publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by John Patrick Publishing (listing 696; the tsv's id for 'Saint Catherine Laboure Church, Middletown, NJ 07748'). The 1 November 2026 issue is image-only; page 1 (rendered) heads it 'Saint Catherine Labouré Church' and lists Rev. Richard M. Osborn as pastor, with the parish office at 20 Shore Acres Ave and (732)-787-1318, the finder's phone for the parish. It names no other parish. St. Ann, Keansburg's masthead names Rev. Richard Vila, so the 2017 'to be determined' linkage of the two has not produced a shared pastor or bulletin: separate rows.
