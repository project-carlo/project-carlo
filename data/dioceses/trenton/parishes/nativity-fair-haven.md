## Publisher

The Church of the Nativity, Fair Haven publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 627 on church-bulletin.org); the parish's homepage 'CURRENT BULLETIN' link is that listing's `jppc.net/onlinebulletins/627template.pdf` redirect. The 1 November 2026 issue is an image-only PDF; page 2, read from a render, is headed "The Church of the Nativity, Rev. Christopher P. Picollo, Pastor" and gives the Mass schedule. It names Nativity alone with its own staff, so it is a single-parish row. Its pastor differs from Holy Cross's (Rev. Michael Lankford-Stokes), so the Cohort 16 parishes share neither a pastor nor a bulletin here.

## Worship sites

One church, 180 Ridge Road, Fair Haven (see `nativity-fair-haven`). The parish's homepage mentions a "Chapel" (Scripture Study meets "either in the Parish Library or the Chapel"), but neither the bulletin's liturgy list nor the site gives Mass anywhere but "in the Church" (the All Souls Memorial Mass: "Monday, November 2 at 7pm in the Church"), so no separate chapel row.

## Website and bulletin provider

The GCatholic website (nativitychurchnj.web.officelive.com) is dead; the finder's `nativitychurchnj.org` is the live WordPress site and redirects from `http://www.nativitychurchnj.org/` to `https://nativitychurchnj.org/`. Its bulletin is the John Patrick listing, so the row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing.
