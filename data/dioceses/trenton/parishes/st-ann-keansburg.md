## Publisher

St. Ann publishes its own weekly bulletin through LPi/ParishesOnline (org `church-of-st-ann-keansburg`, container 03/1344). The 1 November 2026 issue's masthead (page 1) reads "311 CARR AVE, KEANSBURG, NJ 07734 (732) 787-0315" under "CHURCH OF SAINT ANN" and names Rev. Richard Vila as pastor. It names no other parish. The 2017 *Faith in Our Future* decisions left a linkage with St. Catherine Laboure "to be determined"; St. Catherine's current masthead names Rev. Richard M. Osborn as pastor and its own office, so there is no shared pastor and no shared bulletin (see `st-catherine-laboure-north-middletown`).
