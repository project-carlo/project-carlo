## Publisher

St. Maximilian Kolbe publishes its own weekly bulletin, printed by Bon Venture (sponsor page 6497, the 'Bulletins' menu link on its homepage). The survey noted "no publisher" because `probe url` finds no id on the homepage; the link is in the menu. The 1 November 2026 issue, page 1, lists the Mass schedule (Saturday 4:00 pm; Sunday 7:00, 9:00, 11:00 am and 1:00 pm; weekdays Tuesday and Saturday 7:00 am, other weekdays 8:30 am) and Rev. Stephen M. Piga as pastor. It names no other Toms River parish; the other RCCTR parishes have different pastors and Mass tables (see publication_reviews).
