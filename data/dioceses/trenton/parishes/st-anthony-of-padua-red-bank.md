## Publisher

The Oratory Church of St. Anthony of Padua, Red Bank publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 544 on church-bulletin.org), linked from the parish site as `jppc.net/onlinebulletins/544template.pdf`. The 1 November 2026 issue's masthead reads "The Oratory of Saint Philip Neri & The Oratory Church of Saint Anthony of Padua" and its Mass schedule is the Oratory's own (English, Spanish and Latin Masses); it names no other Cohort 16 parish. The pastor is an Oratorian (the site: "Father Alberto, C.O. - Provost", "Pastor"), so the pastor differs from St. James's Msgr. Joe, Nativity's and Holy Cross's. A single-parish row.

## Worship sites

One church, the Oratory Church of St. Anthony of Padua at 121 Bridge Avenue (see `st-anthony-of-padua-red-bank`). The Mass schedule lists no other place. The Oratory's "Parish Office & Center" is a separate building at 27 Oratory Way ("behind the Church and across the street on the corner of Oratory Way and Bridge Ave"), not a Mass site. GCatholic records the church as an oratory since 2016-05-29.

## Website and bulletin provider

The diocese's finder and GCatholic give `www.stanthonysofredbank.net`; it redirects to the live Google Sites page `web.stanthonysofredbank.net`, which links the John Patrick listing. The row is `other` with `page-pdfs` on the listing. `probe url` finds no publication id on the site; the listing is the bulletin.
