## Publisher

Our Lady of Hope Parish (est. 2018, the merger of St. Jerome, West Long Branch and St. Mary, Deal planned in the January 2017 decisions) publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 555). The 1 November 2026 issue's page 2 gives one Mass schedule for both churches and page 3 the Mass intentions for both, with Rev. Mark Nillo as pastor. It names no other parish. The parish site links only listing 555, so the row reads the listing.

## Worship sites

Both former parish churches hold Mass: St. Jerome (`st-jerome-west-long-branch`) and St. Mary, Deal (`st-mary-deal`). The merger ended the parishes, not the churches. The cohort-list note that St. Mary, Deal may be seasonal is not what this issue shows: its November schedule gives Deal Saturday 5 PM, Sunday 8 and 10 AM and Monday-Wednesday 9 AM, and Mass intentions for 31 October-8 November at SMC. GCatholic's saintjeromechurch.org is an unrelated spam site (a joke 'church' blog), not the parish.
