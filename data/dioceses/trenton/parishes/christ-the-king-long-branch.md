## Publisher

Christ the King Parish (Long Branch) publishes its own weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 539 on church-bulletin.org). The 1 November 2026 issue's masthead (page 1, read from a render because every recent issue is image-only) names the parish, its two churches, Our Lady Star of the Sea and Holy Trinity, and the pastor, Rev. Javier A. Diaz. It names no other parish. Cohort 17 also holds Our Lady of Hope (West Long Branch) and St. Michael (West End), each of which prints its own bulletin (see their rows), so this is a single-parish row by the *Rules settled*.

## Worship sites

Two churches: Our Lady Star of the Sea (`our-lady-star-of-the-sea-long-branch`, Chelsea Ave and 2nd Ave) and Holy Trinity (`holy-trinity-long-branch`, 408 Prospect St), both with weekend and weekday Masses in the masthead. GCatholic lists them as separate churches grouped under 'Christ the King Parish' (roster gcatholic:18697, gcatholic:18803). The masthead also gives a 'Chapel of the Sacred Heart of Jesus & Mary' at 111 Chelsea Ave, open 24/7 for Perpetual Adoration, with no Mass listed: not a church row.

## Website and bulletin provider

cklb.org is the parish's WordPress site; its own bulletin page is a week behind the John Patrick listing, so the row reads the listing (`page-pdfs https://church-bulletin.org/539`). GCatholic's christthekingparishlongbranch.org does not resolve (DNS, 2026-10-30).
