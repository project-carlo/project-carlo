The latest, 25 October 2026 (page 2): "31 Asbury Rd, Farmingdale, NJ 07727 · 732-938-5375", the weekend Mass schedule and the chapel's weekday Masses; pastor Rev. Angelito I. Anarcon. It names St. Catherine of Siena alone. ## Worship sites

Two: the church at 31 Asbury Road (`st-catherine-of-siena-farmingdale`) and St. Catherine's Chapel on West Main Street (`st-catherine-s-chapel-farmingdale`). The weekend Masses are at the church (the parish office is at 31 Asbury Rd).
