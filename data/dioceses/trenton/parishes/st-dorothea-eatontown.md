## Publisher

St. Dorothea publishes its own weekly bulletin, PDFs uploaded to WeConnect (uploads.weconnect.com) and linked in order from the homepage. The 25 October 2026 masthead gives 240 Broad Street, Eatontown and names Rev. Michael Sullivan as pastor and Rev. Andrés Serna as parochial vicar, the same two priests as Precious Blood's bulletin: the linkage the January 2017 decisions planned (by 1 July 2019) has happened. It is a shared pastor, not a shared bulletin: each parish prints its own file with its own masthead, Mass intentions and schedule, so the rows stay separate. Page 3 of both prints a combined table of the two parishes' Masses headed 'OUR LINKED PARISHES'; it adds no church to either. Immaculate Conception, Eatontown (the Korean community, same cohort) publishes its own Korean-language bulletin and is not part of this one.
