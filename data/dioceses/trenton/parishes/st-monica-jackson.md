## Publisher

The Church of St. Monica (Jackson) publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 649 on church-bulletin.org) and posts the same PDF on its own site. The 1 November 2026 issue (text layer) is headed "CHURCH OF SAINT MONICA", names Rev. Alex Enriquez as pastor and gives Masses Monday to Thursday 9 am, Saturday 4 pm and Sunday 9 am and 11 am. It names this parish alone. The other Jackson parish, St. Aloysius (listing 640, a different pastor and masthead), is a separate bulletin.

## Worship sites

One church, 679 W. Veterans Highway (see `st-monica-jackson`). The 2017 *Faith in Our Future* decisions put "Status of St. Monica ... to be reviewed periodically, beginning July 1, 2018"; the 2024 cohort list still lists the parish and its bulletin is current in late 2026, so nothing has closed it.

saintmonica.com is live; its Bulletin page lists weekly posts to 25 October 2026. Its 25 October PDF is byte-identical to the John Patrick listing's, which already has 1 November, so the row reads the listing and keeps the parish's own page as the second channel.
