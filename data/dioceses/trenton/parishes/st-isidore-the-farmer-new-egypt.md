## Publisher

The Parish of St. Isidore the Farmer publishes one weekly bulletin through John Patrick Publishing (listing 690 on church-bulletin.org), linked from the parish homepage. The 1 November 2026 issue is image-only; rendered page 1 is headed 'Parish of St. Isidore the Farmer' and gives one Mass schedule for the St. Andrew Worship Site (Jobstown), the Assumption Worship Site (Wrightstown) and daily Mass at the Assumption Chapel (New Egypt). The parish site has no bulletin archive of its own; its bulletin link goes to the listing.

The 2017 decisions (https://dioceseoftrenton.org/decisions, Cohort 4, item 2) planned a linkage of St. Andrew and Assumption by 1 July 2017 and a merger by 1 July 2018; the homepage's 'One Parish' and this masthead show the merger happened. The July 2024 cohort list names it 'St. Isidore the Farmer, Wrightstown', the finder 'St. Isidore the Farmer Parish, 76 Evergreen Rd, New Egypt'. GCatholic still lists St. Andrew, Jobstown and Assumption, Wrightstown as separate churches (stale parishes; the churches still hold Mass).

## Worship sites

Three places hold public Mass in the 1 November 2026 bulletin: St. Andrew, 2489 Monmouth Road, Jobstown (`st-andrew-jobstown`); Assumption, 28 Monmouth Road, Wrightstown (`assumption-wrightstown`); and the Assumption Chapel at the parish office, 76 Evergreen Road, New Egypt (`assumption-chapel-new-egypt`), where weekday Mass is said. The site's Locations page lists all three, with the office, as 'Where to find us'. The chapel has its own street address (the office's), so it is a church row, unlike a chapel with no address of its own on a church campus.

## Website and bulletin provider

saintisidoreparish.church is the finder's website and is current; GCatholic's standrewsjobstown.org has no DNS and churchoftheassumption.com was not tried in the browser (the finder's site is the parish's). The row reads the printer's listing.
