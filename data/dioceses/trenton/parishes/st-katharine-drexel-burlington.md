## Publisher

St. Katharine Drexel Parish, Burlington (Cohort 5). It publishes its own bulletin through ParishesOnline/LPi (container 03/4122). The 1 November 2026 masthead names 'The Parish of St. Katharine Drexel, Burlington, New Jersey' alone, pastor Rev. Jerome Guld. It does not mention Mary, Mother of the Church, Bordentown, which has its own container (03/4119), pastor (Rev. Martin O’Reilly) and masthead; the 2017 decisions put only Sts. Francis and Clare with St. Mary on the linkage and merger track, and made the three Cohort 5 parishes collaborative.

## Worship sites

Two churches, both in the 1 November 2026 Mass schedule: All Saints (502 High Street) and St. Paul (223 E. Union Street). The parish was formed from All Saints and St. Paul (GCatholic lists both with group 'St. Katharine Drexel Parish'). The parish also runs St. Paul School and two cemeteries (Laurel Hill Cemetery and St. Paul Cemetery, site menu): not Mass sites.
