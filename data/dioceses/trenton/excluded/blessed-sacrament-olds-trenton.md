## Why it is excluded

**Blessed Sacrament (Blessed Sacrament-Our Lady of the Divine Shepherd), 716 Bellevue Avenue, Trenton: closed, last Sunday Mass 24 June 2018.** The Monitor's story on the merger says the decision was 'to merge the two Trenton parishes and close the Bellevue Avenue church' and that 'On June 24, during the last Sunday Mass to be celebrated in Blessed Sacrament Church, a letter from Bishop O'Connell was shared'; the parish became part of Sacred Heart on 1 July. I dated the story to 2018, not the 2019 REST date, because the 2017 decisions set the merger 'no later than July 1, 2018' and 24 June 2018 was a Sunday (24 June 2019 was a Monday). The Sunday Gospel Mass moved to Sacred Heart's Holy Cross Church, which is still its 10 AM Mass in the 2026 bulletin and Worship Schedule. Sacred Heart's current bulletin lists no Mass at Bellevue Avenue.

GCatholic still lists the church (18696, with the dead cbs-olds.org site) and John Patrick's listing 583 has no editions.

## What would change this

A current bulletin or schedule listing a public Mass at 716 Bellevue Avenue (the building appears on OSM as a church; a later reopening or use by another community is not excluded by what I read).
