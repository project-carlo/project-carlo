## Why it is excluded

St. Mary, 181 Norman Ave., Roebling is a Romanian Greek Catholic church (GCatholic rite: Romanian; the batch brief's mention of 'St. Mary (Romanian)' agrees). The Romanian Catholic Eparchy of St. George in Canton's parish finder (read 2026-10-30) lists 'St. Mary Church, Roebling' at 181 Norman Avenue, ZIP+4 08554-1125, with a mailing address at St. Basil's in Trenton and St. Basil's phone, (609) 695-6093 (GCatholic gives the same number for both). It is a Romanian Catholic church served from St. Basil, Trenton, not a church of the Diocese of Trenton. It is not the Latin parish church Holy Assumption, Roebling (1290 Hornberger Avenue; `churches/holy-assumption-roebling`). I did not look for a bulletin.
