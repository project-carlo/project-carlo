## Why it is excluded

St. Mary, 411 Adeline St., Trenton is a Byzantine (Ruthenian) Catholic church, GCatholic's 'St. Mary (Ruthenian)'. The Eparchy of Passaic's parish finder lists it as 'Assumption of the Virgin Mary Byzantine Catholic Church' at the same address (ZIP+4 08611-2513) and phone (609-394-5004), and also lists a combined 'Greater Trenton Byzantine Catholic Churches' for Trenton and Roebling. The finder is a JavaScript page, so the entry is a `note`, not a quote. It is not a church of the Diocese of Trenton. Its site `stmarybyzcatholictrenton.org` redirects to `gtbcc.org` (the greater-Trenton site shared with St. Nicholas, Roebling), which a later effort would read for a bulletin; I did not.
