## Why it is excluded

**Holy Spirit Church, 705 2nd Ave, Asbury Park: closed, last Mass 6 June 2021.** The Monitor's 11 June 2021 report says that "On June 6, hundreds of faithful ... gathered for the closing Mass of the venerable church", on the Solemnity of the Body and Blood of Christ (Sunday 6 June 2021). It followed the pastor's October 2020 announcement of the closure and sale (the Monitor, 4 December 2020), which said the parish would consolidate its Masses at Our Lady of Mount Carmel Church. The parish's 1 November 2026 bulletin names Our Lady of Mt. Carmel as its only worship site and never mentions Holy Spirit.

The sale: the 2020 article expected the sale of the church and convent to take 'months or years'; I found no source on whether it has closed. That doesn't affect the exclusion, because the closing Mass is dated by the news report and the current bulletin lists no Mass there.

GCatholic still lists the church (as part of Mother of Mercy Parish), which is a stale entry; the diocese's finder does not list it.

## What would change this

A bulletin or schedule that lists a Mass at 705 2nd Avenue again (the 2020 plan mentioned a possible 'Holy Spirit Chapel' in Mount Carmel's lower level, which would be at Mount Carmel's address, not here).
