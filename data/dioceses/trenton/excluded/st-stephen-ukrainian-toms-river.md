## Why it is excluded

St. Stephen, 1344 White Oak Bottom Rd., Toms River is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic parish: GCatholic's rite is Ukrainian, and the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia's parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-30) has it at the same address and phone as GCatholic (732-505-6053). It is open, with a Sunday liturgy at 9:00 AM in English and 10:30 AM in Ukrainian, but it is not a church of the Diocese of Trenton.

It publishes its own bulletin: `ststephenchurch.us` redirects to `ststephenchurch.us/staging/`, which links `staging/pdf/Bul.<Month><d>,<yyyy>.pdf` (`probe url`, 2026-10-30; the newest was 25 October 2026). The batch brief points to it for that reason. The bulletin is mostly in Ukrainian. If the dataset ever takes Ukrainian Catholic parishes, this is the source for a parish row.


## What would change this

Nothing about the diocese: it is not a church of the Diocese of Trenton. It could be added if the dataset ever covers Eastern Catholic eparchies; its own bulletin source is noted above.
