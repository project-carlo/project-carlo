## Why it is excluded

St. Josaphat, 1195 Deutz Ave., Trenton is a Ukrainian Greek Catholic parish. GCatholic gives its rite as Ukrainian, and the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia's own parish list (ukrcatholic.org/parishes, read 2026-10-30 with `probe get`) carries it at the same address and phone as GCatholic (609-695-3771). It is not a parish of the Diocese of Trenton, which does not list it. GCatholic's website for it, ukrcathchurch.com, does not resolve (`probe url`, 2026-10-30: DEAD-DNS); the archeparchy's list gives only an email address (trenton@ukrcatholic.org). I did not look for a bulletin: the church is outside the diocese.
