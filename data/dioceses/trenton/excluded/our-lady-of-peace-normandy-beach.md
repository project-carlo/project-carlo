## Why it is excluded

**Our Lady of Peace, Route 35 and 7th Avenue, Normandy Beach (Brick Township): closed after Superstorm Sandy (2012) and demolished in 2016.** Shorebeat's 18 May 2016 story says crews had begun 'razing the former Our Lady Of Peace Catholic church', which St. Pio of Pietrelcina had drawn down to seasonal use and which 'was heavily damaged during Superstorm Sandy and never reopened'; the site was approved for 21 houses. `last_mass` is the year only: Sandy struck on 29 October 2012 and no source gives the last Mass's date. St. Pio's August and November 2026 bulletins and its Mass page list no Normandy Beach church.

This record was in `open/` until the hand refresh of 2026-11-07 found the story by web search. GCatholic (18821) still lists the church.

## What would change this

Nothing short of a new church on the site: the building is gone.
