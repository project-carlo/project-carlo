## Why it is excluded

St. Nicholas, 191 Norman Ave., Roebling is a Byzantine (Ruthenian) Catholic church (GCatholic rite: Ruthenian). The Eparchy of Passaic's finder (read 2026-10-30, rendered) lists 'Saint Nicholas Byzantine Catholic Church' at the same street address, with St. Mary, Trenton's phone (609-394-5004); the finder prints ZIP 08854 where GCatholic and the Romanian eparchy's list give Roebling as 08554, which I take for a typo in the finder. It sits next door to the Romanian St. Mary (181 Norman Ave.). It is not a church of the Diocese of Trenton, and its site redirects to `gtbcc.org`. I did not look for a bulletin.
