## Why it is excluded

St. Peter, Riverside was one of the two churches of Jesus the Good Shepherd Parish (formed 2008 from St. Peter and St. Joseph, Beverly). The January 2017 *Faith in Our Future* decision said the parish 'will suspend all operations at St. Peter Church and will use St. Joseph Church, Beverly, only, no later than July 1, 2017'. The Monitor reports the closing Mass on 25 June 2017 and that operations were suspended at the end of June 2017. The parish's current bulletin and site list Mass at 805 Warren Street, Beverly only. GCatholic's St. Peter entry (and the finder's pin for the parish, which sits at this site) are the stale remainders.

Other Riverside Catholic worship sites are not this building: St. Casimir, Riverside belongs to Resurrection Parish, Delran (own row).
